Quick Read for Judges — One Claim, Five Steps

The Claim: Jing-Zhang AI Commons transforms the century-old railway corridor into an open verification infrastructure for AI innovation — using a novel "Shaft Collaboration Protocol" borrowed from mountain tunnel engineering.

Five Steps: Shaft Opening → Parallel Verification → Evidence Accumulation → Public Evaluation → Continuous Backfill

Core Structure: One Spine · Three Cores · Two Wings · Multiple Chains

Element Description
One Spine Jing-Zhang Railway Heritage Park — 9km north-south public vitality corridor
Three Cores North: Zhongzhiyuan (R&D & Trust Verification) · Center: AI Origin Community (Transformation & Talent) · South: Dazhongsi (Application & International Reception)
Two Wings West: Zhongguancun Technology Services · East: Xiaoyuehe Ecological Wing
Multiple Chains East-west cross-links: universities, communities, industrial parks, transit stations

Shaft Collaboration Protocol

Borrowing from the Badaling Tunnel's vertical shaft excavation method — where workers open multiple vertical shafts along a long tunnel to create parallel work faces — we propose transforming closed AI R&D into an open, verifiable, and accountable innovation process.

Four Verification Shafts: Open Evaluation · Low-Speed Testing · Data Sandbox · Community Deliberation

Three Tiers: L1 Disclosure (public red-line definitions & annual publication) · L2 Controlled Testing (limited-speed verification with physical safety backup) · L3 Bounded Operations (public service delivery with expiration & exit triggers)

Annual Cycle: Spring (Shaft Opening) → Summer (Verification) → Autumn (Commons Week & Global Developer Festival) → Winter (Backfill Review)

Three Key Areas

Zhongzhiyuan — AI Innovation Accelerator (North)

R&D & Trust Verification Core. Open Engineering Lab with garden testing courtyard, open model evaluation, low-speed robot testing, shared pilot interfaces, ground-floor exhibition corridor. Physical separation between closed testing and public recreation.

AI Origin Community — Transformation Hub (Center)

Transformation & Talent Incubation Core. The "first kilometer" from campus innovation to urban service. Open-source release, achievement incubation, IP legal clinic, youth collaboration, and talent community spaces.

Dazhongsi — AI Industry Hub (South)

Application & International Reception Core. Four-quadrant station portal integration. AI agents, intelligent terminals, digital content industries. A "city living room" where non-consumption stays are welcome. International collaboration and accessibility services.

JZ-VP (Jing-Zhang Verification Protocol)

Three formalized governance tiers: - L1 Disclosure: Annual AI innovation zone safety red-line publication; open decision-data-code chains - L2 Controlled Testing: Limited speed/geo/perception range; physical safety backup; operator can terminate at any time - L3 Bounded Operations: Public service delivery with explicit expiration dates and exit triggers

Urban Verification Economics

VCI (Verified Competitiveness Index), VDR (Verified Deployment Rate), TFC (Trust Failure Cost), and error-correction cost curves. All numerical examples are SYNTHETIC TEST VECTORS, not actual performance data.

Shaft Spatial Grammar

Seven shaft grammar units translate protocol into urban form: shaft mouth (public entry), shaft tunnel (verification corridor), shaft barrel (data channel), shaft platform (review station), shaft eye (observation point), shaft marker (public registry), shaft skirt (community interface).

Governance & Anti-Capture

JZ-GPoV (Governance Proof of Verification), distributed council, three-tier dispute mechanism, capture stress testing, equivalent mutual recognition, and non-exclusive exit collectively constrain rules, budgets, registries, and emergency powers.

AI Nativity & Verifiability

AI participation in public decisions has strict boundaries: health, education, legal, transportation, and public resource judgments require responsible persons, human review, explanation, appeal, and exit channels. Physical autonomous systems are limited to verified operating envelopes with safety operator override.

Land Use, Transportation & Environment

Five land use categories per GB50137-2011: Educational/Research (A3), Commercial (B), Park Green (G1), Public Administration (A). North-south slow-traffic spine along heritage park; east-west cross-links at university/community/park/station nodes. Blue-green space network with riverfront boardwalks, pocket parks, and ecological monitoring.
