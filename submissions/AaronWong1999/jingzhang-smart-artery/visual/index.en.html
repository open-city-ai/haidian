Global Cases - 7 AI ecosystem references

  • Station F (Paris, FR) — An abandoned station repurposed into the largest startup community; shows railway heritage buildings can become AI incubation and open-collaboration spaces.
  • MaRS Discovery District (Toronto, CA) — An innovation district adjacent to hospital-university-industry; shows medical-AI scenarios can land nearby.
  • Kendall Square (Boston, US) — A high-density innovation block around MIT; shows a near-campus 'AI origin' talent zone and conversion area.
  • Hetao S&T Zone (Shenzhen, CN) — Institutional cross-border openness; shows compliant circulation mechanisms for data factors and cross-border research.

Culture Narrative

Heritage: Jingzhang Railway (Zhan Tianyou, 1909; China's first trunk railway independently designed by Chinese engineers)

Innovation: Zhongguancun's reform-and-opening innovation culture

AI culture: A new AI culture (human-machine collaboration, open, evolvable)

Storyline: The spatial narrative departs from the 'origin' at Qinghuayuan Station and travels along the railway heritage toward the future: a three-stage progression of cultural vein (history) - intelligence vein (innovation) - living vein (human-centered); the wayfinding and signage system is independent of the overall logo, and cultural marks are not confused with the belt's logo.
