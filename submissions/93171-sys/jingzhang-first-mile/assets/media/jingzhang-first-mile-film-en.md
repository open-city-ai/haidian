# Jingzhang First-Mile Line — English narration

1. A century ago, the Jing Zhang Railway proved that China could build its own railway. Today, we ask: how can artificial intelligence truly enter urban life?
2. The challenge is not only the model. It is finding the first real user and closing a loop that is usable, governable, and ready to procure.
3. At Zhong Zhi Yuan, product readiness, data checks, and accountability are completed step by step before technology enters real settings.
4. At Artificial Intelligence Origin, residents bring real tasks. Risk triggers an immediate human handoff. The full process enters the First-Mile Passport.
5. Da Zhong Si carries reuse and the first order. Feedback returns to product and governance, so innovation does not stop at a showcase.
6. In ninety days, baseline, first use, handoff, and reuse pass gates zero through four. Let the century-old railway become artificial intelligence's first mile into everyday life.
