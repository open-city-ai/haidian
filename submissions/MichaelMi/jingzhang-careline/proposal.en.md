# Jingzhang Careline

> **CARE BEFORE AUTOMATION.** AI should first learn how to care for people, public space and its own long-term maintenance.

Every spatial, policy, event and operating move in this submission is an open co-creation proposal for professional teams to develop. It is not statutory planning, government approval or an implementation commitment.

## Design Basis and Source List

The proposal uses the official open-call announcement, cleared Agent taskbook, local professional-standard snapshots and the repository source registry. Submitted site and key-area polygons are provisional constraints only; official polygons, controls, ownership, heritage, transport and municipal data remain missing. ## Three-Level Scope Framework

At the 43.6 km² coordinated research level, Careline links research, industry, talent, data, computing, public needs and human judgment. At the approximately 11.4 km² overall design level, it creates one care spine, three care yards and six east-west stitches. At the approximately 368.4 ha key-area level, it tests three complementary prototypes. [depth:three_level_scope_framework] [depth:overall_spatial_structure] [metric:site_area_sqm]

## Coordinated Research Area: Industry and Future City Research

The identity uses an open rail gauge: parallel lines stand for technical performance and public value, while the opening reserves human judgment. Seven mechanisms are studied without copying their forms: NIST AI RMF, EU TEF, Punggol Digital District, Barcelona Innova Lab, Smart Kalasatama, UK AISI and JRC Living Labs. They inform staged testing, public problem definition, independent evaluation and living-lab participation. ## Overall Design Area: Urban Renewal and Regulatory-Plan-Level Urban Design

Eighteen shared-edge land-use units fully cover the provisional site and place research, education, living, commercial service, green space and public judgment side by side. Reversible 90-day prototypes precede expansion. Concept building footprints are capacity tests, not existing buildings or parcel-level decisions. FAR, height and road redlines remain unknown. ## Detailed Design of Key Areas

Dazhongsi WELCOME is an age-inclusive arrival room with accessible multilingual guidance and visible human service. Beijing AI Origin CO-CREATE puts university research, resident knowledge, intergenerational learning and repair records at one table. Zhongzhiyuan STEWARD provides virtual, controlled and limited-real-world gates for models, robots, edge devices and inclusive interfaces. Exact locations require official polygons and professional review. ## Transport, Rail, Municipal Infrastructure, and Public Services

One north-south Careline Walk and six east-west stitches express connectivity intent, not engineering alignments. Basic toilets, drinking water, seating, shade, lighting, maternal facilities and accessible circulation remain available without AI. Devices must document ownership, energy, offline behavior, human replacement and retirement. ## Blue-Green Network, Public Space, and Urban Character

Green and public spaces combine walking, shade, rainwater, everyday activity, explanation, co-creation and repair. Four low-volume pilgrimage and honor nodes are proposed: Age-Inclusive Response Desk, Urban Technology Repair Archive, Trusted Stewardship Gate and Care Contribution Gauge. They avoid third-party logos and await heritage, green-line, blue-line, traffic and ownership review. ## Renewal Projects, Implementation Policy, and Phasing

The sequence is: complete official data; deploy reversible public interfaces; audit six mobility stitches; run four controlled industry tests; archive repairs and contributions; then hold an annual Careline Week subject to separate permissions and resources. Phasing encodes learning gates, not government schedules or investment commitments.
