Jingzhang Careline

> **CARE BEFORE AUTOMATION.** AI should first learn how to care for people, public space and its own long-term maintenance.

Every spatial, policy, event and operating move in this submission is an open co-creation proposal for professional teams to develop. It is not statutory planning, government approval or an implementation commitment.

Three-Level Scope Framework

At the 43.6 km² coordinated research level, Careline links research, industry, talent, data, computing, public needs and human judgment. At the approximately 11.4 km² overall design level, it creates one care spine, three care yards and six east-west stitches. At the approximately 368.4 ha key-area level, it tests three complementary prototypes. [depth:three_level_scope_framework] [depth:overall_spatial_structure] [metric:site_area_sqm]

Detailed Design of Key Areas

Dazhongsi WELCOME is an age-inclusive arrival room with accessible multilingual guidance and visible human service. Beijing AI Origin CO-CREATE puts university research, resident knowledge, intergenerational learning and repair records at one table. Zhongzhiyuan STEWARD provides virtual, controlled and limited-real-world gates for models, robots, edge devices and inclusive interfaces. Transport, Rail, Municipal Infrastructure, and Public Services

One north-south Careline Walk and six east-west stitches express connectivity intent, not engineering alignments. Basic toilets, drinking water, seating, shade, lighting, maternal facilities and accessible circulation remain available without AI. Devices must document ownership, energy, offline behavior, human replacement and retirement. Blue-Green Network, Public Space, and Urban Character

Green and public spaces combine walking, shade, rainwater, everyday activity, explanation, co-creation and repair. Four low-volume pilgrimage and honor nodes are proposed: Age-Inclusive Response Desk, Urban Technology Repair Archive, Trusted Stewardship Gate and Care Contribution Gauge. They avoid third-party logos and await heritage, green-line, blue-line, traffic and ownership review. Renewal Projects, Implementation Policy, and Phasing

The sequence is: complete official data; deploy reversible public interfaces; audit six mobility stitches; run four controlled industry tests; archive repairs and contributions; then hold an annual Careline Week subject to separate permissions and resources. Phasing encodes learning gates, not government schedules or investment commitments. Risk, Copyright, and Compliance

Main risks are mistaking provisional geometry for official controls, interpreting care as certification, test spillover, digital exclusion, automation overreach, historical distortion and unfunded operations. All scenarios preserve human responsibility, non-digital access, time limits and retirement.
