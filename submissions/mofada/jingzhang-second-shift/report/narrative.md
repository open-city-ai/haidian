# 京张第二班 / JING-ZHANG SECOND SHIFT

本包把夜班、夜归与维护者放到AI创新城市的中心：安全回家、热饭、休息、清楚交接、人工接管和低扰动测试。所有空间与项目均为概念性建议，采用临时粗略边界；官方数据到位后须整体复算。

This package centres night workers, late returners and maintainers in an AI innovation district through safe journeys home, warm food, rest, clear handovers, human takeover and low-disturbance tests. All spaces and projects are conceptual and use provisional geometry; official data trigger full recalculation.
