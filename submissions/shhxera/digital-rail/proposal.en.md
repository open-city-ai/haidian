### Overall Concept: Digital Rail

**"Digital Rail: from the Jing-Zhang Railway to an AI Neural Belt."** In 1909 Zhan Tianyou designed the Jing-Zhang Railway — China's first trunk line designed and surveyed independently, famous for the herringbone (人-shaped) switchback over Badaling. A century later, the same land lays a new kind of track: the **Digital Rail**. The Jing-Zhang Heritage Park becomes the **Memory Rail** (the historical and public spine), while data flows, compute flows and agents run along it as the **AI Neural Belt**. The railway once moved trains and people; the Digital Rail moves data, models and scenarios.

Spatial structure: **One Rail · Three Stations · Two Wings.**

- **One Rail**: the Memory Rail — the heritage park vitality belt: a north-south spine of slow mobility, public space and scenarios, stitching east-west and linking north-south (`geometry/roads.geojson#ROAD-001`).
- **Three Stations** (the three key areas, each with one theme, one landmark, one set of scenarios):
  - **Zhongzhi Station (Acceleration)** — Zhongzhiyuan AI Acceleration Area: full-stack innovation (`geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-001`);
  - **Origin Station (Source)** — Beijing AI Origin Community: open source, co-creation, talent vitality (`geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-002`);
  - **Bell Station (Pendulum)** — Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster: industry clustering and AI health (`geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-003`).
- **Two Wings**: the Zhongguancun Technology Service Wing as the **Traction Wing** (capital, IP, services — the locomotive), and the Xiaoyuehe Scenario Wing as the **Vista Wing** (AI living experience — the scenic carriage).

The naming system, visual identity and logo direction use the "station" vocabulary — platform, ticket, timetable, signal, switch — reading the belt as a never-stopping "timetable".

## Coordinated Research Area: Industry and Future City Study (43.6 km², provisional)

An innovation chain of five segments — **university origin → open-source collaboration → enterprise conversion → public experience → global communication** — translated into five flows along the Digital Rail: talent, data, compute, capital, scenarios.

Six public benchmark cases inform differentiation (not statutory evidence): Silicon Valley (university origin + capital), Zhongguancun Software Park/Shangdi (density, commuting tides), London King's Cross (railway-heritage renewal into an innovation quarter — the closest model for the Memory Rail), Singapore one-north (vertical mixing, green networks), Hangzhou Yunqi Town (developer community and event-driven branding), Seoul DMC (digital infrastructure and street-level experience spillover).

Regional synergies (conceptual): north to Shangdi/Yongfeng R&D clusters; south via Beijing North Station to the CBD (commuter pendulum smoothed by the Digital Rail shuttle ring); west to Wanliu/Century City residential groups; east to the Xueyuan Road university corridor (Tsinghua, Beihang, BUPT, USTB); Beiwei Community as a work-live-innovate commuting triangle with Origin and Zhongzhi stations; Future Science City and Huairou Science City as upstream basic-research partners ("laboratory → incubator → urban scenario" complementarity); Beijing E-Town (Yizhuang) as the manufacturing leg of a "R&D at Jing-Zhang, manufacturing at E-Town" corridor; and a Jing-Jin-Ji regional vision along the Jing-Zhang HSR corridor (data, compute and cultural-tourism scenarios extending toward Hebei). The five required functions map to: full-stack innovation → Zhongzhi Station; world-class ecosystem → Origin Station; AI+ scenario paradigm → Bell Station and Vista Wing; intelligent vibrant city → Memory Rail and station living circles; global AI governance voice → Open-Source Plaza and AI ethics forum (conceptual, Origin Station).

## Overall Design Area: Renewal and Urban Design (11.4 km², provisional; measured 11.41 km²)

Four spatial moves: **east-west stitching** (≥6 "stitch points" — green bridges, underpasses, slow-priority crossings — across the ~320 m conceptual Memory Rail corridor, `geometry/green_space.geojson#GS-001`); **north-south through-connection** (the Digital Rail greenway spine, `geometry/roads.geojson#ROAD-001`, linking three station squares `geometry/public_space.geojson#PS-001/002/003`); **station-domain renewal** (each key area as "station core — platform — 15-minute living circle"); **blue-green network** (corridor + pocket parks + Xiaoyuehe waterfront; conceptual green total 316.1 ha, provisional).

**Keep/change/demolish (conceptual only):** keep heritage park, campus historic buildings, existing rail/stations, sound communities; change low-efficiency factories and old offices into R&D/incubator/mixed use; demolish only as tentative parcel consolidation around station cores — never an official commitment (property ownership and current-condition basemaps are missing; see `assumptions.json` A-CONTROLS-001).
