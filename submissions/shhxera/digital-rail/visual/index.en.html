Zhongzhiyuan AI Acceleration Area

Garden-style independent innovation quarter. Emphasis: national platforms, industry exhibition, Qinghe culture, external transport and low-carbon exchange space.

Beijing AI Origin Community

Campus-adjacent commercialization quarter. Emphasis: university origin innovation, open-source community, talent services, keep/change/demolish and station integration.

Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster

Urban intelligent-economy quarter. Emphasis: leading enterprises, agent-native business forms, commercial services, green-space compound use and four-quadrant connectivity.
