# 京脉智带 — 国际传播叙事（Narrative for International Audience）

## 中文核心叙事（40 字摘要）

京张铁路百年道廊，化作京脉智带：AI 自由集聚、开放共享、人本治理的桥梁城市客厅。

## English Core Narrative (40-word digest)

Along the old Beijing–Zhangjiakou railway corridor, the Jingmai AI Innovation Belt turns heritage tracks into a civic stage where AI thrives openly between people, code and the city.

## 中英对照愿景段落（Vision Statement, CN/EN）

- 中文：京脉智带是北京在城市更新中留给人工智能创新的一段“平等轨道”：开源根栈在城市中部生长，产业场景在城市中验证，公众在城市中参与、监督与共创。它既是一带风景，也是一套可复制、可演进的城市 AI 公共治理试验。
- English: The Jingmai AI Innovation Belt reserves a “shared track” for AI within Beijing’s urban renewal: open-source foundations grow downtown, industrial scenarios are validated in real places, and citizens participate, supervise, and co-create. It is at once a landscape and a reproducible model of public governance for AI in cities.
