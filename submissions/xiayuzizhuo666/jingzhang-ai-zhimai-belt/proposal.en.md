## Three-Level Scope Framework

The plan is organized according to the three-level scope defined in the announcement [source:OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT] [depth:three_level_scope_framework]:

**Coordinated Research Area (43.6 square kilometers)**: Extending north to the North Fifth Ring Road, east to the Jingzhang Expressway, south to West Straight Street, and west to Wanquan River Road, it covers the areas where the Three Zones and Two Wings of Haidian AI industry development are located. This layer addresses strategic issues such as the industrial ecosystem, future urban form, coordination and naming system of the Three Zones and Two Wings, as detailed in the "Coordinated Research Area: Industrial and Future Urban Form Research" section.

**Overall Design Area (11.4 square kilometers)**: The planning scope is set around the Jing-Zhang Heritage Park, encompassing the urban and industrial areas within 1-2 kilometers [data:geometry/site_boundary.geojson#SITE-001]. At this level, industrial strategies are translated into land use structure, transportation and utilities, blue-green Public Spaces, Urban Renewal, and architectural style control, achieving the depth of Regulatory Detailed Planning [standard:MOHURD-CONTROL-DETAILED-PLANNING] [source:STANDARD-MOHURD-CONTROL-PLANNING]. (Urban Design)

**Key-Area Detailed Design Area (368.4 hectares)**: Extending from north to south, it includes the Zhongzhiyuan AI Independent Innovation Acceleration Area (approximately 192.1 hectares), the Beijing AI Origin Community (approximately 104.3 hectares), and the Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster (approximately 72.0 hectares) [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#KEY-001] [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#KEY-002] [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#KEY-003]. This layer conducts detailed design for the three key areas to achieve the Urban Design depth of the Integrated Planning Implementation Plan, as detailed in the "Key-Area Detailed Design" chapter.

The three-level scope is not a collection of fragmented drawings, but a "strategic industry → spatial structure → detailed design" hierarchical transmission chain. Integrated research determines the relationship between the industrial chain and urban form, and the overall design translates the conclusions into land use and facilities. Key areas validate the implementability of specific sites, buildings, transportation, and AI scenarios [depth:three_level_scope_framework]. All spatial implementation recommendations in this plan are conceptual, and do not replace formal planning or constitute government approval conclusions [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK]. (Conceptual Recommendation)

## Coordinated Research Area: Industry and Future City Research

### 3.1 Overall Concept: "Zhi Mai" —— One Line and Two Orders, From Iron Pulse to Wisdom Pulse

The Jing-Zhang Railway Heritage Park corridor has carried out two significant 'infrastructure revolutions' over more than a century: the first epoch (1905-1909) was the **Iron Pulse Epoch** — led by Zhan Tianyou, it was China's first independently designed and constructed mainline railway, spanning approximately 201.2 kilometers, overcoming the hazardous Guan-Gou pass with a zigzag alignment and shaft construction methods [source:HISTORY-JINGZHANG-RAILWAY]; the second epoch (since the 21st century) is the **Smart Pulse Epoch** — where the core area of Haidian, passing through the corridor, evolved into a global AI innovation hub — from Zhongguancun Electronics Street to the National Independent Innovation Demonstration Zone, and finally to the future-oriented AI industry belt [source:HISTORY-ZHONGGUANCUN].

"Xian yi liang ji" interprets this physical corridor as **a spatial carrier of China's spirit of independent innovation**: the same geographical orientation has successively borne the industrial era's steel artery and the digital era's computational artery—**from iron veins to smart veins**. This is the overarching concept of this scheme, running through the naming system, spatial structure, cultural narrative, scene design, and operational mechanisms [depth:overall_spatial_structure] [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK].

**Civil Leap Translation System (Conceptual Recommendation · Cultural Brand Core)**: The proposal draws its theme from the three historical technological symbols of the Jing-Zhang Railway and reinterprets them into spatial and cultural language for the AI era, forming a narrative system that only the Jing-Zhang Railway can establish [source:HISTORY-JINGZHANG-RAILWAY]:

| Historical Symbol (1909) | Technical Implication | AI Era Transcoding (2026) | Spatial Anchoring |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Suzhou Maizi (Mileage and Grade Markers) | A Chinese indigenous number system, marking railway mileage and grades | 'Chinese Autonomous Technological Symbol' → Binary/Open Source Identifier System | Signage System Theme,LOGO Auxiliary Graphics, Alongline Information Kiosks (Conceptual Recommendation) |
| Zigzag Alignment (Qinglong Bridge) | Trade Length for Height, Finding the Optimal Solution within Constraints | An Innovative Methodological Metaphor—System Optimization under Resource Constraints | Governance Agent Design Philosophy, Spatial Structure "Z" Narrative (Conceptual Recommendation) |
| Turnouts and Signal System | Convergence and Scheduling of Multiple Lines | Coordinated Scheduling Logic for Multiple Stakeholders | Coordinated Circuits and Data Flow Network for the Three Zones and Two Wings (Conceptual Recommendation) |

The significance of this translation system: The Jing-Zhang Railway is **China's first major railway line built independently by Chinese people** (1905-1909, led by Zhan Tianyou, challenging the notion that "an engineer capable of building such a railway in China has not yet been born") [source:HISTORY-JINGZHANG-RAILWAY]. Its "self-innovative" gene aligns with the "self-innovative" mission of the AI industry in Haidian — the differentiated narrative of this proposal is **"a walkable civilization leapfrogging history"**: within 9 kilometers, simultaneously touching industrial revolution relics and the forefront of intelligent revolution, where the Suzhou numeral system transforms into binary, the zigzag alignment evolves into governance intelligent bodies, and railway switches become AI network topologies. This narrative cannot be transplanted onto any other geographical carrier.

**Logo and Visual Identity Direction** (Conceptual Recommendation): Use "One Line and Two Discipline" as the visual theme—a zigzag or curve that simultaneously suggests railway tracks and neural network connections, blending the geometric railway switch with AI network topology. The historical layer uses rust brown and copper green with industrial fonts, the innovation layer uses Zhongguancun blue and tech silver with modern sans-serif fonts, and the AI layer uses a gradient spectrum to express data flow. The three layers of information are distinguished through materials, colors, and graphics, forming a "three-layer readable" sign system. ### 3.3 Global Case Study of AI Innovation Ecosystem (7 examples)

Analyze 7 global AI Innovation Ecosystem case studies, distill transferrable mechanisms [source:CASE-GLOBAL-AI-ECOSYSTEM] [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK]:

**Case Study 1: Stanford Research Park in Silicon Valley** —— Over seven decades, the university and industry have deeply coupled, creating a positive feedback loop of "professors-entrepreneurial students-risk capital-technology companies"; the university releases research space through long-term leases rather than sales. Conversion Mechanism: Tsinghua/Northwestern/ release research space along the line through long-term lease rights, establishing a institutionalized channel for the conversion of research outcomes into entrepreneurship; setting up an "AI Sand Hill Road" form of capital aggregation district.

**Case Study Two: Kendall Square, Boston** —— MIT's "Endogenous Entrepreneurship Spillover," The Engine's "Patient Infrastructure" (special laboratories, clean rooms) and LabCentral's shared laboratory model. Conversion Mechanism: Integrate a "Hard Tech Shared Infrastructure Layer" into the AI Origin community, building shared computational laboratories, shared AI training data environments, and a shared embodied intelligence hardware workshop.

**Case Study Three: Singapore One-North** —— The government acts as an "ecological orchestrator," launching the LaunchPad startup cluster and the Kampong AI "work+live" AI community. Transformative mechanism: Establishing an "AI startup stage space adaptation" system (co-working spaces → independent labs → entire floors → standalone buildings), embedding an "office+residence integrated AI innovation community" pilot in Dazhongsi.

**Case Four: Shenzhen Nanshan** —— Full vertical integration of the entire supply chain, with a three-tier mechanism of "computing power dispatching platform + public data openness + scenario access," where "Moli Ying" selects based on the depth of technology rather than revenue. Transformable mechanism: Establish a Haidian version of the "AI Scenario Access List" release mechanism, using the Jing-Zhang Railway Heritage Park along the line as a real urban test field.

**Case Five: Tel Aviv** —— Military-Civilian Technology Diffusion Pipeline, Government as the "First Customer," and the City as a Test Bed. Transformative Mechanisms: The Haidian District Government opens urban governance scenarios to resident enterprises; establishes an "AI Product City Experiment Corridor" and an "AI Safety Test Field."

**Case Six: King's Cross Knowledge Quarter, London** —— a knowledge cluster reborn from a railway hub, a membership collaborative platform for Knowledge Quarter members, and high-density knowledge institutions with occasional collisions. Transformative mechanisms: set up 'Discipline Collision Points' along the main axis of the Jing-Zhang Heritage Park; establish the 'Jing-Zhang AI Knowledge Alliance'; and shape Beijing North Station into a 'Transportation Hub + Innovation Gateway'.

**Case Seven: Hangzhou Future Science City** —— Driven by large-scale scientific facilities, the "Innovation Ecosystem of the Ring Zhejiang Lab" and the "Model Domain Space" full-chain platform. Conversion Mechanism: Establishing the "Innovation Ecosystem of the Ring University Labs" and setting up the "Haidian AI Full-Stack Validation Platform" in Zhongzhiyuan. Forming a gradient sequence of "Source Points-Incubation Points-Acceleration Points-Industry Points" along the relic park.

### 3.4 AI Innovation Ecosystem Landscape

Seven cases collectively reveal the five core elements of a mature AI Innovation Ecosystem, forming a value cycle from "knowledge birth" to "global impact": **Academic Pioneering** (Tsinghua/Northwestern Polytechnical/Chinese Academy of Sciences, with labs to prototype workshops within a 15-minute walk) → **Open Source Collaboration** (Jing-Zhang AI Open Source Community Nodes, hackathon venues + government data openness) → **Entrepreneurial Transformation** (shared infrastructure reducing systemic risks for early-stage startups) → **Public Experience** (Jing-Zhang Heritage Park's linear Public Space embedded in an innovation corridor, a globally rare endowment) → **International Communication** (using AI governance as a differentiating competitive advantage in global discourse) [source:CASE-GLOBAL-AI-ECOSYSTEM].

### 4.4 Jing-Zhang Heritage Park Vitality Belt and Urban Character

### 5.1 Zhongzhiyuan AI Independent Innovation Acceleration Area (approximately 192.1 hectares)

**Location**: Garden-type Artificial Intelligence Innovation District, Full-Stack Independent AI Innovation System and AI Governance Global Discourse [source:OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT] [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#KEY-001].

**Design Intent (Addressing What Issue)**: Addressing the Fragmentation of AI R&D — If chips, frameworks, models, and applications are developed in different physical spaces, integration and validation are delayed, and compatibility issues are discovered too late. "Full-stack" is a statistical term rather than an engineering reality. This area is organized around a "validation loop" rather than a "technical tier": a validation unit consists of a chip adaptation room, a framework testing room, a model training room, and an application simulation room, all within a 2-minute walk, turning full-stack collaboration from a regulatory requirement into a spatial reality (Conceptual Recommendation) [source:CASE-GLOBAL-AI-ECOSYSTEM].

**Conceptual Mechanisms Suggested**:
1. "AI Full-Stack Validation Accelerator" —— providing computational infrastructure for the entire technology stack from chip to application, including adaptation of domestic chips, and compatibility testing of frameworks; 2. "Patient Infrastructure" —— drawing from the The Engine model, providing clean spaces, dedicated power cooling, and long-term leasing for AI hard technologies; 3. "AI Governance International Dialogue Center" —— a physical anchor for global discussions on AI governance rules and ethical standards; 4. "Autonomous Technology Certification" mechanism —— third-party evaluation of the degree of autonomy in the AI supply chain; 5. "Vertical Scenario Anchoring" —— focusing on strategic vertical directions such as AI + life sciences, AI + quantum information, and embodied intelligence [source:CASE-GLOBAL-AI-ECOSYSTEM].

**Spatial Concept**: Optimize layout by leveraging potential sites, integrating building, green spaces, and water systems, and exploring the Qinghe culture, to create an international, low-carbon, and green innovative interaction environment; investigate functional scenarios for green spaces serving AI development (such as AI technology demonstration gardens, outdoor test sites) [source:OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT].

### 5.2 Beijing AI Origin Community (approximately 104.3 hectares)

**Location**: School-adjacent AI Innovation District, centered around the pioneering achievements of Tsinghua, Peking, and the Chinese Academy of Sciences to build a globally leading AI Innovation Ecosystem [source:OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT] [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#KEY-002].

**Design Intent (Addressing What Issue)**: Addressing the issue of "concentration decay" in innovation—where the half-life of top conference papers is 6-12 months and the window for retaining outstanding graduates is 2-3 years. If these are diluted by non-innovative functions, the source advantage of innovation dissipates over time. This area aims to "compress the spatial and temporal distance" to maintain concentration: on a pedestrian corridor of approximately 800m between the Tsinghua Garden Station and Wudaokou Station, a cluster of "innovation micro-spaces" such as 24-hour study rooms, shared prototype workshops, and mini pitch halls. This cluster compresses the physical distance from "laboratory to first pitch" to a 10-minute walk (Conceptual Recommendation) [source:CASE-GLOBAL-AI-ECOSYSTEM].

**Conceptual Mechanism Suggestions**:
1. "15-Minute Innovation Circle" — centered around the Conceptual Origin Lab, with a sequence of spaces including a Concept Validation Center (5-minute walk), Prototype Workshop (8-minute walk), Seed Incubator (10-minute walk), and Early Accelerator (15-minute walk).
2. "Circular Innovation Community" — the Beijing AI Origin Community Alliance, a membership-based cross-institutional collaboration.
3. "Graduate Entrepreneurship Residency" — low-cost initial space for entrepreneurs who have graduated from Tsinghua and Peking Universities and the Chinese Academy of Sciences within the past five years.
4. "Open Source Cultural Landmark" — Open Source Square/Open Source Workshop, a symbolic space for the Global Open Source AI Developers Conference.

### 6.6 Urban Agent Governance (Differentiated Design)

For the official track "Urban Agent Governance" [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK], the proposal puts forward the **"Triple Ring Governance Agent Framework"** (Conceptual Recommendation, governance mechanism direction, not a system implementation commitment) — deepening from "Triple Layer Naming" to "Triple Ring Mechanism," with each ring defining an input-processing-output chain, making it amenable for professional teams to further develop [depth:ai_persona_and_scenarios]:

**Ring One·Cognitive Ring (Information Agent) —— 'One Policy, Multiple Readings'**: Input = planning schemes, policy documents, and citizen feedback (all publicly available sources); Processing = automatically generating readable summaries and differences for different stakeholders; Output = policy difference comparison table, stakeholder-specific summaries. Deepening direction: whitelist of corpus sources, traceability of summaries back to original clauses.

**Ring Two·Simulation Ring (Simulation Agent) —— "Simulate First, Decide Later"**: Input = Planning Scheme Change Draft + Urban Digital Twin Framework; Processing = Simulate impacts on traffic, environment, and sunlight, and output multi-scenario comparisons; Output = Multi-scenario Impact Report and Risk Alerts. Deepening Directions: Model input and output are auditable, scenario assumptions are explicitly stated, and this does not replace the statutory environmental impact assessment.

**Ring Three·Negotiation Ring (Negotiation Agent) —— 'AI-Assisted, Human Judgment'**: Input = Authorized desensitized citizen input from multiple channels; Processing = Identify areas of consensus and disagreement, generating a 'Negotiation Issue Package'; Output = List of issues for use in an in-person negotiation hosted by a human. Deepening direction: Traceable source of opinions, with human-hosted process not constrained by AI-generated content.

**Triple-Hinge Logic**: The Cognitive Loop establishes a "common factual basis" → the Inference Loop provides "decision impact prediction" → the Negotiation Loop organizes "human value adjudication" — AI provides cognitive and inferential capabilities, with the ultimate decision-making authority remaining with humans [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK].

**Principles of Governance (Suggested Approach)**: Human ultimate decision-making authority is non-transferable; AI recommendations must be explainable; affected parties have the right to request Human Review; AI processing does not replace statutory procedures (planning approval, environmental impact assessment, public hearing). It is suggested to set up public engagement nodes every 800m along the heritage park in the form of a "governance information kiosk," and to establish an "AI governance experience center" as a city smart agent governance display space for citizens to interact with [data:geometry/public_space.geojson]. This section provides a mechanism direction, but does not constitute a commitment to system construction or government arrangement [assumption:A-OPERATION-001]. (Urban Agent)
