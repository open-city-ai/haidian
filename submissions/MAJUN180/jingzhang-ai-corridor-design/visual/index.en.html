Key Areas

The three key areas must have industry positioning, spatial strategy, building and public-space actions, transport interfaces, and implementation risk notes.

key_areas.geojson

Zhongzhiyuan AI Indigenous Innovation Acceleration Zone

Garden-type indigenous innovation district. Highlights the national platform, industry display, Qinghe culture, external transport, and low-carbon exchange space.

Beijing AI Origin Community

Near-campus achievement-conversion district. Highlights university source innovation, open-source community, talent services, retain-renovate-demolish, and station integration.

Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster Zone

Urban-type smart-economy district. Highlights leading enterprises, agent new business formats, commercial services, green-space composite use, and four-quadrant intersection connectivity.
