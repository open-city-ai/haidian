Centennial Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt: From Railway Heritage to an AI-Native Innovation District

Within the 11.4 km² overall design scope, using the former Jing-Zhang railway as a blue-green spine, the three key areas (Zhongzhiyuan, Beijing AI Origin, Dazhongsi) are recombined into a world-class AI innovation belt. Named '京张智脉 · Rail-to-Brain', the proposal offers ten-plus AI scenario cards (3+ industry tests), five talent personas and three AI landmark candidates, and responds to the control-plan-depth urban design with recomputable metrics under a provisional boundary.

Study-scope industry and future-city research

Naming and logo

The official name is "京张智脉 · Rail-to-Brain". "Jing-Zhang" refers to the first trunk railway designed and built by Chinese people in 1909, a symbol of national engineering self-reliance 来源; "智脉" stresses the parallel of the linear railway heritage and the AI innovation chain; the subtitle "Rail-to-Brain" targets global developers and talent for English communication. The logo concept evolves "rail section → neural-network node": a navy base (#172235) with gold (#c79838) outlining the rail line and node strokes; the left rail profile (rail + ballast) becomes the input layer of a right-side dotted neural network — the gestalt "from rail to compute". Landmark logos are not to be built as approved works; they are VI and wayfinding candidates only 深度.

Three positionings and five functions

Per announcement 1.5(1), the proposal sets three positionings — AI-native innovation district, railway heritage activation, all-age-friendly (incl. accessibility) — and five functions — industry innovation, living services, cultural display, public interaction, ecological blue-green — each mapped to a land-use code 标准. Three areas, two wings synergy loop

The three areas (Zhongzhiyuan, Beijing AI Origin, Dazhongsi) and two wings (north wing to Shangdi–Xierqi AI compute belt, south wing to Zhongguancun core innovation axis) form a "R&D — translation — industry — living — display" loop 来源 · 深度. Zhongzhiyuan hosts national platforms, proof-of-concept and pilot lines; AI Origin hosts source innovation, open-source release and talent living; Dazhongsi hosts leading enterprises, new agent formats and data factors; the north wing takes compute scheduling and upstream R&D, the south wing takes application scenarios and brand communication.

Global AI innovation ecosystem cases (5–8)

1. San Francisco Mission Bay: started with university hospitals and biotech clusters, then transitioned to AI/data-science clusters. The proposal borrows its "public service before commercial development" sequencing, reworked as the Jing-Zhang version that leads with railway heritage.

2. Toronto Quayside (Sidewalk Labs): the lesson is to include the community and original residents in early co-creation rather than building a "tech enclave". The proposal puts along-the-line residents and the elderly first in scenario-card design 来源.

3. Boston Seaport Innovation District: binds public art and open space to the innovation brand. The Jing-Zhang belt borrows its "open-space-first" practice and treats the Jing-Zhang heritage park vitality belt as the primary public asset 深度.

4. London King's Cross / Knowledge Quarter: railway heritage regeneration drives a knowledge economy. The Jing-Zhang belt is structurally closest to it, but must avoid pure commercialisation and stress public welfare and open source 深度.

5. Singapore Punggol Digital District: a planned experiment of "enterprise + housing + digital infrastructure" in sync; the proposal borrows its phased integrated advancement 深度.

6. Seoul Sangam Digital Media City: government-led coupled with enterprise demand. The proposal keeps the government-led coordination advantage but writes the community in as a partner in the operating agreement.

7. Hangzhou Future Sci-Tech City / Alibaba Cloud Town: a local super-platform-driven model. The proposal turns platform influence into a parallel structure of "multiple national AI teams + multiple open-source communities" to avoid single-platform lock-in.

8. Shanghai Zhangjiang AI Island: a physical island plus an institutional island. The proposal makes the "Origin open-source island" the core of the AI Origin community, open to model evaluation and open-source governance.

Cultural narrative

The metaphor主线 is "rail — compute": the 1909 Jing-Zhang railway, China's first self-designed trunk railway, represents engineering self-reliance, precision, verifiable; the 2026 AI Origin represents open source, evolvable, shareable 来源. The two spirits meet in space at the Jing-Zhang Origin Station (open-source hall), Zhongzhi Cloud Hall (pilot workshop) and Wisdom Eye Tower (governance corridor), forming a three-act narrative of "memory — innovation — governance". Hutong culture, Beijing flavour and developer culture are juxtaposed; old station, old factory, old community against new cloud hall, new block, new bell tower — avoiding presenting conceptual landmarks as approved builds.
