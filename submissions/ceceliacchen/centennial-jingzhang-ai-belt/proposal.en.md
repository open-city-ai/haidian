## Three-level scope framework

The proposal follows the three nested levels: study scope (43.6 km²), overall design scope (11.4 km²) and key-area scope (368.4 ha) [source:OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT].

**Study scope (43.6 km²)** carries the world-class AI innovation ecosystem argument, the AI+transport and continuous green-space systems, naming/logo and long-term operations, corresponding to `design_depth_matrix.json#three_level_scope_framework` [depth:three_level_scope_framework]. The study covers the Qinghe–Dazhongsi section of the Jing-Zhang railway and reserves interface links to the Shangdi–Xierqi AI compute wing (north) and the Zhongguancun core (south) [depth:overall_spatial_structure].

**Overall design scope (11.4 km²)** corresponds to the provisional polygon of `geometry/site_boundary.geojson` and requires control-plan-depth urban design [standard:MOHURD-CONTROL-DETAILED-PLANNING] ·  [depth:land_use_layout]. Its area recomputed in EPSG:4548 is 11,412,825 m²; the difference stems from the roughness of the provisional boundary [metric:site_area_sqm]. Before the official redline, all areas and ratios are treated as directional and declared in `assumptions.json#A-CONTROLS-001`.

**Key-area scope (368.4 ha)** comprises three areas — Zhongzhiyuan (192.1 ha), Beijing AI Origin (104.3 ha) and Dazhongsi (72.0 ha) — corresponding to `geometry/key_areas.geojson` [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK] ·  [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-001] ·  [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-002] ·  [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-003]. The recomputed areas are 1,929,202 m² + 1,043,237 m² + 720,454 m² = 369.3 ha, directionally consistent with the announcement's 368.4 ha; the gap comes from the provisional boundary [metric:key_area_zhongzhiyuan_sqm] ·  [metric:key_area_beijing_ai_origin_sqm] ·  [metric:key_area_dazhongsi_sqm].

## Study-scope industry and future-city research

### Naming and logo

The official name is **"京张智脉 · Rail-to-Brain"**. "Jing-Zhang" refers to the first trunk railway designed and built by Chinese people in 1909, a symbol of national engineering self-reliance [source:OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT]; "智脉" stresses the parallel of the linear railway heritage and the AI innovation chain; the subtitle "Rail-to-Brain" targets global developers and talent for English communication. The logo concept evolves "rail section → neural-network node": a navy base (#172235) with gold (#c79838) outlining the rail line and node strokes; the left rail profile (rail + ballast) becomes the input layer of a right-side dotted neural network — the gestalt "from rail to compute". Landmark logos are not to be built as approved works; they are VI and wayfinding candidates only [depth:existing_conditions_diagnosis].

### Three positionings and five functions

Per announcement 1.5(1), the proposal sets three positionings — AI-native innovation district, railway heritage activation, all-age-friendly (incl. accessibility) — and five functions — industry innovation, living services, cultural display, public interaction, ecological blue-green — each mapped to a land-use code [standard:MNR-LAND-USE-CLASSIFICATION-GUIDE]. The three-positioning → five-function → land-use-code mapping is detailed in `standard_matrix.json` and used as the judgement baseline for every subsequent design [depth:three_level_scope_framework].

### Three areas, two wings synergy loop

The three areas (Zhongzhiyuan, Beijing AI Origin, Dazhongsi) and two wings (north wing to Shangdi–Xierqi AI compute belt, south wing to Zhongguancun core innovation axis) form a "R&D — translation — industry — living — display" loop [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK] ·  [depth:overall_spatial_structure]. Zhongzhiyuan hosts national platforms, proof-of-concept and pilot lines; AI Origin hosts source innovation, open-source release and talent living; Dazhongsi hosts leading enterprises, new agent formats and data factors; the north wing takes compute scheduling and upstream R&D, the south wing takes application scenarios and brand communication.

### Global AI innovation ecosystem cases (5–8)

1. **San Francisco Mission Bay**: started with university hospitals and biotech clusters, then transitioned to AI/data-science clusters. The proposal borrows its "public service before commercial development" sequencing, reworked as the Jing-Zhang version that leads with railway heritage.

2. **Toronto Quayside (Sidewalk Labs)**: the lesson is to include the community and original residents in early co-creation rather than building a "tech enclave". The proposal puts along-the-line residents and the elderly first in scenario-card design [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK].

3. **Boston Seaport Innovation District**: binds public art and open space to the innovation brand. The Jing-Zhang belt borrows its "open-space-first" practice and treats the Jing-Zhang heritage park vitality belt as the primary public asset [depth:blue_green_public_space].

4. **London King's Cross / Knowledge Quarter**: railway heritage regeneration drives a knowledge economy. The Jing-Zhang belt is structurally closest to it, but must avoid pure commercialisation and stress public welfare and open source [depth:heritage_regeneration_pattern].

5. **Singapore Punggol Digital District**: a planned experiment of "enterprise + housing + digital infrastructure" in sync; the proposal borrows its phased integrated advancement [depth:phasing_strategy].

6. **Seoul Sangam Digital Media City**: government-led coupled with enterprise demand. The proposal keeps the government-led coordination advantage but writes the community in as a partner in the operating agreement.

7. **Hangzhou Future Sci-Tech City / Alibaba Cloud Town**: a local super-platform-driven model. The proposal turns platform influence into a parallel structure of "multiple national AI teams + multiple open-source communities" to avoid single-platform lock-in.

8. **Shanghai Zhangjiang AI Island**: a physical island plus an institutional island. The proposal makes the "Origin open-source island" the core of the AI Origin community, open to model evaluation and open-source governance.

### AI full-stack autonomous innovation system

The six-layer "chip — compute — model — data — application — governance" structure lands in space: chip and compute layers in Zhongzhiyuan and the north wing; model layer in AI Origin; data layer in Dazhongsi; application layer spread across the three areas and the blue-green belt; governance layer in Dazhongsi and public space [depth:overall_spatial_structure] ·  [standard:GENERATIVE-AI-INTERIM-MEASURES].

### Zhongzhiyuan AI autonomous-innovation accelerator (192.9 ha)

Positioned as a national AI autonomous-innovation accelerator; lead industries are model/chip pilot, agent evaluation and open collaboration. Spatial structure: a garden-style autonomous-innovation block where the Jing-Zhang heritage park green vein crosses the area, forming a "central green axis + four innovation clusters". Building renewal centres on new smart-manufacturing workshops and renovated old factories, retaining a few railway-industrial-heritage workshops as AI exhibition and open-source museum [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-001] ·  [depth:retain_renovate_demolish]. Transport is integrated with the rail station, slow-first over vehicles. Public space stresses "pilot-as-show": the proof-of-concept workshop and open-source release hall double as public living rooms. AI scenarios: "pilot workshop + compute-network evaluation + proof-of-concept", mapped to NODE-01/02/03/04 [data:visual/assets/scenario-cards.json#NODE-01]. Implementation risk: tenure and the existing building age structure are to be confirmed; some land may fall in the Jing-Zhang heritage park protection belt [standard:BARRIER-FREE-ENVIRONMENT-LAW].

### Beijing AI Origin community (104.3 ha)

Positioned as a campus-adjacent AI source-innovation and open-source community, connecting Tsinghua, Peking, CAS and other universities [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK]. Spatial structure: "twin-track heritage belt + block-style open-source community". Building renewal mixes demolition/renovation/retention: retain railway-worker cultural relics, renovate old communities, build new talent apartments and open-source living rooms [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-002]. Transport achieves station integration and slow-gap repair, linking university — station — community [depth:traffic_rail_slow_parking]. AI scenarios: "open-source release hall + university-industry translation living room + talent-life butler + AI education/law/health", mapped to NODE-05–NODE-10 [data:visual/assets/scenario-cards.json#NODE-05]. Implementation risk: control-plan indicators and university cooperation mechanisms await approval; resolve via renewal policy and co-build agreements [depth:renewal_project_list].

### Dazhongsi AI industry cluster (72.0 ha)

Positioned as a urban intelligent-economy block of leading enterprises + new agent formats. Spatial structure: "four-quadrant intersection + central AI bell tower + green composite". Building renewal features commerce-service composites and green-space composites [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-003] ·  [depth:height_massing_character]. Transport achieves four-quadrant intersection connectivity. AI scenarios: "data-factor theatre + city-agent sandbox + AI-safety governance corridor", mapped to NODE-11–NODE-14 [data:visual/assets/scenario-cards.json#NODE-11]. Implementation risk: balance of ancient-temple heritage protection with high-intensity development, via height zoning and view-corridor control [depth:blue_green_public_space].

1. **Resident AI engineers / entrepreneurs**: young, tech-sensitive, needing high-frequency public space and compute interfaces;

2. **University research teams (Tsinghua, Peking, CAS)**: source innovation, needing roadshow and translation living rooms;

3. **Agent-enterprise product managers**: focused on data factors and governance frameworks;

4. **Community original residents + elderly**: needing daily services, emergency response, accessibility and age-friendly facilities [standard:ELDERLY-SMART-TECH-PLAN-2020-45];

5. **Overseas developers / conference attendees**: needing international wayfinding, accommodation and cultural experience routes;

6. **Public-governance and operations staff**: needing real-time data with human-review mechanisms [standard:GENERATIVE-AI-INTERIM-MEASURES].

## Transport, rail, utilities and public-service facilities

![Mobility and blue-green public-space composite system](assets/figures/mobility-bluegreen.png)

Transport centres on the Jing-Zhang heritage park slow spine, building a four-tier "green slow + railway heritage + arterial micro-circulation + station integration" system [depth:traffic_rail_slow_parking]:

- **Jing-Zhang heritage park slow spine**: runs N–S, ~9.7 km span, belt park width ≥30 m, carrying slow mobility, public activity and AI public scenarios [data:geometry/roads.geojson#ROAD-001];

- **Railway heritage corridor**: protectively displays 1909 rail sections and station relics as cultural landmarks [data:geometry/roads.geojson#ROAD-002];

- **Two arterials**: link Zhongzhiyuan—AI Origin—Dazhongsi, micro-circulation [data:geometry/roads.geojson#ROAD-003];

- **One connector**: serves community—station last mile, slow-first [data:geometry/roads.geojson#ROAD-005].

Parking and non-motorised: "station integration + slow-first + restricted on-street parking"; public bikes, low-speed shuttles and accessible shuttles share roadside space [standard:BARRIER-FREE-ENVIRONMENT-LAW].

New infrastructure and utility fusion [depth:municipal_new_infrastructure]:

- **Distributed energy**: photovoltaics and storage along the Jing-Zhang green vein, with building rooftops forming "BIPV — storage — charging" integration;

- **Edge compute**: pre-installed edge-compute cabinets and liquid-cooling interfaces in new buildings of Zhongzhiyuan and AI Origin;

- **Utility capacity**: rain gardens, permeable paving and reclaimed-water reuse in public space per sponge-city requirements;

- **Data governance**: all utility sensing data managed by "public — authorised — desensitised" tiers; no unauthorized third-party API.

## Blue-green space, public space and urban character

Blue-green and public space follow "one belt + two patches + many nodes" [depth:blue_green_public_space]:

- **One belt**: Jing-Zhang heritage park vitality belt (along the greenway spine), triple role of slow mobility, culture and AI public scenarios [data:geometry/green_space.geojson#GREEN-001];

- **Two patches**: Zhongzhiyuan central green axis, AI Origin twin-track heritage belt;

- **Many nodes**: 14 AI service nodes across the three areas and the green vein — public living rooms, open-source halls, governance corridors [data:visual/assets/scenario-cards.json#NODE-01].

Public-space ratio 7.33% [metric:public_space_ratio], green ratio 12.34% [metric:green_ratio]; blue-green + public space totals 19.67% of the site.

### Three AI landmark candidates (≥3)

1. **Jing-Zhang Origin Station** (AI Origin, NODE-05): a memorial and origin landmark modelled on the 1909 Qinghe old station, housing the open-source release hall, railway-engineer-spirit exhibition and AI-Origin exhibition; a conceptual landmark, not a committed build.

2. **Wisdom Eye Tower / Dazhongsi AI Bell Tower** (Dazhongsi, NODE-12): an AI bell-tower concept outside the ancient-temple view corridor, "rung" daily by a trusted AI system by solar term and public events; a tradition–future dialogue.

3. **Zhongzhi Cloud Hall** (Zhongzhiyuan NODE-03): a dome for open-source release and proof-of-concept at the park centre, the external living room for national AI pilot and open collaboration;

4. **Blue-Green Spine Memorial Garden** (along the Jing-Zhang green vein): small memorial nodes inside the continuous park green, linking railway-engineering spirit (Zhan Tianyou, "father of Chinese railways") with open-source spirit (collaboration, verifiable).

All landmarks are conceptual candidates; wayfinding logos, fonts, figures and enterprise marks are managed as "VI candidates", not committed builds [depth:heritage_regeneration_pattern].

### Cultural narrative

The metaphor主线 is "rail — compute": the 1909 Jing-Zhang railway, China's first self-designed trunk railway, represents **engineering self-reliance, precision, verifiable**; the 2026 AI Origin represents **open source, evolvable, shareable** [source:OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT]. The two spirits meet in space at the Jing-Zhang Origin Station (open-source hall), Zhongzhi Cloud Hall (pilot workshop) and Wisdom Eye Tower (governance corridor), forming a three-act narrative of "memory — innovation — governance". Hutong culture, Beijing flavour and developer culture are juxtaposed; old station, old factory, old community against new cloud hall, new block, new bell tower — avoiding presenting conceptual landmarks as approved builds.
