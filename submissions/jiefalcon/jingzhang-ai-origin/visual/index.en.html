Using the Jingzhang Railway Heritage Park as a layered cultural spine, this proposal converges three cultural layers — the 1909 Centennial Jingzhang industrial heritage, the 1980s Zhongguancun innovation culture, and the 2020s AI new culture — into a single origin point: Centennial.AI Origin / 京张 AI 原点.
