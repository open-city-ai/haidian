Walking and Cycling Network

A north–south, step-free, no-app/no-scan walking main line plus three east–west civic links; exact barriers and engineering await official transport evidence.

Blue-Green Public Space

Shade, rest, rainwater and low-disturbance operation underpin no-data services; sensor density never substitutes for public-space quality.

Buildings

Six small public rooms are adaptive-reuse or reversible test fits; existing fabric, DRR, height, FAR and fire safety remain unknown.

Renewal Projects

Start with charters, synthetic data and paper receipts; then integrate controlled compute and the no-data line; expand derivative receipts and public return only after public evaluation.

AI-Enabled Scenarios

All twelve scenarios require human review and a no-data equivalent; three industry tests use only synthetic, public or explicitly rights-cleared data.

Assumptions

Official boundaries, controls, ownership, buildings, utilities, heritage and legal roles remain missing. Co-op is a governance prototype, not a registered entity. Withdrawal receipts disclose residual effects and never promise perfect erasure.
