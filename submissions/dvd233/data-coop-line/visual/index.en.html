Service journey: walk the no-data line

Use ←/→ or click the gates to walk enter-use-exit end to end; a scenario may not open until its gate passes. This is a concept demo and changes no registered value.

G1 · Collective charter

Every use must be readable and collectively amendable: a scenario card's purpose, actors and boundaries pass the charter table (SCN-01) before any test.

Evidence: Community Data-Charter Table · scenario matrix · design-depth matrix.

G2 · Temporary-use licence

Licences are split by purpose and duration, withdrawable, never overnight commitments: the licence cell (SCN-05) grants the minimum first.

Evidence: Provenance and Licence Clinic (SCN-02) · withdrawal receipt semantics.

G3 · Visible data work

Hours, quality disputes and return rules are published in advance: the studio (SCN-03) makes data labour recognised urban work, not a back-office black box.

Evidence: Visible Data-Work Studio · data-labour clauses.

G4 · Derivative-chain receipt

Models, features, reports and public outputs all register a derivative chain; the withdrawal drill (SCN-09) distinguishes deleting copies, stopping use, delisting outputs and no-guarantee erasure.

Evidence: Derivative-Chain Withdrawal Drill · 8/8 negative failure fixtures.

G5 · Public return

When economic or non-economic value appears, auditable public-return options are agreed before a project starts, and the settlement hall (SCN-10) settles in public.

Evidence: Annual Public-Return Settlement Hall · metrics and recomputation page.

G6 · No-data equivalence

The no-data stop (SCN-08) sets “a human-equivalent main line for every digital path” as a design target and not-observed gate; real-world equivalence has not been verified and is not guaranteed.

Evidence: 100% no-data-equivalence rule coverage (E2 synthetic) · Dazhongsi MVP route recomputation.
