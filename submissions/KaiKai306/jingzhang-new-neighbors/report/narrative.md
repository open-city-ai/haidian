# 京张新邻 / JINGZHANG NEW NEIGHBORS

京张新邻把京张遗址公园方向组织为“一线、三场、两翼、十二邻站”。众智园承担开放工单与受控验证，AI 原点承担能力转译与社区复核，大钟寺承担百业试营与公开发布；中关村科技服务翼和小月河场景赋能翼分别输入专业能力与城市问题。

方案用“一场景一回报”合约连接创新与公共利益：12 张场景卡均登记人工责任、非 AI 等价服务、数据边界、付费劳动、技能转移、公共回报和退出条件。6 类画像、4 项产业测试、3 处制度地标与四季运营共同形成从问题发现到公共复盘的路径。

当前总体范围和三处重点区均为仓库维护的临时粗略约束，不是官方红线。所有面积、比例、慢行长度和建筑原型指标只描述本次提交几何；官方 polygon、控规、道路、权属、市政和专业条件到位后，整包重新计算和深化。

JINGZHANG NEW NEIGHBORS organizes the Jing-Zhang Railway Heritage Park direction as one line, three fields, two wings, and twelve Neighbor Stations. Zhongzhiyuan supports open tasks and controlled validation; AI Origin supports capability translation and community review; Dazhongsi supports business trials and public release. The Zhongguancun and Xiaoyue River wings contribute professional capacity and urban questions.

A one-scenario-one-return contract connects innovation to public interest. Each of twelve scenario cards records human accountability, a non-AI equivalent service, data boundaries, paid labour, skill transfer, public return, and exit conditions. Six personas, four industry tests, three institutional landmarks, and four-season operations create a path from problem discovery to public review.

The overall scope and three key areas remain repository-maintained provisional rough constraints, not official planning boundaries. Every area, ratio, walking and cycling length, and building prototype metric describes only the submitted geometry. Official polygons, regulatory controls, roads, ownership, municipal systems, and professional constraints trigger package-wide recalculation and deepening.
