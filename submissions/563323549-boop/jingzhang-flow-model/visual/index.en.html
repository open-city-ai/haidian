Passenger-Flow Thermometer · Station-Level Demonstration (Simulated)

The "Passenger-Flow Thermometer" algorithm — matured by the proposal's main contributor through public-transit passenger-flow monitoring practice — deployed directly at three Line 13 stations (Dazhongsi, Zhichunlu, Wudaokou): anonymous aggregated sectional-count input → public-standard parameter calibration (Fruin LOS, GB 50157) → four-level temperature state → matched control action.

The algorithm is the portable asset

The four-level color scale (cold blue — teal — warm orange — deep red) mirrors the brand palette. All temperature states on this page are simulated demonstration values; no real monitoring data is connected. If the three-station demonstration gains recognition from the Haidian District authorities, results can be submitted directly to Beijing Subway and other rail operators for evaluation and replication.
