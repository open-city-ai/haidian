# Formal Narrative

This narrative is derived from the structured AI package of the **Z-LINK (Zhi-Lian Jing-Zhang)** proposal. The design concept "AI-native Urban Forum" organizes a one-belt-two-rings-three-cores spatial structure that covers the three positionings (Jing-Zhang cultural belt, urban AI-living belt, AI-integrated innovation belt) and the three-areas-two-wings synergy loop.

## Key Design Decisions

- **Naming**: "Z-LINK" (Zhi-Lian Jing-Zhang) with a three-tier naming system (primary/secondary/tertiary brands)
- **Spatial structure**: one belt (heritage park corridor), two rings (urban AI-living ring + industrial-innovation ring), three cores (Accelerator/Origin/Economy)
- **AI scenarios**: 12 cards covering open-source publication, safety governance, mobility, data elements, and more
- **Pilgrimage landmarks**: three concept suggestions (Civilization-Origin Monument, Full-Stack Tower, Intelligent-Hub Dome)
- **Global activity system**: four-one annual system (developer week, city forum, scenario open days, pilgrimage route)

## Evidence Chain

Geometry, metrics, compliance matrix, drawings, and visual/index.html remain cross-checked deliverables. All provisional boundaries are labeled as such. Metrics are recomputed in EPSG:4548 from the submitted geometry.

## Boundary and Provisional Disclaimer

The package uses provisional boundary geometry in all submitted layers. All area-based metrics and spatial conclusions are directional until official boundary polygons are provided. The organizer-side data gap does not itself block content scoring.
