Embodied City · Jingzhang Brain: An Urban LLM Harness for Heterogeneous Robot–Energy Coordination

> **Proposal in one sentence:** Jingzhang becomes a city-scale runtime that understands events, decomposes missions, budgets energy, calls heterogeneous embodiments, receives observations, and learns inside explicit safety boundaries.

Design Basis and Source List

This formal package treats Jingzhang as a city-scale embodied runtime rather than a robot showcase. Beijing and Haidian policy documents support multimodal embodied models, heterogeneous robots, cross-embodiment planning, testbeds, spatial computing, mobility and power management. Research on Urban LLM Agents, human-AI co-creation, multi-horizon control, city spatial intelligence, transport digital twins, embodied safety, SayCan, PaLM-E and RT-2 informs the interface between task planning and local controllers. The package keeps every boundary, source type, confidence and geometry role explicit; the current site and key areas are provisional and must be recalculated when official polygons arrive.

Three-Level Scope Framework

The coordination area frames the innovation ecosystem and future city narrative; the overall design area turns that narrative into land use, buildings, mobility, blue-green space, energy and phasing; the three key areas test detailed design and daily operations. The spatial idea is one cultural-innovation belt, three anchors, multiple service nodes and a blue-green slow-mobility loop. Geometry is concept-only and never a statutory red line.

Coordinated Research Area: Industry and Future City Research

The industry chain is organised as research, open source, transfer, public experience and international communication. Zhongzhiyuan hosts standards and safety testing, the AI Origin Community hosts near-campus collaboration and open-source publishing, and Dazhongsi hosts enterprise services and international exchange. Five personas—commuters, developers, operators, residents and industry visitors—make the future city legible as everyday services rather than a technology slogan.

Overall Design Area: Urban Renewal and Regulatory-Plan-Level Urban Design

A four-node service skeleton links edge intelligence, green-energy storage and charging, public service, and robot work zones. Existing scaffold geometry is preserved as a transparent design base. FAR, height, rights, road red lines and utilities remain unknown until official controls are published; the proposal therefore records formulas, assumptions and recalculation steps rather than invented precision.

Detailed Design of Key Areas

Zhongzhiyuan is a garden-like full-stack innovation district; the AI Origin Community is a near-campus transfer and talent neighbourhood; Dazhongsi is an urban intelligent-economy and international exchange district. All three use the same event-to-task-to-space-to-audit loop and share the five fixed node types: edge brain, energy hub, UAV pad, ground robot work node and civic coexistence square.

AI Innovation Ecosystem, Personas, and AI+ Scenarios

Twelve scenario cards cover the flagship snow-crowd-energy response, energy balancing, inspection, accessible guidance, cultural interpretation, low-speed mobility, enterprise services, open-source events, heterogeneous robot testing, safety evaluation and civic issue closure. Three cards are industry test scenarios for robot cooperation, energy dispatch and public service. Data minimisation, human review and an offline route are mandatory.

Land Use, Building Scale, and Retain-Renovate-Demolish Strategy

Land-use and building layers distinguish quiet civic space, low-speed test space, adaptable reuse and long-term renewal. Existing structures are prioritised for reversible, low-carbon and publicly useful ground floors. Official controls are missing, so the package does not claim FAR, height, ownership or demolition decisions; unknown values remain explicitly unknown.

Transport, Rail, Municipal Infrastructure, and Public Services

Low-speed autonomous vehicles provide shuttle and clearance, UAVs observe snow from a restricted corridor, quadrupeds inspect surfaces and humanoids support wayfinding. Energy, municipal and civic services share an Energy-to-Action budget. Human help, accessible entrances and low-tech service hours remain available alongside robots.

Blue-Green Network, Public Space, and Urban Character

The heritage park, Qinghe and Xiaoyuehe form a walk-bike-public-service loop. Snow safety, lighting, crowd diversion, evacuation and observability are designed together. Three concept landmarks—Jingzhang Pulse Gate, Energy Compass and Four-Embodiment Living Room—connect railway memory, low-carbon infrastructure and human-machine co-governance.

Renewal Projects, Implementation Policy, and Phasing

Six projects establish a service-first, test-second, scale-third path. Near-term work is offline evaluation and low-risk public information; mid-term work is an isolated test zone and public co-testing; long-term expansion waits for official geometry, controls, responsibility and equipment certification. Cross-department event protocols, skills registration, energy budgets, public explanations and third-party evaluation are proposed.

Risk, Copyright, and Compliance

The risk register covers privacy, complexity, acceptance, operations, policy, spatial dispute, maturity and inclusion. High-risk dispatch requires geofencing, no-fly checks, crowd separation, energy gates, human approval and emergency stop. The LLM never writes motor, vehicle, flight or joint commands. All assets are local or publicly linked and all geometry is clearly provisional.
