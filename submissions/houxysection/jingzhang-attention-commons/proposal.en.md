# Leave White Jing-Zhang ATTENTION COMMONS: Centennial Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt Low-Impact Urban Design

> Centennial Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt low-disturbance Urban Design. It is not about making the city speak everywhere, but about letting technology learn when to remain quiet.

## Three-Level Scope Framework

The three levels of scope address different issues. Approximately 43.6 square kilometers of the Coordinated Research Area address "how innovation ecosystems collaborate," without adding new design boundaries; approximately 11.4 square kilometers of the Overall Design Area address "how culture, industry, life, and Public Space form a continuous experience"; and 368.4 hectares of three key areas address "which space prototypes can be tested, verified, and iterated." [depth:three_level_scope_framework] The proposal compresses the three levels of work onto a single logical chain: the upper level determines public values and element mechanisms, the middle level forms "one axis, three areas, two wings, and multiple blank rooms," and the lower level uses twelve scenarios to validate whether space and governance can coexist.

"The Axis" is an attentional slow-moving axis that unfolds along the context of the Jing-Zhang Heritage Park; it only expresses the intention for continuous Public Spaces without inferring the precise line position of the railway. "Three Pieces" are respectively the low-disturbance validation and governance of Zhongzhiyuan, the deep work and co-learning community of the AI Origin, and the quiet commerce and sensory-friendly transfer at Dazhongsi. The "Two Wings" are not new spatial boundaries: the Zhongguancun Technology Services Wing sends intellectual property, talent, capital, and standard services into the three pieces, while the Xiaoyue River Scenario Enablement Wing brings daily usage feedback back for testing and rule revisions. This structure is supported by [depth:overall_spatial_structure] and [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-SPINE].

Between layers, a "bidirectional validation" is applied. An industrial strategy cannot enter the priority zone project list if it does not find a spatial carrier; a Public Space node cannot be retained simply because it looks good on the drawing if it cannot explain its service targets, operational responsibilities, and exit conditions. The spatial layer coverage, source references, and attention contract collectively form the minimum threshold of evidence. All three priority zones use provisional boundaries, with precision alerts kept low but visible in the drawings; they do not compete with the design theme nor are they hidden. (Provisional Boundary)

## Coordinated Research Area: Industry and Future City Research

## Overall Design Area: Urban Renewal and Regulatory-Plan-Level Urban Design

Overall spatial structure does not seek to increase screen density but rather to embed the "right to be disturbed" within the space. Each AI scenario must submit a six-field attention contract: trigger mechanism, disturbance budget, sensory load, Human Review, non-AI alternatives, and stop and rollback. The contract must be incomplete for the scenario to remain in the laboratory; it can only enter a time-limited pilot if it is complete but has not yet passed user testing. This rule transforms AI-Native innovation from "one more feature" into "one less unnecessary intervention." [metric:attention_contract_field_count] [metric:human_review_coverage_ratio] [metric:non_ai_alternative_ratio] [metric:rollback_coverage_ratio]

Use a subset of the National Land Use Classification within the seamless, non-overlapping zoning boundaries. [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-SPINE] expresses the park green space axis, while the remaining six zones carry out research and education, culture, living, commerce, and station-city services; [metric:land_use_area_sqm] and [metric:land_use_feature_count] are directly recalculated from the geometry. [depth:land_use_layout] The conceptual Building Footprint only indicates the spatial prototypes available for further development, without identifying actual buildings or outputting floor area, height, or legal density. [data:geometry/buildings.geojson#BLDG-001] [metric:building_footprint_area_sqm] [metric:design_building_footprint_ratio]

The update strategy is divided into "operate first, then adapt, and finally construct." Initially, open hours, quiet rules, temporary furniture, and manual services are used to validate needs; subsequently, the building is studied for adaptability based on accessibility, fire safety, structural integrity, ownership data, and operational metrics. Only after formal planning and professional conditions are met, can the construction be discussed. The building massing and appearance, as well as the "dismantle–renovate–retain" strategy, are managed by [depth:height_massing_character] and [depth:retain_renovate_demolish]. The current conclusions are unified as pending measurement and confirmation of legal conditions to avoid misinterpreting the conceptual foundation as a construction decision. (Demolish–Renovate–Retain Strategy)

## Detailed Design of Key Areas

Zhongzhiyuan is defined as the "Silent Validation Yard." Here, it is not about showcasing the loudest models but about validating when models should not appear. Three industrial tests include: cross-system mutual recognition of attention impact registration tools, explainability and exit tests for low-disturbance multimodal assistants, and district-level trials of delivery robots in terms of light, sound, speed, and human takeovers. Each test runs first in a closed or scheduled environment, with failure conditions publicly recorded, before deciding whether to enter the Public Space. Qinghe and the green interface only propose concepts of light intervention, coexistence of walking and green spaces, and rainwater management, without making engineering judgments until cultural heritage, municipal, and transportation conditions are in place.

AI Origin community is defined as the "Leave White Co-Learning Courtyard." The space prioritizes serving researchers, students, entrepreneurs, and nearby residents for deep work, co-learning, result interpretation, and daily life. AI can help with scheduling and matching resources, but it cannot continuously monitor occupants; those without an account can still use quiet seats, bulletin boards, and human services. Open-source results are honored with records of contributions, licenses, maintainers, and replication paths, without turning personal influence into traffic rankings. Campus—parking lot pedestrian relations only express connection demands, with specific entrances and routes awaiting verification by campus management and traffic conditions.

Dazhongsi is defined as the "quiet front desk." Intelligent native commerce does not rely on continuous push notifications but uses a "view sensory previews first, then decide to enter" service model: events are described in advance with light, sound, crowd density, and accessibility conditions; commercial recommendations require active requests; transfer information is provided with both static signage and human inquiry. The quadrants of pedestrian connectivity are a conceptual goal, not a conclusion for bridge or rail integration. The three locations all reference [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-001], [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-002], and [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-003], and are constrained by [depth:three_key_area_detailed_design].

## Land Use, Building Scale, and Retain-Renovate-Demolish Strategy

Land use zoning transforms the "white space" from residual plots into a right of use: the space can remain temporarily unoccupied by commercial content, and can switch to quiet, co-learning, recovery, or maintenance states beyond peak times. The Attention Commons axis corresponds to park green spaces, with cultural co-learning, research validation, educational collaboration, living, commercial, and station-city service zones unfolding on either side; each zone allows for the setting of basic versions without screens, logins, or human services. The co-creation, quiet corners, and continuous evaluation of the Paris Oasis campus, as well as the mechanisms of the Barcelona superblock to reduce traffic interference and return Public Spaces to everyday life, are translated into "small spaces can first change rules, then change facilities." [source:CASE-PARIS-OASIS] [source:CASE-BARCELONA-SUPERBLOCK]

Building expression adopts a "Prototype Base + Condition Checklist" approach, without indicating actual demolish–renovate–retain strategies. Cultural prototype services are available for on-demand tours and documentation. Community prototype services support unregistered services, while research and experimental prototype services test three industrial sectors. Development and office prototype services provide quiet workspaces, and mixed-use and retail prototype services validate low-impact commercial activities. Each feature has `update_strategy=conceptual_pending_survey`, which requires clear confirmation of current as-built conditions, structural safety, fire safety, ownership, cultural heritage protection, accessibility, and statutory planning conditions. [data:geometry/buildings.geojson#BLDG-001] (Demolish–Renovate–Retain Strategy)

The calculable item at present is the conceptual Building Footprint area, not the total built-up area; therefore, [metric:floor_area_ratio], [metric:total_floor_area_sqm], [metric:building_height_m], [metric:statutory_building_density], and [metric:setback_m] remain unknown. This gap is intentionally kept within the evidence boundary. Once credible data is obtained later, establish a classification of "as-is condition—adaptive renovation—awaiting professional judgment," and let the professional team decide whether new construction is necessary, without relying on AI to guess conclusions from aerial photography or rough boundaries.

## Transport, Rail, Municipal Infrastructure, and Public Services

The transportation system adopts a concept network of "one north-south quiet pedestrian and cycling spine, two cycling side branches, and five east-west connectors." [data:geometry/roads.geojson#ROAD-001] to ROAD-008 only indicate connection directions and service relationships. [metric:road_centerline_length_m] represents the conceptual centerline length, which cannot be interpreted as road construction volume. The core task around rail stations is to reduce information anxiety: transfer information is displayed in layers, key changes can be announced upon request, while maintaining fixed signage, human inquiry, and continuous accessible paths. Specific station entrances, bridges, tunnels, crosswalks, and road right-of-way are subject to professional confirmation. [depth:traffic_rail_slow_parking]

Municipal infrastructure and New Infrastructure adhere to the principle of "visible responsibility, invisible disruption." Edge computing or sensing devices can only enter pilot programs after detailing energy, cybersecurity, maintenance responsibilities, data retention periods, and deactivation methods; high-density sensors are not to be used as a substitute for advancedness. Public service facilities operate on a dual-channel system: digital services enhance efficiency, while manual and paper pathways ensure that users without accounts, low digital literacy, or assistive technology can complete the same task. The capacity of facilities, population, and energy consumption data are missing, therefore [metric:service_facility_capacity] remains unknown. [depth:municipal_new_infrastructure]

Parking, logistics, and event traffic do not commit to specific supply volumes. Initial demand will be tested through reservation periods, shared loading and unloading, quiet deliveries, and pedestrian guidance. Any operational scenarios involving machine participation will have on-site safety personnel or clear human point-of-responsibility, and regular service will be restored after a single-point stop. The constraint layer [data:geometry/constraints.geojson#CONSTRAINTS] serves as a general reminder for "pending formal conditions to be replaced," and does not override preservation, blue line, municipal, or traffic control boundaries.

## Blue-Green Network, Public Space, and Urban Character

Public Space is not an AI device showroom. Attention commons in the continuous green chain of blue-green public space prioritize shade, rest, slow travel, quiet interaction, and flood adaptation as foundational values, with digital services relegated to on-demand layers. [data:geometry/green_space.geojson#GREEN-001] forms [metric:green_space_area_sqm] and [metric:green_ratio]; twelve scene rooms form [metric:public_space_area_sqm] and [metric:public_space_ratio]. Two sets of areas are recalculated based on submitted geometry, still subject to temporary overall boundaries. [depth:blue_green_public_space]

Four "holy sites" resist the trend towards internet fame. The first, the "One Second Silent Gate," allows visitors to choose quiet, assistive, or guided modes before entering; the second, the "Offline Bench," has no screens, only providing rest and paper information; the third, the "Jing-Zhang Echo Archive," presents railway and innovation culture materials on demand, defaulting to silent mode; the fourth, the "Maintainer's Blank Wall," records unobtrusive contributions such as repairs, cleanings, translations, and accessibility improvements. The honor system displays the contributors, evidence, licenses, maintenance status, and replication methods without ranking by views. [metric:ai_landmark_count]

Visual identity is oriented towards "negative space brackets + railway switch": two unclosed brackets enclose a central blank space, with a fine line branching like a switch towards "technical capability" and "public choice". The main colors are ink black, paper white, signal amber, and quiet teal, avoiding neon cyberpunk styles. Signage is divided into three levels: permanent static information, demand-driven digital assistance, and time-limited event layers. Cultural narrative juxtaposes the century-old engineering rationality of the Jing-Zhang, the collaborative tradition of Zhongguancun, and the auditable and exitable aspects of AI new culture, without fabricating historical figures or heritage facts.

## Renewal Projects, Implementation Policy, and Phasing

The project list consists of twelve actions of varying weight: Attention Contract template, public registration page, one-second silent door, offline bench, echo archive, maintainer blank wall, three industrial tests, commons of deep work, sensory-friendly transfer pilot, and annual blank map. The first four categories can be initiated from rules, signage, and movable facilities; projects involving equipment, buildings, and transportation must first undergo professional verification. Update projects are managed by [depth:renewal_project_list], and the concept recommendations are not to be written as determined plans. (Conceptual Recommendation)

Phase based on thresholds rather than years. Phase 1 will first complete the rules, co-design, accessibility testing, and light intervention prototypes; Phase 2 will run time-limited pilots in three separate areas, publicly disclosing failure conditions, complaints, human takeovers, and maintenance records; Phase 3 will only expand scenarios that pass review, and close or return non-compliant projects to the lab. [data:geometry/phasing.geojson#PHASE-001], PHASE-002, and PHASE-003 constitute [metric:phase_count], with areas referencing [metric:phase_1_area_sqm], [metric:phase_2_area_sqm], and [metric:phase_3_area_sqm] respectively. [depth:phasing_implementation]

Annual operational recommendations include a "Blank Space Maintenance Week" in the spring, a "Low-Impact Urban Experimentation Season" in the summer, an "Attention Commons Forum" in the fall, and an "Annual Blank Space Map Review" in the winter. Developer engagement should begin with real public issues, submitting replicable solutions, risk assessments, and termination conditions; the enterprise transformation pathway proceeds through issue registration, closed testing, public review, time-limited pilots, and annual renewal, without directly leading to procurement or business attraction through a single event. Activities, funding, talent, and international cooperation are reference mechanisms; no government commitments are made until the responsible parties and resources are confirmed.

## Risk, Copyright, and Compliance

All outcomes are Open Co-Creation proposals and do not replace formal planning nor constitute government approval conclusions. All spatial implementation suggestions are either "Conceptual Recommendations," "Reference Plans," or "Available for Professional Teams to Deepen Research." Major risks include provisional boundaries, gaps in control plans and property rights, insufficient cultural heritage and municipal data, technical maturity, operational costs, public acceptance, and fairness. These are recorded in assumptions.json and managed by [depth:risk_missing_data]. The most critical stopping conditions are: the scenario cannot provide Human Review, non-AI alternatives, or on-site retraction; once any of these conditions occur, the scenario does not enter the public pilot. (Provisional Boundary)

The text, structured data, graphics, web layout, and brochures in this package are generated based on publicly licensed materials by OpenAI Codex, using a CC BY 4.0 license; external facts and case studies retain their original source rights, with only referencing mechanisms employed, without copying protected images, trademarks, or layouts. Five images are all generated from submitted geometry and original graphics; system fonts are rendered as pixels or embedded.PDF Do not distribute font files. `report/copyright_statement.md` records the specific boundaries.

HTML does not load remote scripts, map tiles, fonts, iframes, or forms, nor does it track reviewers. The scenario avoids facial recognition, continuous individual trajectories, and implicit commercial profiling; even if technically feasible in public spaces, it does not equate "can do" with "should do." The final selection is completed by humans, professional teams, and public engagement, with AI responsible only for organizing evidence, proposing testable hypotheses, and acknowledging its own uncertainties.
