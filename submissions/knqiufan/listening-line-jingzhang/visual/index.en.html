LINE 13 ACCESS AUDIT

A 90-day detour, night, rest and accessibility baseline; temporary wayfinding and staffed accompaniment first; permanent connection only after official data and professional review.

P2

QINGHUAYUAN SOUNDSCAPE

P3

EQUIPMENT + WATERFRONT

LAND USE, BUILDINGS AND RENEWAL

Five conceptual collaboration bands cover the provisional boundary. Building envelopes only indicate adaptable reuse capacity. Retain/renovate/demolish requires title, structure, fire, heritage, accessibility, use and carbon evidence. FAR, total floor area and height remain unknown.

Renewal sequence

  1. Inventory existing assets and responsibility
  2. Run reversible ground-floor and forecourt samples
  3. Make durable only after public-value, maintenance and exit tests

TRANSPORT, WALKING AND BLUE-GREEN SPACE

People have default right of way; machines request limited right of way. Six east-west relations are walking links to investigate, not engineering alignments. Waterfront moves follow blue-line, flood and ecology review. Every facility has a responsibility card and basic offline service.
