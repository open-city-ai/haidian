The Jing-Zhang railway heritage park is the cultural spine, linking the three key areas: Zhongzhiyuan AI Self-Innovation Accelerator, Beijing AI Origin Community, and Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster.

Three-Level Scope

  • Coordinated research scope: ≈43.6 km², for regional industry and future-city strategy research.
  • Overall design scope: ≈11.4 km², urban design at the depth of regulatory-detailed-planning linkage research / conceptual renewal strategy.
  • Key areas: detailed design of three key districts.
