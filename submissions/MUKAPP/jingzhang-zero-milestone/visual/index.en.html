Core Concept

The dual-origin naming (railway kilometer zero + programmer zero-index) runs through the whole spatial organization.

Naming & dual origin

The Jing-Zhang Railway's 0 km point is at Beijing North Station (formerly Xizhimen Station) — the kilometer zero of China's first independently built trunk railway. Programmers index from zero; AI is built on 0s and 1s. Primary name: Zero Milestone (ZM); subtitle: Jing-Zhang AI Origin Belt.

Zero-Indexed Axis

The innovation belt is organized as a Zero-Indexed Axis from Km 0.0 in the south to Km 6.0 in the north; every node carries a dual numbering (railway kilometer post + computer index): one axis, seven nodes, three bands, two wings.

Three areas, two wings

The three key areas map to the Interface / Origin / Acceleration segments (Dazhongsi / Wudaokou / Zhongzhiyuan); the two wings are the Zero-Cost Factor Wing (Zhongguancun tech services) and the Zero-Barrier Scenario Wing (Xiaoyuehe scenario enablement).

Five-stage ecosystem

Origination (0) — Validation — Open Source — Experience — Governance, mapping one-to-one to the three areas and two wings; each node carries a state: 0 = pending, 1 = running, -1 = rolled back.

The addressable city

Urban public space is organized as an addressable data structure — every node has a Km index, a state, an accountable party, and a lifecycle; deliverables evolve from static atlases into recomputable data packages (GeoJSON → metrics → matrices → readable text).

Zero Hour

Annual event brand: Zero Hour launch (global release at the stroke of New Year midnight), Spring Open-Source Co-creation Week, Summer Controlled-Test Season, Autumn Release Week, Winter Governance Review Week.

Renewal gate

“Limited-term pilot — annual review — scale up or roll back”: green services stay open; yellow pilots have fixed terms, evaluation, and human review; red pilots are assessed, rectified, or decommissioned — hardware removable, data rollback-capable.

Transport & Slow Traffic

The conceptual road network — transverse arterial bands (RD-KM0–6), east-west longitudinal collectors (RD-W1/E1), and the axis greenway — totals about 32.8 km, including about 9.5 km of greenway.

Four key measures

  • Dazhongsi four-quadrant pedestrian connectivity
  • Zhichunlu station slow-traffic connection
  • Wudaokou & Qinghua East Road slow-traffic stitching
  • Heritage-park cross-ring-road nodes

10-minute service circle at each node

Every Km node is equipped with public toilets, accessible drinking water, AED, human service counters, and No-AI equivalent service points, so basic errands never depend on smart terminals.

Data boundary

Road redlines, cross-sections, station boundaries, ridership, and parking supply are missing; centerlines are directional concepts pending official data.
