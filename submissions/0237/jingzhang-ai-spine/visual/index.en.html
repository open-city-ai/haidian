Cultural Narrative Centennial Jing-Zhang · Zhongguancun · AI New Culture

  • Jing-Zhang Railway history layer:Zhan Tianyou, the switchback railway, and the 1909 opening symbolize the beginning of Chinese self-reliant innovation.
  • Zhongguancun innovation layer:From Electronics Street to a global science-and-technology innovation center, representing iterative entrepreneurship and pioneering culture.
  • AI new-culture layer:Open source, collaboration, trustworthiness, and inclusiveness, emphasizing future urban values of intelligent agents co-evolving with humanity.
  • Spatial expression:The railway-heritage linear corridor serves as a timeline, and nodes as milestones, forming a readable urban cultural system.
