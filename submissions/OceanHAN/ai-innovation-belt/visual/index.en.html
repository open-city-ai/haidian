MAINLINE Double-Track Verification · Merge must be reversible

Open-source collaboration semantics "urban renewal as Pull Requests" ↔ verifiable public semantics "the city remains after AI departs". Every AI scenario merged into the mainline must first prove a working no-AI baseline, pass an unplug test, and leave a public dividend usable without models, accounts, or network after exit. All mechanisms are landed as machine-readable assets (mainline-contracts / pipeline / gates / state-machine / raci / tabletop) directly recheckable by professional and operations teams.

Honest enum: not authorized · not field-run / 未获授权 · 未现场运行 —— all proposals are conceptual suggestions or signable mechanisms, not confirmed government arrangements; live performance stays unknown.

Five-stage mainline acceptance chain

COMMITproposal→MERGEreview→LIVEgray rollout→BLACKOUTunplug drill→LEGACYdividend

Maps to git semantics issue→PR→review→merge→revert→archive; no scenario passes the no-merge (no-AI baseline) gate into gray rollout.

Dual gates · project × scenario

  • Project maturity G0–G7: concept→boundary→survey→scheme→authorization→go-live→stable→archive
  • Scenario admission C0–C7: problem→data boundary→no-AI baseline→synthetic→unplug-scheme→exit drill→gray live→dividend
  • Missing no-AI baseline is no-merge; on failure default to preserve status quo, isolate risk, disclose the gap

Governance state machine · 8 states

proposed → reviewed → staged → live → degraded → blackout_drill → retained / removed_archived; exit drill and dividend audit cannot be skipped; the operator cannot sign their own dividend.

Offline public dividend safety net · hard stop

Each scenario pairs a minimum service usable without power/network/model; hard-stop and isolate on any of: no-AI path unavailable / no named human / cannot unplug / sensitive data overreach / dividend without maintenance owner.

Implementation Path and Governance Protocol

10 renewal projects advance in three phases (mainline through → kernel co-build → city-wide release), each with lead party and key indicators (conceptual); every scenario must pass the five-rule mainline merge acceptance before launch.

implementation + governance

Five-Rule Acceptance (conceptual)

  • 1 Open proposal—purpose, scope, data and exit conditions submitted publicly
  • 2 Public review—Merge Review months / disclosure periods, adoption traceable
  • 3 Minimal rollback—time/segment/cohort-limited or one-click suspension, minimal pilot
  • 4 Explainable records—logs, decision bases, human takeover auditable
  • 5 Archive on release—version, responsible party, retrospectives archived

Regional signable interfaces (conceptual)

  • Beiwai community: resident co-design and youth-talent services
  • Future Science City: long-term basic research and compute validation
  • Huairou Science City: instruments, measurement, standards methods
  • Beijing E-Town: embodied intelligence, robotics, safety testing
  • Zhangjiakou / BTH: green power, compute, cross-regional scenarios
