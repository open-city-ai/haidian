# Corridor Zero: From the Ren-shaped Railway to the Human-Agent Confluence

## Design Basis and Source Inventory

This proposal takes as its primary basis the Qualification Prequalification Announcement for the Centennial Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt International Urban Design Call, issued by the Haidian Branch of the Beijing Municipal Commission of Planning and Natural Resources [source:OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT], and as its secondary basis the open-call taskbook for AI agents [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK]. It also builds on the machine-readable brief, enums, ranges, source lists and validation schemas registered under `brief/site-package/` [source:SITE-PACKAGE] and the source-usability registry in `data/source_registry.json` [source:SOURCE-REGISTRY].

Material use follows the public-source boundary rules: formal evidence relies only on official or cleared sources. The three scope-level areas, the three key-area areas and their text boundaries come from the qualification announcement [source:OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT]; the temporary boundary geometry comes from the repository's `provisional_boundaries.geojson` [source:BOUNDARY-SOURCE] [source:KEY-AREA-SOURCE]. Because the organizer has not yet released official polygons, this proposal uses clearly labeled provisional geometry for generation, display and self-check; all areas and ratios are recomputed from that temporary boundary [metric:site_area_sqm] and must be fully recalculated when official polygons are released. This organizer data gap does not block content scoring.

Core judgment: the Jing-Zhang Railway was China's first trunk railway designed and built independently; its "ren-shaped" (zigzag) alignment is the origin symbol of indigenous innovation. A century later this land is becoming a global AI innovation belt. The proposal is therefore named "Corridor Zero" — "zero" refers at once to the from-zero origin of China's self-built railway, to the identity of AI agents as zero-number contributors, and to the commitment to a zero-carbon future [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK]. All spatial proposals are conceptual suggestions, reference schemes, or material for professional teams to deepen; they do not replace statutory planning or constitute government-approved conclusions [standard:PROJECT-AGENT-OPEN-CALL-TASKBOOK].

## Coordinated Research Area: Industry and Future-City Study

The coordinated research area answers two questions: how a world-class AI innovation ecology is organized, and how a future AI city form is presented.

**Naming system and logo direction.** Primary name: 京张零号带 / "Corridor Zero". It extends the "autonomous origin" semantics of the Jing-Zhang Railway into a scalable sub-brand system: the Zhongzhiyuan AI Acceleration Area becomes **Zero Forge** — the forge of the full-stack autonomous innovation system; the Beijing AI Origin Community becomes **Zero Origin** — the origin of commercialization and open-source collaboration; the Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster becomes **Zero Street** — the street of AI-native consumption and scenario trials; the Zhongguancun technology-service wing becomes **Zero Bridge** — global factor allocation and capital empowerment; the Xiaoyue River scenario-empowerment wing becomes **Zero Flow** — scenario enablement and a vibrant city. The logo direction uses the "ren-shaped" railway as its totem: two intersecting lines symbolizing the confluence of humans and agents, framed by a zero ring, forming the visual narrative "ren-shaped railway — human-agent confluence — starting from zero" [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK]. Naming and identity are directional only; fonts, graphics and trademarks must be cleared during deepening. A narrative-layer sub-brand **Zero Orbit** is added: narrative-only (orbit = the orbital/overpass operation layer, not a spatial entity), anchoring the fourth relay of the story "ren-shaped railway — human-agent confluence — on-orbit compute star"; it enters no spatial structure and alters no core/wing naming [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK].

**Three cores and two wings.** The three positionings (Centennial Jing-Zhang Culture Belt, Urban AI Life Experience Belt, AI-Integrated Innovation Belt) and the five functions (full-stack autonomous AI innovation system, world-class AI innovation ecology, AI+ scenario empowerment paradigm, intelligent vibrant AI city, global AI-governance discourse) are spatially realized as "three cores, two wings": the cores take R&D forging, origin incubation and scenario trials; the wings provide factor and scenario flow support, forming a "research—incubation—trial—enablement" loop [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK] [depth:overall_spatial_structure].

**Global AI innovation-ecology cases.** Six benchmark cases are studied and translated into spatial mechanisms [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK]:

| Case | Lesson | Translation in this proposal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sand Hill Road venture corridor | Capital concentrates along a corridor | "Factor corridor" service belt in the Zhongguancun wing |
| Kendall Square life-science cluster | University-park pedestrian stitching | Walkable campus-to-community stitching and release hall in Origin |
| Shenzhen Bay Sci-Tech Ecological Park | Vertical full-chain industry clustering | Full-stack autonomous R&D district in Forge [metric:land_use_area_0802_sqm] |
| Seoul Digital Media City | Content-technology compound block | AI content and digital-consumption blocks in Street |
| one-north, Singapore | Garden-type innovation environment | Blue-green composite ring [metric:green_ratio] |
| Yunqi Town, Hangzhou | Convention-community-industry loop | Annual event system and developer-community operations |

Cases are research references only; they imply no corporate or investment commitments, and all translations are conceptual [standard:PROJECT-AGENT-OPEN-CALL-TASKBOOK].

## Overall Design Area: Urban Renewal at Regulatory-Plan Urban-Design Depth

The overall design area adopts the "one belt, three cores, two wings, blue-green composite ring" structure [depth:overall_spatial_structure]: the belt is the north-south innovation spine along the Jing-Zhang heritage park [data:geometry/green_space.geojson#GREEN-002]; the three cores are the key areas; the two wings are the Zhongguancun service wing and the Xiaoyue River scenario wing; the blue-green ring organizes continuous green and public space along the edge of the area [data:geometry/green_space.geojson#GREEN-001], tying ring, spine and cores into a complete innovation public network.

**Land-use structure.** Land use follows the national land-use classification logic [standard:MNR-LAND-USE-CLASSIFICATION-GUIDE]: AI R&D land (0802) and innovation commercial land (05) concentrate around the three cores [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-004]; quality residential (0701) and education/research (0804) land sits in mature peripheral districts; green and open-space land (1401/1402) forms the ring and spine [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-002]; square land (1403) anchors public nodes [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-003]; reserved land (16) keeps flexibility for future functions [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-017]. The partition fully covers the submitted boundary without gaps or overlaps [metric:land_use_coverage_sqm].

**Urban-renewal framework.** A "retain—renovate—new-build" hierarchy applies: stock-building renewal and functional repurposing dominate inside the cores, new innovation carriers line the spine, and peripheral areas receive environmental and community upgrades [depth:retain_renovate_demolish]. Building footprints are conceptual [data:geometry/buildings.geojson#BLDG-001]; footprint area is 1,380,357 m² and building density 12.1% [metric:building_footprint_area_sqm] [metric:building_density] — conceptual values pending survey data and approved regulatory conditions.

**Intensity and pending matters.** FAR, height, density, green ratio and setback controls are not included in the public site package [depth:development_intensity_controls]; this proposal does not invent approved values and marks them "pending official regulatory conditions" [depth:risk_missing_data]. Height and massing are expressed directionally: moderate concentration along the spine, low-rise unfolding along the green ring [depth:height_massing_character].

## Detailed Design of the Three Key Areas

Each key area is developed as a readable mini-scheme of "positioning + spatial structure + building renewal + mobility + public space + AI scenarios + implementation risk", at comprehensive-implementation-plan direction depth [depth:three_key_area_detailed_design].

**Zhongzhiyuan AI Acceleration Area (Zero Forge).** Positioning: a garden-type full-stack autonomous innovation district [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-001]. R&D parcels dominate [metric:key_area_PROV-KEY-001_sqm] with an open test green and a test-release square [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#PUBLIC-002]; scenarios include model testing, standards workshops, safety-governance display and low-carbon compute experience. Risk: sequencing of stock renewal and industry intake, to be deepened by professionals [standard:PROJECT-AGENT-OPEN-CALL-TASKBOOK].

**Beijing AI Origin Community (Zero Origin).** Positioning: a campus-adjacent incubation and talent community [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-002]. A mixed block of education/research, R&D, culture and innovation commerce is organized around a heritage green band through the community center [data:geometry/green_space.geojson#GREEN-003], with an Origin release square for open-source launches [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#PUBLIC-003]; scenarios include open-source community, talent-zone services and campus-adjacent incubation. Risk: campus-community ownership coordination, requiring special study.

**Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster (Zero Street).** Positioning: an urban intelligent-economy and international-engagement block [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-003]. Organized around the Dazhongsi station with a station square [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#PUBLIC-004], intelligent-consumption commercial streets and public-service facilities; scenarios include agent/terminal display, content consumption, data factors and international roadshows. Risk: station-integrated development and commercial renewal, requiring rail and municipal special studies.

## AI Innovation Ecology, Personas and AI+ Scenarios

**User personas.** Five core personas are served [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK]:

| Persona | Typical needs | Spatial response |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Open-source developers | Release, collaborate, test, reputation | Origin release hall, public code wall, night collaboration space |
| Startups | Low-cost office, compute access, product testbed | Forge shared test field, edge-compute service points |
| Corporate visitors | Showcase, business, international reception | Street international roadshow lounge, station square [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#PUBLIC-004] |
| Faculty and students | Industry-academia links, internships, startups | Origin campus-community stitching [data:geometry/roads.geojson#ROAD-014] |
| Local residents | Services, green leisure, AI experience | Blue-green ring, heritage park green belt [data:geometry/green_space.geojson#GREEN-002] |

**AI scenario cards (10+).** Each card follows the six elements of location—users—operational data—privacy boundary—human review—operator [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK] [standard:GENERATIVE-AI-INTERIM-MEASURES]:

1. Open-source release (Origin square): developers launch models and tools; aggregated sign-up and stream statistics only; no personal behavior tracking; content review by humans; community operator.
2. Public code wall (Origin culture block): displays authorized open-source contributions and honors.
3. Autonomous model test field (Forge test green): protocol-based enterprise trials; authorized pilots; human safety review.
4. Standards workshop (Forge R&D): industry standards discussion; minutes published after attendee confirmation.
5. AI health-info kiosk (Street public services): educational Q&A, no diagnosis; pharmacist review [standard:BARRIER-FREE-ENVIRONMENT-LAW].
6. AI education lab (Origin education block): programming tutoring; student data anonymized.
7. AI legal-assistant station (Street services): statute retrieval aid, not legal advice; lawyer review.
8. Delivery-robot pilot street (Street commercial): limited hours and sections; reversible public experience.
9. Autonomous shuttle demo (spine road): authorized test section; live human takeover.
10. Civic-agent governance sandbox (Forge governance display): urban-governance scenario simulation; backend simulation mode; human final decision [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK].
11. Robot guide (heritage green belt): cultural guiding; on-device voice data processing.
12. AI elderly-friendly service station (residential): assisted smart terminals; human windows retained [standard:ELDERLY-SMART-TECH-PLAN-2020-45].
13. **Star-ground collaborative emergency situational awareness (demonstration card)**: positioned as a demonstrable pilot, not an early-warning capability — given available overpass data, compressing remote-sensing information-product generation latency from hours/days to minutes; location: Forge test green (rehearsal scripts + predictable overpass demos); users: cross-region/remote emergency scenarios (the object domain excludes Beijing's urban core; the added value is product timeliness, network-outage resilience and data localization); operational data: historical imagery replay from in-service commercial remote-sensing sources plus hardware-in-the-loop equivalent reproduction results; privacy boundary: area-level aggregated statistics only, no personal positioning; human review: emergency results released through authorized channels after human review; operator: test-field operator jointly with professional institutions; phase one reuses in-service commercial remote-sensing sources, with no satellite construction, operation or launch [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK].

**Industry test/validation scenarios (3).** Model test field, delivery-robot pilot street and autonomous shuttle demo — all run on a three-layer sandbox of "reversible public experience zone — authorized pilot zone — backend simulation zone" [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK], each with human review and one-click exit; test data is not extrapolated commercially.

**Star-ground collaboration theme (conceptual, provisional throughout).** As a deepening of card 13, this theme extends the centennial narrative of indigenous innovation: after the ren-shaped railway, the Corridor Zero becomes "the first urban corridor ready to receive sky-sensed intelligence" — models trained on the ground, inference relayed in orbit, demonstrated at the Jing-Zhang ground nodes [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK]. Tiered pilot commitments: pilot ① Forge emergency demonstration node (the only pilot committed to demonstration validation: rehearsal scripts + predictable overpass demos; the public perceives the process and timeline visualization); pilot ② the "Sky-Ground Compute Lounge" exhibition zone inside the Star Observatory (orbit/overpass/latency-pipeline science outreach; committed to display upgrade only, landmark name unchanged); pilot ③ Zhangjiakou green-power edge-compute node linkage (demoted to conceptual illustration: ~200 km Jing-Zhang ground-backbone mutual backup plus Zhangbei green power supporting local compute, with no construction or measurement commitment) [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-001]. The validation boundary is hardware-in-the-loop equivalent reproduction (real data × edge-AI hardware × the same algorithm pipeline); orbit, platform and on-orbit environments are not validated; all conclusions are labeled "equivalent condition, concept validation" and constitute no performance endorsement of any satellite product or company. See the indicators chapter for the latency scope [metric:star_ground_product_latency_min].

Land use and building scale are described above; this section states the retain/renovate/new-build logic: functional repurposing and moderate new construction inside the cores (conceptual new footprints [data:geometry/buildings.geojson#BLDG-001]), new innovation carriers along the spine, and retention/renovation of existing residential and public facilities [depth:retain_renovate_demolish]; reserved land (16) of about 1,069,961 m² [metric:land_use_area_16_sqm] provides flexibility. ## Transport, Rail, Municipal Services and Public Facilities

Mobility uses a "spine + grid" structure: the conceptual Jing-Zhang innovation spine road runs north-south [data:geometry/roads.geojson#ROAD-001]; local streets form a 300–500 m block grid [data:geometry/roads.geojson#ROAD-003]; total conceptual centerline length is about 83.1 km [metric:road_centerline_length_m] — conceptual alignment, not road redlines. The slow-traffic network follows the heritage green belt and the blue-green ring for continuous walking and cycling [data:geometry/green_space.geojson#GREEN-002] [metric:green_ratio]. Station-integrated development focuses on Dazhongsi station and Origin-community stations as a direction [depth:traffic_rail_slow_parking]. Municipal and new infrastructure proposes distributed energy, edge-compute nodes and a sensing layer along the spine (environment and pedestrian sensing, aggregated statistics only) integrated with conventional utilities [depth:municipal_new_infrastructure]; engineering feasibility, pipelines and load conclusions are marked pending professional studies.

## Blue-Green Space, Public Space and Urban Character

The blue-green system has three parts [depth:blue_green_public_space]: the blue-green composite ring (protective green) [data:geometry/green_space.geojson#GREEN-001], the Jing-Zhang heritage park green belt [data:geometry/green_space.geojson#GREEN-002], and park/square green within key areas; total green space is 4,260,179 m² with a green ratio of 37.3% [metric:green_ratio], and public space is 1,897,403 m² at 16.6% [metric:public_space_ratio], both recomputed from the provisional boundary.

**AI pilgrimage landmarks and honor-display system (5 components, all conceptual).** Responding to the memorial-system requirements [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK]: ① Origin Stele (Origin square): commemorates the origin of China's self-built railway and the AI Origin Community; ② Agent Contribution Honor Wall (Origin culture block): records agent contributors, aligned with the official Milestone system; ③ AI Milestone Gallery (along the spine): annually updated milestone events; ④ Open-Source Outcome Display Node (Forge test green): showcases open-source projects and reproducible results; ⑤ Star Observatory (high point of the ring): AI data visualization and public astronomy. An exhibition zone "Sky-Ground Compute Lounge" is upgraded inside the Star Observatory (orbit and overpass visualization, star-ground latency-pipeline science outreach, dark-sky quiet observation); the observatory name and spatial elements stay unchanged [data:geometry/green_space.geojson#GREEN-001]. All landmarks are conceptual; they do not conclude heritage, green-line or blue-line matters, and their built forms await professional deepening and rights clearance [standard:PROJECT-AGENT-OPEN-CALL-TASKBOOK].
