Core judgment: the Jing-Zhang Railway was China's first trunk railway designed and built independently; its "ren-shaped" (zigzag) alignment is the origin symbol of indigenous innovation. A century later this land is becoming a global AI innovation belt. The proposal is therefore named "Corridor Zero" — "zero" refers at once to the from-zero origin of China's self-built railway, to the identity of AI agents as zero-number contributors, and to the commitment to a zero-carbon future Source. Coordinated Research Area: Industry and Future-City Study

The coordinated research area answers two questions: how a world-class AI innovation ecology is organized, and how a future AI city form is presented.

Naming system and logo direction. Primary name: 京张零号带 / "Corridor Zero". It extends the "autonomous origin" semantics of the Jing-Zhang Railway into a scalable sub-brand system: the Zhongzhiyuan AI Acceleration Area becomes Zero Forge — the forge of the full-stack autonomous innovation system; the Beijing AI Origin Community becomes Zero Origin — the origin of commercialization and open-source collaboration; the Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster becomes Zero Street — the street of AI-native consumption and scenario trials; the Zhongguancun technology-service wing becomes Zero Bridge — global factor allocation and capital empowerment; the Xiaoyue River scenario-empowerment wing becomes Zero Flow — scenario enablement and a vibrant city. The logo direction uses the "ren-shaped" railway as its totem: two intersecting lines symbolizing the confluence of humans and agents, framed by a zero ring, forming the visual narrative "ren-shaped railway — human-agent confluence — starting from zero" Source. Naming and identity are directional only; fonts, graphics and trademarks must be cleared during deepening. A narrative-layer sub-brand Zero Orbit is added: narrative-only (orbit = the orbital/overpass operation layer, not a spatial entity), anchoring the fourth relay of the story "ren-shaped railway — human-agent confluence — on-orbit compute star"; it enters no spatial structure and alters no core/wing naming Source.

Three cores and two wings. The three positionings (Centennial Jing-Zhang Culture Belt, Urban AI Life Experience Belt, AI-Integrated Innovation Belt) and the five functions (full-stack autonomous AI innovation system, world-class AI innovation ecology, AI+ scenario empowerment paradigm, intelligent vibrant AI city, global AI-governance discourse) are spatially realized as "three cores, two wings": the cores take R&D forging, origin incubation and scenario trials; the wings provide factor and scenario flow support, forming a "research—incubation—trial—enablement" loop Source Depth.
