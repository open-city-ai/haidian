Rail Commons: Haidian Public Space Revival

Verified small repairs, explicit accountability and reversible AI services turn public space from a drawing layer into auditable civic infrastructure.

The SITE and all three key areas use rough provisional polygons. Station, road, parcel, heritage and engineering relationships remain pending official evidence and professional review.
01

Overview Map

The provisional boundary is visually recessive; repair loops, accountable nodes and stop conditions carry the design.

Design judgment

Public-space revival is not a one-off makeover. It cycles through finding a gap, a small trial, human review, and restoration or expansion.

Non-digital fallback

Paper guides, staffed help desks, on-site stewards and an appeal path must coexist with every AI service.
