## Source and Rights Boundary

The authoritative and background references actually used are recorded in `sources.json` with publisher, URL or repository path, access date, authority, permitted use and prohibited use. The package does not redistribute external photographs, map tiles, logos, portraits, paper figures, commercial cartography or third-party fonts. International cases are cited as background mechanisms only; their text and visual assets are not copied.

The site and three key-area polygons come from the repository-maintained provisional boundary file. They are reproduced as `provisional_constraint` data for generation, visualization and intake checks, not as official planning boundaries. Official announcements and local standards snapshots are cited as public or cleared references and remain subject to their publishers' terms.

## Generation Toolchain

- Python 3.13.13 for deterministic local generation.
- Shapely 2.1.2 and PyProj 3.7.2 for topology, clipping and EPSG:4548 recomputation.
- Pillow 12.3.0 for original bilingual raster diagrams and multi-page PDF boards.
- The repository's `render_proposal_html.py` for offline report HTML.
- The repository's deterministic, spatial, visual, professional and participant preflight tools for validation.

The installed Windows DengXian font was used only to rasterize text locally. No font file is included or redistributed. HTML uses a system-font fallback stack and has no remote font request.
