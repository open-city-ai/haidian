# Rail Commons / 轨脉共生

Rail Commons treats public-space revival as a sequence of accountable repairs: establish a field baseline, try a small reversible intervention, retain staffed and paper alternatives, stop when a safety/privacy/ownership gate fails, restore the site, and retest before expansion.

The proposal maps this method across three scope levels and gives the three key areas distinct public-realm responsibilities: Garden Lab Commons in Zhongzhiyuan, the Learning Porch in Beijing AI Origin Community, and the Urban Civic Exchange in Dazhongsi. All three remain scale-free reference schemes because the repository polygons are provisional and not station-, road- or parcel-anchored.

The complete evidence layer is in GeoJSON, `metrics.json`, `sources.json`, `assumptions.json` and the three matrices. The primary and English reports, figures, offline visual pages and A3/A0 PDFs are aligned presentation layers. No number or image in those layers supersedes the structured evidence.
