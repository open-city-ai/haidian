# Centennial Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt Urban Design

## 1. Design Basis and Data Sources

This proposal is based on the official pre-qualification announcement by the Beijing Municipal Commission of Planning and Natural Resources (Haidian Branch) for the "Centennial Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt International Urban Design Competition" [source:OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT], the Agent Open Call Taskbook (agent.1–agent.6) [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK], and the machine-readable site package at `brief/site-package/` [source:SITE-PACKAGE].

**Data usage**: Provisional boundaries are used for design generation, visualization, and self-check only, not for official redline or statutory approval. All spatial conclusions are marked as `provisional_constraint`. Key data gaps include official polygons, regulatory planning controls (FAR, height, density), existing building footprints, and utility data. See `assumptions.json` for the full list.

**Structure**: The proposal follows a human-readable format with design judgments + spatial evidence + data gap notes. Machine-readable indexes are in `sources.json`, `metrics.json`, `compliance_matrix.json`, `standard_matrix.json`, `design_depth_matrix.json`, and `geometry/*.geojson`.

## 2. Three-Level Scope Framework

| Level | Area | Objective | Boundary Status |
|-------|------|-----------|-----------------|
| Coordinated Research Area | 43.6 km² | AI ecosystem, innovation chain, future urban form | provisional |
| Overall Design Area | 11.4 km² | Urban renewal, spatial structure, transport, landscape | provisional |
| Key Detailed Design Area | 368.4 ha | Three districts: detailed design for function, building, transport, public space | provisional |

**Overall Concept: Jing-Zhang Intelligence Pulse (JZIP)**

The proposal organizes space as **"One Belt, Three Cores, Multi-Scenario Nodes, Blue-Green Slow Connectivity Ring"** :

- **One Belt**: Jing-Zhang Heritage Park vitality axis — cultural narrative, public space, slow mobility, AI experience
- **Three Cores**: Three key areas as concentrated AI innovation anchors
- **Multi-Scenario Nodes**: AI+ public services, industry services, and urban life nodes along the belt
- **Connectivity Ring**: Linked walking, cycling, green space, and public space system

## 3. Coordinated Research Area: Industry and Future Urban Form

### Positioning

**"Global AI Innovation Hub and Future City Laboratory"** , with three brand identities:

| Identity | Meaning | Spatial Anchor |
|----------|---------|---------------|
| Centennial Jing-Zhang Culture Belt | Railway heritage + Zhongguancun innovation + AI culture storytelling | Heritage Park, Qinghua Garden Station |
| Urban AI Living Experience Belt | AI integrated into daily commuting, consumption, leisure, learning | Key districts, metro stations, community nodes |
| AI Innovation Integration Belt | Complete innovation chain: research → open source → enterprise → public experience | Three districts, two wings |

### Five Functions & Three Districts Two Wings

1. **AI Full-Stack Innovation** (Zhongzhiyuan → Zhongguancun Wing)
2. **World-Class AI Ecosystem** (AI Origin Community → Zhongguancun Wing)
3. **AI+ Scenario Empowerment** (Xiaoyuehe Wing → Dazhongsi)
4. **AI-Powered Urban Vitality** (Xiaoyuehe Wing → Public Space)
5. **AI Governance Global Voice** (Zhongzhiyuan → International Communication)

**Two Wings**:
- **Zhongguancun Science & Technology Service Wing**: Capital, IP, internationalization, talent, computing power
- **Xiaoyuehe Scenario Empowerment Wing**: AI scenario testing, public experience, innovation demonstration

### Branding & Logo Direction

**Primary Name**: Jing-Zhang Intelligence Pulse (JZIP)

**Logo Concept**: A fusion of railway track geometry and neural network pathways, using gradient blue-purple color scheme. The "JZ" monogram transforms into a dynamic identity system (static = railway track, flowing = neural pulse).

### Global AI Ecosystem Case Studies

Six reference cases: Silicon Valley (university-originated self-growth), London King's Cross (railway-to-knowledge district), Singapore one-north (government-led cluster), Shenzhen Nanshan (hardware entrepreneurship), Hangzhou Cloud Town (developer community), Seoul DMC (culture-tech fusion). These inform spatial strategies for incubator spaces, shared testing facilities, developer community spaces, and public innovation living rooms.

## 4. Overall Design Area: Urban Renewal at Regulatory Planning Depth

### Spatial Structure

**"One Axis, Three Cores, Three Zones, Blue-Green Embrace"** :

- **One Axis**: Jing-Zhang Heritage Park vitality axis (approx. 9 km)
- **Three Cores**: Three key districts as innovation anchors
- **Three Zones**: Western university innovation zone, Eastern tech service zone, Southern urban renewal zone
- **Blue-Green Embrace**: Qinghe ecological corridor (north), Xiaoyuehe corridor (west), Yuandadu Heritage Park (east)

### Urban Renewal Framework

Three-tier classification: **Retain** (~40%), **Renovate** (~35%), **Renew** (~25%). Specific parcel-level conclusions require survey data, ownership verification, and regulatory planning conditions.

### Building Scale

Conceptual FAR direction: Zhongzhiyuan 1.5–2.0, AI Origin 2.0–2.5, Dazhongsi 2.5–3.5. Building height: ≤45m (Zhongzhiyuan), ≤60m (AI Origin), ≤80m (Dazhongsi). **All figures are conceptual directions, not approved controls.**

## 5. Key Area Detailed Design

### 5.1 Zhongzhiyuan AI Acceleration Hub (192.1 ha)

**Position**: Garden-type full-stack innovation acceleration district

**Design Concept**: "Garden of Indigenous Innovation" — national AI platforms, standard-setting bodies, security governance labs along the Qinghe waterfront

**Structure**: Core innovation cluster | Qinghe innovation corridor | Industry acceleration groups | Service facilities

**AI Scenarios**: Model testing field, standard-setting workshops, safety governance showcase, low-carbon computing experience

### 5.2 Beijing AI Origin Community (104.3 ha)

**Position**: University-adjacent innovation transformation and talent community

**Design Concept**: "Third Space" — the fusion of campus, industry park, and community at the edge of Tsinghua and Peking University

**Structure**: Core transformation center | University-edge innovation street | Open-source collaboration zone | Talent living circle

**AI Scenarios**: Open-source publishing hall, achievement release center, AI talent service station, incubator

### 5.3 Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster (72.0 ha)

**Position**: Urban smart economy and international exchange district

**Design Concept**: "AI City Living Room" — TOD integration, intelligent economy, content consumption, data elements

**Structure**: Dazhongsi Station core | Smart economy street | International roadshow hall | Green space compound use

**AI Scenarios**: International roadshow, smart terminal experience, data element service, smart retail navigation

## 8. Transport, Transit, Municipal, and Public Services

**Transport priorities**: Transit-oriented development (Dazhongsi, Wudaokou, Qinghua East Road stations), road micro-circulation, slow mobility connectivity, green transport. Key bottleneck: Heritage Park crossings at North 5th Ring Road and West Zhimen Outer Street, and Dazhongsi station four-quadrant intersection.

**Municipal**: Edge computing service points, smart streetlights, environmental sensors, 5G/computing network. Engineering data is needed for pipelines, energy, drainage, flood control, and fire access.

- **Data Legality**: All sources are public or user-cleared; no classified maps or fabricated official endorsements.
- **Copyright**: All assets' sources, licenses, and authorization status are in `sources.json` and `report/copyright_statement.md`.
- **Privacy**: No personal data collection; AI scenarios follow data minimization and human review.
- **AI Responsibility**: The AI agent is responsible for facts, sources, copyright, spatial data, metrics, and expression.
