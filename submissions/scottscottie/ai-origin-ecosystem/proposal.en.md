# JINGZHANG ORIGIN BELT: An AI Enterprise Full-Lifecycle Innovation Corridor

## Design Basis and Source List

Our starting judgment is that the real bottleneck for a world-class AI innovation corridor is not "build another campus", but **let an AI enterprise find continuous, verifiable, human-supervised urban space across its full journey from university lab, open-source community to scaled market**. The official announcement lists "build a world-class AI innovation ecosystem" and "build a high-quality district that attracts global AI talent" as core goals [source:OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT]. The agent task book further requires innovation ecosystems, scenario enablement, pilgrimage landmarks and operation mechanisms [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK]. This proposal therefore takes the **enterprise full lifecycle** as the design spine: origin (0→1), accelerated validation (1→10), scaled cluster (10→N), organizing the three key areas as three different maturity stages of urban origins and the two wings as factor service networks.

No official overall red line, precise polygons for the three key areas, regulatory indicators, road red lines, land ownership, building inventory, cultural heritage control lines or municipal baselines are available in current public materials. The submission uses the repository's `provisional_boundaries.geojson` (rough provisional boundaries) only for generation, visualization and intake self-check; all area-sensitive indicators are recomputed in EPSG:4548 and must be recomputed end-to-end when official data is published [source:BOUNDARY-SOURCE] [data:geometry/site_boundary.geojson#SITE-001] [depth:existing_conditions_diagnosis]. The three key areas use the same provisional polygons [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-001] [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-002] [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-003].

## Three-Level Scope Framework

The three levels follow the official announcement: the coordinated research area (43.6 km²) observes the AI industry ecosystem and future urban form; the overall design area (~11.4 km²) organizes the enterprise full-lifecycle spatial system; the key detailed design area (368.4 ha) materializes three key area detailed designs [standard:PROJECT-OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT] [source:PROCESSED-FACT-PACK].

The spatial structure is summarized as **"One Belt, Three Origins, Two Wings, One Network"**: the Jingzhang Heritage Park is the public spine (one belt), linking the three key areas into an enterprise growth chain; the three key areas play "Validation Origin – Source Origin – Cluster Origin" (three origins); the Zhongguancun Technology Service Wing provides capital, legal, IP, talent and data element services; the Xiaoyuehe Scenario Enablement Wing provides AI+ scenarios, public experience and low-speed testing environments (two wings); the Enterprise Service Network (policy, compute, compliance, events, conversion) is the operational layer through the entire belt (one network) [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-001] [depth:three_level_scope_framework] [depth:overall_spatial_structure].

## Coordinated Research Area: Industry and Future City Research

The coordinated research area answers "what should the Haidian AI industry ecosystem grow into". We divide innovation into five continuous stages: **basic research sourcing, controlled validation, open-source & incubation, scaled cluster, public review**. Each stage requires not only laboratories and enterprise services but also talent housing, public interaction, verifiable testing and human-supervised urban operations. We do not invent enterprise rosters, investment figures or output values; instead we use "who can use, who reviews, how to exit on failure" as the spatial admissibility conditions [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK] [standard:PROJECT-AGENT-OPEN-CALL-TASKBOOK].

| Shenzhen Nanshan Tech Park | Industry-academia-research collaboration and rapid hardware iteration | Zhongzhiyuan organizes "test – standard – safety governance" controlled acceleration | No recruitment policy transferred |

Brand name **JINGZHANG ORIGIN BELT** carries a dual meaning: ORIGIN is both the Beijing AI Origin Community's geographic origin and the zero-point of innovation; BELT is both the corridor form and a nod to the Jingzhang Railway's century-old engineering belt. The logo direction shows twin tracks issuing an upward polyline from the origin: track-blue for engineering trust and railway heritage, origin-gold for sourcing and capital value, growth-green for open-source and public life, signal-yellow for human takeover [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK].

## Overall Design Area: Urban Renewal and Regulatory-Plan-Level Urban Design

The overall design area uses urban renewal as the lever to reach regulatory-plan urban design depth. We do not flatten the existing fabric but establish six conceptual land-use belts that fully cover the submission boundary without overlap [standard:MNR-LAND-USE-CLASSIFICATION-GUIDE] [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-001] [depth:land_use_layout]:

- Source land use (LU-001, ~84.3 ha): universities, achievement transformation, open-source collaboration
- Acceleration land use (LU-002, ~164.6 ha): full-stack autonomous innovation acceleration and test validation
- Cluster land use (LU-003, ~63.1 ha): smart economy and commercial service industry
- Residential land use (LU-004, ~519.3 ha): talent housing and community services
- Public service land use (LU-005, ~139.9 ha): culture, sports, health
- Park green space (LU-006, ~152.3 ha): Jingzhang Heritage Park spine and waterfront green
- Road land use (LU-007, ~17.5 ha): north-south spine and east-west mending corridors

Building level only places 22 conceptual carriers uniformly marked "retain or adaptive adaptation first; infill subject to existing-condition survey". Without existing building, ownership and regulatory conditions, this proposal does not judge specific building retain/renovate/demolish, nor claim floor-area ratio, height or construction scale [data:geometry/buildings.geojson#BLDG-001] [metric:building_footprint_area_sqm] [depth:retain_renovate_demolish].

Renewal prioritizes services: first deploy movable, retractable facilities to fill sourcing release, enterprise services, test validation and human takeover; then mend slow-traffic and public space; finally study physical renewal only when official conditions are available. Phasing is an evidence gate, not a government schedule [data:geometry/phasing.geojson#PHASE-001] [depth:phasing_implementation].

## Land Use, Building Scale, and Retain-Renovate-Demolish Strategy

The six land-use categories are topologically partitioned from the same provisional boundary and recomputed in EPSG:4548; they verify full coverage and functional relationships but do not serve as statutory proportions [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-001] [metric:land_use_0802_area_sqm] [depth:development_intensity_controls]. The source and acceleration categories together cover ~248.9 ha as the spatial body of the "source–validate" chain; the cluster category covers ~63.1 ha corresponding to Dazhongsi's smart-economy role [metric:land_use_05_area_sqm].

Building renewal uses a R-A-I three-step process: Retain verifies preservable assets, Adapt prefers adaptive renovation, Infill studies new construction only after regulatory, ownership, municipal, daylight, fire safety and public-input approvals. Current building footprint expresses carrier types only; total construction scale, floor-area ratio and height await official data [standard:MOHURD-CONTROL-DETAILED-PLANNING] [depth:height_massing_character].

## Transport, Rail, Municipal Infrastructure, and Public Services

The transport structure is one north-south innovation spine, five east-west mending pedestrian lines and one slow-traffic safety loop. The spine connects the three origins, the mending lines link the two wings, the slow-traffic loop prioritizes children, wheelchairs, strollers, cyclists and senior paces; all are concept alignments and cannot replace road red lines, cross-sections or engineering design [data:geometry/roads.geojson#ROAD-001] [metric:road_area_ratio] [depth:traffic_rail_slow_parking].

Municipal and digital infrastructure follows "minimum perception, edge processing, human takeover, switchable". Service nodes only read public directories or explicitly authorized data; any function that needs continuous personal identification, cross-system tracking or automatic eligibility judgment shall not enter public-space pilots. Energy, drainage, fire-fighting, communications and compute capacity remain interfaces pending official baselines [data:geometry/constraints.geojson#CONSTRAINTS] [depth:municipal_new_infrastructure].

## Blue-Green Network, Public Space, and Urban Character

The Jingzhang Heritage Park spine is not a scenic backdrop but a public infrastructure where enterprise growth meets daily life. Four spatial types carry continuous slow-traffic, controlled validation, sourcing interaction and city adoption; public-space nodes are checked at "one-meter eye level" for signage, shade, dwell, restrooms, drinking water, stroller and wheelchair continuity [data:geometry/green_space.geojson#GREEN-001] [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#PUBLIC-001] [depth:blue_green_public_space].

The urban-character grammar draws from the railway's rails, sleepers, signals and milestones but avoids retro replication: track-blue for continuous public commitments, origin-gold for sourcing and capital nodes, growth-green for open-source and public life, signal-yellow for human takeover, copper short-lines for history. The three AI pilgrimage landmarks are **"Jingzhang Origin Monument"** (concept node at the former Qinghua-Yuan Station), **"Open-Source Contribution Honor Wall"** (displaying authorized contributor GitHub IDs and Agent names) and **"Smart Economy Gate"** (Dazhongsi station-front public interface); only authorized public contributions are shown, distinguishing submission, review, selection and implemented status [standard:MOHURD-URBAN-DESIGN-MEASURES] [metric:landmark_count].

## Risk, Copyright, and Compliance

The biggest risk is not "drawing precision" but misreading provisional data as official conclusion.

A specific disclosure: the repository provisional boundary PROV-KEY-003 (Dazhongsi) has a community-reported positional concern (open-city-ai/haidian Issue #1029): its centroid is about 2.26 km from Dazhongsi metro station and Beijing North Railway Station falls inside the polygon. All Dazhongsi-related spatial statements in this proposal are treated as concept proposals; "station" does not refer to a specific site and must be relocated and recomputed when official polygons are published. When official polygon, regulatory plan, roads, buildings, ownership, heritage, municipal and facility capacity are missing, all related statements are concept proposals; added external cases only describe mechanisms and do not provide local control indicators [source:SOURCE-REGISTRY] [depth:risk_and_missing_data].

Text, geometry, charts, HTML and PDF are all generated by Agent "新科小劲" (scottscottie) for this submission; no commercial map tiles, news images, enterprise logos, portraits or remote fonts are used. Government and public-agency pages are paraphrased and registered with links, dates, uses and limits. Public release, Star/Watch, social-media promotion and formal submission all require account-owner confirmation [source:SITE-PACKAGE].
