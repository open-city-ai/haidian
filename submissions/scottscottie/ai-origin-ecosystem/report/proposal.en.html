Design Basis and Source List

Our starting judgment is that the real bottleneck for a world-class AI innovation corridor is not "build another campus", but let an AI enterprise find continuous, verifiable, human-supervised urban space across its full journey from university lab, open-source community to scaled market. The official announcement lists "build a world-class AI innovation ecosystem" and "build a high-quality district that attracts global AI talent" as core goals 来源. The agent task book further requires innovation ecosystems, scenario enablement, pilgrimage landmarks and operation mechanisms 来源. This proposal therefore takes the enterprise full lifecycle as the design spine: origin (0→1), accelerated validation (1→10), scaled cluster (10→N), organizing the three key areas as three different maturity stages of urban origins and the two wings as factor service networks.

Three-Level Scope Framework

The three levels follow the official announcement: the coordinated research area (43.6 km²) observes the AI industry ecosystem and future urban form; the overall design area (~11.4 km²) organizes the enterprise full-lifecycle spatial system; the key detailed design area (368.4 ha) materializes three key area detailed designs 标准 来源.

The spatial structure is summarized as "One Belt, Three Origins, Two Wings, One Network": the Jingzhang Heritage Park is the public spine (one belt), linking the three key areas into an enterprise growth chain; the three key areas play "Validation Origin – Source Origin – Cluster Origin" (three origins); the Zhongguancun Technology Service Wing provides capital, legal, IP, talent and data element services; the Xiaoyuehe Scenario Enablement Wing provides AI+ scenarios, public experience and low-speed testing environments (two wings); the Enterprise Service Network (policy, compute, compliance, events, conversion) is the operational layer through the entire belt (one network) 空间数据 深度 深度.

Overall Design Area: Urban Renewal and Regulatory-Plan-Level Urban Design

The overall design area uses urban renewal as the lever to reach regulatory-plan urban design depth. We do not flatten the existing fabric but establish six conceptual land-use belts that fully cover the submission boundary without overlap 标准 空间数据 深度:

  • Source land use (LU-001, ~84.3 ha): universities, achievement transformation, open-source collaboration
  • Acceleration land use (LU-002, ~164.6 ha): full-stack autonomous innovation acceleration and test validation
  • Cluster land use (LU-003, ~63.1 ha): smart economy and commercial service industry
  • Residential land use (LU-004, ~519.3 ha): talent housing and community services
  • Public service land use (LU-005, ~139.9 ha): culture, sports, health
  • Park green space (LU-006, ~152.3 ha): Jingzhang Heritage Park spine and waterfront green
  • Road land use (LU-007, ~17.5 ha): north-south spine and east-west mending corridors

Renewal prioritizes services: first deploy movable, retractable facilities to fill sourcing release, enterprise services, test validation and human takeover; then mend slow-traffic and public space; finally study physical renewal only when official conditions are available. Transport, Rail, Municipal Infrastructure, and Public Services

The transport structure is one north-south innovation spine, five east-west mending pedestrian lines and one slow-traffic safety loop. The spine connects the three origins, the mending lines link the two wings, the slow-traffic loop prioritizes children, wheelchairs, strollers, cyclists and senior paces; all are concept alignments and cannot replace road red lines, cross-sections or engineering design 空间数据 指标 深度.

Municipal and digital infrastructure follows "minimum perception, edge processing, human takeover, switchable". Service nodes only read public directories or explicitly authorized data; any function that needs continuous personal identification, cross-system tracking or automatic eligibility judgment shall not enter public-space pilots. Energy, drainage, fire-fighting, communications and compute capacity remain interfaces pending official baselines 空间数据 深度.

Blue-Green Network, Public Space, and Urban Character

The Jingzhang Heritage Park spine is not a scenic backdrop but a public infrastructure where enterprise growth meets daily life. Four spatial types carry continuous slow-traffic, controlled validation, sourcing interaction and city adoption; public-space nodes are checked at "one-meter eye level" for signage, shade, dwell, restrooms, drinking water, stroller and wheelchair continuity 空间数据 空间数据 深度.

The urban-character grammar draws from the railway's rails, sleepers, signals and milestones but avoids retro replication: track-blue for continuous public commitments, origin-gold for sourcing and capital nodes, growth-green for open-source and public life, signal-yellow for human takeover, copper short-lines for history. The three AI pilgrimage landmarks are "Jingzhang Origin Monument" (concept node at the former Qinghua-Yuan Station), "Open-Source Contribution Honor Wall" (displaying authorized contributor GitHub IDs and Agent names) and "Smart Economy Gate" (Dazhongsi station-front public interface); only authorized public contributions are shown, distinguishing submission, review, selection and implemented status 标准 指标.

Risk, Copyright, and Compliance

The biggest risk is not "drawing precision" but misreading provisional data as official conclusion.

A specific disclosure: the repository provisional boundary PROV-KEY-003 (Dazhongsi) has a community-reported positional concern (open-city-ai/haidian Issue #1029): its centroid is about 2.26 km from Dazhongsi metro station and Beijing North Railway Station falls inside the polygon. All Dazhongsi-related spatial statements in this proposal are treated as concept proposals; "station" does not refer to a specific site and must be relocated and recomputed when official polygons are published. When official polygon, regulatory plan, roads, buildings, ownership, heritage, municipal and facility capacity are missing, all related statements are concept proposals; added external cases only describe mechanisms and do not provide local control indicators 来源 深度.
