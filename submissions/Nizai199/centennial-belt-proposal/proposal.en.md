## Three-Level Scope Framework

> **【Corresponding Agent Task】** Announcement 1.3.1~1.3.3, agent.1 **【Scoring Criteria】** Spatial Clarity · Functional Appropriateness

The proposal is organized according to the three levels defined in the announcement: the Coordinated Research Area focuses on approximately 43.6 square kilometers of AI industry ecology, strategic positioning, innovation chain, and future urban form; the Overall Design Area focuses on approximately 11.4 square kilometers of the urban and industrial areas surrounding the Jing-Zhang Heritage Park, requiring the formation of an overall framework for Urban Renewal, industrial space layout, traffic and municipal support, and Urban Character control; the key area focuses on approximately 3.684 hectares of three detailed design regions (Zhongzhiyuan AI Independent Innovation Acceleration Area of about 192.1 ha, Beijing AI Origin Community of about 104.3 ha, and the Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster Area of about 72.0 ha), requiring the clarification of functional types, building scale, demolition-renovation-retention categories, Public Space connectivity, and traffic organization [depth:three_level_scope_framework] [depth:overall_spatial_structure]. (Key-Area Detailed Design Area) (Demolish–Renovate–Retain Strategy)

**Story Anchor (Rails Not Yet Cooled)**: This axis is inherently a "rail" — the Jing-Zhang Railway opened in 1909, and by 2026, its ground segment had been developed into the second phase of the Jing-Zhang Heritage Park (approximately 9 kilometers long, 53 hectares in area, directly serving about 70 communities and 450,000 people along the line) [source:SRC-PUBLIC-MATERIALS]. The rails have not cooled; they have simply shifted their burden: from transporting coal and people, to supporting urban public life. The entire design of this proposal is to integrate a new "Smart Vein" onto this "rail that has not yet cooled."

The depth items of the three-level work framework are constrained by [depth:three_level_scope_framework] and [depth:overall_spatial_structure], spatial evidence is based on [data:geometry/site_boundary.geojson#SITE-001] and [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-001], tasks are based on [standard:PROJECT-OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT], and the scope index is navigated by the three-level scope table in [source:PROCESSED-FACT-PACK] `project_scope_summary.csv`.
