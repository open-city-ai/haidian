# Jing-Zhi River: Conceptual Urban Design for the Centennial Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt

> Version and Iteration Notes: Version v0.2 builds upon the conceptual framework of version v0.1, translating the overall concept of "Jingzhichuanliu" into a one-belt-three-kernels-two-wings-multiple-nodes spatial structure. This generates a complete calculable geometry (land use / buildings / roads / green space / public space / phasing / constraints / key areas), establishes 26 indicators and three evidence matrices, and provides accompanying A3 documents, A0 panels, and offline HTML displays. Future versions will recalibrate and deepen the Demolish-Renovate-Retain Strategy and engineering conclusions once the official polygon and control plan conditions are in place. (Demolish–Renovate–Retain Strategy)

## Design Basis and Source List

This proposal is the Open Co-Creation outcome for the "Centennial Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt Urban Design International Call for Entries," with the main language being Chinese. The proposal is based primarily on the Qualification Pre-Review Announcement issued by the Haidian Branch of the Beijing Municipal Commission of Planning and Natural Resources on May 2026, [source:OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT]; and the scope for open co-creation is defined by excerpts from the task book for global intelligent bodies, [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK]. The machine-readable tasks, scope, enumerations, indicators, and source lists are derived from the site-package package maintained in the repository, [source:SITE-PACKAGE]; and the formal/background/provisional purpose boundaries for public data are registered in `data/source_registry.json`, [source:SOURCE-REGISTRY]. `data/processed/agent_fact_pack.md` is merely a reading navigation layer and does not constitute a new authoritative source [source:PROCESSED-FACT-PACK].

This plan uses the provisional rough boundaries provided by the maintainer until the official precise redlines are obtained [source:BOUNDARY-SOURCE], [source:KEY-AREA-SOURCE]. `geometry/site_boundary.geojson` and `geometry/key_areas.geojson` are both marked as `provisional_constraint` and `official_boundary=false`, and can only be used for plan generation, visualization, and design discussions, but not as official redlines, approval references, or for recalculating precise areas. [data:geometry/site_boundary.geojson#SITE-001] [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-001]

The professional depth of the proposal shall be implemented in accordance with the following local standard references: [standard:PROJECT-OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT] to constrain the mission of the official announcement within the three layers of scope; [standard:PROJECT-AGENT-OPEN-CALL-TASKBOOK] to constrain the six agent tasks and co-creation boundary clauses; [standard:MOHURD-URBAN-DESIGN-MEASURES] to constrain the Urban Design coordination and style control; [standard:MOHURD-CONTROL-DETAILED-PLANNING] to constrain the depth of control detailed planning and the expression of pending confirmation items; [standard:MNR-LAND-USE-CLASSIFICATION-GUIDE] to constrain the expression of land use classification. [standard:MOHURD-ARCH-DESIGN-DEPTH-2016] Due to the official document not yet being in the database, it should be used as a missing data item and a reminder for deepening, without being mistaken for an authoritative basis that has already been met. The design depth is verified through 15 required depth items, starting from the current condition diagnosis and data gaps [depth:existing_conditions_diagnosis].

The text uses verifiable citation formats: [source:...], [standard:...], [depth:...], [data:...], [metric:...], allowing reviewers to trace back from a text to GeoJSON for spatial evidence, from metrics to recompute results, and from sources to see the data boundaries. ## Three-Level Scope Framework

Announcement defines three working levels: the Coordinated Research Area covering approximately 43.6 square kilometers, the Overall Design Area covering approximately 11.4 square kilometers, and the Key-Area Detailed Design Area covering approximately 368.4 hectares. [data:geometry/site_boundary.geojson#SITE-001] expresses the Overall Design Area; three key areas are respectively expressed by [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-001], [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-002], and [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-003]. [metric:site_area_sqm] is the recalculated area for the Overall Design Area, and [metric:key_area_count] is the count of the three key areas, both derived from the submitted geometry. Authoritative data are in GeoJSON format, not as stated in this paragraph.

The three levels of scope are transmitted from industrial strategy, overall Urban Design, to detailed design of key areas. Coordinated research addresses "how to organize the world-class AI innovation belt," with the overall design translating judgments into spatial structure, land use, transportation, blue-green spaces, and appearance. The detailed design of key areas verifies the functions, buildings, Public Spaces, and AI scenarios of three areas. Given that the current three levels are provisional rough polygons, the areas, proportions, and project locations mentioned in the text retain the recalibration requirements; after the official polygons are completed, the site boundaries, key areas, land use, roads, green spaces, public spaces, buildings, phasing, and all metrics must be recalculated. [depth:three_level_scope_framework] Verify each item of this framework.

The spatial organization of this plan is structured as "One Belt, Three Cores, Two Wings, and Multiple Nodes": One Belt is the Jing-Zhi River Blue-Green Main Axis (Jing-Zhang Heritage Park Vitality Belt); Three Cores correspond to Zhongzhiyuan, AI Origin Community, and Dazhongsi; Two Wings are the Zhongguancun Technology Services Wing and Xiaoyue River Scenario Enablement Wing; Multiple Nodes are AI scenario nodes distributed along the main axis. This structure aligns with the "Three Major Orientations, Five Major Functions, and Three Zones and Two Wings" as detailed in the integrated research chapter. [depth:overall_spatial_structure] Verify the overall structural expression.

## Coordinated Research Area: Industry and Future City Research

The Coordinated Research Area focuses on the AI industry ecosystem, future urban forms, and brand systems. The overall concept of this plan is named "Jingzhi Flow": "Jing" represents the century-long history of the Jing-Zhang Railway and the urban roots of Beijing, "Zhi" represents the new quality of artificial intelligence as a productive force, and "Flow" borrows from the phrase "Jianliu Bu Xi," translating the imagery of the Jing-Zhang Railway, which was the earliest symbol of modern industrialization in China, into a flow of global AI innovation. The English name "Jingzhi Flow" considers pronunciation, visual appeal, and international dissemination. [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK] It requires a naming system, English name, and logo direction; this plan suggests the main title "Jingzhi Flow / Jingzhi Flow," the subtitle "Century Jing-Zhang · AI Innovation Belt," and the logo direction as a dynamic symbol of "railway tracks + data flow" flowing in parallel. The three positioning lines "Century Jing-Zhang Cultural Belt, Urban AI Life Experience Belt, AI Integration Innovation Belt" serve as the brand slogan system, and the logo graphic direction is an abstract dual-line flow that can be extended and adapted for signage systems. This naming and visual direction do not replace the statutory planning nor confuse with the overall cultural identifier system of "One Belt." [standard:PROJECT-AGENT-OPEN-CALL-TASKBOOK]

Five functional relationships: The Full-Stack Independent AI Innovation System corresponds to Zhongzhiyuan, the World-Class AI Innovation Ecosystem corresponds to the AI Origin community, AI-Enabled Scenario empowerment new paradigm corresponds to the Xiaoyue River Scenario Enablement Wing, intelligent AI vibrant city corresponds to Jingzhi River Blue-Green Main Axis and Public Space system, and AI governance global discourse power corresponds to the standard setting, safety governance, and honor display system. ## Overall Design Area: Urban Renewal and Regulatory-Plan-Level Urban Design

The Overall Design Area is organized according to the depth of Urban Design in the Regulatory Detailed Planning. The core judgment is: with the Jing-Zhang Heritage Park as the main axis of Urban Renewal, transform inefficient spaces, traffic discontinuities, rail transit nodes, and service gaps into renewal opportunities, forming four categories of renewal logic: "retain the existing condition—gradual renovation—functional update—partial new construction," to avoid large-scale demolition and reconstruction. [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-0802-001] expresses the structure of research and AI development land use, [data:geometry/buildings.geojson#BLDG-001] expresses the distribution of Building Footprints, [data:geometry/roads.geojson#ROAD-002] expresses the Jing-Zhi River Greenway axis, and [metric:building_footprint_area_sqm] and [metric:building_density] verify the scale and density of the building footprint. [depth:land_use_layout] and [depth:development_intensity_controls] respectively verify the Land-Use Layout and Development Intensity expressions.

Due to the absence of official numerical values for the Floor Area Ratio, Building Height, Building Coverage Ratio, green space ratio, setback distances, building control lines, and road red lines, this scheme lists them as unknown or design-suggested values, [standard:MOHURD-CONTROL-DETAILED-PLANNING]; they will be replaced with confirmed values once the formal control plan and task book attachments are finalized. No AI-predicted values should be used as substitute review indicators. [depth:height_massing_character] For the guidance on height, massing, façade, and character, a "layered approach" is suggested: low to mid-rise human-scale interfaces are recommended on both sides of the main axis, mid to high-rise research and development office interfaces are suggested in the industrial clusters, and high-intensity mixed-use development is recommended around the rail nodes. However, these are expressed as direction for further development rather than control values.

The current condition diagnosis section of this plan identifies four categories of issues: first, discontinuous Public Spaces and pedestrian connectivity gaps; second, insufficient pedestrian connectivity around rail transit stations; third, mismatch between existing inefficient spaces and industrial service needs; and fourth, blue-green corridors being fragmented by roads and buildings. [depth:existing_conditions_diagnosis] Document the current judgments and data gaps, with the current buildings, ownership, municipal, and fire safety conditions awaiting official data to further refine the conclusions of the Demolish–Renovate–Retain Strategy. [depth:retain_renovate_demolish] Only provide the method framework and pending calibration list.

## Detailed Design of Key Areas

Three key areas are the core of the detailed design in this plan, each providing a "location + spatial structure + building renewal + traffic and pedestrian access + Public Space + AI scenarios + implementation risks" readable small plan. Refer to [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-001], [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-002], [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-003], and ensure that they meet the depth of the Integrated Planning Implementation Plan by [depth:three_key_area_detailed_design]. Currently, all three areas are provisional rough polygons, and specific parcel-level conclusions can only serve as directional designs. [metric:zhongzhiyuan_area_sqm], [metric:ai_origin_community_area_sqm], and [metric:dazhongsi_area_sqm] are the recalculated areas for the three areas.

**Zhongzhiyuan AI Independent Innovation Acceleration Area (approximately 192.1 hectares)**: positioned as a garden-type full-stack independent AI innovation district, hosting the Full-Stack Independent AI Innovation System and global AI governance discourse. The spatial structure is defined as "Qinghe Interface—Testing and Validation Plaza—Innovative Interaction Green Corridor." Spatial actions include: enhancing the Qinghe waterfront low-carbon innovation corridor, setting up an industrial testing and validation plaza, and organizing open testing and green spaces. AI scenarios include autonomous model testing, standard-setting workshops, safety governance demonstrations, and low-carbon computing experience. Implementation risks include the confirmation of the river blue line, flood control, and ecological conditions.

**Beijing AI Origin Community (approximately 104.3 hectares)**: Located as a campus-type hub for the conversion of research outcomes and a talent community, it carries the world-class AI Innovation Ecosystem. The spatial structure is an open ecosystem that integrates "campus—industrial park—neighborhood" through a pedestrian-friendly network. Spatial actions include: an open-source release hall and a near-campus conversion street, Transit-Station Integration, and talent residence and community service amenities. AI scenarios include open-source communities, outcome releases, talent special zone services, and near-campus incubation. Implementation risks include the confirmation of campus boundaries, ownership, and the first-floor business types.

**Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster (approximately 72.0 hectares)**: positioned as a city-type intelligent economy and international exchange district, hosting new emerging smart industries. The spatial structure is defined as "Dazhongsi Station Quadrants—Data Element Parlor—International Pitch Parlor." Spatial actions include: pedestrian connectivity through the quadrants at the Dazhongsi Station, display of intelligent entities and smart terminals, commercial services, and business interfaces. AI scenarios include the display of intelligent entities, content consumption, circulation of data elements, and international pitch events. Implementation risks include the confirmation of station works, road intersections, and municipal pipeline conditions.

## Blue-Green Network, Public Space, and Urban Character

Blue-Green Space is structured around the Jing-Zhang Blue-Green Axis, integrating the Jing-Zhang Heritage Park Vitality Belt, Qinghe and Xiaoyuehe Blue-Green Corridors. It proposes a network of pathways, cycling lanes, and green spaces that are north-south connected and east-west linked. [data:geometry/green_space.geojson#GREEN-001] represents a continuous park green space, while [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#PUBLIC-200] is the original community open square. The metrics [metric:green_ratio] and [metric:public_space_ratio] are used to verify the green space ratio and Public Space ratio. The depth of the blue-green public space is [depth:blue_green_public_space], and the [standard:MOHURD-URBAN-DESIGN-MEASURES] constrains the overall Urban Design coordination.

Urban Character blends the historical and cultural narratives of the Jing-Zhang Railway, the innovation culture of Zhongguancun, and the new AI culture into a tripartite storyline. [standard:PROJECT-OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT] and [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK] constrain the boundaries of the urban character and cultural expression. The cultural narrative suggests a three-part spatial storyline: the southern segment with heritage site culture, the middle segment with innovation culture, and the northern segment with AI future culture. This is expressed through signboards, identifiers, symbolic systems, and public art components. [source:OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT] and [standard:PROJECT-AGENT-OPEN-CALL-TASKBOOK] constrain the brand and cultural system. All fonts, images, portraits, trademarks, and corporate logos must be cleared, and no conceptual landmarks should be written as approved constructions.

This proposal suggests the inclusion of at least 3 AI pilgrimage sites or honor display nodes: the Jing-Zhang Heritage Cultural Square (honoring railway pioneers and the century-old project), the Origin Community Open Source Honor Wall (recording the contributions of developers and the open-source community, [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK]), and the Zhongzhiyuan Governance Contribution Exhibition Hall (displaying safety governance, standard setting, and the results of global AI governance discourse). These, together with the Jing-Zhang Heritage Park, the Zhongguancun innovation culture, and the developer community operations, form a Public Space component library, corresponding to nodes such as [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#PUBLIC-206]. ## Metrics, Area Recalculation, and Compliance Matrix

The indicator system is divided into three categories: spatial indicators that can be recalculated directly from the submitted geometry, control indicators that require official land use plans for support, and performance indicators that need to be calibrated with operational data.
