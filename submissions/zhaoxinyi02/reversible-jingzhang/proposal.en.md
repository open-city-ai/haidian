# Jing-Zhang Reversible City | REVERSIBLE JINGZHANG — Every Future Needs a Return Path

> **Each future must come with a return ticket.** Not by asking what AI can do, but by asking: When an error occurs, who can stop it, how can it be recovered, who will fix it, and can the city still function normally? The second round of visual QA has broken down the master plan, land use, key areas, traffic blue-green, and indicator evidence into five different graphic syntaxes to reduce redundant master plans.

"Reversible City" is not about repeatedly tearing down and rebuilding cities or denying long-term construction. It refers to: when AI technology is still unstable and the social impact is uncertain, experimenting in a small-scale, removable, time-limited, and responsibility-assigned manner. Residents can refuse or exit, services can revert to manual operations, data can be deleted, models can be rolled back, facilities can be removed, and Public Spaces can be restored. The truly irreversible railway heritage, ecological foundation, human dignity, and basic public services should be prioritized for protection. In short: **large-scale long-termism, small-scale experiments are reversible; cities can learn, but residents should not bear the cost of irreversible consequences for technology.**

Seven categories of conceptual functional zones fully cover the provisional site: northern reversible R&D and safety validation, Qinghe ecological and open testing interface, near-school technology transfer and learning community, mixed zone of talent living and care services, century-old Jing-Zhang cultural public lobby, AI-Native commercial and international exchange, and southern portal and public transportation services. [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-001] [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-007] [depth:land_use_layout]

The building layers adopt an "preserve first, then diagnose, and finally decide" evidence workflow: documentation pending → safety/ownership/heritage/usage performance investigation → retain maintenance, repair enhancement, adaptive reuse, or professional demolition and reconstruction argumentation. The 28 Building Footprints only express reversible research and development, incubation, learning, community service, mixed-use, and commercial service space prototypes, and do not correspond to actual building units or form a demolish–renovate–retain strategy conclusion. [data:geometry/buildings.geojson#BLDG-001] [depth:retain_renovate_demolish] (Demolish–Renovate–Retain Strategy)

Floor Area Ratio, Building Height, density, setback distances, and total building massing shall all be maintained as unknown; no "professional-looking" false numbers shall be used to replace the control plan. The massing principle is small grain, open ground floor, modular internal fit-out, ground-level reconfigurability, shared courtyards, and low nighttime disturbance, pending the completion of solar, fire safety, structural, traffic, municipal, cultural heritage, and ownership documentation for further refinement. [metric:floor_area_ratio] [depth:development_intensity_controls] [depth:height_massing_character]

The unique lever of Urban Renewal is the "exit budget": any temporary facility, digital service, event, or space renovation must first register the deactivation time window, evidence of data deletion, restoration responsibility, human fallback capacity, maintenance resources, and unresolved complaints before it is even considered for a limited trial. Without an exit budget, no project enters the limited trial.

## Land Use, Building Scale, and Retain-Renovate-Demolish Strategy

Land classification only expresses functional relationships and does not alter the statutory use. The seven zones are sequentially divided by a single provisional site, ensuring shared boundaries, complete coverage, and no gaps; this confirms the self-consistency of the submitted data but does not prove that any statutory use has been altered. The combination of research and education, community, residential, cultural and sports, commercial, and transportation services addresses the concept of "work-life-study-social" continuity, and must be calibrated with official master plans, current land ownership, population, and facility baseline data. [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-003]

Conceptual Building Footprint area was recalculated using EPSG:4548, merely describing the ground projection of the scheme prototype and unable to derive total floor area, investment, or Development Intensity. [metric:building_footprint_area_sqm] Each prototype follows a DfD (Decomposition for Design) checklist: detachable connections, material passports, equipment leasing/recycling, ground restoration, fire and accessibility reviews, maintenance, and decommissioning responsibilities. Building positions, areas, and forms are design language elements, not corresponding to actual building units; the official building geometry must be rebuilt in geometry/buildings.geojson after it is in place, rather than forcing the prototype to be applied directly.

The Demolish–Renovate–Retain Strategy is not a game of red, yellow, and blue images, but a set of evidence cards for each building. After obtaining official current status and ownership data, we first assess the historical, usage, structural, safety, fire safety, carbon, and social value. We then compare the full lifecycle impacts of retaining and maintaining, renovating and enhancing, adaptively reusing, and demolishing and rebuilding. Each card must also document the impacts on occupants during relocation, accessibility, material destinations, and reversibility; any specific conclusions still require a professional team and legal procedures. [depth:retain_renovate_demolish]
