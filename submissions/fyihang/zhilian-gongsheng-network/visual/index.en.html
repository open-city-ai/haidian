SC-06 Accessibility gap repair

SC-04 Paper-and-digital paired wayfinding

Provides paper fallback, staffed enquiry and an accessibility walkover; missing paper information pauses digital prompts.

SC-10 Jing-Zhang memory interpretation

Uses removable interpretation components only after cultural and rights review; a copyright or safety issue removes the component.

SC-07 Shared learning time

Supports permission-dependent learning and referral without presuming institutional partnership or outcomes.

SC-09 Civic feedback and appeal

Uses paper intake and a named closure owner; it collects no data assets, personal profiles or smart-terminal traces.

SC-08 Low-carbon transfer service

Explains shade, cycling, rainwater and maintenance conditions; it makes no energy or computing deployment claim.

SC-05 Stormwater maintenance log

Records rain-garden and permeable-edge maintenance; drainage or infiltration failure triggers repair and temporary closure.

SC-03 Dazhongsi staffed station guidance

Uses staffed static guidance at the station-city interface; crowding, failed guidance or station objection triggers downgrade.

Civic Evidence Weave | AI spatial trials

Three protocols make the relation among spatial anchors, human review and retain/adjust/remove decisions visible. Observation is limited to facility status, aggregate paper comments and signed checks; no identity, trajectory or biometric data is collected.

ai_spatial_evaluation_matrix.json

T-01 Walking and wayfinding

Entries, continuous step-free routes and paper signs are walked together. Missing alternative route, emergency clearance or paper fallback pauses the trial.

T-02 Public inquiry and feedback

Ground-floor service, staffed counter and paper closure record form one civic-review condition. No digital collection starts without a named closure owner.

T-03 Reversible component and upkeep

Reversible component, fire/service clearance and named maintenance are signed before, during and after operation. A missing record removes or pauses the component.

Human decision

Safety, accessibility, rights and service staff jointly decide retain, adjust or remove. An algorithm cannot independently decide admission, allocation, enforcement, evaluation or refusal of service.
