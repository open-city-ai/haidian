Why Beijing Needs to Break New Ground Again

FRONTIER DENSITY

Haidian already concentrates universities, research, models, open-source communities and firms. The gap is not another showcase, but a path into the real city.

FIRST-USE CAPACITY

Beijing has published super-test-field and full-stack-agent directions. Urban design must supply sites, service baselines, accountable roles and stop conditions.

THE JING-ZHANG METHOD

The 1909 railway proved independent capacity for a complex modern system. Today AI must be tested, adopted and corrected while leaving shared capability behind.
