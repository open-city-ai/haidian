V0.7 Core Proposition

The proposal treats an AI city as more than a collection of devices, screens and demonstrations, and Jing-Zhang as more than a governance protocol. It asks Beijing's larger urban question: how can Haidian's dense concentration of models, talent, firms and research become durable capability for industry, markets, public services and everyday life? JING-ZHANG BREAKS NEW GROUND is therefore a conversion network from frontier innovation to shared urban capability. Its one-sentence proposition is: organize Haidian's frontier innovation along the century-old Jing-Zhang corridor as a SOURCE—STACK—PROVE—LIVE & MARKET—ENABLE—COMMONS network, so AI can be created, validated, used, exchanged and governed, and ultimately become replicable shared urban capability. Every institutional, spatial and operational move remains a conceptual recommendation for further planning, engineering, legal, ethical and public review 来源 标准.

The railway history is not reduced to a visual track metaphor. China’s National Railway Administration describes the 1905–1909 Jing-Zhang Railway as the first state-owned trunk railway surveyed, designed, built and managed by Chinese people. Beijing’s railway heritage park restores parts of the former alignment, preserves Qinghuayuan Station and reconnects spaces once divided by the railway 来源 来源. The first Jing-Zhang “broke new ground” by proving independent capacity for a complex modern engineering system. The second must prove that AI can move from laboratories into a real city while balancing industrial value, public interest and safe governance. This is a design thesis, not a historical quotation.

The six nodes are neither six new districts nor a one-way pipeline. SOURCE gathers universities, institutes, open-source communities and original research. STACK connects chips, compute, frameworks, models, agents and data tools. PROVE validates technology and institutions in laboratories, bounded streets and real-user settings. LIVE & MARKET brings capabilities that pass boundary review into commerce, work, mobility, culture and public services. ENABLE provides capital, talent, intellectual-property support, professional services, housing and infrastructure. COMMONS leaves open methods, public space, service baselines, failure evidence and urban memory to the city. The network overlays the official Three Zones and Two Wings without creating statutory zones or replacing the official three-level scope 空间数据 空间数据 深度.

The former protocol remains, but is repositioned as a Trusted Operations Layer for the network. The AI City API / Jing-Zhang AI Civic Protocol is a federated machine-and-operations interoperability contract. The Jing-Zhang AI Constitution is a versioned civic compact revised through scenarios, participation, professional review and operating evidence. Human takeover, non-digital fallback, appeal/redress and capability return keep essential service available through failure or exit. Seven minimum interfaces cover: (1) identity/provenance; (2) data purpose/retention; (3) capability/limitations; (4) human authority/non-digital fallback; (5) appeal/redress; (6) interoperability/portability; and (7) versioning/pause/retirement/public memory. The former Return Ticket remains a public-readable receipt within this layer, not the overall brand. None is an existing platform, statutory permit, enacted law or government approval process.

“Return” does not transfer city data or public authority to a technology provider. The process spans all three dependency packages and is governed by 深度:
