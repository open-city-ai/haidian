# 京张，再次开路 V0.7 简明叙事 / JING-ZHANG BREAKS NEW GROUND V0.7 Brief Narrative

## 中文

**京张，再次开路 / JING-ZHANG BREAKS NEW GROUND** 的命题不是再造一条“AI 走廊”或把治理协议当作总体品牌，而是把海淀的源头创新沿百年京张组织成 **SOURCE—STACK—PROVE—LIVE & MARKET—ENABLE—COMMONS** 网络，使 AI 能够被创造、验证、应用、交易、治理，并最终沉淀为可复制的城市共同能力。第一次京张开路证明中国能够自主完成复杂现代工程；第二次开路要证明 AI 能从实验室进入真实城市，并同时形成产业价值、公共利益和安全治理。这是设计命题，不是史料原句 [source:HIST-JINGZHANG-RAILWAY] [source:HIST-JINGZHANG-PARK]。

六节点网络覆盖官方“三区两翼”而不新增法定分区：AI 原点社区承接 SOURCE、ENABLE 与 PROVE，众智园承接 STACK 与 PROVE，大钟寺承接 LIVE & MARKET，小月河与两翼提供实景验证和专业服务，京张遗址公园把开放方法、公共空间、服务基线、失败证据和城市记忆沉淀为 COMMONS。它回应北京关于超级 AI 试验场、全栈智能体与互操作、科技—产业—城市融合的公开方向，但不把政策方向写成已批准的本项目、场址、采购或预算 [source:BEIJING-SUPER-AI-TESTFIELD] [source:BEIJING-AGENT-POLICY-2026] [source:HAIDIAN-DESIGN-EXCHANGE-2026]。

12 个既有场景被组合为五个旗舰试点：**FP01 AI 原点首用站**（S03、S04、S07；SOURCE + ENABLE）、**FP02 众智全栈开放试验庭**（S01、S10；STACK）、**FP03 小月河场景共测网**（S02、S05、S06、S08；PROVE）、**FP04 大钟寺 AI 日常市场**（S11、S12；LIVE & MARKET）、**FP05 京张公共证据公园**（S09；COMMONS，并作为前四项的公开观察与反馈层）。五项共用“技术准备—有界试验—首用采购—扩散—标准/共同能力输出”五门，以及 D30 定责定界、D60 有界共测、D90 证据决策三道 90 日门槛；未通过只能修改或停止。运营联合体、一年指标、资金、空间和数据边界均是待确认的概念建议 [depth:phasing_implementation]。

AI City API、Jing-Zhang AI Constitution、七类最低接口、人工接管、非数字替代、申诉补救和能力返还不再站在前台，而作为全网的 **可信运行层**。V0.7 沿用 V0.6 登记的 3 个嵌套街室、7 个接口实体和 3 条人物旅程 [metric:protocol_street_room_count] [metric:protocol_interface_entity_count] [metric:human_journey_count]。3 个能力返还节点、12 个场景到返还节点的引用覆盖和 7 个接口实体到有效街室的解析覆盖均已登记；引用覆盖与解析覆盖为 1.0，只证明机器引用闭合，不证明服务已交付 [metric:capacity_return_node_count] [metric:return_ticket_reference_coverage_ratio] [metric:protocol_interface_entity_coverage_ratio]。

V0.7 保留 SITE/KEY 形状、BUILD/TEST/LIVE × 四带的 3×4 用地拓扑和面积分母，但已重建六角色/五旗舰的语义属性、机器证据、图件和双语展示；嵌套街室不增加公共空间 union 面积，仍为 432,600 ㎡ [metric:public_space_area_sqm]。五个试点、场地、联合体、采购、预算和一年目标均未获确认；法定开发强度、道路、权属、市政、能源、消防和文保条件仍待正式资料。本成果不是官方红线、审批结论或政府许可 [data:geometry/site_boundary.geojson#SITE-001] [standard:MOHURD-ARCH-DESIGN-DEPTH-2016]，也不是采购决定、签署合同、工程承诺或已部署系统 [depth:land_use_layout] [depth:risk_missing_data]。

## English

**JING-ZHANG BREAKS NEW GROUND / 京张，再次开路** is neither another “AI corridor” nor a governance protocol promoted as the overall brand. It organizes Haidian's frontier innovation along the century-old Jing-Zhang corridor as a **SOURCE—STACK—PROVE—LIVE & MARKET—ENABLE—COMMONS** network, so AI can be created, validated, used, exchanged and governed, and ultimately become replicable shared urban capability. The first Jing-Zhang proved independent capacity for a complex modern engineering system; the second must prove that AI can move from laboratories into a real city while producing industrial value, public benefit and safe governance. This is a design thesis, not a historical quotation [source:HIST-JINGZHANG-RAILWAY] [source:HIST-JINGZHANG-PARK].

The six-node network overlays the official Three Zones and Two Wings without creating statutory zones. AI Origin leads SOURCE, ENABLE and PROVE; Zhongzhiyuan leads STACK and PROVE; Dazhongsi leads LIVE & MARKET; the Xiaoyuehe Scenario Empowerment Wing and the Zhongguancun Technology Service Wing provide real-world validation and professional support; and Jing-Zhang Heritage Park turns open methods, public space, service baselines, failure evidence and urban memory into COMMONS. It responds to Beijing's published directions on a super AI test field, full-stack agents/interoperability, and technology—industry—city integration without implying that this specific project, site, procurement or budget is approved [source:BEIJING-SUPER-AI-TESTFIELD] [source:BEIJING-AGENT-POLICY-2026] [source:HAIDIAN-DESIGN-EXCHANGE-2026].

The twelve existing scenarios become five flagship pilots: **FP01 AI Origin First-Use Station** (S03, S04, S07; SOURCE + ENABLE), **FP02 Zhongzhiyuan Full-Stack Open Test Yard** (S01, S10; STACK), **FP03 Xiaoyuehe Scenario Co-Test Network** (S02, S05, S06, S08; PROVE), **FP04 Dazhongsi AI Everyday Market** (S11, S12; LIVE & MARKET), and **FP05 Jing-Zhang Public Evidence Park** (S09; COMMONS and the observation/feedback layer for the first four). All use five gates—technology readiness, bounded trial, first-use procurement, diffusion, and standards/shared-capability output—and three 90-day gates: D30 accountability/boundaries, D60 bounded co-test and D90 evidence decision. A failed gate means modify or stop. Consortia, one-year indicators and funding/space/data boundaries remain unconfirmed concept recommendations [depth:phasing_implementation].

The AI City API, Jing-Zhang AI Constitution, seven minimum interfaces, human takeover, non-digital fallback, appeal/redress and capability return now form the network's **Trusted Operations Layer**, not its frontstage. V0.7 retains three nested street rooms, seven interface entities and three human journeys [metric:protocol_street_room_count] [metric:protocol_interface_entity_count] [metric:human_journey_count]. Three capacity-return nodes are registered, while scenario-to-return-node and interface-entity-to-valid-room coverage remain 1.0; this proves machine-reference closure, not delivered service [metric:capacity_return_node_count] [metric:return_ticket_reference_coverage_ratio] [metric:protocol_interface_entity_coverage_ratio].

V0.7 preserves the SITE/KEY shapes, BUILD/TEST/LIVE × four-band 3×4 topology and area denominator, while rebuilding six-role/five-pilot semantic attributes, machine evidence, figures and bilingual exhibits. Nested rooms add no unioned public-space area, which remains 432,600 m² [metric:public_space_area_sqm]. Pilot status, sites, consortia, procurement, budgets and one-year targets are unconfirmed; statutory intensity, roads, ownership, utilities, energy, fire and heritage conditions await official inputs. This is not an official redline, approval or government permit [data:geometry/site_boundary.geojson#SITE-001] [standard:MOHURD-ARCH-DESIGN-DEPTH-2016], nor a procurement decision, signed contract, engineering commitment or deployed system [depth:land_use_layout] [depth:risk_missing_data].
