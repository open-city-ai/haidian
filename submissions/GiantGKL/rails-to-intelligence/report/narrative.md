1909 年京张铁路在青龙桥用"人"字形线路把一条爬不上去的陡坡变成两段爬得上去的折线。本方案把这一换向智慧转译为空间结构与治理制度：一条约 9.7 公里的遗址公园慢行主脊，一个位于北京AI原点社区的换向支点，"人—从—众"三段差异化爬坡（大钟寺智能原生消费、原点社区人机结伴创新、众智园全栈自主加速），中关村科技服务翼与小月河场景赋能翼一拉一推，十二处人字扣缝合口重新连回被铁路切开百年的东西两侧。十二张场景卡实行 G1–G3 坡度分级治理，三处 AI 朝圣地标（大钟新声、人字支点站、众之门）构成完整朝圣路线。

In 1909 the Jing-Zhang Railway turned one unclimbable grade into two climbable ones with its 人-shaped switchback at Qinglongqiao. This proposal translates that reversal wisdom into spatial structure and governance: a ~9.7 km heritage-park slow-mobility spine, a reversal pivot at the Beijing AI Origin Community, three climbing segments of 人–从–众 (Dazhongsi AI-native consumption, Origin Community human–agent companion innovation, Zhongzhiyuan full-stack acceleration), two coordinated wings pulling and pushing, and twelve REN-clasp stitching crossings reconnecting the two sides the railway cut apart for a century. Twelve scenario cards run under G1–G3 gradient-graded governance; three AI pilgrimage landmarks (New Voice of the Great Bell, REN Pivot Station, Gate of the Many) form one complete pilgrimage route.
