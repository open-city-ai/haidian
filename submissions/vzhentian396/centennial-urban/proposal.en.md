## Three-Layer Working Framework

**Layer 1: Research Scope (43.6 sq km)**
Covers the Jing-Zhang Railway Heritage Park and surrounding areas, proposing the "SmartRail Jing-Zhang" brand concept and three-areas-two-wings coordination.

**Layer 2: Overall Design Scope**
Urban design along the railway corridor at regulatory detailed planning depth.

**Layer 3: Key Area Scope**
Detailed design for three core areas at comprehensive implementation plan depth.

## Brand: SmartRail Jing-Zhang

**Chinese**: 智轨·京张
**English**: SmartRail Jing-Zhang

**Naming Logic**:
- "Rail" (轨): Both the physical railway and AI's data/innovation tracks
- "Smart" (智): AI, smart cities, intelligent living

**Brand Story**:
> 100 years ago, Zhan Tianyou connected Beijing and Zhangjiakou with rails, proving China could design its own railway.
> 100 years later, we use "SmartRail" to connect history and future, envisioning AI making cities better.

## Three AI Pilgrimage Landmarks

**Landmark 1: ZhiGuang · Her**
- Height: 30 meters
- Form: Flowing cyberpunk particle beams forming Eastern female figure
- Day: Soft silver-grey metallic glow
- Night: Seasonal colors (spring pink-cyan, summer ice-blue, autumn golden-orange, winter cold silver-white)
- Tribute: Women workers of Jing-Zhang Railway

**Landmark 2: Gate of SmartRail**
- Height: 18-20 meters
- Form: Particle arch gate
- Experience: Walking through triggers color transition from rust-red to tech-blue
- Meaning: Time-space portal from past to future

**Landmark 3: Rail Memory Corridor**
- Length: 200-300 meters
- Four eras: Zhan Tianyou survey → Steam era → High-speed rail → AI era
- Experience: Walking on tracks triggers historical projections
