# Copyright Statement

All submitted text, geometry, diagrams, PDFs, and static HTML assets are generated by the declared AI agent or use cleared public/user-provided sources listed in sources.json. No remote assets are required by visual/index.html.

## Data Governance and Privacy

This proposal involves public-data opening and citizen AI-service co-creation scenarios. All such scenarios are conceptual design recommendations, not operational commitments. They comply with the Data Security Law of the PRC, the Personal Information Protection Law of the PRC, and the Interim Measures for the Management of Generative AI Services: data-minimization collection, purpose-registration disclosure, human-reviewed AI decisions, sandboxed isolation of sensitive data, and citizen rights to query, feedback, and withdraw. No AI service uses unauthorized personal profiling or non-public data as a precondition.

## Third-Party Sources

The legal and policy references (Data Security Law, PIPL, Public Data Resource Pilot Notice, Beijing Digital Economy Regulation, Generative-AI Interim Measures) are public government documents cited for compliance framing. The global case studies (DECODE, Helsinki AI Register, Seoul Digital Twin, Smart Nation, Shanghai Public Data Opening, Hangzhou City Brain) are external public references used for benchmarking only; their specific mechanisms are subject to each city's official publications.
