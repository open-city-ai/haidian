## 贡献墙与荣誉体系条款（正式文本） / Contribution & Honor Wall Clauses (formal text)

正文「贡献墙与荣誉体系治理条款」的正式条款如下，与 proposal.md 同等发布：

1. **自愿加入 (Opt-in)**：任何个人或团队的标识进入贡献墙、荣誉墙或碑刻展示，均以其显式确认授权为前提；未确认的贡献仅以匿名聚合统计呈现。
   Attribution on any wall or engraving requires the contributor's explicit confirmation; non-consenting contributions appear only as anonymized aggregates.
2. **最小展示 (Data minimization)**：默认展示字段限于项目代号、贡献哈希前缀和自愿公开的昵称；不收集、不展示真实姓名、头像、联系方式或其他可直接识别个人身份的信息。
   Displayed fields default to project codename, contribution-hash prefix, and an optionally public nickname; directly identifying information is not collected or displayed.
3. **可撤回 (Withdrawal)**：贡献者可随时申请撤回。线上展示在承诺时限内移除（时限于实施决策门 G2 核定并公开）；实体碑刻以整版重刻或铭牌遮蔽处理。撤回不影响相关开源成果按其自身许可证继续存在。
   Contributors may withdraw at any time. Online displays are removed within a committed turnaround (set and published at gate G2); engraved portions are re-engraved or covered. Withdrawal does not affect the underlying open-source works under their own licenses.
4. **申诉与审核 (Appeal & review)**：异议、更正与下架请求由拟议的开源社区委员会（含社区代表与法务支持）受理，处理过程留痕、结果按年度汇总公开；不接受未成年人署名。
   Objections, corrections, and takedown requests are heard by a proposed community committee (with community representatives and legal support); handling is logged with annual public summaries. Attribution by minors is not accepted.
5. **数据边界一致性**：上述条款与方案场景卡声明的个人信息最小化、脱敏聚合、不可追溯个人的数据边界一致。
   These clauses are consistent with the personal-data minimization, de-identified aggregation, and non-traceability boundaries declared in the scenario cards.
