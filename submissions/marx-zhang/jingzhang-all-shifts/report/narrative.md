正式叙事以 `proposal.md` 与完整英文译稿 `proposal.en.md` 为准。下列内容是三处重点区运行与审计升级包的可单独审阅说明；结构化字段位于 `visual/assets/three-area-operations-audit.json`。

## 三处重点区运行与审计升级包

本包是三处同班场可单独阅读的概念性工作包，把方案中“人工入口、无扫码服务、无障碍连续路线”的共同承诺转成可比较的核验顺序。它**不**选定地块、不确认开放时段、不招募人员、不授权服务，也不形成法定空间控制。三处重点区 polygon 均仍为临时约束范围；正式几何发布后必须整体替换与复算。

## 三处共用的五道门

| 门 | 决策 | 最低证据 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| G0 | 付费共创 | 角色、报酬、数据与停用条件书面确认 |
| G1 | 现场与责任核验 | 范围、责任、消防、无障碍、设备隔离与撤场条件通过 |
| G2 | 可逆开放 | 仅在已核验的时段运行；不接生产系统 |
| G3 | 独立审计 | 审看可达、劳动、隐私、申诉与停用记录 |
| G4 | 扩大—修正—停止 | 根据证据决定，不以招商、客流或效率替代审计 |

## 审计维度

| ID | 维度 | 记录什么 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A1 | 可达与无障碍 | 人工入口、无扫码服务、连续无障碍路线 |
| A2 | 人工交接与申诉 | 人工升级可用、问题有处理责任与复核路径 |
| A3 | 数据最小化与隐私 | 只保留必要去标识事件；不记录绩效、情绪、轨迹或生产工单 |
| A4 | 劳动、安全与撤场 | 付费参与、设备隔离、当班责任、可撤场 |
| A5 | 公共连续性与公开复盘 | 时段、停用、修复与月度摘要可解释 |

## 众智园维护院

- **运行重点：** 研发—维护—带薪学习在公共首层交班
- **可逆活动：** 非生产性维护演示、带薪学习、人工交接
- **审计证据：** 授权范围、设备隔离、人工升级、去标识服务记录
- **暂停 / 不开放：** 未完成设备/用电隔离或人工入口失效
- **共同不可让渡条件：** 人工入口、无扫码服务、连续无障碍；禁止个人评分、情绪识别、隐蔽追踪、自动惩罚与生产系统接入。

## AI 原点交接院

- **运行重点：** 学习—照护—人工服务在多模态导视下交接
- **可逆活动：** 无扫码服务演练、照护转接、无障碍导视核验
- **审计证据：** 连续路线、人工响应、申诉闭环、低刺激恢复空间可达
- **暂停 / 不开放：** 无障碍路线中断、人工服务或申诉入口缺失
- **共同不可让渡条件：** 人工入口、无扫码服务、连续无障碍；禁止个人评分、情绪识别、隐蔽追踪、自动惩罚与生产系统接入。

## 大钟寺交换院

- **运行重点：** 换乘—配送—小店服务—夜间安全离开分流协同
- **可逆活动：** 遮雨交接、短停补给、小店数据边界与人工求助演练
- **审计证据：** 人车分流、无追踪导向、夜间照明检查、应急转介
- **暂停 / 不开放：** 步行/配送冲突无法缓解，或出现个人路线追踪
- **共同不可让渡条件：** 人工入口、无扫码服务、连续无障碍；禁止个人评分、情绪识别、隐蔽追踪、自动惩罚与生产系统接入。


## 暂停与撤场条件

- 人工入口缺失
- 无障碍路线阻断且未修复
- 个人评分或自动惩罚
- 情绪识别、身份推断或隐蔽追踪
- 接入生产系统或生产性工单
- 设备、用电、消防或人身安全条件不通过
- 责任角色、申诉路径或撤场预案缺失

## 使用与披露边界

任何核验缺失、失败或不完整，均意味着不安装、不开放。任何汇总记录都只是拟议模板，仍需单独批准的数据治理方案。本包不提供基线、绩效目标、投资估算或本地需求证明。


## Three Key Areas Operating and Audit Upgrade Pack

This is a standalone, concept-only working pack for the three Shift Yards. It turns the proposal's shared human-access, no-scan and accessibility commitments into a comparable sequence of checks. It does **not** select a parcel, confirm an opening hour, recruit people, authorise a service or define a statutory spatial control. Every key-area polygon remains a provisional constraint and must be replaced and recalculated when official geometry is released.

## Common five-gate sequence

| Gate | Decision | Minimum evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| G0 | Paid co-design | roles, compensation, data boundary and pause conditions agreed in writing |
| G1 | Site and duty verification | scope, duty, fire, accessibility, isolation and removal conditions pass |
| G2 | Reversible activation | operate only in verified windows; no production-system connection |
| G3 | Independent audit | review access, labor, privacy, appeal and pause records |
| G4 | Scale, revise or stop | decide from evidence, not investment, footfall or efficiency claims |

## Audit dimensions

| ID | Dimension | What is recorded |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A1 | Access and accessibility | staffed threshold, no-scan service, continuous accessible route |
| A2 | Human handover and appeal | staffed escalation works; issues have duty and review path |
| A3 | Data minimisation and privacy | necessary de-identified events only; no performance, emotion, traces or production tickets |
| A4 | Labor, safety and removal | paid participation, equipment isolation, on-duty responsibility and removability |
| A5 | Public continuity and open review | time windows, pauses, repairs and monthly summary are explainable |

## Zhongzhiyuan Maintenance Yard

- **Operating focus:** R&D, maintenance and paid learning share a public ground floor
- **Reversible activity:** non-production maintenance demo, paid learning and staffed handover
- **Audit evidence:** authorisation, equipment isolation, staffed escalation and de-identified service records
- **Hold / do not open:** equipment/power isolation incomplete or staffed access unavailable
- **Shared non-negotiables:** Staffed access; no-scan service; continuous accessibility; no personal scoring, emotion recognition, covert tracking, automated punishment or production connection.

## AI Origin Handover Yard

- **Operating focus:** learning, care and staffed service connect through multimodal guidance
- **Reversible activity:** no-scan service drill, care handover and accessible-wayfinding check
- **Audit evidence:** continuous route, staffed response, appeal closure and quiet recovery access
- **Hold / do not open:** accessible route blocked, or staffed service/appeal route unavailable
- **Shared non-negotiables:** Staffed access; no-scan service; continuous accessibility; no personal scoring, emotion recognition, covert tracking, automated punishment or production connection.

## Dazhongsi Exchange Yard

- **Operating focus:** transfer, delivery, small-business service and safe night departure are separated and coordinated
- **Reversible activity:** sheltered handover, short-rest supply, shop-data boundary and staffed-help drill
- **Audit evidence:** flow separation, non-tracking guidance, night-lighting check and emergency referral
- **Hold / do not open:** walking/delivery conflict cannot be mitigated, or individual route tracking appears
- **Shared non-negotiables:** Staffed access; no-scan service; continuous accessibility; no personal scoring, emotion recognition, covert tracking, automated punishment or production connection.


## Pause and removal conditions

- staffed access absent
- accessible route blocked and unrepaired
- personal scoring or automated punishment
- emotion recognition, identity inference or covert tracking
- production-system or production-ticket connection
- equipment, power, fire or personal-safety condition fails
- duty role, appeal route or removal plan missing

## Use and disclosure boundary

A failed, missing or incomplete check means no installation and no opening. Any aggregate record is a proposed template only and requires a separate approved data-governance protocol. The pack provides no baseline, performance target, investment estimate or proof of local demand.
