# 京张晨昏带 · 方案叙事

## THE DAYNIGHT BELT · Proposal Narrative

本包以晨昏线（昼夜分界）为时间母题，提出"京张晨昏带"概念：与城市订立"时间契约"，把京张遗址公园带组织为 24 小时全时段青年友好公共空间带。空间结构为"一轴三时区两色带"——京张遗址公园 24h 活力主脊串联三座青年全时段节点广场：晨光台（众智园·晨时区）、24小时书房（AI 原点社区·全时段）、午夜穹顶（大钟寺·夜时区）；冷昼蓝与暖夜琥珀双色语汇随晨昏线渐变。三层范围（统筹研究 43.6 km² / 总体设计 11.4 km² / 重点区域 368.4 公顷）逐级落实，用地、建筑、交通、蓝绿空间与青年场景以 GeoJSON 与指标复算支撑；绿地 26.3%、青年广场核心 2.4%、体育 3.6%、社区服务 1.8%，公共生活底盘约 34%。所有空间建议为概念方案，待正式边界与控规条件补齐后由专业团队深化。

This package uses the terminator (day/night divide) as a temporal motif, proposing the "Daynight Belt" concept: a "time contract with the city" organizing the Jing-Zhang Heritage Park belt into a 24-hour all-day youth-friendly public space belt. The "one spine, three time zones, two color bands" structure links the 24h Active Spine through three youth all-day node plazas — Dawn Terrace (Zhongzhiyuan, morning), the 24h Library (AI Origin, all-day) and the Midnight Dome (Dazhongsi, night) — with cool daylight blue and warm amber graded along the terminator. The three scope levels are implemented step by step, with land use, buildings, transport, blue-green space and youth scenarios supported by GeoJSON and recomputed metrics (green 26.3%, youth plaza cores 2.4%, sports 3.6%, community service 1.8%; a public-life floor of about 34%). All spatial proposals are conceptual, pending official boundaries and regulatory conditions.
