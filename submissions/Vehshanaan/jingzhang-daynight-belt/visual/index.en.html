AI Origin Community · All-day (culture/study)

All-day youth culture core: culture, education and community service organized around the 24h Library plaza; Qinghuayuan station site as cultural anchor (heritage scope per the heritage authority).

  • 24h Library: all-day study / developer star registry

Dazhongsi · Night (night vitality/consumption)

Night vitality & consumption belt: youth-friendly commercial street renewal with Midnight Dome as the core; night lighting grading and safety fallback.

  • Midnight Dome: night-life market / main stage
  • Adaptive night lighting (S05)
  • Risk: night noise & operating permits need double confirmation
