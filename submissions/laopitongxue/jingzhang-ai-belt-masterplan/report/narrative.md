# 京张共同地 / Jing-Zhang Common Ground

**一句话承诺：让人留得下，让城市连得上，让系统退得回。**

本方案把城市思想置于造型之前：以公正与留居权为价值底线，以共同资源和可纠错治理为操作系统，以日常城市与生态过程构成空间底盘，只在站点、客流、可达性、公共服务和可负担机制同时得到证据支持时启用 TOD 与增量开发。总体结构为“一条共同地、三座公共庭、四张保障网、十二种日常”。

方案刻意不把 AI 当作城市主角。十二个场景均同时声明公共问题、空间动作、AI 辅助、非 AI 替代、最小数据、人审、申诉、回滚、KPI 与停止条件；医疗、执法、居住资格、就业和基本公共服务不交由自动系统终局决定。

当前 GeoJSON 是用于内容评审和关系测试的概念模型：临时总体范围约 11.413 平方公里，九类用地形成无缝分区，三处重点区、公共庭、慢行缝合、气候共同地、原型建筑和四阶段项目门均可被同源重算。由于仓库公开 Issue 已揭示临时边界与公开遗产对象的空间错位，任何坐标、面积、强度或项目落位都不得用于法定红线、产权、工程或投资决策。

完整论证见 `proposal.md` 与 `proposal.en.md`；证据链见 `sources.json`、`assumptions.json`、`metrics.json` 及三套矩阵；可视化与 PDF 均由同一组结构化数据派生。

**Promise: enable people to stay, reconnect the city, and keep every system correctable.**

The proposal places urban thought before form. Justice and the right to remain are the value floor; commons governance and reversibility are the operating system; everyday urbanism and ecological process form the spatial framework. Transit-oriented intensification is conditional on verified stations, ridership, real walking access, public-service capacity, and affordability safeguards. The spatial proposition is “one common ground, three public courts, four safeguard networks, and twelve everyday lives.”

AI is deliberately not the protagonist. Every scenario names the public problem, spatial intervention, AI support, non-AI fallback, minimum data, human review, appeal, rollback, KPI, and stop rule. Automated systems never make final decisions on medical diagnosis, enforcement, housing eligibility, employment, or access to essential public services.

The submitted GeoJSON is a conceptual model for content review and relational testing. It must be re-anchored and recalculated when official boundaries, statutory plans, cadastral, heritage, transport, utility, building, ecological, and demographic data become available.
