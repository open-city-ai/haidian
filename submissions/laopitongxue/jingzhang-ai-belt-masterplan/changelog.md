## v0.1 - 2026-08-10

Concept and evidence baseline / 概念与证据基线

- 确立“一块共同地、三座公共庭、四道保障、十二种日常”的总体框架，以“留得下、连得上、退得回”作为公共价值底线。 / Established the framework of one common ground, three public courts, four safeguards, and twelve daily lives, with the public-value promise to stay, connect, and reverse.
- 以仓库暂定边界启动参数化空间原型、分期门槛和证据链；从首版起即声明其仅供内容审阅与设计测试，不是法定红线、控规、权属或建设依据。 / Started the parametric spatial prototype, phasing gates, and evidence chain from repository-provisional boundaries, disclosed from the outset as content-review and design-testing inputs rather than statutory redlines, controls, tenure, or construction evidence.

### Precision boundary / 精度边界

任何官方总体边界、重点区 polygon、控规、地籍、道路、轨道、市政、文保或实测数据的新增与更正，都应触发新版本，并重算 GeoJSON、指标、矩阵、正文、图件和 PDF。 / Any new or corrected official overall boundary, key-area polygon, control plan, cadastre, road, rail, utility, heritage, or surveyed dataset must trigger a new version and regeneration of GeoJSON, metrics, matrices, narratives, figures, and PDFs.
