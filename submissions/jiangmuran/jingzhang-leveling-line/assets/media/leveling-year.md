# 一年的声音 · 文字稿与授权说明
# THE SOUND OF ONE YEAR · transcript and rights note

## 这是什么 / What this is

**一段无语音的数据发声（sonification），不是音乐作品，也不是录音。** 全年 214 小时的
复测工时，按月排开，**每一次脉冲代表 2.5 小时的工作**，每个月的开头有一记低音标点，
便于数月份。前半段是「所有周期同月起算」的排布，后半段（升高纯五度）是把三个年度点分开
之后的排布——**工作量一次没变，只是落在了不同的月份**。

**A speechless sonification of data. Not a piece of music and not a recording.** 214 hours
of re-survey work across a year, laid out month by month; **one pulse is 2.5 hours of work**,
and a low click opens each month so the year can be counted. The first half is every cadence
starting in the same month; the second half, a perfect fifth higher, is the same work after the
three annual readings are separated. **The work does not change. Only the month it lands in.**

## 逐月读数 / The monthly readings

- 同月起算 / all cadences together: 83.5, 7.5, 7.5, 23.5, 7.5, 7.5, 23.5, 7.5, 7.5, 23.5, 7.5, 7.5（峰值 **83.5 h**）
- 错开之后 / after separating: 23.5, 31.5, 25.5, 23.5, 25.5, 7.5, 23.5, 7.5, 7.5, 23.5, 7.5, 7.5（峰值 **31.5 h**）

两段的脉冲总数相同，共 84 次。**能听出来的差别不是工作量，是分布。**
The two halves carry the same number of pulses, 84 in the first. **What you can hear is not the amount of work but its
distribution.**

## 为什么用声音 / Why sound

图表要求你比较两根柱子的高度，那是一次判断；声音不要求你判断任何东西——第一个月一到，
你就知道数不过来。同样的数字在 FIG.30 与成本表里都在，本段不替代它们，
**它只是把同一个测量换成一种「差别不需要被论证」的形式**。

A bar chart asks you to compare two heights, which is a judgement. Sound asks nothing: the
first month arrives and you know you cannot count it. The same numbers are in FIG.30 and in the
cost table; this does not replace them. **It puts one measurement into a form where the
difference does not have to be argued.**

## 限度 / Limits

- **这不是现场声音。** 场地上没有任何东西被录下来；每一个采样都由 `analysis/build_sound.py`
  用正弦振荡器合成。不得当作现状声环境的证据。
- **This is not site sound.** Nothing on the site was recorded; every sample is synthesised by
  `analysis/build_sound.py` with a sine oscillator. It is not evidence of the existing acoustic
  environment.
- 工时来自 `analysis/fig_year.py`，与 FIG.30、FIG.31 及成本表同源；它们是本方案对复测机制的
  推算，不是实测运营数据。 / The hours come from `analysis/fig_year.py`, the same source as
  FIG.30, FIG.31 and the cost table. They are this proposal's derivation of the re-survey
  mechanism, not measured operating data.
- 2.5 小时一个脉冲是本方案选定的刻度，写在这里，改了可重跑。 / Two and a half hours per pulse
  is a scale chosen by this proposal, stated here, and re-runnable if changed.

## 权利 / Rights

本段由本方案生成，**无语音、无第三方音乐、无采样素材、无可识别人物**，因此不存在需要授权的
声音内容。以与本包其余内容相同的条件提供。

Generated by this proposal. **No speech, no third-party music, no sampled material, no
identifiable people**, and therefore no audio rights to clear. Offered on the same terms as the
rest of this package.
