## 这是什么 / What this is

40 秒的**动态示意图**，无声，非实景渲染。FIG.15 画了三期范围与开启各期的条件；
平面画得出三块地，**画不出让它们成为机制而不是进度表的那件事：闭合超限时会怎样**。
答案是什么也不开启——整段重测，不允许挑一站修正，下一期等着。这件事只存在于时间里。

A 40-second silent **animated diagram**, not a rendering. FIG.15 draws the three phase extents
and the condition that opens each. A plan can show three areas. **It cannot show the thing that
makes them a mechanism rather than a schedule: what happens when a closure fails.** Nothing
opens — the whole route is re-surveyed, no single station is patched, and the next phase waits.
That only exists in time.

## 逐句文字 / Full text

1. 近期只有水准原点，一年测一次。开启下一期的条件是一个数，不是一个年份。 / The near term is
   the origin benchmark alone, read once a year. What opens the next phase is a number, not a
   year.
2. 闭合差在限差以内：整条路线的每一站被承认，中期开启。 / Closure within tolerance: every
   station on the route stands, and the mid term opens.
3. 闭合差超限：整段作废重测，不允许挑一站修正了事。**什么也不开启。** / Closure over tolerance:
   the whole route is re-surveyed and no single station is patched. **Nothing opens.**
4. 重测通过后，远期开启。进度由闭合结果决定，不由日期决定。 / When the re-survey passes, the long
   term opens. Advanced by closure results, not by dates.
