## 这是什么 / What this is

54 秒的**动态示意图**，不是实景渲染，也不是任何建成状况的记录。画面里的每一条线都由随包
几何生成：主脊与两条附合路线取自 `geometry/roads.geojson`，测点取自
`geometry/public_space.geojson`，路线长度与架站数由 `analysis/fig_closure.py` 在 EPSG:4548
下算出（10.6 公里、107 个架站）。制作脚本为 `analysis/build_media.py`。

A 54-second **animated diagram**, not a rendering and not a record of any built condition.
Every line is generated from the shipped geometry: the spine and the two survey routes from
`geometry/roads.geojson`, the benchmarks from `geometry/public_space.geojson`, route lengths
and setup counts computed by `analysis/fig_closure.py` in EPSG:4548 (10.6 km, 107 setups).
Built by `analysis/build_media.py`.

## 逐句文字 / Full text

1. 水准测量的方法不是「测得准」，是「测得回来」。 / Levelling is not about measuring
   precisely. It is about measuring your way back.
2. 从已知点出发，沿路线逐站传递高程。 / Depart a known point and carry the height forward,
   one setup at a time.
3. 两条附合路线共 10.6 公里、107 个架站，全部由随包几何算出。 / Two survey routes, 10.6 km
   and 107 instrument setups, computed from the shipped geometry.
4. 绕一周回到原点。回到原点时的差值，叫闭合差。 / Come back round to the origin. What is
   left over is called the closure error.
5. 闭合差在限差以内，整条路线的每一站才被承认。 / Within tolerance, every station on the
   route stands.
6. 超限，整段作废重测，不允许挑一站修正了事。 / Over tolerance, the whole route is
   re-surveyed. No single station may be patched.
7. 本方案把这条规矩用在读数错了会伤到人的地方。 / This proposal applies that rule where a
   wrong reading can hurt someone.

## 限度 / Limits

- **高差的起伏是示意的。** 站序、路线长度、架站数与「路线闭合于出发点」这四件事由随包数据
  决定；每一站的具体高差没有实测值，画面上的起伏只用来表现「累积」这件事本身，不得当作
  高程数据读取。
- **The rises and falls are illustrative.** The station order, route lengths, setup counts and
  the fact that each route closes where it started come from the shipped data. Individual
  height differences are not surveyed; the profile shows accumulation, and must not be read as
  elevation data.
- 边界为仓库临时边界，全部空间内容为概念建议，不替代法定规划。 / The boundary is the
  repository's provisional one; all spatial content is conceptual and does not substitute for
  statutory planning.
