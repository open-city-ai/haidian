**One line**: read "Jing-Zhang" in full — a heritage slow line above, the HSR racing below, green power and compute running up from Zhangjiakou, models, talent, and scenarios running down from Haidian; urban design translates this two-way relationship into "Surface + Under Lines, three stations one gate, two platforms eight crossings, a twin-river cooling loop", under a Two-Way Protocol that pairs every up-line collection with a down-line public return and a named way to stop.
