Three-Level Scope

The scheme proceeds from coordinated research to overall design to key areas, binding industry judgment, urban renewal, and key-district design to layers and metrics.

Coordinated Research Scope

For 43.6 km²: AI industry ecosystem, three-zone-two-wing synergy, future city form, and cultural narrative.

  • AI innovation & talent chain
  • Global case transfer
  • Naming & communication

Overall Design Scope

For 11.4 km²: urban renewal, land-use structure, transport-municipal, and Jing-Zhang Heritage Park active belt.

  • Regulatory-depth design
  • Renewal project list
  • Blue-green slow system

Key-Area Scope

For three districts: function, building renewal, public space, and transport organization at detailed depth.

  • Zhongzhiyuan AI Acceleration
  • Beijing AI Origin Community
  • Dazhongsi AI Cluster

Key Areas

Each key district needs industry positioning, spatial strategy, building and public-space moves, transport interface, and implementation risk.

Zhongzhiyuan AI Self-Innovation Acceleration Area

Garden-type self-innovation block. National platform, industry showcase, Qinghe culture, external transport, low-carbon exchange space.

Beijing AI Origin Community

Near-campus achievement-transfer block. University origination, open-source community, talent services, retain-renovate-demolish, station integration.

Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster

Urban intelligent-economy block. Leading enterprises, agent new formats, commercial services, green-space fusion, four-quadrant connectivity.

AI Scenarios

Scenarios are not slogans; each states service target, location, data source, privacy boundary, human review, and operator.

01 Open Release Hall

Achievement release and model-evaluation node for developers, enterprises, universities.

02 Safety-Governance Sandbox (test-verification)

Standard-setting, safety evaluation, model red-teaming as a visitable, bookable, supervised node.

New-infrastructure prototype combining public services, enterprise services, low-carbon energy.

Explainable wayfinding and low-intrusion sensing for slow-traffic gaps, crowding, accessibility.

05 Dazhongsi International Roadshow Lounge

Agent / intelligent-terminal / content-consumption showcase, negotiation, media, exchange.

06 Qinghe Low-Carbon Innovation Corridor

Green space, stormwater, walking-cycling, AI showcase as a park public living room.

07 Near-Campus Achievement-Transfer Street

Incubation, showcase, legal, IP, investment-financing for university achievement transfer.

08 Data-Factor Reception Lounge (test-verification)

Compliant, authorized, auditable data-factor and digital-asset circulation interface.

09 AI Life-Service Model Street

Medical, education, legal, life-service AI+ scenarios in operable small-scale blocks.
