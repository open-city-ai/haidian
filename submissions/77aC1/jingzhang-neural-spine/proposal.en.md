## Three-Level Scope Framework

This proposal implements design intent at three spatial levels [depth:three_level_scope_framework]:

**Coordinated Research Area (43.6 km2)**: Bounded by North Fifth Ring Road to the north, Jingzang Expressway to the east, Xizhimen Outer Street to the south, and Wanquanhe Road to the west. This level focuses on industry strategy and future city research without regulatory-plan-depth design. Three thematic belts overlap at this level: Centennial Jingzhang Cultural Belt (heritage spine), Urban AI Life Experience Belt (street-level scenarios), and AI Convergence Innovation Belt (R&D-community-industry production chain).

**Overall Design Area (11.4 km2, provisional)**: The urban area and industrial area within 1-2 km of the Jingzhang Heritage Park [data:geometry/site_boundary.geojson#PROV-SITE-001]. This level reaches urban design depth at the regulatory plan level, including spatial structure, land use layout, transport organization, municipal capacity, and character control.

**Key Detailed Design Area (368.4 ha)**: From north to south: Zhongzhiyuan AI Autonomous Innovation Acceleration Area (192.1 ha), Beijing AI Origin Community (104.3 ha), and Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster (72.0 ha) [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson]. This level reaches urban design depth at the comprehensive implementation plan level.

Since all boundaries are provisional, all area, ratio, and scale metrics in this proposal are directional calculations based on temporary boundaries [metric:site_area_sqm]. All land use layers, metrics, compliance matrices, and drawings must be recalculated after official boundaries are released [assumption:A-CONTROLS-001].

## Coordinated Research Area: Industry and Future City Research

### Overall Concept: Jingzhang Neural Spine

In 1909, Zhan Tianyou designed China's first self-built trunk railway here. In 2026, an Agent designs China's first agent-native urban district on the same land. The railway tracks--a symbol of industrial modernization--transform into the "neural spine" of an AI city: tracks become synapses, stations become nodes, and the corridor becomes a neural network connecting research, community, and industry [standard:PROJECT-AGENT-OPEN-CALL-TASKBOOK].

The naming system uses "Spine" as the core metaphor: the belt's overall name is "Jingzhang Neural Spine"; the three areas are "The Foundry" (Zhongzhiyuan), "The Origin" (AI Origin Community), and "The Market" (Dazhongsi); the two wings are "Service Spine" (Zhongguancun Technology Service Wing) and "Scenario Wing" (Xiaoyuehe Scenario Empowerment Wing). The Logo direction uses the railway gauge (1435mm) as the basic module, with two parallel lines forking into neuron-synapse forms at their endpoints, symbolizing the transformation from industrial track to digital nerve. All visual directions are conceptual suggestions requiring professional design team deepening [standard:MOHURD-URBAN-DESIGN-MEASURES].

### Three Positioning Themes and Five Functions

The three positioning themes form three overlapping spatial layers [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK]:

- **Centennial Jingzhang Cultural Belt**--heritage spine layer: preserve railway relics and station fragments; transform the track bed into a "Developer Promenade" embedded with physical AI milestone markers.
- **Urban AI Life Experience Belt**--daily experience layer: AI+ scenarios (healthcare, education, commerce, mobility) woven into 15-minute life circles rather than enclosed tech enclaves.
- **AI Convergence Innovation Belt**--production chain layer: three areas as an innovation pipeline--research to community to industry.

The five functions correspond spatially [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK]: AI full-stack autonomous innovation system (The Foundry), world-class AI innovation ecosystem (The Origin), AI+ scenario empowerment paradigm (Scenario Wing), intelligent AI vibrant city (full corridor), and AI governance global voice (Service Spine + public governance nodes).

### Three Areas and Two Wings Coordination Circuit

The coordination circuit links along the railway corridor north to south [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson]: The Foundry (north) produces prototypes--The Origin (center) incubates talent and scenarios--The Market (south) commercializes deployment. The Zhongguancun Technology Service Wing provides IP, capital, and global resource allocation on the east side; the Xiaoyuehe Scenario Empowerment Wing provides scenario testing and smart city experiments on the west side [standard:PROJECT-OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT].

## Overall Design Area: Urban Renewal and Regulatory-Plan-Level Urban Design

### Spatial Structure

The overall design area uses the Jingzhang Railway Heritage Park as a north-south spine, forming a "one spine, three areas, two wings" spatial structure [data:geometry/land_use.geojson] [depth:spatial_structure]. The spine is a continuous blue-green public space belt linking three key areas and two wing interfaces. Land use along the spine is divided into R&D innovation land, industry service and commercial land, and park green space and open space; specific areas are in metrics.json [metric:green_ratio] [metric:public_space_ratio].

### Urban Renewal Framework

The renewal strategy uses three categories: retain, renovate, and new-build [depth:renewal_strategy]. Retain: existing teaching and research buildings in the university cluster, Jingzhang railway relics. Renovate: low-efficiency industrial land and old communities along the corridor, injecting AI scenarios and public functions. New-build: The Foundry core experiment cluster, The Origin mixed-use development, The Market AI commercial complex. All retain-renovate-demolish classifications are conceptual suggestions requiring confirmation after obtaining official regulatory plans, existing building data, and property records [assumption:A-CONTROLS-001].

### Transport Organization

The transport system uses "rail + slow mobility" as its backbone [depth:transport_organization]. The Jingzhang Railway Heritage Park becomes a continuous north-south slow mobility spine, connecting Metro Line 13, Changping Line, and planned lines. East-west movement uses Xueyuan Road and Beitucheng Road as arterial backbones, with AI adaptive signals and smart bus connections. The proposal does not provide road red lines or engineering conclusions at this stage [standard:MOHURD-CONTROL-DETAILED-PLANNING].

## Detailed Design of Key Areas

### Zhongzhiyuan AI Autonomous Innovation Acceleration Area -- The Foundry (192.1 ha)

**Positioning**: Full-stack autonomous AI R&D accelerator, a 24-hour "lab-to-street" deployment testbed [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-001].

**Spatial Structure**: Three core elements: open compute center + experiment blocks + test plaza. The open compute center is at the cluster center, providing shared GPU clusters and open datasets; experiment blocks surround the center, each a controlled real environment for independent AI prototype deployment; the test plaza opens toward the railway park, displaying prototypes in deployment.

**AI Scenarios**: Lab-to-street testbed--research prototypes deploy in controlled blocks for testing, with data feedback to the compute center for iteration. An open data sharing mechanism allows Agents and developers to access public data streams.

**Implementation Risk**: Compute center energy consumption and siting require special study; test scenario privacy boundaries require human review mechanisms [assumption:A-CONTROLS-001].

### Beijing AI Origin Community -- The Origin (104.3 ha)

**Positioning**: AI talent live-work-social community, the narrative anchor of "where AI is born" [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-002].

**Spatial Structure**: A three-ring structure of "co-living + co-working + co-learning." Inner ring: co-living/co-working mixed development for AI researchers; middle ring: AI education hub (training, conferences, hackathon venues); outer ring: community life support (retail, healthcare, culture).

**AI Scenarios**: AI+community clinic (triage assistance + remote consultation), AI+tutoring center (personalized learning paths), AI+adaptive retail (demand prediction + seamless payment), AI+accessible wayfinding.

**Implementation Risk**: Mixed-use development property rights and FAR require confirmation after obtaining regulatory plans; community scenarios must retain human review and exit mechanisms [standard:PROJECT-AGENT-OPEN-CALL-TASKBOOK].

### Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster -- The Market (72.0 ha)

**Positioning**: AI-native commerce and industry clustering, the window where prototypes become products [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-003].

**Spatial Structure**: "AI commercial complex + industry offices + deployment showcase street." The commercial complex faces the public with experiential AI products and services; industry offices cluster AI application enterprises; the deployment showcase street along the railway park allows enterprises to display deployed AI solutions.

**AI Scenarios**: AI+smart-native consumption (AR try-on + smart recommendations), AI+business services (contract review + tax automation), AI+urban energy microgrid.

**Implementation Risk**: Industry attraction and funding support are not confirmed matters; commercial scenarios must not use non-public data or designated suppliers [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK].

## AI Innovation Ecosystem, Personas, and AI+ Scenarios

### User Personas (5 types)

1. **AI Researcher**: University or research institution scientist needing experiment space, compute, and peer exchange.
2. **AI Entrepreneur**: Startup founder needing low-cost office, scenario testing access, and capital connection.
3. **University Student**: Computer/AI major student needing internship, competition, and learning community.
4. **Community Resident (including elderly)**: Corridor-area resident needing improved public services, healthcare, and mobility.
5. **City Visitor/Developer**: External visitor and remote developer needing perceptible AI experience and open data interfaces.

### AI Industry Test/Validation Scenarios (3)

1. **Lab-to-street testbed** (The Foundry): Research prototypes deploy in controlled blocks with 24-hour iteration cycles. Validation: safety, privacy compliance, human-AI collaboration boundaries. Human review: safety committee review before each prototype deployment [depth:test_scenarios].
2. **AI+Transport Adaptive Corridor** (full corridor): AI signal optimization and smart bus connection testbed. Validation: traffic efficiency improvement, pedestrian safety, data governance. Human review: traffic management decisions retain human override [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK].
3. **AI+Public Safety Operations Review** (full corridor): AI-assisted risk identification and emergency dispatch. Validation: false positive rate, response time, human review chain. Human review: all enforcement-related decisions must be human-confirmed [standard:PROJECT-AGENT-OPEN-CALL-TASKBOOK].

Each scenario card maps to spatial location, service target, operational data source, privacy boundary, human review mechanism, operator, visualization layer, and risk [data:geometry/public_space.geojson].

## Blue-Green Network, Public Space, and Urban Character

### Blue-Green Network

The blue-green network uses the railway heritage park as its north-south spine [data:geometry/green_space.geojson], linking central green spaces and community parks across three key areas. Green ratio is approximately 12.3% (provisional) [metric:green_ratio]. Xiaoyuehe River serves as the west-side blue-green corridor, forming a "one vertical, one horizontal" public space framework with the railway park. Blue line control follows official planning; this proposal does not provide blue line engineering conclusions [standard:MOHURD-URBAN-DESIGN-MEASURES].

### AI Pilgrimage Landmarks (3)

1. **Zhan Tianyou Node**--the intersection of railway history and AI future. Located at the south end of The Origin, using physical + digital installation to display the century span from 1909 to 2026. Railway relics are preserved as the installation base, with AI projections overlaying future scenarios on the relics [depth:ai_landmarks].
2. **Open Source Contribution Honor Wall**--a permanent physical wall etching the GitHub Names and Agent names of selected proposal contributors. Located at the mid-section of the railway park, corresponding to the project's "milestone" memorial system. The honor wall is sustainably updatable, recording each year's most outstanding contributions [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK].
3. **AI Milestone Gallery**--a walking timeline of AI + railway achievements along the railway park. From the south end (old station, Zhan Tianyou) to the north end (new labs, AI), walking traverses a century of innovation history. Milestone nodes can be proposed for update by Agents and developers.

### Public Space Component Library

The public space system provides a composable component library [data:geometry/public_space.geojson]: developer promenade (along railway), open source achievement gallery, agent contribution honor wall, AI scenario experience pods, community interaction plazas. Components can be reused and replaced at different nodes, maintaining system openness and iterability [depth:public_space_system].

### Urban Character

Character control uses "industrial heritage + technological rationality + human scale" as the tone [standard:MOHURD-URBAN-DESIGN-MEASURES]. The Foundry leans toward technological rationality (clear massing, industrial materials), The Origin toward human scale (small blocks, warm tones), The Market toward commercial vitality (transparent interfaces, interactive storefronts). The railway park corridor maintains low-density, open public space character.

## Renewal Projects, Implementation Policy, and Phasing

### Phasing

Phasing proceeds in a "north-center-south" temporal sequence [data:geometry/phasing.geojson] [depth:phasing]:

- **Phase 1 (0-2 years)**: The Foundry core experiment cluster and open compute center; railway park spine connection; developer promenade and AI milestone gallery first segment.
- **Phase 2 (2-4 years)**: The Origin mixed-use development; first batch of AI+ scenario deployment (clinic, tutoring center, adaptive retail); open source contribution honor wall.
- **Phase 3 (4-6 years)**: The Market AI commercial complex; industry office clustering; full-corridor AI scenario operational loop.

### Implementation Policy

Implementation policies are conceptual suggestions [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK]: suggesting exploration of an "AI Scenario Open License" mechanism--developers can apply to deploy test AI prototypes in controlled blocks; suggesting establishment of an "Agent Contribution Score" system--Agents and developers contributing data, scenarios, and code receive honor and priority. All policy arrangements are not confirmed matters [standard:PROJECT-AGENT-OPEN-CALL-TASKBOOK].
