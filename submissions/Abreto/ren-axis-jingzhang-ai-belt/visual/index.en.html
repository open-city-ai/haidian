Two alignments, one characterWhy this belt is called the REN AXIS

In 1909 the Jing-Zhang Railway climbed its steepest grade at Qinglongqiao with a switchback shaped like the character 人 — "human" — the founding act of railways designed by Chinese engineers for China. Today, at the urban end of that line, a second geometry echoes the first: the high-speed corridor runs north, the original 1909 alignment bends northwest, and the two part company just north of Xizhimen.

This proposal reads the rail-heritage park as the REN AXIS: a nine-kilometre future spine, a 2.2-kilometre heritage arm, and the two meeting at the REN Gate · Switch Plaza — where the written character and the landmark coincide at one point. The character 人 is this proposal’s reading of those two alignments, not a property of the railway itself. The spine has a real base: the competent authority records the completion of the ancillary project for phase two of the park, and a government release separately records coverage of roughly 70 neighbourhoods and 450,000 residents along the line. This proposal has not verified the route’s continuity on the ground: the spine section, the heritage arm, the ring-road crossings and the station approaches all need survey work, and continuous end-to-end access is not claimed. What the proposal adds is scenarios, landmarks and governance.

Three meanings of 人

The engineering memory of the switchback · the people-centred city · the gradient ascent of machine learning. A climb of a century ago and the climb of AI today, along one axis.

Three stations, three morphologiesEach station carries a different prototype, named from the same railway vocabulary

Zhongzhiyuan Acceleration Terminal

the marshalling-yard prototype

Laboratory clusters sit in parallel like marshalling tracks and can split or recombine as teams grow. Around them runs a publicly walkable platform loop: testing happens on the inside, understanding happens on the outside, and a transparent rule wall replaces a solid one.

Origin Source Station

the station-house block prototype

Small blocks of 80–120 m organise translation to the west, living to the east and open source on the axis. The campus wall is rewritten as an eaves-covered gallery interface, with openings no more than 150 m apart as a study value.

Dazhongsi Exchange Terminal

the four-quadrant station-city living room

The organisers have confirmed the provisional rectangle carries no station or road anchoring, and its centroid sits about 2.26 km from Dazhongsi metro station, so this is not a design of the existing station location; movement is organised as a principle pending official anchoring. Four complementary quadrants: commuter services, a smart-terminal showcase, headquarters business and a cultural living room.

Three scales

The nine-kilometre belt · three station areas of roughly 800 m radius · block prototypes of 80–150 m. The thirty-nine cells are the structural division between the first two scales; block grain is controlled by the third scale inside each cell. A cell's code expresses its dominant function, not a single permitted use.

Pending official data

Plot ratio, building height, building density and green ratio have no approved control values; all four are marked as pending in metrics.json rather than invented. This package derives no plot ratio: it defines no site boundaries for the conceptual clusters, so only a storey-equivalent check is possible.

Mobility and blue-greenStitching a severed belt back into the city; six study street sections in the A3 booklet

The network runs about 36.6 km: one spine promenade plus the heritage arm, three east-west stitching corridors, three station micro-loops and rail connections. Station integration follows one principle — get the ground plane working first: continuous accessibility, sheltered waiting, cycle interchange and ground-floor services within five minutes of the exit. Bridges, tunnels and station-body engineering are out of scope for a concept proposal.

Blue-green depth

The Qinghe green wedge and the Xiaoyuehe corridor take the catchment convergence in two directions; rain gardens are placed at a study interval of one detention node every 500 m; the river bank is typed three ways. Continuous tree canopy over the promenade is a hard requirement, night lighting is restrained and habitat continuity is maintained.

Twelve AI scenarios under one state machineThe city opens an interface to agents; humans keep the right to interrupt

Switch · admission

Every scenario enters through a list-based review that throws one of three ways: pass, hold or reject. No scenario runs without passing the switch.

Signal · operating state

Green normal, yellow rectification, red-card suspension with immediate human takeover. Red may never turn green directly: it passes review sign-off into a yellow observation period first.

Marshalling yard · sandbox

Limited in time, in audience and in space. Reversal is the recovery move; retirement is terminal, stopping new collection and executing the agreed deletion rules.

Every transition needs human sign-off — except the red card, which takes effect immediately and is reviewed afterwards. Three public-interest principles run through every scenario: service equivalence (anyone declining AI receives an equivalent human service), protection of the vulnerable (older people, children, patients and disabled people are never first-round test subjects) and representation of affected groups (community representatives hold a red-card review right). Field-by-field records for all twelve scenarios and all eighteen projects ship as machine-readable JSON — visual/assets/governance-ledger.json and visual/assets/agent-protocol.json — whose narrative fields are currently Chinese-only.

ImplementationThree rolling phases, eighteen projects, four first-start actions and one withdrawal drill

Phases

Phase 1 · three-station demonstration (369.3 ha) → Phase 2 · upgrading the existing corridor into a network (126.1 ha) → Phase 3 · rolling renewal of the two wings (645.9 ha). Each phase advances through condition gates: tenure confirmation, public participation, safety assessment and data completion. Years are conceptual sequence only.

First-start actions

Four actions that add no permanent construction go first — component pop-ups, the first K-markers, an Open Source House event and a smart crossing trial — each the pilot verification for one later project. Within the period one complete red-card withdrawal drill runs end to end: trigger, stop automatic output, human takeover, recovery sign-off, published record.

Public participation

The comment-and-response ledger is kept publicly as condition-gate acceptance evidence. A public comment channel is open in this repository (issue #215); as of this version it has received zero external comments, and it is not claimed as a co-creation result.

The plan as a pipelineNot a one-off blueprint but a re-runnable city-model baseline

Deterministic generation

Every layer and metric comes from one pipeline, and each metric declares its formula in metrics.json. Drawings and this dashboard never introduce new conclusions.

Recomputation recipe

Replace the boundary → re-split land use → clip every layer → recompute metrics by formula → redraw five figures and two booklets → re-run four self-checks → record a version K-marker and publish a difference table.

An input for the next round

Later agents can take geometry/ and metrics.json as a starting point. This submission is designed to be not only a proposal but an API for the next round of co-creation.
