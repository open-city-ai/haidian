# Pinyin Axis REN AXIS——Centennial Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt Urban Design

In 1909, the Jing-Zhang Railway at Qinglong Bridge used a "person" character-shaped return curve to send trains up the steep slope from Nan Kou to Badaling—this was the spiritual origin of Chinese design for mainline railways [source:JZ-HERRINGBONE-PAPER]. Today, at the city's starting point of this railway, another geometric fact echoes it: two track corridors still truly diverge within the city—Jing-Zhang High-Speed Railway corridor heads north, while the Jing-Zhang legacy line corridor (now the ground segment of Line 13) turns northwest. The fork point is just north of Xizhimen. This proposal reads the Jing-Zhang heritage park as the **Person Axis**: with a 9-kilometer future spine running north-south along the high-speed railway corridor, and a 2.2-kilometer historical branch line running along the legacy line's route to the northwest. The two arms converge at the "Person Gate·Switching Square" at the southern portal, forming a "person" character inscribed on the city's plane—where the character shape, landmark, and actual railway geometry coincide.

"In" this scheme has three layers of meaning: it is the engineering memory of a zigzag line, the value bottom color of a people-oriented city, and a metaphor for the method of gradient ascent in machine learning—climbing a hill a century ago and today's AI climbing on the same axis. The spatial structure thus unfolds into **one axis, two arms, three stations, and two wings**: the Wisdom·Acceleration Station bears full-stack independent innovation and AI governance, the Origin·Innovation Station bears near-university innovation and open-source transformation, and the Big Clock·Intersection Station bears intelligent native business forms and urban consumption; the Zhongguancun Technology Services Wing and the Xiaoyue River Scenario Enablement Wing form a loop on the east and west sides. The 9-kilometer main spine is also a **slope narrative**: starting from the human-shaped entrance is the departure, the middle segment of three stations is the turnaround and acceleration, and ending at Qinghe Green Wedge is the summit—scene density, interface intensity, and landmark sequence are all organized along this "slope".

Overview of the Proposal: One, the dual-arm structure is derived from OSM publicly verifiable real bifurcations, not a graphical rhetoric; two, 12 AI scenarios are uniformly managed by a railway vocabulary state machine, including red card suspensions, manual takeovers, and deactivation; three, 39 land-use units, approximately 36.6 kilometers of pedestrian network, and 30 indicators are generated by a deterministic pipeline, ready for a full recalculation once official data is in place; four, three holy sites are aligned with the commemorative mechanisms announced by the call for proposals; five, the first action includes a complete red card recall drill. **Uniform Statement: This proposal is an AI-generated Open Co-Creation recommendation based on provisional rough boundaries. All spatial content is a Conceptual Recommendation and reference solution, intended for in-depth research by professional teams, and does not constitute a government review conclusion. Once the Official Planning Boundary and control conditions are in place, the entire area, proportions, and spatial relationships must be recalculated. This statement covers the entire document.**[source:PROVISIONAL-BOUNDARIES][depth:risk_missing_data]

## Three. Coordinated Research Area: Industrial and Future City Research

**Naming and Visual Identity**: The Chinese main name is "ren zhi zhong," translated as "REN AXIS" in English. The logo features a "ren" character formed by two pen-stroke switch points, with an ascending arrow above it; the primary color, signal green, draws from the railway signal system, complemented by platform gray and K-post gold. The brand vocabulary is derived from the railway system—switch points, signals, marshalling yards, turnaround, and K-posts—which also serve as operational terms for the governance mechanism, spatial grammar, and tripartite integration of brand system and governance process [depth:overall_spatial_structure].

**Synergistic Circuits of Three Major Orientations and Five Key Functions**: The century-old Jing-Zhang Cultural Belt is carried by the human-axis cultural narrative system (K-standard chronology, station memories, pilgrimage landmarks); the Urban AI Life Experience Belt is carried by the Xiao Yuehe Scenic Wing and 12 scene nodes; the AI Convergence Innovation Belt is carried by the three stations and two wings industrial ecosystem. The five key functions form a closed loop: Originating Point (knowledge and outcomes) → Crowd Wisdom Acceleration (engineering, standards, and governance) → Big Clock Confluence (productization and consumption) → Zhongguancun Service Wing (capital and professional services) → Xiao Yuehe Scenic Wing (application validation and data feedback) → Feedback to the Originating Point for Further R&D. Haidian's "1+X+1" industrial system and the "Three Zones and Two Wings" pattern are the overarching context for this circuit [source:HAIDIAN-1X1][source:THREE-AREAS-WINGS]. The industrial foundation is a factual statement: Haidian has over 2,000 AI companies, with 130 registered large models, and core industry scale accounting for about one-third of the national total (as of the 2026 Zhongguancun Forum Annual Conference release) [source:HD-AI-FACTS-2026]—the task of this belt is not to attract from scratch, but to provide a main axis of Public Space for the densest AI industry. The 2025 Edition of the Haidian District Urban Renewal Guidelines has listed artificial intelligence innovation districts and station-town integration as renewal directions, and this plan is a concept refinement under that direction [source:HD-RENEWAL-GUIDE-2025].

**Mechanisms Translated from Global Examples** (for retrieval see sources.json[source:GLOBAL-CASE-PUBLIC-REFS]): Kendall Square — Mixed-use and Ground-Level Activation Update Agreement; King's Cross — Heritage Buildings as Pre-Operational Public Rooms; Station F — High-Frequency Encounters at Co-located Projects; one-north — Mixed Work, Live, and Learn Professional Clusters; Nanshan Science Park — Rolling Upgrades of Existing Industrial Parks; Banqiao Science Valley — Co-located Test Sites and Urban Interfaces; Loongate Collaboration Zone — Cross-District Rule Interfaces; MaRS — Problem-Solving Service Routing. ## Four. Overall Design Area Urban Renewal and Control Detailed Urban Design

**Overall Structure: One Axis with Two Arms, Three Stations, and Two Wings, at Three Scales**. The main spine has a real foundation: the Jing-Zhang Railway Heritage Park Phase II was completed and opened in August 2026, with a 9-kilometer long linear green corridor from Xizhimen to the North Fifth Ring Road fully completed (approximately 53 hectares, directly serving about 450,000 people along the 70 communities in the area) [source:JZ-PARK-PHASE2-OPEN] (Phase I opened a 2.4-kilometer segment in 2023 [source:JZ-PARK-PHASE1]) — this plan builds upon and updates this existing Public Space spine, defining the future arms along the high-speed rail corridor direction [data:geometry/roads.geojson#RD-SPINE], with a 2.2-kilometer historical branch arm extending northwest along the original route [data:geometry/roads.geojson#RD-ARM-HIST]. The two arms diverge at the Renzi Gate·Switch Square [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#PS-004]. Spatial organization is divided into three levels: a 9-kilometer-wide overall strip, three station areas with a radius of approximately 800 meters, and street blocks within the station areas with a prototype scale of 80 to 150 meters. The 39 land-use units serve as the structural division between the first and second levels, with the street block pattern controlled by the third-level prototype within each unit. During the deepening phase, the current road network and property demarcations will be integrated [depth:overall_spatial_structure].

**Gradient Narrative: Incorporating "Climbing the Slope" into the Space**. The southern segment (Renzimen to Dazhongsi) marks the departure: a scale of urban portals, the highest and densest interface, where the arms diverge; the middle segment (Beisanhuan North Third Ring to Yaxue Lu) is a return and acceleration: three east-west corridors act as three switches, with scene density increasing along the axis, where historical side stories unfold their narrative threads; the northern segment (Liu Dao Kou to Qinghe) is the ascent: buildings step back, green wedges expand, and the experimental clusters of Zhongzhiyuan are hidden within gardens. The height of the interface, scene intensity, landmark sequence, and one-day experience (refer to Chapter 9) are all organized along this slope—thus, "gradient ascent" transforms from a name into the spatial operating system of the entire plan.

**Update Strategy Four-Level Staircase**: Operate First (activity periods, open rules, demand for sign and way furniture verification) → Light Renovation and Sharing (open the first floor and idle spaces after fire safety inspection) → Performance Retrofitting (energy conservation, barrier-free access, facade) → Necessary Update (enter statutory procedures). The Demolish–Renovate–Retain Strategy is a method framework rather than a conclusion for each plot [depth:retain_renovate_demolish]: retain the architectural elements that embody the memory of the railway industry and revitalize them, retrofit inefficient buildings and commercial carriers, remove only when legally recognized, and concentrate new construction in the station front cluster. This aligns with the national requirement to prevent large-scale demolition and construction, emphasizing retention and utilization as the main approach [source:MOHURD-63-NO-MASS-DEMOLITION], and organizes within the framework direction of the Beijing Urban Renewal Regulation [source:BJ-RENEWAL-REGULATION].

**Character and Intensity**[depth:height_massing_character][depth:development_intensity_controls]: The character should lean towards "low basements, open ground floors, readable roofs, and restrained night scenes"—maintaining a human-scale continuous shaded environment along the axes, allowing for identifiable public buildings at three stations without obscuring historical resources; materials are layered with durable brick and stone, metal mesh, and replaceable digital interfaces, echoing "technological rationality × railway memory × campus humanities." Official control values for the Floor Area Ratio, height, density, and green space ratio are missing, with the four metrics in the metrics.json file marked as pending confirmation; the net intensity implied by the conceptual scale is expressed in three scenarios (upper limit around 8.7, middle around 4.4, low disturbance), all of which are ranges to be verified in the control plan rather than selected intensities.

## Six, AI Innovation Ecosystem, Talent Profile, and AI-Enabled Scenario

**Six Types of Portraits** are design evaluation tools: Frontline researchers need quiet spaces for interdisciplinary encounters; startup engineers and developers require low-cost spaces, open-source communities, and release scenarios; AI product operators and design talents need scene test fields and consumer interfaces; university students need a learning internship and entrepreneurship connection channel [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-E-W]; community residents (including the elderly) need accessible, understandable, and rejectable public services (with accessibility based on mandatory national standards [standard:GB-55019-2021-ACCESSIBILITY]); and international visitors need bilingual city interfaces and cultural narratives. The three public interest principles run through all scenarios: service equivalence (those not using AI receive equivalent human services), protection of the vulnerable (elders, young children, the sick, and the disabled are not the first test subjects), and the right of affected groups to review with a red card. A verification table for the exclusion moments, mandatory bottom lines, and non-intelligent equivalence paths for each portrait should be included in a machine-readable log and delivered in the package, with the same source presented on electronic displays and A3 ledger pages.

**12 AI Scenarios**[metric:scenario_node_count] are distributed along the axis [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#PS-005], with 3 of them serving as industrial testing validations. The three that most impact the space are as follows: SC-01 Axis Commuter Assistant — intelligent crosswalk guidance along the North Third Ring Road seam, aggregating anonymous pedestrian flows while retaining conventional crosswalk facilities, serving as the first test stone for stitching the corridor; SC-09 Smart Gardening and Ecological Monitoring (Testing) — vegetation identification and irrigation optimization in Zhongzhiyuan Green Corridor, collecting only vegetation and environmental data, reverting to scheduled maintenance if the system fails, serving as a prototype for "AI in the Garden"; SC-12 Urban Model Field — Zhongzhiyuan Exhibition Hub opens a public urban model based on this package's GeoJSON and indicators, with data and metrics.json alignment as a mandatory prerequisite, serving as the public interface for "Solutions as Pipelines." The remaining nine (barrier-free mobility, robot delivery testing, AI tour guide, open-source market, enterprise Copilot, health navigation, learning pods, nighttime care, and autonomous shuttle testing) each have designated locations, minimum data requirements, human fallback options, and exit buttons. Full-field governance records are available in the appendix and on the dashboard.

**Railway Terminology State Machine**: Switches=Admission (Pass/Deferred/Rejected); Signals=Operational Status (Green Normal, Yellow Rectification, Red Hold and Manual Takeover; Red cannot directly transition to Green, must be re-verified and signed off to transition to Yellow for Phase I observation); Marshalling Yard=Sandbox; Reversing=Restore; K-Markers=Version Milestones (Physical K-Markers for cultural entities, version K-Markers for data recalculation). All scenarios follow a lifecycle of "Concept→Sandbox→Pilot→Operation→Retirement", with retirement being the final state. State transitions require manual sign-off, and model records and failure logs are publicly retained. The legal obligations involved in each scenario (Personal Information Minimization, Data Classification and Grading, Content Generation Marking, Intelligent Connected Vehicle Testing Regulations, Public Image Management) are individually registered as "Legal Interface" fields [source:CN-LEGAL-INTERFACE-REFS]—registration serves as an operational transparency layer, not as a substitute for legal procedures, and this scheme does not make compliance determinations.

## Appendix: English Summary

**REN AXIS — the Jingzhang (Beijing–Zhangjiakou) AI Innovation Belt.** In 1909, the Jingzhang Railway climbed its steepest grade with a switchback shaped like human — the signature of China's first self-engineered trunk line. Today, two rail corridors still fork north of Xizhimen: the high-speed corridor running north, the original 1909 alignment bending northwest. This proposal reads the rail-heritage park as the REN AXIS: a nine-kilometre future spine plus a 2.2-kilometre heritage arm, meeting at the REN Gate where the character is written on the city itself.The axis is organized as a gradient — departure at the southern gate, switchbacks through three stations, summit at the Qinghe green wedge — so that scene intensity, interface height and landmarks all climb with it. Three stations differentiate the belt: Zhongzhiyuan Acceleration Terminal (a marshalling-yard prototype of recombinable lab clusters ringed by a public "platform loop"), Origin Source Station (station-house blocks of 80–150 m with eaves-gallery campus edges), Dazhongsi Exchange Terminal (a four-quadrant station-city living room oriented to the real interchange).
