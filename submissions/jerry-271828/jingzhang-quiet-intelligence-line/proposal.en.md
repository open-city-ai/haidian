# Jing-Zhang Jingzhi Line: A Choice for Intelligent Urban Communities

**Jingzhang Quiet Intelligence Line · A civic right-of-way for optional intelligence**

The judgment in this case is straightforward: the advanced nature of future cities should not be predicated on residents' continuous connectivity or constant perception. Along the century-old Jing-Zhang railway, a set of parallel public rights pathways will be formed — the S track provides active-choice intelligent services, while the Q track offers an equivalent path without sensors, applications, or accounts; the two tracks only switch at the "choice threshold" where clear notification and manned stations are present. Each scenario must simultaneously demonstrate visible choices before data collection, visible states during service provision, and the ability to switch to human assistance, as well as the ability to obtain receipts for exit or deletion after service. When all AI is turned off, the pathways must provide a non-degraded "quiet baseline" of shading, lighting, signage, seating, accessibility, manned counters, and emergency response. The scheme's slogan is: **Intelligence can choose, quiet can be reached, service can be taken over by humans.**

## Three-Level Scope Framework

The three layers of the scope adopt a progressive approach of "strategy—structure—prototype," rather than simply scaling up a single comprehensive plan. The Coordinated Research Area addresses how the AI value chain, talent chain, public service chain, and cultural dissemination can synergize in Haidian. The Overall Design Area translates these synergistic relationships into the land use, active transportation, blue-green spaces, renewal, and public service backbone along the Jing-Zhang corridor. The three key areas test, through operational space prototypes, whether research and development, transformation, international exchanges, and residents' daily lives can coexist. Each layer sets the same baseline: intelligent services must be opt-out, ensuring that even if they are declined, the same public service outcome is still achieved. Thus, the governance principle is not an appendix but an integral part of the overall structure.

[depth:three_level_scope_framework] Responsible for the three-level division of labor, [depth:overall_spatial_structure] responsible for the dual-track structure layout. The Overall Design Area is approximately 11.413 square kilometers [metric:site_area_sqm]; the number of key areas is three [metric:key_area_count], but their areas are not used for new verification conclusions. The overall structure is summarized as "**one line, dual tracks, three stations, six thresholds, twelve scenes**": the one line is the Jing-Zhang cultural and green backbone; the S/Q dual tracks separately carry voluntary intelligent and seamless passage; the three stations correspond to Zhongzhiyuan, Origin Community, and Dazhongsi; six thresholds are used for pre-collection diversion; twelve scenes collectively locate technology, human interaction, and exit processes.

The layer system makes the framework into a verifiable object: overall and key boundary areas are shown in [data:geometry/site_boundary.geojson#SITE-001] and [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-002]; land use and capacity tests are found in [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-001] and [data:geometry/buildings.geojson#BLDG-001]; public rights of way are located in [data:geometry/roads.geojson#ROAD-S-001] and [data:geometry/roads.geojson#ROAD-Q-001]; operational scenarios and phasing are detailed in [data:geometry/constraints.geojson#SCENE-01] and [data:geometry/phasing.geojson#PHASE-001]. ## Coordinated Research Area: Industry and Future City Research

The industrial strategy shifts from "demonstrating AI" to "verifying how AI can be reliably used in cities." The upstream sector forms models, chips, data, and open-source initiatives through academic institutions and research platforms. The midstream sector develops shared services through evaluation, security, compliance, intellectual property, and scenario matchmaking. The downstream sector conducts small-scale, reversible verifications in areas such as transportation, healthcare, education, law, consumption, facility maintenance, and cultural tours. The three key zones are responsible for full-stack autonomous verification, on-campus technology transfer, and city-level product experiences. Enterprises do not receive an unrestricted test bed, but rather a "scenario contract" that includes space, data, human resources, time slots, exit conditions, and post-mortem reviews.

Six official source case studies only extract mechanisms, not forms: Helsinki's open 3D base map hints at a common factual layer [source:HELSINKI-3D]; Amsterdam's algorithm registry hints at the need for traceability of who is using what system where must be checkable [source:AMSTERDAM-ALGORITHM-REGISTER]; Punggol's open digital platform hints at the need for a unified interface for cross-system testing [source:PUNGGOL-OPEN-DIGITAL-PLATFORM]; Barcelona Decidim hints at the traceability of public opinions and responses [source:BARCELONA-DECIDIM]; AI Verify hints at repeatable testing before going live [source:SINGAPORE-AI-VERIFY]; Seoul Smart City Center hints at co-location for showcasing, validating, and public communication [source:SEOUL-SMART-CITY-CENTER]. These are background studies and do not represent that the project has adopted the corresponding systems. The relevant restrictions are recorded in assumptions.json under A-CASES-001.

Future urban form is thus not a high-density sensor landscape, but rather "a replaceable technical layer and a continuously supplied public layer." The ground layer prioritizes continuous pedestrian and bicycle paths, shading, rainwater management, and human services; the optional smart layer can be accessed through booking or on-site selection; the operational layer records model versions, service disruptions, human takeovers, complaints, and recoveries.

Industrial evaluation is not just about the number of companies, but also about three public performance metrics: whether the quiet baseline is maintained, whether choices occur before data collection, and whether the gap in outcomes, times, prices, and accessibility between non-AI paths and AI paths converges. The latter remains unknown until there are operational samples, rather than being pre-filled with attractive scores.

## Overall Design Area: Urban Renewal and Regulatory-Plan-Level Urban Design

Overall structure views the long Jing-Zhang corridor as a continuous urban stitcher. Fourteen land use units [metric:land_use_zone_count] are directly divided by temporary site polygons, fully covering and not overlapping; the northern segment leans towards R&D at Zhongzhiyuan, Qinghe Greenway, and flexible blank spaces, the middle segment organizes the transformation of the original community near the school, focusing on open-source education and enterprise services, while the southern segment connects talent living, international exchanges at Dazhongsi, and the Jing-Zhang cultural interface. All land use codes come from the allowed collection in the documentation package, with geometry in [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-001], corresponding to [standard:MNR-LAND-USE-CLASSIFICATION-GUIDE] and [depth:land_use_layout].

Eighteen building capacity test blocks [metric:building_capacity_block_count] are used solely to test the relationship between Public Space, pedestrian and functional mix, with their combined gross floor area recorded as [metric:building_footprint_area_sqm]. They are not intended to represent existing buildings or to generate demolition decisions; `intervention_status=capacity_test_only` and `demolition_decision=false`. Development Intensity, total gross floor area, Building Height, Building Coverage Ratio, and setback distances are all recorded as unknown, with "completed depth response but conclusions pending" noted by [depth:development_intensity_controls] and [depth:height_massing_character]. This approach is more suitable for subsequent professional teams to build upon rather than deriving pseudo-precise scales based on assumed floor counts.

The update method is "small units, reversible, Public Space first, then construction." It starts with testing S/Q dual tracks through ground markings, signage, tree arrays, movable human counters, and temporary shading structures; then, it advances low-efficiency interface updates, first-floor publicness, pedestrian connectivity breakpoints, and service facility improvements in the three key areas; finally, it decides on building implementation based on formal control detailed planning, traffic, municipal, fire safety, cultural heritage, and property conditions. ## Detailed Design of Key Areas

**Zhongzhiyuan AI Independent Innovation Acceleration Area** is positioned as the "Full-Stack Validation Garden." Along the Qinghe side, continuous greenbelts, rainwater management, and seamless passage are prioritized. The inner side is designed for model safety evaluation, joint debugging of edge devices, standard workshops, and a human takeover hall. Industrial testing is not a closed-park demonstration: each test must have a public schedule, device range, exit route, and stopping conditions; the Q track allows continuous passage, while the S track enters after encountering a selection threshold. Spatial evidence follows [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-001], with formal line positions awaiting official key area boundaries and engineering specializations.

**Beijing AI Origin Community** is positioned as a "near-school open-source transformation community." The campus—industrial park—community are seamlessly connected through all-weather walking and cycling paths. The first floor organizes open-source releases, technology transfer, intellectual property, enterprise services, talent living, and resident shared facilities. The Silent Shared Hall offers paper-based reservations, manned inquiries, and account-free services; the Open-Source Release Hall can use translation, search, and accessibility aids, but all outputs are confirmed by the releaser. Here, the test is "can technology help collaboration without making participation thresholds into app installations," as seen in [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-002].

**Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster** is positioned as a "city-level smart product living room." It features public roadshows and long-term exhibitions around rail transit connections, quadrants of pedestrian continuity, corporate displays, content consumption, and international exchanges. At the same time, it retains clear non-digital signage, manual ticketing, and general consumption pathways. The Jing-Zhang Memory Station organizes railway time, engineering spirit, and the innovation history of Zhongguancun into a walkable narrative; any personalized explanations are optional. For more details, see [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-003].

Three prototypes were uniformly reviewed by [depth:three_key_area_detailed_design], each addressing at least the functions, building interfaces, traffic and pedestrian access, blue-green Public Spaces, AI scenarios, human takeover, implementation dependencies, and exit strategies. ## Land Use, Building Scale, and Retain-Renovate-Demolish Strategy

The land use is composed of a mixed sequence of research and development, culture, education, residential, community services, commerce, park green spaces, and blank spaces. Each zone is expressed using `land_use_code`, and must not be replaced by ad-hoc categories such as "AI land use." The current land use structure is a conceptual zoning plan that does not alter existing land ownership or use; formal deepening should overlay official parcels, current land use, and control plan overlays to form recommendations for "retention, minor updates, functional replacement, comprehensive updates, and blank spaces," recording the basis and approval process.

The Demolish–Renovate–Retain Strategy employs four thresholds: the first is a property rights and current condition survey; the second is an assessment of structural integrity, fire safety, energy efficiency, and historical value; the third is a comparison of public interest and full-life carbon impact; and the fourth is the fulfillment of legal procedures and public communication. If any threshold is missing, it does not enter the demolition list. Therefore, [data:geometry/buildings.geojson#BLDG-001] only expresses eighteen reversible capacity blocks, with the total base area [metric:building_footprint_area_sqm] used to test the relationship of street block openness, not to approve the scale of construction. The completion of [depth:retain_renovate_demolish] means "the methods, evidence thresholds, and gaps are clear," not "individual building decisions are complete."

Morphological control emphasizes the "track scale, quiet interface, and readable technology." The ground floor of buildings prioritizes continuous eaves, openable public rooms, and manual service areas; equipment should avoid occupying the main pedestrian interface, and sensing states should be expressed with low-glare, understandable signage; rooftop facilities should be grouped and shielded, with maintenance paths reserved; the volumes facing the Jing-Zhang heritage site and green corridor should maintain segmentation and transparency. Specific heights, setback rates, spacings, and skyline profiles need to be determined after formal control planning, landscape sightlines, cultural heritage, and solar access analyses, responding to the gap alerts in [standard:MOHURD-ARCH-DESIGN-DEPTH-2016].

## Transport, Rail, Municipal Infrastructure, and Public Services

The transportation system includes S perceptible tracks, Q seamless throughways, and six east-west public feeder lines [metric:choice_switch_count]. The two longitudinal lines are not two sets of differently ranked roads: the S tracks offer voluntary navigation, congestion alerts, accessibility coordination, or low-speed connections; the Q tracks rely on standard signage, continuous paving, perceivable information, and human inquiry to accomplish the same arrival task. Six choice thresholds are set before data collection, rather than providing an exit button after users enter the perception zone. The total network concept length is seen in [metric:road_network_length_m] and [data:geometry/roads.geojson#ROAD-Q-001].

Priority should be given to establishing a continuous chain for track stations and key intersections: ground-level crosswalks and accessibility features should precede complex automated shuttle systems; bicycle parking should be close to the entrance but not obstruct blind paths; on busy days, appointments should supplement flow management, with manual guidance as a fallback; and low-speed robots should be subject to time limits, speed limits, priority for pedestrians, and on-site stoppers. Since complete pedestrian and vehicular traffic models, entrance and exit points, parking, and road network models are missing, this case does not specify road right-of-way widths, parking requirements, or capacity values, and a dedicated interface will be left for [depth:traffic_rail_slow_parking].

Municipal infrastructure and New Infrastructure adopt the principle of "safe even when offline." Lighting, flood control, energy, charging, edge computing, and facility inspection must be in local manual mode, with faults visible, data minimized, and clear maintenance responsibility; public services should have human counters, telephone/paper channels, general payments, and accessible alternatives. Community health scenarios should only provide navigation and transfers, not automatic diagnosis; legal and intellectual property scenarios should only provide material support, not replace professional advice; public safety scenarios should only aggregate information, not automatically make disposal decisions. These facilities are located at [data:geometry/constraints.geojson#SCENE-06] and are managed and deepened by [depth:municipal_new_infrastructure].

## Risk, Copyright, and Compliance

Main risks are categorized into six types. First, the Provisional Boundary may cause misinterpretation of area and location, hence all drawings are repeatedly marked as provisional. Second, the lack of control plans and building-by-building documentation may lead to implementation misinterpretation, thus buildings are only subjected to capacity testing, with statutory indicators kept unknown. Third, the implementation of smart services may create implicit coercion, hence pre-screening, Q-track, regular payments, and manual services are set up. Fourth, algorithm errors and equipment interruptions may affect safety, hence manual take-over, stop permissions, and offline manual mode are retained. Fifth, operational costs may cause the non-AI paths to degrade, hence continuous auditing of results, time, pricing, and accessibility equivalence is maintained. Sixth, the display and branding materials may have copyright issues, hence all charts are generated by this case, and examples are only used for textual mechanism research.

Assumptions and gaps are recorded as A-BOUNDARY-001, A-CONTROLS-001, A-BUILDING-001, A-MOBILITY-001, A-PARITY-001, A-CASES-001; [depth:risk_missing_data] is responsible for converting the gaps into the data list for the next phase. The proposal does not claim approval, land ownership, construction scale, or implementation commitment; all spatial actions are to be considered as "Conceptual Recommendation/Reference Proposal/For Professional Team Deepening." Privacy is handled with the minimum necessary, clear purpose, short-term storage, on-site notification, revocability, and Human Review; high-impact services involving health, law, public safety, and accessibility must not be decided solely by models.
