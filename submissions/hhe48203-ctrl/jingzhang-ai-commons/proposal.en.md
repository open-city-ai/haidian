# Coexistence of Intelligence and Ecology: Jing-Zhang AI Commons

**Jing‑Zhang AI Commons · Proof to Place**

This proposal understands the "AI Innovation Belt" as a suite of urban public capabilities, rather than a set of technological decorations: research outcomes are first validated, risks are disclosed, and the public can understand and opt out before they are integrated into everyday spaces. All spatial locations, land uses, and buildings are Conceptual Recommendations and reference schemes for Open Co-Creation, available for professional teams to deepen their research; they do not replace formal planning, nor do they constitute government approval, engineering feasibility, investment, or implementation commitments.

## Coordinated Research Area: Industry and Future City Research

### Overall Concept and Identity System

Main name is "**Zhi Gua Coexistence**", with the English name as **Jing-Zhang AI Commons**, and the dissemination short name as **JZ·AI Commons**. The naming retains the "Gua" from the Jing-Zhang Railway — spanning, connecting, and opening — while using Commons to emphasize that AI capabilities should become understandable, participatory, and reusable public resources. The logo consists of two open trajectories, three verification nodes, and an open gap: the dual tracks represent the cultural legacy of the century-old project and contemporary open intelligence, the three nodes correspond to three key areas, and the gap represents public access and Human Review; the graphic is composed of original geometry from this plan, without using any corporate logos or unauthorized materials. [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK]

The spatial structure is summarized as "one track and three ports with two wings and twelve stations." The one track is the north-south AI Commons public spine. The three ports are the Zhongzhiyuan "full-stack validation port," the AI Origin community "open-source transformation port," and the Dazhongsi "intelligent entity life port." The two wings are connected to the Zhongguancun technology service network and the Xiao Yuehe scenario empowerment network. The twelve stations are removable, stoppable, and auditable AI scenario nodes. It organizes the five major functions into a cycle: research and computing form prototypes, validation and governance form credible evidence, transformation and community form applications, Public Space forms feedback, and open activities send knowledge back to the research end. [data:geometry/roads.geojson#ROAD-SPINE-001] [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#SC-01] [metric:scenario_node_count] [depth:overall_spatial_structure]

## Overall Design Area: Urban Renewal and Regulatory-Plan-Level Urban Design

The overall design takes a structure prioritizing "public spine first, node differentiation, and reversible architecture." A continuous green spine connects the three key areas, the Jing-Zhang heritage park context, and six east-west stitching lines into a public network. Research and development, education, talent living, community services, culture, and smart indigenous commercial reference zones are arranged around it. The land use polygon is divided by a single boundary topology, covering the entire submitted range without overlap, and avoids leaving unexplained gaps with several conceptual color bands. [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-001] [metric:land_use_zone_count] [depth:land_use_layout]

Update strategies are not about "tearing down and rebuilding," but rather ensuring that existing spaces first possess three types of interfaces: a shareable ground floor and meeting facilities, metered and disconnectable computational/electricity interfaces, and a public display interface for validation results. The industrial space is structured in a three-tier gradient of "closed research and development—controlled testing—public disclosure," while the living space is supplemented by childcare, sports, nighttime learning, and accessibility services. These two types of spaces intersect through a public node within a five-minute walk. This logic is used to guide subsequent site investigations, without making specific conclusions about individual parcels, companies, or buildings. [source:URBAN-DESIGN-MEASURES]

The control plan depth in this package is reflected in three columns: "Known, Suggested, and To Be Confirmed." Known items include the announced tasks, provisional boundaries, and the topology generated by this scheme. Suggested items include land use relationships, public spine, building prototypes, and phased conditions. To be confirmed items include the floor area ratio (FAR), height, density, green space control, road red lines, four lines, municipal capacity, and ownership. `metrics.json` keeps these values as `null`, rather than deriving seemingly complete planning metrics from the building prototypes. [metric:floor_area_ratio] [metric:building_density] [metric:road_ratio] [depth:development_intensity_controls] (Provisional Boundary)

## Detailed Design of Key Areas

Three key areas all use temporary rough polygons. The following is a directional small-scale plan, focusing on the consistency of "function-space-scene-governance," not comprehensive implementation conclusions at the parcel level. [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-002] [depth:three_key_area_detailed_design]

**Zhongzhiyuan | Full Stack Validation Hub.** Centered around the "Model Wind Tunnel in the Garden": research and experimental spaces are arranged around a controlled test garden, with public green spines only displaying desensitized validation methods, risk boundaries, and result summaries. Three categories of industrial experiments—model health checks, edge-side energy consumption, and robotic coexistence—use independent data domains, scheduled time windows, and one-click stop functions; Qinghe culture and garden environment are accessed through low-disturbance signage, integrating into the public interface. External transportation only prioritizes shuttle services, without judging the Fifth Ring Road project plan.[data:geometry/buildings.geojson#BLDG-001]

**AI Origin Community | Open Source Conversion Hub.** Convert the advantage of being near a school into an open clinic that transforms "papers into urban issues": universities, entrepreneurs, residents, and public service personnel collectively define the problems, with prototypes first validated in indoor or digital sandbox environments before entering short-term public trials. Spatially, it adopts small-scale shared ground-level spaces, reservable workstations, family-friendly services, and nighttime learning nodes; the "Open Source Origin" landmark showcases contribution chains rather than celebrity worship. The transit station connection only expresses a preference for walking and cycling, without assuming the openness of campus, park, or proprietary spaces.

**Dazhongsi | Smart Entity Life Hub.** Establish a "transparent experience—correction—exit" public testing market oriented towards smart entities, smart terminals, and content consumption. Any proxy services must display the responsible party, data usage, a summary of pricing/recommendation logic, contact information for human customer service, and a method for closing; the Public Space prioritizes a quadrants-based pedestrian connectivity concept and non-motorized vehicle order, with architectural prototypes favoring small units, short leases, and shared displays rather than large enclosed campuses. The "Smart Entity Public Forum" is used for public debriefing of failed cases. [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#PUBLIC-LANDMARK-003]

Three small schemes share a set of building update thresholds: first, verify safety, ownership, cultural heritage protection, and current use; then, decide to retain or renovate; any conclusions regarding demolition, new construction, and height must await official controls and professional evaluation. A shared operational threshold: scenes must have an exit button, Human Review, log retention period, complaint channels, and termination conditions. Differentiation is reflected in re-verification in the north, re-conversion in the center, and re-emphasis on public experience in the south. [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK]

### Proof-to-Place Governance Chain

Each scenario progresses through eight steps: "Issue Registration—Minimum Data—Sandbox Testing—Ethics/Security Review—Short-Term Public Pilot—Public Feedback—Independent Audit—Open Reuse or Exit." Low-risk scenarios can operate on a small scale with clear notification and an opt-out mechanism; medium-risk scenarios must be limited to specific populations, time windows, and data domains; high-risk scenarios involving personal safety, children, healthcare, payments, or law enforcement must be present with professional oversight and cannot be directly deployed using this scheme. This chain embeds AI-Native decision-making as traceable, rather than replacing traditional services with a new screen. [depth:overall_spatial_structure]

## Transport, Rail, Municipal Infrastructure, and Public Services

Traffic strategies prioritize addressing the "ability to walk, bike, or transfer continuously," without first drawing new motor vehicle infrastructure. The north-south spine connects the three ports, with six horizontal conceptual lines serving the South Portal cultural integration, Dazhongsi quadrants connection, Xiao Yuehe scene wings, AI Origin site connection, innovation service micro-circulation, and Zhongzhiyuan Qinghe Garden connection; the total central line length is approximately **16.4 kilometers**, for network comparison purposes only. [data:geometry/roads.geojson#ROAD-X-002] [metric:conceptual_mobility_length_m] [depth:traffic_rail_slow_parking]

Transfer tracks and parking with a "try-before-you-buy" approach: there should be a continuous and barrier-free route from the station entrance to the public spine, clear pedestrian and bicycle separation, orderly parking of non-motorized vehicles, and adequate nighttime lighting; specific station-city engineering, road cross-sections, parking supply, and signal organization require traffic surveys and data from relevant authorities. As a result, the road area and road ratio are therefore maintained at unknown values. [metric:road_area_sqm] [metric:road_ratio]

New Infrastructure only defines interfaces: Edge-side computational pods must meter energy consumption, be powerable and movable; public data interfaces must minimize data collection, specify storage duration and responsible parties; robot test zones must have physical barriers, emergency stop mechanisms, and human observation; public services must retain non-digital channels. Municipal capacity, fire safety, flood prevention and drainage, pipeline relocation, and energy load must all be addressed in separate specializations and are not concluded in this plan. [source:CONTROL-DETAILED-PLANNING] [depth:municipal_new_infrastructure]

## Renewal Projects, Implementation Policy, and Phasing

Nine categories of update projects are organized as follows: "prerequisites—small-scale prototypes—evaluation—exit/extension": 1) continuity diagnosis of public spines; 2) six cross-link pedestrian and bicycle and accessibility trials; 3) model health check station; 4) end-to-side computational and energy ledger; 5) open-source transformation clinic; 6) transparent intelligent body experience; 7) three public deliberation landmarks; 8) bilingual signage and railway cultural content; 9) scene registration, audit, and complaint platform. Projects are not a fixed engineering list; any project involving architecture, roads, cultural heritage, or municipal works will only conduct desk research or remove prototypes if the prerequisites are not met. [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK]

Phasing is condition-triggered rather than calendar-driven. Phase one establishes scenario registration, risk tiering, public communication, and low-disturbance prototypes; phase two forms a common testing protocol and open activities among the three ports; phase three waits for the official geometry, master plan, as-built conditions, and specialized data, which will be integrated by a professional team into an approvable outcome. Long-term operation proposes the "one season one question, one year one loop": in the spring, release city issues and open data descriptions; in the summer, conduct controlled build/test week; in the autumn, host public experiences and independent audits; in the winter, release contribution spectra, failure debriefs, and the next year's exit list. Developer communities are recorded based on contributions rather than traffic, including code, data descriptions, manual reviews, and community services; international communication uniformly uses the Proof-to-Place narrative. ## Risk, Copyright, and Compliance

**Risk of Data.** Official boundaries, control plans, current conditions, traffic, cultural heritage, municipal, and ownership data gaps for seven categories have entered `assumptions.json`; the Provisional Boundary is expressed with low-contrast dashed lines, and any area conclusions should be marked with a provisional designation. Replacing the official geometry requires a full recalculation; partial "corrections" of numbers are not allowed. [source:SOURCE-REGISTRY]

**AI and Privacy Risks.** Public spaces shall not default to facial recognition, continuous tracking of individuals, or using automated scoring as the final decision for welfare, law enforcement, medical, educational, or payment purposes; high-risk scenarios require Human Review, on-site emergency stops, minimal data collection, and exit options. The list of model suppliers, cloud service providers, and companies is not a necessary condition for the scenario to be established, to avoid supplier lock-in.

**Planning and Implementation Risks.** All roads, buildings, landmarks, events, phases, policies, and funding arrangements are Conceptual Recommendations; desk studies or temporary prototypes are permitted only when preliminary documentation is lacking. A professional team should further complete planning, architecture, transportation, municipal, fire safety, cultural heritage protection, landscape design, accessibility, data protection, and public engagement reviews. [standard:MOHURD-URBAN-DESIGN-MEASURES]
