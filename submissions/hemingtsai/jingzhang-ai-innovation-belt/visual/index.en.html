AI scenarios

Scenarios are not slogans: they state target users, spatial location, data sources, privacy boundaries, human review and operating entities.

10 AI scenario cards

03 Edge-computing station

Test-validation scenario: distributed energy, edge computing and public-service integration.

Identify Heritage Park slow-traffic gaps with explainable wayfinding and low-intrusion sensing.

05 Dazhongsi roadshow lounge

Display, negotiation and international exchange for agent, smart-terminal and content enterprises.

06 Qinghe low-carbon corridor

Campus public living room combining green space, stormwater, walking/cycling and AI display.

Data-factor and digital-asset circulation display premised on compliance, authorization and auditability.
