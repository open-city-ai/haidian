# Formal Narrative

## 方案叙事 / Proposal Narrative

本包是"百年京张AI创新带城市设计国际方案征集"的 AI agent formal 投稿，核心叙事为"京张智脉共生带"：以京张遗址公园为历史与公共空间主轴，以众智园、北京AI原点社区、大钟寺三处重点片区为创新锚点，形成"一带三核、多点场景、蓝绿慢行复合环"。

The package is a formal AI-agent submission to the Centennial Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt international urban-design open call. Its narrative is the "Jing-Zhang AI Pulse Symbiotic Belt": the Jing-Zhang Railway Heritage Park as the historical and public-space spine, three key areas (Zhongzhiyuan, Beijing AI Origin Community, Dazhongsi) as innovation anchors, forming "one belt, three cores, multi-node scenarios, a blue-green slow-traffic composite loop".

## 证据体系 / Evidence System

- 空间证据：geometry/ 下 9 个 GeoJSON 图层（site boundary、key areas、land use、buildings、roads、green space、public space、constraints、phasing），全部标注 provisional 边界状态。
- 指标证据：metrics.json 全部由提交几何在 EPSG:4548 下复算；管控与绩效指标按 known/unknown/pending 分类。
- 任务证据：compliance_matrix.json 覆盖公告 1.3、1.4、1.5 与 agent.1-agent.6 共 23 条必选任务。
- 深度证据：design_depth_matrix.json（15 项，其中 6 项因资料缺口标记 data_gap）与 standard_matrix.json（6 项标准）。
- 成果证据：proposal.md / proposal.en.md 双语全文、report/proposal.html 与 report/proposal.en.html、visual/index.html 与 index.en.html、drawings/a3-booklet.pdf 与 a0-boards.pdf（双语）。
- 自检证据：self_check.json 反映 deterministic validation、spatial review、visual packaging check、professional evidence review 全部通过。

Spatial evidence: nine GeoJSON layers under geometry/, all labeled with provisional-boundary status. Metrics: recomputed from submitted geometry in EPSG:4548. Tasks: 23 mandatory items in compliance_matrix.json. Depth: design_depth_matrix.json (15 items, 6 marked data_gap pending official data) and standard_matrix.json (6 standards). Deliverables: bilingual proposal, rendered HTML, offline visualization, A3 booklet and A0 boards. Self-check: all four gates pass.

## 边界与诚信声明 / Boundary and Integrity Statement

本方案使用临时（provisional）边界与重点区 polygon，原因在于组织方尚未发布官方几何数据；该数据缺口不阻断内容评分，但所有面积、比例与图纸在官方 polygon 发布后必须重算。方案不声称官方批准、审定控规、最终权属、最终规模或保证实施；缺少控规、道路红线、权属、市政、文保或工程条件的结论均降级为待确认事项。

This proposal uses provisional boundary and key-area polygons because official organizer geometry is not yet published; the data gap does not block content scoring, but all areas, ratios and drawings must be recalculated once official polygons are released. The proposal claims no official approval, approved RDP, final ownership, final scale, or guaranteed implementation; conclusions lacking official RDP, road-redline, ownership, municipal, heritage or engineering conditions are downgraded to pending-confirmation items.

This narrative is derived from the structured AI package; geometry, metrics, compliance matrix, drawings, and visual/index.html remain the authoritative cross-checked deliverables.
