# 京张共步 / CO-WALK JINGZHANG

百年前，京张铁路教机器沿轨道前进；AI 时代的京张，要教机器在城市里礼让人。

方案以 9.25 公里“共步地面”串联三座共步园、两片支撑翼和十二个服务站。前三个场景验证低速机器人礼让、多品牌互操作与离线人工接管；后九个场景把技术转化为无障碍导航、健康服务、移动课堂、社区配送、文化导览与极端天气补给。每项基本服务同时保留无 AI 的人工等价路径。

“共步协议”规定人优先、意图可读、停靠退路、失联停车、人工接管与到期复审；安全、无障碍、隐私、运维和公众接受五道闸门决定服务能否上线。空间、场景、治理与实施因此形成同一条可审计证据链。

A century ago, the Jingzhang Railway taught machines to follow rails. In the AI era, Jingzhang should teach machines to yield in the city.

A 9.25 km Co-Walk ground links three gardens, two support wings, and twelve stations. Three scenarios stress-test yielding, multi-vendor interoperability, and offline human takeover; nine translate capability into accessible navigation, health services, mobile learning, delivery, cultural guidance, and extreme-weather support. Every essential service retains a staffed no-AI equivalent.

The Co-Walk Protocol establishes people first, readable intent, off-path docks, stop on disconnect, human takeover, and expiry review. Five gates - safety, access, privacy, operations, and public trust - decide whether a service may launch. Space, scenarios, governance, and delivery therefore form one auditable evidence chain.
