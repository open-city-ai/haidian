## Derivation of visual assets｜图件派生关系

Every figure under `assets/figures/`, every drawing in `drawings/`, and the plan drawing inside
`visual/index.html` is derived from this package's own `geometry/*.geojson` and `metrics.json`
by re-runnable generator code. Figures are an explanation layer; the authoritative data remains
the GeoJSON, `metrics.json` and the three matrices.

`assets/figures/` 下每张图、`drawings/` 中每张图纸、以及 `visual/index.html` 内的平面图，均由
本包自身的 `geometry/*.geojson` 与 `metrics.json` 通过可重跑的生成程序派生。图件是解释层，
权威数据仍为 GeoJSON、`metrics.json` 与三张矩阵。
