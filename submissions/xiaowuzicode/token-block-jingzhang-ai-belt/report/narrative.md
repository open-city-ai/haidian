## The proposition｜主张

A century ago the Jing-Zhang line was built to carry things. **Token Block (京张令牌)**
proposes that the same alignment now runs them: the 9.6 km heritage park corridor becomes one
continuously operated, publicly bookable urban AI test track — one line, one operating
protocol, one booking calendar.

一百年前京张铁路用来运东西，今天同一条线用来跑东西：把遗址公园廊道整体做成一条持续运营、
公众可预约的城市AI测试轨道——一条线、一套运行协议、一张预约表。

## The structure｜结构

Six bands run south to north. Three are **stations** where the line opens to the city and they
coincide exactly with the three required key areas; two are **switches** where the wider
Zhongguancun fabric joins and they coincide exactly with the two wings; one is a **running
section**. The railway lexicon is the naming system, the operating model and the governance
vocabulary at once.

## What makes it checkable｜为何可核验

The land-use partition is generated by slicing the submitted boundary programmatically, not
drawn by hand: measured gap 1.8e-16 and overlap 0 square degrees. Every metric is recomputed
from that geometry in EPSG:4548, so an independent reviewer reproduces the numbers rather than
trusting them.

用地分区由程序切分提交边界生成而非手绘，实测缺口 1.8e-16、重叠 0 平方度；全部指标在
EPSG:4548 下由该几何复算，评审者可独立复现而不必相信。

## What it refuses to claim｜它拒绝主张的内容

The submitted boundary is the repository's **provisional rough boundary**, not an official
redline. Floor area ratio, total floor area, building height, building density, road area and
road area ratio are recorded as `unknown` with reasons, because the official controls are
absent. No demolition conclusion is issued for any real parcel. Constraint layers that the site
package marks as non-agent-editable are left unauthored rather than invented. All spatial,
event, brand and policy proposals are conceptual suggestions for professional teams to deepen.

提交边界为临时粗略边界，不是官方红线；容积率、总建筑规模、建筑高度、建筑密度、道路面积与
道路面积率因官方管控条件缺失一律记为 `unknown` 并写明原因；不对任何真实地块给出拆除结论；
场地包中标记为 agent 不可编辑的约束图层保持空缺而非代拟；全部空间、活动、品牌与政策建议
均为供专业团队深化的概念建议。
