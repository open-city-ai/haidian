A-CONTROLS-001pending_professional_confirmation

Detailed regulatory controls, road redlines, ownership, municipal utilities, and engineering constraints must be confirmed from official attachments before statutory use.

影响：The package can be reviewed as a formal AI submission for the submitted design layers only; statutory controls still need professional confirmation.

A-BOUNDARY-001pending_official_data

The submitted site boundary and the three key-area polygons are the repository's provisional rough boundaries, not an official redline. All areas, ratios and the land-use partition must be recomputed together once official polygons are published.

影响：Every area and ratio in metrics.json is conditional on this boundary. The design's load is deliberately carried by sequence and relation, which survive a boundary correction.

A-CONSTRAINT-LAYERS-001intentionally_unauthored

Water system, heritage protection, regulatory control and existing road/rail layers are marked editable_by_agent=false in the site package enum. This package leaves them unauthored rather than inventing them; geometry/constraints.geojson carries only agent-definable bookable track segments and located scenario nodes.

影响：An empty official constraint layer is a disclosed data gap; a fabricated one would be a false authority claim.

A-INTENSITY-001pending_official_data

Floor area ratio, total floor area, building height and building density are recorded as unknown because planning_limits.json records them as missing official controls. Only a relative massing intent is stated.

影响：No development-intensity conclusion may be drawn from this package.

A-ROAD-AREA-001pending_official_data

Road area and road area ratio cannot be recomputed from centrelines because approved redline widths and cross-sections are not published. Whether each east-west stitch crosses at grade, over or under is an engineering question this package does not answer.

影响：Mobility claims are limited to location, continuity and justification, not engineering feasibility.

A-EXISTING-BUILDINGS-001pending_official_data

No current-building survey, ownership record or official parcel geometry exists in the public package. The thirteen building footprints are indicative renewal handles whose suggested retain/renovate/new-build action derives from the structural role of their band, not from any judgement about a real existing building. No demolition conclusion is issued for any real parcel.

影响：Retain/renovate/demolish is delivered as a method plus an explicit refusal, not as a parcel-level conclusion.

A-PARTITION-RESIDUAL-001accepted_tolerance

The land-use partition is generated by slicing the submitted boundary, so its area equals the site area by construction. A residual of about 12 sqm (1.1e-6 relative) remains between land_use_total_area_sqm and site_area_sqm as a floating-point artefact of reprojection to EPSG:4548. It is not a topological gap; measured gap and overlap are 1.8e-16 and 0 square degrees respectively.

影响：Well inside the 0.01% topology tolerance used by spatial_review.py.

A-CASE-STUDIES-001qualitative_reference_only

The eight international ecosystem references are qualitative design references drawn from publicly known practice. This package makes no quantitative claim about any of them, cites none as authoritative data, and uses none to justify a numeric target.

影响：Case-study material is design reasoning, not evidence.
