RENZI BELT / THE SWITCHBACK: Urban Design Proposal

Offline electronic presentation. Taking the Qinglongqiao switchback alignment (1909) as its motif, the proposal organizes space as one spine, three cores, and two wings: Ascend (heritage) — Switchback (originality at the AI Origin Community) — Descend (innovation) — Extend (daily life). Authoritative data live in GeoJSON, metrics.json, matrices, and self-check results; this page is the human-readable visual layer.

02 Renzi Platform · Open Source Hall

Release, code contribution display and small roadshows for universities, communities and startups.

03 Ascending · Zhongzhiyuan Test Field

Standards, safety evaluation and red-team testing as visitable, reservable, supervised nodes.

Developer-themed trail on the northern spine: milestones, contributor plaques, code landscape devices.

Time-limited pilots: low-speed robot delivery, inspection, AI-guided tours.

09 Zhongguancun · Service Station

One-stop enterprise services: IP, financing, compute, data compliance.

10 Honor Wall · Contributor Gallery

Open-source honor system: annual awards, agent contribution wall, renewable monument.

12 Signal Screens · AI Service Status

T-01 Zhongzhiyuan Three-Gate Test

Virtual simulation → controlled sandbox → real blocks, gated progression; human review at every level, auto-return on failure.

T-02 Xiaoyue River Low-Speed Pilot

Fixed hours, fixed routes, speed/load limits; open operational data, resident appeal channel.

T-03 Dazhongsi Agent Commerce Sandbox

Voluntary merchant participation, explicit AI labeling, time-limited operation; human bypass always available.
