# REN Belt——Centennial Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt Urban Design Scheme

> **Concept in One Sentence:** A century ago, Zhan Tianyou used a "person-shaped" turnaround track to enable China's first railway, designed independently, to climb a slope that even direct traction could not conquer; today, on the same slope in Haidian, we use path innovation rather than brute force to ascend the steep hill of full-stack AI autonomy. 'Person,' a stroke and a bend, mutually support—human and machine, core and wings, legacy and future.
>
> **English abstract:** REN Belt takes its name from the 〈human〉-shaped (herringbone) switchback that Zhan Tianyou designed in 1908 to climb an impossible grade—the founding icon of Chinese engineering self-reliance, on this very corridor. REN = 〈human〉 and echoes 〈benevolence〉: a human-centred AI district. The plan proposes one heritage-park spine, six functional segments, seven east–west stitching streets, a three-core-two-wing innovation loop drawn as the switchback itself, ten AI scenario cards, three pilgrimage landmarks, and a naming system that doubles as a machine-resolvable address space for agents — demonstrated by this very submission.

## Coordinated Research Area: Industry and Future City Research

### One. Overall Concept: REN Belt

**Concept Origin.** The person-shaped turnaround track (1908) at the Qinglong Bridge Station of the Jing-Zhang Railway is a path of innovation by Chinese engineers in response to constraints: using a zigzag segment to climb a slope that the traction cannot ascend. Today, Haidian faces the steep slope of the full-stack autonomy of AI—comprising computational power, algorithms, data, and ecosystems—likewise cannot be climbed by brute force in a straight line, but requires the design of paths through systems, spaces, and ecosystems. This plan names one belt as the **"Person-shaped Belt" (English name: REN Belt)**: "person" represents the technical memory of the person-shaped design, embodying the value anchor of "people-oriented" (Annex 1.3(3) aims to create a high-quality urban district that attracts global AI innovation talents), the one stroke and one bend supporting each other symbolize the spatial metaphor of human-machine collaboration and the two wings of the main belt; REN rhymes with "ren," making it easy to explain the urban district's stance on humane AI when communicating internationally [standard:PROJECT-AGENT-OPEN-CALL-TASKBOOK].

### Two. Three Major Orientations, Five Major Functions, and the Synergistic Loop of the Three Zones and Two Wings

Three key positioning elements (the Centennial Jing-Zhang Cultural Belt, the Urban AI Living Experience Belt, and the AI Integration Innovation Belt) and five functional areas (Full-Stack Independent AI Innovation System, World-Class AI Innovation Ecosystem, AI-Enabled Scenario Empowerment New Paradigm, Intelligent AI Vibrant City, and AI Governance Global Discourse Power) are derived from the announcement and task book [source:OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT] [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK]. This plan will depict the collaborative relationship of the Three Zones and Two Wings as a **human-shaped climbing loop**.

- **Origination Community (Inspiration Source, Near-School Type)** —— World-Class AI Innovation Ecosystem: Here, original innovations from Tsinghua, Peking, and the Chinese Academy of Sciences complete their first phase of climbing (incubation, transformation, and open-source);
- **Turning Point: Zhongzhiyuan (Peak Point, Garden Type)** —— Full-Stack Independent AI Innovation System and AI Governance Global Discourse: A high point for the national platform, standard setting, and security governance.
- **Dazhongsi (Market End, Urban Type)** —— Intelligent Natively Born New Business Forms: Intelligent Bodies, Intelligent Terminals, and Content Consumption Integrate with the City and Market.
- **The Two Wings Support Two Rail Tracks**: The Zhongguancun Technology Services Wing (globalized configuration of elements, Zhongguancun IP and capital empowerment) and the Xiaoyue River Scenario Enablement Wing (AI scenario empowerment and intelligent AI vibrant city).

The zigzag back-and-forth represents the iterative enhancement of innovation elements across three intervals: research → incubation → market → feedback for further research. The overall spatial structure diagram (Figure 1) is organized around this loop, rather than a generic bubble chart. In terms of regional collaboration, the belt outputs models and standards to the North Wei community, the Future Science City, the Huairou Science City, the Economic and Technological Development Zone, and the Jingjinji region, while internally it absorbs elements [depth:overall_spatial_structure].

### Five. Urban Form Provisions for the Future

Artificial intelligence changes not individual buildings but the way spaces are organized. This proposal puts forward three implementable form proposals: First, **scene overlay layer** —— AI scenarios (testing, demonstration, service) are overlaid on the basic land use in an overlay manner without changing the nature of the basic land use. Scene access is managed through a list + time limit + Human Review (one of the innovative ideas in land space planning, a standardized expression of spatial industry integration); Second, **self-adaptive and evolving blank spaces** —— strategic blank spaces (occupying 6.3% [metric:land_use_ratio_16]) serve as a buffer for form evolution; Third, **cities readable by intelligent entities** —— planning outcomes, naming systems, and operational data are kept machine-readable, ensuring that future city service intelligent entities become native residents of the district rather than external systems.

## Overall Design Area: Urban Renewal and Regulatory-Plan-Level Urban Design

### Spatial Structure: One Ridge Six Segments Seven Transverse Axes

The Overall Design Area is a strip approximately 9.7 km north-south and 1.3 km east-west. The structural framework of this scheme is **one ridge, six segments, and seven transversals** [depth:overall_spatial_structure]:

- **One Spine**: The Jing-Zhang Heritage Park Greenway, which runs through the "person-shaped track" of the Jing-Zhang site, is approximately 94 meters wide and stretches for a total length of 9,720 meters [metric:spine_greenway_length_m], serving as a cultural spine, green spine, and pedestrian spine all in one [data:geometry/roads.geojson#ROAD-001].
- **Six Segments** (North→South): Qinghewan Gateway Segment, Zhongzhiyuan Segment (Garden Type), Academy Synergy Segment, Original Point Community Segment (Near-School Type), Zhi Chun Seam Segment, Dazhongsi Segment (Urban Type), with an additional West Straight Gate Gateway Coda.

With Urban Renewal as a tool and artificial intelligence development as a guide [standard:PROJECT-OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT]: The update potential areas are concentrated on the inefficient spaces adjacent to the Jing-Zhang Heritage Park and the existing mixed residential and industrial areas in the Zhi Chun seam([data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-023], LU-027) and the existing commercial facilities of Dazhongsi ([data:geometry/buildings.geojson#BLD-016]). ### planning indicator framework concept

In accordance with the announcement requirements, we have developed a **monitoring framework rather than numerical commitments** for the planning indicator system: this plan proposes the AI Innovation Index (a composite index of patent/contributions to open-source projects/participation in standards), talent density (proportion of R&D personnel among employed population), Scenario Access (number of open scenarios/total list count), pedestrian connectivity rate (percentage of pedestrian track discontinuity points eliminated), and renewal activation rate (proportion of renewal projects completed and put into use). All indicators' current values and target values are currently recorded as pending confirmation (such as the Floor Area Ratio [metric:floor_area_ratio] and height control both being in an unknown state) [depth:metrics_recalculation].

### Three Industry Testing and Validation Scenarios
