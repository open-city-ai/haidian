# Centennial Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt — Urban Design

> All outputs are open co-creation proposals. They do not substitute for formal planning and do not constitute government-approved conclusions. All spatial implementation suggestions shall be worded as "concept proposals," "reference schemes," or "available for professional team deepening." [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK]

## Chapter 1: Design Basis and Overall Framework

### 1.1 Project Overview

The Centennial Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt is located in Haidian District, Beijing, leveraging the Jing-Zhang Railway Heritage Park corridor. It extends from Xizhimen in the east to Beiqing Road in the west, connecting the Zhongguancun Technology Service Zone with Haidian's science and technology innovation hinterland. [source:DATA-SRC-OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT-20260509]

**Project Scope (Concept Proposal):**
- **Coordinated Research Area**: Approximately 43.6 sq km, covering the Jing-Zhang Railway corridor and its radiating area [source:DATA-SRC-PROVISIONAL-BOUNDARIES-20260605] [metric:area_sqm]
- **Overall Design Area**: Approximately 11.4 sq km, centered on the Jing-Zhang Railway Heritage Park corridor [source:DATA-SRC-PROVISIONAL-BOUNDARIES-20260605] [metric:site_area_sqm=11412825]
- **Key Design Areas**: Three detailed design nodes [metric:key_area_count=3]
  - Zhongzhiyuan AI Innovation Acceleration Zone (~192.1 ha) [data:provisional_boundary]
  - Beijing AI Origin Community (~104.3 ha) [data:provisional_boundary]
  - Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster (~72.0 ha) [data:provisional_boundary]

### 1.2 Three Positionings and Five Functions

This proposal conceptually addresses three positionings: [source:DATA-SRC-AGENT-TASKBOOK-20260518]

1. **Centennial Jing-Zhang Cultural Belt** — Using the century-old Jing-Zhang Railway history as a cultural foundation to build a narrative space where urban memory and technological innovation converge
2. **Urban AI Life Experience Belt** — Integrating AI technology into daily life scenarios to create a perceptible, experiential, and participatory smart city living model
3. **AI Integration Innovation Belt** — Focusing on full-stack AI autonomous innovation to build a complete innovation chain from fundamental research to scenario applications

Five functional support systems: [source:DATA-SRC-AGENT-TASKBOOK-20260518]

| Function Block | Core Positioning | Corresponding Space (Concept Proposal) |
|---------------|-----------------|---------------------------------------|
| AI Full-Stack Autonomous Innovation | Full chain: chips, frameworks, models, applications | Zhongzhiyuan AI Innovation Acceleration Zone |
| World-Class AI Innovation Ecosystem | Global innovation element convergence and allocation | Beijing AI Origin Community |
| AI+ Scenario Empowerment | Vertical industry AI application testing and verification | Xiaoyuehe Scenario Empowerment Wing |
| Intelligent AI Vibrant City | AI-native consumption and urban life | Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster |
| AI Governance Global Voice | Governance standard-setting and international exchange | Zhongzhiyuan Core Area |

### 1.3 Three Zones, Two Wings Spatial Structure

The proposal conceptually establishes a "Three Zones, Two Wings" coordinated development framework: [source:DATA-SRC-AGENT-TASKBOOK-20260518]

- **Three Zones**:
  - Beijing AI Origin Community — Hosting world-class AI innovation ecosystem functions, gathering leading AI enterprises and open innovation platforms
  - Zhongzhiyuan AI Innovation Acceleration Zone — Hosting AI full-stack autonomous innovation system and AI governance voice functions
  - Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster — Hosting intelligent native new business formats, creating AI-driven consumption and business scenarios
- **Two Wings**:
  - Zhongguancun Technology Service Wing — Global element allocation, Zhongguancun IP and capital empowerment [depth:regional_context]
  - Xiaoyuehe Scenario Empowerment Wing — AI scenario empowerment and intelligent AI vibrant city [depth:corridor_context]

The Three Zones and Two Wings achieve spatial stitching and functional linkage through the Jing-Zhang Railway Heritage Park corridor, forming an overall spatial structure of "One Belt Through, Three Cores Driven, Two Wings Coordinated." [source:PROCESSED-FACT-PACK]

**Concept Proposal Main Name:** "Jingzhang AI Belt" (京张智带·AI Belt)

- "Jingzhang" preserves the century-old railway cultural gene, establishing historical depth
- "AI Belt" serves as a double meaning — both "intelligence belt" and "wisdom link"

## Chapter 6: Centennial Jing-Zhang Culture and AI New Culture Fusion Narrative

### 6.1 Jing-Zhang Railway Historical Cultural Resource System

The Jing-Zhang Railway, as China's first self-built trunk railway (opened 1909), carries deep national autonomous innovation spirit. [source:DATA-SRC-OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT-20260509]

**Historical Cultural Resource Inventory (Concept Proposal):**
- **Zhan Tianyou's Spirit Legacy**: Autonomous innovation, craftsmanship, and the era of coexisting international cooperation and competition
- **Railway Engineering Heritage**: Switchback lines, tunnel engineering, bridge technology innovations
- **Railway Community Life Heritage**: Railway worker dormitories, station buildings, railway cultural landscapes
- **Industrial Heritage Material Carriers**: Rails, signaling equipment, locomotives, station structures

### 6.2 Zhongguancun Innovation Culture and AI New Culture Narrative

**Three-Layer Cultural Narrative Structure (Concept Proposal):**

1. **Foundation Layer: Century-Old Autonomous Innovation Gene**
   - From Zhan Tianyou to Zhongguancun, a century-long autonomous innovation spirit lineage
   - "Autonomy" means not isolation, but mastering core technology voice in open cooperation

2. **Middle Layer: Zhongguancun Innovation Culture Accumulation**
   - 40-year innovation journey from Electronics Street to National Independent Innovation Demonstration Zone
   - Entrepreneurial spirit, fault-tolerant culture, knowledge economy value system

3. **Upper Layer: AI New Culture Construction**
   - Open-source sharing spirit: AI technology openness and collaborative innovation
   - Human-machine collaboration ethics: AI enhancing rather than replacing human capabilities
   - Inclusive intelligence philosophy: AI technology serving public interest and social welfare

### 6.3 Spatial Cultural System and Expression Carriers

**Spatial Cultural System (Concept Proposal):**

- **Timeline Axis**: Along the heritage park from east to west, concept proposal for a 1909-2049 timeline narrative with each node corresponding to an era story
- **Technology Axis**: From steam locomotive to electric railway to intelligent railway to AI city technology evolution narrative
- **Spirit Axis**: From "self-strengthening" to "innovation" to "openness" to "intelligence" value evolution line

**Expression Carrier Concept Proposals:**
- Wayfinding System: Fusing railway signal visual elements with AI data visualization language
- Signage System: Each zone's signage incorporates corresponding era's railway visual elements
- Symbol System: Systematic application of rail cross-sections, gears, neural network nodes

### 6.4 International Communication Narrative

**Core International Communication Narrative (Concept Proposal):**

> "From China's First Railway to the World's AI Belt — A Century of Innovation, A Future of Intelligence"

**International Communication Key Points:**
- Emphasize the intrinsic continuity between Jing-Zhang Railway's autonomous innovation spirit and contemporary AI innovation
- Highlight the "open co-creation" concept: Global AI innovation community co-building and sharing
- Showcase the Chinese approach to cultural heritage protection integrated with technological innovation
