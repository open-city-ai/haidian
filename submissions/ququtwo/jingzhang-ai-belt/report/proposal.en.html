Chapter 1: Design Basis and Overall Framework

1.1 Project Overview

The Centennial Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt is located in Haidian District, Beijing, leveraging the Jing-Zhang Railway Heritage Park corridor. It extends from Xizhimen in the east to Beiqing Road in the west, connecting the Zhongguancun Technology Service Zone with Haidian's science and technology innovation hinterland. 来源

Project Scope (Concept Proposal):

  • Coordinated Research Area: Approximately 43.6 sq km, covering the Jing-Zhang Railway corridor and its radiating area 来源 指标
  • Overall Design Area: Approximately 11.4 sq km, centered on the Jing-Zhang Railway Heritage Park corridor 来源 指标
  • Key Design Areas: Three detailed design nodes 指标

    • - Zhongzhiyuan AI Innovation Acceleration Zone (~192.1 ha) 空间数据 - Beijing AI Origin Community (~104.3 ha) 空间数据 - Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster (~72.0 ha) 空间数据

The Jing-Zhang Railway, as China's first self-built trunk railway (opened 1909), carries deep national autonomous innovation spirit. 来源

Historical Cultural Resource Inventory (Concept Proposal):

  • Zhan Tianyou's Spirit Legacy: Autonomous innovation, craftsmanship, and the era of coexisting international cooperation and competition
  • Railway Engineering Heritage: Switchback lines, tunnel engineering, bridge technology innovations
  • Railway Community Life Heritage: Railway worker dormitories, station buildings, railway cultural landscapes
  • Industrial Heritage Material Carriers: Rails, signaling equipment, locomotives, station structures

1. Foundation Layer: Century-Old Autonomous Innovation Gene - From Zhan Tianyou to Zhongguancun, a century-long autonomous innovation spirit lineage - "Autonomy" means not isolation, but mastering core technology voice in open cooperation

2. Middle Layer: Zhongguancun Innovation Culture Accumulation - 40-year innovation journey from Electronics Street to National Independent Innovation Demonstration Zone - Entrepreneurial spirit, fault-tolerant culture, knowledge economy value system

3.
