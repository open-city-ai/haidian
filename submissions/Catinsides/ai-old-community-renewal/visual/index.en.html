7. Personas

5 personas cover youth / middle / elderly / children / disabled; weak-group representatives participate in evaluation.

60+ Retired Teachers / Senior Employees

Core group in Xueyuanlu / Beitaipingzhuang legacy neighborhoods; care about health, aging, community atmosphere.

Applicable: AI elderly in-home / AI building butler / AI chronic-disease

30+ Dual-Income Families

Xueyuanlu → Dazhongsi legacy neighborhoods; care about after-school and commute; time-sensitive.

Applicable: AI after-school / AI shared parking / AI mediator

18–25 University Students / Graduates

Beihang / USTB / BUPT; AI Origin segment; care about experiments, jobs.

Applicable: AI Community Lab / developer community / pilgrimage path

Residents with Disabilities / Accessibility Needs

Elderly with disability, wheelchair, visually impaired; emphasize human fallback.

Applicable: Accessible AI interface / AI elderly in-home / on-site guidance

Small Merchants / Market Vendors

Dazhongsi legacy market vendors; care about footfall, income, policy.

Applicable: AI market nutritionist / AI-native consumption / community canteen

9. Cultural Narrative

In 1909, Zhan Tianyou's Qinglongqiao switchback proved China could build its own modern railway — the origin of technological sovereignty.

In 2026, the AI innovation belt answers: how does a city organize itself in the intelligent era — the next century's urban paradigm origin.

Logo concept: two rails crossing into a 人 (person) glyph (homage to Qinglongqiao) + community ribbon. Final wordmark / typeface / colors by professional design team.

Wayfinding: Jingzhang elements (rail / timetable / station names) + AI elements (data flow / node / interface). Avoid commercial / religious symbols.
