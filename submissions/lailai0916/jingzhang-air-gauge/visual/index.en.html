AI Scenarios04 / 07

AI Scenarios

Twelve nodes correspond to twelve scenario cards. Four industry test-and-validation scenarios carry a TVS mark. Every card states its data boundary and human review.

SC-01TVS

Street-Sensor Collocation Bench

Records devices and reference instruments, not people. Specialists review calibration; site operators maintain it.

SC-02PUBLIC

Lower-Exposure Walking Comparison

Uses tiered environmental and route data, stores no travel identity, and sends anomalies to an operator.

SC-03TVS

Indoor Air Evidence Room Verification

Records room and test batch, identifies no visitor, and requires qualified testing plus property review.

SC-04TVS

Ventilation Commissioning Bench

Uses indoor conditions and equipment state without personal health data. A professional engineer signs the review.

SC-05TVS

Sensor Product-Claim Test

Compares documentation, collocation, and drift. A review panel publishes a limited test record.

SC-06PUBLIC

Accessible Air-Information Post

Provides tactile, audio, and high-contrast explanation, with no cameras or biometrics.

SC-07PUBLIC

Seasonal Cycling Sample Line

Repeats a fixed route without rider identity. Researchers assess temporal and spatial comparability.

SC-08PUBLIC

Indoor–Outdoor Transition Prompt

Compares place-level observations and ventilation state. A person decides whether to ventilate, filter, or suspend the prompt.

SC-09PUBLIC

Blue–Green Maintenance Observation

Records vegetation, dust, and maintenance. It never assumes greenery reduces pollution.

SC-10PUBLIC

Outdoor-Worker Rest Navigation

Shows facilities and environmental state without occupation or shift logs. Service operators co-evaluate it.

SC-11PUBLIC

Event-Day Air Operations Desk

Uses site observations and manual inspection. Organisers retain authority to pause, reroute, or remove a notice.

SC-12PUBLIC

Open-Data Challenge Forum

Publishes versions, missingness, licences, appeals, and corrections without releasing individual logs.
