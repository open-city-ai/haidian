# Jing-Zhang Intelligence Commons

"Jing-Zhang Wisdom Vein" is not a linear park adorned with technology, but a suite of urban public infrastructure that connects knowledge, space, scenarios, and public judgment. The proposal organizes the Zhongzhiyuan, Beijing AI Origin Community, and Dazhongsi into three cores of "validation, transformation, and interaction" around the cultural main axis of the Jing-Zhang Railway's century-long timeline. The Zhongguancun Technology Services Wing is responsible for talent, capital, intellectual property, and international services, while the Xiaoyue River Scenario Enablement Wing is responsible for urban service validation. Along the twelve stations, developers, businesses, residents, students, and visitors can all enter this innovative system. All spatial actions are Conceptual Recommendations or reference schemes for professional teams to deepen their research, not replacing formal planning, nor constituting a government review or implementation commitment.

## Three-Level Scope Framework

The Coordinated Research Area covers approximately 43.6 square kilometers, addressing "how Haidian can form a world-class AI Innovation Ecosystem and future urban form"; the Overall Design Area announcement covers about 11.4 square kilometers, addressing "how industry, renewal, transportation, utilities, blue-green spaces, and urban appearance form a set of Urban Design"; the key area announcement covers approximately 368.4 hectares, addressing "how three core districts can achieve validation, transformation, and urban interaction through different mechanisms". Three levels are not three unrelated layers, but rather a hierarchical convergence from strategy, space to project evidence. [depth:three_level_scope_framework] [depth:overall_spatial_structure]

The proposal summarizes this transmission as "One Pulse, Three Cores, Two Wings, and Twelve Stations." One pulse is the main pulse of Jing-Zhang culture and Public Space; three cores are respectively the Zhongzhiyuan "Full Stack Validation Core," the Dazhongsi "Open Source Transformation Core," and the Dazhongsi "Intelligent Nativist Interaction Core"; two wings continue the Zhongguancun Technology Services Wing and the Xiaoyue River Scenario Enablement Wing from the task book; twelve stations are small-scale public interfaces distributed along the main pulse that are accessible, testable, and subject to Human Review. The structure is not a new redline but a working framework, with layers explained by [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-SPINE-001], [data:geometry/roads.geojson#ROAD-SPINE-001], and [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#SCN-01]. (AI Origin)

The north-south six segments express a functional gradient from Dazhongsi smart original business, talent living, nearby school research, open-source transformation to Zhongzhiyuan full-stack validation. The central continuous open space reduces the visual weight of the provisional boundary, focusing the design on corridors, horizontal stitching, and public nodes. ## Coordinated Research Area: Industry and Future City Research

### Naming and Identification

Main name "Jing-Zhang Zhi Mai" combines the railway "meridians," innovative "intelligence," and public "lifeline"; the English name `Jing-Zhang Intelligence Commons` emphasizes commons rather than a closed campus. The naming hierarchy is "Zhi Mai / Three Cores / Two Wings / Twelve Stations," which facilitates spatial navigation, activities, digital cataloging, and version iteration with a shared syntax. The logo direction features two parallel tracks forming an open parenthesis in the middle, with a dot in the center representing human final judgment; the sleepers are composed of alternating short and long modules, which can map binary but do not directly mimic corporate logos. The main color is taken from the olive green of railway vegetation, complemented by brick red, slate gray, and paper white; the system only uses original geometry and system fonts. [standard:PROJECT-AGENT-OPEN-CALL-TASKBOOK]

### Six International Case Studies and Transformable Mechanisms

The examples are provided only for background comparison and do not support the project's redline, master plan, or performance commitments. Kendall Square's insight is that research institutions, businesses, and community public interfaces must be planned together, not just with research buildings; one-north's insight is to combine research, living, green spaces, and test and commercialization platforms into walkable combinations; STATION F's insight is that shared services and high-density project operations form a network better than a "company directory"; Toronto MaRS's insight is to connect science to the market using laboratories, capital, customer, and consulting platforms; Helsinki Maria 01's insight is that old buildings can be transformed into innovative campuses through community co-construction and progressive expansion; Pittsburgh Robotics Factory's insight is that embodied intelligence requires continuous support from prototypes, testing, to manufacturing scaling. [source:CASE-KENDALL-SQUARE] [source:CASE-ONE-NORTH] [source:CASE-STATION-F] [source:CASE-MARS-TORONTO] [source:CASE-MARIA01] [source:CASE-PITTSBURGH-ROBOTICS]

Local transformation does not replicate form but distills six mechanisms: public interface prioritization, mixed daily use, project-based community engagement, scientific commercialization services, reversible utilization of old carriers, and prototyping to scaled validation. These are applied to twelve stations, two wings of service, three core divisions, and three stages of operation. Ecological indicators use "connectivity quality" rather than fabricated metrics of enterprises, output value, or investment: recording public activities, inter-institutional collaboration, the cycle from application to Human Review, accessible feedback loops, and open-source contributions. Operational data only enter the next version after they have genuinely occurred and been authorized.

### Circuits of Five Synergistic Functions

A full-stack autonomous innovation system is validated in Zhongzhiyuan for trustworthy models, embodied intelligence, and edge-side computing power; a world-class innovation ecosystem connects AI Origin community with universities, open-source communities, incubators, and professional services; AI-Enabled Scenarios enable public test entries in the Xiao Yuehe Wing and Shierzhan; an intelligent and vibrant city through accessible active mobility, public culture, living services, and smart native markets is perceived by the general public; global AI governance discourse is formed by public audit rooms, rule version libraries, and international events, creating reusable knowledge. Each output retains human final judgment and exit mechanisms.

## Overall Design Area: Urban Renewal and Regulatory-Plan-Level Urban Design

The overall structure is not "big tear down and big build up," but rather "open space prioritization, preliminary site investigation, reversible updating of carriers, and scenario operation validation." `land_use.geojson` cuts the continuous main artery and the two wings into complete zones; `buildings.geojson` uses several modules to verify the volumetric relationships of ground-level openness, shared atriums, and mixed research and development living spaces, but does not represent any existing buildings; `roads.geojson` uses one longitudinal and five transverse roads to express north-south connectivity and east-west integration; `phasing.geojson` expresses the dependency sequence. [data:geometry/buildings.geojson#BLDG-001] [data:geometry/phasing.geojson#PHASE-001] [depth:land_use_layout]

The control detail depth in this scheme is manifested as "comprehensive control items with clear evidence status," rather than filling in numbers arbitrarily. Land use compatibility, Floor Area Ratio (), height, density, green space ratio, setback distances, road red lines, four lines, municipal facilities, and public safety measures are all included in the pending verification list.[metric:floor_area_ratio] With [metric:building_height_m] Identify as unknown. `constraints.geojson` should remain as an empty set, avoiding the use of agent-generated lines in place of official controls. [data:geometry/constraints.geojson#constraints-empty-by-design] [standard:MOHURD-CONTROL-DETAILED-PLANNING] [depth:development_intensity_controls]

Building forms adopt four translatable principles: the ground-floor interface along the wisdom veins should be public, transparent, and accessible; prioritize reversible connections between new and old, not equating "futurism" with unconventional massing; conduct sightlines, scale, vibration, and heritage assessments before approaching railway sites and potential cultural heritage objects; prioritize roofs for maintainable shading, rainwater management, energy, or public activities, rather than purely aesthetic forms. [depth:height_massing_character] Update decisions use an "investigate - safety and value assessment - retain - renovate - update when necessary" threshold process, without making demolition–renovate–retain conclusions in the absence of existing buildings and ownership. ## Detailed Design of Key Areas

Three key areas all use provisional polygons, therefore the following locations, areas, and internal structures are provided as directional designs and will need to be completed with official key areas, parcels, buildings, and control conditions before being redrawn. [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-001] [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-002] [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-003] [metric:key_area_count] [depth:three_key_area_detailed_design]

### Zhongzhiyuan: Full Stack Validation Core

Positioned as a garden-type full-stack self-innovative district. Spatially, it forms a three-part validation chain with "reliable model arena, embodied intelligence low-speed test loop, and edge-side computing and energy coordination station." The green public interface serves for display, rest, standard workshops, and public explanation, without disguising the closed test area as Public Space. Building updates prioritize adaptable separable experimental units, equipment handling, and shared evaluation spaces; in terms of transportation, only the concept of connection to external public transportation and the slow travel system is proposed, awaiting verification of the Fifth Ring Road, Qinghe, flood control, road conditions, and safety. The operational lever is the test application, risk tiering, on-site observation, third-party review, and public summary of results. (Walking and Cycling Network)

### Beijing AI Origin Community: Open Source Transformation Core

Located as a campus-oriented hub for technology transfer and talent living community. The space is organized into three types of public interfaces: "Open Source Zero-Kilometer, Campus Technology Transfer Living Room, and Talent Living Collaboration Station." The first category records open-source contributions and versions, the second connects technology, legal, intellectual property, scenarios, and customers, and the third integrates living, childcare, sports, learning, and nighttime collaboration into the innovative daily routine. Updates are implemented in small steps: starting with the first floor, idle periods, and shared facilities, and then deciding on further actions based on usage evidence; it must not be assumed that the university, park, or owner has agreed to the renovation. The focus on pedestrian and cycling infrastructure is on the seamless continuity between campus, park, community, and transit stations.

### Dazhongsi: Intelligent Native Interaction Core

Located as a city-type smart-native business, consumption, and international exchange district. Spatially, it is defined by the interfaces of "smart-native marketplaces, urban model public audit rooms, and global AI lounges," transitioning from public experience to professional discussion; the quadrants of pedestrian stitching only express the desired relationships without providing conclusions on bridges, tunnels, or construction feasibility. The ground floor of the buildings prioritizes services such as pitch sessions, displays, short-term work, community consumption, and non-motorized vehicle parking; the mixed use of public green spaces must comply with green space regulations, traffic safety, and activity permits. Operations are managed through public experience routes that integrate commercial promotion with model accountability, data sources, and human appeals, avoiding mere product launch shows.

## Land Use, Building Scale, and Retain-Renovate-Demolish Strategy

The land structure is organized around continuous open spaces rather than enclosing various sectors within gated industrial parks. At the southern end, enhance smart indigenous business, culture, and talent living. In the middle section, strengthen on-campus research, open-source conversion, and community services. At the northern end, strengthen full-stack R&D, security validation, and low-carbon computing power. Each zone adopts warehouse enumeration codes `05`, `07`, `08`, and `14`, but these are design languages, not the approved land uses. [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-01-W] [standard:MNR-LAND-USE-CLASSIFICATION-GUIDE]

The built fabric is composed of [metric:building_footprint_area_sqm] concept carrier prototypes, occupying a proportion of the submitted boundary as indicated by [metric:building_footprint_ratio]. These footprints are used solely to verify the relationships between Public Spaces, ground-level openness, research and development modules, and east-west connections, with a confidence level of low; they do not represent the current number of buildings, construction scale, or potential demolition targets. Formal deepening requires the addition of use, age, structure, safety, ownership, energy consumption, historical value, and user opinions for each building, followed by a multi-scheme comparison involving preservation, restoration, adaptive reuse, localized updates, and new construction.

Buildings scale, Floor Area Ratio, and height should not be filled with fictional values. At this stage, what is deliverable is a method: comparing Public Space solar and wind environments, views of the site, fire and municipal service capacity, mixed-use functions, reversible structures, and lifecycle carbon. Each professional deepening update should modify `assumptions.json` and the model hash to allow changes to be traceable. [standard:MOHURD-ARCH-DESIGN-DEPTH-2016]

## Transport, Rail, Municipal Infrastructure, and Public Services

Traffic structure is "one vertical and five horizontal": one vertical is the Jing-Zhang Smart Pulse Public Experience Greenway, and the five horizontal lines serve the four quadrants of Dazhongsi, the southern and central segments of the scene wings, the proximity school connection of the AI Origin, the central and northern segments of the innovative community, and the external connection of Zhongzhiyuan. [data:geometry/roads.geojson#ROAD-SPINE-001] [metric:crosslink_count] All line positions are restricted within the provisional site, expressing demand relationships rather than road boundaries. Formal deepening must use track entrances and exits, road sections, public transportation, passenger flow, parking, non-motorized vehicles, accessibility, fire safety, and municipal data for accessibility and safety checks. [depth:traffic_rail_slow_parking]

Pedestrian strategies should address the fundamental question of whether a continuous, safe, and barrier-free arrival is possible, before delving into smart solutions. AI should only assist in aggregating public voluntary reports of obstacles, generating explainable alternative routes, and scheduling activity days; any recommendations must provide common signage, on-site personnel, and paper maps as fallback options. Parking should not focus on a total quota, but first distinguish between commuter, visitor, barrier-free, loading/unloading, and activity peak periods. Prioritize rail connections, shared mobility, and standardized non-motorized vehicle parking to reduce the occupation of Public Spaces.

Municipal infrastructure and New Infrastructure adopt "pluggable service units": edge-side computational power processes only explicitly authorized tasks, distributed energy and storage require specialized energy and fire safety measures, public Wi-Fi and sensing devices use minimal data collection and shortest retention, and model outputs retain human appeals. Facility locations, capacities, piping, and engineering feasibility are pending official documentation; do not commit to construction before official confirmation. [data:geometry/constraints.geojson#constraints-empty-by-design]

## Risk, Copyright, and Compliance

Nine key data gaps have been entered into `assumptions.json`: official overall boundary, official three key areas, control plan, road and traffic, buildings and ownership, cultural protection, municipal safety, statutory blue-green control, and Public Space maintenance. The most critical risk is not "insufficiently precise maps," but misreading the conceptual precision as planning precision; therefore, all map boundaries are expressed using low-contrast dashed lines to indicate provisional boundaries, and all derived numerical values are only used for internal recalculations. If official maps are not re-calculated in their entirety after their arrival, this plan will lose consistency. [data:geometry/site_boundary.geojson#SITE-001] [depth:risk_missing_data]

Conceptual Recommendation for AI Risk Management: Use scenario-level controls; do not employ secret, corporate internal, or personal privacy data; medical, legal, security, and public service outputs are for informational purposes only; high-risk scenarios must be manually approved, taken over on-site, audited in logs, and subject to public appeals; model failures and minority opinions are entered into a public audit room. Any activities, recruitment, policies, funding, partners, construction, or operations are conceptual recommendations and do not constitute determined government decisions. All original content and external background references are found in `report/copyright_statement.md` and `sources.json`.

The known flaw of this proposal is the lack of official polygons and current professional baseline data. Therefore, it can only serve as a high-fidelity starting point for Content Review and professional deepening, but cannot enter precise area scoring, statutory planning, engineering design, or investment decision-making. Before submission, perform deterministic validation, spatial review, visual packaging check, and professional evidence review; passing through this only represents the completion of machine check and content review, but does not guarantee the excellence, feasibility, or approval of the proposal.
