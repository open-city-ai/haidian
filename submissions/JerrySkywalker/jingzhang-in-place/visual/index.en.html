THREE TEMPORAL LAYERS

Urban continuity is not one uniform “intelligent” axis

Continuing time, life and memory return space from technical spectacle to human time. STATUS × ACTION is a careful decision skeleton below the proposal, not its image of the city.

Long-lived city

Streets, trees, walking, public service and shared memory do not yield to short-cycle equipment. They are the public floor that remains complete with AI off.

Adaptable city

Rooms, thresholds, maintenance and operations may be repaired, cleared and handed over again; each move stays inside rights, accessibility and public-continuity boundaries.

Temporary intelligence

Technology appears only when it is needed, accountable, stoppable and fully resettable. A missing condition returns the outcome to ordinary city or NO-BUILD.
