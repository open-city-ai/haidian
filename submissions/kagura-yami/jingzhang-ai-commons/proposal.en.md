## Overall Design Area: Urban Renewal and Regulatory-Plan-Level Urban Design

Overall structure suggestions are summarized as "one vein, three gateways, two wing interfaces, and multiple community micro-rings." The one vein is responsible for cultural display, slow travel interactions, and scene connection; the three gateways serve Zhongzhiyuan, the AI Origin community, and the connection between Dazhongsi and the urban transportation network; the two wing interfaces introduce technological services and public scenes into the main vein; the community micro-rings integrate residential, educational, research and development, commercial, and green spaces into a 15-minute walking and cycling network. [depth:overall_spatial_structure] This structure prioritizes fixing breakpoints, opening the first floor, combining existing spaces, and improving public accessibility, without making large-scale demolition and reconstruction the default approach.

The Urban Renewal targets can be categorized into five types: "protection constraint areas, retention and restoration areas, adaptive reuse areas, reversible supplementation areas, and areas awaiting professional argumentation." Industrial spaces can form six product types: open research, flexible R&D, pilot testing, result release, technological services, and talent living, supporting teams in their full-cycle migration from short-term workstations to growth-oriented spaces. The building mass emphasizes a gradual relationship with existing blocks, sites, parks, water systems, and residential interfaces, but the Floor Area Ratio, height, density, setback, and total scale are yet to be confirmed by official control plans and survey data. [depth:development_intensity_controls] The urban renewal project list should document spatial value, public interest, data dependency, ownership risks, and reversibility, making the control plan a verifiable research framework rather than a disguised approved planning conclusion. ## Land Use, Building Scale, and Retain-Renovate-Demolish Strategy

The land use organization is suggested to adopt a combination of five types of spaces: "innovative production, technological services, talent living, public culture, and blue-green open." These should be mapped according to the national land space classification system, rather than creating an incompatible classification system. [standard:MNR-LAND-USE-CLASSIFICATION-GUIDE] Among these, innovative production emphasizes the adjacency for research and development, testing, and the release of results; technological services provide interfaces for intellectual property, legal, financial, and international cooperation; talent living complements residential, dining, sports, learning, and care facilities; public culture carries the Jing-Zhang narrative and AI citizenship education; and blue-green open spaces bear the responsibility for ecology, interaction, and low-risk scenarios. This submission has derived a complete land-use zoning from the same provisional site polygon and completed a temporary recalculation; these proportions only describe the conceptual layer, and the official polygons and control plans must be recalculated in their entirety after they are in place. [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-001]

The architectural strategy adheres to the principle of "investigate first, then categorize, prioritize preservation, and ensure reversibility." It is recommended to set the architectural research labels as R0 for preservation constraints, R1 for preservation and renovation, R2 for adaptive reuse, R3 for reversible additions, and R4 for speculative reconstructions; when there is a lack of current outlines, dates, structures, uses, ownership, and cultural heritage documentation, only a classification method can be formed, but no specific conclusions of demolition or new construction can be given. [depth:retain_renovate_demolish] New spaces should preferably adopt variable partitions, shared facilities, visible ground floors, composite time periods, and modular mechanical and electrical systems; the massing, height, roof, and facade should comply with subsequent zoning regulations, solar access, fire safety, cultural heritage, and landscape considerations. ## Transport, Rail, Municipal Infrastructure, and Public Services

Traffic concepts adopt "track priority, pedestrian and bicycle network, public transit integration, and layered management of motor vehicles." Three key areas each set up urban gateways facing the track or main public transit, connected by community micro-rings to the Jing-Zhang main artery. Prioritize identifying slow-moving traffic breakpoints, detour points, dangerous crossings, and conflicts with non-motorized vehicle parking. Propose low-intervention solutions through planar optimization, temporal management, signage, and micro-updates to Public Spaces. Dazhongsi's quadrants, five-ring connections, and crossing nodes are only studied as problems and solution comparisons, without providing bridge or tunnel conclusions, road right-of-way, or engineering feasibility. [depth:traffic_rail_slow_parking]

Municipal and New Infrastructure are suggested to form a "cloud-edge-end-scenario" hierarchy: shared computing power and model services are located in compliant data centers, while district edge nodes handle low-latency tasks. Public terminals are defaulted to collect and retain offline human alternatives. Distributed energy, storage, communication, drainage, fire protection, and pipeline relocation only establish demand lists and capacity verification procedures, to be refined after confirmation by the relevant professional departments. [depth:municipal_new_infrastructure] Public services should first conduct an audit of facility inventory and population needs, then determine the supplementation methods for talent hubs, learning, health navigation, childcare and elderly care, sports, and international services; avoid using AI interfaces to replace necessary offline windows, professional personnel, and accessibility services. [source:DATA-SRC-OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT-20260509]

The north-south smart veins and five groups of east-west connections are expressed by [data:geometry/roads.geojson#ROAD-001]. The temporary network length is recalculated by [metric:road_centerline_length_m]; this value only compares conceptual connectivity relationships and is not used as a basis for road right-of-way, station modifications, bridges, tunnels, or engineering investments. The traffic system is established according to a problem-solution comparison-process-specialized verification flowchart [depth:traffic_rail_slow_parking], while municipal and end-side facilities are established according to a capacity and safety checklist [depth:municipal_new_infrastructure]. Track interfaces, road grades, fire safety, drainage, energy, and communication still require principal professional documentation, while public services must first verify the baseline of facilities and offline accessibility for different user groups.

## Blue-Green Network, Public Space, and Urban Character

The blue-green system uses the Jing-Zhang Heritage Park as the north-south cultural and public space axis, with Qinghe, Xiaoyuehe, and existing green spaces as ecological support, constructing a "continuous shaded pedestrian network—rainwater storage—innovative interaction—low-risk testing—historical display" composite network. [depth:blue_green_public_space] Before the cultural heritage protection, green line, blue line, and implementation boundary are confirmed, the design only proposes node types and connection directions: forest-underneath discussion courtyards, mobile announcement platforms, quiet resting points, barrier-free guided tours, environmental perception poles, and removable experimental components. The architectural style does not pursue a uniform "science-fiction skin," but instead, with durable materials, clear structures, open ground floors, restrained nighttime lighting, and discernible new and old elements, it forms a "rational, open, warm, and evolving" urban atmosphere. [standard:MOHURD-URBAN-DESIGN-MEASURES]

The cultural axis is suggested as follows: "The Jing-Zhang Railway connects the city with the mountains and rivers, Zhongguancun Innovation connects knowledge with industry, and AI Commons connects people, machines, and public interests." Signage should convey information on mileage, versions, contributions, and time, without fabricating history. Sacred landmarks are recommended to adopt reversible exhibitions or the reuse of existing spaces, with construction subject to professional review.

## Renewal Projects, Implementation Policy, and Phasing

The recommendations for implementation adopt a tiered phased approach of "data and mechanisms first, then low-impact spaces, and finally system refinement." Progression in each phase is contingent upon the availability of data, approvals, safety, and public acceptance. [depth:phasing_implementation] In the near term, we can complete the inventory of open-source data, brand prototypes, pedestrian diagnostics, temporary signage, community deliberations, and T01 indoor tests. In the mid-term, after professional arguments, we can advance the adaptive reuse of existing spaces, the demonstration of the three portals, and the blue-green nodes. In the long term, we can reassess cross-zone connections, infrastructure upgrades, and the replication of mature scenarios. Note that the phased approach is a reference path and does not represent a definitive construction timeline, funding, or implementing entity.
