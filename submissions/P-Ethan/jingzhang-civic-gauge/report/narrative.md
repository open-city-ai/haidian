# 京张刻度 / JING-ZHANG CIVIC GAUGE

铁路以统一轨距、里程、时刻、检修和交接建立公共信任。京张刻度把这套历史机制转译为 AI 城市的公共价值校准：北部验证技术是否安全工作，中部验证是否改善日常，南部验证是否被多样人群持续采用。任何项目都必须公开用途、风险、责任人、人工接管和退出条件。

The railway built public trust through a common gauge, mileposts, timetables, maintenance, and handovers. The Civic Gauge translates that legacy into public-value calibration for the AI city: verify safe technology in the north, everyday usefulness in the middle, and inclusive adoption in the south. Every project must disclose purpose, risk, accountable owner, human takeover, and exit conditions.
