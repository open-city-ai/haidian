JING-ZHANG CIVIC GAUGE

Make every urban AI service answerable to a civic gauge

Calibrate technology in the north, co-test everyday life in the middle, and verify inclusive adoption in the south. Every service stays visible, reviewable, reversible, and paired with a human channel.

THE CIVIC CALIBRATION CHAIN

  1. Commission a public problem
  2. Run a controlled test
  3. Conduct a time-limited public trial
  4. Review and take over with people
  5. Adopt, change, or exit
