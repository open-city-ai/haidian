# 设计叙事摘要 / Design Narrative Abstract

“京张恰好”不以更大模型塑造 AI 城市，而以公共任务、最小充分模型、近端数据、离线等价、平稳降级与人类责任建立可信的城市采纳程序。

JUST ENOUGH JING-ZHANG defines an AI city through public tasks, minimum-sufficient models, near-edge data, offline equivalents, graceful degradation, and accountable human decisions rather than model scale alone.
