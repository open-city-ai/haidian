05

Twelve scenarios and four pilgrimage landmarks

Four validation scenarios treat reproducible failure as knowledge; every scenario also names its data boundary, offline equivalent, human owner, and exit condition.

SCN-01…12 · LM-01…04
SCN-01

Offline Multilingual Guide

A one-time-download guide to Jing-Zhang history, places, and public services that remains useful offline.

SCN-02 · TEST

Accessible Near-Edge Navigation Trial

Tests personalized accessible routes against real surfaces, slopes, closures, and rest points.

SCN-03 · TEST

Lightweight Public-Service Q&A Trial

Answers low-risk questions from a bounded policy corpus, showing a source and human service point for every response.

SCN-04

Microclimate Stay Advice

Turns site-level heat, humidity, wind, shade, and seating status into understandable stay options.

SCN-05

Facility Repair Assistant

Matches common asset identifiers to manual steps on a site terminal; staff confirm every work order.

SCN-06

Jing-Zhang Cultural Story Small Model

Generates age-appropriate bilingual short stories from edited historical material and shows the original archival trail on site.

SCN-07 · TEST

On-Device Model-Scale Experiment

Lets participants compare rules and differently sized models on one public task for quality, latency, and conceptual resource targets.

SCN-08

Offline Emergency Information Board

Stores evacuation routes, assembly points, and emergency cards locally; connected mode only synchronizes signed updates.

SCN-09 · TEST

Low-Speed Robot Yielding Trial

Tests low-speed carriers yielding to children, wheelchairs, and cane users on a closed, visible loop.

SCN-10

No-Account Event Reservation

Allocates public-event places through one-time ticket numbers and an on-site standby mechanism.

SCN-11

Small-Model Update and Repair Station

Provides signed, reversible, documented on-site updates for public terminals.

SCN-12

Public-Comment Summarizer

Clusters comments that participants agreed to publish into traceable summaries, retaining minority views and links to originals.
