Spatial concept and naming logic

From the REN-shaped switchback to the MindLine: in 1909 a single REN character carried the railway over the Guangou pass. Today its first stroke reads as knowing - conscience, culture, the inner life - and its second stroke as doing - action, technology, the running of the railway; the two strokes lean on each other to form the character for a person. Knowing comes before doing, and that word order is the value: culture stands above technology.

The name comes from the unity of knowing and doing (Wang Yangming): the twin rails read as a knowing rail (culture) and a doing rail (technology), meeting at Origin Station. In the English name, Mind is knowing and Line is doing as well as the railway line. Zhan Tianyou, who studied abroad and then built the line through the Guangou pass, embodies that unity.

Testing and validation scenarios

T1 · SN-013

Zhongzhiyuan embodied-robot public-service testing ground

A closed testing area at Vanguard Station validating cleaning, delivery and guiding robots together with their safety boundaries.

Non-AI equivalent service
Outside the testing area, cleaning, delivery and guiding services are still delivered by people, so public service quality does not drop because of the trial.
Exit and stop condition
Any risk to personal safety, loss of equipment control or failure of the enclosure triggers an immediate power-down, site clearance and suspension of all test items.
Incident response and rollback
A one-touch stop and on-site response procedure applies; after an incident the system rolls back to the last stable version and reopens only after review and rectification.
T2 · SN-014

Heritage-park autonomous shuttle micro-loop test segment

A low-speed shuttle test segment along the middle greenway, staffed by safety operators and opened segment by segment rather than belt-wide.

Non-AI equivalent service
Walking and cycling run the full length and conventional bus connections are unaffected, so the test segment is never the only way through.
Exit and stop condition
Takeover frequency above the threshold, obstacle-avoidance failure or severe weather suspends the test on that segment and restores normal circulation.
Incident response and rollback
An incident stops operation and closes the segment at once, rolls back to human-driven shuttles, and reopening proceeds segment by segment after independent review.
T3 · SN-015

City-scale multi-agent governance sandbox

Multi-agent governance simulation on synthetic data inside a trusted data space; conclusions only inform human decisions.

Non-AI equivalent service
Existing human analysis, joint review and public-notice procedures stay unchanged, and sandbox conclusions never enter any enforcement step.
Exit and stop condition
A synthetic-data leakage risk, unauthorised access to real data, or any direct use of a simulation result for enforcement halts the sandbox immediately.
Incident response and rollback
Data isolation and version rollback apply; on an anomaly the trusted data space is frozen, the last trusted snapshot is restored and the parties concerned are notified.

Scenarios and landmarks are conceptual recommendations. AI public services must be suspendable, appealable and transferable to humans, and a non-AI equivalent must remain. Scenarios touching personal information need separate assessment and data minimisation; landmarks sit outside heritage protection scopes and require dedicated review. Urban AI prompts rather than adjudicates, and the final judgement always rests with people.
