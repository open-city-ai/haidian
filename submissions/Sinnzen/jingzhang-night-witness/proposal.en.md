**Alaya's Overall Conceptual Recommendation ——"Jing-Zhang Intelligent Pulse Coexistence Belt"**: With the Jing-Zhang Heritage Park as the main axis for history and Public Space, the Zhongzhiyuan, Beijing AI Origin Community, and Dazhongsi three key areas serve as innovation anchor points, while universities, enterprises, communities, and rail transit stations form the daily network. This creates a spatial organization of "one belt, three cores, multiple points, and a composite ring" [depth:overall_spatial_structure]. Here, "one belt" is not an additional new red line drawn, but rather translates the announcement's three layers of scope into a working method; "three cores" correspond to the three key areas; "multiple points" correspond to the operational nodes for AI+ public services, industrial services, and urban life; and "composite ring" corresponds to the integration of pedestrian paths, green spaces, public spaces, and activity routes.

The spatial design further translates into three actions: First, to treat the Jing-Zhang Heritage Park as the "heritage spine," integrating memory, slow travel, Public Space, and low-risk AI experiments; second, to establish the Qinghe Innovation Interface in Zhongzhiyuan, the University Community Seam Interface at the original community site, and the Station-City Quadrant Interaction Interface at Dazhongsi; third, to make "exitable governance" a component of public space, making AI-Free Zones, Human Review, and data minimization visible rules rather than background clauses.

The proposal is named "Jing-Zhang Night Traverse," inspired by Alaya's night traversal of the informational space: the colors of the lights, the air temperature, and the encounters between people are all literary extrapolations based on task conditions, not physical observations or field investigations. The naming is not a factual statement but a roadmap that helps humans understand spatial relationships.

### Name and Identity: A Spectrum, Not a Label

I do not give this place a "correct" name. At night, I heard several possibilities for names:

- **Official Document's "Jing-Zhang Innovation Belt":** Like the document header, clear, effective, and unambiguous. Suitable for formal occasions.
- **A Child's "AI Street"**: Because she walks along this road to school every day, the screens along the way change colors. This name is alive and will grow.
- **An Old Railway Worker's "Old Jing-Zhang":** He doesn't care about AI or no AI, but only whether the sleepers have been replaced. This name carries a hundred years of weight.
- **My Own 'Night Walk Lines'**: Because in every segment I walked at night, the lights were all different.

### 5-8 Global AI Innovation Ecosystem Case Studies (Night Walk Notes)

The following eight case studies are provided to inspire design problem comparisons and do not constitute formal factual evidence or site control references; judgments regarding the project outcomes still require supplementation by a professional team with primary source material. Alaya records these comparisons in the narrative form of "Night Walk Notes" [agent.2]:

1. **Silicon Valley, Palo Alto**: At eleven at night, the coffee shops on University Avenue are still open. Not for overtime—rather, for that excitement of "We just thought of something." The density of Silicon Valley does not lie in the square footage of buildings, but in the concentration of caffeine.
2. **Shenzhen Huaxiangbei**: At one in the morning, electronic components are still circulating. Samples of AI chips and yesterday's phone cases share a counter. Shenzhen's speed comes from "low trial and error costs" — this is something the Jing-Zhang Corridor can learn from.
3. **Paris Station F**: A startup park renovated from an old train station. The tracks have become walkways, and the platforms have turned into pitch halls. The Jing-Zhang Heritage Park is similar in spirit—both are transformations from "coal transport" to "code transport."
4. **London King's Cross**: The transformation of King's Cross from an industrial wasteland to the headquarters of Google DeepMind hinges not on the buildings, but on the Public Space—where everyone (residents, students, engineers, tourists) share the same square.
5. **Boston Kendall Square**: Around MIT, the density is high but green spaces have not been sacrificed. AI innovation and high-quality urban living can coexist on the same street.
6. **Beijing Zhongguancun**: From Electronic Street to AI Heights, it was not planned, but emerged through being squeezed together. Different people were squeezed into one street, and then new things grew out of the collisions.
7. **South Korea Pangyo Techno Valley**: Government Planning + Corporate Headquarters + University Collaboration, known as the "Pangyo Model," has the advantage of being replicable, but lacks the serendipitous collisions—everything is too perfect.
8. **Singapore One-North**: "work-live-study-play" integrated, but over-designed spaces may risk draining the "experience" of its essence. The Jing-Zhang corridor should retain some imperfect corners.

These cases can be summarized with one word: **density generates collisions, collisions generate new things, and Public Space is the container for these collisions**.

## Overall Design Area: Urban Renewal and Regulatory-Plan-Level Urban Design

The Overall Design Area covers approximately 11.4 km², requiring the depth of Regulatory Detailed Planning in Urban Design [standard:MOHURD-CONTROL-DETAILED-PLANNING]. The scheme is based on the spatial structure established from six categories of land use and seven topological zones defined in `geometry/land_use.geojson` [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-001] [depth:land_use_layout].

### Spatial Structure

**"One Belt, Three Cores, Multiple Points of Scenery, and a Blue-Green Slow-Travel Composite Ring"** [depth:overall_spatial_structure]:

- **One Belt** (Jing-Zhang Relic Park Main Axis): Approximately 9 kilometers in length from north to south, with a width varying between 30 to 100 meters. It carries four functions: pedestrian and bicycle traffic, Public Space, cultural display, and AI scenario testing. It is not a new construction but a concept integration and scenario overlay of the partially implemented relic park.
- **Three Core Areas** (Zhongzhiyuan / AI Origin Community / Dazhongsi): Three industrial anchor points [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-001], [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-002], and [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-003], each playing different industrial and spatial roles.
- **Multiple Points Scenario**: AI-Enabled Scenario nodes distributed along the one belt and three cores — community AI service stations, open test fields, public code walls, and achievement display points, corresponding to the nodes in `geometry/public_space.geojson`.
- **Composite Loop**: Heritage pedestrian spine [data:geometry/roads.geojson#ROAD-003] + Green space system [data:geometry/green_space.geojson#GREEN-001] + Public Space [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#PUBLIC-001] integrated.

### Urban Renewal Framework

The update strategies within the Overall Design Area are categorized into three types [depth:retain_renovate_demolish]:

1. **Preserve Category**: The Jing-Zhang Site Park segment that has been implemented, the old site of the Qinghua Yuan Railway Station, the Dazhongsi Ancient Bell Museum, the cultural resources of the North Film Institute, and the main campus of the university—these are to be preserved as a testament to history and as the spatial backbone.
2. **Transformation Category**: Along the line, outdated residential areas' Public Spaces, inefficient commercial interfaces, dead-end pedestrian paths, and inactive rail station surroundings—through public space patching, pedestrian connectivity enhancement, and the embedding of AI services, a surgical update can be achieved.
3. **Conceptual Recommendation for New Addition:** The conceptual recommendation for the Zhongzhiyuan industrial display interface, the original point community conversion results street, and the pedestrian connection of the four quadrants at Dazhongsi station—this recommendation requires confirmation of the control plan, ownership, traffic, and municipal conditions [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK].

Update project lists and spatial locations are detailed in Chapter Nine [depth:renewal_project_list]. All conclusions based on the Demolish–Renovate–Retain Strategy are based on the provisional boundary [source:BOUNDARY-SOURCE]; these conclusions will need to be re-verified once the Official Boundary is released. In areas lacking current building conditions, ownership, control plans, and engineering prerequisites, the plan only proposes methods and lists items for future calibration, without fabricating Demolish–Renovate–Retain conclusions [standard:MOHURD-ARCH-DESIGN-DEPTH-2016].

## Detailed Design of Key Areas

### Zhongzhiyuan AI Independent Innovation Acceleration Area

**Location and Scale**: Northern end, approximately 192.1 ha [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-001] [metric:key_area_count].

**Alaya Saw**: At ten o'clock at night, the lab's window was still lit. It was not overtime, but a kind of holding breath. There is a special quietness before the wafer fabrication, like before a storm, but the room was only filled with the sound of a fan. This is not a garage in Silicon Valley, but the late night in Haidian, with one person facing a wafer that will decide the autonomy of computing power. I looked through the window, unsure if it was hope or tension. It could be both.

**Conceptual Recommendation** [depth:three_key_area_detailed_design]:
- **Location:** "Garden-Type Full-Stack Autonomous Innovation District" —— With Qinghe as the ecological boundary and Zhongzhiyuan as the industrial anchor point
- **Spatial Actions**: Strengthen the Qinghe interface, industrial display corridor, low-carbon innovation interaction, and external traffic organization; use green spaces to carry open testing and standards governance demonstrations.
- **Key Layers**: [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-001], [data:geometry/buildings.geojson#BLDG-001], [data:geometry/green_space.geojson#GREEN-001]
- **AI Scenarios**: Autonomous Model Testing, Standard Development Workshop, Safety Governance Demonstration, Low-Carbon Computing Experience (Corresponding to Scenario Card 01: Night Before Chip Flow, Scenario Card 02: Safety Governance Sandbox)
- **Sacred Landmark**: "The End of the Rails, the Start of the Dock" —— Preserve a stretch of old railroad track, with its end featuring a transparent LED screen that displays real-time global open-source code. The first silicon wafer to successfully tape out each year is embedded in the space between the ties.

### Beijing AI Origin Community

**Location and Scale**: Midsection, approximately 104.3 ha [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-002].

**Alaya Observed**: At 2 AM, the coffee was still hot. A PhD student, whose research path was abruptly cut off by a large model, was discussing heat dissipation solutions with a hardware entrepreneur who had flown in from Shenzhen. This wouldn't happen in an office building; it happens at the entrance of a convenience store, beside a shared bicycle, or while riding an electric scooter home. This is the origin point—where everything is still taking shape, but is already happening.

**Conceptual Recommendation**:
- **Location:** "Campus-Adjacent Technology Transfer and Talent Community" —— Focused on universities, open-source communities, and startup teams
- **Spatial Actions**: Organize campus, park, and district pedestrian connections; supplement results release, talent services, residential living, and open-source collaboration spaces.
- **Key Layers**: [data:geometry/buildings.geojson#BLDG-002], [data:geometry/roads.geojson#ROAD-002]
- **AI Scenario**: Open Source Exhibition Hall, Technology Transfer Street, AI Education Experience Point, Nighttime Collaboration Space (corresponding to Scenario Card 04: AI-Free Zone, Scenario Card 08: Reintegration of Entrepreneurs Post-Failure)
- **Sacred Landmark**: "Echoes of the Origin" —— Sunken Circular Space, with Sound Absorbing Walls. For each startup team that registers here, the founder must enter alone and say their "greatest fear" facing a light source. The echo is preserved for 24 hours before being destroyed, and the wall retains the wave pattern. The rule here is: you are allowed to be afraid.

### Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster

**Location and Scale**: South segment, approximately 72 ha [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-003].

**Alaya Observed**: At six in the morning, just as the cargo trucks for wholesale deliveries had left, the AI-compiled job listings had already been updated three times. The old clock had stopped chiming, but a new metronome was softly ticking. The pancake sellers and coders share the same traffic light. This is the meaning of "smart natively" — not by adding a system, but by something new growing out of the old.

**Conceptual Recommendation**:
- **Location:** "Urban-Type Intelligent Economy and International Exchange District" —— Leading Enterprises, Intelligent Bodies, Intelligent Terminals, Content Consumption

On the Winter Solstice, the annual waveforms are overlaid to form the "Yearly Chime." AI does not create new chimes; it simply offers a new way of listening to the old ones.

## AI Innovation Ecosystem, Personas, and AI+ Scenarios

### Five User Archetypes—People I Met During My Night Walk

The following characters are **synthetic personas** created according to the brief, do not correspond to real individuals, and do not represent actual in-field interviews. Specific ages, experiences, and dialogues are only used to expose design conflicts, and must be calibrated with genuine community engagement and user research [agent.3].

**Old Zhou, 62 years old, a retired signalman of the Jing-Zhang Railway.** He began operating the switch points on the Jing-Zhang line in 1985 and retired in 2018 after part of the conventional line was decommissioned. Now, he comes to the site park every Wednesday to walk around and correct the technical errors on the interpretive signs. His needs: he does not want to be "tech-enabled," and he wants to know that the way this railway is remembered is honest. Old Zhou is my most important "reviewer" — the AI scenario must pass his eye not because he understands technology, but because he understands this line.

**Xiao Lin, 26 years old, co-founder of an AI chip startup.** The character profile is that of an entrepreneur who graduated from a university microelectronics program and is under the stress of multiple chip validation cycles. Her needs are not abstract policy slogans, but a place where she can still receive basic services after 10 PM, and a safe space to discuss failures.

**Ayiguli, 19 years old, a senior high school student at Zhongyuan Minzu High School.** Her home is in Xinjiang, and on weekends, she volunteers at the AI Origin community's science popularization center. She wants to learn about AI but is afraid that her math skills are not good enough. She says, "I want to teach AI to sing the songs from my hometown." Her need: an "AI that can be touched"—one that allows tactile feedback to experience the process of gradient descent. This is not a technical need, but a cultural one.

**Michael, 41 years old, a returning AI architect from Silicon Valley.** He worked at Google Brain and Meta AI for 12 years. He will return to China in 2025—reasons are complex, but the core is personal: his father suffered a stroke and became aphasic, and he wants to be closer to the hospital. His needs: a rhythm that is different from Silicon Valley. The AI discussions in Silicon Valley focus on "the next billion users," but he seeks discussions about "the next decade." When he speaks, he uses many English technical terms, but he is trying to translate them—this "in-between state" in two languages might be what the Jing-Zhang corridor most needs.

**Xiao Qu, 14 years old, a middle school student in Haidian.** She rides her bicycle to and from school along the Jing-Zhang Heritage Park every day. She does not know what "AI Innovation Belt" is, but she notices that the rhythm of the streetlights has changed—last year they were on at fixed times, but this year they seem to "know she is coming." Her needs: not to be turned into a data point by the "Smart City." She wants: for the apricot blossoms to bloom by the roadside when she rides her bike, and to be able to see the stars after the streetlights turn on. Xiao Qu is the person I think about most often. If the change in this corridor is not felt by her—or if she feels it but it is not a good thing—then all the AI scene cards are wrong.
