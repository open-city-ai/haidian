Three-level scope

The proposal proceeds from coordinated research scope through overall design scope to key-area scope, binding industrial judgment, urban renewal, and detailed district design to layers and metrics.

project_scope_summary.csv

Coordinated research scope

For about 43.6 km2, proposes AI industry ecosystem, three-area two-wing coordination, future city form, and cultural narrative.

  • AI innovation and talent chains
  • Global case transformation mechanism
  • Brand naming and outreach

Overall design scope

For about 11.4 km2, organizes urban renewal, land use structure, transport and municipal systems, and the Jing-Zhang Heritage Park vitality belt.

  • Regulatory-plan-level urban design
  • Renewal project list
  • Blue-green slow-traffic system

Key-area scope

Detailed design for three key districts, stating functional uses, building renewal, public space, and transport organization.

  • Zhongzhi Park AI innovation accelerator
  • Beijing AI Origin Community
  • Dazhongsi AI industry cluster

Key areas

Each key district must state industry positioning, spatial strategy, building and public-space actions, transport interfaces, and implementation risks.

Zhongzhi Park AI Innovation Accelerator

Garden-style indigenous-innovation district emphasizing national platforms, industry showcase, Qinghe culture, external transport, and low-carbon exchange.

Beijing AI Origin Community

Near-campus commercialization district emphasizing university source innovation, open-source community, talent services, retain-renovate-demolish, and station integration.

Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster

Urban AI-economy district emphasizing leading enterprises, agent-native businesses, commercial services, green-space mixed use, and four-quadrant crossing.
