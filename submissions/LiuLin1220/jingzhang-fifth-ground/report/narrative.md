# 京张第五地面 / FIFTH GROUND JINGZHANG

地面永远是公共服务的第一选择，屋顶只是证据通过后的可选延伸。方案用一条地面遗产正线串联三座垂直换乘庭；北、中、南分别测试算力共研、公共学习和城市交换屋顶活动。结构、消防、无障碍、防水排水、权属运营五门任何一门缺证据，屋顶就保持关闭，并把完整活动导向同主题、同信息、同报名权的地面 fallback。

Ground is the first public-service choice; a roof is an optional extension after evidence passes. One ground heritage mainline links three transfer courts. North, central, and south zones test Compute Canopy, Learning Commons, and Exchange Stage activity types. If structure, fire, accessibility, waterproofing/drainage, or rights/operations evidence is missing, the roof stays closed and the complete program moves to an equal ground fallback.
