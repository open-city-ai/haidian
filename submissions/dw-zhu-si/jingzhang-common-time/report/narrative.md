# 共时京张 P0–P1 空间深化任务书 / Spatial Deepening Brief

## 1. 交付对象 / Deliverable

附册 `drawings/p0-p1-spatial-deepening.pdf` 以真实 OpenStreetMap 公开语境为底，分别表达众智园 VERIFY、北京 AI 原点 LEARN 和大钟寺 LIVE 三处重点区。每区包含 10 个候选观察点、3 个候选行动区、2 条候选剖面线；附册另提供 4 条关系剖面，共 12 条。

The supplement uses public OpenStreetMap context for the VERIFY, LEARN and LIVE key areas. Each area contains ten candidate observation points, three candidate action zones and two candidate section lines, followed by four relation sections—twelve sections in total.

## 2. 空间任务 / Spatial Tasks

- VERIFY：分离公众解释、受控测试和后勤流线；核验清河生态边缘、老站房身份与安全阈值。
- LEARN：优先核验城市—校园边界、过街、社区共学界面和遗产保护控制。
- LIVE：优先解决地铁站口、路缘、配送、无障碍和觉生寺文保本体的关系。

- VERIFY separates public explanation, controlled testing and logistics while checking the Qinghe ecological edge, station identity and safety thresholds.
- LEARN checks city-campus boundaries, crossings, community learning interfaces and heritage controls.
- LIVE addresses station exits, kerbs, delivery, accessibility and the protected Juesheng Temple precinct before adding AI services.

## 3. 现场核验 / Field Verification

每个观察点须形成：GPS 与方向照片、宽度/高差/坡度、分时流量、障碍和风险、访谈同意、原始文件索引与两人复核。至少覆盖工作日早/午/晚/夜、周末和雨后；默认不采集人脸、车牌或精确个人轨迹。

Each observation point requires GPS and directional photography, widths/levels/slopes, time-based counts, barriers and risks, interview consent, raw-file indexing and two-person verification. Surveys must cover weekday morning/noon/evening/night, weekends and post-rain conditions; faces, licence plates and precise personal trajectories are excluded by default.

## 4. 六类专业硬门 / Six Professional Gates

1. 交通与站城：客流、红线、接管和无障碍未核验时，不确定桥隧或工程线位。
2. 景观生态气候：蓝线、水文、树冠、热环境和季节调查未完成时，不宣称生态绩效。
3. 遗产与公共叙事：保护范围、原址/复原/迁置身份和公众解释未确认时，不新增永久构件。
4. 建筑市政消防：产权、结构、疏散和容量未确认时，FAR、高度、建筑量和设备参数保持 unknown。
5. 公共利益与无障碍：未完成受影响群体参与和真实路线测试时，不宣称公平、安全或便利改善。
6. AI 与数据治理：未完成合法来源、最小化、人工回退、申诉、迁移和退役合同，不接入真实运行数据。

1. Transport and station-city interface: no bridge, tunnel or engineering alignment before passenger flow, redline, takeover and accessibility evidence.
2. Landscape, ecology and climate: no performance claim before blue-line, hydrology, canopy, heat and seasonal surveys.
3. Heritage and public narrative: no permanent addition before protection boundaries, original/reconstructed/relocated identity and public interpretation are confirmed.
4. Buildings, municipal systems and fire safety: FAR, height, floor area and equipment capacity remain unknown before ownership, structure, evacuation and capacity evidence.
5. Public interest and accessibility: no equity, safety or convenience claim before affected-community participation and real-route testing.
6. AI and data governance: no operational data connection before lawful sourcing, minimisation, human fallback, appeal, migration and retirement contracts.

## 5. 状态边界 / Status Boundary

OpenStreetMap 不是官方边界、地籍、测绘或施工底图；候选尺寸不是现状尺寸或规范结论。总体范围与三重点区官方 polygon、现场证据、专业签署、真实业主、采购路径和人民币成本到位后，才允许把相应 HOLD 改为 GO。

OpenStreetMap is not an official boundary, cadastral record, survey or construction base. Candidate dimensions are not existing-condition measurements or code conclusions. A HOLD may become GO only after official polygons, field evidence, professional sign-off, real owners, procurement routes and RMB costs are available.
