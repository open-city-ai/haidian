Five-Layer Open Urban Stack

Turning Haidian's existing AI innovation assets into the first operating modules of the Open Urban OS.

L1
OPEN SCIENCE — JZ Open Research Agenda Bank, cross-university joint labs, global Maintainer Residency; publicly funded projects release papers/model cards/evaluation methods/reproducible experiments; AI safety, ethics and open-source licensing service center.
L2
OPEN INFRA — Shared compute, models, data spaces, evaluation and embodied-AI testing facilities; OpenLine Sandbox provides tiered testing environments; parks, public buildings and districts listed as applicable urban testbeds.
L3
OPEN VENTURE — Open-source commercialization accelerator; JZ Open Source Fund (equity + long-term funding for critical open infrastructure); enterprise procurement alliance pulling products to maturity with real orders.
L4
OPEN STREET — AI scenarios built as services residents use and rate daily; every pilot publishes its objective, data boundary, accountable party, trial period and opt-out; Future School as the standard node type.
L5
OPEN COMMUNITY — JZ Open Innovation Alliance and a neutral operating entity; contribution-credit and reputation system (code/docs/testing/translation/outreach/issue discovery); Lead Learners organize node-level community life.
