One Herringbone · Two Openings · Infinite Loops

Rooted in the 1909 Jingzhang Railway's "Herringbone lay-out", this proposal carries that first self-built mainline railway's "split-and-rejoin" spirit into an innovation loop for the AI era, forming a spatial structure of One Belt · Three Hubs · Two Wings — AI-Rail Weaving. The Jingzhang Heritage Park green corridor is the north-south AI-Rail mainline, threading three hubs — Zhizhi Marshaling Yard (acceleration), Origin Terminus (ecology) and Dazhongsi Switch (industry) — while the Zhizhi Signal Wing (capital & IP) and the Xiaoyue River Scenario Branch (scenarios & a vibrant city) feed in material as two wings, composing a closed loop of "research — agglomeration — production — scenario — capital".
