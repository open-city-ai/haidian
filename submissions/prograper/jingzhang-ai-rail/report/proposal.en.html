The leading concept is the Herringbone Intelligence-Rail (JZ AI-Rail): taking the 1909 Jing-Zhang Railway's "herringbone" switchback alignment — the spirit of "fork and reconnect" — as its genetic motif, it turns China's first self-built trunk railway into an innovation loop for the AI era 来源.
