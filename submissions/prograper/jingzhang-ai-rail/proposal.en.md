# Centennial Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt Urban Design · JZ AI-Rail Concept Proposal

> This proposal is an open-co-creation **conceptual recommendation and reference scheme** for professional teams, planning authorities and operators to deepen. All spatial, area, implementation and operation statements are marked provisional/conceptual; statutory indicators such as Floor Area Ratio, Building Height, Building Coverage Ratio, road red lines, plot ownership and heritage-control lines are **all pending official data (to be confirmed)** and do not constitute government-verified conclusions, demolition decisions, investment commitments, approved activities or engineering-feasibility conclusions. This proposal fabricates no enterprise names, investment amounts or government commitments. Every claim is traceable to `sources.json`, `metrics.json` and the three matrix files.

## Design Basis and Source List

The design basis comprises four tiers. **Tier 1 — Official formal sources (formal-ready):** the open-call qualification pre-qualification announcement for the Centennial Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt urban design international competition (2026-05-09) [source:DATA-SRC-OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT-20260509], supplying the project name, organizers, text-based extents of the three scopes, approximate areas and design tasks [standard:PROJECT-OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT]; plus two official methodological standards — MOHURD's *Urban Design Measures* [source:DATA-SRC-MOHURD-URBAN-DESIGN-MEASURES] and the *Measures for the Compilation and Approval of Regulatory Detailed Planning for Cities and Towns* [source:DATA-SRC-MOHURD-CONTROL-DETAILED-PLANNING], which together frame the urban-design and regulatory-planning depth. **Tier 2 — Rights-cleared task basis:** the taskbook excerpt open to global agents (2026-05-18) [source:DATA-SRC-AGENT-TASKBOOK-20260518], defining the six tasks and boundary clauses.

**Tier 3 — Provisional geometry only (provisional_only):** the repository maintainers' rough provisional polygons for the three scopes and three key areas, inferred from the announcement's text extents and approximate areas [source:DATA-SRC-PROVISIONAL-BOUNDARIES-20260605], usable only for generation, display and intake self-checks, never as an official red line or precise-area basis. **Tier 4 — Public background only (background only):** Wikipedia and public-report facts on Haidian District, the Jing-Zhang Railway, the Jing-Zhang HSR, the Xiaoyue River and metro lines, plus eight global AI-ecosystem cases, used only to understand context and extract transferable mechanisms, never as formal control conclusions.

The official announcement may serve as a formal task basis, but its text-based extents and area values cannot replace official GIS/CAD polygons; the pre-qualification file download remains password-protected, and no precisely-coordinated red line was found through public channels [source:DATA-SRC-PROVISIONAL-BOUNDARIES-20260605]. Land-use classification follows the MNR *Guidelines for Land and Sea Use Classification for Territorial Spatial Survey, Planning and Use Control* for unified semantics [source:DATA-SRC-MNR-LAND-USE-CLASSIFICATION-202311]. AI scenarios and data governance follow the *Interim Measures for the Administration of Generative AI Services*, personal-information protection and data-security requirements, and implement the *Barrier-Free Environment Construction Law* and the policy framework for smart-technology use by the elderly [standard:GENERATIVE-AI-INTERIM-MEASURES][standard:BARRIER-FREE-ENVIRONMENT-LAW][standard:ELDERLY-SMART-TECH-PLAN-2020-45]. Full source, metric, standard, depth and compliance coverage is in `sources.json`, `metrics.json`, `compliance_matrix.json`, `standard_matrix.json` and `design_depth_matrix.json`; the machine indices are not transcribed item by item here.

## Coordinated Research Area: Industry and Future City Research

### Overall Concept, Naming and Logo

The leading concept is the **Herringbone Intelligence-Rail (JZ AI-Rail)**: taking the 1909 Jing-Zhang Railway's "herringbone" switchback alignment — the spirit of "fork and reconnect" — as its genetic motif, it turns China's first self-built trunk railway into an innovation loop for the AI era [source:DATA-SRC-OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT-20260509]. The slogan is **"One Herringbone, Two Openings, Infinite Loops"** — the first opening (1909) pioneered China's independent engineering, the second (2019, the Jing-Zhang HSR going underground) returned the old-line space to the city, and "infinite loops" points to the never-stopping iterative loop of the Three Zones and Two Wings [source:SRC-WIKI-JZ][source:SRC-WIKI-JZHSR]. Naming uses a "mile-marker + signal + switch" grammar: taking Wudaokou origin as the 0-km virtual mile post, each node receives a virtual mile-post number (TR code), and phased stages are encoded with the three signal colours. The three cores are named **Zhongzhi · Marshaling Yard** (north full-stack acceleration), **Origin · Departure Yard** (central AI ecosystem origin) and **Dazhongsi · Switch Yard** (south industrial interchange); the two wings are the **Zhongguancun Technology Services Wing** (capital and IP) and the **Xiaoyue River Scenario Enablement Wing** (scenarios and an energetic city) [depth:key_area_names][depth:wing_names]. The logo direction is a **"herringbone rail + cursor"**: two rails fork at the base and converge at the apex, readable at once as the character "人" (person), a network fork and a cursor insertion point, with sleepers laid out in binary 0/1 [depth:logo_direction]. Naming and logo are conceptual directions only and involve no licensed fonts or trademarks.

### Three Positionings, Five Functions, and the Three-Zones-and-Two-Wings Feedback Loop

**The three positionings** each have a concrete anchor: the Centennial Jing-Zhang Cultural Belt is carried by the heritage track and narrative of the Jing-Zhang Railway Heritage Park Intelligence-Rail spine; the Urban AI Life Experience Belt is carried by the Xiaoyue River Scenario branch and its wayside stations, making AI a perceivable part of daily life; the AI-Integrated Innovation Belt is carried by the three cores, fusing research, acceleration and industry into a rail that grows [standard:PROJECT-AGENT-OPEN-CALL-TASKBOOK].

**The five functions** map one-to-one onto space (to be refined once official regulatory data arrive): the AI full-stack independent innovation system lands at Zhongzhi · Marshaling Yard; the world-class AI innovation ecosystem lands at Origin · Departure Yard; the new AI-plus-scenario enablement paradigm lands at the Xiaoyue River Scenario branch; the intelligent energetic AI city lands at Dazhongsi Switch Yard and the overall urban space; and global discourse on AI governance is jointly supported by Zhongzhiyuan and the global return loop.

**Three-Zones-and-Two-Wings coordination loop:** Origin Community (research/ecosystem) → Zhongzhiyuan (acceleration/full-stack) → Dazhongsi (industry/AI-native) → Xiaoyue River wing (scenario testing and vitality feedback) → Zhongguancun Technology Services wing (global factors, Zhongguancun IP and capital return) → back into Origin research, forming a closed loop of "research—aggregate—industry—scenario—capital" [source:DATA-SRC-AGENT-TASKBOOK-20260518]. Regionally, northward it links via the Jing-Zhang HSR/rail corridor to the compute and large-science facilities of the Future Sci-Tech City and Huairou Science City; southeastward it coordinates with the E-Town (Yizhuang) for industrialization verification; and northwestward it radiates across the Beijing–Tianjin–Hebei region via the Jing-Zhang HSR [source:DATA-SRC-OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT-20260509].

### World-Class AI Innovation Ecosystem: Five-Stage Full-Stack Chain and Global Cases

A five-stage full-stack chain is organized along the Intelligence-Rail's longitudinal axis — **basic research → R&D → incubation and acceleration → industrialization → capital services** — so that innovation actors "graduate without leaving the belt" along the corridor [depth:feedback_loop_zh]. Basic research anchors the Origin Community, relying on the open sharing of national key laboratories in the Xueyuan Lu university cluster and forming a "northward intelligence source" synergy with the large-science facilities of the Future Sci-Tech City and Huairou Science City; R&D stations large-model, embodied-intelligence and multi-agent research buildings at Origin and Zhongzhiyuan; incubation and acceleration uses Zhongzhiyuan as the "marshaling yard" with a pilot base and testing-and-validation field, using compute vouchers to let early teams draw compute on demand; industrialization anchors Dazhongsi and links the Xiaoyue River wing to make trustworthy scenarios such as healthcare and education the demand-side engine; and capital services are carried by the Zhongguancun Technology Services wing, operating Zhongguancun IP and providing laddered capital and global-factor allocation [source:DATA-SRC-AGENT-TASKBOOK-20260518].

**Eight factors** are implemented as mechanisms: land (reserved "blank + flexible development" plots at Zhongzhiyuan/Dazhongsi, indicators pending official regulatory planning), space (walkable mixed blocks along the axis), industry (the five-stage chain with graded upgrading), capital (seed—R&D—acceleration—industry—M&A laddered capital, with no investment amounts committed), talent (Origin Community as a global talent habitat), compute (shared pool + compute vouchers), data (sandboxes and cross-border channels under the compliant framework of the data-basic-regime pilot zone), and scenarios (the Xiaoyue River branch injecting daily life) [standard:MOHURD-URBAN-DESIGN-MEASURES].

These mechanisms draw on readable summaries of eight global AI-ecosystem cases: **Kendall Square** (university anchor + landlord/resident association, → Origin Community); **Mission Bay** (a single redevelopment vehicle integrating brownfield and housing affordability); **King's Cross** (a railway-heritage corridor turned into an innovation district's public living room, with a national flagship AI institution entering, → Jing-Zhang Heritage Park spine); **one-north** (a mission-driven land agency sequencing the full chain, → Zhongzhiyuan full stack); **Seoul Digital Media City** (infrastructure-first + anchor tenants, → Jing-Zhang brownfield regeneration); **Jätkäsaari** (an urban living lab, → Xiaoyue River scenario wing); **Hangzhou Future Sci-Tech City** (national laboratories + leading-enterprise anchors, HSR as innovation infrastructure, → northward intelligence source); **Shenzhen Nanshan** (university town + HQ + national laboratory vertically integrated, → Haidian university belt) [source:EXT-GLOBAL-001][source:EXT-GLOBAL-004][source:EXT-GLOBAL-008]. The transferable mechanisms of each case are itemized in the case-study table below; all are conceptual references and constitute no commitment to any enterprise or government.

## Overall Design Area: Urban Renewal and Regulatory-Plan-Level Urban Design

### Spatial Structure and Function Layout

The Overall Design Area is organized as **"One Belt · Three Cores · Two Wings — Intelligence-Rail Weave"**: the **belt** is the Jing-Zhang Railway Heritage Park Intelligence-Rail green corridor (a composite spine of heritage track, data flow and a living belt), running north from Zhongzhiyuan at the 5th Ring Road, through the Wudaokou origin, south to Dazhongsi/Jimen Bridge; the **three cores** are Zhongzhi · Marshaling Yard, Origin · Departure Yard and Dazhongsi · Switch Yard; the **two wings** are the Zhongguancun Technology Services wing and the Xiaoyue River Scenario branch; plus multiple nodes (wayside AI stations and scenario nodes) [depth:spatial_structure]. Overlaid on the spine are a longitudinal main loop (research—acceleration—industry), a transversal empowerment loop (the two wings injecting capital/IP/scenario/vitality) and a global return loop (global factors returning to the origin via Zhongguancun).

The conceptual land-use layout (provisional, not an official land-category baseline) forms functional zones within the Overall Design Area: approx. 51.6% urban residential, 13.7% research, 11.6% park green, 8.0% education, 6.3% commerce and services, 4.4% urban community-service facilities, 2.9% culture and 1.5% plazas, exactly tiled with shared edges [metric:land_use_tiling_exact], with the residential, research and park-green shares recalculated by zone [metric:land_use_share_0701][metric:land_use_share_0802]. These proportions support the "research—aggregate—industry—scenario—capital" logic: research and education carry the head and tail of the full-stack chain, green and plazas support innovation exchange and talent living, commerce and community services serve daily life, and culture carries the three-layer narrative of Jing-Zhang/Zhongguancun/AI [standard:MNR-LAND-USE-CLASSIFICATION-GUIDE]. These land-use shares are conceptual recommendations; official regulatory land-use categories and shares are pending confirmation.

### Urban Renewal Overall Framework and Pending Regulatory Conditions

Urban renewal follows a retain–renovate–demolish–build classification as a conceptual guide rather than a conclusion: prioritize retaining buildings and spaces of historical, cultural and community value (the Jing-Zhang old roadbed, the Qinghuayuan Station site memory node, universities and existing communities) [source:SRC-WIKI-JZ]; renovate low-efficiency industry and old blocks into elastic "AI-ready" space; and reserve "blank + flexible development" plots at Zhongzhiyuan and Dazhongsi to adapt to rapidly iterating industry. **Important: official Floor Area Ratio, Building Height, Building Coverage Ratio, building control lines, green ratio, set-backs, road red lines, plot ownership and heritage-control lines have not been obtained and are all marked pending official data (to be confirmed); this proposal gives no reviewed values** [standard:MOHURD-CONTROL-DETAILED-PLANNING][metric:far_height_density_controls]. Total building scale, development intensity and the retirement/renovation/retention ratios must all be recomputed by professional teams once regulatory planning and current-condition baselines are completed.

The renewal objects and function shares, public space, transport organization, municipal capacity and character control reinforce one another: a conceptual green ratio of approx. 13% [metric:green_space_ratio] supports talent living and innovation exchange; approx. 182.7k m² of public space [metric:public_space_area] serves as the living room of the three cores; an approx. 24.11 km slow-mode corridor on the Intelligence-Rail spine [metric:corridor_length_m] organizes innovation exchange and transport; and character control, on a keynote of "the firmness of a rail, the lightness of a cursor," uses an overall urban-design guideline to anchor public space, urban temperament and building-control direction [standard:MOHURD-URBAN-DESIGN-MEASURES].

### Zhongzhiyuan AI Independent Innovation Acceleration Area (Zhongzhi · Marshaling Yard, 192.1 ha)

Positioned as the "marshaling/test-run" engine depot of AI full-stack independent innovation [depth:key_area_names]. Spatial structure: the Marshaling·Engine-Segment Park serves as the northern green core, organizing full-stack innovation blocks, a pilot base, a testing-and-validation field and a shared-compute machine hall. Building renewal (conceptual): elastic "AI-ready" industry land adapts to rapid iteration, with specific development intensity pending regulatory planning. Transport/walkability: carries the northern segment of the Intelligence-Rail spine and external links to the 5th Ring Road, with a test-run loop slow-mode system. Public space: the test-run loop, a marshaling rest platform and an engine-inspection corner [depth:park_segment_engine]. AI scenarios: SC-12 Zhongzhiyuan full-stack integration testing field (industry testing and validation) and the start of the SC-04 autonomous-shuttle test segment. Implementation risk: dependence on the far-northern location and compute facilities, and the land-elasticity demand under rapid industrial iteration, both pending assessment.

### Beijing AI Origin Community (Origin · Departure Yard, 104.3 ha)

Positioned as the "departure yard" and 0-km origin of the world-class AI innovation ecosystem, carrying the innovation memory of the Xueyuan Lu university cluster and Wudaokou as the "center of the universe" [source:EXT-HD-024][source:EXT-HD-025]. Spatial structure: the Origin·Departure-Segment Park serves as the ceremonial green core, organizing basic research, incubators, maker spaces and a global talent community. Building renewal (conceptual): universities and existing communities are primarily retained and renovated, with specific retire/renovate/retain decisions pending the current-condition baseline. Transport/walkability: integrated interchange with Line 13 at Wudaokou and Line 15 at Qinghua East Rd West [source:SRC-WIKI-M13][source:SRC-WIKI-M15], with an Origin plaza and a Qinghua East Rd West interchange confluence. Public space: the Origin plaza, the 0-km mile post and an ecological starting platform. AI scenarios: SC-01 smart health, SC-02 adaptive learning pod, SC-08 intelligent legal pod, and SC-14 Origin pilgrimage guide. Implementation risk: complex ownership across the university campus and existing communities, and the transit-interchange scale, both pending assessment.

### Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster (Dazhongsi · Switch Yard, 72.0 ha)

Positioned as the industry port where AI "switches" from R&D into AI-native new business forms and urban consumption [source:DATA-SRC-OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT-20260509]. Spatial structure: the Switch·Industry-Segment Park serves as the southern public core, organizing an AI-native consumer district, a switch market, an AI-native business lounge and a digital-twin base node. Building renewal (conceptual): no enterprise buildings or owned-space renovation is involved; it relies mainly on flexible public space and elastic business formats. Transport/walkability: an "interchange court" links the Dazhongsi stations of Lines 13/12 with business space [source:SRC-WIKI-M13][source:SRC-WIKI-M12], with an industry-port interchange court. Public space: the switch market, an AI-native business lounge and a waterfront vitality node. AI scenarios: SC-03 AI-native consumption, SC-10 robot last-mile delivery validation, and SC-11 an unmanned-retail and smart-patrol test district (industry test). Implementation risk: integration of the existing commercial and transit-hub interface, and the delimitation of test time-slots and street segments, both awaiting professional deepening.

### Testing-and-Validation Scenarios and Privacy/Human-Review Boundaries

The four testing-and-validation scenarios use the belt as a "real-city AI testing ground" rather than a showroom: SC-04 autonomous shuttle (safety operator aboard, bounded test segment), SC-10 robot last-mile delivery (bounded time-slots and street segments), SC-11 the unmanned-retail and smart-patrol test district, and SC-12 the Zhongzhiyuan full-stack integration testbed. These scenarios follow one principle: scope and time-slots are clearly delimited, data is desensitized, operations and safety are subject to laws, regulations and approval, and they are never described as approved operations.

Hard privacy and human-review boundaries: data minimization, anonymized desensitization, explicit consent and deletion rights, no indiscriminate continuous surveillance, human-expert review checkpoints for high-stakes scenarios, and parallel cash and human-window channels retained for the elderly and those without smart terminals [standard:ELDERLY-SMART-TECH-PLAN-2020-45]. Any health, legal, government, financial or eldercare high-stakes recommendation has AI serve only as an aid prominently labeled "not a final conclusion," with final judgment made by humans and professional teams [source:DATA-SRC-AGENT-TASKBOOK-20260518].

## Land Use, Building Scale, and Retain-Renovate-Demolish Strategy

## Blue-Green Network, Public Space, and Urban Character

### The Jing-Zhang Railway Heritage Park Vitality Belt

The Jing-Zhang Railway Heritage Park is the Intelligence-Rail spine, running from Zhongzhiyuan at the 5th Ring Road, through the Wudaokou origin to Dazhongsi, unbroken north-south and threading the three cores [source:DATA-SRC-OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT-20260509]. Conceptually it is sequenced into three segments with distinct motifs: the northern "Marshaling·Engine Segment" takes test-running and marshaling as its imagery (test-run loop, marshaling rest platform); the central "Origin·Departure Segment" takes departure and ecology (Origin plaza, 0-km mile post); and the southern "Switch·Industry Segment" takes switching and interchange (AI-native market, business lounge) [depth:park_segment_engine][depth:park_segment_origin][depth:park_segment_switch]. The green layer includes the Jing-Zhang Railway Heritage Park green corridor, the Xiaoyue greenway and pocket parks [data:geometry/green_space.geojson#GS-PARK-SPINE][data:geometry/green_space.geojson#GS-XIAOYUE-GREENWAY]. The Xiaoyue River courses along the Yuan-dynasty capital rampart line, joining the Yuan-Dadu city-wall ruins into a historic blue-green corridor [source:EXT-HD-052] that forms the waterfront thread of the "Urban AI Life Experience Belt"; shoreline, greenway width and open space follow the official blue-line/green-line planning.

### AI Pilgrimage Landmarks (5)

This proposal proposes five conceptual landmarks, three of which are core selections: the **Developer Walk** (a linear walkway along the old rail alignment encoding inspection/switch/signal imagery as a developer's train of thought) [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#PS-LM-DEVELOPER-WALK]; the **Open-Source Outcomes Gallery** (a gallery in "train-carriage" units emphasizing reproducibility and runnability) [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#PS-LM-OPEN-SOURCE-GALLERY]; the **Agent Contribution Honor Wall** (an honor display using station seals/mile posts as its motif, recording contributions and distinguishing the four states of submitted/reviewed/accepted/landed) [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#PS-LM-AGENT-HONOR-WALL]; the **Origin · Zero-Mile Post** (a 0-km virtual mile post echoing "One Herringbone, Two Openings, Infinite Loops") [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#PS-LM-ORIGIN-ZERO-MILE]; and the **Herringbone-Switch Fork** (commemorating the forking spirit of the 1909 herringbone alignment, turned into an AI innovation loop) [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#PS-LM-HERRINGBONE-SWITCH]. All five landmarks are expressed in the restrained materials of rail-ink, signal-red and intelligence-rail cyan with equal-width digital encoding, and their content is based on real history, real mile posts and authorized contributor information — no fabricated names, no entertainment, no influencer-style or vulgar presentation [standard:MOHURD-URBAN-DESIGN-MEASURES]. Landmarks are conceptual siting only; final siting awaits verification by professional teams and heritage authorities.

### The Centennial Trilogy Cultural Narrative and Cultural Tour Routes

This proposal fuses the century-old Jing-Zhang culture, Zhongguancun culture and the new AI culture into a single narrative spine — **"The Centennial Trilogy: One Herringbone · Three Departures"** (international tagline: One Track · Three Departures): **Part One · The Road of Self-Reliance (1909)**, where the Jing-Zhang Railway's "herringbone" alignment opened the era of China's self-built engineering, carried spatially by the Heritage Park rail-paving section and the Qinghuayuan Station memory node [source:SRC-WIKI-JZ]; **Part Two · The Road of Innovation (Zhongguancun entrepreneurship)**, from an "electronics street" to "China's Silicon Valley," carried by the Xueyuan Lu university cluster and the Zhongguancun Technology Services wing; and **Part Three · The Road of Intelligence (AI-age open co-creation)**, from "self-designed" to "self-intelligent," carried by the three cores and the AI pilgrimage landmarks. The three parts share a single track, with expression carriers including rail/switch paving, binary ties, station-seal nodes, the 0-km mile post, code poetry and rail inscriptions (the cultural identity system is homologous with but layered over the belt-wide logo, never conflated with it) [depth:signage_direction].
