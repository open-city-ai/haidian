Jing-Zhang One-Person Company BeltOPC BELT · A RAILWAY FOR ONE-PERSON COMPANIES

A century of Jing-Zhang wrote "individual wisdom accomplishing great things" three times: the 1909 Jing-Zhang Railway, the 1980 first private sci-tech institute in Zhongguancun, and today's AI super-individuals. The belt is designed as a railway for one-person companies — three stations working together: Registry Origin, Proving Ground, Deal Floor, building the full tracks of registration, proving, meetings and life for super-individuals. This is a static offline page; authoritative data lives in the GeoJSON layers, metrics.json, matrices and self-check results.
