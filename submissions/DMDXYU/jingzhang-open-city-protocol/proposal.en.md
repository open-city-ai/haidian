# Jingzhang Open City Protocol: From Railway Heritage to a Verifiable AI Public Interface

## Design Basis and Evidence Register

This formal proposal uses the official call, the maintained site package, the agent taskbook, the allowed design space, the source registry, and the structured metrics and geometry supplied with this package. The proposal treats provisional boundaries as design constraints only. They are not official redlines, approval documents, or precise area evidence. All claims remain subject to human planning, transport, accessibility, heritage, municipal, legal, and public-participation review.

The evidence chain is recorded in `sources.json`, `assumptions.json`, `metrics.json`, `compliance_matrix.json`, `standard_matrix.json`, and `design_depth_matrix.json`. The local figures, GeoJSON layers, offline HTML, and drawings are explanatory and auditable artifacts; they do not replace official controls.

## Three-Level Scope Framework

The study scope covers approximately 43.6 square kilometres, the overall design scope approximately 11.4 square kilometres, and the three key areas approximately 368.4 hectares in total. The three levels connect an AI innovation ecosystem strategy, an overall urban renewal framework, and detailed spatial actions for Zhongzhiyuan, Beijing AI Origin Community, and Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster.

The central concept is an open city protocol: the Jingzhang heritage park becomes a public interface, while compute, data, scenarios, and civic feedback become modular services with an open evidence chain and a human final review. Replace the provisional polygons with official geometry before any formal redline, precise area, or statutory control is asserted.

## Regional AI Ecosystem and Future City Research

The regional strategy links universities, research institutes, enterprises, developers, public services, and international exchange through a sequence of research, open collaboration, enterprise conversion, public experience, and communication. The “one belt, three anchors, multiple scenarios, and a blue-green slow-mobility loop” is a design framework, not an additional statutory boundary.

The proposal recommends a protocol-based identity system for the centennial railway heritage, urban AI life, and integrated innovation belt. Any logo, font, image, company mark, or event identity must be cleared before publication. Global cases and activity models are reference directions for further professional study, not government commitments.

## Overall Urban Renewal and Planning-Depth Design

The overall design combines land use, building footprints, roads, green space, public space, constraints, and phasing. It prioritizes rail integration, road micro-circulation, accessible walking and cycling, public service nodes, low-carbon infrastructure, edge computing prototypes, and a continuous heritage-park public realm.

Where current control plans, ownership, infrastructure, fire safety, flood control, or heritage conditions are unavailable, the proposal records a pending condition rather than inventing a statutory number. Conceptual intensity and building actions are design suggestions only.

## Detailed Design of Three Key Areas

Zhongzhiyuan is proposed as a garden-like full-stack innovation accelerator with a river-facing low-carbon innovation edge, testing spaces, standards workshops, and safety governance displays. Beijing AI Origin Community is a near-campus conversion and talent neighborhood linking campus, parks, enterprise space, open-source publishing, housing, and daily services. Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster is an urban smart-economy and international exchange district organized around station integration, four-quadrant walking connections, enterprise services, intelligent terminals, and data-oriented public interfaces.

Each area requires a later check of official polygon, ownership, transport, municipal, heritage, and implementation conditions. The current key-area geometry is provisional.

## AI Ecosystem, User Profiles, and AI+ Scenarios

The proposal addresses open-source developers, start-up teams, enterprise visitors, residents, and university users. Ten scenario cards cover an open-source publishing hall, safety governance sandbox, edge-compute station, AI walking navigation, international pitch lounge, low-carbon river corridor, near-campus conversion street, data-element lounge, AI life-service street, and a global AI activity route.

Every scenario requires data minimization, authorization, explainability, privacy protection, an operational owner, and human review. AI may assist with walking gaps, public-space maintenance, service navigation, and event-risk review; it cannot replace approval, professional advice, or public deliberation.

## Land Use, Building Scale, and Retain-Renovate-Demolish Strategy

The land-use and building layers express a concept proposal for spatial structure and renewal. Retention, renovation, demolition, and new construction remain subject to current surveys, ownership, heritage controls, engineering conditions, and statutory planning. Unknown controls remain unknown in the structured metrics rather than being converted into false precision.

## Mobility, Rail, Municipal Services, and Public Facilities

The mobility framework connects rail stations, the heritage park, surrounding roads, walking and cycling routes, accessibility improvements, bicycle parking, low-speed autonomous services, and public-service nodes. Municipal and energy strategies are implementation directions that require infrastructure, safety, flood, fire, and operations review.

## Blue-Green Network, Public Space, and Urban Character

The heritage park, Qinghe and Xiaoyuehe corridors, campus edges, enterprise districts, and neighborhoods form a connected blue-green public-space system. Three proposed AI pilgrimage and recognition nodes are the Open Source Contribution Wall, the Safety and Standards Commons, and the Jingzhang AI Public Interface. They are conceptual public-space and communication devices, not approved construction projects.

## Renewal Projects, Policy, and Phasing

The initial project list includes walking-gap repair, the Zhongzhiyuan river innovation edge, the Origin Community conversion street, Dazhongsi station walking links, AI public-service and edge-compute nodes, and a global AI activity route. Early actions can use light public-realm works, open workshops, and service pilots; medium and long-term actions depend on official controls, ownership, funding, and infrastructure.
