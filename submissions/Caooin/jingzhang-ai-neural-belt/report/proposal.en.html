Project Overview

**Proposal Title:** Jing-Zhang AI Neural Symbiosis Belt (京张智脉·共生带)

**Author:** Trae AI Urban Design Agent

**Date:** August 11, 2026

This is a conceptual urban design proposal responding to the Haidian Centennial Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt open call. All spatial recommendations are conceptual suggestions, not substitutes for statutory planning, and do not constitute government approval.

**Three Positionings:**

  • Centennial Jing-Zhang Cultural Belt: Heritage of railway history, dialogue between industrial heritage and AI civilization
  • Urban AI Life Experience Belt: AI technology integrated into daily urban space, perceivable, participatory, experiential
  • AI Integration Innovation Belt: Building a full-stack independent innovation ecosystem, connecting global AI innovation networks

---

Task 1: Overall Concept and Brand Identity

**Name System:**

  • Primary Name: Jing-Zhang AI Neural Symbiosis Belt (JZ-NSB)
  • Chinese: 京张智脉·共生带
  • The name merges "Jing-Zhang" (historic railway), "Neural" (AI neural networks as urban veins), and "Symbiosis" (mutual thriving of history/future, tech/human, human/AI, industry/city)

**Visual Identity: "Neural Railway · Spacetime Fold"**

  • Logo concept: Twin railway tracks merging into neural synapses, symbolizing the shift from industrial age physical connection to intelligent age wisdom connection
  • Double helix: One strand representing Jing-Zhang history, one representing AI future, intertwined
  • Color system: Neural Blue (#0066FF), Heritage Red (#CC3333), Growth Green (#33CC66)

**Spatial Structure: One Belt, Three Cores, Two Wings, Neural Loop Model**

  • **One Belt**: 8km Jing-Zhang Neural Park public spine along the heritage corridor
  • **Three Cores**: Zhongzhiyuan Crowd Intelligence Valley (full-stack AI R&D), AI Origin Commune (open source community), Intelligence Harbor @Dazhongsi (smart industry)
  • **Two Wings**: Zhongguancun Service Wing (capital, IP, professional services), Xiaoyuehe Scenario Wing (AI+life testbed)
  • **Four Neural Loops**: Ideation, Commercialization, Experience, Governance circuits

Task 4: AI Public Space and Pilgrimage Landmarks

**AI Public Space Principle:** "AI is the invisible gardener, not the surveillance camera." Five tiers of AI involvement, including a mandatory **No-AI Quiet Zone** with no cameras, no sensors, no devices - completely offline.

**Smart Furniture:** Solar-powered benches (no cameras/screens), interactive light floor tiles (no data storage), privacy-preserving smart lighting, transparent compute cabins whose waste heat heats public spaces in winter.

**Three AI Pilgrimage Landmarks:** 1. **Jing-Zhang Time Gate** @ Qinghuayuan Station: A 19.09m double-helix sculpture connecting 1909 (railway opening), 2026 (today), and 2049 (future), with a semi-underground "Time Capsule" chamber where visitors can write letters to the future. 2. **Open Source Pantheon** @ AI Origin Commune: A 20m cylindrical hall with an LED dome where each star represents an open source AI project. The "Eternal Flame of Open Source" flickers with every real code merge globally. A Wall of Contributors honors ordinary developers, not just famous names. 3. **World Intelligence Ethics Pillar** @ Zhongzhiyuan: A 9.9m white marble pillar inscribed with global AI ethics principles, updated annually through a multi-stakeholder "Global AI Ethics Parliament." It is a living monument, with an open API for any AI system to query ethical guidelines.

Seven categories of AI-native new business types are defined, with an explicit list of prohibited business types (deepfake shops, unauthorized data trafficking, algorithmic discrimination consulting, AI fortune telling, pure-AI medical/legal services without human oversight).

Task 5: Cultural Narrative Integration

**Three Cultural Threads:** 1. Jing-Zhang Railway Culture (1909-present): Self-reliance, craftsmanship, the spirit of Zhan Tianyou 2. Zhongguancun Culture (1980-present): "First mover" courage, learning by doing, tech entrepreneurship 3. AI New Culture (1956-present, 2022-explosion): Open source, knowledge sharing, global co-creation, human-AI symbiosis

**Narrative Arc: "Three Waves of Innovation, One Jing-Zhang Thread"** > In 1909, the Jing-Zhang Railway opened, breaking the claim that "China cannot build railways." In the 1980s, Zhongguancun's first private tech company launched China's information age. In 2026, large AI models open the third wave - building not physical rails, but neural connections between people, knowledge, AI, and the future.

Six-node walking narrative sequence from north to south: Origin (1909 departure) → Creation (2026 making) → Home (daily symbiosis) → Intersection (diversity) → Dialogue (ancient-future conversation) → Voyage (2049 departure)

Cultural elements include: the "Railway Track × Synapse" super-symbol, "Three Generations of Innovators" public sculpture series, four-layer soundscape design, and a creative "JZ AI Dialect" merging railway and AI terminology.

Task 6: Global Event System and Long-Term Operations

**Jing-Zhang AI Week - Annual Global Gathering**

  • Modeled after SXSW, held the first week of September annually for 7 days
  • Seven programming tracks: Opening Night, Science Summit, Industry Expo, Global Open Source Hackathon, Governance & Ethics Forum, Public Open Days, Closing Future Night
  • Seven AI-native features: AI planning assistant, 100+ language interpretation, personalized schedules (opt-in only), zero-waste goal, public "What AI Does Not Do" transparency list

**Three-tier Community Architecture:**

  • Core: JZ Open Source Community Council (multi-stakeholder governance)
  • Active: Five-level Contributor tier system (Visitor → Passenger → Mechanic → Driver → Trackman)
  • Periphery: Global "Friends of JZ" network in AI innovation cities worldwide

"Never-ending AI Week" daily event calendar: daily lunch talks, weekly paper presentations, monthly hackathons, quarterly governance roundtables.

**Sustainable Business Model (5-year target):**

  • 30% space rents (30% below market), 20% event revenue, 15% memberships, 20% services, 10% government public service purchases, 5% donations
  • 50% of profits reinvested into open source community and public good
  • 10% of compute capacity permanently allocated to public good and academic research

**Four-Phase Implementation Roadmap (2026-2049):** 1. Launch (2026-2027): Framework, pilots, first AI Week, 50 companies 2. Growth (2028-2030): Three cores built out, 500 companies, top-3 global AI event 3. Maturity (2031-2035): Global leadership, 2000 companies, 20 listed companies 4. Vision (2036-2049): Continued evolution toward the PRC centennial
