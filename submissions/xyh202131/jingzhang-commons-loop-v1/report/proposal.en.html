Jing-Zhang Commons Loop — From Railway Heritage to a Verifiable Everyday Belt

The proposal treats the AI Innovation Belt as a loop that can return to daily life. People bring a problem from the railway memory corridor or a public space; researchers, companies, residents, and operators make a small prototype; the prototype is explained in plain language, tested with a human fallback, minimized for data, and publicly corrected before it can enter another discussion. The algorithm is not the new landmark, and temporary data is not presented as a statutory fact. All boundaries, building objects, and phases are conceptual and subject to professional review.

Coordinated Research Area: Industry and Future City Research

The coordinated structure is one co-creation spine, two service wings, three translation courtyards, and four public gates. The spine follows railway memory and slow mobility, connecting issue collection, explanation, testing, and return. The Zhongguancun technology-service wing supports intellectual property, finance, international cooperation, and talent services. The Xiaoyue River scenario wing supports community, health, education, blue-green, and care. The three courtyards are a verification courtyard at Zhongzhi Park, a translation courtyard at the Beijing AI Origin Community, and an adoption courtyard at Dazhongsi. Industry research is not a target of simply attracting more companies. Transport, Rail, Municipal Infrastructure, and Public Services

Transport is organized as railway memory as line, readable slow mobility as surface, and interchange services as gates. ROAD-001 is the conceptual commons spine; three cross links connect the key areas, communities, and service wings. It is a relationship line, not a road redline or construction drawing Depth Spatial data Metric Metric.

Each public gate provides three ways to arrive: plain text, graphic/color-band guidance, and human enquiry. Transit and rail interchange, crossings, bicycle parking, loading, emergency access, and fire routes need transport, municipal, and safety review.
