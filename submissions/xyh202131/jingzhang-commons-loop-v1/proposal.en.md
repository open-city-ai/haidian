# Jing-Zhang Commons Loop — From Railway Heritage to a Verifiable Everyday Belt

The proposal treats the AI Innovation Belt as a loop that can return to daily life. People bring a problem from the railway memory corridor or a public space; researchers, companies, residents, and operators make a small prototype; the prototype is explained in plain language, tested with a human fallback, minimized for data, and publicly corrected before it can enter another discussion. The algorithm is not the new landmark, and temporary data is not presented as a statutory fact. All boundaries, building objects, and phases are conceptual and subject to professional review.

## Three-Level Scope Framework

The three levels use different evidence strengths. The coordinated research area is approximately 43.6 million square metres and frames industry, future-city research, and cross-area collaboration. The overall design area is approximately 11.4 million square metres and frames renewal, transport, blue-green, and public-service relationships. The three key areas together are approximately 3.684 million square metres and frame conceptual spatial components. The submitted boundary is maintained as provisional geometry: it can be recomputed, but it is not a statutory boundary [depth:three_level_scope_framework] [data:geometry/site_boundary.geojson#SITE-BOUNDARY-PROVISIONAL].

Each level follows the same loop: problem, prototype, review, and keep/modify/pause/return. The coordinated level asks who collaborates; the overall level asks which public skeleton is made legible; the key-area level asks how one place can be tried. The levels transfer relationships, not unverified rights or engineering conclusions. Once official polygons are supplied, geometry, metrics, drawings, and visualization must be recomputed together [source:BOUNDARY-SOURCE] [source:KEY-AREA-SOURCE].

## Coordinated Research Area: Industry and Future City Research

The coordinated structure is one co-creation spine, two service wings, three translation courtyards, and four public gates. The spine follows railway memory and slow mobility, connecting issue collection, explanation, testing, and return. The Zhongguancun technology-service wing supports intellectual property, finance, international cooperation, and talent services. The Xiaoyue River scenario wing supports community, health, education, blue-green, and care. The three courtyards are a verification courtyard at Zhongzhi Park, a translation courtyard at the Beijing AI Origin Community, and an adoption courtyard at Dazhongsi. The four gates connect transit, campus/community, enterprise/open space, and railway memory to daily routines.

Industry research is not a target of simply attracting more companies. It connects computing, model safety, intellectual property, public service, education, and care through a problem-and-feedback chain. Before a team enters a public test, it must state the service recipient, minimum data, human takeover, stop condition, and review log; without these, the idea remains an indoor workshop. This responds to the requirement to coordinate plan, section, public space, and urban character [depth:overall_spatial_structure] [standard:MOHURD-URBAN-DESIGN-MEASURES].

Six public method references were progressively read: one-north for work-live-play-learn integration, STATION F for one-roof support, International House Helsinki for one-stop newcomer services, MIT Kendall Square for mixed research, housing, retail, and open space, Barcelona 22@ for district renewal, and MIT InnovationHQ for interdisciplinary translation. They are method references only; they do not provide Beijing boundaries, intensities, transport, or policy evidence [source:CASE-ONE-NORTH] [source:CASE-STATION-F] [source:CASE-HELSINKI-IHH] [source:CASE-KENDALL-SQUARE] [source:CASE-BARCELONA-22AT] [source:CASE-MIT-IMPACT].

The overall level uses a visible public skeleton instead of assumed development quantities. Land use, buildings, transport, green space, public space, and phasing are all written as replaceable GeoJSON layers. Statutory intensity stays unknown. Building coverage, floor-area ratio, height, setbacks, parking, and road sections are not filled with invented values. The three key areas are three translation actions rather than three closed campuses. Zhongzhi Park translates research into model-safety, low-carbon, and urban-problem experiments. The Beijing AI Origin Community translates campus, neighbourhood, and care needs into learning and help interfaces. Dazhongsi translates innovation services into arrival, adoption, feedback, and redistribution. The translation courtyard has a shared classroom, neighbourhood help desk, readable child and older-adult interfaces, and a closable voice/text entry. The adoption courtyard has a transit forecourt, enterprise service window, and commons return wall. All three use the same public label sequence: what is the problem, what data is used, who can stop it, and how to respond. Node count, area, and connection length describe design objects, not built objects [metric:key_area_count] [metric:key_area_total_area_sqm] [metric:public_space_node_count] [metric:loop_spine_length_m].

## AI Innovation Ecosystem, Personas, and AI+ Scenarios

The ecosystem does not turn everybody into a generic “user.” Five design personas are an open-source student/developer, a startup team needing verification and service continuity, a neighbour caring for children or an older adult, an international visitor or creative worker, and a field operator responsible for maintenance and complaints. These are design instruments, not personal records [metric:persona_count] [depth:overall_spatial_structure].

The package contains twelve cards: a slow-mobility audit; an accessible route assistant; a human-fallback health navigator; an Origin Community learning companion; an enterprise-service copilot; a model-safety co-test room; a low-speed delivery sandbox; a bilingual railway-memory guide; a public-space maintenance signal; a community energy and blue-green board; an open-source contribution desk; and a global-event translation route. Four cards are explicitly industry-validation cards: the mobility audit, enterprise-service copilot, model-safety co-test, and low-speed delivery sandbox.

Every card records the user, place, minimum data, privacy boundary, human fallback, stop button, and measurement. The cards can therefore be revised or removed without making the public service dependent on AI. Their counts are coverage checks, not real pilots or performance claims [metric:scenario_card_count] [metric:industry_validation_scenario_count] [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK].

## Land Use, Building Scale, and Retain-Renovate-Demolish Strategy

The land-use structure uses five conceptual categories: railway/transport-related 05, research and education 0802, public administration and services 0804, park green space 1401, and commercial services 0702. `land_use.geojson` uses adjacent latitude bands to keep the conceptual topology readable and states its provisional status. It describes a public skeleton and mixed-function relationship, not land transfer or statutory use [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-01] [standard:MNR-LAND-USE-CLASSIFICATION-GUIDE] [depth:land_use_layout].

Retain, renovate, and demolish follow a cautious order. Existing objects that can host public services and memory interpretation are retained for reversible reuse. Objects needing access, daylight, energy, or safety improvements enter a renovate list. Demolition or new construction is only a later professional review topic after ownership, survey, structure, and public impact are verified. The fifteen building objects are conceptual; the footprint area is geometry, not statutory coverage [depth:retain_renovate_demolish] [data:geometry/buildings.geojson#BLDG-01] [metric:building_footprint_count] [metric:building_footprint_area_sqm].

Character rules are written as testable language: a legible small-scale frontage along the spine, public doorways at nodes without a single oversized visual centre, and separate review of roofscape, ground-floor edges, night lighting, and signs. Height, setbacks, floor area, and fire separation remain unknown until official survey, character, and regulatory data arrive.

## Transport, Rail, Municipal Infrastructure, and Public Services

Transport is organized as railway memory as line, readable slow mobility as surface, and interchange services as gates. `ROAD-001` is the conceptual commons spine; three cross links connect the key areas, communities, and service wings. It is a relationship line, not a road redline or construction drawing [depth:traffic_rail_slow_parking] [data:geometry/roads.geojson#ROAD-001] [metric:road_feature_count] [metric:road_length_m].

Each public gate provides three ways to arrive: plain text, graphic/color-band guidance, and human enquiry. Transit and rail interchange, crossings, bicycle parking, loading, emergency access, and fire routes need transport, municipal, and safety review. Parking is not given a capacity promise; conflicts and time periods are recorded first, then shared parking or shuttle options can be studied.

Municipal services follow “service first, intelligence second.” Water, toilets, seating, shade, accessibility, lighting, network, and first-aid information must have a conventional usable path. AI may assist navigation, translation, maintenance, and service routing, but it is not the only entry. Underground utilities, drainage, power, fire, communications, and operation rights are missing, so the municipal layer is a review list only [depth:municipal_new_infrastructure] [data:geometry/constraints.geojson#DATA-GAP-MUNICIPAL].

## Blue-Green Network, Public Space, and Urban Character

The blue-green network combines a continuous green band beside the spine, the three translation courtyards, and rest, rain, and shared-activity nodes. `green_space.geojson` represents access and ecological continuity as a conceptual layer; `public_space.geojson` represents nine public interfaces. Both retain provisional descriptions [depth:blue_green_public_space] [data:geometry/green_space.geojson#GREEN-LOOP-001] [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#PUBLIC-NODE-001] [metric:green_space_feature_count].

Public space is designed to be understandable, sit-able, participatory, and escapable. Signage, rest edges, weather alternatives, low-stimulation corners, human service desks, removable displays, and a return wall keep the space usable without an AI account. The area metrics are geometric relationships only; hydrology, ecology, microclimate, accessibility, and maintenance performance require new professional calculations [metric:green_space_area_sqm] [metric:green_ratio] [metric:public_space_area_sqm] [metric:public_space_ratio].

The cultural narrative has four acts: repairing a route, seeking knowledge, making together, and returning value. Three public tribute landmarks are the Commons Experiment Walk, Railway Memory Court, and Commons Return Wall. They are conceptual public-space components, not religious facilities, rights claims, or built-status statements [metric:pilgrimage_landmark_count] [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK].

## Renewal Projects, Implementation Policy, and Phasing

Eight conceptual renewal projects are listed: P1 commons-spine slow-mobility identity; P2 three translation courtyards; P3 accessible and human-companion service pack; P4 cleared railway-memory guide; P5 enterprise-service copilot; P6 model-safety co-test room; P7 blue-green rest and weather alternatives; and P8 global-event and neighbourhood return mechanism. Each records recipient, prerequisite evidence, dependencies, exit condition, and evidence output. It does not provide construction cost or an administrative promise [depth:renewal_project_list] [source:PROJECT-AGENT-OPEN-CALL-TASKBOOK].

The policy sequence is public issue, small trial, independent review, and keep/modify/pause/return. Traditional and smart services are parallel. Data is minimized, aggregated, and deleted on a stated schedule; complaint, accessibility, child, and older-adult services keep human channels. Ownership, transport, safety, fire, heritage, privacy, algorithmic, and public-space questions must be confirmed by the relevant professionals and rights holders.

The three phases are a discussion order rather than a construction schedule. G0 establishes public explanations, human help, cleared content, and rollback logs. G1 connects the three courtyards only when data, privacy, safety, and service capacity are reviewed. G2 uses an annual review to continue, modify, or end the loop. Each phase has a pause and return exit [depth:phasing_implementation] [data:geometry/phasing.geojson#PHASE-01] [metric:phase_count] [metric:phase_area_total_sqm].

## Risk, Copyright, and Compliance

The current site and key-area geometries are provisional-only. The official redline, road and rail controls, water, heritage, public facilities, and intensity conditions are incomplete; areas, locations, and relationships are therefore conceptual. `constraints.geojson` draws data-gap notices only, not planning constraints [depth:risk_missing_data] [data:geometry/constraints.geojson#DATA-GAP-OFFICIAL-BOUNDARY] [source:BOUNDARY-SOURCE].

The agent generated the package text, code, diagrams, layout, and figures locally. Case references record public URLs, method summaries, publishers, dates, and reuse boundaries; no external image, trademark, building drawing, or unlicensed long text is copied. Railway culture is represented abstractly and needs item-by-item clearance before public display. The package license is COMMUNITY-DISPLAY-ONLY and does not authorize treating conceptual layers as engineering, planning, or commercial documents.

The package does not upload personal data, create face or individual-trace systems, or let model output make a medical, enforcement, eligibility, or safety decision alone. Every card keeps a human path, an ordinary digital or paper path, and a stop method. The generative-AI public-service, accessibility, and older-adult references are boundary notes, not local regulatory control [source:DATA-SRC-GENERATIVE-AI-INTERIM-MEASURES] [source:DATA-SRC-BARRIER-FREE-ENVIRONMENT-LAW] [source:DATA-SRC-ELDERLY-SMART-TECH-PLAN-2020-45].

Manifest, geometry, metrics, bilingual displays, PDFs, and HTML must remain one version. Once the self-check is persisted, any edit requires a manifest refresh and another four-gate run. This task stops at local participant preflight and `git push --dry-run`; it does not upload or create a pull request.
