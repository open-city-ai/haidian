Twin-track Jing-Zhang: the front-stage spatial master plan

Read the space before the governance: the continuous civic track is an ordinary public-life base that anyone can enter; the intermittent proof track is a voluntary, announced, bounded, removable overlay and must not be read as continuous construction or an already-built test facility. The three switchyards support co-creation, verification, and publication; the failure siding supports stopping, staffed takeover, detour, and recovery. No account, QR code, or AI is required.
