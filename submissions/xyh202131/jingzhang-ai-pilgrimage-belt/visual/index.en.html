Non-AI-First Public City: Seven Rights and Two Paths to the Same Task

Provide a complete technology-independent service before optional AI: people enter through a physical rights legend and complete the basic task through the primary paper, oral, physical-wayfinding, and staffed route. AI is an optional branch only after plain-language disclosure and separate voluntary consent. Both routes rejoin the same outcome, cost rule, accountable queue, appeal and correction rights, and stop/recovery process. “Permanent” means a design right that cannot be removed from a future service window, not a claim of current staffing or approval.
Seven non-removable rights

No account/QR, complete non-AI path, continuous accessibility intent, staffed handoff, consent withdrawal, appeal/correction, and screen-free quiet. Field accessibility compliance still requires survey, co-testing, and professional review.

Three places, not one template

Accept four groups separately

Stopping does not remove service

A hard failure stops automation and grants AI no continuation privilege. The ordinary route and non-AI task remain, or a safe refusal and accountable handoff are provided. Stay stopped or retire until independent retest and place restoration close.
