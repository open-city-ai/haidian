ZERO-ONE JINGZHANG: Centennial AI Innovation Belt Design on the Seed Belt

Taking the Beijing-Zhangjiakou (Jing-Zhang) Railway Heritage Park as the SEED BELT, and anchoring the innovation progression on three nodes — the AI Origin Community (0), Zhongzhiyuan (1), and Dazhongsi (N) — this proposal builds a three-in-one urban design concept spanning the Centennial Jing-Zhang Cultural Belt, the Urban AI Life Experience Belt, and the AI-Integrated Innovation Belt; its theme is 'AI augments people rather than replacing them.' All spatial claims are conceptual proposals, and provisional boundaries are subject to full recalculation once the official versions are released.

Design Basis and Source List

This proposal takes as its primary basis the Prequalification Announcement for the International Solicitation of Urban Design for the Centennial Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt (百年京张AI创新带城市设计国际方案征集资格预审公告) issued by the Haidian Branch of the Beijing Municipal Commission of Planning and Natural Resources. The announcement defines the three-tier design scope, the three key detailed design areas, the design tasks, and the deliverable depth requirements, and constitutes the object to which every chapter, layer, and indicator in this submission package responds Source Standard. The secondary basis is the agent-facing task book (brief/site-package/agent_taskbook.json), which sets out ten co-creation principles, three positionings, five functions, and six agent tasks (naming and logo, ecosystem case studies, scenario cards, pilgrimage landmarks, cultural narrative, long-term operation) and is the direct source of the "ZERO-ONE" conceptual system Source Standard.

Machine-readable bases come from the site package brief/site-package/ (design_brief, allowed_design_space, enums, ranges, schemas, and the provisional boundary geometry) and from the material-use registry in data/source_registry.json: the registry currently classifies materials into four types — formally usable, background, provisional-only, and pending re-verification — and the agent must not upgrade background or provisional materials into official boundaries, statutory regulatory plans, or implementation commitments Source Source. data/processed/agent_fact_pack.md is the reading-navigation layer, summarizing the three-tier scope, task mapping, material availability, and the missing-data list; factual judgments must still return to the registered raw materials Source.

Judgments on the site's existing conditions are grounded in public facts: the Beijing-Zhangjiakou (Jing-Zhang) Railway Heritage Park runs about 9 km from Beijing North Station in the south to the North Fifth Ring Road in the north, radiating over approximately 14 km². Phase I (Qinghua East Road–Zhichun Road section, 16.8 ha) opened in June 2023 and Phase II is under construction. The park preserves the Qinghuayuan Station building whose name was inscribed by Zhan Tianyou himself (a Beijing municipal heritage-protection unit), forms a "three paths, one greenway" (三道一绿) slow-traffic system, and received the 2024 IFLA (International Federation of Landscape Architects) Africa-Asia-Pacific-Middle East Award and the 2025 ASLA (American Society of Landscape Architects) National Award. The surrounding area hosts the "Eight Universities" of Xueyuan Road (Beihang, USTB, CUMT, CUGB, BJFU, CAU, BLCU, BUPT) plus nearly 20 universities and research institutes including Tsinghua, Peking University, Beijing Jiaotong University, and the Chinese Academy of Sciences system; Metro Line 13 serves Wudaokou, Zhichun Road, and Dazhongsi stations along the corridor Source Depth. The above facts are cited only as public background and are not the sole basis for spatial conclusions.

The boundary status must be declared throughout: both the Overall Design Area and the three key detailed design areas in this package use provisional geometry derived from brief/site-package/geometry/provisional_boundaries.geojson, marked with provisional_constraint and official_boundary=false, and are used only for scheme generation, self-checks, visualization, and design discussion — they cannot serve as official red lines, approval bases, precise area bases, or statutory control conclusions Source Source. Coordinated Research Area: Industry and Future City Study

Core Concept and Naming System

This proposal puts forward "ZERO-ONE JINGZHANG" (零一京张): a hundred years ago, the Beijing-Zhangjiakou Railway accomplished China's 0→1 of independent innovation; a hundred years later, ZERO-ONE on the Seed Belt aims to launch the 0→1 of the intelligent era from here. The naming system maps the binary minimal units 0/1 onto the digital world and "Jingzhang" onto the physical track, forming a twin-line narrative of "from iron rails to computing tracks, from the first rail of the industrial age to the first line of code of the intelligent age" Source Standard. The three districts are named accordingly: the AI Origin Community = 0 (place of origin), Zhongzhiyuan = 1 (place of breakthrough), and Dazhongsi = N (place of explosion); the green axis is named "SEED BELT," where "seed" is a pun on the AI random seed — the starting point of all AI operation and innovation — mirroring the real regeneration in which green grew from the retired railway Depth.

The Logo direction is "track → sprouting": along a rail line, digital sprouts 0→1→N grow successively from the gaps between sleepers, with a blue-green primary color symbolizing new life growing from the track and a dark blueprint-style rendering; candidate taglines "From the first rail to ZERO-ONE" and "Where the track ends, seeds sprout" are offered for further development. The visual identity and wayfinding symbol system uniformly adopt a symbol library of "rail elements (sleepers/spikes/signal lights) × digital elements (0/1/sprouts)"; all brands, fonts, images, and logos may be used only after rights clearance Source.

The three-layer cultural narrative. Present layer: the continuous lineage of Zhongguancun from the electronics street to the internet to AI. Future layer: the open-source spirit, geek culture, AI ethics, and human-machine symbiosis. The main thread is the seed metaphor — the Jing-Zhang Railway was the "first seed" of the nation's independent innovation, Zhongguancun made it sprout, and in the AI era the Seed Belt will let it grow into a forest Source Depth.
