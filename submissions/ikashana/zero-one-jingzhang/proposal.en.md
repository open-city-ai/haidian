# ZERO-ONE JINGZHANG: Centennial AI Innovation Belt Design on the Seed Belt

## Design Basis and Source List

This proposal takes as its primary basis the *Prequalification Announcement for the International Solicitation of Urban Design for the Centennial Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt* (百年京张AI创新带城市设计国际方案征集资格预审公告) issued by the Haidian Branch of the Beijing Municipal Commission of Planning and Natural Resources. The announcement defines the three-tier design scope, the three key detailed design areas, the design tasks, and the deliverable depth requirements, and constitutes the object to which every chapter, layer, and indicator in this submission package responds [source:OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT] [standard:PROJECT-OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT]. The secondary basis is the agent-facing task book (`brief/site-package/agent_taskbook.json`), which sets out ten co-creation principles, three positionings, five functions, and six agent tasks (naming and logo, ecosystem case studies, scenario cards, pilgrimage landmarks, cultural narrative, long-term operation) and is the direct source of the "ZERO-ONE" conceptual system [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK] [standard:PROJECT-AGENT-OPEN-CALL-TASKBOOK].

Machine-readable bases come from the site package `brief/site-package/` (design_brief, allowed_design_space, enums, ranges, schemas, and the provisional boundary geometry) and from the material-use registry in `data/source_registry.json`: the registry currently classifies materials into four types — formally usable, background, provisional-only, and pending re-verification — and the agent must not upgrade background or provisional materials into official boundaries, statutory regulatory plans, or implementation commitments [source:SITE-PACKAGE] [source:SOURCE-REGISTRY]. `data/processed/agent_fact_pack.md` is the reading-navigation layer, summarizing the three-tier scope, task mapping, material availability, and the missing-data list; factual judgments must still return to the registered raw materials [source:PROCESSED-FACT-PACK].

Judgments on the site's existing conditions are grounded in public facts: the Beijing-Zhangjiakou (Jing-Zhang) Railway Heritage Park runs about 9 km from Beijing North Station in the south to the North Fifth Ring Road in the north, radiating over approximately 14 km². Phase I (Qinghua East Road–Zhichun Road section, 16.8 ha) opened in June 2023 and Phase II is under construction. The park preserves the Qinghuayuan Station building whose name was inscribed by Zhan Tianyou himself (a Beijing municipal heritage-protection unit), forms a "three paths, one greenway" (三道一绿) slow-traffic system, and received the 2024 IFLA (International Federation of Landscape Architects) Africa-Asia-Pacific-Middle East Award and the 2025 ASLA (American Society of Landscape Architects) National Award. The surrounding area hosts the "Eight Universities" of Xueyuan Road (Beihang, USTB, CUMT, CUGB, BJFU, CAU, BLCU, BUPT) plus nearly 20 universities and research institutes including Tsinghua, Peking University, Beijing Jiaotong University, and the Chinese Academy of Sciences system; Metro Line 13 serves Wudaokou, Zhichun Road, and Dazhongsi stations along the corridor [source:PROCESSED-FACT-PACK] [depth:existing_conditions_diagnosis]. The above facts are cited only as public background and are not the sole basis for spatial conclusions.

The boundary status must be declared throughout: both the Overall Design Area and the three key detailed design areas in this package use provisional geometry derived from `brief/site-package/geometry/provisional_boundaries.geojson`, marked with `provisional_constraint` and `official_boundary=false`, and are used only for scheme generation, self-checks, visualization, and design discussion — they cannot serve as official red lines, approval bases, precise area bases, or statutory control conclusions [source:BOUNDARY-SOURCE] [source:KEY-AREA-SOURCE]. The organizer's boundary data gap does not block content scoring; once the official polygons are released, the site boundary, key areas, land use, roads, green space, public space, buildings, phasing, and all indicators must be recalculated [data:geometry/site_boundary.geojson#SITE-001] [depth:risk_missing_data].

## Three-Tier Scope Working Framework

The proposal organizes its work according to the three-tier scope defined in the announcement. The Coordinated Research Area covers approximately 43.6 km² and addresses questions of AI industrial ecology, strategic positioning, the innovation chain, and future urban form; the Overall Design Area covers approximately 11.41 km² (recalculated from this package's geometry as 11,412,825 m²) and addresses the overall urban-regeneration framework, industrial spatial layout, transportation and municipal support, and urban-character control [metric:site_area_sqm] [data:geometry/site_boundary.geojson#SITE-001]; the three key detailed design areas total approximately 368.4 ha per the announcement (369.3 ha recalculated) and address detailed design questions for the three districts [metric:key_area_total_area_sqm] [metric:key_area_count]. The mapping of the three tiers to the announcement tasks and agent.1–agent.6 is recorded item by item in `compliance_matrix.json`, while the main text retains only readable conclusions [source:PROCESSED-FACT-PACK] [depth:three_level_scope_framework].

The three tiers are not three unrelated drawings but a step-by-step implementation of "strategy—structure—plot": the Coordinated Research Area determines the industrial chain and urban form; the Overall Design Area translates those judgments into regeneration projects, spatial structure, and facility capacity; and the key detailed design areas verify the implementability of specific plots, buildings, transportation, public space, and AI scenarios. This proposal aligns this relationship with the core concept "0→1→N" — the Coordinated Research Area answers "how the innovation process is organized," the Overall Design Area answers "how the Seed Belt links the three districts," and the key detailed design areas correspond respectively to the three innovation nodes: the AI Origin Community (0, origin), Zhongzhiyuan (1, breakthrough), and Dazhongsi (N, explosion) [depth:overall_spatial_structure].

## Coordinated Research Area: Industry and Future City Study

### Core Concept and Naming System

This proposal puts forward "ZERO-ONE JINGZHANG" (零一京张): a hundred years ago, the Beijing-Zhangjiakou Railway accomplished China's 0→1 of independent innovation; a hundred years later, ZERO-ONE on the Seed Belt aims to launch the 0→1 of the intelligent era from here. The naming system maps the binary minimal units 0/1 onto the digital world and "Jingzhang" onto the physical track, forming a twin-line narrative of "from iron rails to computing tracks, from the first rail of the industrial age to the first line of code of the intelligent age" [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK] [standard:PROJECT-AGENT-OPEN-CALL-TASKBOOK]. The three districts are named accordingly: the AI Origin Community = 0 (place of origin), Zhongzhiyuan = 1 (place of breakthrough), and Dazhongsi = N (place of explosion); the green axis is named "SEED BELT," where "seed" is a pun on the AI random seed — the starting point of all AI operation and innovation — mirroring the real regeneration in which green grew from the retired railway [depth:overall_spatial_structure].

The Logo direction is "track → sprouting": along a rail line, digital sprouts 0→1→N grow successively from the gaps between sleepers, with a blue-green primary color symbolizing new life growing from the track and a dark blueprint-style rendering; candidate taglines "From the first rail to ZERO-ONE" and "Where the track ends, seeds sprout" are offered for further development. The visual identity and wayfinding symbol system uniformly adopt a symbol library of "rail elements (sleepers/spikes/signal lights) × digital elements (0/1/sprouts)"; all brands, fonts, images, and logos may be used only after rights clearance [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK].

### Three Positionings, Five Functions, and Three Districts Plus Two Wings

"ZERO-ONE" uses the Seed Belt to link the three positionings given in the task book: the Centennial Jing-Zhang Cultural Belt carries the memory of the rails (the history of 0), the Urban AI Life Experience Belt carries the sprouting scenarios (the life of N), and the AI-Integrated Innovation Belt carries the breakthrough engine (the industry of 1) [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK]. The five functions map as follows:

| Five functions | Spatial anchor | Core mechanism |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AI full-stack independent innovation system | Zhongzhiyuan (1) | Full-stack independence across chips, models, frameworks, compute, and data; accelerator clusters and open-source evaluation |
| World-class AI innovation ecosystem | AI Origin Community (0) | University incubation, talent special zone, open-source system, technology transfer |
| New paradigm of AI+ scenario empowerment | Xiaoyue River Scenario Empowerment Wing | Scenario experiment belt along the Xiaoyue River and intelligent urban vitality |
| Intelligent AI-vibrant city | Seed Belt | Intelligent public spaces, slow-traffic experience, AI-native living |
| Global voice in AI governance | ZERO-ONE Award + Hall of Honor (Dazhongsi, N) | International-level award, bell-ringing ceremony, laureate name inscriptions |

The synergy loop of the three districts and two wings: the Origin Community in the middle originates (ideas and talent), Zhongzhiyuan breaks through to the north (in the same direction in which the Jing-Zhang Railway once left the city), Dazhongsi diffuses southward into the urban hinterland (scenarios and businesses), the Zhongguancun Technology Service Wing provides global allocation of factors, IP, and capital empowerment, and the Xiaoyue River Scenario Empowerment Wing provides a laboratory for scenario deployment and urban vitality. The spatial circulation narrative is the innovation-process narrative: the innovation chain, industrial chain, talent chain, and urban service chain form a walkable spatial continuum along the Seed Belt [depth:overall_spatial_structure] [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-KEY-001-1].

### Global AI Innovation Ecosystem Case Studies

The proposal selects six public cases as mechanism references (qualitative descriptions; no unverified investment figures are cited; sources are listed in the References section and `sources.json`) [source:SOURCE-REGISTRY]:

| Case | Model highlights | Transferable mechanism |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Boston Kendall Square | University incubation anchored on MIT with a biotech-and-AI translation loop, hailed as "the world's most innovative square mile" | Spatial organization of the Origin Community: "innovation next to campus, translation on site" |
| London King's Cross | A Victorian railway hub and coal yards wholly redeveloped into a knowledge-innovation district combining universities, enterprises, and public space | Highly isomorphic with Jingzhang: railway industrial heritage becomes an innovation container; the Seed Belt is "Jingzhang's King's Cross" |
| Singapore Jurong Lake District (JLD) | A national lake-district new town exploring integrated park-and-governance under the Smart Nation strategy | Zhongzhiyuan's park governance, standards, and safety exhibition space |
| Shenzhen | Scenario-driven innovation and hardware ecosystem: continuous upgrading from electronics market to smart hardware | The "scenario-as-market" logic of Dazhongsi's AI-native commercial street |
| Silicon Valley / Palo Alto | University–capital–industry loop; venture capital and alumni networks form invisible infrastructure | Capital and IP empowerment functions of the Zhongguancun Technology Service Wing |
| Zhongguancun itself | A continuous lineage from electronics street → science park → internet → AI; a living sample of China's technological innovation | The local basis of the "present layer" in the three-layer cultural narrative |

The experience transfer follows four principles: heritage activation over demolition-and-rebuild (King's Cross); incubation density over building scale (Kendall Square); governance and standards space upfront (Jurong); open scenarios as attraction (Shenzhen). Entrepreneurship and AI-ecosystem cases such as Tel Aviv and Tokyo/Yokohama may serve as references for further study but do not enter this round's core conclusions.

### Innovation Ecosystem Map, Factor Mechanisms, and Future Urban Form

The innovation ecosystem map unfolds along the Seed Belt in a five-level structure: basic research (Xueyuan Road university belt) → open-source collaboration (Origin Community) → independent breakthrough (Zhongzhiyuan full-stack system) → ecosystem explosion (Dazhongsi's agent, smart-terminal, content, and data-factor businesses) → governance and honor (ZERO-ONE Award and the Hall of Honor), with each level corresponding to explicit spatial types and operating mechanisms [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK] [depth:overall_spatial_structure]. The factor mechanisms cover eight categories: land (flexible supply and coordinated regeneration), space (shared laboratories, accelerators, exhibition galleries), industry (industrial-chain attraction and scenario orders), capital (Zhongguancun wing funds and incubation services, conceptual proposal), talent (talent special zone services and youth creation pods), compute (Zhongzhiyuan hub and edge compute nodes), data (data-factor services under compliance prerequisites), and scenarios (open scenario lists along the Seed Belt and the Xiaoyue River) [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK].

The future urban form study answers how AI changes work, life, socializing, learning, transportation, and public services: AI-native neighborhoods (operating interfaces where buildings and algorithms cooperate), human-machine shared public spaces (low-speed robots, an overridable safety net, and human-priority principles), continuous green space (the Seed Belt as a cultivation vessel), and an international live-work atmosphere (Dazhongsi's international exchange interface). All of the above are conceptual proposals; industry stock data (number of enterprises, talent density, R&D investment) will be calibrated after official statistics are released and do not constitute this round's indicator conclusions [depth:risk_missing_data].

## Overall Design Area: Urban Regeneration and Urban Design at Regulatory-Detailed-Plan Depth

The Overall Design Area is required to reach the urban design depth of a regulatory detailed plan (控制性详细规划) [standard:MOHURD-CONTROL-DETAILED-PLANNING]. In the absence of official regulatory-plan conditions, this proposal first establishes a verifiable methodological framework and structural judgments; any conclusion belonging to statutory control is labeled "pending confirmation of formal regulatory-plan conditions" and never impersonates approved indicators [metric:floor_area_ratio] [depth:development_intensity_controls].

**Overall spatial structure: "one belt, three cores, multiple nodes and scenarios."** The one belt is the Seed Belt (the green axis of the Jing-Zhang Heritage Park and its flanking urban frontages), merging three axes into one: the historical axis, the slow-traffic axis, and the innovation-scenario axis. The three cores are the three key detailed design areas 0/1/N. The multiple nodes and scenarios are AI service nodes along the Seed Belt and its two wings. East-west stitching (the Seed Belt linking communities and campuses on both sides) and north-south through-connection (continuous slow traffic from Beijing North Station to the North Fifth Ring Road) are the two operating lines of the structural organization [depth:overall_spatial_structure] [data:geometry/green_space.geojson#GS-SEED-1].

**Overall urban-regeneration framework and low-efficiency space identification.** A four-dimensional screening of "land-use efficiency—building age—ownership complexity—rail station accessibility" is proposed to identify regeneration targets, distinguishing four response types: retain, renovate, renew, and new-build. This package does not issue plot-level retain/renovate/demolish conclusions; identified results must be verified against existing buildings, ownership, and regulatory-plan data and then developed by professional teams [depth:retain_renovate_demolish] [depth:land_use_layout].

**Industrial function proportions and spatial organization patterns.** In the conceptual land-use structure, scientific research and education (A3) is approximately 3.703 million m², commercial services (B1) approximately 4.125 million m², industry (M2) approximately 2.223 million m², residential (R2) approximately 1.190 million m², and R&D (R_D) approximately 0.172 million m², forming a graded organization of "university incubation—tower-block economy—industrial parks—community amenities" [metric:land_use_A3_area_sqm] [metric:land_use_B1_area_sqm] [metric:land_use_M2_area_sqm] [metric:land_use_R2_area_sqm] [metric:land_use_R_D_area_sqm]. A3 lands concentrate in the Origin Community and along Xueyuan Road, M2 in Zhongzhiyuan, and B1 in Dazhongsi and the southern section, consistent with the positioning of the three districts [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-KEY-002-1] [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-KEY-003-1].

**Building scale and intensity.** The conceptual building footprint is approximately 259,000 m² and the conceptual total floor area approximately 2.726 million m² (floor counts are conceptual assumptions, not statutory floor area, used only to express spatial structure) [metric:building_footprint_area_sqm] [metric:total_floor_area_sqm]. Control indicators such as floor area ratio (FAR), building height, building density, setbacks, and road red lines are uniformly recorded as `status=unknown` until the official regulatory-plan conditions are released; the recalculation path is "official boundary and regulatory-plan conditions released → recalculate per the formula total_floor_area / official_site_area" [metric:floor_area_ratio] [depth:development_intensity_controls] [depth:height_massing_character]. The conceptual massing constitutes no statutory control value.

**Integrated carrying-capacity assessment.** An assessment framework covering rail passenger flow, public-service radii, municipal loads, and slow-traffic capacity is proposed as the basis for ranking and phasing regeneration projects; engineering conditions for pipelines, energy, drainage, flood control, and fire protection are missing and are listed as prerequisites for formal detailed development [depth:municipal_new_infrastructure] [depth:risk_missing_data].

### Zhongzhiyuan AI Independent Innovation Acceleration Area (1, approx. 192.1 ha per the announcement)

**Positioning**: A garden-style full-stack independent-innovation neighborhood and the independent-breakthrough engine from 0 to 1, hosting full-stack independent innovation across chips, models, frameworks, compute, and data, as well as open-source model evaluation [metric:key_area_zhongzhiyuan_ai_acceleration_area_area_sqm] [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-KEY-001-1]. **Spatial structure**: Industrial R&D land forms the core cluster; the Qinghe River frontage is strengthened into a low-carbon green innovation exchange belt, with industrial exhibition and standards/governance exhibition space [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#PS-001]. **Building regeneration**: The main direction is renewing low-efficiency industrial land into accelerator clusters and shared laboratory space; specific retain/renovate/demolish decisions await verification against ownership and existing-building data. **Traffic and slow mobility**: External connections toward the North Fifth Ring Road are organized; internal slow-traffic circulation is seamlessly linked with the northern section of the Seed Belt [data:geometry/roads.geojson#RD-SPINE-N]. **Public space**: ZERO-ONE Breakthrough Plaza serves as the park's public living room, hosting achievement launches and open testing [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#PS-001]. **AI scenarios**: Open-source model evaluation ground, standards-setting workshops, safety-governance exhibitions, low-carbon compute experiences (conceptual proposals). **Implementation risks**: The Qinghe blue line, ecological and flood-control conditions, and the energy and network capacity of compute infrastructure all require professional re-verification.

### Beijing AI Origin Community (0, approx. 104.3 ha per the announcement)

**Positioning**: A campus-adjacent technology-transfer and talent community — the place where AI's "first line of code" happens: origin, ideas, talent [metric:key_area_beijing_ai_origin_community_area_sqm] [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-KEY-002-1]. **Spatial structure**: With the Qinghuayuan Station building (name inscribed by Zhan Tianyou himself; a Beijing municipal heritage-protection unit) as the core anchor, "ZERO-ONE ORIGIN" memorial and exhibition space is organized; the university belt along Xueyuan Road opens campus frontages to form an inter-university shared laboratory belt [data:geometry/constraints.geojson#CON-HERITAGE-PARK]. **Building regeneration**: Mainly functional replacement and ground-floor business renewal, preserving the historic station building and heritage elements; specific schemes require a dedicated heritage impact assessment. **Traffic and slow mobility**: Integrated development at Wudaokou Station and slow-traffic stitching of campus—park—neighborhood, supplementing space for achievement launches, talent services, and open-source collaboration [data:geometry/constraints.geojson#CON-RAIL-13]. **Public space**: ZERO-ONE ORIGIN Plaza (the Origin Museum and the digital platform interface of the "first line of code" departure station) [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#PS-002]. **AI scenarios**: Inter-university shared laboratory belt, open-source launch hall, technology-transfer street, personal learning tracks (conceptual proposals). **Implementation risks**: Campus boundaries and ownership, heritage-protection approvals, and station-integration engineering coordination are all items pending confirmation.

### Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster (N, approx. 72.0 ha per the announcement)

**Positioning**: An urban smart-economy and international-exchange neighborhood — the ecosystem explosion point from 1 to N: agents, smart terminals, content consumption, and data-factor businesses [metric:key_area_dazhongsi_ai_industry_cluster_area_sqm] [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-KEY-003-1]. **Spatial structure**: Integrated development around Dazhongsi Station organizes the "ZERO-ONE TRANSFER LIVING ROOM" (a human-machine transfer hub with pedestrian priority); pedestrian connectivity across the four quadrants of the intersection stitches back the urban fabric cut apart by rail and road [data:geometry/constraints.geojson#CON-RAIL-13]. **Building regeneration**: Commercial-service land is renewed into an AI-native commercial street and scenario testing grounds, with planned green space used in composite ways; specific retain/renovate/demolish decisions await verification against regulatory plans and ownership. **Traffic and slow mobility**: Rail-transfer priority, with integrated organization of non-motorized vehicle parking and the pedestrian network [data:geometry/roads.geojson#RD-X1]. **Public space**: The Hall of Honor Plaza hosts the ZERO-ONE Award ceremony, bell-ringing ritual, and laureate name inscriptions (conceptual proposal) [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#PS-003]. **AI scenarios**: AI-native street, ZERO-ONE TRANSFER LIVING ROOM, international roadshow living room, data-factor salon (compliance prerequisites). **Implementation risks**: Coordination of rail station retrofits, engineering conditions at road intersections, and the operating entity and rights-clearance requirements for international events.

### Twelve Scenario Cards (6 registered + 6 extended)

The thematic principle is "AI augments people rather than replacing them." Each scenario card provides its positioning, spatial anchor, users, value, risks, and human-review mechanism, and maps to one of the six registered scenario IDs and to spatial layers [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK] [standard:PROJECT-AGENT-OPEN-CALL-TASKBOOK].

**01 Inter-University Shared Laboratory Belt (extended)** — Positioning: A cross-university open research platform that lets instruments, data, and compute flow among nearly 20 universities and research institutes. Anchor: The Xueyuan Road frontage of the Origin Community — shared nodes along A3 scientific research & education land [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-KEY-002-1]. Users: University researchers. Value: Upgrades "near-campus" from geographic proximity to collaborative proximity. Risk: Confidentiality of research data and competitive outputs. Human review: Sharing applications and achievement publications are approved by an inter-university joint mechanism; sensitive data never leaves its domain.

**02 Urban Health Station (registered · ai-health-service-navigation)** — Positioning: A community node for AI-assisted grassroots health screening and chronic-disease management; the deepening direction introduces the concept of rapid pathogen diagnostics (mNGS-type technology), realizing a closed service loop of "samples in the community, diagnosis in the cloud, verification by doctors." Anchor: Community nodes along the Seed Belt and the public-service belt of the Origin Community. Users: Local residents and seniors. Value: Converts the service density of large hospitals into community accessibility. Risk: Sensitivity of medical data; boundaries of diagnostic responsibility. Human review: Test results must be signed by licensed physicians; stations do not replace outpatient clinics; data stored on a minimization basis [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#PS-1].

**03 Personal Learning Track (extended)** — Positioning: AI tutors and adaptive learning — a "track-style" progression path from basic education to lifelong learning. Anchor: Learning nodes on Xueyuan Road and in seed communities. Users: Students and working learners. Value: Distributes educational resources evenly along the Seed Belt. Risk: Protection of minors' data. Human review: Learning-path recommendations are explainable, parents and teachers can intervene, and no single-dimensional evaluation conclusions are issued.

**04 Urban Health Check Dashboard (extended)** — Positioning: AI monitoring of urban vital signs and governance decision support; the "urban diagnosis" metaphor echoes mNGS-style testing thinking, identifying slow-traffic breaks, public-space heat, and facility maintenance needs. Anchor: Data nodes across the Overall Design Area. Users: Sub-district offices and management authorities. Value: Shifts governance from experience-driven to vital-sign-driven. Risk: Excessive surveillance and individual profiling. Human review: Only aggregate indicators are output; individual data cannot be traced back; governance decisions require human sign-off [data:geometry/roads.geojson#RD-SPINE-N].

**05 ZERO-ONE Transfer Living Room (registered · ai-traffic-walkability)** — Positioning: The human-machine transfer hub at Dazhongsi — a pedestrian-priority transfer hall connecting rail, slow traffic, and low-speed feeder services. Anchor: Between the four quadrants of Dazhongsi Station and the Hall of Honor Plaza [data:geometry/constraints.geojson#CON-RAIL-13]. Users: Commuters and visitors. Value: Converts rail passenger flow into neighborhood vitality. Risk: Human-machine mixed-traffic conflicts. Human review: Transfer circulation is verified by traffic simulation; robot speed limits and avoidance rules are publicly inspectable.

**06 AI-Native Street (extended)** — Positioning: A demonstration and experimentation street for AI-native commercial forms, combining agent services, smart-terminal experiences, and content consumption. Anchor: The core section of B1 commercial-service land in Dazhongsi [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-KEY-003-1]. Users: Consumers and entrepreneurs. Value: Makes "scenario-as-market" a mechanism for attracting business. Risk: Boundaries of consumer-data collection. Human review: Personalized recommendations require explicit user consent; an "algorithm-free" consumption channel is provided.

**07 Rail-Memory AR Trail (registered · ai-cultural-guide)** — Positioning: An AR heritage guide to the Beijing-Zhangjiakou Railway — a 9 km walkable historical narrative from Beijing North Station to the North Fifth Ring Road. ### Industrial Test and Validation Scenarios (3)

All three test scenarios are bounded by "limited scope, low-speed and monitorable, human-overridable," and are provided as conceptual proposals for professional teams to develop [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK]:

- **Low-speed autonomous driving test corridor**: Designated sections of the Seed Belt to validate coordination among low-speed feeder, delivery, and cleaning robots; depends on road red lines, traffic simulation, and operating permits, and human-machine mixed-traffic rules must be publicly reviewed.
- **Medical diagnostics testbed (mNGS direction)**: Community application testing of rapid pathogen-diagnosis technology anchored on the Urban Health Station; depends on medical qualifications, data compliance, and ethics review; test results are used for research validation only and no diagnostic conclusions are released externally.
- **Open-source model evaluation ground**: A public evaluation and red-teaming site for open-source models in Zhongzhiyuan; evaluation datasets and indicators are transparent and reproducible, test reports are released only after manual review and sign-off, and unauthorized data is never evaluated.

### AI+ Healthcare Deep Scenario and Research Showcase

AI+ healthcare is the deep scenario of this round: the three-tier system of "Urban Health Station—Medical Diagnostics Testbed—Overridable Safety Net" responds to the "AI augments people" theme, and the mNGS-type rapid pathogen-diagnosis concept emphasizes the division of labor of "technology runs the tests, doctors make the decisions, communities provide the services" [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK] [depth:three_key_area_detailed_design]. The research showcase sits on the Xueyuan Road university belt: the Inter-University Shared Laboratory Belt and the Origin Museum serve the function of "visible research," with achievement showcases, open-source releases, and talent services laid out along A3 land [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-B39994-1].

All AI scenarios follow four principles: data minimization, open sources, explainability, and human review. Urban agents may assist in identifying slow-traffic breaks, public-space heat, facility maintenance, enterprise-service needs, and event safety risks, but they cannot replace planning approvals, must not output unauthorized individual profiles, and must not claim official implementation commitments [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK] [depth:risk_missing_data].

## Land Use, Building Scale, and Retain-Renovate-Demolish Scheme

**Land-use layout.** The conceptual land-use classification follows the *Guidelines for Land Use and Sea Use Classification in Territorial Spatial Survey, Planning, and Use Control* (国土空间调查、规划、用途管制用地用海分类指南), is complete, closed, and seamless, with a total area of 11,412,877 m² consistent with the recalculated Overall Design Area [standard:MNR-LAND-USE-CLASSIFICATION-GUIDE] [metric:land_use_area_sqm] [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-KEY-001-1]. The five main land-use categories break down as: A3 scientific research & education 32.4%, B1 commercial services 36.1%, M2 industry 19.5%, R2 residential 10.4%, and R_D R&D 1.5% — industry and research categories together exceed half, supporting the "innovation belt" character [metric:land_use_A3_area_sqm] [metric:land_use_B1_area_sqm] [metric:land_use_M2_area_sqm] [metric:land_use_R2_area_sqm] [metric:land_use_R_D_area_sqm]. The land-use structure is a conceptual proposal; plot-level uses are governed by the formal regulatory plan.

**Building scale.** The conceptual building footprint is approximately 259,000 m² and the conceptual total floor area approximately 2.726 million m² — indicative massing for expressing spatial structure (floor counts are conceptual assumptions), constituting neither statutory floor area nor development-intensity conclusions [metric:building_footprint_area_sqm] [metric:total_floor_area_sqm] [metric:building_density]. The FAR indicator is `status=unknown` and will be recalculated after formal regulatory-plan conditions are released [metric:floor_area_ratio] [depth:development_intensity_controls].

**Retain-renovate-demolish scheme (a methodological framework, not plot-level conclusions).** Four response types are proposed: retain — heritage-protection units and buildings with historical value or sound structure (e.g., the Qinghuayuan Station building), paired with functional activation; renovate — existing spaces undergoing functional replacement and ground-floor business renewal; renew — functional upgrades and spatial restructuring of low-efficiency land and buildings; new-build — incremental supply on vacant land and land freed by renewal [depth:retain_renovate_demolish] [data:geometry/constraints.geojson#CON-HERITAGE-PARK]. Data on existing building footprints, ages, ownership, and engineering quality are missing; this package issues no plot-level retain/renovate/demolish conclusions. The identification process and review checklists are in `assumptions.json` and `missing_data_checklist.csv` [depth:risk_missing_data].

**Space supply and operation strategy.** Three supply types — "flexible space, shared laboratory space, movable components" — are proposed to cope with AI industry uncertainty: buildings provide standardized R&D offices, parks provide shared testing and pilot-production space, and public space provides movable display and service boxes (echoing the Heritage Park's existing movable-box practice), with space operation and scenario operation linked [depth:land_use_layout] [depth:height_massing_character].

## Blue-Green Space, Public Space, and Urban Character

**Seed Belt and blue-green network.** A north-south through blue-green system is organized on the skeleton of the Jing-Zhang Heritage Park green axis, coordinating mobility needs among the Qinghe River, Xiaoyue River, and surrounding universities, enterprises, and communities to form a composite slow-traffic network of "footpath—cycle track—greenway" (the three paths and one greenway) [data:geometry/green_space.geojson#GS-SEED-1] [data:geometry/roads.geojson#RD-SPINE-N]. The conceptual green space is approximately 1.99 km² (green ratio 17.5%) and the conceptual public space approximately 1.01 km² (8.8%), together about 26% of the overall area, providing the capacity base for innovation exchange and daily recreation; the design intent of these ratios is "make talent willing to walk and residents willing to stay," and the numerical recalculation is in `metrics.json` [metric:green_ratio] [metric:public_space_ratio] [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#PS-1]. The blue-green scheme identifies slow-traffic breaks, overpass nodes across ring roads, and landscape nodes at the park's north and south ends, and proposes composite-use strategies for parking, sports, innovation exchange, technology testing, application showcases, and public services [depth:blue_green_public_space] [standard:MOHURD-URBAN-DESIGN-MEASURES].

**The AI pilgrimage landmark trio.** The three landmarks form a public-space narrative of "origin—co-creation—honor"; all are conceptual proposals and do not constitute approved construction:

1. **ZERO-ONE ORIGIN** (around the Qinghuayuan Station building): the physical carrier of China's first station of independent innovation. The conceptual proposal converts it into the AI Origin Museum and the "first line of code" departure station (a digital platform), preserving Zhan Tianyou's station-name calligraphy and the rail memory, strictly aligned with heritage-protection requirements [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#PS-002] [data:geometry/constraints.geojson#CON-HERITAGE-PARK].
2. **SEED PLAZA** (the core node of the Seed Belt green axis): an open-source co-creation plaza with a developer promenade, an open-source achievement gallery, and an agent-contribution honor wall; the honor display system uses a public-space component library of "movable boxes + honor plaques + digital stele," linked to the Milestone stele system of the solicitation itself [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#PS-1].
3. **HALL OF ZERO-ONE** (Dazhongsi): the permanent venue of the ZERO-ONE Award, with the bell-ringing ceremony (the bell tolls for humanity's AI breakthroughs), laureate name inscriptions, and an AI milestone wall, modeled on the spatial organization of international honors such as the Nobel and Turing awards [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#PS-003] [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#PS-001].

**Urban character and wayfinding system.** The character scheme fuses three layers of genes — Jing-Zhang Railway history and culture, Zhongguancun innovation culture, and AI new culture — and proposes urban tonality, architectural character, roof forms, massing, and frontage guidance, with control graded into three tiers: "official control—design suggestion—conditions pending confirmation"; pseudo-precise control lines are strictly prohibited where heritage or regulatory-plan grounds are lacking [standard:MOHURD-URBAN-DESIGN-MEASURES] [depth:height_massing_character]. The wayfinding symbol system follows the unified library of "rail elements (sleepers/spikes/signal lights) × digital elements (0/1/sprouts)," with a blue-green primary color and dark blueprint style, consistent with the Logo system; all brands, fonts, images, portraits, and enterprise logos must be rights-cleared [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK].

**The three-layer cultural narrative.** Historical layer: in 1909 Zhan Tianyou led the construction of the Beijing-Zhangjiakou Railway — China's first trunk railway designed independently by Chinese people — the Qinghuayuan Tunnel opened to traffic in 2019, and Phase I of the Heritage Park opened in 2023 — green grew after the rails retired. Present layer: the continuous lineage of Zhongguancun from the electronics street to the internet to AI. Future layer: the open-source spirit, geek culture, AI ethics, and human-machine symbiosis. The main thread is the seed metaphor — the Jing-Zhang Railway was the "first seed" of the nation's independent innovation, Zhongguancun made it sprout, and in the AI era the Seed Belt will let it grow into a forest [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK] [depth:existing_conditions_diagnosis]. International communication copy directions: "From the first rail to the first line of code" / "Where China's first track met the world's next leap".
