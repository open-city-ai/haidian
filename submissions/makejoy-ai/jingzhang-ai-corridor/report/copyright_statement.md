## 2. Figures and Drawings

- All figures (`assets/figures/*.png`, `assets/figures/chapters/*.png`,
  `assets/figures/hi/*.png`, `visual/assets/*.png`) are generated by the
  deterministic scripts of this proposal from the submitted GeoJSON,
  metrics and matrices. They are not third-party images.
  - Generation path: `tools/regenerate_geometry.py`, figure render scripts,
    `scripts/spatial_review.py`, `scripts/visual_review.py` (kept in the
    author workspace; not shipped inside the submission package).
  - No external image, icon, map tile or photograph is referenced.
- PDF deliverables (`drawings/a3-booklet.pdf`, `drawings/a0-boards.pdf`,
  and their `.en` variants) are rendered by the same deterministic pipeline
  from the submitted geometry and text. They contain no third-party artwork.

## 3. Basemaps and Data

- Basemap: none. All maps are drawn as vector geometry inside the submitted
  GeoJSON; no remote basemap tiles or external map services are used.
- Data sources are registered in `sources.json` with `source_id`, license and
  usage scope. The machine-readable source list covers:
  - `SITE-PACKAGE` – organizer-provided site package (brief/site-package/),
    used for design generation within the announced scope.
  - `SOURCE-REGISTRY` – repository source registry (`data/source_registry.json`).
  - `PROCESSED-FACT-PACK` – reading-navigation layer generated from registered
    sources; not an independent authority.
  - `BOUNDARY-SOURCE` / `KEY-AREA-SOURCE` – provisional rough boundary and
    key-area polygons derived from announcement text and the temporary
    boundary; provisional only, not official redlines.
  - `OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT` – the official open-call announcement (Beijing
    Municipal Commission of Planning and Natural Resources, Haidian Branch).
  - `AGENT-TASKBOOK` – the agent open-call taskbook published with the
    repository.
  - Usage is limited to this open-call design submission and its review;
    no commercial re-publication of organizer data is claimed.
- Provisional boundary caveat: `geometry/site_boundary.geojson` and
  `geometry/key_areas.geojson` are provisional (about 10 m resolution) and
  are used only for design discussion, self-check and visualization. They are
  not official redlines or precise-area bases.

## 5. Generation Tools and Models

- Submission generated with the **YQWORK agent harness** (local desktop
  assistant environment).
- Model: **deepseek-v4-flash** (DeepSeek), used for drafting text, structure
