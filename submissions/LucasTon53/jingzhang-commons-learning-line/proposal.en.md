# Jing-Zhang Centennial Line: Transform the AI Innovation District into an Open Urban School

## Design Basis and Source List

This scheme's judgment starting point is not "attaching an AI label to traditional industrial parks," but rather understanding innovation as a learnable, testable, explainable, and reversible urban knowledge infrastructure. The scheme's name is "Jing-Zhang Commons Learning Line / 100-Year Jing-Zhang," with the core proposition being: allowing researchers to validate in a real city, enabling residents to understand and choose, inviting students to participate, and facilitating businesses to complete the loop from prototype to adoption. The logo concept consists of two parallel lines: one representing the engineering line of the Jing-Zhang Railway, and the other representing the shared line of knowledge openness; three dots correspond to three city colleges. The fonts, graphics, and colors are all custom directions for this scheme, without using any third-party trademark assets. [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK] [standard:PROJECT-AGENT-OPEN-CALL-TASKBOOK]

This package uses temporary rough polygons from the repository, which are only for submission entry, topological checks, and conceptual diagrams. The 43.6 square kilometers, 11.4 square kilometers, and 368.4 hectares mentioned in the announcement are approximate values officially published; the area recalculated by temporary polygons [metric:site_area_sqm] in this package cannot replace the Official Boundary. Once the official boundary is in place, all areas, scales, zoning, drawings, and panels must be recalculated in EPSG:4548. [source:BOUNDARY-SOURCE] [depth:existing_conditions_diagnosis]

## Three-Level Scope Framework

The three levels of scope are transmitted through a single "learning chain." The Coordinated Research Area identifies the network of regions for knowledge production, transformation, service, and dissemination; the Overall Design Area transforms this network into a continuous sequence of Public Spaces, pedestrian interfaces, and reservation points for courses; and the three key areas are validated through three different urban academy prototypes. The northern Zhongzhiyuan is the "Autonomous System Training Institute," responsible for standards, robotics, and safety validation; the central Beijing AI Origin Community is the "Open Source Research Common," responsible for campus-town collaboration, open source release, and talent living; the southern Dazhongsi is the "Urban Product Experience Institute," responsible for smart terminals, content consumption, and international dissemination. The Zhongguancun Technology Services Wing provides capital, legal, intellectual property, and computing power interfaces, while the Xiaoyue River Scenario Enablement Wing provides community, environmental, and daily service experiments. [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-001] [depth:three_level_scope_framework]

"Linear Three Institutes, Two Wings Co-Learning, Twelve Lesson Points" is a spatial organization method, not additional statutory redlines. Each lesson point has an intended audience, open hours, data minimization rules, human point of responsibility, and an exit mechanism; each institute is equipped with a public interface and a controlled experimental interface to avoid directly pushing high-risk tests into everyday Public Spaces. [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#SCN-01] [metric:scenario_count]


The proposal is organized according to the three levels defined in the announcement: the Coordinated Research Area focuses on the 43.6 square kilometers of AI industry ecosystem, strategic positioning, innovation chain, and future urban form; the Overall Design Area focuses on the 11.4 square kilometers of urban and industrial areas surrounding the Jing-Zhang heritage park within 1-2 kilometers, requiring the formation of an overall framework for Urban Renewal, industrial space layout, traffic and municipal support, and Urban Character control; the Key-Area Detailed Design Area focuses on 368.4 hectares of three detailed design regions, requiring the clarification of functional types, building scale, classification of demolish–renovate–retain strategy, Public Space connectivity, and traffic organization. The three layers of scope are mapped to `compliance_matrix.json` to ensure that the required tasks in sections 1.3, 1.4, and 1.5 of the announcement, as well as agent.1-agent.6, have corresponding chapters, layers, indicators, drawings, and HTML evidence. (Demolish–Renovate–Retain Strategy)

## Land Use, Building Scale, and Retain-Renovate-Demolish Strategy

Four land use concept bands are assigned to R&D training and research, heritage park and public learning, industrial services and urban products, and community life and talent support, respectively. The Demolish–Renovate–Retain Strategy adopts a decision tree approach: prioritize retention for historical and high-use intensity buildings; prioritize renovation and functional replacement for existing buildings that meet structural and fire safety conditions; use detachable components for the course boxes, exhibition corridors, and responsibility platforms; and only discuss new construction after due consideration of ownership, control plan, structural integrity, fire safety, cultural heritage, and economic viability. [depth:retain_renovate_demolish] [depth:height_massing_character]

If the current buildings, ownership, control plan, and engineering conditions are lacking, the scheme can only propose methods and a list for calibration, but cannot fabricate the Demolish–Renovate–Retain Strategy conclusions.

Land use classification is based on [standard:MNR-LAND-USE-CLASSIFICATION-GUIDE].
