# 京张会呼吸 / THE POROUS GROUND CONTRACT — Executive Narrative

## 中文

让地面打开，不让噪声直送到人。方案不建设另一套环境看板，而以**2个落位概念+1个未锚定类型**写“源—路径—接收者”空间合同：北部S1是一条长窄受控后台经签署证据门到较窄公共步道和小型静侧复核袋的单向交接；中部S2是两条非共线交叉路径、共享遮荫庭、两种不等占用边和独立静侧学习带；南部T3只做未锚定的采用/退出类型，在道路、绿地、公共空间和建筑图层均为零。S1/S2全部尺寸是E0设计参数包络并明确NOT_MEASURED。AI只比较B1人工规则以外的可解释候选，不能稳定胜出就删除。当前没有真实断面、授权、E3/E4或效果；所有试点均为NO-GO。90日协议把基线、消融、可逆样段、负向结果、停止与RESTORED签收放进同一工程闭环。

## English

Open the ground, not a direct noise path. The contract is **two located concepts plus one unanchored type**. Northern S1 is one directional hand-off from a long narrow controlled backstage through a signed evidence gate to a narrower public walk and small quiet review pocket. Central S2 has two non-collinear crossing paths, one shared shaded court, two unlike occupied edges and a separate quiet learning strip. Southern T3 remains an unanchored adoption/exit type and has zero road, green, public-space or building features. Every S1/S2 dimension is an E0 design-parameter envelope marked NOT_MEASURED. AI only compares explainable alternatives against manual B1 and is deleted when it cannot win stably. There is no real transect, authority, E3/E4 evidence or measured outcome; every pilot is NO-GO. The 90-day protocol joins baseline, ablation, reversible mock-up, adverse results, stopping and signed RESTORED evidence in one engineering loop.
