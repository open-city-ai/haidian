# Jing-Zhang Zhìhuán: An Open Intelligent Urban Community with One Track and Three Rings in Twelve Scenarios

> **JingZhang Commons — One Rail, Three Loops, Twelve Scenes**
> This outcome is an Open Co-Creation initiative, not a substitute for formal planning, and does not constitute a government review, investment, approval, or implementation commitment.

## Summary of the Proposal

"Jing-Zhang Zhui-Huan" interprets the century-old railway as a city-wide community agreement: in the past, the railway connected people and goods, and today this heritage Public Space can connect knowledge, talents, enterprises, residents, and intelligent entities. The proposal includes "one track and three rings, six stations, and twelve scenarios": one track is the cultural and active public spine of the Jing-Zhang Heritage Park; the three rings are the northern "full-stack validation ring," the central "open-source transformation ring," and the southern "intelligent living ring"; the six stations are service interfaces across park, campus, community, and rail station areas; and the twelve scenarios transform AI from an abstract industrial term into experiential, auditable, and exitable public services and industrial testing.

Conceptual Recommendation: Design does not depict the provisional polygon as a "approved red line". `geometry/site_boundary.geojson` and three `KEY_AREA` instances are used only for intake topology, relative proportions, and map organization; see [data:geometry/site_boundary.geojson#SITE-001] [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-001] [source:BOUNDARY-SOURCE]. Spatial actions are conceptual recommendations; a full recalculation must be performed after official polygons, control plans, ownership, roads, utilities, fire safety, and cultural heritage data are in place [depth:risk_missing_data].

## Overall Concept, Naming, and Visual Identity for One Belt

Main name "Jing-Zhang" emphasizes the linear memory of the railway and an innovative feedback loop; the English name **JingZhang Commons** emphasizes public knowledge, shared spaces, and joint governance. The naming system unfolds according to "Track—Ring—Station—Scene," which can be used for spatial signage as well as annual events and digital directories.

The logo direction consists of two unenclosed parallel lines: one representing the engineering rationality of the Jing-Zhang Railway in 1909, and the other representing collaborative agreements in the era of open intelligent times; three bronze-colored nodes represent three categories of innovation rings, with the ends of the lines remaining open, indicating that the plan still requires completion through collaboration between the public and professional teams. The graphic uses only geometric lines and system fonts drawn by this scheme, without the use of corporate trademarks, personal portraits, or unauthorized fonts [depth:height_massing_character].

The visual system is centered around Haidian Technology Blue `#17324D`, Railway Vermilion `#C8493A`, Ruins Bronze `#C2933D`, Park Green `#2A7C68`, and Paper Beige Green `#F3F6F3`. The double railway lines carry the information hierarchy, while mile markers serve as evidence labels. Bronze-colored nodes are used only for key areas and public honor nodes, avoiding cyber neon and entertainment-oriented "AI" aesthetics. This identification is available for professional branding teams to deepen, but it does not constitute an adopted urban brand scheme.

### Three Zones and Two Wings Synergistic Loop

The northern Zhongzhiyuan is responsible for "whether it can ensure safety verification," the central AI Origin community is responsible for "whether it can transition from research to society," and the southern Dazhongsi is responsible for "whether it can be adopted in high-density urban life"; the Zhongguancun Technology Services Wing provides legal, intellectual property, capital, and international services, while the Xiaoyue River Scenario Enablement Wing provides public services and daily experiences. Information, talent, and scenario feedback circulate along the Jing-Zhang public spine, with success not measured by the number of single-time business recruitment or events [depth:overall_spatial_structure].

## Overall Design Area: Urban Renewal and Regulatory-Plan-Level Urban Design

### One Track Three Rings Six Stations

"One Track" is expressed by a continuous green corridor of the heritage park and two low-carbon mobility lines [data:geometry/green_space.geojson#GREEN-001] [data:geometry/roads.geojson#ROAD-001]; "Three Rings" are connected by three east-west stitching corridors linking the park's functional sides; "Six Stages" are divided into two types of interfaces in the north, middle, and south: industrial test stage / ecological reception stage, open-source release stage / talent living stage, and track connection stage / international showcase stage.

Land use zoning is based on the principles of complete coverage, shared boundaries, and replaceable recalculations [depth:land_use_layout]. The central area consists of park greenways and pedestrian service corridors; the west side, from north to south, includes research and testing, education and research, and business services; the east side, from north to south, includes innovative services, talent living, and urban lounges. All codes follow the land use classification guide, but only express design recommendations and do not claim to reflect approved land uses [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-001].

Development intensity remains unspecified. The conceptual Building Footprint is used only to illustrate the granularity of the spatial carrier and the first-floor public nature, and is not used to derive the Floor Area Ratio, Building Height, Building Coverage Ratio, or Demolish–Renovate–Retain Strategy [data:geometry/buildings.geojson#BLDG-001] [metric:building_footprint_area_sqm] [depth:development_intensity_controls] [depth:retain_renovate_demolish]. Formal deepening must overlay ownership, current surveying, control plan, cultural heritage, and engineering conditions.

## Detailed Design of Key Areas

![Differential Positioning of Three Key Areas, Project Anchors, and Risk Conditions](assets/figures/key-areas.png)

### Zhongzhiyuan AI Independent Innovation Acceleration Area: Full Stack Validation Loop

Conceptual goals are to transform the closed test into an innovative district that is "viewable, bookable, and regulable." The northern section sets up a model safe governance sandbox, joint testing of edge-side computing and low-carbon energy, open standard workshops, and Qinghe innovation interface; it manages external display and sensitive test zone partitioning. Qinghe Public Spaces are only suggested with lightweight reversible facilities, and no engineering sections are proposed for flood control, blue lines, and ecological conditions until verified [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-001] [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#PUBLIC-001].

### Beijing AI Origin Community: Open Source Transformation Loop

Conceptual goals are to shorten the "paper/code—prototype—trial—review—transformation" walking distance. Arrange open-source release halls, intellectual property and ethics consultations, transformation hubs, talent living services, and community co-evaluation rooms along the interfaces of campuses, parks, and neighborhoods; serving both university teams and allowing residents to have knowledge, feedback, and exit rights regarding the scenarios entering Public Spaces [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-002] [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#PUBLIC-002].

### Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster: Smart Living Ring

The conceptual goal is to verify whether agents, smart terminals, and content services can be responsibly adopted in a real, high-density, mixed urban environment. Propose an international grand hall for transit connections and smart agent bazaars, public service experience platforms, and optimization of non-motorized traffic order around rail transit connections and quadrants. Any station-city integration, bridge and tunnel renovations, or intersection modifications must be separately argued by traffic, municipal, and rail transit professional teams [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-003] [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#PUBLIC-003].

All three key areas are expressed in six columns for function, building carrier, Public Space, traffic organization, operational responsibility, and data gaps [depth:three_key_area_detailed_design], without using a provisional rectangle to replace the site design.

## AI Public Space, Pilgrimage Landmark and Honor Display

Four conceptual landmarks along the public spine form a narrative of "Engineering—Open Source—Pact—Dialogue," without pursuing high-energy visual spectacles [metric:pilgrimage_landmark_count]:

1. **Signal Tower Zero**: Explain the model validation, standards, and safety governance using railway signal language; lightweight and reversible.
2. **Open Source Milestone Walkway**: Record reusable contributions by time and license, allowing contributors to correct or retract inappropriate content.
3. **Intelligent Agent Convention Square**: Publicly display the rules regarding public interest, privacy, explainability, and human ultimate judgment.
4. **Centennial Dialogue Platform**: Let the history of railway engineering, the innovation history of Zhongguancun, and the new culture of AI engage in dialogue within the same public forum.

The honor system does not rank based on funding amount or traffic, but rather on public value, reusability, completeness of evidence, and contributions to long-term maintenance; any permanent display requires confirmation from the maintainer, rights holder, public, and relevant authority [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#PUBLIC-001].

## Land Use, Building Scale, and Retain-Renovate-Demolish Strategy

`land_use.geojson` is generated by a shared tangent to create a comprehensive zoning that fully covers the provisional site without overlapping. A strip of approximately 13% in the central area is conceptualized as a green corridor park zone, with an adjacent 8% serving as a low-carbon transit backbone, while the remainder is allocated according to the north, center, and south relationships for research and development, education, services, living, and the city living room [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-004] [metric:green_ratio].

The buildings adopt a four-tier decision tree of "preserve first—low-disturbance renovation—reversible insertions—update when conditions are ripe." Due to the lack of current as-built drawings and property rights, this package does not designate any actual buildings for demolition or new construction; the nine `BUILDING_FOOTPRINT` are merely prototype functional carriers [depth:retain_renovate_demolish]. The height, massing, and roof form follow the principle suggestion of "reducing visual impact by lowering the buildings near the heritage and green corridors, increasing public space at the ground level, and forming a continuous pedestrian interface across the street," with all numerical values pending official master planning and specific conditions [depth:height_massing_character] [metric:building_height_m].

## Transport, Rail, Municipal Infrastructure, and Public Services

Traffic is organized around a concept network of "two longitudinal low-carbon lines + three east-west stitching lines": the pedestrian main artery is close to the heritage park, with cycling and service connections running alongside, serving three categories of innovation loops [data:geometry/roads.geojson#ROAD-001] [metric:mobility_corridor_count]. The routes express connections, not road boundaries, bridge and tunnel schemes, or construction alignments [depth:traffic_rail_slow_parking].

Municipal and new infrastructure are advised to adopt "shared interfaces rather than separate server room lists": end-side computing power, distributed energy, environmental sensing, and public service devices should first undergo a capacity review in five areas—energy, security, maintenance, network, and privacy—before determining scale and location [depth:municipal_new_infrastructure]. Road area, pipeline, fire safety, flood control, and energy load are all unknown [metric:road_area_sqm].

## Blue-Green Network, Public Space, and Urban Character

The blue-green system is not a residual after industrial development but forms the carrying base for the One Track Three Rings. A central continuous green corridor connects the north, center, and south, while three horizontal shared green corridors integrate universities, parks, communities, and rail transit stations. The area and proportion of green spaces are recalculated using the same geometry [data:geometry/green_space.geojson#GREEN-001] [metric:green_space_area_sqm] [metric:green_ratio] [depth:blue_green_public_space].

Public Space operates in a time-segmented manner, following the sequence of "quiet daily use—collaborative display—controlled testing—annual events," to avoid intense year-round activities at all nodes. Urban Character retains the scale, materials, and construction honesty of the railway project, expressing it in contemporary terms through the use of copper, weathering metals, gray bricks, and replaceable information panels; no historical elements should be falsified or cultural significance reduced to technological decoration.

## Jing-Zhang Centennial—Zhongguancun—AI New Cultural Narrative

The narrative is divided into three chapters: **Autonomous Engineering** discusses the independent engineering capabilities represented by Zhan Tianyou and the Jing-Zhang Railway; **Open Innovation** speaks to the innovative culture formed by knowledge flow, market experimentation, and community collaboration in Zhongguancun; **Collaborative Intelligence** explores how humans and intelligent agents can collaborate within public rules. Each historical fact in the signage must be traceable back to publicly available historical records, and AI-generated explanations must be identified and open to correction by experts or the public.

International promotion does not trumpet the slogan "Future cities have been realized," but rather narrates with "A century of engineering. A commons for intelligence," emphasizing the continuity between century-long engineering and an open commons. The cultural sign system and overall logo are managed separately to avoid the historical resources being branded and swallowed up.

## Global AI Innovation Ecosystem and Long-Term Operations

Annual operations follow a "Seasons per Year with One Archive Each" approach: spring for task release, summer for scenario testing and public evaluation, autumn for the Global AI Commons Week, and winter for evidence auditing and maintainer meetings; the entire year maintains an open scenario directory and contribution archive. The suggested organizational structure includes a Public Interest Committee, a Technical Expert Group, a Residents/Users Group, a Developer Community, and an Independent Data Ethics Group; any high-risk scenario can be paused.

The path from activity to transformation is "public problem identification—small-scale prototyping—third-party evaluation—public feedback—professional deepening—application procurement/community adoption—continuous auditing." This approach does not replace long-term operations with a single roadshow, nor does it treat talent, businesses, funding, or policy support as already determined [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK] [standard:PROJECT-AGENT-OPEN-CALL-TASKBOOK].
