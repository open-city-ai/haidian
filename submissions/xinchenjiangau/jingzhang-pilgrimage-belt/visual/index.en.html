Three-level scope

The proposal proceeds from coordinated research scope to overall design scope to key-area scope, binding industrial judgment, urban renewal, and key-area design to layers and metrics.

project_scope_summary.csv

Coordinated research scope

For ~43.6 km²: AI industrial ecology, three-area two-wing coordination, future urban form, and cultural narrative.

  • AI innovation and talent chains
  • Global case transfer mechanism
  • Naming and communication

Overall design scope

For ~11.4 km²: urban renewal, land-use structure, transport and municipal systems, and the heritage-park vitality belt.

  • Control-plan-depth urban design
  • Renewal project list
  • Blue-green slow-mobility system

Detailed design for three key areas, explaining function, building renewal, public space, and transport organization.

  • Zhongzhiyuan AI Acceleration Area
  • Beijing AI Origin Community
  • Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster

Key areas — Three Halls

Each key area states its industrial positioning, spatial strategy, building and public-space actions, transport interface, and implementation risks.

key_areas.geojson

Origin Hall · Zhongzhiyuan

Garden-type autonomous innovation district. National platforms, industrial display, Qing River culture, external transport, and low-carbon exchange space.

Translation Hall · Beijing AI Origin Community

Near-campus commercialization district. University sourcing, open-source community, talent services, retain-renovate-demolish, and station integration.

Application Market · Dazhongsi

Urban-type intelligent economy district. Leading enterprises, agent-driven new formats, commercial services, green-space mixed use, and four-quadrant pedestrian linkage.
