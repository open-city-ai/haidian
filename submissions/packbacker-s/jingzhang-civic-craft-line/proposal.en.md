# THE CIVIC CRAFT LINE｜京张百工线

## Design Basis and Source List

The Civic Craft Line is an open-call concept package, not a statutory plan, approval document or engineering scheme. It relies on the repository brief, the agent taskbook, registered public sources and the listed planning references. The submitted design envelope and three key areas are provisional public-data constraints. They are useful for coherence checks, but they are not official redlines, parcels or permissions. Official polygons must trigger a full recalculation of every design layer and metric, rather than a cosmetic map replacement. ## Three-Level Scope Framework

The proposal links the coordinated research area, overall design area and three key areas through one evidence chain. Research asks how an innovation ecosystem becomes a public cycle; overall design asks how renewal, public space and daily services host it; key-area design asks who learns, tests, maintains, takes over and reviews a service. One line, three workshops, two wings and a hundred civic craft nodes make this hierarchy legible without inventing new statutory boundaries. [depth:three_level_scope_framework] [depth:overall_spatial_structure] [metric:site_area_sqm] [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-001]

## Coordinated Research Area: Industry and Future City Research

The strategic loop is: raise a public problem, make a prototype, explain it through open learning, test it in a bounded setting, maintain and review it, then return findings to the community. Rather than copying a named innovation district, the proposal draws from transferable types: research districts, university-transfer neighborhoods, public digital-service labs, developer communities, industrial-heritage public space and science-service networks. The brand uses a dark-blue field, copper line and three hand-made dots: reasoned infrastructure, time depth and the handoff between people, tools and common space. [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK] [standard:PROJECT-AGENT-OPEN-CALL-TASKBOOK] [depth:industry_ecosystem_strategy] [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-001]

## Overall Design Area: Urban Renewal and Regulatory-Plan-Level Urban Design

Four conceptual functional units cover the submitted envelope without overlap: innovation making and trusted testing; a craft park for open learning; application conversion and collaboration services; and community support with inclusive access. They are internal concept layers, not a land-use adjustment. The Craft Walking Line and two service connectors identify relationships that require field measurement, not road alignments or rail proposals. Renewal follows a careful sequence: understand official constraints, test reversible public-service prototypes, then deepen only what clears professional and public review. ## Detailed Design of Key Areas

Make Workshop at Zhongzhiyuan is a visible interface for prototypes, safety explanation, trusted testing and standards dialogue. Learn Workshop at the AI Origin Community is a shared room for courses, open collaboration, talent support and plain-language explanation. Use Workshop at Dazhongsi is an everyday testbed where release, repair, feedback and service review are visible. Each workshop includes a public-space action, a human handoff and a gate before any real deployment. The three provisional key-area polygons are task-scale diagrams, not exact extents. ## AI Innovation Ecosystem, Personas, and AI+ Scenarios

Five perspectives define the service contract: researchers need reproducible test space; learners need open access; small teams need explainable support; residents need equal offline and accessible service; operators need named maintenance and exit conditions. Ten scenario cards link place, data, human review and stop conditions: prototype usability desk, trusted-service label, repair station, open course roster, accessible route prompt, anonymous community question desk, environmental observation, science-service consultation, public-event access notice and a review wall. The first three are test or validation scenarios, not confirmed operations. [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK] [depth:ai_scenarios_and_personas] [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#PUBLIC-001]

## Land Use, Building Scale, and Retain-Renovate-Demolish Strategy

Height, massing, street interface and urban character are a later design question of legibility, shade and maintainability; they are not numerical controls in this concept package. [depth:height_massing_character]

The submitted land-use layer is a topologically complete conceptual partition. A single conceptual footprint makes the making space inspectable but says nothing about an existing building inventory, property rights, demolition, retention or construction. FAR, height, density, parking, total floor area and statutory building scale are intentionally unknown. A professional follow-up must establish official inventory and ownership first, then compare retain, repair, renew or build alternatives with public communication and risk conditions. [standard:MNR-LAND-USE-CLASSIFICATION-GUIDE] [depth:retain_renovate_demolish] [metric:building_footprint_area_sqm] [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-002]

## Transport, Rail, Municipal Infrastructure, and Public Services

The transport idea is a daily chain of walking, cycling, transfer, rest, wayfinding, repair and offline help. The three submitted connector lines are conceptual prompts for field verification of continuity, accessibility breaks, shade, night reading, event-day flow and walking experience. Infrastructure follows public service before device deployment: no sensing, edge computing, distributed energy or new equipment is promised before power, drainage, fire, maintenance, emergency and accessibility conditions are reviewed. [standard:MOHURD-CONTROL-DETAILED-PLANNING] [depth:traffic_rail_slow_parking] [depth:municipal_new_infrastructure] [data:geometry/roads.geojson#ROAD-002]

## Blue-Green Network, Public Space, and Urban Character

Blue-green and public space are the operational ground for waiting, learning, discussion, repair and rest. The proposal suggests continuous vegetation, stay-able common rooms, rain-friendly interfaces and humanly supervised night lighting. It keeps paper guidance, staffed help, quiet rest, child and elder support, accessible routes and a right not to provide personal data as equivalent public-service paths. Three reusable landmarks are not monuments: a prototype explanation table, a learning long table and a maintenance-review wall. [standard:MOHURD-URBAN-DESIGN-MEASURES] [depth:blue_green_public_space] [data:geometry/green_space.geojson#GREEN-001] [metric:green_ratio] [metric:public_space_ratio]

## Renewal Projects, Implementation Policy, and Phasing

Implementation is organized as three gates, not a fixed construction list. Read the site by completing official constraints and public needs; run small reversible tests through guidance, courses, service tables and feedback walls only after site approval; hand over only services that clear safety, accessibility, privacy, maintenance and public-feedback review. A Craft Open Week, public-problem workshop, developer maintenance day and service-review exhibit are discussion prototypes, not confirmed events. [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK] [depth:renewal_project_list] [depth:phasing_implementation] [data:geometry/phasing.geojson#PHASE-001]

## Metrics, Area Recalculation, and Compliance Matrix

constraints.geojson is intentionally an empty collection: it records that official constraints are not yet available as public geometry, rather than inventing their location. The assumptions and self-check identify the pending review path. [data:geometry/constraints.geojson#CONSTRAINTS]

Known values are internally recalculable in EPSG:4548 from submitted concept layers: a provisional envelope of about 11.41 km2, a conceptual footprint of about 310,807 sqm, a green ratio of about 12.34 percent, a public-space ratio of about 7.33 percent and three key areas. These values describe this submission package only. FAR, height, floor area, parking, capacity and investment remain unknown. Compliance, standards and design-depth matrices connect each task to text, drawings, geometry, sources and review. ## Risk, Copyright, and Compliance

The central risk is mistaking provisional geometry and aspirational language for approved spatial decisions. The package therefore repeats the provisional boundary notice in geometry, metrics, assumptions, sources, figures, HTML and PDFs. Public scenarios use only voluntary, public or aggregated information and must provide human handoff, complaint feedback, accessible alternatives and stop conditions. All figures, symbols and text are original to this package; no remote map, photo, trademark, portrait or external asset is loaded.
