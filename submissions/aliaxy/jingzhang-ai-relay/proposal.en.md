# Jing-Zhang Centennial · Intelligent Relay Belt —— Urban Design Conceptual Proposal for the AI Innovation Corridor along the Jing-Zhang Heritage Park

**Overall Concept**: In 1909, the Jing-Zhang Railway demonstrated with its "person-shaped" alignment that Chinese engineers could construct their own railways; in the 1980s, Zhongguancun grew on both sides of this railway, becoming China's earliest innovation district; today, this linear site once again takes up the baton of "self-innovation and self-development." This plan reads the Jing-Zhang Heritage Park as a **relay track**: the green spine is the track, the three key areas are three **relay stations**, the Zhongguancun Technology Services Wing and the Xiaoyue River Scenario Enablement Wing are the **approach zones** on either side, and the AI scenario nodes are the **handoff points** along the way. The main name of the plan is **"Century Jing-Zhang·Smart Relay,"** with the English name **"The Centennial Relay — Jingzhang AI Innovation Belt,"** abbreviated as **"Smart Relay / AI Relay Belt."**

This scheme is a **Conceptual Recommendation and Reference Proposal** for the global open call for smart agents, intended for in-depth study by professional teams; it does not replace formal planning and does not constitute a government approval conclusion. All spatial geometry is based on the temporary rough boundaries generated from the warehouse registration ([source:BOUNDARY-SOURCE]), recalculated in EPSG:4548. Upon the Official Planning Boundary release, a complete recalculation must be performed.

## Coordinated Research Area: Industry and Future City Research

**Innovation Chain Assessment**: Along this corridor, Haidian has gathered universities, research institutions, leading AI companies, and open-source communities. However, the innovation chain is spatially fragmented—university achievements are confined within campuses, corporate demonstrations are showcased within their parks, and public experiences are largely absent. This proposal suggests an relay-style innovation chain: "Origin (universities/original community) → Acceleration (Zhongzhiyuan full-stack system) → Transformation (Dazhongsi intelligent economy) → Experience (ruins park green spine) → Dissemination (global event system)," with each link having a spatial carrier that can be precisely located [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK] [depth:overall_spatial_structure].

**Three Key Positions, Five Major Functions, and the Synergistic Loop of the Three Zones and Two Wings**: The Hundred-Year Jing-Zhang Cultural Belt is the "track" of historical legitimacy, the Urban AI Life Experience Belt is the public interface of the "track," and the AI Convergence Innovation Belt is the competition itself on the "track." The five major functions are distributed along the belt: the Full-Stack Independent AI Innovation System and AI governance global discourse authority are placed in R1 Zhongzhiyuan; the World-Class AI Innovation Ecosystem is placed in R2 Origin Community; intelligent native new business models are placed in R3 Dazhongsi; the AI-Enabled Scenario empowerment new paradigm is carried by the Xiaoyue River Scenario Enablement Wing and the Green Spine Scenario Nodes; and the intelligent AI vibrant city is borne by the entire belt's Public Space system. The two wings are connected to the main spine through four east-west stitching chains (refer to the transportation chapter), integrating the Zhongguancun science and technology service elements and Xiaoyue River scenario spaces, forming a "main spine relay, two wings supply" loop [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK].

Common Conclusion: World-class AI Innovation Ecosystem = **Density (proximity to fount institutions), Interface (public accessibility), Elements (computing power/data/capital/scenario-as-a-service), and Narrative (place cultural recognition)**. With all four elements present, what is missing is the Public Space system and operational mechanism to weave them together — this is precisely what Urban Design can provide [depth:existing_conditions_diagnosis].

**Future Urban Form**: AI does not change the form of buildings, but rather the timetable of spaces—blurring the boundaries between office and residence, normalizing nighttime collaboration and 24/7 testing, and sinking logistics and services into the street infrastructure layer. Therefore, this proposal does not seek iconic large-scale developments, but rather proposes "cell-level updates + ribbon-like public systems": maintaining the current road network and property fabric, and investing in green spines, plazas, seam chains, and end-side facilities ([data:geometry/public_space.geojson#PUB-004], [metric:scenario_node_count]).

**Naming System and Visual Identity Direction (.1)**: Main name "Jing-Zhang Centennial · Intelligent Relay Belt"; station names R1 Acceleration Station/R2 Source Station/R3 Transformation Station; green spine mainline named "Zhi Gua" (Intelligent Track); east-west pedestrian chain named "Seam Chain 1-4"; annual event "Jing-Zhang AI Relay Week." Logo direction: abstracts the "human-shaped" gradient of the Jing-Zhang Railway as an upward ∧ line, combining with the semantic of data pipeline/relay baton; suggested color scheme in three colors — platform green (ruins park), signal orange (scene vitality), deep track blue (full-stack autonomy); all fonts, graphics must be original or licensed, this proposal only provides direction, no unauthorized fonts, images, trademarks, or character images [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK]. The relationship between the visual identity system and the cultural sign system is discussed in the cultural chapter: the former is the overall brand of the one belt, while the latter is the sign of historical narrative expression, both share the "human-shaped" theme but are used in layers.

**Urban Renewal Overall Framework** [depth:retain_renovate_demolish]: Due to the lack of current building and ownership data, the plan expresses the **Demolish–Renovate–Retain Strategy**—rather than reaching **conclusions**—through methods. The three-tier renewal model includes: 1) **Retain and Enhance** (retaining existing research or residential buildings and transforming the first-floor facades and facilities); 2) **Transform and Insert** (incorporating open-source collaboration and technology transfer functions into underutilized buildings); 3) **Local New Construction** (for the station square and landmark nodes). Each conceptual mass in `buildings.geojson` is annotated with the three modes as method illustrations, and this should not be interpreted as a conclusion regarding the demolition, renovation, or retention of any actual plot.

## Land Use, Building Scale, and Retain-Renovate-Demolish Strategy

Land-Use Layout and proportions are detailed in Table [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-001] of the Overall Design chapter; this chapter supplements the architectural and demolish–renovate–retain logic [depth:height_massing_character] [depth:retain_renovate_demolish]. (Demolish–Renovate–Retain Strategy)

**Building Form and Height Districting (Conceptual Recommendation, Pending Zoning Confirmation)**: Within 150 meters of the Green Spine, multi-story buildings are recommended (eaves ≤ 24 meters), to ensure the park's skyline aligns with the scale of the archaeological site. In the middle segment of the Research and Development Belt, mid- to high-rise buildings are suggested (conceptually 9 stories/approximately 38 meters). Along the Third Ring Road interface in the Dazhongsi segment, height can be increased moderately (conceptually 6-12 stories), but the surrounding cultural heritage areas should transition with lower-rise buildings. The roof forms are encouraged to promote the "fifth facade" treatment: incorporating photovoltaics, rooftop gardens, and hidden equipment. All height values are design recommendations ([data:geometry/buildings.geojson#BLDG-010] `height_status` field individually annotated as `design_suggestion_pending_official_controls`).

**Metric Recalculation**: The conceptual base [metric:building_footprint_area_sqm] of 93.9 million square meters, the total conceptual scale [metric:total_floor_area_sqm] of 750 million square meters, and the conceptual intensity [metric:floor_area_ratio_concept] of 0.66, can all be recalculated for each group of volumes in the `geometry/buildings.geojson` file [depth:metrics_recalculation] under EPSG:4548. **Dismantle-Renovate-Retain Strategy**: The methodological approach is "three-tier update model + negative list" (preserve cultural heritage, preserve green and blue lines, preserve properties with unclear ownership); the proportions and objects must be determined after a census of the existing buildings. ## Transport, Rail, Municipal Infrastructure, and Public Services

**Traffic Structure** [depth:traffic_rail_slow_parking]: Maintain the existing arterial road pattern (conceptual indication of the positions of North Third Ring Road, Zhi Chun Lu, Cheng Fu Lu, and Tsinghua Dong Lu corridor, [data:geometry/roads.geojson#ROAD-004]), and add two design elements at two levels: 1) Two north-south **service streets** (Innovation Service Street West and Scene Service Street East, at the level of minor arterial circulation, [data:geometry/roads.geojson#ROAD-001]); 2) Four **east-west connectivity chains** (pedestrian priority, Chain 1: Dazhongsi to Quadrant Four, Chain 2: North side of North Third Ring Road, Chain 3: Open Source Square to Wudaokou direction, Chain 4: Zhongzhiyuan, [data:geometry/roads.geojson#ROAD-011]). The green spine's "smart track" slow-moving mainline is fully connected within the green spine ([data:geometry/roads.geojson#ROAD-003]), with the east-west arterial roads connected by existing bridge underpasses and intersection renovations (engineering feasibility pending specialized argumentation, this plan does not provide conclusions on bridge or tunnel construction).

## Blue-Green Network, Public Space, and Urban Character

**Blue-Green System** [depth:blue_green_public_space]: Total green space area [metric:green_space_area_sqm] 170.5 million sqm, green space ratio [metric:green_ratio] 14.9% (including park green space [data:geometry/green_space.geojson#GREEN-002] + North Fifth Ring Road green belt + 3 pocket parks); Public Space area [metric:public_space_area_sqm] 46.8 million sqm, public space ratio [metric:public_space_ratio] 4.1% (including Three Stations Plaza + Three Segments of Vitality Fields [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#PUB-005]). North-South connection relies on the green spine, while east-west connection depends on the seam chain; Qinghe and Xiao Yuehe rivers serve as off-site blue line resources, connecting through the Qinghe Gateway Green Corridor and the Scene Empowerment Wing (blue line conditions not obtained, water-related conclusions pending confirmation). Proportion of road land use [metric:road_land_ratio] 7.0% ([metric:road_land_area_sqm] 79.3 hectares), with the intention of returning street space to slower modes as much as possible.

**AI Public Space and Pilgrimage Landmarks (agent.4, ≥3 locations)**:

1. **"Character Gate"**(R1 Acceleration Station): A portal construction/observation frame inspired by the Jing-Zhang Railway's character-shaped alignment, serving as a monument to "innovative self-reliance," and also a spatial prototype (conceptual landmark) with a full-band logo (the location and structure need further refinement).
2. **「R2 Strategy Source Station」**(R2 Strategy Source Station): A growing wall inscribed with the names of open-source contributors and agents, paired with a digital twin interface, forming the main node of the honor display system—capable of initially accommodating all participating agents from this open-source call (responding to the Co-Creation Charter's "contributions are memorable").
3. **「Dazhong·New Sound」**(R3 Transformation Station): A digital soundscape installation derived from the cultural heritage of Dazhongsi ancient bells. The ancient bell samples (require authorization from the venue) and generative soundscapes will "relay" the chimes at the hour (conceptual landmark, with cultural preservation conditions taking precedence).
4. **"Milestone Pathway" (Throughout the Route):** Set kilometer markers along the smart rail line, with each kilometer corresponding to a milestone in China's history of independent innovation and AI history. The endpoint leaves room for the future.

Public Space Component Library (Concept): Smart Poles, Bookable Test Field Perimeter, Mobile Podiums, Shadow and Seat Modules, Accessible Wayfinding Posts —— Unifying the "platform" design language, drawing from the form memory of railway station platforms.

**Urban Character and Cultural Narrative (agent.5)**: The urban character is a blend of the "railway industrial base" of "Station Green + Brick Red + Steel Gray" with a light and transparent contemporary layer. The narrative thread is "three relays of independent innovation": 1909 Tsinghua University built the Jing-Zhang Railway (engineering autonomy) → 1980s Zhongguancun Electronic Street (market autonomy) → 2020s AI full-stack autonomy (technological system autonomy). Spatial cultural carriers: the Tsinghua Garden Station and other site buildings (utilization must be approved by cultural heritage authorities) serve as narrative anchors, the milestone path as a linear gallery, and the three stations square as contemporary chapters. Signage system direction: borrow the semantic language of railway signals (armboard signal shapes, station sign formats, mileage markers), bilingual + machine-readable (each sign has an open data interface and accessible voice, facilitating human and intelligent body "reading" of the city); the cultural signage system and one belt one logo system are layered: the logo for brand recognition, the signage for narrative and navigation, sharing the arched motif. International communication narrative (conceptual copy direction): "A century ago, this line carried coal and courage." Today it carries code and community."——A century ago, this line transported coal and courage, today it conveys code and community.
