# Tianyou Second Track · TIANYOU CONTEXT RAIL——From Standard Gauge to Public Context

> In 1909, Zhan Tianyou led trains across mountains and rivers on a unified track gauge. In 2126, the city will need a second gauge: allowing different AI systems to understand the same city without overstepping human rights boundaries. By 2226, the city will be capable of self-learning, self-maintenance, and self-adaptation, but humans must continue to hold the switches, signals, and emergency brakes.

## Design Basis and Source List

This proposal addresses not "how much AI can fit into Haidian," but rather "who decides what AI knows, what it can do, and how to stop it when it goes wrong" when AI becomes an infrastructure for roads, education, healthcare, research, and business services. Therefore, the core product is not a super app or a closed-off campus, but a set of **public context gauge** with physical space: the gauge corresponds to interoperability protocols, the ties correspond to source and authorization records, the signal lights correspond to permission gates, the switches correspond to choices and exits, the timetables correspond to regular reviews, and the emergency brakes correspond to human takeovers. [source:OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT] [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK] [standard:PROJECT-OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT] [standard:PROJECT-AGENT-OPEN-CALL-TASKBOOK]

This version is build 1.5, which has brought the conceptual vision into calculable layers, conditional trigger projects, governance gates, and truly readable drawings; the version number is only used for asset tracking and does not indicate government approval or planning clearance.

The design is based on the `brief/site-package/`, registration documents, and the processed fact package; the processing package is merely a navigation tool and not an additional authoritative source. **English executive thesis.** Tianyou Context Rail imagines a city where artificial intelligence becomes civic infrastructure without becoming an invisible sovereign. The proposal translates railway engineering into a public protocol: gauge means interoperability, sleepers mean provenance, signals mean permission, switches mean human choice, timetables mean periodic revalidation, and emergency brakes mean human override.The physical corridor is therefore inseparable from governance. Every context station is a place where residents can see which system is active, inspect its purpose, refuse optional data use, request a human service, report harm, and trigger a pause.No resident must surrender a personal profile to receive a baseline public service.

The plan works across three scales.At the coordinated research scale, Haidian becomes the steward of a federated context protocol rather than a warehouse of regional data.At the overall design scale, the historic Jingzhang corridor becomes a climate-adapted public spine linking research, neighbourhood life and enterprise adoption. At the detailed scale, three Context Works form a complete innovation chain: Proof Yard in Zhongzhiyuan tests safety and standards;Skill Foundry in the AI Origin Community converts local knowledge into licensed and reversible civic skills;Adoption Exchange in Dazhongsi helps enterprises and public services understand cost, accountability and exit before deployment. This is a proposal for professional deepening, not an approved government plan.

The 2126 horizon tests whether common protocols can remain open across generations.The 2226 horizon tests a harder condition: a city may learn, repair and reconfigure itself, but it may not remove human authority over public decisions.Expansion is conditional on evidence.A scenario that fails accessibility, privacy, safety, labour, climate or non-digital service tests must stop. The city records failures as public learning instead of hiding them as technical debt.This makes the Second Rail an institution of accountable change rather than a monument to automation.

Third layer, approximately 368.4 hectares of three key areas become Proof Yards, Skill Foundries, and Adoption Exchanges, validating the complete "research—test—transform—adopt—maintain—retire" chain. ## Overall Design Area: Urban Renewal and Regulatory-Plan-Level Urban Design

Overall structure is "one track, three workshops, two wings, and twelve stations." The one track forms a public pedestrian and contextual spine along the Jing-Zhang Heritage Park; the two wings connect the eastern side innovation institutions with the western side community, schools, and public services. Five east-west branch lines prioritize identifying breakpoints, without committing to bridge or tunnel engineering when road rights-of-way, traffic volumes, and municipal conditions are lacking. Six categories of conceptual land uses are fully zoned according to standard codes: northern validation and research, knowledge public service and educational collaboration, original skill forging, AI life and community services, Dazhongsi adopting exchange, and southern cultural portal. [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-01] [data:geometry/roads.geojson#ROAD-01] [depth:land_use_layout]

![Six engineering components of public contextual gauge](assets/figures/context-protocol.png)

Urban Renewal methods adhere to the principle of "first investigate, then evaluate, and finally consider reversibility": first complete the investigation of the building's year of construction, structure, ownership, use, carbon emissions, and cultural value; then distinguish between preservation, restoration, adaptive reuse, reversible insertions, and necessary updates; only after obtaining official control, ownership negotiation, and professional evaluation should the discussion of demolition take place. The 12 building footprints in this package are merely functional test fits, used to demonstrate how a rhythm can be formed with public ground floors, artificial services, experiments, learning, adoption, and cultural spaces in the corridor, not as a judgment of the current buildings, a demolition list, or building permits. [data:geometry/buildings.geojson#BLDG-01] [depth:development_intensity_controls] [depth:height_massing_character] [depth:retain_renovate_demolish]

In 2126, the second rail could serve as a public protocol layer between cities; in 2226, buildings and facilities could adapt to climate, population, and usage feedback, but every spatial change must include a source, impact assessment, public objection, and reversible path. The sense of futurism does not come from floating towers, but from a city's courage to expose its algorithmic boundaries, failures, and corrections on its streets.

## Detailed Design of Key Areas

**Zhongzhiyuan / Proof Yard.** The northern focus area is dedicated to full-stack independent innovation, secure governance, and standard setting. Spatially, it is composed of a "model wind tunnel—city agent rehearsal field—open observation platform—Qinghe climate room," forming a test chain that is viewable but access-controlled. Red team testing is separated from public display; any testing involving real individuals must not use data collected from open spaces. Low-speed robots operate only under geofenced conditions, with speed limits, and with physical emergency stops.KPI Not "number of uploads," but test coverage, unresolved high-risk items, success rate of manual takeovers, and event recovery time.[data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-001]

**Beijing AI Origin Community / Skill Foundry Skills Forge.** The central focus area converts university, open-source community, startup, and resident knowledge into urban skills. Each skill must carry a source, license, scope of application, version, validity period, maintainer, prohibited uses, test records, and decommissioning explanation. Spatially, it sets up a public learning hall, open-source workshop, achievement release small plaza, talent living services, and human service windows. Personalized education can only be an optional enhancement; free public versions and teacher services do not rely on personal profiles. [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-002]

**Dazhongsi / Adoption Exchange Uses an Exchange Station.** The southern focus area is not a terminal product showroom but rather a "pre-deployment clinic" for businesses and public entities: clarifying total costs, supplier lock-in, data flows, employee impact, cybersecurity, failure recovery, and exit funds. Around the rail station, commercial interface, and public cultural spaces, form business adoption clinics, international collaboration lounges, terminal experiences, and railway memory banks; the four quadrants at the intersections must await verification of transportation, municipal, and rail conditions. [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-003] [depth:three_key_area_detailed_design]

![Three Spaces Divided by Context Works](assets/figures/key-areas.png)

Three key areas share the same permit ledger but do not share unnecessary original data. Proof Yard issues a "testable" proof, Skill Foundry issues a "reusable" statement, and Adoption Exchange issues an "adoptable/not adoptable" report; a public schedule continuously records whether the proofs remain valid after model updates. In this way, the north, center, and south are not three isolated islands but a line of innovation with signals, transfers, maintenance, and decommissioning.

## Land Use, Building Scale, and Retain-Renovate-Demolish Strategy

Six land use categories are conceptual structures rather than control zone parcels, using registered spatial land use codes, and are fully covered, seamless, and non-overlapping within the temporary overall boundary. [standard:MNR-LAND-USE-CLASSIFICATION-GUIDE] [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-02] The program_code layer describes future functions but does not replace the `land_use_code`. Research and education zones prioritize the allocation of shared experimental, open learning, and small transformation spaces; community service zones ensure daily life and non-digital services; commercial service zones accommodate clinics and international exchanges; cultural zones address railway memory and public interpretation.

The architectural strategy adopts the principles of "carbon retention priority, ground floor public space priority, reversible insertion priority, and nighttime zone priority." Buildings with higher current value or structural usability are prioritized for adaptive reuse; new elements should be modular, repairable, and movable; AI equipment rooms should not encroach on the public ground floor; switch hall, manual windows, accessible restrooms, workers' rest areas, and nighttime safety points become small but stable public bases. Professional deepening rules are proposed for Building Height, massing, roof forms, and sightlines: protect the continuity of the rail space, avoid forming enclosed walls along the line, conduct on-site evaluations for key sightlines, and zone the nighttime light environment; no unsupported numerical values are provided. [standard:MOHURD-ARCH-DESIGN-DEPTH-2016]

The current Building Footprint area is only the re-calculated value of the 12 reversible prototype test fits, and does not represent the current built volume, demolition and construction quantities, or the planned total. Formal deepening must complete current surveying, structural assessment, title and leasehold investigations, carbon assessment, cultural heritage review, control plan conditions, and fire and municipal infrastructure verification, before forming a per-building demolish–renovate–retain table. ## Transport, Rail, Municipal Infrastructure, and Public Services

The second track is a continuous, shaded, and barrier-free public pedestrian line; the eastern wing accommodates continuous cycling and connects with innovation institutions; the western wing serves the community, schools, parks, and daily services; five east-west branch lines at this stage only express "the need for stitching," without treating the analysis lines as bridges, tunnels, or road rights-of-way. [data:geometry/roads.geojson#ROAD-02] [depth:traffic_rail_slow_parking] Priority is given to transferring at the station for the last 200-500 meters: safe crosswalks, continuous ramps, redundant elevators, nighttime visibility, bicycle parking, and indoor-outdoor transitions for rain and snow weather.

![Traffic Slow Zone and Blue-Green Public Space Composite System](assets/figures/mobility-bluegreen.png)

Municipal infrastructure and New Infrastructure follow the principle of "edge priority, minimal data, offline availability, and manual recovery." Edge-side computational power processes only necessary real-time tasks; data requiring inter-institutional exchange is shared through licensed and source fields; basic public services have offline caches, paper workflows, and manual windows; any model updates must be re-verified. Energy, drainage, flood prevention, fire safety, underground spaces, road right-of-way, and computational heat loads are not yet obtained, and this plan does not claim capacity feasibility. [data:geometry/constraints.geojson#CONSTRAINT-01] [depth:municipal_new_infrastructure]

Parking policies do not encourage the addition of more private cars to enhance accessibility in the innovation district. Prioritizing public transit, walking, cycling, barrier-free shuttle services, and shared logistics is preferred. Robot and delivery vehicle settings include time-of-day and zone-specific, low-speed rules, and cannot encroach on pedestrian paths, fire safety, or children's play areas. All engineering actions will involve traffic simulation, barrier-free co-verification, utility probing, emergency drills, and public engagement.

## Risk, Copyright, and Compliance

The first risk is the absence of boundaries and control conditions. The overall boundary, key areas, railway centerline proxy, and all design layers are not official planning boundaries; after the official polygon release, the land use, roads, green spaces, Public Spaces, buildings, phasing, indicators, drawings, and HTML must be rebuilt. The second risk is the lack of ownership, buildings, municipal, traffic, cultural heritage, and fire safety documentation; therefore, no demolition is specified, no commitment to cross-road works, no commitment to intensity, and no funding is promised. The third risk is turning an AI city into a surveillance city; therefore, minimal data, purpose limitations, human takeover, equal non-digital channels, event transparency, and decommissioning funds are release conditions. [depth:risk_missing_data] (Official Planning Boundary)

The fourth risk is that "the future" only serves high-income talent. This plan includes the elderly, children, people with disabilities, front-line workers, small and medium merchants, and device-less populations in the rights baseline; AI premium services must not form closed enclaves; open access to foundational Skill, barrier-free information, and Public Spaces must be supported by public funds. The fifth risk is long-term maintenance and model lock-in; procurement will require exportable, migratable, versioned, and traceable source and license information, with no exit options for non-compliant schemes entering public infrastructure.
