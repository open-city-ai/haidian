### North China University of Architecture and Technology Method Calibration and Hangzhou's Inspiration in Digital Governance

This round of deepening does not replicate any university works' images or specific solutions, but translates the methods from Beijing Architecture University's public teaching and practice into the evidence language of this project: people-centric design, full lifecycle, spatial flexibility, and digital operation and maintenance entering the design together; heritage renewal adopts small-scale physical interventions combined with digital content and multi-party collaboration, addressing real usage and maintenance issues first, then adding interactive technology. [source:BUCEA-GOOD-HOUSE-20250626] [source:BUCEA-TIANQIAO-WALK-20250626] The drawings thus add the hierarchy of "overall structure—management zone—site section—module construction—operational rules," retaining Beijing Architecture University's common analytical expression approach but not claiming to represent the school's official design style.

The Hangzhou city brain case provides a layered governance inspiration: central aggregation, inter-departmental coordination, dashboard display, and specific scenario services should be kept separate. Digital systems should serve human decision-making rather than replace enforcement. [source:HANGZHOU-CITY-BRAIN-20201022] This plan adopts a three-tier architecture of "on-site signage/human services—district station—joint command point." Booking only manages the activity zone and does not bind ordinary park access to the app. In the event of a network outage, it falls back to paper-based contingency plans, fixed signage, radio, and manual patrols. The above cases are background-only and do not support the project's redline, capacity, cost, or engineering feasibility conclusions.

## Coordinated Research Area: Industry and Future City Research

### Three Key Orientations, Five Major Functions, and the Three Zones and Two Wings Loop

The three defining positions are not three parallel slogans, but three different time scales within the same space: the Jing-Zhang Cultural Belt preserves the city's memory over a century, the Urban AI Experience Belt allows ordinary people to perceive and choose whether to participate in AI scenarios, and the AI Integration Innovation Belt enables models, computing power, data, scenarios, and governance to form an auditable circuit. The five functional areas along the circuit are divided as follows: Zhongzhiyuan takes on the "Full-Stack Independent AI Innovation System" and "AI Governance Global Discourse," the Origin Community takes on the "World-Class AI Innovation Ecosystem," the Xiaoyue River Scenario Enablement Wing organizes the "AI+Scenario Enabling New Paradigm" and the "Intelligent AI Vibrant City," the Zhongguancun Technology Services Wing provides intellectual property, legal, talent, capital, and international services, and Dazhongsi translates capabilities into city-scale intelligent native business forms. [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK]

Resource loops encompass eight categories of elements: land that only expresses spatial types without committing to supply; space that enhances reuse through shared ground floors, public living rooms, and reservable test segments; industry that forms a validation chain through model evaluations, edge-side intelligence, and smart terminals; funding that only proposes transparent and diverse mechanisms for research, without writing fiscal commitments; talent that forms long-term relationships through on-campus transformation, living services, and reputation contributions; computing power that adopts a concept architecture of collaborative edge nodes and centralized platforms, with capacity awaiting specialized argumentation; data that is managed through authorization, minimum necessity, and version tracking; and scenarios that adopt admission, sandbox, review, exit, and archiving protocols. Together, they constitute the future urban form of "capabilities being described, tested, and governed before entering the city."

These need to form a baseline in future operations, and no fabricated performance metrics are to be filled in this time.

### High-capacity large parks' spatial carrying logic

"Capacity for a large number of people" does not mean creating a super large hard plaza, but rather distributing peak pedestrian flow across five management zones, three reserved activity lounges, and continuous public promenades. Spatially, this involves multiple entrances, multiple destinations, loop paths, bidirectional daily passage, and partial enclosures for activity areas. Operationally, large events are broken down into multiple concurrent small scenes to avoid all viewers converging on a single entrance at the same time. The submitted layers include an additional joint operation and manual command point, 5 management/service stations, and 8 emergency logistics transition ports. [data:geometry/constraints.geojson#CMD-01] [metric:management_sector_count] [metric:joint_command_point_count] [metric:emergency_logistics_gate_count]

Peak visitor numbers are not included in this iteration. The formal capacity should be the minimum value among "available activity area capacity, net evacuation capacity, public transport capacity, on-site personnel configuration, and emergency/fire response capacity," and each activity should be individually calculated. [metric:peak_visitor_capacity_persons] The net evacuation width is also kept as unknown, [metric:minimum_clear_egress_width_m] which must be calculated after the road right-of-way, fencing, stalls, tree pits, accessible and fire lanes are all positioned. [depth:metrics_recalculation]

### Protocol for Entry, Operation, and Exit of the Open Model Scenario

The originality of the "Open Model Loop" is not just in its spatial form, but in framing each AI scenario as a pauseable contract for urban operation. Any scenario must pass through five gates:`G0 任务匹配` Confirm the service recipients align with public interest;`G1 数据最小化` Confirm the source, license, preservation period, and exit method;`G2 空间安全` Confirm general passage, accessibility, fire safety, meteorological, and equipment isolation; `G3 人工责任` Identify the site supervisor, professional reviewers, complaint intake, and physical closure method;`G4 限时试运行` Record events, reviews, and sunset date. If any do not meet the criteria, the scenario does not enter Public Space.[data:geometry/constraints.geojson#SC-01] [depth:municipal_new_infrastructure]

Run-time issues such as model output overreach, failed notifications, blocked accessible pathways, uncontrolled equipment, unresolved complaints, or weather or crowd conditions exceeding approved parameters should be addressed by the on-site supervisor pausing the technical functions and maintaining normal passage; decisions involving permits, enforcement, or emergency command should be left to authorized personnel. Exiting is not a failure but a part of the agreement: remove the equipment, delete or archive data as legally permitted, restore the ground and signage, and publicly review the outcome before deciding whether to retry after corrections or permanently exit. This agreement connects "perceptible scenarios" with "manageable cities," but does not imply that any scenario has been approved for operation. [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK] [standard:PROJECT-AGENT-OPEN-CALL-TASKBOOK]

### Zhongzhiyuan AI Independent Innovation Acceleration Area: Full Stack Validation and Governance Entry Point

The location is "the last kilometer validation before the model enters the city." The spatial structure adopts the concept of "a Qinghe Innovation Interface, a trusted evaluation core, and multiple controlled test courtyards." Building updates prioritize the identification of adaptable reuse research spaces, with public ground floors configured for model card reading, test scheduling, safety instructions, and professional review; no demolition judgments are made on the existing buildings. Slow travel connects the northern main corridor and the east-west Knowledge Cross Street with public transportation and the park entrance, without reaching a feasibility conclusion on specific cross-ring or road engineering. Public Spaces use enclosed test courtyards alongside continuous open passage paths to avoid testing occupying daily passage. AI scenarios concentrate on opening model evaluations, edge-side safety sandboxes, and coexistence experiments. Before implementation, Qinghe flood prevention, road red lines, municipal capacity, fire safety, and ownership data must be obtained.

### Beijing AI Origin Community: Open Source Collaboration and On-Site Conversion of Research Findings

The positioning is "an open-source conversion hub from original innovation to usable public products." The spatial structure adopts "a street for near-campus technology transfer, three shared living rooms, and a continuous living service interface." The architectural strategy prioritizes low-disturbance reuse, identifying and releasing candidate spaces for incubation, talent residence, and community services after completing the existing building survey; it does not pre-judge the ownership of campus or enterprise buildings. The focus on pedestrian movement is on the conceptual connection between the campus, park, and rail station, prioritizing the repair of pedestrian continuity, accessibility, and non-motorized vehicle parking. Station integration must be deepened by a transportation-specific study. Public Spaces include an open-source release platform, a public data authorization pod, and a talent service station. Implementation risks include the campus interface, the quality of existing buildings, ground-floor activities, resident impact, and activity noise, which require co-creative workshops and phased operating rules.

### Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster: Smart Natively Generated Business and International Exchange Gateway

The positioning is "to integrate intelligent agents, smart terminals, and content innovation into the real urban consumption and business environment." The spatial structure adopts "an international developer's living room, a smart terminal experience street, and a quadrants-based pedestrian connection concept." Building updates prioritize improving the public interfaces and shared ground floors around the enterprises, without specifying the renovation of enterprise buildings. The quadrants at the rail stations can be integrated through ground-level crossings, signage, organization of non-motorized vehicles, and the integration of station and city entrances, forming alternative combinations. Underground or bridge-tunnel solutions require separate engineering arguments. Public Spaces will host international roadshows, content displays, and data governance dialogues, while retaining ordinary commercial services for those not participating in AI experiences. Before implementation, the plan must be supplemented with station, road, pipeline, traffic flow, enterprise interfaces, and ownership documentation.

Three zones operate through three traceable object cycles: model versions enter the Origin Community from Zhongzhiyuan to form open-source or service interfaces, and then are accepted at Dazhongsi for a real but controlled urban experience; talent and enterprise needs flow back into the Origin Community and Zhongguancun Technology Services Wing; event, complaint, exit, and repair records return to the governance node in Zhongzhiyuan. This loop is a reference model, not a predetermined operational or governmental arrangement.

### low-cost modules, building physics, and boundaries of structural rationality

Construction strategies adopt a cost-staged approach of "prioritize the reuse of existing spaces—micro-updates on the ground—temporary service modules—permanent works only when necessary." In the short term, only paint coatings for signage, a dual-track information system with QR codes and printed materials, movable seating, and manual rules are provided; the second level adds standardized shading, drinking water, first aid, and management modules; the third level only transforms shared ground floors or public lobbies after obtaining ownership, structural, and municipal conditions. The total investment and annual operating costs are kept at unknown values, [metric:concept_capex_cny] [metric:annual_operating_cost_cny] calculated based on verified engineering quantities, benchmark unit prices, maintenance and replacement cycles, and contingency fees, without using the slogan of "low cost" to replace cost documents.

The module prototype uses a 3 m coordinated grid and a conceptual single span not exceeding approximately 6 m as a layout starting point, facilitating ordinary transportation, reassembly, and component replacement; this is merely an early coordinated modulus, not a structural finalization. It is recommended to prioritize the use of lightweight galvanized steel frames with bolt connections, replaceable shading panels, and drywall interior finishes, with supports provided at the ends or appropriate locations; the dimensions of the components, joints, column bases, foundations, seismic and wind loads, impact, and fatigue must be calculated by registered structural professionals. The foundation should prioritize reversible above-ground counterweights or low-disturbance methods, but their applicability depends on geotechnical conditions, underground utilities, tree roots, and cultural heritage constraints. [data:geometry/buildings.geojson#BLDG-001]

Building physics adopt a layered approach with "few air conditioning boxes and many open shading elements": continuous canopies are responsible for summer shading and rain retention, while enclosed service boxes only handle winter insulation and equipment protection; open modules maintain cross-ventilation, and enclosed entrances are equipped with adjustable wind barriers or vestibules; roof slopes, overflow, snow accumulation, condensation, waterproofing details, and surface drainage must be specifically calculated. Permeable paving, shallow swales, and tree pits are only used as source control concepts, do not occupy fire safety clear widths, and are not deployed when there is a lack of groundwater or flood control data. Daylighting, shading depth, wind environment, thermal comfort, lighting glare, energy consumption, and noise must be based on a review of Beijing's hourly weather data and specific site models.
