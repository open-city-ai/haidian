Core Concept: From Railway to Neuro-Pulse

Over a century ago, Zhan Tianyou led the construction of the Jingzhang Railway — the first trunk railway designed and built by the Chinese people [source:WEB-JINGZHANG-RAILWAY-HISTORY]. This railway was the "track" of national self-strengthening. A century later, this proposal transforms this corridor into China's first AI "neuro-pulse" — connecting cultural memory, living experience, and innovation.
