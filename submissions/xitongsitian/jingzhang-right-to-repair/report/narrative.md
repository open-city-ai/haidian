# 京张可修线 / JING-ZHANG REPAIR LINE

方案把百年京张 AI 创新带理解为城市 AI 的现场维修基础设施：设备可拆换、模型可回退、数据可纠错、服务可人工接管、材料可循环。空间结构为“一线三厂两翼十二工位”，分别在众智园、北京 AI 原点社区和大钟寺组织模型与机器人、开源文档与技能、设备循环与公共服务三类维修场。

本包使用仓库提供的临时粗略边界，只作生成、讨论和拓扑校验；它不是官方红线、审批依据或精确面积来源。完整中英文论证见 `proposal.md` / `proposal.en.md`。

The proposal treats the Centennial Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt as on-site repair infrastructure for urban AI: replaceable devices, reversible models, correctable data, human service takeover, and circular materials. Its spatial framework is one line, three depots, two wings, and twelve workstations.

Repository-provided provisional geometry supports generation, discussion, and topology checks only. It is not an Official Planning Boundary, approval basis, or precise-area source.
