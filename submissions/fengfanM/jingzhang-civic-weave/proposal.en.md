# Jing-Zhang Wisdom Weave: Verifiable Public Intelligence and AI Life Experiment Belt

**JINGZHANG CIVIC WEAVE — A Verifiable Public-Intelligence Belt**

"Jing-Zhang Intelligence Weave" does not treat AI as a label for the architectural facade but defines it as a set of services that the public can see, ask about, reject, review, and correct. The spatial structure is **"one spine one loop three nodes five fabric pieces"**: one spine is the century-old Jing-Zhang cultural spine and the blue-green slow travel spine; one loop is the public smart ring for research, transformation, testing, living, and dissemination that continuously loops back; three nodes correspond to Zhongzhiyuan, AI Origin community, and Dazhongsi; five fabric pieces are five types of reconfigurable carriers for R&D, transformation, public services, living culture, and blue-green open spaces. All on-ground actions are Conceptual Recommendations, to be further developed by professional teams after official polygon, control plan, traffic, cultural heritage, municipal, and ownership data are fully available.

## Coordinated Research Area: Industry and Future City Research

Overall naming is "Jing-Zhang Smart Weave," with the English name **JINGZHANG CIVIC WEAVE**. The logo is composed of two interwoven lines: the deep blue line represents the century-old railway and verifiable evidence, the aqua green line represents Public Space and continuous growth, and the orange nodes represent the moment when humans make the final judgment. The identification system does not use corporate trademarks, personal images, or unauthorized fonts; the primary language is "Smart Weave," with secondary nodes using the verbs "Source, Verify, Meet, Explore," and tertiary signage displaying "Where does the data come from, Who is responsible, How to exit." This is the brand direction, not an identifier adopted by the government. [standard:PROJECT-AGENT-OPEN-CALL-TASKBOOK]

Three key orientations are translated as spatial actions: the Hundred-Year Jing-Zhang Cultural Belt corresponds to the track spine narrative; the Urban AI Living Experience Belt corresponds to public services that are resident-friendly and visitor-understandable; and the AI Integration Innovation Belt corresponds to a closed loop from fundamental research to scenario validation. The five functional areas form a "research-open source-transformation-testing-governance" cycle, with each of the Three Zones and Two Wings playing different roles. The Zhongguancun Technology Services Wing provides legal, intellectual property, capital, and internationalization interfaces; the Xiaoyue River Scenario Enablement Wing takes on low-risk, reversible urban testing.

Six cases have been translated into six executable but non-replicating mechanisms: continuous Public Space, thematic district collaboration, open incubation living room, cultural content production, research transformation interface, and scenario triple helix. Each mechanism must simultaneously address "space carrier, operator, data boundaries, and how to exit after failure," thus avoiding merely pasting foreign place names into reports. The industrial ecosystem follows a basic process of Open Call, tiered testing, independent review, and public feedback; no commitments are made regarding companies, investment amounts, output values, or policies without source support. [depth:overall_spatial_structure]

## Overall Design Area: Urban Renewal and Regulatory-Plan-Level Urban Design

Overall structure is defined as "one ridge, one ring, three nodes, and five fabric pieces." The rail ridge continuously organizes north-south slow travel, cultural narratives, and rainwater adaptation; the public smart ring links demand proposal, sandbox testing, independent evaluation, public display, and iterative exit; the three nodes form recognizable north, central, and south anchor points; and the five fabric pieces support spatial adjustments through first-floor shared spaces, reversible modules, and public interfaces. [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-001] [depth:land_use_layout]

The control plan depth in this scheme is manifested as "complete objects, traceable evidence, and no fabrication of unknowns": land use is fully zoned, buildings are expressed with conceptual bases and typological update candidates, roads only express connectivity relationships, green spaces and Public Spaces can be recalculated, and the third phase scope fully covers the area. [data:geometry/buildings.geojson#BLDG-001] [data:geometry/phasing.geojson#PHASE-001]. But the Floor Area Ratio, height, Building Coverage Ratio, road redlines, four lines, and setbacks are all unknown; the scheme does not generate legally binding conclusions merely because of the completeness of the drawings. [depth:development_intensity_controls] [metric:floor_area_ratio]

Urban Renewal adopts a typological approach of "Preserve Value, Reduce Disturbance, Enhance Interfaces, and Reassess": objects with cultural, structural, or continuous use value are prioritized for retention; underperforming but upgradable objects are studied for adaptive reuse; gaps in public services are filled with reversible modules; and demolition is only discussed after a comparison of ownership, structure, cultural preservation, and carbon emissions. The current architectural layer of 36 bases is a spatial capacity prototype rather than a status quo survey or a list for the Demolish–Renovate–Retain Strategy. [depth:height_massing_character] [depth:retain_renovate_demolish] [metric:building_prototype_count]

## Detailed Design of Key Areas

Three key areas use provisional polygons, detailing the design expression of "location, spatial framework, carrier combination, public interface, scenarios, operations, and prerequisite conditions," without forming specific plot approval conclusions. [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-001] [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-002] [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-003] [depth:three_key_area_detailed_design]

**Zhongzhiyuan: Full-stack Validation Garden.** Focused on safety assessment, edge-side computing power, open-source toolchains, and standard co-creation, the garden divides closed testing and public display into different security levels. Qinghe Interface serves as a low-disturbance innovation garden, where the ground floor features a shared experimental front hall, equipment reservation, and governance consultation; the "Model Evidence Hall" only displays publicly available testing methods, limitations, and correction records. External transportation, riverways, and the Fifth Ring Road interface must await specialized review.

**Beijing AI Origin Community: Near School Open Source Converter.** Organize a pedestrian sequence through the "Achievements Exhibition Hall - Open Source Collaboration Corridor - Talent Living Station - Legal and Intellectual Property Clinic." Update with a priority on adaptive reuse and activating the first floor in small increments, providing collaborative spaces with varying lease terms and confidentiality levels; university achievements, personal data, and corporate information can only enter the service process with authorization. The connections at Wudaokou and the west entrance of Tsinghua East Road only express a pedestrian relationship and cannot be interpreted as an approved station-city plan.

**Dazhongsi: City Intelligent Native Living Room.** Four-quadrant pedestrian stitching as a conceptual framework, connecting intelligent bodies, smart terminals, content consumption, international showcases, and daily commerce. Public Space prioritizes addressing arrival, pause, shade, non-motorized vehicle order, and accessibility, before adding an exitable AI guide; "Data Element Living Room" only discusses authorization, auditing, and dispute resolution, without entertaining personal data transactions. All underground, tunnel, road, and rail interfaces require professional argumentation.

Ten fabric pieces adopt standard land use codes, merged to form six structural categories: research, green spaces and squares, commercial services, community services, education, and culture. [metric:land_use_research_area_sqm] [metric:land_use_green_open_area_sqm] [metric:land_use_commercial_area_sqm] [metric:land_use_community_area_sqm] [metric:land_use_education_area_sqm] [metric:land_use_culture_area_sqm]

Urban design strategies are not demolition schedules but a four-tier decision gate: first, conduct a value, structural, carbon, and ownership survey; then determine what to retain and adaptively reuse; prioritize reversible modules for addressing service gaps; and only discuss demolition or new construction after a full process comparison and statutory procedures permit. The conceptual Building Footprint area totals approximately 22.6 hectares, used only to validate spatial relationships, not to calculate formal building scale. [metric:building_footprint_area_sqm] [metric:building_height_m]

Height and massing follow the qualitative principles of "continuous track spine interface, moderate recognition of three joints, and friendly community scale": the first floor is transparent but does not expose sensitive R&D, the roof prioritizes the study of public energy and rainwater performance, and low-disturbance and reversible construction is used at cultural resource locations. Precise height, Floor Area Ratio, density, setback, and fire safety distances must be derived from official/professional sources and cannot be inferred from this layer.

## Transport, Rail, Municipal Infrastructure, and Public Services

Traffic strategies are based on the principle of "connect people first, then connect devices": a north-south greenway connects the three junctions, three east-west public stitch lines link the park, community, and urban interface, with the west side innovation service line and the east side public experience line forming an auxiliary loop. The conceptual network length is approximately 30.0 kilometers, representing connections rather than road boundaries, cross-sections, or bridge and tunnel engineering. [metric:concept_mobility_length_m] [depth:traffic_rail_slow_parking]

Address pedestrian crossings, accessibility, transfer information, and non-motorized vehicle order in the vicinity of the site before discussing mixed-use development; AI navigation can only serve as an auxiliary tool, not a substitute for traffic management and on-site signage. For slow-moving traffic discontinuities, a four-step process of "reversible temporary transition - manual observation - review by multiple departments - formal engineering" should be followed, and construction lines should not be drawn until traffic and safety arguments are completed. [data:geometry/roads.geojson#ROAD-002]

New infrastructure adopts a layered service model: the public layer provides network access, public data catalogs, and an artificial service desk; the innovation layer offers authorized computing power reservations and test environments; the governance layer maintains minimal audit logs, appeals mechanisms, and circuit breakers. Municipal capacity, energy load, fire safety, flood prevention, and pipeline data are not yet available and are listed as prerequisites without capacity commitments. [data:geometry/constraints.geojson#CONSTRAINTS] [depth:municipal_new_infrastructure]

## Risk, Copyright, and Compliance

The primary risk is not "lacking a beautiful rendering," but rather miswriting rough polygons, public text, or AI speculations as legal facts. `assumptions.json` therefore registers gaps in boundary lines, key areas, master plans, roads, land ownership, buildings, cultural heritage, municipal, and public service categories; any gap that remains unclosed automatically downgrades related conclusions to Conceptual Recommendation. [depth:risk_missing_data] [source:SOURCE-REGISTRY]

Privacy aspects include data minimization, explicit consent, client-side prioritization, tiered retention, revocability, and human appeal; high-impact judgments are not automatically enforced. Fairness requires accessible testing, non-intelligent alternative pathways, and community co-evaluation; when technical maturity is insufficient, models may be disabled without affecting basic services. Spatial disputes are resolved through official geometry, public opinion, and professional review, ensuring algorithms do not adjudicate ownership or approvals.

All core diagrams are derived from GeoJSON, metrics, and matrices included in this package, using local system fonts and original graphics, without remote map tiles, news images, logos, portraits, or generative renderings. Copyright and generation details are found in `report/copyright_statement.md`. This proposal does not claim official approval, government endorsement, guarantee of implementation, commitment of investment, statutory zoning, or final engineering feasibility.
