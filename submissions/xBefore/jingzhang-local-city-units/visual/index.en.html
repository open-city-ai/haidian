02 / UNIT TYPES

宽 · 密 · 厚WIDE · FINE · THICK

同一原则穿过不同地方网络后，不是产生三种造型，而是产生三套完整的城市组织方式。One rule passing through different local networks produces three complete forms of urban organization, not three shapes.

把大项目变成城市街区

Turn large projects into city districts

  • 公共街与基础市政先行，切分大尺度项目。
  • Public streets and utilities subdivide large projects first.
  • 公共外圈容纳共享服务，安全内核保持受控研发。
  • A civic outer ring serves shared uses; secure cores retain controlled R&D.
  • 物流和设备后勤外置，不与公共基准争用地面。
  • Freight and secure logistics move to outer edges.
  • 实施工具：供地、片区更新与基础设施交付条件。
  • Instrument: land, district renewal and infrastructure conditions.

让存量首层组成公共网络

Make existing ground floors a public network

  • 保留细颗粒建筑与街巷，不新建贯穿式大路。
  • Retain fine grain; do not cut a new boulevard through it.
  • 首层、前场、巷道和院落以开放协议接成网络。
  • Connect ground floors, forecourts, alleys and courts by agreement.
  • 不假设校园内部通行，必须提供公共替代路线。
  • Never assume campus passage; provide a public alternative.
  • 实施工具：更新许可、租约、时段开放与维护协议。
  • Instrument: permits, leases, timed access and maintenance agreements.

把站点、社区与开发做成一个剖面

Design station, community and development as one section

  • 四象限地面连续优先于未经论证的桥隧工程。
  • Ground-level four-quadrant continuity precedes unproven bridges or tunnels.
  • 站务、社区服务、文化与产业共同进入复合基座。
  • Station, community, cultural and industry uses share a mixed podium.
  • 体量向社区和文化遗产递降，数值高度保持 unknown。
  • Massing steps down to communities and heritage; numeric height stays unknown.
  • 实施工具：站城联合开发与区域综合更新门槛。
  • Instrument: station joint-development and comprehensive-renewal gates.
