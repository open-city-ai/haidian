# Jing-Zhang Smart Track: Reconstruct the Conceptual Recommendation for Urban Design of the Centennial Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt with the Track of the 'Person' Character

## Design Basis and Source List

This scheme is based on the first reference to the announcement by the Haidian Branch of the Beijing Municipal Commission of Planning and Natural Resources titled 'Qualification Pre-Review Announcement for International Proposals for the Centennial Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt Urban Design' [source:OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT], and the second reference is the open call task book for global intelligent entities [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK]. Machine-readable references come from the site package [source:SITE-PACKAGE], the source registry [source:SOURCE-REGISTRY], and the processed fact pack [source:PROCESSED-FACT-PACK], the spatial starting conditions are derived from [source:BOUNDARY-SOURCE] and [source:KEY-AREA-SOURCE], the scope of publicly citable materials and submission discipline are based on the public brief within the public brief index (`brief/public-brief.md`, `brief/README.md`) [source:PUBLIC-BRIEF]. Professional standards responses are based on [standard:PROJECT-OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT], [standard:PROJECT-AGENT-OPEN-CALL-TASKBOOK], [standard:MOHURD-URBAN-DESIGN-MEASURES], [standard:MOHURD-CONTROL-DETAILED-PLANNING], [standard:MNR-LAND-USE-CLASSIFICATION-GUIDE], and [standard:MOHURD-ARCH-DESIGN-DEPTH-2016].

The existing conditions diagnosis is organized [depth:existing_conditions_diagnosis], with conclusions based on three verifiable judgments rather than impressionistic descriptions. First, the core asset of this region is a **line** rather than a piece of land: the Jing-Zhang Railway Heritage Corridor is the only continuous Public Space carrier that spans all three layers, connecting universities, industrial parks, residential communities, and business districts, thus this plan identifies it as the sole main spine of the overall structure. Second, the core contradiction of this region is **horizontal fragmentation**: the corridor is repeatedly severed by north-south expressways such as the Fifth Ring Road North, Tsinghua East Road, and West Zhi Men Avenue, which damages the north-south continuity experience and the east-west connections between campuses, parks, and communities, making "east-west stitching and north-south connectivity" not a rhetorical device but the primary spatial task. Third, the core opportunity of this region is **existing stock rather than new development**: all three key areas are located in highly urbanized areas, and the plan must primarily rely on updating, weaving, opening up interfaces, and operational strategies.

Data boundaries must be clearly delineated. As of the submission of this document, the organizing body has not provided official `SITE_BOUNDARY` and `KEY_AREA` precise polygons. This proposal uses the temporarily rough provisional boundaries marked as `provisional_only` [source:BOUNDARY-SOURCE] and [source:KEY-AREA-SOURCE] for generating, displaying, and self-checking purposes, as shown in [data:geometry/site_boundary.geojson#SITE-001]. ## Three-Level Scope Framework

The plan is organized in three progressive layers as determined by the announcement, with a single sentence defining the deliverable boundaries for each layer: the Coordinated Research Area (approximately 43.6 square kilometers) delivers the **judgment** on industrial ecology and future urban form; the Overall Design Area (approximately 11.4 square kilometers) delivers the **structure and layers** for Urban Renewal and in-depth Urban Design; the Key-Area Detailed Design Area (approximately 368.4 hectares) delivers the **detailed design actions** for three areas. These three layers are not three sets of unrelated drawings; the judgment determines the structure, the structure constrains the actions, and the actions validate the implementability of the judgment. This framework is constrained by the [depth:three_level_scope_framework] and [depth:overall_spatial_structure], with the range index guided by the three-layer range table in [source:PROCESSED-FACT-PACK], and the tasks based on [standard:PROJECT-OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT].

### Overall Concept, Naming System, and Visual Identity Direction (in response to agent.1)

**Overall Concept: The Track of the 'Person' Character.** The most famous engineering legacy of the Jing-Zhang Railway is the 'person' character-shaped turnaround line that Zhan Tianyou adopted in the Qinglong Bridge area—using a single turn to solve what seemed an insurmountable slope challenge. This proposal directly translates this historical prototype into an urban proposition for the AI era: the 'person' character is both a technical symbol of the Jing-Zhang Railway and an ethical symbol of "people-centeredness." If the AI Innovation Belt has only computational power and industry but lacks a human scale, it misses the best lesson left by the railway. Therefore, the proposal advocates: a track scaled to the human, and a design that gains access through a turn.

**Main Name: Jing-Zhang Intelligent Track. English Name: Jingzhang Intelligent Track, Abbreviation JIT.** "Track" refers to both railway tracks, innovative tracks, and the urban growth trajectory. The abbreviation JIT in the computing context stands for "just-in-time compilation," which precisely echoes the core mechanism of this proposal—transforming knowledge from academic sources into usable urban spaces and open scenarios **just-in-time**. The brand short name is "JIT / Smart Track."

**Naming System (Tiered, Scalable, and Amenable to Professional Team Deepening):**

**Visual Identity and Logo Direction (Directional Suggestion, Not Final Draft):** Taking the "person" character fold line as the sole motif, the acute angle formed by the two strokes serves as the core of the logo; the fold line can be read as three layers of imagery — the Jing-Zhang Railway's zigzag line, the cross-section of a track, and the convergence point of two data streams. Extension system suggestion: the angle of the fold line is fixed, while the stroke thickness varies with scale, forming a consistent language from the logo to signage, from ground paving to digital interfaces; the main color suggestion is "embankment gray + signal red + data blue," where signal red comes from the railway signaling system and is used to emphasize nodes and honor symbols. District identifiers are generated by rotating or cropping the main logo, ensuring they are traceable to the same source. All fonts, images, trademarks, and portraits must use clearable assets during the deepening phase; this submission does not include any unlicensed third-party visual assets.

The structure is expressed by [data:geometry/site_boundary.geojson#SITE-001] and [data:geometry/green_space.geojson#GREEN-001], which can be read as four layers: **One Track** forms the continuous green spine axis of the Jing-Zhang heritage corridor, creating an 'M' intersection at the northern boundary of the original community, with the main spine running north and the two side spines branching southwest and southeast; **Three Cores** are the three key areas identified by the announcement, each tasked with full-stack self-innovation and governance authority, a world-class innovation ecosystem, and intelligent native new business models; **Two Wings** are the Zhongguancun Technology Services Wing (factors and capital empowerment) and the Xiaoyue River Scenario Enablement Wing (scenario and vibrant city), aligning with the direction of the 'M' strokes; **Five Stations** are the five smart track stations along the track, integrating slow travel nodes, edge-side computing, public services, and Scenario Access into the smallest operational unit. ## Coordinated Research Area: Industry and Future City Research

The task of the Coordinated Research Area is to address "how a world-class AI Innovation Ecosystem can grow within an existing city." The judgment of this plan is that Haidian lacks the **shortest path from academic papers to real-world applications** and **a visible public interface** for external observation. Therefore, the industrial strategy is organized into five segments of innovation chains: university and research institution innovation source → open-source collaboration and talent aggregation → enterprise transformation and productization → urban scenario validation → international dissemination and attraction. Each segment is anchored in different spaces: the innovation source is anchored in the near-campus interface of the original community, open-source and talent are anchored at the 'person' stage and the release venue, transformation is anchored at Zhongzhiyuan and Dazhongsi, validation is anchored at the Little Moon River Wing and three AI service zones, and dissemination is anchored at the pendulum hall and the entire activity route. This chain does not add new pseudo-precise red lines but instead connects back through the Public Spaces and aesthetic coordination required by [standard:MOHURD-URBAN-DESIGN-MEASURES] to the [depth:overall_spatial_structure] and visible layers.

## Overall Design Area: Urban Renewal and Regulatory-Plan-Level Urban Design

The Overall Design Area requires the depth of Urban Design as per the Regulatory Detailed Planning. This scheme is developed in accordance with the requirements.[standard:MOHURD-CONTROL-DETAILED-PLANNING] Break down the depth into reviewable components and simultaneously declare which elements must remain blank due to the lack of official control conditions. The deliverable parts are: a fully closed land structure, a Public Space network framed by the green spine, pedestrian and transit organization, indicative Building Footprints and update logic, phase III implementation recommendations, and recalculable indicators. The undeliverable parts are: Floor Area Ratio, Building Height, Building Coverage Ratio, setback, road right-of-way, and municipal capacity, which are statutory control indicators.

The land use structure is constrained by [depth:land_use_layout], geometrically defined in [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-001], comprising [metric:land_use_polygon_count] land use polygons that fully cover the submission boundary without any overlaps, confirmed by `scripts/spatial_review.py` to have zero coverage gaps. The structural logic is "green spine as the backbone, with defined qualities on either side": the green spine and its two side spines are unified as park green spaces and plaza land uses, forming a north-south public backbone; the green spine's sides are allocated dominant functions based on their districts—Zhongzhiyuan as the primary research and development area, the Original Point Community as a blend of research and educational land uses stitching the near-school interface, Dazhongsi as a carrier of intelligent original consumption and international exchanges with commercial service and cultural land uses, mid-section with residential, community service, medical, and sports land uses ensuring daily life, and green buffer and open space are set along the North Fifth Ring Road and at the northeast corner of Zhongzhiyuan.

The Development Intensity portion is handled at the [depth:development_intensity_controls] as **a method rather than a numerical value**. This plan does not provide specific Floor Area Ratio and height numbers but instead offers three principles for organizing development intensity for the professional team to deepen: first, intensity decreases from the green spine to both sides to ensure adequate sunlight and openness for Public Spaces; second, intensity is concentrated around rail transit stations and the five villages to form compact, station-city integrated units; third, intensity is reduced in proximity to cultural and historical resources to preserve the character and line of sight. These three principles can be directly translated into district-level development intensity caps once the official control plan conditions are obtained, without needing to overturn the structure. Correspondingly, in the `metrics.json`, `floor_area_ratio`, `building_height_m`, `building_density`, and `total_gfa_sqm` are all labeled as  with the reason provided to avoid creating an illusion of precision with speculative values.

## Detailed Design of Key Areas

Three key areas are mandatory, as indicated by [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-001], [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-002], and [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-003], with a total count of [metric:key_area_count], and a depth verified by [depth:three_key_area_detailed_design]. Each district is to be expressed in five segments: "positioning—spatial actions—AI scenarios—implementation dependencies—pending confirmation conditions," to avoid generic descriptions such as "developing demonstration areas."

![Index and Spatial Action Diagrams for Three Key Areas](assets/figures/key-areas.png)

**Intelligence Core · Zhongzhiyuan AI Independent Innovation Acceleration Area (approximately 192.1 hectares, announced area).** Positioned as a garden-type full-stack independent AI innovation district, it undertakes two functions: the AI full-stack independent innovation system and global discourse power in AI governance. The spatial actions are four: first, along the Qinghe interface, open a continuous low-carbon innovation interaction corridor, transforming the originally back-facing riverfront garden boundary into a display face facing the Public Space; second, set up "Zero Number Pavilion" in the middle of the area as an open testing and standard governance public entrance (see [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#PUB-L02]); third, use the east-west pedestrian connection to link the garden with the adjacent cities, mitigating the horizontal discontinuity caused by the Fifth Ring Road to the north; fourth, use green spaces as open testing grounds, achieving the diversified utilization of green spaces. AI scenarios include model evaluation, standard-setting workshops, safety governance displays, and embodied intelligence public testing. Implementation depends on the Qinghe Blue Line and ecological flood control conditions, negotiations over the ownership of the garden, safety and compliance permits for testing activities. Conditions to be confirmed include the control plan intensity, river management requirements, and the feasibility of rail transit connections. (Full-Stack Independent AI Innovation System)

**Origin·Beijing AI Origin Community (approximately 104.3 hectares, announced area).** Positioned as a campus-type conversion and talent community, it bears the functions of a world-class AI Innovation Ecosystem, serving as the intersection point of the 'person' and the spiritual origin point. The spatial actions are four: first, using the 'person' character stage as a landmark and honor stage, making the open-source contributions explicit in the urban space (see [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#PUB-L01]); second, organizing the triple-layered slow travel connection between the campus, park, and neighborhood, focusing on addressing the breakpoints at the west end of Tsinghua East Road and Wudaokou direction; third, arranging the conversion street along the near-campus interface, opening the first-floor space as a continuous interface for incubation, legal services, intellectual property, and investment and financing services; fourth, addressing the "far to live, slow to come" issue by embedding talent apartments and community services. The AI scenarios include open-source release, near-campus incubation, talent services, and AI slow travel navigation. Implementation depends on the willingness of the school and park for spatial collaboration, adjustment of the first-floor business types, and the functional replacement spaces of existing buildings. The conditions to be confirmed include the boundary of university land use, ownership relationships, and the feasibility of building renovations.

**Intelligence Hub · Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster (approximately 72.0 hectares, announced area).** Positioned as a city-type intelligent economy and international exchange district, it undertakes the functions of emerging smart-native industries. The spatial actions are four: first, organizing a quadrants walking connection around Dazhongsi Station to address the division of urban functions by the intersection (see [data:geometry/roads.geojson#ROAD-006]); second, setting up a "pendulum hall" as an international premiere and roadshow landmark, translating the ancient clock of Dazhongsi into the "rhythm" symbol of the AI era; third, integrating planned green spaces with commercial services to form a continuous interface that can be displayed, consumed, and paused; fourth, using the data element living room to carry data and digital asset services under the premise of compliance. The AI scenarios mainly include smart terminal and intelligent body displays, content consumption, data element services, and low-speed shuttle validation. Implementation depends on Transit-Station Integration coordination, review of traffic organization at intersections, and cultural resource protection requirements. The pending conditions are the Official Boundary of the cultural relic protection scope and the construction control area, as well as the conditions for tracks and municipal pipelines.

The common requirement for all three areas is that all spatial actions are expressed as Conceptual Recommendations and reference solutions, available for professional teams to further develop and study. No conclusions regarding the Demolish–Renovate–Retain Strategy, bridge and tunnel feasibility judgments, or underground space engineering, shall be made without involving specific parcels of land. No unauthorized modifications to enterprise buildings or proprietary spaces shall be undertaken.

### Three Industry Testing and Validation Scenarios

**The first, a test field for embodied intelligence and service robot Public Spaces (anchored to SCN-11).** The test content involves the navigation, obstacle avoidance, and human-robot interaction of service robots in a real but controlled public space; the spatial conditions are temporary enclosable and tiered open sites within the Green Spine; the access boundaries are limited time periods, limited areas, public announcements, and the presence of safety personnel; the evaluation metrics are the number of human-robot conflict events, task completion rate, and public acceptance; the exit mechanism is an immediate pause and review upon the occurrence of a safety incident. **The second, a low-speed shuttle and micro-circulation validation field (anchored to SCN-12).** The test content involves low-speed shuttle services and last-mile logistics around Dazhongsi station; the spatial conditions are quadrants of pedestrian connectivity and micro-circulation side streets; the access boundaries are low-speed limitations, separated road rights, and no occupation of accessible passage spaces; the evaluation metrics are shuttle travel time, punctuality rate, and the number of conflict points; the exit mechanism is subject to immediate termination by traffic management authorities. **The third, a sandbox for evaluating public service intelligent bodies (anchored to SCN-02).** The test content involves the reliability and explainability of intelligent bodies for government, life, and medical health services in real consultation scenarios; the spatial conditions are a closed evaluation space within Zero Number Hub; the access boundaries are no use of personal privacy data and no connection to unauthorized business systems. Evaluation metrics include response accuracy rate, appropriate rejection rate, and human intervention rate; the exit mechanism is to revert to the laboratory if the standards are not met. The three scenarios are for testing and validation purposes only and must not be described as approved for operation.

## Land Use, Building Scale, and Retain-Renovate-Demolish Strategy

Land use classification strictly adopts the land use code semantics from [standard:MNR-LAND-USE-CLASSIFICATION-GUIDE], geometrically seen in [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-002], with depth constrained by [depth:land_use_layout] and [depth:development_intensity_controls]. The land use is composed of [metric:land_use_polygon_count] map elements that are fully covered, boundary-sharing, and seamless. The core decision in land use organization is to set the "Green Spine" as the sole structural Public Space, with other functions layered around it, thereby making public space the organizer of the land use structure rather than a leftover.

### Public Space and three holy sites (responding to agent.4)

AI holy site landmarks number at [metric:landmark_count], all located on the Public Space layer: **"People" Platform** is situated at the original community, serving as the spiritual origin and honor platform for open contributors, embodying the physical form of the "People" character, with the inscription system growing incrementally by "sleeping berth" units, ensuring that contributions are remembered for the long term; **Zero Station** is located at Zhongzhiyuan, taking the imagery of the "zero number" of a railway maintenance depot, serving as a public entry point for open testing and standard governance, also a place where the public can understand "how AI is tested"; **Pendulum Hall** is located at Dazhongsi, taking the imagery of ancient bells and translating them into rhythmic symbols of the AI era, bearing the responsibility for international premieres and roadshows. These three landmarks collectively form a narrative loop of "origin—testing—release," avoiding the landmarks becoming mere tourist attractions.

The honor display system is named the **Sleeper Program**: it uses sleepers as the smallest inscription unit, continuously arranged along the Green Spine to record open-source contributions, scene co-creators, and youth teams; it employs a constructible, replaceable, and removable system to avoid irreversible occupation of public resources. The Public Space component library is suggested to standardize five categories of components—rest stops, contribution sleepers, foldable test enclosures, signposts, and ground data pattern paving—to form a lightweight toolkit that is replicable and can be implemented in phases. All of the above are Conceptual Recommendations and do not involve bridge and tunnel construction, underground space, or engineering feasibility judgments, nor do they violate heritage protection, green space, and traffic safety constraints. (Phased Implementation)

### Jing-Zhang Centennial, Zhongguancun, and the Integration Narrative with AI New Culture (Responding to agent.5)

Cultural narratives are organized according to the **[standard:MOHURD-URBAN-DESIGN-MEASURES]**'s landscape integration requirements, forming a three-layer temporal overlay: the **Jing-Zhang Railway of 1909** represents the starting point of China's autonomous engineering capabilities, with its "person" shaped line symbolizing the "ingenious solution" technical spirit; **Zhongguancun since the 1980s** represents the tradition of grassroots innovation vitality and knowledge transformation; and **the current AI culture** represents open-source, co-creation, and global collaboration. The common thread among them is "autonomy" and "co-creation," which are the main axes of this plan's narrative: from autonomous road construction, to autonomous innovation, to autonomous intelligence. Spatially, the narrative anchor points are the railway cultural resources such as the Tsinghua Garden Railway Station, which are linked to form a walkable story line; the sign and identification system uses the "person" letter as a unifying theme, but clearly distinguishes itself from the overall logo of the Belt and Road — the cultural identification system serves narrative recognition, not replacing the brand logo system. The main axis of the international communication copy is suggested to be "From a Zigzag to a Breakthrough: A Turning Point, Centuries of Connectivity." All historical references are based on public facts, without distorting history, and do not use unlicensed images, trademarks, thesis images, or copyrighted materials.
