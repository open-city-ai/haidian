Core Concept

The “Crossing” is the most distinctive spatial memory the Jingzhang Railway left to Beijing: between Xizhimen and Qinghe, Level Crossings No.1 to No.5 were built, and Crossing No.5 became “Wudaokou — the Center of the Universe”. A signal light is the city's earliest automated decision system — a crossing means rules, encounter and order. AI is the new generation of the “signal system”, and the AI Innovation Belt is the level-crossing where AI meets urban life: not avoidance, not isolation, but rule-based encounter.

This proposal develops “One Belt, Five Crossings”: the 9-km Jingzhang Railway Heritage Park green corridor is the innovation spine, organizing the Three Areas and Two Wings into five encounter nodes — North · Zhongzhiyuan (AI full-stack × industry), Mid · AI Origin Community (talent × innovation), South · Dazhongsi (agents × consumption), West · Zhongguancun Tech-Service Wing (capital × services), East · Xiaoyuehe Scenario Wing (scenarios × daily life). Each crossing = one governance “signal rule” + one public encounter space + a set of perceivable AI scenarios.

Three Areas × Two Wings — One Belt, Five Crossings

Five Crossings

  • North · Zhongzhiyuan: AI full-stack “detonation point”; new campus + Tencent HQ + XueZhiYuan rail micro-center
  • Mid · AI Origin Community: near-campus innovation ecosystem; Origin Tower radiating 3 km²; 100k AI students
  • South · Dazhongsi: agents × content consumption × smart terminals; ByteDance ecosystem + LJJ renewal + dual metro
  • West · Zhongguancun Tech-Service Wing: startup IP and capital ecosystem; central S&T district renewal
  • East · Xiaoyuehe Scenario Wing: embodied AI, AI+health, AI+film; 6.41 km waterfront corridor

Mobility, Blue-Green Space & Renewal Projects

Mobility

  • Continue the “three paths + one green belt” (walk/run/cycle) to stitch east-west slow-mobility breaks
  • Four-station integration concept: Dazhongsi / XueZhiYuan / Wudaokou / Sidaokou (new Line-13 station)
  • Connect to the Huilongguan cycle expressway, enabling long-distance riding from the Three Hills Five Gardens to the North 2nd Ring

Blue-Green Public Space

  • Blue-green skeleton: 9-km heritage park green corridor + Xiaoyue River waterfront (6.41-km restored reach)
  • Iconic nodes (conceptual): south wall “Signal Gate”, north wall “Marshalling-Yard Eye”
  • 4 pilgrimage landmark concepts: AI Origin Sanctum / Track of Time / Full-Stack Assembly Line / Smart Dispatch Tower
