# Civic Test Loop

The proposal turns the Jing-Zhang heritage corridor into a conceptual civic AI test loop. A heritage public spine connects three differentiated cores: Zhongzhiyuan for testing and governance, AI Origin Community for open contribution and translation, and Dazhongsi for urban exchange and intelligent-native services. Two wings connect technology services and everyday scenario empowerment.

Every scenario card records a spatial carrier, data boundary, human review, exit route and operating output. The design is intentionally evidence-first: provisional geometry is quiet, public-space and mobility relationships are visible, and statutory controls remain pending until official materials are available.
