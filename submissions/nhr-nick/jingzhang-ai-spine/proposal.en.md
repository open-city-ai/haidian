## Overall Concept and Naming System

### Conceptual Positioning

The name "Jingzhang AI Spine" carries three layers of meaning:

1. **Jingzhang**: Inherits the century-old heritage of the Jingzhang Railway; the railway heritage is not a relic but a cultural spine connecting past and future.
2. **AI (Intelligence)**: AI as the core driving force, from basic research to industrial applications to urban governance, building a full-stack intelligent ecosystem.
3. **Spine**: The corridor serves as a pulse—not merely a physical connection, but a converging artery of information flow, talent flow, and innovation flow.

### Logo Direction

The logo adopts a "railway track + circuit" dual-line fusion graphic: two parallel lines transition from traditional railway track texture on the left to circuit traces on the right, symbolizing the transition from industrial civilization to intelligent civilization. ## Three-Tier Scope Framework

### Coordinated Research Area (43.6 km²)

North to North Fifth Ring Road, east to Beijing-Tibet Expressway, south to Xizhimen Outer Street, west to Wanquanhe Road [source:SITE-PACKAGE]. This is the full-domain horizon for industrial ecosystem and future city research, focusing on:

- Full-chain distribution of Haidian AI industry: basic research (universities) → technology R&D (Zhongguancun) → product incubation (Zhongzhiyuan) → scenario deployment (Xiaoyuehe)
- Spatial correlation of cultural resources along the entire Jingzhang Railway Heritage
- Regional connectivity of transportation and slow-mobility systems
- AI talent persona matching with residential, work, and leisure space needs

### Overall Design Area (11.4 km²)

Centered on urban areas and industrial zones within 1-2 km of the Jingzhang Heritage Park [source:SITE-PACKAGE]. The "One Spine, Three Cores, Two Wings" spatial structure is implemented here:

- **One Spine**: Jingzhang Railway Heritage Park cultural spine, approximately 9 km from Qinghuayuan Station to Beijing North Railway Station, a continuous public space and slow-mobility corridor
- **Three Cores**: Zhongzhiyuan Acceleration Core (192.1 ha), AI Origin Community Core (104.3 ha), DaZhongSi Industry Core (72.0 ha)
- **Two Wings**: Zhongguancun Technology Service Wing (west) and Xiaoyuehe Scenario Empowerment Wing (east)

### Three Key Areas Detailed Design

#### Zhongzhiyuan · Accelerator Core (192.1 ha)

Positioned as the spatial carrier for "AI Full-Stack Self-Reliant Innovation System" and "AI Governance Global Discourse Power" [source:THREE-AREAS-WINGS]. Design strategies:

- **Innovation Acceleration Workshops**: AI chip, large model, and embodied intelligence accelerators along the south side of North Fifth Ring Road, each with shared labs, testing grounds, and exhibition halls
- **Developer Village**: Low-density courtyard-style offices integrated with dining, fitness, childcare, and other services for a "5-minute walk work-life circle"
- **Northern Section of Jingzhang Railway Heritage Park**: Preserving railway track elements, embedding AI interactive installations and open-source contribution display walls to create a "Developer Promenade"
- **AI Governance Experimental Zone**: Urban intelligent agent governance sandbox with visual display of public data reading, proposal reasoning, public feedback, and human review

#### AI Origin · Community Core (104.3 ha)

Positioned as the spatial carrier for "World-Class AI Innovation Ecosystem" [source:THREE-AREAS-WINGS]. Design strategies:

- **AI Origin Square**: Linear civic square centered on the Qinghuayuan Station heritage site, featuring the "AI Origin" landmark public space
- **Innovation Community**: Mixed-use layout of R&D offices, youth apartments, startup incubators, and community retail, suggested FAR 2.5-3.5
- **AI+ Public Service Hub**: Integrated AI medical consultation station, AI education center, AI legal consultation point, and public service navigation terminals
- **Core Section of Centennial Jingzhang Cultural Narrative**: Triple narrative convergence of railway heritage + Zhongguancun innovation culture + AI new culture

#### DaZhongSi · Industry Core (72.0 ha)

Positioned as the spatial carrier for "Intelligent Native New Business Models" [source:THREE-AREAS-WINGS]. Design strategies:

- **AI Industry Headquarters Cluster**: Headquarters offices along DaZhongSi East Road, focusing on AI large model and AI+ industry application enterprises
- **Intelligent Native Commercial Block**: Commercial deployment of robot delivery, unmanned retail, AI shopping guides
- **DaZhongSi Heritage Cultural Node**: Placing ancient bell culture alongside AI culture to create an "Ancient-Modern Dialogue" cultural node
- **Industry Exhibition Center**: Permanent AI industry achievement exhibition hall with open-day mechanisms

## AI+ Scenario Cards (12)

### Card 1: AI Slow-Mobility Navigation (ai-traffic-walkability)

**Users**: Commuters, residents, students
**Scenario**: Last-kilometer from metro to office, AI navigation recommends optimal walking/cycling routes based on real-time pedestrian flow, weather, and accessibility status, automatically avoiding construction breakpoints and congested sections
**Data Inputs**: Public road data, metro station information, survey feedback
**Public Value**: Eliminating slow-mobility breakpoints, improving connection efficiency, ensuring barrier-free access
**Risk**: Route recommendations must not be misinterpreted as approved transportation plans [scenario:ai-traffic-walkability]

### Card 2: AI Community Health Station (ai-public-services)

**Users**: Community residents, elderly
**Scenario**: AI health consultation terminals at community entrances for basic vital sign detection and health counseling; data stays local, results referred to community health centers in real time
**Data Inputs**: Public medical guidelines, voluntarily provided health data
**Public Value**: Improving grassroots medical service accessibility, reducing unnecessary hospital visits
**Risk**: AI recommendations must not replace professional diagnosis; human review process must be clearly communicated

### Card 3: Developer Promenade (jingzhang-heritage-narrative)

**Users**: AI developers, students, tourists
**Scenario**: Interactive installations along the Jingzhang Railway Heritage Park displaying open-source project contribution graphs and AI milestone timelines; developers can scan QR codes to link their GitHub contributions
**Data Inputs**: Public open-source data, railway historical materials
**Public Value**: Making technical contributions visible and tangible, building developer community belonging
**Risk**: Personal contribution display requires authorization

### Card 4: AI Origin Square Experience (ai-origin-community)

**Users**: Residents, visitors, students
**Scenario**: Interactive floor projection at AI Origin Square; visitors trigger generative AI visual experiences showing the parallel narrative of AI development and Jingzhang Railway evolution
**Data Inputs**: Public AI history materials, Jingzhang Railway archives
**Public Value**: Creating perceivable AI cultural public space
**Risk**: Visual content requires review to avoid inappropriate associations

### Card 5: Robot Delivery Corridor (robotics-autonomous-mobility)

**Users**: Office workers, residents
**Scenario**: Dedicated low-speed robot delivery channels connecting the three cores, linking delivery sorting stations to building lobbies; robots travel along dedicated paths in the Jingzhang Heritage Park
**Data Inputs**: Public road data, delivery demand data
**Public Value**: Reducing last-mile delivery interference with slow-mobility systems
**Risk**: Low-speed pilot requires supervision and review capability

### Card 6: AI Legal Consultation Point (ai-public-services)

**Users**: Entrepreneurs, SMEs
**Scenario**: AI legal consultation terminals in the Community Core providing basic legal Q&A, contract review assistance, and IP consultation; complex issues referred to professional lawyers
**Data Inputs**: Public legal databases, policy documents
**Public Value**: Reducing SME legal compliance costs
**Risk**: AI recommendations must not replace professional legal advice

### Card 7: Jingzhang Cultural Tour Route (jingzhang-heritage-narrative)

**Users**: Tourists, students, culture enthusiasts
**Scenario**: AR tour routes along railway heritage sites; scanning heritage points with phones overlays historical imagery and AI-generated scene comparisons for a "centennial time-travel" narrative experience
**Data Inputs**: Public railway historical materials, current spatial data
**Public Value**: Transforming static heritage into experienceable cultural narrative
**Risk**: AI-generated imagery must be labeled; must not be misrepresented as historical photographs

### Card 8: Youth Night School Space (youth-friendly-public-space)

**Users**: Young students, entrepreneurs
**Scenario**: 24-hour youth night school spaces in the Community Core and Accelerator Core with self-service AI programming environments, project collaboration whiteboards, and light refreshments; nighttime security ensured through intelligent security systems
**Data Inputs**: Public space usage data
**Public Value**: Extending youth public space availability hours
**Risk**: Nighttime safety requires human-AI collaborative assurance

### Card 9: AI Scenario Sandbox (ai-origin-community)

**Users**: AI enterprises, developers, government
**Scenario**: AI scenario sandbox zone in the Accelerator Core where enterprises test AI applications (e.g., intelligent customer service, AI inspection) in a controlled environment; public participation in experience and feedback
**Data Inputs**: Voluntarily provided test data, public feedback
**Public Value**: Accelerating AI application transition from testing to deployment
**Risk**: Test data requires compliance review; public feedback requires human review

### Card 10: Intelligent Agent Contribution Wall (civic-agent-governance)

**Users**: AI developers, community members
**Scenario**: Physical + digital dual-mode contribution wall along the railway heritage park recording participating Agent names, contributors, and key design decisions
**Data Inputs**: Public GitHub contribution records
**Public Value**: Establishing permanent memorial for the first Agents participating in real urban design
**Risk**: Display content requires review

### Card 11: AI Education Station (ai-public-services)

**Users**: K-12 students, adult learners
**Scenario**: AI education terminals in the Community Core providing personalized learning path recommendations and basic subject Q&A; teachers can remotely intervene and guide
**Data Inputs**: Public educational resources and curriculum standards
**Public Value**: Expanding quality education service coverage
**Risk**: AI tutoring must not replace professional teacher judgment

### Card 12: Industry Exhibition Open Day (enterprise-services-ecosystem)

## AI Pilgrimage Landmarks (3)

### Landmark 1: AI Origin Stone

Located at the center of AI Origin Square, using the "Y-shaped" track of Qinglongqiao Station as a prototype, constructed in steel and stone with an AI chip-shaped LED light strip at the top. The inscription reads "1909-2026: From Y-Track to Artificial Intelligence," symbolizing the century-long heritage of Zhan Tianyou's spirit and AI innovation. At night, the light strip pulses with real-time GitHub commit activity, making global developer contributions visible in physical space.

### Landmark 2: Developer Contribution Wall

A 200-meter-long physical + digital dual-mode contribution wall along the middle section of the railway heritage park. The physical wall engraves the names of selected proposal contributors and Agents; the digital wall displays complete contribution graphs and design iteration history through offline HTML visualization. Updated annually, recording the most outstanding Agent contributions.

### Landmark 3: Jingzhang Timeline Gallery

A 1.5 km linear cultural gallery between Qinghuayuan Station heritage site and DaZhongSi, with century-old rail track elements embedded in the ground and AI interactive light curtains suspended above. Walking along the gallery, every 100 meters corresponds to a historical era node; light curtain content changes with pedestrian position. This is the physical carrier of the triple narrative of Centennial Jingzhang Culture, Zhongguancun Culture, and AI New Culture.

## Centennial Jingzhang Cultural Narrative

### Triple Cultural Fusion

The cultural narrative of Jingzhang AI Spine is not three parallel independent stories, but the interweaving of three cultural layers:

1. **Centennial Jingzhang Culture**: Zhan Tianyou's spirit—self-reliant innovation, overcoming difficulties, building railways for the nation. The railway heritage is both a spatial spine and a spiritual spine.
2. **Zhongguancun Culture**: The entrepreneurial spirit of China's Silicon Valley after reform and opening—daring to be first, industry-academia-research fusion, the courage to go from 0 to 1.
3. **AI New Culture**: Open-source collaboration, Agent participation, human-AI co-creation—an unprecedented urban design paradigm where Agents participate in real urban construction for the first time.

### Narrative Spatial Structure

The narrative unfolds along the Jingzhang Railway Heritage Park from north to south:

- **North Section · Origins** (Accelerator Core): Against the backdrop of railway industrial heritage, telling the pioneering story of "Chinese people independently building the first trunk railway," mapping to AI full-stack self-reliant innovation
- **Middle Section · Convergence** (Community Core): Triple convergence of railway heritage + Zhongguancun innovation + AI experimentation; "Y-shaped" track and AI chip spatially overlaid; past and future meet here
- **South Section · Blossoming** (Industry Core): Ancient bell culture and AI industry juxtaposed in "Ancient-Modern Dialogue"—DaZhongSi's morning and evening bells and AI large model computing power

### Cultural Tour Routes

Three themed tour routes:
- **Railway Origins Line** (9 km): Qinghuayuan Station → Qinglongqiao Elements → Y-Track → DaZhongSi Station, approximately 2.5 hours
- **Innovation Convergence Line** (5 km): Zhongguancun Exhibition Hall → AI Origin Square → Developer Contribution Wall → Industry Exhibition Center, approximately 1.5 hours
- **AI Experience Line** (6 km): AI Slow-Mobility Navigation → Community Health Station → AI Scenario Sandbox → Robot Delivery → Timeline Gallery, approximately 2 hours

- Recommended total gross floor area: approximately 3.4-4.0 million m² (composite FAR approximately 3.0-3.5)
- Zhongzhiyuan Accelerator Core: FAR 2.0-2.5, primarily low-density accelerator spaces
- AI Origin Community Core: FAR 2.5-3.5, mixed R&D and community
- DaZhongSi Industry Core: FAR 3.5-4.5, primarily high-rise headquarters offices

### Renovation Principles

- **Preserve**: All above-ground railway heritage relics, historic buildings, existing communities
- **Renovate**: Inefficient industrial buildings updated as innovation spaces; aging commercial upgraded as AI scenario testing grounds
- **Demolish**: Only illegal structures and dangerous buildings that seriously obstruct corridor continuity; requires case-by-case justification

## Transportation, Rail, Municipal, and Public Service Facilities

### Transportation Strategy

- **Rail Connection**: Strengthen slow-mobility connections between Line 13, Line 15, Changping Line stations and core areas; AI navigation terminals at each station
- **Slow-Mobility Priority**: Jingzhang Railway Heritage Park entirely motor-vehicle-free; continuous pedestrian and cycling paths
- **Low-Speed Shuttle**: Low-speed autonomous shuttle buses within core areas connecting three cores and two wings
- **Event Day Transportation**: Temporary traffic management plans for large events; AI real-time allocation of shuttle resources

### Public Service Facilities

- 3 community health service centers (one per core)
- 2 nine-year schools (Community Core, Accelerator Core)
- 3 community elderly service stations
- 20+ AI public service terminals
