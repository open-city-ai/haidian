# 方案叙事摘要 / Proposal Narrative Summary

**智境复兴带** 的核心愿景是：让百年前詹天佑独立自主的工程精神，在AI时代以一种新的方式延续——不是通过钢铁，而是通过代码、数据和算法；不是铁路连接山与山，而是AI连接人与未来。

本方案以"三区两翼"空间框架为骨架：
- **众智源**（北）以全栈自主AI创新为引擎，是这条带的大脑
- **AI原点**（中）以世界级创新生态为核心，是这条带的心脏  
- **钟鸣谷**（南）以AI城市生活体验为场景，是这条带与普通市民相遇的地方

京张铁路遗址公园不只是一条绿带，它是连接百年历史与AI未来的时间轴。五个AI朝圣地标沿线布置，让全球任何一个AI从业者来到这里，都能感受到技术传承的历史重量。

十二张场景卡描绘了AI在这里可以生成的具体生活：医疗影像里的辅助诊断、社区里的AI教育陪伴、市集上的溯源验真、无障碍服务的普惠触达。这不是科幻，这是正在发生或可预见的现实。

六类用户画像告诉我们，这条带需要服务的不只是穿着格子衬衫的工程师，还有用AI查天气的老人、带孩子体验科技的父母、从世界各地专程来打卡的朝圣者。

文化叙事的核心是三重叠加：百年京张的工程自主精神 + 中关村三十年的创业创新基因 + AI时代的开放共创价值观。这三层不是简单的时间叠加，它们共享同一个DNA：**自主与共生**。

---

**The core vision of the Intelligent Renaissance Belt** is to carry forward the spirit of Jeme Tien Yow's engineering self-reliance from a century ago — not through steel and iron this time, but through code, data, and algorithms; not connecting mountains with railways, but connecting humanity with AI.

The Three-Zone Two-Wing framework forms the backbone: Zhongzhi Origin as the brain, AI Origin as the heart, and Bell Valley as the place where the Belt meets everyday life.

The Jingzhang Railway Heritage Park is not just a green corridor — it is a timeline connecting a century of history with an AI future. Five AI Pilgrimage Landmarks along its length ensure that any AI practitioner visiting from anywhere in the world can feel the weight of technological heritage.

The six user personas remind us that this Belt must serve not only engineers in flannel shirts, but also elderly residents using AI for weather forecasts, parents bringing children to experience technology, and pilgrims traveling from around the world specifically for this destination.

The cultural narrative at its core is a triple overlay: the engineering self-reliance spirit of the Jingzhang Railway + the entrepreneurial DNA of Zhongguancun's thirty years + the open co-creation values of the AI era. These three layers share a single DNA: **autonomy and symbiosis**.
