# Jing-Zhang Open Source Vital Thread: Verifiable, Experiential, and Sustainable AI Urban Coexistence Belt

This plan transforms the century-old Jing-Zhang into an "open-source wisdom vein" that continuously generates public value. Spatially, it connects the Jing-Zhang Heritage Park with universities, communities, industrial parks, and rail stations. In terms of industry, it forms a "validation—transformation—exchange" triad with Zhongzhiyuan, Beijing AI Origin Community, and Dazhongsi. In governance, it constrains every AI scenario with data minimization, Human Review, public logs, and reversible pilot projects. The overall structure is summarized as "one vein, three anchors, and five living rooms": a main vein of heritage and Public Space, three differentiated innovation anchor points, and five public living rooms accessible to citizens—Standard Plaza, Developer Honor Corridor, Origin Open Source Hall, Centennial Time Station, and City Validation Institute.

## Three-Level Scope Framework

Three levels of scope are coordinated to advance the framework with "mechanism establishment, overall framework definition, and key areas for validation" [depth:three_level_scope_framework]: The Coordinated Research Area discusses the relationship between AI ecosystems, talent, and future cities; the Overall Design Area organizes Urban Renewal, spatial structure, transportation infrastructure, and blue-green networks; three key areas will implement functional, scenario, Public Space, renewal prototypes, and implementation dependencies on reviewable layers. The overall structure is verified by [depth:overall_spatial_structure], with the spatial bottom board seen in [data:geometry/site_boundary.geojson#SITE-001] and [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-001].

"Continuous" pathways, cycling routes, cultural narratives, and public service interfaces are established along the Jing-Zhang Heritage Park site for the "One Vein"; "Three Anchors" respectively undertake full-stack validation, open-source transformation, smart economy, and international exchange; "Five Halls" bring technological activities from enclosed campuses into everyday urban life. The land is organized into five consecutive conceptual zones [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-001], which fully cover the Provisional Boundary and avoid overlap, expressing structural relationships rather than statutory land use adjustments. The current model contains 5 land use zones [metric:land_use_zone_count], 3 key areas [metric:key_area_count], 5 composite corridors [metric:mobility_corridor_count], and 5 public halls [metric:public_space_node_count].

## Coordinated Research Area: Industry and Future City Research

The industrial mainline is not about building another closed "AI Park," but forming a cyclical open innovation chain: universities and research institutions propose problems, the Origin Community organizes open-source collaboration and talent services, Zhongzhiyuan provides secure evaluations and full-stack validation, Dazhongsi completes enterprise services, exhibition transactions, and international dissemination, while Public Space serves as the interface for citizen oversight and feedback of genuine needs. This forms three positions: the "City Memory Interface" for the cultural and technological narrative of Jing-Zhang, the "Public Infrastructure" for open R&D and industrial validation, and the "Trusted AI Living Experiment Field" where everyone can choose to enter or exit.

"The Three Zones and Two Wings" in this plan are interpreted as three key areas and two spill-over networks: the West Wing connects to universities, research institutions, young talents, and open-source communities, while the East Wing connects to corporate headquarters, business services, and international exchanges. The five functional areas correspond to "source creation and collaboration, testing and validation, enterprise services, public experience, and cultural dissemination." They are design responses to the [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK] task requirements, not new statutory zoning categories.

Brand name is "Jing-Zhang Open Source Intelligence Spine," with the English name "Jing-Zhang Open Source Intelligence Spine." The original logo features two continuous main veins formed by the Jing-Zhang railway tracks, code curly braces `{ }` representing open interfaces, and three nodes symbolizing three innovation anchors. The primary colors are Jing-Zhang deep blue `#0B3558`, open source green `#009E73`, and public vitality orange `#E69F00`; the layout uses system fonts and high-contrast legends to ensure offline display and color-vision friendliness. The brand is only used for this concept plan and does not use unauthorized corporate logos, personal likenesses, or third-party fonts.

International cases are provided as mechanisms for reference: one-north's research and development—industry—lifestyle mixed organization [source:CASE-ONE-NORTH]; the Public Testing and Corporate Support Platform of Seoul AI Hub [source:CASE-SEOUL-AI-HUB]; Cambridge Foundry's provision of affordable community spaces in the vicinity of Kendall Square [source:CASE-KENDALL-SQUARE]; STATION F's use of tiered projects to serve startup teams [source:CASE-STATION-F]; Mila Ventures' connection of research outcomes with corporate incubation [source:CASE-MILA]; and Barcelona 22@'s use of an updated framework to coordinate innovative activities and urban environments [source:CASE-BARCELONA-22AT]. Local adaptations fall into six categories of mechanisms: mixed-use districts, validation hubs, public living rooms, growth services, school-based conversions, and phased updates; they do not directly transplant their Development Intensity, financial tools, or governance powers.

## Overall Design Area: Urban Renewal and Regulatory-Plan-Level Urban Design

Overall space adopts a "central heritage public spine + innovative living tapestry on both sides." Five conceptual zoning areas sequentially undertake blue-green heritage, industry-research collaboration, living services, innovative mixed-use, and hub exchange functions [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-001]. Five traffic-mixed corridors [data:geometry/roads.geojson#ROAD-001] horizontally connect to universities, communities, key enterprises, and rail transit nodes, ensuring that all activities do not flow solely along a single axis longitudinally. At the building level, no large-scale demolition and reconstruction is done, but rather eight replicable retention and renovation prototypes and small-scale insertions are used to validate ground-level openness, shared rooftops, low-carbon updates, and integrated services [data:geometry/buildings.geojson#BLDG-001] [metric:renewal_prototype_count].

Land use structure responds to [depth:land_use_layout], but does not alter the current statutory use; development intensity controls respond to [depth:development_intensity_controls], but the Floor Area Ratio, total gross floor area, and Building Height remain unknown due to the lack of formal control plans. Formal deepening is required after verifying the plot, ownership, and existing buildings, and should be compiled in accordance with the order of "first preserve, then renovate, and finally supplement" the plot plan. This should be checked for compatibility with roads, facilities, open spaces, and construction controls using [standard:MOHURD-CONTROL-DETAILED-PLANNING].

Public realm control employs three verifiable principles: prioritize accessible public or shared functions at the ground level along the main spine; ensure at least one continuous and barrier-free path across road nodes; and prevent new volumes from obscuring important heritage narrative nodes. Specific setback, height limitations, solar access, and fire safety conclusions must await the review of statutory documentation and specialized arguments; they are currently only listed as issues for the next phase of review.

## AI Innovation Ecosystem, Personas, and AI+ Scenarios

Human capital is not a single category of "high-end R&D personnel." The proposal serves six key stakeholder groups: Open-source developers require collaboration, release, and reputation for contributions; university students, faculty, and researchers need proximity to campus conversion and compliance consultation; startup teams require low-cost space, testing environments, and customer validation; established companies and international visitors need corporate services, exhibition, and cross-cultural hospitality; residents, seniors, and children need barrier-free public services, quiet relaxation, and digital alternatives; and public service personnel and facility maintainers need explainable alerts, clear responsibilities, and human intervention interfaces. Each stakeholder group can opt for non-digital processes, and Public Spaces do not require facial recognition or continuous personal trajectory tracking as a prerequisite for use.

All twelve scenarios are conceptual pilots. The scenarios adhere to the common rule of "public or authorized data—minimum data collection—Human Review—traceable audit—capable of being taken offline at any time." Industrial validation scenarios are marked as "validation" in the table.

## Land Use, Building Scale, and Retain-Renovate-Demolish Strategy

Five land use categories are established based on the principles of the national spatial purpose classification [standard:MNR-LAND-USE-CLASSIFICATION-GUIDE], without creating new statutory land use codes. The architectural strategy is managed by [depth:height_massing_character] and [depth:retain_renovate_demolish], using a four-step judgment process: first, verify safety, ownership, and historical value; retain what can be preserved; update those that can be adapted through structural, mechanical, and first-floor modifications; and only enter demolition studies for objects that cannot meet safety or public connectivity requirements after specialized analysis.

The total gross floor area of the 8 building prototypes [metric:renewal_prototype_count] is 861,226.918 square meters [metric:building_footprint_area_sqm], corresponding to [data:geometry/buildings.geojson#BLDG-001]. These serve as conceptual bases for validating layout relationships and are not a census of existing buildings or construction volumes. The proposed form language is "low-rise public base + adaptable research and development floors + shared roof," with important public interfaces maintaining small-scale entrances and walkable ground floors. The total gross floor area, Floor Area Ratio, and Building Height remain unknown and must be recalculated after formal zoning plans, current surveying data, cultural heritage, solar access, fire safety, and traffic capacity information are all available.

## Transport, Rail, Municipal Infrastructure, and Public Services

The transportation system aims for "get-off-and-walk, uninterrupted crossings, and traversable parks" [depth:traffic_rail_slow_parking]. Five conceptual mobility corridors [metric:mobility_corridor_count] are designated to carry Jing-Zhang continuous slow travel, Qinghe ecological connection, campus-park integration, Dazhongsi quadrants connection, and low-speed validation loops. Prioritizing accessibility continuity, transfer distances, crosswalk wait times, and non-motorized vehicle parking at Dazhongsi station, Wudaokou, Qinghua Donglu Xi Kou, and the north Fifth Ring Road intersection, parking will adhere to the principle of prioritizing shared existing spaces, with new parking spaces determined by traffic assessments.

![Pedestrian and Rail Access Integration and Blue-Green Network](assets/figures/mobility-bluegreen.png)

Municipal and New Infrastructure Response [depth:municipal_new_infrastructure]: Co-locate edge-side computational nodes with existing public service facilities, allowing for independent power disconnection and public disclosure of energy consumption; rain gardens serve as heat environment monitoring sample areas; activity spaces reserve detachable power and network interfaces. Due to [data:geometry/constraints.geojson#CONSTRAINTS] currently being empty, underground utility lines, river blue lines, road red lines, fire lanes, cultural heritage protection areas, and power capacity limits shall not be inferred from this plan and must be verified by the relevant professional departments before implementation.

Public services adopt a tri-channel approach of "digital assistance + manned counter + paper/static alternatives." In scenarios involving health, education, legal, and business services, the model is not allowed to make the final decision; results that involve rights, funds, diagnosis, and safety must be confirmed by authorized personnel and must retain mechanisms for appeal, revocation, and data deletion.

## Blue-Green Network, Public Space, and Urban Character

The blue-green system is structured with the Jing-Zhang Heritage Park as its spine and the Qinghe River and other ecological interfaces as horizontal connections, forming four conceptual green spaces [data:geometry/green_space.geojson#GREEN-001]. Design actions include mending north-south pedestrian and bicycle connectivity gaps, increasing continuous shaded areas and rain gardens, setting up accessible platforms for pause and stay at transportation nodes, and using lightweight modular facilities to host exhibitions and sports activities. This system is implemented to realize [depth:blue_green_public_space], with the current model green space ratio [metric:green_ratio] serving only for comparative purposes.

Five public living rooms [metric:public_space_node_count] form a recognizable landmark system: the Standard Plaza presents test rules and results through public displays; the Developer Honor Corridor records approved open-source contributions; the Origin Open Source Hall hosts releases and collaborations; the Centennial Time Station connects railway history with contemporary oral accounts; and the Urban Validation Institute places industrial testing in an environment that is visible, isolated, and monitored [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#PUBLIC-001]. They are marked with a consistent set of "tracks-braces-triangles" signage, which maintains low-glare lighting at night and provides equivalent information for those who do not use smart devices.

Do not emulate ancient landscapes or represent AI with large screens. Preserve the railway linearity, industrial materials, and mature trees as a backdrop of time. New facilities should express openness through reversible timber and steel components, transparent display interfaces, and durable paving. The protection scope, construction controls, and display content for important historical resources must be confirmed by formal conservation documentation.
