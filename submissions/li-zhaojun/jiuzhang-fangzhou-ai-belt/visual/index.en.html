Spatial Structure

One Spine, Three Tings, Two Wings, Nine Chapters

Conceptual skeleton: "One Spine, Three Tings, Two Wings, Nine Chapters" - the Green Spine runs north-south along the Jingzhang Railway heritage corridor; three tings form station-stop style spaces; two wings represent industry and scenario sides; nine chapters are nine categories of AI scenario cards that together form a perceivable "land-industry-services-measurement-intelligence" content system.

AI Innovation Ecosystem

AI Full-Stack Autonomous Innovation + World-class Ecosystem

The proposal implements the AI Full-Stack Autonomous Innovation System (per agent taskbook function 1) and World-class AI Innovation Ecosystem (function 2) via four layers:

  • Compute Layer: Compute Station (BLDG-009), edge compute nodes along Green Spine (5 sites)
  • Model Layer: Full-Stack Pavilion (BLDG-007) with open-source model hub + governance sandbox (BLDG-008)
  • Data Layer: Data-Element Reception (SC-AI-06) + Data-Element Service Building (SC-AI-08) + Open Data Clearing
  • Application Layer: 13 SC-AI scenario cards + 3 SC-AI-IND industrial test venues

AI Scenario Cards (13 cards, incl. SC-AI-SEN)

Scenario Cards - 8 Unified Fields Each

Each scenario card carries 8 unified fields: required input, AI role, non-AI fallback, operator, human reviewer, failure-stop conditions, data retention, pilot evaluation. All model performance, latency, and security targets are labelled as "design target / to be validated", not verified capabilities.

Risk & Compliance Disclosure

13 Assumptions, Boundary Clauses, Asset Ledger

The proposal distinguishes formal, background, and provisional data via sources.json source_registry. Per-asset rights ledger is registered with author/generator, license, attribution location, and permitted-use scope for each asset (logos, renders, fonts, maps, code, OSM derivatives).

Open data gaps: Official polygon, approved regulatory conditions, land/building survey data, cross-region agreements, fiscal budget, and external-party authorisations are not yet available. All spatial recommendations are conceptual and require professional re-validation.
