AI Scenarios

01 心跳列车

As Line 13 trains pass, in-ground lights brighten, follow, and retain warmth. Visual and tactile, not loud.

02 草地剧场

Grass by day, stage by night. People are protagonists; AI is lighting designer.

03 旧铁轨耳语

Scan QR for railway history and AI soundscapes at whisper level.

04 呼吸门

South entrance, warm white breathing light, like a porch light welcoming home.

05 雾中光河

North section fiber optics in mist light with footsteps; flowing, transient, no gathering.

06 儿童声音绘画墙

Children's voices become colored ripples; gentle, fun, not frightening.

07 开发者Demo角

Semi-outdoor prototype demos and peer exchange; casual and informal.

08 清河风语

Wind devices transform Qinghe wind into light bands and low harmonics.

09 社区电影夜

2-4 times/month; directional speakers control sound range.

10 脉搏晨跑

Foot lights pulse with running rhythm; tricolor gradient along 9 km.

11 AI铁路故事

Keyword-generated Jing-Zhang railway stories on e-ink displays; quiet, low power.

12 脉搏数据墙

Real-time park vitals; anonymous data, no personal surveillance.

North section viaduct pillars project a century of Jing-Zhang imagery as trains pass; diners watch across the road.
