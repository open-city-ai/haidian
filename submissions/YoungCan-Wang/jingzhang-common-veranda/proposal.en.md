# Jing-Zhang Common Veranda

**JING-ZHANG COMMON VERANDA** does not arrange AI exhibits along a street. It treats the nine-kilometre heritage route as Haidian's shared veranda: a place to shelter, rest, transfer, meet, and understand what data an AI scenario uses, who is responsible, and when it must stop. The spatial proposition is “one spine, three verandas, six gates, twelve scenarios”; the governance proposition is “every access has an explanation, every trial has an exit.” All spatial moves are Conceptual Recommendations for professional deepening, not statutory plans or government approvals.

## Overall Design Area: Urban Renewal and Regulatory-Plan-Level Urban Design

The structure combines one north-south public spine, six east-west gates, and six topology-safe program bands. A single set of cut lines produces a gap-free land-use partition; mobility links then turn colored areas into walkable relationships. [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-001] [standard:MOHURD-CONTROL-DETAILED-PLANNING] [depth:land_use_layout]

Regulatory-plan-level work also means designing the unknowns: FAR, height, road redlines, setbacks, and density remain pending, with required evidence and downstream impacts stated explicitly. [depth:development_intensity_controls] The schematic building prototypes total about **12.67 ha** of footprint and are not a building survey or floor-area forecast. [data:geometry/buildings.geojson#BLDG-001] [metric:building_footprint_area_sqm]

Reversible civic interfaces come first: shade, accessible stopping points, legible signs, isolated power, and staffed review desks. Height and massing remain pending official controls; current guidance covers open ground floors, segmented mass, heritage sightlines, and low-disturbance nights. [depth:height_massing_character]

## Land Use, Building Scale, and Retain-Renovate-Demolish Strategy

The six program bands use public land-use terminology without claiming approved zoning. [standard:MNR-LAND-USE-CLASSIFICATION-GUIDE] The veranda crosses program boundaries through shade, rest, drainage, signs, service, and exit interfaces rather than changing ownership.

The building handoff uses retain, adaptive reuse, new-build pending confirmation, and data pending. It names no demolition target and treats every footprint as a prototype rather than inventory. [depth:retain_renovate_demolish]

Massing guidance favors segmented, low-pressure, permeable public edges. Height, fire separation, structure, heritage views, and roof equipment require professional deepening. [standard:MOHURD-ARCH-DESIGN-DEPTH-2016]

## Transport, Rail, Municipal Infrastructure, and Public Services

The spine prioritizes walking, cycling, and universal access; six east-west gates turn campus, park, neighborhood, and station connections into separate verification tasks. Current lines are conceptual, not engineering alignments. [data:geometry/roads.geojson#ROAD-001] [depth:traffic_rail_slow_parking]

Eight conceptual projects cover shade baseline, six gates, the three veranda prototypes, contribution landmarks, an operations desk, and an official-data replacement drill.
