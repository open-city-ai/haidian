Jing-Zhang Common Veranda v0.2

JING-ZHANG COMMON VERANDA is neither a street lined with AI exhibits nor a closed technology campus beside a historic railway. It treats the Jing-Zhang corridor as Haidian's civic veranda for the future: a place to walk, transfer, learn, work and meet, while also seeing what data an AI scenario uses, who is responsible, when it pauses and how a person can exit.

Version 0.2 turns “one spine, three verandas, six gates and twelve scenarios” into a spatial system. One north-south civic spine links railway culture and daily services. Three key areas become test, learning and living verandas. Six east-west city gates stitch campuses, communities, universities and transit. Twelve scenario nodes share one access—explanation—human—exit protocol. Every move remains a Conceptual Recommendation for professional deepening and does not replace statutory planning or government approval.

Five spatial tensions

  • Continuity versus fracture: the rail heritage creates a strong north-south line, while roads, walls and superblocks weaken east-west urban connections.
  • Innovation versus civic access: research resources are concentrated, yet residents and visitors rarely see how research produces public value.
  • Transit versus the last kilometre: rail and arterial access are strong, but walking, cycling and accessible connections from stations to campuses and communities need site-by-site verification.
  • Heritage versus daily life: rail, Zhongguancun and AI narratives are rich but lack one everyday shared carrier.
  • Intelligence versus accountability: AI scenarios are expanding, while clear human owners, explanation desks, pause conditions and appeal routes remain spatially absent. 深度

Identity and culture

The Common Veranda mark combines open brackets, twin rail lines and an action-orange node. The brackets express access, the rails connect heritage and collaboration, and the orange node keeps people in the loop. Wayfinding uses track number + veranda action + status colour, keeping open, test, pause and staffed-service states legible without a phone.

The cultural sequence runs from 1909 engineering agency, through Zhongguancun open innovation, to accountable AI authorship and continuously maintained civic intelligence. Zhan Tianyou becomes a method of verifiable, transferable engineering—not only a portrait. Zhongguancun becomes organisational openness—not only an industry label. AI culture makes responsibility, provenance and repair records visible.

Overall Design Scope: Urban Renewal and Control-Plan-Level Urban Design

One spine

The civic spine combines rail heritage interpretation, all-weather slow mobility, continuous tree canopy, rain gardens, public services and scenario accountability. It is not one project that must be built at once, but a public-realm standard that can be tested and linked in sections. 空间数据 深度

Six gates

Six east-west city gates turn crossings of rail, roads, walls and campus edges into six independent verification tasks. Each gate needs continuous walking, cycle parking, accessible ramps, night lighting, legible signs, staffed help and stormwater buffering. Bridges, tunnels and structural works remain subject to professional investigation. 指标

Six program bands and building renewal

Program bands express dominant civic relationships, not statutory zoning. Buildings follow a five-level handoff: retain value, open the ground floor, adaptively reuse, add reversibly, or mark pending confirmation. Valuable existing fabric is retained. Suitable research, education and commercial ground floors gain public interfaces. New veranda components remain separate, maintainable and replaceable. Buildings without ownership or structural evidence receive no demolition conclusion. 深度

The 22 building footprints remain conceptual prototypes, totalling approximately 12.67 ha. They are not an existing-condition inventory or a confirmed development scale. 指标

Detailed Design of the Three Key Areas

Each key area uses the same grammar: existing context, civic axis, east-west gate, central commons, six program nodes and professional handoff conditions. Real building context supports orientation; coloured axes and nodes remain conceptual. 指标

01 Zhongzhiyuan: Responsible Test Veranda

Zhongzhiyuan does not become an unattended test field. It turns testing into observable, explainable and interruptible civic innovation. The public observation veranda is physically separated from the test lane. Test intake, risk explanation, a human steward and a manual off control appear before entry. Red-team testing, low-speed robotics and compute-energy auditing form a public evidence chain.

The six nodes are test intake and explanation, a low-speed robotics sandbox, red-team courtyard, energy and safety audit kiosk, public observation veranda and human takeover desk. Campus permission, fire and industrial safety, test limits, data rights and an on-site human owner are prerequisites. Boundary crossing, link loss, non-reproducibility or unavailable takeover stops a trial.

02 Beijing AI Origin: Open Learning Veranda

AI Origin turns boundaries between university, research campus, startup community and residents into an interface where knowledge can be seen, discussed and reused. The open-source release gate checks licence and provenance. Public learning steps support courses and community explanation. Research-to-civic tables connect models and public-service problems. The accessible learning loop and rail rain garden ensure the place first works as everyday civic space.

The six nodes are the release gate, public learning steps, research-to-civic table, accessible learning loop, rail rain garden and community review desk. Open hours, IP, minor protection, event noise and station access require joint professional and operational confirmation.

03 Dazhongsi: Everyday Civic Veranda

Dazhongsi tests how AI enters ordinary city life. Transit arrival, commercial ground floors, community services and rail culture meet in an all-weather civic living room. Responsible retail, elder navigation and community appeals are staffed by default. The Centennial Civic Ledger Clock displays running, paused, complained-about and repaired scenarios—not only technical achievements.

The six nodes are the transit arrival living room, responsible retail desk, elder navigation, Civic Ledger Clock, night co-governance garden and community appeal desk. Traffic, commercial ownership, privacy, advertising, noise and night safety remain prerequisites.

AI Ecosystem, Personas and AI+ Scenarios

Six principal user groups are researchers, startups, university students, nearby families, elderly and disabled users, and city operations/public-service staff. Each gains a public capability that does not require being an industry insider: quiet staying, accessible arrival, explanation, appeal, pause or continued participation.

Twelve scenarios form four groups: industry testing, open learning, civic care, and governance/culture. Every scenario identifies its space, minimum data, human owner, public information, pause condition and exit route. Land Use, Building Scale and Retain/Renew/Remove Strategy

Mobility, Rail, Utilities and Public Facilities

Mobility has four levels: transit gateways; six east-west city gates; a continuous walking, cycling and accessible spine; and local loops completing the last kilometre. Any crossing of rail, express roads, bridges, tunnels or underground links remains a conceptual question rather than an engineering-feasibility claim. 深度

Utilities follow a low-tech base + removable smart layer. Seating, shade, ramps, drinking water and signs work without power. Sensors, edge compute, metering and lighting controls have separate power, permission and manual shutdown. Public services do not depend on collecting more data, and high-impact decisions hand off to a person. 深度

Renewal Projects, Policy and Phasing

Delivery has four steps. First, measure and complete the civic baseline of shade, ramps, seating, signs and lighting. Second, test six city gates, open hours and staffed service with movable components. Third, link test, learning and living verandas through scenario and operating ledgers. Fourth, enter professional deepening when the official base, controls, utilities, fire, heritage and engineering evidence arrive. 深度

Operating rhythms are daily service and care, monthly maintainer salons, quarterly open evaluation and an annual Open Veranda Week. Performance focuses on public open hours, accessibility issue closure, published test-result cards, complaint response, pause and repair records—not unverified industrial or visitor promises.
