Jing-Zhang Common Veranda

JING-ZHANG COMMON VERANDA does not arrange AI exhibits along a street. It treats the nine-kilometre heritage route as Haidian's shared veranda: a place to shelter, rest, transfer, meet, and understand what data an AI scenario uses, who is responsible, and when it must stop. The spatial proposition is “one spine, three verandas, six gates, twelve scenarios”; the governance proposition is “every access has an explanation, every trial has an exit.” All spatial moves are Conceptual Recommendations for professional deepening, not statutory plans or government approvals.
