The Five-Way Symbiosis Protocol

The core innovation is not another zoning diagram but a **Symbiosis Interface Protocol** — standardized interfaces that let different urban actors coexist, negotiate, and evolve:

| Symbiosis | Mechanism | Spatial Carrier | |---|---|---| | **Inherited** Heritage↔Innovation | Railway timeline segments become physical stages for AI product public testing | GREEN-001 heritage park axis | | **Campus-Industry** Campus↔Industry | Shared ground floors, publishing halls, IP clinics as "transformation interfaces" | BLDG-003 transformation complex | | **Human-AI Intelligence** Human↔AI | AI suggests/sorts/simulates; humans confirm/permit/appeal/shutdown. 12 scenarios all have manual takeover points | ROAD-001 corridor | | **Blue-Green** Green↔Urban | 5 green spaces + 6 public spaces as four "interface types": heritage/riverside/community/traffic | GREEN-001~005, PUBLIC-001~006 | | **Day-Night** Day↔Night | "Temporal passports" for 8 low-rise buildings and 6 public spaces: R&D by day, open courses at dusk, community learning at night, quiet mode after 22:00 | PHASE-001 |

**Why "symbiosis" instead of "integration"?** Integration implies boundaries disappear. But for AI and humans, labs and residences, railway heritage and new technology, boundaries protect both sides. **Symbiosis acknowledges boundaries, then makes them manageable, negotiable, and evolvable through standardized interfaces.**

Spatial Structure: One Belt, Three Cores, Six Seams, Multiple Nodes

  • **One Belt**: Jing-Zhang Heritage Park symbiosis axis — core green belt + slow-mobility corridor + segmented AI display nodes
  • **Three Cores**: Zhongzhiyuan (technology-governance symbiosis), AI Origin Community (knowledge-community symbiosis), Dazhongsi (industry-life symbiosis)
  • **Six Seams**: East-west pedestrian/cycling connections stitching campus-park-community gaps
  • **Two Wings**: Xiaoyuehe Scenario Empowerment Wing (controlled low-risk testing) + Zhongguancun Technology Service Wing (legal/IP/standards/capital/talent services)

Governance and Operations (Conceptual)

  • **Developer Council**: quarterly meetings, ≥40% developer representation
  • **Scenario Opening**: submit → council review → safety audit → deposit → KPI → exit/restore (≤90 days)
  • **Community Co-Governance Committee**: 4 residents + 3 enterprises + 2 universities + 1 government observer
  • **Complaint mechanism**: 15 working-day written reply; 3+ complaints trigger automatic AI downgrade to manual review
