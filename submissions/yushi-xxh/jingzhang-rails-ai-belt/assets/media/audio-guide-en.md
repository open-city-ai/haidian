# Jingzhang AI Symbiosis Belt · three-minute audio guide (v2.2)

This three-minute audio guide explains the proposal in four parts: what it is, what its criterion is, how the ten scenarios land, and what its authority is not. The transcript and captions match sentence by sentence; no information exists only inside the audio.

Part one, what it is. In 1909, Zhan Tianyou designed the "人字形" switchback on the Jing-Zhang Railway: a train gained climbing capacity through a reversing path, with no new equipment. This proposal translates the switchback into an urban AI governance contract. Before any intelligent capability moves forward on the belt, it must register a forward state, a switchback path, residual traces and an acceptance decision. Evolution presupposes rollback.

Part two, the criterion. Three red lines: it can be stopped, it can be appealed, and a non-AI equivalent service remains. Anyone can press the physical stop button at the Zero Switchback Kiosk without an app, an account or a network. The offline review page has a smart-layer toggle; switching it off shows, for each scenario card, how the same thing is done and who is responsible without AI.

Part three, how the ten scenarios land. The ten scenarios cover open-source release, the safety-governance sandbox, edge compute, slow-traffic navigation, the international roadshow, the low-carbon corridor, achievement transfer, the data-element room, life services and the global AI week route. All ten cards are registered as machine-readable Switchback Protocol instances. In eighty rule checks, seventy injected defects were intercepted and zero were missed.

Part four, the boundary of its authority. This is a concept suggestion and a reference scheme for professional teams to deepen. It is not a government decision, not an approval conclusion, and not an implementation commitment. Until official boundaries, regulatory plans, road red lines and ownership records are published, spatial conclusions stay provisional or unknown. The Jing-Zhang Railway taught the city how to reverse a train; the Symbiosis Belt asks every AI capability to register its reverse path before moving forward.
