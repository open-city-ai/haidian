Zero Switchback Kiosk · physical stop (concept prototype)

Fixed signage + always-on lighting + a paper duty log + a staffed counter + a physical stop button. Enquiry, complaint and deactivation require no app, account or network. Position, size, materials and accessible clearances are set by a professional team from on-site survey and accessibility codes. Status: concept proposal, not sited, not built, not run.
