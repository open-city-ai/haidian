Assumptions and Replacement Rule

  • A-GEO-001 · The overall design area exists only as a provisional rough polygon (PROV-SITE-001) fitted from the announcement's textual boundaries; its lines are not road redlines or statutory lines, and all areas and ratios must be recomputed when official polygons arrive.
  • A-GEO-002 · The six land-use zones (LU-01 to LU-06) are derived exactly from PROV-SITE-001 by shared horizontal cuts with no gaps or overlaps; their boundaries are conceptual design lines, not parcel or ownership boundaries.
  • A-GEO-003 · The three key-area rectangles (PROV-KEY-001/002/003) are placeholder extents under the announced area constraints, not parcel or ownership boundaries; key-area areas are recomputed from provisional geometry and shown separately from announced areas.
  • A-GEO-004 · Green-space shapes (GR-*) are concept proposals, not existing or approved green land; green-space area and green ratio are concept values and must be recomputed when official data arrives.
  • A-GEO-005 · Public space is expressed as point/line/small polygons with no full measurable polygon; a polygonal recompute area of 0 does not mean there is no public space (the public-life argument uses the point/line network), and it must be recomputed when official polygons arrive.
  • A-GEO-006 · All footprints are low-confidence agent-generated concept massing with no existing-building survey; retain/renovate/demolish must not be treated as a survey conclusion and must await official status survey and ownership data.
  • A-GEO-007 · The road network (RD-*) is a concept network whose projected segment lengths are summed, not the existing road length; road redlines and sections are missing and must be replaced by official road data.
  • A-BOUNDARY-001 · None of the three scope levels or three key areas has an official precise polygon; only provisional rough boundaries may be used for generation, display and self-check, never as an official redline, approval basis or precise area basis.
  • A-CONTROL-001 · Regulatory control conditions (FAR, height, density, green ratio, setbacks, building control lines) are missing; development intensity and building scale are recorded as unknown or concept values, never as approved indicators.
  • A-LAND-001 · Land ownership, parcel boundaries and demolition pathways are unverified; all spatial actions prioritize leasing, shared use or temporary permits and must not infer demolition or ownership conclusions.
  • A-HERITAGE-001 · Heritage control ranges for the Jing-Zhang railway park and the Qinghuayuan station site are missing; the heritage corridor is an attention corridor only, not a heritage control line.
  • A-MUNICIPAL-001 · Utility, fire, flood-drainage and sponge-city conditions are missing; municipal capacity and interfaces remain concepts pending verification.
  • A-ROAD-001 · Road redlines and sections are missing; section width ranges are concept suggestions, not engineering or traffic-organization conclusions.
  • A-OPS-001 · Operators for all scenarios and long-term operations are proposed and pending confirmation; they must not be written as confirmed arrangements.
  • A-GOV-001 · The public coordinator, governance seats and independent review panel are proposed and pending confirmation; they must not be stated as confirmed government arrangements.
  • A-FUND-001 · Funding, budgets and sources are proposed and pending confirmation; no funding or fiscal commitment may be made.
  • A-DATA-001 · Indicator data stewards and data-governance owners are pending confirmation; no indicator may be claimed measurable before its baseline is established.
  • A-METRIC-001 · All four concept-indicator baselines are unknown; the 2030 target values are conceptual scenarios supported only by an explicit assumption and must be reset after baselining.
