Key Areas: Three Station Prototypes

The three key areas are not cloned science parks but three testable, reversible, auditable pilot prototypes; pilots enter and exit through the seven-step protocol.

key_areas.geojson

Zhongzhiyuan · Validation (N)

Graded test center + O&M compliance workshop + validation plaza; enclosed test segment with physical separation and credential entry; tests registered by risk grade.

  • Enclosed certification / O&M audit / human-robot collaboration
  • Test-data privacy and site safety grading as risks

AI Origin Community · Co-learning (M)

Developer co-learning center + compute-collaboration building + plaza; developer camps, open-source co-creation, compute reservation, accessible learning.

  • AI+education / open-source collaboration / multilingual services
  • University-boundary and IP as risks

Dazhongsi · Experience (S)

AI-native experience pavilion + enterprise-service hub + pilot gateway plaza; receives rail-arriving crowds; delivery handover and night services.

  • Delivery handover / AI guiding / AI-native consumption
  • Shared-road safety and commercial sustainability as risks

Pilot Corridor Grading & Service Loop

Three right-of-way grades confine pilot risk to the smallest auditable scope: shared slow-mobility (speed-limited, yielding) → time-segregated (roadbed closed in specified windows) → enclosed test (physical separation, credential entry); the service loop runs inside the site perimeter linking the three stations and the Zhongguancun wing.
