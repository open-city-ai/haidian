## Coordinated Research Area: Industry and Future City Research

### Naming System and Visual Identity (agent.1)

**Main Name: Zhima·Jing-Zhang; English Name: The Neural Corridor.** Naming Logic: The Jing-Zhang Railway, China's first independently designed and constructed mainline railway, has been transformed into a linear space that runs through the innovative heart of Haidian. It is reimagined as the city's "neural main artery" — where the tracks are akin to axons, stations to synapses, and the Three Zones and Two Wings to neural nodes — all encapsulating the "Centennial Jing-Zhang Cultural Belt, Urban AI Living Experience Belt, and AI Fusion Innovation Belt" [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK].

Naming System (Conceptual Recommendation): The overall name for the project is "Intelligence Vein·Jing-Zhang The Neural Corridor"; the main axis space is named "Century Rail Commons"; the three key nodes are named respectively — Zhongzhiyuan "The Kernel" (a full-stack independent innovation system), Beijing AI Origin Community "The Origin" (a world-class AI Innovation Ecosystem), and Dazhongsi "The Exchange" (a smart-native new business model). Two wings are named — Zhongguancun Technology Services Wing "The Bridge" and Xiaoyue River Scenario Enablement Wing "The Stream." All names are original generic combinations and do not replicate existing city, park, or company names, nor do they use any unauthorized trademarks or fonts.

**Logo and Visual Identity Direction**: Take the "person-shaped" turnaround route of the Qinglong Bridge as the theme —— a classic symbol of China's railway engineering self-innovation —— abstracting the "person" into a continuous line that gradually changes from double rails to neural synapses/data pulses, symbolizing the century-long legacy from the person-shaped to the intelligent body. The main color is suggested to be a gradient of "Jing-Zhang Green" (vegetation in the relic park) and "Electronic Blue" (digital pulse); the font is recommended to use an open-source commercial font (such as Source Han Serif) to ensure clean global dissemination rights. This direction is merely a visual concept, and the final VI must be deepened and completed by a professional design team, including trademark search.
