01 / SHUTDOWNShutdown test

In an approved window, disable non-essential AI. Static signs, original signal timing, paper, phone, cash, staffed, and emergency paths must stand alone.

02 / PARITYEquivalence test

Run the same real task through AI and non-AI paths; compare completion, time, cost, access breakpoints, and dignity as paired evidence.

03 / WHOLE TRIPCompleteness test

Co-test the entire chain from station or neighborhood entrance through crossing, waiting, transfer, and service counter. One broken link blocks expansion.

02 / KEY AREAS / BUILDINGS / RENEWAL

Three courtyards
three forms of daily life

Future Hutong does not copy gray-brick roofs. It keeps the relational spaces that make a hutong work: front street, porch, court, and bounded back room.

01

Zhongzhiyuan / Toolmaking Courtyard

Interoperability, traffic shadow simulation, safety standards, and frontline repair. Success and failure both enter the public benchmark.

Make tools; publish error, energy, incident, and retirement records.
02

AI Origin / Co-Research Courtyard

Translate research into plain language before community review, prototyping, and human sign-off. Learning can happen without postponing the trip home.

Research reaches the neighborhood; ground floors support care and repair.
03

Dazhongsi / Hundred Trades Courtyard

Small shops, public service, accessible transfers, and everyday international exchange. Tools meet ordinary people in real routines.

Cash, paper, telephone, and staffed service always remain.

Front street makes the entrance legible; porch lowers social pressure; court hosts common life; back room protects professional testing boundaries.

03 / MOBILITY / BLUE-GREEN PUBLIC REALM

Signals begin
in shadow mode

"Smart" is not a permit. Compare options with aggregate or synthetic data, then let transport, bus, accessibility, and safety professionals decide whether a pilot may begin.

Now: demonstrate on a fixed cycle; read no live data.

  1. Intersection-level aggregate queues, bus arrivals, and button requests enter a shadow model.
  2. Compare pedestrian safety, bus reliability, adjacent-junction spillover, and the worst-served group together.
  3. A transport professional approves advice; the controller retains original timing.
  4. Only an authority-approved, time-limited pilot may go live; anomalies trigger immediate rollback.
  5. A public receipt names who benefits, who bears cost, who approved, and why a test stopped.
Do not collectNo facial recognition by default; personal traces never become the price of public convenience.
Protect firstPedestrian safety and access, bus reliability, cycling continuity—then general vehicle efficiency.
