Trajectory Loop: Centennial Jing-Zhang AI Civic Spine

This is a conceptual reference scheme for professional teams to deepen. It does not replace statutory planning, ownership review, transport engineering, public procurement, or government approval. The package uses provisional geometry because official CAD/GIS/PDF redlines and regulatory controls were not included in the public submission data.

Design basis and three scopes

The proposal uses the public call announcement, the open-city-ai/haidian taskbook and local professional-standard snapshots. The 43.6 km2 coordinated research area is used for regional innovation and future-city strategy. The 11.4 km2 overall design area is the computable study surface for the public spine, walking loop, public rooms, and renewal project packages. The 368.4 ha key-area scope contains Zhongzhiyuan AI Acceleration Area, Beijing AI Origin Community, and Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster.

The scopes form an evidence chain: research strategy, spatial structure, then phased project packages. geometry/constraints.geojson stores the research-area constraint separately from geometry/site_boundary.geojson, so the two area scales are not mixed.

Spatial structure and key areas

The concept is organized as three centres, two wings, and one civic belt. Zhongzhiyuan is the open-model and standards-testing centre; AI Origin Community is the near-campus transformation and talent-service centre; Dazhongsi is the station-city, intelligent-economy, and international-display centre. The western wing connects Zhongguancun technology services, while the eastern wing links Xiaoyuehe scenario empowerment. The Jing-Zhang AI Civic Spine ties them together.

The three key areas are conceptual templates: an open model living room and testing plaza at Zhongzhiyuan; a results-to-community street at AI Origin; and a station-front public room and intelligent-economy living room at Dazhongsi.

AI ecosystem, scenarios, and people

The ecosystem is expressed through eight interfaces: land, space, industry, finance, talent, compute, data, and scenarios. The package includes twelve scenario cards, three industry testbeds, and six user personas. Suggested scenarios include open-model release, algorithm safety testing, urban-renewal Q&A, walking navigation, low-carbon energy scheduling, heat-recovery visualization, heritage guidance, accessibility assistance, talent services, startup demonstrations, night wayfinding, and public co-creation.

All scenarios require data minimization, explainability, opt-out, human review, offline-equivalent service, and an appeal record. No scenario is represented as an approved or fully deployed operation.

Land, buildings, mobility, blue-green space, and phasing

The land-use layer fully partitions the provisional overall design boundary into four conceptual zones: AI mixed innovation and talent services; community public services and adaptive reuse; heritage park and blue-green open space; and low-carbon municipal and research support. Building geometry shows six conceptual footprints for retain, repair, shared ground floor, and measured renewal. It is not a demolition, FAR, height, or ownership conclusion.

The mobility concept targets a 10.8 km heritage AI spine, a 16.2 km walking loop, and twelve east-west links. Blue-green space is shown as three framework fragments and four public rooms. Phasing is divided into 0-2 years for public-first pilots, 3-5 years for neighborhood links and adaptive-reuse pilots, and 5-10 years for a continuous civic spine and multi-party operations platform.

Brand, culture, and long-term operation

The visual identity direction uses two open trajectory lines: one for railway memory and one for an open AI future. The international line is: “Trace the railway. Test the future. Share the city.” Cultural storytelling follows four words: rail, street corner, compute, and memory. Suggested annual operations include an open data workshop, public scenario testing, an AI city forum, and an annual public-benefit review.

Metrics, sources, and risks

The computable provisional site area is 11,412,825.386 sqm. Recalculated green ratio is 0.381698 and public-space ratio is 0.072918. Announcement targets and provisional calculated values are separated in metrics.json; official geometries must replace provisional geometry and trigger recalculation before regulatory use.

Sources, assumptions, compliance, design-depth evidence, and copyright boundaries are recorded in the accompanying JSON and report files. The package contains no third-party stock imagery, unauthorized portraits, non-public spatial data, or claimed government endorsement.
