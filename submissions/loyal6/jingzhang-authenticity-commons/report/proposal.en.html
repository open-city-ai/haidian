Jing-Zhang Authenticity Commons

Verify Before Trust / 先验证，再相信

Jing-Zhang Authenticity Commons (JZAC) is not a truth authority. It is civic infrastructure that makes evidence visible, uncertainty speakable, mistakes reversible and the core service equally accessible without a smartphone. Its spatial idea is “one spine, three belts, three anchors, six stations and two reciprocal wings.” A railway-heritage public spine carries the Century-old Jing-Zhang Cultural Belt, Urban AI Life Experience Belt and AI Convergence Innovation Belt. Zhongzhiyuan, Beijing AI Origin Community and Dazhongsi become evaluation, translation and trusted-procurement anchors. Six repeatable station types provide public verification, evaluation, evidence dossiers, correction and recovery, trusted procurement and screenless service. The Zhongguancun Technology Services Wing converts dossiers into IP, capital, procurement and risk-engineering references; the Xiaoyue River Scenario Enablement Wing converts real civic needs into tests and service improvement.

Industry and Future-city Research

JZAC turns trust from marketing into a public capability that no actor may monopolise. An enterprise receives no permanent certification; it receives a versioned, purpose-bound, expiring and revocable dossier. The dossier separates supplier claims, blind-test evidence, scope limits, human review, remediation and appeal. Start-ups can use it in incubation; procurers inspect evidence relevant to a use; investors see maturity and unresolved risks; insurers may inspect reproducibility without the commons setting premiums or promising cover. The Zhongguancun wing organises IP, legal, capital and risk services. The Xiaoyue River wing organises health, education, merchant, mobility, heritage and community-correction scenarios Source Standard.

The future-city form is not a sensor-saturated smart district. Retained brick fabric, railway memory, trees and ordinary civic rooms carry reversible interiors, open ground floors and small staffed counters. Screens are optional; paper, telephone, audio, signed communication and face-to-face service have equal standing. Continuous facial recognition, cross-context tracking, automated blacklists and unappealable reputation scores are prohibited.

Overall Urban Renewal and Control-plan-depth Urban Design

The overall structure is a railway-heritage verification green spine, three cross-connections and six staffed interfaces. The land-use layer is a complete non-overlapping partition into trusted commerce, inclusive community services, culture and public display, media literacy and education, evaluation R&D, and ecology/open testing. It is a design partition, not a statutory amendment. Five centre lines express walking, cycling and transit connections, not road redlines. Twelve building polygons are illustrative retain/adapt units; none commits demolition or construction before survey, ownership, structure and heritage review Spatial data Spatial data Depth.

Each station shares a spatial protocol: visible step-free entry; at least two staffed communication modes; private consultation; clean/contaminated file separation; waiting and companion seats; a public methods wall; paper receipts; device isolation and secure storage. Major evaluation workshops require licensed-data isolation and red-team retesting. FAR, height, density, fire egress and equipment loads remain professional preconditions Depth Metric.

Detailed Design of the Three Key Areas

Zhongzhiyuan - evaluation and standards anchor. An evaluation workshop, open benchmark yard, standards room and pre-release gate organise the northern anchor. Public demonstrations never expose test data. Results move through claim, test, remediation and retest before entering the technology-services wing. The announced 192.1 ha is authoritative; submitted geometry is provisional Spatial data.

Beijing AI Origin Community - translation and correction anchor. Near-campus release, open-source collaboration, media-literacy classrooms, the dossier office and recovery station occupy a walkable ground-floor network. A mislabelled person or small business can freeze a propagated label, inspect evidence, obtain independent review and synchronize a correction. The announced area is 104.3 ha Spatial data.

Dazhongsi - trusted procurement and screenless anchor. Transit connections support procurement, merchant contract/invoice verification, creator rights, bilingual exchange and a no-smartphone counter. A pitch must show test limits and failures, not replace validation with a demonstration. The announced area is 72.0 ha Spatial data.

Land Use, Building Scale and Retain/Adapt/Remove Method

The renewal method is retain first, reversible adaptation first, programme before structure. Category A retains and repairs railway heritage and potentially valuable buildings; B opens ground floors and adapts interiors for stations; C is any supplementary volume that may be considered only after survey and ownership confirmation. The building layer contains 12 conceptual units, not an existing-building inventory, and no demolition commitment. A later survey must classify each verified building by identity, safety, heritage value, lifecycle carbon and operating fit Standard Depth.

All statutory development controls remain unknown. Only provisional area, conceptual footprints, design green space, six station spaces and centre-line length are recomputable. A design green ratio is not a statutory green ratio; illustrative footprints cannot establish density; no floor area is derived from invented storeys.

Mobility, Transit, Municipal and Public-service Facilities

Mobility follows public transport, slow spine, cross-stitch and the final accessible 50 metres. Dazhongsi emphasises transit and intersection linkage; the Origin Community emphasises campus-park-community ground-floor continuity; Zhongzhiyuan emphasises cross-ring-road and Qinghe access. Five submitted centre lines are clipped to the provisional boundary and are recomputable, but cross-sections, intersections, parking and under-bridge space need official transport information Spatial data Depth.

The core intake can operate offline. Licensed evaluation data is isolated and deleted under policy. Energy, compute, drainage, flood, fire, network redundancy and heat loads require engineering confirmation. Because no official utility geometry exists, the constraints layer is empty and documents the gap instead of drawing fictional pipes Spatial data Depth.

Blue-green Space, Public Space and Character

The rail green spine is an everyday civic room, not a technology spectacle. It provides shade, stormwater, cycling and pause; a cross-greenway links the Xiaoyue River wing; six polygons represent staffed civic interfaces. Accessibility, night safety, seating and companion space precede interactive devices. Design-space ratios are internal measures, never approved green controls Spatial data Metric.

The palette is railway navy, muted teal, warm amber, brick red and paper white. New elements are light, demountable and set apart from retained fabric. Public methods walls state: provenance is not truth; detectors make errors; uncertainty may remain; results can be appealed. No facial-recognition boxes, surveillance aesthetic, giant screens or cyberpunk imagery is used.

Renewal Projects, Policy and Phasing

In 0-18 months, pilot staffed screenless service, public verification, licensed benchmarks and a correction ledger, gated by privacy, accessibility and safety review. In 18-36 months, after official geometry, ownership and engineering evidence, adapt six stations and connect three anchors; test dossier exchange with incubation and procurement. In 36-60 months, subject to independent evaluation, form corridor governance and explore insurance-risk reference and international evaluation exchange. Every phase may stop, reverse or narrow; none is described as approved Spatial data Depth.

Eight work packages cover screenless protocol, multimodal benchmark, three-anchor/six-station adaptation, victim recovery, trusted procurement, media literacy, creator/heritage records and independent annual audit. Public core service remains free. Professional evaluation, training, service contracts and research grants may support operations; case data may never be sold.

Risk, Rights and Compliance

The highest risk is false authority: an uncertain detector packaged as a final decision may deny service, damage reputation and exclude people. High-impact adverse decisions therefore require two-person review; all receipts show scope, expiry, revocation and appeal; cross-service profiling and face databases are prohibited; health, education, civic or business service is never automatically denied; corrections return to the original channel; sensitive cases use minimum fields and expiry. Independent governance includes public-service, research, SME, accessibility, legal and clinical voices Depth Source.
