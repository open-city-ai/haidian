First proof · Public and industry pilots, operating return, and use envelopes

A Public Pilot · Version Line All-Ages Co-Pilot

Railway-heritage park—green spine—Dazhongsi service nodes: physical map, shade, and human help remain available; users initiate AI advice; operators review heat safety and accessibility. Track breaks, help response, and non-AI use; stop when advice cannot be explained.

B Industry Pilot · Zhongzhiyuan Last-500-Metre

Digital replay → safety / permission gate → speed-capped field test → independent evaluation. A critical failure in reproducibility, human takeover, emergency stop, incident, or sunset drill returns the pilot to simulation.

2035 design targets

Every high-impact scenario has a model card, human channel, appeal, emergency stop, and exit; at least two model-to-street chains remain reproducible; every expansion publishes construction, operations, exit, validation, and actual-use cost; existing parks, ground floors, under-bridge space, and adaptable buildings are reused first, and public space remains safe when AI fails.

Assumptions and data gaps

A-BOUNDARY-001 supports overall conceptual measurement only; the local HD00-1603 evidence does not replace the provisional boundary. Overall transit-station connection, parking supply, building height, FAR, municipal capacity, ownership, and heritage control remain unknown; any formal submission must first add a source, responsible owner, and review receipt.

Provisional boundary, existing buildings, regulatory plan, parking, transit stations, municipal capacity, and heritage data remain explicit gaps. Spatial and operational recommendations on this page are conceptual reference proposals.
