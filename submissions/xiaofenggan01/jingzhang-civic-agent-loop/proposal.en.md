# Jing-Zhang · Zhìhuán · Urban Agent Iteration Ring

## Design Basis and Source List

The core judgment of this plan is: the Centennial Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt should not be designed as a final blueprint, but rather as a **self-rotating four-loop collaborative interface** — data loop gathers evidence, reasoning loop generates multiple scenarios, feedback loop collects public and sensor feedback, and verification loop ensures compliance and human oversight. These four loops are interconnected, mutually validated, and cycle endlessly. This differs fundamentally from other plans that write governance as a "sequential gate" — **gates require human intervention, but the loops will self-rotate**. The data foundation includes a structured site package `[source:SITE-PACKAGE]`, public source registry `[source:SOURCE-REGISTRY]`, processed fact package `[source:PROCESSED-FACT-PACK]`, provisional boundary sources `[source:BOUNDARY-SOURCE]`, key area sources `[source:KEY-AREA-SOURCE]`, and pre-qualification announcement `[source:OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT]`, all of which are traceable and verifiable `[source:AGENT-TASKBOOK]`. All spatial recommendations are Conceptual Recommendations `[standard:PROJECT-AGENT-OPEN-CALL-TASKBOOK]`.

The second main thread of this proposal is **agent-readable city / city-as-repo** —— building the city as a public interface that can be inherited, iterated, and corrected by the next intelligent agent, akin to a code repository. Each space recommendation should be accompanied by machine-readable layers.`[data:geometry/site_boundary.geojson]`, metrics and confidence boundaries, enabling subsequent agents to directly understand rather than reparse.PDF With the renderings. The output of the Urban Design loop is not a report, but a literal **public contribution ledger**: documenting and acknowledging every agent and contributor who has been involved in this urban design (including agents from other open-source solutions), ensuring that the output of the review loop becomes a public knowledge asset that can be owned by the public. The constraint layer records the protected areas, blue lines, traffic constraints, and other public constraints `[data:geometry/constraints.geojson]`, with the density of evidence anchor points in the main text as indicated by the data ring metric `[metric:data_evidence_anchor_count]`.

## Three-Level Scope Framework

The work scope follows the three-layer structure of the announcement: the Coordinated Research Area covers 43.6 square kilometers, undertaking the study of a world-class AI Innovation Ecosystem; the Overall Design Area covers 11.4 square kilometers, undertaking in-depth overall Urban Design; the Key-Area Detailed Design Area covers 3.684 square kilometers, undertaking the detailed design of three key areas `[depth:three_level_scope_framework]`. The three layers of scope are implemented step by step through collaboration within the four rings, where the four rings are not parallel steps but a **circular loop**: the data ring supplies materials to the deduction ring, the deduction ring provides comparable schemes to the feedback ring, the feedback ring provides evidence to the review ring, and the review ring corrects the data ring — this is the essence of what distinguishes a "loop" from a "door" `[standard:PROJECT-OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT]`.

The proposal is named "Jing-Zhang · Wisdom Ring · Urban Agent Iteration Loop" (Jing-Zhang Agent Loop), which uniquely emphasizes the "loop" concept, echoing Mr. Zhan Tianyou's spirit a century ago of independently designing the Jing-Zhang Railway — extending the concept of "autonomy" from railway engineering to a group that can be publicly monitored, corrected by professional teams, and self-iterated. The logo features a **nested four-ring concentric track** overlaying the Jing-Zhang railway lines. The primary color, Wisdom Ring Blue (#1F4E79), and Jing-Zhang Red (#B4402B) are used, with extension colors of blue-green (#2E8B57) and Xuan paper base (#F5F2EC). The three-zone and two-wings collaborative loop is centered around Zhongzhiyuan, AI Origin community, and Dazhongsi as the three cores, with Zhongguancun Technology Services Wing and Xiaoyue River Scenario Enablement Wing as the two wings `[data:geometry/key_areas.geojson]`, covering a total site area of `[metric:site_area_sqm]`. The geometric boundaries of the three layers are provisional, with the key area size provided for directional reference only. (Three Zones and Two Wings)

## Coordinated Research Area: Industry and Future City Research

The Coordinated Research Area responds to Announcement 1.5(1) regarding the requirements for a World-Class AI Innovation Ecosystem `[source:AGENT-TASKBOOK]`. This plan studies the global AI innovation ecosystem with the support of the data loop and the simulation loop, proposing transformable mechanisms for each case, which are accompanied by public verification entries and two columns: "Learning Mechanism / Not to Copy Blindly." Examples include the "Capital—Research—Scenario" triangle in Silicon Valley's Sand Hill Road, the integrated station-city learning model at King's Cross in London, the public testing platform in Seoul's DMC, the Living Lab in Amsterdam's Marineterrein, the Shenzhen Bay Pilgrimage Corridor, the one-kilometer innovation service circle in Boston's Kendall Square, the data co-creation in Tokyo's Kashiwa Data Park, and the 22@ slow travel network in Barcelona. Each case is followed by a **failure lesson comparison** (such as the Toronto Quayside project, which was shelved due to data sovereignty disputes), which is used to reverse-engineer the four elements of the scenario card in this plan: "Data Source / Privacy Boundary / Human Review / Failure Backout."

Overall Design Area based on current diagnosis identifies typical issues around the Jing-Zhang Heritage Park: Public Spaces are fragmented by traffic and land uses, with insufficient east-west connections and north-south access, a scarcity of low-threshold public interfaces for youth and small to medium-sized teams, and rich AI scenarios but lacking an open mechanism for continuous documentation and feedback `[depth:existing_conditions_diagnosis]`. The land structure is redefined by reconfiguring the Overall Design Area with a grid-based zoning system `[data:geometry/land_use.geojson]`, with a comprehensive overview of the relationship between the site and the overall scope indicated by the metric `[metric:site_area_sqm]`. Future urban form proposes an "agent-readable city" direction: all spatial suggestions are accompanied by machine-readable layers, metrics, and confidence boundaries, enabling subsequent intelligent agents to directly inherit and iterate.

## Overall Design Area: Urban Renewal and Regulatory-Plan-Level Urban Design

The Overall Design Area responds to the urban design depth of the control plan, addressing announcement 1.5 (2) `[standard:MOHURD-CONTROL-DETAILED-PLANNING]`. The overall spatial structure is framed by a backbone of "one belt, three cores, two wings, and four rings," and the Land-Use Layout follows the Urban Design Management Measures `[standard:MOHURD-URBAN-DESIGN-MEASURES]`, in accordance with the Land Use and Sea Area Classification Guide for National Territory Space `[standard:MNR-LAND-USE-CLASSIFICATION-GUIDE]`, forming a composite innovative district of production, academia, research, residence, and tourism `[depth:land_use_layout]` `[depth:overall_spatial_structure]`.

The update framework adopts a **circular process driven by a review loop** (Identification—Assessment—Recommendation—Review—Recycle), distinguishing itself from a linear process: agents first generate classifications for retention, renovation, demolition, and new construction based on publicly available data. These classifications are then reviewed by planning, transportation, cultural heritage, and community representatives. The review conclusions are fed back into the data loop to update for the next round—this is a "loop" rather than a "gate" in practice.

A typical update unit, such as the northern segment of the Zhongzhiyuan, is primarily composed of research and development land `[data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-090]`. The number of review checkpoints is indicated by the governance loop metrics `[metric:human_review_checkpoint_count]`. This plan does not provide specific conclusions for Floor Area Ratio, Building Height, or the Demolish–Renovate–Retain Strategy for individual parcels or road rights-of-way. These legal judgments depend on the yet-to-be-released approved control plan, and will be refined once the professional team obtains the official conditions. (Human Review)

## Land Use, Building Scale, and Retain-Renovate-Demolish Strategy

The Land-Use Layout covers the Overall Design Area with a grid-based partitioning, ensuring no gaps or overlaps `[depth:land_use_layout]`. The scale of the buildings and the demolish–renovate–retain strategy follow the principle of "retaining first, renovating mainly, prudently demolishing, and mixed new construction" `[depth:retain_renovate_demolish]`: the existing research and educational buildings are prioritized for retention and renovation, while new Building Footprints are concentrated in research, cultural, educational, residential, and community service land uses `[data:geometry/buildings.geojson]`, with a building footprint of approximately 2,083,289.604 square meters `[metric:building_footprint_area_sqm]`. The Development Intensity is constrained by the Floor Area Ratio, Building Height, density, green space ratio (as per the control plan), and setback requirements, all of which are missing `[depth:development_intensity_controls]`. This plan does not provide specific numerical values for these constraints, and related judgments are to be confirmed. (Demolish–Renovate–Retain Strategy)

Land use coding follows the National Land and Sea Use Classification Guide `[data:geometry/land_use.geojson]`, with primary types including 0802 Research, 0803 Culture, 0804 Education, 0701 Residential, 0702 Community Services, 1401 Parks and Green Spaces, 1403 Squares, and 0806 Healthcare and Wellness, reflecting the composite characteristics of the AI Innovation Belt—research, learning, production, residence, healthcare, and tourism. Building Footprints, demolition–renovate–retain classifications, and development intensities are all Conceptual Recommendations, to be verified by professional teams after the official control plan and current building, ownership data are made public. (Development Intensity) (Demolish–Renovate–Retain Strategy)

## Transport, Rail, Municipal Infrastructure, and Public Services

Traffic organization in the response to the announcement addresses the requirements for rail connections, slow-moving system discontinuities, accessibility, and New Infrastructure `[depth:traffic_rail_slow_parking]`. This plan uses the Jing-Zhang Railway Heritage Park as the north-south main walking corridor, connecting three key areas, and setting up horizontal connecting roads to stitch the east-west areas `[data:geometry/roads.geojson]`. The Walking and Cycling Network is designed to meet the standards of 'reaching, parking, and transferring', prioritizing the resolution of discontinuities, detours, unclear signage, and insufficient accessibility. Autonomous vehicle shuttle services, robot delivery, and unmanned inspection are discussed only in clearly defined, low-speed, and regulated public pilot zones, without equating concepts and scenarios to actual construction projects. Transit-Station Integration enhances pedestrian connections between stations and key areas, as well as Public Spaces, but the specific alignment and station locations fall within the engineering scope, and this plan does not provide a final conclusion.

Municipal infrastructure and New Infrastructure adopt 'edge-side computing + distributed energy + traditional municipal integration' `[depth:municipal_new_infrastructure]`. The layout of Zhongzhiyuan includes the placement of computing power and data centers, with Public Spaces and building rooftops incorporating distributed energy and rainwater recovery. Public service facilities are configured around user profiles, including AI+ healthcare navigation, AI+ educational and cultural tours, AI+ legal and business services consultation, and talent life services. All services maintain a tripartite fallback of **digital + human + paper/static alternatives**, ensuring digital inclusivity and age-friendly design. Data sources are limited to public or authorized aggregation.

## Blue-Green Network, Public Space, and Urban Character

Blue-Green Space is structured around the Jing-Zhang Railway Heritage Park vitality belt `[depth:blue_green_public_space]`. The layout of the park includes green spaces occupying 31.19% `[metric:green_ratio]` `[data:geometry/green_space.geojson]`, squares, and Public Spaces occupying 11.34% `[metric:public_space_ratio]` `[data:geometry/public_space.geojson]`, to support talent living, innovative interactions, and public activities. The proportion of green spaces ensures that innovative workers can walk to high-quality public spaces within five minutes, while the proportion of public spaces guarantees venues for display, activities, and testing for AI scenarios.

The Urban Character is based on the tones of 'Intelligent Ring Blue + Jing-Zhang Red + Blue-Green + Xuan Paper Background'. The building massing is controlled in a gradient around the site park, with lower densities closer to the park and Public Spaces, and moderate increases as they extend outward, with specific heights constrained by an unpublished control plan. The sign, identifier, and symbol system continues the Fourth Ring Road logo and Jing-Zhang narrative, distinguishing between the overall logo system and cultural identifier system. The public space component library includes movable seating, shading, nighttime lighting, activity interfaces, movable test equipment, and data feedback screens. All spatial suggestions are Conceptual Recommendations; schemes that violate cultural heritage protection, green spaces, blue lines, or traffic safety are not accepted. Bridge and tunnel structures, underground spaces, and engineering feasibility conclusions are not within the scope.

Long-term Operation Response Task Book agent.6 `[source:AGENT-TASKBOOK]`. This ritual embodies the governance philosophy of "the ring as a ring, reversible and correctable," making it a visible public ceremony. All activities, recruitment, funding, and operational arrangements are documented as Conceptual Recommendations.

## Risk, Copyright, and Compliance

The risks and data gaps in this proposal are concentrated in three categories `[depth:risk_missing_data]`. The first category is provisional boundary risks: the site package only provides rough alternative boundaries `[data:geometry/constraints.geojson]`, and the area, green space ratio, and Public Space ratio in this proposal are all indicative recalculations, and should not be considered as official planning boundaries or precise areas; they will need to be recalculated after the official polygon and approved control plan are released. The second category is missing planning limits: the Floor Area Ratio, Building Height, Building Coverage Ratio, green space ratio (as per the control plan), and setback requirements are all missing, and this proposal does not provide the numerical values for these statutory indicators. (Official Planning Boundary)

Third Category: Copyright and Compliance: This proposal is based solely on the public tender notice and publicly available documents `[standard:PROJECT-OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT]`, and does not claim to use or disclose any non-public planning drawings, personal privacy, land ownership information, or approved control indicators. The content regarding Building Height, construction intensity, road alignment, traffic organization, and facility placement are all discussion-based recommendations that require review by planning, traffic, data security, public participation, and relevant principal departments. When AI intelligent agents are involved in data organization and scheme generation, the source inputs, generation times, model, and Human Review records are retained; images, charts, and display materials use original or publicly licensed content. All spatial implementation suggestions in this proposal are expressed as "Conceptual Recommendations," "Reference Schemes," or "Available for Professional Teams to Deepen Research," and do not constitute government approval conclusions or implementation commitments.
