# The Continuity Line — English Abstract

The Continuity Line treats urban AI as fallible public infrastructure. Its
distinctive system has three parts: a declared service floor for every
scenario; a continuity path that does not share the same source of failure;
and a recovery timetable that includes human takeover, backlog processing,
data reconciliation, and controlled reopening.

The three provisional focus areas form a failure-time sequence rather than
three copies of an AI district. Zhongzhiyuan becomes Continuity Works for
isolated stress tests before failure. Beijing AI Origin becomes Continuity
Yard for operating manuals, reserve resources, and protected human shifts
during failure. Dazhongsi becomes Recovery Front for reconciling offline
transactions, cached information, and queued public-service requests after
failure.

All submitted spatial extents are explicitly provisional. They support
concept generation and deterministic review only; they are not official
redlines, statutory land-use controls, demolition decisions, or engineering
coordinates. The Chinese proposal, structured data, five figures, A3
booklet, A0 boards, and offline visual page are the controlling submission
materials.
