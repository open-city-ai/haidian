SWITCHBACK · Urban design proposal for the Centennial Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt

The proposal translates the reversing logic of the Jing-Zhang railway switchback into an urban design structure: the ascent reach (Zhongzhiyuan) does full-stack independent innovation, the reversing point (AI Origin Community) turns results into open-source release and transfer, the departure reach (Dazhongsi) faces the market and international exchange, and the Zhongguancun and Xiaoyuehe wings return capital and real scenario data — a spatial, operable and verifiable AI innovation loop.

Design Basis and Source List

The first basis for this proposal is the official pre-qualification announcement for the Centennial Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt urban design competition, which fixes the project purpose, the three scope levels, the design tasks and the required depth. This package therefore presents itself as a concept and reference scheme for professional teams to deepen, not as a substitute for statutory planning.

Site material comes from the public and rights-cleared items registered in the repository. The narrative does not copy those machine indexes 来源.

One precision fact must be stated first: the official redline of the overall design area and the exact polygons of the three key areas are not public. Peer review and known data disputes. Before submitting, this package searched the repository's Issues. Two public re-checks bear directly on its spatial premises and must appear in the narrative, because they change where its conclusions apply. First, an independent OpenStreetMap check reports that the provisional overall-design-area polygon does not intersect the OSM-mapped Jing-Zhang Heritage Park, with a nearest distance of about 412.5 m, while the provisional research-area polygon matches it completely (upstream Issue #846, reproducible). Second, the centroid of PROV-KEY-003 (Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster) is reported to fall near Beijing North Station, about 2.26 km from Dazhongsi metro station (upstream Issue #1029, reproducible). Neither has been adjudicated by an official polygon. This proposal does not judge which is right, but it must state what they mean for the work 来源 来源.

The dividing line is clean. The design logic is relative: ascent, reversing, departure and the two return arms describe relative positions and an operating sequence inside the corridor, so even if the whole corridor is displaced by several hundred metres, the structure, the land-use topology, the stitching strategy and the scenario cards still hold. The absolute siting is not reliable: whether the green spine actually sits on the heritage remains, and whether the Bell Platform and the four-quadrant pedestrian connection are actually at Dazhongsi station, both depend on the official polygon. The proposal therefore binds every Dazhongsi-related move to the key area as named in the announcement, not to the coordinates of the current provisional rectangle. Once official data is released those moves are re-sited together with the key area, and the design logic does not need rewriting 空间数据.

Naming overlap and attribution. The 人 motif is widely used in this open call: a community count records more than 25 proposals using the switchback motif, and chucky1102's RENLINE also uses the 折返 / Switchback word pair (upstream Issue #1119). Reusing the motif here is neither coincidence nor a claim of exclusivity — the Jing-Zhang switchback is public urban memory, and the symbol belongs to everyone. This proposal claims originality in only three things: translating the switchback geometry into a four-part innovation loop of ascent, reversing, departure and return wings; landing that loop as a topologically complete land-use partition with seven design layers; and attaching every conclusion to a recomputable evidence chain. Wherever it overlaps with others' work, it acknowledges that here and claims no priority 来源.

Three-Level Scope Framework

The three scope levels are not three parallel drawing sets but one converging chain from strategy to ground. The coordinated research area (about 43.6 km²) answers questions of industrial ecosystem and future urban form; its output is strategy and mechanism, and it carries no submitted geometry. The overall design area (about 11.4 km²) answers spatial structure, urban renewal and infrastructure capacity, and is the only level with complete submitted geometry. The key-area scope (about 368.4 ha) answers detailed design for three districts, producing small schemes that professional teams can deepen 深度 来源.

Only the middle level carries full geometry for a reason. The research area's conclusions are mostly industrial and governance mechanisms; drawing them as a precise land-use partition would manufacture false precision. The key areas, without an official redline, can express design direction but not parcel-level conclusions. This trade-off is recorded as an explicit assumption in assumptions.json and triggers recomputation once official data arrives 深度.

The overall concept is SWITCHBACK (人字轨). The most famous engineering legacy of the Jing-Zhang railway is the switchback at Qinglongqiao: where the gradient could not be climbed directly, the train reversed once and traded a change of direction for height. That century-old logic is structurally identical to how AI capability actually accumulates — not by a straight sprint, but by reversing several times through research, application, feedback and research again. The proposal translates this directly into spatial structure. The northern Zhongzhiyuan reach is the ascent, doing the 0-to-1 full-stack work. The central AI Origin Community is the reversing point, where results change direction into open-source release and talent gathering. The southern Dazhongsi reach is the departure, doing the 1-to-N market and international work. The Zhongguancun technology-service wing and the Xiaoyuehe scenario-empowerment wing are the two return arms, sending capital and real scenario data back to the ascent so that the belt is a closed loop rather than a one-way corridor 空间数据 深度.

Coordinated Research Area: Industry and Future City Research

Concept, name and naming system. The belt is named 人字轨 in Chinese and SWITCHBACK in English, in full "Centennial Jing-Zhang · SWITCHBACK AI Innovation Belt". The naming system uses railway operating grammar throughout, so that every spatial object carries its position in the innovation loop inside its own name: ascent (Zhongzhiyuan), reversing point (AI Origin Community), departure (Dazhongsi), west arm for factor return (Zhongguancun technology-service wing) and east arm for scenario return (Xiaoyuehe scenario-empowerment wing). The value of the system is not that it sounds good but that it is extensible and self-explaining — any new node acquires its name, and its coordinates in the overall narrative, simply by stating which of the four roles it plays 来源 标准.

Visual identity and logo direction. The logo is built from two reversing strokes and reads three ways at once: the Chinese character 人 (human — human-centred governance, with Agents used to augment people), a railway turnout symbol (the Jing-Zhang engineering legacy), and a feedback-loop arrow (the iterative logic of AI). The two strokes carry different colours to separate "ascent" from "return". Application guidance: the primary mark is used for the belt as a whole; the three areas and two wings use angle variants of the same reversing stroke as sub-marks; the cultural signage system (heritage interpretation, historical display) is managed separately from the belt logo so that cultural signage is never treated as a brand extension. All typefaces, images, trademarks, portraits and corporate marks require cleared rights before use; this package contains no uncleared material 标准.

Where the three positionings and five functions land. The centennial Jing-Zhang cultural belt lands on the heritage frontage and cultural land along the green spine; the urban AI living-experience belt lands on the wing gateways, the scenario nodes and the embedded community services; the AI convergence-innovation belt lands on the R&D land and the industry-service frontage. The five functions map as follows: full-stack independent innovation and a global voice in AI governance sit in the ascent reach; the world-class innovation ecosystem sits at the reversing point; intelligent-native industries sit in the departure reach; AI+ scenario empowerment sits on the east arm; global factor allocation sits on the west arm 标准 空间数据.

Overall Design Area: Urban Renewal and Regulatory-Plan-Level Urban Design

The structure of the design area is the superposition of four elements — one spine, two roads, four wedges and two arms. The green spine is a continuous park along the Jing-Zhang alignment, roughly 190 m wide and about 9.72 km long, carrying slow mobility, heritage display and AI public space at once. The secondary roads sit about 320 m either side of the spine and separate through traffic from slow movement. The four transverse wedges connect the spine to the wings and exist specifically to stitch east-west links severed by arterials. The two switchback arms open south-west and south-east from the reversing point as public space and as the pedestrian gateways to the wings 空间数据 深度.

The renewal framework follows an "interfaces first, hinterland later" sequence. The reason is that without an existing-building survey, tenure and regulatory conditions, any parcel-level retain-renovate-demolish judgement is unreliable, whereas judgements about public-space interfaces, slow-mobility continuity and scenario-opening mechanisms are robust and, once made well, create the conditions for later hinterland renewal. Near-term effort is therefore concentrated on public space and station connections in the reversing and departure reaches, leaving hinterland renewal until official data arrives 深度 来源.

The land-use partition covers the whole submitted boundary with no gaps and no overlaps; recomputed coverage is 100.0% across 13 classes 指标 空间数据. The partition was not hand-drawn parcel by parcel: the whole boundary was split topologically by one consistent set of cut lines, so adjacent uses share exactly identical boundary coordinates — which is why the spatial review passes 标准.

Total floor area, floor area ratio and building height are not concluded here. This is not evasion but the result of two hard constraints. First, approved regulatory FAR, height, density and setback conditions are not in the public material. Second, the Agent taskbook explicitly lists FAR and height conclusions as forbidden for an Agent. The package therefore submits only a conceptual distribution of building footprints and records the related metrics as unknown in metrics.json with a reason and a precondition 指标 深度.

Capacity assessment is limited in the same way. Utility, energy-load, drainage, flood, fire and traffic-capacity data are all unavailable, so the proposal states what must be assessed and when the assessment is triggered, without concluding any capacity figure 深度.

Scenario governance contract: adopting the community Switchback Protocol v1.0. Rather than inventing another state machine, this package adopts the Switchback Protocol contributed by chucky1102 / RENLINE under CC-BY-4.0 as the minimum operating contract for all its scenario cards (upstream Issue #1119). The reason is direct: dozens of proposals have each invented their own states, time limits and review cycles, and the vocabulary mismatch makes the mechanisms impossible to compare side by side. Reviewers and later operating teams need one shared interface, not another set of new words. Copyright in the protocol text stays with its author; it is used here with CC-BY-4.0 attribution, and none of its text, drawings, geometry or schema structure is reproduced 来源.

Under the protocol the 14 cards split into 7 green (normal operation) and 7 yellow (controlled pilot). The three industry test scenarios, together with the data-element, consumption-personalisation, community-health and legal-desk cards where algorithmic risk is higher, all start yellow and climb the gates in order: virtual evaluation, then controlled site, then limited real street. Red is not called failure in the protocol but switchback - revert to the last stable state and publish the reason. That is the same idea as the spatial concept: reversing is not failing, it is a planned restart. The belt-wide defaults are kept as given (human takeover target within 5 minutes, appeals acknowledged in 1 working day and resolved in 7, a 90-day public review, automatic red after two missed reviews), with the protocol's own disclaimer preserved: these are design targets, not government commitments, and formal limits follow the operating agreement 深度.

One governance rule for all scenarios. Every AI scenario follows five principles: data minimisation, preference for public sources, explainability, the right of appeal, and a human stop authority. No scenario may output unauthorised personal profiles, collect city-held personal data under the name of scenario opening, or describe a test scenario as approved operation 来源.

Transport, Rail, Municipal Infrastructure, and Public Services

The core mobility judgement is that this corridor's real problem is not longitudinal discontinuity but severed lateral connection. North-south, the heritage corridor can be made continuous. East-west, several arterials and the railway cut the corridor into segments, so adjacent parcels on either side are neighbours that cannot reach each other. Investment is therefore directed at lateral stitching rather than new longitudinal capacity 深度 空间数据.

Four layers organise this. The green spine carries north-south slow mobility. The two secondary roads collect vehicles and separate through traffic from walking. Nine transverse branch roads and four green-wedge cycleways carry east-west stitching, about 14.1 km in total. Five transit connections link the spine toward existing station directions 指标. One caveat matters: station directions are inferred from public information, and exact siting, exits and interchange organisation must be verified against official data. The connection segments here express direction, not alignment 指标.

geometry/constraints.geojson records 4 major slow-mobility barriers (toward the North 5th, 4th and 3rd Ring Roads and Xizhimenwai Street). They are the most worthwhile near-term stitching targets. How each is crossed — at grade, under or over — depends on bridge and tunnel feasibility, structural safety and utility conditions, and must be studied separately by professional teams. Only position and functional requirement are proposed 空间数据.

For utilities and public services, the proposal argues for treating new and traditional infrastructure together: edge-compute stations built with community services and public-space management rooms, distributed energy combined with green space and roof resources, so that independent siting costs fall. But energy load, utility capacity, drainage, flood and fire data are missing, so facility standards, service radii and capacity conclusions are all listed as preconditions for formal deepening 深度.

Blue-Green Network, Public Space, and Urban Character

The blue-green and public-space system consists of the green spine, four transverse wedges, protective green space along the North 5th Ring Road and two plazas. Recomputed green space is 1.90 km², a green ratio of 16.7%; public space is 0.56 km², or 4.9% 指标 指标. The design meaning of the green ratio is this: keeping a sixth of an innovation corridor as continuous green is not about meeting a standard but about letting people who work at high intensity reach continuous open space within a three-to-five-minute walk. That is a spatial precondition for retaining talent, and the precondition on which an "urban AI living-experience belt" can exist at all 空间数据.

Cultural narrative: from engineering legacy to innovation culture. The cultural value of the Jing-Zhang railway is not the track but the attitude it records — finding a solution under constraint. Zhan Tianyou's switchback is the physical evidence of that attitude. The core of Zhongguancun's innovation culture is the same: making, under constraint, something nobody has made before. The new AI culture continues the same line today through open-source collaboration, reproducibility and work others can keep deepening. These are not three parallel historical exhibits but three expressions of one disposition. The proposal therefore does not suggest a "railway theme park" but a heritage frontage designed as a continuously updatable narrative carrier: the Reversing Marker records contemporary contributors, the Ascent Beacon explains contemporary infrastructure, and the Bell Platform carries the contemporary release ceremony, so that history and the present speak one spatial grammar 标准 深度.

Signage, marks and symbols. Three tiers are proposed: belt level (the reversing-stroke symbol with unified colour and type rules), district level (angle variants for the three areas and two wings) and node level (scenario-card numbering and pilgrimage-route marking). Cultural signage — heritage interpretation and historical information — forms its own system so it is never confused with brand identity. Metrics, Area Recalculation, and Compliance Matrix

The metric set contains 56 entries, 51 known and 5 unknown. Every known metric is recomputed from the submitted GeoJSON in EPSG:4548, with formula, source files, confidence and assumptions recorded per entry in metrics.json. The narrative explains only what the numbers mean for the design 指标 深度.

Metrics fall into three classes handled differently. Spatial metrics recomputable directly from the submitted geometry — site area, area by land-use class, green ratio, public-space ratio, road-land ratio, footprint area, phase areas — are reliable except for boundary precision, and must be recomputed in full once the official boundary is released 指标. Control metrics that require official regulatory conditions — FAR, height, density, setback, road redlines and facility standards — are all unknown here with a stated reason, because neither the basis nor the mandate exists 指标. Performance metrics that require long-term operating data — scenario usage frequency, event participation, slow-mobility accessibility, talent retention — cannot be given a number at submission stage; only a monitoring method and a responsible party are proposed.

The core metrics can be read one by one against their design intent: a green ratio of 16.7% supports walk-accessible daily open space; a public-space ratio of 4.9% supports innovation encounter and public activity; a road-land ratio of 10.2% covers only the structural network expressed here and excludes roads inside parcels, so it is not directly comparable with a city-wide road ratio 指标.
