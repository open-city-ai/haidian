# SWITCHBACK · Urban design proposal for the Centennial Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt

## Design Basis and Source List

The first basis for this proposal is the official pre-qualification announcement for the Centennial Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt urban design competition, which fixes the project purpose, the three scope levels, the design tasks and the required depth. The second basis is the open-call taskbook addressed to Agents, which sets the three positionings, five functions, three areas and two wings, and six required tasks [source:OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT] [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK]. The relation between them is simple: the announcement decides *what must be answered*, and the Agent taskbook decides *in what role and within what boundary it may be answered*. This package therefore presents itself as a concept and reference scheme for professional teams to deepen, not as a substitute for statutory planning.

Site material comes from the public and rights-cleared items registered in the repository. Before use, every item was checked against the source-usability registry, separating material admissible for formal judgement, material usable only as background, and provisional material usable only for temporary generation and self-check [source:SOURCE-REGISTRY]. No background or provisional item has been promoted into an official boundary, a statutory control or a government commitment. Complete provenance, licence and limitation records live in `sources.json`; complete metric, standard and design-depth coverage lives in `metrics.json` and the three matrix files. The narrative does not copy those machine indexes [source:PROCESSED-FACT-PACK].

One precision fact must be stated first: **the official redline of the overall design area and the exact polygons of the three key areas are not public**. The submitted `geometry/site_boundary.geojson` and `geometry/key_areas.geojson` therefore use the repository's provisional rough substitutes, all flagged `official_boundary=false`, `geometry_role="provisional_constraint"` and `boundary_precision="provisional_rough"` [source:BOUNDARY-SOURCE] [source:KEY-AREA-SOURCE]. They may be used for generation, visualisation, design discussion and local self-check only, never as an official redline, an approval basis or a precise-area basis. Recomputed in `EPSG:4548`, the submitted area is 11.41 km², a deviation of 0.1% from the announcement's stated 11.4 km². That deviation is itself evidence of a provisional boundary, not a correction to the official figure [metric:site_area_sqm]. Once official data is released, land use, roads, green space, public space, buildings, phasing and every metric are recomputed as a whole rather than file by file.

**Peer review and known data disputes.** Before submitting, this package searched the repository's Issues. Two public re-checks bear directly on its spatial premises and must appear in the narrative, because they change where its conclusions apply. First, an independent OpenStreetMap check reports that the provisional overall-design-area polygon **does not intersect** the OSM-mapped Jing-Zhang Heritage Park, with a nearest distance of about **412.5 m**, while the provisional research-area polygon matches it completely (upstream Issue #846, reproducible). Second, the centroid of `PROV-KEY-003` (Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster) is reported to fall near Beijing North Station, about **2.26 km** from Dazhongsi metro station (upstream Issue #1029, reproducible). Neither has been adjudicated by an official polygon. This proposal does not judge which is right, but it must state what they mean for the work [source:PEER-REVIEW-846] [source:PEER-REVIEW-1029].

The dividing line is clean. The **design logic is relative**: ascent, reversing, departure and the two return arms describe relative positions and an operating sequence inside the corridor, so even if the whole corridor is displaced by several hundred metres, the structure, the land-use topology, the stitching strategy and the scenario cards still hold. The **absolute siting is not reliable**: whether the green spine actually sits on the heritage remains, and whether the Bell Platform and the four-quadrant pedestrian connection are actually at Dazhongsi station, both depend on the official polygon. The proposal therefore binds every Dazhongsi-related move to the **key area as named in the announcement**, not to the coordinates of the current provisional rectangle. Once official data is released those moves are re-sited together with the key area, and the design logic does not need rewriting [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-003].

**Naming overlap and attribution.** The 人 motif is widely used in this open call: a community count records more than 25 proposals using the switchback motif, and chucky1102's RENLINE also uses the 折返 / Switchback word pair (upstream Issue #1119). Reusing the motif here is neither coincidence nor a claim of exclusivity — the Jing-Zhang switchback is public urban memory, and the symbol belongs to everyone. This proposal claims originality in only three things: translating the switchback geometry into a **four-part innovation loop** of ascent, reversing, departure and return wings; landing that loop as a **topologically complete land-use partition with seven design layers**; and attaching every conclusion to a **recomputable evidence chain**. Wherever it overlaps with others' work, it acknowledges that here and claims no priority [source:SWITCHBACK-PROTOCOL].

## Three-Level Scope Framework

The three scope levels are not three parallel drawing sets but one converging chain from strategy to ground. The coordinated research area (about 43.6 km²) answers questions of industrial ecosystem and future urban form; its output is strategy and mechanism, and it carries no submitted geometry. The overall design area (about 11.4 km²) answers spatial structure, urban renewal and infrastructure capacity, and is the only level with complete submitted geometry. The key-area scope (about 368.4 ha) answers detailed design for three districts, producing small schemes that professional teams can deepen [depth:three_level_scope_framework] [source:SITE-PACKAGE].

Only the middle level carries full geometry for a reason. The research area's conclusions are mostly industrial and governance mechanisms; drawing them as a precise land-use partition would manufacture false precision. The key areas, without an official redline, can express design direction but not parcel-level conclusions. This trade-off is recorded as an explicit assumption in `assumptions.json` and triggers recomputation once official data arrives [depth:existing_conditions_diagnosis].

![SWITCHBACK overall spatial structure: ascent, reversing point, departure and the two return wings](assets/figures/site-overview.png)

The overall concept is **SWITCHBACK (人字轨)**. The most famous engineering legacy of the Jing-Zhang railway is the switchback at Qinglongqiao: where the gradient could not be climbed directly, the train reversed once and traded a change of direction for height. That century-old logic is structurally identical to how AI capability actually accumulates — not by a straight sprint, but by reversing several times through research, application, feedback and research again. The proposal translates this directly into spatial structure. The northern Zhongzhiyuan reach is the **ascent**, doing the 0-to-1 full-stack work. The central AI Origin Community is the **reversing point**, where results change direction into open-source release and talent gathering. The southern Dazhongsi reach is the **departure**, doing the 1-to-N market and international work. The Zhongguancun technology-service wing and the Xiaoyuehe scenario-empowerment wing are the two **return arms**, sending capital and real scenario data back to the ascent so that the belt is a closed loop rather than a one-way corridor [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#PUBLIC-001] [depth:overall_spatial_structure].

| Level | Core question | Answer in this proposal | Output and data anchor |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Research area 43.6 km² | How is a world-class AI ecosystem organised | A four-part innovation loop — ascent, reversing, departure, return — with the wings supplying factors and scenarios | Strategy and mechanism; no geometry submitted; coverage recorded in the task matrix |
| Design area 11.4 km² | How do structure, renewal and infrastructure land on a plan | Green spine + secondary roads on both sides + four transverse wedges + two switchback arms | A complete land-use partition and seven design layers [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-001] |
| Key areas 368.4 ha | How do the three districts reach detailed-design depth | Role, spatial moves, AI scenarios, landmark and implementation dependencies for each | Key-area layer and three district schemes [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-002] |

## Coordinated Research Area: Industry and Future City Research

**Concept, name and naming system.** The belt is named 人字轨 in Chinese and SWITCHBACK in English, in full "Centennial Jing-Zhang · SWITCHBACK AI Innovation Belt". The naming system uses railway operating grammar throughout, so that every spatial object carries its position in the innovation loop inside its own name: ascent (Zhongzhiyuan), reversing point (AI Origin Community), departure (Dazhongsi), west arm for factor return (Zhongguancun technology-service wing) and east arm for scenario return (Xiaoyuehe scenario-empowerment wing). The value of the system is not that it sounds good but that it is extensible and self-explaining — any new node acquires its name, and its coordinates in the overall narrative, simply by stating which of the four roles it plays [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK] [standard:PROJECT-AGENT-OPEN-CALL-TASKBOOK].

**Visual identity and logo direction.** The logo is built from two reversing strokes and reads three ways at once: the Chinese character 人 (human — human-centred governance, with Agents used to augment people), a railway turnout symbol (the Jing-Zhang engineering legacy), and a feedback-loop arrow (the iterative logic of AI). The two strokes carry different colours to separate "ascent" from "return". Application guidance: the primary mark is used for the belt as a whole; the three areas and two wings use angle variants of the same reversing stroke as sub-marks; the cultural signage system (heritage interpretation, historical display) is managed separately from the belt logo so that cultural signage is never treated as a brand extension. All typefaces, images, trademarks, portraits and corporate marks require cleared rights before use; this package contains no uncleared material [standard:PROJECT-AGENT-OPEN-CALL-TASKBOOK].

**Where the three positionings and five functions land.** The centennial Jing-Zhang cultural belt lands on the heritage frontage and cultural land along the green spine; the urban AI living-experience belt lands on the wing gateways, the scenario nodes and the embedded community services; the AI convergence-innovation belt lands on the R&D land and the industry-service frontage. The five functions map as follows: full-stack independent innovation and a global voice in AI governance sit in the ascent reach; the world-class innovation ecosystem sits at the reversing point; intelligent-native industries sit in the departure reach; AI+ scenario empowerment sits on the east arm; global factor allocation sits on the west arm [standard:MOHURD-URBAN-DESIGN-MEASURES] [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-001].

**Global AI ecosystem cases.** The six cases below are long-documented public urban innovation districts. Only their mechanisms are drawn on; no unverified output, investment or company figures are cited.

| Case | Mechanism | Transferable lesson |
| --- | --- | --- |
| King's Cross and the Knowledge Quarter, London, UK | A long-cycle comprehensive renewal of railway brownfield and goods yards, combined with libraries, universities, research institutes and corporate headquarters into a knowledge cluster. | The closest analogue to the Jing-Zhang corridor: treat railway remains as a public-space armature rather than an obstacle, and hold quality through one long-lived development and operating body. |
| 22@ Poblenou innovation district, Barcelona, Spain | A dedicated planning instrument converting former industrial land to knowledge-activity land, trading development capacity for public space, affordable housing and facilities. | The lesson is institutional rather than formal: renewal needs an operable rule set for use conversion and public-good consideration. |
| one-north, Singapore | A government-led, single-developer research-industry-living-leisure district emphasising walkable scale and shared facilities. | Mixing rather than zoning and sharing rather than self-building keep dense innovation districts alive; this maps to embedded community services and shared test facilities here. |
| Station F, Paris, France | A single very large incubator inside a historic railway freight building, gathering accelerators, corporate programmes and public events. | A mega-venue buys density and serendipity but risks detaching from the city; this proposal distributes the same functions as a node network along the spine. |
| Mila and the innovation quarter, Montreal, Canada | A university AI institute as the source, drawing surrounding blocks into an urban interface where research, entrepreneurship and public debate coexist. | The public accessibility of a research institute is itself an urban asset; this maps to the campus-to-market street and the release hall. |
| Stanford Research Park and the Sand Hill Road corridor, USA | University, research park and venture capital coexisting within a very short commute, producing high-frequency talent-capital interaction. | Spatial proximity of factors can be designed; this underpins the Zhongguancun wing as the factor-return arm here. |


**Regional innovation synergy.** Northward, the ascent reach connects to the basic-research capacity of the Future Science City and Huairou Science City. Southward, the departure reach connects to the manufacturing and deployment capacity of the economic-development area. Westward, the factor-return arm plugs into Zhongguancun's capital, legal and IP services. Eastward, the scenario-return arm plugs into the Xueyuan Road university cluster and the real urban scenarios along the Xiaoyuehe. Together these four directions form a research-transfer-manufacture-scenario division of labour across the region. Spatially, this proposal is responsible only for making the interfaces clear: each direction has a defined gateway node, a slow-mobility connection and an operating counterpart [source:OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT].

**A planning-innovation direction worth studying.** The proposal suggests "use flexibility plus public-good consideration": within the innovation district, research, commercial-service and community-service uses may convert into one another within a bounded share, on condition that the converting party supplies a proportionate amount of public space, shared test facilities or talent housing. The idea derives from the Barcelona 22@ experience, but it can enter a regulatory plan only after a qualified planning institution has assessed it. No conversion ratio and no density bonus is proposed here [standard:MOHURD-CONTROL-DETAILED-PLANNING].

## Overall Design Area: Urban Renewal and Regulatory-Plan-Level Urban Design

The structure of the design area is the superposition of four elements — one spine, two roads, four wedges and two arms. The **green spine** is a continuous park along the Jing-Zhang alignment, roughly 190 m wide and about 9.72 km long, carrying slow mobility, heritage display and AI public space at once. The **secondary roads** sit about 320 m either side of the spine and separate through traffic from slow movement. The **four transverse wedges** connect the spine to the wings and exist specifically to stitch east-west links severed by arterials. The **two switchback arms** open south-west and south-east from the reversing point as public space and as the pedestrian gateways to the wings [data:geometry/roads.geojson#ROAD-001] [depth:overall_spatial_structure].

The renewal framework follows an "interfaces first, hinterland later" sequence. The reason is that without an existing-building survey, tenure and regulatory conditions, any parcel-level retain-renovate-demolish judgement is unreliable, whereas judgements about public-space interfaces, slow-mobility continuity and scenario-opening mechanisms are robust and, once made well, create the conditions for later hinterland renewal. Near-term effort is therefore concentrated on public space and station connections in the reversing and departure reaches, leaving hinterland renewal until official data arrives [depth:renewal_project_list] [source:SITE-PACKAGE].

![Land-use structure and the three-level scope framework](assets/figures/land-use-structure.png)

The land-use partition covers the whole submitted boundary with no gaps and no overlaps; recomputed coverage is 100.0% across 13 classes [metric:land_use_coverage_ratio] [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-001]. The partition was not hand-drawn parcel by parcel: the whole boundary was split topologically by one consistent set of cut lines, so adjacent uses share exactly identical boundary coordinates — which is why the spatial review passes [standard:MNR-LAND-USE-CLASSIFICATION-GUIDE].

Total floor area, floor area ratio and building height are **not concluded here**. This is not evasion but the result of two hard constraints. First, approved regulatory FAR, height, density and setback conditions are not in the public material. Second, the Agent taskbook explicitly lists FAR and height conclusions as forbidden for an Agent. The package therefore submits only a conceptual distribution of building footprints and records the related metrics as unknown in `metrics.json` with a reason and a precondition [metric:floor_area_ratio] [depth:development_intensity_controls].

Capacity assessment is limited in the same way. Utility, energy-load, drainage, flood, fire and traffic-capacity data are all unavailable, so the proposal states what must be assessed and when the assessment is triggered, without concluding any capacity figure [depth:municipal_new_infrastructure].

## Detailed Design of Key Areas

The three key areas share one scheme structure: role, spatial structure, building-renewal direction, mobility, public space, AI scenarios and implementation dependencies. Because each plays a different part in the reversing loop, their design emphases differ rather than repeat [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-001] [depth:three_key_area_detailed_design].

![Index of the three key detailed-design areas (same scale)](assets/figures/key-areas.png)

**Zhongzhiyuan AI Independent-Innovation Acceleration Area (ascent reach, about 192.1 ha).** Its role is full-stack independent innovation and a voice in AI governance. Three moves are proposed: an open test loop along the Qinghe frontage that strings test grounds, edge-compute stations and a low-carbon energy display into a visitable circuit; a standards-governance display axis on the east side of the spine, turning normally closed evaluation and standards work into a bookable, observable urban frontage; and a strategic reserve inside the district for AI infrastructure demand that cannot yet be predicted [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-001]. Building renewal is dominated by research and laboratory space, expressed only as a conceptual footprint distribution given the absent survey. Mobility relies on the Qinghe-direction transit connection and the two secondary roads; slow movement runs on the test loop and the spine. AI scenarios are SC-04, SC-05 and SC-06. The landmark is ★B The Ascent Beacon. Dependencies include the Qinghe blue line and flood conditions, safety and regulatory authorisation for the test ground, and a review of external traffic organisation — all requiring separate professional study.

**Beijing AI Origin Community (reversing point, about 104.3 ha).** Its role is transfer to market, open-source release and talent gathering. It is the only direction-changing node on the belt and the most spatially intense part of the proposal. The core move is the Reversing Plaza plus the two arms: the plaza carries open-source release, honour display and public participation, while the arms stitch campus, park and neighbourhood at walking scale and open gateways toward Zhongguancun and the Xiaoyuehe [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#PUBLIC-001] [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#PUBLIC-003]. Building renewal favours cultural display, campus-adjacent transfer services and talent housing support. AI scenarios are SC-01, SC-02 and SC-03. The landmark is ★A The Reversing Marker. Dependencies include campus and park tenure edges, ground-floor use and property coordination, and station-integration conditions.

**Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster (departure reach, about 72.0 ha).** Its role is intelligent-native industries and international exchange. The core move is a four-quadrant pedestrian connection at the station, the Bell Platform plaza and frontage renewal: four quadrants cut apart by a junction are re-joined into one operable whole by a continuous pedestrian system, with the Bell Platform as the ceremonial space for annual releases and international roadshows [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#PUBLIC-002]. AI scenarios are SC-12, SC-13 and SC-14. The landmark is ★C The Bell Platform. Dependencies include station exits and utilities, junction channelisation and traffic-safety assessment, and heritage and retail-tenure review. Because the key-area polygons are provisional, all three sets of conclusions hold only as directional design, never as parcel-level or engineering conclusions.

The scenario-space-operation mapping can be checked directly in the structured data: each card corresponds to one `SCENARIO_NODE` point in `geometry/public_space.geojson`, whose position is a conceptual suggestion and not an approved siting [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#SN-04]. The three industry test scenarios (SC-04, SC-05, SC-07) concentrate in the ascent and stitching reaches because testing needs relatively controllable boundary conditions and lower pedestrian density; consumption and international-exchange scenarios concentrate in the departure reach because they need transit accessibility and commercial density.

The Xiaoyuehe scenario-empowerment wing is the east arm. Its purpose is to make real urban settings — communities, campuses, streets, the river — the *source* of testing and feedback rather than the backdrop of a display. A public experience route follows from this: enter at the south gateway, run north along the spine, change direction at the reversing point into the two arms, then return to the spine and continue north to the ascent reach. The same route is a resident's daily walk and an international visitor's pilgrimage [data:geometry/roads.geojson#ROAD-002].

**Scenario governance contract: adopting the community Switchback Protocol v1.0.** Rather than inventing another state machine, this package adopts the Switchback Protocol contributed by chucky1102 / RENLINE under CC-BY-4.0 as the minimum operating contract for all its scenario cards (upstream Issue #1119). The reason is direct: dozens of proposals have each invented their own states, time limits and review cycles, and the vocabulary mismatch makes the mechanisms impossible to compare side by side. Reviewers and later operating teams need one shared interface, not another set of new words. Copyright in the protocol text stays with its author; it is used here with CC-BY-4.0 attribution, and none of its text, drawings, geometry or schema structure is reproduced [source:SWITCHBACK-PROTOCOL].

Under the protocol the 14 cards split into 7 green (normal operation) and 7 yellow (controlled pilot). The three industry test scenarios, together with the data-element, consumption-personalisation, community-health and legal-desk cards where algorithmic risk is higher, all start yellow and climb the gates in order: virtual evaluation, then controlled site, then limited real street. Red is not called failure in the protocol but switchback - revert to the last stable state and publish the reason. That is the same idea as the spatial concept: **reversing is not failing, it is a planned restart.** The belt-wide defaults are kept as given (human takeover target within 5 minutes, appeals acknowledged in 1 working day and resolved in 7, a 90-day public review, automatic red after two missed reviews), with the protocol's own disclaimer preserved: **these are design targets, not government commitments**, and formal limits follow the operating agreement [depth:risk_missing_data].

**One governance rule for all scenarios.** Every AI scenario follows five principles: data minimisation, preference for public sources, explainability, the right of appeal, and a human stop authority. No scenario may output unauthorised personal profiles, collect city-held personal data under the name of scenario opening, or describe a test scenario as approved operation [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK].

## Land Use, Building Scale, and Retain-Renovate-Demolish Strategy

The land-use logic is "spine in the middle, industry to the east, living to the west". R&D land concentrates east of the spine because the eastern hinterland is more continuous and better suited to research and pilot-test space at scale. Housing and community services concentrate to the west, next to existing communities, so that renewal can be embedded with low disturbance. Education land concentrates in the Xueyuan Road and reversing reaches, directly facing the campuses. Commercial service land concentrates at the two gateway reaches, drawing on transit accessibility [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-001] [standard:MNR-LAND-USE-CLASSIFICATION-GUIDE].

**Building scale and the retain-renovate-demolish question must be written as pending.** The submitted `geometry/buildings.geojson` contains 142 footprints totalling 0.82 km², or 7.2% of the design area. A real retain-renovate-demolish classification needs four datasets — building quality, tenure, structural safety and owner intent — and the absence of any one of them invalidates the classification. The proposal therefore offers a method and no conclusion [depth:retain_renovate_demolish].

The suggested method has four steps: screen out the objects that must be dealt with on building quality and structural safety; rank them by tenure complexity; judge priority by their functional value inside the reversing loop; and set the pace according to owner intent. The method can be applied directly once official data arrives, without rebuilding the scheme [depth:height_massing_character].

## Transport, Rail, Municipal Infrastructure, and Public Services

The core mobility judgement is that this corridor's real problem is not longitudinal discontinuity but **severed lateral connection**. North-south, the heritage corridor can be made continuous. East-west, several arterials and the railway cut the corridor into segments, so adjacent parcels on either side are neighbours that cannot reach each other. Investment is therefore directed at lateral stitching rather than new longitudinal capacity [depth:traffic_rail_slow_parking] [data:geometry/roads.geojson#ROAD-006].

![Mobility and blue-green public space as one composite system](assets/figures/mobility-bluegreen.png)

Four layers organise this. The green spine carries north-south slow mobility. The two secondary roads collect vehicles and separate through traffic from walking. Nine transverse branch roads and four green-wedge cycleways carry east-west stitching, about 14.1 km in total. Five transit connections link the spine toward existing station directions [metric:transverse_branch_length_m]. One caveat matters: station directions are inferred from public information, and exact siting, exits and interchange organisation must be verified against official data. The connection segments here express direction, not alignment [metric:transit_connection_count].

`geometry/constraints.geojson` records 4 major slow-mobility barriers (toward the North 5th, 4th and 3rd Ring Roads and Xizhimenwai Street). They are the most worthwhile near-term stitching targets. How each is crossed — at grade, under or over — depends on bridge and tunnel feasibility, structural safety and utility conditions, and must be studied separately by professional teams. Only position and functional requirement are proposed [data:geometry/constraints.geojson#CON-02].

For utilities and public services, the proposal argues for treating new and traditional infrastructure together: edge-compute stations built with community services and public-space management rooms, distributed energy combined with green space and roof resources, so that independent siting costs fall. But energy load, utility capacity, drainage, flood and fire data are missing, so facility standards, service radii and capacity conclusions are all listed as preconditions for formal deepening [depth:municipal_new_infrastructure].

## Blue-Green Network, Public Space, and Urban Character

The blue-green and public-space system consists of the green spine, four transverse wedges, protective green space along the North 5th Ring Road and two plazas. Recomputed green space is 1.90 km², a green ratio of 16.7%; public space is 0.56 km², or 4.9% [metric:green_ratio] [metric:public_space_ratio]. The design meaning of the green ratio is this: keeping a sixth of an innovation corridor as continuous green is not about meeting a standard but about letting people who work at high intensity reach continuous open space within a three-to-five-minute walk. That is a spatial precondition for retaining talent, and the precondition on which an "urban AI living-experience belt" can exist at all [data:geometry/green_space.geojson#GREEN-001].

**Cultural narrative: from engineering legacy to innovation culture.** The cultural value of the Jing-Zhang railway is not the track but the attitude it records — finding a solution under constraint. Zhan Tianyou's switchback is the physical evidence of that attitude. The core of Zhongguancun's innovation culture is the same: making, under constraint, something nobody has made before. The new AI culture continues the same line today through open-source collaboration, reproducibility and work others can keep deepening. These are not three parallel historical exhibits but three expressions of one disposition. The proposal therefore does not suggest a "railway theme park" but a heritage frontage designed as a **continuously updatable narrative carrier**: the Reversing Marker records contemporary contributors, the Ascent Beacon explains contemporary infrastructure, and the Bell Platform carries the contemporary release ceremony, so that history and the present speak one spatial grammar [standard:MOHURD-URBAN-DESIGN-MEASURES] [depth:blue_green_public_space].

**Signage, marks and symbols.** Three tiers are proposed: belt level (the reversing-stroke symbol with unified colour and type rules), district level (angle variants for the three areas and two wings) and node level (scenario-card numbering and pilgrimage-route marking). Cultural signage — heritage interpretation and historical information — forms its own system so it is never confused with brand identity. Every symbol, typeface and image requires cleared rights; historical photographs and portraits may not be used without authorisation [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK].

**AI public space and pilgrimage landmarks.** The proposal offers 4 landmarks and honour-display nodes. They share three requirements: each must carry a real function rather than being a photo prop, each must be publicly enterable, and none may be over-entertained. All are conceptual position and function suggestions only [metric:ai_landmark_count].

| Landmark | Location | Design intent | Boundary and rights requirement |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ★A The Reversing Marker | Reversing Plaza, AI Origin Community | A public structure derived from the switchback geometry, whose surface is an updatable contributor honour interface recording open-source contributions and Agent proposals entering the public knowledge base. | Honour data comes only from public repository records, is removable on request, and shows no personal identity data. |
| ★B The Ascent Beacon | Ascent north reach, Zhongzhiyuan | A display structure explaining compute, energy use and standards governance to the public, turning normally invisible infrastructure into a readable urban object. | Displayed data must be verifiable public or authorised data, with method and date stated. |
| ★C The Bell Platform | Bell departure reach, Dazhongsi | A release platform echoing the Dazhongsi place name and its bell imagery, serving as the ceremonial public space for annual releases and international roadshows. | Kept clear of the heritage monument; form, material and lighting require heritage and character review. |
| ★D The Switchback Junction Deck | Junction of the west arm and the green spine | A viewing and reversing deck across a slow-mobility barrier; the one elevated point from which the ascent-reverse-departure structure can be read as a whole. | Crossing form, structure and engineering feasibility require separate professional study; only position and function are proposed here. |


**A public-space component library.** To keep quality consistent across phases, one set of components is proposed: seating and shelter units derived from the reversing geometry, updatable contributor display panels, scenario-card markers, accessible wayfinding and tiered night lighting. The library lets space built at different times by different parties still read as one belt [depth:blue_green_public_space].

## Metrics, Area Recalculation, and Compliance Matrix

The metric set contains 56 entries, 51 known and 5 unknown. ![Metric recomputation and the evidence chain](assets/figures/metrics-evidence.png)

Metrics fall into three classes handled differently. **Spatial metrics recomputable directly from the submitted geometry** — site area, area by land-use class, green ratio, public-space ratio, road-land ratio, footprint area, phase areas — are reliable except for boundary precision, and must be recomputed in full once the official boundary is released [metric:land_use_coverage_ratio]. **Control metrics that require official regulatory conditions** — FAR, height, density, setback, road redlines and facility standards — are all unknown here with a stated reason, because neither the basis nor the mandate exists [metric:floor_area_ratio]. **Performance metrics that require long-term operating data** — scenario usage frequency, event participation, slow-mobility accessibility, talent retention — cannot be given a number at submission stage; only a monitoring method and a responsible party are proposed.

The core metrics can be read one by one against their design intent: a green ratio of 16.7% supports walk-accessible daily open space; a public-space ratio of 4.9% supports innovation encounter and public activity; a road-land ratio of 10.2% covers only the structural network expressed here and excludes roads inside parcels, so it is not directly comparable with a city-wide road ratio [metric:road_land_ratio].
