Overview map

Three-level scope

The coordinated research scope organizes the innovation chain, the overall area supplies a recomputable spatial framework, and three key areas test concrete services. Every polygon retains its provisional precision label.

North / Technical Proof

Models, compute, data, safety, and energy enter reproducible test gates.

Central / Open Translation

Research becomes open components, public explanations, and challengeable demonstrations.

South / Civic Experience

Residents and operators verify mobility, care, maintenance, and cultural services.

Key areas

Land-use structure

Six topology-sharing program bands cover the submitted boundary. Their shares come from this package, not a statutory land-use or intensity plan.

Buildings

Gray building blocks are program-supply test envelopes, not surveys, ownership records, or parcel intervention decisions.

Renewal projects

Protocol first, temporary pilots, professional development, then long-term stewardship. Every phase has data, access, safety, and exit gates.

Mobility and walking · Blue-green public space

One walking spine, one cycling verification route, and four east-west links remain conceptual alignments for professional transport study.
