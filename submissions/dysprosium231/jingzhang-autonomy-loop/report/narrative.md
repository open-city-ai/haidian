# 方案速读 / Executive summary

## 中文

**百年京张·自主回路（JZ Autonomy Loop）** 把京张遗址公园做成一条 9.72 公里的变宽"学习脊"，在重点区放宽至约 300 米、连接段收窄至约 140 米，形成串珠形而非等宽的绿廊。脊线把众智园（研发）、小月河翼（验证）、AI 原点社区（发布）、大钟寺（消费）、中关村科技服务翼（反馈）五个环节串成一条闭合回路——核心主张是把 AI 的"研发—验证—反馈"闭环铺进城市公共空间，而不是关在园区围墙内。

空间上，脊把每一段城市切成东西两侧：西侧研发与服务，东侧居住、消费与生活。6 条东西缝合线消解现状断点，3 条 15 分钟场景回路承载 12 张 AI 场景卡（含 4 个产业测试验证场景）与 6 类用户画像。3 处 AI 朝圣地标分别是大钟寺"零号站台"广场、AI 原点社区"开源纪念walk"荣誉展示带与众智园"自主之环"开放测试广场。

文化叙事概括为"两次自主"：1909 年京张铁路的自主设计，与今天 AI 全栈技术的自主命题；Logo 方向取詹天佑人字形折返线闭合为回路的单笔画母题。

证据上，30 个用地面完整覆盖提交边界，缺口与重叠均为 0 平方米；全部空间指标在 EPSG:4548 复算。容积率、总建筑规模、建筑高度控制、绿地率控制、退线与道路用地比例共 6 项管控指标，因公开场地包缺少经批准的控规条件而一律保持"待正式数据补齐"，未以推测值填充。底图为仓库登记的临时约束范围，非官方红线；官方边界发布后全部图层与指标需整体重算。

## English

**Centennial Jing-Zhang · Autonomy Loop (JZAL)** turns the Jing-Zhang heritage park into a 9.72 km variable-width Learning Spine — about 300 m across the key areas, about 140 m along the connectors — a beaded corridor rather than a constant-width band. The spine threads Zhongzhiyuan (research), the Xiaoyuehe wing (validation), the AI Origin Community (release), Dazhongsi (consumption) and the Zhongguancun service wing (feedback) into a closed loop. The central claim: lay the AI research-validation-feedback cycle into public space instead of sealing it behind a park wall.

Spatially the spine cuts each segment into a west side of research and services and an east side of housing, consumption and daily life. Six east-west stitches dissolve existing breaks; three 15-minute scenario loops carry twelve AI scenario cards (four of them industry test-and-validation) and six user personas. The three AI pilgrimage landmarks are Platform Zero at Dazhongsi, the Open-Source Memorial Walk in the Origin Community, and the Ring of Autonomy at Zhongzhiyuan.

The cultural narrative is "two autonomies": the self-directed design of the 1909 railway, and full-stack AI autonomy today. The logo direction takes Zhan Tianyou's switchback and closes it into a one-stroke loop.

On evidence: 30 land-use polygons cover the submitted boundary with zero gap and zero overlap, and every spatial metric is recomputed in EPSG:4548. Six control metrics — floor area ratio, total floor area, height control, green-ratio control, setback and road-area ratio — stay pending official data because the public site package contains no approved regulatory conditions; no guessed value is substituted. The base is a registered provisional constraint, not an official redline; all layers and metrics require full recomputation once official data is published.
