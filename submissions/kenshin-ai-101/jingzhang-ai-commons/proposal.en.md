# Next Station, Everyone: Jing-Zhang Open Call

> **A century ago, the Chinese people built the Jing-Zhang railway themselves; today, let everyone submit one update to this city.**

![Next stop, everyone — Jing-Zhang Open Source Line Main Visual](assets/figures/hero-next-stop.png)

## One-Word Proposal

**Not to make cities look like AI, but to allow AI to be openly tested within the city.**

In 1909, the Jing-Zhang Railway was completed and put into operation. This mainline railway, surveyed, designed, constructed, and managed by Chinese people, made an era believe: the path to the future could be built by us. [source:JINGZHANG-1909] A century later, Haidian has a dense foundation in research, modeling, technology transactions, and talent, but AI is still often confined to laboratories, press conferences, and interfaces that only a few can understand. [source:HAIDIAN-2025-STATS]

Therefore, this plan no longer aims for an end point of "science and technology park," but proposes the **Jing-Zhang Open Line / THE OPEN LINE**: allowing the model to step off the screen, accompany the elderly to find a more reassuring medical route, help children identify unreliable generated content, enable small business owners to receive intelligent services without infringing on customer privacy, and allow developers to see for the first time how their single line of code can become a part of urban public value.

When someone walks through the Jing-Zhang Railway Heritage Park, they should not just capture a landmark. They can leave behind a city issue, try out a public service, reject an unsuitable proposal, or submit an open-source contribution that makes the neighborhood better. **You are not a tourist; you are the next contributor.**

## Integrate the collaborative logic of GitHub into urban governance truly.

Urban Renewal is not about posting code terms on landscape signs, but about making urban renewal like open-source software, leaving a record of versions, reviews, and rollbacks: **Issue → Multiple Proposals Fork → Neighborhood Sandbox Test → Professional and Public Review → Small-Scale Implementation Merge → Annual Release → Rollback if Inappropriate**. The original plan's open protocol, scenario sandbox, Human Review, and exit mechanism thus gain a set of urban narratives that everyone can understand. [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK] [depth:phasing_implementation] (Jing-Zhang)

The twelve stations are no longer named primarily after technical functions but are instead named after twelve genuine aspirations: **let me understand, let me navigate, let me learn, let me achieve, let me ask clearly, let me test safely, let me share my results with the world, let me find my first user, let me gain patience capital, let me avoid price discrimination, let me leave a contribution rather than data, let me exit at any time.** Each aspiration is then mapped to existing scenarios in healthcare, education, commerce, transportation, open-source, evaluation, and capital services, preserving professional integrity while also making clear how this innovation pathway relates to ordinary people. [depth:public_service_facilities]

## A typical day in the life of an ordinary person is a cross-section of future living.

08:30, an elderly person departs from the community, using an accessible healthcare route with explainable features; when road conditions change, manual shuttle services immediately take over. 12:00, a student replicates a model in the Open Source Results Corridor in 15 minutes, while viewing the license, data card, and known errors. 16:00, a team in Zhongzhiyuan presents transparent risk cards in the Trust Ring, answering "who manufactured, what data was used, who was harmed by the error, and how to stop it immediately." 20:00, residents vote in the AI Night School to decide whether to preserve, modify, or terminate a scenario. Here, technology is not a landscape effect but a must-explain and correctable neighborhood. [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#PUBLIC-001] [depth:municipal_new_infrastructure]

Three commitments run through all scenarios: **least data — data not collected unless necessary; Human Review — high-impact decisions are not automated; expiration — each scenario has a deactivation date.** These three commitments are both the electric blue, artificial confirmation yellow, and rust red in the visual system, and they are also the unchanging public value agreement from brand to space and operation in the scheme.


## Technical Framework: Jing-Zhang Smart Vein /JINGZHANG AI COMMONS

"Jing-Zhang Smart Vein" is not a corridor that decorates parks with technology, but a set of open protocols that treat urban space as a public innovation infrastructure. The overall structure is "one vein, three cores, two wings, and twelve stations": the Jing-Zhang Heritage Park bears the north-south public memory and the main pedestrian axis; Zhongzhiyuan, AI Origin community, and Dazhongsi respectively bear the core functions of full-stack validation, open-source transformation, and intelligent native consumption; the Zhongguancun Technology Services Wing and Xiaoyue River Scenario Enablement Wing provide capital/IP rights services and public scene entrances; twelve types of AI scenario stations link research and development, testing, display, learning, living, and governance into a daily route that can be repeatedly operated. [source:OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT] [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK] [depth:overall_spatial_structure]

Space strategy does not rely on large-scale demolition and construction at once, but adopts the order of "agreement first, scenario second, and space third": first establish mechanisms for data minimization, risk tiering, Human Review, and exit strategies; then use removable facilities, open hours, and reservation sandboxes to verify public value; finally, a professional team will decide whether to proceed to engineering detail based on the Official Planning Boundary, control detailed planning, ownership, traffic, utilities, and cultural heritage conditions. This approach not only responds to the rapid iteration characteristic of the AI industry but also avoids using short-term technology trends to dictate long-term urban assets. [standard:MOHURD-CONTROL-DETAILED-PLANNING] [depth:phasing_implementation] [data:geometry/phasing.geojson#PHASE-001]

Brand identity upgraded to "Turnout + Sleepers + Open Bracket": rust-red rails represent the history of self-built infrastructure, electric cyan branches represent the city's allowance for multiple parallel experiments, yellow turnouts represent the review by humans and the public, and the never-closed bracket represents that anyone can still contribute. The main slogan is **Next Stop, Everyone / NEXT STOP: EVERYONE**; the city brand is **Jing-Zhang Open Line / THE OPEN LINE**; "Jing-Zhang Zhi Mai" is retained as the name for the planning technology system. The logo uses a sans-serif system and original geometry, without embedding any corporate trademarks. [standard:PROJECT-AGENT-OPEN-CALL-TASKBOOK] [depth:height_massing_character]

Haidian 2025 Annual Report records 92 national key laboratories, 123 registered large model applications, 57,900 technology contracts, and a transaction amount of 405.3 billion yuan in technology contracts; these district-level data only prove the foundation of "origination—transformation—open collaboration" at the regional level, and are not used to infer the number of enterprises, output value, or construction scale within the project boundary. [source:HAIDIAN-2025-STATS] The National Bureau of Statistics Annual Report shows that the national R&D expenditure in 2025 was 392.62 billion yuan with an intensity of 2.80%; this plan therefore focuses on adding "open validation, public understanding, and daily talent experience" as transformation elements in the urban space, rather than mechanically distributing national data to Haidian or project plots. [source:NBS-2025]

Three Testing and Validation Scenarios constitute the minimum executable closed loop: T1 Model Safety and Public Expression Testing is conducted in Zhongzhiyuan for closed data evaluation and a public understandable display, outputting risk cards instead of certification conclusions; T2 Barrier-Free Multimodal Navigation is tested on a small scale, short cycle, and volunteer-consented route within the main artery, retaining human guidance and the option to exit at any time; T3 Smart Terminal and Content Interaction Testing is carried out in the indoor or semi-indoor operational space of Dazhongsi, with devices not allowed to enter unauthorized public areas. ### Three Sacred Sites and Cultural Narratives

"Origin Scale" uses annual public contributions to form an updatable ground timeline; "Open Source Signal Tower" translates models, data sets, tools, and public services into light/text information without commercial rankings; "Centennial Co-Creation Station" showcases the continuous history from engineering autonomy to open-source intelligence using railway station platforms, ties, and timetables. All three are conceptual components of removable, low-intervention Public Spaces, do not occupy cultural heritage control lines, do not use corporate logos, and do not display faces or individual trajectories. [standard:MOHURD-URBAN-DESIGN-MEASURES] [depth:blue_green_public_space]

The cultural narrative unfolds in three acts: "Engineering Connection—Knowledge Connection—Intelligent Connection." The Jing-Zhang Railway demonstrates that infrastructure can reshape regional connections; Zhongguancun culture shows that knowledge collaboration can shorten the time between research and application; the new AI culture must prove that technological progress can be understood, questioned, and collectively refined by the public. The wayfinding system presents historical years, open contributions, and risk boundaries simultaneously, transforming the "pilgrimage" from viewing technological wonders into understanding how public innovation is formed.

### Long-term Operation: A Public Calendar, Three Types of Members, Four Gates

The annual calendar consists of the Spring "Open Source License Month," the Summer "Urban Scene Season," the Autumn "Global AI Co-Creation Week," and the Winter "Public Value Audit." Developers, community/school members, and enterprise/organizational members have different rights to propose and review scenarios, but this does not confer exclusive promotional qualifications. Scenarios must pass through data licensing gates, ethics and safety gates, site and professional gates, and public feedback and exit gates before entering the urban space. Each project must publish its goals, data dictionary, responsible parties, trial period, complaint channels, and termination conditions. Evaluation metrics include accessibility, problem-solving rate, Human Review rate, exit response time, and public satisfaction, with public value not being replaced by exposure. ### 05 Centennial Railway—Zhongguancun—AI New Cultural Narrative and Guiding

Narrative theme: "Three Leaps of Connection": Act One, "Rails Connecting the City," tells of the engineering autonomy and modernization of the Jing-Zhang Railway; Act Two, "Knowledge Connecting the World," speaks of the collaborative culture in Zhongguancun, transitioning from research and education to market transformation; Act Three, "Intelligence Connecting Everyone," emphasizes that AI must be understandable, question-able, and correctable. The three acts together answer: Innovation is not the myth of a single hero or enterprise, but the capability formed by infrastructure, public institutions, and continuous contributions. [source:OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT]

Cultural tours adopt a "seven stations with two durations" format. The 60-90 minute public route sequentially passes through the Centennial Co-Creation Station, the Railway Engineering Archive Window, the Origin Scale, the Open Source Achievements Gallery, the Model Transparent Pavilion, the Contribution Honor Wall, and the Global Developer Living Room; the half-day professional route adds the Zhongzhiyuan Validation Workshop, the Origin Community Open Source Release, and the Dazhongsi Scene Experience.
