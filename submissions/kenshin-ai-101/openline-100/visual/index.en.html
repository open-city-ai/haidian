Seven spaces × axis–river weave

Each segment has a dominant public behavior—from trusted-test watching to quiet R&D, school–city porch, Origin plaza, community living room, underpass dark exhibit, and Next Station Hall. Segment 03 stays medium intensity so the mid-axis climax remains at Origin Plaza.

Merchant opt-in, anonymous aggregation, non-surveillance walking aids, curator review; no personal trajectories or uncleared platform internals.
