One spine (the heritage slow-mobility axis along the old Jing-Zhang rail corridor), three cores (Zhongzhiyuan AI Acceleration Area, Beijing AI Origin Community, Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster), two wings (Zhongguancun technology-service wing × Xiaoyuehe scenario-empowerment wing), and multiple operable AI scenario nodes.

The package includes: naming and visual-identity direction; 8 global AI ecosystem case studies; 12 AI scenario cards; 3 industry test-validation scenarios; 6 user personas; 3 AI pilgrimage landmarks; a fused Jing-Zhang / Zhongguancun / AI cultural narrative; an annual event system and long-term operation mechanism; a topology-safe conceptual land-use partition (90 cells) with derived green/public-space/building/road/phasing layers; and metrics fully recomputable in EPSG:4548.

All spatial content uses the maintainer's provisional boundary (provisional constraint, not an official redline) and is conceptual advice for professional deepening — not statutory planning or government conclusions. Statutory controls (FAR, height, setback) are pending official data.
