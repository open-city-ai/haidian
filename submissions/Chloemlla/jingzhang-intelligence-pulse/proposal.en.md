# Jing-Zhang Smart Vein —— Revitalizing Centennial Rails as the City's Pulse

## Design Basis and Source List

The design basis for this plan is categorized into four types, all of which are registered for availability status in `data/source_registry.json` and listed individually in `sources.json` to avoid misusing background materials as formal references.

The first category consists of official formal-ready documents: the official pre-qualification announcement, which specifies the project objectives, three layers of scope, industrial goals, key areas, and output requirements `[source:OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT]`; the design brief provided with the site package, which enumerates, delineates the design space, and specifies data schemas `[source:SITE-PACKAGE]`; the agent task book, which defines three key positions, five major functions, the Three Zones and Two Wings, six agent tasks, and boundary conditions `[source:AGENT-TASKBOOK]`. Formal deliverables will be prepared in accordance with the Urban Design Management Measures `[source:MOHURD-URBAN-DESIGN-MEASURES]`, the Compilation and Examination and Approval Measures for Urban and Town Control Detailed Planning `[source:MOHURD-CONTROL-DETAILED-PLANNING]`, and the Guide for the Classification of Land Use and Sea Area Use in Territorial Spatial Investigation, Planning, and Control (2023) `[source:MNR-LAND-USE-CLASSIFICATION-GUIDE]`. The corresponding relationships will be recorded item by item in the `standard_matrix.json`. (Regulatory Detailed Planning)

The second category is the processed navigation layer data: `PROCESSED-FACT-PACK` for agent navigation, with no additional authoritative sources `[source:PROCESSED-FACT-PACK]`.

The third category is the provisional clues: the provisional rough boundary of the site package `[source:BOUNDARY-SOURCE]` and the Provisional Boundary of the key area `[source:KEY-AREA-SOURCE]` are only used for work comparison and cannot be used as official planning boundaries or for precise areas. They have been inferred from public materials and are clearly marked. (Official Planning Boundary)

The fourth category is background research references: a global overview of AI Innovation Ecosystem cases `[source:ECOSYSTEM-CASE-REVIEW]`, which are provided solely for the reference in terms of spatial and operational mechanisms, and do not represent the current status or any commitments of any park.

The above evidence is part of a complete Evidence Chain formed with the `assumptions.json` (9 assumptions), `compliance_matrix.json` (23 tasks covered), `standard_matrix.json` (6 standards), and `design_depth_matrix.json` (15 depths). The main text is interconnected through machine-readable references `[source:...]`, `[standard:...]`, `[depth:...]`, `[data:...]`, and `[metric:...]`, corresponding to the official main control standard `[standard:PROJECT-OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT]` and the smart agent open call taskbook standard `[standard:PROJECT-AGENT-OPEN-CALL-TASKBOOK]`.

All spatial design recommendations are conceptual suggestions and reference solutions, available for professional teams to deepen their research; all outcomes are Open Co-Creation suggestions, which do not replace formal planning and do not constitute government approval conclusions. (Conceptual Recommendation)

First Layer Coordinated Research Area: Focus on the entire Haidian AI Innovation Belt and the Jing-Zhang Cultural Corridor. The work targets strategic industry, future urban form, and cultural narrative. The outcomes are strategic judgments on the coupling of industry-space-scenario, without precise boundary expression.

Second Layer Overall Design Area: This package expresses the overall design area with a temporary rough boundary `[data:geometry/site_boundary.geojson#SITE-001]`, covering an area of approximately 1,141 hectares, corresponding to the metric `[metric:site_area_sqm]`. This range is located along both sides of the Jing-Zhang Heritage Park Vitality Belt, with the work goal being an Urban Design at the control plan depth. The outcomes will be conceptual schemes for spatial structure, land use zoning, building massing, pedestrian networks, and the blue-green system.

Third Layer Key-Area Detailed Design Area: Identify three key areas within the overall scope and implement detailed design for each `[data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#K1]` `[data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#K2]` `[data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#K3]`, corresponding to the metric `[metric:key_area_count]`.

## Coordinated Research Area: Industry and Future City Research

The Coordinated Research Area responds to the requirements of announcement 1.5(1) regarding the AI Innovation Ecosystem, Three Zones and Two Wings, future AI city forms, AI culture, AI+transportation, and continuous green space systems `[standard:PROJECT-OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT]`.

**Location and Naming (AI Agent Task One: Overall Concept and Functional Integration Plan for the Jing-Zhang).** The task book provides three key orientations for the Jing-Zhang Cultural Corridor: the Centennial Jing-Zhang Cultural Belt, the Urban AI Life Experience Belt, and the AI Integration Innovation Belt `[source:AGENT-TASKBOOK]`. Five functional components include the Full-Stack Independent AI Innovation System, the World-Class AI Innovation Ecosystem, the AI-Enabled Scenario Empowerment New Paradigm, Intelligent AI Vibrant City, and Global AI Governance Discourse. The plan is named "Jing-Zhang Intelligence Vein," drawing inspiration from the century-old railway as the city's artery and AI as the city's core, advocating for "reinvigorating the century-old railway as the city's heartbeat." The logo design features a set of north-south pulse curves overlapping with the equal rhythm of the track section, symbolizing the dual metaphor of "tracks as veins and code as flow." Visual elements such as the logo, font, and images are used only for conceptual indication within this package, with all placeholders used to indicate replaceable content and no unauthorized materials used.

**Naming System and Visual Identity Direction (Smart Agent Task One).** Establish a unified naming system: the main brand "Jing-Zhang Intelligence Pulse /   Intelligence Pulse," with the main slogan "Let the century-old railway tracks become the city's heartbeat again"; sub-brands are named according to the "geography + function" rule, such as Beijing AI Origin Community, Zhongzhiyuan AI Independent Innovation Acceleration Area, Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster, and JINGZHANG Pulse Festival (JINGZHANG Pulse); functional scenarios are numbered uniformly from SC-01 to SC-12. The visual identity direction is divided into three layers: the main logo (pulse curve × track section), auxiliary graphics (equal rhythm/valves/bridge truss motifs), and usage rules (do not mix with cultural identification systems, do not cover ownership and historical identifiers). Naming and visual elements are concept placeholders, and no unlicensed fonts, images, or trademarks are used `[source:AGENT-TASKBOOK]`.

**AI Governance and Rule-Making Authority (Fifth Functional Pillar).** Focusing on the theme of AI governance global rule-making authority for the official announcement `[standard:PROJECT-OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT]`, make "governance" the fifth functional pillar: organize AI governance test corridors along the Smart Vein Belt, with content including scenario ethics review, data minimization, Human Review, and citizen participation review mechanisms (see SC-08 and the privacy boundaries of each scenario card `[source:AGENT-TASKBOOK]`). Shift the governance rules from post-hoc review to mechanisms that are pilotable and reviewable during operation; and use governance rule displays and annual AI governance dialogues, among other carriers, to form a global issue field. Governance mechanisms and rule displays are conceptual directions and are not expressed as established regulatory arrangements or government decisions.

**Spatial Structure Determination.** The overall spatial structure is defined as "one belt, three cores, two wings, multiple points, and one ring": the one belt is the Jing-Zhang Intelligence Axis (approximately 9.4 kilometers of north-south intelligence green belt and slow mobility spine `[metric:design_north_south_spine_length_m]`); the three cores are the Zhongzhiyuan AI Independent Innovation Acceleration Area, the Beijing AI Origin Community, and the Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster `[depth:overall_spatial_structure]`; the two wings are the Zhongguancun Technology Services Wing and the Xiaoyue River Scenario Enablement Wing; the multiple points are the 12 AI scenario cards landing points on the urban interface; and the one ring is the blue-green slow mobility loop connecting the three cores and the waterfront green belt `[metric:design_slow_mobility_network_length_m]`. This structure corresponds individually to the land use `[data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-001]`, roads `[data:geometry/roads.geojson#ROAD-001]`, green spaces `[data:geometry/green_space.geojson#GREEN-001]`, Public Spaces `[data:geometry/public_space.geojson#PUBLIC-001]` layers.

**Conceptual Strategies for East-West Integration and North-South Connectivity (Task Four).** The Jing-Zhang Railway once served as a spatial boundary dividing the city into east and west. This plan adopts "integration" as the core conceptual strategy: integrating the intelligent vein green belt and the blue-green slow travel loop to integrate the east and west areas, connecting the three consecutive east-west lines to establish cross-vein connections `[metric:design_east_west_connector_count]`, repairing pedestrian connections fragmented by the tracks with ground-level crosswalks, pedestrian bridges, and accessible ramps, making the heritage park vitality belt simultaneously a shared public corridor for both wings; in the north-south direction, the intelligent vein spine will penetrate approximately 9.4 kilometers `[metric:design_north_south_spine_length_m]`, connecting the three cores, heritage nodes, and waterfront green belts, forming a continuous urban public spine. Integration and connectivity are conceptual strategies that do not involve bridges, tunnels, underground spaces, or engineering feasibility conclusions `[depth:overall_spatial_structure]` `[depth:traffic_rail_slow_parking]`.

**Ecological Case Studies (Task 2 of Intelligent Body Mission: Full-Stack Independent AI Innovation System and World-Class AI Innovation Ecosystem).** Based on a comprehensive review of 7 global cases `[source:ECOSYSTEM-CASE-REVIEW]`: (1) the Stanford Research Park and the venture capital corridor in Silicon Valley, , demonstrating the "academy-capital-enterprise" loop; (2) Kendall Square in Boston, , demonstrating how a single industrial park can transform into a mixed-use area with high-frequency interactions; (3) King's Cross in London, UK, demonstrating how a railway heritage area can be updated to simultaneously carry cultural memory and innovative industries; (4) Adlershof in Berlin, Germany, demonstrating how a science city and urban living areas can share public facilities; (5) Shenzhen Nanhan Science Park, demonstrating how open scenarios and hardware supply chains can mutually benefit each other; (6) Hangzhou Future Science City, demonstrating how scenario-based recruitment and the agglomeration of digital economy can be achieved; (7) DMC Digital Media City in Seoul, demonstrating how media and AI content industries can agglomerate. These are transformed into three mechanisms for this plan: upgrading "entrepreneur-to-entrepreneur" to a "entrepreneur-developer-government-university-citizen" co-creation loop; upgrading "functional zoning" to "functional pearls on the intelligence vein." Upgrade "one-time recruitment" to "Scenario Access + Activity Operations + Continuous Transformation." The task book requires a coordinated loop for the Three Zones and Two Wings: the original community outputs talent and open culture, Zhongzhiyuan outputs computing power and engineering capabilities, Dazhongsi outputs industrial support and urban services, the Zhongguancun wing outputs technological services, and the Xiaoyuehe wing outputs scenario experiments. These five elements form mutual inputs and outputs.

**AI Innovation Ecosystem Map (Task Two).** Transform a global case review into an actionable ecosystem map, clearly defining six types of subjects and three types of flows: subjects include academic institutions and research organizations, AI companies, developers and open-source communities, parks and operating institutions, government and public services, and citizens and scenario users; flows include talent flow (origin community output → Zhongzhiyuan engineering), computational power flow (shared computational street → scenario testing), and data and scenario flow (public/authorized scenarios → product iteration). The map places each element within the Three Zones and Two Wings: Zhongzhiyuan takes on "computing power + engineering," the Original Point Community takes on "talent + open source," Dazhongsi takes on "industrial support + urban services," the Zhongguancun Wing takes on "technology services," and the Xiao Yuehe Wing takes on "scenario testing," corresponding to the research and development land use block `[data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-012]` and the community service land use block `[data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-020]`. The spatial mechanisms are detailed in `[depth:overall_spatial_structure]`, and examples are based on `[source:ECOSYSTEM-CASE-REVIEW]`. The elements, flows, and placements in the map are conceptual illustrations and do not constitute a list of enterprises, investment amounts, output values, or policy commitments `[source:AGENT-TASKBOOK]`.

**Land, space, industry, capital, talent, computing power, data, and scenario eight types of mechanisms (Agent Task Two).** Eight types of conceptual mechanisms are proposed for a full-stack self-innovative system: land mechanisms focus on updating with existing resources and functional replacement, space mechanisms lead with smart veins green belts and three core agglomeration, industry mechanisms couple with "leading companies + developer communities + scenario companies," capital mechanisms aim for a combination of market and public funding (without specifying specific amounts or commitments), talent mechanisms are supported by housing, education, and community services, computing power mechanisms coordinate shared computing streets and edge-side computing, data mechanisms operate under the principles of public and authorized data and minimalism, and scenario mechanisms promote Scenario Access and reversible pilot rolling. Among them, the Zhongguancun Technology Services Wing supports three cores and two wings with financing connections, intellectual property, computing power, and compliance services, serving as the concrete carrier for the "technology service" flow. All eight types of mechanisms are conceptual directions and do not constitute confirmed matters for industrial recruitment, capital support, or policy arrangements `[source:AGENT-TASKBOOK]` `[depth:overall_spatial_structure]`.

**Cultural Narrative (Task Five).** The Jing-Zhang Railway was China's first major railway line built independently, with a century-old track serving as both a transportation memory and the starting point of China's independent innovation spirit. The proposal organizes the narrative around five cultural themes: "Track, Station, Bridge, Valve, and Code." Track corresponds to the Smart Green Belt, Station to the transformed station nodes, Bridge to pedestrian bridges crossing the green belt, Valve to the translation of the railway's "operating timetable" into "urban rhythm," and Code to the code and open-source spirit of Zhongguancun. This narrative, together with the Jing-Zhang Heritage Park's vitality belt, the innovation culture of Zhongguancun, and the developer community, forms a cultural thread that can be explored, experienced, and inherited. For more details, see the pilgrimage landmarks in the Blue-Green Space chapter.

## Overall Design Area: Urban Renewal and Regulatory-Plan-Level Urban Design

Overall Design Area responds to Announcement 1.5 (2), achieving Regulatory Detailed Planning depth within an area of approximately 1,141 hectares for Urban Design `[metric:site_area_sqm]`, and truthfully noting the "planning conditions missing" as items to be confirmed.

**Industrial Goals and Functional Layout.** The industrial goals are "full-stack self-innovation plus world-class ecology," and the functional layout follows the principle of "green along the veins, nuclei gathering at the veins, business wings expanding, and multiple living points" `[depth:overall_spatial_structure]`. The Land-Use Layout is shown in `[data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-001]`: with a 200-meter-wide Intelligent Vein Green Belt as the axis, the Zhongzhiyuan area to the east of the green belt focuses on research and development and industrial support, while the original community area to the west of the green belt focuses on research, education, culture, and mixed residential use. The Dazhongsi area in the southern segment focuses on industrial clustering, commerce, and public cultural activities. The entire site consists of 48 land-use parcels `[metric:land_use_parcel_count]`, with a combined area of approximately 452 hectares `[metric:research_development_land_area_sqm]` for research, development, and cultural and educational uses, aligning with the "full-stack innovation" goal.

**Development Intensity and Building Height.** The approved Floor Area Ratio (), Building Coverage Ratio, Building Height, setback distances, and road right-of-way limits, which are the statutory control conditions, do not appear in the publicly available materials. Therefore, this plan does not provide a statutory development intensity conclusion, listing it as a pending confirmation item `[depth:development_intensity_controls]`. The plan proposes a height massing logic: buildings step back and lower towards the green belt along the Smart Pulse Greenway, while the core areas are allowed to form landmark volumes, with a gradual transition in the periphery. This logic is reflected in the `[depth:height_massing_character]` and the `[data:geometry/buildings.geojson#BLDG-001]` block diagram.

**Current Condition Diagnosis.** The current conditions are judged based on public clues: the existing Jing-Zhang Railway site, the Qinghe and Xiaoyuehe water systems, existing track stations and roads, the Dazhongsi and related cultural and heritage resources, and numerous existing industrial parks and unit lands `[depth:existing_conditions_diagnosis]`. Existing constraints are expressed in `[data:geometry/constraints.geojson#CON-001]` (cultural heritage), `CON-002` (existing tracks), `CON-004` (Qinghe), and `CON-005` (Xiaoyuehe). Engineering current drawings, ownership, and building conditions are missing and listed as data gaps.

**Standard Implementation.** This layer implements the requirements of the Urban Design Management Measures for spatial structure, appearance, and Public Space `[standard:MOHURD-URBAN-DESIGN-MEASURES]` and the control plan for land use nature, intensity, and amenities `[standard:MOHURD-CONTROL-DETAILED-PLANNING]`; the legal land classification will be determined after the control plan and the relevant authorities confirm `[standard:MNR-LAND-USE-CLASSIFICATION-GUIDE]`.

## Detailed Design of Key Areas

Three key areas each form a "location + spatial structure + building renewal + traffic slow zone + Public Space + AI scenario + implementation risk" readable small plan, with boundaries defined as provisional polygons `[data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#K1]` `[data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#K2]` `[data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#K3]`, and conclusions can only serve as directionally designed plans `[depth:three_key_area_detailed_design]`.

**K1 Zhongzhiyuan AI Independent Innovation Acceleration Area.** Positioning as an "accelerator from model to product," it focuses on training, evaluation, engineering, and acceleration services. The spatial structure is defined by the "Intelligence Vein Greenway + Shared Computing Street" as its backbone; architectural updates are primarily through functional replacement, with the addition of shared laboratories and test workshops; transportation is organized by the Intelligence Vein Axis for direct access and an internal slow-moving loop for connectivity; Public Space includes the Zhongzhiyuan Pulse Square `[data:geometry/public_space.geojson#PUBLIC-003]`; AI scenarios include spaces for intelligent industry testing and result release; implementation risks mainly stem from the uncertainty of industrial support and operational capabilities.

**K2 Beijing AI Origin Community.** Positioned as the "origin of talent and open-source culture," it focuses on talent communities, incubation, education, and international developer exchanges. The spatial structure is organized around "green belts + community wards," preserving the existing fabric of academic and institutional courtyards; architectural updates prioritize retention and enhancement, with the insertion of youth talent apartments and community services `[data:geometry/buildings.geojson#BLDG-008]`; transportation is prioritized with rail connections and a preference for active modes; Public Spaces include the Origin Star Track Square `[data:geometry/public_space.geojson#PUBLIC-002]`; AI scenarios include open-source co-creation, AI education labs, and entrepreneur services; implementation risks mainly stem from complex property rights and diverse update stakeholders.

**K3 Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster.** Positioning as a "hub for industry and urban public life," it focuses on industry aggregation, business services, cultural experience, and public transportation hub. The spatial structure is organized around "integrated station-city development + mixed commercial and cultural streets"; architectural renewal is mainly through mixed development, incorporating cultural facilities and talent services; transportation utilizes the existing rail station for integrated transfer `[data:geometry/roads.geojson#ROAD-003]`; Public Spaces include the Dazhongsi Wisdom Plaza `[data:geometry/public_space.geojson#PUBLIC-001]`; AI scenarios include the Dazhongsi AI City Living Room and public safety operation review; implementation risks mainly involve the integration of station development and cultural heritage protection management.

**Dazhongsi Intelligent Native Consumption and Business Scenarios (Agent Task Four).** In the Dazhongsi station-city integrated area, propose a direction for intelligent native new business forms: intelligent native retail (experience-based stores with AI product selection and self-checkout), digital business experience (data visualization and AI analysis display), AI lifestyle consumption (integrated smart travel, health, and life services), and business and meeting services for enterprises. The business forms are introduced in a reversible manner, "pilot first, then expand," as specified in `[data:geometry/public_space.geojson#PUBLIC-001]`, without any unauthorized brand names or determined business arrangements, all of which are conceptual directions `[source:AGENT-TASKBOOK]`.

Three zones serve a total of 6 types of profiles (see the AI scenario chapter), with the metric criteria defined in `[metric:key_area_count]`, and the drawings can be found in the A3/A0 collection.

The Land-Use Layout follows the classification codes from the Guide for Land and Sea Use Classification in National Land Space Investigation, Planning, and Control (2023) `[standard:MNR-LAND-USE-CLASSIFICATION-GUIDE]`, and implements the depth items `[depth:land_use_layout]` with `[data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-001]`: Park and green spaces 1401 form the Smart Pulse Green Belt and the waterfront green belt; research and development land 0802 form the two wings and the R&D plots; culture 0803 and education 0804 serve talent and the community; residential land 0701 and community services 0702 form the west wing living cluster; commercial land 05 is located at nodes such as Dazhongsi; protective green spaces 1402 are arranged along existing tracks and roads. The entire site is seamlessly and non-overlappingly covered by 48 parcels `[metric:land_use_parcel_count]`, with the total area aligning with `[metric:site_area_sqm]`, which can be recalculated under EPSG:4548.

The Building Footprint is illustrated by 19 individual parcels `[data:geometry/buildings.geojson#BLDG-001]`, with types covering AI research and development, laboratories, culture, incubators, transportation hubs, talent apartments, education, community services, mixed-use, offices, retail, and residential uses, with a total building footprint area of 69.7 million square meters `[metric:building_footprint_area_sqm]`. The classification of the Demolish–Renovate–Retain Strategy is based on three concepts of intensity: retention enhancement (preserving the existing academic institutions, cultural heritage, and unit courtyard patterns, such as the original point community), functional replacement (replacing the existing industrial parks with AI industries and public services, such as Zhongzhiyuan and Dazhongsi), and adaptive renewal (adding new research and development, education, culture, and talent housing plots). `[depth:retain_renovate_demolish]` clearly defines this classification as a conceptual tiering, and the specific demolition, renovation, and retention for each plot require further refinement after confirmation of ownership, current buildings, and control plans. ## Transport, Rail, Municipal Infrastructure, and Public Services

The transportation system is organized as "one axis, three connections, one ring, and multiple branches" `[data:geometry/roads.geojson#ROAD-001]` `[depth:traffic_rail_slow_parking]`: the one axis is the north-south Smart Vein main axis, connecting the three cores and the site park vitality belt; the three connections are three east-west connection lines, facilitating east-west connections across the veins, corresponding to the metric `[metric:design_east_west_connector_count]`; the one ring is the blue-green slow travel ring, connecting Qinghe, Xiaoyuehe, and the green belt; the multiple branches are the park access roads and local arrival roads. The total length of the slow travel network is approximately 37.6 kilometers `[metric:design_slow_mobility_network_length_m]`, with the main axis length being about 9.4 kilometers `[metric:design_north_south_spine_length_m]`. Subway connections are organized to integrate with existing stations (Dazhongsi, Qinghe direction), with a low-speed connection and transfer space in front of the station `[data:geometry/roads.geojson#ROAD-002]`. Parking and non-motorized vehicles are organized within the hub and block, with pedestrians prioritized along the green belt.

Municipal and New Infrastructure `[depth:municipal_new_infrastructure]`: Edge-side computing and distributed energy are conceptually embedded within the plot of the park, complementing AI scenarios to provide reversible lightweight pilot infrastructure; digital municipal services (pipeline sensing, energy consumption monitoring) are an overlay layer to traditional municipal services, without replacing professional engineering calculations. Public service facilities are laid out in a two-tier system: the three cores are configured with innovation service platforms, talent living services, and cultural public spaces; the neighborhoods are configured with community services along the Smart Axis on both sides `[data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-025]`. Road alignments, rail alignments, bridge and tunnel engineering, and municipal pipeline schemes are not provided and are listed as requiring professional refinement `[depth:risk_missing_data]`.

## Blue-Green Network, Public Space, and Urban Character

Blue-Green Space is organized as "One Vein and Two Belts and One Ring" `[depth:blue_green_public_space]`: One Vein is a green corridor approximately 200 meters wide, known as the Wisdom Vein Green Belt `[data:geometry/green_space.geojson#GREEN-001]`, serving as the backbone for ecological and public life; Two Belts are the Qinghe River Waterfront Green Belt `[data:geometry/green_space.geojson#GREEN-002]` and Xiao Yuehe River Waterfront Green Belt `[data:geometry/green_space.geojson#GREEN-003]`, along with the southern Green Wedge `[data:geometry/green_space.geojson#GREEN-004]`; One Ring is a pedestrian and bicycle loop connecting the three cores with the waterfront. Green space ratio is approximately 18.5% `[metric:green_ratio]`, and the proportion of Public Space is about 1.8% `[metric:public_space_ratio]`, both of which are design targets recalculated from the submitted layers. The green space ratio and square land use require official map verification. Public space is anchored by 5 squares and parks `[data:geometry/public_space.geojson#PUBLIC-001]` to `PUBLIC-005`, combined with the Walking and Cycling Network, rail transit connections, and cultural nodes.
