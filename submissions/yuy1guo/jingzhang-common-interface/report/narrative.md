# 京张友好接口 / Jingzhang Friendly Interfaces

The proposal treats AI as a civic interface rather than a decorative smart-city layer. A resident should be able to understand the service, choose whether to use it, reach a human fallback, and see how a decision can be reviewed or stopped.

The spatial concept connects the Jingzhang heritage park, Zhongzhiyuan, the AI Origin Community, Dazhongsi, the Zhongguancun Technology Service Wing, and the Xiaoyuehe Scenario Empowerment Wing through an everyday slow spine, four interface bands, six node families, and three phases. All spatial geometry remains provisional and conceptual until official data and professional confirmation are supplied.
