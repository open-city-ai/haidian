The City Poses.
The Public Accepts.

The world co-solves, and the public may send an answer back.

Beyond technical validation, the proposal adds the missing public procedures: who may pose a question, who bears the consequences, when testing may happen, how the public accepts an answer, and how a failed project exits. One question ledger, one validation chain, one everyday care network and 12 scenario nodes carry these procedures in space; G0–G4 gates check rights, risk, field testing, public adoption and annual review for every question.

PROV-KEY-003 is not anchored to Dazhongsi Station, about 2.26 km away. This round uses “Dazhongsi” as a taskbook label only; it implies no station exit, quadrant or passenger-flow relationship, and the polygon is not shifted on our own.

02

Three-Level Scope

Each level carries its own decision scale rather than enlarging one map three times.

01

Coordinated Research Area

Decides which questions deserve joint answers across districts and institutions. v2.0 proposes a public question catalogue, case-mechanism translation, an annual Open Challenges conference and a co-solving network, adding no precise redlines.

02

Overall Design Area

Decides how answers enter everyday urban systems. SITE-001 serves as the provisional working base for one question ledger, one validation chain and one everyday care network.

03

Key Areas

Decides where tests may run, at what risk level, and who accepts them. All three areas are provisional rough polygons, paired with 12 scenario cards and the G0–G4 gates.

03

Key Areas

The three key areas share one review framework: positioning, spatial organisation, existing buildings, access and public space, AI scenarios, operational responsibility and stop conditions. All extents are provisional rough polygons.

PROV-KEY-001 · provisional rough

Zhongzhiyuan AI Independent-Innovation Acceleration Area

Evidence first. High-risk tests stay in controlled indoor or enclosed venues; a public evidence gallery shows methods, versions, failures and stop reasons. Testing stops when data rights are unclear, attack risks remain open or no energy baseline exists.

PROV-KEY-002 · provisional rough

Beijing AI Origin Community

Questions first. A continuous ground-floor interface links question clinics, open-source collaboration, releases and daily services. Work stops on data overreach, misleading service or pressure on everyday community life.

PROV-KEY-003 · provisional rough

Southern Functional Prototype

Dazhongsi is a taskbook label; the location is unconfirmed. Until the official key area arrives, only a portable set of “disclosure-card market + human help desk + civic review forum” is defined; the current polygon is not read as the station surroundings. Misleading promotion, unanswered complaints or the unconfirmed location each trigger a stop.

A-BUILDING-003 · pending_survey_and_professional_confirmation

A-TENURE-004 · pending_official_data

A-TRAFFIC-005 · pending_official_data
