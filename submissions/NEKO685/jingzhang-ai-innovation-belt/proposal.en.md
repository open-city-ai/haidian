# Jing-Zhang Smart Vein —— Centennial Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt Overall Urban Design Conceptual Proposal

## Design Basis and Source List

This plan is based primarily on the qualification pre-review announcement issued by the Haidian Branch of the Beijing Municipal Commission of Planning and Natural Resources for the "Centennial Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt Urban Design International Scheme Call" ([source:OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT],[standard:PROJECT-OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT]), and secondarily on the structured basis provided by the machine-readable task book registered by the maintainer in the `brief/site-package/` directory ([source:SITE-PACKAGE]). The design tasks, three key areas, three positioning principles, five functions, the Three Zones and Two Wings, and the six intelligent body tasks all come from the `brief/site-package/agent_taskbook.json` ([source:AGENT-TASKBOOK],[standard:PROJECT-AGENT-OPEN-CALL-TASKBOOK]), subject to ten co-creation principles that ensure the public interest is always prioritized, the Conceptual Recommendation is clear, and both structure and readability are emphasized ([source:AGENT-TASKBOOK]).

Urban Design data is based on `data/source_registry.json` ([source:SOURCE-REGISTRY]) and `data/processed/agent_fact_pack.md` ([source:PROCESSED-FACT-PACK]): formal available data includes 5 records (announcements, agent task documents, Urban Design management regulations, detailed planning compilation approval methods, land and sea use classification guide), and provisional-only data includes 1 record (Provisional Boundary geometry). All spatial conclusions in this plan are based solely on the formal available data and provisional boundary, and no data from unregistered sources are used. Before the Official Planning Boundary is established, `geometry/site_boundary.geojson#SITE-001` and the three areas in `geometry/key_areas.geojson` are all marked as `provisional_constraint` ([source:BOUNDARY-SOURCE], [source:KEY-AREA-SOURCE]), and are only used for scheme generation, self-check, visualization, and design discussions; they are not to be used as the official planning boundary, approval basis, or for precise area recalculation. This data gap does not affect content scoring, and all layers and metrics will be recalculated according to [depth metrics_recalculation] once the official polygon is released.

This proposal simultaneously states: all spatial recommendations are **Conceptual Recommendations, references, or materials for further research by professional teams**, and do not constitute legal planning conclusions, government approval results, investment commitments, or engineering feasibility judgments ([standard:PROJECT-AGENT-OPEN-CALL-TASKBOOK]). The text body is based on `proposal.md` as the sole main document, while `geometry/*.geojson` and `metrics.json` serve as machine evidence. [depth:existing_conditions_diagnosis] records the current condition diagnosis boundary—due to the lack of official current condition base maps, the current condition diagnosis is only at the district scale, and the block-scale current condition remains to be supplemented by professional teams.

## Three-Level Scope Framework

The plan is organized according to the three-layer scope defined in the official announcement ([standard:PROJECT-OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT]): the Coordinated Research Area covers approximately 43.6 square kilometers, bounded by the North Fifth Ring Road to the north, the Jingzhang Expressway to the east, Xisi Street to the south, and Wancuahu Road to the west, and is responsible for the research on AI industry ecology, innovation chain, and future urban form ([metric:site_area_sqm] corresponding to the core mapping area of the overall design scope); the overall design scope covers approximately 11.4 square kilometers, focusing on the urban and industrial areas within 1-2 kilometers around the Jingzhang Site Park, and is responsible for the in-depth Urban Renewal overall design at the control plan level ([depth:three_level_scope_framework]); the key-area detailed design scope covers approximately 368.4 hectares, extending from north to south, including three detailed design areas: the Zhongzhiyuan AI Independent Innovation Acceleration Area, the Beijing AI Origin Community, and the Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster ([metric:key_area_area_sqm], [metric:key_area_count]). (Overall Design Area) (Key-Area Detailed Design Area) (Jing-Zhang)

Three-tier scope progressively implemented: comprehensively study and determine the spatial coordination framework for the industrial chain, talent chain, and urban service chain; overall design translates the strategic vision into land use, buildings, roads, blue-green spaces, and phased layers ([data:geometry/site_boundary.geojson#SITE-001], [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-001], [data:geometry/roads.geojson#ROAD-001]); detailed design for key areas verifies the overall structure at the parcel scale ([data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-001]). Given that the submitted boundary is provisional ([source:BOUNDARY-SOURCE]), the work depth, area recalculation, and graphical representation for the three-tier scope are temporary solutions. After replacing with the Official Boundary, the geometric generation, metric recalculation, and rendering of the drawings and HTML need to be re-run ([depth:metrics_recalculation]). The three-tier scope framework, depth items, and task coverage are mapped individually in `compliance_matrix.json`, ensuring that sections, layers, indicators, drawings, and HTML evidence are provided for required announcements 1.3, 1.4, 1.5, and agent.1—agent.6 tasks.

## Coordinated Research Area: Industry and Future City Research

### Overall Concept, Naming System, and Logo Direction (agent.1)

This proposal presents an overall concept **"Jing-Zhang Intelligence Spine"** (English name **Jing-Zhang Intelligence Spine**, abbreviated **JZI Spine**), with the main brand slogan as **"Person Track · Intelligence Spine"** — taking inspiration from the "person track" zigzag railway created by Zhan Tianyou at Badaling (one of the most recognizable engineering and cultural symbols of the Jing-Zhang Railway), which is reinterpreted as the return of talent, computing power, and innovation in the AI era; the sub-slogan "Centennial Track, One Line Continues" connects historical and cultural heritage with a technological future ([source:AGENT-TASKBOOK], [standard:PROJECT-AGENT-OPEN-CALL-TASKBOOK]).

The naming system adopts a "one belt, three cores, and dual wings" structure: one belt, which is the **Jing-Zhang Smart Vein** (cultural belt, experience belt, and innovation belt triple overlay); three cores, namely the **Zhongzhiyuan Full-Stack Acceleration Core** (Full-Stack Independent AI Innovation System and global AI governance discourse), the **AI Origin Community Ecosystem Core** (world-class AI Innovation Ecosystem), and the **Dazhongsi Intelligent Economy Core** (new intelligent-born business models); two wings, the **Zhongguancun Technology Service Wing** (global element configuration, Zhongguancun IP and capital empowerment) and the **Xiaoyue River Scenario Enablement Wing** (AI scenario empowerment and intelligent AI vibrant city). Logo direction: the geometric shape of a zigzag return track forms the backbone, with binary pulse waveforms superimposed, creating a double entendre symbol of "zigzag track + pulse"; the color system primarily uses steel track gray (history), Haidian blue (technology), and innovation green (ecosystem) three colors; visual identification extends to signage, scenario cards, activity systems, and Public Space component libraries. The above naming and logo are conceptual directions, containing no trademark or copyright materials, awaiting refinement by a professional branding team ([agent.1] corresponds to [standard:PROJECT-AGENT-OPEN-CALL-TASKBOOK]). (Zhongguancun Technology Services Wing)

### Three Key Orientations, Five Major Functions, and the Synergistic Loop of Three Zones and Two Wings

Three key positioning goals are to establish a century-old Jing-Zhang cultural belt, a metropolitan AI lifestyle experience belt, and an AI integration innovation belt; five major functions include the Full-Stack Independent AI Innovation System, a world-class AI Innovation Ecosystem, AI-Enabled Scenario empowerment new paradigm, intelligent AI vibrant city, and AI governance global discourse power. The Three Zones and Two Wings collaborative loop design is: **origin—accelerate—transform—feedback** loop: the AI Origin community, relying on its proximity to the university, takes on the role of source innovation ([data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-002]), Zhongzhiyuan takes on the full-stack independent AI innovation and governance standard setting ([data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-001]), and Dazhongsi takes on the role of intelligent economic transformation and international exchanges ([data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-003]); the Zhongguancun Technology Services Wing provides capital, IP, and element configuration, while the Xiaoyue River Scenario Enablement Wing provides application testing and vibrant scenarios. The two wings provide feedback to the three cores to form a collaborative loop ([source:AGENT-TASKBOOK]). This loop translates to space as "one belt and three cores, with coordinated dual wings and a blue-green composite ring" overall structure ([depth:overall_spatial_structure]), presented by the agent.1 item in `compliance_matrix.json` and the task coverage area in `visual/index.html`.

## Overall Design Area: Urban Renewal and Regulatory-Plan-Level Urban Design

### Spatial Structure: "One Belt, Three Cores, Dual Wings, and a Blue-Green Composite Ring"

The Overall Design Area is centered around the **Jing-Zhang Ruins Park Vitality Belt** as the cultural and Public Space axis, connecting the Zhongzhiyuan, AI Origin community, and Dazhongsi in a north-south direction; on either side, the **Zhongguancun Technology Services Wing** and the **Xiaoyue River Scenario Enablement Wing** are located; the Qinghe—Xiaoyue River—Jing-Zhang Ruins Park Blue-Green Space and walking and cycling system form a **composite ring** ([depth:overall_spatial_structure], [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-001], [data:geometry/green_space.geojson#GREEN-001]). (Walking and Cycling Network) In terms of land structure, research and development uses (0802) and industrial service uses (05) are arranged along both sides of the archaeological park belt. The western wing focuses on technological services and community services, while the eastern wing emphasizes talent communities and living services. The edge areas are designated for blank land uses (16) to accommodate future development ([depth:land_use_layout]). The land use zones consist of 26 parcels ([metric:land_use_parcel_count]), covering the submitted boundary without gaps or overlaps ([data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-001]).

### Urban Renewal Overall Framework and Functional Proportions

Update the framework according to the logic of "preserve—renovate—update—rebuild—leave blank" ([depth:retain_renovate_demolish]): the cultural and historical resources along the Jing-Zhang site park will focus on preservation and revitalization; the inefficient industrial spaces within the three cores will focus on functional updates; the living areas in the two wings will focus on minor updates and community service supplementation; and the blank areas will be reserved for future industrial and infrastructure flexibility. The functional proportions (Conceptual Recommendation) are as follows: research and innovation service land use at about 30%, industrial service and commercial land use at about 20%, residential and talent community land use at about 25%, green spaces and open spaces at about 20%, and roads and other uses at about 5%, with the final proportions to be determined by the official control detailed planning ([standard:MOHURD-CONTROL-DETAILED-PLANNING]). Due to the lack of official control detailed planning conditions, all Building Height, Floor Area Ratio, setback, and other intensity indicators are listed as items to be confirmed ([depth:development_intensity_controls]), and speculative values should not be used to substitute for determined indicators; architectural form and style control will only serve as a conceptual guide ([depth:height_massing_character]).

### Urban Design Implementation Pathway

In accordance with the requirements of the Urban Design Management Measures, the overall design coordinates the plane and three-dimensional space, Public Spaces, Building Height, massing, style, and color control requirements ([standard:MOHURD-URBAN-DESIGN-MEASURES]): the architectural interface along both sides of the relic park belt forms a "low-level cultural interface—middle-level innovation interface—high-level industrial interface" three-tier stepped guidance; the public space system consists of three key square areas, the relic park belt, and the riverside greenway ([data:geometry/public_space.geojson#PUBLIC-001]); street furniture, signage, and a unified style component library for public art respond to the "Human Track·Wisdom Vein" visual identity. All controls are at the Conceptual Recommendation level, awaiting confirmation of official urban design conditions ([depth:height_massing_character]).

AI Scenarios: Autonomous model evaluation, red team testing observation, standard setting workshop, low-carbon computing experience ([metric:scenario_node_count]). ### Beijing AI Origin Community (Zhonghuan)

Location: **Institution-Adjacent Technology Transfer and Talent Special Zone**, leveraging nearby universities and research institutions to form a "originating—incubation—transfer—publication" full chain ([data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-002]). Spatial structure: centered around the Open Source Plaza, organizing the Technology Transfer Street, Open Source Collaborative Space, Results Publication Hall, and Talent Apartments; integrating the campus—park—community with a slow travel network, and connecting with transit stations ([depth:three_key_area_detailed_design]). ## Land Use, Building Scale, and Retain-Renovate-Demolish Strategy

Land-Use Layout is based on the guidelines for land use and sea use classification in the National Land Space Investigation, Planning, and Use Control () ([standard:MNR-LAND-USE-CLASSIFICATION-GUIDE]), covering a total of 26 parcels. The classification includes research and development land (0802), industrial service and commercial land (05), urban residential land (0701), community service facilities land (0702), educational land (0804), park green space (1401), and blank land (16) ([data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-001], [metric:land_use_parcel_count]). Research and development and industrial service land are concentrated in the vicinity of the three cores and the relic park belt, supporting AI full-stack innovation and intelligent economic activities; residential and community service land are located in the wings, serving talent and residents; park green spaces form a continuous blue-green network along the relic park belt and Qinghe—Xiaoyuehe ([metric:green_space_area_sqm], [metric:green_ratio]).

In terms of building scale, the scheme generates 53 concept Building Footprints ([data:geometry/buildings.geojson#BLDG-001],[metric:building_count]), with a total footprint area of approximately 18.4 hectares ([metric:building_footprint_area_sqm]), primarily consisting of types such as AI research and development, industrial services, talent communities, education and research, and community services; the Building Height and Floor Area Ratio are listed as pending confirmation due to the lack of official development intensity controls ([depth:development_intensity_controls]), and the form guidance is managed by [depth:height_massing_character]. The demolish–renovate–retain strategy is based on the concept recommendation ([depth:retain_renovate_demolish]): retain the existing texture of the heritage park belt and the university community; renovate the inefficient spaces along the three cores; update the functions of key sites; new constructions are concentrated in the concept building footprint areas; and the peripheral areas are left blank (16) to retain development flexibility. (Conceptual Recommendation) (Demolish–Renovate–Retain Strategy) All conclusions of the Demolish–Renovate–Retain Strategy await confirmation of the existing building conditions, ownership, and control plan data and do not constitute site-level demolition–renovation conclusions.

## Transport, Rail, Municipal Infrastructure, and Public Services

Traffic strategies prioritize **walking and cycling first, rail transit integration, and green travel** ([standard:PROJECT-OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT]): The north-south Jing-Zhang Smart Vein main corridor connects the three cores ([data:geometry/roads.geojson#ROAD-001],[metric:road_network_length_m]), while the east-west connecting roads integrate the two wings with the heritage park belt.
