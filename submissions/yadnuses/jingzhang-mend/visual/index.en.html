Three-Level Scope 三层范围

The scheme proceeds from the Coordinated Research Area to the Overall Design Area and then to the Key-Area Detailed Design Area, binding industrial judgment, urban renewal, and key-area design to layers and metrics.

project_scope_summary.csv

Coordinated Research Area 统筹研究范围

For about 43.6 km², proposes the AI industrial ecology, the three-zones-two-wings synergy, future urban form, and the cultural narrative.

  • AI innovation chain and talent chain
  • transfer mechanism from global cases
  • brand naming and communication

Overall Design Area 总体设计范围

For about 11.4 km², organizes urban renewal, land-use structure, transport and municipal works, and the Jing-Zhang Heritage Park vitality belt.

  • regulatory-plan-level urban design
  • renewal project list
  • blue-green and slow-mobility system

Key-Area Detailed Design Area 重点区域范围

Detailed design of the three key areas, covering functional formats, building renewal, public space, and transport organization.

  • Zhongzhiyuan AI Independent Innovation Acceleration Area
  • Beijing AI Origin Community
  • Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster

Key Areas 重点区域

Each of the three key areas must state its industrial positioning, spatial strategy, building and public-space moves, transport interfaces, and implementation risks.

Zhongzhiyuan AI Independent Innovation Acceleration Area 众智园AI自主创新加速区

Garden-type independent-innovation neighborhood: national platforms, industrial exhibition, Qinghe culture, external transport, and low-carbon exchange spaces.

Beijing AI Origin Community 北京AI原点社区

Campus-adjacent results-translation neighborhood: university source innovation, open-source community, talent services, demolish-renovate-retain, and station integration.

Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster 大钟寺AI产业聚集区

Urban-type intelligent-economy district: leading enterprises, agent-based formats, commercial services, compound green-space use, and four-quadrant intersection connection.
