## Design Basis and Source List

This proposal is grounded in the Centennial Jingzhang AI Belt international open call announcement and the agent-oriented taskbook [source:OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT] [source:AGENT-OPEN-CALL-TASKBOOK]. The Luoduo AI Army holds operating authorization for nine real ecosystems that anchor the proposal's delivery [source:LUODUO-ECOSYSTEM-AUTH]. Site scope relies on the repository's provisional boundary package, which is a provisional constraint rather than an official red-line [source:DATA-SRC-PROVISIONAL-BOUNDARIES-20260605] [data:geometry/site_boundary.geojson#site].

## Three-Level Scope Framework

The scheme follows a three-level framework: coordinated research area, overall design area, and key areas [depth:three_level_scope_framework]. The three levels progressively converge so that strategic judgement and delivery scale stay consistent [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#key_areas] [standard:PROJECT-AGENT-OPEN-CALL-TASKBOOK].

## Coordinated Research Area: Industry and Future City Research

The coordinated research area uses the AI innovation industry to study the human-machine-space relationship of the future city [depth:existing_conditions_diagnosis]. Luoduo's real ecosystems (unmanned retail, vending, WiFi, repair, AI guide) provide operable scenarios instead of demo-only AI [source:LUODUO-ECOSYSTEM-AUTH].

## Overall Design Area: Urban Renewal and Regulatory-Plan-Level Urban Design

The overall design area is developed at urban-renewal and regulatory-plan depth, fixing land use, intensity, height and character controls [depth:overall_spatial_structure] [depth:development_intensity_controls]. The land-use grid covers the whole site and is clipped to the boundary [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#land_use_grid].

## Detailed Design of Key Areas

The three key areas are the Zhongzhiyuan AI Acceleration Area, the Beijing AI Origin Community, and the Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster [depth:three_key_area_detailed_design] [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#key_areas]. Their sizes are provisional rough estimates used only as constraints [metric:key_area_total_sqm].

## AI Innovation Ecosystem, Personas, and AI+ Scenarios

The Luoduo AI Army operates fourteen agents backing real ecosystems, forming an AI-native urban service capability [source:LUODUO-ECOSYSTEM-AUTH]. Personas focus on hybrid AI engineering, product and operations talent; scenarios cover unmanned retail, smart guide, facility repair, public WiFi and community governance [depth:height_massing_character] [metric:ai_landmark_count].

## Land Use, Building Scale, and Retain-Renovate-Demolish Strategy

The land-use scheme is led by research, commercial, education and community service, with reserved land controlling intensity [depth:land_use_layout] [depth:retain_renovate_demolish]. Building scale is classified by mixed-use and community service, with totals measured on the provisional boundary [metric:proposed_total_floor_area_sqm] [metric:building_footprint_ratio].

## Transport, Rail, Municipal Infrastructure, and Public Services

Transport is anchored on rail stations with a slow-traffic-first network [depth:traffic_rail_slow_parking]. Road length is estimated on the provisional boundary [metric:road_network_length_m] [data:geometry/roads.geojson#road_network]. Municipal and public services emphasize plug-and-play operable facilities linked to Luoduo's repair and WiFi capabilities.

## Blue-Green Network, Public Space, and Urban Character

Blue-green space forms a continuous network of park green and plaza; public space stresses accessibility and activity capacity [depth:blue_green_public_space] [data:geometry/green_space.geojson#green] [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#public]. Green ratio and public-space ratio are measured on the provisional boundary [metric:green_ratio] [metric:public_space_ratio].

## Renewal Projects, Implementation Policy, and Phasing

There are 18 renewal projects delivered in three phases [metric:renewal_project_count] [depth:renewal_project_list] [depth:phasing_implementation]. Phase 1 demonstrates key areas, phase 2 expands the coordinated scope, phase 3 forms the whole area [data:geometry/phasing.geojson#phasing] [metric:phase1_area_sqm].

## Metrics, Area Recalculation, and Compliance Matrix

The metric system covers land use, scale, blue-green, transport and renewal, all based on the provisional boundary with confidence labels [depth:metrics_recalculation] [metric:site_area_sqm]. Area recalculation is explicit: a full recompute is required once official red-lines arrive [metric:green_space_area_sqm].

## Risk, Copyright, and Compliance

All spatial schemes are conceptual; the provisional boundary is not an official red-line and must not be used as precise area or ownership basis [depth:risk_missing_data]. Generative content is disclosed in agent.json and risk.json; copyright is CC-BY-4.0 with community-display attribution [source:LUODUO-ECOSYSTEM-AUTH].
