# 开源地表 / Open-Source Ground

京张走廊已经集聚高校、科研、AI企业、轨道、社区和公园，公共转化接口仍由围墙、首层门槛和横向断点切开。方案以铁路遗产公园为连续公共基底，以五条横向接入带缝合机构、社区和站点，以十五分钟开放单元配置服务，以地表协议单元管理入口、时段、权限、维护与退出。北、中、南形成环境学习、知识编译和公共服务编排三种原型；众智园、北京AI原点和大钟寺分别以独立平面、剖面和场景深化。

The corridor already concentrates universities, research, AI companies, transit, communities and parks, while walls, inaccessible ground floors and transverse breaks interrupt public transformation. The proposal uses the railway heritage park as a continuous public base, five transverse accesses to stitch institutions, communities and stations, Fifteen-Minute Open Units to distribute service, and Ground Protocol Units to govern access, time, permissions, maintenance and exit. Three corridor prototypes and three independently designed key areas turn the concept into spatial and operational detail.

完整中文正文见 `proposal.md`，等义英文版见 `proposal.en.md`。来源只回溯原始官方、公共机构、大学、业主、学术和开放许可资料；结构化空间证据、QGIS核验、指标、假设与版权记录共同形成可复查证据链。
