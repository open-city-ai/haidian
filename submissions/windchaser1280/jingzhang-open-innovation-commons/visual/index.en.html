Four Governance Zones

G1 Open Experience

Exhibitions, heritage guidance and open-source events

Cited sources; no mandatory login; human service available

G2 Assisted Service

Navigation, community help, space matching and ecological work orders

Data minimization; explanation, appeal and human confirmation

G3 Controlled Testing

Model, robot, device and microgrid validation

Permit, isolation, logs, observer, emergency stop and exit

G4 No Automated Decision

Approvals, enforcement, benefits, medical, emergency and critical control

Authorized people decide; AI cannot directly create legal or bodily consequences
