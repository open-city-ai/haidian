# 方案叙事 / Proposal Narrative

**京张开放创新公地 / Jing-Zhang Open Innovation Commons** 将京张铁路遗产廊道重构为开放创新的公共基础设施。方案以一条连续南北公共脉络连接众智园、北京AI原点社区和大钟寺三处重点区域，并通过轨道站点、校园入口、企业园区、社区中心和蓝绿空间形成东西向缝合。

The proposal reframes the Jing-Zhang railway heritage corridor as civic infrastructure for open innovation. One continuous north-south spine links Zhongzhiyuan, Beijing AI Origin Community, and Dazhongsi; east-west interfaces reconnect transit, campuses, enterprises, neighbourhoods, and blue-green space.

方案不是单一科技展示带，而是完整的创新链：高校策源、开源协作、共享验证、企业转化、城市应用和国际发布。十二张AI场景卡将技术落实到真实空间、数据边界、人工复核和运营责任；其中四张明确为产业测试/验证场景。六类人物画像保证研究者、初创团队、企业、居民、国际人才和城市运营者均有清晰的日常体验与权益保护。

The concept is an operating ecosystem rather than a technology showcase: university origination, open-source collaboration, shared validation, enterprise translation, urban application, and international launch. Twelve AI scenario cards define place, data boundaries, human review, operators, and metrics, including four industrial test/validation scenarios. Six personas make everyday use and rights visible.

四级AI治理分区将空间划分为开放体验、辅助服务、受控试验和禁止自动决策四类风险环境。规划审批、执法处罚、福利资格、医疗诊断、应急指挥和关键基础设施控制均保留授权人员最终决定权。四个地标与贡献展示组件——京张百年时标、开放贡献墙、可信创新灯塔和全球协作环——共同表达记忆、贡献、验证和交流。

Four AI governance levels distinguish open experience, assisted service, controlled testing, and no-automated-decision environments. Authorized people retain final control over planning approval, enforcement, benefit eligibility, medical diagnosis, emergency command, and critical infrastructure. Four landmark components express memory, contribution, validation, and exchange.

实施采用0—2年低风险启动、2—5年空间更新与共享设施、5年以上网络成熟与滚动更新三阶段。长期运营由建议性的公地理事会协调年度任务、场景准入、数据治理、绩效公开和社区审议，并以共享设施、专业服务、会员、培训、活动和研究合作形成多元收入及公共再投入。

Implementation proceeds through low-risk activation, spatial renewal and shared facilities, then a mature network with rolling updates. A proposed Commons Council coordinates annual missions, scenario admission, data governance, performance disclosure, and community review, supported by diversified operating income and public reinvestment.

本提交使用临时粗略边界。法定用地、容积率、建筑高度、道路红线、权属、市政、消防、河道、防洪和文保控制仍为 provisional 或 unknown；提案不构成法定规划、施工图或政府实施承诺。完整中英文等值文本分别见 `proposal.md` 和 `proposal.en.md`。

The submission uses rough provisional boundaries. Statutory land use, FAR, height, road redlines, ownership, utilities, fire safety, waterways, flood control, and heritage controls remain provisional or unknown. The proposal is not a statutory plan, construction design, or government implementation commitment. Full equivalent texts are in `proposal.md` and `proposal.en.md`.
