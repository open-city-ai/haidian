Transport & Slow Mobility

A "main handoff-rail greenway + three-station connection + pedestrian stitching axes" composite network: the main handoff-rail greenway runs about 15.6 km north–south; three rail connections at Wudaokou, Qinghua East Road West, and Dazhongsi stations; pedestrian and cycleway axes stitch campus, park, and block within key areas.

Blue-Green Network & Public Space

Using the Jingzhang Heritage Park vitality belt as the spine, coordinating Qinghe, Xiaoyuehe, universities, enterprises, and community mobility into a north–south-through, east–west-connected path, cycleway, and green-space system; the public-space component library integrates with pilgrimage landmarks.
