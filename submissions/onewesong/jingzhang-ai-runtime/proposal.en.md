# Jing-Zhang AI Runtime

> **The City as an AI Runtime**

A century ago, the Jing-Zhang Railway moved people, goods and resources into the modern city. Today, the AI Urban Runtime brings intelligence into the city while giving the city the ability to test, observe, approve, suspend and roll back AI.

## Core Idea

The proposal treats the innovation belt as a runtime environment rather than an AI showcase. Every AI scenario follows Proposal → Simulation → Sandbox → Human Validation → Production → Observability → Feedback.

The spatial framework is **one core, one loop, three environments, two planes and multiple sandboxes**:

- **Sandbox / 众智园**: controlled AI experimentation, robotics, autonomous mobility, infrastructure and prototypes.
- **Staging / AI 原点社区**: real human validation with residents, students, workers and visitors.
- **Production / 大钟寺**: real-world AI commerce, services, offices, tourism and civic agents.
- **Control Plane / 中关村科技服务翼**: policy, standards, capital, IP, talent, open source and compute.
- **Experience Plane / 小月河场景赋能翼**: healthcare, education, mobility, retail, culture and public services.

## Urban Versioning and Rollback

Every AI scenario has a version. A robot delivery system may progress from v0.1 Sandbox to v0.3 Staging and finally v1.0 Production. Each release keeps its change, evidence, metrics, feedback and decision records.

Every scenario must retain a non-AI path, human override, exit condition and rollback plan. AI failure should disable the AI overlay without disabling ordinary urban life. Recovery nodes, human service desks and non-AI accessibility paths become urban resilience infrastructure.

## Three Key Areas

**Sandbox — 众智园:** Build / Test / Break.

**Staging — AI 原点社区:** Meet / Experience / Validate.

**Production — 大钟寺:** Deploy / Operate / Scale.

The Jing-Zhang heritage public-space system forms the Runtime Spine and closes the continuous urban feedback loop.

## Flagship AI Scenarios

Robot Delivery Street; Autonomous Mobility Sandbox; AI Health Navigator; AI Education Commons; AI Retail District; AI Civic Agent. The formal package will expand these into at least ten complete scenario cards with spatial, user, data, privacy, human-review, operator and risk mappings.

## Urban Metrics

The proposal introduces four conceptual indicators: **AI Reversibility Index**, **Urban AI Permission Index**, **Human Baseline Availability**, and **Runtime Cycle Time**. These are design indicators, not statutory planning values, and must be formally sourced and recalculated before final professional submission.

## Implementation

Phase 1 builds the runtime; Phase 2 stages the city; Phase 3 scales the network. Annual activities can include Jing-Zhang AI Runtime Week, Developer Walk, Open City Demo Day, AI Urban Design Review and community experience days. All implementation, policy, funding and operational arrangements remain conceptual recommendations until formally confirmed.

> **The city is not a showcase for AI. It is the runtime environment in which AI earns the right to operate.**
