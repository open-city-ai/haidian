JING-ZHANG AI RUNTIME

The City as an AI Runtime

This version uses maintainer-defined provisional geometry. It is not a statutory redline, approval basis, or precise area statement. Recalculation is required after official geometry is released.

Concept

The Jing-Zhang heritage public space becomes the Runtime Spine, connecting Sandbox, Staging and Production states through a continuous urban delivery loop.

Key Areas

Zhongzhiyuan is the experiment and testing environment; Beijing AI Origin Community is the human validation environment; Dazhongsi is the production AI industry and public-service environment.

Scenarios

The proposal covers robot delivery, autonomous mobility, AI health navigation, AI education, AI retail, civic agents, AI tourism, digital-twin review, developer mobility labs and a community AI help desk.

Safety and rollback

Every AI scenario requires a non-AI path, human override, exit condition and rollback plan. AI failure must not disable ordinary urban functions.

Metrics

The AI Reversibility Index, Urban AI Permission Index, Human Baseline Availability and Runtime Cycle Time are conceptual/proposed metrics and do not represent statutory planning values.
