# 京张补给线 / JING-ZHANG CIVIC SUPPLY LINE

## 中文摘要

“京张补给线：让 AI 创新先守住城市日常”提出“一线六点两翼一账本”的城市设计概念。它承诺：无 App、断网或停电、极端天气、夜间价格波动时，一杯水、一顿饭、一个座位与一条安全回家路仍可获得。每个 AI 试点必须公开责任人、人工替代、停止、召回、退出和事故处置。

三处重点区各设两个互补微型补给点：众智园以“验证补给站 / 召回接管站”验证受控冷链、低速配送、人工接管和召回；北京 AI 原点社区以“共厨照护站 / 公共转介站”组织社区共厨、照护餐、公共服务转介和青年服务驻留；大钟寺以“夜间补给站 / 安全归途站”连接基本商品、小商户界面与站城归途。中关村科技服务翼概念性提供合规、保险和供应链支持，小月河场景赋能翼提供蓝绿降温、雨洪和应急补给支撑。

方案包括六个定性全球案例、十二张场景卡（其中三张为产业测试验证）、七类用户画像、四个朝圣地标、RACI 责任矩阵、资源与授权闸门、六项负例桌演、AI-OFF 基本服务和三阶段可退出路径。完整证据和复算以 GeoJSON、`metrics.json`、来源、假设和三个矩阵为准。

所有空间、规模、活动、项目和运营安排均为待专业深化的概念建议，不代表法定规划、政府批准、投资承诺、工程可行性或地块拆改结论。当前临时边界只用于概念生成、讨论和复算；获得可信官方数据后必须整体更新图层、指标、图件和中英文报告。

## English Abstract

“JING-ZHANG CIVIC SUPPLY LINE: AI Innovation That Keeps Daily Life Within Reach” proposes a One Line, Six Points, Two Wings, One Ledger urban-design concept. It commits that without an app, during a network or power outage, in extreme weather, or amid nighttime price volatility, a cup of water, a meal, a seat, and a safe way home will remain within reach. Every AI pilot must publish its accountable owner, human alternative, stop, recall, exit, and incident response.

Each of the three key areas has two complementary micro-supply points: Zhongzhiyuan's Validation Supply Point / Recall and Takeover Point validate controlled cold chain, low-speed delivery, human takeover, and recall; Beijing AI Origin Community's Community Kitchen and Care Point / Public Referral Point organize the community kitchen, care meals, public-service referral, and youth service residency; Dazhongsi's Night Supply Point / Safe-Way-Home Point connect essential goods, small-merchant interfaces, and the station-city journey home. The Zhongguancun Technology Services Wing conceptually supplies compliance, insurance, and supply-chain support, while the Xiaoyue River Scenario Enablement Wing supports blue-green cooling, stormwater, and emergency supply.

The proposal contains six qualitative global cases, twelve scenario cards including three industry tests, seven user personas, four pilgrimage landmarks, a RACI matrix, resource and authorization gates, six negative tabletop exercises, AI-OFF basic services, and a three-phase exitable path. GeoJSON, `metrics.json`, sources, assumptions, and the three matrices remain the authority for complete evidence and recomputation.

Every spatial, scale, activity, project, and operating arrangement is a conceptual recommendation pending professional deepening. None is a statutory plan, government approval, investment commitment, engineering-feasibility conclusion, or parcel-level demolition decision. Current provisional boundaries support only concept generation, discussion, and recomputation; once trusted official data is available, layers, metrics, figures, and both language reports must be updated together.
