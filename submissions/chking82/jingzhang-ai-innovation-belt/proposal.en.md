# Centennial Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt: Urban Design Scheme

## Overview of the Proposal

In 1909, the Jing-Zhang Railway overcame the steep climb of Guan-Gou Pass with the "person-shaped" line at Qinglong Bridge, becoming China's first independently designed and constructed mainline railway—this was the first time Chinese engineers used "wisdom" to conquer geographical obstacles. Today, Haidian is once again "climbing" with full-stack AI self-innovation. This plan reads the railway tracks as data streams, and AI computing power and developers as "nodes," proposing the overall concept of **Jing-Zhang Intelligence Vein (Jing-Zhang Intelligence Vein, abbreviated as JZ·AI)**: with the Jing-Zhang Heritage Park as the main axis of history and Public Space, and the Zhongzhiyuan, Beijing AI Origin Community, and Dazhongsi as three key areas for innovation, transforming the "century-old railway vein" into the "AI innovation vein."

**One Sentence**: Read the Jing-Zhang Heritage Park as a "Wisdom Pulse" — the climbing wisdom of the zigzag railway in 1909, corresponding to today's full-stack independent innovation climb in AI, organized by a north-south Public Space main axis to form a world-class AI innovation belt.

**Five Key Judgments**:
Its first, the **"One Belt, Three Cores, Multiple Points, Scenarios, Blue-Green Slow Travel Composite Ring" structure** — the Smart Vein Axis (Jing-Zhang Heritage Park Vitality Belt) connects three key areas (Smart Vein · Zhiyi Station / Smart Vein · Origin Station / Smart Vein · Big Clock Station), with the Zhongguancun Service Wing and Xiao Yuehe Scenario Wing forming a ring; its second, the **"Smart Vein One-Day Line" public experience route** links 10 AI scenarios and 3 holy sites, making the plan walkable, viewable, and participatory; its third, **naming as a system** — "Jing-Zhang Smart Vein" establishes a three-tier naming system from districts, nodes to events, with the logo theme extending from the dual railway lines and data nodes to guide signs, activities, and honors; its fourth, **full-package machine-readable and "solution as pipeline"** — 11 land units and over 30 verifiable indicators are produced by a deterministic pipeline, allowing re-running and evolution with official data in place; its fifth, the **"Smart Vein" AI Innovation Index Five-Dimensional Framework** supports long-term operations and the accumulation of brand assets.

## Coordinated Research Area: Industry and Future City Research

The core task of the Coordinated Research Area is to build a world-class AI Innovation Ecosystem. This plan follows the innovation chain of "university-originated innovation - open-source collaboration - enterprise transformation - public experience - international dissemination." It involves sorting out the resources of Haidian universities and research institutions, leading enterprises, computational power, algorithms, and data elements, as well as incubation platforms, listed companies, unicorns, and technology service resources. It proposes a spatial coordination framework for the AI innovation chain, industrial chain, talent chain, and urban service chain. The naming and logo direction serve the overall recognizability of the "Centennial Jing-Zhang Cultural Belt, Urban AI Life Experience Belt, and AI Convergence Innovation Belt."

### Five Functional Zones and the Synergistic Loop of Three Zones and Two Wings (agent.1/agent.2)

Five Functional Areas——Full-Stack Independent AI Innovation System, World-Class AI Innovation Ecosystem, AI-Enabled Scenario Empowerment New Paradigm, Intelligent AI Vibrant City, AI Governance Global Discourse Power——In the framework of the Three Zones and Two Wings, the collaborative loop is as follows:

1. The **Zhongzhiyuan AI Independent Innovation Acceleration Area** undertakes "full-stack autonomy + governance voice": the national artificial intelligence platform, standard setting, security governance, and full-stack technology validation.
2. **Beijing AI Origin Community** undertakes "proximity school innovation source + open-source ecosystem": university technology transfer, open-source collaboration, talent special zone, and brand events.
3. **Dazhongsi AI Industry Agglomeration Zone** undertakes "Intelligent Nativized New Business Forms + International Exchange and Cooperation": leading enterprises, intelligent bodies, intelligent terminals, content consumption, data elements, and international roadshows.
4. **Zhongguancun Technology Services Wing** provides global configuration of elements and empowerment through Zhongguancun IP and capital.
5. The **Xiaoyue River Scenario Enablement Wing** provides an AI Scenario Access test field and an intelligent and vibrant urban interface.

The collaboration of the Three Zones and Two Wings is not a geographical tripartite puzzle, but a functional division of labor on the innovation chain: Conceptualization (Origin Community) → Validation (Zhongzhiyuan) → Transformation (Dazhongsi) → Services (Zhongguancun Wing) → Experiments (Xiaoyuehe Wing), forming a closed loop.
