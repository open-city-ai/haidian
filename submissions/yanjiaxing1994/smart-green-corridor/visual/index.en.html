Zhongzhi Park — AI Autonomous Innovation Accelerator

Garden-style autonomous innovation district. Retains all 5 R&D buildings (Xuebeiyuan), activates ground-floor grey spaces, adds rooftop sports and sunken plaza youth center, with riverside slow-traffic system along the Qing River. Features junior pump-track skatepark and pet-friendly zone.

Wudaokou — Beijing AI Origin Community

University-adjacent technology transfer district. Leverages Tsinghua Science Park, Zhiyuan Tower, and Dongsheng Tower along a 1.3 km Jing-Zhang Heritage Park segment. Adds 1.3 ha youth activity lawn, street skatepark, and Railway Memory Innovation Interactive Hall.

Dazhong Temple — AI Industry Cluster

Urban intelligent economy district. Fully retains the Sports Park and Shunxinyuan green space. Features bowl skatepark, Ancient Bell Cultural驿站, cycling loop, and four-quadrant intersection connectivity. Integrates data-asset theater and low-carbon edge-computing nodes.
