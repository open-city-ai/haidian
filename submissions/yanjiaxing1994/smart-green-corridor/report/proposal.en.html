Core mechanism — a closed "Micro-Renewal → Strong Links → Operations Activation" loop. This is not a one-off physical makeover but a self-sustaining renewal mechanism: (1) Micro-renewal — low-cost, low-risk interventions (ground-floor gray-space, slow-traffic connections, pocket facilities, lightweight single-story structures) that avoid the capital, approval, and social cost of demolition-and-rebuild (all 19 projects are low-intervention on retained assets, §7.1-7.3); (2) Strong links — the 9km "Three Paths, One Green" corridor physically ties the three districts, campuses, parks, and transit stations into one network, while AI scenario data flows (fitness, events, footfall, energy) add a "digital link" across districts (§7.6 RACI cross-agency coordination); (3) Operations activation — sustained use via ≥8 monthly events, the three-district league system, open-source community, and brand IP, with operating revenue (60/30/10 government/social/campus-enterprise) reinvested in maintenance (§7.7) and usage/satisfaction feeding the next round of updates — a use→feedback→iterate loop.

Differentiated claim. Unlike demolition-and-rebuild renewal (capital-heavy, slow, socially disruptive) or pure landscape beautification (not operable, not measurable, decays after one investment), this proposal is a "light-asset, operable, measurable, linear AI-ecosystem renewal": it unfolds the AI innovation chain (source→transfer→open source→experience) along the 9km railway heritage corridor as a linear AI innovation ecosystem — distinct from point-district or new-town models (consistent with the global case-study conclusion) — and every spatial intervention is bound to an operation mechanism and measurable indicators (cost framework, RACI, DPIA), keeping the concept→design→operation path continuously transformable.

C: Urban Intelligence Traffic Optimization Experiment (Wudaokou-Dazhongsi segment, 10 checkpoints + 5 sensors): Aggregated sensor data for crowd management and facility maintenance optimization. Target: <2hr fault response, 24hr advance holiday plans. Success criteria include a ≥20% congestion-index reduction in slow-traffic peak hours and a >40% navigation-recommendation adoption rate, with all algorithms published open-source / as technical white papers and a full privacy impact assessment completed before any personal-data mechanism (e.g., WiFi probe) is enabled. Landmark System and Public-Space Component Library

Three core landmarks along the 9km greenway anchor the "lookout tower – digital sculpture – cultural bell tower" sequence.

Landmark 1 — Qinghe North Lookout (Zhongzhi Park, 30m, light steel + spiral stairs + 8m platform): 360° view of the northern greenway, Qinghe wetland, Western Hills skyline, and Olympic Tower; mid-level (15-20m) circular data-visualization LED ring of daily carbon, renewables ratio, and AI innovation indices (30-min refresh); base (0-5m) café + info center (~80 m²). Night: low-power LED (Jing-Zhang Blue), IDA dark-sky compliant, ring dims to 30% at night, off after 22:00 except aviation obstruction light.

Landmark 2 — Open Source Light (Wudaokou, 12m LED matrix cube, 4m³, 4,096 controllable pixels over shallow pool): real-time GitHub trending / Haidian project contribution data (15-min refresh, public API); community-contributed content (reviewed before display); "Open Source Moment" — full-matrix 1Hz synchronous flash at 20:00 nightly. Night: adaptive luminance, fiber-optic starfield (~200 points) in the pool floor, off after 22:00; directional shields limit light spill <30°.

Landmark 3 — Bell Reimagined (Dazhongsi, 18m Corten-steel bell tower, abstracted bianzhong section): newly cast bell (1:5 scale of the Yongle Bell, ~1.2t, C3), rung weekly by community representatives (rotation + draw); interactive projection of pentatonic bell tones triggered by Leap Motion; base open rest space (~80 m², 10 sleeper-timber bench sets). Night: warm 2700K narrow-beam lighting, projection to 21:00 (22:00 on bell-ring Sundays), safety lighting only after.

Component Library (five categories, standardized for 9km quality consistency and district differentiation):

Accessibility and Inclusive Design

Accessibility is a base parameter, not an add-on, following GB 50763-2012 and the Beijing 2022 Winter Paralympics accessibility best practices.

Slow-traffic: ramps ≤5% at all grade changes (no step-only routes), tactile warning paving at crossings; main ramps ≥1.8m with dual-height handrails (85/65cm) and braille start/end markers; non-slip surfaces, joint height difference ≤5mm, elastic polyurethane joint fillers.

Visually impaired: continuous tactile guide paving along the walking path, branched to all new public buildings; solar voice navigation posts every 500m (~18, button at 90cm) announcing location, direction/distance, and facilities, with open Bluetooth beacons (iOS/Android accessible apps); braille sub-plates on all interpretation/signage; tactile bell model (1:10 Yongle Bell) at the Bell Culture Station.

Sports facilities: wheelchair viewing platforms (≥8 m², slope ≤5%, width ≥1.5m, shading + accessible WC ≤50m) at all three skate parks; Dazhongsi court-side accessible viewing seats and ≥1 ITTF-compliant adaptive table-tennis table; Zhongzhiyuan rooftop wheelchair lift (GB 50763, ≥250kg, emergency stop + battery backup).

Children: Dazhongsi locomotive-themed climbing structure (1:2 JF1 replica, EN 1177 surfacing, height-graded 1.5/2.5/3.5m), Wudaokou natural-exploration lawn (log balance, bird-nest boxes, dragonfly/frog observation deck), water depths ≤30cm or invisible stainless grilles (gap ≤8cm), non-toxic/non-thorny/non-high-allergen planting per GB 51192-2016 Appendix B.

Elderly: low-impact hydraulic fitness equipment at Zhongzhiyuan with large-print + voice instructions; Qinghe riverside rest pavilions every 200m with handrails and 105° backrests; Dazhongsi "walk-first" lane and chess/card corners; one-button large-text mode (1.5×, WCAG AAA ≥7:1 contrast) on all smart fitness stations and navigation posts, ≤3-step touch operations.

Digital inclusion: offline/non-digital alternatives for every AI service (paper AR guide, staffed fitness hours 7:00-9:00 & 17:00-19:00, static direction signs, physical bulletin boards); all public digital interfaces default-support large-text mode; core signage bilingual (EN at 70% of CN size), AR adds Japanese/Korean options; PWA architecture for offline use, total page size ≤5MB first load, incremental ≤500KB.
