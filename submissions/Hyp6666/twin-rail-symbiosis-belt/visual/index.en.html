Twin-Rail Symbiosis System

The technical essence of the Jing-Zhang railway's two parallel rails is translated into the organizing logic of the AI-era city.

brand + spatial

Iron Rail

Carries a century of heritage: the Heritage Park, Qinghuayuan Station, Zhongguancun innovation culture.

  • Centennial Jing-Zhang Culture Belt
  • Culture display and public art
  • Brick & steel industrial-memory materials

Light Rail

Carries AI industrial innovation: computing, algorithms, data, talent, scenarios.

  • AI-Fusion Innovation Belt
  • Zhongzhiyuan–Origin–Dazhongsi cores
  • Glass, light steel, digital media

Sleepers

East-west slow-traffic corridors, blue-green public space, transit connections that weld history to future.

  • Urban AI Life Experience Belt
  • Station-city integration and slow stitching
  • Distributed energy and edge computing

Three Key Areas

A "three-rail" positioning: Acceleration Rail (Zhongzhiyuan), Origin Rail (AI Origin), Application Rail (Dazhongsi).

key_areas.geojson

Zhongzhiyuan · Acceleration Rail

Garden-style full-stack autonomy district carrying autonomy and global AI-governance voice.

  • National AI platform and standards
  • Qinghe Low-Carbon Gallery
  • Safety-governance sandbox and testbed

AI Origin Community · Origin Rail

University-adjacent transfer and talent community linking Tsinghua/PKU/CAS.

  • Campus-park-district slow stitching
  • Open-source hall and transfer street
  • Wudaokou station-city integration

Dazhongsi · Application Rail

Urban smart-economy and international-exchange district.

  • Agents and content-consumption businesses
  • Data-element reception hall
  • Dazhongsi four-quadrant connectivity
