Three Key Areas (Cores)

Wisdom Core · Zhongzhiyuan AI Acceleration Area

announced area (provisional boundary)：192.1 ha
Positioning
The bearer of the AI full-stack autonomous innovation system and global AI-governance voice — an “accelerator from model to product”.
Spatial structure
“One axis, two belts, three clusters”. Axis = north heritage park green belt; belts = west full-stack innovation (computing center, foundation-model R&D, data services) and east test & verification (low-altitude inspection/delivery corridor, robot scenario field); clusters = computing, full-stack R&D, ecological buffer.
AI scenarios
Low-altitude intelligent inspection & delivery corridor (industry test scenario), civic agent governance lab (AI governance)
Implementation risk
Computing-center energy use and municipal capacity need special assessment; low-altitude routes involve airspace management and sector permits — all pending; the scheme only sets conceptual direction.

Origin Core · Beijing AI Origin Community

announced area (provisional boundary)：104.3 ha
Positioning
A world-class AI innovation ecosystem and global developers' “pilgrimage site” — “where China's AI set off”.
Spatial structure
“Herringbone junction, three rings around”. The former Qinghuayuan Station area is the “人” junction (Origin Plaza); west ring = R&D west, east ring = open-source community & incubators, south ring = innovation retail & talent apartments.
AI scenarios
Low-speed autonomous shuttle loop (industry test scenario), century Jing-Zhang AR guide, developer home (24h open-source space)
Implementation risk
Heritage-protection scope and building-renewal methods require heritage-authority confirmation; Origin Plaza involves site display and subway safety, needing special study.

Bell Core · Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster

announced area (provisional boundary)：72.0 ha
Positioning
AI-native new business formats and rail-hub TOD — “where the bell of the AI industry rings”.
Spatial structure
“One station, two streets”. Dazhongsi rail hub is the core; west street = Dazhongsi commercial & AI retail experience, east street = AI business offices; the heritage park belt passes through; the station plaza and “Smart-Pulse Bell” form the cultural anchor.
AI scenarios
Rail-transfer MaaS navigation, open scenario market (industry matchmaking), and the start of the robot delivery corridor
Implementation risk
Commercial renewal involves extensive ownership coordination; TOD development needs rail-authority collaboration — both listed as pending items.
