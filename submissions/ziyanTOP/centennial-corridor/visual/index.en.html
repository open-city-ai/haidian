Three Zhi-Mai Cores · three key areas

The three cores respectively carry near-campus translation (Origin Core), garden-style full-stack autonomy (Zhongzhi Core), and intelligent-economy international exchange (Dazhong Core). They correspond to agent.2 / agent.3 / agent.4 tasks.

● Dazhong Core · Dazhongsi AI industry cluster · ~72.0 ha · intelligent economy, international exchange, four-quadrant connectivity
