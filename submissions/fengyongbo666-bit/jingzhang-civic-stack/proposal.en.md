# Jing-Zhang Urban Design Open Call: A Pluggable AI Public Life System on a Centennial Railway

> **A Century-Old Railway Bears Witness to the Next Generation Urban Agreement.** The proposal does not treat AI as a showcase device within the park, but rather as a pluggable, auditable, pausable, and human-takeoverable public life infrastructure. The railway memory serves as a shared coordinate, the city stack as an open interface, with human dignity as the highest privilege.

## Three-Level Scope Framework

Three layers are not three independent diagrams, but a "compilation chain" from strategic assumptions to street validation. Approximately 43.6 km² at the strategic research layer proposes innovation collaboration, talent circulation, and future urban interfaces in the Beijing-Tianjin-Hebei region; approximately 11.4 km² at the overall design layer compiles judgments into a public spine, three urban stacks, two wings of mixed weaving, and twelve scene ports; and 368.4 ha at the key area layer use architectural prototypes, Public Spaces, active travel, operations, and safety gates to test implementability. The three layers of work separately answer "why collaborate here," "how does space carry," and "how does a street and a scene get validated," managed by [depth:three_level_scope_framework] and [depth:overall_spatial_structure].

The spatial structure is named "**One Ridge·Three Viaducts·Two Wings·Twelve End Ports**". The one ridge is the Jing-Zhang Public Spine facing all people; the three viaducts, from north to south, are the Zhongzhiyuan "Model Annulus", the AI Origin Community "First Submission Station", and the Dazhongsi "Intelligent Body Forum"; the two wings are not closed-off parks but rather mixed blocks of residential care and research and development transformation; the twelve end ports connect AI scenarios to real Public Spaces. The end ports follow the same open protocol: minimum data, clear purpose, Human Review, appealability, pausability, and exitability. The structure is anchored in [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-001], [data:geometry/roads.geojson#RD-001], and [data:geometry/constraints.geojson#S01].

## Coordinated Research Area: Industry and Future City Research

The overarching layer proposes "AI will no longer be confined to industrial parks, with innovation spreading through public life." Haidian serves as the core interface for open-source foundations, model safety, research and development conversion, and talent density; the Future Science City can host interdisciplinary validation in life sciences and energy; the Huairou Science City connects major scientific facilities with research data governance; the Beijing Economic-Technological Development Area links embodied intelligence, manufacturing validation, and scaled scenarios; and the Tianjin and Hebei nodes connect manufacturing supply chains, port logistics, and regional applications. Here, the focus is on collaborative interfaces and validation issues, without making any policy commitments for any region. Each cross-regional project must have defined responsibility entities, data boundaries, and exit conditions, and cannot merely draw a "collaborative arrow."

Six international cases provide mechanisms for reference: [source:CASE-ONE-NORTH] inspiring research, entrepreneurship, living, and proximity to Public Space; [source:CASE-22BARCELONA] inspiring industrial renewal and mixed-use districts concurrently; [source:CASE-HELSINKI-TESTBED] inspiring small-scale testing—feedback—iteration in real urban environments; [source:CASE-PARIS-SACLAY] inspiring the interface between research and technology transfer; [source:CASE-KENDALL-SQUARE] reminding of the need to compensate for the public realm, housing, and accessibility in high-density innovation clusters; [source:CASE-MARS-TORONTO] inspiring the organization of entrepreneurial services and cross-institutional networks by intermediary platforms. The migration results are not "copying parks," but six local rules: open ground floors, short-chain conversions, real testing, public feedback, affordable living, and third-party audits.

The future city form is composed of five stacked layers: the Heritage Layer preserves shared memories; the Public Domain Layer provides basic rights that are independent of devices; the Urban Computing Layer provides minimal, tiered-authorized computing power and data; the Scenario Port Layer allows for application plugging and unplugging; and the Governance Layer holds the rights to pause, appeal, and exit. As a result, industrial spaces do not pursue a single landmark but aim for a short closed-loop from "university/ laboratory—safe testing—prototyping services—public scenarios—feedback review." The annual output of the coordinating layer is case updates, a list of collaboration interfaces, talent and scenario gaps, and regional trial reviews, without replacing urban quality evaluation with investment attraction figures.

## Detailed Design of Key Areas

Three key areas use the same public protocol but with different primary tasks, forming a "space-scenario-operation-gate" quadruple in [depth:three_key_area_detailed_design]. The "Model Year Ring" at Zhongzhiyuan in the northern area focuses on safety testing and open-source evaluation: it is centered around the Shared Experiment Institute, a low-speed embodied intelligence loop, a model red team courtyard, and an ecological buffer zone. If industrial testing exceeds its authority, leaks, or if the safety officer is unreachable, it must be physically or logically isolated. Its entrance is not a corporate lobby but a publicly visible testing explanation, version record, and complaint window.

The middle AI Origin community "First Submission Station" is a translator that converts outcomes from campuses to cities. The space consists of an open-source street, a micro-prototype shared layer, a community service co-ordination desk, a talent daily service station, and a railway memory station; the first floor is open to the public during designated hours, and research outcomes must undergo safety, ethics, accessibility, and community value reviews before entering the real world. The building prototype retains small-scale courtyards and circulation relationships without sacrificing management efficiency by closing off large courtyards.

The southern Dazhongsi "Intelligent Body Forum" places intelligent body economy and public deliberation in the same venue: creators, small businesses, residents, and urban operators can submit scenario proposals, review model cards, initiate appeals, and collectively review outcomes. The forum retains paper announcements, telephone, and manned booths to ensure that those without smartphones, difficulty reading, or unwilling to share data can still access equal public services. Three landmarks—the first submission station, the model annulus, and the Intelligent Body Forum—are original concepts of this scheme, serving as a "sanctuary" to honor contributors rather than for commercial sponsorship.

![Detailed design for three key areas: Model Annulus, First Submission Station, and the Spatial, Scenic, and Phased Gates of the Agent Assembly.](assets/figures/key-areas.png)

All buildings, pathways, and scene nodes within the focus area must still be calibrated using on-site measurements and evidence in eight categories: ownership, fire safety, accessibility, traffic, utility lines, cultural heritage protection, and public participation. The initial segment should be validated as a minimum unit of "800 meters of a continuous public spine + one device-free service kiosk + one AI scenario stop." If any category of safety or rights evidence is missing, the pilot should be scaled back or withdrawn, rather than being covered by design renderings.

## Land Use, Building Scale, and Retain-Renovate-Demolish Strategy

The land use adopts eight design codes: research and development 0802, residential 0701, commercial services 05, culture 0803, community services 0702, roads 1207, parks 1401, and squares 1403, with coding based on [source:LAND-USE-CLASSIFICATION]. The land use expression reflects the structural allocation, not the statutory nature; each element retains its area, design logic, and `conceptual_non_statutory` status in [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-001]. The spine does not form a single "technology office wall," but rather through the interweaving of residential, care, cultural, and research and development uses, it enables work, study, care, and daily consumption to occur within a walkable scale.

The building layer includes [metric:building_prototype_count] prototype footprints, with a geometric union of [metric:building_footprint_area_sqm]. This value only describes the area occupied by the scheme prototypes in three key areas, and does not represent the total existing building stock, total gross floor area, or the statutory Building Coverage Ratio. Prototypes are categorized as shared experiments, incubation, mixed-use, community services, cultural, talent residences, and "under investigation for potential retention"; all height fields are explicitly unknown. Volume control is intended to be a "continuous but penetrable low to mid-rise base + node setbacks + roof public/ecological fifth facade," with the final height subject to comprehensive review of solar access, landscape, cultural heritage, aviation, and fire safety.

Preserve the Demolish–Renovate–Retain Strategy execution checklist: this must include evidence cards for structural safety, built era and historical value, full lifecycle carbon, current users and lease agreements, property rights, fire safety and accessibility, renovation costs, and public opinion. Only when all eight criteria are met can the buildings be classified. Preservation should be the priority, followed by restoration, with replacement as the last option; construction should not proceed if there are no acceptable solutions for relocation, rent, and business continuity. The current gross floor area, demolition area, and number of title-confirmed parcels remain unknown, respectively constrained by A-EXISTING-001 and A-CONTROLS-001 to avoid the common misinterpretation of "conceptual model as demolition decision."

## Transport, Rail, Municipal Infrastructure, and Public Services

The traffic structure is "a continuous pedestrian/bicycle main chain + six lateral interfaces + three stack nodes connections." [data:geometry/roads.geojson#RD-001] is the conceptual central line, with a total length of [metric:conceptual_road_length_m], not claiming to replicate the existing track or road right-of-way. The main chain prioritizes pedestrian, cycling, wheelchair, and emergency passage; the lateral interfaces test track connections, school/community crossings, industrial logistics, and side street micro-circulation. Parking strategies focus on shared, staggered, and peripheral transfer approaches, but the number of parking spaces, station coverage, and road grades must be determined after analyzing traffic volumes, station entrances, fire safety, and ownership data. [depth:traffic_rail_slow_parking] manages this data gap.

![Traffic Slow Zones and Dual Networks of Blue-Green Public Spaces: Public Spine, Horizontal Interfaces, Cooling Pockets, and Key Nodes](assets/figures/mobility-bluegreen.png)

New municipal infrastructure follows the principles of "periphery priority, minimal collection, operational independence, and manual takeover": scene ports are reserved only for power, communication, sensing, and service interfaces; computational power and data are partitioned into public/internal/sensitive zones, with sensitive data not flowing beyond the spatial display. Since there are no baseline measurements for underground networks, power supply, reclaimed water, flood management, and computational capacity, [depth:municipal_new_infrastructure] does not provide capacity conclusions. Instead, a five-step verification process is executed: load forecasting, redundancy assessment, operational responsibility definition, and emergency drill.

Public services adopt the minimum standard of "one person in addition to a single device." Each co-located station provides a screen, telephone, paper guide, and staff; automated suggestions must display sources, versions, confidence levels, and appeal entries; medical, legal, emergency, minor safety, and child welfare matters are directed to professional human processes. The capacity of public services, service radius, and population demand will be calculated after population and facility inventories; this plan initially uses three types of journey validations: a wheelchair user transitioning from the transit station to public services, an individual without a smartphone completing a single transaction, and a caregiver finding a safe place to rest and seek assistance at night.

## Metrics, Area Recalculation, and Compliance Matrix

Indicators are divided into three categories: announcement magnitude, provisional boundary recalculation, and contestant design counting. The announcement magnitude retains its original unit and cites the source; geometric indicators are uniformly calculated in EPSG:4548. Counting indicators are derived from file elements or document numbering. ## Risk, Copyright, and Compliance

Main risks are managed according to the "trigger-responsibility-action-evidence" framework. Spatial disputes: where official boundaries or ownership conflict with temporary scopes, A is the overall control for the plan, and the action is to freeze related permanent works and fully recalculate; AI safety: where there is overreach, leakage, deviation, or automatic high-risk decision-making, A is the scene operator, and the action is to isolate, transfer to manual processing, notify affected parties, and conduct an independent review; Fair Inclusion: where the success rate for device users decreases, A is the public service department, and the action is to revert to manual or paper-based processes and rectify the procedures; Public Acceptance: where continuous complaints arise due to noise, events, or tests, A is the local operator, and the action is to reduce hours, reduce areas, or exit; Operations and Maintenance: where budgets and responsibilities are not locked, A is the project owner, and the action is to not proceed to the next phase. (Official Boundary)

Data governance is implemented through purpose limitation, minimum necessity, tiered authorization, short-term retention, auditability, deletability, and deviation auditing and event notification. Residents do not lose access to public services if they refuse to provide non-essential data; appeals are handled by a clear human point of contact, preventing users from repeating the same argument with the same model. Higher thresholds are applied to data related to children, health, law, assistance, and public safety; third parties are not allowed to re-use experimental data for advertising profiles. Necessary audit evidence is retained after scenario suspension, while other data is deleted according to the published retention period.
