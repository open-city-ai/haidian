## Three-Level Scope Framework

The proposal is organized according to the three-tier scope determined in the announcement [standard:PROJECT-OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT]:

- **Coordinated Research Area**: Approximately 43.6 square kilometers, bounded by the North Fifth Ring Road to the north, Jingzhang Expressway to the east, Xizhimenwai Avenue to the south, and Wancuihe Road to the west. The work goal is the strategic study of the AI Innovation Ecosystem, supply chain coordination, and the future urban form [metric:research_area_sqm].
- **Overall Design Area**: Approximately 11.4 square kilometers, with the overall design scope focusing on the urban and industrial areas within 1-2 kilometers around the Jing-Zhang Heritage Park, achieving the depth of Regulatory Detailed Planning. This will form the overall framework for Urban Renewal, Land-Use Layout, traffic and municipal infrastructure support, and aesthetic control [metric:site_area_sqm] [depth:three_level_scope_framework]. (Urban Design)
- **Key-Area Detailed Design Area**: A total of 368.4 hectares, extending from north to south as follows: Zhongzhiyuan AI Independent Innovation Acceleration Area (approximately 192.1 hectares), Beijing AI Origin Community (approximately 104.3 hectares), and Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster Area (approximately 72.0 hectares), reaching the depth of Urban Design as specified in the Integrated Planning Implementation Plan [metric:key_area_count] [metric:key_areas_total_sqm] [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-001] [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-002] [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-003].

Three levels of scope are progressively implemented: comprehensive research and determination of ecological and functional strategies, overall design translating these strategies into land use structure, green spine system, and update framework [depth:overall_spatial_structure], and key areas detailing the framework into buildings, Public Spaces, and implementation projects [depth:three_key_area_detailed_design]. The sources, derivation methods, and applicable limitations of the provisional boundaries are fully annotated in the `provisional_boundaries.geojson` attributes; layers that need to be recalculated after replacing the official boundaries are all nine design layers and all indicators, with the recalculation process consistent with the current generation process (recalculating areas under the EPSG:4548 projection). ## Coordinated Research Area: Industry and Future City Research

**Overall Concept and Naming System (In Response to agent.1)**: This plan innovatively names the corridor as **"Jing-Zhang Smart Corridor" (Jing-Zhang Smart Track)**, playing on the dual meaning of "tracks of the century-old Jing-Zhang Railway" and "tracks of the intelligent era": a century ago, the Jing-Zhang Railway was the first major railway line designed by Chinese people; a century later, this corridor becomes the first "smart track" where global intelligent entities participate in urban co-creation. The naming system is "one belt, one spine, three zones, two wings, and multiple nodes": **Jing-Zhang Smart Track Innovation Belt** (overall brand); **Jing-Zhang Smart Track Green Spine** (the linear Public Space framework of the heritage park); **Zhongzhiyuan Acceleration Zone, AI Origin Community, Dazhongsi Aggregation Zone** (three zones); **Zhongguancun Technology Services Wing, Xiaoyue River Scenario Enablement Wing** (two wings); 12 scene nodes (multiple nodes). Visual identity direction: The logo abstracts the double tracks of the Jing-Zhang Railway as two parallel evolving "tracks" that intersect into an open loop at the site of the old Qinghua Garden Railway Station, symbolizing "from self-constructed to Open Co-Creation"; the main color is Jing-Zhang Qing (railway heritage green) and Jing-Zhang Blue (AI technology blue), with the auxiliary color derived from the light green of the Qinghe-Xiaoyue River water system. The above is the Conceptual Recommendation; naming, branding, and trademark usage must be professionally cleared and approved [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK].

**Global AI Innovation Ecosystem Case Examples (In Response to agent.2, 6 Examples)**:

1. **King's Cross Area in London** —— Benchmark for Updating Railway Heritage into a Knowledge-Based Innovation District: With the railway heritage serving as a Public Space anchor, it is co-built by the university (UCL) + corporate headquarters + public realm. Transferable experience: Railway heritage anchor + spillover of university resources, with high similarity to the segment of Tsinghua University and Beihang University in the King's Cross area.
2. **Seaport District in Boston**——Public realm prioritized, mixed lab and headquarters layout. Transferable experience: investing in Public Space as a prerequisite for supplying industrial land.
3. **Singapore one-north**——research institutions, industries, and green corridors interweave at the neighborhood scale, promoting a "work–live–learn" mix. Transformable experience: use continuous green corridors to organize neighborhood-scale innovative districts.
4. **Toronto Quayside (Terminated)**——Negative Case: Unclear governance boundaries between urban data collection and platformized operations led to a collapse in public trust. Transformative Experience: All scenario cards in this plan include privacy boundaries and Human Review clauses, rejecting the default logic of "data for services."
5. **Barcelona 22@**——New Type of Innovation District + Reallocated Public Space in Superblocks. Transferable Experience: Release innovation space through pedestrianization and micro-updates in existing urban areas rather than extensive demolition and reconstruction.
6. **The Grand West Lake Sci-Tech Innovation Corridor** —— governance and ecological organization experience for a strip-shaped innovation corridor. Translatable experience: organize the flow of cross-park elements with a "Grand Corridor + Multiple Platforms" approach, corresponding to the "Three Zones and Two Wings" synergistic loop in this corridor.

Ecological mechanism design supported by case studies: Four segments of the chain—basic research (universities and laboratories) —technology transfer (incubators and test scenarios) —industrial acceleration (Zhongzhiyuan full-stack system) —capital and global services (Zhongguancun Technology Services Wing). Each segment corresponds to a clear spatial carrier and scene interface [metric:ai_rd_land_sqm]. The ecological map and industry-space mapping are detailed in Chapters 6 and 7, corresponding to the items in the harmonious rule matrix.

**Judgment of Future Urban Form**: Intelligent cities are not "cities filled with sensors," but "cities with clear interfaces" — where spatial rules are readable (structured task book), contributions are traceable (honors system), and processes are verifiable (Evidence Chain). This aligns with the very structure of this call for submissions: transforming the Urban Design task into an open interface that agents can read is itself an experiment in the future governance form of cities [standard:PROJECT-AGENT-OPEN-CALL-TASKBOOK].

## Overall Design Area: Urban Renewal and Regulatory-Plan-Level Urban Design

The Overall Design Area is structured around "one spine connecting three zones and two wings extending along one belt" [depth:overall_spatial_structure]: the Jing-Zhang Tram Green Spine runs from the North Fifth Ring Road southward to Dazhongsi, connecting three key areas; the eastern and western sides are connected by three seam corridors that link the innovation belt with the university cluster along University Road and the small Yuehe living belt [data:geometry/roads.geojson#ROAD-002] [data:geometry/roads.geojson#ROAD-003] [data:geometry/roads.geojson#ROAD-004].

**Land Use Structure (In Response to Announcement 1.5(2))**: The entire area is divided into 13 land use units, fully covering the submitted boundary with no gaps or overlaps [metric:land_use_total_sqm] [depth:land_use_layout] [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-001]. Among these, the AI R&D land use (codes 0802/0803/0806) covers approximately 444.2 hectares, accounting for about 38.9% of the Overall Design Area [metric:ai_rd_land_sqm]; the Jing-Zhang Green Spine park green space (code 1401) forms a continuous open framework running north-south [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-013]; in the Dazhongsi section, smart indigenous commercial spaces (code 05) and track micro-centers (code 1207) are arranged to accommodate station-city integration functions. The land use classification follows the expression in the Land Use Classification Guide [standard:MNR-LAND-USE-CLASSIFICATION-GUIDE].

**Development Intensity and Building Height (To Be Confirmed)**: The Floor Area Ratio, Building Height, Building Coverage Ratio, etc., have not yet obtained official documents. This plan does not set specific numerical values but instead proposes directional recommendations: the first interface on both sides of the Green Spine should maintain a low and continuous urban interface, with innovative research and development functions concentrated around the rail transit stations. The height should gradually rise from the Green Spine to both sides. All numerical values will be re-evaluated after the official control plan is released [depth:development_intensity_controls] [depth:height_massing_character]. The scale of the buildings is expressed conceptually through the Building Footprint (10 representative footprints, with a total area of approximately 19.3 hectares), which does not serve as a conclusion for development volume at the plot level [metric:building_footprint_area_sqm] [data:geometry/buildings.geojson#BLDG-001].

**Urban Renewal Overall Framework**: The focus is on preservation and updating, with demolition and new construction serving as secondary measures. The space under the Academy Bridge Road and the existing industrial buildings along the route are listed as objects for retention and renovation [data:geometry/buildings.geojson#BLDG-008]; the inefficient commercial land in front of Dazhongsi Station is listed as an object for functional replacement; and the Green Spine area focuses on weaving Public Spaces. All classifications for the demolish–renovate–retain strategy are marked as Conceptual Recommendations [depth:retain_renovate_demolish]. (Demolish–Renovate–Retain Strategy)

**Innovative Indicator System**: See Chapter 11 for the indicator system. All indicators can be recalculated or annotated with their sources using GeoJSON [standard:MOHURD-CONTROL-DETAILED-PLANNING].

## Detailed Design of Key Areas

**Zhongzhiyuan AI Independent Innovation Acceleration Area (North Zone, approximately 192.1 hectares)** [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-001]: positioned as a spatial carrier for a full-stack independent innovation system. The spatial structure is defined as "one core and two clusters": Acceleration Plaza Core (open-source achievement exhibition corridor) + West Wing R&D Cluster + East Wing Computing and Data Infrastructure Cluster. Conceptual buildings include the Wisdom Source R&D Tower, a cluster of full-stack independent laboratories, and AI corporate headquarters courtyards [data:geometry/buildings.geojson#BLDG-001] [data:geometry/buildings.geojson#BLDG-002] [data:geometry/buildings.geojson#BLDG-009]. Public Spaces open towards the Green Spine from the Acceleration Plaza. Implementation risks: the current land use and ownership conditions of the North Zone are unclear, and the entire layout is directional design. Further refinement will be carried out after the official key area polygon and ownership data are released.

**Beijing AI Origin Community (Central Area, approximately 104.3 hectares)** [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-002]: positioned as the origin of a world-class AI Innovation Ecosystem — the intersection of developers, young talents, and universities. The spatial structure is "one square and one ring": AI Origin Square (core of the Honor Monument) + Developer Cycling Ring [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#PUBLIC-001] [data:geometry/roads.geojson#ROAD-006]. The primary functions include incubation, talent apartments, and AI+Education Future Schools [data:geometry/buildings.geojson#BLDG-003] [data:geometry/buildings.geojson#BLDG-004] [data:geometry/buildings.geojson#BLDG-010]. Provide dynamic commercial service land uses to energize the interface in the Wudaokou direction [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-006]. Implementation risks: Involves university land use boundaries, and the seam corridor is merely a conceptual intention.

**Dazhongsi AI Industry Agglomeration Zone (South Area, approximately 72.0 hectares)** [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-003]: positioned as a demonstration area for the integration of station and city with new smart native business models. The spatial structure is "one hub and one district": Dazhongsi Track Micro-Center + Smart Native Commercial District [data:geometry/buildings.geojson#BLDG-006] [data:geometry/buildings.geojson#BLDG-007]. The forecourt accommodates the endpoint of the North-South Green Spine and urban passenger flow [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#PUBLIC-003]. Implementation risks: The conditions for track integration require coordination with the rail operator, and this section is only conceptually reserved here.

The provisional boundaries for the three zones are rough rectangular approximations. All references to boundary locations, street relationships, and scale in the above conclusions are directional designs and should be refined after the official boundaries are replaced. ## AI Innovation Ecosystem, Personas, and AI+ Scenarios

**User Profiles (6 Categories)**: 1. AI Researchers (universities and laboratories, requiring reservable experimental spaces and computational interfaces); 2. Open Source Developers (needing places for showcasing, exchanging, and contributing to be remembered); 3. Young Talents/University Students (needing low-cost living and entrepreneurial services); 4. Community Residents (needing daily life that is not intruded upon by technology and channels for participation); 5. Enterprise Service Operators (needing scenario lists and compliance interfaces); 6. Urban Governance Managers (needing verifiable decision Evidence Chains).

**12 AI Scenario Cards** (among which SC-02, SC-04, SC-09, SC-12 are industrial Testing and Validation Scenarios, totaling 4 cards) [metric:scenario_node_count]:

- **SC-01 AI Commuter Assistant**: Dynamic guidance and congestion alerts for transit station connections. Space: segment from Dazhongsi Station to Wudao Kou Station; Data Boundary: anonymous aggregated passenger flow data only; Human Review: operational authorities review anomalies daily; Operating Entity: Conceptual Recommendation for a joint effort between the rail operator and the street.
- **SC-02 Unmanned Delivery Test Segment (Testing Validation)**: A low-speed unmanned delivery test corridor is set up in the southern segment of the Green Spine. It will be managed under temporary closure. Space: Green Spine segment near Dazhongsi [data:geometry/roads.geojson#ROAD-001]; Privacy Boundary: Camera data processed locally and not stored with facial recognition; Human Review: Safety officers present throughout.
- **SC-03 AI Park Health Station**: A kiosk along the Green Spine for self-service health monitoring and exercise recommendations. Data boundaries: user active authorization and delete as used.
- **SC-04 Intelligent Crosswalk and Adaptive Signal (Testing Validation)**: Safety enhancement testing for crosswalks at the Academy Road intersection. Data boundary: only trajectory detection, no identity recognition.
- **SC-05 Developer Services Co-Pilot**: One-stop service desk for government and legal affairs, as well as space reservations, tailored for resident teams (corresponding to the enterprise-service-copilot scenario library).
- **SC-06 Jing-Zhang Memory AI Guided Tour**: Historical narrative tour along the ruins park, content verified by humanities and history professionals and uploaded after review.
- **SC-07 Public Safety Coordination Dashboard**: Joint alert for high passenger volumes and extreme weather, with human commanders always in the loop (corresponding to public-safety-operations-review).
- **SC-08 AI Classroom and Future School**: An AI literacy space for primary and secondary schools within the Origin Community.
- **SC-09 Low-Area Inspection and Green Space Maintenance (Testing Validation)**: Testing of the whitelist for airspace and time periods for the green spine's green space inspection.
- **SC-10 Smart Parking and Slow Travel Scheduling**: Station-area non-motorized vehicle parking tidal scheduling.
- **SC-11 Community Aging Support AI Partner**: Home-based aging support services along the Xiaoyuehe Living Belt, with strict manual backup.
- **SC-12 Scenario Access Data Sandbox (Testing Validation)**: A desensitized city data mechanism for developers to test within a compliant sandbox is a core institutional innovation of this plan—scenario access is premised on clear data governance boundaries.

Each scene card maps to layers (spatial location), service targets, operational data, privacy boundaries, Human Review, operating entity, and risks. The scene—space—operation mapping is found in the task coverage section of `visual/index.html`. All of the above are Conceptual Recommendations and testing intentions and do not constitute approved operations.

## Blue-Green Network, Public Space, and Urban Character

**Blue-Green System**: The total area of green spaces and open spaces is approximately 376.2 hectares, with a green space ratio of about 33.0% [metric:green_space_area_sqm] [metric:green_ratio]. The structure is "one ridge and one corridor with multiple gardens": the Jing-Zhang Green Ridge (linear park), the Xiaoyue River West Bank Waterfront Green Corridor, three entrance parks, and multiple pocket parks [data:geometry/green_space.geojson#GREEN-001] [data:geometry/green_space.geojson#GREEN-002].

It is composed of three squares (AI Origin Square, Zhongzhiyuan Acceleration Square, Dazhongsi Station Square) and the developer's promenade [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#PUBLIC-001] [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#PUBLIC-004].

**AI Pilgrimage Landmarks and Honor Display System (Responding to agent.4,3 Landmarks)** [metric:ai_landmark_count]:

1. **AI Origin Stele (Origin Stele)**: Located at the AI Origin Square, this is the permanent memorial core of this call for submissions—contributor GitHub IDs and Agent names will be inscribed on the stele, with annual additions for outstanding contributions. The conceptual form of the stele: a segment of "broken and reconnected" rail, symbolizing the continuity of the Jing-Zhang Railway over a century.

**Cultural Narrative (In Response to agent.5)**: The overarching narrative is "Railways carrying the speed of China, corridors carrying intelligence." It unfolds in three progressive layers: Centennial Jing-Zhang (the spiritual origin of independent innovation) — Zhongzhiyuan (the knowledge economy and indigenous innovation) — AI Era (Open Co-Creation and global collaboration). The spatial carrier is the "Intelligent Jing-Zhang 9.6 km Cultural Tour Line," divided into three thematic segments from north to south: North segment "Path of Autonomy" (Zhongzhiyuan, showcasing a full-stack autonomous technology narrative), Middle segment "Night of the Origin" (AI Origin community, developer culture and university memories), and South segment "Gate of Openness" (Dazhongsi, a narrative of intelligent living facing the global audience). The sign system is modeled after railway mileposts, integrating visual syntax from code comments; international communication texts are produced in English simultaneously, aligning with bilingual websites and GitHub participation paths.

**Urban Character**: The overall tone is "Railway Memory + Technological Rationality" —— preserving heritage elements such as tracks, platforms, and signal facilities as anchors for Public Spaces, with new buildings maintaining an industrial feel through restrained volumes and materials like metal and exposed concrete; roof forms are encouraged to incorporate greenery and photovoltaics (directional suggestion).
