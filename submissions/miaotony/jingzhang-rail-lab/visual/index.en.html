JINGZHANG UTILITY
THE FOURTH UTILITY

A century ago, cities modernized by turning water, electricity, gas, and heat into utilities delivered to every door. Today every industry is racing to adopt AI — yet access still works like electricity in 1900: self-generation everywhere, no shared grid. Along the 9-km Jingzhang Railway Heritage Park, we plug intelligence into the city's utility network — building the Fourth Utility: on tap, metered by usage, accessible to everyone, with universal service.

In 1909 the Jingzhang Railway opened here — China's first independently designed and operated public-infrastructure-grade railway. From railway (moving people), to pipelines (moving energy), to JINGZHANG UTILITY (moving computed intelligence): utility-ization is this city's recurring pattern of modernization. [source: beijing.gov 2026-03]

02 / LAND USE · SPACE ACCOUNTS
32 PARCEL ACCOUNTS

Land-Use Zoning · 32 Parcel Accounts

What separates a utility from a pilot field is being meterable and settleable. The Space Ledger opens an "account" for every renewal parcel within the 11.4 km²: land-use code, hectares, demolish/retrofit/retain category, FAR-transfer quota, and rent gradient are all kept on the books. The "Land-use zoning" layer on the twin map above is the spatial placement of the 32 accounts — the central 320.4 ha green corridor (Jingzhang Railway Heritage Park – AI Experiment Line) is the largest public account.

Account Rules (Space Ledger summary)

  • Demolish / retrofit / retain: each account is tagged with a disposal strategy, aligned with the Heritage Park Phase II "merge / demolish / set back / infill" four-strategy framework. [source: bjhd 2025-03]
  • FAR transfer: floor area yielded by the green corridor is credited to adjacent accounts, with every transfer booked and auditable.
  • Rent gradient: low-rent long leases at the Source Plant incubate → market rents in the Metering District feed back, with the spread going into the universal-service fund.
  • Official anchor: the belt offers ~1,000,000 m² of available renewal floor area and nearly 10 million m² of industrial space. [source: Beijing Daily/kw 2026-04]

04 / THREE PLANTS · BLUE-GREEN
SOURCE — TRANSFORMER — METERING

Three Plant-Station Profiles (Detailed Design of Key Areas) · Blue-Green Public Space

Electricity starts at the power plant, is stepped up at the substation, and reaches homes metered through the service hall. Intelligence organizes the city along the same chain: Source Plant (Zhongzhiyuan, 192.1 ha) → Transformer (Beijing AI Origin Community, 104.3 ha) → Metering District (Dazhongsi, 72.0 ha), strung together by the 9-km green-corridor iDUCT, flanked by the Zhongguancun science-service wing and the Xiaoyue River scenario-enablement wing. [source: ghzrzyw 2026-05-09]

Blue-Green Public Space

Green and open space ratio 41.2%, public space ratio 14.6% (our geometry). The central green corridor is Heritage Park Phase II, 530,900 m² (Xizhimen–North Fifth Ring, 9 km; five clusters — Jingzhang Waterscape, Community Vitality, Jingzhang Heritage Memorial, Youth International Exchange, and Nature & Leisure — fully open by end of 2026; Phase I, 2.4 km / 16.8 ha, opened June 2023). The east wing connects to the Qinghe and Xiaoyue River blue-green corridors; the waterfront green accounts (LU-04 Xiaoyue River scenario wing, LU-10 Xueyuan Road seam park, LU-15 North Fifth Ring ecological interface) handle stormwater detention and reclaimed-water cooling intake for the iDUCT. Waste-heat recovery keeps the blue-green space "warm" even in winter: thermal-mist wetlands, snow-melt paths, heated pools — the utility's waste heat becomes the park's felt comfort. [source: bjhd 2025-03]

07 / SERVICE HALLS · STATION TOD
RAIL-INTEGRATED UTILITY HALLS

Service Halls · Rail Station Integration

Power companies have service halls; so does JINGZHANG UTILITY: transactions (connection / tariffs / billing), experience (showrooms for the ten services), and appeals (a review window for algorithmic decisions). The three Service Halls are stacked directly on Line 13 stations — one station per plant. The announcement names Dazhongsi Station's "four-quadrant pedestrian connection" and station-city integration at Wudaokou and Qinghuadongluxikou; this scheme upgrades them into utility interfaces.
