# JZ COMMON PORTS / 京张接口公地

## Design Basis and Source List

The proposal uses the official open-call announcement, the cleared Agent taskbook, the repository source registry, local standard snapshots, and provisional polygons. The polygons organize intake visualization only: they are not an official planning boundary, statutory control, engineering alignment, or ownership statement. The package therefore separates a human reading layer from geometry, metrics, matrices, assumptions, and self-check evidence. JZ COMMON PORTS is an open co-creation recommendation for professional teams to deepen, not an approved plan. ## Three-Level Scope Framework

At the 43.6-square-kilometre Coordinated Research Area, the work aligns the Three Zones and Two Wings through ecosystem and service mechanisms. At the 11.4-square-kilometre Overall Design Area, it converts those mechanisms into an urban-renewal, mobility, public-space, and service framework. At the 368.4-hectare Key-Area Detailed Design Area, it tests three differentiated common ports. The spatial idea is one heritage commons spine, three open ports, two resource-feedback wings, and twelve reversible interfaces. Every figure mutes the provisional outline and foregrounds design relationships. ## Coordinated Research Area: Industry and Future City Research

The belt brand is JZ COMMON PORTS. Its mark combines a railway switch with API braces to form {JZ}, using geometry rather than proprietary type or corporate marks. Seven comparison cases - Kendall Square, Station F, one-north, Maria 01, Vector Institute, Knowledge Quarter, and Shenzhen Nanshan - contribute mechanisms rather than copied forms: university-enterprise transfer, shared incubation, mixed research and daily life, adaptive reuse, talent networks, cultural-scientific collaboration, and dense innovation services. These become a transfer street, shared service counters, a fifteen-minute talent-support network, low-disturbance reuse, cross-institution mentors, joint curation, and transit-adjacent public interfaces. Case material is background only. [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK] [standard:PROJECT-AGENT-OPEN-CALL-TASKBOOK] [depth:overall_spatial_structure]

## Overall Design Area: Urban Renewal and Regulatory-Plan-Level Urban Design

The design organizes a low-contrast provisional boundary with a high-contrast public spine, transverse access links, three port callouts, and phased reversible projects. Land-use polygons remain a complete topology-safe partition, while buildings, roads, green space, public space, and phasing remain separately auditable. The concept prioritizes reuse, retrofit, and reversible insertion before new construction. Floor area ratio, building height, density, road redlines, utilities, fire access, heritage controls, and ownership remain pending official data. No numerical development control is invented. [standard:MOHURD-CONTROL-DETAILED-PLANNING] [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-001] [depth:development_intensity_controls]

## Detailed Design of Key Areas

Zhongzhiyuan becomes the Validation Port: a garden-like interface for full-stack testing, safety governance, standards workshops, and a Qinghe-facing low-carbon commons. Beijing AI Origin Community becomes the Open-Source Port: a near-campus transfer street, release room, mentor network, and everyday talent services connected by walking and cycling. Dazhongsi becomes the Living Port: transit-integrated intelligent retail, international exchange, content and terminal demonstrations, and a four-quadrant pedestrian repair concept. Each is a directional scheme inside a provisional polygon and requires ownership, traffic, utilities, heritage, safety, and accessibility review. ## AI Innovation Ecosystem, Personas, and AI+ Scenarios

Six personas guide the interfaces: open-source developers, start-up teams, enterprise visitors, nearby residents, university communities, and older or disabled users. Twelve cards cover an open-source release room, safety sandbox, edge-compute kiosk, explainable walking assistant, international demo lounge, low-carbon Qinghe corridor, campus transfer street, data-governance salon, everyday service street, global AI week route, accessible co-testing station, and model-responsibility desk. ## Land Use, Building Scale, and Retain-Renovate-Demolish Strategy

The submitted land-use layer is a complete partition of the temporary design envelope and uses registered categories. It is a design allocation, not a statutory land-use decision. The building layer illustrates one adaptable footprint so that footprint metrics and interface logic can be reviewed; it is not a parcel-level demolition conclusion. The professional sequence is retain sound and valued fabric, renovate interfaces and accessibility, remove only after evidence and approval, and add reversible lightweight components before permanent construction. Official control conditions remain pending. [standard:MNR-LAND-USE-CLASSIFICATION-GUIDE] [data:geometry/buildings.geojson#BLDG-001] [depth:retain_renovate_demolish]

## Transport, Rail, Municipal Infrastructure, and Public Services

A north-south heritage commons spine and three transverse connectors form a walking and cycling skeleton between the ports and their surrounding campuses, neighbourhoods, enterprises, and transit stations. The proposal identifies integration questions around Wudaokou, Qinghuadongluxikou, Dazhongsi, and the Fifth Ring Road but does not draw official road or engineering lines. New infrastructure is conceived as shared edge-compute, consent, audit, and human-service counters paired with conventional power, drainage, fire, and maintenance systems. Capacity and alignment require official data and specialist confirmation. [data:geometry/roads.geojson#ROAD-001] [depth:traffic_rail_slow_parking] [depth:municipal_new_infrastructure]

## Blue-Green Network, Public Space, and Urban Character

The Jing-Zhang Railway Heritage Park is treated as a civic commons where heritage, innovation culture, and daily care are equally visible. Four honour nodes recognise contribution rather than celebrity: a Responsibility Beacon at Zhongzhiyuan, an Open-Source Growth-Ring Court at AI Origin, a Civic Echo Gallery at Dazhongsi, and a Century Interface Marker along the heritage spine. They are removable and updateable concepts. Materials, lighting, heritage impact, structure, fire safety, accessibility, and copyright require professional review. [standard:MOHURD-URBAN-DESIGN-MEASURES] [data:geometry/green_space.geojson#GREEN-001] [depth:blue_green_public_space]

## Renewal Projects, Implementation Policy, and Phasing

Phase 1 pilots reversible interface furniture, accessible co-testing, a public responsibility desk, and temporary wayfinding after site permissions. Phase 2 deepens the three ports through coordinated public-space and adaptive-reuse studies. Phase 3 links the ports through long-term community stewardship, annual open-source and public-value events, scenario access rules, international communication, and an auditable contribution archive. The operating loop is publish a question, test with consent, display evidence, allow pause, conduct human review, and record what changed. Dates, budgets, operators, policy support, and construction remain recommendations pending authorization. ## Risk, Copyright, and Compliance

The largest limitation is the absence of official exact boundaries and several regulatory, ownership, transport, municipal, heritage, and building datasets. Provisional geometry is visibly qualified throughout. All spatial interventions and operating mechanisms are conceptual recommendations for professional teams to deepen.
