Provisional geometry

The organizer's missing official geometry does not reduce content scoring. It does require complete recalculation before statutory or engineering use.

Sources and copyright

The package uses public sources as evidence and contains no remote map tiles, external photos, corporate logos, portraits, or third-party renderings.
