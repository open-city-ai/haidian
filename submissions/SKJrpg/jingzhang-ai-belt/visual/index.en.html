Overview Map · Full-Line Station Map

Reading the nine-kilometre park as a line you can use every day

The mainline is not a sightseeing list but a continuous everyday public space. Six stations link railway identity and public life; ten transfer nodes connect universities, research institutes, science parks, public culture, transport hubs and the waterfront greenway; the Zhuanhe waterfront, as a sub-node of Kilometer Zero, first repairs the real breakpoint of “passed but never entered”.

Part 3

School-Enterprise Co-Creation · AI Scenarios

Universities produce talent and knowledge; enterprises raise real problems; the park becomes the public interface

“School-enterprise co-creation” builds no closed park; it sets a few open interfaces in the everyday park, putting university talent and research capacity, enterprise scenario needs, and resident and commuter feedback on one visible iteration chain.

From open problems to verifiable improvement

Parkpublic display, experience, feedback

The park first serves those who pass daily, then carries Jing-Zhang memory, and only lastly arranges open exchange at a few nodes; exchange functions must not crowd out passage, rest, exercise and accessible use.

Concept suggestion

AI Co-Creation Table

A physical model and a no-login public screen form the main experience; an optional QR code only serves those who wish to continue.

  • Anonymous participation, no face recognition, collecting only the minimum data needed to complete feedback.
  • Publicly show problems, suggestions and improvement results in an experience-feedback-iterate loop.
  • The first round does not test grand urban visions but improves the Kilometer-Zero node itself: whether the entrance is on the way, whether information is clear, whether it is worth staying.

Sources · Assumptions · Evidence Boundaries

Make what is confirmable clear, and what still needs verification equally clear

This page is a human-readable offline summary; the structured source, assumption, geometry and metrics files remain the basis for review.

Main sources

  • Public call announcement and agent taskbook: confirm the three levels, tasks and concept-design boundaries.
  • Public reports on the Jing-Zhang Railway Heritage Park, and material on Qinghuayuan Station and the former Xizhimen station: support the nine-kilometre, station and railway-culture clues.
  • Public standards on urban design, regulatory detailed planning, land-use classification and accessibility: they constrain the method of expression, not this project's statutory values.
  • provisional rough boundary:for generation only, display, self-check and design discussion。

Key assumptions

  • The official overall boundary and the three key-area extents are not yet released; they must be replaced and recomputed once official data arrives.
  • Floor-area ratio, building height, building density, statutory green ratio, setback, road redlines, ownership, municipal and heritage controls all await professional confirmation.
  • The AI co-creation table and station activities are conceptual suggestions and do not represent approval, investment, engineering feasibility or implementation commitment.

Field-evidence limitation:current node judgments come mainly from participant memory, public sources and two existing path photos, no new photos this round;Zhuanhethe waterfront entrance still lacks new site photos and continuous behavior observation, follow-up needs weekday/weekend site surveys。
