Mobility · Blue-green public space · Buildings and renewal · Renewal projects and phasing · AI scenarios

  • Mobility: grounded in the real Xueqing/Xueyuan/Xitucheng network (OSM); the Spine slow corridor follows the Jing-Bao rail corridor past six rail stations (Beijing North, Jimenqiao, Xitucheng, Xueyuanqiao, Liudaokou, Xuezhiyuan), with six E-W slow stitch segments healing the severance (conceptual alignments).
  • Blue-green: the heritage park belt plus real parks (Yuan Dadu Wall Relics Park, Xiaoyue River park, etc.); squares and promenades anchored at rail stations.
  • Buildings: 42 conceptual footprints classified retain/renovate/new-build by land-use code, renewal-led, all low-confidence design quantities.
  • Phasing: Phase 1 Beijing North-Jimen gateway (2026-2029), Phase 2 Xitucheng-Wudaokou Origin Community (2029-2032), Phase 3 Liudaokou-Xuezhiyuan Zhongzhiyuan (2032-2035) - conceptual.
  • AI scenarios: 12 nodes matching 12 scenario cards (incl. 3 industry test/validation scenarios), anchored at real stations and places; see the card table in proposal.md ch.6.
