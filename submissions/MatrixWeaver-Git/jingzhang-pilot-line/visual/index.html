13 / GAPS

假设｜专业资料缺口

A-BOUNDARY-001

Official precise site and key-area polygons are unavailable.

Replace geometry, recompute every dependent metric and regenerate all artifacts after cleared official files arrive.
A-CONTROLS-001

Approved land-use, FAR, height and other regulatory controls are unavailable.

Reconcile the design with approved planning conditions and update the standard/depth matrices.
A-ROAD-001

Official road redlines, junction geometry, transit interfaces and parking requirements are unavailable.

Replace with surveyed/approved networks and rerun accessibility, length and conflict checks.
A-OWNERSHIP-001

Parcel ownership, tenure and implementation rights are unavailable.

Overlay cleared cadastral and ownership records and establish rights before project approval.
A-EXISTING-001

A surveyed existing-building inventory and engineering condition assessment are unavailable.

Complete survey and structural assessment, then classify retain, renovate and remove building by building.
A-UTILITIES-001

Verified municipal capacity, underground utilities and energy/carbon baselines are unavailable.

Obtain utility surveys and operator capacity letters, then test loads and revise phasing.
A-HERITAGE-001

Precise heritage protection boundaries and conservation requirements are unavailable.

Overlay official heritage files and obtain conservation review before physical intervention.
A-DELIVERY-001

Investment, procurement, operator commitment and approved construction schedules are unavailable.

Establish business case, accountable operators, procurement route and approved programme before delivery.
