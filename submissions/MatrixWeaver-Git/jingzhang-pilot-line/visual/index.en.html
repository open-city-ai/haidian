14 / OPEN CALL

OPEN CHALLENGES: MAKE FAILURE PUBLIC KNOWLEDGE

The annual open challenge is a continuous mechanism for problem publication, admission, evidence registration, remediation, retesting and exit—not a one-off event. Launch reporting publishes real baselines instead of substituting promises for operating evidence.
