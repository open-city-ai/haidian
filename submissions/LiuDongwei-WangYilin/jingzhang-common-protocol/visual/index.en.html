Land-Use Layout

Research, education, housing, community service, commerce, culture, green space, and mobility form a complete conceptual partition for spatial recalculation, not a statutory land-use change.

Transport and Walking

One Co-Tempo Corridor, three cross-connections, and twelve stopping nodes prioritize accessibility, human help, night return, and alternatives that do not require a device.

Blue-Green Public Space

Shade, shelters, reading tables, low-tech information, human-service desks, and removable test pockets form a shared base for different tempos.

Buildings

Conceptual carriers use adaptable ground floors, quiet upper levels, and removable technical interfaces. Actual retention, renewal, scale, height, and ownership remain pending surveys.

Renewal Projects

The corridor, Calibration Ring, open-source classes, Translation Clinic, Translation Beacon, Return Tables, and scenario nodes advance according to evidence maturity.

Assumptions

Until official boundaries, controls, ownership, streets, infrastructure, heritage, and building surveys are available, every spatial action remains a conceptual recommendation.

Jingzhang Co-Tempo is an open co-creation proposal. It does not replace statutory planning and is not an approval, construction authorization, or implementation commitment.
