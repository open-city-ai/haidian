## Coordinated Research Area: Industry and Future City Research

### Overall Concept: Jing-Zhang Renzi Chain

**Design Judgment**: Use the "human character chain" as the overall spatial and spiritual concept for the innovative belt. First, the Jing-Zhang Railway of 1909 was the first major railway line designed, constructed, and surveyed by Chinese people, with the "human" character-shaped alignment created by Zhan Tianyou at Qinglong Bridge being a landmark engineering feat in railway history, serving as the deepest collective memory of this corridor [source:THREE-AREAS-WINGS]; second, the "human" character in the Chinese context simultaneously refers to "talent" and "people-oriented," aligning with the dual goals of the AI Innovation Belt, which aims to attract global AI innovation talent and promote people-oriented governance [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK]; third, the "chain" character translates the railway's "station-line" organizational logic into the "innovation chain-value chain-scenario chain" of the AI era: the Jing-Zhang Railway was originally a chain of stations, and the AI Innovation Belt can similarly be organized according to the principle of "one station-one scenario, one link segment-one function."

**Naming System (Conceptual Recommendation)**: The overall name for the belt is suggested as "Jing-Zhang Renzi Chain" (JZ-Renzi, abbreviated as JZ-Renzi, for international promotion "The Renzi Belt"). The three key areas are named with the "Research·Creation·Industry" triad, while retaining their official names, for the Public Space layer:

- Zhongzhiyuan AI Independent Innovation Acceleration Area = "Chain Segment·Research" (Research Acceleration and Governance Standards)
- Beijing AI Origin Community = "Chain Segment·Creation" (Original Innovation and Open Source Community)
- Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster = "Chain Segment·Industry" (Intelligent Nativized New Business Models and Industry Transformation)

The wings are named as follows:

- Zhongguancun Technology Services Wing = "Chain Wing·Service" (Element Allocation and IP Capital)
- Xiaoyue River Scenario Enablement Wing = "Chain Wing·Scenario" (Scenario Experimentation and Urban Experience)

The naming system and logo direction are Open Co-Creation suggestions, awaiting official branding process confirmation [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK][depth:ai_cultural_narrative].

**Logo Direction (Conceptual Recommendation)**: The theme is based on a "person" shape: two track lines diverge from a single point at the southern end (West Straight Gate, now Beijing North Station; Dazhongsi was the current track hub, not the Jing-Zhang Railway station). They branch out, pass through an "∞" shaped node (origin point at Qinghua Garden—Wudao Kou), and converge to the north (Zhongzhiyuan), resembling the combination of a "person" and a Möbius ring. The left stroke takes the warm copper color of the tracks (historical), while the right stroke takes the data blue color (AI). An overlapping circular node represents talent and scenarios. The logo graphic and font are self-drawn direction suggestions and do not contain any third-party trademarks, fonts, or image materials [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK].

In the three major orientations, the "Century Jing-Zhang Cultural Belt" is carried by the Vitality Spine Cultural Narrative (refer to the Cultural Narrative chapter); the "Urban AI Life Experience Belt" is carried by the Xiaoyue River Scenario Enablement Wing and 14 scenario nodes (refer to the AI-Enabled Scenario chapter); the "AI Integration Innovation Belt" is carried by the industrial collaboration of the Three Chains and Two Wings (refer to this chapter). **Three Zones and Two Wings Synergistic Loop**: This plan organizes the five zones into a "human character" synergistic loop — the original point community "Innovate" (left upper stroke of the human character) proposes problems and original innovation, Zhongzhiyuan "Research" (top of the human character) overcomes problems and standard governance, Dazhongsi "Industry" (right lower stroke of the human character) transforms problems into products and business forms, Zhongguancun Technology Services Wing provides elements, IP, and capital (left stroke extension), and Xiaoyue River Scenario Enablement Wing provides a test bed and user feedback (right stroke extension). The loop is "propose—overcome—transform—enable—feedback," with any stage's output flowing back to other stages; for example, data and demands from Dazhongsi's industrial transformation can feed back to the original point community's open-source community, and Xiaoyue River's test feedback can feed back to Zhongzhiyuan's research and development selection [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK][depth:industry_ecology].

**Roof Ridge Gradient (Concept)**: Complementary to the 'Human Zygote Synergistic Loop', this proposal presents a north-south gradient narrative along the vitality ridge—south segment 'Departure' (origin community: portal scale, highest density of talent and open-source vitality, most active interface); middle segment 'Turnback and Acceleration' (Dazhongsi and academic belt: three seam corridors like a switch—North Third Ring Road, Chengfu Road, North Fourth Ring Road—sequentially stitching transit stations and industrial carriers, increasing scene density along the axis; north segment 'Climax' (Zhongzhiyuan and Qinghe Green Wedge: largest building setbacks, most green space, national platform anchors and governance standards converge here). The gradient organizes a one-day public path experience rhythm of 'portal—acceleration—climax' (concept, non-regulatory indicator).

**Future AI City Form (Conceptual Definition)**: This proposal introduces the concept of "Adaptive Blocks" — characterized by "data visibility, scenario flexibility, and interface adaptability." Public Spaces are equipped with standardized smart interfaces (power/data/shade/signage integrated pole), while business spaces undergo flexible reconfiguration every six years (industrial functions modularized). Street spaces are dynamically adjusted based on "time of day, pedestrian flow, and weather" (dual modes for event days and workdays). Operational parameters (concept): Smart poles are built according to the principle of "standardized interfaces and replaceable equipment," with business reconfiguration achieved through "modular functions and prefabricated interior finishes" to minimize disruption. Dynamic adjustments only affect non-traffic elements of public spaces (stalls, shading, lighting), without altering the road boundary or traffic organization. Iteration mechanism (concept): Each adaptive unit is subject to a "three-year evaluation—six-year reconfiguration decision" node, with evaluation criteria including usage rate, energy consumption, accessibility compliance, and public satisfaction. If not meeting the standards, the unit reverts to the standard mode. "Anchor institutions + compact mixed-use neighborhoods" are the core mechanisms driving the rise of innovation districts [source:SRC-INNOVATION-DISTRICTS]. This plan's three-chain nodes correspond to "anchor institution-led" nodes (Zhongzhiyuan = national platform anchor, Original Community = university anchor, Dazhongsi = leading firm anchor). International reviews also indicate that innovation districts can be differentiated by functional types such as research, entrepreneurship, and industrial transformation. The "research—innovation—entrepreneurship" three-chain nodes are organized according to this typology [source:SRC-ID-CLASSIFICATION].

### Urban Renewal Overall Framework

**Design Judgment**: Through a dual model of "Acupuncture Urban Renewal + Linkage Urban Renewal": the internal linkage is updated as a whole unit of blocks (original point community, Dazhongsi, Zhongzhiyuan), while the linkages between them are connected by an active spine to stitch the gaps (crossing, overpass, cycling connection) [source:OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT][depth:urban_renewal]. "Acupuncture Urban Renewal" corresponds to the requirement of "retaining, utilizing, and improving as the main approach, strictly controlling large-scale demolition and reconstruction" established by the Ministry of Housing and Urban-Rural Development in its notice on preventing large-scale demolition and reconstruction during the implementation of urban renewal actions (2021) [source:SRC-NMOHURD-NO-DEMOLITION], and originates from the urban acupuncture theory — precise intervention at key nodes (acupoints) to drive the vitality of the area (refer to the empirical research on micro-updates of old communities in Chongqing in 2022) [source:SRC-URBAN-ACUPUNCTURE]. This plan's original point square, Wudaokou experience street, and the four quadrants of Dazhongsi are such acupoint nodes. International case studies include San Diego I.D.E.A. Public Space is shown to be a critical element in the successful renewal of an innovation district, aligning with the scheme's "overall renewal of internal blocks within the district + stitching together the vitality spine public space" [source:SRC-IDEA-DISTRICT]. The demolish–renovate–retain strategy is conceptually illustrated (based on the `renewal_class` attribute in `geometry/buildings.geojson` recalculated by the base area: new construction about 60%, renovation about 24%, and retention about 16%, see the chapter on "Land Use, Building Scale, and Demolish–Renovate–Retain Strategy") [depth:retain_renovate_demolish][data:geometry/buildings.geojson#BLD-002]. All demolish–renovate–retain descriptions are presented as Conceptual Recommendations and do not serve as block-level demolish–renovate–retain conclusions [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK].

**Update Objects and Functional Uses**: Update objects are categorized into four types—(1) low-efficiency industrial buildings transformed into AI research and incubation carriers; (2) comprehensive renovation of old residential areas (installation of smart service facilities without dismantling the main structure); (3) insertion of public experience functions into inefficient spaces along the vitality spine; (4) blank plots (16 categories, approximately 46.0 hectares) reserved for long-term strategic purposes [metric:land_use_16_area_sqm][data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-16-1]. The suggested implementation path for updates is the integration of "campus—park—street": universities opening up and sharing resources (flexibilizing campus boundaries), park functions becoming more diverse (ground-level public spaces), and streets filling in service gaps (15-minute AI service circle) [depth:renewal_project_list].

## Cultural Narratives, Signage, and International Communication (agent.5 Response)

### Jing-Zhang Railway Historical and Cultural Resource System

The Jing-Zhang Railway (commenced in 1905 and fully operational by September 1909) was the first major railway line built independently by the Chinese, with its "person-shaped gradient" (the Qinglong Bridge Badaling section, listed as a National Key Cultural Relic Protection Unit in 2006) and the engineering spirit of Zhan Tianyou, along with the stations along the line (such as the Tsinghua Yuan Station and Qinghe Station), forming the deepest historical resources of the Innovation Belt [source:THREE-AREAS-WINGS][source:SRC-JZ-PARK-OPENING]. The operational facts of Jing-Zhang cultural chain (as reported publicly): the Jing-Zhang High-Speed Railway has transported over 56 million passengers in five years since its opening (as of December 2024) [source:SRC-JZ-HSR-5Y-2024]; the century-old Qinglong Bridge Station (site of the person-shaped line) has transformed into a Great Wall Cultural Exhibition Hall (June 2024) [source:SRC-JZ-QINGLONGQIAO-2024]—the revitalization operation of distant cultural nodes provides a homologous reference for the "culture + operation" of the Innovation Belt. **Heritage Dynamics (Public Reports, Background Only, v1.6)**: In the 2026 National Two Sessions, a  member suggested promoting the joint application for World Cultural Heritage status for the Jing-Zhang Railway (including the Zigzag Railway) and the Jing-Zhang High-Speed Railway [source:SRC-JZ-WORLD-HERITAGE-2026] —— This is a member's suggestion through a public deliberation channel, not an official stance from the State Administration of Cultural Heritage; this plan's narrative of the "Jing-Zhang Cultural Chain" aligns with it (conceptually).

**Superior Planning Alignment (Public Document)**: The "Jing-Zhang Sports and Tourism Belt Construction Plan" (issued in January 2022) [source:SRC-JZ-SPORTS-TOURISM-PLAN-2022] provides the overarching planning context for the "Sports and Tourism Belt" — the cultural narrative, pilgrimage landmark system, and annual event system of this plan align with it (conceptual, not repeating the planning content). **Bridgeway Column Historical Gallery Real Segment (Public Report)**: The space under the 13th Line railway bridge has been fully revitalized — the painted station names sequence at the bridge piers (West Straight Gate, Nan Kou, Qing Long Bridge), the "Suzhou Code" mileage markers, and the weatherproof running path (Beijing Daily report in December 2025) [source:SRC-JZ-13LINE-GALLERY-2025] — the component library "Column Historical Gallery" (C-04) of this plan anchors to a real segment, filling in the blank segments (conceptual). ### Operational Performance Indicators and Benchmarking Framework (Concept)

This proposal includes an operational performance monitoring perspective, separate from the formal indicator system: operational performance indicators do not enter formal calculations, are not used as conclusion data, and are only used as monitoring concepts with no target values.
