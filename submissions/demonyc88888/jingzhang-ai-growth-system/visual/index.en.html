Scope Study

The three scope layers are not isolated maps, but a continuous study framework from regional judgement to implementable nodes.

The proposal first clarifies the innovation ecosystem across 43.6 km², then organizes the public framework, slow-mobility blue-green links, renewal projects, and AI scenarios within the 11.4 km² overall design area. Finally, the three key areas are translated into spatial and operational tasks for professional design development.

Persona Analysis

The six persona groups are not mutually exclusive labels, but six participation roles in one urban AI growth chain.

The proposal reframes people from passive footfall into urban participants who raise problems, validate together, support operations, and receive feedback. Each persona must correspond to real tasks, spatial entry points, AI capabilities, human fallback, and refusal boundaries, preventing public-space AI from becoming one-way extraction.

P1

Nearby Daily Residents

Focus on walking safety, rest and supply points, senior/child-friendly service, community problem feedback, and public-service accessibility; need offline human counters, non-smartphone entry, and clear feedback receipts.

P2

Commuting Young Passers-by

Focus on continuous travel from stations, communities, and universities to river-park and industry nodes; need explainable detours, night help, cycling/walking support, and low-disturbance prompts.

P3

University and Developer Co-Creators

Focus on real problems, rapid prototypes, testing sites, and review feedback; need open workstations, prototype workshops, scenario passports, and controlled testing opportunities.

P4

Cultural Explorers and Public Experience Users

Focus on Centennial Jing-Zhang memory, Xiaoyue River experiences, heritage park activities, and traceable interpretation; need low-disturbance displays, content-rights notices, and optional participation.

P5

AI Enterprise Product Validators

Focus on compliance assessment, user testing, device trials, product launches, and procurement conversion; need Zhongzhi Park admission, Dazhongsi classification operations, and clear IP boundaries.

P6

Public Operators and Human Fallback Teams

Focus on device maintenance, human takeover, complaint closure, risk review, and annual operations; need readable logs, work orders, stop conditions, and asset ledgers.

12 Initial AI Scenarios

Scenarios first enter the city at small scale, with reversible deployment and human staffing, before any decision to scale into a network.

All 12 scenarios enter the scenario passport system, recording data boundaries, responsible parties, human takeover, stop conditions, and exit/recovery. SC-07 to SC-10 are industry testing and validation scenarios; they enter productization or scaling discussion only after passing public-value, community-acceptance, technical-performance, and operational-feasibility assessment.

SC-01

Urban Problem Desk

The Origin Community and mobile service points organize problems raised by residents, passers-by, and operators into reviewable scenario passports.

SC-02

One-Day Youth Prototype Workshop

Translates real urban problems into low-fidelity models, service flows, and testing plans, forming first-round prototypes for discussion.

SC-03

North-South Walking Companion

Provides detours, rest, help, and human service along the continuous walking experience line without turning route service into mandatory tracking.

SC-04

Xiaoyue River Ecological Co-Management

Uses environmental and facility data to help identify water, planting, heat, and facility anomalies, generating maintenance work orders.

SC-05

Jing-Zhang Memory Co-Creation Station

Organizes oral histories, railway archives, and community content into traceable interpretation, with source and authorization recorded.

SC-06

Night Safety and Human Assistance

Station loops and river-park spaces prioritize human-staffed response for facility faults, help requests, and night-safety feedback.

SC-07 · Industry Validation

Embodied Device Shared-Path Testing Yard

Tests avoidance, stopping, takeover, and exit/recovery for delivery, cleaning, inspection, and other devices in controlled areas of Zhongzhi Park.

SC-08 · Industry Validation

Trusted Agent Governance Sandbox

Reviews agent permissions, tool calls, logs, bias, safety, and human takeover, preventing direct deployment without governance.

SC-09 · Industry Validation

Smart Terminal Public Testing Street

Tests usability, accessibility, stability, maintenance cost, and human-service boundaries of public terminals in real pedestrian flow.

SC-10 · Industry Validation

Enterprise Agent Deployment Service Desk

Supports product deployment, API access, compliance checks, user testing, and operations handover at Dazhongsi.

SC-11

AI Content Consumption and Copyright Lab

Tests interactive content, digital copyright, willingness to pay, and minor protection, with source and authorization recorded for all content.

SC-12

Public AI Operations and Feedback Room

Aggregates public value, acceptance, technical performance, and operations data to support continue, adjust, or exit decisions.
