# 摘要 | Executive summary

## 中文

**京张人字轨 · 可运行的AI试验轨。** 1909 年，京张铁路用青龙桥的人字形折返线，以当时的技术翻越关沟——不硬爬直线，用一次折返换取高度。人工智能的进步同样不是直线：训练—实测—反馈—再训练。本方案把这条百年智慧变成一带的运行逻辑。

**空间结构：一轨三站两环十二桩。** 一条 9.72 公里南北贯通的主轨（京张遗址公园的慢行与试验通道）；三站——自主站（众智园，192.1 公顷）、原点站（AI原点社区，104.3 公顷）、钟声站（大钟寺，72.0 公顷）；两环——联挂环（中关村科技服务翼）与月河试验环（小月河场景赋能翼）；12 处东西缝合桩 K0–K11，同时是导视、铭牌、端侧算力与班次卡的站点。

**核心机制：折返回路。** 研发在原点站的开源机务段与成果发布厅；实测在 44 米公共铺装带内的可编程试验道与自主站开放测试入口；示范在钟声站的站城广场、回声钟房与消费实验街；反馈回到自主站的标准与安全治理组团。可编程试验道与慢行主道同材同标高：默认是人行与休憩空间，只在公示时段按班次开放实测，现场人工接管，夜间不测试。

**可核验成果。** 九个几何图层由仓库登记的临时粗略边界派生：60 面无缝用地分区（覆盖残差 50.8 平方米、重叠 0）、264 个更新建筑基底建议、23 条概念线网、3 期分期。36 项指标分三类：空间指标可在 EPSG:4548 下复算（总体范围 11.41 平方公里、绿地率 16.22%、公共空间率 5.27%）；容积率、高度、密度等依赖官方控制条件的指标保持“待正式数据补齐”，不以推测值制造精确感。任务覆盖矩阵覆盖公告 1.3/1.4/1.5 与 agent.1–agent.6 共 23 项，四道自检门全部通过。

**边界。** 所有空间落地建议均为概念建议、参考方案，可供专业团队深化研究；不替代法定规划，不构成政府审定结论；临时边界不得作为官方红线或精确面积依据，官方几何发布后整包复算。

## English

**Jingzhang Switchback · The Living Test Track.** In 1909 the Jingzhang Railway crossed the Guangou pass with the technology of its day by building the herringbone switchback at Qinglongqiao - trading one reversal for altitude instead of forcing a straight climb. AI advances the same way: train, test, feedback, retrain. This proposal turns that century-old intelligence into the operating logic of the belt.

**Structure: one track, three stations, two loops, twelve milestones.** A continuous 9.72 km main track along the heritage park; three stations - AUTONOMY (Zhongzhiyuan, 192.1 ha), ORIGIN (Beijing AI Origin Community, 104.3 ha) and BELL (Dazhongsi, 72.0 ha); two loops - the Coupling Loop for the Zhongguancun technology-service wing and the Moonriver Test Loop for the scenario-empowerment wing; and twelve east-west stitching milestones K0-K11 that double as wayfinding, nameplates, edge-compute pods and service-card stops.

**Core mechanism: the switchback loop.** Research at the ORIGIN open-source Depot and release hall; field testing on the programmable lane inside the 44 m public paving band and at the AUTONOMY open-test entrance; demonstration at the BELL station square, Echo Bell House and retail pilot street; feedback returning to the AUTONOMY standards and safety-governance cluster. The programmable lane shares material and level with the walking lane: pedestrian and resting space by default, opened only in published windows by service card, with on-site human override and no night testing.

**Verifiable output.** Nine geometry layers derive from the registered provisional rough boundary: a 60-face seamless land-use partition (50.8 m² residual, zero overlap), 264 proposed renewal footprints, 23 concept network lines and three phasing extents. Of 36 metrics, spatial ones are recomputable in EPSG:4548 (11.41 km² design area, 16.22% green ratio, 5.27% public-space ratio), while FAR, height and density stay pending official data rather than inventing precision. The compliance matrix covers all 23 announcement and agent-taskbook requirements, and all four self-check gates pass.

**Boundary.** Every spatial suggestion is a concept proposal and reference scheme for professional teams to develop further. It does not replace statutory planning and is not a government decision; the provisional boundary is not an official redline or precise-area basis, and the whole package is recomputed when official geometry is released.
