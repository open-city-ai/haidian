CITY HANDSHAKE

The city does not give power to AI. People may delegate bounded, auditable, revocable actions to Agents.

Submission boundary: this package uses provisional geometry. After official SITE_BOUNDARY / KEY_AREA release, spatial layers, metrics, drawings, PDFs and HTML must be recomputed. This is an auditable design proposal, not an official redline or implementation commitment.
