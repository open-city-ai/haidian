Spatial overview

One civic-life spine, three prototypes, nine longitudinal/cross links and twelve stoppable scenarios; extents and alignments must be rebuilt with official data.

From ordinary arrival and staffed choice to bounded interfaces, recovery space and exit replay.

Eight repairable component families

Six user journeys

Paper, staffed, no-phone and no-AI routes are primary service routes, not footnotes.

Twelve scenarios, thirty-six state assignments

Every card names three uses and a human stop rule; S-01 through S-04 are industry tests/validation.
