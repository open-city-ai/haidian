# JING-ZHANG · THE NINTH ART BELT: A Century-Old Corridor Where the World Creates Together

## Design Basis and Source Inventory

This proposal rests on three layers of evidence: **official public documents** (the pre-qualification announcement by the Haidian Branch of the Beijing Municipal Commission of Planning and Natural Resources, which defines the three scope levels, the three key areas, design tasks, and deliverable depth) [source:OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT]; **the cleared agent taskbook** (three positionings, five functions, three areas and two wings, six agent tasks) [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK]; and **repository working files** (source registry, planning limits, standard snapshots, provisional geometry) [source:SOURCE-REGISTRY].

The data status must be stated up front: the announcement contains no boundary drawings or spatial data, so the overall design boundary and the three key-area polygons used here are repository provisional estimates, deviating 0.02%–0.43% from the official text values. They are used only for generation, display, and self-check, never as an official redline or precise-area basis [source:BOUNDARY-SOURCE]. Statutory regulatory controls (FAR, building height, density, green ratio, setbacks) are not in the public package; all volumes and intensities in this proposal are marked as concept design values [depth:development_intensity_controls].

The design judgement starts from a simple observation: the three new business types the announcement names for the Dazhongsi area — **agents, intelligent terminals, content consumption** — form a complete chain from production tools to distribution channels to consumption scenes; games and interactive media are the most active testbed on that chain. In 1909 the Jing-Zhang railway became the first trunk line designed and built by Chinese engineers — the "ninth art" of its era, a national-scale work completed in the language of engineering. Handing this century-old corridor to AI-era creation is the motif of this proposal.

## Three-Level Scope Framework

The three levels cascade from industrial strategy to overall urban design to district-level detailed design [depth:three_level_scope_framework].

**Coordinated research area (~43.6 km²)**: bounded by the N 5th Ring Road to the north, the Jingzang Expressway to the east, Xizhimen Outer Street to the south, and Wanquanhe Road to the west (official text values) [source:OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT]. This level answers "what to do": with Haidian's "1+X+1" industrial system as background, it establishes the AI content industry as one of the AI+ vertical directions and organizes the three-area-two-wing synergy loop [source:HAIDIAN-1X1].

**Overall design area (~11.4 km²)**: the urban districts 1–2 km around the Jing-Zhang heritage park. This package uses the provisional boundary polygon; the EPSG:4548 recomputation gives 11,412,825 m², +0.11% versus the official text value [metric:site_area_sqm] [data:geometry/site_boundary.geojson#SITE-001]. This level answers "where": all design layers (land use, buildings, roads, green space, public space, phasing) are generated inside this boundary with no topological gaps or overlaps.

**Key detailed-design area (~368.4 ha)**: from north to south, Zhongzhiyuan (~192.1 ha), Beijing AI Origin Community (~104.3 ha), and Dazhongsi (~72.0 ha), per the official planning-limits file [metric:key_area_zhongzhiyuan_sqm] [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-001]. This level answers "how it gains character": the three key areas become the proposal's three stations — Engine, Inspiration, and Publishing.

The limits of provisional geometry must be explicit: the three key-area polygons are rough rectangles positioned from announcement names, north-south order, and location clues; they express no road redlines, parcels, or ownership boundaries. When official redlines arrive, all layers, metrics, and drawings must be regenerated as a whole, not patched file by file [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-002].

## Industry and Future-City Research for the Coordinated Research Area

### Overall concept: Jing-Zhang · The Ninth Art Belt

**Chinese name**: 京张·第九艺术带. **English name**: THE NINTH ART BELT. Film is the eighth art; games and interactive media are called the ninth art — the most complete and most demanding testbed for AI-native content: engines define experience, compute defines boundaries, communities define longevity.

**Naming system "One Track, Three Stations, Two Wings"**: the Creative Mainline (the heritage-park slow-mobility spine) is the one track; Engine Station (Zhongzhiyuan), Inspiration Station (Origin Community), and Publishing Station (Dazhongsi) are the three stations; the Zhongguancun Tech-Service Wing (west: IP/capital/professional services) and the Xiaoyuehe Scenario Wing (east: scenario opening and living experience) are the two wings [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-SPN]. The three official positionings map as follows: **Centennial Jing-Zhang Cultural Belt** = the cultural-narrative layer of the Creative Mainline; **Urban AI Living Experience Belt** = the two wings and the Xiaoyuehe scenario layer; **AI Integrated Innovation Belt** = the industrial innovation layer of the three stations [standard:PROJECT-AGENT-OPEN-CALL-TASKBOOK].

**Logo direction**: the double-line switchback angle of the Jing-Zhang railway's herringbone track as the skeleton, with a pixel-matrix node at the turning point — the two lines stand for the railway heritage and AI content, the pixels for creation by everyone. This is a visual suggestion only; the final identity system requires professional design and full font/trademark clearance [depth:overall_spatial_structure].

### Global AI innovation ecosystem cases (5–8)

Drawn from public reports and institutional websites (retrieved 2026-08-13); mechanisms only, no endorsement of any enterprise [source:NINTH-ART-CASES]:

1. **Station F (Paris)**: a derelict freight station turned into the world's largest startup campus — the "station as incubator" narrative directly supports the low-disturbance renewal strategy of Inspiration Station.
2. **Kendall Square (Cambridge)**: innovation density within 1 km of MIT — the spatial recipe of university-origin transfer, supporting the near-campus positioning of the Origin Community.
3. **London Knowledge Quarter**: patchwork renewal across many institutions — incremental weaving for areas with complex ownership, supporting the two-wing weaving strategy.
4. **Shenzhen Nanshan Sci-Tech Park game belt**: the engine–publishing–esports vertical ecosystem, supporting the industrial depth of Publishing Station.
5. **Tokyo Shibuya–Akihabara**: fusion of content consumption and urban experience — the street-level expression of intelligent terminals and content consumption, supporting the Dazhongsi experience street.
6. **Toronto Waterfront experience**: public space first, but governance frameworks must come first — which is why every scenario card here carries privacy and human-review clauses.
7. **Helsinki Kalasatama**: an old harbour district organized as an "experiment neighbourhood" — supporting the test-scenario layout of the Engine garden R&D community.
8. **Seoul DMC**: policy continuity from landfill to digital-content cluster — supporting long-term operation of the whole belt.

### Three-area-two-wing synergy loop

Zhongzhiyuan outputs "engines and foundations", the Origin Community outputs "talent and works", Dazhongsi outputs "publishing and consumption", the west wing supplies "copyright and capital", the east wing supplies "scenarios and data" — a creation loop: foundation → talent → works → publishing → scenario feedback → foundation upgrade [source:THREE-AREAS-WINGS]. Spatially: the Creative Mainline carries the flow of works, the three cross links carry the flow of production factors, and the two wings carry services and scenarios.

## Overall Design Area: Urban Renewal and Regulatory-Plan-Depth Urban Design

### Spatial structure and land use

The overall structure is "One Track, Three Stations, Two Wings": the Creative Mainline is a north-south slow-mobility and green spine along the Jing-Zhang heritage park, about 9.6 km in concept alignment, divided into Engine, Inspiration, and Publishing segments by the three key areas [data:geometry/roads.geojson#RD-M-01]. The land-use partition cuts the provisional boundary into 11 features: research land (0802) 14.7%, commercial and business land (05) 35.0%, urban residential land (0701) 16.1%, park green (1401) 34.1% (concept values recomputed on the provisional boundary) [metric:land_use_area_0802_sqm] [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-A1].

The design intent of the land-use structure: the high green share (34.1%) is not decoration but "creative-environment infrastructure" for the content industry — the means of production of the ninth art are attention and emotion, both of which need continuous, quiet, wanderable green corridors [metric:green_ratio]. The high commercial share (35.0%) matches the three asset-light business types — incubation, publishing, experience — rather than the traditional office-tower logic.

### Urban renewal framework

The renewal framework is "three-station renewal + two-wing weaving": the three stations are the priority renewal units for underused space; the two wings are the campus-park-neighbourhood fusion and tech-service weaving units. The conceptual retain/renovate/new-build classification: retain the heritage park and the low-disturbance zones around universities; renovate underused buildings into workshops and experience spaces; build concept workshops, the Creation Tower, and the virtual-production stage — parcel-level decisions remain pending items subject to existing-condition surveys and regulatory plans [depth:retain_renovate_demolish].

### Transport, rail, municipal and supporting facilities

The transport strategy centres on "one mainline + three cross links": the Creative Mainline is the slow-mobility spine (concept half-width 12 m); the three cross links run east-west at the Engine, Inspiration, and Publishing stations, stitching the park to the city on both sides; the Qinghe and Xiaoyuehe riverside trails form the blue-green slow loop [data:geometry/roads.geojson#RD-M-02]. Station-area integration for Dazhongsi, Wudaokou, and Qinghua East Road West stations is a concept suggestion. On utilities, the proposal offers a "tidal compute" concept — off-peak electricity and idle compute serving a rendering farm at night, distributed energy and edge-compute cabinets co-located with community facilities — a fusion-model suggestion only, with no energy-load or utility-capacity calculations [standard:MOHURD-CONTROL-DETAILED-PLANNING].

### Heritage-park vitality belt and urban character

The three park segments couple with the three station plazas into "three acts of creation": the northern Engine act (technology display), the middle Inspiration act (creative life), the southern Publishing act (release celebrations). Character tones by segment: "engine blue" in the north, "academic green" in the middle, "publishing gold" in the south; building massing and roof forms follow the three-tone control-guideline direction. The use of the Tsinghua station cultural resource and the Beijing Film Academy art resource is developed in the culture-narrative section [standard:MOHURD-URBAN-DESIGN-MEASURES].

## Detailed Design of the Three Key Areas

![Three key areas index and design tasks](assets/figures/key-areas.png)

### Engine Station · Zhongzhiyuan AI Autonomy Acceleration Area (~192.1 ha, official value)

**Positioning**: full-stack AI autonomy engine + garden-style creation district [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-001]. **Spatial structure**: "one core, one workshop quarter, one camp" — the engine core (compute centre, foundation-model labs, chip-design workshop, concept layout), the garden R&D workshop quarter (rendering-engine and creation-tool workshops), and the Qinghe riverside creation camp (riverside green + creation stage). **Building renewal**: concept volumes of 5–10 storey low-mid workshop clusters, low density with interwoven green, answering the announcement's "garden-type" requirement. **Transport**: N 5th Ring Road external connections as a concept scheme; the Engine cross link joins the park to the R&D quarter. **AI scenarios**: tidal compute scheduling showcase, AI engine open test field (industry test/validation). **Implementation risks**: the national-platform opportunity and site conditions must be confirmed against official regulatory plans; Qinghe culture work depends on heritage and water-authority materials.

### Inspiration Station · Beijing AI Origin Community (~104.3 ha, official value)

**Positioning**: university-origin creative incubation + near-campus creation district [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-002]. **Spatial structure**: "one courtyard quarter, one plaza, one line" — creative incubation courtyards (around Tsinghua, Peking University and CAS transfer), the Inspiration Plaza (Gate of the Ninth Art), and the Wudaokou–Qinghua East Road West station-area slow line. **Building renewal**: low-disturbance organic renewal, 4–8 storey courtyard concept volumes; keep the neighbourhood fabric around universities, convert underused space into incubation and open-source community lounges. **Public space**: the Tsinghua station memorial court — the first "work" of the Jing-Zhang railway as the origin anchor of AI creation. **AI scenarios**: Jing-Zhang railway immersive drama, AI game-jam camp. **Implementation risks**: campus-park-neighbourhood fusion involves many property owners and must proceed by incremental weaving.

### Publishing Station · Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster (~72.0 ha, official value)

**Positioning**: AI-native content publishing and experience + urban intelligent-economy district [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-003]. **Spatial structure**: "one tower, one stage, one street" — the AI Creation Tower (landmark of the Publishing Plaza, 12 storeys in concept), the virtual-production stage, and the intelligent-terminal experience street. **Transport**: four-quadrant pedestrian connection at the Dazhongsi station intersection as a concept scheme, plus bicycle parking organization. **New business types**: agents, intelligent terminals, and content consumption map to three spatial groups: the tower (publishing and releases), the stage (production), the street (consumption). **AI scenarios**: AI works global release hall, intelligent-terminal experience street, AI copyright registration field (industry test/validation). **Implementation risks**: composite use of planned green space must respect green-space management; public-environment upgrades around enterprises involve property coordination.

## AI Innovation Ecosystem, Talent Personas and AI+ Scenarios

### Five user personas

1. **Independent creator** (solo type): needs low-cost workshops, rendering compute, and an outlet for works — matched by workshops and tidal compute.
2. **University student team** (growing type): needs incubation courtyards, mentor networks, and a release stage — matched by Inspiration Station incubation and the creation festival.
3. **Overseas creator** (mobile type): needs multilingual services, short-term residency, and a copyright channel — matched by the residency programme and the copyright wing.
4. **Player and derivative creator** (experience type): needs the experience street, the arena, and community identity — matched by Publishing Station experience spaces.
5. **Retired digital creator** (long-tail type): needs low-barrier tools and an accessible environment — matched by the accessible creation workshop.

## Land Use, Building Scale and Retain/Renovate/New-Build

The land-use partition is presented in the three-level framework section; this section adds the building-scale logic. The concept gross floor area is about 5.226 million m², the sum of concept footprints (804,000 m²) times concept floor counts; the concept FAR is about 0.46 and concept building density about 7.0% — low-confidence design values, not statutory controls [metric:total_floor_area_sqm] [metric:floor_area_ratio]. The logic of the scale: light industry at the three stations, light services on the two wings, heavy green in the environment — the competitiveness of the content industry lies in environment and community, not tower height.

Retain/renovate/new-build concept classification: **retain** (the heritage park, university-adjacent neighbourhoods, Qinghe/Xiaoyuehe blue-green spaces); **renovate** (underused buildings in the three stations, converted to workshop, incubation, and experience functions); **build new** (concept workshops, the Creation Tower, the virtual-production stage, talent housing). The five statutory controls — FAR, building height, building density, green ratio, setbacks — are uniformly marked as pending official data, with the recalculation path stated for when data arrives [depth:development_intensity_controls].

## Transport, Rail, Municipal and Public Service Facilities

The slow-mobility system is "one mainline, three cross links, two riversides": the Creative Mainline (9.6 km concept alignment) links the three park segments; the three cross links run east-west at station locations; the Qinghe and Xiaoyuehe riverside trails close the waterfront loop [data:geometry/roads.geojson#RD-M-06]. On rail, Dazhongsi station integration and Wudaokou / Qinghua East Road West station integration are concept suggestions; bicycle parking is organized at two levels (station quadrants + workshop clusters). On utilities, the "tidal compute" concept is proposed: off-peak night power driving a rendering farm and idle-time training scheduling, edge-compute cabinets co-located with community facilities — a fusion-model suggestion, not an energy-load or utility-capacity conclusion [depth:municipal_new_infrastructure].

Public service facilities follow the "creation service chain": innovation service platforms (copyright registration, model evaluation, release roadshows) in the Tech-Service Wing; talent living services (talent apartments, community study halls, sports facilities) at Inspiration Station and the campus-fusion band; new infrastructure (edge compute, distributed energy) co-located with utility stations [data:geometry/buildings.geojson#BD-E-01].

## Blue-Green Space, Public Space and Urban Character

The blue-green system is "one track, two rivers, four gardens": the three segments of the Creative Mainline park form the main green axis; the Qinghe and Xiaoyuehe riverside greens are the two waterfront bands; four pocket parks sit inside neighbourhoods. The green ratio of 38.2% (concept value) is intended to make the "creation environment" a public good of the belt [metric:green_ratio] [data:geometry/green_space.geojson#GS-SPN].

### Three AI pilgrimage landmarks (concept suggestions)

1. **Engine Sanctum** (Zhongzhiyuan · Engine Plaza): an exhibition space honouring AI infrastructure builders — chips, models, and engines are "pilgrimaged" here, and the honour display gallery records the milestones of self-reliant innovation.
2. **Gate of the Ninth Art** (Origin Community · Inspiration Plaza): an interactive arch of pixel matrices; passing through it means entering the creation zone — everyone can create.
3. **AI Creation Tower** (Dazhongsi · Publishing Plaza): the first-release lighthouse for global AI works; its facade screens release new works and milestones, and the "AI Bell" at the top tolls at major releases — echoing Dazhongsi's bell culture.

Together with the Tsinghua station memorial court, they form the "creation pilgrimage route": from the Engine Sanctum, through the Gate of the Ninth Art, to the AI Creation Tower [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#PS-03]. All landmarks are concept suggestions, unapproved, and must not be stated as confirmed construction arrangements.

### Character control direction

The three tones (engine blue / academic green / publishing gold) govern roof forms, massing, and colour: flat roofs with equipment terraces in the Engine segment express technical transparency; pitched roofs with courtyard fabric in the Inspiration segment echo the campus context; tower-plus-megascreen facades in the Publishing segment express release celebration. Specific height, massing, and colour controls follow official regulatory plans and urban design guidelines [standard:MOHURD-URBAN-DESIGN-MEASURES].

## Renewal Project List, Policies and Phasing

Renewal project list (concept suggestions): **Engine Station renewal unit** (compute centre and workshop clusters, dependent on the national-platform opportunity and regulatory-plan confirmation); **Inspiration Station renewal unit** (incubation courtyards and station-area integration, dependent on multi-owner coordination); **Publishing Station renewal unit** (Creation Tower, virtual-production stage, experience street, dependent on green-space composite-use policy); **two-wing weaving units** (IP/copyright centre in the Tech-Service Wing, Xiaoyuehe scenario trail); **park linkage unit** (slow-mobility connection and gap-stitching of the three park segments) [depth:renewal_project_list].

Phasing has three periods; phasing.geojson covers the whole boundary without overlap [data:geometry/phasing.geojson#PH-1]: **near term (2026–2028) two-station opening** — Engine and Publishing stations first, the compute foundation and the content-publishing double gateway; **mid term (2028–2031) belt connection** — Inspiration Station, the Qinghe creation band, and the South Gateway take shape, the mainline runs through; **long term (2031–2035) full-belt operation** — the campus-fusion band and Tech-Service Wing renew as a whole, entering the international operation period.

### Global AI activity system and long-term operation (concept suggestions)

**Annual activity system**: the Jing-Zhang AI Creation Festival (spring, whole belt), the 48-hour Creation Marathon (summer/autumn, rotating among the three stations), the AI Works Global Release Season (winter, Creation Tower). **Developer community operation**: the open-source community lounge operates year-round; scenario opening follows the "apply–test–evaluate–exit" closed loop. **International communication and conversion**: "Ninth Art Belt" as the international visual identity, a residency programme for overseas creators, and the release season as the brand-asset builder. All of the above are concept suggestions, not confirmed government arrangements or investment commitments [standard:PROJECT-AGENT-OPEN-CALL-TASKBOOK].
