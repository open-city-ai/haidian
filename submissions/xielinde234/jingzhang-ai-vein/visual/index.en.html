The first trunk railway independently designed by Chinese engineers, built in 1909 under Zhan Tianyou, finds new spatial expression in the AI era of 2026.

Three Key Areas

Three key areas correspond to the 'source — heart — outlet' segments of the innovation chain, at the urban design depth of comprehensive planning implementation.

01

Multi-Wisdom Park (Source Station)

Innovation Chain RoleTechnical source of AI full-stack innovation · Basic Research
Area192.1 ha
LocationNorthern heritage park · Qinghe interface

Spatial Structure

Organize 3-4 research clusters (large models, embodied intelligence, AI for Science), place a distributed computing hub at the center, set up a 'Researcher's Promenade' along the northern heritage park as a communication green corridor, and reserve an autonomous vehicle shuttle test segment connecting to the Core Station.

AI Scenarios

AI+research assistance, computing sharing platform, open-source model incubator, AI for Science lab

Landmark

'Centennial Track' Memorial — fusing rail fragments with circuit board patterns, NFC interaction, echoing 1909 engineering and 2026 AI innovation

02

AI Origin Community (Core Station)

Innovation Chain RoleHeart of world-class AI innovation ecosystem · Innovation Ecosystem
Area104.3 ha
LocationCentral corridor · Near-campus interface

Spatial Structure

Centered on the 'Open Source Plaza' to organize a public space network, with cafes, co-working spaces, and maker spaces as third places, an AI life experience zone (AI+healthcare, AI+education, AI+legal), and east-west connection to the Xiaoyue River Scenario Wing.

AI Scenarios

Agent contribution honor wall, open-source achievement gallery, AI community council, AI+adaptive learning space

Landmark

Agent Honor Wall — living wall, real-time display of AI agent contributions, e-ink screen + physical plaques, annual engraving

03

Dazhongsi (Market Station)

Innovation Chain RoleAI industry commercialization outlet · Industry Application
Area72.0 ha
LocationSouthern corridor · Around Dazhongsi Station

Spatial Structure

Organize smart commercial streets (unmanned retail, AI shopping guides, virtual fitting), enterprise service hub (AI accelerator, compliance service center, data trading platform), industry showcase window, and TOD complex (integrating commerce, office, and public services).

AI Scenarios

AI+smart commerce experience, robot delivery corridor, AI+enterprise compliance service, native intelligent consumption scenarios

Landmark

'Open Source Lighthouse' — LED light array code waterfall, developer exchange space at the base, illuminating the industry outlet

1 Track · 3 Stations · 2 Wings

One Track, Three Stations, Two Wings — Spatial Structure

One Track: the Jingzhang Heritage Park AI innovation axis running north-south. Three Stations: Source, Core, and Market. Two Wings: the Zhongguancun IP Wing and the Xiaoyue River Scenario Wing.

Three Cultural Layers

The cultural narrative follows "from iron track to intelligence track — the continuation of a century of innovation spirit," organizing three cultural layers.

1909

Jingzhang Railway Culture

Spirit：Self-reliance, engineering patriotism
Spatial Carrier：Qinghuayuan Station ruins, rail remnants
1980s

Zhongguancun Innovation Culture

Spirit：Reform breakthrough, tech patriotism
Spatial Carrier：Zhongguancun Ave, startup story nodes
2020s

AI New Culture

Spirit：Open-source co-creation, AI for good
Spatial Carrier：Open Source Plaza, Honor Wall, developer community
