# RENEW·JZ narrative and evidence index

This repair package presents a concept suggestion for urban-renewal coordination along the Jingzhang corridor. Its fixed contract is the three-area/three-node mapping: Zhongzhiyuan AI Self-Innovation Acceleration Area - UNIT·BLOCK; Beijing AI Origin Community - KEEP·BLOCK; Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster - SUPERVISION PAVILION. The two official wings are 中关村科技服务翼 / Zhongguancun Technology Service Wing and 小月河场景赋能翼 / Xiaoyuehe Scenario Empowerment Wing. Future Science City, Huairou Science City, E-Town and Jing-Jin-Ji are supplemental collaboration objects only.

The package contains 13 required proposal sections, 10 scenario cards, three test protocols, five persona groups, eight benchmark cases, nine GeoJSON layers, 14 bilingual figures, four bilingual A0/A3 PDFs, 23 compliance requirements, 15 design-depth items and the full rights/QC record. All geometry and derived metrics are provisional concept evidence, not official survey, statutory control or current-state conclusions.

Figure and page checks are recorded in `visual/assets/figure_qc.json`; the final local render set is retained in the shared workspace under `work/qa-3881-final/`.
