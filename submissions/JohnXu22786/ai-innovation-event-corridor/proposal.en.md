# Jingzhang AI Innovation Event Corridor: RELAY·JZ

## Basis, evidence and limits

Design intent: translate the open-call brief into traceable scope, deliverables and verification order rather than present concept assumptions as site facts. The public announcement and taskbook are the basis [source:DATA-SRC-OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT-20260509] [standard:PROJECT-AGENT-OPEN-CALL-TASKBOOK]; package geometry and counts are provisional and are recalculated through [metric:land_use_feature_count] and [metric:scenario_node_count]. Official GIS/CAD, ownership, heritage, transport, fire, accessibility and event baselines remain data gaps, so each siting, capacity and existing-condition statement stays provisional until a named responsible party verifies it.

This is a concept package for the Haidian Centennial Jingzhang AI Innovation Belt open call. The announcement and taskbook support scope and task response. Geometry, areas, nodes and programs are provisional package data, never a redline, statutory plan, approval, investment or partnership commitment. Official GIS/CAD, ownership, heritage, access, transport and event baselines were not supplied.

## Vision, three positionings, five functions and three areas/two wings

The design intent is to connect the three positionings, five functions, three areas and two wings to explicit spatial, industry, scenario and operations closures. Cell Workshop, Pattern Platform and Growth Garden remain reversible motifs; N01-N08, L01-L03, P01-P05 and O01-O06 make them testable. The crosswalk is supported by [source:DATA-SRC-OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT-20260509] and [standard:PROJECT-AGENT-OPEN-CALL-TASKBOOK], while boundaries, responsibilities, partnerships, transport capacity and statutory planning inputs remain provisional. Counts are reconciled in [metric:scenario_node_count] and the bilingual count contract.

RELAY·JZ translates relay into content relay (events and contributions), spatial relay (east-west stitching and north-south continuity), and capability relay (developers, enterprises and communities). The core idea keeps Cell Workshop / 元胞坊, Pattern Platform / 智纹台 and Growth Garden / 生长庭.

Three positionings: P1 Centennial Jingzhang cultural belt; P2 urban AI living-experience belt; P3 AI-integrated innovation belt. Five functions: F1 full-stack AI self-innovation, F2 world-class AI ecosystem, F3 AI-enabled scenario paradigm, F4 lively intelligent city, and F5 global voice in AI governance. Three areas are AI Origin Community, Zhongzhi Innovation Accelerator Area and Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster; the two wings are the Zhongguancun Technology Service Wing and Xiaoyuehe Scenario Enablement Wing. Their boundaries and responsibilities remain provisional.

## Agent 3: SC-01 to SC-10 scenario cards

The ten cards use a common contract: user, problem, node, minimised data, AI input/output, human review, offline/accessibility fallback, operator, KPI and shutdown condition. Their purpose is to make AI public services rejectable and auditable, not autonomous or mandatory. Each card maps to compliance_matrix.json#scenario_crosswalk, [metric:scenario_card_count] and one or more nodes; baseline, sample boundary, consent, safety, accessibility and responsible parties still require confirmation before a pilot.

Each card states user, problem, node, minimised data, AI input/output, human review, offline/accessibility fallback, operator, KPI and shutdown condition.

### SC-01 Event scheduling and registration
- User: 参会者/居民
- Problem: 多会期信息分散
- Spatial node: N01 PS-LM00 Forum Plaza
- Data minimisation: 匿名报名意向、时间窗、无障碍偏好
- AI input/output: 活动元数据与偏好 -> 候选日程/冲突提示
- Human review: 活动运营员逐项确认
- Offline/accessibility fallback: 纸质日程、人工台、电话入口；轮椅与大字版

## TP-01 to TP-03 industry test protocols

The three protocols define object, node, baseline, target, sample boundary, cycle, responsibility, risk threshold and exit so that enterprise and developer claims can be tested without exposing secrets. TP-01 is a small public-code safety test, TP-02 a multilingual accessible navigation test, and TP-03 a non-confidential problem-to-scenario conversion test. Protocol count and anchors are mirrored in [metric:protocol_count] and compliance_matrix.json#protocol_crosswalk; all participants, agreements, baselines and legal reviews remain provisional.

### TP-01 Developer co-creation safety test
- Object and nodes: 匿名公开样例任务；N02
- Baseline and concept target: 基线：首次通过率/人工耗时；目标：耗时下降20%且安全扫描100%通过
- Sample boundary and cycle: ≤20组自愿团队、公开代码；8周
- Responsible role: 开发者社区运营员+安全导师
- Risk threshold: 密钥泄露、建议错误率或人工拒绝超阈值
- Exit: 撤下模型建议，转纸面任务卡；复盘后扩展/停止
- Status: proposed small pilot only; not approved and not a partnership or deployment commitment.

### TP-02 Multilingual accessible event navigation
- Object and nodes: 公开会期信息；N01-N03
- Baseline and concept target: 基线：问路次数/字幕抽检；目标：问路下降15%、字幕准确率≥95%
- Sample boundary and cycle: ≤3个会期、自愿反馈；3个月
- Responsible role: 公共体验服务组+无障碍顾问（待聘）
- Risk threshold: 安全播报错误或求助>5分钟
- Exit: 切换人工台和纸质指引；记录纠错后再评估
- Status: proposed small pilot only; not approved and not a partnership or deployment commitment.

### TP-03 Enterprise problem to protocol conversion
- Object and nodes: 非保密问题摘要；N02/N06
- Baseline and concept target: 基线：登记到协议耗时；目标：中位≤14天、退出条件完整率100%
- Sample boundary and cycle: ≤10家自愿企业；12周
- Responsible role: 产业转化协调组+法务/数据顾问（待核）
- Risk threshold: 商业秘密疑虑、责任人缺失或风险超阈值
- Exit: 删除摘要、停止撮合、保留非敏感过程记录
- Status: proposed small pilot only; not approved and not a partnership or deployment commitment.

## Agent 4: public space, landmarks and component library

Agent 4 turns east-west stitching and north-south continuity into surveyable public interfaces, three concept landmarks, an honor/contribution record and five removable components. Forum Plaza, Code Dock and Loop Gallery are not confirmed sites; their access, fire, heritage, ownership and maintenance conditions must pass decision gates.
