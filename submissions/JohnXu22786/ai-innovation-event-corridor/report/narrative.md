# Formal narrative

RELAY·JZ turns relay into a reversible urban operating loop: Cell Workshop makes small public modules, Pattern Platform connects open problems to safe tests, and Growth Garden keeps daily community and blue-green operations visible. Three concept landmarks and eight scenario nodes make the loop legible without claiming that any site, partnership or approval already exists.

SC-01—SC-10, TP-01—TP-03 and O01—O06 are bounded by minimal data, human review, offline/accessibility fallback and shutdown conditions. Official geometry, heritage, ownership, transport, accessibility and data-security inputs remain the trigger for recalculation or physical deployment.
