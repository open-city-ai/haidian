## Coordinated research: industry and future-city study

### agent.1: three positions, five functions and task chain

Three positions: a public explanation interface for district carbon accounting; a low-carbon innovation and exchange environment carried by blue-green slow mobility; and a reversible, auditable AI test ground. Five functions: carbon ledger and indicator explanation; green-space and low-carbon mobility; enterprise/university validation; public and accessible service; annual operations and international communication.

Task chain: call/taskbook → three zones/two wings → three-node service choreography → S01–S12 tests → human review/appeal/stop → quarterly review → phased update. The ledger compares like-for-like fields but does not profile people; the ring offers mobility and blue-green experience but promises no reduction; the block is a reversible test unit but promises no zero-carbon building.

## Overall design, renewal and control-depth study

The differentiating method is “carbon ledger—green belt—test unit”: publish interpretable fields, confidence and provenance in a public interface; bind each AI output to a walkable, observable, human-controllable node; and decide continue, degrade or delete rather than auto-deploy. It differs from generic TOD or smart-traffic proposals because the ledger is a contestable public service and every path has paper, human and power-off versions.

Brand suggestion: GREEN CARBON RING. The ring line, Jingzhang rust and low-carbon cyan are concept visual language, not an official logo. Heritage marks, institutional marks, station logos and existing landmark graphics require separate permission; the figures use only original vector symbols.

## Key-area detailed design

N1 Green-Carbon Block: building edge—semi-outdoor corridor—planting/rain strip—slow path—public space; entry from the slow ring, human advice desk, paper ledger, removable shade and booked test table.  
N2 Carbon-Wisdom Pavilion: bilingual screen/paper panel, side human help desk and waiting seat; a version light shows update and confidence, never identity.  
N3 Green-Energy Ring: walking mainline, cycling/shuttle line and service/emergency line; rain garden, pocket public space and accessible wayfinding form a continuous experience. Slope, width, fire, rail and municipal controls require engineering review.

Journey: enter at N3 → bilingual wayfinding/human option → read source, confidence and update at N2 → optionally submit aggregate feedback → join a reversible test at N1 → operator logs event → monthly review continues, degrades or stops. Missing data, a threshold breach or an appeal returns the service to paper, human help and static notice.

P3 annual operation: public summary, developer community and scenario entry/exit; two consecutive unexplained or unmaintained cycles delete the scenario.

### Carbon accounting—space—operation and phase gates

The ledger accepts only aggregate fields such as zone, month and energy/mobility/green category. The model performs unit conversion, interval estimation and anomaly prompts; it does not produce personal footprints or automatically dispatch facilities. Outputs land at N1 Green-Carbon Block, N2 Carbon-Wisdom Pavilion and N3 Green-Energy Ring as paper cards, static signs and human Q&A. Missing factors, insufficient samples, unexplained differences, privacy incidents or no human role trigger static fallback and card shutdown; raw feedback is deleted per card while de-identified version logs support audit.

| Phase | Preconditions | Role / output | Acceptance and maintenance | Exit |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Near 1–3 years | No engineering before official boundary/baseline; start with paper fields, accessibility walk and desk tests | Initiator R; accountable owner unconfirmed; dictionary, cards, static signs | Human review, complaint closure and provenance complete; monthly maintenance | Missing fields, owner or explanation blocks expansion |
