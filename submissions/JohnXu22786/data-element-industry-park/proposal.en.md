# DATA·JZ Data-Element Loop

> Data flows like water; the loop gives it a readable public channel. Registration is the entry, controlled testing the middle, and literacy the public exit.

This is a concept proposal, not an approved site, policy, engineering design, partnership, funding commitment or operating arrangement. It retains the three-node idea: the Data-Element Service Centre on the Zhongzhiyuan side publishes directory and registration guidance; the Data Circulation Test Field on the Beijing AI Origin Community side hosts controlled tests; the Data Literacy Hall on the Dazhongsi side provides bilingual public learning and referral. The walk-and-cycle-first loop is a provisional spatial organizer. Official boundaries, controls, ownership, heritage, fire and accessibility records must be checked by a professional team before any further decision.

### AI planning iteration

Directory, spatial, mobility/blue-green and public-service agents submit alternatives using only authorized synthetic or aggregated inputs. A human panel compares at least two concept scenarios against accessibility, fire, heritage, ownership, privacy and source boundaries, publishes a difference note, and feeds the resident forum into the next round. Only aggregated data; human review for key decisions; no excessive surveillance. Offline fallback is paper, staffed service, class and on-site/telephone referral.

The spatial structure is one loop, three nodes, three landmarks and two wing interfaces. All boundaries, paths and nodes are provisional concepts, not redlines, rail alignments, bridges, utilities or engineering conclusions. ## Detailed design of key areas

**N1 Service Centre / Zhongzhiyuan.** Components are entry wayfinding, public directory wall, registration guidance desk, authorised-operation intake and appeal referral. Inputs are public metadata and voluntarily supplied non-sensitive material. Outputs are completeness prompts, human explanation, referrals and aggregate public notes; no ownership, approval, trading or legal determination. Missing rights, source or appeal closure pauses the service.

**N2 Test Field / Beijing AI Origin Community.** Components are a controlled test room, observation window, report wall, human takeover desk and offline cabinet. It accepts synthetic, voluntary or de-identified test inputs only. The sandbox assistant gives checklist prompts, never an audit conclusion and never handles personal data. Temporary inputs are deleted on exit; necessary audit summaries follow the rights boundary. T01–T03 are small, pausable and reviewable.

**N3 Literacy Hall / Dazhongsi.** Components are Jing-Zhang memory wayfinding, bilingual gallery, paper classes for children and older people, staffed questions and accessible rest points. Paper, icons, staff and tactile low-position signs remain available without an app or login. Eight service groups—residents, older people, children/companions, youth developers, enterprises, tourists, low-digital-literacy users and people needing accessibility support—receive differentiated service, not individual profiling.

**Three concept landmarks and honor display.** Registration Hall makes public directories and contribution plaques readable; Sandbox Window shows admission, testing, review and exit without exposing raw data; Literacy Lesson teaches rights and help routes to children, older people and visitors without collecting identity. Honors record only public, verifiable research, community contribution or compliance practice; they are not a commercial ranking or registered mark.

The internal DATA·JZ identity direction uses an orbit-and-node mark, blue/green for public space, orange only for provisional/risk notices, and dark ink for text. N1/N2/N3 subtitles are bilingual. The logo and honor boards document color, type hierarchy, graphic syntax and prohibited uses; no third-party marks, portraits or photographs are embedded. The local embeddable Noto Sans SC font is used in the generated deliverables.

## Land use, building scale and retain-renovate-demolish approach

Concept land-use families are data service, innovation/testing, public green, slow mobility and public service. The classification vocabulary does not alter statutory zoning. The broad park-green layer in the figure is for structural reading only and is not the formal geometry ratio. Existing buildings and reversible fit-out are preferred; official surveys, ownership, heritage, fire and accessibility checks precede any decision.

## Transport, rail, municipal and public-service facilities

Walking, cycling and accessible continuity are priorities. Existing rail and bus access is an arrival concept, not a new station or alignment. No road redline, section, bridge, tunnel, capacity or utility design is proposed. Signs, seats, lighting, staff and paper information address gaps conceptually; engineering measures require later professional work. The three nodes share public service, and all retain paper/staff fallback during a network outage.

## Blue-green space, public realm and urban character

Rail memory, greenbelt and data loop form three readable layers. The component library includes public boards, shade seating, low/tactile signs, rest points, movable class kits, low-glare lighting and appeal referrals. No sensor, screen or identity tracking is a condition of entry. Figure colors are paired with text and line types. Blue/green, heritage, ownership, fire and access conditions remain to verify.

## Risk, copyright and compliance statement

The primary risk is mistaking a concept for a decision. Every narrative, figure, HTML, PDF and manifest marks provisional status, source, formula, confidence and recomputation trigger. The data boundary is public, synthetic, voluntary or de-identified input only; only aggregated data; human review for key decisions; no excessive surveillance. No individual-identifying tracking or large-scale behavior profile is proposed. Eight additional cases have itemized URLs, dates, permitted uses, prohibited uses and unresolved rights boundaries in `sources.json`. No third-party logo, portrait, photograph or paper figure is embedded; DATA·JZ is an internal concept mark.

Complaints, annual public notes, annual activities, human referral and audit summaries are minimum trust mechanisms. A service that cannot explain its source, provide a manual fallback, pass accessibility checks or execute an exit does not advance. All work is an open co-creation suggestion and reference scheme; it does not replace statutory planning or constitute an official conclusion, cooperation, investment/funding promise or engineering feasibility finding.
