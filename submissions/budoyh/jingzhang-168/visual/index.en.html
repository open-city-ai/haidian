GOVERNANCE / SEPARATION OF POWERS

Scheduling, admission, stopping and audit do not belong to one actor—or to a model.

Accountability is a set of veto, signature and recusal powers, not placeholder organisation names. Named institutions, legal mandate, budget, capacity and SLO remain pending.

Ten proposed functional roles | holders and authority pending

TOTimetable Operator: schedules admitted slots, resolves conflicts and executes return.
PTCPublic Time Council: sets fairness, quiet and access rules, hears appeals and leads public review.
SASPScenario Admission & Safety Panel: technical admission, stopping and return acceptance; never substitutes for permission.
DHLDuty Human Lead: activates, pauses, runs the non-AI equivalent and safeguards people.
DSData Steward: may veto collection, export and non-compliant retention.
AOAccessibility Officer: rejects inaccessible routes or channels and may stop service.
NLNeighbor Liaison: represents nonparticipants on quiet, routes and public-space impact.
PAProfessional and Permitting Authority Liaison: interfaces with competent decisions without claiming authority.
OPSArea, Counter and Maintenance Operator Team: delivers field service, maintenance and return.
IEIndependent Evaluator: reviews outcomes, harms, distribution, appeals, SLO and restoration evidence.
