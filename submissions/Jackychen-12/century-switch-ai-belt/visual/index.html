  • A-CONTROLS-001 pending_professional_confirmation
    Detailed regulatory controls, road redlines, ownership, municipal utilities, and engineering constraints must be confirmed from official attachments before statutory use.
  • A-BOUNDARY-002 provisional
    The submitted site boundary and three key-area polygons are provisional placeholders derived from public announcement extents, not official redlines.
  • A-KEYAREA-003 provisional
    Official announcement areas for Zhongzhiyuan (1.921 km2), Beijing AI Origin Community (1.043 km2) and Dazhongsi (0.72 km2) are used as anchors; the provisional key-area polygons are not used as a precise-area basis.
  • A-CONCEPT-004 design_proposal
    The 'Century Switch' (three stations, two tracks, one belt) spatial structure is an agent design proposal translating the three-tier scope into a walkable narrative spine, not an approved plan.

    Building density, floor area ratio, height, green ratio controls and setbacks in metrics.json are conceptual references; official control values are listed as unknown.
