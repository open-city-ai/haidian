Three-Level Scope

The proposal is organised in three progressive scopes — coordinated research, overall design, and key-area detailed design — binding industry judgments, urban renewal, and key-area design to geometry layers and metrics.

project_scope_summary.csv

Coordinated Research Area

43.6 km2: AI industry ecosystem, Three-Zone Two-Wing coordination, future urban form, and cultural narrative.

  • AI innovation and talent chains
  • Global case-transfer mechanism
  • Brand naming and communication

Overall Design Area

11.4 km2: urban renewal framework, land-use structure, transport and municipal support, Jing-Zhang Heritage Park activation belt.

  • Regulatory-plan-level urban design
  • Renewal project list
  • Blue-green slow-travel system

Key Areas

Three key areas for detailed design, specifying programme, building scale, retain-renovate-demolish classification, public-space connectivity, and mobility.

  • Zhongzhiyuan AI Innovation Acceleration Zone
  • Beijing AI Origin Community
  • Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster

Key Areas

All three key areas must have industry positioning, spatial strategy, building and public-space actions, transport interface, and implementation risk statements.

key_areas.geojson

Zhongzhiyuan AI Innovation Acceleration Zone

Garden-style autonomous innovation district. National platform, industrial display, Qinghe culture, external transport, and low-carbon innovation exchange space.

Beijing AI Origin Community

Near-campus technology-transfer district. University source innovation, open-source community, talent services, retain-renovate-demolish, and station-area integration.

Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster

Urban AI economy district. Leading enterprises, intelligent agents, commercial services, mixed green-space use, and four-quadrant pedestrian connectivity.

AI Scenarios
