Three-level scope

The proposal progresses from the coordinated research area to the overall design area to the key detailed design area, binding industrial judgments, urban renewal, and district design to layers and metrics.

three-level scope

Coordinated research area

AI industry ecosystem, three-areas-two-wings synergy, future-city form, and cultural narrative across 43.6 km².

  • AI innovation & talent chains
  • Global case transfer mechanisms
  • Branding and communication

Overall design area

Urban renewal, land-use structure, transport and municipal services, and the heritage-park vitality belt across 11.4 km².

  • Regulatory-plan-level urban design
  • Renewal project list
  • Blue-green slow-mobility system

Key detailed design area

Detailed design of three key districts: function and programs, building renewal, public space, and transport organization.

  • Zhongzhiyuan AI Acceleration Area

Key areas

Each key area documents positioning, spatial strategy, building and public-space moves, transport interfaces, and implementation risks.

key_areas.geojson

Zhongzhiyuan AI Acceleration Area

approx. 192.1 ha. Bearer of the AI full-stack self-made innovation system and governance voice: R&D clusters + shared pilot + self-made reserve area, Compute Lighthouse and open-source developer camp.

Beijing AI Origin Community

approx. 104.3 ha. World-class AI ecosystem and university-incubator-fund walking circle: Origin Plaza at the heart, start-up community, agent co-creation workshops, and talent apartments in mixed use.

Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster

approx. 72 ha. Intelligence-native consumption and business district: the Intelligent Machine Gallery as skeleton, robot retail, digital consumption laboratory, and AI art performances.

Land use / Mobility / Blue-green public space

The land-use partition covers the full submitted boundary with no gaps or overlaps and correct shared-edge topology; areas are recalculated in EPSG:4548. The green-corridor slow-mobility spine plus transverse links form a one-spine multi-link network, integrated with the blue-green public space system. The Jing-Zhang heritage green corridor is the north-south spine, connecting the Xiaoyuehe blue line in a one-corridor-one-river-many-parks structure; three main plazas and pocket parks form a hierarchical public-space network.

AI scenarios

12 AI scenario cards cover transport, culture, health, services, governance, logistics, education, legal, lifestyle, public space, and industry test-and-validation, all retaining human review.

12 scenario cards
01 AI+Transport · Smart slow-mobility assessment

Sensing pedestrians and cyclists along the corridor identifies gaps; pilots after transport-authority review.

02 AI+Culture · Digital Jing-Zhang guide

AR reconstruction of century-old railway scenes; content reviewed by heritage experts.

03 AI+Health · Community health station

Chronic-disease management and medical navigation; localized data; human review.

04 AI+Services · Enterprise service copilot

One-stop Q&A on policy, compliance, and scenario applications with links to official sources.

05 AI+Governance · Public-safety operations review

Incident monitoring with a closed human-review loop; no unsupervised decisions.

06 AI+Logistics · Low-speed autonomous delivery

Restricted routes, low speed, supervised pilot.

07 AI+Education · Century-railway STEM lab

AI literacy courses for primary and secondary students.

08 AI+Legal · Smart compliance advisory

For start-ups, citing public regulations with uncertainty disclosed.

09 AI+Lifestyle · Smart convenience & shared spaces

Nighttime services and youth third places.

10 AI+Public space · AI art installations

Reviewed by a public-art commission.

Efficiency and scheduling tests for compute infrastructure.
