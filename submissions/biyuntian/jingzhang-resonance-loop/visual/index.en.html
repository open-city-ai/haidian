R01–R06 delivery cards: six interfaces, not just one concept line

Every interface begins with an observable everyday question and closes with a reversible action, an offline retained benefit and a stop condition. Anchors guide later verification only; they are not coordinates, works boundaries or permits.

R01–R02 · north

Railway-memory threshold / campus–park transition. Walk shade, seating, orientation, rain refuge and accessibility gaps first; retain a printed route map, issue card and movable seating.

Exit: where heritage, crossing, fire or accessibility conditions are unclear, retain observation and staffed interpretation only.

R03–R04 · middle

Trusted-test return court / open-source knowledge front desk. Temporary shade, printed explanation, result cards and consultation appear only when the court and campus-edge front desk remain useful every day.

Exit: if movement is blocked, no one can take over, or rights/maintenance are unclear, remove display and return to staffed service.

R05–R06 · south

Cross-line safety stitch / station-city everyday gate. Check continuity at peaks, in rain and at night; use only temporary static guidance, accessible stopping and staffed help.

Exit: for road/rail works, crowding, complaint or safety risk, restore ordinary static service.
