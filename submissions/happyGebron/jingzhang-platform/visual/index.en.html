key_areas.geojson

Zhongzhiyuan AI Self-Innovation Acceleration Area

A garden-type self-innovation block. Key expressions: national platforms, industry exhibition, Qinghe culture, external transport, and low-carbon exchange space.

Beijing AI Origin Community

A university-adjacent transformation block. Key expressions: university source innovation, open-source community, talent services, retain/renovate/demolish, and station integration.

Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster Area

An urban smart-economy block. Key expressions: leading enterprises, agent-based new business forms, commercial services, composite green-space use, and four-quadrant intersection connectivity.

AI scenarios

Scenarios are not slogans: each must state service object, spatial location, data sources, privacy boundaries, human review, and operating entity.

12 scenario cards + 3 test scenarios
01 Open-source release hall

Achievement release and model-evaluation node for developers, enterprises, and universities.

02 Civic agent sandbox

Tests transport, service, and operations agents in controlled public space.

03 Slow-traffic gap diagnosis

Uses public data and human review to identify heritage-park slow-traffic gaps.

04 Talent life butler

Connects housing, learning, consumption, sports, and social services.

05 AI safety-governance corridor

Displays standard setting, trusted evaluation, and safety-governance capabilities.

06 Campus-enterprise transformation lounge

Supports Tsinghua, PKU, and CAS achievement-transformation meetings and roadshows.

07 Data-element theater

Dazhongsi-area display of data assets, smart terminals, and content consumption.

08 Low-carbon computing station

Explains the integration of distributed energy, on-device computing, and public-service facilities.

09 Jingzhang memory route

Links railway heritage, Zhongguancun innovation culture, and AI new culture.

10 Global AI activity-week route

Organizes developer festivals, scenario open days, competition roadshows, and urban experience routes.

11 Platform shared waiting pavilion

Composes shared work, meetings, flash displays, and rest in a hybrid waiting space using the waiting-platform semantics.

12 Open-source collaboration contribution wall

Digital contribution wall displaying public participation in open source, standards, datasets, and community governance.

Governance: each scenario card is anchored to concrete nodes or space types in the report and carries data governance (data sources and privacy boundaries), maturity stages (M0 concept / M1 pilot / M2 operation), pilot period, human review, exit mechanism, and quantitative KPIs (conceptual indicators, not committed targets); 01 open-source release hall, 02 civic agent sandbox, and 03 slow-traffic gap diagnosis are the pilot test scenarios.

Inclusivity: six personas are covered - older adults, children, people with disabilities, low-income residents, caregivers, and non-smart-device users; every digital scenario provides an offline equivalent service and an algorithm appeal channel, with an annual third-party manual accessibility inspection (assumptions.json A-ACCESSIBILITY-001).
