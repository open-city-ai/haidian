Core Philosophy
Human-AI Co-living
The Jing-Zhang Railway heritage is no longer solely a human memorial space; it becomes an urban public space where humans and digital life converse bidirectionally and cohabit. People input memory into the site; digital life translates memory into landscape light and shadow. The two nourish each other.
🤝

Rejecting "AI as Spectacle"

We do not pursue flashy technology stacking. The ultimate goal of all AI and digital installations is to serve the emotions and memories of ordinary people. Digital life is not an object to be viewed, but a presence that walks with you in the park.

🌳

Innovation District Within a Park

43.8% green ratio (including waterfront green belts); nearly 30% of land is public open space. R&D and commercial functions are embedded within the park framework, not the traditional "buildings + green space" campus model. Green first, city second.

🔓

Open Source as Public Good

Interactions on public infrastructure are open-sourced to all. Anyone can write an interaction for a bench, leave a ring on the path. The city is not the designer's product—it is the living result of all its residents.

🛤️

The Rails Remember Everything

The rails of Jing-Zhang do not speak, yet they remember everyone who walked upon them. We hope AI will remember too—children's laughter, the silence of elders, a sentence someone left one evening.

Signature Design
Guangcheng Signature · Three Core Creative Nodes
This is the soul of the proposal—what sets it apart from ordinary industry planning. All three nodes are located in the Central Core, AI Origin Community, forming the most concrete spatial expression of "human-AI co-living."
01
🛤️

Innovation Annual Ring Path

"Every step, treading upon time."

Laid along the old railway bed, the path surface embeds sensing and light matrices. Century-old Jing-Zhang history, Zhongguancun innovation events, and public memories of visitors are engraved layer by layer as annual ring patterns.

Each new visitor interaction generates a new ring. The rings are never deleted; they accumulate continuously—the site grows as people arrive.

Material: echoes railway sleeper texture | Time markers: 1909→1949→1978→1999→2026→∞
02
🪑

Open-Source Bench

"Public infrastructure, created by all."

Not merely resting furniture but open-source interactive carriers. Lightweight sensing modules are integrated; citizens can develop custom interactions through open APIs; touch and voice leave emotions, short phrases, and stories.

The benches belong to the city; interactive creativity is open-sourced to all. Anyone can contribute their own small design. All submissions undergo community review before going live.

Material: rust-colored metal frame + wooden seat | Tech modules embedded and hidden | Interactive lights at night
03
🌿

AI Symbiosis Garden

"No fixed script. Every moment is one of a kind."

The core immersive node. Physical plant landscapes and digital life landscapes are interwoven and coexisting. AI digital life evolves dynamically with pedestrian flow, seasons, and weather; physical plant growth in turn influences digital behavioral logic.

No fixed performance script. When a person enters, digital life senses their position and state, approaching or withdrawing—genuine bidirectional interaction.

Tech: ground projection + mist + ambient sound | No large screens | Existing trees + native wildflowers
