Three-level scope

Coordinated research, overall design, and key-area detail progressively bind industry judgment, urban renewal, and district design to layers and metrics.

Coordinated research area

AI ecosystem, three-area two-wing synergy, future city form, and culture over 43.6 km².

Overall design area

Urban renewal, land-use structure, transport, and the heritage-park vitality belt over 11.4 km².

Key-area scope

Detailed design of three districts: function mix, building renewal, public space, and transport.

Key areas

Each key area states industrial positioning, spatial moves, building renewal, public space, transport interface, and implementation risk.

Zhongzhiyuan AI Acceleration Area

Garden-style full-stack district: national platform, industry display, Qinghe culture, transport, low-carbon interaction.

Beijing AI Origin Community

Near-campus transfer and talent community: open source, talent zone, retain-renovate-demolish, station integration.

Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster

Urban intelligent-economy district: leading firms, agent formats, commercial services, four-quadrant connectivity.

AI scenarios

Scenarios state service objects, spatial location, data source, privacy boundary, human review, and operating entity.

01 Open-source release hall

Result release and model evaluation for developers, firms, and universities.

02 Safety-governance sandbox

Standard-setting, safety evaluation, and red-teaming as visitable, reservable, regulated nodes.

03 Edge-computing station

New-infrastructure prototype combined with public services and low-carbon energy.

04 AI slow-mobility navigation

Explainable signage and low-intrusion sensing for gaps, crowding, and accessibility.

05 Dazhongsi international roadshow hall

Display, negotiation, and exchange for agent, terminal, and content firms.

06 Qinghe low-carbon innovation corridor

Green space, stormwater, walking/cycling, and AI display as a public living room.

07 Near-campus result-transfer street

Incubation, display, legal, IP, and investment services for university transfer.

08 Data-factor reception hall

Data-factor and digital-asset circulation shown on a compliant, authorized, auditable basis.

09 AI life-service sample street

AI+ healthcare, education, law, and life services in small-block street space.

10 Global AI activity-week route

A walkable route from heritage culture to open-source, industry, and roadshow.
