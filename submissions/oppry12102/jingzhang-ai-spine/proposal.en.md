# Jing-Zhang AI Spine: Urban Design Proposal for the Centennial Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt

> Jing-Zhang does not lack compute — it lacks a public interface that makes innovation trustable: every figure is recalculable, every AI is stoppable, every project is answerable.

**One-line test standard (anyone can verify it themselves)**: every AI on this belt must answer three questions at once — **recalculable?** (every figure has a source and a formula), **stoppable?** (with the AI off, a staffed counter still completes the same task), **answerable?** (when something goes wrong you can find a name, an appeal and a fallback). If any one of the three cannot be answered, it does not go live. This is the engineering wisdom of the Jing-Zhang Railway's herringbone ("人"-shaped) switchback, translated into the AI era: the switchback exists so the train can calculate the gradient, come to a stop, and have every switchback signed and accountable — the "Spine" of this proposal is not a formal metaphor but this public innovation interface itself; recalculable, stoppable and accountable are the three disciplines it must pass.

## A Century in Dialogue: From Self-Strengthening in the First Industrial Revolution to Leadership in the Fourth

This roughly nine-kilometre Jing-Zhang green corridor is where two of China's self-strengthening stories — from two industrial revolutions — overlap on the same ground [source:DATA-JINGZHANG-HISTORY].

In 1909, a poor and weakened China was told that "the engineer who could build the Beijing–Zhangjiakou Railway had not yet been born." Zhan Tianyou (Jeme Tien-yow), leading an all-Chinese team, independently designed and built the country's first trunk railway. His herringbone switchback translated the steep 28‰ grade at Qinglongqiao into engineering wisdom a train could climb, stop on, and be held accountable for at every switchback — a self-strengthening project in which China learned from and caught up with the advanced technology of the First Industrial Revolution [source:DATA-JINGZHANG-HISTORY]. The Qinghuayuan Station, opened the same year, lies within today's provisional extent of the Beijing AI Origin Community; in 1949 it became the first stop of the leadership's "journey to the capital for the examination," and after closing in 2016 it rests southwest of Chengfu Road. One small station anchors three identities at once: the 1909 origin of self-strengthening, the 1949 starting point of the "examination," and the 2026 origin of innovation.

One hundred and seventeen years later, on the same ground, China has become one of the leaders in artificial intelligence. With an AI core-industry scale of 350 billion yuan, 130 registered large models and 26 unicorns, Haidian carries a core share of China's role in global intelligent competition [source:DATA-HAIDIAN-AI-INDUSTRY]. The centenary Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt is not a fresh start on a blank slate, but the continuation and elevation of this self-strengthening thread into the Fourth Industrial Revolution — translating the herringbone railway's recalculability, stoppability and accountability on a grade into this proposal's three delivery disciplines for AI systems: Recalculable, Stoppable, Accountable.

This is the conception of the proposal: to make the Jing-Zhang AI Spine a public innovation interface that runs "from historical self-strengthening to intelligent leadership." As the flagship detailed-design anchor (see "Key-Area Detailed Design"), the Beijing AI Origin Community overlays the triple memory of Qinghuayuan Station, the engineering wisdom of the herringbone and the contemporary discipline of the Accountability Corridor in a single place — letting a passerby, beside the rail relics, read a nation's two self-strengthenings: the first time, we learned and caught up with the world's advanced technology; this time, we co-define the rules of the intelligent age with the world [source:DATA-JINGZHANG-HISTORY] [depth:overall_spatial_structure].

The overall concept of this proposal is "**Jing-Zhang AI Spine**" (京张智脊): translating the Jing-Zhang green corridor that runs through the country's densest cluster of universities and research institutes into a trustable **public innovation interface**. Three delivery disciplines run through this design — **Recalculable — Stoppable — Accountable** — not as a design theme, but as self-check requirements on every design landing point.

- **Recalculable**: every spatial conclusion (area, ratio, layer count, scenario node) is independently recalculable from the spatial layers and the indicator-recalculation table; the main text cites only recalculable metrics and locatable layers, never pseudo-precise numbers that cannot be reproduced from structured data [data:geometry/site_boundary.geojson#SITE-001] [metric:site_area_sqm]. Anyone who walks into this corridor can see the source and formula behind every number.
- **Stoppable**: every AI scenario carries a "no-AI equivalent path" and a stop condition — when any of manual substitution, data boundary, service window or safety floor is unmet, the scenario closes instead of degrading and soldiering on [metric:public_space_ratio] [depth:metrics_recalculation]. Any ordinary person, after the system is switched off, can still complete the same task at a staffed counter.
- **Accountable**: every renewal project has lead/participating roles, preconditions, KPI, manual fallback and a governance interface; where responsibility or budget is missing it is listed unactivated, not written as a landing commitment [depth:renewal_project_list]. Anyone who suffers a loss can find the name, the appeal channel, and the backstop.

The spatial substrate of this "spine" is a **~9 km green corridor running through the country's densest cluster of universities and research institutes**: at the south end Xizhimen adjoins Beijing Jiaotong University, along Xueyuan Road the eight universities and several Chinese Academy of Sciences institutes are distributed in sequence, and at the north end Wudaokou gathers Tsinghua University and Peking University [source:DATA-ALONG-UNIVERSITIES]. The meaning of Jing-Zhang AI Spine is to translate this "stock-density" innovation corridor into a public innovation interface — not to simply expand R&D floor area where factors are dense, but to make verification, openness and accountability a public good at the points of highest encounter; the three disciplines then guarantee that this interface is recalculable, stoppable and accountable.

The three delivery disciplines do not stay at the level of text; they land on three drawable spatial images in the three key areas, turning "recalculable, stoppable, askable" into spaces that can be seen, walked and pointed at: "Zhizhiyuan · Ledger Plaza" (Recalculable Ledger Plaza) makes "Recalculable" visible — rendering computing load, energy use, model testing and standards-governance results into a real-time visible public ledger, so anyone passing by can read how much compute is being consumed, the one-shot pass rate of a model test, and which formula yields each conclusion [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-001] [metric:green_ratio]; "Dazhongsi · Stoppable Service Street" makes "Stoppable" walkable — a street of staffed service counters where each service point declares its no-AI equivalent path, human takeover role, current gate, operating mode and accountable discipline (aligned with the five-field criteria of the Service Equivalence Baseline SEB v0.2, see the scenario cards below), so after the system is switched off, a person can still walk the same task to completion [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-003]; "Origin Community · Accountability Corridor" makes "Accountable" pointable — writing each renewal project's responsible party, appeal interface and backstop path directly on the ground, so anyone who suffers a loss can find the name, the appeal and the backstop by walking the corridor [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-002] [depth:renewal_project_list].

**Industry scale anchors (public data)**: with about 2.6% of Beijing's land, Haidian produces over 1/4 of the city's GDP (over ¥1.3 trillion, ~6.4% average annual growth during the 14th Five-Year Plan), with tech-related industries above 70% of the total; the AI core industry is ~¥282.2 billion (2024, nearly 80% of Beijing), gathering 1,900+ AI firms (about 70% of Beijing) and 104 registered large models (nearly 70% of Beijing), forming a full "chip—framework—model—application" chain [source:DATA-HAIDIAN-AI-INDUSTRY]. ## Transport, Rail, Municipal and Public-Service Facilities

The transport plan organizes rail-station integration, road micro-circulation, slow-mobility breakpoints, external transport, parking, non-motorized parking and green transport, focusing on North Fifth Ring Road, the Jing-Zhang Heritage Park ring-crossing nodes, Wudaokou, Qinghua East Road West, Dazhongsi Station and transport links around key enterprises. This proposal's transport concept is organized in four layers, all kept provisional [depth:traffic_rail_slow_parking]: the **rail transfer layer** verifies station entrances, transfers, service windows and accessibility, and does not claim reachability without knowing positions, connections, frequencies or capacity; the **walking/cycling layer** verifies four-quadrant crossings, campus-park interfaces and the Qinghe green corridor, and does not draw solid lines without knowing routes, gradients, lighting or safety; the **micro-circulation layer** defines the responsibilities of manual transfer, closure rerouting and paid movement, and does not activate without actors, schedules, OD or substitution budgets; the **static transport layer** proposes candidate modules for dispersed non-motorized parking and accessible drop-off, and does not fix points or quantities without verifying ownership, capacity, fire safety and conflicts. The four layers add no roads, change no signals, and assume no rail connections. Layer evidence cites [data:geometry/roads.geojson#ROAD-001], [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#PUBLIC-001] and [data:geometry/constraints.geojson#CONSTRAINTS]; where road redlines, utilities, fire safety and municipal conditions are missing, assumptions state the gap rather than writing strategy as approved conditions.

**Commuting base and scenario-KPI anchors (public data)**: Beijing rail transit averaged ~9.9 million daily trips in 2024 (weekday average ~11.19 million); Metro Line 13, which runs through the innovation belt, has a peak daily ridership of ~1.6 million, currently the most crowded commuting corridor in north Beijing, and is being split into Lines 13A/13B (about 19 new stations) to add capacity [source:DATA-RAIL-METRO-RIDERSHIP]. Meanwhile, the Jing-Zhang HSR has carried ~57.42 million passenger trips since opening on 30 December 2019 (as of January 2025); the ground-level old line from Beijing North to Qinghe has been released as Heritage Park space, and Qinghe Station has become a three-in-one hub of HSR, Metro Line 13 and the Changping Line (single-day peak ~44,500) [source:DATA-JINGZHANG-HSR]. These ridership data support two design judgments: station integration and slow-mobility breakpoint repair must absorb high-intensity commuting flows, and the KPI baselines of scenarios such as "AI slow-mobility navigation" and "global AI activity-week route" (e.g. slow-mobility continuity rate, activity participation) are set against this commuting base, not invented.

Blue-green space takes the Jing-Zhang Heritage Park vitality belt as its skeleton, coordinates the Qinghe and Xiaoyuehe rivers and the travel needs of surrounding universities, enterprises and communities, and forms a north-south connected, east-west linked system of walking, cycling and green space; it identifies slow-mobility breakpoints, ring-crossing nodes, and the park's south- and north-end landscape nodes, and proposes composite-use strategies for parking, sports, innovation exchange, technology testing, application display and public services. The Jing-Zhang Heritage Park continuous green belt is the conceptual surface of a continuous green corridor for recovery and handoff, recalculated at ~2.46 million m² [data:geometry/green_space.geojson#GREEN-001]; Three public nodes — the Dazhongsi Station four-quadrant public node, the AI Origin Community public-activity interface and the Zhizhiyuan Qinghe innovation-exchange interface — jointly constitute the public interface [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#PUBLIC-001]. Green ratio ~21.56% and public-space ratio ~10.89% are recalculable from the provisional geometry but describe only the submitted layers, not existing conditions or planning performance [metric:green_ratio] [metric:public_space_ratio]. Digital services in the green corridor can be switched off; shading, seating, accessibility, drinking water, static wayfinding and safety contact form the non-digital chassis; the public interface shows who is on duty, what is closed, how to appeal and when to reopen. If staff are insufficient or maintenance incomplete, services close rather than squeezing back-office time [depth:blue_green_public_space].

Urban character fuses the Jing-Zhang Railway's historical culture, Zhongguancun's innovation culture and AI innovation culture, using cultural resources such as Qinghuayuan Railway Station and the Beijing Film Academy to propose urban tone, architectural character, roof form, massing, interface and public-art guidance; wayfinding, cultural symbols, international-communication narrative, AI pilgrimage landmarks, contribution walls and honor-display systems all require cleared sources. Character control distinguishes official control, design suggestion and to-be-confirmed conditions, and does not fabricate pseudo-precise control lines without heritage-protection or regulatory-planning basis [standard:MOHURD-URBAN-DESIGN-MEASURES].

### AI Pilgrimage Landmarks and Honor-Display System

This proposal proposes three conceptual landmarks, all positioned as "open co-creation suggestions / references", requiring professional design deepening, heritage and green-space recheck, and font/image clearance before implementation; they do not constitute approved construction [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK]:

1. **Jing-Zhang Kilometer Zero · AI Timeline** (south-end public space of the Jing-Zhang Heritage Park): juxtapose the industrial memory of the railway's 1909 opening with the development thread of artificial intelligence as a walkable, touchable public narrative axis — a pilgrimage starting point "from steam to intelligence" [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#PUBLIC-001] [depth:blue_green_public_space].
2. **Open-Source Contribution Wall** (Beijing AI Origin Community): publicly display open-source community contributors, models and code milestones in an updatable, cleared, human-reviewed way, as a spiritual landmark of the developer community; displayed content removes personal privacy and obtains attribution authorization [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-002].
3. **Future City Test Ground** (Zhizhiyuan Qinghe interface): translate the testing process of full-stack autonomy, standard-setting and safety governance into a visitable, bookable, supervised public test ground, making "testing" itself a perceivable urban scene [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-001] [depth:three_key_area_detailed_design].

The honor-display system shares one rule with the three landmarks: the contribution wall, milestone wall and annual list record only public, verifiable, cleared content; they do not collect personal behavior trails, do not output unauthorized personal profiles, and retain manual takedown and correction channels [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK]. No landmark is entertainment-driven, internet-famous or vulgar, and none violates heritage-protection, green-space, blue-line or traffic-safety constraints [standard:PROJECT-AGENT-OPEN-CALL-TASKBOOK].

The three evidence gates obey the same stop discipline: unmet preconditions stay at the previous gate; a single-group failure cannot be offset by an overall average; an official-geometry change triggers wholesale recalculation. The accountability model defines roles only and invents no institutions, in four classes: the accountable role denotes the single lead party that, once authorized in the future, is answerable for the outcome — this proposal does not unilaterally name government departments, property owners or enterprises; the responsible role is the professional and operational party actually executing; the consulted role is the rights holders, public and regulatory professionals who must be involved before a decision; the informed role is the users who keep learning of progress through public evidence. Any action package missing a single accountable role, a duty roster or an escalation contact does not enter routine operations [depth:renewal_project_list].

Phasing is distinguished from the 100-day call design period: the call period is the time requirement for delivering results; implementation phasing is the advancement path of urban renewal and project construction. The near-term pilot is the Beijing AI Origin Community (~1,226,400 m²); the mid-term renewals are Zhizhiyuan (~2,259,200 m²) and Dazhongsi (~2,679,900 m²) [data:geometry/phasing.geojson#PHASE-001]; the near-term pilot starts with lightweight facilities, operating activities and service platforms, while mid-term renewal and long-term governance await formal regulatory-planning, municipal, transport and ownership conditions. The annual activity system, developer-community operations, scenario-open days, public experience routes and international-communication mechanisms all state their operating object, frequency, responsibility boundary, translation path and risk — never propaganda slogans.

The first 100 days compress the near-term pilot into eight checkable steps (dates only sequence the work; the evidence gates decide whether to advance, never crossing G0—G2 [depth:phasing_implementation]):

- **D1–14**: freeze the provisional boundary and the missing-data checklist, and publish the evidence ledger's "verified facts — remaining unknowns — design consequences".
- **D15–30**: complete the card-by-card self-check of the ten scenario cards' "no-AI equivalent path + stop condition + manual fallback", and fill in the five SEB v0.2 fields.
- **D31–45**: use the brand component library and reversible components to build the three key-area spatial-image prototypes (Recalculable Ledger Plaza / Stoppable Service Street / Accountability Corridor).
- **D46–60**: re-run the "three-accountability" desktop drill offline, with all negative examples blocked and passing samples receiving desktop clearance only.
- **D61–75**: open the pilot at small scale only after passing G0 and obtaining permits.
- **D76**: deliberately rehearse one shutdown, handoff to humans, and recovery.
- **D77–90**: independently re-test and publish the failure and rollback record.
- **D91–100**: decide to continue, revise or withdraw per G1/G2.

## AI Planning Method Workflow (Diagnose — Generate — Simulate — Review — Iterate)

This proposal's AI planning method is a reproducible five-step workflow, not a one-shot generation [depth:metrics_recalculation]:

1. **Diagnose**: identify existing conditions, breakpoints and constraints from the provisional boundary, constraint layers and the missing-data checklist [data:geometry/constraints.geojson#CONSTRAINTS].
2. **Generate**: generate land-use, building, road, green-space, public-space and phasing layers from the three-level scope and three-core positioning.
3. **Simulate**: recalculate area, green ratio, public-space ratio and building footprints under EPSG:4548.
4. **Review**: run the deterministic / spatial / visual / professional four-gate self-check and manually spot-check drawings.
5. **Iterate**: after official polygons replace provisional ones or review comments arrive, re-run the scaffold, self-check and drawing/HTML generation.

This workflow guarantees that every step of "diagnose—generate—simulate—review—iterate" is traceable to a layer, metric or self-check evidence [standard:PROJECT-AGENT-OPEN-CALL-TASKBOOK].
