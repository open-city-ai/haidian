OI is position; IT is type

The two-level identifier contract separates “where” from “what.” OI-xx means only a relative north-to-south candidate position; IT-xx means only a portable interface type. One OI may combine several ITs. When formal boundaries arrive, relationships move instead of preserving false coordinates.
