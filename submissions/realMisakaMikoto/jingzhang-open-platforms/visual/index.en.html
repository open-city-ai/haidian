Not another park design

Evidence that Phase II is open changes the brief: the object of design moves from landscape itself to access, staying and public service across its edges.

The nine interfaces are not promised sites. They are candidate types awaiting official boundary, existing-condition, title, heritage and operator checks.

Protocol: Discover → Try → Audit → Stop → Restore.

04 / PILOTS

Four 4–8 week pilots write stop and recovery first

All are permission-dependent concepts with no funding or government commitment. Operator, authority, minimum data, fallback, baseline and public acceptance metric are mandatory before start.

PILOT 01 · PUBLIC SERVICE AGENT

Repair and complaint routing

Operator
Local service desk + accountable department contact
Minimum data
Issue class, coarse location, case state; no face data
Baseline / test
Manual routing time; no rise in misrouting and clearer first explanation
Fallback
In-person, phone and paper cases

Stop on repeated critical misrouting, failed human takeover or unauthorised disclosure. Restore the human queue and notify affected people.

PILOT 02 · SLOW MOBILITY

Last-500-metre chain

Operator
Joint park and neighbourhood audit team
Minimum data
Anonymous barrier point, detour time, facility status
Baseline / test
Manual audit; closure rate of critical breaks and assisted-trip success
Fallback
Fixed signs, volunteers and phone assistance

Stop when a route enters danger or a closed area, or an assisted user cannot complete it. Withdraw digital advice and publish a human-verified route.

PILOT 03 · CONTROLLED ROBOTICS

Bounded robot logistics

Operator
On-site safety officer + logistics accountable party
Minimum data
Device state, anonymous obstruction event, takeover log
Baseline / test
Manual delivery; zero injury, successful takeover, 100% pedestrian priority
Fallback
Human trolley or walking delivery

Stop after any contact, geofence escape, takeover failure or forced pedestrian yielding. Physically remove the unit and complete an independent review.

PILOT 04 · EDGE ENERGY

Edge-compute energy sandbox

Operator
Facilities team + independent safety review
Minimum data
Device power, temperature and task latency; no personal content
Baseline / test
Cloud baseline; lower energy per task without safety or comfort loss
Fallback
Return to existing compute and human service

Stop on power, temperature, noise or information-security breach. Disconnect the sandbox, retain audit logs and restore the existing system.

05 / GOVERNANCE

Build the right to exit into the public interface

Facial recognition and continuous individual tracking are prohibited by default. Data minimisation, edge processing, opt-out, non-digital access, human escalation and incident logs are shared safeguards.

DiscoverName the problem, rights holders and evidence gap.
TrySmallest place, shortest time, least data.
AuditPublish baseline, metric, incidents and bias.
StopHuman help and a stop path stay reachable.
RestoreReturn to existing service; notify and review.
  1. Evidence and rights verificationObtain official boundaries, title, survey, heritage, utilities and operators before siting.
  2. One low-cost pilot per areaFour to eight weeks; human fallback remains active and any red line stops the trial.
  3. Validated nine-interface expansionAdd interfaces only when service, equity, safety and operations all pass.
  4. Open Platform Week + quarterly challengesKeep standards, failure records and improvement tasks public.
