# 京张开放站台 / JING-ZHANG OPEN PLATFORMS

## 中文执行叙事

“京张开放站台”不重做已经开放的京张铁路遗址公园，而把设计对象转向公园与轨道、校园、社区、园区之间的日常公共接口。公众承诺是“走得通、坐得住、办得成”：完整通行链优先照顾轮椅、老人、儿童家庭和首次到访者；停留空间提供不消费也可使用的休息、遮阴、避雨、照护和安静条件；公共事务既可由 AI 辅助解释和分流，也始终保留人工、纸面、电话与申诉渠道。

三层工作框架对应 43.6 平方公里统筹研究范围、约 11.4 平方公里总体设计范围和 368.4 公顷重点区口径。总体结构是“一条公园基底、九个相对候选接口、三座公共学习站、两翼服务网络”。`OI-01`—`OI-09` 只表示北—南相对候选位置，`IT-01`—`IT-09` 只表示科研园区、校园社区、铁路遗产、轨道公交、蓝绿安全、公共服务、生产物流、夜间安静和市政算力等可迁移类型，二者均不是已选址工程。北部“标准试验站”、中部“开源原点站”、南部“公共回声站”是可随正式边界移动的功能原型，不是确定建筑点位。

方案准确承接任务书的百年京张文化带、都市 AI 生活体验带、AI 融合创新带三大定位和五大功能，以众智园 AI 自主创新加速区、AI 原点社区、大钟寺 AI 产业集聚区、中关村科技服务翼、小月河场景赋能翼组成“三区两翼”。北纬社区、未来科学城、怀柔科学城、经开区和京津冀只作为概念交换口，图中机制不表示已经签约、投资或互认。三处重点区分别形成责任水岸试验廊、静声共译庭和四象限修补台三组“平面—剖面—首证据—停止门”原型。

方案面向八类人群编制十二张场景卡，覆盖无障碍导航、暑热暴雨安全路线、最后 500 米接驳、活动与安静模式、铁路文化导览、社区健康转介、青少年实验室、创业服务、多语服务、设施报修、受控机器人物流、边缘计算与能源。四个建议试验分别为 6 周公共服务智能体、6 周慢行接驳、4 周受控机器人物流和 8 周边缘计算与能源；每项都设置 RACI、许可、资源类别、未来基线、人工替代、隐私、验收、停止与恢复门。T1 已形成只读离线合成 tabletop：4 个正例、10 个负例，零网络、零个人数据、零仓库写入；现场状态为 `not_authorized_not_run`，真实绩效为 `null`，当前决定为 `do_not_advance`。其余三项同样未获现场授权、未运行。

当前总体与三处重点区几何均为 provisional constraint。粗略边界与公众所见公园约有 412.5 米错位，临时大钟寺重点区中心与大钟寺站关系呈约 2.26 公里的低置信异常，因此不提供地块级拆改、确定 FAR、高度、投资或工程线位。投稿者控制的概念图层已完成可复演复算，但 7,236.000 m² 构筑物基底、16,239.547 m² 微气候原型、38,568.923 m² 公共接口包络和 17,642.407 m² 分期条件门都不是现状、产权、法定控制或工程规模。本项目全程为线上研究，没有现场踏勘、居民访谈、客流/OD、满意度、树木、管线或设备测试。正式 polygon、权属、控规、道路、市政、文保和运行数据到来后，必须从源几何重新计算图层、指标、图件和 manifest 哈希。

默认禁止人脸识别和持续性个体追踪，优先数据最小化、边缘处理、主动退出和非数字路径。技术故障不能中断基础通行和人工服务。三座学习站展示标准、开源共学、公众反馈以及可复演的公共贡献，不建设夸张科技纪念物。GitHub 合并只表示进入开源仓库，不代表官方资格、评选、批准、资金或实施承诺。

## English Executive Narrative

Jing-Zhang Open Platforms does not redesign the completed Jing-Zhang Railway Heritage Park. It focuses on the everyday public interfaces between the park, transit, campuses, neighbourhoods, and innovation districts. Its promise is to help people get through, stay comfortably, and complete the task. Complete journeys prioritise wheelchair users, older people, families, and first-time visitors; stopping places provide non-commercial rest, shade, rain protection, care, and quiet; AI may explain and route a request, but human, paper, telephone, and appeal channels always remain.

The framework covers the 43.6-square-kilometre research scope, the approximately 11.4-square-kilometre overall design scope, and the 368.4-hectare key-area brief. One park base is connected through nine relative candidate interfaces, three movable public learning stations, and two service wings. OI-01 through OI-09 identify relative north-to-south candidate positions only; IT-01 through IT-09 identify relocatable interface types. Neither set is a confirmed work site. The northern Standards Test Station, central Open-Source Origin Station, and southern Public Echo Station are public-function prototypes, not confirmed building sites.

The proposal explicitly adopts the brief's three positions and five functions. Zhongzhiyuan AI Independent Innovation Acceleration Area, AI Origin Community, Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster, Zhongguancun Technology Service Wing and Xiaoyuehe Scenario Enablement Wing form the Three Areas and Two Wings. Beiwei Community, Future Science City, Huairou Science City, Beijing ETDZ and Jing-Jin-Ji are conceptual exchange ports only; no agreement, investment or mutual recognition is claimed. The three key areas are developed as plan-section-first-evidence-stop-gate prototypes.

Eight user groups and twelve scenario cards cover accessible navigation, heat and storm safety, last-500-metre transfer, event and quiet modes, traceable railway interpretation, health referral, youth learning, startup support, multilingual service, maintenance triage, controlled robot logistics, and edge-computing energy. Four proposed pilots last four to eight weeks and each requires a RACI, permission, resource categories, a future baseline, a human alternative, privacy safeguards, acceptance gates, stop conditions, and recovery. T1 now has a read-only offline synthetic tabletop with four positive and ten negative fixtures, zero network, zero personal data and zero repository writes. Its field state is `not_authorized_not_run`, real performance is `null`, and the current decision is `do_not_advance`. The other three pilots are likewise not authorized or operating.

All overall and key-area geometry remains provisional. The rough boundary shows an approximately 412.5-metre mismatch with the publicly perceived park, while the temporary Dazhongsi area has an approximately 2.26-kilometre low-confidence relationship to Dazhongsi Station. Participant-controlled concept geometry has been reproducibly recalculated, but the 7,236.000 m² building-prototype footprint, 16,239.547 m² microclimate prototypes, 38,568.923 m² public-interface envelopes and 17,642.407 m² condition-gate envelopes are not surveyed conditions, property, statutory controls or works quantities. No parcel development, FAR, height, investment, or engineering alignment is claimed. The work is entirely desktop-based: there has been no field survey, interview, passenger or OD count, satisfaction study, tree or utility survey, or live equipment test. Official geometry and professional evidence must trigger a complete recalculation.

Face recognition and persistent individual tracking are prohibited by default. Data minimisation, edge processing, opt-out, screen-free channels, human takeover, and incident records are built into the operating concept. Technology failure cannot remove basic movement or human service. A GitHub merge would place the package in an open repository; it would not constitute official qualification, selection, approval, funding, or implementation.
