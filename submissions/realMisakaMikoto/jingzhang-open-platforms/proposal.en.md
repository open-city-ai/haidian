# JING-ZHANG OPEN PLATFORMS

**Subtitle: Everyday AI Public Interfaces Along the Completed Park**

The public promise has three parts: **get through, stay comfortably, and complete the task**. “Get through” means that wheelchair users, families with pushchairs, pedestrians, cyclists, and transferring passengers can understand and complete a cross-park journey. “Stay comfortably” means shade, rain protection, quiet, care, and places where no purchase is required. “Complete the task” means that entrepreneurship support, health referral, maintenance reporting, and cultural learning retain human, paper, and telephone paths alongside digital assistance.

This package is based on online research, public records, and the repository site package. It does not claim a field survey, resident interview, passenger count, origin-destination survey, tree inventory, utility survey, or live equipment test. Every interface, pilot duration, and acceptance gate is a conceptual recommendation for professional deepening. A merged GitHub pull request would not constitute planning approval, competition selection, funding, construction authority, or official endorsement.

## Design Basis and Source List

The official call governs the three working scales and the three key areas. Public records that the supporting works were completed and the full Jing-Zhang Railway Heritage Park opened change the design starting point: the park is treated as an operating public base, not as an empty landscape to be designed again. The proposal therefore focuses on the unverified cross-connections, everyday public services, and operating responsibilities between the park, metro and bus, campuses, communities, and innovation districts. Completion and opening do not prove that any interface alteration, device, or pilot has been authorized. [E:PARK-PHASE2-OPEN-20260806] [source:PARK-PHASE2-OPEN-20260806]

Evidence is used in four levels. Adopted controls define limits; official built or operating records describe a dated condition; official drafts, initiatives, and international cases provide mechanisms only; open data and Agent-derived material can create testable hypotheses but not statutory facts. The source register records scale, date, licence, confidence, permitted use, and what each item cannot support. A page being publicly accessible does not automatically make it suitable for a formal spatial claim. [E:SOURCE-REGISTRY] [source:SOURCE-REGISTRY]

No official overall polygon, precise key-area polygon, ownership survey, road redline, utility survey, current building survey, or approved regulatory plan was available. The repository’s locked provisional geometry is therefore retained without moving it to fit a public map. OSM passed a licence-screening step but is not used in this package’s geometry or basemap. Heritage statements are limited to registered material about Qinghuayuan Station; the entire Haidian corridor is not presented as one heritage designation.

## Three-Level Scope Framework

The 43.6-square-kilometre coordinated research area examines collaboration among industry, services, transport, and open scenarios across the “three areas and two wings.” [metric:announced_coordinated_research_area_sqm] The approximately 11.4-square-kilometre overall design area tests how the park base can be stitched laterally into everyday urban life. [metric:announced_overall_design_area_sqm] The 368.4-hectare key-area brief organizes the three required relocatable prototypes for Zhongzhiyuan, the Beijing AI Origin Community, and Dazhongsi. [metric:announced_key_detailed_design_area_sqm] [metric:key_area_count] The submitted provisional polygon recalculates to 11,412,825.386 square metres in EPSG:4548; that reproducible low-confidence value only exposes why a draft geometry and the announced scope cannot replace one another. [A:A-AREA-MISMATCH-001] [metric:site_area_sqm]

The structure is “one park base, nine relative candidate interfaces, three public learning stations, and two service wings.” The nine candidates are relationship types rather than selected works: OI-01 park–research campus, OI-02 park–campus/community, OI-03 park–railway heritage, OI-04 park–metro/bus last 500 metres, OI-05 park–blue-green safety, OI-06 park–public service, OI-07 park–production logistics, OI-08 park–night-time quiet, and OI-09 park–municipal edge-computing and energy. Each must be relocated and checked after official geometry, desktop evidence, permissions, and future field verification become available.

The three learning stations are the northern **Standards Test Station**, the central **Open-Source Origin Station**, and the southern **Public Echo Station**. They are movable public-function prototypes, not fixed buildings. The provisional Dazhongsi key area has a particularly low-confidence relationship to the commonly understood station area; the southern station therefore expresses a task—recalibrating arrival, public life, and service interfaces—without assigning a parcel or station entrance. [A:A-KEY-AREAS-001] [source:KEY-AREA-SOURCE]

## Coordinated Research Area: Industry and Future City Research

The proposal combines three positions: an open validation belt for full-stack independent innovation, an AI public-service belt for ordinary people, and an auditable learning belt for global developers. Its five functions are technical validation, translation and incubation, public service, cultural learning, and long-term operation. Zhongzhiyuan hosts standards, safety governance, and shared testing; the AI Origin Community connects university knowledge, low-cost incubation, youth third spaces, and neighbourhood services; Dazhongsi explores transit arrival, intelligent terminals, small-business service, and public life. The west wing supports intellectual property, capital, and international services, while the east wing hosts controlled embodied-AI, health, and media tests. Together they form a loop of question, small test, public evidence, human review, and either stop or scale. [E:THREE-AREAS-TWO-WINGS] [source:THREE-AREAS-TWO-WINGS]

Six international cases are mechanism comparisons, not templates. Knowledge Quarter suggests an institutional network rather than a single icon. Kendall Square informs the relationship between innovation work and everyday public space. JTC LaunchPad/one-north highlights shared facilities and low-entry incubation. Paris-Saclay shows why a large research district must also address mobility and daily life. Barcelona 22@ offers a framework for mixed-use renewal of an existing employment district. Toronto’s Quayside review makes data governance, public accountability, and exit conditions central. None of their densities, investment models, legal powers, or performance figures is transferred to Beijing. [A:A-CASES-001] [source:CASE-TORONTO-QUAYSIDE]

The name “Open Platforms” translates a railway platform from a place of waiting into a civic interface for testing services together. The proposed mark is a continuous railway-oxide-red line gently opened by nine public-blue crossbars, with park green as the inhabitable base. “Open” means open questions, rules, evidence, corrections, and appeals—not unconditional access to data or land. The three learning stations display standards, open learning, public feedback, and contributions that can be replayed and corrected, rather than monumental technology or corporate rankings. [E:AGENT-TASKBOOK] [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK]

## Overall Design Area: Urban Renewal and Regulatory-Plan-Level Urban Design

The overall design treats each candidate interface as a configurable Open Platform section. Its minimum kit contains a legible continuous path; a stopping place usable by a wheelchair user beside a companion; shade, rain protection, and water information; a human help or service point; paper, voice, and screen-free alternatives; a visible responsibility notice; a removable device zone; and a clearly marked bypass. The same responsibility grammar can adapt to campus, neighbourhood, station, river, or innovation-park conditions. When official polygons arrive, relationships can move without preserving false coordinates.

“Get through” begins with the complete journey of the least advantaged traveller, not a centreline. “Stay comfortably” treats non-commercial rest, care, night-time quiet, and extreme weather as public infrastructure. “Complete the task” gives every AI-assisted service an accountable person, human takeover, refusal right, time limit, receipt, and appeal. Screens, sensors, and edge nodes remain removable. If technology fails, basic movement and human service must still work.

Actions follow four levels: retain and maintain useful space; repair interfaces with signs, seating, accessible transitions, or calibrated lighting; test demand through removable kiosks, desks, and devices; and discuss permanent construction only after planning, ownership, heritage, fire, transport, municipal, and public-process gates are complete. Present evidence cannot determine demolition, new-build height, or development intensity for any parcel. [A:A-CONTROLS-001] [source:HAIDIAN-URBAN-RENEWAL-GUIDE-2025]

Urban character keeps the railway, park, and existing neighbourhoods in the foreground. New pieces should be low, reversible, maintainable, and visually quiet. They first support passage, rest, enquiry, and repair; only then, within permission, may they host testing or display. Road rights, setbacks, fire access, utilities, structures, and operating agreements remain professional follow-up items.

## Detailed Design of Key Areas

All three key areas use a platform–interface–pilot–evidence framework, but address different public problems. Because every polygon is provisional, and the Dazhongsi geometry is particularly uncertain, the designs define functional combinations and spatial relationships rather than parcel development, demolition, exact station entrances, building heights, or approved floor area. [E:KEY-AREA-SOURCE] [source:KEY-AREA-SOURCE]

**Zhongzhiyuan / Standards Test Station.** A shared-testing inner loop and public observation edge connect OI-01 and OI-07 relationship types. Existing ground floors, sheltered edges, and hardstanding should be reused where confirmed. Movable test benches, a human observation seat, and a physical stop control support controlled robot logistics and edge-computing trials. Ownership, fire safety, park access, insurance, device permission, and a named incident owner are opening gates.

**AI Origin Community / Open-Source Origin Station.** An open table, shared laboratory, community foyer, and youth third space connect OI-02, OI-06, and OI-08. The prototype supports an open youth laboratory, startup service desk, multilingual assistance, and community health referral. It recommends time-sharing and indoor–outdoor interface improvements but makes no structural claim about existing buildings. Safeguarding, professional referral, accessibility, and human review are mandatory.

**Dazhongsi / Public Echo Station.** An arrival–rest–service–feedback sequence explores OI-04, OI-06, and OI-09. The proposal asks for continuous ground-level movement, quiet seating, explanation, and a public feedback desk, while leaving the precise location unresolved. Last-500-metre information, maintenance triage, event quiet mode, and public-service assistance cannot be sited before boundary calibration, transit coordination, accessibility review, and data approval.

The stations also function as AI contribution and honour nodes. They recognise an accessibility break that was repaired, a service corrected after public objection, an open standard, a documented energy reduction, or a responsible incident review—not the largest model or a company leaderboard. Every item shows source, version, authorised contributor, validity date, and correction path. If official boundaries change, the station function moves with the verified interface.

## AI Innovation Ecosystem, Personas, and AI+ Scenarios

Eight groups guide design: G1 long-term residents and older people; G2 children and families; G3 disabled and mobility-impaired people; G4 students and researchers; G5 startups and one-person companies; G6 employees and international visitors; G7 service and logistics workers; and G8 city and park operators. Their needs include familiar routes, quiet, care, accessible and non-visual information, affordable learning, compliance support, multilingual arrival, safe work and rest, maintenance, energy accountability, and recovery after failure. A pilot fails if its convenience transfers cost or risk to the least advantaged group. [E:AGENT-TASKBOOK] [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK]

The twelve readable scenario cards carry the same contract. S01 accessible navigation uses only obstacle category, time, and aggregated completion; it retains paper, telephone, and human guidance and stops at any impassable break. S02 heat and storm safety uses public alerts and facility status, retaining signs and broadcasts. S03 last-500-metre transfer uses authorised service and aggregated waiting information, never personal OD traces. S04 event flow and quiet mode uses zone-level crowding, complaints, and time bands, with reduction or cancellation as the fallback.

S05 traceable railway interpretation stores source, version, and language, with a human guide and paper correction card. S06 community health referral collects only fields required for a human referral and never diagnoses. S07 the youth open laboratory records course, anonymous work, and equipment status, with safeguarding, consent, screen-free materials, and human mentors. S08 the startup service desk records question category, referral, and closure; legal, investment, and policy certainty always moves to a qualified person.

S09 multilingual visitor and small-business assistance records language, task category, and anonymous feedback; inconsistent critical translations are withdrawn. S10 maintenance and complaint triage records facility ID, problem, time, owner, and state, while telephone, paper, and a service desk remain available. S11 controlled robot logistics records speed, stop, human takeover, and near misses in a permitted test environment; boundary or emergency-stop failure triggers power-off and removal. S12 edge-computing and energy records aggregate load, energy, temperature, and alarms under independent metering and a complete power-off bypass.

For each card, the location is an interface type, not a confirmed coordinate; the operator and permission gate must be named before opening; the baseline is collected only after authorization; acceptance compares task completion and the worst-affected group; privacy follows data minimisation; and every stop condition has a human recovery path. Face recognition and persistent individual tracking are prohibited by default. The four 4–8-week pilots combine these cards, but none is presently operating. [A:A-DATA-PRIVACY-001] [source:SOURCE-REGISTRY]

Land-use design starts from the operating park and existing urban functions. Provisional geometry checks schemas, relationships, and the future recalculation workflow; it does not create a surveyed current condition, statutory control, or construction commitment. [M:building_density] [metric:building_density]

The supply order is retain first, then light repair, then reversible testing, and permanent construction only after evidence. Existing park, roads, public facilities, and confirmed buildings are maintained. Ground-floor access periods, signs, accessible transitions, shade, seating, and shared rooms may be considered as light interventions. Movable service desks and equipment test demand without fixing a building. No parcel is marked for demolition, because provisional misalignment cannot justify a “low-efficiency” label or displacement conclusion.

The three learning stations should first occupy existing or temporary space. Any future building would require new review of views, trees, heritage, daylight, fire access, accessibility, energy, operation, and whole-life maintenance. Floor-area ratio remains pending official data because both the approved control and official polygon are absent; dividing a conceptual building footprint by provisional area would not create a valid FAR. [M:floor_area_ratio] [metric:floor_area_ratio]

The nine interfaces use a relationship-before-parcel method. Professional teams first verify the barrier and public task, then choose a retain, repair, or addition site on official data. ## Transport, Rail, Municipal Infrastructure, and Public Services

Transport is evaluated interface by interface rather than described as a generic smart corridor. OI-04 addresses the last 500 metres between transit and park. OI-02 checks whether campus and neighbourhood edges work for different ages and abilities. OI-03 protects heritage while providing a bypass. OI-05 supplies heat and storm alternatives. OI-07 limits low-speed logistics by time, speed, emergency stop, and human observation. Ordinary signs and staff come first; digital guidance is an optional layer and cannot remove basic mobility when it fails.

The acceptance unit for “get through” is a complete task: enter from a station or street, understand direction, cross an interface, find rest and help, reach the service or innovation-district edge, and leave by a non-digital route. A future authorized test must be replayed by wheelchair users, older people, families with children, first-time visitors, and service workers. This proposal has not performed such a replay and has no measured OD, passenger volume, detour, or waiting-time evidence. [A:A-PARK-INTERFACE-001] [source:PARK-PHASE2-SUPPORT-20260713]

Municipal and digital systems follow “basic services first, digital equipment disconnectable.” OI-09 requires independent metering, temperature monitoring, fire and cybersecurity review, equipment withdrawal, and a manual bypass. It cannot draw on essential park lighting, emergency communication, or accessibility supplies without verified capacity. The actual capacity of water, toilets, drainage, lighting, waste, first aid, power, and communication is unknown and remains a survey item.

Public service uses “three stations, one seated desk, many channels.” Every station retains a human conversation desk, paper directory, telephone, voice path, and screen-free icons. AI may explain, route, and query status, but health, legal, investment, policy, emergency, and child-protection matters transfer to qualified people. “Complete the task” means an owner, deadline, receipt, appeal, and closure—not merely a fast generated answer.

## Blue-Green Network, Public Space, and Urban Character

Because the park is open, blue-green design protects continuity and improves interface experience instead of covering the existing landscape with a new image. OI-05 organizes heat, heavy rain, possible standing water, shade, drinking-water information, and alternative routes as an updateable safety layer. OI-08 uses quiet periods, low-stimulation guidance, event capacity reduction, and resident feedback. `green_ratio` and `public_space_ratio` remain pending; the proposal does not turn rough geometry into precise current-condition percentages. Formal green, river, tree, and open-space boundaries must be projected and recalculated together. [M:green_ratio] [metric:green_ratio]

“Stay comfortably” means non-commercial seating, companion space beside wheelchairs, family sightlines, backs and armrests, rain and shade protection, drinking-water information, low-stimulation corners, safe night-time visibility, and human help. Components should be maintainable, replaceable, and avoid unnecessary hard surface. Without field evidence on trees, drainage, sunlight, noise, or behaviour, the proposal does not prescribe seat quantities, species, paving areas, or lux levels. Every place must identify maintenance responsibility and preserve basic passage during events.

The three learning stations are pilgrimage and honour nodes whose significance comes from public learning. The Standards Test Station displays standards, failed tests, and stop drills. The Open-Source Origin Station displays open tools, youth work, and neighbourhood questions. The Public Echo Station shows complaint closure, accessibility repairs, and annual reviews. Entries carry source, contributor permission, version, validity, and correction routes, rather than unauthorised portraits, corporate marks, or model rankings. [E:QINGHUAYUAN-HERITAGE-STATUS] [source:QINGHUAYUAN-HERITAGE-CONTROL]

Railway oxide red expresses continuity, park green the ecological base, and high-contrast public blue the lateral interface. Typography and imagery require traceable licences; wayfinding combines Chinese, English, icons, and non-visual alternatives. The mark avoids brains, circuits, neon technology pods, and trade-show aesthetics. Any component near heritage awaits accurate protection lines and competent-authority review. ## Renewal Projects, Implementation Policy, and Phasing

Projects are sequenced by evidence gates rather than construction momentum. P0 verifies official polygons, ownership, road rights, heritage, utilities, accessibility, permissions, and public-process requirements, creating a register for the nine candidates. P1 places one low-cost learning station and one reversible service in each area without permanent work. P2 extends a network only after tests pass and the worst-affected group does not bear new cost. P3 may consider long-term spatial work, an annual Open Platforms Week, and quarterly challenges. A failed gate returns to paper or stops the project. [A:A-PILOTS-001] [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK]

Four pilots are proposed only after authorization. **T1 Public Service Agent** runs six weeks across S06, S08, S09, and S10. Week one freezes public processes, vocabulary, and responsibility; acceptance requires a human path, owner, deadline, receipt, and appeal for every sampled task. High-risk misinformation, absent ownership, or privacy leakage stops automation. **T2 Slow-Mobility Connection** runs six weeks across S01–S04 at one approved OI-04/05-type interface. An authorized team establishes baselines for wheelchair users, older people, families, and first-time visitors; any impassable route, evacuation conflict, or incorrect warning stops guidance.

**T3 Controlled Robot Logistics** runs four weeks for S11 in a closed or expressly permitted environment. It compares with an authorized manual workflow and passes only if yielding, speed control, emergency stop, human takeover, and accessible clearance pass; faster delivery alone is insufficient. Boundary breach or failed stop powers down and removes the device. **T4 Edge Computing and Energy** runs eight weeks for S12 under independent metering. It freezes devices, energy, temperature, and alarm baselines, requires explainable load and automatic degradation, and stops on power, heat, fire, cybersecurity, or equipment-integrity alarms.

Long-term operation combines monthly interface inspection, quarterly public challenges, an annual Open Platforms Week, and a versioned evidence cabinet. Challenges must state an owner, data boundary, human alternative, and exit plan. The annual event displays rejected and corrected projects as well as successful ones. Participation by developers, residents, students, service workers, and operators cannot become a prerequisite for basic service. International communication must distinguish proposal, submission, test, review, selection, approval, and implementation.

Policy directions include small reversible-test procurement, data-minimisation templates, cross-department responsibility sheets, accessibility and privacy red teams, rights ledgers, and recovery budgets. Funding, operators, permits, and schedules remain decisions for authorized institutions and professional teams; this proposal makes no external commitment.

## Risk, Copyright, and Compliance

The highest risk is provisional data creating the illusion of a known location. The repository rough boundary and the publicly perceived park show an approximately 412.5-metre mismatch, while the temporary “Dazhongsi” key-area centre has an approximately 2.26-kilometre low-confidence relationship to Dazhongsi Station. These are warnings to verify boundaries, not authority to move locked geometry, select parcels, or calculate works. Until official polygons arrive, interfaces remain relative candidates and key-area prototypes remain relocatable. [A:A-KEY-AREAS-001] [source:BOUNDARY-SOURCE]

The second risk is describing desktop work as field fact. There has been no site visit, interview, measurement, OD or passenger count, satisfaction survey, accessibility replay, tree survey, utility investigation, or live-system connection. All baselines are future opening steps. Park opening does not approve interface construction; international cases do not transfer legal powers; district averages do not describe a key area. Professional deepening needs authoritative geometry, current surveys, ownership, planning controls, roads, utilities, heritage review, operations, and public participation. [A:A-PARK-INTERFACE-001] [source:PARK-PHASE2-OPEN-20260806]

The third risk is digital convenience weakening basic rights. Face recognition and persistent individual tracking are prohibited by default. Each service collects the minimum necessary fields, prefers edge processing and aggregation, and provides opt-out, paper, telephone, screen-free, and human paths. AI does not make final health, legal, investment, policy, emergency, or child-protection decisions. Operators, purpose, retention, appeal, stop conditions, and recovery are visible. If a service cannot explain itself, it stops first. [A:A-DATA-PRIVACY-001] [source:SOURCE-REGISTRY]

Only public, cleared, or self-produced material is included. International cases are factual and methodological summaries; protected images, plans, brands, and expressive content are not copied. OSM is not used in package geometry or basemaps; any future use requires a fixed snapshot, query, hash, and ODbL attribution. The name, mark, people, company logos, fonts, photographs, and AI-generated assets require separate rights records. [E:OSM-LICENSE-SCREENING] [source:OSM-LICENSE-SCREENING]

This is an Agent open-call concept and professional-deepening resource, not a statutory plan, government decision, engineering conclusion, investment estimate, procurement promise, or implementation permit. A GitHub merge only places files in the open repository. External communication must preserve the distinct states of proposed, submitted, tested, reviewed, selected, approved, and implemented.
