The Jing-Zhang Law. In 1909 the Jing-Zhang Railway wrote speed onto the ground; in 2019 the 350 km/h design-speed line went under it through the Qinghuayuan Tunnel, and the old surface alignment became a heritage park — every acceleration buries the speed deeper and gives the ground back slower. The third layer (AI) obeys the same law: compute underground, robots at night, screens off the street; intelligence surfaces only at twelve wells. The city is not lit up by AI; it is quieted by AI.

Assumptions assumptions.json, each bound to the body text

  • A-CONTROLS-001 — Approved regulatory controls (floor area ratio, height, density, green ratio, setbacks) are not public, so they are honestly left unknown; conceptual storey counts express spatial intent only.
  • A-BOUNDARY-001 — All three scope levels and the three key areas use the repository's provisional boundary (official_boundary=false, provisional_rough). Once the official boundary is released, every layer, drawing, metric and conclusion is recomputed as a whole rather than replaced file by file.
  • A-TUNNEL-001 — The Qinghuayuan Tunnel alignment is an indicative line inferred from public reporting, used only for the narrative, the low-disturbance advisory band and drawing. It is not a planning boundary, a vertical design or an engineering feasibility conclusion.
  • A-HEAT-001 — Installed compute capacity (13 MW), the waste-heat availability factor (0.7 x 0.85) and the heating load (60 W/sqm) are concept estimates. Power supply, cooling technology, heat-network connection and acoustic setbacks require professional verification.
  • A-PROTO-001 — buildings.geojson holds massing prototypes. It identifies no existing building or tenure; the retain / renovate / new-build labels are conceptual categories and working assumptions, not a demolish-renovate-retain conclusion.
  • A-OSM-001 — OSM features are existing-condition context only (attributed under ODbL) and are not survey-verified; formal design must rely on official survey and tenure data.
  • A-EXPT-001 — The scheme pre-registers three falsifiable spatial hypotheses (see the implementation chapter) and a monitoring approach with the Zhichun Road control segment. This is conceptual research design; data collection must be lawful and subject to human review.
