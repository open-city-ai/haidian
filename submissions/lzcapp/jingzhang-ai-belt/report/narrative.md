### Three-layer cultural-resource fusion

1. **Jing-Zhang railway heritage**: the heritage park as the skeleton, linking the Tsinghua Garden station, Beijing Film Academy and other industrial and cultural relics, telling the modern engineering spirit of "China's first mainline railway designed by Chinese engineers".
2. **Zhongguancun innovation culture**: continuing the "dare to be the first" entrepreneurial tradition, stressing university sourcing, enterprise conversion and open-source collaboration.
3. **AI new culture**: with trustworthiness, inclusivity, low-carbon and accessibility as core values, translating technical capability into participatory public experience.

Fusion strategy: take "Centennial Jing-Zhang — AI Symbiosis" as the main line, presenting the historical engineering spirit, innovation tradition and AI new culture continuously across public space, events and wayfinding, rather than three disconnected slogans.
