## 0 Executive Summary and Compliance Statement

### 0.1 The concept in one sentence

**Turn the century-old Jing-Zhang railway corridor from a line that divides the city into a line that keeps learning** — a linear AI innovation belt whose spatial spine is a continuous 9.67 km heritage park, whose operating mechanism is a set of 14 renewable and retirable scenario cards, and whose discipline is the honest registration of every data gap.

### 0.2 Five propositions

1. **Continuity before capacity.** The 9.67 km spine must never be interrupted by parcel development [metric:heritage_park_length_m]; its value comes from being walkable end to end.
2. **Research land is the lead actor, not a garnish.** Scientific research land (0802) covers 215.3 ha, 18.86% of the master design area and 29.09% of developable land [metric:land_use_ratio_0802] [metric:research_land_share_of_developable_ratio].
3. **East-west stitching is the real problem.** 7 reconnection points and 22 pedestrian access points are proposed [metric:ew_reconnection_count] [metric:pedestrian_access_count] — locations and priorities only, never engineering conclusions.
4. **Scenarios are governed objects, not demos.** All 14 scenario nodes retain a human veto; human-review coverage is 100.00% [metric:scenario_node_human_review_ratio].
5. **Gaps are declared, not filled with invented numbers.** Eleven metrics are registered as `unknown` because the site package marks the underlying official data as missing.

## 1 Overall Concept and Naming System

The proposal is named **The Learning Line**. A learning line is not a line that has been designed once and then executed; it is a line that keeps a feedback loop running: *observe → propose → test in real space → evaluate → retire or scale*. The century-old Jing-Zhang railway is the physical carrier of that loop, and AI is what makes the loop fast enough to matter.

The naming system follows one rule: **every name must be usable by a resident who has no interest in AI.** Names are therefore anchored in place, not in technology.

| Level | Name | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Belt | The Learning Line / 京张智带 | The line learns; the city learns with it |
| Spine | Heritage Park Greenway | 9.67 km continuous, never interrupted |
| Key area A | Zhongzhiyuan Accelerator | 192.9 ha |
| Key area B | AI Origin Community | 104.3 ha |
| Key area C | Dazhongsi Cluster | 72.0 ha |

Deliberately **banned** from the naming system: 'AI Valley', 'Smart City Demonstration Zone', 'Metaverse Park', and any name that would read as dated within five years.

## 4 Regional Innovation Synergy

Regional synergy is expressed as a division of labour rather than a list of cooperation slogans.

| Partner | What flows out of the belt | What flows in |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Zhongguancun core | Governance templates, tested scenario cards | Talent, capital, research base |
| Northern Hebei (Zhangjiakou) | Compute demand, model workloads | Green power, large-scale compute |
| Xiongan | Reusable scenario-card methodology | Greenfield validation contrast |
| Other AI districts nationwide | Open datasets, evaluation standards | Peer review, competing benchmarks |

The single most transferable output of this belt is not a building type but the **scenario card as a governance instrument** — a format that any city can adopt.


<a id="s5-planning"></a>

## 5 Integrated Planning and Space-Industry Fusion

Planning and industry are fused through the **scenario unit**: a parcel is not defined only by land use code but also by the scenario card it is expected to host. There are 10 renewal units averaging 13.8 ha [metric:parcel_count] [metric:parcel_mean_area_sqm].

Reserved land (code 16) accounts for 26.7 ha, 2.34% of the master design area [metric:land_use_area_16_sqm] [metric:land_use_ratio_16]. Reserved land is treated as a **planning instrument, not leftover space**: it is the physical room the belt keeps for scenarios that have not been invented yet, and it must remain reversible. This is an AI-era reading of Beijing's master plan principle of 'reduction, quality improvement, reserved land and added green'. [source:SRC-BJMP]


<a id="s6-ecosystem"></a>

## 6 Full-Stack AI Innovation System and Ecosystem

The full-stack AI innovation system is organised in six layers, each with a spatial counterpart. AI service zones cover 668.1 ha, 58.54% of the master design area, across 5 zones [metric:ai_service_zone_area_sqm] [metric:ai_service_zone_ratio] [metric:ai_service_zone_count]. [data:geometry/ai_service_zones.geojson]

| Layer | Content | Spatial counterpart |
| --- | --- | --- |
| L1 | Compute and energy | Compute service zone |
| L2 | Data and corpora | Data service zone |
| L3 | Models and toolchains | Research land clusters (0802) |
| L4 | Testing and validation | Test-bed zone, embodied-AI test street |
| L5 | Products and incubation | Incubation zone, accelerator |
| L6 | Governance and dialogue | Governance dialogue centre |

The non-obvious layer is L6. Most innovation districts treat governance as a compliance cost applied after the fact; here it is given dedicated space and placed **in front of** deployment, because the bottleneck for urban AI is rarely capability and almost always permission. [source:SRC-AI]


<a id="s7-benchmark"></a>

## 7 Global Benchmark Cases

Eight international and domestic cases were examined. Each is cited for one specific transferable lesson rather than as a general model to copy.

| ID | Case | What it demonstrates | Why it matters here |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| B-01 | Station F, Paris | A 1927 rail freight station turned into the world's largest single-building startup campus | Supports converting railway heritage into innovation capacity; the Dazhongsi freight yard unit follows the same logic |
| B-02 | one-north, Singapore | Long-horizon district with deliberately undetermined plots released over decades | Supports the reserved-land instrument and the exit mechanism for scenario units |
| B-03 | Kendall Square, Boston | Extreme walkable proximity between research, industry and housing | Supports the compact three-area layout and the controlled number of pedestrian access points |
| B-04 | King's Cross Knowledge Quarter, London | Public realm delivered before commercial floorspace; heritage structures reused rather than replaced | Supports the public-space-first phasing strategy |
| B-05 | The High Line, New York | A disused elevated rail line as a continuous linear public space with a dedicated operating body | Supports the programming and long-term operation model of the heritage park spine |
| B-06 | Pan-Canadian AI Strategy / AI triangle | Three cities with differentiated rather than duplicated AI roles | Supports the differentiated division of labour with other Beijing science cities |
| B-07 | Zhangjiang AI Island, Shanghai | Scenario admission lists as an operational instrument | Supports how the scenario card and its admission list are written |
| B-08 | Hetao Shenzhen-HK Cooperation Zone | Cross-jurisdiction compute and data interfaces | Supports reserving cross-regional compute and data interfaces |

The common thread is that none of these places succeeded by announcing an industry. They succeeded by making a specific piece of the city usable first.


<a id="s8-factors"></a>

## 8 Factor Guarantee Mechanisms

Eight factor-guarantee mechanisms are proposed. All are **suggestions on mechanism design**, not conclusions on institutional arrangements, funding or personnel.

| Factor | Problem observed | Proposed mechanism |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Compute | Small teams cannot afford it while installed capacity sits idle | Open compute platform billed hourly with off-peak scheduling; public quota weighted towards open-source projects |
| Data | Data exists but cannot legally move | Tiered open catalogue plus a sandbox where data is usable but not visible, supplemented by synthetic data |
| Scenarios | Pilots are one-off demonstrations with no path to renewal | Scenario admission list plus annual street-level test-bed intake batches |
| Talent | Researchers commute in and leave; the belt is empty at night | Talent housing within walking distance, free third spaces, short-stay footholds |
| Space | Laboratory requirements change faster than buildings | Switchable laboratory standards (redundant floor height, loading, power, exhaust) and a scenario-unit whitelist |
| Capital | Financing and IP services are scattered across the city | A concentrated financing and IP service interface in the technology-service wing |
| Institutions | Compliance is discovered after deployment | A governance dialogue centre providing up-front advice; annual filing suggested in place of item-by-item approval |
| Open-source community | Community activity has no physical home | Regular meetup venues along the spine and a physical display interface for open data and code |

The belt carries 14 scenario cards [metric:scenario_node_count]. A scenario card is a governed object with a defined owner, a defined spatial node, a defined data boundary, a mandatory human review path and — critically — a **defined exit condition**. Human-review coverage across all cards is 100.00% [metric:scenario_node_human_review_ratio]. [data:geometry/scenario_nodes.geojson]

| SC-03 | Dazhongsi AI-native business building | Building operation | PH-3 | Withdrawn if energy savings are not reproducible across two annual cycles |
| SC-04 | Beitaipingzhuang AI pilot-production works | Pilot manufacturing | PH-2 | Withdrawn if pilot slots stay unbooked for two consecutive intakes |

| SC-06 | Zhichunlu urban sensing and digital twin hub | Sensing and digital twin | PH-1 | Withdrawn if the twin is not used in an actual planning decision within a year |
| SC-07 | Origin Community open compute platform | Open compute | PH-1 | Withdrawn if external (non-resident) usage share falls below the agreed floor |
| SC-08 | Wudaokou embodied-AI test street | Embodied AI | PH-1 | Suspended on any incident rate above threshold; physical fence and remote e-stop |
| SC-09 | Wudaokou talent community smart living hall | Living services | PH-1 | Withdrawn if the space is not used on non-event days |

## 11 AI Industry Test and Validation Beds

Six physical test beds are proposed, each tied to a spatial condition that cannot be reproduced in a laboratory. Every test bed operates on annual admission batches and is suspended automatically when its incident threshold is exceeded.

| ID | Test bed | Spatial condition | What it validates |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| TB-01 | Embodied-AI test street | Branch-level controllable street section with redundant human-machine separation | Obstacle avoidance and interaction of service and delivery robots in real pedestrian flow |
| TB-02 | Autonomous shuttle loop | Campus-scale low-speed loop with defined pick-up points | Dispatching, boarding and mixed-traffic safety |
| TB-03 | Low-altitude logistics corridor | Defined take-off and landing points away from residential windows | Last-mile delivery and emergency supply drops, noise impact |
| TB-04 | Smart health and elder-care consortium | Real elderly population in situ | Health monitoring, fall alerting and companion robots |
| TB-05 | Urban digital twin sandbox | Full-belt geometry and operational data | Simulation and comparison of planning and operating options |
| TB-06 | Open compute platform | Physical compute room plus public access interface | Compatibility and energy efficiency of domestic compute stacks |


<a id="s12-privacy"></a>

## 12 Privacy, Human Review and Governance Boundary

Privacy and human review are treated as **design constraints on space**, not as legal boilerplate. Three rules apply to every one of the 14 scenario nodes:

1. **Edge processing and anonymisation by default.** Raw imagery does not leave the node.
2. **A human veto is always retained.** No automated decision affecting an individual may execute without a reviewable human path — coverage 100.00% [metric:scenario_node_human_review_ratio].
3. **Facial recognition may not be used for individual tracking**, and every sensing device must be physically identifiable, queryable and switch-off-able by the public.

A personal information impact assessment must be completed before any node goes live. See assumption `A-SCENARIO-GOVERNANCE`.


<a id="s13-publicspace"></a>

## 13 Heritage Park AI Public Space and Stitching

The heritage park is the single most important public space move: a continuous 9.67 km green spine along the railway corridor [metric:heritage_park_length_m], supported by 214.9 ha of green and open space, 18.83% of the master design area [metric:green_open_space_area_sqm] [metric:green_open_space_ratio]. [data:geometry/green_space.geojson] [data:geometry/public_space.geojson]

Stitching across the corridor is organised at three scales: 7 east-west reconnections at city scale, 22 pedestrian access points at neighbourhood scale, and continuous edge treatment at street scale. The proposal identifies **where** stitching is needed and in what priority; it does not specify bridge or tunnel forms, structural solutions or clearances, which are explicitly outside the permitted scope.

<a id="s14-landmarks"></a>

## 14 AI Pilgrimage Landmarks and Honour Display

Rather than a single iconic tower, the proposal distributes **pilgrimage landmarks** along the line: places an AI practitioner would travel to see, and a resident would walk past every day. The honour display system records contribution rather than status — a physical contribution wall accumulating two years of records before any evaluation mechanism is introduced.


<a id="s15-components"></a>

## 15 Public Space Smart Component Library

A public-space smart component library standardises the physical objects that carry AI in the street: seating with power, shaded waiting points, sensing poles with visible identification, and a maintenance access standard. Components are specified so that they can be **removed without trace** — reversibility is the design requirement, because the half-life of any specific device is shorter than that of the street.


<a id="s16-culture"></a>

## 16 Centennial Jing-Zhang Culture and New AI Culture

The cultural narrative connects two moments a century apart: the 1909 Jing-Zhang railway as China's first self-designed trunk line, and the present attempt to build a self-reliant AI stack. The proposal treats this parallel as a **working method rather than a slogan**: both are cases of building capability under constraint, and both are best commemorated by keeping the artefact in daily use rather than behind glass.


<a id="s17-signage"></a>

## 17 Signage, Wayfinding and Symbol System

Signage is bilingual by default and physical by principle. Any information that a visitor needs in order to act must exist on a physical surface, not only in an app: a district that can only be navigated with a working phone and account excludes personas P-04 and P-08 by design.


<a id="s18-events"></a>

## 18 Global AI Innovation Event System

The event system runs at three cadences: a global annual conference, quarterly open test-bed intake days, and weekly community meetups along the spine. The weekly cadence matters most — it is what makes the belt inhabited between conferences.


<a id="s19-operation"></a>

## 19 Developer Community and Long-Term Operation

Operation is organised in three layers: a platform layer (compute quota, data catalogue, evaluation standards), a spatial layer (public space maintenance, programming, component upkeep) and a community layer (open-source community self-governance). The allocation of these roles is a **conceptual suggestion** and does not constitute a conclusion on institutional structure, mandates or staffing.

Three health indicators are proposed in place of the conventional 'number of registered enterprises': (a) external developer calls on open data and compute; (b) annual renewal and retirement rate of scenario cards; (c) spontaneous use of public space on non-event days. Together they describe whether the line is still learning.

<a id="s23-guidelines"></a>

## 23 Urban Design Guideline Points

Design guidance is given as intent plus a small number of reverse clauses. The three reverse clauses are the operative part:

1. **The continuity of the heritage park spine may not be broken** for any reason, including construction hoarding lasting beyond one review cycle.
2. **No unlabelled facial recognition device may be deployed in public space.** Every sensing installation must be identifiable, queryable and switchable-off.
3. **Reserved land may not be converted into irreversible construction.**

Numerical guidance (frontage ratio, setback, height) is deliberately withheld until the statutory conditions in section 20 are released, because guidance calibrated against invented numbers becomes sunk cost that has to be undone.
