H0–H3 tiered right of way

H0 human quiet zone · heritage, children, rest; autonomous machines are prohibited
H1 slow-mobility priority zone · people have absolute priority; machines enter briefly for a task
H2 low-speed shared zone · control by time slot and zone, intent signalling, emergency stop and takeover
H3 peripheral connection zone · accessible station-to-city connection, no crossing of the heritage core

In ordinary pedestrian space the reference machine speed is ≤1.2 m/s; all other speeds await local risk assessment and approval, and 20 km/h is not presumed.

Twelve Shichen · time-slot shared movement in Jing-Zhang

Twelve operating clauses across one day hang on the shared spine and on existing nodes: let the park sleep first, then let people have the peak, and keep logistics on H3 only. This is not the Fuxing Island timetable with the place names swapped.

Zi / Chou / Yin · H0 quiet return to depot · H3 recharging and maintenance · H2 off-peak cleaning
Mao / Chen / Si · doors open and machines yield · school and work peak · shared-work workshop
Wu / Wei / Shen · shared meals and rest · guided slow walk · campus stitching
You / Xu / Hai · homeward yielding · evening care for elders · patrol handover

Must not be written into this clock: low-altitude delivery across the park, platooning of self-driving shuttles, L4–L5 full autonomy, or a blanket speed limit.

Three field acceptance gates · fail one and the whole corridor is downgraded

Three of the twelve clauses are raised to gates that can be counted on the spot. A failure is recorded against the machine type and the time slot; swapping in another machine to keep occupying the route is not accepted. Human passage is never downgraded.

G-01 Return-to-depot closure · after 23:00 the number of autonomous machines inside H0 must be 0; three consecutive nights of FAIL leave that machine type on H3 only
G-02 Yielding at peak · at the Chen and You peaks the RD-001 centreline must not be occupied; ≥1 near-miss that day bars that machine type from the same peak the next day
G-03 Logistics kept outside · the number of delivery machines inside the park and seating areas must be 0; a failure closes park-side delivery for that day
Five field components · quiet return-to-depot post · pull-off bay · handover bay · emergency-stop post plus screen-free window · H3 recharging loop

Until P0 is complete, only closed drills are run. If any gate fails repeatedly, the opening permission for that segment reverts to the previous stage.

Guideline translation · the Jing-Zhang implementation playbook

Only the procedures of the Fuxing Island guideline are translated, not the scenarios on the island. The four stages hang on the existing gates, field components and renewal projects.

S1 Planning assessment · if the transport, pedestrian-flow, heritage and accessibility assessments are not closed, the spine must not be opened
S2 Construction in step · beacons, emergency stops and handover bays are accepted together with the civil works; the three gates must be drilled before handover
S3 Test, certify, connect · testing → compatibility verification → connection; missing geofence, takeover, emergency stop or logs means no connection
S4 Operational review · human-in-the-loop, geographic and temporal red lines, revision at most every 12 months; if any Five Friendliness item is unmet, there is no opening

Not carried into Jing-Zhang: low-altitude delivery, dedicated autonomous-driving lanes, L4–L5 full autonomy, brands certified elsewhere, first-purchase and first-use schemes, and fiscal subsidies.

Whole-lifecycle governance gates

  • Before entry: classification, safety inspection, insurance, and data and accessibility impact assessment
  • In operation: geofencing, identity and intent signalling, logs, emergency stop, human safety marshal
  • In an anomaly: protect people first; safe degradation on network loss, low battery or positioning failure
  • After operation: review of incidents, near misses and complaints; downgrade or exit if standards are not met

Taskbook coverage

  • One spine and three zones, Five Settings and Five Friendliness Principles, many nodes and twin substrates
  • Eight categories of human users plus six categories of machine agents
  • Three industrial stages of testing and validation, plus human alternatives
  • Public opt-out, privacy, ethics, liability and long-term operation

Drawing precision grades

A Recomputable · scope areas, green space ratio, public space ratio, road network length: recomputed directly from the submitted geometry in EPSG:4548
B Structurally reliable · spine direction, the division of labour across three areas, node attribution, phasing sequence: the structure holds, positions are indicative
C Positionally indicative · spine alignment, cross-section widths, loop dimensions, plot boundaries: uncertainty of about ±200 m, must be replaced by survey
D Not usable · red lines, tenure, heritage control lines, vertical levels and utilities: not provided by this proposal

Not constructible at this stage: shared-corridor works along the whole line, investment promotion based on the drawings, performance promised on the basis of the drawings.
