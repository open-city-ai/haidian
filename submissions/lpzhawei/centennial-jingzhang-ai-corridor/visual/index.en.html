Three-tier scope

The proposal uses a tiered scope — coordinated research, overall design, key districts — to bind industrial judgement, urban renewal and detailed district design to the layers and metrics.

project_scope_summary.csv

Coordinated research scope

Across 43.6 km² — propose the AI industry ecosystem, three-district two-wing coordination, future-city form and cultural narrative.

  • AI innovation & talent chain
  • Global case translation mechanism
  • Brand naming & communication

Overall design scope

Across 11.4 km² — organise urban renewal, land-use structure, transport & municipal, and the Jing-Zhang heritage park vitality belt.

  • Control-planning-depth urban design
  • Renewal project list
  • Blue-green slow-traffic system

Key-district scope

Detailed design in the three key districts, covering functional mix, building renewal, public space and transit organisation.

  • Da Zhong Si AI Industry Cluster

Key areas

Each of the three key districts carries an industry positioning, a spatial strategy, building and public-space actions, a transit interface and an implementation risk note.

key_areas.geojson

Zhong Zhi Yuan AI Acceleration District

Garden-style independent-innovation block. National platform, industrial exhibition, Qinghe culture, external transit and low-carbon public space.

Beijing AI Origin Community

Near-campus transformation block. University source innovation, open-source community, talent services, demolition/retention and station integration.

Da Zhong Si AI Industry Cluster

City-style intelligent-economy block. Leading enterprises, agent-native new business formats, commercial services, compound green use and four-quadrant intersection connectivity.
