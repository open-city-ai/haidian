Each area includes its positioning, area, and core spatial actions. Together, the three areas form a north-south functional chain: "R&D acceleration — community origin — industrial carrier."

area cards
Zhongzhiyuan AI Acceleration Area192.1 ha · North
Positioning
Northern hub of the Jingzhang AI Nexus. Leveraging railway heritage and computing infrastructure to create a pilot acceleration zone for AI startup teams.
Core Spatial Actions
  • Establish computing hub and computing service platform nodes
  • Stitch northern Railway Heritage Park with blue-green corridors
  • Deploy 3 accelerator clusters and shared pilot workshops
Beijing AI Origin Community104.3 ha · Central
Positioning
The origin community at the corridor's center, emphasizing "AI returns to daily life." Mixed-use blocks and public portals host the intersection of algorithm communities and urban life.
Core Spatial Actions
  • Origin Plaza as public portal and event node
  • Mixed-use blocks: R&D / residential / commercial vertical stacking
  • Qinghe slow-mobility portal connecting station to blue-green system
Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster72.0 ha · South
Positioning
Industrial carrier area at the corridor's southern end. Capitalizing on Dazhongsi's existing commercial and office assets, pivoting toward corporate headquarters, AI applications, and platform economy.
Core Spatial Actions
  • Renew stock commercial / office assets into headquarters clusters
  • Establish AI application experimental blocks and showcase windows
  • Strengthen transit interchange with metro and AI spine
