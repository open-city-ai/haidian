# Jing-Zhang Wisdom Co-Existence —— Open Intelligent Community Urban Design

> Jing-Zhang Intelligence Commons / Open Intelligent Community. The proposal conceives of the "AI Innovation Corridor" as a suite of urban infrastructure that can be used, tested, and governed by researchers, businesses, residents, students, city managers, and international visitors, rather than a closed-off campus or a one-off image project. Current boundaries are provisional and rough data, and the drawings and metrics are provided solely to serve the current open-source call for proposals and professional discussions.

## Design Basis and Source List

The primary control basis for the proposal is the announcement from the Haidian Branch of the Beijing Municipal Commission of Planning and Natural Resources, the task book for the intelligent body within the warehouse, the site package, the Source Registry, and five mandatory standards. The work sequence is "confirming source qualifications—identifying gaps—establishing a three-tier scope—generating spatial layers—recalculating indicators—forming drawings—automatic review." The announcement provides the task hierarchy for comprehensive research, overall design, and three key areas; the task book supplements open collaboration, scenarios, brand, and operational requirements; professional standards constrain Urban Design, control plan expression, and land use classification. Any content derived from case studies or open data is marked as background research and does not replace local statutory conditions. ## Three-Level Scope Framework

Coordinate the research level to address "how the global AI innovation network relates to the scientific research, industry, living, and cultural resources in Haidian"; at the overall design level, address "how 11.4 square kilometers of Urban Renewal can form a continuous space, operational projects, and verifiable indicators"; and at the key area level, address "how Zhongzhiyuan, AI Origin community, and Dazhongsi can form three complementary detailed design prototypes." The three levels are not scaled proportionally, but rather close in from a strategic network, spatial system, to block-level actions. [depth:three_level_scope_framework] [depth:overall_spatial_structure]

The core structure is defined as "one spine, three courts, two wings, and twelve scenes." The "one spine" is the "Centennial Wisdom Track Corridor" formed along the historic Jing-Zhang site and the Walking and Cycling Network. The "three courts" are sequentially the northern Court of Universal Intelligence Protocol, the central Origin Open Source Station, and the southern Great Bell Future Plaza. The "two wings" are the Zhongguancun Technology Services Wing and the Xiaoyue River Scenario Enablement Wing. The twelve scene nodes link research and development, validation, public services, cultural narratives, and global dissemination into a continuous experience. The structure is anchored to [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-001], [data:geometry/roads.geojson#ROAD-001], [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#PUBLIC-001], and [metric:key_area_count].

## Coordinated Research Area: Industry and Future City Research

The common inspiration from the six international cases is not about replicating the park-like form, but about forming a sustainable feedback loop of "knowledge production—technology transfer—public value—talent living." MIT Kendall Square mixes research, experimentation, residence, retail, museum, and open spaces; Mila connects research and industry through open science, ethics, and diverse communities; Vector emphasizes the transition from research to trusted adoption; AI Singapore forms a task-driven network through national problem challenges, talent development, corporate co-creation, and open-source products; Seoul Yangchun AI Hub supports the entire lifecycle of enterprises through education, incubation, research and development infrastructure, and industry-academia-research collaboration; ETH AI Center connects science and society through interdisciplinary approaches, inclusive and trustworthy AI, entrepreneurship, and public communication. [source:CASE-MIT-KENDALL] [source:CASE-MILA] [source:CASE-VECTOR] [source:CASE-AISG] [source:CASE-SEOUL] [source:CASE-ETH]

The translation of Jing-Zhang is four principles: its first, space transforms from "enterprise exclusive parks" into accessible community interfaces; its second, innovation shifts from one-way demonstrations to public problem posing, controlled data, sandbox validation, public feedback, and knowledge archiving; its third, talent services encompass work, learning, residence, social interaction, and international engagement; its fourth, historical railways are not decorative symbols but public spines organizing pedestrian narratives, time markers, and global exchanges. At the research level, industrial judgment only proposes networks and mechanisms, without fabricating company lists, investment scales, or policy promises. [standard:PROJECT-AGENT-OPEN-CALL-TASKBOOK] [depth:overall_spatial_structure]

## Overall Design Area: Urban Renewal and Regulatory-Plan-Level Urban Design

Overall space is organized by a century-long trackage corridor that connects six types of land uses: AI R&D and open-source collaboration, parks and ecological open spaces, technology services and vibrant commercial activities, research and education with technology transfer, talent communities and living services, and urban rail transit and integrated transportation facilities. The zoning uses the standard `land_use_code`, fully covering the Provisional Boundary without overlap; its purpose is to establish structural proportions and spatial adjacency relationships, not to replace formal land plots or approval plans. [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-001] [standard:MOHURD-CONTROL-DETAILED-PLANNING] [depth:land_use_layout]

Urban Renewal employs the "retain the skeleton, modify the facade, and supplement the nodes" approach: retaining buildings that can continue to be used and railway cultural cues; transforming the closed ground floor, boundary walls, and single office facade into public service and innovative interaction interfaces; and supplementing small-scale new prototypes to add functions for open-source collaboration, trust verification, international roadshows, and talent services. [data:geometry/buildings.geojson#BLDG-001] Only expresses eighteen conceptual Building Footprints; the demolish–renovate–retain status must be confirmed after current surveying, ownership, structural safety, fire safety, and cultural heritage verification. The Floor Area Ratio, total building scale, Building Coverage Ratio, height, and setback remain unknown. [depth:development_intensity_controls] [depth:retain_renovate_demolish] (Demolish–Renovate–Retain Strategy)

## Detailed Design of Key Areas

Northern Zhongzhiyuan is positioned as the "Zhongzhi Protocol Courthouse": it features a green public courtyard as a shared interface for safe governance, standard collaboration, and the display of low-carbon technologies. This serves as an organizational model for evaluating tests, red team testing, edge device and robot validation. The ground floor of the buildings uses bookable test corridors and exhibition galleries, while upper levels house research and collaboration. Along the Qinghe River side, ecological and flood protection conditions take precedence, and any structures must undergo subsequent specialized argumentation. This area does not pursue iconic high-rise buildings but emphasizes visible and credible processes and reversible experimental facilities. [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-001]

The central area of the Beijing AI Origin Community is positioned as the "AI Origin Open Source Hub": it views the pedestrian connections between universities, parks, communities, and rail stations as a conversion infrastructure. Open source release halls, contribution archives, a near-campus conversion street, talent life management, and small residential services are arranged around the public hall; nighttime activities are managed by sound environment and time tiering. The southern area of Dazhongsi is positioned as the "Dazhong Future Plaza": it forms a quadruple quadrant public interface around station-city pedestrian connections, smart terminals, content consumption, data element compliance display, and international roadshows. The three areas collectively implement a mixed model of "public foundation + professional platform + daily services." [depth:three_key_area_detailed_design] [metric:key_area_count]

## AI Innovation Ecosystem, Personas, and AI+ Scenarios

Six archetypes are identified: researchers, developers/entrepreneurs, students, residents/elders, urban operators, and international visitors. Researchers require interdisciplinary meetings, experimental ethics, and pathways for technology transfer; developers need access to computing resources, testing environments, peer reputation, and cost-effective spaces; students require courses, mentors, and real-world problems; residents particularly focus on accessibility, privacy, low disturbance, and recourse; urban operators need auditable data and Human Review; international visitors require multilingual guided tours, short-term collaboration, and clear public entry points. These archetypes are used only for designing service journeys, and do not save individual trajectories or form commercial labels. [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK] [depth:municipal_new_infrastructure]

Twelve scenario cards are as follows: 01 Open Source Model Co-Creation Station; 02 Urban Problem Challenge Platform; 03 Trustworthy Data Sandbox (Validation); 04 Model Security Red Team Field (Validation); 05 Edge Side Intelligent and Robot Slow Test Ring (Validation); 06 Accessible Companion Assistant; 07 Centennial Jing-Zhang Multilingual Memory Guided Tour; 08 AI Talent Life Steward; 09 Research—Industry Matching Lounge; 10 AI-Native Business Co-Creation Market; 11 Climate and Blue-Green Co-Protection Platform; 12 Global Contribution Honor Archive and Live Forum. Each node is annotated with `scenario_id`, totaling [metric:scenario_node_count], among which three are explicitly designated as industrial test and validation scenarios. [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#PUBLIC-001] (Testing and Validation Scenario)

The governance floor is data minimization, purpose limitation, authorization revocability, result explainability, Human Review, security classification, and event archiving. Public service agents only provide suggestions and navigation, not automatic administrative decisions; the test field first uses synthetic or authorized data in a sandbox before entering public demonstration; residents can obtain equivalent basic services without using smart functionalities. The operational loop is "open issue—data and ethics review—sandbox validation—public demonstration—human-centric evaluation—knowledge archiving."

## Land Use, Building Scale, and Retain-Renovate-Demolish Strategy

The land use zones were recalculated through EPSG:4548 projection as a conceptual structure test. The Building Footprint area [metric:building_footprint_area_sqm] and the design building footprint ratio [metric:design_building_footprint_ratio] were derived from the union of eighteen prototype footprints; these objects are individually labeled "Preservation Retrofit, Adaptive Reuse, Supplemental New Construction," but their status is a strategy tag rather than a current condition assessment. Formal deepening must include a site-by-site addition of property rights, age, structural condition, fire safety, intensity of use, cultural heritage value, and tenant impact surveys. [standard:MNR-LAND-USE-CLASSIFICATION-GUIDE] [depth:height_massing_character]

The architectural style adopts a restrained system of "Railway Warm Red + Technological Blue": it preserves the industrial and railway elements of brick, steel, and scale, using blue elements to mark new, variable, testable, and removable interventions; the first floor emphasizes transparency, shading, seating areas, and continuous barrier-free access. The roof prioritizes rainwater management, photovoltaics, light activities, and habitats, without any unverified giant screens. The total gross floor area [metric:total_floor_area_sqm], Floor Area Ratio [metric:floor_area_ratio], Building Height [metric:building_height_m], density [metric:building_density], and setback [metric:setback_m] are all pending formal zoning confirmation.

## Transport, Rail, Municipal Infrastructure, and Public Services

Traffic strategies aim for "trains to the courtyard, walking to the door, cycling in a loop." The century-old smart rail slow-moving spine provides a continuous north-south experience, while five east-west stitch corridors connect the technology service wing with the scenario empowerment wing. Three courtyards serve as nodes for turning, resting, wayfinding, and activity dispersal. The total length of the road centerlines is calculated in [metric:road_centerline_length_m]. OSM rail and road features are used only for background identification; station entrances, red lines, crossing methods, and traffic organization must be corrected based on official documentation and specialized design. [data:geometry/roads.geojson#ROAD-001] [source:OSM-CONTEXT] [depth:traffic_rail_slow_parking]

Municipal infrastructure and New Infrastructure take a "traditional facilities first, smart services overlay" approach. After electricity, communication, drainage, fire protection, and emergency services are in place, edge computing, sensing, robot charging and swapping, and digital signage are then deployed; all equipment should have offline fallback, manual takeover, and maintenance responsibilities. Public services are composed of shared meeting rooms, intellectual property, legal services, financing and investment, talent accommodation, childcare, health, and cultural services to form a 15-minute innovation living network. The capacity of facilities, pipeline routing, and energy solutions must be reviewed after all engineering documentation is complete. [depth:municipal_new_infrastructure]

## Blue-Green Network, Public Space, and Urban Character

A century-long trammel of smart tracks weaves a narrative of railway heritage, green spaces, rainwater management, slow travel, and public learning; three east-west green connections form a "Pact, Open Source, Future" city living room at the north, middle, and south. The submitted green space area [metric:green_space_area_sqm], green space ratio [metric:green_ratio], Public Space area [metric:public_space_area_sqm], and public space ratio [metric:public_space_ratio] are recalculated by the union of layers. They are design proportions under the Provisional Boundary, not statutory green space ratios or approved public space indicators. [data:geometry/green_space.geojson#GREEN-001] [depth:blue_green_public_space]

Appearance is not a uniform style but a unified set of reading rules: Warm red marks historical and permanent elements, tech blue marks new technologies and variable facilities, white paper carries public information, and deep black is used for readable text. Green is reserved for the ecosystem only. Mileposts simultaneously display the railway's era, contemporary open-source contributions, and the next urban challenge, forming a continuous narrative of "past-present-future." Nightscapes control brightness and timing to avoid continuous disturbance to residents and habitats. [standard:MOHURD-URBAN-DESIGN-MEASURES]

V2 does not understand "digitization" as screens, sensors, or visual styles, but rather establishes a traceable chain of decision-making: **site parameters → spatial rules → scenario simulation → performance thresholds → human sign-off → version differences**. This method references the AA Landscape Urbanism approach to combining multi-scale mapping, GIS, script simulations, and policy design; it references Foster's integration of infrastructure with Public Space, as well as Arup's coordination of transportation, water, energy, data, and implementation systems, but does not claim any institutional involvement or endorsement. Secondary pages are only used for cross-referencing; all design references prioritize the firm's official pages, and do not reuse reference article images. [source:CASE-MASDAR-FOSTER] [source:CASE-MASDAR-ARCHIPOSITION] [source:CASE-CONSTANTA-FOSTER] [source:CASE-CONSTANTA-GOOOOD]

The spatial model is controlled by six groups of parameters: temporary range, main ridge orientation, east-west seam position, three courtyards public ground, intervention envelope, and phased threshold. `visual/assets/computation_protocol.json` records the parameters, dependent files, recalculation order, and manual review points; after the formal boundary is in place, the model must first generate a difference table, then update the layers, metrics, text, HTML, and drawings. ## Differentiated Three-Areas and Urban Section

The Collective Intelligence Courthouse adopts "Low-Density Testing Courtyard": long-oriented public courtyards connect R&D, Red Teams, and end-side testing, with permanent structures and temporary facilities managed in layers; the Origin Open Station adopts "Diagonal Exchange Hall": integrating daily traversals between universities, communities, and parks into the shared ground floor; the Great Clock Future Plaza adopts "Station-City Dual Fields": using two urban lounges on either side and a central barrier-free connection to form a quadrants-based transfer and activity interface. The three no longer use the same rectangular template, with their public ground surfaces, building envelopes, and slow-moving loops separately modeled in GeoJSON. [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#PUBLIC-001] [data:geometry/buildings.geojson#BLDG-001] [data:geometry/roads.geojson#ROAD-010]

Focus areas should be developed in accordance with a five-layer profile of "underground utilities—ground-level pedestrian and rainwater management—first-floor public interface—upper research and development/living—rooftop energy and habitat." Dimensions, tree canopies, pipe lines, structural, and fire data are missing; therefore, the drawings should express the design rules and test thresholds without fabricating construction-level sections.
