Coordinated Research Area: Industry and Future City Research

The overall name is "Jingzhang Symbiotic Corridor" (Chinese: 京张智脉共生带), English subtitle "AI Pulse Symbiotic Belt", evoking the Jingzhang railway "pulse/vein" and the symbiosis of AI compute, data, talent, and urban life along the heritage corridor. The visual identity direction uses a "rail-line — pulse-wave — symbiotic-ring" motif: a pulse polyline running north–south nested with concentric rings representing the three belts, set in a neutral sans-serif to avoid unauthorized commercial fonts or stock assets StandardDepth.

The proposal answers the announcement's calls for a world-class AI innovation ecosystem, industry-chain synergy, three-zones-two-wings, future city form, and continuous green space. The three positions are the Centennial Jingzhang Culture Belt, the Urban AI Living-Experience Belt, and the AI Convergence Innovation Belt; the five functions are the AI full-stack self-reliant system, world-class AI innovation ecosystem, AI+ scenario empowerment paradigm, intelligent AI vibrant city, and global discourse power in AI governance Source. The three-zones-two-wings synergy loop takes Beijing AI Origin Community (world-class ecosystem), Zhongzhiyuan (full-stack autonomy & governance), and Dazhongsi (intelligent-native new formats) as the core, linked outward to the Zhongguancun technology-service wing and the Xiaoyuehe scenario-empowerment wing, forming a "research—conversion—experience—governance" closed loop Depth.

Global AI innovation ecosystem cases (readable summaries, conceptual borrowing):

  1. Montreal/Toronto Vector Institute and AI supercluster: public compute and talent networks supporting basic research and industry conversion.
  2. San Francisco Bay Area: innovation density coupling venture capital, top universities, and open-source communities.
  3. Tel Aviv: high-density startups with military-civilian conversion mechanisms.
  4. Singapore Smart Nation: city-scale data trust and regulatory sandbox.
  5. Shenzhen Nanshan–Yuehai: hardware supply chain co-located with AI terminal manufacturing.
  6. Amsterdam AI ecosystem: people-centered ethical governance and public experiment fields.

These experiences translate into spatial, operational, and scenario mechanisms: public compute nodes, developer-community hubs, sandbox districts, and open scenario operation SourceDepth.

Overall Design Area: Urban Renewal and Regulatory-Plan-Depth Urban Design

The overall design area (~11.4 km²) is organized at regulatory-plan depth around the structure "one pulse stringing three zones, two wings bearing synergy": the Jingzhang heritage-park vibrant belt is the central vein linking the three key areas and extending toward the two wings Spatial dataDepth. Land use nests scientific-research land (0802), public-administration & public-service mixed land (08), commercial-service land (05), urban community-facility land (0702), and park green space (1401) along the corridor into "research—service—living—green" composite cells MetricMetricMetric.

The agent geometry gives a conceptual land-use partition: research and mixed public-service land carries AI full-stack innovation, commercial-service land carries intelligent-native consumption and business, community-facility land carries talent living services, and park green space and squares carry public activity. The building footprint is about 435,007 m², a conceptual massing placeholder only; specific floor-area ratio, building height, and development intensity must follow official regulatory conditions and are not stated here as statutory indicators MetricDepth.

Renewal targets are mainly inefficient existing plants, railway-heritage ancillary facilities, and scattered older communities, classified into retain, renovate, demolish, and build-new; the specific retain-renovate-demolish list and ownership checks are listed as pending confirmation, to be deepened after official status survey and ownership data arrive Depth. The urban character is "low-density, green-connected, walkable, memorable"; roof form and massing respect the linear-heritage scale of the Jingzhang railway and avoid large masses blocking corridor views Depth.

Detailed Design of Key Areas

Zhongzhiyuan AI Self-Reliant Acceleration Zone (~192.1 ha, PROV-KEY-001) is positioned as the carrier of the AI full-stack self-reliant system and governance discourse. Its spatial structure centers an accelerator cluster surrounded by a public compute center, open-source community hub, and regulatory-sandbox district; building renewal mainly renovates existing R&D volumes and reserves gap land for major platforms Spatial dataDepth.

Beijing AI Origin Community (~104.3 ha, PROV-KEY-002) is positioned as a world-class AI innovation ecosystem and a high-quality urban district talent aspire to. A "work—live—socialize" loop is formed by mixed community, talent apartments, an international talent lounge, and open labs; the slow-traffic network directly reaches the Jingzhang heritage park, strengthening daily perceptible AI public space for talent Spatial data.

Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster (~72.0 ha, PROV-KEY-003) is positioned for intelligent-native new formats, carrying AI+ consumption, AI+ business, and showcase experience. A ring commercial circulation strings experience stores, a release hall, and a developer market; night economy and public-experience routes combine into AI urban scenarios that are perceptible, demonstrable, and promotable Spatial data.

Each key area forms a readable mini-scheme of "positioning + spatial structure + building renewal + slow traffic + public space + AI scenario + implementation risk"; boundaries are provisional and conclusions are directional, to be rechecked for area and building volume after official redlines arrive Depth.

AI Innovation Ecosystem, Personas, and AI+ Scenarios

The proposal defines five user personas mapped to space and scenarios: ① AI researchers/engineers (research land & open-source hub), ② startup founders (accelerator & sandbox district), ③ international talent & visitors (origin-community lounge), ④ local residents (community facilities & public space), ⑤ urban governors (regulation & compute operations) Depth. Each persona corresponds to a clear spatial anchor, data need, privacy boundary, and human-review mechanism Source.

AI scenario cards (at least 10, including at least 3 industry test/validation scenarios):

  • S1 Public compute reservation & carbon dashboard (industry test/validation): compliant compute booking for SMEs and universities with energy/carbon visualization, data anonymized, human-reviewed.
  • S2 Autonomous shuttle loop test (industry test/validation): bounded L4 shuttle and slow-traffic testing, fully pausable and traceable.
  • S3 AI-assisted community clinic (industry test/validation): community-level triage and referral suggestions, assistive only, finalized by physicians.
  • S4 AI+ education personalized learning partner: adaptive tutoring in schools and communities, protecting minors' privacy.
  • S5 AI+ legal self-service pod: standardized legal Q&A, complex matters escalated to human lawyers.
  • S6 AI+ living-services steward: accessible travel, grocery, repair aging-friendly assistant.
  • S7 Developer market & model expo: offline open-source model release and experience.
  • S8 Jingzhang heritage AI guide & AR time-capsule: augmented-reality narrative of railway heritage.
  • S9 Urban-operation AI inspection (green, parking, waste): auto-detected events dispatched, human-confirmed.
  • S10 Public-experience-route smart guide: multilingual, accessible visitor routing.
  • S11 Crowd monitoring & dispersal: large-crowd warning, hints only, no individual identification.
  • S12 Low-carbon mobility incentive: walk/cycle points and carbon co-benefit.

All scenarios uphold data minimization, human review, and traceable operation ownership; excessive surveillance and non-reviewable systems are prohibited; test scenarios are explicitly labeled "test/validation" and do not constitute approved operation Standard.

Transport, Rail, Municipal Infrastructure, and Public Services

Circulation centers on "rail-station integration + micro-circulation + continuous slow traffic". The proposal gives 4 conceptual road centerlines in geometry/roads.geojson, totaling about 21.5 km, as the slow-traffic and bus-feeder skeleton; specific alignment, redline, and engineering are pending confirmation MetricDepth. Around rail stations, TOD integration organizes walk transfers, fills slow-traffic gaps, and optimizes non-motorized and parking organization Source.

Municipal and new infrastructure emphasize distributed energy, edge compute, and traditional municipal fusion: edge compute nodes and distributed PV are placed near communities and key areas, forming a "center—edge" synergy with the public compute center; traditional municipal capacity, energy load, and underground-space feasibility are pending confirmation and do not constitute engineering conclusions Depth. Innovation-service platforms and talent living services fall in the origin community and accelerator; public-service facilities are organized by a 15-minute living circle Depth.
