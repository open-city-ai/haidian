# Jingzhang Symbiotic Corridor

## Design Basis and Source List

This proposal is grounded in the Centennial Jingzhang AI Innovation Belt international open call pre-qualification announcement, the agent-oriented open-call taskbook, and relevant Ministry of Housing and Urban-Rural Development and Ministry of Natural Resources technical regulations [source:OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT][source:AGENT-TASKBOOK]. Sources follow the cleared public materials in `data/source_registry.json` and `brief/site-package/`; the taskbook, announcement, and three-zones-two-wings notes form the controlling basis [source:SITE-PACKAGE][standard:PROJECT-OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT].

Spatial scopes use officially published textual areas and boundary descriptions: the overall design area is about 11.4 km², the coordinated research area about 43.6 km², and the key detailed-design area about 368.4 ha [data:geometry/site_boundary.geojson][data:geometry/key_areas.geojson]. Because the official precise redline has not been delivered with the public package, the key-area and overall boundaries are taken from `provisional_boundaries.geojson` strictly as a `provisional_constraint`, never as a statutory redline or precise area [source:MOHURD-CONTROL-DETAILED-PLANNING].

Machine-readable evidence is centralized in `sources.json`, `metrics.json`, `compliance_matrix.json`, `standard_matrix.json`, and `design_depth_matrix.json`; the prose explains design judgments without substituting GeoJSON/JSON for narrative [depth:metrics_recalculation]. All spatial recommendations are expressed as "concept proposals", "reference schemes", or "material for professional teams to deepen", and do not constitute government-approved conclusions [standard:PROJECT-AGENT-OPEN-CALL-TASKBOOK].

## Three-Level Scope Framework

The coordinated research area, overall design area, and key detailed-design area form a progressively implemented three-level framework. The research area (~43.6 km²) studies industry strategy and future urban form; the overall area (~11.4 km²) performs urban-renewal and regulatory-plan-depth urban design; the key area (~368.4 ha) performs detailed design at comprehensive-implementation depth [data:geometry/site_boundary.geojson][depth:three_level_scope_framework].

The overall design polygon is not yet delivered; the proposal keeps the provisional overall boundary (PROV-SITE-001) as a constraint, with an area of 11,412,825 m² matching the announced textual area [metric:site_area_sqm]. If the provisional boundary is replaced by the official redline, all boundary-derived land use, green space, public space, and metrics must be recalculated; the layers in `geometry/` are tagged `agent_generated_design` to enable fast recalculation [data:geometry/land_use.geojson].

The three key areas are Zhongzhiyuan AI Self-Reliant Acceleration Zone (~192.1 ha), Beijing AI Origin Community (~104.3 ha), and Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster (~72.0 ha); their provisional polygon areas match the officially announced subarea values [metric:ka_zhongzhiyuan_sqm][metric:ka_beijing_origin_sqm][metric:ka_dazhongsi_sqm]. Key-area planning reaches detailed-design depth, but boundaries and some ownership/status conditions remain provisional, so building form, retain-renovate-demolish, and circulation are directional designs to be deepened after official data arrives [source:THREE-AREAS-WINGS].

## Coordinated Research Area: Industry and Future City Research

The overall name is "Jingzhang Symbiotic Corridor" (Chinese: 京张智脉共生带), English subtitle "AI Pulse Symbiotic Belt", evoking the Jingzhang railway "pulse/vein" and the symbiosis of AI compute, data, talent, and urban life along the heritage corridor. The visual identity direction uses a "rail-line — pulse-wave — symbiotic-ring" motif: a pulse polyline running north–south nested with concentric rings representing the three belts, set in a neutral sans-serif to avoid unauthorized commercial fonts or stock assets [standard:PROJECT-AGENT-OPEN-CALL-TASKBOOK][depth:overall_spatial_structure].

The proposal answers the announcement's calls for a world-class AI innovation ecosystem, industry-chain synergy, three-zones-two-wings, future city form, and continuous green space. The three positions are the Centennial Jingzhang Culture Belt, the Urban AI Living-Experience Belt, and the AI Convergence Innovation Belt; the five functions are the AI full-stack self-reliant system, world-class AI innovation ecosystem, AI+ scenario empowerment paradigm, intelligent AI vibrant city, and global discourse power in AI governance [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK]. The three-zones-two-wings synergy loop takes Beijing AI Origin Community (world-class ecosystem), Zhongzhiyuan (full-stack autonomy & governance), and Dazhongsi (intelligent-native new formats) as the core, linked outward to the Zhongguancun technology-service wing and the Xiaoyuehe scenario-empowerment wing, forming a "research—conversion—experience—governance" closed loop [depth:overall_spatial_structure].

Global AI innovation ecosystem cases (readable summaries, conceptual borrowing):
1. Montreal/Toronto Vector Institute and AI supercluster: public compute and talent networks supporting basic research and industry conversion.
2. San Francisco Bay Area: innovation density coupling venture capital, top universities, and open-source communities.
3. Tel Aviv: high-density startups with military-civilian conversion mechanisms.
4. Singapore Smart Nation: city-scale data trust and regulatory sandbox.
5. Shenzhen Nanshan–Yuehai: hardware supply chain co-located with AI terminal manufacturing.
6. Amsterdam AI ecosystem: people-centered ethical governance and public experiment fields.
These experiences translate into spatial, operational, and scenario mechanisms: public compute nodes, developer-community hubs, sandbox districts, and open scenario operation [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK][depth:land_use_layout].

## Overall Design Area: Urban Renewal and Regulatory-Plan-Depth Urban Design

The overall design area (~11.4 km²) is organized at regulatory-plan depth around the structure "one pulse stringing three zones, two wings bearing synergy": the Jingzhang heritage-park vibrant belt is the central vein linking the three key areas and extending toward the two wings [data:geometry/land_use.geojson][depth:overall_spatial_structure]. Land use nests scientific-research land (0802), public-administration & public-service mixed land (08), commercial-service land (05), urban community-facility land (0702), and park green space (1401) along the corridor into "research—service—living—green" composite cells [metric:lu_0802][metric:lu_05][metric:lu_1401].

The agent geometry gives a conceptual land-use partition: research and mixed public-service land carries AI full-stack innovation, commercial-service land carries intelligent-native consumption and business, community-facility land carries talent living services, and park green space and squares carry public activity. The building footprint is about 435,007 m², a conceptual massing placeholder only; specific floor-area ratio, building height, and development intensity must follow official regulatory conditions and are not stated here as statutory indicators [metric:building_footprint_area_sqm][depth:development_intensity_controls].

Renewal targets are mainly inefficient existing plants, railway-heritage ancillary facilities, and scattered older communities, classified into retain, renovate, demolish, and build-new; the specific retain-renovate-demolish list and ownership checks are listed as pending confirmation, to be deepened after official status survey and ownership data arrive [depth:retain_renovate_demolish]. The urban character is "low-density, green-connected, walkable, memorable"; roof form and massing respect the linear-heritage scale of the Jingzhang railway and avoid large masses blocking corridor views [depth:height_massing_character].

## Detailed Design of Key Areas

Zhongzhiyuan AI Self-Reliant Acceleration Zone (~192.1 ha, PROV-KEY-001) is positioned as the carrier of the AI full-stack self-reliant system and governance discourse. Its spatial structure centers an accelerator cluster surrounded by a public compute center, open-source community hub, and regulatory-sandbox district; building renewal mainly renovates existing R&D volumes and reserves gap land for major platforms [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-001][depth:three_key_area_detailed_design].

Beijing AI Origin Community (~104.3 ha, PROV-KEY-002) is positioned as a world-class AI innovation ecosystem and a high-quality urban district talent aspire to. A "work—live—socialize" loop is formed by mixed community, talent apartments, an international talent lounge, and open labs; the slow-traffic network directly reaches the Jingzhang heritage park, strengthening daily perceptible AI public space for talent [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-002].

Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster (~72.0 ha, PROV-KEY-003) is positioned for intelligent-native new formats, carrying AI+ consumption, AI+ business, and showcase experience. A ring commercial circulation strings experience stores, a release hall, and a developer market; night economy and public-experience routes combine into AI urban scenarios that are perceptible, demonstrable, and promotable [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-003].

Each key area forms a readable mini-scheme of "positioning + spatial structure + building renewal + slow traffic + public space + AI scenario + implementation risk"; boundaries are provisional and conclusions are directional, to be rechecked for area and building volume after official redlines arrive [depth:three_key_area_detailed_design].

## AI Innovation Ecosystem, Personas, and AI+ Scenarios

The proposal defines five user personas mapped to space and scenarios: ① AI researchers/engineers (research land & open-source hub), ② startup founders (accelerator & sandbox district), ③ international talent & visitors (origin-community lounge), ④ local residents (community facilities & public space), ⑤ urban governors (regulation & compute operations) [depth:metrics_recalculation]. Each persona corresponds to a clear spatial anchor, data need, privacy boundary, and human-review mechanism [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK].

AI scenario cards (at least 10, including at least 3 industry test/validation scenarios):
- S1 Public compute reservation & carbon dashboard (industry test/validation): compliant compute booking for SMEs and universities with energy/carbon visualization, data anonymized, human-reviewed.
- S2 Autonomous shuttle loop test (industry test/validation): bounded L4 shuttle and slow-traffic testing, fully pausable and traceable.
- S3 AI-assisted community clinic (industry test/validation): community-level triage and referral suggestions, assistive only, finalized by physicians.
- S4 AI+ education personalized learning partner: adaptive tutoring in schools and communities, protecting minors' privacy.
- S5 AI+ legal self-service pod: standardized legal Q&A, complex matters escalated to human lawyers.
- S6 AI+ living-services steward: accessible travel, grocery, repair aging-friendly assistant.
- S7 Developer market & model expo: offline open-source model release and experience.
- S8 Jingzhang heritage AI guide & AR time-capsule: augmented-reality narrative of railway heritage.
- S9 Urban-operation AI inspection (green, parking, waste): auto-detected events dispatched, human-confirmed.
- S10 Public-experience-route smart guide: multilingual, accessible visitor routing.
- S11 Crowd monitoring & dispersal: large-crowd warning, hints only, no individual identification.
- S12 Low-carbon mobility incentive: walk/cycle points and carbon co-benefit.

All scenarios uphold data minimization, human review, and traceable operation ownership; excessive surveillance and non-reviewable systems are prohibited; test scenarios are explicitly labeled "test/validation" and do not constitute approved operation [standard:PROJECT-AGENT-OPEN-CALL-TASKBOOK].

## Transport, Rail, Municipal Infrastructure, and Public Services

Circulation centers on "rail-station integration + micro-circulation + continuous slow traffic". The proposal gives 4 conceptual road centerlines in `geometry/roads.geojson`, totaling about 21.5 km, as the slow-traffic and bus-feeder skeleton; specific alignment, redline, and engineering are pending confirmation [metric:road_length_m][depth:traffic_rail_slow_parking]. Around rail stations, TOD integration organizes walk transfers, fills slow-traffic gaps, and optimizes non-motorized and parking organization [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK].

Municipal and new infrastructure emphasize distributed energy, edge compute, and traditional municipal fusion: edge compute nodes and distributed PV are placed near communities and key areas, forming a "center—edge" synergy with the public compute center; traditional municipal capacity, energy load, and underground-space feasibility are pending confirmation and do not constitute engineering conclusions [depth:municipal_new_infrastructure]. Innovation-service platforms and talent living services fall in the origin community and accelerator; public-service facilities are organized by a 15-minute living circle [depth:blue_green_public_space].

## Blue-Green Network, Public Space, and Urban Character

The Jingzhang heritage-park vibrant belt is the central blue-green and public-space backbone, linking the three key areas and extending to the two wings; the Qinghe and Xiaoyue rivers form blue-green veins with connected walkways and cycle paths [data:geometry/green_space.geojson][depth:blue_green_public_space]. Green space is about 6,607,414 m², green ratio about 0.579; public space is about 466,128 m², public-space ratio about 0.041 [metric:green_space_area_sqm][metric:green_ratio][metric:public_space_area_sqm][metric:public_space_ratio].

Public space carries technology testing, application showcase, and historical-culture display; the urban tone is low-density, green-connected, walkable, with building character respecting the linear-heritage scale and roof form/massing avoiding corridor-view blockage [depth:height_massing_character]. AI public space and intelligent-native new formats are strengthened by three AI landmarks: ① the Jingzhang railway-heritage "AI Time-Capsule" (heritage AR narrative & developer memorial), ② the "Light of Origin" developer beacon tower (open-source contribution honor display), ③ the AI Living-Experience Ring (experiential consumption & public-activity loop) [standard:PROJECT-AGENT-OPEN-CALL-TASKBOOK]. Landmarks, signage, logo, and imagery use cleared or self-generated assets, avoid over-entertainment, and are explicitly labeled concept schemes rather than approved construction [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK].
