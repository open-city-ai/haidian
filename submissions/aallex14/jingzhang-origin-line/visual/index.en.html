Mobility & Slow Traffic

One greenway, multi-point connections

Origin Line Slow-Mobility Greenway

A walking-and-cycling spine running the full corridor along the heritage alignment, linking the five stations and three key areas.

4 Cross-Street Connectors

Cross-street slow-mobility links at Dazhongsi, Zhichun Rd, Wudaokou and Qinghuayuan, stitching the blocks on both sides of the corridor.

Zhongzhiyuan Cycling Loop

A park-level cycling loop around the Collective Intelligence Ring, connecting to the corridor's north end for commuting and test scenarios.

Heritage Rail Alignment

The Jing-Zhang railway alignment is kept as a dashed line beside the green corridor — a linear carrier for cultural narrative and digital exhibits, not a transport function.

All alignments are conceptual (A-CONCEPT-GEOMETRY-001) and imply no road redlines or engineering sections; the formal scheme requires a professional traffic study.
