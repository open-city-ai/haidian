## 生成方法披露 / Generation method disclosure

- Geometry: site boundary and key areas locked from `brief/site-package/geometry/provisional_boundaries.geojson`; design layers (land use, buildings, roads, green/public space, phasing) generated by a cookie-cutter partition of the provisional site boundary in EPSG:4326, with all areas recomputed in EPSG:4548; method recorded in the proposal risk chapter and assumptions.json.
- Metrics: recomputed from submitted geometry with formulas and source files recorded in `metrics.json`.
- Figures, PDFs, and HTML: derived from the same geometry and metrics for human readability; authoritative data remains in GeoJSON/JSON.
