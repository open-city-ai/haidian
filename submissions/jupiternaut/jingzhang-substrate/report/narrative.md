# Formal Narrative

京张衬底 / THE SUBSTRATE treats the already-built Jing-Zhang heritage park as a chip substrate. Six bands and three strips organize the overall design area. Twelve vias exchange the slow walking clock and the fast interchange clock. Zhongzhiyuan, Origin Community, and Dazhongsi are hard macros: compute, host interface, and southbridge. AI services are halt-able userspace microcode. FAR and height stay pending official data. Geometry, metrics, matrices, drawings, and visual/index.html remain the cross-checked deliverables.
