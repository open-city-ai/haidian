# Jing-Zhang Century Link: One-Rail-Three-Cores AI Open Corridor

## Design Basis and Source List

This proposal takes the Haidian Branch of Beijing Municipal Commission of Planning and Natural Resources' "Qualification Pre-examination Announcement for the Centennial Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt Urban Design International Solicitation" as the primary basis [source:OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT]. It also integrates the agent taskbook [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK], structured site package [source:SITE-PACKAGE], public source registry [source:SOURCE-REGISTRY], processed fact pack [source:PROCESSED-FACT-PACK], provisional boundary source [source:BOUNDARY-SOURCE] and key area source [source:KEY-AREA-SOURCE]. Core standards include [standard:MOHURD-URBAN-DESIGN-MEASURES] and [standard:MNR-LAND-USE-CLASSIFICATION-GUIDE]; design depth is constrained by 15 depth items including [depth:three_level_scope_framework] and [depth:overall_spatial_structure].

Because official precise polygons are not yet public, this proposal uses the provisional boundaries from `brief/site-package/geometry/provisional_boundaries.geojson`. All spatial suggestions are marked as "concept proposal" / "for further study by professional teams" and shall not be treated as official redlines, approval basis, or precise area basis. Data gaps do not block content scoring.

## Three-Level Scope Framework

Per the announcement, the work covers three nested scopes: coordinated research area (43.6 km²), overall design area (11.4 km²), and three key areas totaling 368.4 ha. The proposal builds a cascading framework: strategic (AI ecosystem) → overall (land use, transport, blue-green, character, renewal) → key areas (detailed design of Zhongzhiyuan, AI Origin Community, Dazhongsi) [depth:three_level_scope_framework]. Boundary and area recalculation evidence: [metric:site_area_sqm], [metric:key_area_count] and three key-area metrics.

## Coordinated Research Area: Industry and Future City Research

Naming: "Jing-Zhang Century Link" (JZ-Century) with positioning "One Century, One Rail, Open AI". Visual identity uses three colors: railway brown-grey, electric-blue, and greenway-green. The three positions (centennial cultural belt, urban AI life experience belt, AI fusion innovation belt) and five functions (full-stack innovation, world-class AI ecosystem, AI+ scenario empowerment, intelligent vitality city, global AI governance discourse) drive the spatial concept [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK]. Six global cases inform the design (King's Cross, JID Singapore, Station F, Pangyo, Quayside, Shibuya/Marunouchi); mechanisms are translated into six action principles: public-space first, scenario opening, testbeds, developer community, data governance, station-city integration.

## Overall Design Area: Urban Renewal and Regulatory-Plan-Level Urban Design

Overall structure: One Rail (Jing-Zhang smart link main axis) + Three Cores (Zhongzhiyuan full-stack innovation, AI Origin Community open-source ecosystem, Dazhongsi intelligent native economy) + Two Wings (Zhongguancun technology service wing, Xueyuan Road–Xiaoyuehe scenario empowerment wing) + One Ring (blue-green smart ring) [depth:overall_spatial_structure]. Urban renewal uses a four-tier framework: retain–renovate–build–pending [depth:retain_renovate_demolish]. Official FAR, building height, and density are not in the public task package and are listed as pending data [depth:development_intensity_controls].

## Detailed Design of Key Areas

- **Zhongzhiyuan AI Acceleration Park**: garden-style full-stack innovation block, with intelligent agent sandbox, low-carbon computing station, AI governance round-table hall as reservable public test nodes.
- **Beijing AI Origin Community**: campus-park-block slow-traffic stitching; open-source release hall, university-enterprise transformation lounge, talent services and residential amenities.
- **Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster**: city-style intelligent economy and international exchange block; four-quadrant pedestrian connection around Dazhongsi station; intelligent terminal commercial street, data factor theater, international roadshow lounge.

## AI Innovation Ecosystem, Personas, and AI+ Scenarios

Five personas: university faculty and researchers; open-source developers and indie teams; startups and growth companies; leading companies and international visitors; surrounding residents and young talent. Ten AI scenario cards map to spatial carriers, service targets, and operating bodies (open-source release hall, urban agent sandbox, slow-traffic breakpoint diagnosis, low-carbon computing station, AI governance round-table hall, university-enterprise transformation lounge, data factor theater, intelligent terminal commercial street, etc.) [metric:scenario_card_count].

## Land Use, Building Scale, and Retain-Renovate-Demolish Strategy

Land use follows MNR classification [standard:MNR-LAND-USE-CLASSIFICATION-GUIDE]: research ~18%, commercial ~12%, residential ~22%, education ~8%, transport ~14%, green/public ~18%, pending ~8%. Renewal tiers: retain ~35% (heritage, education, public facilities), renovate ~40% (industrial, community interfaces), new build ~15% (main axis, ring, station plazas, new infrastructure), pending ~10% (uncertain tenure/regulatory). **No specific FAR, building height, or density is provided** in this proposal—all such parameters are pending regulatory confirmation.

## Transport, Rail, Municipal Infrastructure, and Public Services

Transport follows a three-tier model: rail main axis (Jing-Zhang corridor), public transit connectors (three key areas + two wings), slow-traffic stitching (continuous pedestrian/cycling along the main axis and blue-green ring) [data:geometry/roads.geojson#ROAD-001 etc.]. Parking relies on distributed public lots and P+R at rail stations. Municipal and public services use a "municipal corridor + public service node" pattern; specific capacity and engineering schemes await official data.

## Blue-Green Network, Public Space, and Urban Character

Blue-green system organizes the "blue-green smart ring" along the inner overall boundary, linking the Jing-Zhang heritage park, Qinghe and Xiaoyuehe waterfront corridors, Zhongzhiyuan garden-style blocks, AI Origin Community campus green corridor, and Dazhongsi urban green wedges. Public space is organized in three levels: main axis, ring, and 10+ AI scenario nodes [metric:scenario_node_count]. Character is governed by three colors (railway brown-grey, electric-blue, greenway-green); specific height, massing, and facade language await detailed design. No hard height/density parameters are prescribed.

## Renewal Projects, Implementation Policy, and Phasing

Eight conceptual renewal projects (U-01 to U-08) cover the main axis phase 1, blue-green ring demo segment, intelligent agent sandbox, AI Origin release hall, Dazhongsi four-quadrant connection, data factor theater, and the two wings. Phasing: near-term (2027-2028), mid-term (2029-2031), long-term (2032-2035). Specific approval, fiscal, and developer schedules shall follow official release [depth:risk_missing_data].
