## Three-Level Scope Framework

### Coordinated Research Area (approximately 43.6 km²)

From the North Third Ring Road to the north, from the Jingzhang Expressway to the east, from Xizhimen Outer Street to the south, and from Wanshuihe Road to the west [source:SRC-2026-BJ-GH-QUAL-PREANNOUNCEMENT]. This area connects the core innovation chain of the Zhongguancun Science City to the North Third Ring Road, linking key intellectual resources including Tsinghua University, Peking University, and the Chinese Academy of Sciences.

This plan proposes three integrated strategies at this level [depth:regional_strategy]:
1. **Coordination of Innovation Networks:** Link the Beigu Community → Future Science City → Huairou Science City, forming the Beijing AI Innovation Axis
2. **Transport Integration**: With the Jing-Zhang High-Speed Rail as the longitudinal backbone, and rail stations as TOD anchor points, a three-layer transportation system of "railway + active travel + low-speed autonomous driving" is constructed.
3. **Ecological Coordination**: The Qinghe-Xiaoyuehe Blue-Green Network intersects with the Jing-Zhang Heritage Park at a cross, forming the regional ecological framework.

**Boundary Status**: The Coordinated Research Area uses provisional boundaries [data:geometry/site_boundary.geojson#SITE-001], and recalculations of the total area and cross-zone coordinated analysis will be required after the official polygon replaces the Provisional Boundary.

### Overall Design Area (approximately 11.4 km²)

Design the urban area within 1-2 kilometers around the Jing-Zhang Heritage Park, which falls under the depth of Regulatory Detailed Planning [standard:MOHURD-URBAN-DESIGN-MEASURES]. This proposal presents a "One Vein, Three Zones, and Two Wings" spatial structure, which is detailed in the next chapter. Provisional Boundary notes: Currently, the use of PROV-SITE-001 provisional boundary is in place, with the polygon being a rough fit based on the textual boundaries described, with a precision level of `provisional_rough`. Once the official boundaries are finalized, the following layers need to be recalculated: `land_use.geojson` (all land use zones), `green_space.geojson` (green space boundaries), `public_space.geojson` (Public Space boundaries), `phasing.geojson` (phasing areas), and all area-related indicators. ## Detailed Design of Key Areas

**Design Points**: The building mass should primarily consist of mid-to-low-rise research and development buildings (Conceptual Recommendation of 5-8 floors), emphasizing a campus-style layout. **Location**: "AI Living Room" — Open Source Ecology, Innovation Incubation, Public Experience, International Exchange.

**Spatial Structure** (Conceptual Recommendation):
- Open Source Square: Core Public Space, featuring ground-level plaques honoring open source contributors, capable of accommodating 2000 people [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#PS-001]

### Twelve AI Scene Cards [depth:agent.3_scenario_cards]

#### Scene 1: Smart Learning Neighborhood — AI Adaptive Learning Space
- **Track**: AI+Public Services
- **Spatial Location**: AI Origin Community, Adjacent to the University Interface
- **Target Audience**: College Students, Lifelong Learners
- **Scene Description**: An AI-adaptive learning system integrated with physical learning spaces. AI analyzes learners' knowledge gaps and learning styles, recommending personalized learning paths. Physical spaces offer focus pods, collaborative tables, and experiment stations.
- **Operational Data**: Anonymized Learning Behavior Data, Course Knowledge Graph
- **Privacy Boundary**: Data localization processing, allowing learners to delete their learning records at any time.
- **Human Review**: Educational experts regularly review the quality of the learning pathways.
- **Operating Entity**: Conceptual Recommendation to be jointly operated by a university and an AI company.

#### Scene 2: Community AI Health Hub
- **Track**: AI+Public Services
- **Spatial Location**: Residential Clusters
- **Target Audience**: Local residents, particularly seniors and chronic disease patients
- **Scene Description**: Community-level AI Health Management Station, equipped with AI health monitoring devices (blood pressure, blood sugar, heart rate, body posture analysis), AI triage and remote doctor consultation. Emergency situations automatically trigger a call to 120.
- **Operational Data**: Vital Signs Data (Anonymized, Compliant with Medical Data Protection Regulations)
- **Privacy Boundary**: Medical-grade data encryption, patient autonomous authorization sharing
- **Human Review**: Remote doctor final diagnosis, AI provides only auxiliary suggestions
- **Operating Entity**: Conceptual Recommendation by Community Health Center + AI Health Enterprise Collaboration

#### Scene 3: Experiment Street for Unmanned Retail
- **Track**: AI+Public Services
- **Spatial Location**: Dazhongsi AI Industry Agglomeration Zone [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#KEY-003]
- **Service Objectives**: Consumers, Retail Technology Companies
- **Scene Description**: Visual identification of unmanned stores, robot delivery, and AI personalized recommendation windows. Each store is a "technological experiment unit" where businesses can test AI retail solutions in a real-world environment.
- **Operational Data**: Consumer Behavior Data (Anonymized)
- **Privacy Boundary**: There is clear data collection notification within the store, and customers can choose "Privacy Mode."
- **Human Review**: Regularly review algorithmic fairness.
- **Operating Entity**: Conceptual Recommendation by a Joint Venture of Commercial Real Estate + AI Retail Enterprise

#### Scene 4: Autonomous Shuttle Loop
- **Track**: AI+Transportation
- **Spatial Location**: Three Zones Linked [data:geometry/roads.geojson#RD-008]
- **Service Objectives**: Innovators within the Innovation District, Visitors
- **Scene Description**: Low-speed (≤25km/h) autonomous shuttle vehicles connect three key areas, with fixed stop stations. Serving as both a means of transportation and an AI experience project (in-vehicle AR tour).
- **Operational Data**: Vehicle Trajectories, Passenger Counts
- **Privacy Boundary**: No personal identity data is collected, only passenger flow is counted.
- **Human Review**: Safety officers will initially monitor vehicles in person, gradually transitioning to remote monitoring.
- **Testing and Validation Scenario**: This is one of the testing and validation scenarios for the AI industry.

#### Scene 5: AR Railway Time-Space Corridor
- **Track**: Jing-Zhang Cultural Heritage and Urban Narrative
- **Spatial Location**: Jing-Zhang Relic Park along the entire route [data:geometry/green_space.geojson#GS-001]
- **Service Object**: All Visitors
- **Scene Description**: AR trigger points are deployed along the site park, and scanning the markers will reveal a three-layer historical overlay of a steam train crossing in 1909, the Zhongguancun Electronic Street in the 1980s, and the AI Coexistence Belt in 2026. The entrance square features a cultural wall titled "Personnel Track → Electronic Street → Smart Vein" [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#PS-006].
- **Operating Data**: AR Interactive Data
- **Privacy Boundary**: AR content is fully locally rendered and does not upload user images.
- **Human Review**: Historical and cultural content is reviewed by humanities experts.

#### Scene 6: Citizen AI Advisory Hall
- **Track**: AI+Public Services
- **Location**: AI Origin Community Public Space
- **Target Audience**: Local residents, community organizations
- **Scene Description**: An AI-assisted community deliberation space. AI summarizes the background of issues, simulates the impacts of different scenarios, records the meeting, and generates multilingual minutes. Citizens can ask the AI questions in natural language to understand the policy background. AI does not make decisions; it merely assists in ensuring information symmetry.
- **Running Data**: Issue Data, De-Identified Public Comments
- **Privacy Boundary**: Participation in meetings is anonymous, and data is used only for meeting records.
- **Human Review**: All AI outputs are reviewed by community workers.

#### Scene 7: 24-Hour AI Maker Workshop
- **Track**: Youth-Friendly Public Space
- **Spatial Location**: AI Origin Community [data:geometry/buildings.geojson#BLD-009]
- **Target Audience**: AI Research Geeks, Entrepreneurs, College Students
- **Scene Description**: A 24/7 shared creative space. It provides shared GPU computing power, 3D printing, laser cutting, and electronic workstations. An AI assistant helps with code debugging, creative brainstorming, and technical documentation generation. It is equipped with a coffee bar and rest pods.
- **Operating Data**: Equipment Usage Data
- **Privacy Boundary**: User codes and data are locally isolated.
- **Testing and Validation Scenario**: This is the second scenario for AI industry testing and validation.

#### Scene 8: Generative Public Art Wall
- **Track**: Youth-Friendly Public Space
- **Spatial Location**: Along the Archaeological Park, Original Point Community Segment
- **Service Object**: All Visitors
- **Scene Description**: A large LED/electronic ink art wall, where AI generates constantly changing public art based on real-time environmental data (weather, foot traffic, season) and citizen submissions. Citizens can submit text/image prompts to participate in the creation. AI and artist co-curated exhibitions.
- **Operating Data**: Environmental Data, Participation Data
- **Privacy Boundary**: No collection of personal information, content AI reviewed + human reviewed
- **Human Review:** Artist Review of AI Generated Content

#### Scene 9: Smart Fitness Greenway
- **Track**: Youth-Friendly Public Space
- **Spatial Location**: Along Xiao Yuehe [data:geometry/green_space.geojson#GS-005]
- **Target Audience**: College Students, Local Residents
- **Scene Description**: AI motion stations are deployed along Xiao Yuehe, where cameras capture movement poses. AI analyzes the correctness of running postures and stretching actions, providing improvement suggestions. Along the way, screens display the motion data and rankings (anonymous). Smart lighting adjusts at night based on the runner's position.
- **Operating Data**: Movement Posture Data
- **Privacy Boundary**: Local processing of pose data, not uploaded to the cloud or stored as personal images.
- **Testing and Validation Scenario**: This is the third scenario for AI industry testing and validation.

#### Scene 10: Zero Carbon AI Monitoring Tower
- **Track**: AI+Public Services
- **Spatial Location**: Zhongzhiyuan Ecological Buffer Zone [data:geometry/green_space.geojson#GS-003]
- **Target Audience**: Managers, Public
- **Scene Description**: An AI-driven environmental perception tower that real-time monitors air quality, carbon emissions, noise, and the heat island effect. AI predicts environmental changes over the next 24 hours and recommends control strategies (such as architectural shading and optimized ventilation). The tower's display screen showcases real-time environmental data and progress toward carbon neutrality to the public.
- **Operating Data**: Environmental Monitoring Data
- **Privacy Boundary**: Pure environmental data, not involving personal privacy.

#### Scene 11: Universal Accessibility AI Navigation
- **Track**: AI+Public Services
- **Spatial Location**: Entire Area
- **Service Targets**: Visually Impaired, Hearing Impaired, Mobility Impaired Individuals
- **Scene Description**: Through the use of smartphones/smart glasses + spatial audio, real-time navigation is provided for individuals with visual impairments. All Public Space signage is equipped with voice announcements and Braille. AI identifies road obstacles and issues advance warnings. Sign language digital human translation services are deployed at public service nodes.
- **Operational Data**: Navigation Path, Obstacle Recognition
- **Privacy Boundary**: Does not collect user identity information

#### Scene 12: AI Nutritional Kitchen
- **Track**: AI+Public Services
- **Spatial Location**: Each park's amenities
- **Service Target**: Park Workers, Residents
- **Scene Description**: AI recommends personalized meals based on user health data and preferences, with smart cooking robots assisting in meal preparation. A traceability system tracks the entire process from farm to table. AI predicts meal peaks and dynamically adjusts supply.
- **Operating Data**: Dietary Preference Data
- **Privacy Boundary**: Users have the autonomy to choose whether to share health data.

Core Public Space Carrier. Approximately 9 kilometers in length and 100-200 meters in width. The design is based on the principles of "preserving the memory of the rail tracks and overlaying AI experiences." Segment themes:
- South Segment (Dazhongsi): AI Waterfront + Commercial Display Interface
- Midsection (Original Point Community): Cultural Experience + Public Activity Interface

**Xiaoyuehe Blue-Green Space** [data:geometry/green_space.geojson#GS-002]:
Along the riverside ecological corridor of Xiao Yuehe, connect the scene empowerment nodes of the eastern wing.

### Three Major AI Landmarks [depth:agent.4_landmarks]

**1. Open Source Plaza** [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#PS-001]
- Location: At the AI Origin community core, west side of the ruins park
- Design Concept: The plaza's ground is paved with an "Open Source Timeline," narrating milestone open-source projects such as Linux, Python, and TensorFlow. At the center is the Intelligent Entity Honor Wall. Surrounding it are the AI Public Experience Pavilion, Maker Workshop, and International Exchange Center.
- Activity Capacity: Can accommodate 2000 people for open-source project launches, hackathons, and AI technology salons.
- Brand Significance: Symbolizes the "Open Source Co-Creation" spirit, aligning with the "Coexistence" concept of the AI Coexistence Belt.

**2. Agent Honor Wall** [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#PS-003]
- Location: Center of Open Source Square
- Design Concept: Physical+Digital Twin Monument. The physical side features weathering steel (railway element) inscribed with the GitHub and Agent names of contributors from each open-source call. The digital side includes an AR interactive screen where users can access a summary of each contributor's proposal.
- First Release Significance: The selected schemes from this "Centennial Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt Urban Design Open Call" will become the inaugural inscriptions on the Honor Wall. (Centennial Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt Open Call for Urban Design)
- Operating Mechanism: Updated after each open call, forming a continuous accumulation of urban memory landmarks.

**3. Developer's Promenade** [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#PS-002]
- Location: Along the Jing-Zhang Heritage Park linearly extending through three zones.
- Design Concept: The road surface is marked with a timeline of programming language development (Fortran→C→Python→Rust→AI frameworks), with "Algorithm Gardens" interactive installations along the way (using physical devices to demonstrate sorting algorithms, pathfinding algorithms, etc.). Every 500 meters, there is an outdoor programming station (power source + WiFi + shading).
- Cultural Narrative: Intersecting with the AR Railway Time-Space Corridor to form a "Technology Timeline" (a juxtaposed narrative from railway technology → information technology)

### Urban Character Control (Conceptual Recommendation)

- **Building Aesthetic**: Modern and minimalist with a technological flavor, avoiding excessive decoration. The research and development buildings will primarily use glass, metal, and concrete as their basic material language.
- **Height Control**: Conceptual Recommendation focuses on mid-to-low-rise buildings (5-12 stories) along the contour of the heritage park, forming a stepped profile. Actual building heights will be determined by the control plan and aviation height restrictions.
- **Color Palette**: Gray tones (in response to railway industrial heritage) + blue tones (representing Zhongguancun technology) + green tones (for ecological sustainability)
- **Night Lighting**: Conceptual Recommendation uses smart adjustable-color-temperature LED lights to avoid light pollution. For the heritage site park segment, warm tones with low illumination are recommended, while for the technology park segment, a moderate blue tone can be used.

## Centuries of Jing-Zhang culture, Zhongguancun culture, and AI new culture integrated narrative [depth:agent.5_culture]

### "Three Lines, One Soul" — Tripartite Cultural Narrative

**1909 Line — Railway Spirit**: Zhan Tianyou supervised the construction of the Jing-Zhang Railway, and at Qinglong Bridge, he designed the world-renowned "person-shaped" line. This was the first major railway line designed and built independently by the Chinese, embodying the "pioneering spirit" of engineering for the country.

**1980 Line — Zhongguancun Spirit**: From "Electronic Street" to "China's Silicon Valley," Zhongguancun has witnessed the rise of China's information industry. Companies such as Lenovo, Baidu, and ByteDance have gone global from here. This line represents the market expansion spirit of "innovation and entrepreneurship."

**2026 Line — AI Coexistence Spirit**: The Jing-Zhang AI Coexistence Belt marks a paradigm shift from "Made in China" to "Intelligent Made in China" to "Human-Machine Coexistence." This line represents the new era spirit of "open-source collaboration, technology for good, and human-machine coexistence."

**Narrative Mainline**: "Every time China achieves a self-reliant technological breakthrough, it happens on the Jing-Zhang line."

### Guiding System (Conceptual Recommendation)

**Spatial Narrative Route**: Developer Walkway + AR Railway Time-Space Corridor Form Dual-Track Narrative
- "Railway Tracks": Along the ground traces of the heritage park's railway tracks, reflecting on the century-long railway history from 1909 to 2019
- "Smart Rail": Along the AR Identifier System, Visioning the AI City Ecosystem for 2026 and Beyond

**Direction for the Identification System**:
- Main Identifier: Unified "Jing-Zhang AI Co-Existence Belt" brand identifier (Logo+Name)
- Historical Markers: Set up interpretive signs and information codes at the rail site nodes
- Directional Signage: Unified Signage for Walking, Cycling, and Autonomous Shuttle Access
- Art Identifier: AI-generated Public Art as Spatial Landmark

### Conceptual Recommendation for Urban Atmosphere

The core contradiction in the city's character lies in finding a balance between "frontier technology" and "historical depth." The proposal suggests a base theme of "industrial poetics" — retaining the steel texture of the railways, overlaying the light and transparent quality of AI. The primary building materials are steel, glass, and concrete, with the landscape themes centered around lines, tracks, and grids. Public art employs means of light and shadow, algorithms, and generative design.
