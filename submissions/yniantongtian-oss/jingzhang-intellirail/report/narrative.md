# Curatorial Narrative

Jing-Zhang Intelli-Rail treats the railway legacy as a civic operating spine rather than a decorative theme. Three differentiated hubs organise trustworthy AI (VERIFY), near-campus transfer (TRANSFER), and urban applications (USE); two transverse service wings connect enterprise services and everyday neighbourhood life. A permanent ordinary-service base keeps walking, accessibility, signs, counters, shade and blue-green systems working when removable AI sidebands are off.

Six non-identifying user journeys test public benefit and veto conditions. All twelve scenarios now carry both a verification contract and a proposed public-data contract covering necessary data, sensitivity, minimisation, retention ceiling, access, human review, appeal and emergency stop. Five work packages (WP0-WP4) bind phase, suggested accountable roles, public artifacts, metrics/release gates and critical dependencies; time alone never advances a phase.

The bilingual visual system uses an original double-track cover, submitted geometry, three human-scale node/section zooms, and the G0-G3 evidence chain. The package is ready for content review with provisional spatial evidence. All positions, areas, and engineering conclusions require full-package recalculation when official overall and key-area polygons become available.
