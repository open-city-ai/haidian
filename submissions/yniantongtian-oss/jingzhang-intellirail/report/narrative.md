# Curatorial Narrative

Jing-Zhang Intelli-Rail treats the railway legacy as a civic operating spine rather than a decorative theme. Three differentiated hubs organise trustworthy AI, near-campus transfer, and urban applications; two transverse service wings connect enterprise services and everyday neighbourhood life. Twelve scenarios and three testbeds proceed only with explicit operators, data boundaries, human review, appeal, and exit mechanisms.

The package is ready for content review with provisional spatial evidence. All positions, areas, and engineering conclusions require full-package recalculation when official overall and key-area polygons become available.
