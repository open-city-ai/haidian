Jing-Zhang Intelli-Rail: Centennial Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt Urban Design

JING-ZHANG INTELLI-RAIL / Heritage in Motion, Intelligence in Common

Jing-Zhang Intelli-Rail translates the century-old railway logic of “line–station–timetable–maintenance” into an urban innovation mechanism. The heritage spine carries continuous public space; the three key areas respectively carry trustworthy verification, near-campus transfer and city-scale application; the two wings connect universities, enterprises, communities and blue-green systems. Before any AI scenario enters public space, it must answer six questions: where it sits, how ordinary service remains available, what is measured, who reviews it, when humans take over, and how it exits. These six questions become the expansion conditions, so the speed of technological change remains subordinate to public value and verifiable evidence.

One-Page Read: What This Belt Becomes on the Ground

The proposal can be read through four layers. The spatial layer is one spine, three hubs, two wings and multiple points: a continuous heritage walking-and-cycling spine, three hero sequences—Zhongzhiyuan “river–court–gallery,” AI Origin “campus–street–hall,” and Dazhongsi “station–hall–street–green”—two transverse service wings, and twelve removable scenario nodes. The service layer requires every AI plug-in to preserve an ordinary-service path, so streets, signs, counters, exhibitions and accessible movement still work after shutdown. The verification layer registers measurement, comparison, human takeover and stop conditions for all twelve scenarios. The operations layer returns public questions through “sandbox–evidence–review–expand/exit” into the annual public-value audit. Source

Each key area has one spatial promise: Zhongzhiyuan makes trustworthy technology measurable, AI Origin lets innovation move out of campus, and Dazhongsi lets intelligent services enter the urban ground floor and fall back to ordinary service. These three statements constrain the spatial design, scenarios, projects and measurement plan that follow. Depth

Coordinated Research Area: Industry and Future City Research

The industrial ecosystem uses a six-link chain: research–open source–incubation–verification–scaling–urban feedback. Zhongzhiyuan turns trustworthy models, full-stack autonomy, safety governance and standards collaboration into publicly legible verification interfaces; AI Origin turns university outputs, open-source maintenance, early-stage entrepreneurship and talent services into a walkable near-campus network; Dazhongsi puts agents, terminals, content consumption, enterprise services and international release on the urban ground floor. The two wings share compute access, legal/IP services, investment, exhibitions, education and daily services to reduce duplication. Source

No case-study metric is transplanted directly to Jing-Zhang.

A “future city” is judged through perceptible outcomes: whether walking is continuous, ordinary service remains available, low-barrier innovation space increases, residents receive clear benefits, data is minimized, humans can take over in time, accessibility is continuous, and energy improvements are measured. AI performs bounded tasks; it receives no unauthorized control over the city.

Overall Design Area: Urban Renewal and Regulatory-Plan-Level Urban Design

The overall design uses the heritage spine as the public base. Conceptual land use is grouped into AI R&D and innovation, blue-green/open space, industrial and commercial services, and community services/support. The spine prioritizes walking, cycling, stormwater, heritage interpretation and open activity; transverse corridors connect rail stations, universities, communities and water systems; new volume is considered only after ownership, fire safety, structure and regulatory conditions are verified. “Jing-Zhang Intelli-Rail” uses a double-track identity system: navy represents a century of engineering rationality, jade represents open ecology, and warm-copper nodes mark knowledge entering public life. The identity extends from the logo to milestones, tactile strips, signs, exhibitions and digital interfaces. Each hub may use an auxiliary color, but the spine keeps one common visual skeleton. The fixed international communication line is: Heritage in Motion, Intelligence in Common — a railway legacy turned into a public proving line for urban AI.

Urban renewal uses five actions: retain, repair, adaptive reuse, add, and investigate. Every action first checks historic value, structure, fire safety, ownership and the public interface before form is discussed; buildings.geojson expresses design building-base samples only and is not an existing-building survey or demolition decision. Detailed Design of Key Areas

All three key areas share a “permanent urban base + removable AI sideband” rule. The permanent base carries movement, shade, accessibility, counters, seating and basic signs; the AI sideband carries sensing, recommendations, model demonstrations or dynamic content. When AI is shut down or removed, the permanent base must still work independently. Spatial data

Zhongzhiyuan AI Autonomous Innovation Acceleration Area

Hero sequence: river–court–gallery. The Qinghe side forms a low-disturbance public edge, leading into a shared testing court, then the standards collaboration hall and safety-governance gallery. The testing court is arranged to be containable, observable and emergency-stoppable; safety trials must be capable of closing independently without blocking ordinary walking. Daytime supports public evaluation and small demonstrations, while nighttime preserves quiet movement and low-level lighting. Flood control, traffic, ownership and testing-safety review are prerequisites for the next stage. The first stage permits only removable installations and tabletop/contained verification.

Beijing AI Origin Community

Hero sequence: campus–street–hall. University interfaces enter a technology-transfer street linking an open-source release hall, IP/legal services, shared prototyping workshops and talent services. Ground floors prioritize short-cycle, small-team and shared spaces so “innovation” is not locked inside a single campus. The ordinary path provides human consultation, paper guidance and directly accessible public halls; AI plug-ins assist appointment, matching and content. Until campus interfaces, fire safety, IP authorization and housing supply are confirmed, only public activities, service prototypes and reversible interior works are proposed.

Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster

Hero sequence: station–hall–street–green. The rail exit first reaches a legible international reception hall, then an intelligent-terminal/content experience street and data-compliance salon, and finally returns to freely usable public green space. Four-quadrant station connectivity takes walking continuity as the first test; commercial activity must not interrupt ordinary movement. When AI recommendations are off, physical shops, human counters, signs and public green space continue to operate. The current provisional key-area geometry may still diverge from the real Dazhongsi anchor, so all location relationships are recalculated when official geometry arrives.

Transport, Rail, Municipal Infrastructure, and Public Services

Transport uses a “continuous walking-and-cycling spine + three transverse stitching types + station interfaces” structure. Priority is existing crossings and wayfinding, then under-bridge/at-grade walking-and-cycling improvements, and only then evaluation of new crossing works; bridges, tunnels or station-city engineering require separate transport, structural, municipal, fire and accessibility studies. Spatial data

Municipal and new infrastructure use shared interfaces, zoned resilience and measurable operations. Energy, communications, compute and emergency interfaces prioritize reuse of existing facilities; edge-compute expansion first records same-task energy and thermal management; stormwater, permeability and shade systems are renewed with park space. Current pipeline, flood, fire, water and power data remain insufficient, so constraints.geojson stays a data-gap layer. Depth

Public services prioritize everyday life: health, childcare, learning, sport, culture and talent services retain offline entry points. Bilingual signs and appointment assistance serve international visitors without creating exclusive systems. Bicycle parking sits near stations and public entrances; motor-vehicle parking must not occupy continuous accessible routes, fire access or the heritage spine, while statutory supply standards await formal transport assessment.

Blue-Green Network, Public Space, and Urban Character

The blue-green system connects Qinghe, Xiaoyue River, railway heritage, campus edges and community open spaces, supporting thermal comfort, stormwater, biodiversity, movement and informal exchange. Four AI pilgrimage landmarks use low-carbon, maintainable and updatable forms: Centennial Zero Kilometer, Open-Source Contribution Wall, Trustworthy AI Beacon and Urban Loop Theater. When power or digital content is unavailable, each landmark still provides basic recognition and public use. Depth

The honor-display system follows “traceable contribution, failed trials also retained.” Open-source maintenance, public-problem solving, accessibility improvements and trustworthy verification can enter the annual record; corporate sponsorship does not purchase exclusive naming or rights to public data. Cultural narrative braids Jing-Zhang engineering history, Zhongguancun research/entrepreneurship culture and open-source/responsible-innovation culture into three timelines, with sources and permissions registered for historical and oral material.

Public space follows “quiet everyday use first, graded entry for events.” The everyday layer secures shade, seating, drinking water, toilets, sport and commuting; the community layer supports classes, markets and small performances; the city layer supports developer festivals and international events. Event growth must be matched by stronger noise, safety, traffic, accessibility and restoration requirements. Standard

Risk, Copyright, and Compliance

The primary data risk remains provisional geometry and missing formal control conditions; official polygons trigger package-wide recalculation. When regulatory, ownership, heritage, municipal, fire and traffic conditions are incomplete, building-scale and engineering conclusions remain unresolved. Depth

AI risks are governed in four layers: data minimization, purpose limitation, retention and consent withdrawal; model scope and error disclosure; operational human takeover, graceful degradation, emergency stop and ordinary service; and fairness testing across age, ability, language and digital access. High-risk public decisions are not automated, and every service provides a non-digital alternative and appeal channel.

The proposal, bilingual figures, HTML and PDFs are submission outputs; global cases are cited for public mechanisms only and no third-party case images are copied. The visual pages remain offline and load no remote scripts, map tiles, fonts, iframes, forms or trackers. The license remains COMMUNITY-DISPLAY-ONLY, and every concrete implementation requires review by relevant professional teams, rights holders and competent authorities. Source
