# Jing-Zhang Intelligence Spine

## Proposal Narrative

Jing-Zhang Intelligence Spine uses the century-old Beijing-Zhangjiakou Railway cultural corridor as its historical backbone, with Zhongzhiyuan, Beijing AI Origin Community, and Dazhongsi serving as three innovation anchors.

The proposal organizes space as One Belt, Three Cores, Multi-Scenario Nodes, Blue-Green Slow-Mobility Composite Loop. Geometry, metrics, compliance matrix, drawings, and visual/index.html remain cross-checked deliverables.
