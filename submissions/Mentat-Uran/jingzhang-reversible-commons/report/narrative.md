# Centennial Jing-Zhang Reversible Commons | Reading Note

The report is organised around one proposition: “AI is an option, not a prerequisite for access.” Three concurrent settings—the Making Commons, Learning Commons, and Everyday Commons—respond to the Three Zones, while the Zhongguancun Technology Services Wing and Xiaoyue River Scenario Enablement Wing act as interfaces on demand. The Reversible Assembly Yard, Dual-Path Service Hall, and Centennial Time Garden are three catalytic local interventions linking space, industry, public life, and operation.

Read the package as overall relationships → three commons → the six-gate contract of 11 scenarios → G0–G3 evidence gates. Official written areas are not official polygons. Current geometry is a `provisional_constraint`; missing controls, ownership, engineering information, and operators are `unknown` or `not_authorized`; all unperformed outcomes are `not_run`. GeoJSON and metrics are machine-readable sources, while the proposals, five figures, A3/A0 outputs, report, and visual are synchronised explanatory layers.

The proposal uses the inferential holistic framework of `wanghongyang-perspective` to structure relationships and compare scenarios. It does not represent Wang Hongyang’s personal opinion and does not replace fieldwork, statutory planning, or professional engineering judgement. Every spatial action is an Open Co-Creation Conceptual Recommendation.
