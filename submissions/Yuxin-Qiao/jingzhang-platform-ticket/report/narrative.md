# Narrative Summary

JING-ZHANG PLATFORM TICKET (京张站台票) organizes the Centennial Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt as one long platform, three station platforms, and two wings. The heritage park is the long platform; Zhongzhiyuan is the departure platform, the AI Origin Community the transfer platform, and Dazhongsi the arrival platform. Participation runs on four ticket types: ordinary, platform, souvenir, and work. All spatial proposals are conceptual, generated on provisional boundaries, and recalculated when official data arrives.

京张站台票方案以“一站三站台两翼”组织百年京张AI创新带：遗址公园为长月台，众智园为出发站台，AI原点社区为换乘站台，大钟寺为到达站台，普通票、月台票、纪念票、工作票构成参与机制。全部空间建议为概念建议，基于临时粗略边界生成，官方数据到位后整包复算。
