# JINGZHANG RESPONSE

English Name: JINGZHANG RESPONSE

Subtitle: An Auditable Urban Care Signalling System

Core Declaration: Every automation leaves a visible trace of responsibility.

This outcome is a Conceptual Recommendation and reference proposal for community open-source submissions, available for professional teams to deepen their research; it does not replace formal planning and does not constitute a government approval conclusion. All areas and ratios based on provisional boundaries are merely recalculable working values; they must be recalculated in their entirety once the official polygons are in place. (Provisional Boundary)

## Design Basis and Source List

"The Jing-Zhang Response" categorizes the evidence into four levels: official announcements and legal standards answer "what are the tasks and responsibilities"; official historical, heritage, and construction public information answer "why careful continuation is necessary"; international cases answer "which mechanisms are worth comparing"; and the provisional geometry answers "how to first run through the topology, graphics, and self-inspection." These four levels must not be mixed. The project name, 43.6 square kilometers of Coordinated Research Area, 11.4 square kilometers of Overall Design Area, 368.4 hectares of key areas, and the three district areas' approximate values come from the announcement [source:OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT]. Actual submitted polygons come from [source:PROVISIONAL-BOUNDARIES], both marked as `official_boundary=false`, `geometry_role=provisional_constraint`, and `boundary_precision=provisional_rough` in [data:geometry/site_boundary.geojson#SITE-001] and [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-001].

Data governance follows the purpose classification of [source:SOURCE-REGISTRY], with [source:PROCESSED-FACT-PACK] serving only for navigation, and [source:SITE-PACKAGE] providing EPSG:4326 exchange and EPSG:4548 measurement rules. Land use, Public Space, and Urban Character respond to [standard:MOHURD-URBAN-DESIGN-MEASURES], the control detailed planning responds to [standard:MOHURD-CONTROL-DETAILED-PLANNING], and classification terminology responds to [standard:MNR-LAND-USE-CLASSIFICATION-GUIDE]. ## Three-Level Scope Framework

Three levels of work are conveyed through a single "responsibility chain." The Coordinated Research Area discusses innovative ecosystems, regional collaboration, and public value, without drawing new precise red lines; the Overall Design Area forms a spatial syntax with "time tracks, care seams, responsive units, and experimental side lines." Three key areas validate different types of care and innovation mechanisms. The scope levels are indicated in [data:geometry/site_boundary.geojson#SITE-001], and the key areas are indexed in [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-001], [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-002], and [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-003]. [depth:three_level_scope_framework]

"Time Track" borrows the visible order of railway interlocking to connect the century-old Jing-Zhang culture, Zhongguancun innovation culture, and future public AI; "Care Seam" is an east-west pedestrian path that is accessible, shaded, emergency-ready, and service-continuing; "Response Unit" binds a city service to a space, a responsible party, data boundaries, and rollback conditions; "Test Side Track" allows robots or agents to sandbox test in a limited time, limited population, and limited space. The overall structure is not a technical coverage network but a set of city rules prioritizing the rights of the elderly, children, caregivers, people with disabilities, night workers, and ordinary residents. [depth:overall_spatial_structure]

![Timeline Track, Care Seam, Responsive Unit, and Experimental Side Line Spatial Structure Diagram](assets/figures/land-use-structure.png)

Three key areas play different roles: Zhongzhiyuan is the "open-source acceptance field" for engineering validation and multi-operator interoperability; Beijing AI Origin Community is the "Jing-Zhang responsive zero point" for daily needs, talent services, and public care; Dazhongsi is the "city interlock hall" for cultural learning, youth activities, and long-term operations. These three areas are connected by public protocols rather than a single platform, with the wings providing technological services and real-world scenarios. All locations are merely conceptual mappings of the Provisional Boundary, and cannot be used to infer ownership, demolition and construction, road redlines, or engineering feasibility. ## Coordinated Research Area: Industry and Future City Research

International comparisons only extract mechanisms, not replicate forms. Helsinki 3D illustrates that urban models require open formats, versions, and public reuse [source:CASE-HELSINKI-3D]; Punggol Digital District highlights that physical AI needs a tiered research, testing, deployment, and operational feedback process [source:CASE-PUNGGOL-PDD]; Decidim reminds us that proposals, discussions, decisions, and feedback should leave traceable records [source:CASE-BARCELONA-DECIDIM]; Milton Keynes integrates technology experiments into urban industry and service strategies, rather than treating them as isolated items [source:CASE-MILTON-KEYNES]; King's Cross demonstrates that updating railway industrial heritage requires long-term Public Space and mixed-use organization [source:CASE-KINGS-CROSS]; Amsterdam's algorithm lifecycle includes monitoring and decommissioning post-deployment as part of responsibility [source:CASE-AMSTERDAM-ALGORITHM]. The nearby cities of Paris emphasize the accessibility of daily services and care performance [source:CASE-PARIS-15M]. Toronto Quayside serves as a negative governance reminder: data governance, public trust, and exit rights should precede technological scaling [source:CASE-TORONTO-QUAYSIDE].

Form four layers of an innovative ecosystem: the first layer is the knowledge spout from universities, research institutions, and open-source communities; the second layer is shared infrastructure for computing power, data, evaluation, security, and standards; the third layer is scenarios proposed by enterprises, public sectors, and communities; the fourth layer is mechanisms for appeals, audits, fault records, and decommissioning. Spatially, [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-01] provides functional response units, [data:geometry/phasing.geojson#PHASE-01] provides tiered advancement, and [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#PUBLIC-AXIS] provides a public-facing interface. The industrial value is not just counted in the number of enterprises or activities, but also observed in reuse agreements, cross-operator interoperability, issue resolution times, no AI fallback channels, and public objection handling. [depth:overall_spatial_structure]

The historical significance of the Jing-Zhang Railway, designed and constructed by Chinese engineers, supports a "verifiable and self-responsible" narrative, but it cannot be reduced to mere technological decoration [source:JINGZHANG-HISTORY-NRA]. The public planning and phased construction of the heritage park indicate that this line has already taken on roles in urban stitching and Public Space [source:BEIJING-OPEN-SPACE-2021] [source:JINGZHANG-PARK-PHASE1] [source:JINGZHANG-PARK-PHASE2]. Future urban research thus views AI as a reversible public service capability rather than ubiquitous sensing infrastructure; the background of Zhongguancun and the AI Origin community is only used to identify the source and transformation relationships, without fabricating company lists, output values, or recruitment promises [source:BEIJING-AI-DISTRICT-2024].

## Overall Design Area: Urban Renewal and Regulatory-Plan-Level Urban Design

The overall design divides the approximately 11.413 square kilometers temporary polygon into continuous responsive units, fully covering and not overlapping; this is merely a conceptual functional tendency derived from a deterministic cut from the same boundary, and cannot replace the control plan. Land use evidence is located at [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-01], building prototypes are located at [data:geometry/buildings.geojson#BLDG-01], concept pedestrian areas are located at [data:geometry/roads.geojson#ROAD-AXIS], and pending constraints to fill data gaps are marked at [data:geometry/constraints.geojson#GAP-BOUNDARY]. The four files together explain the design intent, calculable relationships, and parts that cannot be concluded. [depth:land_use_layout]

Urban Renewal does not start with the conclusions of "teardown, modify, and preserve," but rather sets three gates. The first is the Evidence Gate: verifying ownership, age, structure, use, fire safety, cultural heritage protection, and control plan; the second is the Public Value Gate: comparing the carbon, accessibility, and service impacts of retaining and repairing, adaptive reuse, and new construction; the third is the Authorization Gate: decided by the responsible department, rights holder, professional team, and public process. The current buildings.geojson is merely a "future professional deepening replaceable spatial prototype," with its approximately 8.2 hectares of base working value unable to identify any existing buildings, let alone support demolition. [depth:retain_renovate_demolish] [depth:height_massing_character]

Each AI service uses a "Declare—Sandbox—Release—Observe—Roll back" five-state process. The service passport must specify the owner, purpose, data, retention, model/version, evaluation, human review, non-AI channel, appeal, release trigger, rollback trigger, incident, audit, and retirement. Services that do not pass the sandbox do not enter the Public Space; when safety, fairness, accessibility, or privacy anomalies are observed, they are either escalated to human review or rolled back. This mechanism references [source:NIST-AI-RMF] and [source:UNESCO-AI-ETHICS], and is subject to [source:PIPL]. It is a governance recommendation, not an existing administrative process. [depth:development_intensity_controls]

The detailed design for the area references [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-002], with site integration and building updates awaiting traffic, ownership, and current condition surveys.

The Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster is defined as a "cultural and operational hub." Conceptual actions include: urban interlocking hall, youth open-source night school, verifiable heritage learning, equitable appointment of public facilities, festival population response, and long-term failure archive exhibition. The four-quadrant pedestrian connectivity only expresses user journeys that should be prioritized for resolution; it does not draw conclusions about bridges, tunnels, or underground engineering. Commercial, content, and smart terminal experiences are retained as non-tracked, non-personalized general choices. The detailed design of the area references [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-003]. [depth:three_key_area_detailed_design]

Three sites will collectively feature three categories of sacred landmarks: the Jing-Zhang Response Zero Point will display "Who is Responsible and How to Exit"; the Urban Interlock Hall will showcase cross-subject coordination and fault replay; and the Open Source Acceptance Field will demonstrate repeatable testing, versions, and evidence. ## AI Innovation Ecosystem, Personas, and AI+ Scenarios

Six composite personas are used to challenge the design, not corresponding to real individuals: elderly residents focus on shade, rest, artificial services, and emergency assistance; wheelchair researchers focus on continuous accessibility and fallbacks in case of equipment failure; caregivers and children focus on visible boundaries, low stimulation, and non-intrusive tracking; international students or developers focus on multilingual support, open-source collaboration, and short-term services; nighttime maintenance and delivery workers focus on lighting, supplies, speed limits, and human-machine collaboration; community and emergency coordinators focus on authoritative information, manual takeovers, and cross-organizational communication. The personas only define needs, not collect personal trajectories or infer identities. [source:WCAG22]

Twelve scenario cards each include user, space, data, AI role, human fallback, and stopping conditions:

- S01 Cool Path Response: Follow routes indicated by public weather announcements and on-site manual observations for shade, drinking water, and rest. In case of equipment failure, maintain fixed signage.
- S02 Accessible Continuity: Report elevator or ramp outages and provide alternative paths with manual confirmation, without making implicit assumptions about disability status.
- S03 Flood Safety Diversion: Only cite authoritative warnings and on-site controls. In case of conflict, prioritize manual safety commands.
- S04 Night Care Route: Provide lighting, on-duty presence, and facility information, without activating facial recognition or continuous tracking.
- S05 Drug and Material Delivery: Low-speed robots operate on designated side lanes and must be capable of manual remote stopping, yielding, and accident recording.
- S06 Child-Friendly Commuting and Play: Information is directed towards guardians and schools for confirmation, without establishing a long-term behavior record for children.
- S07 Multi-Operator Robot Races: Test priority, emergency stops, and interoperability logs in the open-source acceptance field.
- S08 Verifiable Heritage Learning: Each historical interpretation refers back to authoritative sources, and controversial content provides channels for manual corrections.
- S09 Fair Access to Public Facilities: Public Queue Rules, Maintain Phone or On-Site Channels, and Examine Differences in Wait Times Across Different Populations.
- S10 Festival Crowd Response: Use aggregate counts and manual patrols only, without inferring flow as individual intent.
- S11 Facility Fault Diagnosis: The model proposes candidate causes, and engineering personnel confirm them before a work order is issued or the facility is closed.
- S12 Appeal Withdrawal Deletion: Any service can view records, request corrections, choose a non-AI path, and trigger an end-of-life evaluation.

Four industry tests form an audit trail: T01 Multi-Operator Robot Interlock, validating yield, remote stop, and log mutual recognition; T02 Public AI Service Interoperability, validating service passports, minimal identity, and human fallback; T03 Extreme Weather Degradation, validating safe exit in the absence of network, sensor failure, and conflicting authoritative warnings; T04 Accessibility and Fairness Challenges, inviting users with different abilities and languages to discover failures. Any test that does not meet the preset threshold remains in the Sandbox and is not released for demonstration purposes. [depth:municipal_new_infrastructure]

## Transport, Rail, Municipal Infrastructure, and Public Services

Traffic strategy is not about drawing new roads, but rather making "continuity reliability" the top criterion. roads.geojson includes a conceptual time track and six care seams: the time track serves walking, cycling, heritage learning, and low-speed experimentation; the care seams connect the sites, communities, universities, and public services on either side. All lines are clipped by the Provisional Boundary, with a line work value of approximately 16.73 kilometers [metric:road_centerline_length_m] [data:geometry/roads.geojson#ROAD-AXIS], which does not represent road right-of-way, bridge and tunnel schemes, or construction feasibility. [depth:traffic_rail_slow_parking]

For transit connections, the proposal first outlines the user journey: whether there are accessible paths, shaded resting points, manned services, and night lighting after getting off the train; for robots, the proposal first determines yielding, speed limits, remote parking, and accident handling; for bicycles, the proposal first addresses gaps and safe parking solutions; for automobiles, the proposal only proposes a parking demand survey and shared evaluation, without specifying supply quantities. Key interfaces are marked as [data:geometry/constraints.geojson#GAP-ROAD] pending data, to be finalized before entering professional refinement, requiring road, station, and traffic specialty approvals.

Municipal and public services are to be provided through an "interface list" rather than capacity commitments: edge-side computational power requires energy, cooling, network, and maintenance responsibilities; shade, drinking water, toilets, and resting places require facility baselines and operations and maintenance; flood diversion requires authoritative warnings, drainage, and containment; emergency services require clear human command. Without pipeline, fire protection, flood control, energy, and facility capacity, no equipment quantities or load calculations should be made. ## Risk, Copyright, and Compliance

The highest risk is not that the model is "not smart enough," but that responsibility, boundaries, and exit strategies are invisible. Spatial risks include the absence of official polygons, roads, parcels, existing buildings, cultural heritage sites, and utility and facility data; AI risks include purpose drift, over-collection, differential errors, vendor lock-in, inability to appeal, and retention of data post-exit. Corresponding controls are to fail closed: mark unknown or provisional if data is insufficient, halt at the Sandbox if tests do not meet criteria, and revert to manual or rollback if operation fails. [data:geometry/constraints.geojson#GAP-BOUNDARY] [depth:risk_missing_data]

Privacy controls follow the principles of minimum necessity, specific purpose, shortest retention, tiered access, and revocability [source:PIPL]. Explicitly prohibit facial recognition for full-domain identification, social scoring, continuous tracking of children, commercial guidance based on vulnerable characteristics, and models making high-impact public decisions alone. Public metrics use only aggregated or deterministic geometry; individual information must be obtained by a future responsible party for legality, sensitivity, automated decision-making, and security assessments. This plan is not legal advice and does not declare any specific system to be compliant.

AI output boundaries referenced [source:NIST-AI-RMF] and [source:UNESCO-AI-ETHICS]: models can propose candidates, explain, and check leads; schemas, geometry, metrics, hashes, and rejection conditions are executed by deterministic code; professionals confirm planning, engineering, conservation, and safety; the public has non-AI channels, appeals, and corrections; maintainers retain the judgment for release and real-world adoption. Toronto experience reminds us to prioritize trust and exit over expansion [source:CASE-TORONTO-QUAYSIDE]. [depth:development_intensity_controls]
