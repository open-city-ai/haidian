## 1. Background and Vision

The Jingzhang Railway was Chinas first railway fully designed and built by Chinese engineers, led by Zhan Tianyou in 1905-1909. After the new high-speed Jingzhang rail opened in 2019, the original 15km corridor became available for urban transformation.

This proposal converts the historic rail corridor into a composite greenway infrastructure serving ~180,000 residents with slow mobility, ecology, and cultural heritage goals.

## 2. Three Strategic Nodes

### 2.1 Xizhimen Heritage Park (18.6 ha)
Historic memory anchor. Preserves original station platform with AR overlay of century-old railway history.

### 2.2 Qinghe TOD Innovation Hub (42.3 ha, FAR=2.5)
Transit-oriented development with 85,000 sqm industry space. AI+planning innovation cluster near Tsinghua/Peking University.

### 2.3 Shahe Ecological Wetland (31.5 ha)
Ecological restoration of 18.4 ha wetland vegetation for migratory bird habitat.
