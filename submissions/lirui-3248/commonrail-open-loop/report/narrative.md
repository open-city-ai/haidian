# CommonRail Ground — Formal Narrative Index

The complete Chinese narrative is `../proposal.md`; its full English counterpart is `../proposal.en.md`. The rendered report, five bilingual figure pairs, A3 booklet, A0 boards, offline visual page, nine GeoJSON layers and structured evidence files are cross-checked deliverables.

The design thesis is a legible, segment-by-segment public ground floor: one continuous civic ground, seven all-season urban rooms, three civic engines and two knowledge wings. All location-specific geometry is conceptual and uses a maintainer-defined provisional boundary. Statutory controls, ownership, surveyed buildings, retain/renovate/demolish decisions, utilities and investment timing remain explicitly unresolved.
