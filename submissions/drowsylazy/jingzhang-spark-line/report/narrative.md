# 方案叙事 / Formal Narrative

本叙事由结构化 AI 方案包派生，正文以 `proposal.md` 为准，本文件为可选摘要。方案以京张铁路这一中国自主设计建造的第一条干线铁路为"星火之源"，以"京张·星火带 / JING-ZHANG SPARK LINE"为标识，组织"星火主脉＋三区(迸发→蕴育→燎原)＋双翼＋生态廊"空间结构：京张遗址公园星火主脉为公共展示带，众智园(FOUNDRY·算力/模型)、AI原点社区(SEED·人才/开源)、大钟寺(MARKET·产业/场景)按AI全栈链路组织，中关村(INPUT·要素)与小月河(OUTPUT·场景)双翼协同，清河-小月河生态廊串联公共空间与AI场景。以2025年度及2026年上半年北京、海淀官方原始数据论证AI时代的产业、就业与生活图景。

This narrative is derived from the structured AI package; `proposal.md` is authoritative and this file is an optional summary. Taking the Jing-Zhang Railway as the source of the spark of China's self-driven innovation, the proposal develops "JING-ZHANG SPARK LINE / 京张·星火带" and a structure of Spark-Line spine, three zones (ignite-nurture-spread), two wings and an ecological corridor, evidenced by authoritative 2025-2026 Beijing/Haidian data.

方案覆盖公告与面向智能体任务书的全部必选任务：命名与Logo方向、三大定位与五大功能、三区两翼协同、不少于10张AI场景卡、3个产业测试验证场景、5类用户画像、3个AI朝圣地标、文化叙事与长期运营机制。所有空间落地建议均为概念建议、参考方案或可供专业团队深化研究的内容，不替代正式规划，不构成政府审定结论。

The proposal covers all mandatory announcement and agent-taskbook tasks: naming and Logo direction, three positionings and five functions, three-zone two-wing synergy, 10+ AI scenario cards, 3 industry test/validation scenarios, 5 user personas, 3 AI pilgrimage landmarks, cultural narrative and long-term operation mechanisms. All spatial recommendations are concept suggestions, reference schemes or material for professional teams to deepen; they do not replace formal planning or constitute government conclusions.

由于官方多边形尚未公开，本方案使用 provisional 临时边界生成空间图层并在 EPSG:4548 下复算指标。官方边界与控规条件到位后，全部 geometry、metrics、图纸与 HTML 需重算。完整来源、指标、标准与深度证据见 `sources.json`、`metrics.json`、`standard_matrix.json`、`design_depth_matrix.json` 与 `compliance_matrix.json`。

Because official polygons are not yet public, the proposal uses provisional boundaries to generate spatial layers and recomputes metrics in EPSG:4548. Once official boundaries and regulatory conditions arrive, all geometry, metrics, drawings and HTML require recalculation. Complete source, metric, standard and depth evidence lives in sources.json, metrics.json, standard_matrix.json, design_depth_matrix.json and compliance_matrix.json.
