JING-ZHANG SPARK LINE / 京张·星火带

The Jing-Zhang Railway is China's first self-designed, self-funded and self-operated trunk railway - the spark of "self-driven innovation" was lit in 1909. This proposal takes "spark" as the motif and develops a structure of one Spark-Line spine, three zones (ignite - nurture - spread), two wings and an ecological corridor: Zhongzhiyuan (FOUNDRY - compute/model), AI Origin Community (SEED - talent/open source) and Dazhongsi (MARKET - industry/scenario) form the AI full-stack pipeline, with Zhongguancun (INPUT - factors) and Xiaoyue River (OUTPUT - scenarios) wings in synergy. It uses authoritative 2025 and 2026H1 data to argue the AI-era industry, employment and life picture, carrying the flame of self-driven innovation into an intelligent age. This page is an offline presentation; authoritative data lives in the GeoJSON, metrics.json, matrices and self-check results.
