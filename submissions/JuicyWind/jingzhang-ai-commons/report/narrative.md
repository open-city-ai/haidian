# Narrative Summary

JuicyWind’s formal submission reframes the Jingzhang corridor as the “Co-Creation Bloom Belt.” The proposal uses the Jingzhang Heritage Park as a public spine, three bloom cores as differentiated innovation gardens, two petal wings as service and ecological supports, and an AI stewardship layer as the maintenance and review system.

The package is built around four operational goals:

- Make AI verification, open-source translation, and urban application visible within the same public ribbon;
- Ensure the proposal is grounded in reviewable geometry, metrics, and compliance matrices, not only narrative;
- Create a bilingual delivery path with `proposal.md` / `proposal.en.md`, paired drawings, and figure assets;
- Retain the provisional boundary warning and require recalculation once official polygons are available.

The submission package includes:

- a spatial concept that is both artistic and practical;
- a three-level scope that aligns the 43.6 km² strategic network, the 11.4 km² urban design belt, and the 368.4 ha key area cores;
- a series of AI co-creation scenarios linked to graphically referenced layers;
- a metrics-driven review framework that flags unknowns and supports future recalculation.

This narrative is a concise companion to the full proposal. The complete evidence base remains in the geometry files, matrices, self-check results, drawings, HTML, and metrics deliverables.
