0 Proposal Overview

Based on the 2026-05-09 eligibility announcement of the "International Design Solicitation for the 100-Year Jingzhang AI Innovation Belt Urban Design" by the Haidian Branch of Beijing Municipal Commission of Planning and Natural Resources, together with the agent-oriented design brief. Until the official SITE_BOUNDARY and three KEY_AREA polygons are released, this design package is generated from brief/site-package/geometry/provisional_boundaries.geojson as a scoreable, reviewable formal package. Boundaries and areas are provisional_constraint (official_boundary=false) for design discussion/visualization only, not statutory conclusions; rerun the pipeline and recompute all metrics once official polygons are published. This organizational data gap does not block content scoring.

The proposal follows the agent.1~agent.6 response hierarchy: concept & naming → three positionings → regulatory-depth design → key-area detailed design → scenarios/personas/testing → cultural narrative & activity operations. All judgments trace back to design_brief.json, sources.json, standard_matrix.json and design_depth_matrix.json.

1 Concept Naming and Overall Positioning

1.1 Master Concept: Jingzhang Smart-Vein Living Belt

The Jingzhang Railway was China's first self-built trunk line; its "zigzag" (人字形) switchback remains a world-class engineering feat of taming mountain terrain with intelligence a century ago. This proposal uses "from the zigzag railway to the smart-vein city" as the keynote: extending century-old engineering wisdom into an urban operating system for the intelligent era, where AI is no longer an add-on but an infrastructure that, like the old rails, carries growth, channels flows and ignites innovation.

The concept is expressed as "One Belt, Three Cores, Multiple Scenarios, and a Blue-Green Slow Ring":

  • One Belt: the ±11.4 km² overall innovation belt (SITE-001) along the Jingzhang heritage park and rail corridor.
  • Three Cores: Zhongzhi Park · Open-Source Source-Ecosystem Core (192.1 ha, north), AI Origin Community · Pilot-to-Transfer Core (104.3 ha, mid), and Dazhongsi · Cultural Salon Core (72.0 ha, south), forming the "source-creation → piloting → transfer → listing" relay.
  • Multiple Scenarios: 10 AI scenario cards (SC-01~SC-10) distributed along the belt serving 5 user personas.
  • Blue-Green Slow Ring: green-blue space (green ratio ≈ 12.3%) merged with the slow-traffic ring connecting the three cores and all scenarios.### 1.2 Three Positionings and Five Functions

See geometry/land_use.geojson and design_depth_matrix.json.

3.1 PROV-KEY-001 Zhongzhi Park · Open-Source Source-Ecosystem Core (192.1 ha, north)

Positioned as the global open-source AI source hub and proof-of-concept center.

  • Land function: mainly B+M R&D offices and public technology platforms, with an open-source community center, open code plaza and evaluation center.
  • Spatial form: extending the historical axis of the Jingzhang locomotive depot to shape a ceremonial innovation sequence of "Open-Source Plaza — Source Tower — Shared Workshop."
  • Public space: Open-Source Plaza (L2) anchors the belt's public activity axis, hosting the contribution wall and annual releases.
  • Slow mobility: campus-level car-free slow ring + P+R transfer station feeding Tier-1 express lines.
  • Site indicators: dominated by high-density R&D blocks, with flexible "plug-in" module plots for elastic growth.

3.2 PROV-KEY-002 AI Origin Community · Pilot-to-Transfer Core (104.3 ha, mid)

Positioned as the pilot-maturing and talent community between "school-lab-market."

  • Land function: pilot-maturing bases, compute hub, talent housing and commercial support combined.
  • Spatial form: compact mixed blocks around the "AI Origin" station-city node, "residence-above / research-below" and job-housing balance.
  • Public space: the Full-Stack Achievement Hall (L3) with community-level AI living lab and experiment plaza.
  • Slow mobility: the T-01 low-speed unmanned delivery corridor passes its core segment here, with seamless transfer to the rail branch line.
  • Site indicators: medium-high intensity mixed development; FAR gradient decreases from the station-city center to the community periphery.

3.3 PROV-KEY-003 Dazhongsi · Cultural Salon Core (72.0 ha, south)

Positioned as the cultural salon and AI culture-tourism experience center.

  • Land function: cultural display, immersive experience, commercial leisure and hotel services.
  • Spatial form: with the Dazhongsi AI Star Hall (L4) as the spiritual landmark, reshaping the south gateway image.
  • Public space: pre-station culture plaza, waterfront promenade and festival event ground.
  • Slow mobility: connecting the south rail station to build a "rail + cultural slow-tour" route.
  • Site indicators: culture-commerce oriented, low-medium density open layout, highlighting landmark open space.

The three key areas total ±369.3 ha, about 32% of the overall design scope, forming the spatial anchor of the "One Belt, Three Cores"; detailed plot indicators, layers and recomputation are in geometry/ and metrics.json.

5.1 Blue-Green Space Skeleton

With the Jingzhang heritage park as the green axis, two north-south blue-green corridors as threads, and community pocket parks as nodes, a "one axis, two corridors, multiple nodes" blue-green skeleton is built (geometry/green_space.geojson). Green ratio ≈ 12.3%; waterfront promenades, tree-lined avenues and plaza green corridors weave a combined ecology-and-slow-mobility layer.

5.2 Four AI Pilgrimage Landmarks (L1~L4, ≥ 3 satisfied)

  • L1 Jingzhang Smart-Vein Gate: the belt's north gateway, using a "zigzag" steel structure to interpret railway memory merging with AI, serving both guide and landmark functions.
  • L2 Open-Source Plaza: the Zhongzhi Park core, hosting the AI innovation contribution wall, annual flagship releases and the open-source carnival.
  • L3 Full-Stack Achievement Hall: an enterprise exhibition hall in AI Origin Community showcasing China's AI full-stack independent-innovation achievements.
  • L4 Dazhongsi AI Star Hall: the south gateway cultural salon, injecting immersive AI culture-tourism experiences.

5.3 AI-Native New Business

Around four categories—"open-source source creation, pilot transformation, culture experience, life service"—AI-native business is cultivated: open-source community operation, affordable-compute services, AI edge-hardware piloting, generative culture-tourism content, and community-life Agent services, forming a self-sustaining economic ecology that reinvests in blue-green and public-space maintenance.

5.4 Overall Urban Character

With "smart-vein symbiosis" as the character theme, a three-segment character pattern is formed: northern innovation-and-manufacturing, central vitality-and-mixed-use, southern culture-and-quality; building colors and forms extend the calm base tone of Jingzhang industrial heritage, blended with glass, perforated panels and other modern AI vocabulary, highlighting the dual expression of "historic weight + futuristic lightness."## 6 AI Culture-Integration Narrative and Activity Operation System

6.1 Cultural Narrative Thread

With "from the zigzag railway to the smart-vein city" as the through-line, the independent-innovation spirit of the century-old Jingzhang Railway is translated into an urban manifesto for contemporary AI full-stack independent innovation. Three cultural threads converge—engineering wisdom, rail memory, science-and-technology dual innovation—carried by four vehicles (heritage parks, museum clusters, landmark installations, digital content), making the "do-it-yourself, self-reliant innovation" spirit perceivable, inheritable and experiential.

6.2 Annual Activity System

A "1+1+4+N" framework for year-round operations:

  • 1 flagship conference: GAIW (Grand AI Innovation Week), aggregating releases, competitions, roadshows and achievement exhibitions.
  • 1 public experience route: the "Smart-Vein Pilgrimage" themed route linking landmarks L1~L4.
  • 4 seasonal brand actions: Spring Open-Source Launch / Summer Pilot-Acceleration / Autumn Innovation Gala / Winter Popular-Science Study.
  • N developer-community operations: year-round hackathons, Open Days, Meetups and open-source contributor incentives.

6.3 Contribution-Memory Mechanism

An AI innovation contribution wall and open-source contributor honor system record every code commit, patch contribution and scenario co-creation, fostering sustainable community belonging and open governance.## 7 Renewal Project List and Phased Implementation
