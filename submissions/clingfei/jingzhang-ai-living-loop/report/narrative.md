# 方案摘要 / Narrative Summary

## 中文摘要

**京张AI生活回路**以京张铁路遗址公园为轴、AI场景为环，构建"一带三站、双翼一环"的总体结构，串联众智园AI自主创新加速区、北京AI原点社区、大钟寺AI产业聚集区三处重点区域，联动中关村科技服务翼与小月河场景赋能翼。

- 一带：京张遗址公园活力带（约9.7km），承担轨道遗产展示、AI公共空间与慢行主轴三重职能。
- 三站：众智园站（全栈创新）、原点站（原点生活）、大钟寺站（场景体验）三处车站式节点。
- 双翼：西侧中关村科技服务翼、东侧小月河场景赋能翼。
- 一环：AI生活回路（约18.1km），由京张主轴、小月河绿带与知春路/成府路两条绿廊围合，是可步行、可测试、可体验的场景闭环。

用地概念分区：科研38.5%、居住22.5%、公园18.2%；39组概念建筑基底，概念容积率约1.58（待控规确认）。绿地率18.8%、公共空间率2.1%（EPSG:4548复算）。

AI任务书响应：12张场景卡（4张产业测试验证）、5类用户画像、6个全球创新生态案例、3处AI朝圣地标、"轨枕×神经节点"Logo方向、"人字形铁路—中关村—AI原点"文化叙事、四季活动体系与开发者社区运营。分期：近期（2026-2028）原点社区示范，中期（2028-2031）众智园全栈区，远期（2031-2035）大钟寺与南部门户。

边界声明：所有空间数据基于临时粗略边界（provisional constraint），非官方红线；官方图件发布后全部图层与指标整体重算。

## English Summary

The **Jing-Zhang AI Living Loop** uses the heritage railway park as its spine and AI scenarios as its circuit, forming a "One Belt, Three Stations, Two Wings, One Loop" structure that connects the Zhongzhiyuan AI Acceleration Area, the Beijing AI Origin Community, and the Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster, linking the Zhongguancun tech-services wing and the Xiaoyuehe scenario wing.

- One Belt: heritage-park vitality spine (approx. 9.7 km) with three functions — railway-heritage display, AI public space, and the slow-mobility spine.
- Three Stations: Zhongzhiyuan (full-stack innovation), Origin (origin living), Dazhongsi (scenario experience).
- Two Wings: Zhongguancun tech-services (west) and Xiaoyuehe scenario (east).
- One Loop: the AI Living Loop (approx. 18.1 km) enclosed by the heritage spine, the Xiaoyuehe greenway, and the Zhichun/Chengfu green links — a walkable, testable, experiential scenario circuit.

Concept land-use mix: R&D 38.5%, residential 22.5%, park 18.2%; 39 concept footprints; concept FAR approx. 1.58 (pending regulatory-plan confirmation). Green ratio 18.8%, public-space ratio 2.1% (recomputed in EPSG:4548).

Agent taskbook response: 12 scenario cards (4 test-validated), 5 personas, 6 global ecosystem cases, 3 AI pilgrimage landmarks, a "sleeper × neural-node" logo direction, a "switchback railway—Zhongguancun—AI origin" cultural narrative, a four-season event system, and developer-community operations. Phasing: 2026–2028 Origin Community demonstration, 2028–2031 Zhongzhiyuan full-stack area, 2031–2035 Dazhongsi and the southern portal.

Boundary statement: all spatial data is based on provisional constraints, not an official redline; all layers and metrics must be recalculated when official polygons are released.
