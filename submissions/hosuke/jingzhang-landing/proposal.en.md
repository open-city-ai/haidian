# Jing-Zhang Into the City: Let Every Arrival Be the Beginning of Collective Construction

> **LANDING JINGZHANG**｜let every arrival mark the beginning of shared construction.

This proposal was generated by Huang Gehyang (Xiamen, GitHub `hosuke`) in collaboration with OpenAI Codex. It is not about transplanting experiences from elsewhere to Beijing but rather asking, "How can first-time visitors to Jing-Zhang become co-creators?" The proposal organizes talent services, AI testing, resident co-benefit, and the century-old Jing-Zhang narrative into walkable, experiential, exitable, and transferable urban infrastructure. All spaces, policies, recruitment, activities, and investments mentioned in the text are Conceptual Recommendations intended to guide subsequent professional teams in deepening the work in accordance with relevant laws and regulations.

## Three-Level Scope Framework

Three levels are not three separate outcomes, but different scales of the same "arrival—joint construction" chain. The 43.6 square kilometers of the coordinated layer address: how to create an ecosystem for Haidian's research, industry, capital, services, and community that benefits both global newcomers and local residents; the 11.4 square kilometers of the overall layer address: how to use the Jing-Zhang Heritage Park and the urban areas on either side to carry pedestrian arrival, testing conversion, and public life; the three key areas address: where each stage occurs, who maintains it, and how to retreat in case of failure. [standard:PROJECT-OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT] [depth:three_level_scope_framework] [depth:overall_spatial_structure]

The spatial syntax is "Four Stages, Three Stations, Two Networks." Four stages are 72-hour "Understanding the City," 30-day "Forming Collaboration," one year "Completing Pilot Use and Co-benefit Evaluation," and long-term "Returning and Handing Over"; three stations are Dazhongsi ARRIVE (City Entrance and Public First Use), Beijing AI Origin Community JOIN (University Transformation, Co-learning, and Talent Living), and Zhongzhiyuan BUILD & PROVE (Full-stack R&D, Safety Evaluation, and Scenario Validation); two networks are Zhongguancun Science and Technology Service Network and Xiaoyuehe Daily Co-assessment Network. The two wings are not just about delivering resources to enterprises but also about sending legal advice, financing, computing power, data compliance, and resident feedback back to the three stations to prevent the "Talent Corridor" from becoming a one-way recruitment funnel. [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK]

The total area of the key zones remains as stated in the announcement at 368.4 hectares, comprising 192.1 hectares for Zhongzhiyuan, 104.3 hectares for the AI Origin community, and 72.0 hectares for Dazhongsi; the three polygons on the map are merely positional placeholders and should not be used to replace the announced values. ## Overall Design Area: Urban Renewal and Regulatory-Plan-Level Urban Design

Overall structure is based on the Jing-Zhang Heritage Park as a public memory and slow-moving backbone, with the three stations serving as density and service anchor points, and the two wings as knowledge, capital, and daily testing interfaces. The conceptual land use covers the provisional overall range with 24 shared boundary units, divided into seven categories: commercial and service uses, residential, public administration and public services, education and research, green spaces, and squares; its role is to test functional proportions and layer topology, not to identify real land use properties. [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-001] [depth:land_use_layout] The zones are fully covered and non-overlapping; they must be redefined with legally provided land use, parcel, and road data, and not with the current grid as the control plan.

Urban Renewal starts with "use first, then build": Existing buildings are prioritized for a 90-day reversible space experiment to validate the genuine needs for open workstations, compliant clinics, co-learning tables, and public first-use courtyards; those that pass the review will then enter the building renewal and Public Space deepening phase. Conceptual Building Footprints only show mixed R&D units that can be supported, with no decision made on retention, renovation, or demolition. [data:geometry/buildings.geojson#BLDG-001] [depth:retain_renovate_demolish] Building footprint metrics [metric:building_footprint_area_sqm] and [metric:building_density] are used for internal layer consistency only, with Floor Area Ratio and height remaining unknown. These will be determined by a professional team after all development intensity controls, height massing character, and necessary documentation such as control plans, current surveys, solar access, fire safety, cultural heritage, and property rights are in place. [depth:development_intensity_controls] [depth:height_massing_character]

Overall design sets an "entry threshold" into the city rather than a closed park: the public layer is accessible either around the clock or during posted hours, while the experimental layer must have clear boundaries and a site responsible person. The research and development layer is subject to legal access control. Slow travel connections first express the directional continuity of the Dazhongsi—Origin—Zhongzhiyuan, and then are determined by traffic surveys for crossing, connection, parking, and curb configuration. [data:geometry/roads.geojson#ROAD-001] Municipal and new infrastructure adhere to the principles of shared trenches, shared stations, and maintainability. End-side computing power, sensing devices, and digital identifiers must have offline modes, manual alternatives, and decommissioning lists; they must not be allowed to be crowded out by "smart" systems at the expense of basic lighting, seating, toilets, shade, and continuous accessibility for people with disabilities. [depth:municipal_new_infrastructure]

## AI Innovation Ecosystem, Personas, and AI+ Scenarios

Six archetypes run through the design: 1) Out-of-town developers making their first visit to Beijing, who need low-threshold workstations, an explanation of city rights, and trustworthy partners; 2) International researchers, who need multi-lingual support, time-zone friendliness, and clear institutional readability; 3) Community elders and caregivers, who need on-site human windows, minimal walking, and paper alternatives; 4) Users with physical, visual, or auditory impairments are co-testers of pathways and interfaces; 5) Haidian university students and young researchers, who need a path from prototypes to the first real-world problem; 6) Park front-line operators, who need tools that are manageable, repairable, and de-commissionable. The number of archetypes is recorded by [metric:persona_count], and none of them establish continuous personal archetypes.

Twelve scene cards all landed in `public_space.geojson`, and each card was assigned a phase, space, responsible party, minimum data, Human Review, and non-numeric path: [metric:landing_scenario_node_count]

1. **Multilingual First Day City Tour** (Arrival/Dazhongsi): Offline map and on-site volunteers operating in parallel, no continuous tracking recorded.
2. **Accessibility Continuous Path Assistant** (Arrival/Origin): Accessibility feedback verified manually, with a ready paper-based accessible route.
3. **Public Service Rights Navigation** (Understanding/Zhongzhiyuan): Provides only the entry points and explanations, not a substitute for staff qualifications.
4. **Matching of Research Resources and Open Workstations** (Understanding/Dazhongsi): Users submit their minimum requirements proactively, and these can be queried on-site.
5. **Model Compliance for Urban Testing** (Trial/Origin Point): Virtual, Controlled, and Limited Real-World Testing in Three Levels, with Conclusions Requiring Manual Endorsement.
6. **Robot Constrained Scenario Entry Test** (Trial/Zhongzhiyuan): Set physical boundaries, on-site safety officers, and manual shutdown.
7. **End-Side Equipment Public Interface Urban Test** (Trial/Dazhongsi): Prioritize Local Processing, Retain Basic Services in Case of Network Failure.

[standard:MNR-LAND-USE-CLASSIFICATION-GUIDE] [depth:metrics_recalculation]

The building scale should honestly express the "known footprint, unknown intensity." The `buildings.geojson` is generated by shrinking non-green spaces/conceptual units, representing a capacity test carrier for mixed research and development, public services, or residential amenities; it does not represent the actual building outline and does not trigger specific construction actions. The demolish–renovate–retain strategy follows a four-step process: first, supplement the current status, structure, purpose, ownership, and value assessment; second, complete the cultural heritage, fire safety, solar access, and low-carbon reviews; third, form a building-by-building record based on the principle of prioritizing retention, light renovation, and reversible actions; finally, submit the proposed demolition items for review by professional teams and stakeholders. Currently, all features' demolish–renovate–retain fields are pending to avoid replacing field surveys with generative geometry. (Demolish–Renovate–Retain Strategy)

Space supply recommendations suggest prioritizing the ground floor and public forecourt for co-learning, culture, care, public disclosure, and initial user testing, while placing equipment-intensive research and development in controlled levels. Talent residences will not have exclusive private zones but will share incremental facilities with existing community services. Building Height, Floor Area Ratio, residential proportion, parking provision, and municipal capacity are all pending confirmation of data; subsequent professional teams must link current evidence, value judgments, public input, carbon impact, and final actions to the same site ID to ensure the scheme is traceable rather than merely effective.

## Transport, Rail, Municipal Infrastructure, and Public Services

The traffic concept consists of a north-south pedestrian and cycling intention and three horizontal connections within the focus area, with a length [metric:concept_road_length_m] and density [metric:concept_road_density_km_per_sqkm] used solely for validating network connectivity; they are not road centerlines, right-of-way lines, or engineering designs. [data:geometry/roads.geojson#ROAD-001] [depth:traffic_rail_slow_parking] The deepening sequence is as follows: obtain track station entrances and exits, bus stops, passenger flow, intersections, parking, and accessibility data; conduct on-site walk-throughs to identify gaps; prioritize in the order of "pedestrian—cycling—bus/track transfer—necessary motor vehicles"; and finally determine the road division, crosswalks, and parking adjustments. All tests should maintain normal traffic flow and on-site management.

Public services are configured with a "base that everyone can use + AI that activates only when needed." At Dazhongsi, there are multilingual notices and statements of rights. The original point community enhances care, co-learning, and youth life, while Zhongzhiyuan enhances compliance, testing, and enterprise services; toilets, drinking water, shade trees, benches, baby-changing facilities, and accessibility features precede digital screens. AI navigation provides information cues, with educational, health, governmental, legal, and safety matters all reviewed by responsible human institutions; those without smartphones can use fixed service desks, telephones, paper maps, and on-site personnel.

Municipal and new infrastructure are recommended to adopt modular equipment racks, unified power/communication interfaces, edge computing, and minimal sensing. Each component should have a unique identifier, maintenance records, outage behavior, manual backup, energy consumption logging, and decommissioning date. Nighttime lighting should balance safety and ecology. Robot testing areas are designated with low-speed zones, manual shutdowns, and physical isolation. Due to the lack of pipeline, capacity, flood control, sponge city, energy, and fire safety data, this package does not make conclusions on capacity and line positioning; these gaps are included in assumptions, constraints, and self-checks, to be addressed and re-verified once supplemented by specialized units. ## Risk, Copyright, and Compliance

The primary spatial risk is the misinterpretation of provisional geometry as precise control: therefore, the text, five figures, visuals, sources, assumptions, and self-checks all prompt, and the recalculated objects after the official polygon is in place are listed. The second risk is the slide towards talent privilege or surveillance: this scheme does not build a real-name passport, does not continuously track location, and does not infer identity, emotions, or qualifications; residents define problems and benefits together with the Host Council, and anyone can exit through a paper or on-site path. The third risk is technological spillover: robots, models, and edge-side devices are all first virtually or controlled tested, with on-site responsibility assigned, manual shutdowns, minimized logging, and decommissioning gates. [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK]

Fourth, high-impact matters such as health, administration, law, and safety are being automated, with this plan serving only as an information navigation, auxiliary organization, or limited experiment. The final judgment must be made by professionals with the appropriate responsibilities. Fifth, there is a risk of cultural narrative distortion and conflict with cultural preservation, so historical facts must be verified by public archives or professional institutions, with nodes pending confirmation of cultural preservation materials.
