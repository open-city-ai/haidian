Jing-Zhang Scenario Ledger

AI does not fail to enter the city because the technology is immature. It fails because the city has no ledger of where anything may be tried.

Split the overall design area into 72 registrable, claimable, measurable, revocable scenario parcels. Who tests which AI on which parcel, under what liability, and by when they must exit — all of it inspectable.
