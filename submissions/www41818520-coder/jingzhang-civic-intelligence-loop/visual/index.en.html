overall structure
The Jing-Zhang public baseline connects AI Origin, Zhongzhiyuan and Dazhongsi through a visible, human-governed civic task loop.

A trusted contribution loop

AI is not displayed as a finished answer. A city question passes through collaboration, bounded verification, optional experience and correction before a cleared contribution is archived.

governance by design
Three non-negotiable lines: public interest, human review and data minimisation. Every correction is evidence for the next design cycle.

Three public working interfaces

Each station is a place to do something, not an AI gallery. By day they are calm and professional; at nights and weekends they can open to the city.
