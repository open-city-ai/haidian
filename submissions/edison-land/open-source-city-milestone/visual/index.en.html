Mobility

One spine, three parallel lines, four east-west stitches, three rail interchanges. All alignments are conceptual and are not road redlines, rail alignments or engineering conclusions.

North-south continuity

The main line walking spine runs 9.72 km from the North 5th Ring Road to Xizhimenwai Street; a western commuting cycleway and an eastern campus–industry cycleway run parallel to it.

East-west stitching

Four stitching walks land at the Qinghe interface, Wudaokou, Beitaipingzhuang and the Dazhongsi four quadrants; three interchanges connect the northern station, Wudaokou Station and Dazhongsi Station.

Parking, cross-section, pipeline and municipal capacity data is unavailable, so road_area_ratio is marked unknown.

Blue-green and public space

A continuous green belt, cluster pocket parks and protective green at both ends, overlaid with four inscription landmarks.

Blue-green system

Green and open space 227.8 ha, green ratio 19.96%; public space 44.0 ha, share 3.85%. The share is modest, but all of it sits on spine nodes and station fronts.

Inscription system (4 AI pilgrimage landmarks)

M0 Commit Zero · M1 Contributor Wall · M2 Milestone Array · M3 Merge Point.

The governing principle is growability: reserved slots and a stated inscription rule let it keep being written year after year. Siting requires heritage review.

Buildings

Building footprint 181.3 ha, coverage 15.89% — a suggested footprint organisation only.
