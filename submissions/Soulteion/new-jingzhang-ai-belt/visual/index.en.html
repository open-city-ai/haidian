Operations

Four-season matrix: AI Innovation Week (spring) · Open-Source Festival (summer) · AI Governance Forum (autumn) · Ren Festival release season (winter).

  • Scenario-opening lists & booking platform
  • Roadshows, evaluation & data exchange driving international conversion
