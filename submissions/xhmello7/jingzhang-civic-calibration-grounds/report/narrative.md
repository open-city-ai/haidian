# Jingzhang Civic Calibration Grounds

The submission organizes Jingzhang as one real railway-heritage spine, three urban rhythms and three site-specific interfaces. The north supports quiet research commuting around Qinghe, the Fifth Ring and Zhongzhiyuan; the middle connects university life, heritage learning and east-west walking; the south uses the real Dazhongsi station-city context for arrival, disclosure, appeal and public memory.

The public OpenStreetMap extract is used only as a traceable background for roads, stations, railway, parks and selected building candidates. Repository site and key-area geometries remain provisional. The coordinated research scope of 43.6 km2 and the approximately 11.4 km2 provisional computation geometry are different planning layers, not competing site-area claims.

The single civic lifecycle is Problem Intake -> Calibration at Zhongzhiyuan -> Release Gate at Dazhongsi -> Civic Verification in Jingzhang public space -> Scale, Recalibrate or Rollback/Retirement. Two Dazhongsi flagship cases demonstrate this mechanism; two mapped cases remain a secondary scenario library.

Retirement Memory is a daily public walk, not a display screen. Shade, seating and accessible movement work at all times. Durable archive plates, staffed appeal and replacement service, and railway maintenance history make correction, suspension, replacement and retirement publicly legible without exposing personal data.

The package reaches competition-level conceptual urban-design depth on a real public basemap. It does not claim statutory planning, survey-grade detailed design or construction documentation. Official boundaries, road redlines, station engineering, ownership, building surveys, heritage controls, utilities, traffic counts and cost data remain required for the next stage.
