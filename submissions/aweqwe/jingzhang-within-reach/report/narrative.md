# Formal Narrative Routing Note

The complete formal narrative is `proposal.md`, with `proposal.en.md` as its required English counterpart. This routing note does not introduce a second design story.

The package is controlled by the following evidence chain:

1. `brief/site-package/agent_taskbook.json` is the highest agent-facing design mandate.
2. `sources.json` and `assumptions.json` separate formal, background, provisional, and unknown evidence.
3. `geometry/*.geojson` and `metrics.json` hold spatial and recalculable evidence.
4. `compliance_matrix.json`, `standard_matrix.json`, and `design_depth_matrix.json` map obligations and professional depth.
5. `drawings/`, `report/proposal*.html`, and `visual/index*.html` present the same A01-A08, five-state, and no-single-point-of-failure concept to human reviewers.

The package retains the provisional-boundary warning and does not claim approved planning controls, engineering feasibility, named implementation commitments, or field performance without evidence.
