# 方案摘要 Narrative Summary

「双轨 · 京张」以双轨为骨架：时光之轨（京张遗址公园文化慢行主轴）承载百年京张文化带，星河之轨（AI创新功能带）承载都市AI生活体验带，双轨交汇处承载AI融合创新带。三站（启航站·众智园、原点站·AI原点社区、回响站·大钟寺）与两翼（星图翼、试航翼）构成“启航→原点→回响→试航→星图→反馈研发”的协同回路，命名采用“当代诗性 + 历史文化注脚”的双层体系，旅程语法为 0 → 1 → ∞。

方案与北京总规“减量集约、留白增绿”及海淀分区规划“两心聚一核”“智慧平行城市”同构，空间上依托已建成的9公里京张遗址公园与地铁13/12号线换乘枢纽，全部落地建议为概念方案，待官方边界与控规条件补齐后复算。

Twin Tracks · Jing-Zhang organizes the corridor with two tracks: the Time Track (heritage park slow spine) carries the cultural belt and the Star Track (AI belt) carries the urban AI-life belt, meeting in interchange zones. Three stations (Set Sail, Origin, Echo) and two wings (Star Chart, Trial Voyage) form a closed R&D-transfer-landing-testing-capital loop with a dual naming system (contemporary layer, historical footnotes) and the journey grammar 0 → 1 → ∞. Aligned with Beijing's reduction/green-growth planning and Haidian's "two hearts, one core" and "parallel smart city", all spatial proposals are concepts pending official boundaries and controls.
