Zhongzhiyuan AI Acceleration Area

About 192.1 ha, a garden-style full-stack independent-innovation district: open-source square at the northern spine end, core research blocks to the west, strategic reserved land to the east, and a pedestrian loop linking research, display and testing.

Beijing AI Origin Community

About 104.3 ha, a near-campus technology-transfer and talent community: co-creation square mid-spine, mixed vitality and core research blocks on both flanks, stitched campus-park-neighbourhood slow mobility.

Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster

About 72.0 ha, an urban intelligent-economy and international-exchange district: Commons square at the southern spine end, gateway commerce tied to the Dazhongsi station connection, and a study of four-quadrant pedestrian continuity.

Slow mobility and blue-green public space

The Commons heritage greenway runs about 9.7 km north-south with slow-priority service roads and eleven cross streets; transit connections lead towards Dazhongsi and Wudaokou stations; each key area has a pedestrian loop. Green ratio is about 15.5 percent and public-space ratio about 2.9 percent.
