# Formal Narrative · 京张智源线 Jingzhang AI Commons

「京张智源线」把百年京张铁路廊道重塑为一条开放共创的 AI 创新带：在约 1141.3 公顷临时总体范围内，一条 160 米宽、南北贯通约 9.7 公里的"智源脊"遗址公园构成公共空间骨架，两翼交替布置科研、商业、文化、教育、社区服务与保留居住街坊，北端安排战略留白，形成"一脊两街三区"的总体结构。众智园、北京 AI 原点社区、大钟寺三处重点片区共享"一片区一广场一步行环路"的设计语法，分别承载造与测（工作台）、研转用（公共门厅）、用与反馈（城市客厅）三种角色。方案登记 AI 慢行、京张遗产叙事与城市智能体治理三条赛道，10 张场景卡中含低速机器人配送、AI 慢行导航、AI 遗产导览三个产业测试场景，全部以数据最小化、人工复核、非数字替代与停止条件为治理前提。全部 known 指标（范围面积、绿地率约 15.5%、公共空间占比约 2.9%、绿地/公共空间/建筑基底面积、重点片区数量）均按 EPSG:4548 从提交几何复算，与 metrics.json 完全一致；控规类指标因官方数据缺失标记为 unknown。

Jingzhang AI Commons reframes the century-old Jing-Zhang railway corridor as an open co-creation belt for AI innovation: within the roughly 1,141.3-hectare provisional design area, a 160-metre-wide Commons Spine heritage park runs about 9.7 kilometres north-south as the public-space backbone, flanked by alternating research, commercial, cultural, education, community-service and retained-residential blocks, with strategic reserve land at the north. The three key areas — Zhongzhiyuan, the Beijing AI Origin Community and Dazhongsi — share a grammar of one square and one pedestrian loop each, playing the roles of workbench, public hall and urban lounge respectively. Ten scenario cards include three industry testing pilots (low-speed robot delivery, AI slow-mobility navigation, AI heritage guide), all governed by data minimisation, human review, non-digital alternatives and stop conditions. All known metrics are recomputed from submitted geometry in EPSG:4548 and match metrics.json; regulatory metrics remain unknown pending official data.
