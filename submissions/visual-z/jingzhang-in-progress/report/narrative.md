# 京张进行时 / JINGZHANG IN PROGRESS

## 中文叙事

每一道临时工程，必须预演并留下至少一项永久公共收益。 本方案不把已经开放的京张遗址公园重新包装成未来公园，也不把公众带入生产作业面。它把每个项目开工前必须交付的公共价值拆成三层：CONTINUITY 连续通行、OPEN-WORKS BUFFER 开放工地缓冲、HANDOVER 永久移交。公众开放的是信息、监督与硬隔离后的公共缓冲；危险区始终由专业责任体系管理。

三个重点区承担等权但不同的验证：P1众智园“1:1建造院”验证机器人急停、失联和构件缺陷识别；P2原点“公共模型室”验证7/30/90日影响、公开改模和问题闭环；P3大钟寺“不断档街区”验证通行、基本服务、商户与配送连续。SC-01—12都带人工负责人、非AI路径、测试、硬停止与回滚，最后经MET记录和接收人签认进入G6。

100日窗口仅在方案被选择、真实接口通过G0且取得同意后启动。G0—G8把权属安全、无障碍、人车隔离、轨道、环境、居民权益、永久交付、成本采购和AI治理逐项设为硬门槛。RACI不替代法定权限；没有接收人、OPEX与缺陷责任的构件不计永久收益。

## English narrative

Every temporary work must rehearse and leave at least one permanent public benefit. The proposal neither repackages an already-open heritage park as a future park nor admits the public to a production zone. It defines three pre-start deliveries: CONTINUITY, an OPEN-WORKS BUFFER, and HANDOVER. Information and scrutiny are open; hazards remain under competent professional control.

The three equal-status, differentiated fields test different claims. P1, the Full-Scale Works Yard at Zhongzhiyuan, tests robot emergency stop, link loss, and component-defect detection. P2, the Public Model Room at AI Origin, tests 7/30/90-day impacts, public model changes, and issue closure. P3, the Stay-Open District at Dazhongsi, tests continuity for routes, essential services, businesses, and deliveries. Every SC-01–12 record carries a human owner, non-AI route, test, hard stop, rollback, MET record, and receiver sign-off.

The 100-day window is conditional on selection, a real interface passing G0, and required consent. G0–G8 gate ownership/safety, accessibility, separation, rail, environment, resident rights, permanent delivery, cost/procurement, and AI governance. RACI never substitutes for statutory authority. Without a receiver, OPEX, and defects responsibility, no component counts as a permanent benefit.
