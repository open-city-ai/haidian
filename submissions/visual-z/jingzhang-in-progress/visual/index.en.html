  1. a-boundary-provisional — The overall and three key-area polygons are repository provisional geometry, not official redlines.

  4. a-construction-boundary — Works redlines, hoarding, vehicle gates, hazards, emergency routes, and stage plans are unavailable.

  7. a-existing-survey — Cleared building-by-building condition, use, structure, service, tree, drainage, and retain/renovate/demolish surveys are missing.
  8. a-safety-assessment — Hazard distances and public-buffer dimensions require project-specific professional assessment.
  9. a-accessibility-baseline — Day/night/rain, wheelchair, low-vision, school, healthcare, delivery, and emergency continuity baselines are not measured.
