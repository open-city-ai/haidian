## Coordinated research: industry and future-city strategy

### Ecosystem and global cases

Elements—space, capital, talent, compute, data, scenarios and governance—are transferred across stations rather than piled into one park. [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK] [depth:overall_spatial_structure]

Eight global cases (mechanism only, no fabricated local performance): Station F; MaRS; Smart Kalasatama; One-North; 22@; public experiential tech venues in Tokyo; Robotics Row; publicly described Shenzhen Nanshan AI nodes. Cases inspire mechanisms; they do not justify invented tenants, investment or fiscal promises. [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK]

### Three areas and two wings

- **Zhongzhiyuan Verification Station** — full-stack autonomy and governance voice.
- **AI Origin Transfer Station** — open ecosystem and campus translation.
- **Dazhongsi Experience Station** — intelligent-native urban economies.
- **Zhongguancun service wing** — capital, IP and professional services.
- **Xiaoyuehe scenario wing** — real-city trial feedback.

This is a conceptual coordination loop, not a new administrative boundary. [standard:MOHURD-URBAN-DESIGN-MEASURES] [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#PUBLIC-001]
