# Jing-Zhang Smart Waiting Line: AI Climate Resilience and Public Health Experimentation Belt

"Zhihou" is not about letting AI predict everything, but about making the city aware of when to change its operating mode, who should make the final judgment, and when to return to normal. The proposal interprets the Jing-Zhang Heritage Park as a century-long railway timeline, and overlays a future-oriented urban operation calendar on it: the same space switches between six states—daily, high temperatures, heavy rain, nighttime, events, and failures. All spatial suggestions are Conceptual Recommendations, reference proposals, or subject to further research by professional teams, and do not replace formal planning or constitute government approval conclusions.

## Three-Level Scope Framework

The Coordinated Research Area covers approximately 43.6 square kilometers and addresses regional innovation ecology, climate services, and urban operational mechanisms; the Overall Design Area covers approximately 11.4 square kilometers and addresses temporal spatial structure, Urban Renewal, active transportation, public services, and phased development; three key areas totaling approximately 368.4 hectares respectively validate autonomous climate intelligence, public health collaboration, and climate-tech living. [depth:three_level_scope_framework] [depth:overall_spatial_structure]

Overall structure is "a timeline, three seasonal laboratories, six waiting gates, and twelve reversible scenes." The timeline corresponds to the Jing-Zhang north-south Public Space connection [data:geometry/roads.geojson#ROAD-SPINE]; the three laboratories correspond to three temporary focal areas; the six waiting gates are horizontal stitching concepts, not road engineering. The twelve scenes are governed by a common agreement for early warning triggers, manual takeovers, non-AI equivalent services, and exit recovery. The land uses are divided into five segments with shared boundary zones [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-01], achieving full coverage and no overlap, but not claiming legal designation.

## Coordinated Research Area: Industry and Future City Research

"Zhuihou Industry Ecology" is not a single climate technology track, but rather organizes a verifiable chain by integrating models, edge-side devices, urban operations, health services, building energy, Public Space, and governance assessment: universities and research institutions propose methods; Zhongzhiyuan undertakes model and device validation and interoperability verification; Origin Community undertakes public understanding, health service, and social acceptance verification; Dazhongsi undertakes product service, international exchange, and commercial scenario verification before scaling; the two wings provide technology services, capital, intellectual property, and Xiao Yuehe public experience interfaces.

Six cases are provided for method translation only: the open digital platform of Singapore's Punggol Digital District serves as a model for cross-system interface and real-scenario testing [source:JTC-PDD]; Kalasatama highlights short-cycle pilots and resident co-creation [source:HELSINKI-KALASATAMA]; Barcelona's climate shelter network demonstrates the adaptability of existing facilities to seasonal changes [source:BARCELONA-SHELTERS]; Rotterdam's water squares illustrate the dual functionality of Public Spaces for both sun and rain [source:ROTTERDAM-WATER-SQUARE]; Beijing's climate adaptation action provides a local policy context [source:BEIJING-CLIMATE-ACTION]; the WHO Heat Health Action Plan highlights eight mechanisms for governance, early warning, vulnerable populations, communication, health systems, exposure reduction, monitoring, and evaluation [source:WHO-HHAP-2026]. These cases do not prove that the Beijing project can be directly replicated.

Brand name: "Jing-Zhang Climate-Time Line". The logo direction consists of railway mileage scales and six operational loops, without using third-party fonts, corporate logos, or images of individuals. The naming system, "Waiting Gates, Seasonal Laboratories, Operation Calendar, and Climate-Time Protocol," forms an extendable public language corresponding to agent.1 and agent.2.

## Overall Design Area: Urban Renewal and Regulatory-Plan-Level Urban Design

The design innovation point is to add a "time state layer" to traditional two-dimensional land use. Five conceptual land use units fully cover the Provisional Boundary: [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-01] to LU-05 respectively undertake climate technology life, culture, health services, open R&D, and autonomous validation; the blue-green time axis [data:geometry/green_space.geojson#GREEN-SPINE] connects three public waiting living rooms. The space remains a general park, passage, and community service in the daily state; opens the waiting points in the high-temperature state and adjusts the activity schedule; suspends low-lying activities and resumes post-rain inspection in the heavy rainfall state; strengthens continuous lighting and manual patrol in the nighttime state; sets capacity, noise, and evacuation conditions in the activity state; and closes automation and retains manual service in the fault state.

Buildings [data:geometry/buildings.geojson#BUILD-01] only indicate eight types of adaptable spatial carriers and do not identify actual buildings. [metric:building_footprint_area_sqm] is a conceptual base area; [metric:floor_area_ratio] and [metric:total_floor_area_sqm] are kept unknown. The Demolish–Renovate–Retain Strategy follows the rule of "survey first, then classify": no individual conclusions are drawn until the ownership, era, structural safety, energy consumption, historical value, and user opinions are complete. [depth:land_use_layout] [depth:development_intensity_controls] [depth:height_massing_character] [depth:retain_renovate_demolish]

## Land Use, Building Scale, and Retain-Renovate-Demolish Strategy

Temporal land use is not an additional land classification, but rather an operational field added to the national classification code: `normal / heat / rain / night / event / fallback`. [metric:temporal_state_count] records six states. The ability of Public Space to switch states depends on management rights, fire evacuation, accessibility, cultural heritage protection, facility capacity, and the responsible party; if any condition is unclear, it remains in the normal state. Building updates prioritize adapting to the first floor, walkways, courtyards, and reversible components, with heavy asset construction deferred.

The base for eight concept carriers is only used for graphical and spatial relationship testing, and does not provide height, number of floors, or Floor Area Ratio. The interface near the archaeological park emphasizes scale, materials, shading, winter solar exposure, nighttime luminance, and maintainability; specific aesthetic controls await formal protection and planning conditions. [standard:MOHURD-URBAN-DESIGN-MEASURES] [depth:height_massing_character]

## Transport, Rail, Municipal Infrastructure, and Public Services

Traffic strategy is a north-south seasonal pedestrian timeline with six horizontal waiting gates. [metric:conceptual_mobility_length_m] is the total conceptual relationship line length, not the engineering mileage. In the high-temperature state, continuous shade, drinking water, seating, and short-distance waiting information are prioritized; in the heavy rain state, risk segments are closed first, alternative routes are announced, and on-site verification is conducted; at night, continuous lighting, visible staff, and accessibility are prioritized. Dazhongsi and Wudao Kou stations integrate only pedestrian connections, without providing bridge, tunnel, or road engineering solutions. [depth:traffic_rail_slow_parking]

Municipal and new infrastructure adopt "interfaces before equipment": Verify power supply, communication, flood control, fire safety, cybersecurity, data governance, maintenance budget, and responsible parties before formal connection. Edge-side computational power, environmental sensing, and distributed energy can be degraded offline without relying on remote cloud services for life safety. [depth:municipal_new_infrastructure]

## Renewal Projects, Implementation Policy, and Phasing

Phase 1 [data:geometry/phasing.geojson#PHASE-1] only includes baseline measurements, verification of six waiting gates, three reversible public living rooms, and an operations manual; Phase 2 PHASE-2 builds a pilot network of three seasonal laboratories; Phase 3 PHASE-3 outputs interfaces, procurement, data, accessibility, and exit criteria after evaluation. Phasing is not a construction commitment but a decision-making sequence. [depth:renewal_project_list] [depth:phasing_implementation]

Each project must pass through six gates: the Source Gate, the Spatial Safety Gate, the Data Minimization Gate, the Human Accountability Gate, the Non-AI Equivalence Gate, and the Exit Recovery Gate. It is recommended to form a "City Operations Calendar," seasonal public facility agreements, climate health communication templates, a public disclosure system for pilot failures, and annual smart-resilience reports. Expanding pilots should not rely solely on model metrics but should also incorporate user feedback, professional review, cost considerations, and equity.

## Annual Operations, Participation in Equity, and Pilot Translations (agent.6)

The year is divided into a spring baseline and accessibility audit, a summer heat operation run, an autumn open-source challenge and international showcase, a winter decommissioning of equipment and annual review. A represents street or facility owners, R represents on-site operations and professional teams, C includes public health, fire safety, cybersecurity, accessibility specialists, and community members, and I includes residents, workers, and visitors. Developer outcomes are sequentially reviewed through public APIs, sandbox red team testing, on-site blind testing, fairness/cost audits, small-scale procurement, and annual renewal evaluations; any failure in safety, fairness, maintenance, budget, or public acceptance results in a return to the sandbox or cessation. Participation channels include offline workshops, child and senior group blind testing, outdoor worker roundtables, accessibility hotlines, public objection registers, and third-party mediation; service coverage is audited based on walkability, time of day, language, disability, and digital access.
