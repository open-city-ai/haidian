# Jing-Zhang Open Source Track: Verifiable AI Urban Public Protocol

## Design Basis and Source List

This plan addresses a more fundamental issue than "what AI devices to put in the city": how can the Jing-Zhang Innovation Belt form a public interface that is understandable, verifiable, exitable, reversible, and buildable upon, when technology, stakeholders, and needs are constantly changing? The success criteria are not the number of sensors or large screens, but five observable outcomes: **understandable, verifiable, exitable, reversible, and buildable upon**. This is also the design bottom line for the "Jing-Zhang Open Source Track."

The plan is based on the three-level scope, three key areas, and design tasks determined by the official announcement, incorporating three additional orientations, five major functions, the Three Zones and Two Wings, and six agent tasks. [source:OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT] [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK] [standard:PROJECT-OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT] [standard:PROJECT-AGENT-OPEN-CALL-TASKBOOK] The repository's documentation and fact package only serve a navigational role and do not add any authoritative conclusions. [source:SITE-PACKAGE] [source:SOURCE-REGISTRY] [source:PROCESSED-FACT-PACK]

The submission package therefore uses the temporary rough boundary of the warehouse, marked as `provisional_constraint`; it is only for open co-creation, visualization, and intake self-check and is not an official redline or a basis for approval or precise area calculation. ## Three-Level Scope Framework

"Track" here is not a new railway line but a shared protocol. The railway forms a long-term network not only because of advanced vehicles but also because different vehicles adhere to a common gauge, signals, and maintenance rules. Similarly, AI cities require common interfaces: each scenario must specify its purpose, minimum data requirements, human responsibilities, exit conditions, failure fallbacks, and maintenance entities.

Three key positioning concepts are translated into three operational "tracks": the century-old Jing-Zhang cultural belt corresponds to the "Memory Track," the urban AI living experience belt corresponds to the "Daily Track," and the AI integration innovation belt corresponds to the "Validation Track." Five functional areas are translated into a single closed loop: full-stack independent innovation is responsible for validation, a world-class innovation ecosystem is responsible for sharing, AI-Enabled Scenarios are responsible for real feedback, an intelligent vibrant city is responsible for daily perceptibility, and AI governance voice is responsible for opening up rules and failure experiences. The Three Zones and Two Wings are not five isolated islands: the Zhongguancun Technology Services Wing inputs capital, legal services, intellectual property, and international networks, while the Xiaoyue River Scenario Enablement Wing inputs real urban issues. After the three zones complete validation, sharing, and transformation, the experiences are then fed back into the two wings.

## Detailed Design of Key Areas

![Functional Differences of Three Key Areas and Project Anchors](assets/figures/key-areas.png)

Three provisional rough polygons are only used for organizing the conceptual design; the following actions are reference schemes for further in-depth study by professional teams. [depth:three_key_area_detailed_design]

### Zhongzhiyuan: PROVE / Technical Trustworthiness

Zhongzhiyuan does not revolve around "displaying the latest models," but around "publicly demonstrating the boundaries within which a capability is usable." The space is organized around four prototypes: the Verifiable Model Lab, the Public Evaluation Hall, the Standards Co-Creation Studio, and the Qinghe Innovation Living Room.[data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-001]

- Conceptually set up a "Model Examination Station" on the north side public interface, making the applicable range, known failures, source of test data, and human responsibility into readable labels.
- Conceptualize an internal low-speed space for the setting of a "Robot Shared Street," which will only conduct test runs that are isolatable, stoppable in emergencies, and supervised by humans.
- Preserve continuous green interface in the Qinghe direction to connect open testing, active transportation, and low-carbon education; blue line, flood control, and ecological conditions not met, cannot form engineering conclusions.
- Form a "standard—test—review—publicize" governance loop, turning failure records into public knowledge assets.

### AI Origin in Beijing: SHARE / Community Mainturable

The core of AI Origin is not to commemorate a technological starting point, but to allow each generation of developers, students, entrepreneurs, and residents to make verifiable and reversible contributions. Four prototypes include a spatialized open-source release hall, an on-campus incubation workshop, a contributors' apartment service station, and an origin forum gallery. [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-002]

- "Open Release Hall" adopts four minimum disclosures: project metadata, license, reproducibility instructions, and maintenance status.
- "Contributor Passport" only records contributions proactively submitted by the user and does not serve as a personal credit score. It allows for anonymous submission, exporting, and deletion.
- "Near-School Results Clinic" integrates technical support, product development, legal advice, intellectual property, ethics, and operational mentoring into a single appointment interface.
- First-floor Public Space retains non-consumptive seating, accessible pathways, nighttime safety, and human services, avoiding the exclusion of everyday life from the innovative community.

### Dazhongsi: SCALE / Service Options

Dazhongsi bears the transition from "technical demonstration" to "optional urban services." Four prototypes are the Smart Agent Application Theater, International Roadshow Living Room, Data Compliance Meeting Room, and Edge Product Experience Corridor. [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-003]

- The applied theater must simultaneously showcase "Applicable," "Not Applicable," "Who is Responsible," and "How to Exit," without writing the trial as deployment.
- Dazhongsi Station Quadrant Four Pedestrian Connection is conceptual in nature; the road right-of-way, track facilities, utility lines, and engineering feasibility await professional verification.
- Priority should be given to business and consumer interfaces that support comparable, triable, and revocable smart services, while prohibiting the covert collection of personal profiles.
- International roadshows and subtitle agents use authorized voice recordings provided by the event organizers, with manual simultaneous interpretation for review, without preserving voiceprints.

## Land Use, Building Scale, and Retain-Renovate-Demolish Strategy

The land use GeoJSON is a fully topological partition, with Building Footprints, green spaces, Public Spaces, roads, and phases all derived from the same Provisional Boundary. Area recomputation is performed in EPSG:4548; numerical precision only indicates computational reproducibility and does not reflect the accuracy of the official boundaries. (Official Boundary)

The architectural strategy adopts a "survey first, then prioritize, and finally act" gatekeeping approach:

1. **Survey Gates**: Complete the current profile, including era, structure, purpose, ownership, vacancy rate, energy consumption, fire safety, and cultural heritage information.
2. **Value Gates**: Evaluate the cultural, social, industrial, carbon, and spatial alignment values separately.
3. **Action Gates**: Proposals for retention, restoration, adaptive reuse, localized renewal, or new construction should be formed.

Currently, all 12 bases are marked as `conceptual_reuse_or_infill_pending_survey`, and should not be interpreted as individual conclusions for demolish–renovate–retain. [data:geometry/buildings.geojson#BLDG-001] The Building Height adopts a qualitative direction of "trackside low disturbance, node recognizable, continuous skyline, and ground floor accessible"; the specific height, density, setback, Floor Area Ratio, and construction scale are all unknown. [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#PUBLIC-001] [metric:public_space_ratio] These nodes are primarily composed of reversible facilities and operational prototypes, with specific ranges that must be verified through ownership, cultural heritage, green space, fire safety, and traffic reviews.

Three AI pilgrimage sites are not giant sculptures but three types of public rituals:

1. **Zero Gauge / Origin Gauge**: Display the replication instructions, licenses, and maintenance status of the project at the AI Origin, commemorating the contributions that are "continuously buildable."
2. **Failure Specimen Rack / Museum of Useful Failures**: Display known failures, applicable boundaries, and repair records in Zhongzhiyuan, making safe governance a visible culture.
3. **After the Demo / một ngày sau**: Continuously track Dazhongsi to document maintenance, exit, and public value one day, 30 days, 90 days, and one year after the demo, rather than just commemorating the release moment.

Honors are displayed not by funding amount or exposure, but by contributions organized into five categories: "reality, maintenance, public issue resolution, accessibility, and failure disclosure." The Public Space component library includes dual-track wayfinding, responsibility tags, manned service flags, offline maps, retractable contribution plaques, and modular shaded seating.

Culture narrative is composed of three layers of time: the Jing-Zhang railway represents shared gauge and long-term maintenance; Zhongguancun represents the continuous transformation from research to industry; and AI New Culture represents the open boundaries for accountability, acknowledging failure, respecting withdrawal, and allowing successors to continue building. The international communication sentence is: **A railway moved people by sharing a gauge. OpenRail moves intelligence by sharing accountable rules.**

## Risk, Copyright, and Compliance

The greatest risk is not "inadequate precision," but using precise visuals to mask data gaps. The risk list includes [depth:risk_missing_data].

1. **Spatial Risk**: official polygons not obtained. All areas and boundary overruns must be recalculated after the formal boundaries are in place.
2. **Legal Risks**: Absence of control plan, road red lines, cultural heritage protection, green space blue lines, ownership, and approval conditions. No formation of height, Floor Area Ratio, Demolish–Renovate–Retain Strategy, or engineering conclusions.
3. **Safety Risks**: Robotic systems, edge-side energy, and dynamic traffic must be tested in isolated scenarios under professional supervision.
4. **Privacy Risks**: Contributions, navigation, consultations, subtitles, and slow-moving data must be minimized, prioritize anonymity, be explicit in purpose, and allow for deletion.
5. **Operational Risks**: Scenarios without maintainers shall not be launched; each scenario requires termination conditions and exit strategies.
6. **Equity in Risk**: Digital services must not eliminate human channels, and must not turn vulnerable groups into passive data sources.
7. **Copyright Risk**: All graphics are generated from the submission package data program and do not use commercial maps, remote tiles, corporate logos, portraits, or unauthorized images; the source and generation method are detailed in `report/copyright_statement.md`.
