# Formal Narrative

京张智轨带（JINGZHANG INTELLIGENCE RAIL）把百年京张铁路的自主创新基因转译为一条城市轨道：记忆轨（遗址公园绿带）与智轨（AI创新要素带）并行，众智园、AI原点社区、大钟寺三座智轨站为创新锚点，中关村科技服务翼与小月河场景赋能翼为支线。

Geometry, metrics, compliance matrix, drawings and visual/index.html remain cross-checked deliverables. All spatial proposals use provisional boundaries and are conceptual suggestions for professional teams to deepen; they do not replace statutory planning.
