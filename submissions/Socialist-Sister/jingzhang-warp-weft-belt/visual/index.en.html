Pilgrimage landmarks (5 sites, all conceptual suggestions)

Per design-system s10.1 and ch3 s3.5; they form the "Memory - Origin - Future" temporal narrative anchors along the warp line.

L01 Weave-Origin Concept suggestion

Site: former Qinghuayuan Station

Memory x AI origin community: commemorative display, digital narrative, origin ruler

Design rationale: Physical evidence of the old Jing-Zhang station - origin of China's self-built railway

Risk & licensing: Heritage use requires heritage-department approval; preservation per heritage authority

L02 Weave-Heart Plaza Concept suggestion

Site: Wudaokou

Railway-crossing memory x AI-vibrant street plaza; Weavers' Wall honor board

Design rationale: Wudaokou was the former Jing-Zhang level crossing; urban vibrancy center

Risk & licensing: High traffic pressure; location requires traffic assessment (concept)

L03 Herringbone Line - Earth Weave Concept suggestion

Site: mid-park, Jing-Zhang heritage park

60-degree herringbone geometry as land art / paving narrative

Design rationale: Homage to Zhan Tianyou's engineering wisdom (cultural interpretation, not reconstruction)

Risk & licensing: Must be labeled: historic herringbone line is at Qinglongqiao; this is an homage

L04 Bell-Web Concept suggestion

Site: Dazhong Temple Bell Museum area

Digitized bell AI soundscape art + data-sensing installation

Design rationale: Dual context: ancient bell culture + AI industry cluster

Risk & licensing: Construction & acoustic environment per heritage/planning authorities

L05 Weave-Source Gate Concept suggestion

Site: north end of Zhongzhiyuan (near Qinghe)

City gateway & innovation display node facing Qinghe; future-milestone clock

Design rationale: Warp-line north closure, linking the Qinghe ecology

Risk & licensing: Waterfront & gateway location; approval pending (concept)
