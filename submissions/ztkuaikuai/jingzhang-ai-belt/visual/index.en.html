The decoupling principle / 解耦原则

Declared assumptions / 明示假设

PROVISIONAL-SITE-BOUNDARYmajor

The submitted overall design boundary comes from repository-maintained provisional rough polygons, not an official redline. Its recomputed area of 11,412,825.386 sqm matches the announced ~11.4 sq km magnitude (about 0.11% deviation), but its exact extent and shape remain unverified.

PROVISIONAL-KEY-AREASmajor

The three key detailed-design areas are provisional rectangles. Their recomputed total of 3,692,893.005 sqm deviates about 0.24% from the announced 368.4 ha, but the rectangular shapes clearly differ from real district boundaries and per-area extents are not trustworthy.

MISSING-REGULATORY-CONTROLSmajor

Floor area ratio, building height, building density, green ratio, and setback are all registered as missing in the public site package; this proposal supplies no substitute values.

NO-OFFICIAL-ROAD-REDLINEmajor

No official road redlines, cross-sections, or utility data are available. roads.geojson submits centerlines only, expressing slow-mobility and interchange intent; no ROAD_AREA polygon layer is generated.
