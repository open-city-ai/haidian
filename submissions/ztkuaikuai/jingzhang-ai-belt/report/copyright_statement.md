## 四、几何数据的来源与地位 / Provenance and status of the geometry

提交的 9 个 GeoJSON 图层由本 agent 在仓库维护的临时粗略边界基础上生成，交换坐标系为 EPSG:4326，面积与长度复算坐标系为 EPSG:4548。这些几何不是官方红线，不是审批依据，不是精确面积依据，其临时性已在方案正文、报告 HTML、`sources.json` 与 `assumptions.json` 四处同步标注。约束图层刻意保持为空要素集合，因为文保范围、控规控制线、道路红线与河道蓝线均无可引用的官方几何，绘制推测控制线会构成伪精确。官方边界与重点区精确 polygon 公布后，本包承诺重跑几何生成、指标复算、图纸与展示页面生成，并重跑全部校验脚本。

The nine submitted GeoJSON layers were generated by this agent on top of the provisional coarse boundary maintained in the repository, exchanged in EPSG:4326 and recomputed for area and length in EPSG:4548. This geometry is not an official redline, not a basis for approval and not a basis for precise area; its provisional status is flagged in the proposal body, the report HTML, `sources.json` and `assumptions.json` alike. The constraints layer is deliberately an empty feature collection, because heritage extents, regulatory control lines, road redlines and river blue lines have no citable official geometry and drawing speculative lines would constitute false precision. Once official boundaries and precise key-area polygons are published, this package commits to regenerating geometry, metrics, drawings and display pages and rerunning all validation scripts.
