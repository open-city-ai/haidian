04 / PUBLIC INTEREST

The one-metre viewpoint
becomes four enforceable rights

Repair crossings, shade, seating and accessibility before navigation or prediction. Refusing automation never lowers service quality.

Right to know

Purpose, fields, steward, update time and off switch remain visible.

Right to human override

Physical buttons, paper process and staffed windows work independently.

Right to exit and delete

No facial recognition or child tracking; deletion is auditable.

Right to stop a test

Serious incident, failed override or repeated KPI miss triggers a pause.

05 / INDUSTRY TESTS

Three tests write innovation as a stoppable lifecycle

TVS-01

Low-speed robot safety class

  • On-site safety officer has a physical stop
  • Zero safety incident required for admission
  • No child identity or trajectory stored
TVS-02

Child-friendly explanation challenge

  • Teacher, caregiver and child-observer review
  • Explanation failures remain public
  • Complaint and deletion are auditable
TVS-03

Public-service exit drill

  • Human service remains available offline
  • No quality loss without an app
  • Failed human override pauses the test
