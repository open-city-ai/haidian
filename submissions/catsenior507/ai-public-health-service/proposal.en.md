## AI + Public Services: Urban Design Centered on Healthcare System Intelligence

### Abstract

This proposal takes "AI + Public Services" as its core direction, focusing on **AI reshaping the healthcare system** — the most publicly valuable proposition — to build the world's first urban design paradigm centered on medical AI in the Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt. The proposal advances the core concept of "From Individual Experience to Collective Intelligence," establishing three technological pillars: **Medical Data Deep Training Infrastructure**, **Doctor Experience Distillation Platform**, and **AI Triage & Cross-Department Collaboration System**, to break through the bottleneck of healthcare's long-standing dependency on individual physician experience, transforming quality medical capability from a scarce resource into a replicable public good.

## Chapter 2: Vision — From Individual Experience to Collective Intelligence

### 2.1 Problem Diagnosis

Current healthcare systems face a structural contradiction: **quality diagnosis and treatment capability is highly dependent on individual physician experience**. Training an excellent doctor takes 15-20 years, and their experience is deposited in personal judgment, difficult to transfer at scale. This leads to:

1. **Resource inequality**: Top hospital expertise cannot be replicated to primary care at low cost
2. **Fragmented collaboration**: Cross-department consultation relies on manual coordination with significant information loss
3. **Experience attrition**: Physician retirement or departure means permanent loss of decades of clinical experience
4. **Blind patient navigation**: Patients lack effective triage information, making healthcare-seeking pathways highly random

AI provides the key to resolving this contradiction: **transforming tacit knowledge dispersed in individual physicians' minds into trainable, deployable, iterable explicit intelligent systems**.

### 2.2 Core Proposition

The core proposition of this proposal is: building a **Medical Collective Intelligence Infrastructure (MCII)** in the Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt, reshaping public healthcare through three technological pillars:

**Pillar One: Medical Data Deep Training Infrastructure**
Leveraging Haidian District's rich medical resources, establish a privacy-compliant federated learning network connecting medical institutions, enabling AI models to learn clinical decision patterns from massive de-identified medical records, imaging, pathology, and genomics data — without data leaving the hospital.

**Pillar Two: Doctor Experience Distillation Platform**
Beyond training on data, "distill" experts' tacit judgment logic. Through cognitive task analysis, clinical pathway modeling, and diagnostic decision tree comparison, transform senior physicians' diagnostic reasoning processes into interpretable AI models. This does not replace doctors but "compiles" their experience into knowledge modules callable by primary care physicians and AI assistants.

**Pillar Three: AI Triage & Cross-Department Collaboration System**
Based on the above training outcomes, build a patient-facing AI triage system and physician-facing cross-department collaboration platform. Patients describe symptoms in natural language; AI completes preliminary triage and plans optimal care pathways. When physicians face complex cases, AI automatically matches relevant specialty expertise and generates collaborative diagnosis and treatment recommendations.

These three pillars form a progressive "data → experience → service" transformation chain, whose spatial placement requires deep coupling with the Three Areas and Two Wings framework.
