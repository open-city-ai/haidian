Jing-Zhang First Station 京张·第一站

One hundred years ago the Jing-Zhang Railway departed from Qinghuayuan Station — the first station out of Beijing on the first trunk railway built by the Chinese people. Seventy-six years ago the CPC Central Committee stepped onto Beijing soil here — the first station of the new China's journey of governance. Today, the Centennial Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt is to become the "First Station" of a world-class AI innovation district: One Rail, Three Stations, Two Wings — where everyone and every agent entering the belt departs from here.
