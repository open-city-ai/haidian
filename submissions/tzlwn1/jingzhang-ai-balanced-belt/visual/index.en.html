Overview map

The heritage park acts as the public-space armature, giving one belt, three cores, located scenarios and a blue-green slow-mobility loop. The corridor runs about 9.7 km north-south and 1.3 km east-west, so the strategy is sectioned along the line and carried at the nodes, with drawings in three side-by-side sections.

Land-use partition

81 parcels close over the corridor with no gaps and no overlaps, verified by the repository spatial review. Bands run across and sections along; the park spine takes 16 per cent of the width and runs the whole length, and reserved land sits on the eastern edge where the data gap is largest. Floor area ratio, height and density stay pending.

Slow mobility and blue-green public space

One slow-mobility spine runs the whole length, with seven east-west connectors and two cycleways, thirteen line features in total. Spine-connector intersections are the stitching priorities and are carried as project JZ-01. The green ratio is about 21 per cent and the public-space ratio about 5 per cent, counted separately rather than merged.

Buildings and renewal projects

Thirteen indicative renewal clusters total roughly 13 hectares of footprint, all marked pending survey, representing neither existing buildings nor approved volume. Retain-renovate-demolish follows "no classification without survey": only the method is given, with no demolition conclusion for any building. Projects JZ-01 to JZ-06 each state lead, precondition, window, resources, approval interface, milestone, acceptance and exit.

AI scenarios

All ten scenarios carry coordinates and can be checked one by one against the public-space layer by scenario_id. Three industry test scenarios state the object under test, admission, safety evaluation, exit conditions and a proposed operator. Every scenario keeps a staffed kiosk, physical signage and a degradation path, collects no personal traces and substitutes for no planning approval.

  • A-CONTROLS-001 (pending_professional_confirmation): Detailed regulatory controls, road redlines, ownership, municipal utilities, and engineering constraints must be confirmed from official attachments before statutory use.
  • A-BOUNDARY-002 (pending_official_geometry): The corridor boundary and the three key-area outlines are provisional geometry generated by the participant from public inputs, not an official redline or a precise-area basis.
  • A-ROADS-003 (pending_professional_confirmation): Road boundary lines, cross-sections, under-bridge title and intersection forms have not been obtained.
  • A-RAIL-004 (pending_professional_confirmation): Rail station design and the extent of railway-heritage protection are unconfirmed.
  • A-OWNERSHIP-005 (pending_professional_confirmation): Parcel ownership, campus edges and ground-floor title are unconfirmed.
  • A-SURVEY-006 (pending_field_survey): No survey of existing buildings, structural safety or heritage value is available.
  • A-UTILITY-007 (pending_professional_confirmation): Utility pipelines, energy capacity, drainage, flood control and fire-safety conditions have not been obtained.
  • A-OPERATOR-008 (proposal_not_agreed): Proposed operating bodies, cross-regional cooperation interfaces and policy instruments have not been agreed by any organisation.
