# 京张夜班 / JINGZHANG NIGHT SHIFT

一条南北连续的安静夜行主脊与六处东西向光闸相交，连接受控夜测、近校转译和夜间公共服务三座夜班场。十二个接力节点均有责任角色、停止条件、恢复证据和非 AI 后备。光闸只转换照明、导视和运营状态，不是实体门禁或追踪设施。

One continuous north-south quiet spine intersects six east-west light gates and links controlled testing, near-campus translation and civic night service. Every relay node has an accountable role, stop condition, recovery evidence and non-AI fallback. A light gate changes lighting, wayfinding and operating state; it is never a barrier or tracking device.

全部几何为仓库临时约束，仅作概念设计、拓扑检查与复算。三阶段均覆盖全场地：先基线和纸面演练，再开放三个最小原型，最后只扩展证据达标的项目。

All geometry is repository-provisional and supports concept, topology and recalculation only. Every phase covers the full site: baseline and paper rehearsal, three minimum prototypes, then evidence-gated expansion.
