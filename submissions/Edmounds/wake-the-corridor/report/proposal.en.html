WAKE THE CORRIDOR: The Park Is Open — Now Wake the Buildings

On 6 August 2026, phase 2 of the Jingzhang Railway Heritage Park opened: a 9 km green corridor now runs continuously from Xizhimen to the North 5th Ring. The "line" is finished. This proposal answers the next question: how do the half-vacant buildings, the corners locked inside compound walls, and the grey space under the viaducts on both sides of that line wake up on schedule? WAKE THE CORRIDOR proposes no new landmark. It proposes a mechanism: turn Beijing's existing "underused-building base ledger" policy instrument into an open, matchable, reversible urban open-source system, so that every sleeping space can be seen, matched, and reversibly trialed — so the next Zhongkun Plaza does not have to wait fifteen years. This proposal is an open co-creation suggestion; it does not replace statutory planning and does not constitute a government-approved conclusion. All spatial suggestions are concept proposals, reference schemes, or material for professional deepening.

Design Basis and Source Inventory

The controlling basis is the official announcement's three scope levels, three key areas, and design tasks Source Standard; participation follows the agent-facing taskbook's ten co-creation principles, six required tasks, and boundary clause Source Standard. Professional baselines for urban design and regulatory-plan depth follow the MOHURD Urban Design Administrative Measures and the Regulatory Detailed Planning Measures Standard Standard; land-use expression uses the national territorial-space classification Standard.

What separates this proposal from a vision text is that its mechanism stands on policy instruments already in force: the Beijing Urban Renewal Regulation lists upgrading of old underused buildings and traditional commercial facilities as industrial-category renewal and allows lawful use conversion of existing buildings Source; the trial Implementation Measures for Renewing Old Underused Buildings already require a base ledger and a project library, with owners able to self-register based on vacancy and tenant demand Source; the companion technical guideline was issued in March 2024 Source. The proposal invents no new statutory tool. It only asks: what happens when that ledger becomes open, AI-readable, and matched in real time to the belt's industrial demand?

Source usability follows data/source_registry.json Source; the processed fact pack is a navigation layer, not a new authority Source. Newly registered public sources include municipal and district renewal policies, Zhongguancun Science City AI factor policies, official-media reports on the park completion and the Zhongkun Plaza regeneration, and seven domestic and international mechanism cases — each recorded with publisher, URL, retrieval date, and use limits; media-reported figures are handled strictly as reported values, never as audited statistics. The submitted boundary and the three key areas come from the repository's provisional rough polygons (provisional constraints) and cannot support official redlines, approval bases, or precise-area conclusions Spatial data Source.

The diagnosis rests on three verifiable facts. First, industry: official media report 2,000+ AI enterprises, 130 registered LLMs, and an RMB 350B+ core AI industry in Haidian, with 70%+ of the district's AI enterprises and 80%+ of its talent concentrated along the belt; the same report gives a figure of "nearly 10 million sqm of industrial space, with about 1,000,000 sqm renewable" Source Metric. Second, space: that million square metres has no public, parcel-level, searchable ledger — policy demands the ledger, but the book is closed to innovators. Third, the sample: Zhongkun Plaza in Dazhongsi took roughly fifteen years of case-by-case negotiation from its 2008 opening through prolonged vacancy to its 2023 rebirth as tech offices plus the open "Dazhongsi 1733" district Source Source; meanwhile Shanghai's Mosu Space reportedly converted 13,000 sqm of existing shell space into move-in-ready incubation space in 38 days Source. The gap between fifteen years and 38 days is not construction capability — it is the information and institutional interface. That is exactly the problem an AI innovation belt should let AI help solve.

Three-Level Scope Framework

The three scope levels carry three ledger functions. The coordinated research area (~43.6 km²) is the rules layer: general rules for registering, matching, trialing, and exiting underused space, linked to the citywide regulation and building measures, exporting a reusable ledger data standard to Beiwei, Future Science City, Huairou Science City, the E-Town, and Beijing-Tianjin-Hebei Source Depth. The overall design area (~11.4 km²) is the ledger layer: a complete concept zoning of the corridor with slow-mobility stitching and interface design; the submitted provisional boundary recomputes to about 11.41 million sqm Metric Spatial data. The three key areas (~368.4 ha combined) are the pilot layer: each validates one wake mode and produces a replicable playbook Spatial data Metric.

The provisional-boundary limits must be stated plainly: the current SITE_BOUNDARY and three KEY_AREA polygons are rough substitutes calibrated by the repository maintainer from announcement text and approximate areas in EPSG:4548. They are provisional constraints for concept generation, display, and self-check only Source. Once official polygons are published, all nine geometry layers, every area metric, all five figures, both drawing sets, and both HTML deliverables must be re-cut and recomputed; triggers and recomputation paths are recorded in the assumptions file, and no area in this text may be read as precise.

The spatial grammar is "one ledger, one line, three yards, many points". One ledger: an open inventory of underused space across the design area, covering four asset classes — whole or partial underused buildings, compound and campus leftover corners, under-viaduct grey space along the park, and redundant basement or plant-floor space. One line: the completed 9 km heritage park is no longer a design object but the ledger's public interface — awakened spaces are displayed, accessed, and commemorated there Source. Three yards: Dazhongsi validates "whole-building wake", the AI Origin Community validates "courtyard wake", Zhongzhiyuan validates "quick-fit wake" Depth. Many points: eight under-bridge and pocket "micro wake points" Metric Spatial data.

Coordinated Research Area: Industry and Future-City Research

Naming system and visual identity (agent.1). The belt keeps its official name; the action brand is "京张醒楼 / WAKE THE CORRIDOR". The naming logic: Zhan Tianyou did not build a new city on empty land — he opened a line through existing terrain; Haidian's tradition has always been innovation inside existing fabric — Zhongguancun grew out of a street, 768 grew out of an old factory. "Wake" translates urban renewal into a verb everyone understands. The system extends downward: the inventory is the Wake Ledger, pilot spaces are Wake Rooms, corridor nodes are Wake Points, the annual festival is Wake Week, and the commemoration system is the Wake Milestones. The logo direction is an original geometry: one warm-lit cell in a 3×3 window grid, extensible into a progress bar (awakened area / ledger area), map pins, and building plaques; the palette is Signal Green (go), Rust Orange (Jingzhang industrial heritage), and Night Blue (still sleeping). All marks and colors are original to this proposal; no unlicensed fonts, trademarks, or corporate identities are used Source Standard.

Closing the loop across the three positionings, five functions, and three-areas-two-wings. The wake mechanism threads the three positionings into one circuit: the Centennial Jingzhang culture belt supplies the ledger's cover and index (heritage narrative and public interface); the urban AI life-experience belt supplies its readers (the public experiencing AI city life inside awakened spaces); the AI innovation belt supplies its writers (enterprises and developers posting demand into the matching queue). Among the five functions, full-stack self-reliant innovation and a world-class AI ecosystem depend on affordable, fast-entry physical space — the Wake Ledger is the supply-side operating system; AI+ scenario empowerment, the intelligent vibrant city, and global AI governance voice are delivered through the scenario cards, the wake points, and the open-ledger governance mechanism respectively Depth. The division of labor: the three key areas are test yards for the three wake modes; the Zhongguancun service wing provides legal, appraisal, capital, and registration services (the ledger's professional-verification bench); the Xiaoyuehe scenario wing provides controlled testing and public feedback (the scenario-acceptance bench).

Future urban form. A city fit for AI-driven productivity is not taller buildings but shorter waits: the time from "we need a pilot workshop" to "keys in hand", and from "the building emptied" to "a new program moved in". The Wake Ledger makes these two durations the belt's core competitive metrics. This is the proposal's concrete answer on territorial-planning innovation: extend planning's object from static land-use indicators to the temporal state of space (sleeping / diagnosed / trialing / awake), giving stock-era planning an operable middle layer Standard Depth.

Overall Design Area: Urban Renewal and Regulatory-Depth Urban Design

Renewal framework. Renewal objects follow the ledger's four asset classes; the pathway is one six-state loop: register → diagnose → match → reversible trial → confirm or exit → debrief into the ledger. Owners register voluntarily (self-registration is already provided for Source); professionals run safety and suitability diagnosis per the technical guideline Source; AI works only in the matching state — retrieving and ranking space attributes (clear height, loading, tenure status, available time windows) against demand attributes (pilot-line height, server-room conditions, affordable rent bands) into suggestion lists; every trial is reversible — light retrofit, time- and program-limited, with explicit exit clauses; successful trials are confirmed into formal renewal procedures, failures return to the ledger with lessons recorded. Every state has a human decision point; AI never crosses tenure, safety, or approval judgments Depth.

Concept land-use zoning. land_use.geojson partitions the provisional boundary seamlessly into 18 concept cells — no overlaps, no gaps — using national land-use codes as concept roles Spatial data Standard Depth: the central corridor band is park green throughout (indicative alignment of the built heritage park); the west band runs, south to north, Dazhongsi commercial, residential repair, Zhichun research, Origin-Community research, Qinghua-East education, and Zhongzhiyuan research; the east band holds residential infill, the university belt, Wudaokou commercial, and a reserved white cell. Zoning is a concept overlay, not a statutory land-use adjustment: commercial ~0.98M sqm, research ~2.26M sqm, education ~2.43M sqm, residential ~2.08M sqm, green ~2.66M sqm, reserved ~1.00M sqm — all recomputed from geometry in EPSG:4548 Metric Metric.

Intensity and character controls. Floor-area ratio, height, density, green ratio, and setbacks are uniformly marked pending official data until regulatory controls are published, with reasons and recomputation paths in the metrics file Metric Depth. Only three character principles: awakened buildings do not alter the skyline (whole-building wake prioritizes facade repair and internal reprogramming); both park edges keep park-scaled friendly interfaces (open ground floors, sun-facing terraces); industrial relics (gantry crane, rails, tank cars) remain as character vocabulary, continuing the approach already proven by park phase 2 Source.

Transport, rail, and municipal concepts are detailed in their own chapter; total building floor area is marked pending due to the missing public stock inventory Metric. Project list, policy, and phasing follow in their chapters — all as concept suggestions.

Key-Area Detailed Design

Each key area validates one wake mode as a complete mini-scheme of "positioning + spatial structure + building renewal + mobility + public space + AI scenarios + implementation risk". All three polygons are provisional; every conclusion below is directional Spatial data Depth.

Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster (~72 ha) — WHOLE-BUILDING WAKE. Positioning: whole-building regeneration showcase for AI-native consumption and business. It holds the belt's most mature local evidence: Zhongkun Plaza's full path from 70% vacancy to tech offices plus an open district Source, and Dazhongsi 1733's low-threshold, high-sociability open-district operation Source. The structure uses Line-13 Dazhongsi station and the park's "3rd-Ring gateway terrace" as twin entrances; the concept prototype (whole-building wake demonstration building Spatial data) walks the six-state loop through one building: register the underused building → structural and fire diagnosis → match AI-native programs (AI experience retail, robot-restaurant pilots, model-product launch halls) → floor-by-floor time-limited trials → confirm mature programs. The public-space anchor is the "Lights-On Terrace" honor wall — one lamp lit per awakened space Spatial data. Risk: fragmented strata ownership and debt recomposition are complex; the multi-party platform precedent applies, and this proposal draws no conclusions on tenure disposal.

Beijing AI Origin Community (~104.3 ha) — COURTYARD WAKE. Positioning: open regeneration of campus-compound corners and ground-floor interfaces. Existing evidence on Xueyuan Road: the Petroleum Compound's "six spaces, one station" path that cleared idle sheds into ~8,000 sqm of shared civic space across a multi-owner compound Source, and 768 Park's preserve-first factory regeneration Source. The structure leaves compound fabrics untouched and does three things only: register wall-edge corners along the corridor into the ledger; open ground floors part-time to the public and startup teams (concept prototype: courtyard-wake shared ground floor Spatial data); form a developer plaza at Wudaokou station as the community living room Spatial data. AI scenarios focus on students and young researchers: open research-topic boards, cross-campus compute study rooms, mentored scenario problem banks. Risk: multi-owner coordination is slow; the proven responsible-planner co-governance mechanism applies, one compound at a time, with no uniform deadline.

Zhongzhiyuan AI Acceleration Area (~192.1 ha) — QUICK-FIT WAKE. Positioning: pilot and verification carrier for full-stack self-reliant innovation, benchmarked against Mosu Space's reported 38-day shell-to-move-in speed Source. The structure pairs the reserved white cell with research cells: the concept "quick-fit pilot shed" (high bay, heavy loading, leasable whole or by time slot Spatial data) hosts controlled testing for embodied AI, robotics, and edge hardware; the "open test yard" makes controlled scenarios publicly visible Spatial data. Factor interfaces plug directly into Zhongguancun Science City instruments — compute subsidies, scenario funds, model vouchers as demand signals Source. Risk: pilots face safety and environmental thresholds; every test scenario runs "apply–control–review–exit" and never enters public roads or airspace without permits.

AI Ecosystem, Personas, and AI+ Scenarios

Six personas (agent.3). ① Startup founders: teams of three to five whose worst pain is affordable, fast-entry office and pilot space; the ledger's first value is cutting space search from months to days. ② Building owners / asset managers: holding half-vacant assets, lacking credible programs and channels; registering earns diagnosis services and matching-queue priority. ③ Students and young researchers: needing third places beyond compound walls and real problems to work on; courtyard-wake ground floors and wake points are their home turf. ④ Residents along the line (including the elderly): 450,000 residents just gained a 9 km park Source; awakened ground floors should restore convenience services, day care, and places to sit; every smart service keeps a staffed counter and traditional alternatives in parallel Standard. ⑤ Renewal administrators and responsible planners: needing a live, parcel-level renewal workbench — the ledger is that workbench. ⑥ Embodied-AI engineers: needing test space with explicit clear heights, loading, and power redundancy; the quick-fit zone standardizes their admission.

Land Use, Building Scale, and Retain-Renovate-Demolish

Land-use layout is as described above: 18 concept cells fully cover the provisional boundary, with class areas recomputable from geometry Spatial data Metric. At building level, the package submits six wake prototypes with a combined footprint of about 15,000 sqm Metric Metric: the whole-building demonstration building, the Space Depot ledger hall, the courtyard-wake shared ground floor, the Wudaokou AI-native format prototype, the quick-fit pilot shed, and the wake station. They are placeholders for spatial reasoning; each records a low-confidence concept floor quantity in its attributes, but none represents an existing building, approved massing, or any retain-renovate-demolish conclusion.

On retain-renovate-demolish, the position is a method, not a list: without public building inventories, tenure, and structural records, parcel-level conclusions would be both impossible and irresponsible. The wake mechanism itself is the decision procedure: the six-state loop requires registration, diagnosis, and reversible trial first; only when retention and reprogramming are proven unworkable — after structural appraisal and public-interest comparison — may professional teams discuss demolition. This aligns with the regulation's stance of strictly controlling large-scale demolition Source Depth. FAR, height, and density remain pending official data, with recomputation paths recorded Metric Metric.

Transport, Rail, Municipal, and Public Services

The mobility concept is organized around reaching awakened spaces. The skeleton is one slow spine and seven east-west stitch links: the spine follows the built park's three-lane slow system indicatively for about 9.6 km Metric Spatial data; the seven links address existing breakpoints at Dazhongsi, the 3rd-Ring terrace, Dayuncun, Chengfu Road, Qinghua East Road, Shuangqing Road, and Jianting Bridge, extending phase 2's logic of reopening nine side streets — concept centerlines, not road redlines Source Metric Depth.

Rail interfaces prioritize station-and-building stock renewal: Dazhongsi's metro-integrated sunken plaza already proves stations and underused buildings can renew together Source, and the regulation explicitly promotes integrated station-area stock renewal Source; ledger entries near Line-13 stations receive a "station-city renewal" tag boost in matching. Municipal and new infrastructure add no new sprawl: edge-compute nodes go into the existing plant rooms and power redundancy of awakened buildings first; under-bridge wake points use low-power solar-plus-storage kits; all municipal capacity and energy-load conclusions await professional calculation — this proposal draws no engineering conclusions Depth. Public services are topped up through the wake-station prototype, with staffed counters alongside smart services.

Blue-Green Space, Public Space, and Urban Character

The blue-green attitude is relay, not redraw: the 9 km park and its three-lane system are built, and connections to the Qinghe greenway and Dongsheng Bajia country park are open Source. The green layer registers the corridor as six concept segments (~2.66M sqm, green ratio ~23.3% Metric Spatial data); design effort concentrates on the interface: awakened ground floors along the corridor must open toward the park, courtyard wake stitches compound greens to the park, and under-bridge wake points turn the corridor's shadow sections into roofed public living rooms Metric Depth.

Three AI pilgrimage landmarks (agent.4) — all regenerated from stock, no new towers. First, the Space Depot: a ledger hall concept anchored at the welded-rail factory's gantry-crane node — beneath the giant crane, a live wall of the city's space ledger, where developers worldwide come to "read the city's books"; it shifts the pilgrimage object from a building to the mechanism itself Spatial data. Second, the Lights-On Terrace: an honor lamp wall on the Dazhongsi 3rd-Ring gateway terrace — one lamp per awakened space, engraved with the space's name and its contributors (AI agents credited by name), forming the belt's honor display system. Third, the Wake Milestones: kilometre markers along the 9 km spine reusing railway milestone form, each pointing to the nearest awakened space and recording its wake day, doubling as the wayfinding skeleton. All three share a component library — rail steel sections, signal-light vocabulary, ledger data faces — restrained, maintainable, never gimmicky Standard.

The character keynote is "rust and signal": keep the rust tones and structural logic of industrial relics, light awakened spaces in signal green; building massing respects the existing skyline, and landmarks earn recognition through public accessibility, not height Depth.
