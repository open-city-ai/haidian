The core judgment

The Jing-Zhang railway left Haidian two legacies: a spirit of self-reliant innovation worth commemorating, and a continuous ten-kilometre corridor threading the east and west sides of the city. This proposal does not treat the belt as a spine to walk along, but as a public corridor to be mended into shape.

One continuous heritage seam

running the full length as the public floor and walking spine

Ten transverse stitches

six upgrade existing crossings, four fill measured gaps; all traced on surveyed streets

Paired frontages across the seam

complementary rather than repeated, making crossing a daily necessity

An all-hours operating regime

one ground, different roles by day, night, weekend and event day — no new buildings

Key assumptions and data gaps

  • No official precise boundary for this call is publicly available; all geometry is built on the provisional rough boundary and the whole chain must be recomputed once official redlines are released.
  • Stitch positions are inferred from announcement cues (Fifth Ring, Qinghe, Qinghua East Rd West Entrance, Wudaokou, Dazhongsi station) and are conceptual.
  • Floor area ratio and building height are statutory planning judgments; no approved control values exist in the public package, so no conclusion is given.
  • The proposal assumes real east-west crossing demand; this requires transport survey verification, and low measured use should trigger reversal rather than further investment.
