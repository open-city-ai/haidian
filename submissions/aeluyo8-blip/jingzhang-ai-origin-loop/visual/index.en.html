JINGZHANG AI ORIGIN LOOP · 京张AI原点回环

An innovation loop that leaves its origin and returns. One belt (Heritage Park vitality belt), one loop (Origin Loop slow-mobility green circuit), three cores (Zhongzhiyuan · Origin Community · Dazhongsi) and two wings (Zhongguancun Technology Service Wing · Xiaoyue River Scenario Empowerment Wing) organise an 11.4 km² overall design and three key-area detailed designs, so that innovation returns to its origin — through validation, transfer, scenario and service — and settles into public knowledge. Authoritative data remain in GeoJSON, metrics.json, matrices and self-check results; this page is the offline human-readable display.

Key-Area Detailed Design

The three key areas map to key_areas.geojson (provisional); functions, public space, transport and AI scenarios are conceptual proposals.

key_areas.geojson

Zhongzhiyuan · Validation Core

Garden-style full-stack independent innovation and standards-governance district. A riverside low-carbon living room on the Qinghe frontage, with R&D blocks and testing centres forming a "test—standard—display" validation loop.

  • Model red-team testing ground
  • Safety-governance showcase
  • Low-carbon compute experience

Origin Community · Creation Core

Campus-adjacent technology transfer and talent community. The Origin Plaza is the community living room, stitching campus, park and blocks, complemented by release, open-source and talent services.

  • Origin Light · open-source reflux station
  • Tsinghua Garden Station Time Gallery
  • AI education laboratory

Dazhongsi · Transfer Core

Urban smart economy and international exchange district. Transit-oriented around the station forecourt with four-quadrant pedestrian links; smart business and content consumption form the transfer loop.

  • International roadshow loop living room
  • Data-element compliance salon
  • AI+health-tech businesses

AI Scenario Cards (12)

Scenarios map to spatial nodes/layers and follow data minimisation, public sources, explainability and human review; 02/03/11 are industry test/validation scenarios.

01 Origin Light · open-source reflux station

Origin Plaza: public model contribution wall and releases that settle contributions into reusable public knowledge.

02 Model red-team testing ground

Zhongzhiyuan testing centre: viewable validation node for standards, safety evaluation and red-team testing (industry test).

03 Edge-compute station

Overall-area node: new-infrastructure prototype combined with public services and low-carbon energy (industry test).

04 AI slow-mobility navigation

Heritage Park slow spine: explainable wayfinding and low-intrusion sensing for gaps and accessibility needs.

05 International roadshow loop living room

Dazhongsi forecourt: showcase and exchange for agents, smart terminals and content-consumption businesses.

06 Qinghe low-carbon innovation corridor

Zhongzhiyuan waterfront: green space, stormwater, walking/cycling and AI showcase as a public living room.

07 Campus-adjacent transfer street

Origin incubators: incubation, legal, IP and investment services for university results.

08 Data-element compliance salon

Dazhongsi area: city service interface for data elements with compliance, authorisation and auditability.

09 AI+health living street

Around medical land: AI healthcare, home care and chronic-disease management at operable block scale.

10 AI education laboratory

Origin education land: AI literacy, experience and evaluation for universities and the public.

11 Loop race · low-speed autonomous shuttle

Origin Loop: low-speed testing and public experience of autonomous shuttles and delivery robots (industry test).

12 Global AI Week loop route

Belt public-space system: a walkable experience route from heritage culture, open source, industry showcase to international roadshows.
