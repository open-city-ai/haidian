Why is AI native here?

Physical AI loads, test boundaries and live data chains are the planning object; embodied-control robustness is the test object. Optional event attribution is deleted unless it beats equal-budget non-AI baselines on held-out events.

What remains unknown?

On-site vibration baseline, real user, title, road redlines, rail/heritage controls, utilities and fire conditions remain unavailable. These gaps are gates from L0 to L1, not footnotes.
