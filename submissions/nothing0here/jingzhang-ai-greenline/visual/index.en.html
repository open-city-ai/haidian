1. Overview Map

The AI Green Nerve Loop uses the Jing-Zhang Railway Heritage Park as its historic spine, connects the three key areas north-south, stitches campus, park and community east-west, and distributes twelve AI scenario nodes along the greenway and rail stations.

Boundaries are provisional constraints for generation, display and intake self-checks only; all layers and metrics must be recalculated when official polygons are published.

2. Three-Level Scope Framework

The Coordinated Research Area (c. 43.6 km²) answers industry-ecosystem and future-city-form questions; the Overall Design Area (c. 11.4 km²) delivers urban renewal at regulatory-plan depth; the Key-Area Detailed Design Area (c. 368.4 ha) details the three key areas.

Three Zones and Two Wings collaboration loop

University origination → AI Origin Community incubation → Zhongzhiyuan full-stack acceleration and governance → Dazhongsi AI-native applications and consumption → Zhongguancun wing factor enablement → Xiaoyue River wing public experience → global communication and governance feedback.
