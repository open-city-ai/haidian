In 1909 Zhan Tianyou crossed Badaling with the ren-shaped switchback — climbing by reversal, exactly how AI learns by iteration. RENLINE translates that switchback wisdom into a spatial prototype: one spine, two tracks, three switchbacks; seven east-west stitches; ten K-mark waypoints. One stroke of ren is the heritage track of the centennial railway, the other the future track of urban AI life; their junction is human-centered AI fusion innovation.

Blue-green and public-space system

The ~160 m green spine runs the full belt (1.537M m²) with two pocket parks adding community green; the promenade is composite with the spine, and five K-mark plazas (Bell K1 / Zhichun Switchback K4 / Origin K5 / Xueyuan Stitching K7 / Summit K9) anchor the nodes — 0.60M m² of public space in total. Links to the Qinghe and Xiaoyue rivers are conceptual directions. The vitality belt takes youth-friendliness as its first yardstick: night economy, the AI Marathon, affordable desks, K-mark science waypoints, talent apartments and late-night canteens close the youth loop; accessible and non-smartphone paths designed in parallel.
