# Jing-Zhang Interlocking Belt · THE INTERLOCKING BELT: Turning the Century-Old Jing-Zhang Railway's Interlocking Safety Mechanism into an Open Governance Protocol for AI Cities

## Design Basis and Source Register

This proposal is organized under the "Centennial Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt International Urban Design Solicitation — Pre-qualification Announcement" issued by the Haidian Branch of the Beijing Municipal Commission of Planning and Natural Resources, using the provisional rough boundaries, key areas, enums, metrics, and source register in `brief/site-package/` as machine-readable basis [source:OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT] [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK]. Every design judgment is decomposed into traceable sources, recomputable metrics, verifiable layers, and human-reviewable assumptions; deliverables at regulatory-detailed-planning urban design depth and comprehensive implementation plan depth are carried by GeoJSON, metric tables, A3 booklets, A0 boards, and HTML displays, not replaced by narrative.

Source-use boundaries follow `data/source_registry.json` [source:SOURCE-REGISTRY]: 7 formal-usable sources, 1 background source, 1 provisional-only source; agents must not upgrade background_only or provisional_only material into official boundaries, statutory regulatory plans, formal scoring basis, or government implementation commitments. `data/processed/agent_fact_pack.md` is a reading-navigation layer, not a new authority [source:PROCESSED-FACT-PACK].

The organizer has not yet published official `SITE_BOUNDARY` and `KEY_AREA` polygons; this package temporarily uses `provisional_boundaries.geojson` [source:BOUNDARY-SOURCE] [source:KEY-AREA-SOURCE]. In this package `geometry/site_boundary.geojson` and `geometry/key_areas.geojson` are marked `provisional_constraint`, `official_boundary=false`, for proposal generation, self-check, visualization, and design discussion only — not as official redlines, approval basis, precise-area basis, or statutory control conclusions. Once official polygons are released, boundary, land use, buildings, roads, green space, public space, phasing, and metrics must be recomputed. The provisional boundary is used only for proposal generation, presentation, and concept review; the full package will be recomputed once official polygons are released. Eligibility, scoring, and acceptance are determined by maintainers/reviewers per the official rules.

The overall concept is the **"Jing-Zhang Interlocking Belt · THE INTERLOCKING BELT"**: taking the century-old railway **interlocking** safety philosophy — switch and signal interlocked, conflicting combinations blocked, block sections keeping safe spacing, and unified dispatch — as the prototype (the Jing-Zhang Railway, opened in 1909, enforced the discipline of "no confirmation, no movement" through manual point operation, signal display, and dispatch coordination; this proposal inherits that safety philosophy, not claiming that electric interlocking machines were installed in 1909), and translating it into an **open governance protocol for AI cities**: data access has switches, scenario state has signals, conflict detection has an interlocking table, public and personal data have block-section isolation, and every anomaly is reviewed by human dispatchers. The concept responds to the three positionings (Centennial Jing-Zhang Cultural Belt, Urban AI Life Experience Belt, AI-Integrated Innovation Belt) and five functions (full-stack indigenous AI innovation, world-class AI innovation ecosystem, new paradigm of AI+ scenario enablement, intelligent AI vitality city, and global discourse power in AI governance), turning "safety" from a railway-engineering heritage into a public good of AI-city governance; the protocol specification is openly adoptable as machine-readable JSON (`visual/assets/interlocking-protocol.json`, CC-BY-4.0, while the proposal package as a whole remains COMMUNITY-DISPLAY-ONLY) [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK] [standard:PROJECT-OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT] [standard:PROJECT-AGENT-OPEN-CALL-TASKBOOK].

## Three-Level Scope Working Framework

The proposal follows the announcement's three levels: the overall research scope (43.6 km²) answers AI industry ecosystem, strategic positioning, innovation chain, and future urban form; the overall design scope (11.4 km²) delivers urban regeneration framework, industrial spatial layout, transport-utility support, and urban character control; the key-area scope (368.4 ha) deepens function, building scale, retain-renovate-demolish classification, public-space connectivity, and transport organization for three detailed-design areas. All three levels are mapped in `compliance_matrix.json` to announcement 1.3/1.4/1.5 and agent.1–agent.6 [depth:three_level_scope_framework] [depth:overall_spatial_structure].

**Spatial structure: one spine, three stations, two wings, multiple nodes.** The "spine" is the Jing-Zhang Heritage Park vitality belt, the main line of the whole belt: heritage, slow mobility, blue-green, and AI experience unfold along it, mirroring the railway main line and signal trunk. The "three stations" are the three key areas — Zhongzhiyuan (north segment, full-stack innovation station), Beijing AI Origin Community (middle segment, open-source conversion station), Dazhongsi (south segment, intelligent-native new-business station) — mirroring interlocking railway stations: each has switches (access control), signals (state visibility), an interlocking table (conflict rules), and a dispatch desk (human review). The "two wings" are the Zhongguancun Technology Service Wing (west, global factor allocation, IP and capital enablement) and the Xiaoyuehe Scenario Empowerment Wing (east, AI scenario enablement and vitality city), mirroring branch lines and marshalling yards. The "multiple nodes" are AI scenario nodes, service stations, and pilgrimage landmarks distributed across spine, stations, and wings [data:geometry/site_boundary.geojson#SITE-001] [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-001] [depth:overall_spatial_structure].

## Overall Research Scope: Industry and Future City

The core task of the overall research scope is a world-class AI innovation ecosystem. The proposal organizes factor access along the innovation chain through the interlocking protocol: **switches = data and scenario access control** (who may access, routed where, authorization state visible), **blocks = factor safety spacing** (public, personal, and enterprise data in separated zones), **interlocking table = conflict rule base** (conflict combinations among scenarios and data, space and ownership, events and safety pre-registered; conflict blocks action), **dispatch = human final judgment** (blocks, alarms, and authorization changes enter human review). This protocol translates the railway engineering ethic of "safety first, conflict blocks action" into an open standard for AI-city governance, providing an operable spatial and rule carrier for global discourse power in AI governance [standard:PROJECT-AGENT-OPEN-CALL-TASKBOOK] [depth:overall_spatial_structure].

Future-city research answers how AI changes work, life, socializing, learning, mobility, and public services: AI mobility systems, continuous green space, innovation-service facilities, and an international living-working atmosphere are anchored into locatable functional zones, nodes, corridors, and scenarios. Naming and logo serve the overall recognition of the "Centennial Jing-Zhang Cultural Belt, Urban AI Life Experience Belt, AI-Integrated Innovation Belt": primary name **京张联锁带**, English **THE INTERLOCKING BELT**; visual identity uses the "switch—signal—lock" motif — three rail lines converge at a lock-shaped node where a signal lamp lights up, conveying "controlled access, conflict blocks action, safety is visible"; palette uses Jing-Zhang railway rust red (heritage), Zhongguancun innovation blue (technology), and AI teal-green (ecology); all fonts and graphics are self-drawn with no third-party copyright dependency [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK] [depth:brand_identity_direction].

Global AI events, developer communities, open scenarios, and pilgrimage routes are expressed as "conceptual proposals / reference schemes / available for professional teams to deepen", not as confirmed government events or implementation arrangements [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK].

**Scenario Interlock Fulfillment Matrix**: each scenario card maps to a spatial carrier, interlocking rule, test gate, responsible role, stop condition, and evidence reference; the machine-readable version is `scenario_interlock_matrix.json` (complementary to `compliance_matrix.json` task mapping — a scenario-layer refinement of it). Test gates are honestly graded in four levels: `desk_review` (completed in-package desktop review) / `simulation` (not run; requires real site, data, or authorization) / `pilot` (not authorized; requires official site authorization and an operator) / `full_operation` (not authorized; requires formal operation permits). All scenario cards are **target designs: not deployed, not authorized, not run**; the 3 ★ industry test scenarios (SC-02/SC-08/SC-10) are all `simulation` — no pilot is claimed to have run. Typical interlocking rules: SC-02 red-team tests that fail safety evaluation are blocked; SC-10 turns red when the test/operation boundary fails or no safety dispatcher is on duty; SC-08 pauses circulation interfaces when data grading is unclear or the authorization chain breaks. Cross-scenario conflict combinations are pre-registered in the interlocking table (e.g., SC-10 vs school commuting hours forces red; SC-02 vs SC-04 time-separated block sections); conflict blocks action, and any state change requires human review at the dispatch desk with records [data:scenario_interlock_matrix.json#SC-01] [data:visual/assets/interlocking-protocol.json#ILP-CONFLICT-03] [data:visual/assets/interlocking-protocol.json#ILP-DISPATCH-01].

**Interlocking Protocol — open adoption**: the protocol specification is published as machine-readable JSON (`visual/assets/interlocking-protocol.json`) under CC-BY-4.0 for open adoption (attribution required; the submission package as a whole remains COMMUNITY-DISPLAY-ONLY, and this license covers the protocol specification text only). The protocol is layer-complementary to the community governance template (Switchback Protocol, chucky1102, CC-BY-4.0): Switchback governs the lifecycle state machine of a single scenario card (vertical); this protocol governs conflict interlocking across scenarios (horizontal); the two may be combined. The mechanism genealogy acknowledges community proposals including the Switchback Protocol, JINGZHANG SWITCH, and The AI Line — see the `acknowledgments` field in the protocol JSON.

**Dispatcher experience and the Interlocking Diary**: the Interlocking Belt answers "who is responsible" through an experience. JZ-05 Interlocking-Protocol Public Education Platform hosts a "Be a Dispatcher for a Day" simulator — visitors act as dispatchers at the interlocking table, practicing clear / review / block decisions on conflict combinations (test∩slow-mobility, test∩school hours), with every operation recorded; the platform also exhibits the "Interlocking Diary": the work discipline of 1909 Jing-Zhang Railway pointmen and signalmen as the opening chapter ("no confirmation, no movement"), set against the same discipline of an AI-scenario dispatcher in 2026, using two generations of "dispatchers" to explain why human review is needed in the interlocking protocol. The experience is a conceptual suggestion; specific locations and equipment await official boundaries and operator confirmation.

AI-governance recommendations follow data minimization, public sources, explainability, and human review: city agents assist in identifying slow-mobility gaps, public-space heat, facility maintenance, enterprise-service demand, and event-safety risks, but do not replace planning approval, output unauthorized personal profiles, or claim official implementation commitments.
