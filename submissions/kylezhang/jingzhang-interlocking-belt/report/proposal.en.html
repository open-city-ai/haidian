Overall Research Scope: Industry and Future City

The core task of the overall research scope is a world-class AI innovation ecosystem. The proposal organizes factor access along the innovation chain through the interlocking protocol: switches = data and scenario access control (who may access, routed where, authorization state visible), blocks = factor safety spacing (public, personal, and enterprise data in separated zones), interlocking table = conflict rule base (conflict combinations among scenarios and data, space and ownership, events and safety pre-registered; conflict blocks action), dispatch = human final judgment (blocks, alarms, and authorization changes enter human review). This protocol translates the railway engineering ethic of "safety first, conflict blocks action" into an open standard for AI-city governance, providing an operable spatial and rule carrier for global discourse power in AI governance 标准 深度.

Future-city research answers how AI changes work, life, socializing, learning, mobility, and public services: AI mobility systems, continuous green space, innovation-service facilities, and an international living-working atmosphere are anchored into locatable functional zones, nodes, corridors, and scenarios. Naming and logo serve the overall recognition of the "Centennial Jing-Zhang Cultural Belt, Urban AI Life Experience Belt, AI-Integrated Innovation Belt": primary name 京张联锁带, English THE INTERLOCKING BELT; visual identity uses the "switch—signal—lock" motif — three rail lines converge at a lock-shaped node where a signal lamp lights up, conveying "controlled access, conflict blocks action, safety is visible"; palette uses Jing-Zhang railway rust red (heritage), Zhongguancun innovation blue (technology), and AI teal-green (ecology); all fonts and graphics are self-drawn with no third-party copyright dependency 来源 深度.
