# 京张工具房 JINGZHANG TOOLROOM：从工程标尺到回声钟庭的公共 AI 城市链

**中文阅读导引**：本方案把 1905–1909 京张铁路的工程理性、2019–2020 智能高铁的系统协同，以及大钟寺“声音被听见、意义被重述”的公共回声，转译为一条概念性城市能力线：**01 众智园工程标尺（先检验）→ 02 北京 AI 原点社区学习站台（先转译）→ 03 大钟寺回声钟庭（先归还）→ 回到 01 的继续、修改或暂停**。

空间不是确定建筑，而是可进入的公共接口：可拆检验台、连续遮棚、纸面说明、人工接管、可停留工作台、反馈回执和可退出的试点。每个 AI 场景都需写明责任角色、服务对象、最小数据、人工兜底、成功指标与停止条件。

**English guide**: The proposal translates engineering discipline, system coordination and civic echo into a conceptual route: **01 Engineering Ruler (test) → 02 Learning Platform (translate) → 03 Echo Court (return) → continue, revise or pause at 01**. It proposes public interfaces rather than fixed construction: test benches, covered edges, paper guidance, human handover, open worktables, response receipts and reversible pilots.

All geometry, spatial relations and scenes are conceptual / provisional and are not for statutory planning, engineering, ownership, heritage-repair or approval use.
