# Jing-Zhang AI Force Field · An Intelligent Urban Innovation Belt

## I. Overview

The Jing-Zhang AI Force Field is an AI-driven urban innovation belt along the century-old Jing-Zhang Railway heritage corridor. Core metaphor: "force field" — AI permeates urban space like gravity.

## II. Spatial Structure

~11.4 km², three key areas: Zhongzhiyuan (~192.1 ha), AI Origin Community (~104.3 ha), Dazhongsi (~72.0 ha).
