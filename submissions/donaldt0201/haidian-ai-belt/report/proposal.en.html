1.1 Overall Concept: "Jing-Zhang Intelligent Pulse - Three Leaps"

This proposal presents "Jing-Zhang Intelligent Pulse Belt" (JZ-IPB) as the overall concept. Both share the same geographical corridor, forming a historical depth "from steam to computing, from rails to neural networks."
