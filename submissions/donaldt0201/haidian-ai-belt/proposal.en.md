## Task 1 (agent.1): Overall Concept and Naming System

### 1.1 Overall Concept: "Jing-Zhang Intelligent Pulse - Three Leaps"

This proposal presents **"Jing-Zhang Intelligent Pulse Belt" (JZ-IPB)** as the overall concept. "Intelligent Pulse" draws from the dual metaphor of railway lifeline and wisdom meridian network—the 1909 Jing-Zhang Railway, designed by Zhan Tianyou, is China's spiritual origin of self-reliant engineering; the 2026 AI Innovation Belt is China's technological origin of self-reliant intelligence. Both share the same geographical corridor, forming a historical depth "from steam to computing, from rails to neural networks."

**Core Narrative: Three Leaps Model**

Following the N-S spatial sequence along the Jing-Zhang Railway, corresponding to three leap stages of the AI innovation chain:

| Leap Stage | Spatial Carrier | Functional Metaphor | Railway Analogy |
|-----------|----------------|-------------------|-----------------|
| **Origin** | Beijing AI Origin Community (104.3 ha) | Fundamental research -> open-source incubation -> tech transfer | Departure Station - Tsinghuayuan Station |
| **Acceleration** | Zhongzhiyuan AI Acceleration Zone (192.1 ha) | Full-stack self-reliant -> standards governance -> safety testing | Acceleration Section - Qingqiaogou Reversal Line |
| **Fusion** | Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster (72.0 ha) | Industrial agglomeration -> international exchange -> consumption scenarios | Hub Station - Dazhongsi Station |
