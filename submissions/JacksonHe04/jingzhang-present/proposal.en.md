# JINGZHANG, PRESENT / 京张在场

> A future city needs more than systems online. It needs everyone present.

**Status.** An open co-creation proposal by OpenAI Codex (GPT-5) and JacksonHe04. All spatial moves are conceptual references for professional development. They do not replace statutory planning or constitute an approved government decision. [source:OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT] [assumption:A-BOUNDARY-001]

## Thesis

Most AI district proposals ask what a system can do. JINGZHANG, PRESENT asks who must be present, who has authority, and how the city continues when automation fails. "Present" is a roll call, a physical condition, an accountable present tense, and a possible inscription for everyone whose verified public contribution endures.

The proposal delivers one north-south Presence Line, four east-west interfaces, three Presence Grounds and two enabling wings. Zhongzhiyuan becomes **technology present**, the AI Origin Community becomes **knowledge present**, and Dazhongsi becomes **everyday life present**. The Zhongguancun wing makes enabling resources present; the Xiaoyuehe wing makes public experience present.

## The Presence Contract

Every urban AI scenario must publish eight fields: public purpose; beneficiaries; allowed and prohibited data; AI role; human approval, refusal and stop authority; no-account/no-device/no-AI equivalent service; failure and exit path; operator, appeal channel, metrics and review date. A scenario without traceable sources, a named accountable operator, a human fallback or a stop condition cannot enter a real-city pilot. [source:OECD-AI-PRINCIPLES] [source:UNESCO-AI-ETHICS]

## Fourteen scenarios, nine publics

Four industrial validation scenarios test model boundaries, embodied navigation, edge-compute transparency and data/domain co-production. Ten public scenarios cover accessibility, multilingual public service, community learning, health navigation, education, last-100-metre logistics, responsible retail, culture, legal information and night operations. Medical and legal systems explain information and routes only; they do not diagnose or issue definitive legal advice. Workers are not optimisation parameters: rest, appeal and freedom from hidden performance monitoring are part of the contract. [source:ILO-GENAI-WORK] [metric:scenario_count]

## Public space and landmarks

The four landmarks are the **Presence Hall Zero**, where a system must defend its limits before city testing; the **Visible City Backend**, where maintenance, energy and handovers become legible; the **Register of Presence**, a versioned wall for developers, residents, workers, experts and maintainers; and the **Next-Century Coordinate**, which adds only one publicly reviewed problem and one verified advance each year.

Eight open components translate governance into space: Presence Table, Human Help Window, Fixed Truth Panel, Rest and Repair Bay, Accessible Route Kit, Open Test Edge, Quiet Commons and Version Marker.

## Ninety-day pilot

Days 0-15 co-write three contracts with the nine publics. Days 16-45 run AI and non-AI services side by side. Days 46-75 conduct cross-review by professionals, user representatives and independent safety/ethics observers. Days 76-90 decide Go, Hold or Stop. Go only continues within the tested boundary; scale-up requires a new review. [data:geometry/phasing.geojson#PHASE-01-01]

## Evidence and limits

The submitted study geometry measures about 11.41 km² and remains provisional. Land use is a non-overlapping conceptual partition; buildings are typologies, not demolition instructions; connections are intentions, not engineered alignments. FAR, height, ownership, infrastructure capacity and implementation approvals remain unknown pending official data. [metric:site_area_sqm] [assumption:A-CONTROLS-002]

JINGZHANG, PRESENT does not promise a city run automatically by AI. It proposes a city where no system can make responsibility disappear, leave a person without help, or erase failure. A century ago the railway connected places. The next century should reconnect technology with common life.
