Design logic

The land-use partition is generated from the provisional boundary by polygonizing 11 organic blocks (13–63 vertices), following curved corridors and oblique street grids to mimic real urban fabric instead of a mechanical grid.

  • Green main axis first: curved vitality belt, 136K m² north gateway wedge
  • Industry along the axis: research 29.4% leading, commerce toward south core
  • Education corridor preserved: Xueyuan Rd university belt
  • Residential embedded in green: 5-min walk to the park belt

Concept zoning, not statutory regulatory plan; FAR, height, density and setback await official data.
