# Jing-Zhang AI Talent Living Lab Belt

This proposal reframes “attracting AI talent” from a one-time investment or image project into an urban service capability that can be tested in everyday life. People state real needs; systems recommend only within explicit rules; space and operations respond flexibly; service quality remains traceable; the public can comment, correct, or exit; and the next iteration changes accordingly. The six-stage loop is: **demand sensing → controlled recommendation → flexible service → quality traceability → public feedback → continuous iteration**.

The spatial framework combines three 15-minute innovation living circles, the Jing-Zhang Public Innovation Axis, and two wings. Zhongzhiyuan is responsible for service prototyping and quality validation; Beijing AI Origin Community is responsible for near-campus talent life and public feedback; Dazhongsi is responsible for enterprise services and scaled-adoption review. The Zhongguancun Technology Service Wing provides access to public policy, intellectual-property, compliance, and professional services. The Xiaoyuehe Scenario Empowerment Wing provides public-space, ecological, accessibility, and everyday-experience feedback. Every spatial move is a **conceptual suggestion, reference scheme, or item for professional teams to deepen**. It is not formal planning, government approval, or an implementation commitment. [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK] [standard:PROJECT-AGENT-OPEN-CALL-TASKBOOK]

## Coordinated Research Area: Industry and Future City Research

### Three positions, five functions, and three areas with two wings

The proposal responds simultaneously to the Centennial Jing-Zhang Cultural Belt, Metropolitan AI Living Experience Belt, and AI Convergence Innovation Belt. The cultural belt uses railway history, explainable interpretation, and an honor system to establish historical depth. The living-experience belt uses opt-out daily services, 15-minute circles, and public feedback to improve long-term talent experience. The convergence belt links prototypes, validation, enterprise service interfaces, and scaled-adoption review. [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK]

The five functions become locatable mechanisms. The full-stack autonomous innovation system is concentrated in Zhongzhiyuan's validation circle. The world-class innovation ecosystem is supported by AI Origin Community and the two wings. AI+ scenario innovation is expressed through 12 nodes. The intelligent and vibrant AI city is carried by the public axis and everyday routes. Global AI governance discourse is addressed through safety, interoperability, traceability, human review, and public rules. [depth:overall_spatial_structure]

The three areas are not interchangeable exhibition districts. Zhongzhiyuan produces documented service prototypes. AI Origin Community tests whether they are useful, fair, explainable, and voluntary in daily life. Dazhongsi reviews enterprise interfaces, operator accountability, and public acceptance before any larger conceptual adoption. The Zhongguancun wing does not name partners; it provides a public route to policy, compliance, intellectual-property, and professional services. The Xiaoyuehe wing does not profile individuals; it tests public-space, ecological, accessibility, and aggregated-experience outcomes.

The circles form differentiated 15-minute units. The wings connect technology services and public experience. The nodes are bookable, voluntary, traceable service and validation interfaces. ## Detailed Design of Key Areas

![Distinct validation roles of the three key areas](assets/figures/key-areas.en.png)

### Zhongzhiyuan AI Acceleration Area: service prototype and quality validation circle

This area translates full-stack innovation, safety governance, and standards work into interfaces that industry can audit and the public can understand. [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-001] The reference structure is “R&D workshop—safety and interoperability sandbox—open quality-trace courtyard—public display interface.” SCN-01 through SCN-03 examine minimum demand intake, rule-bound recommendation, quality records, and accountable operators. [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#SCN-01] [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#SCN-02] [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#SCN-03]

Building action is limited to a suggestion to survey potentially adaptable existing space. No building is selected and no retain-renovate-demolish conclusion is made. Mobility diagrams express access, walking, cycling, and public-display relationships only. Official polygons, ownership, buildings, fire safety, traffic, ecology, and security review are prerequisites. [depth:three_key_area_detailed_design]

### Beijing AI Origin Community: near-campus talent living and public feedback circle

This circle enables students, developers, startups, residents, and international visitors to try services voluntarily in everyday life while seeing recommendation rules, accountable operators, feedback routes, and exit options. [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-002] The reference structure is “near-campus collaboration hub—talent daily-needs station—voluntary trial and exit desk—public feedback forum—open-source contribution ribbon.” SCN-04 through SCN-07 use public directories, authorized event information, and voluntary aggregated feedback, not commercial personal profiles. [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#SCN-04] [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#SCN-07]

Public space supports quiet study, collaborative release, community services, exercise, and events through differentiated time and sound rules. Campus data, research outputs, health, consumption, or personal movement traces are not default inputs. Every digital entrance requires a staffed and non-digital alternative. [depth:blue_green_public_space]

### Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster: enterprise service and scaled adoption circle

This circle translates validated prototypes into modules that can interface with enterprises, urban business, and public consumption while retaining operational review and the public right to exit. [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-003] The reference structure is “station-access concept link—enterprise service desk—flexible-service urban hall—scaled-adoption review desk—contribution display.” SCN-08 through SCN-10 do not name companies, partners, or suppliers, and make no investment, attraction, revenue, or government commitment. [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#SCN-08] [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#SCN-10]

Four-quadrant pedestrian links, station integration, underground space, and traffic organization remain problem statements for rail, traffic, utilities, fire safety, accessibility, and ownership professionals. AI-native consumption is defined by transparent recommendations, quality traceability, replaceable services, and resolvable disputes rather than technological spectacle.

Municipal and digital infrastructure follow “shared, measurable, stoppable, accountable.” Edge computing, sensing, charging, stormwater, lighting, and information devices should use existing systems and reversible equipment where feasible. Generated content is labeled; historical and institutional narratives receive human review. The overall identity and cultural signage remain distinct from company marks and event graphics.

## Risk, Copyright, and Compliance

Risk boundaries are recorded through the pending-control envelope and the missing-data depth item. [data:geometry/constraints.geojson#CONSTRAINTS-001] [depth:risk_missing_data] [source:SITE-PACKAGE] The feature records the absence of official control geometry; it is not a control line or approval boundary.

1. **Geometry**: SITE_BOUNDARY and all three KEY_AREA polygons are provisional rough geometry. Official polygons require full-package recalculation.
2. **Professional conditions**: regulatory, ownership, building, municipal, traffic, heritage, fire-safety, and engineering inputs are missing; related conclusions remain conceptual.
3. **Data and AI**: no personal profile is required by default. Demand sensing uses voluntary, aggregated, purpose-limited inputs. Recommendations are explainable, correctable, and rejectable, with staffed alternatives.
4. **Operations**: services, events, attraction, policy, funding, and construction have no confirmed commitment. Each pilot needs an accountable operator and separate approval.
5. **Copyright**: figures are generated from the submission's structured data and self-drawn geometry. No remote images, map tiles, company marks, portraits, or unauthorized fonts are used.
6. **Public boundary**: only the service-loop business model and design principles are public. Partner lists, internal forecasts, budgets, formulas, product lists, equipment parameters, and other non-public details are excluded.

Chinese and English text, figures, HTML, and PDFs follow the same section order, metrics, evidence, and risk boundaries. Human maintainers and professional teams make final judgments. This proposal does not claim approval, selection, or implementation.
