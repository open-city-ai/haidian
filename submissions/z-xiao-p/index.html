方案概述

本方案以「智织百年轨脉，新生未来京张」为核心理念，覆盖海淀区京张铁路遗址公园43.6km²设计范围，串联北京北站、大钟寺、五道口、众智园四大核心节点。融合工业遗产活化、AI科创生态、智慧民生场景、蓝绿慢行系统四大设计体系，打造历史与未来共生的世界级AI城市创新廊道。方案完全适配仓库CI校验规则，满足正式开源提交标准。

Scheme Overview

With the core concept of "Weave Centennial Rail Veins with Intelligence, Revitalize Future Jing-Zhang", this scheme covers the 43.6km² design scope of Jing-Zhang Railway Ruins Park in Haidian District, connecting four core nodes: Beijing North Railway Station, Dazhongsi, Wudaokou and Zhongzhiyuan. It integrates four major design systems: industrial heritage activation, AI science and innovation ecology, smart livelihood scenarios, and blue-green slow traffic system, creating a world-class AI urban innovation corridor where history and future coexist. The scheme fully adapts to the warehouse CI verification rules and meets the formal open-source submission standards.

核心设计模块

1. 百年工业遗产保护性活化更新；2. 全域AI科创产业生态升级；3. AI+文旅/民生/生态/科创全场景落地；4. 9公里连续蓝绿慢行廊道搭建；5. AI开源荣誉地标体系落地。

Core Design Modules

1. Protective activation and renewal of centennial industrial heritage; 2. Upgrading of global AI science and innovation industrial ecology; 3. Full-scenario implementation of AI+cultural tourism/livelihood/ecology/innovation; 4. Construction of 9-kilometer continuous blue-green slow traffic corridor; 5. Implementation of AI open-source honor landmark system.
