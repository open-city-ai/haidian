# Jing-Zhang Civic Intelligence Spine

**English Name: Jing-Zhang Civic Intelligence Spine; Abbreviation: JZ-CIS.** "Co-Wisdom" is not about letting AI take over the city, but about organizing models, data, space, and human judgment into a public capability that can be openly discussed, human-reviewed, and exited. "Spine" refers not only to the north-south historical axis formed by the century-old Jing-Zhang Railway, but also to an open protocol for the circulation of knowledge, talent, scenarios, and governance along the line. This outcome is an Open Co-Creation initiative that does not replace formal planning nor constitute a government approval conclusion. (Human Review)

Consequently, this plan divides the spatial expression into three categories: publicly disclosed factual tasks, recalculable conceptual design layers, and statutory or engineering conditions that require confirmation by professional teams.

The land use adopts a six-segment conceptual zoning and fully covers the Provisional Boundary: research and development validation, ecological interaction, open learning, talent living, intelligent native business, and flexible pending verification. The eight Building Footprints only express the prototype of the spatial carrier; the height, number of floors, total floor area, specific demolition-renovate-retain strategy, and ownership remain unknown. ## Detailed Design of Key Areas

![Three differentiated spatial areas and operational leverage points](assets/figures/key-areas.png)

### Zhongzhiyuan AI Independent Innovation Acceleration Area - Trustworthy Evaluation Garden

Conceptual Recommendation for a garden-type innovation district focused on full-stack R&D, standard validation, security governance, and international technology exchange. The spatial structure is defined as "R&D Island - Evaluation Corridor - Qinghe Gate Corridor"; architectural suggestions prioritize adaptation to existing R&D spaces and provide shared testing ground floors. A slow-moving trans-corridor connects public green spaces with the park's entrance and exit. AI scenarios include model safety evaluation, edge device sandbox, and public governance exhibition corridors. All are conceptual recommendations that require further development after the Official Boundary, traffic, and cultural heritage conditions are finalized. [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#KEY-001]

### Beijing AI Origin Community - Open Source Transformation Living Room

Located as a campus-type conversion for technology transfer, talent learning, and developer communities. The spatial structure is defined as "Open Source Release Hall - Shared Workshop - Talent Living Ring"; it is recommended to open the first floor, courtyards, and pedestrian interfaces in a low-impact manner to form visible connections between the campus, park, and community; any renovation of university or proprietary spaces must be confirmed by the rights holder and a professional team. [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#KEY-002]

### Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster - Intelligent Body Marketplace Living Room

Positioned as a test bed for intelligent bodies, smart terminals, content consumption, and international business. Propose a reference scheme for continuous walking, non-motorized vehicle parking, and a public ground floor around the quadrants of the rail station; "Marketplace" emphasizes removable displays, service experiences, and small-scale scenario validation, without claiming that the station-city integration project has been approved. [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#KEY-003] [depth:three_key_area_detailed_design]

The common rules for the three key areas are: Public Space before brand installations, genuine needs before technological demonstrations, minimal data before full data collection, and Human Review before automatic decisions.

## Blue-Green Network, Public Space, and Urban Character

The green space concept area for the Jing-Zhang Co-Intelligence Axis is approximately 275.3 hectares, accounting for 24.1% of the Provisional Boundary; the area of four public living rooms, after deduplication, is about 20.9 hectares, accounting for 1.8%. These are calculated from the concept layer, not the approved green space ratio. [metric:green_space_area_sqm] [metric:green_ratio] [metric:public_space_area_sqm] [metric:public_space_ratio]

Urban Character adopts three principles: "railway structure's restraint, innovative spaces' openness, and public interfaces' warmth." These principles preserve linear memories and industrial scale cues; new interventions use reversible elements, clear structures, and low-glare media; the ground floor prioritizes shading, seating, drinking fountains, accessibility, and rain gardens. Professional guidance is based on people-oriented design, historical continuity, and the harmonious coordination of Public Spaces and building massing, rather than flashy technological facades. [standard:MOHURD-URBAN-DESIGN-MEASURES] [depth:blue_green_public_space]

Three AI pilgrimage/honor nodes are cultural public prototypes that can be further developed by professional teams: 1) The "Trusted AI Pledge Portico" in Qinghe showcases principles of explainability and safety; 2) The "Open Source Annular Ring" at the Origin Point records public contributions rather than personal veneration; 3) The "Fail Museum Platform" at Dazhongsi displays corrected model errors and governance learning. The honor system only uses contribution IDs, project licenses, and revocable authorizations, without displaying unauthorized images or trademarks.
