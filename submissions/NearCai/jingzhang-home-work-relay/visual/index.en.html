Missing evidence and triggers
  • Approved regulatory controls, road redlines, ownership, municipal utilities and engineering conditions are not available in the public site package.
  • The unchanged repository provisional SITE_BOUNDARY is a rough geometry inferred from announcement text and an approximate 11.4 sq km area.
  • The three unchanged KEY_AREA polygons are rough locational proxies and are not official detailed-design boundaries.
  • Existing housing stock, unit counts, rents, affordability, tenure and occupancy data are not available.
  • Official protection boundaries and construction-control zones for Qinghuayuan Station and Jing-Zhang railway heritage are not registered in the site package.
  • AI does not decide eligibility, rent or housing allocation and does not score individuals or households; every service requires human review, a non-AI path and an exit mechanism.
  • The three phases express implementation order, not committed dates, budgets, ownership agreements or construction approvals.
  • The proposed 90-day sequence is a test protocol, not an authorized schedule; no operator, site permission, participant cohort, procurement, insurance, budget or field result is confirmed.
  • The three industry-validation protocols define proposed baselines, instruments, acceptance and stop logic but contain no observed field measurements or certified conclusions.
  • Links to northern communities, Future Science City, Huairou Science City, Beijing E-Town and the Jing-Jin-Ji network describe exchange objects and decision boundaries only.
  • Brand stewardship, service levels, annual Relay Week and international communication checks are proposed governance assets without an appointed steward or recurring budget.
