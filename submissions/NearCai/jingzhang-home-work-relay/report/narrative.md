# 行止有居——京张居业接力

## 可转换之家与居业短链

“行止有居”回应京张铁路百年流动史中的一个当代问题：当研究者、创业团队、青年家庭、国际开发者、服务与维护劳动者，以及老年人和残障使用者更换项目、团队或人生阶段时，如何仍能留在熟悉的住房、工位与公共服务网络中。方案不把“人才吸引”理解为一次性抵达，而把居住稳定、工作转换与日常照护组织成可以连续接续的城市能力。

空间概念为“一线、三站、四居、六接力”。一条居业接力公共主线串联众智园项目研居站、北京AI原点家庭转换站与大钟寺抵达换乘站；四种居住状态覆盖抵达短住、项目租住、家庭及无障碍可变居住、社区长期居住，但不与四个建筑基底原型一一对应；六项接力连接租期、户型、工位、服务、私宅同意和离开/回流。十二个场景卡把这些关系落实到从抵达解释、人工核验、租期与户型转换，到慢行、照护、维修、断网降级、数据撤回和回流交接的完整旅程。

AI 只承担解释、匹配、预约、提醒、聚合反馈和流程衔接，不决定资格、租金或住房分配，不对个人和家庭评分。所有服务保留人工复核、无 AI 路径、私宅数据撤回和离线降级。三项产业验证聚焦可逆内装、住研邻接的噪声与安全、以及智能家居“门内默认禁入”；三处地标“第一把钥匙”“居业里程表”“回程票档案”把编组、换挂、直通的铁路文化转译为可参与的公共记忆。年度“居业接力周”与全年开发者社区、场景开放、地标维护、公共体验路线和双语传播共同形成长期运营体系。

本包原样继承仓库 provisional `SITE_BOUNDARY` 与三处 `KEY_AREA`，仅用于概念生成、可视化和 intake 自检。Issue #846 与 #1029 指出的区位疑问仍未解决，因此所有设计图层均标记为 `design_proposal`，不声称官方红线、控规条件、道路工程或拆改留结论。仅 GeoJSON 可复算面积、绿地/公共空间比例、建筑基底、节点、场景与分期面积为 known；租金、住房存量、单元数、可负担性、真实通勤、服务容量、FAR、高度和留驻率保持 unknown，等待官方资料与专业团队深化。

# A HOME IN MOTION AND AT REST - THE JING-ZHANG HOME-WORK RELAY

## Convertible Homes and Short Links Between Living, Work and Services

“A Home in Motion and at Rest” reframes the Jing-Zhang Railway's century-long history of movement as a present-day urban question: when researchers, startup teams, young families, international developers, service and maintenance workers, older people, and disabled people change projects, teams or life stages, how can they remain connected to familiar homes, workplaces and public services? The proposal treats talent attraction not as a single arrival but as a continuous urban capacity that joins residential stability, work transitions and everyday care.

The spatial concept is “one line, three stations, four housing states and six relays.” A public Home-Work Relay line links the Zhongzhiyuan Project Live-Work Station, the Beijing AI Origin Family Transition Station and the Dazhongsi Arrival Transfer Station. Four housing states cover arrival stays, project-linked rentals, adaptable family or accessible living, and long-term community living, but they do not map one-to-one to the four submitted building-footprint prototypes. Six relays connect tenure, layout, workplace, services, consent inside the home, and departure or return. Twelve scenario cards follow the full journey from arrival explanation, human verification and tenure or layout transition to walking links, care, maintenance, offline fallback, data withdrawal and return handover.

AI is limited to explanation, matching, booking, reminders, aggregated feedback and process handoffs. It does not determine eligibility, rent or housing allocation, and it does not score individuals or households. Every service retains human review, a non-AI route, withdrawal of in-home data and offline degradation. Three industry validation settings address reversible interiors, noise and safety at live-research adjacencies, and “no entry by default inside the door” for smart-home systems. The First Key, Home-Work Mileage Board and Return Ticket Archive translate coupling, switching and through-running into participatory public memory. The annual Home-Work Relay Week joins year-round developer-community, open-scenario, landmark, public-route and bilingual communication operations.

The package inherits the repository's provisional `SITE_BOUNDARY` and three `KEY_AREA` geometries unchanged, solely for concept generation, visualisation and intake checks. The locational questions in Issues #846 and #1029 remain unresolved, so every design layer is marked `design_proposal` and makes no claim to official redlines, statutory controls, road engineering or definitive retain-renovate-demolish decisions. Only areas recomputed from GeoJSON, green and public-space ratios, building footprints, nodes, scenarios and phase areas are known. Rent, housing stock, unit counts, affordability, observed travel time, service capacity, FAR, height and retention remain unknown pending official data and professional development.
