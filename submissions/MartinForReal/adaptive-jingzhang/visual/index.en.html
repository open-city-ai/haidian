Metrics and evidence

Model stability is not public legitimacy

The 64 fixed-seed runs show how candidate topologies respond to disclosed proxy weights. Persistence, detour, and single-edge-loss results support comparison inside this conceptual model only; they do not prove demand, capacity, constructability, safety, or social consent.

Zero-jitter comparison: rerunning the same 64 seeds with the per-edge jitter set to zero changes the status of 8 of the 24 candidate edges, with a largest frequency change of 0.546875; persistent edges rise from 6 to 11, the disagreement band falls to 0 edges, and the persistence graph stays connected. The disagreement band is a product of this rule set under jitter, not an observed dispute in the city, and the jitter is a declared perturbation rather than measurement error. This record is computational transparency material, not a design conclusion. It cannot show that any corridor ought to be built.

The denominators and the rounding rule are stated here rather than left implicit. The only ratio denominator on this page is the provisional SITE-001 area recalculated from the submitted geometry, 11,412,825.386 m², that is 1,141.3 ha; the official announcement publishes an overall design area of 11,400,000 m², that is 11.4 km². The two differ by 12,825.386 m², which is 1.28 ha, or 0.1125% of the official value. That difference is a bookkeeping difference between a provisional working polygon and an official text area, not a measured boundary difference. Every percentage is rounded ROUND_HALF_UP to two decimal places and every hectare display to one decimal place. Both results for the same numerator are written out: the reference green space of 199.5 ha gives 17.48% provisional and 17.50% official, a difference of +0.02 percentage points; the park green and open space land use, code 1401 at 320.4 ha, gives 28.07% provisional and 28.11% official, a difference of +0.03 percentage points. Every published figure uses the provisional denominator, and the two bases are never mixed.
