Land use

One shared-edge partition; several future choices

The reference partition covers the submitted provisional polygon without gaps or overlaps. It tests relationships among production, research, public service, mixed use, green space, and public space; it is not a statutory land-use plan.

Mobility

Read persistence as a question, not a route approval

Persistent edges are candidates for professional study, disputed edges become explicit design questions, and pruned edges remain in the audit history. Human review must compare access, safety, heritage, cost, operations, and local experience before any pilot.

Blue-green public space

One network, four public duties

Move and include

Use continuous, legible, step-free public routes as a design test, while keeping non-digital navigation available.

Shade and manage rain

Test shade, cooling, and stormwater functions together; confirm hydrology and planting with qualified professionals.

Hold memory and habitat

Make heritage interpretation, biodiversity, and everyday use visible without turning proxy scores into official protection claims.

Maintain and fail safely

Name a maintainer, budget for repair, record complaints, and keep a recovery path when a pilot underperforms.

AI scenarios

Ten scenario cards; one governance contract

Every scenario identifies its place type, minimum data, human review, non-digital fallback, accountable operator, and stop rule. The cards are conceptual service specifications, not authorizations to deploy.

S01

Accessible mobility

Stations, crossings, and public routes; accessibility data; trained human review; a signed-route fallback; a named mobility operator; and a stop rule for unsafe or exclusionary guidance.

S02

Jing-Zhang heritage interpretation

Heritage places; verified archives; curatorial review; printed interpretation; a named heritage operator; and a stop rule for unverified or harmful claims.

S03

Public-service navigation

Civic-service places; current service directories; staff review; counter and telephone fallbacks; a named service operator; and a stop rule when information is stale.

S04

Health-service wayfinding and referral

Health-service places; approved directories only; qualified human review; telephone and desk fallbacks; a named health operator; and a stop rule for clinical or safety risk.

S05

Legal and compliance orientation

Service desks; current public rules; qualified human review; printed and in-person fallbacks; a named compliance operator; and a stop rule before the system is mistaken for legal advice.

S06

Enterprise-service copilot

Business-service places; approved program records; staff review; counter and telephone fallbacks; a named enterprise operator; and a stop rule for confidentiality or eligibility errors.

S07

Low-speed delivery and repair

Controlled test routes; mapped hazards; on-site supervision; manual delivery fallback; a named fleet operator; and a stop rule for conflict, access, or maintenance failure.

S08

Heatwave shade operations

Heat-exposed public spaces; verified weather and shade inputs; staff review; physical signage and staffed refuges; a named public-realm operator; and a stop rule for sensor or coverage failure.

S09

Event-day flow support

Event routes and thresholds; approved event plans; command-post review; steward and signage fallbacks; a named event operator; and a stop rule for crowding or conflicting instructions.

S10

Appeals and model retirement

Every deployed service; decision and complaint logs; independent human review; in-person appeal channels; a named accountable institution; and a binding stop, rollback, or retirement rule.

Non-tradeable gates

Safety; rights; heritage; affordability; accessibility; privacy; human review. Each gate is an independent veto: a favorable weighted score cannot override it.

Review gates

Four checks mean reviewable—never approved

1. Deterministic records

Required files, identifiers, hashes, and declared relationships are machine-checkable.

2. Spatial structure

Submitted geometries can be parsed, measured, and checked for the declared topological relationships.

3. Offline visual review

The proposal can be read without remote scripts, fonts, tiles, trackers, forms, or network requests.

4. Evidence discipline

Official, provisional, conceptual, proxy, and missing evidence remain visibly separate.

Passing these checks does not mean government approval, professional certification, engineering feasibility, confirmed investment, or guaranteed implementation. The recorded state is reviewability only.

Assumptions and limits

Missing evidence does not remove constraints

  • The working-area and key-area polygons are provisional; they are not official boundaries and do not constitute government approval.
  • Nodes and candidate edges are normalized conceptual proxies.
  • Equity, climate, heritage, and maintenance inputs are policy proxies, not observed data.
  • Official floor-area ratio, height, ownership, parcel-level heritage, flood, utility, and origin-destination data remain unavailable.
  • Every spatial placement remains a conceptual recommendation or reference scheme for professional teams to develop further.
  • No element asserts approved planning, engineering feasibility, confirmed investment, or government endorsement.
