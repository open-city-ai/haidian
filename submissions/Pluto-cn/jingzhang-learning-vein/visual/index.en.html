JINGZHANG LEARNING VEIN: From Rail to Learning Rail

The centennial Jing-Zhang railway is re-read as a "Learning Rail": the 9-km Jing-Zhang Heritage Park becomes a wall-less university; Zhongzhiyuan, the AI Origin Community and Dazhongsi host learning-by-making, learning-by-asking and learning-by-using; the Zhongguancun and Xiaoyue River wings organize an industry academy and a life academy.
