# 一页摘要 / One-Page Summary

## 京张先行 GREEN:LIGHT

无人配送、自动驾驶接驳、巡检与导览机器人、移动诊室、移动教室、康复辅具——这些AI场景卡在同一个瓶颈上：**没有一条街愿意、也没准备好让它们上路**，只能在封闭园区里跑演示。

京张铁路留下一条南北9.72公里、贯穿三处重点区域的连续绿廊，是这片城市里唯一一条能从干线一路通到社区门口的连续通道。本方案把它做成一条让AI场景**合法、低速、可监管、可退出**上街的城市通道，并把上街需要的七套设施与规则一次做齐：分级路权、速度与体量约束、补能与后场、感知与监测、归因与复核、分级开放与退出、末端界面。

核心动作是**按断面把主线分成A/B/C三级路权**：A级5.30公里全时物理分道、B级3.31公里高峰降级至边侧、C级1.11公里高峰禁行。**分时路权只是第四道防线**——前三道是物理分离、速度限制与让行公约，第五道是密度超阈值自动清空、任何人可急停、事件可归因、不达标即回滚。分期不是施工顺序，是风险分级开放顺序，每期挂量化门槛与退回条件。

三个AI朝圣地标各解决一种「看不见」：零号先行点让运行结果可见（含失败次数），路权钟让规则可见，后场环让设施与劳动可见。

52项指标从几何复算，5项官方控制指标如实标注为待补齐、不作推定。方案主动报告一个短板：先行点300米步行只覆盖56.0%的居住用地，说明必须向外侧街区加密。全部空间建议为概念建议，可供专业团队深化，不构成审定结论。

## GREEN:LIGHT — Jing-Zhang Goes First

Autonomous delivery, low-speed shuttles, inspection and guide robots, mobile clinics, mobile classrooms, rehabilitation aids — all stuck at one bottleneck: **no street is willing, or ready, to let them out**, so they only ever run demonstrations inside closed campuses.

The Jing-Zhang Railway left a continuous 9.72 km green corridor running through all three key areas, the only continuous channel here that reaches from a trunk line to residential doors. This proposal turns it into an urban corridor where AI scenarios can take to the street **legally, slowly, under supervision and with a way out**, assembling in one move the seven sets of facilities and rules that street entry requires: graded right-of-way, speed and size limits, charging and back-of-house, sensing and monitoring, attribution and review, graded opening and exit, and last-metre interfaces.

The core move is **grading the trunk into A/B/C right-of-way by cross-section**: 5.30 km of Grade A with full-time physical separation, 3.31 km of Grade B with peak-hour demotion to the edge, and 1.11 km of Grade C barred at peak. **Time slicing is only the fourth line of defence** — the first three are physical separation, speed limits and the yielding covenant; the fifth is automatic clearing above density thresholds, a stop button anyone can press, attributable events, and rollback when gates fail. Phasing is not a construction sequence but a risk-graded opening sequence, each phase carrying quantified gates and rollback conditions.

Three AI pilgrimage landmarks each address one kind of invisibility: Point Zero makes operating results visible, including failure counts; the Right-of-Way Clock makes rules visible; the Service Ring makes facilities and labour visible.

Fifty-two metrics are recomputed from geometry, and five official control indicators are reported as pending rather than assumed. The proposal reports one shortcoming on its own initiative: to-door points within a 300 m walk cover only 56.0% of residential land, which means they must be densified outward into the blocks. All spatial suggestions are conceptual proposals for professional teams to develop further, and constitute no approved conclusion.
