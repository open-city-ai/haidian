01 / Protocol

Make urban space the safety boundary of AI

OGP-12 builds neither a district-wide personal profile nor automated public-space admission. Each gate performs one authorized public task and binds minimum data, human authority, fallback, start and stop rules, and an exit asset to a real spatial kit.
