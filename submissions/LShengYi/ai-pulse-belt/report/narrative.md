## Narrative (English)

**AI Pulse Belt** carries the century-old "iron pulse" of the Jing-Zhang Railway as memory and linear spatial skeleton into an AI-era "digital pulse belt." The north-south central Pulse-Belt greenway forms the belt; Zhongzhizui, the Beijing AI Origin Community, and Dazhongsi form the three cores; the Xiaoyue River enabling wing and the Zhongguancun technology-service wing form the two wings — an "one belt, three cores, two wings, multiple nodes" structure. Land use is research-led with commercial, cultural, educational, and residential support: 155 concept parcels seamlessly cover the overall design area (measured 11.413 km2, 0.11% deviation from the official 11.4 km2); 84 concept buildings, 21 green areas (25.0% green ratio), 6 plazas, and 16 AI scenario nodes (status-light wayfinding, industrial test, and health-service guidance) form a verifiable spatial skeleton. Twelve scenario cards (eight-element structure), three industrial test-and-verification scenarios, five user profiles, six ecosystem-map cases, and three AI pilgrimage landmarks (Bell of AI Origins, Tower of AI Light, Pulse-Rail Art Track) anchor the AI ecosystem in locatable, operable, governable nodes; the Pulse Protocol (P1 declare — P2 test — P3 release — P4 review) with five rollback-trigger classes defines operating boundaries for every AI service. Implementation proceeds in three phases — near term (2026–2030, key areas first), mid term (2030–2035, greenway completion), long term (2035–2040, southern renewal and reserve land) — under a "secretariat—district stations—professional wings—public committee" governance structure with a three-source funding mix. All geometry is generated from official provisional boundaries; control metrics stay unknown until official conditions are released; the whole package is concept-level, discussable, verifiable, and recomputable once official boundaries arrive.
