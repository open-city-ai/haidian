# Jing-Zhang AI Pulse: Overall Urban Design of the Centennial Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt

## Design Basis and Source List

This proposal takes the *Prequalification Announcement for the International Design Solicitation for the Urban Design of the Centennial Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt*, issued by the Haidian Sub-bureau of the Beijing Municipal Commission of Planning and Natural Resources, as its primary basis [source:OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT] [standard:PROJECT-OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT], and organizes agent tasks around the excerpts of the *Open Call Taskbook for the Urban Design of the Centennial Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt Targeting Global AI Agents*, user-provided and copyright-cleared materials held in the repository's site-package [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK] [standard:PROJECT-AGENT-OPEN-CALL-TASKBOOK]. Together, the two documents define the three positionings, the five functions, the three-areas-and-two-wings layout and the six agent tasks, and serve as the point of departure for every design judgment in this proposal.

Materials are managed in three tiers by purpose [source:SOURCE-REGISTRY] [source:BOUNDARY-SOURCE]. The first tier is formal-ready: the official announcement, taskbook excerpts, and public regulations of the Ministry of Housing and Urban-Rural Development (MOHURD) and the Ministry of Natural Resources (MNR), which may support design depth, land use classification and regulatory-plan-depth requirements. The second tier is background: public reporting on the Jing-Zhang Railway Heritage Park and general industry knowledge, used only for background narrative. The third tier is provisional-only: temporary geometric data such as provisional_boundaries.geojson, usable only for concept generation and self-checks, and not as a basis for official red lines, exact areas or statutory conclusions [source:SITE-PACKAGE] [source:PROCESSED-FACT-PACK].

A clear gap remains in statutory control bases: official regulatory plan data, formal boundary polygons, the baseline of existing buildings and ownership, road red lines and municipal utility drawings are not yet in place. This proposal therefore reaches no conclusion on floor area ratio, building height, retain-renovate-demolish classification, road red lines or engineering feasibility, and uniformly records all such items as "pending completion of formal regulatory plan data" [depth:existing_conditions_diagnosis] [depth:development_intensity_controls] [depth:retain_renovate_demolish].

## Three-Level Scope Framework

The three-level scope implements industry strategy, overall urban design and detailed design of key areas level by level [depth:three_level_scope_framework]. Based on the textual boundary in the announcement, the coordinated research area covers approximately 43.6 km², bounded by the North 5th Ring Road to the north, the Jingzang Expressway to the east, Xizhimen Outer Street to the south and Wanquanhe Road to the west, with the working objective of determining the AI industry ecosystem, the future city form and the synergy among the three areas and two wings. The overall design area covers approximately 11.4 km², comprising mainly the 1-2 km urban and industrial belt around the Jing-Zhang Heritage Park, and is the main arena for regulatory-plan-depth urban design. From north to south, the key areas are Zhongzhiyuan, the Beijing AI Origin Community and Dazhongsi, with an official textual area totaling approximately 368.4 ha.

The overall design area recalculated from this package's provisional boundaries is 11,412,825.386 m² [metric:site_area_sqm]; the recalculated areas of the three key areas are 1,929,201.877 m² for Zhongzhiyuan, 1,043,236.909 m² for the AI Origin Community and 720,454.219 m² for Dazhongsi [metric:key_area_area_zhongzhiyuan_ai_acceleration_area] [metric:key_area_area_beijing_ai_origin_community] [metric:key_area_area_dazhongsi_ai_industry_cluster], corresponding respectively to the provisional key-area layers [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-001] [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-002] [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-003]. It must be declared that all of the above boundaries are provisional boundaries, not official red lines; the minor differences between the recalculated areas and the official textual areas stem from the provisional polygons, and once formal boundaries are in place all layers and metrics must be recalculated [source:KEY-AREA-SOURCE].

## Coordinated Research Area: Industry and Future City Research

**Overall concept and naming system (agent.1).** The proposal suggests the overall concept name "Jing-Zhang AI Pulse" (京张智脉), forming a "pulse-belt" naming system: one belt (the Jing-Zhang AI Pulse) leads the three areas and two wings; the three areas are the Zhongzhiyuan AI Autonomous Innovation Acceleration Area, the Beijing AI Origin Community and the Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster; the two wings are the Zhongguancun technology service wing and the Xiaoyuehe scenario enablement wing; and 14 AI scenario nodes extend from the system [metric:scenario_node_count]. The naming logic emphasizes the dual meaning of "pulse" — an urban "artery" unfolding along the former Jing-Zhang railway corridor, and a "neural pulse" of AI computing power, data and talent flows — echoing the temporal span from the centennial Jing-Zhang railway to the new life of AI. The naming and the logo direction are conceptual proposals that do not replace official naming, and unauthorized use of fonts, graphics and trademarks is not permitted [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK] [standard:PROJECT-AGENT-OPEN-CALL-TASKBOOK].

**Logo and visual identity direction.** Conceptually, the core graphic is "herringbone track × pulse waveform": two intersecting polylines simulate the superposition of the herringbone alignment at Qinglongqiao Station of the Jing-Zhang railway and a digital signal pulse, whose intersection point is the "origin" (the AI Origin Community), implying that a new intelligent direction emerges at a historical fork; the auxiliary graphics are extending track sleepers and data nodes. The color scheme suggests a three-color system of railway rust red (historical layer), Zhongguancun technology blue (innovation layer) and pulse teal (AI vitality layer). The logo provides only a directional description; the official identity must be designed by a professional team with copyright clearance [depth:overall_spatial_structure].

**Three positionings, five functions and the three-areas-two-wings synergy loop.** The three positionings are the centennial Jing-Zhang cultural belt, the metropolitan AI living experience belt and the AI-integrated innovation belt; the five functions are the full-stack AI autonomous innovation system, the world-class AI innovation ecosystem, the new paradigm of AI+ scenario enablement, the intelligent AI-vital city, and global discourse power in AI governance [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK]. The synergy loop is: AI Origin Community (world-class innovation ecosystem) — Zhongzhiyuan (full-stack autonomous innovation and governance discourse power) — Dazhongsi (intelligent-native new business forms), with the two wings providing global allocation of factors, Zhongguancun IP and capital enablement, and scenario enablement and vital-city support respectively. Through five east-west stitching lines, the three areas and two wings circulate talent, data and capital, forming a closed loop of "innovation origination — acceleration and transformation — scenario landing — capital reinvestment".

**Overall spatial structure.** The proposal suggests a spatial structure of "one pulse main axis, five stitching lines, three areas and two wings, and 14 AI scenario nodes" [metric:scenario_node_count] [depth:overall_spatial_structure]. The pulse main axis is the former Jing-Zhang railway corridor, with a conceptual slow-mobility main axis of approximately 9.7 km [metric:greenway_km], linking the heritage park vitality belt and the three areas; the five stitching lines are east-west roads and green belts that stitch the north-south fragmentation caused by the railway division; the 14 scenario nodes are distributed alternately along the main axis and the stitching lines, carrying testing, display, experience and governance functions.

**Global AI innovation ecosystem cases (agent.2).** The following 8 cases are drawn from public-source syntheses, are not commitments, and serve only to distill transferable experience [depth:overall_spatial_structure]:

| Case | Core characteristics (public-source synthesis) | Transferable experience for the local context |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Silicon Valley / Stanford Research Park | University origination—park transformation—venture capital corridor integrated | Physical proximity between research institutions and startups, corresponding to the near-campus layout of the Origin Community |
| Shenzhen Nanshan Science and Technology Park | Leading enterprises drive agglomeration of the industrial chain | Chain-leader-led organization of industrial space |
| Hangzhou West City Sci-Tech Innovation Corridor | Corridor-style spatial organization with multiple innovation polar nuclei | The spatial model of a linear main axis linking functional areas |
| Singapore one-north | "Work-live-play-learn" mixed-use park | Unified brand operation and public living-room design |
| London King's Cross | Railway hub redevelopment with introduction of knowledge industries | Historic station and innovation community coexisting, corresponding to reuse of the Jing-Zhang heritage |
| Shanghai Zhangjiang Science City | Agglomeration of large scientific facilities with tiered space supply | "R&D-pilot-mass production" tiered spatial organization |
| Munich Max Planck and IT cluster | Symbiotic ecosystem of basic research-lead enterprises-SMEs | Governance patience of stable institutions and long-term investment |
| Tokyo Marunouchi | Headquarters agglomeration in a business district with urban quality improvement | Organization of international reception and display functions |

The transferable experience for the Jing-Zhang AI Pulse can be distilled into four points. First, organize the innovation chain as "near-campus origination + park acceleration + scenario validation", corresponding to the AI Origin Community and the Zhongguancun technology service wing. Second, use continuous public space to carry innovation exchange, corresponding to the pulse main axis and the public space system. Third, organize work-residence-service relationships through mixed-function urban blocks rather than single-function parks, corresponding to the industry-city integration within the three areas. Fourth, build long-term assets through unified branding and event operation, corresponding to the event system and developer community operation suggestions below [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK] [source:PROCESSED-FACT-PACK].

## Overall Design Area: Urban Renewal and Regulatory-Plan-Level Urban Design

The overall design area is organized at the urban design depth of a regulatory detailed plan, focusing on how industrial objectives, functional layout, renewal framework, public space, transport and municipal works, and urban character control support one another [standard:MOHURD-CONTROL-DETAILED-PLANNING] [depth:land_use_layout]. The map layer in this package consists of 28 land use units organized into 7 lateral segments and 4 columns (west-side area, Jing-Zhang Heritage Park green belt, east inner column, east outer column) [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-001], with a total land use area that closes with the overall scope [metric:land_use_area_total].

Land use breakdown: residential 2,786,000 m² (0701), research 1,979,000 m² (0802) [metric:land_use_area_0701] [metric:land_use_area_0802], education 1,335,000 m² (0804), commercial 914,000 m² (05) [metric:land_use_area_0804] [metric:land_use_area_05], cultural 31,000 m² (0803) [metric:land_use_area_0803], park and green space 4,081,000 m² (1401), protective green space 151,000 m² (1402) and squares 136,000 m² (1403) [metric:land_use_area_1401] [metric:land_use_area_1402] [metric:land_use_area_1403]. Research, education and commercial functions together form the industrial and talent service base, while green space concentrates along the heritage corridor, forming a conceptual pattern of "green core + functional clusters".

This pattern is a "conceptual land use structure", not a statutory land use plan; functional proportions, road red lines and development intensity all await completion of formal regulatory plan data [depth:development_intensity_controls] [source:SITE-PACKAGE]. The overall urban renewal framework unfolds as "identification of inefficient space — grading of renewal objects — packaging of implementation projects — rolling phased delivery" [depth:retain_renovate_demolish]. The building layer comprises 65 conceptual buildings with a total footprint area of 204,758.129 m² [metric:building_footprint_area_sqm] and a building density of about 1.8% [metric:building_density], reflecting the conceptual orientation of "low-density renewal" — since a large number of existing buildings are not yet counted due to missing baseline data, no statutory retain-renovate-demolish conclusion may be inferred from this [depth:existing_conditions_diagnosis].

Urban character control suggests taking the heritage corridor as the visual axis, controlling the massing and roof forms of buildings along it, and forming a three-tier urban keynote of "historical layer — innovation layer — living layer" [depth:height_massing_character] [standard:MOHURD-URBAN-DESIGN-MEASURES]. All building heights, massing and setback requirements await verification against formal regulatory plan conditions, and this proposal gives no approved numerical values.

## Detailed Design of Key Areas

The three key areas are organized at the urban design depth of an integrated implementation plan, each covering seven aspects: positioning, spatial structure, building renewal, transport and slow mobility, public space, AI scenarios and implementation risks [depth:three_key_area_detailed_design]; the geometric basis is the provisional key-area layers [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-001] [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-002] [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-003], with quantities verified by metrics [metric:key_area_count]. ### Zhongzhiyuan AI Autonomous Innovation Acceleration Area

Positioned as a "garden-type full-stack autonomous innovation acceleration area", it carries the full-stack AI autonomous innovation system, open-source governance and computing hub functions, and is linked to global discourse power in AI governance [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK]. The provisional-boundary recalculated area is 1,929,201.877 m² [metric:key_area_area_zhongzhiyuan_ai_acceleration_area]. The spatial structure suggests unfolding a three-tier space of "test corridor — open-source square — computing park" along the Qinghe riverfront [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-001]. Building renewal suggests, on the premise of verifying the baseline of existing research space, prioritizing the revitalization of inefficient land; building height and massing control await completion of formal regulatory plan data [depth:retain_renovate_demolish]. For transport and slow mobility, external connections toward the North 5th Ring Road are suggested, with the Qinghe waterfront trail linked to the heritage corridor to form an internal slow-mobility loop. Public space takes the open-source square as its core, organized in combination with protective green space and the Qinghe blue-green space [metric:land_use_area_1402]. Suggested AI scenarios include test and validation nodes such as a large model evaluation ground, a safety governance sandbox and a low-carbon computing experience. Implementation risks: the Qinghe blue line, controls around the 5th Ring Road, regulatory intensity and existing ownership all await verification, and any bridge, tunnel or engineering feasibility conclusion is excluded [depth:risk_missing_data] [source:SITE-PACKAGE].

### Beijing AI Origin Community

Positioned as a "near-campus outcome transformation and talent special zone", it relies on the origination of surrounding universities and carries the organizational function of a world-class AI innovation ecosystem [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK]. The provisional-boundary recalculated area is 1,043,236.909 m² [metric:key_area_area_beijing_ai_origin_community]. The spatial structure suggests taking the "maker square — large model roadshow amphitheater — near-campus outcome transformation street" as the core skeleton, with campuses, parks and neighborhoods connected through slow-mobility stitching lines [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-002]. Building renewal suggests organizing functional reallocation around outcome release, talent services and residential living support; specific retain-renovate-demolish classifications await completion of existing baseline and regulatory plan data [depth:retain_renovate_demolish]. For transport and slow mobility, strengthening slow-mobility connections to Wudaokou, the west entrance of Tsinghua East Road and rail stations is suggested, organizing car-free slow corridors between campuses and parks. Public space takes the AI Origin maker square as its core, doubling as a venue for outcome release and community exchange. Suggested AI scenarios include the large model roadshow amphitheater, the open-source square agent release node and the near-campus outcome transformation station [depth:overall_spatial_structure]. Implementation risks: campus boundaries and park ownership are sensitive, and community operation must prioritize public interest with strict human review [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK].

### Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster

Positioned as an "urban intelligent-native consumption and test-validation block", it carries intelligent-native new business forms and the AI living-room square function [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK]. The provisional-boundary recalculated area is 720,454.219 m² [metric:key_area_area_dazhongsi_ai_industry_cluster]. The spatial structure suggests organizing four-quadrant pedestrian connectivity around Dazhongsi Station, forming a spatial sequence of "AI living-room square — intelligent-native commercial street — data element salon" [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-003]. Building renewal suggests composite use of commercial, office and public space while preserving existing commercial vitality; retain-renovate-demolish classifications await completion of formal regulatory plan data. For transport and slow mobility, integrated development of Dazhongsi Station is the core, resolving the four-quadrant fragmentation at the intersection and strengthening pedestrian priority [depth:traffic_rail_slow_parking]. Public space takes the AI living-room square as its core, in composite use with planned green space. Suggested AI scenarios include nodes such as a smart transport test intersection, a drone delivery test point, an AI consumption pod and a data element salon. Implementation risks: the construction phasing of the rail station, intersection engineering conditions and commercial ownership are complex, and test-type scenarios may be opened only after safety and privacy compliance assessment [depth:risk_missing_data].

## Land Use, Building Scale, and Retain-Renovate-Demolish Strategy

The land use structure is expressed in accordance with the territorial spatial land and sea use classification, forming complete and closed land use zones [standard:MNR-LAND-USE-CLASSIFICATION-GUIDE] [depth:land_use_layout] [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-001], and the total land use area closes with the boundary area [metric:land_use_area_total] [metric:site_area_sqm]. Residential, research, education and commercial functions total approximately 7,013,000 m², forming the spatial basis for industry and talent services; green space and squares total approximately 4,368,000 m², supporting the quality of life of talent [metric:green_space_area_sqm] [metric:public_space_area_sqm].

In terms of building scale, the conceptual building layer comprises 65 buildings with a total footprint area of 204,758.129 m² [metric:building_footprint_area_sqm] and a building density of about 1.8% [metric:building_density]; the conceptual total floor area is 1,635,693.039 m², a low-confidence conceptual design quantity [metric:total_floor_area_sqm], with a conceptual floor area ratio of 0.143 [metric:conceptual_far]. It must be emphasized that the conceptual FAR is not the statutory FAR; the statutory FAR status is unknown, to be recalculated once formal regulatory plan data is completed [metric:floor_area_ratio] [depth:development_intensity_controls].

The retain-renovate-demolish strategy proposes only a grading method and reaches no statutory conclusion [depth:retain_renovate_demolish]. It is suggested to establish a baseline ledger of existing buildings in four grades: "verified retention — renovation and upgrading — renewal introduction — pending confirmation". Retention refers to historical and heritage-protection elements; renovation refers to buildings whose functions can be reallocated; renewal introduction refers to the revitalization of inefficient space; pending confirmation refers to objects with missing baseline data. Since existing buildings, ownership, regulatory plans and engineering conditions are not yet in place, the specific retain-renovate-demolish list must be determined after formal regulatory plan data and baseline surveys are completed, and this proposal lists no plot-level retain-renovate-demolish conclusions [depth:existing_conditions_diagnosis] [source:SITE-PACKAGE].

The Jing-Zhang AI Pulse slow-mobility main axis has a conceptual length of 9.733 km [metric:greenway_km], carrying the composite functions of heritage tour, commuting and test experience. In terms of rail, integrated development of Dazhongsi Station and slow-mobility connections at Wudaokou and the west entrance of Tsinghua East Road are suggested; rail alignments and station deepening are outside the scope of this proposal, and engineering conclusions are entirely excluded [depth:traffic_rail_slow_parking].

## Blue-Green Network, Public Space, and Urban Character

The blue-green network takes the Jing-Zhang Heritage Park vitality belt as its spine, coordinating the blue-line constraints of the Qinghe and Xiaoyuehe rivers and the protective green space along the 5th Ring Road [depth:blue_green_public_space] [data:geometry/green_space.geojson#GREEN-001]. The total green space area is 4,232,066.778 m², with a green ratio of about 37.1% [metric:green_space_area_sqm] [metric:green_ratio]; the total public space area is 319,986.638 m², about 2.8% of the area [metric:public_space_area_sqm] [metric:public_space_ratio]. Four public space nodes are suggested: the AI Origin maker square, the Zhongzhiyuan open-source square, the Dazhongsi AI living-room square and the Xiaoyuehe waterfront exchange belt [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#PUBLIC-001]. Heritage protection, green space and blue-line constraints are expressed as conceptual indications, and the formal control scope awaits official verification [source:SITE-PACKAGE].

Urban character suggests the keynote control of "historical layer — innovation layer — living layer" [depth:height_massing_character] [standard:MOHURD-URBAN-DESIGN-MEASURES]. The historical layer preserves the texture of railway industrial heritage along the heritage corridor; the innovation layer expresses AI character through the massing, roof forms and public art of the three areas; the living layer is dominated by block scale and community service space.

**AI pilgrimage landmarks and honor displays (agent.4).** It is suggested to set up more than 3 AI pilgrimage landmark and honor display nodes, all conceptual suggestions and not to be regarded as approved construction: first, the "AI Origin · Founder Memory Hall", located in the Beijing AI Origin Community, tracing the memory of Zhongguancun entrepreneurs and AI developers, in line with Zhongguancun's innovation culture; second, the "Open-Source Square · Agent Release Node", facing the developer community as a public space for code release, contribution walls and honor displays; third, the "Large Model Roadshow Amphitheater", set within the Jing-Zhang Heritage Park green belt, carrying model releases, international roadshows and public experience [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK]. The relationship of the three nodes to the Jing-Zhang Heritage Park, Zhongguancun culture and the developer community is: the heritage park provides a sense of historical depth, Zhongguancun culture provides an innovation genealogy, and the developer community provides continuous content — together constituting a pilgrimage narrative of "history — innovation — future".

**Three-layer cultural narrative and wayfinding symbols (agent.5).** The cultural narrative has three layers. The first is the centennial Jing-Zhang railway culture, based on public general-knowledge historical materials — the Jing-Zhang railway was built in the early 20th century and designed under Zhan Tianyou's direction; Qinglongqiao Station adopted a "herringbone" switchback to overcome the steep Guan'gou slope, and it was one of the trunk railways designed and built by China itself. This proposal makes no assertions beyond general knowledge. The second is Zhongguancun's innovation culture, the "dare to be first" tradition from Electronic Street to science and technology parks. The third is the new AI culture, emphasizing open source, collaboration, human-machine co-intelligence and public governance. The wayfinding symbol system suggests "herringbone track × pulse waveform × rail spike" as the motif, used only for cultural wayfinding and historical interpretation, distinguished from the overall logo system [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK] [depth:overall_spatial_structure]. The international communication narrative is suggested to revolve around "from the herringbone railway to the pulse city": a century ago a "herringbone track" let trains cross the mountains, and today a "pulse" lets knowledge and computing power flow through the city, serving global communication through a translatable, experienceable narrative [depth:existing_conditions_diagnosis].

Building footprint area 204,758.129 m² [metric:building_footprint_area_sqm], building density about 1.8% [metric:building_density], slow-mobility main axis about 9.7 km [metric:greenway_km], and 14 scenario nodes in total [metric:scenario_node_count].

Control indicators such as the statutory floor area ratio have a status of unknown, awaiting completion of formal regulatory plan data [metric:floor_area_ratio] [depth:development_intensity_controls]. Performance indicators such as talent density, the AI innovation index and event participation must be continuously calibrated with operational data, and this proposal gives no values [depth:metrics_recalculation].

Main risks include: provisional boundary risk — all boundaries in this package are provisional boundaries, not official red lines, and all layers and metrics must be recalculated once formal boundaries are in place [depth:risk_missing_data] [source:BOUNDARY-SOURCE]; regulatory-plan gap risk — the statutory floor area ratio, building height, road red lines and retain-renovate-demolish classification all await completion of formal regulatory plan data and are treated here as "unknown/conceptual quantity" [depth:development_intensity_controls] [depth:height_massing_character]; heritage risk — the schematic scope of the Jing-Zhang heritage protection corridor awaits official verification [depth:existing_conditions_diagnosis]; engineering risk — this proposal contains no conclusions on bridges, tunnels, underground space or municipal capacity [source:SITE-PACKAGE].

This proposal does not claim official approval, reviewed regulatory plans, final ownership or implementation guarantees; the English counterpart is provided as proposal.en.md, and figures and HTML are provided bilingually [standard:PROJECT-OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT].
