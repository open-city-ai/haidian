Jing-Zhang AI Pulse: Overall Urban Design of the Centennial Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt

An urban design proposal for the Centennial Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt under the overall concept of the 'Jing-Zhang AI Pulse': a main axis of approximately 9.7 km formed along the former Jing-Zhang railway corridor, linking three areas and two wings and 14 AI scenario nodes to build an overall spatial structure of 'one main axis, five stitching lines, three areas and two wings', offering conceptual suggestions on the AI innovation ecosystem, scenario enablement, cultural narrative and long-term operation for professional teams to develop further.

Design Basis and Source List

This proposal takes the Prequalification Announcement for the International Design Solicitation for the Urban Design of the Centennial Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt, issued by the Haidian Sub-bureau of the Beijing Municipal Commission of Planning and Natural Resources, as its primary basis Source Standard, and organizes agent tasks around the excerpts of the Open Call Taskbook for the Urban Design of the Centennial Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt Targeting Global AI Agents, user-provided and copyright-cleared materials held in the repository's site-package Source Standard. Together, the two documents define the three positionings, the five functions, the three-areas-and-two-wings layout and the six agent tasks, and serve as the point of departure for every design judgment in this proposal.

Materials are managed in three tiers by purpose Source Source. The first tier is formal-ready: the official announcement, taskbook excerpts, and public regulations of the Ministry of Housing and Urban-Rural Development (MOHURD) and the Ministry of Natural Resources (MNR), which may support design depth, land use classification and regulatory-plan-depth requirements. The second tier is background: public reporting on the Jing-Zhang Railway Heritage Park and general industry knowledge, used only for background narrative. The third tier is provisional-only: temporary geometric data such as provisional_boundaries.geojson, usable only for concept generation and self-checks, and not as a basis for official red lines, exact areas or statutory conclusions Source Source.

A clear gap remains in statutory control bases: official regulatory plan data, formal boundary polygons, the baseline of existing buildings and ownership, road red lines and municipal utility drawings are not yet in place. This proposal therefore reaches no conclusion on floor area ratio, building height, retain-renovate-demolish classification, road red lines or engineering feasibility, and uniformly records all such items as "pending completion of formal regulatory plan data" Depth Depth Depth.

Three-Level Scope Framework

The three-level scope implements industry strategy, overall urban design and detailed design of key areas level by level Depth. Based on the textual boundary in the announcement, the coordinated research area covers approximately 43.6 km², bounded by the North 5th Ring Road to the north, the Jingzang Expressway to the east, Xizhimen Outer Street to the south and Wanquanhe Road to the west, with the working objective of determining the AI industry ecosystem, the future city form and the synergy among the three areas and two wings. The overall design area covers approximately 11.4 km², comprising mainly the 1-2 km urban and industrial belt around the Jing-Zhang Heritage Park, and is the main arena for regulatory-plan-depth urban design. From north to south, the key areas are Zhongzhiyuan, the Beijing AI Origin Community and Dazhongsi, with an official textual area totaling approximately 368.4 ha.

The overall design area recalculated from this package's provisional boundaries is 11,412,825.386 m² Metric; the recalculated areas of the three key areas are 1,929,201.877 m² for Zhongzhiyuan, 1,043,236.909 m² for the AI Origin Community and 720,454.219 m² for Dazhongsi Metric Metric Metric, corresponding respectively to the provisional key-area layers Spatial data Spatial data Spatial data. It must be declared that all of the above boundaries are provisional boundaries, not official red lines; the minor differences between the recalculated areas and the official textual areas stem from the provisional polygons, and once formal boundaries are in place all layers and metrics must be recalculated Source.

The three levels are not a detached set of drawings but a progressively converging evidence chain: the coordinated research level answers "why", the overall design level answers "where", and the key-area level answers "how". Coordinated Research Area: Industry and Future City Research

Overall concept and naming system (agent.1). The proposal suggests the overall concept name "Jing-Zhang AI Pulse" (京张智脉), forming a "pulse-belt" naming system: one belt (the Jing-Zhang AI Pulse) leads the three areas and two wings; the three areas are the Zhongzhiyuan AI Autonomous Innovation Acceleration Area, the Beijing AI Origin Community and the Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster; the two wings are the Zhongguancun technology service wing and the Xiaoyuehe scenario enablement wing; and 14 AI scenario nodes extend from the system Metric. The naming logic emphasizes the dual meaning of "pulse" — an urban "artery" unfolding along the former Jing-Zhang railway corridor, and a "neural pulse" of AI computing power, data and talent flows — echoing the temporal span from the centennial Jing-Zhang railway to the new life of AI. The naming and the logo direction are conceptual proposals that do not replace official naming, and unauthorized use of fonts, graphics and trademarks is not permitted Source Standard.

Logo and visual identity direction. Conceptually, the core graphic is "herringbone track × pulse waveform": two intersecting polylines simulate the superposition of the herringbone alignment at Qinglongqiao Station of the Jing-Zhang railway and a digital signal pulse, whose intersection point is the "origin" (the AI Origin Community), implying that a new intelligent direction emerges at a historical fork; the auxiliary graphics are extending track sleepers and data nodes. The color scheme suggests a three-color system of railway rust red (historical layer), Zhongguancun technology blue (innovation layer) and pulse teal (AI vitality layer). The logo provides only a directional description; the official identity must be designed by a professional team with copyright clearance Depth.

Three positionings, five functions and the three-areas-two-wings synergy loop. The three positionings are the centennial Jing-Zhang cultural belt, the metropolitan AI living experience belt and the AI-integrated innovation belt; the five functions are the full-stack AI autonomous innovation system, the world-class AI innovation ecosystem, the new paradigm of AI+ scenario enablement, the intelligent AI-vital city, and global discourse power in AI governance Source. The synergy loop is: AI Origin Community (world-class innovation ecosystem) — Zhongzhiyuan (full-stack autonomous innovation and governance discourse power) — Dazhongsi (intelligent-native new business forms), with the two wings providing global allocation of factors, Zhongguancun IP and capital enablement, and scenario enablement and vital-city support respectively. Through five east-west stitching lines, the three areas and two wings circulate talent, data and capital, forming a closed loop of "innovation origination — acceleration and transformation — scenario landing — capital reinvestment".

Overall spatial structure. The proposal suggests a spatial structure of "one pulse main axis, five stitching lines, three areas and two wings, and 14 AI scenario nodes" Metric Depth. The pulse main axis is the former Jing-Zhang railway corridor, with a conceptual slow-mobility main axis of approximately 9.7 km Metric, linking the heritage park vitality belt and the three areas; the five stitching lines are east-west roads and green belts that stitch the north-south fragmentation caused by the railway division; the 14 scenario nodes are distributed alternately along the main axis and the stitching lines, carrying testing, display, experience and governance functions.

Land Use, Building Scale, and Retain-Renovate-Demolish Strategy

The land use structure is expressed in accordance with the territorial spatial land and sea use classification, forming complete and closed land use zones Standard Depth Spatial data, and the total land use area closes with the boundary area Metric Metric. Residential, research, education and commercial functions total approximately 7,013,000 m², forming the spatial basis for industry and talent services; green space and squares total approximately 4,368,000 m², supporting the quality of life of talent Metric Metric.

In terms of building scale, the conceptual building layer comprises 65 buildings with a total footprint area of 204,758.129 m² Metric and a building density of about 1.8% Metric; the conceptual total floor area is 1,635,693.039 m², a low-confidence conceptual design quantity Metric, with a conceptual floor area ratio of 0.143 Metric. It must be emphasized that the conceptual FAR is not the statutory FAR; the statutory FAR status is unknown, to be recalculated once formal regulatory plan data is completed Metric Depth.

The retain-renovate-demolish strategy proposes only a grading method and reaches no statutory conclusion Depth. It is suggested to establish a baseline ledger of existing buildings in four grades: "verified retention — renovation and upgrading — renewal introduction — pending confirmation". Retention refers to historical and heritage-protection elements; renovation refers to buildings whose functions can be reallocated; renewal introduction refers to the revitalization of inefficient space; pending confirmation refers to objects with missing baseline data. Since existing buildings, ownership, regulatory plans and engineering conditions are not yet in place, the specific retain-renovate-demolish list must be determined after formal regulatory plan data and baseline surveys are completed, and this proposal lists no plot-level retain-renovate-demolish conclusions Depth Source.

Transport, Rail, Municipal Infrastructure, and Public Services

The transport strategy is organized as "rail as skeleton, slow mobility as veins, testing as feature" Depth. The conceptual road network area is 678,055.077 m², with a road ratio of about 5.9% Metric Metric; it must be declared that these are conceptual roads, not red lines, and their alignment and class await completion of formal regulatory plan data Spatial data. The Jing-Zhang AI Pulse slow-mobility main axis has a conceptual length of 9.733 km Metric, carrying the composite functions of heritage tour, commuting and test experience. In terms of rail, integrated development of Dazhongsi Station and slow-mobility connections at Wudaokou and the west entrance of Tsinghua East Road are suggested; rail alignments and station deepening are outside the scope of this proposal, and engineering conclusions are entirely excluded Depth.

Municipal and public service facilities are organized on two tracks: "conventional municipal works + new infrastructure" Depth. Conventional municipal works include road micro-circulation, parking and non-motorized vehicle organization; utility lines and fire conditions await completion of formal engineering data. New infrastructure is suggested to follow the directions of distributed energy, on-device computing and intelligent sensing, deployed in combination with AI scenario nodes. Public service facilities cover innovation service platforms, talent life services and accessibility facilities, and are integrated into the design in accordance with the Law on the Construction of an Accessible Environment Source Depth.

AI pilgrimage landmarks and honor displays (agent.4). It is suggested to set up more than 3 AI pilgrimage landmark and honor display nodes, all conceptual suggestions and not to be regarded as approved construction: first, the "AI Origin · Founder Memory Hall", located in the Beijing AI Origin Community, tracing the memory of Zhongguancun entrepreneurs and AI developers, in line with Zhongguancun's innovation culture; second, the "Open-Source Square · Agent Release Node", facing the developer community as a public space for code release, contribution walls and honor displays; third, the "Large Model Roadshow Amphitheater", set within the Jing-Zhang Heritage Park green belt, carrying model releases, international roadshows and public experience Source. The relationship of the three nodes to the Jing-Zhang Heritage Park, Zhongguancun culture and the developer community is: the heritage park provides a sense of historical depth, Zhongguancun culture provides an innovation genealogy, and the developer community provides continuous content — together constituting a pilgrimage narrative of "history — innovation — future".

Three-layer cultural narrative and wayfinding symbols (agent.5). The cultural narrative has three layers. The first is the centennial Jing-Zhang railway culture, based on public general-knowledge historical materials — the Jing-Zhang railway was built in the early 20th century and designed under Zhan Tianyou's direction; Qinglongqiao Station adopted a "herringbone" switchback to overcome the steep Guan'gou slope, and it was one of the trunk railways designed and built by China itself. This proposal makes no assertions beyond general knowledge. The second is Zhongguancun's innovation culture, the "dare to be first" tradition from Electronic Street to science and technology parks. The third is the new AI culture, emphasizing open source, collaboration, human-machine co-intelligence and public governance. The wayfinding symbol system suggests "herringbone track × pulse waveform × rail spike" as the motif, used only for cultural wayfinding and historical interpretation, distinguished from the overall logo system Source Depth. The international communication narrative is suggested to revolve around "from the herringbone railway to the pulse city": a century ago a "herringbone track" let trains cross the mountains, and today a "pulse" lets knowledge and computing power flow through the city, serving global communication through a translatable, experienceable narrative Depth.
