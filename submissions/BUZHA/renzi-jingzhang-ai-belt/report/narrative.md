# Formal Narrative Index

The canonical human-readable narratives are `proposal.md` (Chinese primary) and `proposal.en.md` (English equivalent). Their rendered offline reports are `report/proposal.html` and `report/proposal.en.html`.

The proposal is organised as an evidence-to-delivery chain: formal and provisional data boundaries; regional and international mechanisms; one spine, three test stations, and two knowledge wings; 13 scenario cards; inclusion and anti-displacement; six funded and reversible implementation packages; metrics, rights, and bilingual equivalence.

Geometry and concept metrics remain auditable in the package but do not claim statutory control, approval, or implementation. Official geometry and control data trigger replacement, recalculation, difference reporting, multidisciplinary review, and bilingual republication.
