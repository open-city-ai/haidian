Blue-Green Space, Public Realm, and Character

Haidian's character is a set of choices rather than a material palette: green space serves everyday life, technology removes friction, and heritage enters long-term use. Railway restraint, durability, and maintainability guide the form; cyberpunk neon, giant screens, and novelty landmarks are avoided. 来源 深度

AI should become almost invisible. People first notice fewer detours, less exposure to rain, a place to sit, and a person to ask. Only then do they discover an Agent routing, translating, maintaining, and learning in the background. The civic space remains useful if the technology stops.

Six concept work packages organise implementation: the minimum Common Eave kit, Qinghe Benchmark Garden, Qinghuayuan Shared Eave, Dazhongsi Four-Corner Commons, transverse campus-community-river-station links, and the Agent Service Contract. Risk, Copyright, and Compliance

This is an open co-creation proposal, not an approved plan. The Shared Eave raises heritage, fire, rights, and operating questions; Four-Corner Commons raises road, rail, fire, and commercial-interface questions. Real implementation requires specialist studies, public engagement, and statutory procedures. 来源 深度
