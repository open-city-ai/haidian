# THE COMMON EAVE: A Nine-Kilometre Civic Room for the Jing-Zhang AI Belt
## Design Basis and Sources

This revision begins with Haidian's own public-planning lineage rather than a generic image of an AI city. The district plan stresses compact renewal and the integration of technology, culture, and ecology; the green-space plan frames a science city within parks; and the urban-renewal guide calls for porous ground floors, shared institutional edges, all-age access, and human-friendly AI. Local public-space projects reinforce a restrained, useful, incremental approach instead of a single iconic megastructure. [source:SRC-HAIDIAN-DISTRICT-PLAN] [source:SRC-HAIDIAN-GREEN-PLAN] [source:SRC-HAIDIAN-URBAN-RENEWAL]

All site and key-area polygons remain provisional constraints. The two civic prototypes define place types and operating logic, not property, road engineering, or heritage intervention. [source:SRC-PROVISIONAL-BOUNDARIES-2026] [depth:existing_conditions_diagnosis]

## Three-Level Scope Framework

The research scale asks how Haidian's AI capacity can create public value; the overall-design scale turns the nine-kilometre heritage park into a longitudinal and transverse civic-room network; and the key-area scale resolves two prototypes that can be drawn, tested, operated, and professionally deepened. Official geometry will trigger package-wide recalculation. [source:SRC-2026-BJ-GH-QUAL-PREANNOUNCEMENT] [depth:three_level_scope_framework]

The Common Eave is not a continuous roof. It is a spatial grammar: at key nodes everyone should be able to pause, find shelter or shade, sit with back support, drink water, locate a toilet, park a bicycle, reach a human helper, and move on a continuous accessible ground. [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#PUBLIC-001] [depth:overall_spatial_structure]

## Research Area: Industry and Future City

A review of the public catalog of 309 merged proposals found 276 line/belt/spine narratives and 200 open/auditable/rollback narratives, but only 13 summaries touching architectural civic prototypes, five naming everyday service facilities, and three foregrounding shade, weather, or microclimate. This is not a ranking; it identifies a reproducible differentiation gap. [source:SRC-PEER-CATALOG-20260810] [metric:peer_catalog_count_reviewed]

Five global references contribute mechanisms rather than imagery: problem-to-PoC work from AI Singapore, urban testbeds from Helsinki, research-community links from Mila, public documentation from Amsterdam, and talent support from Seoul AI Hub. In Haidian they become one relationship among testing, public service, human responsibility, and long-term operation. ## Overall Design Area: Urban Renewal

The structure combines a longitudinal heritage-green spine, transverse campus-community-river-station links, and three civic landmarks. Existing ground floors and buildings are adapted before new construction; reversible elements precede permanent works. FAR, height, road lines, bridges, tunnels, and municipal capacity remain pending formal data and specialist review. [standard:MNR-LAND-USE-CLASSIFICATION-GUIDE] [depth:development_intensity_controls]

The three landmarks avoid spectacle: Qinghe Benchmark Garden discloses aggregated green-AI and ecological maintenance results; Qinghuayuan Shared Eave enables campus-city sharing; and Dazhongsi Four-Corner Commons forms a civic foyer for arrival and intelligent commerce. [metric:landmark_count] [depth:blue_green_public_space]

## Detailed Design of the Key Areas

Zhongzhiyuan receives a benchmark garden for green-AI testing, standards, public observation, and ecological maintenance. The AI Origin Community receives the Shared Eave: quiet study, open classes, repair and shared meals, child care, staffed help, and wet-weather waiting in a reversible structure detached from heritage fabric. Dazhongsi receives Four-Corner Commons: continuous accessible interchange, cycle parking, human service, and civic commerce before any heavier station engineering is considered. [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-001] [depth:three_key_area_detailed_design]

Both flagships record the public problem, spatial move, daily operation, Agent responsibility, offline fallback, and professional trigger. They remain concepts for professional deepening. [metric:flagship_public_design_count] [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-003]

## AI Ecosystem, Personas, and Scenarios

Six personas represent researchers, young founders and international talent, older residents, children and carers, wheelchair and blind users, and public-service and maintenance staff. They describe service friction, not personal profiles. [metric:persona_count] [source:SRC-HAIDIAN-AI-ELDERCARE]

Ten scenarios include three industrial tests—green-AI energy comparison, edge-device interoperability, and controlled low-speed robot co-movement—plus eave service routing, multilingual knowledge translation, non-digital elder support, shade and waiting comfort, time-shared curb negotiation, evidence-based heritage guidance, and public-facility maintenance. Every scenario must publish six fields: public problem, minimum data, human owner, offline fallback, stop threshold, and learning receipt. [metric:scenario_count] [metric:agent_service_contract_fields]

## Land Use, Building Scale, and Adaptation

Auditable concept land-use zones remain complete and non-overlapping, but design attention shifts to public ground and porous edges. Building action follows use first, adaptation second, construction last; structural, fire, heritage, carbon, rent, and displacement evidence is required before any building-level decision. [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-001] [depth:retain_renovate_demolish]

The Shared Eave must be independent, self-supporting, reversible, and set away from heritage fabric. Four-Corner Commons prioritises the ground and existing frontages; bridges, tunnels, underground works, and permanent structures remain subjects for specialist studies. [standard:MOHURD-URBAN-DESIGN-MEASURES] [depth:height_massing_character]

## Mobility, Rail, Municipal Systems, and Public Services

Priority runs from walking and wheelchairs to cycling, transit, service traffic, and finally private through-movement. Four-Corner Commons proposes a ground-first soft X and four open corner rooms, sharing time among crossing, parking, pickup, delivery, and events. It neither controls traffic signals nor claims engineering feasibility. [data:geometry/roads.geojson#ROAD-003] [depth:traffic_rail_slow_parking]

Municipal and AI systems retain human takeover, offline processes, named maintenance, and recovery. Public service must never become scan-only: toilets, water, seating, shelter, cycle parking, and staffed help form the minimum kit. [depth:municipal_new_infrastructure] [source:SRC-ZHONGGUANCUN-PUBLIC-SPACE]

## Blue-Green Space, Public Realm, and Character

Haidian's character is a set of choices rather than a material palette: green space serves everyday life, technology removes friction, and heritage enters long-term use. Railway restraint, durability, and maintainability guide the form; cyberpunk neon, giant screens, and novelty landmarks are avoided. [source:SRC-HAIDIAN-GREEN-PLAN] [depth:blue_green_public_space]

AI should become almost invisible. People first notice fewer detours, less exposure to rain, a place to sit, and a person to ask. Only then do they discover an Agent routing, translating, maintaining, and learning in the background. The civic space remains useful if the technology stops.

## Projects, Policy, and Phasing

Six concept work packages organise implementation: the minimum Common Eave kit, Qinghe Benchmark Garden, Qinghuayuan Shared Eave, Dazhongsi Four-Corner Commons, transverse campus-community-river-station links, and the Agent Service Contract. Phase 0 verifies geometry, flow, accessibility, heritage, rights, and maintenance; Phase 1 tests reversible furniture, wayfinding, and staffed service; Phase 2 professionally deepens the flagships; Phase 3 scales only components with demonstrated public value. [data:geometry/phasing.geojson#PHASE-001] [depth:phasing_implementation]

The annual operating cycle is spring problem collection, summer prototypes, autumn public review, and winter maintenance learning. Each trial can continue, change, pause, or exit. [source:CASE-HELSINKI-TESTBED] [depth:renewal_project_list]

## Risk, Copyright, and Compliance

This is an open co-creation proposal, not an approved plan. The Shared Eave raises heritage, fire, rights, and operating questions; Four-Corner Commons raises road, rail, fire, and commercial-interface questions. Real implementation requires specialist studies, public engagement, and statutory procedures. [source:SRC-2026-BJ-GH-QUAL-PREANNOUNCEMENT] [depth:risk_missing_data]

Diagrams, layouts, and spatial data are deterministically generated locally. Two clearly labelled AI-generated atmosphere images communicate human-scale experience only; they are not site photographs, engineering locations, or dimensional evidence. No third-party photographs, maps, marks, or portraits are embedded. Official sources are linked as method evidence; neighbouring proposals are used only for theme-gap statistics, without copying their text, figures, or mechanisms.
