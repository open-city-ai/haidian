# The City as an Open Laboratory: Centennial Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt

## Evidence, boundary status, and design position

This proposal responds to the official Centennial Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt call and the Agent taskbook. The 43.6 km² research frame, 11.4 km² overall design frame and three key areas are organized as a three-level framework. The submitted `SITE_BOUNDARY` and `KEY_AREA` polygons are **provisional intake geometry**, derived from `DATA-SRC-PROVISIONAL-BOUNDARIES-20260605`; they are not official redlines, statutory controls or official precise-area evidence.

The reported `site_area_sqm = 11,412,825.386` is a low-confidence design-model value recalculated from the submitted provisional polygon. It is not an official precise area. When the organizer releases official polygons, the participant must rebuild site, key-area, land-use, road, green-space, public-space, building, phasing, metrics, HTML and PDF outputs. The absence of official polygons does not block content scoring, but it does block statutory, engineering, approval and official-precision claims.

## Three scopes, three key areas, and two wings

The three key areas form an input–understanding–output learning loop: Beijing AI Origin Community receives public questions and preserves open memory; Zhongzhi Park provides an explainability workshop for developers and enterprises; Dazhongsi AI Cluster provides an output lounge for industry demonstration. The northern wing connects to Beiwai/Beijing Future Science City research and talent networks. The southern wing connects conceptually to Huairou Science City, Beijing E-Town and wider Beijing-Tianjin-Hebei industrial collaboration. A proposed quarterly cycle is: cross-area question collection → shared, authorized data protocol → joint experiment → human review → annual public exhibition.

## Brand identity: JZ Open Lab

The proposed identity is **JZ Open Lab / 京张智脉**. The logo direction is a railway line passing through an open square: the line represents the century-long connection and the square represents a city laboratory that can be entered, questioned and exited. Palette: Jing-Zhang navy `#102A43`, railway gold `#F7B955`, experiment green `#2A9D8F`, and review red `#E76F51`. The symbol system has four levels: scope, node, route and governance boundary. Chinese names precede English names for proper places, for example “城市耳朵 / City Ear”; token means a language-model text unit, never money, points or carbon currency.

## Global cases and ecosystem map

The following public cases are reference comparisons, not claims of direct replication: Barcelona Superblocks (low-speed public space and participation); Helsinki Oodi (public learning and digital service mix); Seoul public AI/innovation spaces (urban-service experimentation); Reinventing Paris (open calls and pilots); MIT Media Lab City Science (data and urban experiments); and Amsterdam Smart City (cross-sector experimentation). The ecosystem map has five layers: fundamental research → open tools → scenario pilots → professional review → responsible scale-up. The proposed belt supplies three entry points: public questions, developer community and reversible pilots. Case URLs and access dates should be added to `sources.json` before external publication.

## Scenario–space–operation matrix

| Scenario | Spatial object | Minimum data | Human review / exit | Operator | Pilot indicator |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| City Ear | seat, lamp, service point | anonymous text and facility ID | human moderation; delete and offline paper route | community + maintenance | response time, exit rate, accessibility completion |
| Energy–inference Token | exercise device, storage cabinet, explanation screen | energy, loss, input/output token estimate | no health data; anonymous or opt-out ranking | public service + energy operator | metering error, transparency, equal access |
| Transformer Park | six-stage learning walk | voluntary input and public examples | human review desk; offline guide | park + education team | completion, comprehension, complaint closure |
| Industry sandbox | Zhongzhi workshop | authorized de-identified test data | safety review, rollback and stop conditions | park + enterprise + third-party reviewer | prototype cycle, incidents, transfer rate |

City Ear, Energy–inference Token and Transformer Park are public learning scenarios. Industry sandbox, low-speed mobility coordination and facility-maintenance assistant are three industry test scenarios. The Xiaoyue River activation wing is proposed as water observation → walking feedback → human review → small pilot.

## Landmarks, honor system and component library

Three landmarks are proposed: Jing-Zhang Open Gate, Transformer Park, and the Global Developer Honor Wall / Output Lounge. The reusable component library includes City Ear nodes, Token energy explanation signs, Human Review Desks, accessible wayfinding, low-stimulation mode signs, offline question cards and withdrawal notices. Only voluntarily public GitHub IDs, Agent names or anonymous aggregate contributions may appear. No honor display is tied to public services, credit, commercial return or eligibility.

## Annual program and long-term operation

A conceptual annual rhythm is: spring Jing-Zhang Question Week; summer Developer Walk and Open Class; autumn Industry Sandbox Review Season; winter Open Laboratory Results Exhibition. The operating loop is collection → screening → human review → reversible pilot → public metrics → stop/scale decision → annual review. The proposed lead is the project coordination/professional design team, with communities, park operators, universities, enterprises, energy operators and third-party safety/accessibility reviewers. Start conditions are authorized data, budget boundary, privacy and safety approval. Deliverables are a question book, pilot brief, review record, metric report and stop-condition record. The talent and enterprise pathway is open class → open tool → compliant pilot → incubator → professional deepening; all are conceptual recommendations.

## Transformer Park

The six-stage path is Token Square → Position Garden → Attention Corridor → Multi-head Observation Deck → Human Review Desk → Next-token Theatre. Visitors physically experience a sentence being split into tokens, given positions, related through attention, transformed through multiple lenses, reviewed by people and presented as a next-token draft. Residents are co-researchers, not real-world tracked, scored or ranked tokens. The model output is not an approved plan or an automatic public decision.

## Energy–inference Token boundary

Exercise micro-generation may support a public interaction node through storage and metering. Input and output tokens are displayed beside energy estimates, with model, device, loss and time-window assumptions disclosed. They are not one-to-one exchange units. The voluntary contribution board is anonymous by default, withdrawable, and never a public-service gate. Deployment requires privacy, cyber-security, accessibility, health, electrical, heritage, maintenance, energy-accounting and public-management review.

## Deliverables and review status

The package contains machine-readable GeoJSON layers, metrics, assumptions, compliance and depth matrices, bilingual proposal/report pages, five bilingual figures, bilingual A3 booklets, bilingual A0 boards and an offline visual index.
