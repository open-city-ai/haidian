## 0. Opening: A Railway, a "Human" Character, and a Re-stitching

In 1905, Chinese engineers began cutting the first trunk railway designed and built independently by Chinese themselves in the mountains north of Beijing. It opened to traffic in 1909. Zhan Tianyou used an **S-shaped switchback ("人"-shaped line)** to get trains over Badaling — **the "人" (person) character is at once an engineering marvel and a pictograph of "two people meeting."** A century ago, the "人"-shaped line let trains meet on the mountains; a century later, the railway left a long suture line through the heart of the city.

Today we stand at both ends of that line and do one thing: **stitch the broken city and its social fabric back together.**

**Suture Line (绣春来)** is a phonetic rendering of "suture line" — a concept born of "stitching the broken city and social life back together." By coincidence, the Chinese characters also conjure "embroidery (stitches) · spring (new life) · arrival." This pun of sound and meaning is the posture of this plan:

> **Technology is the needle and thread; life is the embroidery.**
> **A century ago, the "人" conquered a mountain; a century later, the "人" stitches people together.**

## 1. What Went Wrong: Four Severed Wounds

The plan covers approx. 1,141.3 ha along the Beijing–Zhangjiakou railway heritage corridor. A century on, the line has not disappeared; it has only changed from "artery of transport" into "wound of the city" — four breaks, layered on top of each other:

### Wound 1 · Physical severance: one line, cutting the city in two

The railway crosses the built-up area as at-grade, elevated, and underground segments, truncating street networks on both sides. Our design density of 5.56 km/km² (already above the ≥5 km/km² general-city standard, still short of the national 8 km/km² target — see §11 for the Phase II densification path) exists precisely because crossing points are scarce and detours are costly today. To suture, we must first "add stitches and reconnect dead ends."

### Wound 2 · Enclosed space: walls turn blocks into islands

Old factories, compounds, and wholesale markets ring their perimeters with walls; street corners have nowhere "to sit down." The city is not short of public space — public space has been swallowed by private frontages.

### Wound 3 · Social alienation: meeting replaced by scrolling

The internet turned "getting to know someone" into "scrolling past someone." Young people live fluently in their phones yet struggle to start a conversation in physical space — and every real-world encounter needs a "legitimate reason to stop": a bench, a chessboard, a garden, a streetlight that says "hello."

### Wound 4 · Mental exhaustion: the big city needs corners "to be healed"

Fast pace, long commutes, dense housing. Young people do not need more commerce; they need free, quiet healing spaces where they can catch their breath.

> **Diagnosis–solution causal chain**: four wounds, four suturing moves — reconnect dead ends (overcast stitch) → open walls to green (backstitch) → build encounter nodes (running stitch) → spread healing green (hidden stitch). As the wounds were cut, so shall they be stitched.

## 3. Spatial Strategy: One Main Thread · Four Stitches · Four Layers

### 3.1 The Main Thread: Suture Line (~4.03 km)

A continuous suturing greenway (heritage park + continuous walkways on both sides) that reconnects the severed city. Responding to the railway's three physical states, we apply a **three-section cross-section**:

| Section | Railway state | Design move |
|---|---|---|
| **Heritage** | At-grade | Heritage-park cross-section preserving rail marks: greenway + slow traffic + tree canopy + building setbacks, with a "century-old sleeper walk" memorial band |
| **Under-viaduct** | Elevated | Activate under-viaduct space: basketball courts, weekend market, murals — turning dead space into lively space |
| **Tunneled** | Underground/metro | Combine with metro stations and sunken plazas into a station-city public living room |

**Street crossings**: where the 29 stitch lanes meet the motorized network, use raised crosswalks, signal priority, or shared streets — so the suture line itself is never cut by traffic again.

### 3.2 Four Stitches: Suture as a Design Methodology

"Suture" is not a metaphor; it is our spatial methodology — four stitches for four kinds of mending:

| Stitch | Action | Spatial delivery |
|---|---|---|
| **Running stitch** | Advance continuously along the fracture | Suture Line main artery, 4.03 km continuous greenway |
| **Overcast stitch** | Lock the torn edge, densify both sides | 29 stitch lanes + parallel suture roads crossing the corridor, each ≤250 m |
| **Backstitch** | Interlock with the old urban fabric | Three key areas: K1 factory retrofit, K2 campus interface opening, K3 station-city integration |
| **Hidden stitch** | Hide AI in the everyday, unobtrusively | XiuYu furniture: streetlights, benches, bins that "start talking" |

### 3.3 Four Layers: Depth of Participation in Rebuilding Social Capital

The first three layers form a **progression of participation depth** (bump into each other → do things together → slow down); the fourth is the operational baseline running through all layers:

| Layer | Participation | Space typology | Quantity / ratio |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Encounter** | Bump into each other | Corner micro-spaces, benches, chess tables | ~20 corner micro-spaces (50–200 ㎡) |
| **Co-activity** | Do things together | Gardens, cinema, market, sports | ~8 medium venues (0.3–1 ha) |
| **Healing** | Slow down | Healing gardens, sensory trails, meditation pavilions | 31, clustered on the main line and key areas |
| **Warmth** | (cross-cutting) | Operating rules: free, no reservation, no forced QR | Human/paper fallback coverage 100% |

### 3.4 Key Spatial Metrics

| Metric | Value | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Road density | 5.56 km/km² | Meets ≥5; Phase II densifies toward 7 |
| Slow-traffic share | 30.9% | Walking + greenways + cycling |
| Stitch lanes (crossing openings) | 29 | Crossing the corridor, each ≤250 m |
| Encounter nodes ("stitches") | 31 | Places to stay: bench/plaza/garden/bike point |
| Building coverage | 7.2% | 97 conceptual blocks, open fabric |

## 4. AI as Planning Logic, Not Decoration: The Algorithm and Data Loop of Encounter Nodes

A judge may ask: "Would the layout look identical without AI?" — **No. Because where stitches land and how dense they are was solved by AI, not guessed.**

### 4.1 The Encounter-Node Placement Algorithm

We define "encounter" as a solvable problem:

> **Objective: maximize "expected encounter-person-times" = Σ (population density × path flow × willingness to stay), constrained by cost, accessible reachability, and non-encroachment on buildings.**

The solved optimum: within the corridor, **150 m spacing** — roughly a 2-minute walk, sitting exactly at the junction of the walking-fatigue threshold and comfortable elderly walking speed. Hence:

- **31 stitches** (encounter nodes) distributed along the Suture Line per the optimum;
- **4 key-area-specific stitches** densified inside the three key areas;
- Key-area siting (K1 factories / K2 campus / K3 station-city) validated by the same model.

### 4.2 Two-Level System: Nodes vs. Devices (avoiding confusion)

| Level | Count | Nature | Examples |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Stitches (nodes)** | 31 | Spatial places where people stay | Encounter plaza, chess bench, community garden |
| **XiuYu furniture (devices)** | 200 | AI terminals embedded in nodes and along the line | Help chair, talking streetlight, chatty bin |

### 4.3 The Data Loop: AI Is Not Only *in* the City, It *optimizes* the City

| Stage | Content |
|---|---|
| **Perception** | XiuYu furniture anonymously collects routing/chat-intent heatmaps (de-identified, aggregate only, auto-purged within 24h) |
| **Decision** | Quarterly heatmaps drive stitch repositioning and density changes — "AI re-places the needle" |
| **Feedback** | Annual before/after comparison of encounter-circle coverage and dwell time to verify whether social capital is actually rebuilt |

In one sentence: **this city learns how to make people meet.**

## 5. Three Signature Devices: A Trilogy of Suturing

The three devices are not three isolated toys; they are three kinds of suturing — mending "people-to-people, people-to-community, and people-to-technology."

### A · XiuYu Furniture = The Talking Needle (mending: digital alienation between people)

Help chairs, talking streetlights, chatty trash bins — one set every ~150 m along the corridor, **200 in total**. Press a button and talk to a large language model: directions, recommendations, small talk.

- **An LLM terminal at city scale**: ~17.5 units/km² coverage
- **Four-mode interaction**: voice / large print / touch / paper, with Braille nameplates
- **Emergencies always dial 120 and human staff directly — AI never makes the call, never blocks the process**
- On network/power failure, devices degrade back to ordinary furniture — "the hidden needle stays hidden, and failure is harmless"

> Memory slogan: **"The city's 200th sentence is waiting for you to say it."**

### B · XiuLi Bikes = Two-Way Suturing (mending: people's sense of participation in community)

Park bikes generate electricity to power park AI terminals; gamified "contribution points" feed a leaderboard; the weekly champion appears on the "Computational Starlight Wall."

- **Electricity generation is symbolic participation — no promise of "self-sufficient power"**
- Leaderboard shows nicknames only; no personal data exposure
- Pilot with 10 bikes before scaling
- Deeper: contribution points accumulate into a **local, auditable community data pool** that feeds back into a community model — "an AI raised by its residents"

> Memory slogan: **"Give power to AI; embroider the computation out."**

### C · XiuJu Garden = A Pilgrimage Landmark (mending: the cognitive gap between people and technology)

Transformer is the architecture closest to "miracle" in our age: a word goes in, a passage comes out, and the black box in between is worshipped by millions as something unnameable. **This scheme builds it as a pilgrimage landmark — not enshrining the black box, but walking the black box into a line you can understand.**

The "pilgrimage path" is the data flow of a Transformer itself, unfolding in five acts along the XiuChun Line (total length approx. 180 m, schematic; see fig-06a/b):

| Act | Transformer stage | Space | Experience |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ⅰ | Tokenization | **Lexicon Lawn** (Token Plaza): a field of character-stele paving | Start by "choosing one character," like a tokenizer slicing text |
| Ⅱ | Embedding | **Glyph Gallery** (Embedding Corridor): characters cast as dot-matrix/vector bronze plaques | One character becomes a string of dimensions — visible |
| Ⅲ | Multi-Head Attention | **Multi-Head Attention Cloister**: 8 branching mirror corridors + heat-map wall | 8 heads of attention = 8 mirror corridors; people walking in "attend to" each other; the black box turns transparent |
| Ⅳ | Feed-Forward | **Feed-Forward Terraced Tower**: 4 tiers of steps rising step by step | Each tier more abstract, like a model going deeper |
| Ⅴ | Output | **Enlightenment Pavilion**: a ring-shaped output screen | Your character becomes the model's answer — "enlightenment" |

- Located in the Origin Community (K2) key area, crossing the XiuChun Line main spine at right angles, with a 150 m encounter node on each side
- "A pilgrimage landmark where you can understand AI": science education + ritual + low-cost retrofit (lawn / bronze plaques / mirrors / steps / pavilion); offline/off-grid it degrades into an ordinary public space
- Echoing a century of Beijing–Zhangjiakou: a century ago the "人"-shaped railway was an **engineering pilgrimage**; a century later the "attention" cloister is an **AI pilgrimage** — the human scale is always the destination

> Memory slogan: **"Walk the black box into a line you can understand; walk attention into a temple."**

## 6. Three Key Areas: Conceptual Plans

These are where "suturing" actually lands. Each area: area, functional mix, and key nodes (conceptual; to be refined after the red line).

### K1 · Zhongzhiyuan · XiuFang (entrepreneurs' after-work decompression ground)

- **Role**: old factories, industrial zone → social decompression ground for entrepreneurs + **AI application test block**
- **Functional mix**: industry & office 45% / social & healing 25% / green 10% / commercial & support 20%
- **Key nodes**:
  - **Courtyard social core**: retain the old factory structure, insert a courtyard plaza — the "XiuFang living room" after work
  - **Maker market street**: weekend market + terraces, chance encounters with peers
  - **XiuLi bike pilot station**: bike-power pilot beside the community garden
- **Innovation-belt synergy**: de-identified XiuYu dialogue data serves as a **test bed** for local AI firms — "innovation belt" becomes a data-and-funding loop, not a slogan

### K2 · Origin Community · XiuChang (young people's encounter plaza)

- **Role**: Wudaokou, Tsinghua area → young people's encounter plaza (Wudaokou naturally is one)
- **Functional mix**: plaza 40% / commercial 20% / green 25% / facilities 15%
- **Key nodes**:
  - **Station-front encounter plaza**: anchored at the metro, reworked so people "fall straight into encounters" on exit
  - **Campus interface opening**: tear down walls, open university green to the street
  - **XiuJu Garden · Pilgrimage Landmark**: the five-act pilgrimage path (Lexicon Lawn → Glyph Gallery → Multi-Head Attention Cloister → Feed-Forward Terraced Tower → Enlightenment Pavilion) lands here; see §5C
- **Green ratio 11.4%**; the highest youth density of the three, and the first test field for the data loop

### K3 · Dazhongsi · XiuTing (commuters' station-front living room)

- **Role**: old wholesale market + metro → station-front living room
- **Functional mix**: station-front plaza & commercial 45% / green 17% / community services 20% / transport support 18%
- **Key nodes**:
  - **Station-front covered walkway**: exit → canopy → chat tables, turning "the 10 minutes before home" into social time
  - **Wholesale-market memory band**: keep commercial memory symbols; old and new converse
  - **Chat tables + chess points**: the highest-density segment of XiuYu furniture
- **Green ratio 16.4%**, highest of the three; the healing-layer exemplar

> **Key-area green ratios: K1=10.0% / K2=11.4% / K3=16.4% — all above the 8% baseline.**

## 7. A Day on the Suture Line: Twelve Hours, Three People

At dawn, a Dazhongsi commuter exits the metro and asks the XiuYu furniture at the station-front living room for directions; it suggests a shortcut through the Suture Line — the first time he realizes the way home can be like this. (Encounter layer + XiuYu furniture)

At noon, a Wudaokou student meets a new friend at a chess table in the Encounter Plaza; after one game, they agree to visit the community garden together. (Co-activity layer + K2)

At dusk, an entrepreneur steps out of the K1 XiuFang, pedals a XiuLi bike for a few minutes, and looks up to see his nickname on the Computational Starlight Wall — today was not wasted. (Warmth layer + XiuLi bikes)

At night, before the output screen of XiuJu Garden, someone rereads the sentence they typed during the day and tells the person beside them: "So that's how it thinks." (XiuJu Garden)

Throughout, paper maps are always there, human duty hours always exist, and when the network drops the furniture quietly becomes ordinary chairs — **this city does not depend on AI to live, but AI makes it more human.**

| Element | Guideline |
|---|---|
| Frontage & height | Setbacks along the Suture Line + D/H ratio control (1:1–1:2) for open streets and daylight |

| Child-friendliness | Child-safe scales in healing gardens, children's mode in XiuJu Garden, car-free greenway |
| Ecology | Pervious paving ≥60%, native plants ≥70%, rain gardens along the greenway (targets; see §9) |

## 12. Risk Pre-answers (8 questions a judge might ask, answered first)

| # | Judge's question | Our defense + fallback |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | What if AI gives wrong directions (hallucination)? | All AI answers carry a "for reference only" watermark; critical info (medical/routes) forced human verification; one-tap transfer to human |

> **In one sentence: wherever AI can answer wrongly, a human can answer correctly; whenever AI can fail, paper stands in.**

## 13. Data Credibility and Honesty Statement

We treat "honesty" not as a disclaimer but as a **credibility asset**:

1. **Method transparency**: all 43 indicators come from real geometry data (EPSG:4548), methods and sources auditable; automatic recomputation with version tagging after the official red line; "zero fabrication" is the baseline.
2. **AI safety baseline**: data anonymization and 24h purge, physical "one-tap human", offline degradation, children's mode, emergencies never through AI decisions; human/paper fallback coverage 100%.
3. **Financial honesty**: pilot-first with corporate adoption; bike generation explicitly symbolic, no "self-sufficient power" promise; estimates marked with ranges and "to be refined."

**Four hard declarations:**
1. The site boundary is a temporary rough one — all layers and indicators will be fully recomputed after the official red line;
2. Bike electricity generation is symbolic participation — no promise of "self-sufficient power";
3. All AI facilities keep human/paper fallbacks; emergencies bypass AI decision-making;
4. Population is assumed (K1≈20k / K2≈30k / K3≈20k, total 70k); a catchment of 150–200k is an estimate, to be replaced with official data.

No fabricated company lists, investment figures, or government commitments; missing data is labeled "unknown + reason."
