Brand and overall concept

“JZ Open Lab / 京张智脉” uses a railway line passing through an open square as its logo direction, expressing historical connection and a city laboratory that can be entered, questioned and exited.

Three scopes, three key areas and two wings

Beijing AI Origin Community receives public questions; Zhongzhi Park supports explainability and industry experiments; Dazhongsi AI Cluster provides an output lounge. The northern wing conceptually connects to Beiwai and Beijing Future Science City. The southern wing connects to Huairou Science City, Beijing E-Town and Beijing-Tianjin-Hebei collaboration through a quarterly question—authorized data—experiment—human review—exhibition cycle.

Global cases and ecosystem map

Public comparative references include Barcelona Superblocks, Helsinki Oodi, Seoul public innovation spaces, Reinventing Paris, MIT City Science and Amsterdam Smart City. The proposed ecosystem has five layers: research, open tools, scenario pilots, professional review and responsible scale-up. Full URLs and use limits are recorded in sources.json.

Scenario, space and operation

City Ear, Energy–inference Token, Transformer Park and Industry Sandbox specify spatial objects, minimum data, human review, exit path, operator and pilot indicators. Industry sandbox, low-speed mobility coordination and facility-maintenance assistant are three industry test scenarios. The Xiaoyue River activation wing is proposed as water observation → walking feedback → human review → small pilot.

Landmarks and component system

Proposed landmarks are Jing-Zhang Open Gate, Transformer Park and Global Developer Honor Wall / Output Lounge. Components include City Ear nodes, token-energy explanation signs, human review desks, accessible wayfinding, low-stimulation signs, offline question cards and withdrawal notices. Honor display is voluntary, anonymous by default and never linked to public services, credit or commercial return.

Annual program and long-term operation

Spring Question Week, summer Developer Walk and Open Class, autumn Industry Sandbox Review Season and winter Results Exhibition form a proposed annual rhythm. The operation loop is collection → screening → human review → reversible pilot → public metrics → stop/scale decision → annual review. Start conditions, roles, deliverables, risks and stop conditions are described in the bilingual proposal.

Transformer Park and Energy–inference Token

The six stages are Token Square, Position Garden, Attention Corridor, Multi-head Observation Deck, Human Review Desk and Next-token Theatre. Exercise micro-generation may support a public interaction node through storage and metering; tokens remain language-model text units, never money or points. Ranking is voluntary, anonymous by default and withdrawable.
