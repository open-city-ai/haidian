## Concept: Open-Source City Operating System

The Jing-Zhang Railway is the origin of Chinese independent innovation; Zhongguancun is the origin of Chinese open source. OPEN-JZ merges the two: the city is a continuously evolving codebase — public space, industrial space and governance mechanisms are versionable modules; urban renewal = commit, review, merge, revert. Citizens are reviewers, developers are contributors, government is maintainer. Mapping to the five taskbook functions: KERNEL (autonomous compute/model/standards → Zhongzhiyuan), REPO-NET (open-source collaboration → Origin Community), APPS (scenario opening & testing → Dazhongsi), RUNTIME (public space & life scenarios → MAIN green corridor), GOVERNANCE-PROTOCOL (three stitching lines).

## Spatial Structure: MAIN + REPO + PROTOCOL

- **MAIN line**: Jing-Zhang Heritage Park green corridor, approx. 9 km (recomputed from geometry under EPSG:4548, see recalculation log), slow-traffic first, heritage narrative, AI experience.
- **TRAIN-REPO (Zhongzhiyuan, 192.1 ha)**: computing clusters, model training, AI standards & safety governance; landmark "Zhongzhi Computing Tower" whose night light column shows training-task status.
- **COMMUNITY-REPO (Origin Community, 104.3 ha)**: open-source square, incubator blocks, release hall, talent apartments; Qinghuayuan station heritage transformed into "Origin Station · Open-Source Monument Square".
- **APP-REPO (Dazhongsi, 72.0 ha)**: smart-terminal experience street, content consumption, data-element exchange; "AI Bell Tower Square" recreates bell-drum imagery with data-flow soundscapes.
- **PROTOCOL**: three east-west stitching lines for data flow, scenario testing and ethics governance.

## Two Wings & Regional Collaboration

West wing (Zhongguancun tech-services: venture capital, professional services) connects to the community repo via the middle stitching line for incubation-to-financing loops; East wing (Xiaohe River scenario-empowerment: AI+consumption, AI+culture) connects to the app repo via the southern stitching line for test-to-operation loops. Regional links: Beilong community & Future Science City (north), Huairou Science City (east, compute collaboration), E-Town & Jing-Jin-Ji network (south). ## Land Use, Buildings & Renewal

Five conceptual land-use bands along the green corridor (R&D, education/research, mixed-use, residential, green) plus three key-area anchors (AI-DATA / AI-OS / AI-APP), full coverage without overlap (geometry/land_use.geojson). Building footprints are conceptual (15 buildings, building_footprint_area_sqm recomputed in EPSG:4548); no building-area commitments are made given absent statutory controls. Renewal principle: preserve railway-heritage and heritage stations (locked), renew low-efficiency plots in the three key areas, add new stitching nodes and public spaces.

## Transport, Utilities & Public Services

Station-city integration around existing rail stations (locked existing rail, unchanged); three protocol stitching lines plus two slow-traffic corridors forming a horizontal-stitch/vertical-spine conceptual network (geometry/roads.geojson, 5 lines); new infrastructure: edge compute, distributed energy, urban sensors, digital-twin base along MAIN; public services: evaluation/intermediate-testing/standards platforms, talent apartments, sports/care/education facilities, AI health pods, AI education lab belt.

## Blue-Green & Public Space

Green corridor (MAIN, geometry/green_space.geojson#GRN-001) plus three node greens as a "one belt, three pearls" network; green ratio 17.4% (design-suggested scope, not statutory). Public space: 3 pilgrimage landmarks + 8 scenario nodes as polygon features (see above).
