Public rights and responsibilities

Anyone can request a pause; human, paper and phone routes run alongside optional AI. Timing is a calibration starting point, not an administrative commitment.

rights fields 100%
Rights action Initiator / receiving role Proposed sequence (to calibrate) Protection and equivalent path
Pause request User, neighbour or maintenance worker; counter/paper/phone/digital Confirm same shift; set N0 before independent review Ordinary service continues; accessible bypass and paper explanation remain
Complaint / temporary remedy Public-space operator + accountable owner Aim for 24h remedy and 72h closure or delay note No AI-first requirement; anonymous/group records
Independent review / appeal Reviewer without supplier conflict Aim for five-working-day opinion; N0 during dispute Paper materials; rights holder can withdraw/takedown
Front-line worker protection Repair lead receives stop-work notice; asset holder protects Immediate stop allowed; shift record without personal blame Safe tools, training, reasonable hours, protected report
Night / non-digital / compensation Night operator + community counter 22:00–06:00 equivalence and paid testing calibrated first Paper/phone/human routes; time/transport compensation proposed

Five actions with fields do not mean time limits, staffing or compensation are approved; ordinary mode remains until the field protocol is signed.
