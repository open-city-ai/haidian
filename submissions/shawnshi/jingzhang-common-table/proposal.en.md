# JINGZHANG COMMON TABLE｜京张共食线

Jingzhang Common Table is not a collection of “AI restaurants,” and it does not decide what anyone should eat. It links food-technology research, shared production, small enterprises, market exchange, low-speed delivery, surplus separation, and productive landscapes as an urban operating system that is **traceable, manually controllable, pausable, and able to exit**. Its canonical production-to-soil chain contains seven handoff gates:

`G1 Question intake → G2 Ingredient/object receipt → G3 Shared-kitchen production → G4 Market exchange → G5 Surplus separation → G6 Material receipt → G7 Soil return`

`Source → Plan → Cook → Exchange → Cycle` is a five-stage public shorthand for material movement; it does not replace G1–G7. Only stabilized return material that passes soil and contamination tests, use review, and landscape and environmental approval may follow the conditional arrow into a later sourcing cycle or planting trial. Material without approval leaves through compliant handling rather than being drawn back into a false loop.

A separate seven-stage enterprise pathway runs in parallel: `intake → pair → assess → co-create → market test → venture or exit → conditional return`. It explains how teams and small operators may enter, test, trade, pause, graduate, or leave; company formation, financing, and continued operation are not presumed.

Every boundary crossing creates a Common Table ledger recording the object or batch, provenance and rights, time and place, accountable operator, necessary temperature, allergen, and destination fields, human sign-off, exceptions, and exit state. The ledger tracks food, equipment, and materials rather than people by default. AI may propose schedules, matches, alerts, and translations; qualified or authorized people retain every decision on food safety, allergens, edibility, recall, material destination, and public release.[source:NIST-AI-RMF] [depth:risk_missing_data]

## Coordinated Research Area: Industry and Future City Research

### One Common Pantry Spine

The Common Pantry Spine is a conceptual evidence corridor linking the public edge of the Jingzhang Railway Heritage Park with industry spaces on both sides. It is neither a food-production base nor a committed facility. Public walking, supply cycling, time-windowed delivery, serviceware return, and organic-material collection use legible but distinct paths. Ordinary access, accessible movement, and emergency use take priority; the public may see questions, tests, failures, and human accountability without crossing controlled-production boundaries.[data:geometry/roads.geojson#ROAD-001] [data:geometry/green_space.geojson#GREEN-001] [depth:traffic_rail_slow_parking]

### Four Haidian Technology Protocols

Haidian's published “1+X+1” industry structure places AI at the core, combines it with integrated-circuit design, health, industrial software, advanced scientific instruments, embodied intelligence, and related fields, and uses technology services to connect platforms, talent, space, and scenarios. Common Table converts that district context into a **validation vehicle at the intersection of AI and everyday urban services**, not a new food-industry park or institutional roster.[source:HAIDIAN-INDUSTRIAL-SYSTEM-2026] [source:HAIDIAN-DEVELOPMENT-PLAN-2026]

Beijing's government portal reported 37 universities, 92 national key laboratories, and 96 national research institutions in Haidian in its 2025 account, alongside a high share of Beijing's technology-contract output. The district statistical bulletin also records large-model, open-source computing-stack, RISC-V, and embodied-intelligence results. These are district-level capabilities; they do not show that any corridor organization has joined the proposal, that technology is free to use, or that a product is suitable for food contact.[source:HAIDIAN-OVERVIEW-2026] [source:HAIDIAN-STAT-BULLETIN-2025]

1. **Open-source reproducibility protocol:** publish model versions, data definitions, test scripts, scope, failure examples, and human review; do not publish data without rights or create personal dietary profiles.
2. **Perception and embodied-validation protocol:** use synthetic, public, or authorized data for machine vision, sensors, and robots only within segregated work zones; require emergency stops, human takeover, equipment-cleaning rules, and failure records.
3. **Operations-research and simulation protocol:** compare candidates for bookings, workstations, cold-chain windows, logistics, and resource scheduling; do not replace decisions on food safety, permits, recalls, or material destinations.
4. **Technology-transfer protocol:** retain evidence across `question intake → rights confirmation → controlled validation → compliant pilot production → market/first-order test → graduation or exit`, turning research output into an acceptably specified service rather than a one-off display.[source:HAIDIAN-EMBODIED-AI-2024] [source:HAIDIAN-TECH-TRANSFER-2026] [metric:haidian_technology_protocol_count]

### Five Capabilities to Validate, Not Five Buildings

The precedent review resolves into five shared capabilities: **research and pilot prototyping; licensed shared production and incubation; market and logistics validation; productive-landscape demonstration; and material-circularity and permit interfaces**. They are distributed across three nodes and the continuous corridor rather than translated into one new building per case. Operator readiness, booking demand, accountability, and a compliant pathway must be evidenced before capacity expands.[metric:operating_capability_count] [metric:key_table_count]

### Three Nodes

1. **Zhongzhiyuan Food Systems Lab Table:** controlled validation of standards, simulation, sensing, and robotics; qualified operators and professionals still decide food safety.
2. **AI Origin Commons Kitchen and Reproducibility Table:** open-source reproduction, shared pilot production, classes, operating support, and staged incubation; a qualified kitchen lead controls operations.
3. **Dazhongsi World Market and First-Order Table:** batch-based market tests, a public common table, railway food culture, and first-order validation for small enterprises; no consumer profiling or opaque discriminatory pricing.

### Two Accountable Chains

- **Zhongguancun technology-to-enterprise chain:** presents research, computing, instruments, design, intellectual property, procurement, first orders, and operating advice as interfaces to be matched. No university, laboratory, enterprise, or platform is represented as a committed partner.
- **Xiaoyuehe food-to-material chain:** connects question intake, time-windowed delivery, reusable serviceware, surplus separation, approved receipt, and productive-landscape trials as an auditable path without assuming an existing treatment facility.[depth:overall_spatial_structure]

## Overall Design Area: Urban Renewal and Regulatory-Plan-Level Urban Design

### Three Access Layers and Four Separated Flows

Each node uses the same three-layer prototype: **controlled back-of-house → observable interface → public frontage**. The controlled back accommodates licensed production, equipment tests, batch receipt, and material holding. The observable interface provides read-only data, transparent observation, training, and staffed explanation. The public frontage provides ordinary passage, non-commercial seating, markets, and common tables. Nine building polygons test combinations of these layers across three nodes; they are not nine proposed buildings.[metric:spatial_access_layer_count] [metric:building_cluster_count] [data:geometry/buildings.geojson#BLDG-001]

Visitor, staff, food-logistics, and residual-material flows receive distinct entries, time windows, containers, and responsibility ledgers. Where crossings cannot be avoided, physical separation, cleaning changeover, and human release are prerequisites. The public may observe the problem, method, and failure but may not cross into licensed production, robotic work, or contaminated-material interfaces.[data:geometry/roads.geojson#ROAD-001] [depth:traffic_rail_slow_parking]

### Eight-Field Facility Card and Five-Role Governance

Before any shared facility opens, its public facility card must state eight fields: capability name; hygiene or cleanliness class; minimum batch; booking and fee; accountable operator; intellectual-property and data rights; residual-material destination; and stop condition. An incomplete card may reserve space but cannot count as an operating capability.[metric:facility_card_required_field_count] [depth:renewal_project_list]

The governance matrix separates at least five roles: asset steward, platform operator, licensed food operator, research and evaluation party, and statutory or professional reviewer. Human vetoes remain distinct for food safety, fire, equipment, environment, and data. AI may organize evidence, simulate options, and flag anomalies; it receives no authority to issue permits, decide edibility, or manage incidents.[metric:responsibility_role_count] [source:NIST-AI-RMF]
