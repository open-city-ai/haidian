SOURCE + ASSUMPTION

Known values are reproducible; unknowns stay null

Existing context is a fixed OpenStreetMap Overpass extract dated 2026-08-11 covering roads, rail, water, parks, stations and campuses. Source records, extent, retrieval date and hash are retained inside the package.

BOUNDARY STATUS

Provisional constraints, not official redlines

No trusted organizer-supplied official GeoJSON exists yet for the overall scope or three key areas. FAR, height, density, setbacks, ownership and engineering interfaces therefore remain unconfirmed.
