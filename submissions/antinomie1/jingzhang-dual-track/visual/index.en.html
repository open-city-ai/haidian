13. Sources

  • SITE-PACKAGE — Project brief, enums, allowed design space, ranges, schemas and visual style recommendations.
  • SOURCE-REGISTRY — Usability registry distinguishing formal-ready, background-only, provisional-only and needs-review materials.
  • PROCESSED-FACT-PACK — Agent-readable navigation layer for scopes, required tasks, source-use boundaries and missing-data checklist; not a new authority source.
  • BOUNDARY-SOURCE — Provisional site boundary (PROV-SITE-001) used for generation, visualization and intake self-check; not an official redline.
  • KEY-AREA-SOURCE — Provisional key-area polygons PROV-KEY-001/002/003, kept unchanged from upstream per community consensus; positions not station-anchored (Issue #1029).
  • OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT — Official announcement: project scope levels, areas, key areas, design tasks, language and deliverable context.
  • AGENT-TASKBOOK — Agent-facing open-call principles, six required tasks, scenario/branding/operation requirements and the unified boundary clause.
  • ISSUE-1029 — Independent community verification that PROV-KEY-003 is an area-fit placeholder whose centroid lies near Beijing North Station, about 2.26 km from Dazhongsi metro station; consensus is to keep upstream provisional geometry unchanged and disclose.
  • MOHURD-URBAN-DESIGN-MEASURES — Urban-design purpose, public space and city-character control references (height, volume, style, colour, skyline, interface).
  • MOHURD-CONTROL-DETAILED-PLANNING — Regulatory detailed planning depth and permission-management boundary language; used to separate known conditions, design suggestions and pending confirmations.
  • MNR-LAND-USE-CLASSIFICATION-GUIDE — Land-use classification terminology; land_use_code semantics follow the project enum which mirrors this guide. The local snapshot holds the publication notice; the full classification table lives in an unattached .doc appendix and is disclosed as a data gap.
  • GENERATIVE-AI-INTERIM-MEASURES — Article-scoped governance background for generative-AI public services (scope, unlawful-content handling, complaint channel, security assessment/filing boundary); not a site-specific legal opinion.
  • BARRIER-FREE-ENVIRONMENT-LAW — Accessibility and human-fallback baseline, Article 39 applied only to the expressly listed public-service contexts (medical, social security, finance, living payments).
  • ELDERLY-SMART-TECH-PLAN-2020-45 — Background policy context: parallel traditional and smart service channels for elderly high-frequency scenarios; national policy, not local implementation facts.

14. Assumptions

  • A-CONTROLS-001 — Detailed regulatory controls (FAR, height, density, green ratio, setbacks), road redlines, ownership, municipal utilities and engineering constraints are not public in the site package and must be confirmed from official attachments before any statutory use.
  • A-BOUNDARY-001 — The submitted site boundary and the three key-area polygons are the repository's provisional rough geometry (PROV-SITE-001 / PROV-KEY-001/002/003, official_boundary=false, boundary_precision=provisional_rough), derived from announcement text boundaries and area fitting in EPSG:4548.
  • A-KEY003-OFFSET-001 — Per community verification in Issue #1029, provisional polygon PROV-KEY-003 (Dazhongsi AI industry cluster) has its centroid near Beijing North Station, about 2.26 km from Dazhongsi metro station; the polygon is a sequential area-fit placeholder and is not station-anchored.
  • A-BUILDING-001 — Building footprints and the concept total floor area are conceptual massing derived from the submitted geometry with assumed floor counts; they are not statutory FAR, density, height or demolition/renovation conclusions.
  • A-CONCEPT-001 — All spatial moves, land-use allocations, scenario nodes, landmark plazas, event and operation arrangements are conceptual suggestions or reference schemes for professional teams to deepen; they are not approved planning, confirmed government action or implementation commitments.
  • A-RECALC-001 — All precision-sensitive metrics are recalculated from the submitted geometry in EPSG:4548; when official geometry is supplied, the full chain (geometry, metrics, figures, drawings, visual HTML, matrices and manifest hashes) is regenerated, not patched piecemeal.
