# Formal Narrative / 正式叙事摘要

The Jing-Zhang Civic Loop treats the heritage park as an existing civic spine and differentiates Zhongzhiyuan, AI Origin Community and Dazhongsi as test, collaboration and public-facing industry nodes. This revision adds official Beijing/Haidian evidence for the opened park, planning consultation, heritage controls, slow mobility, greenways, district statistics and existing talent services, plus five official international project cases. Facts, design responses and pending statutory GIS are kept separate.

京张共生回路把遗址公园视为既有公共脊，将众智园、AI 原点社区和大钟寺分别组织为测试、协作与产业公共接口。本轮新增公园、控规公示、文保、慢行、绿道、区级统计和既有人才服务等北京市/海淀区官方证据，并补充五个国外官方项目案例。事实、设计响应与待补法定 GIS 分开记录。

Geometry, metrics, compliance matrices, bilingual proposals, drawings and offline visual pages remain cross-checked deliverables. The heritage and regulatory constraint GeoJSON intentionally remains empty until official georeferenced polygons are supplied; this is a safety decision, not an omission to be filled by inference.
