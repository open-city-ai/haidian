# Jing-Zhang Yuan Shu |JING-ZHANG AI KERNEL

**Jing-Zhang for a Century, the Urban AI Public Core for the Global Community｜The Open Kernel for Urban AI**

This proposal does not view the "AI Innovation Belt" as a collection of buildings with a tech style, nor does it reduce the Jing-Zhang to a generic corridor connecting various parks; it is proposed as an **Urban AI Kernel** at the city level: it connects upward to universities, laboratories, computing power, and the global innovation network, and downward to enterprises, communities, public services, and real urban scenarios, completing the most critical validation, translation, governance, and public release before research enters the city. The core position of Jing-Zhang does not come from its scale or landmark status, but from it becoming the default interface for "how innovation enters the city." The core mechanism is called **City Pull Request (City PR)**: Issues raise real problems, Datasets specify available data, Sandboxes validate solutions within a limited space, Human Review retains professional and public oversight, Pilots conduct small-scale trials, Merge only enters stable operation, and Changelog publicly records changes, with Revert as necessary. Thus, "open source" is not a visual symbol but becomes a common agreement from space, industry, governance to cultural operations.

## Three-Level Scope Framework

The three layers are not three unconnected schemes, but a hierarchical convergence of the same issue from "regional ecology—urban structure—key nodes." The approximately 43.6 km² strategic research layer addresses how AI enterprises, talent, research, capital, public services, and living scenarios form a network; the 11.4 km² overall design layer addresses how the Jing-Zhang heritage park vitality belt becomes a continuous public backbone running north to south, and how it is connected east to west to integrate universities, parks, communities, and rail nodes; the three key area layers address how each core can form a space prototype that can be realistically used, operated, and validated. "AI Kernel" adopts a structure at the overall design level of "one bus, five interfaces, three cores and two wings, and multiple Commons": a **Kernel Bus** runs along the Jing-Zhang historical trajectory to form a city-level main bus; five **I/O Links** connect to research and development, services, communities, tracks, and public life on the east and west sides; the three cores are not three separate parks but rather the three key processors of the entire system, each responsible for validation and safety, open-source innovation and talent translation, and deployment release and global exchange; the two wings serve as directional networks for the service and scene empowerment of Zhongguancun Science and Technology and Xiaoyuehe, without fabricating boundaries when formal polygons are missing; the multiple Commons are a set of reusable, bookable, and verifiable prototype public facilities. This framework actively avoids going "from the master plan directly to the building form." The five layers are not five buildings but a public technology—city protocol—running through the Jing-Zhang cores and wings, giving Jing-Zhang the core role of "the city-level bottom interface for the Beijing AI innovation system." ## Overall Design Area: Urban Renewal and Regulatory-Plan-Level Urban Design

The overall design rewrites Urban Renewal from "demolition and construction projects" to a three-tiered action of "Reversible Layer—Update Layer—Structural Layer." The Reversible Layer can use lightweight facilities, signage, reservation mechanisms, events, and temporary testing to quickly validate; the Update Layer involves Public Space interfaces, first-floor integration, pedestrian integration, and the reuse of existing spaces, which must be combined with an investigation of ownership and the current condition of buildings; the Structural Layer involves roads, tracks, utilities, Development Intensity, and long-term land use, which can only enter formal control decisions after sufficient formal documentation. Currently, [data:geometry/buildings.geojson#BLDG-001] only expresses the conceptual footprint of ten lightweight AI Commons prototypes, [metric:building_footprint_area_sqm] only counts these new prototypes, does not infer the current buildings, and does not make a demolition list. [depth:retain_renovate_demolish]

Land Structure Map [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-001] is topologically closed using registered classification codes to ensure machine-checkability; the concepts on the map such as "AI R&D, green open spaces, industrial services, community services" are program bands, not approved plans. Development Intensity, Building Height, and setback distances are kept unknown because the `planning_limits` do not have sufficient data to support numerical controls. [standard:MNR-LAND-USE-CLASSIFICATION-GUIDE] [standard:MOHURD-CONTROL-DETAILED-PLANNING]. The architectural style is not uniform, but adopts a three-layer language of "industrial/railway memory material scale + readable digital infrastructure + reversible public structures," avoiding the use of high-cost designs to replace innovative ecology. [depth:height_massing_character]

Urban Renewal governance adopts the City PR process: any scenario is first described with an Issue that outlines public problems and beneficiaries; a Dataset clarifies data authorizations and gaps; a Sandbox runs in a simulated or confined area; Human Review is conducted by professionals, operators, and public representatives; a Pilot sets a timeline, scope, and exit conditions; only when evidence is sufficient does it Merge into routine operations; a Changelog publicly documents the reasons and metrics for changes; and in cases of safety, disturbance, bias, or unsustainable costs, a Revert is allowed. This mechanism transforms AI governance from a signboard into a spatial operation protocol, and also provides project screening logic for [depth:renewal_project_list] and [depth:phasing_implementation].

**Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster** corresponds to the Urban Application & Exchange Core: organize intelligent agents, intelligent terminals, content consumption, data services, international roadshows, and track transfer public experiences on a city-type interface, rather than in a closed park. ## Transport, Rail, Municipal Infrastructure, and Public Services

The transportation framework is based on the principles of "north-south continuity, east-west integration, station transfers, pedestrian priority, and test control." The Main Kernel Bus is expressed as a continuous pedestrian/innovative service corridor in [data:geometry/roads.geojson#ROAD-001], with five I/O Links serving as conceptual connections across different urban interfaces; they are not road rights-of-way, and the number of lanes is not determined. The key issue is to create readable last-mile paths between rail stations, park entrances, community services, and Public Spaces, while reserving time-limited, space-limited modes for robot testing to avoid conflicts with all-weather public access. [depth:traffic_rail_slow_parking]

Municipal infrastructure and New Infrastructure adopt a "first interfaces, then capacity" strategy: before defining the capacity numbers, do not fabricate them when the precise conditions of electricity, communication, drainage, fire safety, and underground pipelines are unknown; instead, define which interfaces, monitoring, and governance are needed for future nodes. For example, a computational transparency kiosk should disclose its service capacity, energy consumption categories, and exit mechanism; an edge AI validation station should have network isolation, model version records, and manual shutdown capabilities; outdoor robot testing should have physical geofencing and safety personnel; and activity nodes should reserve for both regular municipal services and emergency requirements. True engineering design must be carried out only after formal utility and fire safety conditions are obtained. [data:geometry/constraints.geojson#DATA-GAP] [depth:municipal_new_infrastructure]
