Jing-Zhang AI Vein: Regenerating a Centennial Railway Corridor for AI Innovation

Offline electronic exhibit. The proposal is organized around 'Jing-Zhang AI Vein', using the Heritage Park as the historical and public-space spine, forming a spatial framework of 'one belt, three cores, multi-point scenarios, and a blue-green slow-mobility composite ring'. Authoritative data remain in GeoJSON, metrics.json, matrices, and self-check results. All spatial proposals are conceptual; boundaries are provisional.
