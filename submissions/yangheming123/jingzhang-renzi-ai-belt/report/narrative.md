# Formal Narrative

The RENZI AI BELT proposal uses the Qinglongqiao human-character switchback as the master motif for a corridor making its third "turn" — from rail, to technology, to intelligence. The package contains a one-belt/three-station/two-wing/multi-loop spatial structure, a bilingual proposal, geometry recomputed in EPSG:4548, matrices, drawings, and an offline visualization. All spatial proposals are conceptual and pending official boundary and regulatory-plan data.
