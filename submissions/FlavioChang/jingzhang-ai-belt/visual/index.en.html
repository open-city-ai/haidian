Concept

“The AI Line”: the line is both the century-old railway and lines of code. Nine quarters string along the Ren Walk green spine; the three stations carry full-stack innovation (Zhongzhi), a world-class ecosystem (Origin), and AI-native business (Dazhongsi), flanked by the Zhongguancun service wing and the Xiaoyuehe scenario wing.

Mobility & slow travel

  • Ren Walk, 9.7 km gap-free, plus the 3.6 km Xiaoyuehe waterfront walk form twin slow axes.
  • Two-vertical, ten-horizontal branch densification; four secondary roads; street share 5.7%。
  • Three transit links join existing metro stations; forecourts host AV shuttles, bikes, and delivery bays.
  • The Ren Bridge over the N. Fourth Ring is conceptual; feasibility pending.
