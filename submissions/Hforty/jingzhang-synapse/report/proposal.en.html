  • Zhongzhiyuan AI Self-Innovation Acceleration Zone (north, official area 1.921 km²). Positioned as a full-stack autonomous innovation system and a node of global AI-governance discourse. Regeneration combines old-factory renovation with new pilot carriers, with public space unfolding continuously along the spine main axis.
  • Beijing AI Origin Community (center, official area 1.043 km²). Positioned as a world-class AI innovation ecosystem. Composed of AI corporate HQs, AI+medical validation, a developer academy and the "Origin Community public living room" plaza, emphasizing mixed use and 24-hour vitality [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#KEY-BEIJING]. Scenario–space–operation integration forms a talent live–research–life loop.
  • Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster (south, official area 0.72 km²). Positioned as smart-native new formats. Dominated by a business-finance core, smart-native pilot manufacturing and rail-station integration, inheriting the Station F "old-station–new-format" lesson [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#KEY-DAZHONG]. Emphasizes east–west seam and station TOD.
