JINGZHANG RENZI LOOP — Urban Design for the Centennial Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt

Taking the herringbone (renzi) switchback of the Qinglongqiao section of the Jing-Zhang Railway as its prototype, the proposal puts forward the RENZI LOOP concept: one belt, two wings and three districts form a herringbone spatial structure, with data-compute-model-scenario-talent circulating along the loop, turning the birthplace of China's self-reliant innovation into a global AI innovation loop and a model of human-machine co-governance.
