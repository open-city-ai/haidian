Key areas

The spatial core of ORIGIN SERVICE DESK: services are not institutions hidden in buildings but street-visible, walkable urban infrastructure with an accountable operator.

key_areas.geojson

Zhongzhiyuan AI Acceleration Area

Garden-type full-stack autonomous innovation block, the "validation end": full-stack test sandboxes, standards workshops, Qinghe low-carbon frontage.

Beijing AI Origin Community (primary)

Near-campus transfer special zone: "one spine (Origin Service Street), three desks (SOURCE/GROWTH/RELEASE), many windows"; windows are mainly ground-floor conversions, workshops infill existing courtyards.

Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster

Urban intelligent-economy block, the "demand end": Dazhongsi station service hub, agent and data-element services, four-quadrant pedestrian connectivity.
