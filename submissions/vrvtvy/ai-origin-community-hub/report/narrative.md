## 原点服务台 ORIGIN SERVICE DESK

把北京AI原点社区组织为近校创新服务网络：一轴（原点服务街）、三台（策源台 SOURCE / 成长台 GROWTH / 发布台 RELEASE）、多窗口（服务窗口网络）。核心主张：AI 创新带的瓶颈是服务而非楼宇；让科研团队在步行一公里内完成从实验室到市场的每一步。

空间结构：服务街串联高校接口与清华园车站遗址；策源台承接知识产权、算力与概念验证；成长台承接孵化、融资与合规；发布台承接路演、开源发布与场景测试。三处重点区形成互补服务链（众智园验证—原点社区转化—大钟寺需求），两翼提供要素与场景支撑。

## ORIGIN SERVICE DESK

The Beijing AI Origin Community is organized as a near-campus innovation service network: one spine (Origin Service Street), three desks (SOURCE / GROWTH / RELEASE) and a service-window network. Core claim: the bottleneck of an AI innovation belt is service, not buildings; research teams complete every step from lab to market within a one-kilometer walk. The Service Street links the campus interface and the Qinghuayuan station heritage site; SOURCE hosts IP, compute and concept validation; GROWTH hosts incubation, financing and compliance; RELEASE hosts roadshows, open-source release and scenario testing. The three key areas form a complementary service chain (Zhongzhiyuan validation — Origin transfer — Dazhongsi demand) with the two wings providing factors and scenarios.
