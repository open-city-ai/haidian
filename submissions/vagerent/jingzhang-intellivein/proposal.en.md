# Jingzhang IntelliVein: Centennial Jingzhang AI Belt Urban Design Proposal

## Design Basis and Source List

This proposal starts from two documents. One is the pre-qualification announcement for the Centennial Jingzhang AI Belt international urban-design call, issued by the Haidian branch of the Beijing Municipal Commission of Planning and Natural Resources. The other is the provisional site package shipped with the repository, holding a rough boundary, key areas, metric definitions, and a source list.

The announcement organizes work in three layers. The coordinated research area is about 43.6 km² and cares about the AI industry ecosystem and future urban form. The overall design area is about 11.4 km², around one to two kilometres of city and industry beside the Jingzhang heritage park, and needs an urban-renewal framework, an industry-space layout, transport and municipal support, and urban-character control. The key detailed-design area is about 368.4 ha, split into three places that need function, building scale, retain-renovate-demolish classification, public-space connectivity, and transport organization.

The provisional boundary in the package is not an official redline. The repository has no official SITE_BOUNDARY and no official key-area polygons. This proposal uses the maintainer-registered provisional boundary for every layer and metric, and writes all areas, ratios, and scales under the principle of "discussable, checkable, recomputable after an official boundary replaces this one". When the official boundary is released, the agent must rerun the scaffold, the self-check, and the drawing generation; it cannot swap a single file.

Three kinds of sources have different uses. Formal-usable sources feed generation and scoring. Background sources only help context. Provisional sources may only generate, display, and self-check; they must not be upgraded to official boundaries, statutory plans, scoring grounds, or government implementation promises.

## Three-Level Scope Framework

The overall concept is Jingzhang IntelliVein. The vein is both a spine and a network.

The Jingzhang heritage park is the historical and public-space spine. The three key areas, Zhongzhiyuan, Beijing AI Origin Community, and Dazhongsi, are the innovation anchors on the vein. Universities, firms, communities, and rail stations form the daily network. Together they make a spatial organization of one belt, three cores, multi-point scenes, and a blue-green slow-mobility composite ring.

The belt is not a newly drawn redline. It translates the announcement's three layers into a working method. The three cores map to the three key areas. The multi-point scenes map to operable nodes of AI-plus public service, industry service, and city life. The composite ring maps to the linkage of slow mobility, green space, public space, and activity routes.

## Coordinated Research Area: Industry and Future City Research

This layer's goal is a world-class AI innovation ecosystem. Haidian's universities and institutes, leading firms, computing-algorithm-data elements, incubators, listed companies, unicorns, and tech services must connect into one chain.

The full-stack autonomous-innovation system this proposal suggests has five segments. Basic research sits in universities and institutes. Open collaboration sits in communities. Engineering conversion sits in firms. Public experience sits in scene nodes. International dissemination sits in the events system. Four ties, talent, data, computing, and capital, link the segments.

## Overall Design Area: Urban Renewal and Regulatory-Plan-Level Urban Design

The overall design area lands six layer types. Each is generated on the provisional boundary and recomputed wholesale when the official boundary arrives.

Land use is organized as research, park green, commercial service, and community facility, arranged as four vertical bands from west to east. Building footprints concentrate in the northern R&D demo patch. Roads first draw one slow-mobility and innovation-service corridor that stitches the three key areas and rail stations. Green and public space dot along the corridor and the heritage park. Phasing follows "corridor first, nodes next, districts last".

Regulatory-plan-depth indicators are not yet available officially. Floor area ratio, building height, and density are marked unknown and kept out of formal conclusions. They will be recomputed from the official boundary once official regulatory conditions are released.

On character, one restrained principle. New massing stays low, continuous, light-coloured, and talks with the industrial remains of the Jingzhang railway without stealing the remains' visual weight.

## Detailed Design of Key Areas

The three key areas each take a role.

Zhongzhiyuan AI Autonomous-Innovation Accelerator sits furthest north, near the North 5th Ring and Qinghe. It receives early output from universities and runs proof-of-concept, pilot, and mid-pilot. Spatially it uses a group of divisible-combinable courtyards that firms rent by stage; a unit that exits can go to the next firm the same day.

Beijing AI Origin Community sits in the middle, near Wudaokou and the west exit of Qinghua East Road. It mixes living, research, and life, putting talent apartments, shared labs, childcare, and community commerce in one walking loop. The thing worth testing most is letting an AI talent meet a collaborator on the way down to buy coffee.

Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster sits furthest south, near Dazhongsi station. It receives grown firms and runs headquarters, delivery, and showcase. Spatially it keeps Dazhongsi's old commercial memory and turns old venues into operable AI experience places.

All three areas connect by the same slow corridor, where what runs is people and data, not cars.

## AI Innovation Ecosystem, Personas, and AI+ Scenarios

This section gives twelve scenario cards, five personas, and three test scenarios.

The twelve scenario cards cover public life, industry service, and city governance. Public life has AI companion stations, community health huts, silver-digit partners, children coding corners. Industry service has computing-share booking, open-model workshops, proof-of-concept stewards, cross-border compliance assistants. City governance has accessibility patrol, green-space care dispatch, noise and congestion early warning, heritage visitor分流.

Five personas. University researchers want near, cheap, fast to try. Startup founders want flexible space and investment interfaces. Industry engineers want computing, data, customers. Community residents want ease, safety, not left behind by technology. Visitors want to understand, join, take away.

Three test scenarios. First, run a one-month closed test of an "AI life steward" in the Origin Community, serving only signed local residents. Second, run proof-of-concept relay in Zhongzhiyuan, where one exiting unit transfers to the next the same day. ## Land Use, Building Scale, and Retain-Renovate-Demolish Strategy

Land scale is recomputed on the provisional boundary. Research land is about 2.67 million m², park green about 2.59 million m², commercial service about 3.37 million m², community facility about 2.78 million m²; the four sum to roughly the overall design area.

Building scale gives only a demo footprint. The AI R&D demo footprint is about 310,000 m², one illustrative building in the north, not the district total. District building scale and retain-renovate-demolish lists are recomputed after official regulatory conditions release.

Retain-renovate-demolish can only give a method now, not a list. The method has three steps. First recognise heritage and industrial remains, always retain. Then recognise structurally sound, well-located existing buildings, renovate into shared labs or community facilities. Last recognise scattered, inefficient, safety-risk ones, only then list for demolition. Any demolition decision returns to official cadastre and rights surveys.

## Transport, Rail, Municipal Infrastructure, and Public Services

On transport, build the slow corridor solid first. The corridor strings the three key areas and nearby rail stations, wide enough for two abreast, with room to stop and gather. Motor vehicles only do外围 relief, never enter the core walking zone.

Rail relies on the existing network; this proposal adds no line, only last-kilometre accessible connections and signage. Municipal建议 connect computing-centre waste heat into nearby heating and detain local rain into green space.

Public services fill a fifteen-minute life circle. Childcare, community commerce, health huts, shared offices land first in the Origin Community, then copy to the other two.

## Blue-Green Network, Public Space, and Urban Character

The blue-green system takes the Jingzhang heritage park as a spine and pushes green fingers toward the two side districts. Green ratio recomputed on the provisional boundary is about 0.1234, public-space ratio about 0.0733, both conceptual.

Public space makes three types. Heritage slow and showcase, community life and exchange, industry demo and trade. The three share one ground-sign system and one furniture language, so people walking through know they are still on the same vein.

Urban character keeps one line. New stays light, low, continuous; remains stay deep, heavy, with空白. Old and new do not compete; the railway remains stay the heaviest protagonist of the belt.

## Renewal Projects, Implementation Policy, and Phasing

Renewal projects first list the spatial carriers behind the twelve scenarios, then the launch projects of the three areas. Launch projects do not pursue one-shot completion; each is an independently checkable, independently operable small unit.

Phasing has three steps. Step one builds the corridor and signage, low cost, fast effect, lets the "vein" be walked first. Step two builds the Origin Community and Zhongzhiyuan launch units, proving scenes and operating model. Step three builds Dazhongsi renewal and key-area deepening, scaling only after the first two steps yield data.

On implementation policy, two suggestions. One is spatial elasticity, units divisible-combinable, exit-transferable. The other is data transparency, every city-level AI application enters a registry residents can check, ask, stop.

Public-acceptance risk, residents may not trust city-level AI. Policy-uncertainty risk, data and computing regulation is still moving.
