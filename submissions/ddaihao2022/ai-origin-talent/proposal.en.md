# AI Origin Talent & Open-Source Collaboration Block

## Design Basis and Source Register

This formal proposal takes the pre-qualification announcement for the Centennial Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt international urban-design call as its primary basis, together with the maintainer-registered provisional boundary, key areas, enums, metrics and source register under `brief/site-package/`. Every design judgment is decomposed into traceable sources, recalculable metrics, verifiable layers and human-reviewable assumptions. The package reaches the urban-design depth of a regulatory-detailed-plan and an implementation-plan synthesis, and uses the maintainer-registered provisional boundary with explicit precision caveats and metric recalculation.

## Three-Level Scope Framework

The work is organized in three levels: a 43.6 km² research scope, an 11.4 km² overall design scope, and a 368.4 ha detailed-design scope across three key areas. The concept, "AI Origin Talent Commons", uses the blue-green public space and rail stations as the public-space spine, the three key areas (AI Origin Core, Near-Campus Transfer Street, Open-Source Collaboratory) as innovation anchors, and universities, enterprises, communities and rail stations as the everyday network, forming a spatial organization of "one belt, three cores, multi-point scenes, blue-green slow composite loop".

## Key-Area Detailed Design

The AI Origin Core is positioned as a themed innovation district emphasizing AI Origin Core is the showcase node where open-source releases, public demos and scenario-open days connect talent with the wider community. and the fusion of AI with urban life. The Near-Campus Transfer Street focuses on Near-Campus Transfer Street converts university research into ventures through proof-of-concept labs, pilot production and IP services., organizing near-campus and community-linked public space and implementation projects. The Open-Source Collaboratory integrates public activity and industry services around Open-Source Collaboratory houses open workshops, co-working and roadshow spaces that sustain a daily developer community.. All three key areas must reach the depth of a planning-implementation scheme.

## AI Scenarios and Talent Personas

Ten AI scenario cards and five user personas are mapped to concrete layers and the compliance matrix, covering AI-enabled public services, industry services and urban-life scenarios anchored at the three key areas and the AI Origin Talent Commons public-space system.

## Land Use, Building Scale and Renewal

Land use follows the national land-classification guide; building height, massing and retain-renovate-demolish classification are proposed as methods with official conditions pending. All scale metrics are recalculated from GeoJSON layers in `metrics.json`.

## Mobility, Rail, Municipal and Public Services

The plan addresses station integration, road micro-circulation, slow-mobility gaps, green transport, parking and non-motorized storage, with municipal and new-infrastructure strategies for distributed energy and edge compute, centered on the Wudaokou Stn.

## Blue-Green, Public Space and Townscape

A north-south, east-west connected blue-green system is built around the Jing-Zhang heritage park and the AI Origin Talent Commons, integrating slow loops, sports, innovation interaction and public display.

## Renewal Projects, Policy and Phasing

Six renewal projects (OT-01..OT-06) are listed with dependencies and evidence references, sequenced into near-term pilots, mid-term renewal and long-term governance, clearly separating the 100-day call period from implementation phasing.

## Metrics, Recalculation and Compliance

Core metrics (scope area, key-area area, slow-coverage, transit coverage, public-space ratio, renewal count) are recalculated from geometry. The compliance matrix maps every announcement and taskbook task to sections, layers, metrics, drawings, HTML, sources, assumptions and self-check items.

## Risk, Copyright and Compliance

This package does not claim official approval, finalized regulatory control, land ownership or guaranteed implementation. All assets declare sources and licenses in `sources.json` and `report/copyright_statement.md`. The HTML pages load no remote scripts, tiles, fonts, iframes or APIs.


## Global Case Benchmarking

This package benchmarks the most relevant international urban practices for this theme, extracting strategies directly transferable to the site. All cases are from public sources and imply no official partnership or endorsement.

### Case 1: Station F (Paris)
Station F is a very large startup campus opened in 2017 in a former Paris rail depot, co-locating startups, mentors and investors with low entry barriers. Its lesson for the AI Origin talent block is that open shared buildings spark chance collaboration.

### Case 2: Kendall Square (Cambridge, MA)
Kendall Square sits next to MIT and is among the densest biotech and technology clusters, showing how spatial proximity of university research to firms speeds talent flow and transfer. For the Near-Campus Transfer Street it argues for proof-of-concept and pilot facilities next to campus.

### Case 3: Kista Science City (Stockholm)
Kista grew around Ericsson and KTH Royal Institute of Technology into a major European ICT cluster, showing how anchor firms, a university and a dedicated engineering talent pipeline build a durable innovation ecosystem.

### Case 4: High Tech Campus Eindhoven (Netherlands)
High Tech Campus Eindhoven is known for open innovation, sharing labs, meeting and dining infrastructure to lower collaboration cost for startups. Its lesson for the Open-Source Collaboratory is that shared facilities beat isolated buildings for cross-team exchange.

### Case 5: MaRS Discovery District (Toronto)
MaRS builds on the University of Toronto and hospital network as a mission-driven urban innovation hub in health and AI, showing that clustering around public research institutions strengthens a talent district. Its lesson for the AI Origin Core is using public institutions as anchors.

### Case 6: Hong Kong Science Park
Hong Kong Science Park offers incubation, shared labs and cross-border talent services linking universities to the Greater Bay Area industry, showing how open labs and talent services support hard-tech entrepreneurship. It informs the combination of Near-Campus Transfer Street and the Open-Source Collaboratory.

### Test & verification scenarios (>=3)

**OT-01 Open Publish Hall**
- Goal: Verify that university and open-source outputs can be published and shown in public space.
- Method: Set a bookable publish desk and code-contribution wall at the Core; log bookings and usage.
- Pass: Pass: monthly usage reaches an operable threshold and data stays aggregated only.

**OT-02 Near-Campus Transfer Validation**
- Goal: Verify that proof-of-concept and pilot facilities on Transfer Street serve startups.
- Method: Register resident teams, validated projects and transfer cases referencing the buildings layer.
- Pass: Pass: pilot-space use and transfer cases trace to layers and the compliance matrix.

**OT-03 Collaboratory Slow-Transfer**
- Goal: Verify smooth slow-mobility connection between the Collaboratory and the rail station.
- Method: Use low-intrusion sensing to find slow-mobility gaps and accessibility needs, referencing the roads layer.
- Pass: Pass: gap list enters public-space and roads layers and the phasing plan.

### Sustainability risks
- **Operational sustainability**:  lightweight facilities risk vacancy without a steady operator; operator and revenue model must be defined.
- **Talent sustainability**:  hype-only events without a daily community will not sustain a talent district; a developer community must be cultivated.
- **Ecosystem sustainability**:  open collaboration needs ongoing shared-facility and compute investment, avoiding one-off construction that falls into disrepair.

## Accessibility & Privacy

### Accessibility design
This package follows WCAG 2.1 AA. Concrete measures:
- **Visual**: HTML color contrast >= 4.5:1; figures provide text descriptions (alt text); A3/A0 boards use large type (title >= 24pt, body >= 14pt).
- **Operation**: interactive elements keyboard-accessible; touch targets >= 44x44px; key service forms support voice input.
- **Cognitive**: key wayfinding is 'conclusion-first' (direction before explanation); Chinese-English terms stay consistent.

### Vulnerable-group care (scenario-specific)
- Older-adult accessibility: gentle ramps, seating and large-print signage at the Core and Collaboratory.
- Disability accessibility: publish hall and roadshow spaces provide wheelchair turning and continuous tactile paths.
- Children and family: activity park and open days provide nature play and care areas.
- Low-income groups: open publish and collaboration spaces are free or low-cost, avoiding membership barriers.

### Data privacy & security
- **Minimal collection**: only trip/service-relevant aggregates are collected; no personal behavior trajectories or biometrics.
- **Storage isolation**: data stored on domestic servers; personal identity separated from content; raw data encrypted.
- **Use limitation**: used only to improve the services described here; not sold, not for commercial recommendation, not shared with third parties without explicit consent.
- **Rights**: citizens may view and track their feedback status; may request deletion of personal data (except statutory retention); may demand human review instead of pure automated decisions.
- **Audit**: an independent third party audits data-privacy compliance annually and publishes the report.

> This section is guided by the Personal Information Protection Law (PIPL), the GDPR and the Data Security Law; detailed rules must be finalized with legal professionals in Phase 1.
