# Jing-Zhang Open-Source Belt · Qinghe Blue-Green Smart Interaction Axis

## Design Basis and Source Register

This formal proposal takes the pre-qualification announcement for the Centennial Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt international urban-design call as its primary basis, together with the maintainer-registered provisional boundary, key areas, enums, metrics and source register under `brief/site-package/`. Every design judgment is decomposed into traceable sources, recalculable metrics, verifiable layers and human-reviewable assumptions. The package reaches the urban-design depth of a regulatory-detailed-plan and an implementation-plan synthesis, and uses the maintainer-registered provisional boundary with explicit precision caveats and metric recalculation.

## Three-Level Scope Framework

The work is organized in three levels: a 43.6 km² research scope, an 11.4 km² overall design scope, and a 368.4 ha detailed-design scope across three key areas. The concept, "Jing-Zhang Qinghe Axis", uses the blue-green public space and rail stations as the public-space spine, the three key areas (Jing-Zhang Open-Source Gateway, Qinghe Blue-Green Interaction Axis, Smart-Interaction Co-Create Yard) as innovation anchors, and universities, enterprises, communities and rail stations as the everyday network, forming a spatial organization of "one belt, three cores, multi-point scenes, blue-green slow composite loop".

## Key-Area Detailed Design

The Jing-Zhang Open-Source Gateway is positioned as a themed innovation district emphasizing Jing-Zhang Open-Source Gateway: a heritage-and-rail gateway hosting open-source collaboration and a public portal. and the fusion of AI with urban life. The Qinghe Blue-Green Interaction Axis focuses on Qinghe Blue-Green Interaction Axis: a blue-green slow corridor for interaction, exercise and waterfront activity., organizing near-campus and community-linked public space and implementation projects. The Smart-Interaction Co-Create Yard integrates public activity and industry services around Smart-Interaction Co-Create Yard: a co-creation yard for showcases, workshops and cross-sector collaboration.. All three key areas must reach the depth of a planning-implementation scheme.

## AI Scenarios and Talent Personas

Ten AI scenario cards and five user personas are mapped to concrete layers and the compliance matrix, covering AI-enabled public services, industry services and urban-life scenarios anchored at the three key areas and the Jing-Zhang Qinghe Axis public-space system.

## Land Use, Building Scale and Renewal

Land use follows the national land-classification guide; building height, massing and retain-renovate-demolish classification are proposed as methods with official conditions pending. All scale metrics are recalculated from GeoJSON layers in `metrics.json`.

## Mobility, Rail, Municipal and Public Services

The plan addresses station integration, road micro-circulation, slow-mobility gaps, green transport, parking and non-motorized storage, with municipal and new-infrastructure strategies for distributed energy and edge compute, centered on the Qinghe Stn.

## Blue-Green, Public Space and Townscape

A north-south, east-west connected blue-green system is built around the Jing-Zhang heritage park and the Jing-Zhang Qinghe Axis, integrating slow loops, sports, innovation interaction and public display.

## Renewal Projects, Policy and Phasing

Six renewal projects (JA-01..JA-06) are listed with dependencies and evidence references, sequenced into near-term pilots, mid-term renewal and long-term governance, clearly separating the 100-day call period from implementation phasing.

## Metrics, Recalculation and Compliance

Core metrics (scope area, key-area area, slow-coverage, transit coverage, public-space ratio, renewal count) are recalculated from geometry. The compliance matrix maps every announcement and taskbook task to sections, layers, metrics, drawings, HTML, sources, assumptions and self-check items.

## Global Case Benchmarking

This package benchmarks the most relevant international urban practices for this theme, extracting strategies directly transferable to the site. All cases are from public sources and imply no official partnership or endorsement.

### Case 1: Paris Coulée Verte René-Dumont
Paris converted the disused Vincennes railway viaduct into a roughly 4.7 km linear park (Promenade Plantee) in 1993, among the world's first rail-heritage elevated parks and a precedent for New York's High Line. Lesson: the Jing-Zhang Open-Source Gateway should use the Jing-Zhang railway heritage as an elevated and linear interaction carrier, turning obsolete infrastructure into continuous public interaction space.

### Case 2: New York High Line
New York's High Line opened its first section in 2009, transforming an abandoned elevated New York Central Railroad line into a public park that activates linear space with planting, art and events and catalyzed surrounding renewal. Lesson: the Qinghe Blue-Green Interaction Axis can borrow the 'heritage line + public program' activation, turning the Jing-Zhang line into an interactive, showcase-ready blue-green axis.

### Case 3: Seoul Seoullo 7017
Seoul converted a disused Seoul Station overpass into a roughly 983 m pedestrian sky garden (Seoullo 7017) in 2017, linking the city with planting and rest nodes. Lesson: the Jing-Zhang Open-Source Gateway can borrow the skywalk idea, turning the heritage viaduct into a continuous walking and interaction path connecting the rail station and open-source spaces.

### Case 4: Rotterdam Water Square Benthemplein
Rotterdam's Water Square Benthemplein, opened in 2013 and designed by De Urbanisten, composites stormwater storage with public activity in an urban square, a model of blue-green climate-adaptive public space. Lesson: the Qinghe Blue-Green Interaction Axis should composite blue-green with stormwater and activity, turning the waterfront into a public room with both ecological and interaction functions.

### Case 5: Barcelona 22@ Innovation District
Barcelona designated the former Poblenou industrial area as the 22@ innovation district around 2000, keeping its fabric while introducing knowledge economy, collaboration spaces and public space into an open-cooperation innovation network. Lesson: the Smart-Interaction Co-Create Yard should center on open-source and co-creation, organizing legacy space into a collaborative, showcase-ready innovation yard.

### Case 6: Freiburg Vauban Sustainable District
Freiburg's Vauban was built from the 2000s with high citizen participation as a low-carbon, car-reduced sustainable district known for co-built governance and public space. Lesson: the Jing-Zhang Open-Source Belt should use citizen and multi-party co-creation as its method, combining open-source collaboration with community governance rather than top-down single operation.

## Accessibility & Privacy

### Accessibility design
This package follows WCAG 2.1 AA. Concrete measures:
- **Visual**: HTML color contrast >= 4.5:1; figures provide text descriptions (alt text); A3/A0 boards use large type (title >= 24pt, body >= 14pt).
- **Operation**: interactive elements keyboard-accessible; touch targets >= 44x44px; key service forms support voice input.
- **Cognitive**: key wayfinding is 'conclusion-first' (direction before explanation); Chinese-English terms stay consistent.

### Vulnerable-group care (scenario-specific)
- Older adults: continuous level paths, rest seating, safe crossings and heritage accessibility.
- Persons with disabilities: ramps, tactile paving and interaction-axis wayfinding.
- Children & families: family activity space and safe play facilities.
- Low-income groups: free, accessible interaction and blue-green space.

### Data privacy & security
- **Minimal collection**: only trip/service-relevant aggregates are collected; no personal behavior trajectories or biometrics.
- **Storage isolation**: data stored on domestic servers; personal identity separated from content; raw data encrypted.
- **Use limitation**: used only to improve the services described here; not sold, not for commercial recommendation, not shared with third parties without explicit consent.
- **Rights**: citizens may view and track their feedback status; may request deletion of personal data (except statutory retention); may demand human review instead of pure automated decisions.
- **Audit**: an independent third party audits data-privacy compliance annually and publishes the report.

> This section is guided by the Personal Information Protection Law (PIPL), the GDPR and the Data Security Law; detailed rules must be finalized with legal professionals in Phase 1.
