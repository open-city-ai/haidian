# 摘要 | Abstract

人字形回路(The Ren Loop):以詹天佑人字形折返与反向传播的百年同构为总体概念,把百年京张AI创新带组织为城市尺度的训练回路——前行轨(遗址公园文化慢行轴)与回传轨(科创服务环)在原点站交汇成「人」字,一环双轨、三站十二口;12张AI场景卡、6类用户画像、4处AI朝圣地标、12项更新项目三期实施。全部空间数据基于临时边界生成,EPSG:4548复算,官方数据发布后整链重算。

The Ren Loop organises the Centennial Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt as a city-scale training loop: the Forward Track (heritage axis) and Return Track (service loop) meet at Origin Station in a REN glyph — one loop, two tracks, three stations, twelve crossings; 12 AI scenario cards, 6 personas, 4 pilgrimage landmarks, 12 renewal projects in three phases. All spatial data is provisional-boundary based, recomputed in EPSG:4548, fully recomputable on official data.
