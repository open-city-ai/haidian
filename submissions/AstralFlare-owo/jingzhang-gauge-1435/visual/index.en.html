Three-level scope

Coordinated Research Area

Research layer for the AI industry ecosystem and future urban form; hosts the One Spine–Three Stations–Two Wings structure and the Three-Zones–Two-Wings synergy loop.

Overall Design Area

11.4 km²

Urban renewal framework at regulatory-detail-planning depth: 44 land-use zones, 430 concept buildings, 9 roads, 18 green parcels, 9 plazas, 3 phases.

Key-Area Detailed Design Area

368.4 ha (three key areas combined)

Implementation-plan-depth design for three key areas, each with positioning + spatial structure + building renewal + mobility + public space + AI scenarios + implementation risks.

Blue-Green Public Space

green/public

The blue-green public-space system is structured around the Jing-Zhang heritage park greenway spine (approx. 9.4 km), linking the Qinghe and wing belts; green ratio 14.45% (green_ratio), public-space ratio 0.38% (public_space_ratio). The 1435 modular component library and the accessible slow-traffic datum are the core carriers of the yielding-track strategy. See the "Blue-Green Space, Public Space and Urban Character" chapter and the mobility-bluegreen figure for details.
