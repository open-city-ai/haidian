# Formal Narrative

This package treats the Jing-Zhang heritage greenway as an already-open public skeleton. Phase 2 opened on 2026-08-06 (about 9 km / 53 ha). Near-term work operates the three slow-traffic ways, patches access gaps, and overlays switch-off AI scenes. It does not rebuild a green corridor.

North / mid / south segments from public reports assign scenes: nature-leisure tests in the north, Origin publish and transfer in Phase 1, station-city events in the south. Cross-district partners keep checkable interfaces only. Year-1 programmes land on places already open to the public.

Geometry, metrics, the compliance matrix, drawings, and visual/index.html remain cross-checked deliverables. Provisional boxes are not official redlines. Schematic green inside the submitted boundary must not be mixed with the reported 53 ha park area. FAR and height stay unknown until official controls arrive.
