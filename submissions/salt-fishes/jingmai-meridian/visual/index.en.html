Phasing Implementation · Four Phases

The four-phase sequence responds to the agent.6 long-term operations task. Phase 1 pilots the meridian five-step loop and AI scenario mechanisms; Phase 2 forms the three differentiated cores; Phase 3 links the nine nodes; Phase 4 enables agent self-learning and self-evolution.

phasing
Phase 1 · 0-2 years
Pilot Period
Qinghuayuan feedback station + Qinghe low-altitude delivery pilot + old Jing-Zhang corridor demonstration segment. Lightweight facilities, operations activities, and service platforms launch to validate the meridian five-step loop and AI scenario mechanisms.
Phase 2 · 2-5 years
Three-Core Period
The three cores take differentiated shape: Zhongzhiyuan R&D engine, AI Origin life prototype, and Dazhongsi industry cluster each form and interlock.
Phase 3 · 5-8 years
Nine-Node Period
Nine nodes interlink, the meridian is through-connected, and AI scenarios move from pilot to routine operation.
Phase 4 · 8+ years
Evolution Period
The civic agent self-learns and self-evolves, and the urban governance knowledge base settles into a long-term brand asset.
