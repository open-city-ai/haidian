01 / OPEN GRAMMAR

SIX WORDS FORM A MINIMAL PUBLIC GRAMMAR

G1Proceed

Continue along this direction; it grants no priority.

Visual
One continuous open line between paired rails.
Tactile
Candidate continuous shallow rib, usable only after non-confusion with tactile paving is proven.
Machine
Direction vector, version and checksum; no destination or identity.
G2Yield

Machines and service flows return priority to people here.

Visual
An inward taper that opens into a gap.
Tactile
Candidate interrupted transverse rib, kept distinct from statutory warning paving.
Machine
Public state requiring slow, stop and clear-path confirmation.
G3Edge

This is a boundary that must not be crossed or requires human confirmation.

Visual
Double line with a closed end cap.
Tactile
Candidate smooth-to-textured transition with no trip-producing level change.
Machine
No crossing, or supervised-test authorization only.
G4Berth

Short stops, loading or handover belong here, outside the continuous clear path.

Visual
A pocket opening away from the travel line.
Tactile
Candidate edge rhythm that remains flush for wheelchairs.
Machine
Berth identifier, permitted state and maximum dwell category; no recipient data.
G5Help

Anyone can obtain phone-free human help or report a problem here.

Visual
An open bracket around a hollow point.
Tactile
Candidate short tactile ring adjacent to a fixed leaning/support surface.
Machine
Broadcasts help-point class and availability only; no helper identity is collected.
G6Change

Rules, route or infrastructure ahead have temporarily changed; read the on-site alternative first.

Visual
Offset sleepers with a prominent version corner.
Tactile
Candidate removable short rhythm, installed only after the alternative route is complete.
Machine
Version, validity window and alternative state; expired or conflicting states are invalid.
