Board 05 consistently uses six Civic AI Overlay planning controls, with the drawing, proposal and metrics each carrying their corresponding evidence layer.

This page presents a conceptual urban-design model. Official polygons, statutory controls, ownership, heritage, road, railway, municipal-capacity and engineering information trigger coordinated review of boundaries, areas, transport and building design; FAR, height, density, population capacity, MW, MVA, water volume and pipe diameter enter specialist interfaces.

The quantity, size and engineering conditions of commissioning pods, bridges, low-altitude logistics and underground interfaces follow specialist studies.
