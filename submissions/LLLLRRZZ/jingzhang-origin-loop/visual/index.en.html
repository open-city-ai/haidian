Dual scenarios | Same form, two destinies (drag the slider)

Scenario A · Polarization persists (warning)

  • Platform ownership; value uplift goes to capital
  • Advertising invades facades; LED screens flood the street
  • Stations become shops; public life is consumerized
  • Full sensor coverage; boundless behavior profiling
  • Encounter disappears; circles become islands

Scenario B · Community ownership (formal)

  • Community-fund ownership; ≥50% revenue reinvested
  • Color constitution: saturated color only for wayfinding and recognition
  • Stations host non-consumption stays and care clusters
  • Three-tier data governance: no technical entry for the forbidden tier
  • Cross-circle encounter structurally enforced
◀ Scenario A (warning)Scenario B (formal) ▶

The form is exactly the same; the destiny is entirely different — the difference is not technology, but distribution. Every spatial move must hold under both scenarios (building-code principle P2).

Open data

Anonymized aggregates (footfall, environment) — public by default, feeding the open-data annual report.

Restricted data

Personal or commercially sensitive: consent, minimal collection, local processing, retention caps, data-trust custody.

Forbidden data

Facial recognition, biometrics, behavior profiling: not collected, not stored, no technical entry point.

A scenario does not exist permanently once approved; it exists temporarily under continuous justification. Every automated decision keeps a human appeal and takeover channel.
