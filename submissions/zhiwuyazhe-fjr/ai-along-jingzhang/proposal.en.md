## Core Judgment and Operating Path

A railway can operate only when its track, signalling, timetable, and responsibility work together. An AI service entering the city also needs a form of acceptance that the public can understand and inspect. The proposal therefore turns “love” from communication language into five public conditions. Every model, robot, guide, or commercial service must be visible, explainable, useful, stoppable, and changeable.

| Five public acceptance questions | Minimum visible evidence | Spatial requirement | If unmet |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Can people see it? | Service state, data source, responsible role, and validity period are readable | Status and staffed service appear before the device | Do not open publicly |
| Can people question it? | Purpose, limits, cost, data, and next responsible person can be explained | Provide human explanation, paper information, and multilingual access | Do not enter trial use |
| Can people use it? | A real task is completed and a non-AI route completes the same basic task | Daily routes remain continuous and testing does not displace ordinary service | Do not expand |
| Can people stop it? | On-site stopping action, appeal, and public pause access exist | Hazardous testing is physically separated and public service can transfer to people immediately | Return to daily mode |
| Can people change it? | Failure, revision, retest, restoration, and retirement are recorded | Reversible components and version notices leave no obsolete device behind | Retest after correction or exit |

All five questions must be satisfied for AI to be considered present in public life. If one answer expires, the service state is downgraded. The questions run through the City Problem Passport, handovers between the Three Zones, all twelve scenarios, and annual review. They allow a resident to judge whether a service deserves to remain without reading a technical report [metric:public_acceptance_test_count] [standard:PROJECT-AGENT-OPEN-CALL-TASKBOOK] [depth:overall_spatial_structure].

`Urban problem enters → AI Origin translates → Zhongzhiyuan validates → Dazhongsi adopts → Xiaoyue River and corridor users provide feedback → continue, modify, or retire`

The Zhongguancun Technology Services Wing supports every stage with capital, intellectual property, legal, standards, talent, computing, and data services. The Jing-Zhang Railway Heritage Park provides a public interface open to everyone. Each zone therefore performs a distinct stage, the wings provide continuous support and correction, and citizens can see where a problem stands and who holds the next responsibility [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK] [standard:PROJECT-AGENT-OPEN-CALL-TASKBOOK] [depth:overall_spatial_structure].

Every problem uses a lightweight City Problem Passport. It avoids irrelevant personal information and keeps only eleven fields needed for collective decisions.

| Required content | Question to answer | Treatment when missing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Problem and place | What happened and in what type of space | Do not match it to a scenario |
| Affected groups | Who uses it and who bears the risk | Do not enter co-creation |
| Non-AI baseline | How is the same task completed today | Do not claim AI improvement |
| Data ceiling | What data are needed at most | Do not collect undeclared data |
| Responsible roles | Who receives, reviews, takes over, and restores | Do not enter a pilot |
| Spatial mode | How daily use remains available and when testing is overlaid | Do not occupy public space |
| Success evidence | What result supports continuation | Do not expand the scope |
| Failure signal | What indicates ineffectiveness or harm | Enter review immediately |
| Human-equivalent path | How the same basic task is completed without AI | Do not launch when absent |
| Appeal and pause | Who may object and who may suspend | Do not open to the public |
| Closure and restoration | When it ends and who restores place and data | Do not start |

Five evidence gates control progression. G0 confirms that a problem is registered. G1 completes baselines, rights, and permissions. G2 allows only a small controlled pilot. G3 requires independent retesting and review by affected groups. G4 decides whether to continue, modify, or retire. A calendar may organise work, but it cannot replace any evidence gate [metric:city_problem_passport_required_field_count] [metric:operational_evidence_gate_count] [data:geometry/phasing.geojson#PHASE-1].

## Coordinated Research Area: Industry and Future City Research

### Three Zones as a Chain

The Three Zones are differentiated by the journey from AI creation to adoption. Zhongzhiyuan is responsible for independent research, trusted validation, and AI governance. The Beijing AI Origin Community translates research outcomes, incubates ventures, and supports public co-creation. Dazhongsi supports enterprise adoption, urban services, and international presentation. Public needs can enter through the park, communities, and Dazhongsi, be translated at AI Origin, validated at Zhongzhiyuan, and return to the city and market. Products that fail can move back along the same chain for revision [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK] [standard:PROJECT-AGENT-OPEN-CALL-TASKBOOK] [depth:overall_spatial_structure].

### Two Wings as a Loop

The Two Wings do not duplicate another research park or lifestyle district. The Zhongguancun Technology Services Wing provides capital, intellectual property, legal, standards, talent, computing, and data services across all three zones. The Xiaoyue River Scenario Enablement Wing accommodates real-world testing, ecological feedback, public experience, and blue-green resilience, and returns operational results to the Three Zones. The Three Zones are stages in the value chain; the Two Wings are shared capabilities [data:geometry/roads.geojson#ROAD-WING-W] [data:geometry/roads.geojson#ROAD-WING-E].

### Jing-Zhang Integrated with the City

The Jing-Zhang Railway Heritage Park is the public spine. It connects history, work, study, leisure, and event-day experiences, while giving the innovation chain an interface open to everyone. The proposal builds on these existing uses rather than treating the park as an empty exhibition hall [source:BEIJING-JINGZHANG-20260330] [source:JINGZHANG-PARK-OPENING-20230202].

## Detailed Design of Key Areas

### Zhongzhiyuan AI Independent Innovation Acceleration Area

Zhongzhiyuan answers “Can it be trusted?” Its central Trusted Validation Ground uses a four-band section. A public observation edge shows the test object, status, responsible person, and result level. A controlled core accommodates small model, robot, and Urban Agent tests. A human-takeover band retains safety staff, physical stops, maintenance, and data isolation. An ecological-restoration edge faces the Qinghe River direction and supports only low-disturbance observation and site reset. The public may observe but cannot cross the hazardous-test boundary [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-001] [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#PUBLIC-TRUST] [depth:three_key_area_detailed_design].

Daily mode retains park circulation, a public foyer, and staffed explanation. Testing mode opens only the bookable observation edge while the test core remains enclosed. Restoration mode checks equipment, data, barriers, circulation, and ecological disturbance item by item. A failed physical stop, boundary breach, or absent responsible person ends the test. Adaptable existing space is preferred; structural, computing, energy, and fire-safety conditions still require professional verification.

### Beijing AI Origin Community

AI Origin answers “Can it be explained?” Its spatial prototype extends from the public edge outside Qinghuayuan Station into the everyday neighbourhood. The first layer preserves a quiet heritage edge and normal visits. The second provides a reversible City Question counter. The third accommodates staffed intake, small co-creation sessions, and outcome explanation. The fourth returns answered questions, failure records, and open-source milestones to the neighbourhood. The Open-Source Milestone Court displays only verifiable contributions and does not rank organisations by company size or funding [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-002] [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#PUBLIC-MILESTONE].

Qinghuayuan Station is near this innovation-living catchment, but its real-world relationship with the formal scope remains subject to an official base map. The station building remains low-intervention and primarily presents existing historical interpretation. External question devices are not fixed to heritage fabric and collect no irrelevant identity data. Paper question cards, staffed intake, and a public status board complete the same task as the digital entry. AI Origin classifies and co-develops suitable questions, Zhongzhiyuan validates them, and mature responses enter trial use at Dazhongsi and corridor public services. This distributed City Question Station gives the heritage asset a continuing public-connective role [source:TSINGHUA-STATION-HISTORY] [source:TSINGHUA-STATION-CONSERVATION] [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#PUBLIC-QST].

### Minimum Replayable Unit

The City Question Station provides an executable offline tabletop rehearsal of the public acceptance contract. `run_tabletop.js` reads only twelve proposal-stage scenario contracts, uses no network, and processes no personal information. Each scenario runs one qualified fixture and five failure branches: missing responsibility, data beyond the ceiling, failed human route, unavailable pause, and unpublished revision. This produces 72 synthetic checks and machine-readable receipts. The current run matched expectations in 72/72 cases: twelve qualified fixtures were released for tabletop purposes only and all sixty negative fixtures were blocked. If any negative fixture is incorrectly released, the whole rehearsal fails [metric:simulation_task_count] [metric:synthetic_negative_branch_count].

The unit lets an operating team rehearse rights and responsibilities before installing devices. The code, twelve contracts, receipts, and task ledger are stored in `visual/assets/public-acceptance-tabletop/` and `simulation.json`, so any reviewer can rerun them offline. Its state remains `not_authorized_not_run`. A 100% synthetic classification result proves only that the rule implementation closes correctly; it does not prove field effectiveness, safety, compliance, or authorisation [metric:simulation_success_rate] [depth:renewal_project_list].

The first product is not a smart screen. It is a five-part public-space sequence in which the same task can be completed without AI, the system can be stopped, and the site can be restored. The staffed counter supports anonymous submission and human help. The public receipt board shows only question ID, responsible role, evidence gate, and next review. ## Land Use, Building Scale, and Retain-Renovate-Demolish Strategy

The land-use GeoJSON is a complete conceptual functional partition. Nine zones share boundaries and achieve a coverage ratio of 1.0. The central heritage and blue-green belt is divided into three segments to prevent small projected-topology errors where long and segment edges meet. North, middle, and south sectors of the west and east wings organise the dominant relationships among research, community service, and commercial service. All classifications use the allowed land-use-code subset in the repository [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-SPINE-N] [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-W-S] [metric:land_use_partition_area_sqm].

The building layer represents only four conceptual functional carriers. Their total footprint is approximately 20,400 square metres, or about 0.18% of the provisional overall boundary. This value checks drawing and project interfaces; it is not a construction-scale recommendation. Existing building stock, floor area, FAR, building coverage, height, structure, and ownership await formal data, so total floor area and FAR remain unknown [metric:building_footprint_area_sqm] [metric:building_coverage_ratio] [metric:floor_area_ratio].

The retain-renovate-demolish strategy uses four decisions after survey. Heritage and legally protected assets enter the conservation category. Buildings in sound structural and operational condition enter retain-and-repair. Adaptable and safe buildings enter renovation and reuse. Demolition or new construction is discussed only when ownership, structure, fire safety, planning controls, public interest, and whole-life assessment all support it. All four carriers in the current GeoJSON are marked “pending survey”; none is a demolition conclusion [standard:MOHURD-CONTROL-DETAILED-PLANNING] [depth:development_intensity_controls] [depth:height_massing_character].

## Renewal Projects, Implementation Policy, and Phasing

Projects advance through evidence gates rather than automatically upgrading in a given year. G0 confirms that a problem merits intake. G1 requires baseline, rights, responsibility, and a non-AI route. G2 allows a small controlled pilot. G3 requires independent retesting, public review, and restoration inspection. G4 decides whether to continue, modify, or retire. A project that fails any gate stays in its earlier state and publishes the reason.

| G2 controlled pilot | Can people use it? | Safety review, same-task route, capacity, notice, appeal, and end date | Whether short public opening is allowed | Keep daily mode |

## Risk, Copyright, and Compliance

The largest spatial risk is misalignment between provisional geometry and real-world named places. The relationship of the Dazhongsi key area and the heritage park is explicitly flagged. Before official boundaries arrive, the proposal makes no conclusion on parcel inclusion, demolition, development scale, road engineering, or investment calculation [data:geometry/constraints.geojson#CONSTRAINT-DZS] [source:OSM-CONTEXT-20260809].

Heritage risk concentrates at Qinghuayuan Station. Any fixing, excavation, service route, lighting, sign, or event arrangement requires heritage, ownership, and safety review. The interior prioritises conservation and existing historical interpretation, while external prototypes are reversible. This principle cannot replace a heritage-impact assessment or formal permission [source:TSINGHUA-STATION-CONSERVATION] [data:geometry/constraints.geojson#CONSTRAINT-HERITAGE].

AI scenarios implement data minimisation, purpose limitation, public notice, human review, complaints, suspension, and exit. The Interim Measures for the Management of Generative Artificial Intelligence Services apply only to the domestic public generative-AI services defined by Article 2. The proposal does not misstate Article 14 as a general right to exit and does not invent a complaint deadline. Models, robots, and commercial validation use separate protocols [source:GENERATIVE-AI-MEASURES] [metric:industry_validation_scenario_count].

Equity risks are reduced through human service, paper information, multimodal wayfinding, accessible continuity, and booking alternatives. The 2020–2022 staged targets in the policy on older people's use of smart technology have passed; this proposal adopts only the design principle that traditional and smart services operate in parallel and does not present that policy as a 2026 local implementation fact [source:ELDERLY-SMART-TECH-2020] [source:BARRIER-FREE-LAW].

Evidence can also expire. Official boundaries, regulations, operating information, and dynamic reporting are rechecked before each new evidence gate and, during long-term operation, at least with the annual public statement. If a source is withdrawn, changes scope, or receives a newer version, every dependent claim, metric, figure, and scenario status is downgraded together. Unreviewed material may remain as a historical record but cannot support pilot expansion [source:SOURCE-REGISTRY] [depth:risk_missing_data].

All prose, structured data, conceptual geometry, and design overlays are produced for this project. The overview uses a visibly attributed OpenStreetMap standard-map snapshot as real-world context; it does not contribute to boundary, road-redline, or area calculations. Third-party web images, trademark assets, and restricted fonts are excluded. The Logo is an AI-generated asset explicitly commissioned and approved by the user; the formal package includes only standardised derivatives. See `report/copyright_statement.md` for the detailed rights statement.
