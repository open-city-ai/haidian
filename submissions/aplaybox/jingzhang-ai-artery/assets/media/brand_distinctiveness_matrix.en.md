## Conclusion

- Of 16 contrast items: 14 low risk, 2 medium risk (both **event-category** adjacency: ZGC Forum and WIC Wuzhen Summit), **0 high-risk items**; mitigations are listed in-table and will be re-checked before any brand freeze.
- The visual motif (rail cross-section × neural-network topology) is low-risk across all four comparanda — within this contrast set no precedent of the cross-section + timeline combination was found; this judgment is limited to the comparanda listed here.
- **Fallback naming pool** (activated if layers 2/3 fail; conceptual reserves only, no registration claims): (1) "京张百年·智能走廊 / CENTENNIAL SMART CORRIDOR"; (2) "AI 原点带 / AI ORIGIN BELT" (aligned with the official key-area name); (3) "清华园智枢 / ZHIYUAN GARDEN HUB".
- All comparanda are public and verifiable; the judgments are participant design judgments not verified against trademark databases or legal search — which is exactly why P-2 layer 2 exists.
