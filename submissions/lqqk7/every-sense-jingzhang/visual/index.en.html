BRAND, CULTURE, AND INTERNATIONAL COMMUNICATION

Visual grammar, cultural narrative and outward communication share one public commitment; marks, landmark names, events and communication plans are concept proposals.

VISUAL GRAMMARParallel sensing lines + open nodesAbstracted from the railway’s common gauge: a common gauge lets different trains share one network, universal design lets different bodies share one city. Parallel lines mean several channels to the same destination; open nodes mean you can pause, ask for help, and exit.
COMMUNICATION LINEOne city. Many ways to sense it.Rendered in Chinese as “一座城市，多种感知，共同行走”. Professional audiences get the test method and failure samples; public audiences get paired AI-on / AI-off comparisons that show what service equivalence means.
CULTURAL BASELINEAI ON = AI OFFWith the network down, the power out, or consent refused, cast text, braille, tactile relief and fixed graphics stay readable; history does not disappear when the system is off.
