# Dazhongsi Area Urban Renewal Implementation Plan — Road-Network Restructuring and Spatial Activation Based on Bio-Physarum Algorithm Insights

> **Roads grow like slime mold, not drawn once. The ordinary path always goes first; AI only adds reversible, stoppable, rollback-able gains.**

## Executive Summary

The Centenary Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt is envisioned as a global AI industry highland and pilgrimage destination. Dazhongsi is one of its three key areas and a flagship carrier of Haidian's Zhongguancun Science City innovation corridor. This proposal does not stack tool counts; it answers a single question: **faced with a fragmented slow-travel system, the tension between heritage protection and development, and insufficient rail-station coverage, how do we reconnect the existing road network at the lowest cost?**

The core thesis is one sentence: **roads grow like slime mold, not drawn once.** A slime mold facing scattered food sources grows a network that is minimally redundant, highly resilient, and near-optimal in total length; Dazhongsi's parks, metro stations, and the Jing-Zhang railway corridor are precisely the "nutrient anchors" that can be reconnected by the same logic. This proposal translates that natural principle into a "one core · three areas · one interface · one link" spatial structure and six renewal projects (JZ-01..06); the ordinary path always goes first, and AI only adds reversible gains.

**Method evidence (recomputable, not formal site geometry)**: real runs produced a 167-edge skeleton, optimal efficiency 19.20 (baseline 1.143, Run7 frozen 2.802), zero heritage hard-crossings — all as method-validation evidence; coordinate offset means none of this is a field conclusion.

**Governance mechanism**: ordinary path first + staged release G0-G3 — G0 ordinary path always open / G1 local recomputation / G2 professional review / G3 bounded field window; any level that fails returns to the previous one; each level keeps stop conditions, reversible measures, and archived receipts.

**Implementability**: municipal road-network cost 29.965 million CNY (with layered investment scope); a three-phase roadmap (near-term pilot → mid-term rollout → long-term governance, each phase with measurable milestones and acceptance metrics); clear division of labor among competent departments, universities, enterprises, and communities; 12 recomputable metrics (site 1141.3 ha, green ratio 12.34%, public-space ratio 7.33%, building footprint 31.1 ha, 3 key areas).

**Public value and honest boundary**: oriented toward "heritage-sensitive, human-machine coexistence, youth-friendly", implementing the GB 50763-2012 accessibility threshold and sponge-city stormwater resilience; this proposal is conceptual research, and all spatial suggestions must be coordinated with statutory planning and approved by the competent authority, never impersonating redlines or approval geometry.

## Three-Level Scope Framework

[depth:three_level_scope_framework] The proposal is organized in three levels to keep conceptual research within bounds: **research scope** (the area and its regional coordination interfaces) → **design scope** (1141.3 ha, recomputable site boundary [data:geometry/site_boundary.geojson#SITE-001]) → **key areas** (3 areas, detailed to building scale). Each level's depth requirements are in the compliance matrix; boundaries and values are recomputable.

[depth:existing_conditions_diagnosis] The existing-condition diagnosis can be summarized in one sentence: **the nutrient anchors are all there; the connections are broken.** Three breaks: first, **fragmented slow-travel** — the Jing-Zhang railway corridor splits the area in two, and slow-travel paths on both sides lack continuous stitching, forcing pedestrians and cyclists to detour; second, **heritage conservation vs. development** — as a cultural-heritage corridor, the Jing-Zhang railway site imposes protection requirements in tension with high-density development ambitions; third, **insufficient rail-station coverage** — the last-kilometer connection between metro entrances and surrounding blocks and industrial buildings is weak. The above is a qualitative diagnosis; quantitative values such as the current number of dead-end roads and slow-travel coverage await official field survey and are not pre-set by this proposal.

## Coordinated Research Area: Industry and Future City Research

The coordinated research scope scales the "nutrient anchor" logic up to the regional scale: as the interface between Haidian's Zhongguancun Science City and the Century Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt, Dazhongsi's road-network renewal is not a single street's engineering but one node in the regional reconnection of innovation elements. The research scope and five coordination lines are defined by the taskbook [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK] and unfold as follows — **innovation-element coordination** (universities, enterprises, and computing power linked inside and outside the area), **transport coordination** (rail and slow-travel plugged into the regional skeleton), **ecological coordination** (the Qinghe blue-green corridor linked to the regional water system), **cultural coordination** (the Jing-Zhang heritage corridor linked to the regional cultural narrative), and **governance coordination** (staged release aligned with regional AI governance). Eight elements (land, space, industry, capital, talent, computing power, data, and scenario) coordinate inside the area under the principle of "ordinary path first, AI only adds gains", grounding future-city industrial vitality in recomputable spatial carriers.

## Overall Design Area: Urban Renewal and Regulatory-Plan-Level Urban Design

[depth:overall_spatial_structure] The overall design centers on one recomputable core concept: the **bio-adaptive road network**. The slime mold (Physarum polycephalum) foraging network has three transferable properties — **minimal redundancy** (total length near-optimal), **high resilience** (still connected after local failure), and **low crossing** (routing around impassable obstacles). These three are exactly what Dazhongsi's road network lacks: the current network is highly redundant, fragmented, and at high risk of heritage crossing. Translation mechanism: set parks, metro stations, the railway corridor, and industrial buildings as "nutrient anchors", and set heritage protection zones and impassable redlines as "obstacles"; the algorithm iteratively grows a "main-vein — branch — slow-loop — green-corridor" four-level network among the anchors.

The overall spatial structure is **one core · three areas · one interface · one link**: the **one core** is the Dazhongsi AI industry cluster core, the **three areas** align with the taskbook's "Three Areas, Two Wings", the **one interface** is the Qinghe innovation interface, and the **one link** is the coordination interface with the region. Development intensity, building height, and urban character are given as conceptual suggestions at regulatory-plan-level urban-design depth, to be coordinated with the control plan and approved by the competent authority; the ordinary path always goes first, and the AI-generated network is layered on only as a reversible suggestion.

## Detailed Design of Key Areas

[depth:three_key_area_detailed_design] The three key areas correspond to three kinds of "break stitching", each with a conceptual plan and project placement without adding new parcel redlines, property rights, or permits:

1. **Jing-Zhang Heritage Park slow-travel break stitching zone** [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-001]: stitches slow-travel across the railway and implements heritage-sensitive design, turning the heritage corridor from a "dividing line" into a "shared belt";
2. **Zhongzhiyuan Qinghe innovation interface zone** [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-002]: an open interface serving universities and enterprises, letting innovation elements flow freely across the interface;
3. **Yuandian community near-campus research-commercialization street** [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-003]: research-commercialization and youth-friendly functions, grounding the "nutrient anchors" in people's daily movement.

## AI Innovation Ecosystem, Personas, and AI+ Scenarios

AI governance is an extension of the method, not an appended scenario. **Ordinary path first + staged release G0-G3**: G0 ordinary path always open — walking, cycling, driving, and manual service are always available, and the slow-travel network does not degrade when AI goes live; G1 local recomputation — algorithmic results must be locally reproducible; G2 professional review — structure, accessibility, safety, and permits must be professionally signed off; G3 bounded field window — entered only after evidence is complete, and any level that fails returns to the previous one.

Personas cover five groups: young makers, university researchers, enterprise engineers, community residents, and visitors. AI+ scenarios run along three tracks: **AI+ transport** (slow-travel break identification and route recommendation, with ordinary walking always prioritized), **AI+ services** (empathetic matching of enterprise and public services, with manual service as fallback), and **AI+ governance** (staged-release receipts and traceable risk). Every AI scenario must pass staged release to move from demo to daily use; each level keeps stop conditions, reversible measures, and archived receipts; ordinary-path completeness 1.0 [metric:ai_off_path_completeness] and human-handoff designation rate 1.0 [metric:human_handoff_designation_rate] form the recomputable AI-off equivalence baseline, and data and models serve spatial governance and public service, with AI only adding reversible gains.

## Blue-Green Network, Public Space, and Urban Character

[depth:blue_green_public_space] A "blue-green slow-loop" links green space and public space: green space [data:geometry/green_space.geojson#GREEN-001] and public space [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#PUBLIC-001] together form the reachable substrate of the slow-travel network; green ratio and public-space ratio are in "Metrics". Sponge-city stormwater resilience and accessibility are co-designed, covering five population groups. Urban character is oriented toward "heritage-sensitive, human-machine coexistence, youth-friendly": the Jing-Zhang heritage corridor keeps a low-impact, legible historical interface, and the blue-green slow-loop controls street scale and interface continuity, so that the natural network and the cultural thread grow together on the same road skeleton.
