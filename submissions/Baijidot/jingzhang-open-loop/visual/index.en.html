The motif organizes space and operations. It is not a new road redline, statutory land-use class or authorization for structural work. Reversible installations and operating links can establish the loop first.

Three key areas: one issue, three stations

An issue moves through safety/engineering, licence/procurement/community adaptation and public release/international interpretation before feedback returns.

Zhongzhiyuan

VALIDATION ZONE
  • Isolatable testing and ecological buffer
  • Staffed safety desk and shutdown plan
  • Output: VALIDATION-PACK

AI Origin

TRANSFER ZONE
  • Licence, legal and procurement evidence
  • Objections and staffed fallback
  • Output: ADOPTION-PACK

Dazhongsi

RELEASE ZONE
  • Bilingual arrival and accessible trials
  • Notice, refusal, deletion and exit
  • Output: RELEASE-RECORD

All three extents are provisional. Route, space, component, owner and data IDs support proposal tracking only; they are not addresses, cadastral references, approvals or asset codes.

Five-gate implementation chain

Machine-readable master: assets/implementation-chain.json. Every gate declares inputs, responsibility, output and failure action.

G0Issue admission

Need, maintainer, spatial host, public interest and exit condition.

Missing: no public deployment
G1ZZY validation

Isolation, retest, data inventory, load and shutdown.

Fail: fix, retest or close
G2Origin transfer

Licence, procurement, objections and staffed fallback.

Fail: narrow or stop
G3Dazhongsi release

Bilingual, accessible, informed choice, deletion and exit.

Fail: return to G2
G4Feedback return

Issues, takeover, incidents and maintenance reviewed annually.

Decide: continue / adjust / exit
