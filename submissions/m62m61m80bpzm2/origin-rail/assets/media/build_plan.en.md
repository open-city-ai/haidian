## 1. Situation Assessment: Why Now, Why This Way

### 1.1 Macro-strategy layer — national will opens a window for "AI + city"

**Basis:**
- **15th Five-Year Plan Proposal** (4th Plenum, Oct 2025): strengthen AI top-level design and systematic deployment, deepen the "AI+" initiative, list embodied intelligence, brain-computer interface, and 6G as new growth engines, and deploy Digital China. **Meaning**: the AI Innovation Belt is not a local decorative project but the spatial landing point of a national industrial strategy.
- **2025 Government Work Report**: continue the initiative, support wide application of large models, develop smart robots and next-generation intelligent terminals; digital-economy core industries to reach ~10% of GDP. **Meaning**: AI scenarios must "land in space"; this project is a pilot for "AI + urban space".
- **Central Economic Work Conference (Dec 2025)**: expand **Beijing International Science and Technology Innovation Center to the Beijing-Tianjin-Hebei region**; make expanding domestic demand the top task. **Meaning**: the belt can absorb innovation spillovers under Beijing-Tianjin-Hebei coordination and is a classic carrier of "investment in things and in people".

**Planning judgment**: At the national level, the belt aligns with three main lines at once — sci-tech innovation, digital economy, and expanding domestic demand — giving it a logical basis for priority access to policy and funding.

### 1.2 Economic layer — dual easing of fiscal and monetary policy provides a funding window

**Basis:**
- Central Economic Work Conference (2025): **more proactive fiscal policy** (deficit ratio ~4%, deficit RMB 5.66 tn) and **moderately loose monetary policy** (first restart in 14 years).
- **Ultra-long special treasury bonds**: RMB 1 tn in 2024, RMB 1.3 tn in 2025 (RMB 800 bn for "two major" construction supporting 1,459 hard-investment projects incl. urban renewal, underground pipelines, sci-tech innovation); **RMB 160 bn earmarked for urban underground pipelines in 2026**.
- **Special bonds**: continue to support urban renewal, affordable housing, and new infrastructure (incl. intelligent compute centers).
- **Central urban-renewal special funds**: ~RMB 97 bn in 2026, focusing on old residential communities and dilapidated housing (benefiting ~8 million households).
- **Urban-renewal funds**: 28 cities, total RMB 455 bn; **Beijing's urban-renewal fund totals RMB 80 bn** (first tranche RMB 30 bn; parent-subsidiary funds + social capital + insurance funds).

**Planning judgment**: 2026–2028 is the most accommodative financing window for Chinese urban renewal. The funding window may close (bond scale and monetary ease shift with the economy), so the plan adopts a rhythm of "seize the window near-term, rely on operations mid-term, rely on ecosystem long-term" — mirroring the phasing strategy in §9.

### 1.3 Industry-trend layer — the AI boom creates hard spatial demand

**Basis:**
- Global AI investment keeps growing at a high rate; China's AI industry maintains double-digit growth; large models move from training to inference and industry application.
- **Compute is the hard constraint**: intelligent compute centers cost ~RMB 200–400k/P (incl. liquid cooling, power upgrades); high-standard data centers RMB 12–18k/kW. Beijing and the Beijing-Tianjin-Hebei region cap PUE of new large data centers (≤1.25), forcing green-power + liquid-cooling high standards.
- **Embodied intelligence enters pre-industrialization**: humanoid robots move from lab to scenarios; Beijing has landmark results such as the Tiangong robot (1.63 m, >6 km/h); embodied intelligence is written into the 15th Five-Year Proposal.
- **Open-source ecosystems become a competitive focus**: DeepSeek, Qwen, GLM lower startup barriers, creating new spatial demand (model workshops, developer residencies, data-annotation bases).

**Planning judgment**: AI spatial demand has upgraded from "office buildings" to "compute + community + scenario" trinity. As Haidian's only linear stock-renewal belt, the Jing-Zhang corridor is the best carrier for these new spatial demands.

### 1.4 Global-competition layer — cities race on AI; Haidian must present an "origin"

**Basis (benchmarks):**
- **Silicon Valley (Palo Alto / Mountain View)**: live-work integrated low-rise innovation communities; 15-minute walk covers work, living, VCs, and café socializing.
- **Kendall Square, Boston**: 20 years of renewal around MIT into "the most innovative square mile", built on zero-distance university–enterprise–capital proximity.
- **Singapore one-north**: government-led phased development mixing R&D, residence, and leisure; famous for "work–live–learn–play" integration.
- **Shenzhen Nanshan / Hangzhou Yunqi Town**: "factor-voucher policy + complete industry chain" and "conference + park + industrial fund" models respectively.

**Transferable mechanisms (three essentials):**
1. **Live-work integration** (Silicon Valley / one-north) → the Talent Origin community and the 15-minute commute circle around Compute Origin R&D zones;
2. **Factor-voucher support** (Nanshan, already practiced in Haidian) → compute/model/data vouchers (Haidian already offers up to RMB 300 mn/year in compute subsidies);
3. **Scenario tendering** (AI-Missions style) → an "open scenario + tender + pilot + review" public-scenario loop.

**Planning judgment**: Global AI-city competition has entered the "mechanism competition" stage. The belt's winning move is not building height but three soft indicators — **live-work density, factor completeness, and scenario openness**.

### 1.5 Local-foundation layer — Haidian endowment and Jing-Zhang resources

**Basis:**
- Haidian hosts **2,000+ AI firms**, nearly 60% of Beijing's large-model companies; the AI Origin Community hosts **400+ firms with ~90% occupancy and ~96% annual growth** — demand is real and vigorous.
- **BAAI (Beijing Academy of AI)** open-sources FlagOpen infrastructure; open-source model ecosystems such as GLM are mature.
- **Jing-Zhang Railway Heritage Park**: 9-km linear corridor; **Phase II completed August 2026** (53 ha, three-track + greenway slow-traffic system); the cultural carrier is ready.
- **Rail network**: Line 13 split (A/B), Line 12 Dazhongsi station, Line 19 Phase II are reshaping corridor accessibility.
- **Brief framework**: "three core areas + two wings", three-tier scope, and an explicit requirement for a "renewal project list, implementation policy, and phasing plan".

**Planning judgment**: Haidian lacks neither demand, ecosystem, nor policy — it lacks an **ordering of these factors into spatial and temporal construction sequence**. That is exactly what this plan provides.

### 1.6 Conclusion of situation assessment

> **Triple window overlap**: strategic window (15th Five-Year + AI+) × funding window (bonds + funds + loose money) × demand window (rapid AI-firm growth, Phase II park completion, metro openings). The window runs roughly 2026–2028: **build aggressively near-term, shift to operations mid-term, and become an ecosystem long-term.**

## 3. Overall Construction Strategy

### 3.1 Spatial skeleton: "One Corridor · Three Origins · Two Wings"

```
   [Wing · Zhongguancun Service]          [Wing · Xiaoyuehe Scenario]
              ↕                                    ↕
   Compute Origin ──── Origin Corridor (Heritage Park main line) ──── Scene Origin
      │                                                │
    Talent Origin (university district talent community)
```

- **One corridor**: the 9-km Jing-Zhang Heritage Park Origin Corridor — cultural narrative and slow-traffic backbone; build it first to attract the rest.
- **Three origins**: Compute (Zhichun Road area; compute + open source), Talent (Wudaokou university district; talent + community), Scene (Dazhongsi; scenarios + consumption) — near-term launch zones.
- **Two wings**: Zhongguancun service wing (enterprise & capital services), Xiaoyuehe scenario wing (AI+ scenario experiments) — mid-term extension.

**Why this way**: ① echoes the brief's official "three core areas + two wings" framework; ② the three origins map exactly to the "factors (compute) — talent — scenes" industrial loop, mutually upstream/downstream; ③ a linear corridor naturally suits phasing — linking three origins first yields visible results quickly.

### 3.2 Development intensity and land use

- **Overall principle**: high density along transit, medium density near parks, low density in the heritage zone; regulatory indicators (FAR, height, green ratio) remain `status=unknown` pending official data, controlled only as "conceptual".
- **Land orientation**: new land prioritizes AI R&D, open-source workshops, talent apartments, and scenario labs; commercial support concentrates around metro stations (M0/support ratios capped to prevent real-estate drift).
- **Basis**: Beijing's elastic industrial land tenure (20 years for general industry; 30–50 for major projects; 20-year parcels priced at ~40% of the 50-year price) sharply lowers entry costs; M0 land permits R&D+support mixes but caps support at 15%–30%, preventing disguised real-estate development.

### 3.3 Retain–renew–demolish strategy

- **Retain** (~60%): industrial heritage, universities, historic buildings — functional conversion (old-factory-to-office costs RMB 1,500–3,000/㎡, far below new construction).
- **Renew** (~30%): underperforming offices, malls — facade, MEP, and fire-safety upgrades.
- **Demolish** (~10%): dilapidated, illegal, or structures conflicting with rail/park — cleared for green space or new origin landmarks.
- **Basis**: central urban-renewal guidance emphasizes "retain-renew-demolish together, retain first" to avoid mass demolition; Beijing's urban-renewal action plan is stock-renewal-led.

## 5. Infrastructure Construction Plan

### 5.1 Rail and transit — "connect rail first, then people"

- **Near-term**: build three transit-interchange nodes around the Line 13 split (Zhichun Road, Wudaokou), Line 12 Dazhongsi, and Line 19 Phase II, with station-city integrated development (TOD).
- **Feeder**: autonomous shuttles + microtransit to close the "last mile" from rail to parks.
- **Basis**: TOD "rail + property" captures land-value uplift to repay construction (domestic park REITs Cap Rates of 5.5%–7.6% validate asset value).

### 5.2 Roads and slow traffic — connect the "Jing-Zhang three tracks + greenway"

- Complete the park's walk/run/cycle three-track system, closing slow-traffic gaps (near-term item #1).
- Leverage the 20-minute healthy-commute-circle concept to link the three origins.

### 5.3 Utility networks and urban lifelines

- Use the 2026 RMB 160 bn pipeline-bond window to include the corridor's gas, drainage, water-supply, and heating networks in an overall renewal package.
- Build utility tunnels in new districts.
- **Basis**: 2026 central funds explicitly support pipeline renewal — the only clearly earmarked central-capital direct-investment window in the next three years.

### 5.4 Digital foundation and city-agent platform

- Build a CIM city-information-model foundation to host scenario data (flows, traffic, energy, scenario operations).
- Connect AI-guided tours and smart-governance pilots to one platform.
- **Basis**: Digital China strategy and the brief's "urban city-agent" requirement; public-data-open platforms supply the data factor.

## 6. Blue-Green and Public-Space Construction Plan

### 6.1 Jing-Zhang Origin Corridor (park connection and landmarks)

- **Near-term**: three origin plazas + Zero-Kilometer Milestone (2026–2028).
- **Mid-term**: open-source showcase gallery, developer walkway, and agent-contribution honor wall along the corridor.
- **Basis**: Phase II of the park is complete; this is "content injection", not "build from zero" — build spiritual landmarks first: low cost, fast results.

### 6.2 Sponge city and green reservation

- Build the whole corridor to sponge-city standards (permeable paving, rain gardens); new projects add green roofs and PV.
- Clear-and-demolish parcels prioritize "green reservation" for future flexibility.
- **Basis**: resilience-city requirements; Beijing's "green reservation" policy; green building (GB55015-2021) as the mandatory baseline under dual-carbon goals.

## 7. Livelihood and Support-Facility Construction Plan

### 7.1 15-minute living circles

- One 15-minute convenient living circle per origin: community services, health stations, culture/sports, markets, elderly care, childcare.
- **Basis**: residential-area design standard (GB50180) 15-minute-circle configuration; the 2025 GWR promotes "one-quarter-hour convenient living circles".

### 7.2 Education, health, elderly care

- Co-build AI literacy spaces with Haidian universities; Talent Origin hosts youth-friendly + elderly-adapted community facilities.
- **Basis**: one-old-one-small service requirements; elderly-adaptation policy.

### 7.3 Accessibility and all-age friendliness

- Barrier-free continuous routes across the whole area (tactile paving, ramps, accessible lifts, information accessibility); child-friendly + elderly-adapted in key areas.
- **Basis**: the Barrier-Free Environment Law (effective 2023-09-01) makes this a legal requirement, not an optional bonus.

### 8.2 Funding structure — "five channels in one"

> Note: Macro figures on bonds and funds in this section are **per public reporting**; exact amounts and uses are subject to the officially released documents of the Ministry of Finance, the NDRC, and the Beijing municipal government. Each item must be re-verified against primary documents before formal approval.

| Channel | Share (conceptual) | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Ultra-long bonds + special bonds (infrastructure/pipelines/compute) | 25–30% | 2026 RMB 160 bn pipeline bonds; RMB 800 bn/yr "two major" |
| Central & municipal subsidies (urban renewal) | 10–15% | 2026 central urban-renewal special RMB 97 bn |
| Urban-renewal & industrial funds (Beijing RMB 80 bn) | 20–25% | Beijing urban-renewal fund RMB 80 bn (first tranche 30) |
| Social capital (REITs/PPP/enterprise self-build) | 25–30% | Park REITs Cap Rate 5.5–7.6% offers exit appeal |
| Operating income (rent/compute/scenario services) | 10–15% | Zhongguancun rents RMB 5–10/㎡·day; market compute leasing |

### 8.3 Financing-tool mix (matched by project type)

- **Public-good** (parks, pipelines, accessibility) → bonds + special bonds + subsidies;
- **Quasi-operational** (TOD support, talent apartments) → renewal funds + affordable-housing specials + REITs;
- **Operational** (compute centers, parks, incubators) → enterprise investment + industrial funds + compute-lease income + REITs exit.

### 8.4 Return and cash-flow logic

- **Near-term**: fiscal + funds lead (negative construction-period cash flow is normal; financed cheaply during the bond window);
- **Mid-term**: compute centers and parks reach occupancy (mature parks' EBITDA margins 30%–50%); operating cash flow turns positive;
- **Long-term**: REITs exit recovers capital, recycled into the next phase — an "invest–operate–exit–reinvest" loop.

## 9. Phased Implementation Plan (three rolling phases)

### 9.1 Near-term (2026–2028): seize the window · establish the origins

| # | Task | Framing | Funding |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Three origin plazas + Zero-Kilometer Milestone | Launch zone | Bonds + renewal fund |
| 2 | Compute center Phase I (200–300P) | Compute-voucher carrier | Enterprise + industrial fund |
| 3 | Corridor slow-traffic gap closure + AI-guided tours | Post-Phase-II content injection | Subsidies |
| 4 | Three test-verification scenario pilots (delivery/tour/shuttle) | Pilot framing | Scenario tendering |
| 5 | Underground networks in the bond renewal package | Matches the 160 bn window | Pipeline bonds |
| 6 | Talent Origin apartment Phase I | Live-work launch | Renewal fund + affordable housing |

**Near-term success criteria**: the three origins take shape, compute is reservable, pilots run, and the funding window is locked.

### 9.2 Mid-term (2028–2031): shift to operations · link the wings

- Open-source showcase gallery and developer walkway fully connected; Compute Origin open workshop expanded.
- Robot delivery / shuttle pilots expand; the two wings (Zhongguancun service, Xiaoyuehe scenario) launch.
- Park occupancy target 70%+; first REITs under preparation.
- 15-minute circles and full-area accessibility pass acceptance.

### 9.3 Long-term (2031–2035): build the ecosystem · sustain

- Full slow-traffic and green-reservation completion; the two wings mature in coordination.
- Memorial system (honor wall, milestones) runs routinely; annual events become a brand (Origin Rail Festival + Developer Alliance).
- Regulatory-control indicators fully recalculated once official data is released.

### 9.4 Milestones and evaluation mechanism

- Annual plan-vs-actual assessment against indicators T1–T7 (§2.2);
- Projects missing milestones two years running trigger a "pause–review–adjust" procedure;
- Third-party evaluation (aligned with the urban-renewal "pilot-before-scale" requirement).
