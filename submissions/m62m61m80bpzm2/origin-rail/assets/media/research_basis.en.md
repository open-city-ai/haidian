## 1. Industry and Ecosystem Basis: Haidian's AI Landscape

### 1.1 Overall: China's leading AI district

- **Enterprise density**: By mid-2026, Haidian hosts **2,000+ AI companies** and **330+ embodied-intelligence robotics companies**, forming a full-stack chain from chips and software to foundation models, data labeling, integration, and application (source: Beijing Municipal People's Congress research report, 2026-06).
- **Foundation-model registrations**: Haidian accounts for nearly 60% of Beijing's registered foundation models (120+), including Zhipu GLM, ByteDance Doubao, Mianbi Intelligence, and Moonshot (source: Haidian District Government work report).
- **Industrial system**: Haidian's **"1+X+1" modern industrial system** places AI at the apex, with embodied intelligence named one of three future industries (source: Haidian 2026 economic and social development plan).

### 1.2 Spatial carriers: AI Origin Community and AI North-Latitude Community

- **AI Origin Community** (east of Wangzhuang Road, west of Zhongguancun Avenue, north of Tsinghua, south of North 4th Ring; radiating 3 km²): **138,900 m²** of industrial space at **90% occupancy**, hosting **400+ AI hard-tech companies** with **2,000+ AI patents and trademarks**; new-company annual growth of **96%** and average revenue growth above **50%**; among Beijing's first AI innovation districts and first-batch global innovation districts (source: Haidian District Government / Zhongguancun Science City).
- **Zhongguancun AI North-Latitude Community**: first phase of **60,000 m²**; university-spinoff projects up to **3 years rent-free**; new companies rent-free for two years with half rent in year three; 1,000 furnished talent apartments at **50% discount** (source: Zhongguancun Science City).

### 1.3 Compute and factor supply: compute vouchers and model vouchers

- **Compute infrastructure**: the Beijing AI Public Computing Center continues to expand into a multi-center, differentiated, domestically-oriented supply ecosystem (source: Zhongguancun Science City measures).
- **Compute subsidies**: up to **RMB 300 million/year**; domestically-made compute subsidized at up to **50%** (max RMB 20 million/year per firm) (source: ibid.).
- **Factor vouchers**: "compute vouchers" and "model vouchers" (up to RMB 100 million/year; 50% API-offset, max RMB 5 million) lower SME barriers (source: ibid.).
- **Industrial funding**: no less than **RMB 9 billion** in innovation special funds plus technology-growth and spinoff-conversion funds for early-stage hard tech (source: Haidian District Government).

### 1.4 Embodied intelligence: from lab to commercial deployment

- **Company landscape**: **297 embodied-AI companies** and **22 humanoid-robot OEMs**; the district is a MIIT robotics SME cluster. Galbot has raised **RMB 6 billion+** at a **RMB 22 billion+** valuation (first investment of the national AI industry fund); Xingdong ERA, Xiaoyu Manufacturing, and Lingxin Qiaoshou lead in sorting, dexterous hands, and tactile sensing (source: International Center for Innovation report, 2026-03).
- **Spatial carrier**: the **Zhongguancun (Haidian) Embodied Intelligence Innovation Park** — China's first (licensed 2025-02), **5.95 ha**, **254,000 m²** GFA (130,000 m² industrial + 22,500 m² commercial), with **100,000 m² of adjacent parkland for outdoor testing**, plus a RMB 10-billion+ guidance fund (source: Beijing Municipal Science & Technology Commission).
- **Action plan**: the "Three-Year Action Plan to Build a National Embodied Intelligence Innovation Highland" targets "a national original-innovation source and demonstration highland by 2026," across six actions including embodied foundation models, robot OEM breakthroughs, core components, high-quality datasets, **whole-region scenario opening**, and ecosystem cultivation (source: Haidian District Government).

### 1.5 Open-source ecosystem: from FlagOpen to XiangShan

- **BAAI**: open-sources Wudao, Aquila, and Emu models; the FlagOpen suite covers training (FlagScale), fine-tuning (FlagEmbedding), and evaluation (FlagEval) across domestic chips; **BGE text-embedding models lead the HuggingFace leaderboard** and power RAG systems worldwide (source: BAAI).
- **Hard-core open source**: the **GLM series** (Tsinghua/Zhipu) and the **"XiangShan" open-source RISC-V processor** (Zhongguancun Lab / Beijing Open-Source Chip Research Institute) (source: Zhongguancun Forum).
- **Events**: the Zhongguancun Forum "AI Theme Day," China Embodied Intelligence Conference, model hackathons, and open-source algorithm competitions run regularly (source: Haidian District Government).

## 2. Spatial and Renewal Basis: the Jing-Zhang Heritage Park and Rail Network

### 2.1 The Jing-Zhang Railway Heritage Park: a 9-km through corridor

- **Full opening**: from Beijing North Station in the south to the North 5th Ring Road in the north, about **9 km**; Phase II opened **August 2026**; about **53 ha** in total, radiating 14 km², nine sub-districts, 70 communities, and 450,000 residents (source: Haidian District Government).
- **Two character zones**: the southern "community vitality section" restores the century-old rails, recreates the Sidaokou streetscape, and reuses the **welding-rail-plant industrial heritage**; the northern "nature and leisure section" links the countryside parks and Qinghe corridor and builds the **"Jing-Zhang Ring" theme plaza**, activating space under viaducts (source: ibid.).
- **Slow-traffic system**: a "three tracks + one greenway" (walkway, jogging track, cycleway + green corridor) runs through, with the cycleway connecting seamlessly to the **Huilongguan cycling expressway** to form a **20-minute healthy commute circle** (source: ibid.).
- **Governance**: a **responsible-planner system** and **"city partner" co-operation mechanism** with universities, institutes, and residents; the strategy is "merge, remove, retreat, supplement" (source: Beijing Municipal Commission of Planning and Natural Resources / Haidian District).

### 2.2 Rail network: Line 13 split and east–west corridors

- **Line 13 capacity split**: between Xierqi and Longze the line splits into 13A/13B L-shaped routes; new/renovated Haidian stations include **Sidaokou, Baofusi, Shangdi Software Park**, easing congestion at Zhichunlu, Wudaokou, and Shangdi (source: Beijing Major Projects Office / Beijing Metro).
- **Line 12**: opened December 2024, from Sijiqingqiao eastward along the 3rd Ring, with **Dazhongsi** station in Haidian, filling the northern east–west gap (source: Beijing Metro).
- **Line 19 Phase II**: construction began February 2026 from Mudanyuan northward through Beishatan, Xisanqi, and the Life Science Park, targeting 2029; the **Changping Line south extension** (Qinghe–Xueyuan Bridge) is open (source: Beijing DRC / Beijing Metro).

### 2.3 TOD and urban renewal: three pioneer districts

- **Dazhongsi**: with Line 12, existing commerce is being upgraded toward an **AI innovation-district pioneer area** (source: Haidian District).
- **Wudaokou**: the "center of the universe" is planned as an **AI innovation-district pioneer area**, merging campus, commerce, and transit through open walls, slow-traffic improvements, and Heritage Park connectivity (source: ibid.).
- **Zhichunlu**: combined with the Line 13 upgrade, low-efficiency buildings are upgraded under a "boundaryless garden city" concept (source: ibid.).
- **Policy basis**: the *Haidian Urban Renewal Guidelines (2025)* propose a **"15-minute convenient life circle merging living and innovation circles,"** integrating blocks, campuses, parks, and communities, and closing "one-old-one-young" service gaps (source: Haidian Housing and Urban-Rural Development Commission).

## 3. Global Case Basis: AI Innovation Cities in Comparison

| Case | Core mechanism | Transferable lesson |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Silicon Valley (Palo Alto / SF) | VC + basic research + failure-tolerant culture | High-density physical mixing; fast-iteration capital |
| Boston Kendall Square | MIT/Harvard walking circle + shared labs (LabCentral) | "Last-mile" research-to-business nesting |
| Singapore one-north (Kampong AI, 2026) | Live-work vertical community (14,500 m² office + 200 homes) + AI Missions | Integrated live-work AI micro-community; government as "super buyer" |
| London King's Cross | Rail-wasteland renewal into the Knowledge Quarter (DeepMind's birthplace) | Culture + technology overlay on industrial heritage |
| Toronto Waterfront | Sidewalk Labs ended over data-governance disputes; became a responsible-AI benchmark | Put privacy, consent, and participation first |
| Seoul Yangjae AI Special Zone | 270,000 m² AI Tech City + KRW 500 bn fund + AI visas | "Talent–capital–simplified regulation" zone toolkit |
| Shenzhen Nanshan | "Model Camp" (compute/corpus/compliance/hardware) + 2-year rent-free + million-yuan compute subsidies; AI & Robotics Bureau | Factor vouchers + scenario tendering + software-hardware coupling |
| Hangzhou Future Sci-Tech City / Yunqi | Alibaba Cloud base + Unitree embodied robotics | Cloud platform + vertical-scenario digital economy |
| Hefei Keda Silicon Valley | USTC + big-science facilities + patient state capital | Original innovation + patient capital |

**Three success codes** (synthesized): ① from "office towers" to **live-work AI communities** (Kampong AI, Seoul AI Tech City); ② from "rent waivers" to **factor vouchers** (compute/data/model vouchers in Nanshan and Haidian itself); ③ from "passive attraction" to **active scenario tendering** (Singapore AI Missions, Shenzhen scenario opening).

## 4. Regulatory and Governance Basis

- **Barrier-Free Environment Law** (effective 2023-09): statutory requirements for accessibility and information accessibility in public buildings, transport, and facilities (source: NPC Standing Committee).
- **Interim Measures for Generative AI Services** (effective 2023-08, CAC et al.): registration, training-data, and content-safety duties; public-space AI applications must follow minimal necessity and human-machine collaboration (source: CAC).
- **Data factors and open data**: Beijing advances data-factor pilot zones and the public-data open platform; Haidian is building a high-end data-labeling demonstration base (source: Beijing EIC / Haidian District).
- **Robotics**: the Beijing Humanoid Robot Innovation Center released "Tiangong"; Beijing promotes "Robot+" scenario opening (source: Beijing EIC).
- **Unmanned-equipment regulation trend**: autonomous delivery, unmanned shuttles, and low-altitude logistics generally follow a "pilot-first, delimited area, scheduled operation, human fallback" framework (source: pilot-city public documents).
