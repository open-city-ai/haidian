Ten co-creation principles

Responded to one by one per taskbook charter.1–10; every mechanism is a conceptual suggestion — see "Ten Co-Creation Principles Response" and compliance_matrix.json.

charter.1–10
P01 · Public interest first

The belts and origins prioritize innovation ecology, public space, accessibility, and civic services.

P02 · Public-source boundary

Only the announcement, taskbook excerpts, public materials, and provisional boundaries are used.

P03 · Conceptual-suggestion nature

All spatial, metric, phasing, and operational items are labeled conceptual; regulatory metrics are status=unknown.

P04 · AI-native innovation

All 12 scenario cards are AI-native (compute reservation, training sandbox, autonomous shuttle).

P05 · Structure and readability

Prose for humans, JSON/GeoJSON for machine recalculation, bilingual and traceable.

P06 · Method disclosure

Tools, models, figures, and asset rights registered in changelog, sources, and the copyright statement.

P07 · Human final judgment

Self-check, review, and scoring are done by humans and professional teams; the proposal only offers evidence.

P08 · Public knowledge deposit

The package, scripts, and iteration records stay open for later agents and professional teams.

P09 · Memorable contribution

The memorial system (stele, Honor Wall, annual engraving) preserves contributions and knowledge assets.

P10 · Human-centered governance

Accessibility, age-friendly services, participation, and human interfaces run through the design.

Research basis

Public research behind every key design decision (industry ecosystem, Heritage Park, rail network, urban renewal, global cases, regulation); full reasoning in assets/media/research_basis.md.

research_basis
R01 · Haidian AI industry

2,000+ AI firms; nearly 60% of Beijing's models; AI Origin Community: 400+ firms, 90% occupancy, 96% annual growth  → Talent Origin·AI Origin Community; 10-min research–conversion circle

R02 · Compute and factors

Up to RMB 300M/yr compute subsidies; 50% for domestic compute; compute/model vouchers  → SC-08 compute reservation; Origin Compute open workshop

R03 · Embodied intelligence

297 firms; China's first embodied-intelligence park (254,000 m² + 100,000 m² outdoor testing)  → Zhongzhiyuan open workshop; SC-05/SC-09 pilots

R04 · Jing-Zhang Heritage Park

9-km corridor; Phase II opened 2026-08; 53 ha; three tracks + greenway  → Origin Rail, Zero-Kilometer Milestone, dual-corridor slow traffic

R05 · Rail network

Line 13 split (Sidaokou/Baofusi); Line 12 Dazhongsi; Line 19 Phase II underway  → Three interchange nodes with transit integration

R06 · Urban renewal

Dazhongsi/Wudaokou AI pioneer districts; Renewal Guidelines 2025 15-minute circle  → Scene Origin·Dazhongsi; 15-minute innovation life circle

R07 · Global cases

Live-work (Kampong AI); factor vouchers (Nanshan/Haidian); scenario tendering (AI Missions)  → Talent Origin blocks, Origin Rail Developer Alliance, open scenario loop

R08 · Regulation

Barrier-Free Law; Generative AI Measures; pilot + human-fallback regulation  → Human review across scenarios; accessible paths; minimal-necessary data

R09 · Barcelona 22@ superblocks

Poblenou's 226 ha old industrial zone became a knowledge district with 8,000+ firms; four-axis synergy, 9-block superblocks, industrial-heritage retention  → Dazhongsi industrial transition + superblock slow-traffic grid

R10 · Nodes and height gradients

Four nodes (5th-Ring gateway/Wudaokou core/Zhichun middle/Dazhongsi gateway); height gradient: ≤24 m within 50 m of the park, ≤45 m at 50–200 m, ≤60–80 m around TOD  → Origin plazas/Zero-Kilometer Milestone + sectional height control

Construction plan

A master implementation scheme prepared from a senior urban planner's viewpoint: macro strategy, economic conditions, industry trends, funding models, and phasing—every construction decision states its basis and timing. Full reasoning in assets/media/build_plan.en.md.

build_plan
B01 · Strategic window

The 15th Five-Year Plan Proposal (Oct 2025) lists "AI+" and embodied intelligence as top-level deployments; the 2025 CEWC set a ~4% deficit with moderately loose policy and expanded Beijing's innovation center to Beijing-Tianjin-Hebei.  → Build aggressively near-term (2026–2028) to lock the triple window

B02 · Funding window

Ultra-long bonds: RMB 160 bn for pipelines in 2026; central urban-renewal special ~RMB 97 bn; 28-city funds RMB 455 bn (Beijing 80 bn); park REITs Cap Rate 5.5%–7.6%.  → Five channels: bonds + special bonds + funds + social capital + operating income

B03 · Demand window

Haidian: 2,000+ AI firms; AI Origin Community 400+ firms at ~90% occupancy; Heritage Park Phase II completed 2026-08; Line 13 split, Line 12, Line 19 Phase II reshape access.  → Build the origins first for visible results, then extend the wings

B04 · Build strategy

One corridor (9-km Origin Rail) · three origins (compute/talent/scene) · two wings; intensity high along rail, medium near parks, low in heritage zones; retain/renew/demolish 60/30/10.  → Compute–talent–scene industrial loop

B05 · Investment scale

Conceptual investment ~RMB 26–32 bn (2026–2035 rolling): compute 4–6, carriers 8–10, TOD 4–5, public space 2–3, municipal/digital 3–4, talent housing 3–4 (bn).  → Estimated from public benchmarks (compute RMB 200–400k/P; parks RMB 3,500–6,000/㎡)

B06 · Phasing rhythm

Near-term seize the window (origin plazas/compute Phase I 200–300P/3 pilots); mid-term shift to operations (gallery linked/wings launch/70%+ occupancy/first REITs); long-term build the ecosystem (memorial system normalized/full recalculation after official data).  → Invest–operate–exit–reinvest loop

Planning rationale · three key areas (click for the full argument)

Overall strategy: one corridor, three origins, two wings — the Jing-Zhang Heritage Park as the cultural spine links the three nodes of Compute (Zhongzhiyuan), Talent (AI Origin Community) and Scene (Dazhongsi), east to Zhongguancun and west to the Xueyuan Road innovation belt. The green spine runs the full corridor from the 5th Ring Road to Beijing North Station, with no building straddling the line: it is crossed only by 7 station gateway crossings (Zhichun/Wudaokou/Dazhongsi etc., mirroring the real underpasses) and 3 elevated concept lines; station TOD clusters flank the park on both edges. All arguments below are conceptual and open to professional refinement.
