Jing-Zhang Commons Line: an open intelligent city on a centennial rail trace

Centennial rail trace × open intelligence × everyday city. A public spine connecting three differentiated cores, two service wings, twelve reviewable scenario stations, regional interfaces, and a human-led operating protocol.

Status: The boundary and three key areas are provisional rough polygons. They are not official redlines, statutory controls, or implementation commitments. Recalculate the complete package when cleared official geometry is available.

No color-only meaning; clear bilingual and generated-content labels; adequate contrast; continuous rest and accessible alternatives; keyboard order, focus state, text alternatives, and no-script readable content.
