EVERY AMBITION NEEDS A STOP, A SUCCESSOR AND A PLACE TO EXIT

Ten detailed risks remain open/monitoring; root risk.json separately maps the eight standard review dimensions.

R-01

Data and privacy

TRIGGER · Purpose drift, excess fields, re-identification or failed deletion

DEGRADE · Freeze access, switch to synthetic/human route and notify affected people

RECOVER · Deletion/isolation proof, privilege review and independent replay

R-02

Physical and field safety

TRIGGER · Robot breach, failed stop, unsafe route or severe weather

DEGRADE · Immediate stop, isolation and human routing

RECOVER · Two-person stop, field check and recovery drill pass

R-03

Service exclusion

TRIGGER · No-account, older, disabled or non-Chinese user loses equivalent service

DEGRADE · Close AI layer and restore desk/phone/paper/tactile route

RECOVER · Worst-group field audit and affected-group co-signoff

R-04

Operations and labour

TRIGGER · No staffing, training, backup, compensation or maintenance budget

DEGRADE · Do not expand; reduce operating hours

RECOVER · Named roles, labour/cost envelope and backup shift confirmed

R-05

Vendor lock-in

TRIGGER · Non-origin team cannot run, export, repair or retire

DEGRADE · Block graduation and payment milestone; retain manual service

RECOVER · Six Teach-back capability replays pass

R-06

Knowledge and rights

TRIGGER · Unknown source, licence, person/mark or withdrawal right

DEGRADE · Hide or hold disputed asset first

RECOVER · Rights register, consent, citation and withdrawal complete

R-07

Planning and engineering

TRIGGER · Treat provisional geometry, concept asset or target as approved control

DEGRADE · Withdraw misleading claim and stop precision design

RECOVER · Official replacement, full-chain recalculation and professional review

R-08

Public acceptance and conflict

TRIGGER · Retaliation or no separation between initial and appeal reviewers

DEGRADE · Pause scenario, protect appellant and reassign review

RECOVER · Independent decision, public de-identified finding and remedy

R-09

Technical maturity

TRIGGER · Irreproducible, excess privilege, drift or late takeover

DEGRADE · Return to simulation/contained state

RECOVER · Independent retest, least privilege and takeover drill

R-10

Resources and climate

TRIGGER · Unmetered energy/water/heat, unknown capacity or failed ecological maintenance

DEGRADE · Pause high-load task and use human patrol

RECOVER · Resource baseline, professional capacity check and maintenance plan
