## I. Core Concept: DAO GENESIS

### 1.1 Why "DAO"

In this proposal, "DAO" carries three layers of historical meaning:

**First Layer: The Railway Path (1909)**
In 1909, Zhan Tianyou presided over the construction of the Jingzhang Railway, solving the steep-gradient challenge with the iconic "herringbone switchback" alignment, thereby opening the path of China's independent engineering. This was not merely a railway — it was a spiritual path proving that the Chinese people could innovate on their own [source:OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT].

**Second Layer: The Path of Innovation (1980–2020)**
From Zhongguancun's Electronics Street to the National Independent Innovation Demonstration Zone, Haidian forged China's path of science and technology innovation. This "path" extended from laboratories to industrial parks, from academic papers to commercial applications, forming a globally renowned innovation ecosystem [source:BJ-KW-THREE-AREAS].

**Third Layer: The Path of Intelligence (2020–Future)**
As AI evolves from a tool into infrastructure, as large models begin to understand cities, generate designs, and optimize governance, a new "path" is emerging — the Path of Intelligence. The Century-Old Jingzhang AI Innovation Belt is the physical carrier of this path [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK].

### 1.2 Why "GENESIS"

"DAO GENESIS" draws from Chapter 42 of *Laozi*:

> "The Dao begets One, One begets Two, Two begets Three, Three begets All Things. All things carry Yin on their backs and hold Yang in their embrace, blending breath to achieve harmony."

This is not mysticism — it is a precise spatial genesis model:

| Original Text | Spatial Mapping | Specific Reference |
|--------------|-----------------|-------------------|
| **Dao** | Innovation Lineage | Jingzhang Railway Heritage Park |
| **One** | Unified Entity | Century-Old Jingzhang AI Innovation Belt |
| **Two** | Two Wings in Synergy | Zhongguancun Technology Services Wing (ZTSW) + Xiaoyue River Scenario Enablement Wing (XRSEW) |
| **Three** | Three Zones in Coordination | ZY-AIIA + BAIOC + DSAIC |
| **All Things** | Infinite Possibilities | AI Scenarios × Public Spaces × Cultural Experiences |

This mapping is not wordplay — it is the underlying logic of the spatial structure: **Starting from a linear railway heritage site, through functional division of labor between two wings, achieving differentiated agglomeration in three key areas, and ultimately opening toward infinite AI application scenarios.**

## III. Coordinated Research Area: Industry & Future City Research

### 3.1 Three Positionings & Five Functions

3. AI Integrated Innovation Belt → Path of Intelligence (future direction)

**Five Functions → Five Unfoldings of "GENESIS":**
1. Full-stack AI independent innovation system → Genesis of Technology
2. World-class AI innovation ecosystem → Genesis of Ecosystem
3. AI+ scenario enablement paradigm → Genesis of Scenarios
4. Intelligent AI-vibrant city → Genesis of Living
5. Global voice in AI governance → Genesis of Rules

### 3.2 Three Zones and Two Wings Synergy Loop

The spatial core of "DAO GENESIS" lies in the functional division of the Two Wings and the differentiated coordination of the Three Zones [source:BJ-KW-THREE-AREAS]:

**Zhongguancun Technology Services Wing (Wing of Intelligence Services):**
- Function: Global allocation of innovation factors, Zhongguancun IP and capital empowerment, technology transfer and commercialization of results
- Spatial carrier: Xueyuan Road corridor, westward extension from Zhongguancun core area
- Relationship with Three Zones: Provides capital and IP support for ZY-AIIA, academic resources for BAIOC, and commercialization channels for DSAIC

**Xiaoyue River Scenario Enablement Wing (Wing of Living Scenarios):**
- Function: AI scenario enablement, intelligent AI-vibrant city, public experience paths
- Spatial carrier: Blue-green space along Xiaoyue River, both sides of the Jingzhang Railway Heritage Park
- Relationship with Three Zones: Provides scenario testbeds, continuous public experience corridors, and blue-green ecological foundations for the Three Zones

**Synergy Loop:** The Wing of Intelligence Services inputs resources (capital, IP, talent) → The Three Zones absorb and transform (R&D, incubation, industrialization) → The Wing of Living Scenarios outputs experiences (scenario validation, public perception, international communication) → Feedback loops back to the Wing of Intelligence Services, completing the cycle.

### 3.3 Global AI Innovation Ecosystem Case Studies

The following eight cases inform the AI innovation ecosystem design [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK]:

**Case 1: Barcelona 22@ Innovation District**
- Background: Transformation from an industrial textile district (Poblenou) to a knowledge-economy innovation district, covering 198 hectares
- Core mechanism: Mixed-use zoning (22a@ planning code), mandatory affordable housing and public space provision, innovation clusters organized around "superblocks"
- Transferable experience: Mixed-use ratio control, industrial heritage revitalization and community integration, public space density standards
- Insight for DAO GENESIS: DSAIC can learn from its industrial land renewal and community coexistence experience

**Case 2: Singapore one-north**
- Background: A 200-hectare integrated science and technology park combining biomedicine, media technology, and IT
- Core mechanism: Government-led (JTC) + international architectural competition (Zaha Hadid's Hive building) + "Work-Live-Learn-Play" integrated planning
- Transferable experience: Government-led industry-academia-research spatial organization, landmark architecture driving regional branding, phased development strategy
- Insight for DAO GENESIS: ZY-AIIA can adopt its "single campus, multiple clusters" spatial organization logic

**Case 3: Seoul Digital Media City (DMC)**
- Background: 343 hectares transformed from a landfill to a global digital content industry hub
- Core mechanism: Dedicated support for digital content industries, Hallyu culture + technology fusion, international co-production and content export
- Transferable experience: Spatial design for content-industry and technology convergence, cultural IP driving innovation district branding
- Insight for DAO GENESIS: AI + cultural content can become a distinctive industrial direction for the Belt

**Case 4: Toronto–Waterloo AI Corridor**
- Background: A 90 km corridor from Toronto to Waterloo, hosting the Vector Institute, Google DeepMind, and others
- Core mechanism: University research cluster (UofT + Waterloo) → AI talent supply → Corporate R&D centers → Startup incubation
- Transferable experience: University-industry-government triangular coordination model, livable amenities for AI talent-dense areas
- Insight for DAO GENESIS: The relationship between BAIOC and universities such as Tsinghua and Beihang can draw from this model

**Case 5: Shenzhen Nanshan Science and Technology Park**
- Background: From tidal flats to a global hardware + software full-stack innovation center; birthplace of Tencent, DJI, and others
- Core mechanism: Market-driven, rapid iteration, hardware supply chain proximity, low-cost urban village spaces
- Transferable experience: Hardware + software + supply chain full-stack innovation ecosystem, multi-tiered spatial supply (from urban villages to headquarters)
- Insight for DAO GENESIS: ZY-AIIA should retain a range of price tiers in its spaces, avoiding exclusively high-end office buildings

**Case 6: London King's Cross Knowledge Economy Quarter**
- Background: 27 hectares transformed from a railway freight yard to a knowledge hub hosting Google DeepMind, Universal Music, and Central Saint Martins
- Core mechanism: Railway heritage revitalization, public space first (Coal Drops Yard), culture and technology mix
- Transferable experience: High-quality revitalization of railway industrial heritage, public space quality driving the knowledge economy
- Insight for DAO GENESIS: The Jingzhang Railway Heritage Park can directly learn from its "heritage + public space + knowledge economy" model

**Case 7: Boston Kendall Square**
- Background: 2.4 km² surrounding MIT, one of the world's most innovation-dense areas
- Core mechanism: University open innovation (MIT Media Lab), biomedical cluster, venture capital within walking distance
- Transferable experience: Dissolution of university-city boundaries, "walking-distance innovation ecosystem" density standards
- Insight for DAO GENESIS: BAIOC should aspire to Kendall Square-level innovation density

**Case 8: Yokohama Minato Mirai 21**
- Background: 76 hectares of reclaimed land, from port to future city laboratory
- Core mechanism: Long-term vision (30-year phased implementation), underground common utility tunnels, smart city construction, public participation
- Transferable experience: Long-cycle phased implementation strategy,预留 elasticity for new infrastructure
- Insight for DAO GENESIS: The Belt's phasing strategy can adopt its "infrastructure first, functions gradually filled" logic

### 3.4 Regional Innovation Synergy

The Belt is not an isolated entity but a critical node in Haidian's — and indeed Beijing's — innovation network [source:BJ-KW-THREE-AREAS]:

- **Northward:** Connects to Beiwu Community (life sciences) and Future Science City (energy technology)
- **Eastward:** Links with Huairou Science City (cluster of large-scale scientific facilities)
- **Southward:** Interfaces with the Beijing Economic-Technological Development Area (high-end manufacturing)
- **Westward:** Extends influence to the Three Hills and Five Gardens historical cultural scenic area

Synergy mechanism: ZY-AIIA undertakes the full chain of "R&D – incubation – acceleration"; BAIOC handles "fundamental research – talent cultivation – open-source ecosystem"; DSAIC manages "commercialization – scenario demonstration – consumer experience." Together with the peripheral science cities, they form a complete innovation loop of "fundamental research → applied research → industrial transfer → scenario validation."

## IV. Overall Design Area: Urban Renewal & Regulatory-Plan-Depth Urban Design

### 4.1 Spatial Structure: "One Lineage · Two Wings · Three Zones · All Things"

![Transport, slow-moving mobility, and blue-green public space composite system diagram](assets/figures/mobility-bluegreen.png)

**One Lineage:** The Jingzhang Railway Heritage Park runs north–south, serving as the physical spine and cultural backbone of the Innovation Belt [source:OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT]. The Conceptual Recommendation (CR) is to establish a continuous innovation corridor along the heritage park, including pedestrian paths, cycling lanes, AI exhibition strips, and public activity spaces.

**Two Wings:**
- West Wing (Wing of Intelligence Services): Zhongguancun Technology Services Wing (ZTSW), along the Xueyuan Road–Xitucheng Road corridor, handling capital, IP, and technology transfer functions
- East Wing (Wing of Living Scenarios): Xiaoyue River Scenario Enablement Wing (XRSEW), along the Xiaoyue River blue-green corridor, handling scenario testing and public experience functions

**Three Zones:** Arranged north to south along the One Lineage [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson]
- ZY-AIIA (North): Full-stack AI innovation acceleration zone
- BAIOC (Center): Innovation ecosystem core
- DSAIC (South): AI-native new business formats

**All Things Nodes:** AI scenario nodes, public spaces, and pilgrimage landmarks scattered throughout the Two Wings and Three Zones, jointly constituting the infinite possibilities at the "All Things" level.

### 4.2 Conceptual Land-Use Layout

Based on publicly available data and provisional boundaries, a Conceptual Recommendation (CR) for land-use layout is proposed [standard:MNR-LAND-USE-CLASSIFICATION]:

| Function Type | Conceptual Ratio | Primary Distribution | Description |
|--------------|-----------------|---------------------|-------------|
| Industrial R&D | 30–35% | ZY-AIIA, DSAIC | AI R&D, testing, incubation spaces |
| Commercial Services | 15–20% | DSAIC, BAIOC | Smart commerce, exhibitions, consumption |
| Education & Research | 10–15% | BAIOC | University expansion, joint laboratories |
| Public Space | 18–23% | Territory-wide, especially along Jingzhang Heritage Park | Parks, plazas, pedestrian spaces |
| Residential Support | 10–15% | BAIOC, Two Wings | Talent apartments, community services |
| Green Space & Water | 15–20% | XRSEW, Heritage Park | Blue-green spatial foundation |

**Note:** The above ratios are Conceptual Recommendations (CR) and must be recalculated once formal regulatory planning conditions are confirmed [standard:MOHURD-CONTROL-DETAILED-PLANNING].

### 4.3 Urban Renewal Overall Framework

**Renewal Strategy:** "Weave, don't demolish" — using the Jingzhang Railway Heritage Park as a suture line to stitch together the urban fabric on both sides, historically divided by the railway [source:OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT].

**Conceptual Renewal Categories:**
- **Retention & Enhancement:** Existing innovation spaces such as Tsinghua Tongfan Science Park, Xueyan Tower — upgrading their smart capabilities
- **Functional Conversion:** Converting aging factories and warehouses along the corridor into AI scenario testbeds
- **Adaptive Reuse:** Activating Jingzhang Railway heritage sites (Wudaokou Station, Qinghuayuan Station, etc.) as cultural exhibition and public spaces
- **Conceptual New Construction:** Proposing conceptual new-build schemes at key nodes (e.g., pilgrimage landmarks); specific construction scale to be confirmed

### 4.4 Building Form & Urban Design Guidelines

**Conceptual Building Height Recommendations:** [standard:MOHURD-URBAN-DESIGN-MEASURES]
- ZY-AIIA: Medium-density R&D park form, buildings primarily mid-rise to mid-high-rise
- BAIOC: High-density innovation community, with selective landmark high-rise towers
- DSAIC: Commercial complex form, connecting with existing urban fabric

**Urban Character Guidelines:**
- Along the Jingzhang Heritage Park: Low-rise, transparent, primarily cultural exhibition functions
- Two Wings: Harmonious transitional gradients with surrounding context
- Three Zones: Each forming its own distinctive character theme

**Note:** Specific building heights, floor area ratios, and other indicators are subject to formal regulatory planning conditions [assumption:A-CONTROLS-001].

### 4.5 Transportation, Rail & Municipal Infrastructure

**Road Micro-Circulation:** The CR is to strengthen east–west connectivity, healing the urban severance caused by the railway heritage [data:geometry/roads.geojson].

**Rail Integration:** The corridor involves Metro Line 13 (Wudaokou Station), Line 15 (Qinghua East Road West Exit Station), and the Changping Line. The CR is to strengthen integrated interchange between stations and the Three Zones.

**Slow-Mobility System:** Continuous pedestrian and cycling paths along the Jingzhang Heritage Park, connecting the Three Zones; a blue-green slow-mobility corridor along Xiaoyue River, forming a "cross-shaped" slow-mobility backbone.

**New Infrastructure:** The CR is to预留 edge computing nodes, 5G/6G base stations, vehicle-road coordination test segments, and drone landing pads as AI-native infrastructure across the Three Zones.

## V. Key-Area Detailed Design

**Positioning:** Full-stack AI independent innovation system and global voice in AI governance [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK]

- One Axis: North–south innovation主轴, connecting to BAIOC
- Three Rings: R&D Ring, Incubation Ring, Acceleration Ring — progressive layers from fundamental research to industrialization
- Multiple Nodes: AI industry testing and validation grounds, open-source community center, international conference center, computing power hub, etc.

**Conceptual Functional Layout:**
- North: AI industry testing and validation scenario cluster (autonomous driving test track, robotics testing zone, AI medical validation center)
- Central: Full-stack R&D cluster (large model training center, chip design validation, AI safety laboratory)
- South: Acceleration and commercialization cluster (unicorn incubation spaces, international corporate R&D centers, AI governance think tanks)

**Conceptual Building Renewal:**
- Retain existing science park buildings and upgrade their smart capabilities
- Convert aging factories into AI testing and validation scenario spaces
- Construct conceptual landmark buildings at key nodes

**AI Scenarios:** Full-territory autonomous driving testing, AI drug R&D validation, large model training and evaluation, AI safety red-blue adversarial exercises

**Implementation Risks:** Complex existing ownership relationships require phased consolidation; some land-use reclassification is pending confirmation [assumption:A-CONTROLS-001].

### 5.2 Beijing AI Origin Community (BAIOC: The Origin Habitat)

**Area:** Approximately 104.3 hectares [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-002]
**Positioning:** World-class AI innovation ecosystem [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK]

**Spatial Structure:** "One Core, Two Belts, Five Communities"
- One Core: AI Open-Source Plaza (central public space with a Contributors' Honor Wall)
- Two Belts: Cultural exhibition belt along the Jingzhang Heritage Park + Academic innovation belt along Qinghua East Road
- Five Communities: Researchers' community, Entrepreneurs' community, Developers' community, International community, Living support community

**Conceptual Functional Layout:**
- Academic Innovation Belt: Collaboration with Tsinghua, Beihang, and other universities; joint laboratories and open research spaces
- AI Open-Source Plaza: Global AI developer pilgrimage site with a contributor honor display system
- International Community: Livable and workable space for global AI talent, including multilingual services, international school, and cultural exchange center

**Cultural Landmark:** "Open Source Stele Forest" — combining digital screens with physical stone steles to display the names and code contributions of global AI open-source contributors. Each "stele" corresponds to an open-source project or a major contribution.

**Implementation Risks:** University land consolidation involves multi-party coordination; international community operations require long-term investment.

### 5.3 Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster (DSAIC: Smart Commerce)

**Area:** Approximately 72.0 hectares [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-003]
**Positioning:** AI-native new business formats [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK]

**Spatial Structure:** "One Street, Two Blocks"
- One Street: AI Smart Commercial Street (extending from Dazhongsi Metro Station toward the Heritage Park)
- Two Blocks: Smart Consumer Block (To C) + Smart Business Block (To B)

**Conceptual Functional Layout:**
- AI Smart Commercial Street: Unmanned retail, AI-guided tours, smart dining, AR try-on/shopping experiences
- Smart Consumer Block: AI-native commercial complex — consumers are also testers
- Smart Business Block: Cluster of AI-powered professional services — AI legal, AI finance, AI consulting

**East–West Suture:** Dazhongsi is located at one of the most severely severed sections east–west of the railway heritage. The CR is to achieve east–west stitching through aerial walkways, underground passages, and ground-level slow-mobility systems, turning the Heritage Park from a barrier into a connector.

**Implementation Risks:** Renovation of existing commercial facilities involves merchant relocation; high heritage conservation control requirements.

### 6.2 AI Scenario Cards

**Scenario 1: AI + Autonomous Driving Full-Territory Testing (Industry Validation Scenario)**
- Location: All roads in ZY-AIIA
- Users: Autonomous driving companies, test engineers
- Function: L4 autonomous driving testing and validation in real urban road conditions
- Data: Vehicle-road coordination sensors, HD maps, traffic flow data
- Privacy boundary: Test data desensitized; no passenger facial data collected
- Operating entity: Park operator + testing companies

**Scenario 2: AI + Drug R&D Validation (Industry Validation Scenario)**
- Location: Northern ZY-AIIA
- Users: Biopharmaceutical companies, AI drug R&D teams
- Function: AI-assisted molecular design, target screening, clinical trial simulation
- Data: Public molecular databases, publicly available clinical trial data
- Privacy boundary: Patient data strictly desensitized; no personal privacy involved
- Operating entity: Professional laboratory operator

**Scenario 3: AI + Large Model Evaluation (Industry Validation Scenario)**
- Location: Central ZY-AIIA
- Users: Large model developers, evaluation institutions
- Function: Standardized large-model capability evaluation, safety red-blue adversarial exercises, industry benchmark testing
- Data: Public evaluation datasets, human-annotated data
- Privacy boundary: Evaluation data contains no real user privacy
- Operating entity: Third-party evaluation institution

**Scenario 4: AI + Personalized Learning**
- Location: BAIOC educational spaces
- Users: Students, lifelong learners
- Function: AI customizes learning paths based on progress and cognitive characteristics
- Data: Learning behavior data (requires user authorization)
- Privacy boundary: Student data encrypted; parents can review and control; no commercial profiling
- Operating entity: Educational institutions + AI service providers

**Scenario 5: AI + Cultural Heritage Digitalization**
- Location: Along the Jingzhang Heritage Park
- Users: All visitors
- Function: AR recreation of century-old Jingzhang Railway scenes; AI-guided historical storytelling
- Data: Historical archives, archaeological data, 3D scans
- Privacy boundary: No personal privacy involved
- Operating entity: Park management + cultural institutions

**Scenario 6: AI + Smart Commercial Experience**
- Location: DSAIC AI Smart Commercial Street
- Users: Consumers, commercial operators
- Function: Unmanned retail, smart recommendations, AR try-on/virtual makeup, AI chefs
- Data: Consumer behavior data (anonymized)
- Privacy boundary: Consumers may opt out of data collection
- Operating entity: Commercial operator

**Scenario 7: AI + Urban Governance Brain**
- Location: ZY-AIIA
- Users: Urban managers, community residents
- Function: Traffic flow prediction, energy optimization, public safety early warning, environmental monitoring
- Data: Urban IoT sensor data
- Privacy boundary: Data desensitized, algorithms transparent, public oversight
- Operating entity: Government + technology partner

**Scenario 8: AI + Green Energy Optimization**
- Location: Territory-wide
- Users: Building operators, residents
- Function: AI-optimized building energy consumption, distributed energy dispatch, carbon footprint tracking
- Data: Building energy data, meteorological data
- Privacy boundary: Household-level data may opt out
- Operating entity: Energy service provider + property management

**Scenario 9: AI + Accessible Mobility**
- Location: Territory-wide public spaces
- Users: Persons with disabilities, elderly, those with young children
- Function: AI real-time navigation, accessible route planning, smart wheelchair/walker coordination
- Data: Public space accessibility facility data
- Privacy boundary: User location data not stored
- Operating entity: Municipal management + AI service provider

**Scenario 10: AI + Public Art Generation**
