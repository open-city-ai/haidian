01 · WHY MAINTENANCE

A city needs repairability

Innovation is measured not only by launch speed, but by whether failure can be found, explained, repaired and restored. One green service spine links a Reliability Yard, Open Repair Academy and Civic Maintenance Market.

02 · SPATIAL SYSTEM

One spine, three yards, two wings, twelve stations

Zhongzhiyuan · Reliability Yard

Models, terminals, assets, robots and service workflows test failure, rollback and human takeover in controlled settings.

AI Origin · Open Repair Academy

Universities, developers, technicians, residents and disabled users repair, adapt, document and teach together.

Dazhongsi · Civic Maintenance Market

Public reporting, small repair services, parts sharing, material passports and annual service review enter everyday life.

03 · SCENARIO CARDS

Twelve AI maintenance scenarios, four for industry validation

SC-01

Public-space fault triage

Collect only location, asset and fault; humans dispatch work.

SC-02

Co-audit accessible routes

Disabled users and professionals make final decisions; no individual tracking.

SC-03

Blue-green health walk

AI flags anomalies; ecology professionals decide intervention.

SC-04 ★

Asset reliability sandbox

Test failure, rollback and human takeover in a controlled cell.

SC-05 ★

Repairability lab for smart terminals

Validate open interfaces, parts access and safe isolation.

SC-06 ★

Maintenance-twin replay

Use synthetic or cleared data; simulate before field deployment.

SC-07 ★

Controlled robotic inspection

Low speed, enclosure, safety steward, emergency stop and equal bypass.

SC-08

Open repair bench and parts library

Repair together, transfer skills and refer to professionals when needed.

SC-09

Assistive-technology adaptation clinic

Users define success and data retention.

SC-10

Building material passports

Inventory first, light retrofit next; discuss demolition only after reviews.

SC-11

Century maintenance archive

Separate fact from inference; dual review by historians and communities.

SC-12

Maintenance Week and public service review

Publish success, failure, complaints, upkeep and exit records.

04 · DELIVERY

Rules and pilots first; space and equipment later

Near term

Create a minimal asset ledger, service protocol, three reversible public rooms and professional baselines.

Medium term

Use pilot evidence to open the academy, parts library, material passports and cross-district courses.

Long term

Expand capital facilities only after planning, ownership, structure, fire, utilities, ecology, heritage and finance gates pass.
