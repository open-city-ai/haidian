# Jingzhang Open Belt, Bringing a Nine-Kilometre Park into Everyday Life

People already walk, cycle, and bring their children to the Jing-Zhang Railway Heritage Park. New park sections, street improvements, and waterfront works are also moving forward. The useful next step is to join what exists, repair difficult crossings, and add shade, waiting space, and public services to routes people use every day.

The spatial idea is simple. One continuous public line connects three courtyards in the north, centre, and south. Zhongzhiyuan has a test yard that can be closed and brought to a safe stop. AI Origin places a staffed service desk at the street edge. Dazhongsi provides an urban living room for commuters, evening classes, and small exhibitions. Six smaller courts along the route handle rainwater, shade, shared learning, public service, testing, and exchange.

## Design Basis and Source List

The masterplan begins with work that is already under way. Phase I and II of Jingzhang Park, the public-space project south of Qinghua East Road, the Xiaoyue River works, and the heritage controls for Qinghuayuan Station are mapped alongside roads, railways, waterways, and public facilities. Public records establish project status and regulatory requirements. They cannot replace official boundaries, ownership records, or construction drawings. OSM supplies urban context only, and important junctions still need field checks. [source:JZ-PARK-PHASE2-20240920] [source:OSM-OVERPASS-20260808]

Area calculations use the provisional boundary in the competition package. Buildings, courtyards, and components in the drawings are concept designs and cannot support decisions on demolition or statutory approval. Four everyday scenes show the intended atmosphere and carry no evidentiary weight. The Qinghuayuan Station photograph and open-map data retain their original licences. Full attribution, queries, and hashes are recorded in the source and copyright files. [source:DATA-SRC-PROVISIONAL-BOUNDARIES-20260605] [standard:MOHURD-URBAN-DESIGN-MEASURES]

Three alternatives were drawn. A technology exhibition belt had a strong image, though displays crowded the everyday route. A single linear park was quiet, with nowhere suitable for testing and service. The selected plan keeps the railway park continuous and adds courtyards only at three existing urban interfaces. This limits construction and allows each part to be tried separately. [depth:existing_conditions_diagnosis]

## Three-Level Scope Framework

The coordinated research area of about 43.6 square kilometres shows how universities, neighbourhoods, research institutions, and public services relate to one another. The overall design area of about 11.4 square kilometres deals with the continuous nine-kilometre walk and cycle route, station access, and blue-green space. The 368.4 hectares of focus areas bring the work down to courtyards and blocks, where entrances, ramps, seats under trees, and test fencing can be judged at a scale of 80 to 150 metres. [source:DATA-SRC-OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT-20260509]

The overall design boundary comes from the provisional site file. The three focus areas are working areas as well, and the drawings do not present them as statutory boundaries. When official coordinates are available, all areas, land-use figures, road lengths, and phasing maps should be recalculated together. For now, the three scales connect regional judgement to a route, an entrance, and a place where someone can sit. [data:geometry/site_boundary.geojson#SITE-001] [metric:site_area_sqm]

## Coordinated Research Area: Industry and Future City Research

An official account from 2021 describes a heritage park about nine kilometres long, crossing nine subdistricts and towns under difficult coordination conditions. Haidian's 2025 statistical bulletin records 92 national key laboratories and 123 registered large models in service. Those district-wide figures cannot be assigned directly to this site. They do suggest that open evaluation, public service, and shared research suit the corridor better than another closed campus. [source:JZ-PARK-CO-CREATION-20211216] [source:HAIDIAN-STATS-20260410]

Eight international cases contribute only lessons that change the space. Railway renewal works when public space comes before development. Urban trials need clear boundaries. Public proposals and algorithm use need a traceable record, and digital-city projects need a planned exit. Zhongzhiyuan therefore hosts bounded testing, AI Origin provides staffed services, and Dazhongsi supports exchange and publication. Universities, communities, businesses, and maintenance teams meet at these three places through visible entrances and ordinary daily routes. [metric:global_case_count]

The route mark draws on rails, sleepers, and a mending stitch. It appears at entrances, on distance markers, on night-time poles, and on removable flags. Its job is to help people find their way without turning the park into a technology fair. Beiwei Community, Future Science City, Huairou Science City, and the Beijing Economic-Technological Development Area are initial research and testing contacts. Cross-district cooperation enters the project list only after the responsible organisations confirm it. [standard:PROJECT-AGENT-OPEN-CALL-TASKBOOK]

## Overall Design Area: Urban Renewal and Regulatory-Plan-Level Urban Design

Phase I of the park is open. The published Phase II plan includes a ginkgo walk, nine local streets, and community activity spaces. The project south of Qinghua East Road has also been approved. It plans to remove about 1,200 metres of fencing and add five non-motorised parking areas, six gardens, and four spaces for all ages. The masterplan distinguishes completed work, approved plans, and new proposals, so other people's projects remain clearly identified. [source:QINGHUA-EAST-SOUTH-20260602]

One Line, Three Yards, and Six Courts forms a network that can be built section by section. The main north-south route first secures walking and cycling. Two principal east-west links connect neighbourhoods, stations, and the Xiaoyue River. Building prototypes stay on hard edges and at existing urban interfaces, leaving the centre of the park open. Testing is concentrated in the north, services in the centre, and exchange in the south. Their building edges, opening hours, and maintenance arrangements differ. [depth:overall_spatial_structure]

Renewal begins with a survey. Statutory floor-area ratios, building heights, and building-by-building decisions must wait until ownership, existing structures, utilities, fire safety, and heritage conditions are known. The functional units and nine building prototypes currently test spatial relationships only. Residents, shopkeepers, and maintenance staff should join the next survey, beginning with the breaks they encounter before deciding where permanent construction is warranted. [source:HAIDIAN-URBAN-RENEWAL-GUIDE-20250716] [data:geometry/buildings.geojson#BLDG-RD-1]

## Detailed Design of Key Areas

The three focus areas use the same drawing scale. Each plan shows surrounding buildings, entrances, main routes, tree canopies, rain gardens, equipment, and testing boundaries. Six sections correspond to cut lines on the plans. Each place begins with a 90-day sample. After one year of use and maintenance, the sample can stay, change, or be removed. [depth:three_key_area_detailed_design]

The Zhongzhiyuan test yard keeps the low-speed loop inside the courtyard while the ordinary footpath continues along its outer edge. Staff supervise the entrance, and the emergency stop faces the people on site. A 3.2-metre test lane is separated from the 2.8-metre public path. The first trial covers only multi-agent right-of-way and remote stopping. Testing ends for the day if equipment crosses the boundary or staff cannot take control. [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#KEY-001]

The AI Origin service yard sits close to the existing talent service. A staffed street desk, accessible ramp, quiet waiting area, and co-learning walk allow an incorrect answer to be handed to a person immediately. Near the former Qinghuayuan Station, all facilities remain low and removable. Their final position must be checked against the heritage-control area. [source:QINGHUAYUAN-HERITAGE-CONTROL-20260214] [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#KEY-002]

The Dazhongsi exchange yard connects the station, the park, and street life. Commuters and shopkeepers use it during the day. Small classes and exhibitions can run in the evening, with movable furniture stored at night. Persistent complaints, blocked evacuation, or poor maintenance lead directly to shorter operating hours. [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#KEY-003]

## AI Innovation Ecosystem, Personas, and AI+ Scenarios

Eight co-design groups come from real relationships with the site. Research teams care about test conditions. Students and teachers need places for shared learning. Older people, families with children, and disabled people pay closer attention to ramps, toilets, and rest points. Shopkeepers, purchasers, maintenance staff, and city operators connect trials to everyday work. These personas represent no real individual, and the project collects no personal movement traces. [metric:persona_count]

Fourteen scenarios are placed across a day. Early morning checks station access, accessible routes, and shade. Midday brings public-service enquiries, shared learning, and handoff to staff. Care, heritage reading, and shopkeeper exchange take place in the late afternoon. Enclosed testing and maintenance inspection begin at night. Six industry tests cover robot interlocks, service handoff, offline fallback, accessibility challenges, tracking-free service, and timed energy use. Each test names its location, responsible organisation, minimum evidence, and stop condition. [metric:scenario_count] [metric:test_scenario_count]

## Land Use, Building Scale, and Retain-Renovate-Demolish Strategy

The provisional site is divided into 11 continuous units in projected coordinates. Research, education, and community functions sit close to existing urban edges. Park green space remains continuous, while commercial and cultural facilities cluster near stations and major streets. The working figure for research land is about 340.1 hectares, and park green space is about 258.8 hectares. Detailed figures for housing, education, commerce, culture, and community services remain in the metrics file. Code 0902 does not appear in the submitted data and stays marked as not applicable. [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-001] [metric:land_use_0802_area_sqm]

Nine building prototypes show possible spatial support, including a test shelter, service desk, co-learning walk, and activity store. Their combined footprints occupy a very small part of the provisional site and do not represent statutory building coverage. After the existing-building survey, each structure can be assessed for retention, repair, or removal through its condition, fire safety, use, and ownership. New facilities favour bolted joints, replaceable panels, and small foundations so they can be adjusted later. [metric:building_footprint_ratio] [depth:retain_renovate_demolish]

The architectural character continues the brick, steel, timber, and muted red-grey colours already found on the railway site. Components near heritage buildings stay low and keep important views open. A more complete service frontage is possible near stations and streets. Only light shelters, seating, and lighting enter the centre of the park. Heights and development intensity will be checked when statutory planning controls become available. [depth:height_massing_character]

## Transport, Rail, Municipal Infrastructure, and Public Services

The movement drawing separates walking, cycling, station access, service vehicles, and waterfront routes. Six sections show actual northern, central, and southern urban conditions. A 3.2-metre clear public path is used as the working dimension, with designated space for cycling, rain gardens, tree pits, and equipment. The approved project south of Qinghua East Road is the first confirmed east-west link. This proposal adds wayfinding, an accessibility review, and post-opening assessment. [source:BEIJING-WALK-CYCLE-STANDARD-20201223] [data:geometry/roads.geojson#ROAD-TRUNK]

Rest points, drinking water, and toilets are checked against a service radius of about 500 metres. The figure comes from the greenway guide and is a guide for review, not a fixed rule for every location. Junction ramps avoid drainage low points, tactile guidance remains continuous, and walking and cycling separate where the route is crowded. Robots operate only inside the enclosed test yard and during set hours. Everyone retains an equivalent route that requires no login or tracking. [source:BEIJING-GREENWAY-GUIDE-20250725]

The municipal plan marks interfaces only. Drinking water, toilets, lighting, drainage, and fire safety need an on-site inventory. Energy, network, and heat-rejection requirements can be calculated after the equipment plan is known. Crossing and utility gaps are recorded in the constraint layer and should be resolved one by one in later design. A concept line cannot stand in for engineering judgement. [data:geometry/constraints.geojson#GAP-MUNICIPAL] [depth:municipal_new_infrastructure]

## Blue-Green Network, Public Space, and Urban Character

Published Xiaoyue River works include 6.41 kilometres of river improvement, 4.94 kilometres of hard-bank works, 19.50 hectares of green space, 14 bridges, and five public toilets. A separate plan lists 11.4 kilometres of waterfront paths and 11 hectares of planting. The two length figures use different scopes and remain separate in the drawings. Along the eastern edge, this proposal addresses access and use without redrawing the water-management project. [source:XIAOYUEHE-PROJECT-20240305]

Six kinds of small court sit along the park. Rain courts take runoff from paving. Shade courts give older people and children somewhere to rest. Co-learning courts stay near schools and the service yard. Test courts occupy areas that can be enclosed, exchange courts sit near stations, and quiet courts meet residential edges. Eight public components have dimensions, materials, and connections, and their first locations appear in the phasing plan. [data:geometry/green_space.geojson#GREEN-TRUNK] [metric:green_ratio]

Four low-intervention landmarks support arrival, rest, reading, and exchange. The distance gate gives directions, the heritage reading stand leaves the protected fabric untouched, courtyard flags can be folded away, and night signals switch off on schedule. Here, the identity system is a wayfinding tool. Its graphics and scenes sit behind people's view of the place. [metric:landmark_count]

## Renewal Projects, Implementation Policy, and Phasing

Nine projects begin with samples in the three courtyards. The first group builds the Zhongzhiyuan test loop, AI Origin service desk, and Dazhongsi evening yard. The second repairs one real crossing, connects one Xiaoyue River rain garden, and adds four heritage reading points. A continuous walking sample, post-opening records, and annual review run through all three groups. Every project has a location, required evidence, maintenance lead, and stop condition. [metric:renewal_project_count] [depth:renewal_project_list]

The first 90 days show how people use a place. One year shows who maintains it. After three years, a longer-term decision can be made. Samples use removable components. The responsible organisation records conflicts and repairs each day and publishes a one-page monthly result. Accessibility, thermal comfort, complaints, and handoff to staff are measured again after one year. At the three-year review, only parts with continued use, clear maintenance, and understandable costs remain. [data:geometry/phasing.geojson#PHASE-01]

The annual programme follows site capacity. The test yard opens in spring. Summer classes move into shade and towards the waterfront. Autumn supports heritage reading and a small developer exhibition. Winter pauses equipment for an energy and maintenance review. Communities, maintenance teams, and public authorities examine route conflicts, repair time, handoff to staff, and resident feedback. Event names, commercial participation, and funding remain operating suggestions, with no commitment from government or business. [source:BEIJING-URBAN-RENEWAL-REGULATION-20221206]

## Metrics, Area Recalculation, and Compliance Matrix

Only figures that can be recalculated appear in the main proposal. The provisional site measures about 11.413 square kilometres. The three focus areas have working areas of about 192.9, 104.3, and 72.0 hectares, totalling about 369.3 hectares. The plan includes 14 everyday scenarios, six industry tests, eight co-design groups, and nine renewal projects. Areas, ratios, and lengths are calculated from GeoJSON, with formulas and confidence levels recorded in `metrics.json`. [metric:key_area_count] [metric:renewal_project_count]

Spatial performance is checked on the drawings and on site. The review asks whether vehicles or equipment break the continuous path, whether ramps work smoothly, whether rest points and toilets cover common routes, whether shade and rain gardens occupy the hottest and wettest places, and whether a person is available when a service gives a wrong answer. The first survey establishes a baseline. The 90-day and one-year reviews use the same measures, making it easier to see where further spending is justified. [depth:metrics_recalculation]

Standards, design depth, and task coverage are stored in three separate matrices. They support review and do not replace drawings or field judgement. When official boundaries, existing-building records, ownership, utilities, fire-safety information, and heritage coordinates arrive, geometry and metrics should be recalculated together, with the version date kept on every drawing. [source:SITE-PACKAGE]

## Risk, Copyright, and Compliance

The risk file records limits in public information, privacy, copyright, and professional review. Provisional boundaries may change the area. Open maps may omit roads or buildings. Existing structures and utilities have not been surveyed, and the heritage-control area has no authoritative GIS coordinates in this package. The competent authorities and professional teams must check all of them before implementation. Concept drawings carry no promise of approval, investment, construction, or operation. [depth:risk_missing_data]

Public services keep a staffed counter and a route for appeal. Low-speed equipment stays inside the enclosed test yard. Ordinary routes require no login and collect no movement trace. Health, talent, and business services provide referral and support, leaving high-impact decisions with staff. Children, older people, and disabled people join co-design with appropriate safeguards and are not used as unprotected test subjects. [source:PIPL]
