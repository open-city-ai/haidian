Jingzhang Open Belt

Nine kilometres of former railway link three parts of the city to places people can use every day.

02　SCALE

From nine kilometres to one courtyard

Read the full corridor first, then see how its three parts meet blocks and crossings.

03　COURTS

Three courts at the same scale

Entrances, shade, rainwater, test edges and staffed positions all appear in plan and section.

The four concept scenes show use and scale only; they are not records of existing conditions.

04　ROUTE

Make the route work first

Movement, rail, water and six sections share one drawing, so each break can be checked on the ground.

05–06　DAILY LIFE

Who arrives where

Universities, communities and operators each have an entry point, while service, walking and testing occupy different hours.

07–08　BUILD

Build and repair one piece at a time

Canopies, seats, rain gardens and service desks begin on short sections, then join into the full line after another measure.

09–10　EVIDENCE

Keep the numbers on the drawing and the mark on the route

Working areas use one boundary, while the railway rivet and stitch appear on signs, canvas and paper.
