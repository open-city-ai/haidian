JINGZHANG OPEN BELT

Bring the nine-kilometre park back into daily life京张开源带

Make the route work first. Give people places to stop, and keep a member of staff close enough to take over when a trial fails.

We put the opened park, phase-two plans, the approved Qinghua East Road project, Xiaoyuehe works and Qinghuayuan heritage controls on one drawing. The proposal repairs the gaps between them.
