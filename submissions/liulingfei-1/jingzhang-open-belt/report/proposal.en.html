Jingzhang Open Belt, Bringing a Nine-Kilometre Park into Everyday Life

People already walk, cycle, and bring their children to the Jing-Zhang Railway Heritage Park. New park sections, street improvements, and waterfront works are also moving forward. The useful next step is to join what exists, repair difficult crossings, and add shade, waiting space, and public services to routes people use every day.

The spatial idea is simple. One continuous public line connects three courtyards in the north, centre, and south. Zhongzhiyuan has a test yard that can be closed and brought to a safe stop. AI Origin places a staffed service desk at the street edge. Dazhongsi provides an urban living room for commuters, evening classes, and small exhibitions. Six smaller courts along the route handle rainwater, shade, shared learning, public service, testing, and exchange.

It plans to remove about 1,200 metres of fencing and add five non-motorised parking areas, six gardens, and four spaces for all ages. Detailed Design of Key Areas

The three focus areas use the same drawing scale. Each plan shows surrounding buildings, entrances, main routes, tree canopies, rain gardens, equipment, and testing boundaries. Six sections correspond to cut lines on the plans. Each place begins with a 90-day sample. After one year of use and maintenance, the sample can stay, change, or be removed. 深度

The Zhongzhiyuan test yard keeps the low-speed loop inside the courtyard while the ordinary footpath continues along its outer edge. Staff supervise the entrance, and the emergency stop faces the people on site. A 3.2-metre test lane is separated from the 2.8-metre public path. The first trial covers only multi-agent right-of-way and remote stopping. Testing ends for the day if equipment crosses the boundary or staff cannot take control. 空间数据

The AI Origin service yard sits close to the existing talent service. A staffed street desk, accessible ramp, quiet waiting area, and co-learning walk allow an incorrect answer to be handed to a person immediately. Near the former Qinghuayuan Station, all facilities remain low and removable. Their final position must be checked against the heritage-control area. 来源 空间数据

The Dazhongsi exchange yard connects the station, the park, and street life. Commuters and shopkeepers use it during the day. Small classes and exhibitions can run in the evening, with movable furniture stored at night. Persistent complaints, blocked evacuation, or poor maintenance lead directly to shorter operating hours. 空间数据

AI Innovation Ecosystem, Personas, and AI+ Scenarios

Eight co-design groups come from real relationships with the site. Research teams care about test conditions. Students and teachers need places for shared learning. Older people, families with children, and disabled people pay closer attention to ramps, toilets, and rest points. Shopkeepers, purchasers, maintenance staff, and city operators connect trials to everyday work. These personas represent no real individual, and the project collects no personal movement traces. 指标

Care, heritage reading, and shopkeeper exchange take place in the late afternoon. Enclosed testing and maintenance inspection begin at night. Six industry tests cover robot interlocks, service handoff, offline fallback, accessibility challenges, tracking-free service, and timed energy use. Renewal Projects, Implementation Policy, and Phasing

Nine projects begin with samples in the three courtyards. The first group builds the Zhongzhiyuan test loop, AI Origin service desk, and Dazhongsi evening yard. The second repairs one real crossing, connects one Xiaoyue River rain garden, and adds four heritage reading points. A continuous walking sample, post-opening records, and annual review run through all three groups. Every project has a location, required evidence, maintenance lead, and stop condition. 指标 深度

The first 90 days show how people use a place. One year shows who maintains it. After three years, a longer-term decision can be made. Samples use removable components. The responsible organisation records conflicts and repairs each day and publishes a one-page monthly result. Accessibility, thermal comfort, complaints, and handoff to staff are measured again after one year. At the three-year review, only parts with continued use, clear maintenance, and understandable costs remain. 空间数据

The annual programme follows site capacity. The test yard opens in spring. Summer classes move into shade and towards the waterfront. Autumn supports heritage reading and a small developer exhibition. Winter pauses equipment for an energy and maintenance review. Communities, maintenance teams, and public authorities examine route conflicts, repair time, handoff to staff, and resident feedback. Event names, commercial participation, and funding remain operating suggestions, with no commitment from government or business. 来源

Metrics, Area Recalculation, and Compliance Matrix

Only figures that can be recalculated appear in the main proposal. The provisional site measures about 11.413 square kilometres. The three focus areas have working areas of about 192.9, 104.3, and 72.0 hectares, totalling about 369.3 hectares. The plan includes 14 everyday scenarios, six industry tests, eight co-design groups, and nine renewal projects. Areas, ratios, and lengths are calculated from GeoJSON, with formulas and confidence levels recorded in metrics.json. 指标 指标

Spatial performance is checked on the drawings and on site. The review asks whether vehicles or equipment break the continuous path, whether ramps work smoothly, whether rest points and toilets cover common routes, whether shade and rain gardens occupy the hottest and wettest places, and whether a person is available when a service gives a wrong answer. The first survey establishes a baseline. The 90-day and one-year reviews use the same measures, making it easier to see where further spending is justified. 深度

Risk, Copyright, and Compliance

The risk file records limits in public information, privacy, copyright, and professional review. Provisional boundaries may change the area. Open maps may omit roads or buildings. Existing structures and utilities have not been surveyed, and the heritage-control area has no authoritative GIS coordinates in this package. The competent authorities and professional teams must check all of them before implementation. Concept drawings carry no promise of approval, investment, construction, or operation. 深度

Public services keep a staffed counter and a route for appeal. Low-speed equipment stays inside the enclosed test yard. Ordinary routes require no login and collect no movement trace. Health, talent, and business services provide referral and support, leaving high-impact decisions with staff. Children, older people, and disabled people join co-design with appropriate safeguards and are not used as unprotected test subjects. 来源
