人机共线 Centennial Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt · Urban Design Proposal Human-Machine Co-Line · THE CO-LINE

In 1909, Zhan Tianyou wrote the character "人" in steel rails at Qinglongqiao, climbing an uncertain mountain gradient with a deterministic track. In 2026, along the same line, we write "人机": a deterministic urban skeleton adapting to the uncertain iteration of AI. This page is the offline electronic exhibit: it translates the overview map, three-level scope, key areas, land use, mobility and slow traffic, blue-green public space, buildings and renewal projects, AI scenarios, core metrics, task coverage, self-check status, sources and assumptions into a human-readable visual briefing.

3Key Areas

The three key detailed-design areas (key_area_count = 3) are organized by railway-functional positioning as Machine Depot — Switchback — Marshalling Yard, each with its industry positioning, spatial actions and AI scenarios.

key_areas.geojson · 192.1 / 104.3 / 72.0 ha

Machine Depot · Zhongzhiyuan N · 192.1 ha

Zhongzhiyuan AI Acceleration Area

Positioning: the "traction segment" of the full-stack independent AI innovation system, anchored by Xuebei Garden (238,300 m², opened July 2026) around Xuezhiyuan Station, Changping Line.

  • Underground integration linking R&D parcels
  • Qinghe River interface + Jing-Zhang Loop 1909 → Commit 0 Plaza
  • Fifth Ring gateway "Open Source Gate" + external transport feeders

The Switchback · Origin Community M · 104.3 ha

Beijing AI Origin Community

Positioning: the "turning place" of innovation; the 1-km campus-adjacent ecosystem around Tsinghua, Peking University and CAS lets research switch back into products — the birthplace of the Human-Machine Co-Line.

  • Switchback Plaza: "where the line turns" at Wudaokou
  • Campus-adjacent commercialization street: incubators, open-source community, showcase windows
  • Low-disturbance organic renewal, avoiding large-scale demolition

Marshalling Yard · Dazhongsi S · 72.0 ha

Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster

Positioning: the "marshalling yard" of agents, smart terminals and content, assembling scattered AI-native businesses into an industrial train (Line 12 / 13 interchange).

  • Four-quadrant pedestrian connectivity closing the approx. 8-minute out-of-station transfer gap
  • Smart business district activating Zhongkun Plaza and other carriers
  • Composite use of green-space underground/edge space for data-factor pilots

5Mobility & Slow Traffic

Rail backbone: Line 13 (13A/13B expansion), Line 12, Changping Line south extension, Lines 10/4/15 and the Jing-Zhang HSR. Slow-traffic network: the "three paths and one green" of the heritage park plus 46 merge points.

roads.geojson · LINE13 / LINE12 / Changping south ext.
Fig. 4 · Integrated mobility, slow-traffic and blue-green system (mobility-bluegreen.png)Rail + slow traffic + blue-green + AI scenario nodes

Rail and slow-traffic levers

  • Rail backbone: Line 13 split into 13A/13B, Line 12, Changping south extension, Lines 10/4/15, Jing-Zhang HSR.
  • Dazhongsi four-quadrant connectivity: closing the approx. 8-minute out-of-station transfer between Lines 12 and 13.
  • Station-city integration: Wudaokou, west entrance of Qinghua East Road and other rail nodes.
  • Three paths and one green: walking, jogging, cycling + green space, connectable to the Huilongguan bicycle expressway.
  • 46 merge points / 9 branch roads: stitching east-west breaks onto the mainline.

Municipal and new infrastructure (conceptual)

  • Distributed energy, edge computing power and public charging along the mainline, combined with elastic reservations in white-space land.
  • AI service facilities (computing-power scheduling, data-circulation sandboxes) embedded at the three stations.

7Buildings

Building massing is conceptual and indicative: 24-60 m mixed heights in the three station core areas and 12-36 m along the mainline, uniformly staying clear of the heritage-park spine; renewal follows "low disturbance, organic renewal, reversibility."

buildings.geojson · A-CONTROLS-001

Building massing (indicative)

  • Three station core areas: 24-60 m mixed heights — precision plus vitality
  • Along the mainline: 12-36 m, preserving park sightlines
  • Buildings stay clear of the heritage-park spine; the Open Source Mainline must not be encroached upon

Retain-renovate-demolish (low-disturbance)

  • Retain: railway heritage and protected elements (former Qinghuayuan Station site), university and research functions
  • Renovate: low-efficiency commercial and industrial carriers (Cold Storage Plant, Zhongkun Plaza, Lanjinglija)
  • New-build: flexible industrial space in white-space parcels (version reservations)
