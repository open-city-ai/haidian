Assumptions Register · assumptions.json

  1. The three-tier scope (coordinated 43.6 km² / overall 11.4 km² / key 368.4 hectares) and the three key areas use the repository's provisional rough polygons as the basis for generation, self-check and visualization.
  2. Area recalculation is unified under EPSG:4548 (Beijing local coordinate system); confidence of provisional-source metrics is marked low.
  3. The polygons in land_use.geojson cover the submission boundary with no gaps and no overlaps; adjacent parcels share boundary coordinates.
  4. Haidian public data packages (basic_info / ai_industry / jingzhang_railway / national_strategic_projects, etc.) are compilations of public statistics and authoritative reports, managed as background_only.
  5. The public status of the Jing-Zhang Heritage Park (approx. 9 km long, approx. 3.3 km², linking approx. 70 communities, serving approx. 450,000 residents, Phase Ⅰ completed and Phase Ⅱ open) is cited as public fact for stock renewal and cultural narrative.
  6. AI industry data (1,900+ AI firms, 89 registered large models, 3,500P computing power, 2.0058 million talent, etc.) are publicly reported figures, not a precise census.
  7. Searched/referenced information is subject to authenticity and timeline verification, distinguishing four categories — historical fact, current status, speculation and vision — without repeatedly restating past issues.
  8. Statutory control values such as FAR, building height and building intensity lack official regulatory plans, existing buildings, ownership or engineering conditions, and are uniformly recorded as status=unknown.
  9. The retain/renovate/demolish scheme is a concept-level classification (retain / renovate / demolish); demolition is limited to dangerous buildings, illegal constructions and genuinely unusable low-quality space, prioritizing retention, reuse and improvement; no statutory conclusions or timing judgments are made.
  10. Professional calculations for municipal pipelines, energy loads, municipal capacity, rail and road engineering conditions lack regulatory-plan conditions; they are items pending confirmation, with no calculation conclusions.
  11. The four strokes of "Ren" (left stroke origin / right stroke application / vertical spine people's backbone / convergence AI origin) and the three belts & three faces are spatial concept mappings of the design scheme, not an officially defined spatial structure.
  12. Concept landmarks ("Ren" Origin Plaza, Jing-Zhang "Ren" Memorial Path, Zhongguancun Innovation Honor Corridor) are spiritual anchors and do not equal approved construction; test scenarios do not equal approved operation.
  13. Long-term operation mechanisms (annual event system, developer community, scenario-opening operations, international communication & investment conversion) and all investment attraction, funding and policy arrangements are concept proposals and directions for deepening, not confirmed government arrangements.
  14. Every AI scenario must pass the six-question technical applicability criteria to be retained; Class C "deliberate non-use" is recorded as auditable evidence that the scheme is "judgmental".
  15. Privacy & digital divide: no personally identifiable sensitive information is collected; public-service scenarios retain human review and manual service channels; traditional and intelligent services run in parallel.
