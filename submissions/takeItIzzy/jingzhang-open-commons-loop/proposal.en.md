# Jing-Zhang Open Source Coexistence Ring: Urban Design for Verifiable Public Intelligence with Three Cores and Two Wings

## Urban Design for Verifiable Public Intelligence with Three Cores and Two Wings

**Jing-Zhang Open Commons Loop / JZ OCL**

> Let every intelligence leave public evidence.

This proposal begins with a more fundamental question than "where to place AI enterprises": when artificial intelligence enters the city, who benefits, who bears the risk, who has the right to review, and how to exit after failure? Therefore, the "Jing-Zhang Open Source Coexistence Ring" does not treat AI as a building facade, screen installation, or pervasive sensing network. Instead, it defines it as a set of public institutions and spatial interfaces: purpose-specific, data-minimal, process-traceable, human-reviewed, and revocable. The century-long timeline of the Jing-Zhang Railway thus serves not only as a cultural backdrop but also as a design ethic—evolving from visible industrial infrastructure to auditable digital infrastructure.

The spatial syntax of the proposal is "one spine, three rings, two wings, and twelve compartments." The one spine is the Jing-Zhang Cultural, Blue-Green Slow Travel, and Public Evidence Coexistence Spine; the three rings are the Zhongzhiyuan "Full Stack Autonomous Ring," the Beijing AI Origin Community "Open Source Transformation Ring," and the Dazhongsi "Intelligent Body Economy Ring"; the two wings are the Zhongguancun Science and Technology Service Interface and the Xiaoyuehe Scenario Empowerment Interface; the twelve compartments are urban AI scenarios with data, verification, and exit conditions. All spatial conclusions are Open Co-Creation proposals, which require further deepening by planning, architecture, transportation, landscape, municipal, cultural heritage, operation, and community professional teams.

## Three-Level Scope Framework

The three scopes are not three independent maps, but a set of work cycles from strategy to space, and then from space back to strategy. The Coordinated Research Area covers approximately 43.6 square kilometers, used to answer how the AI innovation chain in Haidian relates to university innovation, corporate transformation, public life, and international dissemination; the Overall Design Area, which is approximately 11.4 square kilometers [metric:announced_site_area_sqm], is used to answer how Urban Renewal, industrial spaces, transportation and utilities, and blue-green Public Spaces can be integrated; the three key areas cover a combined area of approximately 368.4 hectares, used to ground functions, buildings, public spaces, transportation, scenarios, and implementation thresholds in detailed design. This framework is constrained by the [depth:three_level_scope_framework], and the current number of key areas submitted geometrically is [metric:key_area_count].

The upper tier proposes the principles of "public intelligence" for industry and governance: AI products are not only developed within the park but also validated, explained, and exited in controlled scenarios. The overall tier translates these principles into a coexistence spine, six types of land interfaces, three horizontal connections, a blue-green Walking and Cycling Network, and nine update project packages. The focus tier uses three different innovation cycles to verify whether the same principle can adapt to full-stack autonomy, open-source transformation, and agent economies. Feedback direction is equally important: if the focus areas cannot provide barrier-free access, a public ground floor, Human Review, or low-disturbance maintenance conditions, the overall structure cannot claim to be "public." If scenarios cannot be withdrawn, the upper tier cannot count them as positive contributions to the innovation index.

[data:geometry/site_boundary.geojson#SITE-001] is the temporary working container for the Overall Design Area, while [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-001], [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-002], and [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-003] are the key layer working containers. The Coordinated Research Area is not fabricated as a new polygon but is expressed through industrial mechanisms, case studies, user profiles, and operational models. All three layers of results are linked back to `compliance_matrix.json`: 17 announcement tasks and 6 agent tasks totaling 23 items, each corresponding to the text, layers, indicators, and drawings to prevent grand narratives from overshadowing the essential items.

## Coordinated Research Area: Industry and Future City Research

### One Ridge, Three Rings, Two Wings, Twelve Bays

Overall spatial structure [depth:overall_spatial_structure] prioritizes the first principle not of maximizing industrial floor area, but of shortening the cycle of "knowledge generation—open collaboration—product validation—public benefit—rule revision." A spine along the Jing-Zhang cultural and blue-green slow travel system connects three cores, serving both as a daily commute and leisure path and as a public interface for making technical governance visible. The third ring takes on complementary roles: the northern Zhongzhiyuan organizes standards, safety, model validation, and full-stack collaboration; the central AI Origin community organizes university innovation, open-source release, first-mile transformation, and talent living; the southern Dazhongsi organizes intelligent body products, smart terminals, digital content, enterprise services, and international exchanges. The Zhongguancun Technology Services Wing integrates intellectual property, legal, capital, computing power, and talent services into the third ring; the Xiaoyue River Scenario Enablement Wing integrates ecology, community, public services, and low-disturbance experiments into innovation activities. The wings serve as service interfaces rather than additional precise redlines. (Walking and Cycling Network)

"Twelve Modules" are not twelve closed buildings, but twelve sets of portable scene protocols. Each set of protocols includes the target users, spatial carriers, minimum data, Human Reviewers, and exit conditions; only when these conditions are met can they be expanded from the sandbox, and it cannot be replaced by "deploy first, then govern" as a substitute for public decision-making. The industrial value thus extends from the number of companies to four categories of contributions: open knowledge contributions, verifiable product contributions, public service contributions, and spatial and community contributions. The brand adopts **JZ OCL**: two open parentheses indicate entry and exit, the central track represents the Jing-Zhang Time Spine, and the review nodes represent human ultimate responsibility. The text slogan "Let every smart action leave public evidence" is only an original promotional suggestion for this scheme and does not represent that any institution has adopted it.

### Six International Mechanisms for Reflection

External case studies are used for comparative purposes only and do not transplant scale, control lines, funding commitments, or operational entities to Haidian. Kendall Square's inspiration lies in transforming from an "innovation district" into a mixed-use, livable "innovation community" [source:CASE-KENDALL-SQUARE]; Singapore's one-north showcases professional clusters, living labs, and the integration of work-live-play-learn [source:CASE-ONE-NORTH]; Seoul's AI Hub demonstrates urban support, university research collaboration, phased entrepreneurial services, and PoC support [source:CASE-SEOUL-AI-HUB]. Helsinki's Maria 01 provides a reference for adaptive reuse of old buildings, public ground floors, and pedestrian-bike connections [source:CASE-MARIA-01]; Paris-Saclay offers a reference for university research campuses, full-scale demonstrations, and coexistence of ecological constraints [source:CASE-PARIS-SACLAY]. Barcelona 22@ provides a reference for updating industrial districts, fostering knowledge transfer, and citizen innovation [source:CASE-BARCELONA-22AT]. This initiative incorporates the "minimum operational unit": public ground floors, controlled validation, mixed-use living, cross-stakeholder governance, annual reviews; it does not replicate formal symbols.

Conceptual Recommendation for industrial governance: adopt an "open scenario contract": each participant first declares public issues and exit costs, then applies for space and data interfaces; each test release specifies its purpose, data minimization table, human point of contact, complaint entry, and shutdown conditions; evaluation is not based solely on click or exposure metrics but also considers accessibility, privacy, carbon and energy impact, community disturbance, knowledge openness, and local capability transfer. The city does not act as a single technical buyer but becomes a proponent of verifiable needs and a custodian of public outcomes. This mechanism remains a conceptual recommendation and requires joint deepening by legal, data governance, cybersecurity, procurement, and community teams.

## Overall Design Area: Urban Renewal and Regulatory-Plan-Level Urban Design

The overall design is based on the six types of interfaces in [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-001] to form a complete zoning: research and innovation transformation, education and research, smart body economic services, talent living, community public services, and open-source coexistence green spine. The zoning is not a qualitative assessment of the current land parcels but an answer to what public capabilities different urban interfaces should provide: research spaces must have accessible release or validation interfaces, education spaces must connect cross-institutional pedestrian networks with community learning, commercial services must undertake enterprise services and international exchanges, talent living must be co-located with daily public services, and the green spine must be continuous and capable of supporting low-impact scenarios. This layout is checked by [depth:land_use_layout], and the classification expression refers to [standard:MNR-LAND-USE-CLASSIFICATION-GUIDE], which should not be interpreted as the approved land use nature.

The building layers adopt twelve "capacity and interface prototypes" [data:geometry/buildings.geojson#BLDG-001] to test whether the spatial structure can accommodate functions such as exhibition halls, test chambers, talent services, community learning, and urban lounges, rather than reaching any conclusions about the demolition and renovation of any existing buildings. Development Intensity [depth:development_intensity_controls], height massing [depth:height_massing_character], and the demolish–renovate–retain strategy [depth:retain_renovate_demolish] are set with documentation thresholds: when there is no official control plan, existing buildings, ownership, structural safety, cultural heritage, and fire safety documentation, the Floor Area Ratio, Building Height, statutory Building Coverage Ratio, setback, and total building scale remain unknown. The current conceptual Building Footprint is merely a design prototype and cannot be used to infer development rights. (Demolish–Renovate–Retain Strategy)

The control plan depth in this scheme is manifested as "comprehensive reviewable objects" rather than "fabricated precise control values": the land use is fully covered and non-overlapping; the Building Footprint can be recalculated; roads, green spaces, Public Spaces, and phased developments can be cross-verified; indicators are clearly distinguished between announced approximate values, temporary geometric recalculated values, and pending confirmation values. Any subsequent intensity recommendations must first pass through four gates: official boundaries and parcels, public service and traffic bearing, cultural heritage and blue-green constraints, and municipal fire safety and energy capacity. Only when all four gates have clear authority data does the professional team enter quantitative control. (Official Boundary)

## AI Innovation Ecosystem, Personas, and AI+ Scenarios

### Seven categories of users, not obscuring ordinary people with the term "talent"

The plan records seven key personas [metric:persona_count]: Open-source developers require collaboration, release, and sustainable maintenance; university students and faculty need in-campus transformation and cross-institutional learning; startup teams need low-cost experimentation and compliance support; mature enterprise employees and visitors need product validation, corporate services, and international exchanges; nearby residents need low-impact updates, health service navigation, and daily recreation; youth and teachers need credible courses and screen-free activities; and accessibility users, seniors, and urban maintainers need continuous passage, clear feedback, and fallback human service. These personas are only used to define needs, not to label individuals, predict value, or implement differential pricing. The spatial carriers are respectively linked to [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#PUBLIC-001], [data:geometry/roads.geojson#ROAD-001], and [data:geometry/buildings.geojson#BLDG-001].

## Land Use, Building Scale, and Retain-Renovate-Demolish Strategy

Six categories of conceptual land uses are fully covered by the same Provisional Boundary with no overlap. The research and innovation interface covers approximately 336.11 hectares [metric:land_use_0802_area_sqm], the education and research interface covers approximately 100.69 hectares [metric:land_use_0804_area_sqm], the intelligent body economic service interface covers approximately 158.83 hectares [metric:land_use_05_area_sqm], the talent and living interface covers approximately 133.57 hectares [metric:land_use_0701_area_sqm], the community public service interface covers approximately 145.08 hectares [metric:land_use_0702_area_sqm], and the open-source symbiotic green spine interface covers approximately 267.00 hectares [metric:land_use_1401_area_sqm]. The values are recalculated by the design districts in EPSG:4548 for internal scheme checking purposes and do not represent the current or statutory land use areas.

The combined gross floor area of the twelve architectural prototypes is approximately 48.94 hectares [metric:building_footprint_area_sqm], with the concept base ratio relative to the Provisional Boundary at about 4.29% [metric:building_density_design_ratio]. The low ratio does not express a Development Intensity claim but rather purposefully only delineates the "public interfaces and capacity prototypes that need to be verified," leaving the unknown current buildings in the unknown layer. The total gross floor area, Floor Area Ratio, Building Height, and statutory building density must not be extrapolated from these prototypes; after formal control plan conditions, current surveying, and engineering constraints are incorporated, a capacity model should be re-established by a professional team. (Building Coverage Ratio)

The Demolish–Renovate–Retain Strategy uses a five-step evidence ladder, rather than initially drawing demolition blocks: the first step involves verifying ownership, age, purpose, and safety; the second step involves assessing cultural heritage, historical value, carbon cost, and community memory; the third step involves determining whether the space can be adapted for new functions through ground-level openness, accessibility, and equipment updates; the fourth step involves comparing the full lifecycle costs of retaining and repairing, adapting, locally augmenting, or building anew; and the fifth step involves forming building-specific conclusions and publicly disclosing the rationale. Currently, all [data:geometry/buildings.geojson#BLDG-001] are marked as `adaptive_reuse_or_infill_candidate`, without corresponding to any actual decision regarding the retention, renovation, or demolition of any real building.

Building façade recommendations suggest a juxtaposition of "industrial time layer, campus knowledge layer, and contemporary open layer": preserve recognizable material and scale cues, and add a first floor that emphasizes public activities that are accessible and visible. Equipment and test facilities should use modular, disassemblable, and maintainable construction. Height, rooflines, skyline, and setbacks should only propose relational principles—reducing pressure towards the site, park, river, and community interface, forming a continuous pedestrian interface—without specifying unsupported numerical values. This section collectively addresses [depth:land_use_layout], [depth:development_intensity_controls], [depth:height_massing_character], and [depth:retain_renovate_demolish].

## Transport, Rail, Municipal Infrastructure, and Public Services

Traffic structure follows the principle of "connect people first, then connect computing power." [data:geometry/roads.geojson#ROAD-001] expresses a north-south spine, three east-west connections, and a cycling validation chain linking three cores, with a conceptual central line total length of approximately 21.27 kilometers [metric:road_centerline_length_m]. These lines only describe connection intentions: prioritize repairing accessibility gaps between parks, communities, universities, industrial parks, and rail transit stations, then organize cycling and public transportation connections, and finally consider automated delivery and test vehicles. Due to the lack of road red lines, cross-sections, rights of way, traffic volumes, bridges and tunnels, rail transit station boundaries, and parking data, `road_area_sqm` and `road_ratio` remain unknown, and the line positions in the diagram cannot be used for engineering design.

Track transfer adopts the "outside the station 15-minute public interface" method: each station direction first assesses continuous pedestrian paths, crosswalk waiting, shading and shelter from rain, non-motorized vehicle parking, nighttime lighting, and barrier-free vertical transportation, before deciding on the transfer facilities. Dazhongsi focuses on quadrants of pedestrian connectivity with the city living room, while the original community emphasizes the first kilometer from campus to park to neighborhood. Zhongzhiyuan emphasizes external transportation and the Qinghe interface. Parking is not assumed to increase in total, but is prioritized by shared spaces, staggered usage, loading and unloading windows, and barrier-free parking spaces; any quantity suggestions require traffic surveys to determine. The corresponding professional depth is [depth:traffic_rail_slow_parking].

Municipal infrastructure and New Infrastructure adopt the principle of "interfaces before capacity" [depth:municipal_new_infrastructure]. Edge-side computational waystations only define interfaces for isolating networks, metering energy consumption, managing residual heat and noise, fire shutdowns, and public service degradation; without energy, communication, pipeline, fire safety, and flood control data, no judgment on equipment capacity is made. Public service nodes retain manual windows, telephones, and physical signage to ensure that when digital systems fail, health navigation, business services, cultural tours, and event safety can still operate. All data gaps from unverified constraints are centralized in [data:geometry/constraints.geojson#DATA-GAPS], and are rechecked at each project decision point.

## Blue-Green Network, Public Space, and Urban Character

The Coexistence Spine integrates Jing-Zhang cultural narrative, blue-green ecology, everyday slow travel, and innovative interaction into a continuous public infrastructure. The conceptual green space, as defined by [data:geometry/green_space.geojson#GREEN-001], consolidates an area of approximately 222.41 hectares [metric:green_space_area_sqm], accounting for about 19.49% [metric:green_ratio] of the submitted Provisional Boundary; the conceptual Public Space, as defined by [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#PUBLIC-001], consolidates an area of approximately 113.41 hectares [metric:public_space_area_sqm], accounting for about 9.94% [metric:public_space_ratio]. Green spaces and public spaces may be locally integrated, and the two ratios cannot be simply added together, nor do they represent statutory green space ratios or public space control indicators. The design depth is verified by [depth:blue_green_public_space].

Network priority is given to restoring four types of breakpoints: continuous accessibility across expressways and railways, gate restrictions on public pedestrian and bicycle access within campuses, gaps in shade and parking between transit stops and neighborhoods, and low-impact connectivity between rivers and heritage sites. Ecological spaces should primarily address flood control, temperature reduction, habitat creation, and daily recreation, with scientific education and testing activities only cautiously accommodated; any sensor installations must be removable, energy-efficient, and not obstruct basic passage. Due to the lack of river blue line data, cultural heritage controls, and current tree records, specific crossings, lighting, paving, and activity intensities must be jointly reviewed by the water resources, cultural heritage, landscaping, and community departments.

Four "Pilgrimage and Honor" landmarks [metric:landmark_count] do not pursue grandiose objects: **Open Source First Mile** is a portal from academic achievements to public release; **Centennial Switch** uses a tactile timeline to connect the history of railway engineering with AI infrastructure ethics; **Contribution Annulus** records voluntary open-source code, standards, courses, and community maintenance; **Model Assurance Garden** translates risk-testing methods and exit mechanisms into public-friendly displays. All are conceptual public art and operational suggestions, with names, forms, and contents to be refined by history and culture, accessibility, landscape, and community teams, without the use of unauthorized corporate logos or individual images.

The uniformity of Urban Character comes from a "readable layering of time" rather than a single aesthetic: sites and old buildings retain authentic materials and scale, while new interventions use lightweight, reversible, and clearly marked elements. Nighttime lighting prioritizes safety and minimal disturbance, and digital media do not obscure historical elements. JZ OCL signage uses deep blue, cyan, green, and coral to distinguish evidence, connections, ecological alerts, and exit warnings; all critical information is provided in text, graphics, and high-contrast versions.

## Risk, Copyright, and Compliance

The primary risk is boundary authority: all design layers rely on a temporary rough extent, and the official polygons must be recalculated in full once they are in place; the three `KEY_AREA_PROVISIONAL` prompts should be retained until they are formally replaced. The second category of risk is pseudo-precision: the Floor Area Ratio, height, road area, ownership, Demolish–Renovate–Retain Strategy, cultural heritage protection, blue line, and municipal capacity must not be inferred from the current map. The third category of risk is automation bias and surveillance overreach: no scenario should default to continuous personal tracking, and models must not replace approval, medical diagnosis, public safety command, or basic public service eligibility determinations. The fourth category of risk is operational lock-in: procurement and spatial transformations must reserve data export, manual fallback, equipment removal, and restoration costs.

The fifth category of risk is the displacement of public interest by "innovative activities." Public squares, green spaces, pedestrian zones, and community services are prioritized for daily use, with commercial roadshows and tests controlled by time, capacity, noise levels, and accessibility conditions. Residents and maintainers have the means to halt continuous disturbances. The sixth category of risk involves energy, fire safety, and cybersecurity: edge computing, robots, and model sandboxes can only operate after being isolated, shut down, and with clear capacity and responsibility defined. The seventh category of risk is accessibility and digital exclusion: all critical services must retain physical information, manual windows, or telephone paths. These risks are summarized in [data:geometry/constraints.geojson#DATA-GAPS], assumption tables, and [depth:risk_missing_data].
