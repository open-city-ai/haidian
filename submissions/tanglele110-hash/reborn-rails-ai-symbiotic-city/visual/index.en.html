A Century of Echoes: Three Lives of One Railway

Four identities of Haidian are layered here: railway heritage · university intellect · innovation districts · living communities.
Drag the year axis, or press play, to see how this railway has been rewritten three times.

Physical Track · Memory

The Jing-Zhang Railway Opens

In 1909 the Jing-Zhang Railway, built under Zhan Tianyou, opened to traffic, and Tsinghuayuan Station became a key crossing out of northwest Beijing; in 1949, the "exam-bound expedition to the capital" arrived via Tsinghuayuan Station. Where the physical railway met local roads at crossings, steel rails defined the first urban order of this land. The Jing-Zhang railway heritage forms an unalterable base of historical memory.

Spatial Track · Life

The Heritage Park

The high-speed line moved underground; the railway vanished. The Jing-Zhang Railway Heritage Park — about 9 km and 53 hectares — is stitched together by "three paths and one greenway", fully opened in August 2026, with some 46 planned entrances serving about 450,000 residents in 70 communities along the line. Linear heritage turns from urban barrier into public space: the spatial track begins to sew daily life back together.

Virtual Track · Future

After the Merge

In the AGI era, a track is no longer just steel rails; it carries flows of knowledge, talent, computing power, data, capital, and scenarios — and a "crossing" is the interface where these flows enter blocks, buildings, communities, and public life. The virtual track compiles the city — and history and future converge in one operating system.

ACT 02Concept · What we proposeK2+260

Merging Tracks: From a Blueprint to an Operating System

In the industrial age, the city compiled blueprints into static rules through Regulatory Detailed Planning, and addressed railway and road through the crossing. In the AGI era, the Centennial Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt takes "Directed System – Adaptive System – Virtual Track" as the city's runtime and compilation protocol, and the "AI Crossing" as its spatial address. Urban planning is undergoing its fourth paradigm iteration.

Iteration 1

Functional Zoning

The blueprint era

Exemplified by the Athens Charter: the city divided into dwelling, work, recreation, and transport to solve the order problem of industrial cities.

Iteration 2

Digitization

AI 1.0

CAD and GIS moved planning from hand drawing to digital production, but the core remained static drawings and metric control; planning logic did not fundamentally change.

Iteration 3

Big Data

AI 2.0

Data models enabled simulation and prediction, yet decision power stayed highly centralized — an upgrade at the level of "assisted analysis".

Iteration 4 · This proposal

Agent-Responsive Governance

AI 3.0

Rigid rules lock the baseline while agents continuously sense needs, assist decisions, and adjust dynamically; the planner shifts from "blueprint drafter" to "rule-maker and system coordinator".

ACT 05Systems · The urban chassis · Mobility · Blue-green public spaceK10+420

A Composite Chassis of Mobility, Blue-Green Space, and AI Scenarios

North–south is a continuity problem; east–west is an accessibility problem. The Jing-Zhang park and dual rail corridors already form the north–south spine; east–west stitching lands on the nine corridors crossing the 3rd–5th Rings, Qinghe, the North Moat, and Xueyuan South Road.

ACT 05Slow mobility · Blue-green public space · Municipal and public servicesK10+860

Three Systems, One Chassis

Mobility and Slow Traffic MOBILITY

  • Continuous spine: the park's "three paths, one greenway" — about 9 km of walking and jogging main axis
  • Transverse stitching: nine corridors organize east–west links; the 3rd/4th/5th Ring crossings are stitched at nodes
  • Station-city integration: Xuezhiyuan, Wudaokou, and Dazhongsi station integration and interchange conversion
  • Line 13 capacity expansion: interchange organization preset for both "during works" and "after retrofit" states
  • A barrier-free continuous link for the last 300–500 m outside stations as a design check gate
  • Cycling spine: southward extension of the Huilongguan cycleway + B+R interchange (per planning documents)
  • Four-category parking organization; no invented berth counts or quota requirements
  • Low-speed robots / delivery: elastic pilot road segments + time-based simulation + three safety essentials

Blue-Green and Public Space BLUE-GREEN

  • One spine, two belts, three nodes, multiple networks: Jing-Zhang vitality belt + Xiaoyue River + Qinghe–Bajia country park
  • The heritage park is about 9 km and 53 ha, with some 46 planned entrances
  • Qinghe: 20-year design / 50-year check flood; Xiaoyue River revetment: 50-year
  • Waterfront scenarios are compatible with both "open-discharge" and "closed" operating states; facilities are reversible and liftable
  • Annual runoff capture rate: ≥85% for new buildings and plots, ≥90% for new or renovated green space
  • Three-tier public-space hierarchy: district — area — block
  • The green-space status of Bajia Country Park and the Jing-Zhang park must not be altered without authorization

Municipal and Public Services SERVICES

  • Four facility layers: AI industry services / innovation platforms / talent living / new infrastructure
  • Talent housing advances through affordable-space studies; no mandatory quota is preset
  • Talent life services are differentiated by daily rhythm, responding to night-time and all-age provision
  • Four rules for new infrastructure: near the user end, phase-expandable, off the main-spine interface, co-located and mixed-use
  • Flood conveyance is a rigid precondition; no scheme may narrow river flood sections
  • Rail-transit safety protection zone: 30 m outside the Line 13 viaduct
  • Facility baseline data await official completion; no approved facilities are promised

ACT 06Governance · Operation and iterationK11+300

How the City Evolves Itself

The Adaptive System is a three-stage loop of "sense — decide — act"; the Directed System is the rigid baseline of six value anchors. Every step follows one principle: machine assistance, human final review。

Adaptive SystemSENSE–DECIDE–ACT · CONTINUOUS LOOP
  • Sense | Multi-source demand sensing: the Sidaokou 1.2 km² AI signal-control pilot provides the site's technical basis; the 12345 complaint mechanism and volunteer-service network serve as governance references
  • Decide | Multi-scenario simulation and human deliberation: combined with the "one topic per month" mechanism, community councils, and responsible planners; agents analyze and recommend, humans retain final authorization
  • Act | Reversible pilots and feedback iteration: authorize → trial → validate → implement → evaluate → expand or exit
Directed SystemSIX VALUE ANCHORS · RIGID BASELINE

Human Well-beingPublic interest first, inclusive of all ages; the industry ecosystem never precedes the life ecosystem
