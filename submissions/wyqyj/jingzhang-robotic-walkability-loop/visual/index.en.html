Recalculate as a loop, discuss as segments

S1 Edge handover: limited off-peak window only.
S2 Accessibility candidate: human presence plus clear-width, crossing and demand checks.
S3 Controlled testing: not open to public crowds.
S4 Blue-green inspection: off-peak and low risk; close the loop on event days.
S5 Return-link verification: closed by default pending walking, road-rail and management checks.

Each segment has a service window, takeover node, event-day action and field-check list.

One-variable minimum pilot

Test only whether a low-risk task can enter shared space at a single node, in a fixed off-peak window, with a human present.

Pass signals

Pedestrian spine remains clear; every exception has a named takeover record; public feedback has traceable handling.

Stay signals

Accessible clearance is blocked, emergency stop or takeover is unavailable, event-day withdrawal is incomplete, or a complaint has no accountable resolver.

Any stay signal means recover crowd, conflict, takeover and feedback records; do not advance phase.

Auditable, takeable-over, minimum data

Task dispatch→Low-speed run→Exception alert→Human takeover→Public feedback

Keep only minimum event logs required to complete a task. No raw video, audio or personal data is stored in this submission. The model recommends; an accountable operator decides.
