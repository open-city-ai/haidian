# The AI Line: The Centennial Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt Urban Design

This proposal was generated by an AI agent and belongs to the Open Co-Creation suggestions for a global open-source call. All spatial implementation suggestions are Conceptual Recommendations, reference schemes, and materials for professional teams to deepen their research. They do not replace formal planning, nor do they constitute government approval conclusions or implementation commitments [source:DATA-SRC-AGENT-TASKBOOK-20260518].

This scheme has a unique aspect that needs to be mentioned at the beginning: **the scheme author (GitHub: linzhuo606) is a blind urban user**. The Urban Design framework of the scheme was completed by AI through engineering translation, while the entire accessibility design framework (see the "Accessibility Line" chapter) was defined by the author based on their first-person daily travel experience—this includes the rejection of the "Zone Protection" scheme and the establishment of the principle of "Connectivity First, Integration Without Segregation." This makes the scheme a true human-AI co-creation: humans define the needs and value priorities, while AI is responsible for spatialization and dataization. The scheme's logo takes the "human" track as its theme, and "people-oriented" in this scheme is not a rhetorical device but a design commitment personally overseen by a user who is most reliant on an accessible environment.

## Coordinated Research Area: Industry and Future City Research

**Current Conditions Diagnosis and Strategic Positioning** [depth:existing_conditions_diagnosis]: This region is one of the areas with the highest density of AI innovation resources in China—within the study scope, it gathers a cluster of top universities (Tsinghua, Peking, Chinese Academy of Sciences system, and the university belt along College Road), national-level research platforms, and a large number of AI companies. Since the Jing-Zhang Railway was buried in 2019, the Jing-Zhang Railway Heritage Park has formed a linear Public Space running north-south. The three zones and two wings pattern (AI Origin Community, Zhongzhiyuan Acceleration Zone, Dazhongsi Concentration Area + Zhongguancun Technology Services Wing, Xiaoyue River Scenario Enablement Wing) was established by the task book [source:DATA-SRC-AGENT-TASKBOOK-20260518]. The core issue is that innovation resources are fragmented by ring roads and large compounds, the Jing-Zhang Railway Heritage Park has multiple pedestrian discontinuities, and AI innovation activities lack a perceivable public expression. This plan answers by turning "the belt" into "a line"—**One Spine and Twelve Stations, Three Zones and Two Wings**: the green spine will carry the connection, stations will serve as connectors and scenario enablers, key areas will deepen the industry, and the wings will overflow with elements and scenarios. **Three Positionings × Five Functions mapping (backfill addition)**: the task book's three positionings are each carried by this structure — the **Centennial Jing-Zhang Culture Belt** by the "belt" (heritage-park green spine, Tsinghua Garden Station Time Machine, station-name typology, the Honor Track and the "From the People-Shaped Track to People-Centered AI" narrative); the **Urban AI Life Experience Belt** by the "stations" and the east wing (twelve scenario-open station units, 14 scenario cards, the Xiaoyuehe scenario-enablement wing, the Accessibility Line and youth-friendliness); the **AI Integrated Innovation Belt** by the "three zones" and the west wing (Zhongzhiyuan full-stack acceleration, Origin Community open-source sourcing, Dazhongsi intelligent economy, the Zhongguancun sci-tech services wing). The five functions land as follows: **full-stack independent AI innovation system** → Zhongzhiyuan's one-hub-four-clusters; **world-class AI innovation ecosystem** → the Origin open-source ecosystem and the regional innovation loop (ecosystem map in the coordination chapter); **AI+ scenario-enablement paradigm** → the twelve-station scenario cards and the annual scenario-opening list; **intelligent AI vitality city** → the green-spine vitality belt, the public-space component library and the urban sensing base; **global voice in AI governance** → the Zhongzhiyuan AI Governance and Standards Center, the Global AI Governance Roundtable and the Human Review institution.

**Naming System and Visual Identity Direction (agent.1)**: Main name "Zhigui Jing-Zhang," with the English name "The AI Line." Naming logic: A double entendre between the "tracks" of the railway and the "tracks" of technological evolution, "The AI Line" echoes the promotional style of internationally renowned linear park brands, offering strong international recognition and scalability. The naming system is divided into three levels: the Line (The AI Line / Zhigui Jing-Zhang) — Stations (twelve scene stations, such as Origin Station, Wisdom Clock Station, restoring the type of "station names" of the railway) — Path (The Honor Track). Station siting and count will be calibrated in later phases to real anchors — surviving relics (Tsinghua Garden Station), existing metro stations and footfall breakpoints — so that the count follows the anchors rather than equal spacing. Logo direction: The logo is based on the converging trajectory of the three lines of Zhan Tianyou's "human" character-shaped railway, with the endpoints of the trajectory symbolizing neural network nodes, forming the Chinese character "human" — simultaneously expressing the triple meanings of "Jing-Zhang Engineering Independence," "Artificial Intelligence," and "Human-Centric." The extension system adopts the language of subway maps, where stations are scenes and lines are narratives, directly convertible into signage, peripheral materials, and promotional items. This direction is merely an original concept and does not use any existing trademarks, fonts, or corporate logos. A trademark search is required prior to formal activation [source:DATA-SRC-AGENT-TASKBOOK-20260518].

## Overall Design Area: Urban Renewal and Regulatory-Plan-Level Urban Design

**Overall Spatial Structure** [depth:overall_spatial_structure]: Form the structure of "one spine, twelve stations, three zones, two wings, and multiple corridors." One spine refers to the Jing-Zhang Heritage Park Green Spine (including the Honor Track main axis); twelve stations are the AI scenario stations of Qinghe Bay, Zhongzhi, Acceleration, Shuangqing, Nongda East, Origin, Sidaokou, College, North Film Art, Zaojunmiao, Zhizhong, and South Gateway (composite units of station forecourt, scene nodes, honor displays, and community operations); three zones are three key areas; two wings connect to Zhongguancun Science and Technology Services to the west and Xiao Yuehe Scene Empowerment to the east; multiple corridors include the Zhichun Road, North Fourth Ring Road, Chengfu Road, and Shuangqing Road, etc. [data:geometry/roads.geojson#RD-04]. The spatial structure is detailed in Figures 1 and 2.

**Land-Use Layout and Functional Proportions**: The Overall Design Area is divided into 168 conceptual parcels using the "line network division" method to minimize noise, ensuring full coverage, seamless integration, and no overlap [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-001] [standard:MNR-LAND-USE-CLASSIFICATION-GUIDE]. Calculation Results: Research and development land [metric:research_land_area_sqm] is approximately 2.66 square kilometers (23.3%, including AI research and development, incubation and transformation, and pilot verification clusters), commercial and service land [metric:commercial_land_area_sqm] is approximately 1.29 square kilometers (11.3%).

The remaining classes: residential and community service land [metric:residential_land_area_sqm] is approximately 2.61 square kilometers (22.9%), public management and cultural education land [metric:public_service_land_area_sqm] is approximately 1.49 square kilometers (13.0%), and strategic blank spaces [metric:reserved_flex_land_area_sqm] are approximately 0.17 square kilometers to address the uncertainty of AI business forms. The road land ratio [metric:road_area_ratio] is approximately 12.5%. The industrial space (research and development + commercial and service + blank spaces) totals approximately 36.2%, directly responding to the announcement requirements for "AI company clustering goals and AI industrial space scale." Residential and accompanying facilities account for about 1/4, maintaining a balanced ratio between work, residence, and commercial services [source:DATA-SRC-OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT-20260509].

**Innovative Indicator System (Conceptual Recommendation)**: It is recommended to establish a three-tier indicator system for the "AI Innovation Index" — Ecological Layer (comprehensiveness of full-stack elements, volume of open-source contributions, output of standards and governance), Spatial Layer (supply of industrial spaces, area of Scenario Access, connectivity for active travel), and Humanistic Layer (density of talent, coverage of all-age services, public participation). This package has made the Spatial Layer indicators calculable as a baseline (see the Metrics chapter), while the Ecological Layer and Humanistic Layer indicators are listed as pending due to the lack of official statistical metrics [standard:MOHURD-CONTROL-DETAILED-PLANNING].

**Urban Renewal Overall Framework**: Low-impact updates centered around the "Station-City Weaving" — (1) Potential update spaces identified along both sides of the green spine and around stations in a gradient manner; (2) Update methods mainly focus on retaining and enhancing, with demolition and new construction as secondary options (see the Retain–Renovate–Demolish chapter); (3) Each station corresponds to an update unit forming a minimum update package of "station square + first-floor open interface + functional insertion"; (4) The integration of campus, park, and street focuses on the original community and the college road interface. The implementation project list is available in the Renewal Projects chapter [data:geometry/phasing.geojson#PHASE-1]. All intensity and scale conclusions in this chapter are Conceptual Recommendations. The statutory control planning indicators (Floor Area Ratio, height, density, green space ratio) are missing in the publicly available data package and are marked as to be confirmed [standard:MOHURD-CONTROL-DETAILED-PLANNING], and are not used as definitive indicators.

## The Accessibility Line: Six First Principles from the Blind Author

This chapter is the second main thread of the entire proposal, and its framework does not come from a manual but from the daily travel experiences of the proposal's author—a blind urban user. During the design process, AI once proposed the idea of "dividing quiet pedestrian zones on the green spine to protect the visually impaired," which was rejected by the author based on first principles: "If blind people can walk continuously on the tactile paving, zoning is not necessary, and I do not want to isolate the blind." This statement serves as the overarching principle for this chapter. International experience also supports this judgment: the lessons from Europe's "shared streets" practice show that open spaces without detectable boundaries, relying on eye contact for yielding, are "chaotic, dangerous, and exclusionary" for the visually impaired—issues do not lie in the mixing of crowds but in the loss of detectable order.

**Six Principles** (defined in the author's first-person perspective, AI engineering translation):

1. **Priority to Continuity**: The value of tactile paving lies not in its length but in its continuity—any break renders it ineffective, as trust is lost. The Green Spine guiding strip is laid out continuously for approximately 9,716 meters [metric:tactile_guideway_length_m], along [data:geometry/roads.geojson#RD-13]. This Green Spine pathway runs north-south through the entire Green Spine corridor and connects to twelve station plazas, with lateral branches connecting to each crosswalk node. **Materials and winter regime (a precondition for any continuity commitment, de-watering addition)**: the guiding strip uses monolithically formed, prefabricated high-strength raised units — glue-on strips and nail-on studs are prohibited, since snow-plow scraping that peels them is the top cause of tactile-paving failure in northern cities; chloride de-icing salts are banned within the corridor (mechanical clearing plus eco-friendly de-icers instead); manual snow-clearing plans list the guiding strip and crossing plates as priority surfaces, backed by a spare-parts pool and an annual replacement budget. If the winter regime is not in place, every continuity commitment in this chapter automatically downgrades. Continuity is a matter of three parts construction and seven parts management: leveraging the existing urban sensing framework from this plan, deploy **AI Monitoring for Tactile Paving Occupancy** — identify occupancy (vehicles, shared bicycles, piled goods) and feed it into the governance loop. The loop builds **no dispatch system of its own**: it rests on a pre-signed responsibility matrix (occupancy type × responsible unit × handling deadline × verification method — motor vehicles to traffic police, shared bikes to operator SLAs, shop spill-over to urban management, construction hoarding to the housing authority), and findings drop into the existing 12345 complaint-response and grid-governance platforms as formal, time-bound work orders. Continuity metrics use a **repair-oriented caliber**: per-segment average work-order handling time and re-inspection pass rates are published, audited monthly by blind users in the field — measuring repair speed rather than an instantaneous state. This is also the 13th AI Scenario Card (Industrial Testing and Validation Category): occupancy identification is one of the least privacy-invasive tasks in visual AI, with Human Review retained.
2. **Fusion Rather Than Segregation**: The guide paths directly traverse open plazas and walkways, allowing both the visually impaired and everyone else to share the same space without any designated "exclusive areas." The only separation is **speed separation**—bikeways are separated from pedestrian surfaces by a grade and material, as quiet, high-speed electric bicycles and scooters pose a real physical hazard to those relying on auditory navigation; this protects all pedestrians (children, seniors, and those looking at their devices), regardless of the group, only by speed.
3. **Sound as Interface**: All 36 concept intersections [metric:aps_intersection_count] are planned to include acoustic pedestrian signals (APS; signal facilities are under the traffic-police authority, with per-intersection co-build agreements). Anti-silence is enforced by **preset degradation plans** instead of ad-hoc judgement: automatic night switch to on-demand mode (button / phone / remote wake), directional speakers, adaptive volume; "shutdown" is removed from the complaint-closure options, any silencing requires a written justification to the disability federation or the co-creation council, and the sounding rate is patrolled at the same level as tactile-paving continuity. Each of the twelve stations will feature unique audio signatures for their sound markers (echoing the tradition of railway clocks and the "Intelligent Clock" system), with sound landmarks designed equally with visual landmarks.
4. **Structure Must Be Understandable**: Before crossing an intersection, visually impaired individuals need to mentally model the spatial structure. Three levels of provision: (a) **16 Tactile Model Nodes** [metric:tactile_model_node_count] —— twelve tactile map stations, a full-line tactile sand model at Origin Station, and physical models of complex nodes at the Fifth Ring Node, Dazhongsi Station, and Tsinghua Garden Station (continuing the tradition of the bronze tactile models in the "Blind City" in Marburg, Germany; outdoor tactile components avoid bare metal — dark metal surfaces can exceed 60 °C in a Beijing summer and freeze-stick to bare hands in winter — using low-thermal-conductivity mineral-resin or stone composites, light-colored low-heat-absorption surfaces, relief drainage details and mandatory shading, preferably under pavilion eaves; bronze remains only as symbolic pieces in shaded spots); (b) each intersection is equipped with an audio structural description served from smart-pole-powered audio anchors or high-visibility codes (battery beacons prohibited; e.g., "There is a four-way intersection ahead, with six lanes in both directions, a safety island in the middle, and a metro entrance in the northeast corner"), content versioned with a last-verified date; (c) **Open Source Tactile Model Library** — the library publishes **symbolized derived models** (not raw survey geometry) together with print parameters and acceptance test cards, so that anyone can 3D print them at home or at community reprint points to learn an intersection before leaving home; a **version-and-recall system** requires updates within 90 days of any junction rebuild, with community notification and consumables written into the annual budget. A non-exhaustive public search found no city-scale precedent; it is positioned as an original proposal awaiting validation, in the open-source collaborative spirit of this plan.
5. **Be Patient**: Plan for 9 pedestrian safety islands [metric:refuge_island_count] at crosswalks along the main spine corridors (such as [data:geometry/roads.geojson#RD-02] and other main conceptual corridors intersecting with north-south lines), allowing slower pedestrians to wait calmly for the next cycle when the green light ends — a concept count: in deepening, each intersection undergoes a lane-count / saturation / bus-lane screening, kept where approvable and replaced elsewhere by pedestrian-protection phases or extended greens with curb extensions that shorten the crossing, with reductions accepted. Accompany this with **optional extended green light duration** through both mobile and physical card channels. Rest is likewise time tolerance: benches follow the UK DfT Inclusive Mobility method benchmark (station and key-area segments at most 50 meters apart, elsewhere at most 100 meters; engineering compliance remains with GB 55019-2021 and Beijing local standards), each with armrests, a backrest and an adjacent wheelchair space. "Not having to rush even if you walk slowly" should be a right, not a privilege.
6. **Numbers Should Reflect Reality**: The road network layer of map navigation in Chinese megacities is already quite good, but the "last ten meters" are broken — GPS errors in building spaces range from 5 to 15 meters, making it impossible to find entrances, the starting points of tactile paving, or crosswalk locations. Solution: place pole-powered audio anchors and high-recognition codes at intersections and entrances to correct positioning (no battery beacons); more crucially, establish an **open data layer for accessible facilities** — the coordinates and attributes of each segment of tactile paving, each APS (Accessible Pedestrian Signal), each safety island, and each accessible entrance made machine-readable, **opened to map vendors in tiers by navigation-map qualification** (jointly reviewed with the disability federation and surveying authorities; monitoring data published T+1 or weekly, anonymized — no over-promise of "real-time opening to all vendors"), serving as the first demonstration dataset for the "Data Element Circulation" task in the Dazhongsi area. This package's GeoJSON evidence system is the prototype for this data layer.

**Investment Priorities** (Author's Judgment): The in-station human guidance services for the metro are already mature and reliable, making accessibility improvements merely an added bonus; resources should be concentrated on the open street network—those last one kilometer from the front door to the metro entrance.

**From accessibility to universal inclusion**: the six principles naturally spill over to a much wider public — refuge islands and extended greens serve the elderly, children and stroller users; tactile models double as all-age science exhibits; the guiding strip is everyone's directional reference in rain, fog and darkness (the "curb-cut effect"). Four inclusion commitments are added: (1) **non-digital alternative paths** — every AI scenario and city service keeps a no-phone, no-app way of use (physical buttons, staffed windows, telephone channels), with the tactile models themselves the most important non-digital interface; (2) **affordability** — public accessibility services (inquiry, accompaniment, model-library files) are free of charge, and open-source model files may be printed by communities to lower the access threshold; (3) **a safety net for low-income and digitally disadvantaged groups** — station service units keep staffed hours, and every AI refusal or failure closes with a human fallback; (4) **international visitors covered by the same structure** — a foreigner in a Chinese-language environment faces the same structural problem of "the default channel being unreadable", so the solutions are shared: international pictograms as the base layer of signage and plaques, inquiry columns and audio anchors with live translation (the system answers in whatever language the user speaks), high-recognition codes read aloud in 30+ languages by the user's own phone, and a visitor mode for every service requiring no local phone number or local apps. Accessibility, age-friendliness and internationalization are three names for the same design problem in this proposal. Inclusion is not an appendix to accessibility but its natural extension.

**Unified Boundaries**: All facilities in this chapter are Conceptual Recommendations. Specific engineering practices will be based on the Barrier-Free Environment Construction Law (effective September 2023), national standards, and Beijing local standards. During the detailed design phase, an **inclusive co-creation review mechanism** should be established—where disabled users participate in the entire review process ("Nothing about us without us"). The author of this plan is willing to continue participating as a user [source:DATA-SRC-AGENT-TASKBOOK-20260518] [standard:PROJECT-AGENT-OPEN-CALL-TASKBOOK]. **O&M entity and funding (de-watering addition)**: the 9.7 km line crosses several sub-district jurisdictions where breaks concentrate at boundaries — all accessibility facilities should be bundled under a **single belt-level O&M entity** (e.g. the heritage-park administration) with funding following responsibility; every facility gets a "power–ownership–repair–reserve fund" four-element ledger, components without secured funding do not enter construction drawings, and co-creation review becomes a contract clause tied to warranty retention money, with council expert fees and disabled participants' stipends written into the project budget.
