The Accessibility Line · Six first-person principles from the blind author

The author of this proposal is a blind urban user. During design, the AI's "zoned protection" scheme was vetoed by the author: "If blind people can walk the tactile paving continuously, zoning is not necessary — and I do not want to segregate blind people." The framework below is defined by the author and engineered by AI.

1 Continuity above all

A continuous guiding strip of 9.7 km plugged into all twelve stations; AI monitoring of tactile-paving occupation feeds the existing governance platform, per-segment ticket resolution times are published, and disabled users run monthly blind-test sampling (scenario card 13, testing & validation) — scoring repair speed, not momentary state. One break resets it to zero: continuity is 30% construction, 70% management.

2 Inclusion, not segregation

The guiding strip runs through open plazas; everyone shares the same space and there is no "exclusive zone". The only separation is speed: the cycling lane and walking surface split by level and material — separating speeds, not people.

3 Sound is the interface

Audible pedestrian signals planned at all 36 concept crossings (authority rests with traffic management; an approvable per-crossing co-building caliber); anti-muting relies on preset degradation plans — night auto on-demand mode + adaptive loudness, with "switch-off" removed from complaint-closure options and the in-service rate inspected at the same level as tactile-paving continuity; each of the twelve stations gets a distinct sound signature.

4 Structure must be understandable

16 tactile model nodes (twelve station-area maps + the Origin full-line sand table + 3 complex nodes); every junction gains an audio structural description; the Open Source Tactile Model Library publishes symbolic derived models (exaggerated and simplified per tactile-mapping practice) + acceptance test cards — 3D-print them at home, with community reprint points; models carry embossed version numbers, and junction rebuilds trigger a 90-day update-and-recall (an original concept; our search found no precedent).

5 Time must be forgiving

9 pedestrian refuge islands at arterial crossings + requestable extended green. Walking slowly without fear should be a right, not luck.

6 Digital must fit reality

Solving the "last ten metres": pole-powered audio anchors and high-recognition codes at junctions and entrances (battery beacons prohibited); an accessibility open-data layer (every tactile-paving segment, signal and refuge island machine-readable, opened in tiers by map-provider qualification) as the first demonstration set of data-element circulation.

Public-space component library V2 (rebuilt item-by-item under the author's review)

Five design laws: sound for moving, touch for staying (one hand on the phone, one on the cane when rushing — tactile items only where people stop) · dual-channel, dual-language, usable without a phone · restraint in numbers, components clustered at stations · standard interfaces only, no brand customization · survival first, smart later (sticker-on items such as compute benches deleted).

Star item · shopfront function plaque (fully passive) (the author's original proposal; a public search found no precedent — search not exhaustive): every street-front shop carries a uniform plaque on the door-handle side at 1.35 m centre height — large-print icon + raised braille + high-recognition code in three layers, fully passive (a phone scan reads out the shop's name, business type and open state in the user's language); the fixed layer is engraved for life plus a pluggable business-type layer (swapped within seven days of a lease change, bound to licence updates); the public KPI is sampled usability, not plaque-hanging rate; pilot with ≈200 shops through a full lease-change cycle before wider rollout. So blind people and foreign visitors know what every shop is without stepping inside.

Seats follow the Inclusive Mobility method benchmark (engineering compliance per GB 55019-2021 and Beijing local standards): station areas ≤50 m, ordinary segments ≤100 m (design target ≈130 line-wide, pending field verification), with armrests, backrests and wheelchair berths; one pavilion per station, and accessible toilets at the five major stations (South Gateway / Zhizhong / Origin / Zhongzhi / Qinghe Bay); inquiry columns and audio anchors with multilingual real-time translation; robot services get interfaces only.

Investment priority (the author's judgment): in-station manual guidance is already mature and reliable — concentrate resources on the open streets where no one backs you up: the last kilometre from the front door to the metro entrance. All facilities are conceptual recommendations; engineering follows the Barrier-Free Environment Development Law (2023), GB 55019-2021 and national and local standards; the deepening phase is reviewed and accepted with disabled users' participation.

Assumptions

  • A-BOUNDARY-001 Provisional rough boundary: The overall design scope and the three key areas are generated from the repository's provisional rough polygons (provisional_rough); all areas are approximate. Impact: All spatial metrics and drawings use this provisional caliber; full recalculation once official vector redlines arrive (list in the proposal's metrics chapter).
  • A-CONTROLS-001 Missing statutory controls: FAR, building height, density, green-ratio and other controls of the approved regulatory plan are not in the public data package. Impact: All intensity and height conclusions are conceptual only; the corresponding metrics stay unknown in metrics.json and must not be used as approved indicators.
  • A-EXISTING-001 Missing existing baseline: No cleared public data exists for existing building stock, tenure, heritage construction-control zones or municipal utilities. Impact: The retain/renovate/new split and the renewal list are directional suggestions, not parcel-level conclusions.
  • A-BUILDINGS-001 Conceptual building massing: The 573 footprints in buildings.geojson are placeholder prototypes laid out on a single grid, used only for scale-of-magnitude argument, not architectural or block form intent (floor counts are assumptions; the 47 m-deep prototype fails residential daylight depth and will be re-laid out as block-street-wall-courtyard in form deepening). Impact: Total building scale and FAR are order-of-magnitude calibers, not engineering or statutory schemes; the retain/renovate/new ratio is a strategy caliber on placeholder models.
  • A-ROADS-001 Conceptual road alignments: Road corridors in roads.geojson and the land-use layer are concept alignments inferred from public road names, with uniform indicative widths. Impact: The road-area ratio is an internal concept caliber; precise redlines, sections and engineering alignments follow official data.
  • A-CASES-001 Qualitative global case references: The 7 global AI-ecosystem cases are qualitative common-knowledge summaries without unverified figures. Impact: Cases inform mechanism translation only, not investment, output or benchmarking data.
  • A-SCENARIO-001 Conceptual nature of AI scenarios & operations: The 14 scenario cards (incl. 5 testing & validation), 4 landmarks, events and operating mechanisms are conceptual recommendations; privacy boundaries and human review are design premises. Impact: They constitute no approved operations, event schedules or government commitments; test scenarios must be applied for through the competent authorities.
  • A-CULTURE-001 Indicative cultural-resource locations: Positions of Tsinghua Garden Station, Dazhongsi (Juesheng Temple) and other cultural resources are indicative points inferred from public information. Impact: Heritage protection extents follow the heritage authority's publication; related design follows protection before use.
  • A-RAIL-001 Qinghuayuan HSR tunnel & rail vertical constraints: The Jing-Zhang HSR Qinghuayuan tunnel runs beneath the belt along the former rail corridor; the CN-01/CN-06 alignments and protection widths in constraints.geojson are conceptual inferences from public information; no vertical overlay with metro lines 10/12/13/15 or trunk utilities has been made. Impact: Siting of any founded or trenched structure on the spine and all crossing methods are conceptual; deepening must first obtain rail and metro data and complete the vertical overlay, and works near operating lines need dual safety assessments.
  • A-ACCESS-001 Conceptual accessibility facilities & case references: Facilities of the Accessibility Line chapter (guiding strip, APS, refuge islands, tactile models, the open-source model library, occupation AI monitoring) are conceptual recommendations with quantities derived from the concept road network; international cases (Marburg, the Japanese tactile-block system, NaviLens, Wayfindr, the Singapore BCA code, etc.) are qualitative references. Impact: Engineering follows the Barrier-Free Environment Development Law and national and Beijing standards; deepening is reviewed and accepted with disabled users; cases are not quantitative benchmarks.
