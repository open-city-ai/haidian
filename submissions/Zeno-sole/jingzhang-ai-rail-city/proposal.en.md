# Coexistent Track · Jing-Zhang AI Belt — Centennial Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt Open Call for Urban Design

## Design Basis and Source List

This proposal is based primarily on the "Qualification Pre-Review Announcement for the International Scheme of the Centennial Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt Urban Design" ([source:OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT], [source:DATA-SRC-OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT-20260509]), which defines the three layers, three key areas, design tasks, and depth of results as outlined in sections 1.3, 1.4, and 1.5. The task book excerpt for the intelligent body open-source task has added three positioning statements, five functional areas, the Three Zones and Two Wings, six agent tasks, and a unified boundary clause ([source:AGENT-TASKBOOK], [source:DATA-SRC-AGENT-TASKBOOK-20260518]). The machine-readable site package ([source:SITE-PACKAGE]), public data registration form ([source:SOURCE-REGISTRY]), and processing fact package ([source:PROCESSED-FACT-PACK]) together form the navigation layer for generation and self-inspection.

Spatial data aspects, due to the official precise planning boundary and the polygon of the three key areas not yet entering the site package, this plan uses the registered provisional rough alternative boundary (source:[source:BOUNDARY-SOURCE], [source:KEY-AREA-SOURCE], [source:DATA-SRC-PROVISIONAL-BOUNDARIES-20260605]). All derived layers, metrics, and graphics are labeled with `provisional_constraint`, which are only used for generation, display, and self-inspection, and are not to be used as the Official Planning Boundary, approval basis, or precise area conclusion; after the formal polygon is released, the site boundary, key areas, land use, buildings, roads, green/public space, phasing, and all metrics must be recalculated in their entirety (source:[data:geometry/site_boundary.geojson#SITE-001], [metric:site_area_sqm]).

In terms of professional standards, this plan implements the requirements of the Urban Design Management Measures regarding the coordination of building layout, landscape appearance, Public Space, and urban characteristics ([standard:MOHURD-URBAN-DESIGN-MEASURES], [source:DATA-SRC-MOHURD-URBAN-DESIGN-MEASURES]); it distinguishes between confirmed conditions and conditions to be confirmed, following the principles of the detailed planning compilation and approval procedures ([standard:MOHURD-CONTROL-DETAILED-PLANNING], [source:DATA-SRC-MOHURD-CONTROL-DETAILED-PLANNING]); land use classification references the Guide to Land Use and Sea Area Classification for National Spatial Planning ([standard:MNR-LAND-USE-CLASSIFICATION-GUIDE], [source:DATA-SRC-MNR-LAND-USE-CLASSIFICATION-202311]).  Results are to be developed in accordance with the principles and framework of the depth of preparation of architectural design documents (standard: [standard:MOHURD-ARCH-DESIGN-DEPTH-2016]). The announcement and the agent task book serve as the project task context (standard: [standard:PROJECT-OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT], [standard:PROJECT-AGENT-OPEN-CALL-TASKBOOK]). Each chapter of the text will address the four questions: "What is the design judgment, why, which layer/indicator/standard it corresponds to, and what data gaps still exist" (depth: [depth:existing_conditions_diagnosis], depth: [depth:risk_missing_data]).

Materials usage boundary: formal conclusions only use publicly registered or cleared formal-ready sources; provisional rough polygons are only used for provisional generation and visualization; background case materials are used solely for reference and are accurately annotated ([source:SOURCE-REGISTRY]). This plan uses only publicly registered or cleared sources, does not use sources of unknown origin or unauthorized materials, does not involve personal privacy information, and does not claim official approval or implementation commitment ([source:PROCESSED-FACT-PACK], [standard:PROJECT-AGENT-OPEN-CALL-TASKBOOK]).

## Three-Level Scope Framework

A three-tier scope framework implements the "industrial strategy—overall Urban Design—detailed design of key areas" at progressively finer levels ([depth:three_level_scope_framework]). The Coordinated Research Area covers approximately 43.6 square kilometers, bounded by the North Fifth Ring Road to the north, the Jingzhang Expressway to the east, Xisi Street to the south, and Wancuihe Road to the west, for the study of the AI Innovation Ecosystem, industrial chain coordination, future urban form, and regional innovation synergy relationships (the corresponding Overall Design Area is detailed in [source:OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT] and [metric:site_area_sqm]). The overall design area covers approximately 11.4 square kilometers, primarily focusing on the urban and industrial areas within 1-2 kilometers of the Jingzhang Heritage Park, with all spatial layers of this plan derived within this boundary ([data:geometry/site_boundary.geojson#SITE-001]). (Jing-Zhang) The key area detailed design covers approximately 368.4 hectares, extending from north to south as the Zhongzhiyuan AI Independent Innovation Acceleration Area, Beijing AI Origin Community, and Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster ([data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-001], [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-002], [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-003], [metric:key_area_count]). (Key-Area Detailed Design Area)

The responsibilities for the three key areas are as follows: Zhongzhiyuan will carry the full-stack AI self-innovation and governance (approximately 192.1 hectares, [metric:zhongzhiyuan_area_sqm]); AI Origin Community will carry the world-class AI Innovation Ecosystem and talent community (approximately 104.3 hectares, [metric:beijing_ai_origin_community_area_sqm]); and Dazhongsi will carry the new smart native business model and TOD interaction (approximately 72.0 hectares, [metric:dazhongsi_area_sqm]). These three areas are connected through the vitality veins of the relic park, the concept lines of rail transit connections, and horizontal arterial roads, forming an "North R&D—Central Transformation—South Display" innovation value chain ([data:geometry/roads.geojson#ROAD-011], [metric:road_network_length_m]).

Data gaps that need to be clarified: The three-layer scope and key areas polygon used in this plan are all temporary rough substitutes (`boundary_precision=provisional_rough`), and can only express directional zoning relationships but cannot be used for precise area calculations or Official Planning Boundary conclusions ([source:BOUNDARY-SOURCE], [source:KEY-AREA-SOURCE], [assumption:A-BOUNDARY-001], [assumption:A-KEYAREA-002] see assumptions.json). After replacing the official polygon, the area references in this section, Figure 1 and Figure 2, and all derived indicators must be recalculated ([depth:metrics_recalculation], [metric:site_area_sqm]).

## Coordinated Research Area: Industry and Future City Research

The Coordinated Research Area is for the goals of the "World-Class Artificial Intelligence Innovation District, Global Artificial Intelligence Industry High Ground, and Model Zone for Urban Agents" at the sector and regional levels ([source:OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT]). This plan proposes the overall concept of the "Coexistent Track": translating the linear cultural heritage of the Jing-Zhang Railway into an AI innovation corridor—where the tracks become "data tracks," stations become "innovation nodes," trains become "innovation trains," and railway heritage transforms from "memory" into "engine" ([source:AGENT-TASKBOOK], [standard:PROJECT-AGENT-OPEN-CALL-TASKBOOK]).

Naming and Visual Identity (agent.1): Main name "Symbiotic Track · Jing-Zhang AI Belt," with the English name SYMBIOTIC RAIL · JING-ZHANG AI BELT (abbreviated JZ-SR). The naming system adopts the "Track Family": Vitality Veins=main track, Three Zones=three station segments(Full Stack Station, Origin Station, Aggregation Station), Two Wings=two connecting lines, Five Hearts=five station fields, forming an expandable station segment-based naming system([depth:overall_spatial_structure]). Logo Direction: The dual track lines and circuit board wiring are integrated to form an "∞" shape, symbolizing the co-evolution of the century-old railway and AI; the graphic is expressed in three colors: blue(technology), green(ecology), orange(railway memory), with the prohibition of unauthorized use of existing institution logos and fonts([source:AGENT-TASKBOOK], [source:OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT]).

Three Key Orientations and Five Major Functions (agent.1, agent.2): The three key orientations—Centennial Jing-Zhang Cultural Belt, Urban AI Life Experience Belt, and AI Integration Innovation Belt—correspond to five major functions—AI Full-Stack Independent Innovation System, World-Class AI Innovation Ecosystem, AI-Enabled Scenario Empowerment New Paradigm, Intelligent AI Vibrant City, and AI Governance Global Discourse Power ([source:AGENT-TASKBOOK]). (Full-Stack Independent AI Innovation System) Three Zones and Two Wings Synergistic Loop: Zhongzhiyuan (Full Stack Autonomous+Governance) — Origin Community (Ecosystem+Talent) — Dazhongsi (New Business Models+Display) as the core triangle, with Zhongguancun Technology Services Wing providing capital, IP, and global element allocation, and Xiaoyue River Scenario Enablement Wing providing scenarios, lifestyle, and application test fields. The two wings internally empower the three zones, while the three zones externally radiate to the two wings ([source:AGENT-TASKBOOK]). [depth:overall_spatial_structure]).

Global AI Innovation Ecosystem Case Studies (agent.2, open common mechanism references, no data commitments): 1) Silicon Valley university-industry-capital loop, illustrating the value of on-campus technology transfer and venture capital corridor; 2) Singapore's one-north, illustrating industrial-city integration and the organization of the park around rail transit stations; 3) Tel Aviv's innovation-dense street, illustrating small-scale high-density innovative interactions; 4) King's Cross in London, illustrating the revival of railway heritage and its integration with the knowledge economy; 5) Munich's Isar Valley, illustrating a cluster of large enterprises, research institutions, and small and medium-sized enterprises; 6) Yunqi Town in Hangzhou, illustrating how developer communities and annual events shape an industry brand; 7) Shenzhen Bay Science and Technology Ecological Park, illustrating enterprise services and ecological operations at the park level ([source:PROCESSED-FACT-PACK], [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK]). Transformative Mechanisms: Conversion hubs near the school, developer communities, annual biennales, TOD park organizations, enterprise service networks, and open test fields—landing at the original community, Zhongzhiyuan, and Dazhongsi ([data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-003W], [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-009W], [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-001W]).

Future Urban Form (Announcement 1.5.1.2): This proposal introduces the concept of "evolvable cities" — spatial units organized into "stations" that can adjust their functional ratios according to industrial cycles; AI culture, AI society, and AI cities are layered over the physical space as "Scenario Access," making Public Spaces perceivable as "intelligent interfaces" ([source:OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT], [depth:overall_spatial_structure]). In terms of regional collaboration, the proposal suggests establishing a "track-like" collaboration with the Beini District community, Future Science City, Huairou Science City, Economic Development Zone, and the Jingjinji Innovation Network: this corridor focuses on innovation origins, scenario access, and governance demonstrations ([source:AGENT-TASKBOOK], [metric:scenario_node_count]).

## Overall Design Area: Urban Renewal and Regulatory-Plan-Level Urban Design

The Overall Design Area is the core working layer of this Urban Design, covering approximately 11.4 square kilometers (the submitted boundary measurement is about 11.41 square kilometers, [metric:site_area_sqm], [data:geometry/site_boundary.geojson#SITE-001]), aiming to achieve the depth of urban design under the Regulatory Detailed Planning (refer to [standard:MOHURD-CONTROL-DETAILED-PLANNING], [depth:land_use_layout]). The spatial structure is defined as "one vein, three zones, two wings, and five hearts" ([depth:overall_spatial_structure]): one vein, which is the Jing-Zhang Heritage Park Vital Vein, serving as the main framework for culture, ecology, and active travel; three zones, namely Zhongzhiyuan, Origin Community, and Dazhongsi; two wings, namely the Zhongguancun Technology Services Wing and the Xiaoyue River Scenario Enablement Wing. Five hearts include Qinghe Ecological Heart, Zhongzhi Full-stack Heart, Yuidian Co-creation Heart, Xiaoyuehe Scene Heart, and Dazhongsi TOD Heart ([source:AGENT-TASKBOOK], [source:OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT]).

Urban Renewal Overall Framework (Announcement 1.5.2.2, [depth:retain_renovate_demolish]): Organized into four categories—preserve category for residential and educational facilities with current good quality (such as [data:geometry/buildings.geojson#BLDG-005], [data:geometry/buildings.geojson#BLDG-011]), the renovation category involves the facades of the old industrial zones and street interfaces adjacent to the archaeological park. The new construction category includes the missing functional clusters of Zhongzhiyuan, Yuandian Community, and Dazhongsi. The demolition category will be determined only after professional deepening confirmation through the legal process, and this plan does not provide block-level conclusions on demolition and renovation ([depth:retain_renovate_demolish]). [assumption:A-DATA-004 见 assumptions.json]). Update the objective function: Each update should contribute to one of the industrial space, Public Space, or scene nodes, avoiding "updating for the sake of updating" ([source:OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT], [metric:building_count]).

Industrial Goals and Functional Layout (Announcement 1.5.2.1): Organize land use along the innovation value chain—North segment with Zhongzhiyuan primarily for research and development land use, accommodating full-stack R&D ([metric:land_use_0802_area_sqm], [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-005W]), Middle segment with the Origin Community organizing research and educational land use, enhancing on-campus conversion ([data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-003W], [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-003E]), South segment with Dazhongsi primarily for commercial and service land use, accommodating intelligent consumption and display ([metric:land_use_05_area_sqm], [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-001W]). Community services and residential land uses are arranged along the wings, ensuring a balanced work-residence ratio and comprehensive living amenities ([metric:land_use_0701_area_sqm], [metric:land_use_0702_area_sqm], [metric:land_use_0804_area_sqm], [metric:land_use_1401_area_sqm]). The land use zones are generated by a single set of dividing lines, with adjacent elements sharing boundary coordinates, fully covering the submitted boundary and ensuring no gaps or overlaps ([depth:land_use_layout], [self_check:LAND_USE_TOPOLOGY] — see self_check.json).

Development Intensity and Form ([depth:development_intensity_controls], [depth:height_massing_character]): Due to the absence of approved control plan conditions for Floor Area Ratio, Building Height, density, and setback requirements, this scheme only proposes the form principle of "low along the vein, high by the wings, and concentrated at the stations"—controlling low-rise, low-density interfaces on both sides of the site park to protect the landscape and pedestrian experience, The wings' outer sides allow for multi-story mid-rise clusters.TOD Station areas are permitted to accommodate aggregated massing ([assumption:A-CONTROLS-003 见 assumptions.json]). Conceptual Building Footprint is approximately 77.4 hectares ([metric:building_footprint_area_sqm]), and the conceptual Building Coverage Ratio is about 6.8% ([metric:building_density_ratio]), all of which are design suggestions rather than approved indicators ([standard:MOHURD-URBAN-DESIGN-MEASURES], [depth:height_massing_character]).

Morphology Control (Annex 1.5.2.5, [standard:MOHURD-URBAN-DESIGN-MEASURES]): Use "rail track rust orange + Zhongguancun blue + ecological green" as the urban color tone. The architectural facade, paving, signage, and public furniture should uniformly adopt the "Track Family" symbol system. Along both sides of the heritage park, prohibit large-scale continuous glass curtain wall facades; instead, encourage stepped designs, arcades, and rooftop greening (see [depth:height_massing_character], [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK]). This layer of results corresponds to the "Overall Design" section of the A3 booklet and A0 board (refer to [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-GREEN-SPINE], [metric:green_ratio]).

## Detailed Design of Key Areas

Three focus areas shall achieve the Urban Design depth of the Integrated Planning Implementation Plan ([depth:three_key_area_detailed_design], [source:OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT]). Each focus area will form a "positioning + spatial structure + building renewal + traffic and pedestrian facilities + Public Space + AI scenarios + implementation risks" readable small plan. Because the polygon is a temporary rough boundary, the conclusions below are directional in nature ([source:KEY-AREA-SOURCE], [assumption:A-KEYAREA-002 见 assumptions.json]).

Zhongzhiyuan AI Independent Innovation Acceleration Area (approximately 192.1 hectares, [metric:zhongzhiyuan_area_sqm], [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-001]): positioned as a garden-type full-stack independent AI innovation district, focusing on the Full-Stack Independent AI Innovation System and global AI governance discourse power (source: [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK]). The spatial structure is "two belts and one center" — Qinghe ecological interface belt, full-stack R&D belt, and full-stack innovation square center (refer to [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#PUBLIC-007]). Building updates focus on research and industrial service clusters ([data:geometry/buildings.geojson#BLDG-017], [data:geometry/buildings.geojson#BLDG-018]). It is recommended to retain the existing R&D park, progressively renovate the boundary, and add new open laboratories and accelerators ([depth:retain_renovate_demolish]). In terms of transportation, it relies on the horizontal arterial road and the northern portal connection ([data:geometry/roads.geojson#ROAD-009]). Public Spaces are supplemented by park areas within the park and the Qinghe Ecological Display Plaza ([data:geometry/green_space.geojson#GREEN-010]). AI scenarios are implemented with a safety governance sandbox, computing hubs, and a full-stack demonstration center ([data:geometry/constraints.geojson#SCN-10], [data:geometry/constraints.geojson#SCN-11]). Implementation Risks: The density of industrial land use, computational energy consumption, and power municipal conditions are pending confirmation ([depth:risk_missing_data]).

Beijing AI Origin Community (approximately 104.3 hectares, [metric:beijing_ai_origin_community_area_sqm], [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-002]): positioned as a near-school type of technology transfer and talent community, it carries the world-class AI Innovation Ecosystem ([source:AGENT-TASKBOOK]). The spatial structure is "one street and one ring" — the near-school technology transfer street and the pedestrian loop connecting the campus, park, and district (refer to [data:geometry/roads.geojson#ROAD-006] and [data:geometry/roads.geojson#ROAD-007]). Buildings updated with incubators, co-creation complexes, and educational collaboration centers ([data:geometry/buildings.geojson#BLDG-009], [data:geometry/buildings.geojson#BLDG-011]), complementing open-source release, technology transfer, and talent accommodation functions ([metric:land_use_0804_area_sqm]). Public Spaces are anchored by co-creation squares and campus edge green spaces ([data:geometry/public_space.geojson#PUBLIC-003], [data:geometry/green_space.geojson#GREEN-006]). AI scenarios are realized in open-source release halls and technology transfer waystations ([data:geometry/constraints.geojson#SCN-05], [data:geometry/constraints.geojson#SCN-06]). Implementation Risks: Further professional development is needed for the campus boundary and research data authorization, as well as mechanisms for public engagement in community renewal (see [depth:risk_missing_data], [depth:renewal_project_list]).

Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster (approximately 72.0 hectares, [metric:dazhongsi_area_sqm], [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-003]): positioned as a city-type intelligent economy and international exchange district, it accommodates new smart-native business forms (refer to [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK]). The spatial structure is "one axis and four quadrants" — organized around the rail transit station as the axis, with quadrants connected by pedestrian paths (refer to [data:geometry/roads.geojson#ROAD-012] and [data:geometry/roads.geojson#ROAD-004]). Building updates focus on the intelligent economy center, smart consumption district, and data element service tower (refer to [data:geometry/buildings.geojson#BLDG-001] and [data:geometry/buildings.geojson#BLDG-003]). Public Space is anchored by the TOD station forecourt and the demonstration plaza ([data:geometry/public_space.geojson#PUBLIC-001], [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#PUBLIC-002]). AI scenarios will be implemented at smart mobility experience stations, international demonstration lounges, and data element conference lounges ([data:geometry/constraints.geojson#SCN-01], [data:geometry/constraints.geojson#SCN-02]). Implementation risks include the need for deeper integration with the rail and municipal professions for station integration, commercial updates, and traffic organization (see [depth:traffic_rail_slow_parking], [depth:risk_missing_data]).

## Land Use, Building Scale, and Retain-Renovate-Demolish Strategy

Land-Use Layout ([depth:land_use_layout]) fully covers the submission boundary: commercial service land use covers approximately 310.6 hectares ([metric:land_use_05_area_sqm]), research land use covers approximately 378.1 hectares ([metric:land_use_0802_area_sqm]), residential land use covers approximately 87.8 hectares ([metric:land_use_0701_area_sqm]), community service land use covers approximately 100.2 hectares ([metric:land_use_0702_area_sqm]), educational land use covers approximately 71.3 hectares ([metric:land_use_0804_area_sqm]), and green spaces cover approximately 193.2 hectares ([metric:land_use_1401_area_sqm]). In terms of functional proportions, research and development plus industrial services account for about half, reflecting the industrial attribute of the AI innovation belt. Green spaces and open areas account for approximately 20.8%, supporting the "Innovation Belt in the Park" (see [standard:MNR-LAND-USE-CLASSIFICATION-GUIDE], [depth:land_use_layout]).

Building Scale ([depth:height_massing_character], [standard:MOHURD-ARCH-DESIGN-DEPTH-2016]): Conceptual Building Footprint area approximately 77.4 hectares ([metric:building_footprint_area_sqm]). There are 24 concept buildings ([metric:building_count]), with a Building Coverage Ratio of approximately 6.8% ([metric:building_density_ratio]). Heights and volumes are organized according to the principle of "low along the vein, high by the wings, and gathering at the stations and segments," and are all Conceptual Recommendations. The formal Development Intensity requires official controlled planning conditions (development intensity).[assumption:A-CONTROLS-003 见 assumptions.json], [depth:development_intensity_controls]).

Preserve-and-Modify-Demolish-Build Logic ([depth:retain_renovate_demolish]):
Preserve — current residential and educational facilities ([data:geometry/buildings.geojson#BLDG-005], [data:geometry/buildings.geojson#BLDG-011]);
Modify — outdated park interface and industrial buildings ([data:geometry/buildings.geojson#BLDG-001], [data:geometry/buildings.geojson#BLDG-009]);
