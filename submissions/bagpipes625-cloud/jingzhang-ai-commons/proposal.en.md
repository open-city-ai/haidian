## Three-Level Scope Framework

The proposal is organized according to the three levels defined in the announcement: the Coordinated Research Area focuses on the 43.6 square kilometers of AI industry ecosystem, strategic positioning, innovation chain, and future urban form; the Overall Design Area focuses on the 11.4 square kilometers of urban and industrial areas surrounding the Jing-Zhang heritage park within 1-2 kilometers, requiring the formation of an overall framework for Urban Renewal, industrial space layout, traffic and municipal support, and Urban Character control; the Key-Area Detailed Design Area focuses on 368.4 hectares of three detailed design regions, requiring the clarification of functional types, building scale, classification of demolish–renovate–retain strategy, Public Space connectivity, and traffic organization. The three layers of scope are mapped to `compliance_matrix.json` to ensure that the required tasks in sections 1.3, 1.4, and 1.5 of the announcement, as well as agent.1-agent.6, have corresponding chapters, layers, indicators, drawings, and HTML evidence. ## Coordinated Research Area: Industry and Future City Research

The core judgment for the Coordinated Research Area is that Haidian lacks a continuous public interface for the "conception, validation, transformation, experience, and memory" of single-point innovation resources. Therefore, this plan is named **"Jing-Zhang Smart Vein"**, with the English name **JINGZHANG AI COMMONS**. "Smart Vein" refers to both the historical timeline of the Jing-Zhang Railway and the flow of computational power, knowledge, and talent; "Commons" emphasizes public interest, open collaboration, and the memory of contributions. The identification system does not borrow any corporate trademarks: the main logo consists of two parallel tracks that expand into three open loops in the middle, representing trustworthy experiments, open source releases, and city guesting; the base colors are "railway deep blue, open source green, garden green, and caution orange." This direction is available for professional branding teams to deepen, but it does not constitute an approved logo. [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK] [standard:PROJECT-AGENT-OPEN-CALL-TASKBOOK]

"The Three Zones and Two Wings" in this scheme are not five isolated parks but a cycle: Zhongzhiyuan is responsible for full-stack validation and governance methods, the AI Origin community is responsible for university innovation and open-source collaboration, Dazhongsi is responsible for smart native products entering city life; the Zhongguancun Technology Services Wing provides capital, intellectual property rights, and international services, while the Xiaoyue River Scenario Enablement Wing provides public scenarios and resident feedback. These five functions correspond to five visible spaces: the Experiment Garden, the Open Source Square, the Public Spine, the Smart Living Room, and the Governance Forum.
