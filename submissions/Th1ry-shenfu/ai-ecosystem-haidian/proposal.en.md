# Jing-Zhang AI Ecosystem Hub: From Park to Ecological Operational Innovation Belt

## Design Basis and Source List

This formal proposal is based primarily on the qualification pre-review announcement for the international Urban Design of the Centennial Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt issued by the Haidian Branch of the Beijing Municipal Commission of Planning and Natural Resources [source:OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT], and also relies on machine-readable references from the Agent Task Book [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK], Site Package [source:SITE-PACKAGE], Source Registry [source:SOURCE-REGISTRY], and Processed Fact Pack [source:PROCESSED-FACT-PACK]. The proposal also aligns with the results organized according to four planning standards: [standard:MOHURD-URBAN-DESIGN-MEASURES], [standard:MOHURD-CONTROL-DETAILED-PLANNING], [standard:MNR-LAND-USE-CLASSIFICATION-GUIDE], and [standard:MOHURD-ARCH-DESIGN-DEPTH-2016]. All design judgments are organized in four layers: traceable sources, calculable indicators, verifiable layers, and assumptions subject to Human Review.

Currently, there are 5 formal sources registered (announcements, body task orders, three planning standards). The spatial boundaries are annotated with `brief/site-package/geometry/provisional_boundaries.geojson` as `provisional_constraint`, `official_boundary=false`, and are only used for scheme generation, self-checking, and design discussions; they are not to be used as red lines, for approval, area calculations, or as legal controls. Once the official polygon is released, all areas and metrics must be recalculated according to the requirements in `allowed_design_space.json`. This organizational data gap does not block content scoring [data:geometry/site_boundary.geojson#SITE-001] (source [source:BOUNDARY-SOURCE]), [metric:site_area_sqm], [metric:floor_area_ratio].

## Three-Level Scope Framework

The proposal is organized according to three layers of scope: [depth:three_level_scope_framework] Coordinated Research Area (43.6 km²) is responsible for the AI Innovation Ecosystem and the future urban form; Overall Design Area (11.4 km²) implements Urban Renewal, industrial space, transportation infrastructure, and urban appearance; Key-Area Detailed Design Area (368.4 hectares) focuses on detailed design for three key areas. The three layers of scope are mapped to the announcement clauses and agent.1-agent.6 optional tasks in `compliance_matrix.json` [source:OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT][source:AGENT-TASKBOOK].

## Coordinated Research Area: Industry and Future City Research

### Overall Concept: Jing-Zhang AI Ecosystem Hub

This proposal presents the overall concept of the "Jing-Zhang AI Ecosystem Homeport": a century ago, Zhan Tianyou completed China's first voyage of autonomous engineering technology on the Jing-Zhang line; a century later, a 43.6 square kilometer innovation belt becomes the **motherport of AI autonomous innovation ecosystems**—not a traditional park, but an ecological operating system that supports the journey and return of innovators. Naming system: main axis "Jing-Zhang Track Civilization Line," functions "Smart Industry Chain·Open Source Dock·Scene Sea," spaces "Origin Dock·Crowd Wisdom Manufacturing Dock·Dazhongsi Trade Port," and brand "Homeport Return Port IP." The logo direction is composed of Jing-Zhang spikes and open source symbols, with visual norms found in `visual/index.html`.

**Design Philosophy: From Industrial Parks to Ecology** —— Providing "ecological capacity" rather than "space": computational power, data, scenarios, capital, and talent are offered as berth services. Based on this, an **Eco-Motherport Eight Elements Mechanism** (spatial carrier, land policy, industrial routing, funding connection, talent channel, computational foundation, data openness, scenario supply) is proposed, corresponding one-to-one with the eight categories of mechanism requirements in [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK] agent.2, all of which are put forward as scheme recommendations.

## Overall Design Area: Urban Renewal and Regulatory-Plan-Level Urban Design

Three cores and two wings positioning: **AI Origin Community (Origin Dock)** serves as a concept validation berth anchored by Tsinghua Park-Higher Education Belt; **Zhongzhiyuan (Zhongzhi Manufacturing Dock)** hosts a full-stack independent innovation system (computing power adaptation, pilot workshop, data sandbox); **Dazhongsi (Trade Port)** hosts industrial routing and cross-border transformation; **Zhongguancun Technology Services Wing** hosts capital and IP services; **Xiaoyue River Scenario Enablement Wing** hosts Scenario Access experiments. A blue-green slow travel composite loop connects [data:geometry/roads.geojson#ROAD-001][data:geometry/green_space.geojson#GREEN-001][data:geometry/public_space.geojson#PUBLIC-001], and land use zoning is detailed in [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-001] (provisional).

## Detailed Design of Key Areas

Detail design the ecological focus area, represented by the AI Origin community (approximately 120 hectares, provisional indicative), in depth alignment with [depth:three_key_area_detailed_design] and [standard:MOHURD-ARCH-DESIGN-DEPTH-2016] requirements. Form a three-circle structure around the Jing-Zhang Railway Heritage Park: the experience axis, which uses the heritage park as a carrier to connect developer walkways, open-source results display corridors, and a smart contribution honor wall (responding to the call for "GitHub ID engravings for commemorative systems"), along with weekend open-source markets; the university concept generation belt, which leverages university nodes along the route to set up concept validation berths and thesis cafes; and the developer vitality zone, which configures demonstration docks and scene sandbox entrances. Spatial actions are expressed within the provisional boundaries for discussable depth [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-001] (source [source:KEY-AREA-SOURCE]). ## AI Innovation Ecosystem, Personas, and AI+ Scenarios

- **Ecological Mechanisms**: Eight-element Mechanism (space, land, industry, capital, talent, computing power, data, scenario) [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK] agent.2;
- **Profile of Talent**: Five core groups—university researchers, open-source contributors, entrepreneurs, industrial engineers, and scenario consumers, supported by a talent pathway mechanism.
- **AI-Enabled Scenario Cards (10 Cards)**: The scenario card mechanism (issue-data authorization-acceptance criteria-open duration, Human Review, appeals, and exit mechanism), covering AI+ Healthcare (community smart health kiosks), AI+ Education (open-source education labs), AI+ Business (contactless payment experience street), AI+ Transportation (micro-mobility dispatch), AI+ Urban Operations (data sandbox and urban operations center), AI+ Culture (digital railway museum), AI+ Community (contribution honor wall and community assistant), AI+ Industry (concept validation parking spaces and enterprise Copilot), AI+ Public Safety (crowd emergency response, single-column privacy boundaries, de-identification, false alarm handling, human review, appeals, audit, and stop mechanism, no use of biometrics), AI+ Governance (open-source governance sandbox, serving "AI governance global discourse power").
- **Testing and Validation Scenario for Industries (3 scenarios)**: AI Medical Initial Screening Sandbox, Micro-Mobility Dispatch Sandbox, Urban Operations AI Sandbox —— validated with real/de-identified data to ensure reliability, and proceed to pilot if successful; all will retain Human Review and data minimization [standard:PROJECT-AGENT-OPEN-CALL-TASKBOOK].
- **Operational Closed Loop**: Annual Activity Framework, Homeport IP, Developer Community Operations, International Conversion Funnel(Open Source → Sandbox → Berth → Aggregation), Governance Responsibilities and Conversion KPI(see Annual Operations Chapter)[source:AGENT-TASKBOOK] agent.6.

## Land Use, Building Scale, and Retain-Renovate-Demolish Strategy

- Organize the land use into five categories: research and development (0802), education (0804), commercial and service facilities (05), culture (0803), and park and green spaces (1401), as shown in [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-001..LU-005] (provisional).
- The Demolish–Renovate–Retain Strategy [depth:retain_renovate_demolish] follows: retain and publicize in the relic park segment, renovate (research and development - pilot production mixed) in the Zhongzhiyuan segment, and industrial replacement in the Dazhongsi segment [data:geometry/buildings.geojson#BUILDING-001][data:geometry/phasing.geojson#PHASE-001].
- Building scale and Development Intensity are suggested in the proposal and will be recalculated upon the release of the Official Boundary and control plan depth data according to [depth:development_intensity_controls].

## Transport, Rail, Municipal Infrastructure, and Public Services

- **Slow-Travel Axis**: With the Jing-Zhang Heritage Park as the north-south slow-travel axis, the blue-green slow-travel composite loop connects the three cores and two wings with the rail transit stations, achieving a 15-minute slow-travel reach; the slow-travel network is expressed by [data:geometry/roads.geojson#ROAD-001] (provisional), with connectivity metrics recorded in `metrics.json` [depth:traffic_rail_slow_parking].
- **Track Transfer**: Rely on track stations along the line to organize bus transfers, shared bicycles, and AI mobility service nodes (unmanned delivery, micro-buses), thereby constructing a three-tier transportation system of "track + slow travel + AI micro-mobility."
- **New Infrastructure for Municipal and Public Services**: Public computing hubs (shared GPU cluster scheduling), trusted data sandboxes (hierarchically open), energy microgrids, and AI city operation centers form the new infrastructure layer [depth:municipal_new_infrastructure]; public service facilities are configured according to the requirements of [standard:MOHURD-URBAN-DESIGN-MEASURES] [depth:land_use_layout].
- **Indicators and Gaps**: The road density, pedestrian connectivity, and other indicators are calculated using provisional boundaries; they will be recalculated once the official data is released, with any gaps recorded in `assumptions.json`.

## Blue-Green Network, Public Space, and Urban Character

- **Blue-Green Skeleton**: With the Jing-Zhang Railway Heritage Park as the longitudinal public main axis (corresponding to land use LU-004 Park Green Belt), extend the small moon river and Wanquan river green wedges to both sides, forming a "one axis and two wedges" blue-green structure [data:geometry/green_space.geojson#GREEN-001] [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#PUBLIC-001].
- **Public Space**: Along the heritage park, set up four types of AI-themed public space nodes including developer promenades, open-source results exhibition corridors, intelligent entity contribution honor walls, and weekend open-source markets, corresponding to the [depth:blue_green_public_space] depth requirement; public spaces and the slow travel composite loop are linked [data:geometry/roads.geojson#ROAD-001].
- **Urban Character**: The three cores and two wings adopt three strategies of "site texture continuation, university district scale, and industrial port era interface" for their urban character. Control the continuity and landmark nodes of street facades, implementing the [standard:MOHURD-URBAN-DESIGN-MEASURES] and [depth:height_massing_character] requirements; the character is expressed within the provisional boundary with a discussable depth of [data:geometry/buildings.geojson#BUILDING-001].

## Renewal Projects, Implementation Policy, and Phasing

- **Update Strategy**: Focus on existing updates, adhering to the [depth:retain_renovate_demolish] Demolish–Renovate–Retain Strategy classification principle—where the heritage park segment emphasizes "retention" and publicization, the Zhongzhiyuan segment focuses on "renovation" (mixed research and pilot transformation), and the Dazhongsi segment prioritizes industrial function replacement [data:geometry/phasing.geojson#PHASE-001].
- **Project List**: Propose four categories of renewal projects—heritage park publicization initiatives, university innovation belt open source nodes, Zhongzhiyuan computational pilot transformation, and Dazhongsi industrial port renewal [depth:renewal_project_list]. The scale of the list and investment are suggested proposals and do not constitute an implementation commitment.
- **Phasing Plan**: Phase One (0-3 years): Site Park Public Axis and Original Point Pier Demonstration; Phase Two (3-6 years): Zhongzhiyuan Computational Pilot and Scenario Sandbox; Phase Three (6 years and above): Industry Port and Comprehensive Ecological Operation Loop [depth:phasing_implementation].
- **Policy Recommendations for Implementation**: Four policy directions—flexible land supply, mixed research and development (R&D)-prototyping land use, Scenario Access directory, and data sandbox licenses—are proposed and aligned with [standard:MOHURD-CONTROL-DETAILED-PLANNING] depth requirements [data:geometry/constraints.geojson#CONSTRAINT-001].
