# 方案叙事摘要

「京张零公里 Kilometre Zero」把京张铁路的零公里起点与北京 AI 原点社区叠合为 AI 时代的城市新起点。方案沿京张遗址公园组织“零公里主轴”，串联众智园AI自主创新加速区（加速段）、北京AI原点社区（原点站）与大钟寺AI产业集聚区（换乘站），并以中关村科技服务翼、小月河场景赋能翼为支线，形成“公园骨架＋创新院落＋生活街坊”的概念空间结构。方案提供 12 张 AI 场景卡、5 类用户画像、3 个产业测试验证场景与 3 处 AI 朝圣地标，并围绕百年京张文化、中关村文化与 AI 新文化构建导视、品牌与年度活动体系。

全部空间结论为概念建议：几何图层由仓库 provisional 临时边界派生，面积在 EPSG:4548 下复算（总体范围约 1141.3 ha，概念绿地率约 33.1%，公共空间比例约 23.0%），官方红线发布后须整体复算。本地自检达到 formal-review-ready，PR 见 open-city-ai/haidian#1448。

# Narrative Summary

"Jing-Zhang Kilometre Zero" superimposes the zero-kilometre origin of the Jing-Zhang Railway onto the Beijing AI Origin Community as a new urban starting point of the AI era. Along the heritage park, the proposal organises a Kilometre Zero main axis connecting Zhongzhiyuan (Acceleration Segment), the Beijing AI Origin Community (Origin Station) and Dazhongsi (Interchange Station), with the Zhongguancun and Xiaoyuehe wings as branch lines, forming a conceptual spatial structure of "park skeleton + innovation courtyards + living blocks". It provides 12 AI scenario cards, 5 user personas, 3 industry test-and-validation scenarios and 3 AI pilgrimage landmarks, plus a cultural narrative, wayfinding and annual event system around centennial Jing-Zhang, Zhongguancun and AI culture.

All spatial statements are conceptual: geometry derives from the repository's provisional boundary and areas are recalculated in EPSG:4548 (site about 1,141.3 ha; conceptual green ratio about 33.1%; public-space ratio about 23.0%); full recalculation is required after the official redline is published.
