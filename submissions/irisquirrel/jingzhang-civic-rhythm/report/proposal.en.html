Three-Level Working Scope

The 43.6 km² coordination scope studies regional exchanges in research, education, industry, and public services. The approximately 11.4 km² overall-design scope organises the public spine, three nuclei, two supporting wings, mobility/blue-green network, and six renewal projects. Zhizhi Garden, AI Origin Community, and Dazhongsi are developed as distinct spatial and governance prototypes. Shared project IDs, scenario IDs, metrics, assumptions, and source tags connect all three scales [scope:coordination] [scope:overall] [scope:key-areas].

The central proposition is: move AI innovation from closed campuses into everyday urban life where people can choose, question, correct, appeal, and exit, while every experiment must produce a measurable improvement to real public space.

Coordination-Scope Industry and Future-City Research

The Future Science City interface contributes life-science and energy needs; Huairou Science City contributes large scientific facilities and science communication; the Economic-Technological Development Area contributes robotics, autonomous systems, and manufacturing scale-up; universities across Beijing-Tianjin-Hebei contribute talent and joint courses. Jingzhang is the publicly legible, risk-bounded testing environment. Every exchange records resource, entry condition, responsible role, data boundary, and destination of results [agent.1].

Five steps govern exchange: publish a need, conduct joint R&D, test at Jingzhang, scale outside the corridor when appropriate, and publish a review. Five time protocols govern commuter priority, lunchtime openness, night noise/light, quarterly red-team testing, and annual-event capacity and reuse. The model is an operating protocol, not a calendar of events [agent.2].

Overall Urban Design and Renewal Structure

One public spine combines walking, universal access, blue-green comfort, industrial-heritage narrative, and human services. Three nuclei separate risk: Z1 is a validation nucleus, Z2 a co-creation nucleus, and Z3 an exchange nucleus. Two wings provide professional services and real public-service needs. Six gates connect safety, ecology, translation, mobility, service, and events to reversible delivery.

Renewal follows a hierarchy: retain usable structures; repair public interfaces; insert adaptable ground floors and removable components; expand only after statutory, engineering, public-interest, and operational tests. No landmark architecture is used to conceal unresolved ownership, capacity, or safety conditions Depth.

Detailed Design of Three Key Areas

Z1 Zhizhi Garden - auditable test garden. An ecological buffer, public observation gallery, disconnectable test yards, standard workshops, and a low-carbon edge-compute corridor separate daily public movement from controlled testing while keeping its purpose understandable. Every test discloses purpose, duration, role, data type, and stop condition. It hosts the urban AI safety and rights sandbox.

Z2 AI Origin Community - open translation street. A campus-community seam links an open release hall, shared workspaces, mentor/legal desk, small-scale fabrication, adaptable ground floor, and public-feedback court. Projects move through public problem, prototype, community test, rights review, and translation choice. It hosts low-risk public-service small-model trials.

Z3 Dazhongsi - urban exchange station. At-grade continuity, quadrant numbering, and movable platforms come before any unverified underground connection. International exchange, a data-rights room, cultural interpretation, and night services are added only after capacity and safety checks. It hosts pedestrian digital-twin services and public explanation.

The internal layouts in the figures are topological prototypes, not surveyed building footprints. Each requires measured conditions, ownership, ground-floor use, access, fire, rail, water, and real-user accessibility audits before development Depth.

AI Innovation Ecosystem, Talent Roles, and AI+ Scenarios

Talent is a network of problem owners, developers, model/data stewards, transport and ecological engineers, social workers, accessibility testers, legal and copyright reviewers, red teams, resident observers, operators, and procurement/translation roles. Each role has declared skills, access, conflicts, and accountability. Residents and frontline staff are not free test material; participation requires notice, reasonable compensation, and withdrawal.

The eight-factor audit covers land, space, industry, finance, talent, compute, data, and scenario. Missing a critical condition keeps a proposal in simulation or offline testing. Compute is quota-controlled, energy-metered, network-isolated, and physically disconnectable. Data is minimised, purpose-limited, logged, and deleted on schedule.

Three flagship chains are: T1 pedestrian digital twin and accessibility repair; T2 public-service small model with human takeover; and T3 urban AI safety and rights sandbox. High-risk eligibility, health, safety, and rights decisions remain human. Success is measured by error, takeover, repair, public value, and closure, not automation rate.

Land Use, Building Scale, and Retain/Retrofit/Remove Logic

Land use currently expresses relationships among the public spine, ecological buffer, innovation mix, public service, and transport exchange. Floor area, FAR, height, and demolition quantities remain unknown. Decisions examine structural/fire safety, cultural and community value, adaptability, whole-life carbon, public access, and feasibility. Repairable structures are retained first; temporary components are demountable; new massing requires statutory, daylight, view, and fire checks Depth.

Mobility, Rail, Utilities, and Public Services

Walking and universal access are the first transport layer. Rail protection and underground connections remain preconditions, not promises. Utility checks cover electricity/compute load, fire, drainage/flood, communications, and temporary-event capacity. Human service desks, no-phone routes, continuous numbering, rest points, paper maps, telephone access, and multimodal information keep essential services functional when models fail Depth.

Blue-Green System, Public Realm, and Character

The blue-green system combines the Qinghe interface, shade, removable stormwater units, walking continuity, and seasonal use. Water and ecological claims require professional local data. Permission bands, observation galleries, human desks, and removable platforms make daily space and controlled tests distinguishable. Character comes from rail heritage continuity, maintainable low-carbon components, and clear numbering rather than a generic technological skin Depth.

Renewal Projects, Delivery Policy, and Phasing

Four phases are used: 0 baseline and evidence; 1 reversible 90-day pilot; 2 conditional expansion; 3 long-term operation. Six projects address walking discontinuity, Qinghe comfort/ecology, near-campus translation, Dazhongsi quadrants, inclusive service nodes, and an annual event route. Each passes necessity, data, permission, pilot, public impact, and expansion gates. Failure leads to more evidence, smaller scope, simulation, or exit - never automatic construction [agent.5] [agent.6].

Policy tools include short-term site permission, milestone micro-procurement, model/data cards, community-impact register, non-digital alternatives, independent red teams, resident observers, and staged loss limits. Funding follows evidence and milestones rather than being precommitted to large construction.

The first deliverable is deliberately smaller than the innovation belt: a 100-200 m public-takeover line and one no-phone human service point in Z2, with the precise location subject to site verification. Days 0-15 close the need, non-AI baseline, minimum data, roles, and accessibility co-test plan. Days 16-30 verify clear width, fire, ownership, power, and neighbourhood effects. Days 31-45 rehearse faults, physical disconnection, human takeover, deletion, and place recovery. Only days 46-75 may host a limited, staffed public trial. Days 76-90 force a continue, repair, or remove decision; renewal is never automatic. Failure at any gate returns the project to tabletop rehearsal, relocation, reduction, or cancellation Spatial data.

The conceptual RACI names functions without fabricating appointments. Public-realm design, data stewardship, site management, and independent evaluation perform the work; site, data, operation, and authorisation accountability remains unassigned; accessibility co-testers, fire/engineering, legal/privacy, frontline staff, and residents are consulted; affected publics are informed. Each of JZ-01-JZ-06 registers a cost band, dependency, acceptance test, and exit action. Cost bands guide procurement form only and are not quotations Spatial data.

Twelve Operating Contracts

The scenarios are not a technology menu. Twelve contracts cover accessibility obstruction repair, child-readable test explanation, red-team safety rehearsal, cross-vendor verification, no-phone public service, resident problem-setting, accountable procurement drafting, equivalent accessible publication, conservative walking guidance, bilingual public explanation, data-rights requests, and night-service labour/neighbour impacts.

Every contract uses the same nine-field control: place; user; problem; non-AI baseline; minimum data; bounded AI role; human release; metric; stop and recovery. Child-facing SC-02 collects no image or voice; low-digital-literacy SC-05 requires no account; SC-08 does not infer disability type; SC-12 records sound level bands but never residential audio content. A missing field keeps the scenario at tabletop stage Spatial data.

Z1 concentrates controlled verification: public understanding, red-team rehearsal, and cross-vendor reproducibility. Z2 concentrates co-creation and translation: no-phone service, resident agenda setting, procurement responsibility, and accessible publication. Z3 concentrates everyday adoption and accountability: conservative walking, international explanation, data rights, and night operation. Shared rights are notice, refusal, human takeover, correction, appeal, and exit, but risk, logging, permission, and recovery differ by contract.

Inclusive Journeys and Public Rights

Six journeys cover older and low-digital-literacy users, children and guardians, mobility-disabled users, sight/hearing-disabled users, low-income frontline workers, and international visitors. Every service supports notice, refusal, human takeover, correction, appeal, and exit. Information uses text, sound, touch, numbering, and human support rather than colour, QR code, or voice alone. Severe misleading accessibility advice is a safety incident.

Metrics, Recalculation, and Compliance Matrix

Metrics have four layers: spatial baseline; trial process; public outcomes; long-term translation. Every metric records formula, unit, spatial/time scope, source, confidence, responsible role, and action threshold. Replacement of an official boundary triggers GIS recalculation of area, zoning, public/green ratios, walking length, service coverage, project footprints, cost assumptions, and consistency across text, figures, structured JSON, and manifest Depth.

The compliance matrix covers planning, ownership, rail, transport, fire, water, ecology, heritage, accessibility, event permission, data, AI, procurement, and copyright. An unknown is an explicit precondition to close, not a reason to invent a value.

Risk, Copyright, and Professional Boundaries

Spatial risk is controlled by provisional labels and complete recalculation. AI risk is controlled by tiering, minimum data, consent, limited logging, human final decisions, appeal, and physical disconnection. Community risk is controlled by baseline study, resident observation, complaint times, rent/noise/crowding monitoring, and exit. Delivery risk is controlled by six gates and staged stop-loss.
