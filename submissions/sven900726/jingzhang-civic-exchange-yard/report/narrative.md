# 京张交换场叙事摘要

京张交换场把铁路编组场的“识别—编组—转轨—到达”转译为 AI 城区的公共协议。三个交换场分别承担全栈验证、研究转译和城市应用；四类支线连接高校、企业、社区与国际协作；十二个触点用公共交换凭证记录主动触发、当下需求、公共回报、责任人、最少数据、保存期限、非数字兜底和停止条件。“轻体验城市”让 AI 默认安静，在人需要吃饭、购物、乘车等服务时主动唤起，一次一景、用完即忘，并保障体验、沉默、匿名、拒绝、解释和删除六项权利。

The Civic Exchange Yard turns railway marshalling—identify, assemble, switch, arrive—into a public protocol for an AI district. Three yards validate stacks, translate research, and test urban applications. Four spur types connect universities, enterprises, neighbourhoods, and global collaborators. Twelve touchpoints use a Public Exchange Ticket to register voluntary triggers, present needs, public returns, ownership, minimum data, retention, non-digital fallback, and stop conditions. The Light-Experience City keeps AI quiet by default and lets a person invoke it for dining, shopping, transit, or another present need: one scene at a time, forgotten after use, with rights to opt in, remain silent, stay anonymous, refuse, understand, and erase.
