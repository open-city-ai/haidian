SC05 · embodied_test

Shared Right-of-Way Sandbox

Test yielding, blind spots, near misses, emergency stop, and restoration at enclosed low speed.

SC06 · embodied_test

Curb Handoff Bay

Keep delivery handoff out of tactile paving, fire access, and the sole public movement line.

SC07 · embodied_test

Failure and Takeover Drill

Inject disconnection, localisation drift, and low battery to prove who takes over and how recovery occurs.

SC08 · embodied_test

Non-Identifying Public-Realm Check

Generate facility-defect candidates at the edge without face, behaviour, or worker-performance inference.

SC09 · health_aging

No-Phone Service Finder

Use a paper directory, static map, and human window as baseline; AI explains only verified services.

SC10 · health_aging

Rest-to-Service Route

Include crossings, seats, shade, toilets, works, and access control in an explainable route.

SC11 · health_aging

Caregiver Human Handoff

Send complex care questions to a responsible professional without collecting or inferring diagnosis.

SC12 · health_aging

Service Directory Audit

People recheck location, hours, eligibility, and telephone; an outdated entry is removed.
