# OPEN WINDOW JING-ZHANG

**Formal result version: OPEN WINDOW v1.1; submitting account: JimCarrey-Fan.**


## Design Basis and Source List

Open Window Jing-Zhang uses the railway carriage window as a civic interface for AI. Every public AI service must disclose five fields: purpose, data, limits, human takeover, and public return. The evidence base is the official announcement, agent taskbook, site package, source registry, and processed fact-navigation package [source:OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT] [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK] [source:SITE-PACKAGE]. The submitted overall boundary and three key-area polygons are provisional rough constraints, not official redlines. The recalculated 11,412,825.386 square metres is reproducible but cannot support parcel-level or approval-level conclusions [data:geometry/site_boundary.geojson#SITE-001] [metric:site_area_sqm]. Land classification follows registered codes; FAR, height, ownership, road redlines, utilities, and engineering capacity remain unknown until official controls are supplied [standard:MOHURD-CONTROL-DETAILED-PLANNING] [standard:MNR-LAND-USE-CLASSIFICATION-GUIDE].

## Three-Level Scope Framework

The coordinated research area asks how an innovation ecosystem forms; the overall design area asks how that ecosystem becomes urban structure; the three key areas ask how one AI service becomes visible and governable. The spatial concept is one corridor, three windows, two wings, and twelve nodes. Zhongzhiyuan is the Validation Window, AI Origin Community is the Learning Window, and Dazhongsi is the Civic Window. Technology service and scenario enablement form the two wings [depth:three_level_scope_framework] [depth:overall_spatial_structure]. The framework survives future boundary replacement, while areas, ratios, and coordinates must be recalculated. This separates stable design intent from provisional geometry and prevents rough data from masquerading as statutory control [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-001] [metric:key_area_count].

## Coordinated Research Area: Industry and Future City Research

The scarce urban resource is not another display screen but an interface connecting research, entrepreneurship, assurance, adoption, and public trust. The proposed cycle moves from university and open-source questions to enterprise needs, assurance tests, community learning, civic use, and feedback to research. Helsinki and Amsterdam inform public registers; Singapore AI Verify informs assurance evidence; one-north informs work-live-play-learn mixing; Paris-Saclay informs multi-party evaluation; Brainport informs a permanent portfolio of living labs [source:CASE-HELSINKI-AI-REGISTER] [source:CASE-SINGAPORE-AI-VERIFY] [source:CASE-BRAINPORT-UDI]. Only mechanisms are translated. No foreign legal authority, metric, or governance mandate is transferred. A seasonal operating cycle—open questions, assurance trials, city demonstrations, and winter audit—builds reputation through verifiable records rather than spectacle.

## Overall Design Area: Urban Renewal and Regulatory-Plan-Level Urban Design

Four land-use design roles cover the provisional boundary: validation and R&D mixed belt, public-window greenway, urban application and industry service belt, and learning and community service belt [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-001] [depth:land_use_layout]. The greenway is a public spine, the three windows are anchors, and the wings link universities, firms, communities, and stations. Renewal follows retain usable structures, retrofit ground-floor interfaces, demolish only with sufficient safety and public-connectivity evidence, and add buildings only to fill public or innovation-service gaps. The submitted building footprint is a conceptual carrier test, not an existing-condition survey or approved development quantum [metric:building_footprint_area_sqm]. Spatial, AI, and operational gates must all pass before any node moves from design recommendation to trial.

## Detailed Design of Key Areas

The Validation Window at Zhongzhiyuan contains a synthetic-data clinic, low-speed robot co-road window, and enterprise-agent assurance sandbox. The Learning Window at AI Origin Community contains an open-source release window, model-card reading room, accessible navigation, and community learning table. The Civic Window at Dazhongsi contains an algorithm-register kiosk, multilingual cultural guide, environmental advisory, and maintenance assistant [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-001] [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-002] [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-003]. Three reversible honor landmarks—Responsibility Signal Window, Open-source Release Window, and City Echo Window—make testing status, contributors, complaints, corrections, and public returns visible. All three boundaries remain provisional rough constraints [metric:key_area_count] [depth:three_key_area_detailed_design].

## AI Innovation Ecosystem, Personas, and AI+ Scenarios

Six personas shape the service contract: researchers, startup teams, students and developers, residents including older and disabled visitors, frontline workers, and international visitors [metric:persona_count]. Twelve scenario cards use the same five-window protocol: purpose and beneficiary; minimum data and retention; confidence, limits, and prohibited uses; named human owner, appeal, and non-AI route; public return, evidence, and exit condition. Three scenarios are explicit industry validations, while the remainder address open source, AI literacy, accessible walking, health-service navigation, public algorithm inquiry, cultural guidance, environmental advice, and maintenance support [data:geometry/constraints.geojson#SCENARIO-01] [metric:scenario_node_count] [metric:validation_scenario_count]. Health navigation never diagnoses, mobility advice never replaces safety infrastructure, and maintenance advice never automatically penalizes or identifies a person.

## Land Use, Building Scale, and Retain-Renovate-Demolish Strategy

The land-use layer retains registered classification codes and is checked as a complete, non-overlapping partition of the provisional site. Its design roles do not amend statutory land use [standard:MNR-LAND-USE-CLASSIFICATION-GUIDE] [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-002]. Instead of iconic-object competition, the building strategy prioritizes accessible ground-floor windows for assurance, learning, public service, and feedback. Total floor area, FAR, and height remain unknown because official parcel, control, ownership, and constraint data are absent [metric:floor_area_ratio] [metric:total_floor_area_sqm] [metric:building_height_m]. Retain-renovate-demolish classification requires evidence for structural safety, heritage value, use efficiency, carbon impact, public connectivity, and ownership. Current recommendations are therefore retain first, retrofit interfaces, build to fill gaps, and defer demolition decisions.

## Transport, Rail, Municipal Infrastructure, and Public Services

Walking, cycling, and rail interchange organize mobility. The Jing-Zhang spine connects wayfinding, crossings, accessible transfers, cycle parking, and rest points; low-speed robot trials are separated in space or time from everyday movement. The submitted centerline is conceptual and does not claim road width or redline, so road-area ratio remains unknown [data:geometry/roads.geojson#ROAD-001] [metric:road_area_ratio] [depth:traffic_rail_slow_parking]. New infrastructure follows small node, measurable use, and disconnectable operation. Local processing, short retention, shared maintainable cabinets, offline shutdown, and staffed alternatives are defaults. Power, drainage, flood control, fire safety, communications security, and underground works require professional confirmation. Clear signs, seating, accessible toilets, and staffed help precede AI terminals.

## Blue-Green Network, Public Space, and Urban Character

The Jing-Zhang heritage park forms a continuous public spine linking the Qinghe edge, campus boundaries, station squares, and neighborhood pockets. The recalculated green ratio is 0.123423 and the public-space ratio is 0.073281; both derive from conceptual geometry within a provisional boundary and are comparison values, not statutory targets [data:geometry/green_space.geojson#GREEN-001] [metric:green_ratio] [metric:public_space_ratio]. Experiments remain observable without obstructing everyday movement: test zones are separated, five-window information appears at entrances, temporary boundaries are reversible, and normal use returns after events. Urban character uses railway-scale frames, deep teal, a moving copper point, and low-luminance information rather than a generic futuristic skin. Microclimate, drainage, habitat, and lighting await specialist data [depth:blue_green_public_space].

## Renewal Projects, Implementation Policy, and Phasing

Nine conceptual projects advance through three phases [metric:renewal_project_count]. Phase one opens temporary civic windows, a five-window template, a walking-break audit, and an algorithm-register prototype. Phase two links the assurance loop, open-source release window, and city echo window. Phase three creates an annual route, open scenario archive, and cross-district review [data:geometry/phasing.geojson#PHASE-001] [depth:phasing_implementation]. Policy recommendations require spatial, AI, and operational gates; procurement clauses for model cards, data statements, human takeover, and exit; time-limited trial permits; publication of failures and stops; and review participation by communities, disabled users, and frontline staff. Conflict with official controls, inability to provide a non-AI route, unresolved safety or privacy risk, unclosed complaints, or weak public return stops progression.

## Risk, Copyright, and Compliance

Principal risks are spatial dispute from provisional boundaries; implementation uncertainty from missing controls and ownership; privacy, safety, bias, and automation dependence in AI services; high maintenance cost; event disturbance; and copyright or factual error in generated content [depth:risk_missing_data]. Mitigations are visible data grades, professional review, minimum data, human takeover, non-AI routes, expiring permissions, public complaints, and reversible installations. Every spatial claim is a conceptual recommendation, not approval, construction, procurement, medical, traffic-management, or administrative authorization. Text, graphics, and code were reviewed by the submitter; no copyrighted third-party photographs are included. A change in author identity, official geometry, or permissions requires manifest, sources, assumptions, bilingual displays, and validation to be regenerated.
