## 1. English Executive Summary

**Centennial Jing-Zhang "Rail-First" AI Innovation Belt (A0 Pragmatic Baseline)**

Located in Haidian District, Beijing, along the heritage corridor of the 1909 Jing-Zhang Railway (China's first self-built railway), this proposal redevelops a 1,141-ha corridor into an AI innovation belt organized around a single spatial logic: *rail first, city follows* (先轨后城). Rather than imposing a generic grid, the scheme lets the restored heritage greenway—wrapping the historic rail line—become the primary urban armature, from which light, reversible building forms grow north-to-south over three phases: Phase 1 "rails reactivated" (restore the green corridor and connect slow-traffic gaps), Phase 2 "city follows" (add industry-service and results-conversion functions along the corridor), Phase 3 "ecology matures" (complete community services and low-carbon operations without sustained public subsidy).

Three key areas anchor the design: the Zhongzhiyuan AI self-innovation accelerator (192.1 ha), the Beijing AI Origin Community (104.3 ha, near-campus results conversion), and the Dazhongsi AI industry cluster (72.0 ha, around the transit hub). The plan carries a full AI-scenario matrix (data input → model/agent flow → human review → operator → failure degradation → KPI → exit), seven vulnerable-group personas with baseline-based measurable targets and continuous accessible routes, and a regional-synergy mechanism covering Future Science City, Huairou Science City, the Economic-Development Zone, and the Beijing-Tianjin-Hebei region. Financial modeling relies on public-space operation, industrial rents, sponsorship, and scenario-test fees—not on permanent government subsidies.

**Evidence & confidence.** Every figure is sourced or recomputed within the package (`metrics.json`, geometry GeoJSON). All boundary-derived indicators are marked `provisional / pending_recalculation`; they will be recomputed through the same generation chain once official polygons are published. This submission is a concept proposal, not a government decision, and is not for approval or engineering use.
