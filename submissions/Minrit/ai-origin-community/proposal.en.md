# Jing-Zhang AI Origin Community — Urban Design upon the Centennial Heritage

> **Design motto**: Rivets as the vein, origin as the core; a century of twin tracks, a rebirth of intelligence.
> The Jing-Zhang Railway is the origin of China’s independent innovation spirit; AI is the new origin of contemporary innovation. They share the same "0/1" structure — rails are twin lines, code is binary; the rivet wedges into the sleeper, the origin wedges into the future.

## 0 Abstract and Design Stance

This formal submission targets the **ai-origin-community** track, focusing on the Beijing AI Origin Community within the Centennial Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt (Haidian, Beijing). Three propositions:

1. **Origin growth**: let AI innovation grow from the everyday stitching of "campus–park–community" rather than from a brand-new town;
2. **Heritage stitching**: use the Jing-Zhang heritage park as the axis to stitch Tsinghua, Peking University, Wudaokou and Dazhongsi into an innovation corridor instead of a divider;
3. **Open-source co-creation**: build an "origin bell, 0/1 bridge, release dome" landmark trio that closes the full AI talent loop: release–test–transform–settle.

**Master structure**: one axis (AI Origin Main Axis along the heritage park), one ring (zero-carbon slow-mobility ring: cycling + walking + autonomous shuttle), three fabrics (North Fabric: Zhongzhiyuan AI acceleration area; Middle Fabric: Beijing AI Origin Community — detailed focus; South Fabric: Dazhongsi AI industry cluster).

## 2 Design Concept: Naming and VI Direction

- **Primary name (EN)**: Jing-Zhang AI Origin Community (abbr. JZ-AI Origin).
- **Naming hierarchy**: Belt (Centennial Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt) → Fabrics (North/Middle/South) → Clusters (west talent-apartment quarter, middle university–enterprise lab belt, east open-source plaza & release hall) → Places (Origin Bell, 0/1 Bridge, Release Dome).
- **Logo direction** (concept, not final): a rivet and "0/1" homologue — one dot plus two parallel lines; the dot can evolve into a data-pulse ring. Palette intent: railway grey #2E4057 base, innovation crimson #B03A2E accent, ecology green #7FB069; full VI manual is a follow-up task.
