# JingZhang AI Nexus: Centennial Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt Urban Design Proposal

## Design Basis and Source Inventory

This proposal is based on the "Centennial Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt Urban Design International Solicitation Prequalification Announcement" issued by the Haidian Branch of the Beijing Municipal Commission of Planning and Natural Resources as the primary basis, with the temporary rough boundaries, key areas, enumerations, metrics, and source inventories registered by maintainers in `brief/site-package/` as machine-readable basis [source:OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT] [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK] [source:SITE-PACKAGE]. All design judgments are decomposed into traceable sources, recomputable metrics, verifiable layers, and human-reviewable assumptions. Complete source and standard coverage is maintained in `sources.json`, `standard_matrix.json`, and `design_depth_matrix.json` [source:SOURCE-REGISTRY].

This proposal uses `brief/site-package/geometry/provisional_boundaries.geojson` to generate a temporary formal package. `geometry/site_boundary.geojson` and `geometry/key_areas.geojson` are marked as `provisional_constraint` with `official_boundary=false`, intended only for proposal generation, self-check, visualization, and design discussion [data:geometry/site_boundary.geojson#SITE-001].

## Part 1: Overall Belt Concept and Functional Coordination Design (agent.1)

### 1.1 Brand Naming System

**Primary Name:** JingZhang AI Nexus (京张智脉)

**Naming Hierarchy:**

| Level | Name (ZH) | Name (EN) | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Belt | 京张智脉 | JingZhang AI Nexus | Historical vein of Jing-Zhang Railway meets AI intelligence pulse |
| Core Area 1 | 智源·加速谷 | NexusOrigin Accelerator | Zhongzhiyuan AI Innovation Acceleration Zone |
| Core Area 2 | 智点·原生社区 | NexusPoint Origin Community | Beijing AI Origin Community |
| Core Area 3 | 智集·产业坊 | NexusHub Industry District | Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster |
| Service Wing 1 | 智翼·科创廊 | NexusWing Tech Corridor | Zhongguancun Tech Service Wing |
| Service Wing 2 | 智景·活力带 | NexusScene Vitality Belt | Xiaoyuehe Scenario Empowerment Wing |

### 1.2 Visual Identity and Logo Direction

The Logo concept centers on the "Nexus Vein" imagery: a flowing curve representing the historical trajectory of the Jing-Zhang Railway, from which a digital node network grows, symbolizing AI innovation emerging from historical roots. Primary colors: "Nexus Blue" (#1E3A5F), "Innovation Gold" (#D4A843), and "Ecology Green" (#2E7D32) for the blue-green space system.

### 1.3 Three Positionings and Five Functions

**Three Positionings:** Centennial Jing-Zhang Cultural Belt · Urban AI Life Experience Belt · AI Fusion Innovation Belt

**Five Functions:** AI Full-Stack Independent Innovation System · World-Class AI Innovation Ecosystem · AI+ Scenario Empowerment New Paradigm · Intelligent AI Vitality City · AI Governance Global Discourse Power

### 1.4 Overall Spatial Structure

The spatial organization is "One Belt, Three Cores, Multi-Scenario, Blue-Green Slow-Mobility Composite Ring" [data:geometry/site_boundary.geojson#SITE-001]:

- **One Belt:** Jing-Zhang Railway Heritage Park Vitality Belt, north-south main axis connecting three key areas
- **Three Cores:** NexusOrigin Accelerator (north), NexusPoint Origin Community (middle), NexusHub Industry District (south)
- **Multi-Scenario:** AI+ public service, industrial service, and urban life operation nodes along the belt and wings
- **Blue-Green Composite Ring:** Continuous blue-green space and slow-mobility system connecting Qing River, Heritage Park, and Xiaoyue River

### 2.2 Innovation Ecosystem Map

Haidian's AI innovation ecosystem is built around five loops: "Origin — Collaboration — Conversion — Experience — Dissemination":

1. **Origin Loop:** Tsinghua, Peking University, CAS provide fundamental research and talent
2. **Collaboration Network:** Open-source communities, joint labs, conferences and developer events
3. **Conversion Hub:** Zhongzhiyuan provides acceleration from prototype to product
4. **Experience Interface:** Beijing AI Origin Community and Dazhongsi provide demonstration and feedback
5. **Dissemination Node:** Global AI Innovation Week, international roadshows, digital communication

### 2.3 Zhongzhiyuan Full-Stack System

NexusOrigin Accelerator should build the following independent innovation facilities [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-001]:

- **Independent Model Testing Center:** Domestic chip computing, model evaluation, red team testing
- **Standard-Setting Workshop:** AI safety, ethics and governance standards discussion
- **Safety Governance Sandbox:** Controlled environment for AI application safety boundary testing
- **Low-Carbon Computing Showcase:** Green computing and distributed energy in AI infrastructure

## Part 4: AI Public Space, Smart-Native New Business and Pilgrimage Landmarks (agent.4)

### 4.1 Jing-Zhang Heritage Park AI Public Space

- **East-West Stitching:** Set slow-mobility nodes at Jingzang Expressway and Xueyuan Road crossings
- **North-South Connection:** Extend from Qinghuayuan Station through university district to Dazhongsi
- **Function Integration:** AI display installations, smart lighting, environmental monitoring along the route

### 3.2 AI Pilgrimage Landmarks (3)

1. **Nexus Origin (智脉之源):** At NexusOrigin core, public art installation combining railway track elements with digital network, symbolizing AI growing from historical roots
2. **Origin Plaza (原点广场):** At NexusPoint center, "0"-shaped ring road enclosing open plaza with global AI milestones display
3. **Nexus Gate (智集之门):** At NexusHub entrance, parametric arch connecting historical and modern architecture with LED matrix showing global AI dynamics

### 4.3 Honor Display System

- **Agent Contribution Honor Wall:** Digital screen at Origin Plaza showing open-source contribution records
- **AI Milestone Nodes:** Along Heritage Park recording key AI development events
- **Open Source Achievement Display Nodes:** In three key areas showing conversion from open-source to implementation

## Part 5: Cultural Fusion Narrative Design (agent.5)

### 5.1 Jing-Zhang Railway Historical Culture

The Jing-Zhang Railway was China's first independently designed trunk railway, with Zhan Tianyou's spirit representing the cultural gene of independent innovation.

### 5.2 Triple Culture Fusion Narrative

**Main Narrative:** "From Independent Railway to Independent AI — A Century of Innovation Continuation"

| Culture Layer | Historical Node | Spatial Expression | Contemporary Translation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Jing-Zhang Railway Culture | 1909 completion | Track relics, station ruins | Starting point of independent innovation |
| Zhongguancun Innovation Culture | 1980s S&T reform | Zhongguancun Avenue | Tradition of S&T conversion |
| AI New Culture | 2020s AI wave | Three key areas, open-source community | Leap from follower to leader |
