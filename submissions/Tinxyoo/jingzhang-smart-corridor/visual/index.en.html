Test Validation Scenarios (3)

Each test scenario specifies the test protocol, entry conditions, exit conditions, and stop conditions.

TVS-01~03

TVS-01 Slow-Mobility Breakpoint Diagnostic Test

Protocol: 30-day test · daily manual patrol review
Entry: slow-mobility data ready · accessibility checklist registered · operator confirmed
Exit: accuracy ≥85% · appeal-and-correction closure rate ≥90%
Stop: accuracy <70% for 3 consecutive days · or privacy breach

TVS-02 Data Asset Circulation Test

Protocol: 90-day test · every transaction auditable · monthly expert review
Entry: ≥3 authorized datasets · compliance filing online
Exit: ≥50 circulation transactions · zero compliance incidents · mediation rate ≥80%
Stop: compliance incident · or data breach

TVS-03 Open-Source Release Hall Operation Test

Protocol: 60-day test · weekly statistics on releases/participation/contributions
Entry: operator confirmed · review mechanism online
Exit: monthly average ≥4 sessions · participation ≥200 people · contributions ≥50
Stop: <2 sessions/month for 2 consecutive months · or review incident

AI Pilgrimage Landmarks (3)

Translating "pilgrimage" from a concept into locatable, visitable, and communicable spatial nodes.

PLG-01~03

PLG-01 Agent Contribution Honor Wall

Location: north end of the Railway Heritage Park (near the old site of Qinghuayuan Railway Station)
Design: a bronze-textured wall engraved with Agent names, GitHub IDs, and milestone years
Significance: honoring the first cohort of Agents participating in real urban design

PLG-02 Y-shaped Reversal Tower

Location: Zhongzhiyuan, along the Qinghe frontage
Design: a climbable observation tower taking the Y-shaped reversal line as its formal archetype
Significance: translating Zhan Tianyou's engineering philosophy into a symbol of innovation in the AI era

PLG-03 Open-Source Achievement Showcase Corridor

Location: Dazhongsi Station plaza
Design: an open showcase corridor presenting open-source contributions, evaluation results, and data circulation
Significance: converting "open source" into a public cultural asset of the city
