# 京张开拍：一页叙事 / JINGZHANG OPEN STUDIO: One-page Narrative

京张开拍不是把城市变成影视景区，而是把 AI 生成内容最难回答的四个问题——谁授权、谁劳动、谁复核、谁受益——做成一条可步行、可运营、可退出的城市制作链。众智园在低公众暴露环境里验证源模、虚拟制作、数字人、内容凭证和机器人摄影；AI 原点把研发者、艺术资源、学生、开发者与社区知识持有人组织为共同作者；大钟寺只首映经过清权、无障碍本地化和 Human Final Cut 的版本，并保留投诉、暂停与召回。

The proposal does not turn the city into an entertainment district. It turns four hard questions—who authorized, who worked, who reviewed, and who benefits—into a walkable, operable, and exit-ready production chain. Zhongzhiyuan validates models, virtual production, synthetic performers, provenance, and robot cinematography with low public exposure. AI Origin organizes researchers, artistic resources, students, developers, and local knowledge holders as co-authors. Dazhongsi premieres only rights-cleared, accessible, human-final-cut versions and preserves complaint, pause, and recall paths.

空间结构是“一约三场·两翼十二镜”。《京张公共片约》坚持城市不是免费片场、居民不是默认群演、AI 不是最终作者；三场对应验证、共创、首映；中关村翼提供权利与发行后台，小月河翼提供社区取景与公共反馈；十二镜是十二类场景卡。所有落位为 provisional 概念建议，官方 polygon 和专业资料到来后整包重算。

The structure is One Pact, Three Studios, Two Wings, and Twelve Lenses. The Public Production Pact says the city is not a free set, residents are not default extras, and AI is not the final author. The studios validate, co-author, and premiere; the Zhongguancun wing handles rights and release; the Xiaoyue River wing supports community locations and feedback; twelve lenses are twelve scenario families. Every placement is provisional and the package must be regenerated when official geometry and professional data arrive.
