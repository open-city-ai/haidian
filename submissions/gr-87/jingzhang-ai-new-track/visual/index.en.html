  • A-CONTROLS-001 — 开发强度概念区间（v0.5，非审定值）：总体容积率 1.5–3.0、建筑高度 18–45 m、法定绿地率≥30%、退线按控规分档；三处重点区分档（更新用地占比×容积率）：众智园 30–45%×1.8–2.8 → 概念建筑量 100–240 万 m²、就业 3.5–12 万；原点社区 25–40%×1.5–2.5 → 40–100 万 m²、就业 1.3–5 万；大钟寺 30–45%×2.0–3.0 → 45–95 万 m²、就业 1.5–5 万（按 20–30 m²/岗估）。区间仅作深化参考，不作为法定控制值；官方控规发布前一切强度结论仍为待确认。
  • A-EXISTING-001 — Existing building footprints, parcel boundaries, ownership, heritage control zones and municipal/utility data are not publicly cleared.
  • A-TRANSPORT-001 — Road alignments, rail lines, bridge/tunnel and parking data are schematic indications only (road_class, provisional_alignment).
  • A-PHASING-001 — Phasing is a conceptual near/mid/long-term proposal (2026-2028 / 2028-2031 / 2031-2035) that fully partitions the submitted boundary; it is not a commitment of investment, sequencing approval or government decision.
