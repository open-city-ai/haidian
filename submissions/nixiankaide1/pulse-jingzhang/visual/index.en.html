Key Areas

key_areas.geojson · Three Starts

Zhongzhiyuan · Verify Start

VERIFY START
192.1 ha conceptual area

Full-Stack Independent AI Innovation Acceleration Area (north core). 'One Core, One Ring, One Belt': a Verification Tower + testing/validation cluster, an industry-service ring, and a sports & wellness testing belt facing the Qinghe River. Mainly renovating existing research buildings, with a new Verification Tower at the core; fitness-assessment station pilot clusters and a validation-results visualization plaza, with privacy boundaries and Human Review established first.

AI Origin · Origin Start

ORIGIN START
104.3 ha conceptual area

Composite carrier of a world-class AI innovation ecosystem and the Jing-Zhang Railway zero-kilometer memory (central core · Tsinghuayuan zero-kilometer). 'One Plaza, One Axis, One Corridor': Tsinghuayuan Start Plaza (zero-kilometer post + start gate), Xueyuan Road Innovation Axis, and the Heritage Park main corridor. Heritage protection first, with renovation and mending around the station; inscription + digital-nameplate honor system.

Dazhongsi · Experience Start

EXPERIENCE START
72.0 ha conceptual area

Southern gateway of AI industry agglomeration and life experience (south core). 'One Core, Two Interfaces': the Dazhongsi Vitality Core (smart-court flagship + fitness-assessment experience center), the community interface and the rail interface. Transit-station-integrated TOD concept; smart courts and exercise-prescription blocks embedded in the community grid, serving 'one hour of exercise after work'.

The three areas are linked from north to south along the START LINE corridor, forming the "verify—origin—experience" spatial movement line, totalling 368.4 ha. All three area polygons are provisional; the following conclusions are directional designs for professional teams to develop.

Transport & Slow Mobility

roads.geojson · one vertical, three horizontals

One Vertical

START LINE corridor R-007

Jing-Zhang heritage developer running greenway, running + cycling + walking three-line composite, conceptual section 12–20 m. The belt-wide 22 km is a conceptual target; approx. 9.7 km is measurable within this provisional range.

Three Horizontals

Xueyuan Road · Dazhongsi East Road · Xizhimen Outer Street

East-west distribution and rail connections; intersections are the scenario nodes (fitness-assessment stations, vitality stations, etc.). Cycling and running lanes are separately aligned; motor traffic is channeled away from the core segment.

Rail Connections

Line 13 · Changping Line · Zhichun Road—Xizhimen

The TOD concept organizes mixed development around the Three Starts stations; air rights above stations reserve sports & wellness services (lockers, showers, fitness pre-checks), making "start running right after exiting the station" an everyday commuting option.

Slow-mobility gap-repair priorities: the Heritage Park × Xueyuan Road intersection, the Xitucheng Road crossing point, and the Qinghe green-belt connection all require point-by-point confirmation once official road red lines are available. All of the above are conceptual recommendations.

Qinghe—Xiaoyue River—Heritage Park green network

Assumptions

assumptions.json · 8 items
A-BOUNDARY-001provisional

All geometry is based on the temporary rough boundary of brief/site-package/geometry/provisional_boundaries.geojson (overall design area approx. 11.41 km², three key areas approx. 368.4 ha). The boundary was inferred by the maintainer from the announcement's text limits and areas; it is not an official planning boundary, not an approval basis, and not a precise area basis.

Once the official polygon is released, site_boundary/key_areas/land_use/buildings/roads/green_space/public_space/phasing and all area-type metrics must be recalculated as a package; before recalculation this proposal is for display and discussion.
A-CONTROLS-001pending_professional_confirmation

Regulatory indicators (FAR, building height, development intensity), road red lines, land ownership, municipal pipelines and engineering conditions are subject to official annexes; this proposal presents them only as conceptual recommendations and lists them as items to be confirmed.

The proposal claims no statutory control conclusion; formal content scoring is not blocked by the organizing data gap, but professional review awaits official data.
A-LANDUSE-001design_proposal

The land-use layout and its shares (research approx. 34%, residential approx. 22%, green approx. 14%, sports approx. 6%, etc.) are conceptual design targets expressing the spatial logic of an 'AI × sports & wellness vitality city'; they are not approved regulatory land use.

Land-use zones are recomputable (land_use.geojson covers the boundary without gaps), but may differ from the current cadastre/regulatory plan and require professional verification.
A-BUILDING-001conceptual

The 311 building-footprint massing blocks are conceptual representations arranged along the north-south spine and the Three Starts; they do not represent existing buildings and do not constitute a new-build/demolish/renovate scheme.

Building scale, height and interfaces serve only as spatial imagery; Demolish–Renovate–Retain is expressed qualitatively through the retain/renovate/demolish/new-build logics without plot-level conclusions.
A-TRAIL-001conceptual

The '22 km START LINE corridor' is the belt-wide conceptual target for the whole innovation belt; within this provisional range the measured greenway R-007 is approx. 9.70 km and the north-south spine main street R-001 approx. 9.49 km.

22 km is a conceptual mileage, not completed mileage or the existing condition.
A-SPORTS-001conceptual

The approx. 5.6% share of sports land 0805 is the signature metric of the proposal, above the typical level of ordinary urban districts, supporting the sports & wellness vitality-city positioning; the target must be implemented in the regulatory detailed plan and implementation plan.

The sports-facility layout is a conceptual recommendation; specific locations await professional development.
A-METRICS-001computed

All area and length metrics are recomputed under the EPSG:4548 projection (CGCS2000 3-degree zone, central meridian 117°E); GeoJSON exchange coordinates are EPSG:4326.

The recomputation method and coordinate system are disclosed and can be verified by professional teams.
A-DATA-001cleared

This proposal uses only repository public/cleared materials (announcement, taskbook, standards, provisional boundary) and publicly verifiable case-method information; no non-public planning materials, internal data or personal privacy data are used.
