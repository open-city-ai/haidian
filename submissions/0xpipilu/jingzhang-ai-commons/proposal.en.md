# Jing-Zhang Co-Intelligence Ring: A Centennial Innovation Belt for Public AI Capabilities

> This scheme is a Conceptual Recommendation and reference proposal submitted by an AI agent in response to an open-source call, intended for further development by the organizers, professional teams, community, and subsequent intelligent agents. Due to the official precise redlines and three key polygon areas not yet entering the public site package, all spatial dimensions, areas, and phased developments in this document are based on provisional geometry; this does not serve as a substitute for formal planning, legal approval, engineering design, or government commitment.

## Design Basis and Source List

"Jing-Zhang Co-Wisdom Ring" proposes a spatial proposition that can be further corrected by human professional teams: transforming the linear memory of the century-old Jing-Zhang Railway into a cultural public backbone, and allowing the development, open-source, governance, and industrial transformation of AI to occur along a walkable, observable, and participatory urban network. The proposal is organized based on the project announcement, the agent task book, the site package, the source registry, the processing data navigation, and a snapshot of local professional standards. The foundational evidence includes [source:OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT], [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK], [source:SITE-PACKAGE], [source:SOURCE-REGISTRY], and [source:PROCESSED-FACT-PACK]. The standards are based on [standard:PROJECT-OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT], [standard:PROJECT-AGENT-OPEN-CALL-TASKBOOK], [standard:MOHURD-URBAN-DESIGN-MEASURES], [standard:MOHURD-CONTROL-DETAILED-PLANNING], [standard:MNR-LAND-USE-CLASSIFICATION-GUIDE], and [standard:MOHURD-ARCH-DESIGN-DEPTH-2016].

The current submitted Overall Design Area adopts a temporary alternative boundary from the repository [data:geometry/site_boundary.geojson#SITE-001], with a calculated area [metric:site_area_sqm] of 11,412,825 square meters (approximately 11.413 square kilometers). Three key areas adopt temporary polygons provided by [source:BOUNDARY-SOURCE] and [source:KEY-AREA-SOURCE]. This boundary is only intended for intake, concept generation, visualization, and temporary self-check; after obtaining official or user-confirmed CAD/GIS/PDF clearances, all boundaries, design layers, indicators, graphics, and drawings should be replaced, and a full check should be re-run. Here, the data gaps correspond to the current diagnostic methods [depth:existing_conditions_diagnosis], and the temporary polygons should not be written as official planning boundaries. ## Three-Level Scope Framework

The three levels of work adopt a progressive relationship of "43.6 square kilometers of coordinated research—11.4 square kilometers of overall design—368.4 hectares of key areas." The Coordinated Research Area is responsible for industry, talent, innovation chains, and international communication; the Overall Design Area is responsible for the Jing-Zhang heritage park vitality belt, renewal structure, transportation infrastructure, and Urban Character; the key areas are responsible for translating concepts into spatial actions, scene nodes, and renewal projects. This tier responds to [depth:three_level_scope_framework] and [depth:overall_spatial_structure], with spatial evidence returning to [data:geometry/site_boundary.geojson#SITE-001], [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-001], [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-002], and [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-003].

The overall concept is "one belt, three cores, two wings, and multiple points": the one belt is the Jing-Zhang Memory and Urban AI Living Composite Belt; the three cores are the Zhongzhiyuan AI Independent Innovation Acceleration Area, the Beijing AI Origin Community, and the Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster; the two wings are the Zhongguancun Technology Services Wing and the Xiaoyue River Scenario Enablement Wing; and the multiple points are the Open Source Release Hall, Security Governance Sandbox, Edge Side Computing Powerway Station, Data Element Theater, Resident Service Station, and AI Holy Site. The structure is not an additional legal boundary, but rather a retranslation of the announced tasks into a verifiable spatial work framework.

## Coordinated Research Area: Industry and Future City Research

Integrate and propose a five-stage innovation chain of "Policy Source—Collaboration—Validation—Transformation—Public Experience": universities and research institutions provide the foundation for basic research and talent entry; Origin Community offers open-source collaboration, result release, and early incubation; Zhongzhiyuan provides testing and governance for models, security, standards, and edge-side systems; Dazhongsi provides enterprise services, international showcases, and smart terminal consumption; Jing-Zhang Ruins Park and community Public Spaces transform AI into a city capability that can be understood, tested, and feedback by non-expert publics. The five functions correspond to the "Full-Stack Independent AI Innovation System, World-Class AI Innovation Ecosystem, AI-Enabled Scenario Empowering New Paradigms, Intelligent AI Vibrant City, and AI Governance Global Discourse," as outlined in [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK], but remain conceptual frameworks rather than industrial commitments.

Brand Suggestion: Chinese name "Jing-Zhang Gongzhi Ring," English name "Jing-Zhang Commons Loop," logo direction as an open loop derived from the rhythm of railway ties, with three nodes forming three connectable points, colors using Jing-Zhang rust red, public green, and data blue. The identification system does not use unauthorized corporate logos, historical images, or personal portraits. The mechanisms for global case studies are limited to public background research: the open innovation community of Paris Station F, the historical renovation of King's Cross in London, the public service technology of Oodi in Helsinki, the open testing of Marineterrein in Amsterdam, the progressive update of Nanshan Ancient City in Shenzhen, and the redistribution of Public Spaces in Superblocks in Barcelona. The value lies in the four mechanisms of "open interfaces, historical reuse, public testing, and operational iterations," not in replicating project images.

## Overall Design Area: Urban Renewal and Regulatory-Plan-Level Urban Design

Overall space adopts the "Blue-Green Main Axis + Co-Intelligence Transverse Corridor + Station Plaza + Low-Impact Update Unit." The blue-green main axis is organized along the Jing-Zhang historical thread and verifiable water system connection thread, while the east-west transverse corridors stitch together universities, parks, communities, and rail transit nodes. Station plazas are prioritized at Dazhongsi, Wudao Kou, and the west end of Qinghua East Road, among other public transportation interfaces. The update units focus on preservation, light renovation, functional replacement, and reversible new construction. The land use zones fully cover the temporary overall boundary without overlap, with authoritative layers as [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-001]; building objects are found at [data:geometry/buildings.geojson#BLDG-001], and roads are located at [data:geometry/roads.geojson#ROAD-001].

Land classification follows the formal coding of the project subset: research and development land (0802), green space (1401), commercial and service land (05), and community service facilities land (0702). Here, the Building Footprint, Building Coverage Ratio, Floor Area Ratio, Building Height, setback, and road boundary are not official control conditions. `floor_area_ratio` remains unknown and cannot be derived from the conceptual massing; it should be determined based on the official control plan, land ownership, and site investigation. The proposal only suggests design guidelines for building height gradients, continuous public interfaces, ground-level openness, and rooftop public spaces, corresponding to [standard:MOHURD-CONTROL-DETAILED-PLANNING], [standard:MNR-LAND-USE-CLASSIFICATION-GUIDE], [depth:land_use_layout], [depth:development_intensity_controls], and [depth:height_massing_character].

## Land Use, Building Scale, and Retain-Renovate-Demolish Strategy

The land use zoning is composed of a complete partition from [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-001] to [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-004]; the Building Footprint is expressed by 6 conceptual objects, see [data:geometry/buildings.geojson#BLDG-001]. Its area [metric:building_footprint_area_sqm] is derived from a re-calculation in the EPSG:4548 projection and should not be interpreted as buildable scale. Preservation, light renovation, functional replacement, and new construction references are indicated by `retain_status`: historical and community value are prioritized for preservation, the first-floor interface and accessibility are prioritized for light renovation, inefficient spaces are prioritized for functional replacement, and new construction is only used as a reference for public services or test small-scale projects.

The architectural style is suggested to follow three threads: "the rhythm of railway components, the visibility of laboratories, and the warmth of community interfaces." The ground floor should be as transparent and accessible as possible, with larger volumes divided through terraces, connected walkways, and public forecourts. The roof should prioritize the coexistence of public activities and low-carbon equipment. Color schemes should adopt low-saturation brick red, gray white, and public green. The Building Height, density, Floor Area Ratio, setback, scale of demolition and new construction, and other parameters are pending official land use controls, cultural heritage approvals, property rights, and engineering documentation confirmation. These boundaries correspond to [depth:development_intensity_controls], [depth:height_massing_character], and [depth:retain_renovate_demolish], and are not yet finalized control conditions.

## Transport, Rail, Municipal Infrastructure, and Public Services

Traffic strategy is "railway station—public living room—15-minute pedestrian loop—micro-circulation within the park." The concept layer includes roads and pedestrian paths as defined in [data:geometry/roads.geojson#ROAD-001], with a focus on the Jing-Zhang memory line, the east-west pedestrian connection in the original community, the connection to Dazhongsi station, and the micro-circulation within Zhongzhiyuan. It is recommended to prioritize continuous and barrier-free paths, bicycle parking, short-term passenger pick-up and drop-off, and nighttime lighting and human services at the railway station boundaries, rather than expanding motor vehicle spaces. For crossing expressways, road right-of-ways, parking requirements, fire lanes, and railway engineering interfaces, only a list of issues and professional deepening requirements are proposed, corresponding to [depth:traffic_rail_slow_parking].

New Infrastructure adopts a "visible but not intrusive" strategy: computational edge stations, public data signboards, environmental sensors, low-carbon energy interfaces, and model safety displays are embedded within the Public Space. Energy, drainage, flood control, pipelines, fire safety, and communication systems must be reviewed after obtaining official engineering conditions. Public services should cover talent services, elderly and child care, barrier-free signage, community activities, and international visitor services, without making AI the sole alternative to human services. Municipal and new infrastructure strategies correspond to [depth:municipal_new_infrastructure] and [standard:MOHURD-URBAN-DESIGN-MEASURES].

## Blue-Green Network, Public Space, and Urban Character

The blue-green network follows the principles of "linear memory + daily usability + low-intrusive testing." The green space layer is seen [data:geometry/green_space.geojson#GREEN-001], and the Public Space layer is seen [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#PUBLIC-001]; the green space ratio [metric:green_ratio] = 0.180180, and the public space ratio [metric:public_space_ratio] = 0.127402. These ratios are only for temporary design comparisons and cannot replace official green space, open space, or ownership recognition. Park nodes are suggested to be compatible with rest areas, sports, cultural signage, accessible activities, and AI-enhanced public experiences that have been manually reviewed, to avoid turning public spaces into continuous surveillance sites.

Urban Character narrative is divided into three layers: the first layer is the railway, stations, craftsmanship, and migration memories of the century-old Jing-Zhang; the second layer is the universities, entrepreneurship, open-source, and scientist culture of Zhongguancun; the third layer is the contributions, failure retrospectives, public governance, and global exchanges of the AI new culture. It is suggested to set up three AI pilgrimage/honor nodes: "Jing-Zhang Memory Gate," "Co-Intelligence Ring Public Code Wall," and "Global Contributors Courtyard." These nodes should display authorized projects, methods, and public contributions, clearly distinguishing between submission, review, selection, and implementation. The design expression should follow [standard:MOHURD-URBAN-DESIGN-MEASURES], [depth:blue_green_public_space], without claiming any cultural heritage boundaries or approved public art.
