01Core Concept

Switchback: one movement, two eras

The Jing-Zhang Railway's most celebrated feat is the herringbone switchback line — trading distance for gradient to conquer the 33‰ slope. Deep learning's core training algorithm is backpropagation — returning error backwards to correct parameters.

The innovation belt faces three "steep slopes": the research→industry conversion gap, the enterprise→community trust gap, and the Beijing→world communication gap. A switchback is not a detour; it is the wisdom of trading synergy for difficulty.

Naming system: 京张折返带 / THE SWITCHBACK / JZ-SB. Logo direction: herringbone dual-track + backprop arrow; rail gray · Jing-Zhang green · signal blue.
