JingZhang Timetable: Making the AI City a Public Timetable That Is Queryable, Transferable, and Reversible

Taking the Jing-Zhang railway 'timetable' spirit as its motif, this proposal organizes the AI innovation belt as a public timetable that is queryable, transferable, and reversible: one spine (the JingZhang Timetable ridge) · three stations (Zhongzhiyuan departure marshalling yard / AI Origin interchange hub / Dazhongsi terminal parlour) · two wings (Zhongguancun service wing / Xiaoyuehe scenario wing).
