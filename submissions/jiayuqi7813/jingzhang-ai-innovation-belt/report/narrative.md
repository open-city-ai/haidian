# Formal Narrative / 方案叙事

## English summary

**JingZhang Timetable** turns the Centennial Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt into a public timetable that is queryable, transferable, and reversible — a motif inherited from the Jing-Zhang railway timetable, China's first public "punctuality promise". The spatial skeleton is **one spine, three stations, two wings**: the north-south JingZhang Timetable ridge (heritage-park slow axis and AI service stop board), the Zhongzhiyuan departure marshalling yard, the AI Origin interchange hub, and the Dazhongsi terminal city parlour, plus the Zhongguancun service wing and the Xiaoyuehe scenario wing. The package contains a bilingual proposal, five figures in zh/en, offline visual pages, A3 booklet and A0 boards in zh/en, topology-safe GeoJSON (18 land-use parcels seamlessly covering the provisional site boundary), recomputed EPSG:4548 metrics, compliance/standard/design-depth matrices, and self-check results.

## 中文摘要

**「京张时刻」**把百年京张AI创新带组织成一张可查询、可换乘、可回退的公共时刻表——母题来自京张铁路的「时刻表」，这是中国第一份面向公众的“准点承诺”。空间骨架为**一表·三站·两翼**：南北贯通的京张时刻主脊（遗址公园慢行轴与AI公共服务站牌）、众智园始发编组站、AI原点中转换乘站、大钟寺终到会客厅，以及中关村科技服务翼与小月河场景赋能翼。本包包含中英双语提案、5张中英图件、离线可视化页、A3文册与A0展板（中英）、拓扑安全的 GeoJSON（18 个用地地块无缝覆盖临时边界）、EPSG:4548 复算指标、合规/标准/设计深度矩阵与自检结果。

All geometry, metrics, matrices, and the visual page remain cross-checked deliverables. / 本叙事为结构化 AI 提交包的可读摘要；几何、指标、矩阵、图纸与可视化仍为交叉校验的正式成果。
