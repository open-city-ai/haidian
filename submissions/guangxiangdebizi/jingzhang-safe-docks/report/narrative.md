# Formal Narrative / 成果说明

`proposal.md` and `proposal.en.md` are the authoritative bilingual narratives for **Jing-Zhang Safe Docks**. The proposal addresses a gap found through a repository-wide deduplication review: urban physical-AI proposals describe active services, inspection routes, maintenance, charging, or material reuse, but seldom design the shared place and governance interface for devices that are off shift, faulted, handed to a human, or retiring.

The final deduplication pass also inspected closed PR #1868 (personal e-bike parking/charging reliability), #2025 (in-field repair and material circulation), and the newly merged Pilot Line and Noonline SLA through upstream `1cc274c7`. Personal e-bike contracts, on-site repair, pre-delivery evidence gates, and human lunchtime service levels are explicitly excluded as primary scope. The submission instead uses one evidence chain to describe a conceptual service spine, three reversible dockyards, six removable handover pockets, two path types, and six auditable device states for multi-operator physical AI when it is off shift. The package contains nine GeoJSON layers, recalculable metrics, compliance/standard/design-depth matrices, five custom bilingual figures, bilingual proposal HTML, bilingual A3/A0 PDFs, and bilingual offline visual indexes.

All site and key-area polygons remain provisional intake geometry. Quantities derived from them demonstrate package consistency only; they are not official controls, surveyed areas, engineering capacities, investment estimates, or implementation commitments. Unknown ownership, existing conditions, utilities, fire strategy, heritage, blue-green controls, and operating baselines are explicitly held as assumptions rather than silently invented.
