Three-Tier Scope

  • Coordinated research scope: 43.6 km², bounded north by the North 5th Ring Road, east by the Jingzang Expressway, south by Xizhimen Outer Street, west by Wanquanhe Road (announcement text).
  • Overall design scope: ~11.4 km², 1–2 km around the Jing-Zhang Heritage Park (provisional boundary).
  • Key area scope: three areas totaling ~368.4 ha (Zhongzhiyuan / Origin Community / Dazhongsi, provisional).
  • Spatial syntax: Ren-line mainline (green corridor) → three Epoch station platforms (plaza – station building – greenery) → switch wings → block sections B1–B5.

AI Scenarios

  • 13 scenario cards (10+3) with the full scenario–space–data–model–operation–review–KPI matrix.
  • 3 industrial test-and-validation scenarios: T1 model red-team test ground, T2 edge-intelligence validation node, T3 agent interoperability corridor.

Key Areas

Zhongzhiyuan · Computing Epoch Station

Dazhongsi · Application Epoch Station

Intelligent economy and consumption (B1)
  • Station-front plaza four-quadrant pedestrian connectivity
  • Agent/terminal showcase + data-element lounge
  • International roadshow and urban living room

Mobility × Blue-Green Public Space

  • The Ren-line greenway links the three Epoch stations; the two-wing switch branches connect the service wing and scenario wing.
  • Transit stations (Wudaokou / West Qinghua East Road / Dazhongsi) as 300–500 m public space anchors.
  • The Qinghe waterfront and Xiaoyue River green corridor form the continuous blue-green skeleton.
  • Roads are conceptual centerlines, not road redlines; gaps and engineering conditions await official confirmation.

Land-Use Zones / Buildings / Renewal Projects

  • Land use is re-planned along the Ren-line corridor–station–switch wing and functional zones, seamlessly covering the submission boundary.
  • Building footprints are conceptual (42 footprints covering 12 building types).
  • Demolition/renewal/retention: four strategy tiers — preserve, retrofit, renew, reserve.
  • Renewal projects JZ-01 to JZ-12 are organized in near/mid/long phases with subject/dependency/duration/phased KPIs (conceptual).
