Operation protocol: Departure Protocol

Upgrading the scenario list system into a nameable, checkable, iterable loop: Issue → Review → Sandbox → Bell Witness → Depart → Postmortem, plus Rollback; human final judgment overrides at every step (Human Override).

Departure Protocol

1. Issue

Public submission with full trail and appeal.

2. Review

Origin Community: provenance, idea and public-interest gate.

3. Sandbox

Zhongzhiyuan: controlled validation, admission and stop thresholds.

4. Bell Witness

Dazhongsi Bell of Intelligence: public witnessing and honor records.

5. Depart

Into real city scenarios, time- and area-limited.

6. Postmortem

Xiaoyuehe back-testing: real-use feedback returns.

7. Rollback

Pause, withdrawal and site restoration with human fallback.
