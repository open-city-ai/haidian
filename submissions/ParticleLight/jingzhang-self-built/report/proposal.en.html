JINGZHANG SELF-BUILT BELT: From China's First Self-Built Railway to a Self-Built AI Innovation Belt — Urban Design Concept Proposal

Taking the autonomy with which Chinese engineers built the Jing-Zhang Railway in 1909 as its origin point, this proposal presents the JINGZHANG SELF-BUILT BELT concept: one self-built spine, three self-built cores, two supporting wings and twelve Self-Built Nodes, governed by a Self-Built Covenant (Autonomy · Responsibility · Self-Reflection, with Build / Operations / Exit certificates) that makes every AI entry into the city traceable, answerable and reversible. All spatial recommendations are conceptual, generated on provisional boundaries and subject to full recalculation after official redlines are released.

Coordinated Research Area: Industry and Future City Research

Overall concept "JINGZHANG SELF-BUILT BELT": In 1909, Zhan Tianyou and Chinese engineers self-built the Jing-Zhang Railway — China's first trunk line fully surveyed, designed, constructed and managed by Chinese people. Derided abroad as an "impossible project", it was completed in four years, under budget, connected to the world on the standard gauge, and served for over a century 来源. That was the first proof of "self-built". In 2026 the same corridor faces a second question: can Beijing, on AI — the most critical general-purpose technology of our era — self-build an innovation belt that is "built well, usable, and governable", from models, compute and data to standards, ecosystems and governance, verified by people, auditable and reversible 来源?

The proposal makes "Self-Built" the belt's brand core and design operating system, producing a naming system: 京张自造带 / JINGZHANG SELF-BUILT BELT (JZ Self-Built Belt) with the tagline "自己造 · 造得成 · 用百年 / Built by ourselves. Built to last." The logo direction is a homomorphism of the character "自" (self) and the "人"-shaped (herringbone) track — two parallel rails forming the "自" frame, with the abstracted Qinglongqiao herringbone switchback as the central stroke, symbolizing "autonomy centered on people" 深度. The three areas and two wings receive sub-brands: Zhongzhiyuan = Self-Research Core (full-stack autonomous innovation), AI Origin Community = Co-Creation Core (open source, talent, origin), Dazhongsi = Self-Use Core (smart-native commerce); the Zhongguancun wing = Service Wing (global factor allocation), the Xiaoyue River wing = Test Wing (scenario empowerment and validation), forming the loop "origin produces ideas → Zhongzhiyuan accelerates → Dazhongsi reaches the city → Zhongguancun supplies factors → Xiaoyue River validates scenarios → feedback to origin" 来源. To avoid confusion with existing sibling schemes, this proposal makes an originality statement: the concept, naming and logo direction are independently generated and do not follow prior formulations such as "Intelligence Vein", "Intelligence Spine", "Switchback Belt" or "Open-Source Belt"; the herringbone (人字形) switchback and the "self-built" spirit are public railway heritage that no single scheme should claim exclusively 来源 深度. Logo direction ("自" character + herringbone rail) specification: standard form for primary identity and signage, with minimum usage size and clear space, reverse and greyscale versions for dark/light backgrounds, no stretching or distortion; final fonts and graphic details await professional visual teams working from cleared sources.

Self-Built Covenant Public Component Library and AI-Native Interface

The Self-Built Covenant (Autonomy · Responsibility · Self-Reflection) is not a slogan. The proposal turns the three certificates into standard public-space components at the twelve Self-Built Nodes, making every AI entering the city visible, question-able and stoppable in physical space 来源 深度. The component library is grounded in real regulations and deployments, not label-pasted concepts.

Build Certificate · Information Post: a physical-digital information post disclosing developer, model version, data sources, update date and the human-review entry; located at each node entrance with a unified visual language, QR access to full records, and no personal behavioural tracking. It responds to the Labeling Measures for AI-Generated/Synthetic Content (effective 2025-09-01) on explicit and implicit labelling 来源, EU AI Act Article 50 transparency duties (effective 2026-08-02) 来源, and Washington DC's deployed DTPR digital-trust signage precedent 来源.

Operations Certificate · Status Light: a three-colour status language — green = running normally, yellow = in test / attention needed, red = requires human review or stopped; uniformly coded with public facilities and traffic signals so an AI's operating state is publicly legible. The semantics borrow the urban-rail "green = smooth, yellow = congested, red = interrupted" three-colour precedent (Shanghai Metro's three-colour map, in service since 2009) 来源, turning AI status from invisible to a legible public interface.

Exit Certificate · Human Takeover Station: each node provides a human takeover station and an equivalent non-AI path (paper, manual, on-site service), with published stop conditions and exit/restore procedures, and one-touch transfer to human in emergencies. It implements Article 39 of the Barrier-Free Environment Construction Law requiring medical, social-security, financial and utility-payment service venues to keep on-site guidance and manual processing 来源, and Article 24(3) of the Personal Information Protection Law on the right to explanation of and refusal of automated decisions 来源.

Components and space: the component library acts as a unified "public interface layer" across the twelve nodes and the three cores' public space, co-designed with signage, paving and lighting; the three certificates' records feed a public data ledger (conceptual; operations details await professional teams) 空间数据 深度. The status-light semantics are a design proposal — no city has yet deployed a public AI status light — and formal implementation requires professional standards deepening.
