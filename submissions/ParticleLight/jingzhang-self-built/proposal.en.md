# JINGZHANG SELF-BUILT BELT: From China's First Self-Built Railway to a Self-Built AI Innovation Belt — Urban Design Concept Proposal

## Design Basis and Source List

This concept proposal is anchored on the Qualification Pre-announcement for the International Open Call for Urban Design of the Centennial Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt, issued by Haidian Branch, Beijing Municipal Commission of Planning and Natural Resources [source:OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT], and on the agent-facing open-call taskbook as its task basis [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK]. Machine-readable basis is drawn from the registered three-level scope, provisional boundaries, enums, planning limits, source list and standard snapshots in `brief/site-package/` [source:SITE-PACKAGE] [standard:PROJECT-OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT] [standard:PROJECT-AGENT-OPEN-CALL-TASKBOOK].

The official announcement did not publish precise polygons, and as of 2026-08-07 no verifiable official redline was found on public channels. This proposal therefore uses the maintainer-registered provisional rough boundaries (`provisional_constraint`, `official_boundary=false`, `boundary_precision=provisional_rough`) as the generation and display base [source:BOUNDARY-SOURCE] [data:geometry/site_boundary.geojson#SITE-001]. This organizer data gap does not block content scoring, but all area, ratio and control statements must be recalculated as a whole after official redlines are released and must not be used as approval or precise-area basis [source:SOURCE-REGISTRY] [source:PROCESSED-FACT-PACK]. Data-use boundaries and the missing-data list are recorded in `sources.json`, `assumptions.json` and the compliance matrix [depth:risk_missing_data].

## Three-Level Scope Framework

The proposal organizes work strictly by the announcement's three scopes: the Coordinated Research Area (approx. 43.6 km²) answers how the AI innovation ecosystem and future urban form should be organized; the Overall Design Area (approx. 11.4 km², the submitted boundary) delivers the overall renewal, industrial-spatial, transport-utility and urban-character framework at regulatory-plan urban design depth; the Key Detailed Design Area (approx. 368.4 ha across three key areas) reaches implementation-plan depth [standard:MOHURD-URBAN-DESIGN-MEASURES] [depth:three_level_scope_framework]. The three levels are not three disconnected sheets but a strategy–structure–parcel delivery chain: the research level defines the innovation chain and regional synergy, the overall level defines spatial structure and capacity, and the key-area level verifies the feasibility of parcels, buildings, transport, public space and AI scenarios [depth:overall_spatial_structure] [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-SCOPE-001].

The Coordinated Research Area engages in innovation synergy with Beitai community, Future Science City, Huairou Science City, the Economic-Technological Development Area and the Beijing–Tianjin–Hebei region, forming an innovation loop of "university incubation – open-source collaboration – enterprise transfer – public experience – international outreach" [source:SRC-2026-BJ-KW-THREE-AREAS-WINGS]. The Overall Design Area uses the Jing-Zhang Heritage Park active belt as its spine connecting the three cores and two wings, and the key areas land the three-core positioning at parcel scale. The three levels are mapped requirement-by-requirement into `compliance_matrix.json` for announcement sections 1.3, 1.4, 1.5 and tasks agent.1–agent.6 [source:PROCESSED-FACT-PACK].

## Coordinated Research Area: Industry and Future City Research

**Overall concept "JINGZHANG SELF-BUILT BELT"**: In 1909, Zhan Tianyou and Chinese engineers self-built the Jing-Zhang Railway — China's first trunk line fully surveyed, designed, constructed and managed by Chinese people. Derided abroad as an "impossible project", it was completed in four years, under budget, connected to the world on the standard gauge, and served for over a century [source:DATA-SRC-OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT-20260509]. That was the first proof of "self-built". In 2026 the same corridor faces a second question: can Beijing, on AI — the most critical general-purpose technology of our era — self-build an innovation belt that is "built well, usable, and governable", from models, compute and data to standards, ecosystems and governance, verified by people, auditable and reversible [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK]?

The proposal makes "Self-Built" the belt's brand core and design operating system, producing a naming system: **京张自造带 / JINGZHANG SELF-BUILT BELT** (JZ Self-Built Belt) with the tagline "自己造 · 造得成 · 用百年 / Built by ourselves. Built to last." The logo direction is a homomorphism of the character "自" (self) and the "人"-shaped (herringbone) track — two parallel rails forming the "自" frame, with the abstracted Qinglongqiao herringbone switchback as the central stroke, symbolizing "autonomy centered on people" [depth:overall_spatial_structure]. The three areas and two wings receive sub-brands: Zhongzhiyuan = **Self-Research Core** (full-stack autonomous innovation), AI Origin Community = **Co-Creation Core** (open source, talent, origin), Dazhongsi = **Self-Use Core** (smart-native commerce); the Zhongguancun wing = **Service Wing** (global factor allocation), the Xiaoyue River wing = **Test Wing** (scenario empowerment and validation), forming the loop "origin produces ideas → Zhongzhiyuan accelerates → Dazhongsi reaches the city → Zhongguancun supplies factors → Xiaoyue River validates scenarios → feedback to origin" [source:SRC-2026-HAIDIAN-1X1]. To avoid confusion with existing sibling schemes, this proposal makes an originality statement: the concept, naming and logo direction are independently generated and do not follow prior formulations such as "Intelligence Vein", "Intelligence Spine", "Switchback Belt" or "Open-Source Belt"; the herringbone (人字形) switchback and the "self-built" spirit are public railway heritage that no single scheme should claim exclusively [source:SOURCE-REGISTRY] [depth:overall_spatial_structure]. Logo direction ("自" character + herringbone rail) specification: standard form for primary identity and signage, with minimum usage size and clear space, reverse and greyscale versions for dark/light backgrounds, no stretching or distortion; final fonts and graphic details await professional visual teams working from cleared sources.

Five global AI innovation ecosystem cases yield transferable mechanisms, grounded in verifiable facts [depth:overall_spatial_structure]:

| Case | Area | Verifiable facts | Transferable mechanism |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| San Francisco Mission Bay | ~123 ha | UCSF medical/research campus as anchor; Chase Center (2019), Uber/Visa/OpenAI HQs [source:DATA-SRC-MISSION-BAY] | Single public agency, unified planning on public brownfield, academic campus anchoring high-density R&D |
| London King's Cross | 27 ha | Employment 8,000 (2011) → 27,000 (2019); companies doubled to ~800 in a decade [source:DATA-SRC-KINGS-CROSS] | Single-owner consortium, public/cultural anchors (Central Saint Martins, Francis Crick) derisking private knowledge tenants |
| Singapore one-north | 200 ha | ~400 leading companies, 800 start-ups, 16 public research institutes [source:DATA-SRC-ONE-NORTH] | JTC "Business Park-White" zoning — single-use business park becomes a 15% flexible mixed district |
| Boston Kendall Square | ~1 square mile | ~37,500 employees within half a mile; ~20M sq ft of labs and offices [source:DATA-SRC-KENDALL] | University adjacency + unified land development on a transit node |
| Shenzhen Bay Tech Eco-Park | ~0.2 km² (park) | Shenzhen Bay Tech Park: 1,300+ companies, 150k+ high-end workers, ¥250bn+ annual output [source:DATA-SRC-SHENZHEN-BAY] | State-owned platform as unified developer-operator, shifting from "landlord" to "shareholder" |

Their shared conclusion is that innovation ecosystems are not planned but determined by distance, interfaces and entrances. The proposal translates this into three spatial mechanisms — distance (research–industry walking reach), interface (public ground floor, shared platforms) and entrance (metro stations and the green spine as anchors) [standard:PROJECT-AGENT-OPEN-CALL-TASKBOOK].

Coordinated research also lands on Haidian's current data: Beijing's AI industry reached ¥450bn in 2025 (~half of China), with 2,500+ AI companies and 241 registered foundation models [source:DATA-SRC-BEIJING-AI-2025]; Haidian's core AI output exceeded ¥350bn with 2,000+ AI companies, and the AI Origin Community has already gathered ~400 companies with 74% AI agglomeration and 37 universities and 52 national key laboratories within 1 km [source:DATA-SRC-AI-ORIGIN-COMMUNITY-2026]. The proposal's three-areas-two-wings layout aligns with the official 2026-03-27 release of the "Xuebei/Zhongzhiyuan – AI Origin Community – Dazhongsi" areas with Zhongguancun and Xiaoyue River wings [source:DATA-SRC-HAIDIAN-THREE-AREAS-WINGS-2026], and echoes Haidian's "1+X+1" modern industry system (AI at the apex, strategic emerging and future industries in the body, technology services at the base) [source:DATA-SRC-HAIDIAN-1X1-SYSTEM-2026]. Future-city-form research addresses how AI changes work, life, transport and public services, locating compute, data, scenarios and talent as definable functional zones and corridors rather than abstract technology visions [depth:overall_spatial_structure].

## Overall Design Area: Urban Renewal and Regulatory-Plan-Level Urban Design

The Overall Design Area (submitted boundary approx. 11.4 km²) is organized at regulatory-plan urban design depth: a **Self-Built Spine green corridor** runs north–south (approx. 9.7 km, integrating the Heritage Park, blue-green slow mobility, public activity and AI experience into one continuous public axis); three cores anchor innovation functions; two wings supply factors and scenarios; twelve Self-Built Nodes are placed along the spine [source:BOUNDARY-SOURCE] [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-001] [metric:greenway_length_m]. Land use follows a "one spine, five columns, nine bands" structure: the spine in the middle, two columns on each side for the Service Wing's industrial services and the Test Wing's living scenarios, and nine functional bands from south to north — commerce and business, commercial living, community, education, innovation community, R&D, residential, full-stack research, and the Qinghe riverfront [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-001] [depth:land_use_layout] [depth:development_intensity_controls].

Urban renewal follows the logic of "stitching first, spending within means, retaining as the starting point": eight east–west stitching crossings reconnect the eastern and western city that a century of rail and ring roads divided; inefficient spaces are renewed incrementally; built environments of historic and community value are retained; statutory controls such as FAR, height and density are all marked "pending official regulatory conditions" rather than substituted with AI-inferred values [source:DATA-SRC-MOHURD-CONTROL-DETAILED-PLANNING] [depth:retain_renovate_demolish] [depth:height_massing_character]. The overall design lands transit-station integration, road micro-circulation, non-motorized parking, parking supply, innovation service platforms, talent living services, new infrastructure, distributed energy and edge compute onto layers and metrics [metric:site_area_sqm] [metric:public_space_ratio] [depth:municipal_new_infrastructure].

## Detailed Design of Key Areas

**Self-Research Core · Zhongzhiyuan (approx. 192.1 ha)**: facing the "AI full-stack autonomous innovation system and global AI governance discourse power", it organizes a garden-style full-stack R&D district. Four spatial moves: a low-carbon innovation green front along the Qinghe; the **Self-Built Gate (自造之门)** for full-stack "model–chip–compute–standard" exhibition and open testing; a public deliberation venue for safety governance and standard setting; and improved external transport and campus slow-mobility connections. Industry scenarios include autonomous model benchmark testing, standards workshops, open safety-governance review and low-carbon compute experiences [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-001] [depth:three_key_area_detailed_design].

**Co-Creation Core · AI Origin Community (approx. 104.3 ha)**: facing the "world-class AI innovation ecosystem", it serves campus incubation, open-source collaboration, technology transfer and a talent special zone. The community at Wudaokou has already gathered ~400 companies with 74% AI agglomeration, ~13,000 AI professionals, and 37 universities, 52 national key laboratories and 106 national research institutes within 1 km [source:DATA-SRC-AI-ORIGIN-COMMUNITY-2026]. Four spatial moves: campus–park–neighborhood slow-mobility stitching and a transfer street that catches the "1-km near-campus innovation circle"; the **Origin Stele (原点碑)** and open-source release hall, making contributor honors a "growing" public memorial; completion of release, talent-service, living and night-collaboration spaces; and station integration with the **Herringbone Plaza (人字广场)** (the Qinglongqiao herringbone motif) hosting cultural ceremonies. Scenarios include open-source community events, releases, talent-special-zone services, campus incubation and AI education experiences [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-002] [standard:PROJECT-AGENT-OPEN-CALL-TASKBOOK].

**Self-Use Core · Dazhongsi (approx. 72.0 ha)**: facing "smart-native new business formats", it serves leading enterprises, agents, smart terminals, content consumption, data factors and international exchange, matching the official positioning of platform-company clusters driving agents, content consumption and smart terminals [source:DATA-SRC-HAIDIAN-THREE-AREAS-WINGS-2026]. Four spatial moves: four-quadrant pedestrian connectivity and station–city integration around Dazhongsi station — served by Lines 13 and 12 (opened 2024-12-15), with the station–city interface carrying rail flow [source:DATA-SRC-JINGZHANG-PARK-2026]; the **Bell Platform (鸣钟台)** — where AI public services "ring in on release", a public announcement and memory node dialoguing with the Great Bell Temple's ancient bell; a smart-native consumption street and data-factor living room; and composite use of planned green space with upgraded public environments around leading enterprises [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-003] [metric:ai_scenario_node_count]. The three cores' positioning, architectural form, retain/renovate/renew direction and public space are conceptual suggestions for professional teams to deepen under official boundaries and regulatory conditions.

## Spatial Prototypes and Typical-Block Deepening

To make the concept directly actionable for professional teams, the proposal gives each core a reusable spatial prototype and takes a typical block in Zhongzhiyuan as a block-level deepening example. The three prototypes share one spatial grammar — "public ground floor, shared platforms, street-facing display" — opening innovation to the public, sharing R&D platforms, and making results visible along the street [depth:three_key_area_detailed_design] [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK].

**Prototype 1 · Self-Research Core "Open Engineering Institute in a Garden" (Zhongzhiyuan)**: public ground floor with an open test yard and full-stack showroom where citizens can book visits to "model–chip–compute–standard" tests and safety-governance reviews; upper floors as shared labs plus enterprise R&D platforms, corridors doubling as display galleries; a "Self-Built Gate" public interface along the main street with results made visible. This organizes the "Qinghe riverfront – full-stack display – public deliberation – campus slow mobility" moves into a vertical order [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-001]. Precedents include Shenzhen Bay Tech Eco-Park's "multi-ground" public ground floor (street-level arcades with display) [source:DATA-SRC-SHENZHEN-BAY-URBAN] and Boston's District Hall ground-floor public innovation building [source:DATA-SRC-DISTRICT-HALL].

**Prototype 2 · Co-Creation Core "Open Source Below, Research Above, Display Along the Street" (AI Origin Community)**: ground floor as an open-source release hall and Origin Stele honor wall open to campus–park–neighborhood along the street; upper floors for campus incubation and collaborative R&D, the vertical circulation doubling as a results flow; a Herringbone Plaza culture ritual tied to the metro station. This lands "campus incubation – open-source collaboration – technology transfer – talent zone" in a vertical order within one compound, echoing the Origin Community's already-formed "AI entrepreneurship ecology in one building" (~400 companies, 74% AI agglomeration) [source:DATA-SRC-AI-ORIGIN-COMMUNITY-2026] [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-002].

**Prototype 3 · Self-Use Core "Four-Quadrant Station–City Interface" (Dazhongsi)**: a station plaza directing rail flow through four-quadrant pedestrian connectivity; a smart-native consumption street along the frontage with a data-factor living room and international roadshow hall inside; the Bell Platform hosting the "ring in on release" ritual for AI public services. This organizes "station–city integration – smart consumption – data factors – international exchange" around the rail station [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-003] [metric:ai_scenario_node_count].

**Typical-block deepening · Zhongzhiyuan Open Engineering Institute ~1 km² example**: for a ~1 km² typical block inside the Self-Research Core, the proposal gives conceptual functional zoning and ground-floor uses: autonomous model benchmarking and compute experience in the north, full-stack display and standards workshops in the middle, enterprise R&D and talent services in the south; public interfaces unfold along the Qinghe and the Self-Built Spine, with continuous slow mobility, public ground floors and shared platforms inside; operationally, the Self-Built Covenant's three certificates (Build / Operations / Exit) act as the public gate for entry and exit [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-001] [depth:renewal_project_list]. The example is conceptual; parcel boundaries, ownership and regulatory conditions await official data [source:OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT].

## AI Innovation Ecosystem, Personas, and AI+ Scenarios

**Self-Built Covenant (governance core)**: every AI entering public space must pass "three selfs and three certificates" — Autonomy (traceable source: disclosed developer, model and data), Responsibility (answerable: a named operator and human-takeover path) and Self-Reflection (reversible: published stop conditions and exit/restore mechanisms); correspondingly, **Build, Operations and Exit certificates** serve as the public interface language of the twelve Self-Built Nodes [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK] [source:DATA-SRC-GENERATIVE-AI-INTERIM-MEASURES]. The covenant echoes the co-creation charter's principles of public-interest-first, final human judgment and human-centered governance; every scenario retains an equivalent non-AI manual path [source:DATA-SRC-BARRIER-FREE-ENVIRONMENT-LAW] [depth:risk_missing_data].

**Five user personas**: open-source developers, startup teams, leading-enterprise visitors, surrounding residents and university faculty and students. ## Transport, Rail, Municipal Infrastructure, and Public Services

Transport is framed by "stitching and connection": the Self-Built Spine slow greenway runs north–south; eight east–west stitching crossings reconnect the divided city; station–city integration and four-quadrant pedestrian connectivity are organized around Dazhongsi and Origin-community metro stations; non-motorized parking and parking supply are completed [source:OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT] [data:geometry/roads.geojson#ROAD-SPINE] [metric:stitch_crossing_count]; stitching alignments are in the road layer [data:geometry/roads.geojson#ROAD-EW-01]. The proposal builds on the completed Jing-Zhang Railway Heritage Park: Phase II opened 2026-08-06, connecting the full 9-km green ribbon end to end as an open, fenceless park with 46 entrances, 8 community activity nodes and a "run/walk/cycle + green" three-way-one-green slow system [source:DATA-SRC-JINGZHANG-PARK-2026]; the Self-Built Spine greenway fills the remaining east–west stitching and north–south continuity gaps on this reality. On rail, Dazhongsi is served by Lines 13 and 12 (opened 2024-12), Zhichunlu is a Line 10/13 interchange, and Wudaokou is a Line 13 station, forming multi-point rail connections along the spine [source:DATA-SRC-JINGZHANG-PARK-2026]. Road and slow-mobility layers stay inside the submitted boundary and are cross-checked against public space, green space and industrial nodes; key crossings over expressways and rail are conceptual alignments requiring professional transport review [depth:traffic_rail_slow_parking].

Municipal and new infrastructure cover AI industry service platforms, innovation public services, distributed energy, edge compute and conventional utility integration under a five-element framework of "facility standard, spatial layout, service radius, operation model and phasing logic" [source:SITE-PACKAGE] [depth:municipal_new_infrastructure]. Pipes, energy, drainage, flood control and fire-fighting data are absent and are all listed as prerequisites for formal deepening — no engineering conclusions are fabricated [depth:risk_missing_data].

## Blue-Green Network, Public Space, and Urban Character

The blue-green system uses the Self-Built Spine as its backbone, linking the Qinghe and Xiaoyue River riparian belts with the three cores' campus greens into a continuous "one spine, two rivers, three cores, multiple nodes" network [data:geometry/green_space.geojson#GREEN-001] [data:geometry/green_space.geojson#GREEN-010] [metric:green_space_area_sqm]. Twelve Self-Built Nodes are distributed along the spine as AI scenario public nodes, offering stop-over, experiential and human-takeover public interfaces [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#PUBLIC-01] [metric:public_space_ratio].

Urban character fuses three cultures: **railway culture as structure** (rails, sleepers, signals and mileposts translated into signage and paving language, preserving industrial memory), **Zhongguancun culture as method** (openness, iteration and hacker spirit entering the organization of public activities), and **AI new culture as contract** (technology entering the city must be explainable, auditable and reversible) [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK] [depth:blue_green_public_space]. Railway culture is anchored by the old Qinghuayuan Station (plaque inscribed by Zhan Tianyou; Beijing municipal heritage-protection unit; opened 2023-03-25) and Phase I of the Jing-Zhang Railway Heritage Park (opened 2023-06-29), with 20+ heritage elements already in public space including original rails, a green passenger carriage and the herringbone motif [source:DATA-SRC-JINGZHANG-PARK-PHASE1-2023]. Character controls distinguish official control, design suggestion and pending confirmation; heritage protection ranges and construction-control zones await official GIS data [source:SOURCE-REGISTRY] [depth:risk_missing_data].

## Self-Built Covenant Public Component Library and AI-Native Interface

The Self-Built Covenant (Autonomy · Responsibility · Self-Reflection) is not a slogan. The proposal turns the three certificates into standard public-space components at the twelve Self-Built Nodes, making every AI entering the city visible, question-able and stoppable in physical space [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK] [depth:blue_green_public_space]. The component library is grounded in real regulations and deployments, not label-pasted concepts.

**Build Certificate · Information Post**: a physical-digital information post disclosing developer, model version, data sources, update date and the human-review entry; located at each node entrance with a unified visual language, QR access to full records, and no personal behavioural tracking. It responds to the Labeling Measures for AI-Generated/Synthetic Content (effective 2025-09-01) on explicit and implicit labelling [source:DATA-SRC-AI-CONTENT-LABEL-2025], EU AI Act Article 50 transparency duties (effective 2026-08-02) [source:DATA-SRC-EU-AI-ACT], and Washington DC's deployed DTPR digital-trust signage precedent [source:DATA-SRC-DTPR-DC].

**Operations Certificate · Status Light**: a three-colour status language — green = running normally, yellow = in test / attention needed, red = requires human review or stopped; uniformly coded with public facilities and traffic signals so an AI's operating state is publicly legible. The semantics borrow the urban-rail "green = smooth, yellow = congested, red = interrupted" three-colour precedent (Shanghai Metro's three-colour map, in service since 2009) [source:DATA-SRC-SHANGHAI-THREECOLOR], turning AI status from invisible to a legible public interface.

**Exit Certificate · Human Takeover Station**: each node provides a human takeover station and an equivalent non-AI path (paper, manual, on-site service), with published stop conditions and exit/restore procedures, and one-touch transfer to human in emergencies. It implements Article 39 of the Barrier-Free Environment Construction Law requiring medical, social-security, financial and utility-payment service venues to keep on-site guidance and manual processing [source:DATA-SRC-BARRIER-FREE-ENVIRONMENT-LAW], and Article 24(3) of the Personal Information Protection Law on the right to explanation of and refusal of automated decisions [source:DATA-SRC-PIPL-ART24].

**Components and space**: the component library acts as a unified "public interface layer" across the twelve nodes and the three cores' public space, co-designed with signage, paving and lighting; the three certificates' records feed a public data ledger (conceptual; operations details await professional teams) [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#PUBLIC-01] [depth:blue_green_public_space]. The status-light semantics are a design proposal — no city has yet deployed a public AI status light — and formal implementation requires professional standards deepening.

## Metrics, Area Recalculation, and Compliance Matrix
