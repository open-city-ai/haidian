05Mobility · blue-green public space · character (交通慢行 · 蓝绿公共空间)

Longitudinal continuity is this corridor's greatest asset; lateral severance is its greatest weakness. The railway cut the two sides apart; the heritage park reconnected the length but not the width.

One spine, two wings

The heritage-park slow-mobility spine runs the full length; two diagonal wings leave the Origin Community for the south-west (Zhongguancun services) and north-east (Xiaoyue River scenarios). The spine changes width by rhythm: plazas at the key areas, tree-lined paths between them.

Six lateral stitches

One shared hard requirement: no level breaks, fully accessible, continuous night lighting, supervised sightlines. Continuous accessibility follows the Barrier-Free Environment Construction Law — it is not a design aspiration.

Blue-green and character

A green ratio of 25.5% is a deliberately high choice: the spine is the identity and a necessary support for intense cognitive work. Massing steps low along the spine and higher outward, with a continuous public ground floor and the roofscape under design control.
