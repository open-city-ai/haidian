# RenLine Narrative / 方案叙事摘要

This narrative is a derived, summary-level read of the RenLine (人字带) proposal. It is not a substitute for `proposal.md` (zh) or `proposal.en.md`. For machine-audit details, see `metrics.json`, `compliance_matrix.json`, `standard_matrix.json`, `design_depth_matrix.json`, `sources.json`, `assumptions.json`, and the nine GeoJSON layers.

## Master concept / 主概念

Jing-Zhang RenLine Belt — one autonomous-innovation stroke, one industry-transformation stroke, one Jing-Zhang rail timeline; three zones, two wings, one AI Origin. Inspired by Zhan Tianyou's Ren-shaped switchback at Qinglongqiao in 1905, the proposal reads the century-long arc from autonomous railway building to autonomous AI as a single spatial narrative.

## Spatial structure / 空间结构

The Overall Design Area (about 11.4 km², provisional) reads as a south-north elongated corridor: the rail timeline runs north-south as the Jing-Zhang Heritage Park vitality belt; the three zones are stacked north-to-south; the two wings (Zhongguancun Technology Services Wing in the west, Xiaoyue River Scenario Enablement Wing in the east) extend the corridor outward.

## Key areas / 重点区域

- **Zhongzhiyuan AI Independent Innovation Acceleration Area** (about 192.1 ha, provisional): garden-style full-stack independent-innovation district; Qing River innovation front, AI autonomy, standards, and safety governance.
- **Beijing AI Origin Community** (about 104.3 ha, provisional): near-campus commercialization and talent community; open-source collaboration, showcase and release, talent-zone services.
- **Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster** (about 72.0 ha, provisional): urban intelligent economy and international exchange; four-quadrant pedestrian connectivity around Dazhongsi Station, intelligent-economy clusters, composite green uses.

## Land use / 用地

24 grid cells provide complete coverage without gaps or overlaps; the largest code is 0802 research (about 29.7%) followed by 0701 residential (about 24.3%) and 0702 community service (about 17.0%). The conceptual industry-space supply (research + commercial) totals about 4.10 million m² (about 35.9%).

## AI scenarios and personas / AI 场景与画像

10 AI scenario cards (3 validation scenarios) are distributed across the three zones, the rail belt, and the two wings. Five user personas (R&D engineer, startup developer, university researcher, local resident/senior, international visitor) drive the spatial configuration.

## Operations and brand / 运营与品牌

A three-party operation alliance (government guidance + market operating company + developer/community organizations) implements diversified revenue streams (industry leasing, scenario access, event sponsorship, public-space franchising, standards services, international exchange) and a long-term event system (Global AI Week, Open-Source Developer Conference, Jing-Zhang Culture Season, Talent Summit).

## Phasing / 分期

Near (0–3 years): Origin Community low-disturbance renewal demonstration, south-belt slow-mobility stitching, three validation-scenario pilots, naming and visual system rollout. Mid (3–6 years): Zhongzhiyuan garden district and Qing River front, Dazhongsi station integration and four-quadrant connectivity, two-wing corridors. Long (6–10 years): Dazhongsi intelligent-economy deepening, belt-wide quality uplift, global AI event system normalized.

## Risk and compliance / 风险与合规

Pending official data (boundary polygons, regulatory conditions, existing buildings, ownership, municipal baselines, heritage ranges) are explicitly listed in `assumptions.json`. All spatial recommendations are phrased as conceptual recommendations, reference schemes, or material for professional teams to deepen.
