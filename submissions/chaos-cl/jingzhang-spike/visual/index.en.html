AI Scenarios (12 cards)

Scenarios are not slogans: each card states the spatial carrier, users, type, and privacy/review boundary; 4 are testing & validation scenarios (TVS).

12 scenario cards · 4 TVS
SC-01 Open-source publishing hall

Origin community: publishing, code-contribution display, and small-scale pitching for developers and universities.

SC-02 Indigenous model test loop (TVS)

Zhongzhiyuan: closed-loop training/evaluation; test data desensitized; independent safety review.

SC-03 Safety-governance sandbox (TVS)

Zhongzhiyuan: standard setting, model red-team testing, controlled and visitable environment.

SC-04 Urban agent showroom

Origin community: explainable, human-reviewed agent public services driven by public data.

SC-05 Dazhongsi four-quadrant pedestrian perception (TVS)

Dazhongsi Station: anonymized pedestrian data; improved walking connectivity and static traffic.

SC-06 International pitching lounge

Dazhongsi: showcase, negotiation, and international exchange for agent/terminal/content firms.

SC-07 Green-corridor AI guide

Along the corridor: explainable wayfinding and guide using public cultural materials only.

SC-08 Edge-compute station

Nodes across the area: compute quota management, low-carbon energy mix, compliant log retention.

SC-09 Accessible AI public services

Communities/stations: on-site human fallback per Art. 39 of the Barrier-Free Environment Law.

SC-10 Low-speed delivery test corridor (TVS)

Xiaoyue River Wing: right-of-way application, safety officers, separated from pedestrian flows.

SC-11 AI education open classroom

Origin community/corridor: AI literacy and hands-on classes for students and the public.

SC-12 Data-factor parlor

Dazhongsi: data authorization and audit trail; compliant data-factor circulation display.
