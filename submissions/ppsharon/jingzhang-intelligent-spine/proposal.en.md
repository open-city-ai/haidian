# Jing-Zhang Intelligent Spine: From Steam Backbone to Neural Network

## Abstract

This proposal reimagines the century-old Jing-Zhang Railway corridor in Beijing's Haidian District as a world-class AI innovation ecosystem. Building on the railway's legacy as China's first domestically designed rail line, the "Intelligent Spine" concept positions this linear industrial heritage as the central nervous system of an AI-native urban district. The proposal structures the 11.4 km² site as "One Spine, Three Cores, Two Corridors" — connecting Tsinghua University, Peking University, and CAS research institutes with industry, capital, and global innovation networks.

**Keywords**: AI innovation district, urban renewal, industrial heritage, Jing-Zhang Railway, Haidian District, embodied intelligence, open-source ecosystem

## 6. Key Area Designs

### 6.1 Zhongzhi Park — AI Autonomous Innovation Accelerator (192.1 ha)

**Positioning**: Garden-type full-stack autonomous innovation district
**Node Deep-dive: Security Governance Sandbox**
- Location: Core of Zhongzhi Park, ~2.3 ha, repurposed from low-efficiency industrial/warehouse land
- Layout: G-floor public exhibition (500 m²) + L2 standard-setting workshop (400 m²) + L3 red-team testing lab (600 m², physically isolated) + rooftop viewing platform (400 m²) + B1 equipment room (200 m²)
- Operation: Led by CNCERT/MIIT testing institutions; daily operations by third-party lab company
- Pricing: ¥5,000-20,000/day (enterprise testing)
- KPIs: ≥100 models evaluated annually; ≥10 high-risk vulnerabilities discovered/year

### 6.2 Origin Community — Campus-adjacent Tech Transfer & Talent Community (104.3 ha)

**Positioning**: School-adjacent成果转化 and talent community
**Node Deep-dive: Open Source Launch Hall**
- Location: Core of Origin Community, 3-min walk from Wudaokou Metro Station, ~1.8 ha
- Layout: Main launch hall (800 m², 8m ceiling) + code display gallery (400 m²) + pitch rooms (200 m² × 3) + AI Origin Coffee (300 m²) + rooftop terrace (500 m²) + underground maker workshop (400 m²)
- Operation: Led by Haidian Science & Technology Commission + Open Source China; daily operations by third-party venue operator
- KPIs: ≥24 launch events/year; ≥5,000 developer participations/year; ≥20 projects incubated/year

### 6.3 Dazhongsi — Smart Economy & International Exchange District (72.0 ha)

**Positioning**: Urban-type intelligent economy and international exchange district
**Node Deep-dive: International Roadshow Lounge**
- Location: Core of Dazhongsi Metro Station four-quadrant pedestrian circle, ~2.5 ha
- Layout: International launch hall (1,000 m², 12m ceiling) + enterprise exhibition (600 m²) + business negotiation pods (200 m² × 6) + media center (300 m²) + cultural deck (rooftop, 800 m²) + B1 commercial connection (1,500 m²)
- Operation: Led by Haidian Investment Promotion Center + Zhongguancun Entrepreneurship Street; daily operations by international exhibition company
- KPIs: ≥48 roadshows/year; ≥500M RMB financing facilitated/year; ≥20% international participants

## 9. Public Interest & Inclusivity

**Equity Principles**:
- AI+medical/education/legal services with fair access; all AI diagnostic recommendations labeled "AI-generated, for reference only"
- Public spaces free of charge; AI scenario public zones free or low-cost
- Community-embedded AI service points in old residential areas, not just high-end offices

**Digital Divide Mitigation**:
- Non-digital alternatives: all AI scenarios retain human service windows
- Public information provided in paper, large-print, and voice versions
- "Digital tutors" at each AI service point for elderly users

**Accessibility**:
- Heritage Park trails: tactile guide maps, voice navigation, wheelchair ramps
- AI scenario buildings: automatic doors, ≥1.8m corridors, braille elevators
- Public displays: audio commentary, real-time captions, sign language windows

**Community Engagement**:
- Community planner system + quarterly resident assemblies + online co-creation platform
- Community development fund: 5-10% of commercial revenue reinvested in neighborhood micro-renewal
