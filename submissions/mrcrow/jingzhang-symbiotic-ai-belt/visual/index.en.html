CENTENNIAL JING-ZHANG AI INNOVATION BELT

Jing-Zhang Symbiotic Innovation Belt

AI development must make life greener, more comfortable, liveable, and resilient; existing residents and talent matter equally. The Jing-Zhang green public spine is an open but controlled public interface, while the three key areas support one accountable innovation chain through differentiated roles.

B0 is the overall mapping base, while the registered detailed map provides local existing-condition evidence. 01 Problem, Overall Plan, Four Connections

The task is not to build another isolated AI park. It is to make dispersed universities, firms, communities, transit, and green spaces accessible, collaborative, and responsive through one spine, three cores, and a green foundation. Jing-Zhang is not a channel every project must pass through.

Spatial connection

Walking, crossings, accessibility, and rail interchange.

Innovation-chain connection

Lectures, briefs, prototyping, validation, and industry services.

Daily-service connection

Residents, students, families, older people, children, and night workers.

Resilience-resource connection

Shelter, supplies, people, and professional emergency interfaces.

02 Physical Construction Actions and Status Categories

Maintain and complete green mobility, accessibility, and basic services first; then adapt reusable space and sharing interfaces. New construction is conditional on boundary, ownership, land-use, and demand evidence.

Retain and repair

Continuous green mobility, shade and drainage, ecology, and basic daily services.

Renew and adapt

Existing space, public interfaces, time sharing, and professional interfaces.

Conditional new build

Only after regulatory controls, operational accountability, and demand evidence are in place.

03 Three Key Areas: Differentiated Spatial Prototypes

The three cores are not three generic AI parks. Their official textual areas and roles are fixed; provisional shapes are not official boundaries.

Zhongzhiyuan

192.1 ha | Innovation Incubation Core

Low-carbon R&D, shared prototyping, professional workshops, and controlled testing. Companies and incubated teams continuously offer students frontier lectures, real projects, testing opportunities, and first-hand feedback.

Beijing AI Origin Community

104.3 ha | Talent Vitality Core

Campus-city learning interfaces, all-age public living rooms, and time-shared services; differentiated support during extreme weather.

Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster

72.0 ha | Industry Clustering Core

Product adoption, industrialization, and enterprise services. Controlled night logistics uses back-of-house nodes without displacing quiet life or basic movement.

04 Four Scenarios, T01–T03, Human Accountability

Intelligent mobility is not synonymous with cars. Walking, cycling, accessibility, green space, rail, and ordinary transit come first. Accountable people decide high-risk actions, takeover, stopping, and reopening.

Education and incubation

Zhongzhiyuan, companies, and incubated teams offer students frontier lectures, real projects, testing, and first-hand feedback.

All-age daily life

Public living rooms, rest and shade, water, toilets, care, first aid, charging, parking, and human assistance.

Intelligent mobility

T01 service robots, T02 low-speed shared short shuttles, and T03 last-mile delivery each require controlled space, an operator, and human takeover.

Extreme-weather resilience

Shelter, water, supplies, fallback routes, and staffed service points support differentiated needs first.

05 Phasing, Governance, R1–R7, Evidence Boundaries

Near term: reversible low-risk repairs. Medium term: adapt existing space and controlled validation. Long term: decide construction with official boundaries, regulatory controls, and operational evaluation. R1–R7 retain their official meanings and map to accountability, evidence, and review.

Resources and status

Inventories and verification for spaces, supplies, facilities, people, and mobility capacity.

Needs and matching

Services organized by people, time, scenario, and actual difficulty.

Human accountability

High-risk tasks retain human review, takeover, stop, and non-AI fallback.

Evidence boundary
