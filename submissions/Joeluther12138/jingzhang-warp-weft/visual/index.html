假设

A-CONTROLS-001 pending_professional_confirmation

Detailed regulatory controls, road redlines, ownership, municipal utilities, and engineering constraints must be confirmed from official attachments before statutory use.

The package can be reviewed as a formal AI submission only for provided official boundaries and submitted design layers; statutory controls still need professional confirmation.

A-KEYAREA-PROV-001 pending_professional_confirmation

The three key detailed-design areas (zhongzhiyuan_ai_acceleration_area, beijing_ai_origin_community, dazhongsi_ai_industry_cluster) are drawn as provisional constraint envelopes announced publicly; official redlines and precise areas are not included in the public site package.

Key-area geometry is used only to structure design intensity and narrative focus; it must not be read as an official redline or precise-area basis.

A-FAR-MISSING-001 pending_professional_confirmation

Approved FAR / floor-area controls are absent from the public package, so total floor area and floor_area_ratio are reported as unknown in metrics.json.

No FAR-dependent claim is asserted anywhere in the submission; building floors are suggestions only.

A-BLDG-SUGGEST-001 pending_professional_confirmation

All 21 building footprints, their suggested floor counts, and renovation/retain/new actions are AI-generated massing suggestions with low confidence.

Building layer supports scenario storytelling only and must be replaced by survey-based footprints before any engineering use.

A-PHASE-YEARS-001 pending_professional_confirmation

Phase years 2026-2028 / 2028-2032 / 2032-2045 and the south-to-north stitching order are indicative planning assumptions, not committed schedules.

Phasing polygons quantify proposal areas per stage; timing claims should be re-validated against official implementation plans.

A-ROAD-CL-001 pending_professional_confirmation

Road centerlines are indicative alignment proposals clipped to the site boundary; geodesic lengths are declared but lane counts and cross-sections are not designed.

Roads layer is for network-structure review only; statutory road redlines remain under official control (see A-CONTROLS-001).
