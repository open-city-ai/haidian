# Formal Narrative

This narrative is derived from the structured AI package. Geometry, metrics, compliance matrix, drawings, and visual/index.html remain cross-checked deliverables.

The submission develops the "Jing-Zhang Smart Vein" (京张智脉) concept: a three-zone structure (Centennial Jing-Zhang Culture Belt, Urban AI Living Experience Belt, AI-Integrated Innovation Belt) around the 43.6 km2 study area, an 11.4 km2 overall design area, and three detailed key areas. The master plan covers land use (190 parcels), buildings (56 blocks), mobility (9 road segments including a greenway), green space (12 features), public space (7 plazas), phasing (4 phases), and 15 scenario/constraint features. All geometry is topology-safe and reproducible; metrics are computed from submitted layers and recorded in metrics.json.

Key design positions: an AI industry ecosystem of R&D, open-source, release, services, talent housing, and international exchange; a blue-green spine linking Tsinghua Garden Station heritage and the greenway; human-scale public space at 7 nodes; and 3-stage implementation (Origin Community -> Zhizhi Acceleration Area -> Dazhongsi Cluster). Scenario intelligence uses 12 scenario cards, 3 test scenarios, and 3 AI pilgrimage landmarks with SCN-001..015 and AIZ-001..003 evidence.

The official boundary and key-area polygons come from the provisional attachment (password-protected download, not independently verifiable) and are declared as provisional constraints; all other missing data enters assumptions.json and the risk chapter.
