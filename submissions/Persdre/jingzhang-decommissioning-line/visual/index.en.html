Overview map（总览地图）— one line, three stations, two wings

Legend: parkR&Dcommercialresidentialeducationreservedplaza

Three scopes: the ~43.6 km² research area answers institutional design; the ~11.41 km² overall design area carries the spatial structure (land-use partition of 36 gap-free parcels, 12 concept buildings, a 9.7 km slow-mobility spine with 8 sutures, a 12.1% green-ratio floor); the ~368.4 ha key areas receive the three stations' detailed design.

The covenant: every AI facility entering public space registers five fields on installation day — install date, decommission date, removal custodian, deposit status, restoration standard. Default term 3 years; extension requires re-certification.

Renewal projects: eight projects (DL-01…DL-08) in three phases following the register → re-certify → decommission rhythm; phase 1 starts with the belt-wide legacy inventory.

"Dare to take down — that earns the right to install."
