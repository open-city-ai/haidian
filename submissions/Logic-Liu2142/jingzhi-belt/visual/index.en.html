Overview Map and Overall Concept

The Jingzhi Belt (Belt of Intelligence) takes the 1909 “zigzag” switchback alignment of the Jingzhang Railway as its spiritual origin and reconstructs it as the AI-era “zigzag loop” — university ideation, open-source collaboration, enterprise translation, scenario validation, and governance feedback. Spatially it becomes “one belt, three cores, two wings, multiple nodes, and a blue-green loop.”
