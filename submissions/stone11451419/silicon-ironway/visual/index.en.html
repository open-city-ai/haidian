SILICON IRONWAY · 京张智轨

ONE TRACK · THREE STATIONS · TWO WINGS

In 1909, the Chinese built the Jing-Zhang Railway on their own; today, this track is to become the railway of China's self-reliant AI innovation. The Ironway turns the railway spirit — self-reliance, discipline, punctuality, stoppability — into the public protocol of an AI innovation belt, letting everyone see, question, and stop AI in the city.
