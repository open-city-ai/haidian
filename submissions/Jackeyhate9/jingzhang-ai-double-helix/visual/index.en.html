总览地图

Weaving the centennial railway heritage strand with the AI innovation strand as a double helix along the heritage park spine.

三层范围

Three nested scopes: coordinated research area (~43.6 km2), overall design area (~11.4 km2), and key detailed-design area (~368.4 ha).

Synergy organizes as "two intra-belt wings × three extra-belt axes": northbound with Shangdi/Xierqi via the G6 axis, southeast through the Xueyuan Road university corridor, and westward across the Xiaoyuehe with Huilongguan/Yongfeng for jobs-housing balance.

重点区域

Three key areas north-to-south; two wings for tech services and scenario empowerment.

用地分区

Central heritage-park spine (park green); research and tech services to the west; life services and scenarios to the east.

交通慢行

North-south greenway along the park, cross secondary roads linking the three areas, and an eastern arterial with transit connections.

蓝绿公共空间

A continuous green spine plus three AI public plazas form a walkable blue-green public-space network.

Urban character uses the "railway heritage × intelligent future" motif with an ochre-red × tech-blue dual-tone base weighted differently across heritage, innovation and living stretches.

建筑

AI R&D, talent apartments, and AI-native offices concentrate within the three key areas; retain/renovate/demolish to be refined once official conditions arrive.

更新项目

Phasing: near-term Zhongzhiyuan, mid-term Origin Community, long-term Dazhongsi.

AI 场景

At least 10 AI scenario cards, 3 industry test-validation scenarios, and 5 user personas with privacy and human-review boundaries.

Ecosystem elements form a three-ring "innovation chain × spatial chain × operation chain"; scenarios are organized in validation, experience and service tiers, with accessibility and aging inclusion as a cross-cutting persona across all public scenarios.
