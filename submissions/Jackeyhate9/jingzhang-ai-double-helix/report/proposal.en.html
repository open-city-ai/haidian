Jing-Zhang Intelligence Vein · Double Helix: An AI Innovation Double-Helix on the Centennial Railway

Using the Jing-Zhang Heritage Park as a central green spine, this proposal weaves the centennial railway heritage strand and the AI innovation strand into a double helix, with three areas and two wings growing in synergy, a five-tier function ladder, bidirectional Xiaoyuehe scenario enablement and Zhongguancun service enablement, and inclusive accessible social services into a world-class AI industry highland and pilgrimage destination.

Three-Level Scope Framework

The proposal follows the three announced scope levels: the coordinated research area (about 43.6 km²) addresses the AI industry ecosystem, strategic positioning and future-city form; the overall design area (about 11.4 km² around the Jing-Zhang Heritage Park) must reach regulatory-plan-level urban design depth; the key detailed-design area (about 368.4 ha) must reach comprehensive-implementation-plan depth Spatial data Standard.

The overall spatial structure is "one spine, three nodes, three areas and two wings, a symbiotic double helix": the Jing-Zhang Heritage Park is the north-south cultural-green spine (one strand), the AI innovation corridor is the other strand, and the two intertwine at the three key areas (base-pair nodes), while the Zhongguancun tech-service wing and the Xiaoyuehe scenario wing extend east and west Spatial data Depth.

The three levels are not separate drawing sets: coordinated research decides the industry chain and city form, overall design turns them into renewal projects and facility capacity, and key-area design verifies the implementability of specific parcels, buildings, transport and AI scenarios Depth. The current structure is a conceptual suggestion for professional teams to deepen.

The three levels are presented consistently in assets/figures/site-overview.png (evidence chain) and assets/figures/land-use-structure.png (spatial structure); scope, area and key-area count are all recalculated from the submitted geometry, and text and drawings use the same figures.

Detailed Design of Key Areas

The three key areas are the three base-pair nodes of the double helix, each reaching comprehensive-implementation-plan depth Depth.

Zhongzhiyuan AI Autonomous Innovation Acceleration Area (ZhiMai Hub) is a garden-style full-stack autonomous innovation district carrying the full-stack system and global AI-governance voice. Spatial moves include reinforcing the Qinghe riverfront, building a low-carbon innovation exchange loop and an industry showcase gallery, with green space hosting autonomous-model testing and standards-governance display Spatial data. Supporting actions include industrial test-and-validation grounds (red team, edge compute stations) and an informal R&D-testing circulation.

Beijing AI Origin Community (ZhiMai Origin) is a near-campus outcome-transformation and talent community carrying a world-class ecosystem. Spatial moves stitch campus, park and district by slow traffic, add outcome publishing, talent services, open-source collaboration and living space, and set the "Origin Code Monument" as an honor-display node Spatial data.

Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster (ZhiMai Frontier) is an urban intelligent-economy and international-exchange district carrying intelligence-native formats. Spatial moves organize four-quadrant pedestrian connectivity around Dazhongsi station, renew the public environment around key enterprises, and cultivate agent, intelligent-terminal, content-consumption and data-element scenarios Spatial data.

The three nodes are presented side by side in assets/figures/key-areas.png as a "role — spatial moves — AI scenario" three-part structure: each key area matches at least one industrial test ground, one public experience space and one accessibility service point, so every node can be tested, toured and used.

The three areas do not duplicate each other: Zhongzhiyuan favors testing and standards, Origin Community favors provisioning and transformation, Dazhongsi favors exchange and consumption; the horizontal Qinghe innovation interface and the vertical heritage-park slow axis interlock them into the three complete nodes of the double helix.

Land Use, Building Scale, and Retain-Renovate-Demolish Strategy

Land use follows the national land-use sea-use classification guide, forming complete closed seamless land-use zones Standard. The central green spine is park green (1401), the west is research and tech-service land (0802), the east is living-service and scenario land (0904), and the key areas place business-and-finance (0902) and retail-and-commerce (0901), forming a land structure echoing the double helix Spatial data.

Buildings are dominated by AI R&D, talent apartments and intelligence-native offices, concentrated in the three key areas Spatial data. Building footprint area is recalculated from geometry Metric. Retain-renovate-demolish is presented as a "method + to-be-calibrated list": retain cultural and historical resources along the heritage park, renovate underused industry and ground-floor formats, and locate new R&D, apartments and public facilities in the three key areas; parcel-level conclusions await official tenure, regulatory plan and engineering conditions Depth Depth.

Parcel-level decisions follow three priorities: cultural value first (site entities, old station buildings and centennial track fragments are always retained), interface first (the park frontage and station-district frontage are renovated first), and flexibility first (only renewal typologies and magnitude ranges are proposed; whether and how any parcel changes is decided by formal procedures after rights clearance and as-built surveys).

Transport, Rail, Municipal Infrastructure, and Public Services

The transport plan centers on rail-station integration, road micro-circulation and slow-traffic gap stitching: a north-south slow greenway along the heritage park, horizontal secondary roads linking the three areas, and an eastern urban trunk with rail feeder achieving east-west stitching and north-south through-routing Spatial data Depth.

The slow system is organized in three grades — heritage axis, scenario lane and feeder segment: the heritage axis serves all ages and abilities with a continuous accessible profile, the scenario lane hosts AI living services and restable spaces, and feeder segments seamlessly connect rail stations with the slow main axis, making public space and public transit visible first Spatial data Spatial data.

Municipal and new infrastructure propose distributed energy, edge compute and smart municipal integration serving the AI industry and talent life Depth. Where road redlines, pipelines, fire protection and municipal conditions are absent, they are always written as pending formal data rather than approved conditions Spatial data.

Public service facilities are laid out in three grades: belt-level exchange facilities (international roadshow, AI culture hall) at Dazhongsi and Origin Community; district-level services (sports, culture, community medical) along the Xiaoyuehe and Qinghe interfaces; and block-level livelihood services (childcare, elderly-care stations, accessibility service points) ensuring walk-shed coverage. Elderly-care stations and accessibility points follow the smart-health-elderly principle of running traditional channels in parallel: online booking and offline human service are accepted concurrently Source.
