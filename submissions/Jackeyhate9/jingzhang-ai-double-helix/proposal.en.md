# Jing-Zhang Intelligence Vein · Double Helix: An AI Innovation Double-Helix on the Centennial Railway

## Design Basis and Source List

This proposal takes the prequalification announcement for the "Centennial Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt Urban Design International Solicitation" issued by the Haidian Branch of the Beijing Municipal Commission of Planning and Natural Resources as its first authority, and the global agent-facing open-call taskbook as its task-response authority [source:OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT] [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK]. Machine-readable inputs come from `brief/site-package/` (design brief, enums, land-use classification, ranges, provisional geometry) and from the source-use registry `data/source_registry.json` [source:SOURCE-REGISTRY].

Because the organizer has not yet published a precise redline with a verifiable coordinate system (the qualification-package download remains password-protected), this proposal uses the repository-maintained provisional rough boundaries derived from the announcement text and area constraints, and keeps a "provisional constraint" label across every layer and metric. It must not be treated as an official redline, an approval basis, or a precise-area basis [source:BOUNDARY-SOURCE] [depth:existing_conditions_diagnosis]. This organizer data gap does not block content scoring; after official polygons arrive, boundaries, land use, buildings, roads, green space, public space, phasing and metrics must be recalculated.

Complete sources, standards, design-depth and task coverage live in `sources.json`, `standard_matrix.json`, `design_depth_matrix.json` and `compliance_matrix.json`; the narrative does not repeat machine indexes [source:SITE-PACKAGE]. Boundary interpretation is traceable to the site layer and area recalculation [data:geometry/site_boundary.geojson#SITE-001] [metric:site_area_sqm], and the three key areas are cross-checked via their own layer [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#KEY-001] [metric:key_area_count].

![Evidence chain and package relationship](assets/figures/site-overview.png)

Three additional legal and policy bases, cleared for formal citation by the registry, support the inclusive, compliance and elderly-smart-health scenario work. China's *Barrier-Free Environment Building Law* (Article 39) requires public venues to keep on-site guidance and human-operated services while providing accessible facilities and information accessibility [source:BARRIER-FREE-ENVIRONMENT-LAW]. The *Interim Measures for the Administration of Generative Artificial Intelligence Services* define safety assessment, labeling and personal-information boundaries for generative AI services [source:GENERATIVE-AI-INTERIM-MEASURES]. The *Action Plan for the Development of the Smart Health and Elderly Care Industry (2021–2025)* requires new smart services to keep traditional channels running in parallel for older users [source:ELDERLY-SMART-TECH-PLAN]. In `data/source_registry.json` these are rated approved, approved and background-only respectively; this proposal uses them only within those scopes and granularities, never expanding the citation.

## Three-Level Scope Framework

The proposal follows the three announced scope levels: the coordinated research area (about 43.6 km²) addresses the AI industry ecosystem, strategic positioning and future-city form; the overall design area (about 11.4 km² around the Jing-Zhang Heritage Park) must reach regulatory-plan-level urban design depth; the key detailed-design area (about 368.4 ha) must reach comprehensive-implementation-plan depth [data:geometry/site_boundary.geojson#SITE-001] [standard:PROJECT-OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT].

The overall spatial structure is "one spine, three nodes, three areas and two wings, a symbiotic double helix": the Jing-Zhang Heritage Park is the north-south cultural-green spine (one strand), the AI innovation corridor is the other strand, and the two intertwine at the three key areas (base-pair nodes), while the Zhongguancun tech-service wing and the Xiaoyuehe scenario wing extend east and west [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-001] [depth:overall_spatial_structure].

The three levels are not separate drawing sets: coordinated research decides the industry chain and city form, overall design turns them into renewal projects and facility capacity, and key-area design verifies the implementability of specific parcels, buildings, transport and AI scenarios [depth:three_level_scope_framework]. The current structure is a conceptual suggestion for professional teams to deepen.

The three levels are presented consistently in `assets/figures/site-overview.png` (evidence chain) and `assets/figures/land-use-structure.png` (spatial structure); scope, area and key-area count are all recalculated from the submitted geometry, and text and drawings use the same figures.

## Coordinated Research Area: Industry and Future City Research

The core of the coordinated research area is building a world-class AI innovation ecosystem. The proposal translates the three positionings into spatial language: the Centennial Jing-Zhang Culture Belt is a cultural gene strand, the AI Convergence Innovation Belt is an innovation gene strand, and the Urban AI Life Experience Belt is the cross-link binding them into one "double helix" [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK] [standard:PROJECT-AGENT-OPEN-CALL-TASKBOOK].

**Naming system and logo direction (agent.1)**: the belt's primary name is "京张智脉" (Jing-Zhang Intelligence Vein, "ZhiMai"), fusing the Jing-Zhang railway's herringbone switchback with the DNA double-helix. The double helix is itself the methodological motif of the layered naming system: the top layer is the belt name (ZhiMai), the middle layer is the five-tier functional ladder, and the bottom layer is the three areas, two wings and landmark nodes. The logo motif is a "double-helix rail": two interweaving rising track curves — one ochre-red for centennial railway heritage, one tech-blue for AI innovation — winding into a DNA double helix whose nodes are the three key areas; the "rail → helix → code grid" unit extends into wayfinding, wordmarks, international communication icons and exhibition identity. The visual system uses ochre-red × tech-blue as the dual-tone base, with gold reserved for pilgrimage landmark nodes only, extendable to wayfinding, event identity and international communication.

**Five-tier function ladder and four-way synergy (agent.1)**: functions are organized as "source (provisioning) — R&D — transformation — living — co-building" instead of flat single functions:

| Tier | Spatial carrier | Core content | Synergy path |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| L1 Provisioning | University communities and Origin Community | Original innovation, open-source protocols, talent | Talent–outcome loop with Haidian's university clusters |
| L2 R&D | Zhongzhiyuan full-stack autonomous innovation district | Autonomous models, full toolchains, red-team testing | Gradient intake of R&D overflow from Shangdi software park |

| L5 Co-building | Dazhongsi international exchange district | International roadshows, data elements, co-built events | External links with Xueyuan Road and the Asia-Olympic exchange resources |

**Explicit mapping of the taskbook's five functions across three positionings and three areas/two wings (agent.1)**: the taskbook's five functions and area/wing roles — full-stack autonomous AI innovation, world-class AI innovation ecosystem, an AI+ scenario-empowerment paradigm, an intelligent AI vital city, global AI governance discourse, and AI-native new business formats — are each anchored to the three areas/two wings and three positionings, so functions and space never diverge:

**Comprehensive-planning innovation and AI urban-governance mechanisms (agent.1/agent.3)**: between regulatory-detail depth and conceptual innovation, the proposal offers three transferable planning methods. First, "spatial-industry double-helix validation": use industry-chain stages (provisioning—R&D—transformation—living—co-building) to derive land and facility allocation, then use land capacity to re-verify that industry stages actually land, avoiding generic collage. Second, "AI-assisted city physical examination": a digital-twin base enables periodic recalculation of slow-traffic gaps, green accessibility, aging-care service coverage and scenario-operation heat, extending AI from "scenario showcase" to "planning-implementation monitoring and governance." Third, a "temporary test-zone regime": before official controls and ownership are supplied, low-intensity temporary facilities validate scenario demand first, forming a "test—feedback—consolidate" incremental renewal loop [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK] [depth:metrics_recalculation] [depth:development_intensity_controls]. Digital-twin and examination data stay within public, de-identified, verifiable boundaries; they collect no personal trajectories, and governance models are never presented as approved management systems [depth:risk_missing_data].

**AI+ scenario system (agent.3, staged forward-reference).** Scenarios are organized in three tiers — validation, experience and service: industrial test-and-validation scenarios put AI under real constraints (autonomous-model red team, open-source sandbox, aging-testing ground); public experience scenarios form check-in urban interfaces for residents and visitors; livelihood service scenarios connect daily community life with accessibility and aging-inclusive baselines. The three tiers belong to different operators and data boundaries, detailed in the next section.

These experiences and organizational relationships are design references for deepening, not statutory planning conclusions.

## Overall Design Area: Urban Renewal and Regulatory-Plan-Level Urban Design

The overall design area must reach regulatory-plan depth, translating industry strategy into land use, buildings, transport, municipal services and urban character. The central green spine (the Jing-Zhang Heritage Park vitality belt, land-use code 1401 park green) forms the skeleton; the west carries the Zhongguancun tech-service wing mainly on research and tech-service land (0802); the east carries the Xiaoyuehe scenario wing mainly on living-service and scenario land (0904); the three key areas place business-and-finance (0902) and retail-and-commerce (0901) per their roles [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-001] [standard:MOHURD-CONTROL-DETAILED-PLANNING].

Building footprints, road centerlines, green space and public space are recalculated from submitted geometry [data:geometry/buildings.geojson#B-001] [data:geometry/roads.geojson#R-001] [metric:building_footprint_area_sqm]. Where official regulatory plans, existing buildings, tenure and engineering conditions are absent, floor-area ratio, building height, intensity and parcel-level retain-renovate-demolish decisions are marked `status=unknown` (pending formal data) rather than dressed up as approved controls [depth:development_intensity_controls] [metric:floor_area_ratio].

The renewal framework follows "retain and renovate in parallel, progressive renewal": first stitch slow-traffic gaps, activate the heritage-park interface and rail-station surroundings, then proceed to functional replacement of underused space [depth:land_use_layout] [depth:retain_renovate_demolish].

Regulatory-plan-level urban design is not only about drawing scale: it also re-appraises district value, taking the rail-station walk shed (500 m conventional advisory distance) as the incremental-potential triangle, with high-density provisioning and networking functions inside and green veins and low-intensity living services outside [data:geometry/constraints.geojson#CONSTRAINTS] [depth:height_massing_character]. Any intensity conclusion is labeled "pending official regulatory plan and tenure", keeping reference advice distinct from approved findings.

This level shares the same provisional boundaries and geometry as the coordinated and key-area levels. See `assets/figures/land-use-structure.png`.

## Detailed Design of Key Areas

The three key areas are the three base-pair nodes of the double helix, each reaching comprehensive-implementation-plan depth [depth:three_key_area_detailed_design].

**Zhongzhiyuan AI Autonomous Innovation Acceleration Area (ZhiMai Hub)** is a garden-style full-stack autonomous innovation district carrying the full-stack system and global AI-governance voice. Spatial moves include reinforcing the Qinghe riverfront, building a low-carbon innovation exchange loop and an industry showcase gallery, with green space hosting autonomous-model testing and standards-governance display [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#KEY-001]. Supporting actions include industrial test-and-validation grounds (red team, edge compute stations) and an informal R&D-testing circulation.

**Beijing AI Origin Community (ZhiMai Origin)** is a near-campus outcome-transformation and talent community carrying a world-class ecosystem. Spatial moves stitch campus, park and district by slow traffic, add outcome publishing, talent services, open-source collaboration and living space, and set the "Origin Code Monument" as an honor-display node [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#KEY-002].

**Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster (ZhiMai Frontier)** is an urban intelligent-economy and international-exchange district carrying intelligence-native formats. Spatial moves organize four-quadrant pedestrian connectivity around Dazhongsi station, renew the public environment around key enterprises, and cultivate agent, intelligent-terminal, content-consumption and data-element scenarios [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#KEY-003].

The three nodes are presented side by side in `assets/figures/key-areas.png` as a "role — spatial moves — AI scenario" three-part structure: each key area matches at least one industrial test ground, one public experience space and one accessibility service point, so every node can be tested, toured and used.

![Key area index and design tasks](assets/figures/key-areas.png)

The three areas do not duplicate each other: Zhongzhiyuan favors testing and standards, Origin Community favors provisioning and transformation, Dazhongsi favors exchange and consumption; the horizontal Qinghe innovation interface and the vertical heritage-park slow axis interlock them into the three complete nodes of the double helix.

## Land Use, Building Scale, and Retain-Renovate-Demolish Strategy

Land use follows the national land-use sea-use classification guide, forming complete closed seamless land-use zones [standard:MNR-LAND-USE-CLASSIFICATION-GUIDE]. The central green spine is park green (1401), the west is research and tech-service land (0802), the east is living-service and scenario land (0904), and the key areas place business-and-finance (0902) and retail-and-commerce (0901), forming a land structure echoing the double helix [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-001].

Buildings are dominated by AI R&D, talent apartments and intelligence-native offices, concentrated in the three key areas [data:geometry/buildings.geojson#B-001]. Building footprint area is recalculated from geometry [metric:building_footprint_area_sqm]. Retain-renovate-demolish is presented as a "method + to-be-calibrated list": retain cultural and historical resources along the heritage park, renovate underused industry and ground-floor formats, and locate new R&D, apartments and public facilities in the three key areas; parcel-level conclusions await official tenure, regulatory plan and engineering conditions [depth:retain_renovate_demolish] [depth:height_massing_character].

Parcel-level decisions follow three priorities: cultural value first (site entities, old station buildings and centennial track fragments are always retained), interface first (the park frontage and station-district frontage are renovated first), and flexibility first (only renewal typologies and magnitude ranges are proposed; whether and how any parcel changes is decided by formal procedures after rights clearance and as-built surveys).

## Transport, Rail, Municipal Infrastructure, and Public Services

The transport plan centers on rail-station integration, road micro-circulation and slow-traffic gap stitching: a north-south slow greenway along the heritage park, horizontal secondary roads linking the three areas, and an eastern urban trunk with rail feeder achieving east-west stitching and north-south through-routing [data:geometry/roads.geojson#R-001] [depth:traffic_rail_slow_parking].

The slow system is organized in three grades — heritage axis, scenario lane and feeder segment: the heritage axis serves all ages and abilities with a continuous accessible profile, the scenario lane hosts AI living services and restable spaces, and feeder segments seamlessly connect rail stations with the slow main axis, making public space and public transit visible first [data:geometry/green_space.geojson#GS-001] [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#PS-001].

Municipal and new infrastructure propose distributed energy, edge compute and smart municipal integration serving the AI industry and talent life [depth:municipal_new_infrastructure]. Where road redlines, pipelines, fire protection and municipal conditions are absent, they are always written as pending formal data rather than approved conditions [data:geometry/constraints.geojson#CONSTRAINTS].

Public service facilities are laid out in three grades: belt-level exchange facilities (international roadshow, AI culture hall) at Dazhongsi and Origin Community; district-level services (sports, culture, community medical) along the Xiaoyuehe and Qinghe interfaces; and block-level livelihood services (childcare, elderly-care stations, accessibility service points) ensuring walk-shed coverage. Elderly-care stations and accessibility points follow the smart-health-elderly principle of running traditional channels in parallel: online booking and offline human service are accepted concurrently [source:ELDERLY-SMART-TECH-PLAN].

## Renewal Projects, Implementation Policy, and Phasing

Renewal projects are phased as near-term (Zhongzhiyuan, 2026–2028), mid-term (Origin Community, 2028–2030) and long-term (Dazhongsi, 2030–2033), each phase implementing public space and slow networks in parallel [data:geometry/phasing.geojson#P1] [depth:phasing_implementation]. The project list covers slow-traffic gap stitching, the Qinghe innovation interface, the near-campus transformation street, Dazhongsi station pedestrian connectivity, edge compute nodes and the Global AI Activity Week route [depth:renewal_project_list].

**Phase key performance indicators (KPIs)** set along three tracks — buildable space, operable services and verifiable scenarios — all labeled as conceptual targets calibrated with formal data:

| Phase | Period | Spatial components | Service/operation goals | Scenario validation goals |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Near-term | 2026–2028 | Zhongzhiyuan innovation loop, first Qinghe riverfront segment, edge compute stations | landing red-team test ground and salon season first | cards 1/2/3 online; test ground accepts external validation |
| Mid-term | 2028–2030 | Origin transformation street, slow-traffic stitching segments, accessible demo-point package | transformation platform and developer community become routine | cards 7/9 validated; elderly-care station accessibility acceptance |
| Long-term | 2030–2033 | Dazhongsi four-quadrant pedestrian connectivity, landmark closure | international roadshow and Activity Week system mature | card 10 first Global AI Activity Week; annual evaluation loop |

Phasing adopts a progressive strategy of "use low-intensity temporary facilities to validate scenario demand first, then advance formal construction per regulatory plan and tenure"; any expropriation or demolition statement is marked as proceeding through formal procedures.

**Participating actors and phasing decision gates (agent.6)**: five actor classes with their division of labor support feasibility — government (planning and public-service guarantee), universities (provisioning and talent), enterprises (R&D and transformation), operators (scenario and community operations), and the public/developers (experience and co-building feedback) [depth:phasing_implementation]. Each phase sets a decision gate: before entering the next phase, the previous phase's spatial delivery, operator presence and scenario acceptance must be re-verified; any gap returns to temporary testing or decomposed revision, with no promise of proceeding. All are proposed mechanisms, never stated as confirmed approval arrangements [depth:risk_missing_data].

**Pilot district (pilot)**: the Zhongzhiyuan red-team test ground and edge scenarios (2026–2028) are proposed as the first pilot district to validate the "test—feedback—consolidate" loop and small-scale accessibility services first, then roll the replicable implementation sample to Origin Community and Dazhongsi [depth:phasing_implementation] [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#KEY-001].

**Transferability handoff list (transferability)**: this package doubles as a handoff working draft. Professional planning teams can deepen from `proposal.md`, `geometry/*.geojson`, `metrics.json` and the A3/A0 drawings; operations teams can land pilots from the 10 scenario cards and phase KPI table; communications teams can extend international communication from the brand logo direction, wayfinding system and bilingual website [source:SITE-PACKAGE] [metric:key_area_count]. ## Metrics, Area Recalculation, and Compliance Matrix

Core metrics are recalculated from submitted geometry: total area, green ratio, public-space ratio, building footprint area, building density and key-area count [metric:site_area_sqm] [metric:green_ratio] [metric:public_space_ratio]. Metrics depending on undisclosed official control conditions, such as floor-area ratio and building height, are marked pending formal data [metric:floor_area_ratio] [depth:metrics_recalculation].

Metrics are managed in three classes: spatial metrics directly recalculable from submitted geometry, control metrics requiring official regulatory plans, and performance metrics requiring continuous operational calibration — mapped into `metrics.json`, `assumptions.json` and `compliance_matrix.json` respectively [source:SITE-PACKAGE]. Performance metrics include advisory targets for scenario validation, accessibility acceptance and event operation; any unquantified value is labeled "suggested target, to be calibrated".

The compliance matrix maps bid tasks 1.3, 1.5.2 and 1.5.3 and all agent.1–agent.6 mandatory tasks to sections, layers, metrics and drawings; the two solicitation tracks are covered by their own entries in `standard_matrix.json`, so both the official track and the agent open-call track remain traceable and readable.

## Risk, Copyright, and Compliance

This package is bilingual: the Chinese narrative and `proposal.en.md` are equivalent translations, and A3/A0, HTML and drawings provide both language copies. All text, geometry, drawings and static HTML are generated by the declared agent or use rights-cleared public sources; copyright and licensing are in `report/copyright_statement.md` and `sources.json` [source:SITE-PACKAGE].

Each of the three additional bases has its own boundary: the Barrier-Free Law applies only to service entrances, spatial accessibility and manual alternatives; the Generative-AI Interim Measures only to safety assessment, labeling and personal-information boundaries for AI services; the Smart-Health-Elderly Action Plan only to parallel traditional channels and aging-friendly service design [source:BARRIER-FREE-ENVIRONMENT-LAW] [source:GENERATIVE-AI-INTERIM-MEASURES] [source:ELDERLY-SMART-TECH-PLAN].
