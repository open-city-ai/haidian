Automate the workflow (such as with tools like n8n) to map public metrics (like commits/stars) from major open-source projects on GitHub/Gitee to an array of "Signal Yellow" lights along the pilgrimage path — digitally contributing to the real-time illumination of the physical pilgrimage route (Conceptual Recommendation). Constraints: Use only public metrics, allow for Human Review, do not track privacy, and do not claim as built or operational. ## Overall Design Area: Urban Renewal and Regulatory-Plan-Level Urban Design

The Overall Design Area is based on the provisional SITE_BOUNDARY as the working surface [data:geometry/site_boundary.geojson#SITE-OVERALL-001], organized and expressed according to the depth of Urban Design as per the control detailed planning standards [standard:MOHURD-CONTROL-DETAILED-PLANNING][depth:land_use_layout][depth:development_intensity_controls], **still a Conceptual Recommendation**.

**Overall Framework of Urban Renewal (Concept)**:

1. **Spine Priority**: Along the Jing-Zhang Heritage Park, enhance public accessibility, signage, and lightweight components to avoid intruding into the conservation core [data:geometry/constraints.geojson#CONSTR-HERITAGE-SPINE].
2. **Zone Allocation**: Zhongzhiyuan will carry testing/PoC/governance; Yuandian will carry the near-school open-source hub; Dazhongsi will carry public downzoning and nighttime vitality.
3. **Low-Intensity Space Identification Method**: Prioritize identifying pedestrian and cyclist connectivity gaps, inactive facade segments, and public edges that can be repurposed—propose three categories of update strategies: "bookable test interfaces / third space densification / commercial performance outward extension." **Do not fabricate ownership for the demolish–renovate–retain strategy** [depth:retain_renovate_demolish][A-NO-STATUTORY-FAR-HEIGHT-004]. (Demolish–Renovate–Retain Strategy)
4. **Intensity and Scale**: The Floor Area Ratio, total gross floor area, and statutory Building Height are listed as unknown, to be finalized with the formal control plan [metric:floor_area_ratio] [metric:total_floor_area_sqm]. The conceptual Building Footprint is provided merely for indication [metric:building_footprint_area_sqm] [metric:building_density].

Functional proportions are expressed in the land layers to relate industrial R&D, mixed community services, commercial exhibitions, green spaces, and transportation uses (see the land table in the following section), supporting the "R&D—testing—exhibition—PoC—operation" loop, rather than drawing a new city.
