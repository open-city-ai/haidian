A proposal built on the switchback wisdom of Zhan Tianyou's 1909 railway: one stroke is the Jing-Zhang heritage park cultural spine, the other is the three-area two-wing AI innovation ecosystem, meeting at the Qinghuayuan Station origin point. Three AI pilgrimage landmarks and a twelve-station 'Century of Self-Answers' interpretive trail write the answer of 1909's autonomous engineering and 2026's autonomous intelligence onto the same 43.6 km² corridor. THE HUMAN SWITCHBACK: A Century's Answer

A path shaped like the character 「人」 wrote two chapters of China's story of independent innovation: in 1909, building its own railway; in 2026, building its own intelligence. The switchback is engineering wisdom; the character is "human" itself. A century ago the railway's answer moved goods and people to distant places; today's AI answer delivers capability to every person.

Design Basis and Source Inventory

This proposal takes the official Qualification Pre-announcement of the Centennial Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt International Urban Design Open Call (Haidian Branch, Beijing Municipal Commission of Planning and Natural Resources) as its primary basis, together with the agent-facing taskbook, the site package provisional boundaries, the source registry, and nine professional standards Source Source. The three-level scope — 43.6 km² coordinated research area, ~11.4 km² overall design area, 368.4 ha key areas — are registered facts; the repository currently provides only maintainer-inferred provisional rough geometry, so all spatial conclusions here serve concept generation, display, and self-checking only, never as redlines, property boundaries, approvals, or precise areas Source. Source usability follows the registry: 7 formal-ready, 1 background, 1 provisional-only; nothing is promoted to statutory control or implementation commitment Source.

The narrative anchors come from verifiable public history: in 1905 Zhan Tianyou took charge of the Jing-Zhang railway; facing a maximum 33‰ grade through the Guan'gou section, he imported the South-American mining "switchback" line at Qinglongqiao Station, making the human-character-shaped line an icon of Chinese engineering self-reliance; the line opened in October 1909, two years early and ~300,000 taels under budget Source. A hundred and ten years later, the Jing-Zhang high-speed railway became the world's first BeiDou-navigated 350 km/h intelligent line, starting from Beijing North Station — at the southern tip of this very site Source. 1909 autonomous engineering, 2019 intelligent rail, 2026 intelligent urban belt: three timestamps form the "century of answers" narrative spine. These historical facts serve cultural narrative only; they are not site-condition or engineering evidence.

Structured sources, metrics, standards coverage, and task coverage live in sources.json, metrics.json, standard_matrix.json, compliance_matrix.json, and design_depth_matrix.json; the narrative does not duplicate machine indexes.

Three-Level Scope Framework

The three levels answer one question at three scales: how the Human Switchback organizes innovation synergy regionally, spatial structure along the corridor, and human-scale experience at nodes. The research layer organizes university sourcing, full-stack R&D, technology services, scenario validation, and market conversion into an open ecosystem Source. The overall design layer forms one stroke from the heritage park cultural spine and one from the three-area two-wing innovation belt, meeting at the Qinghuayuan Station origin. The key-area layer deepens Zhongzhiyuan, the AI Origin Community, and Dazhongsi into spatial carriers of "pose the question — solve it — verify it".

This proposal uses provisional rough boundaries inferred by maintainers from announcement text: suitable for concept generation and self-checks, not for official redlines, property judgments, or precise areas Source. Once official boundaries are published, land-use partitions, green and public-space layers, the three core metrics, drawings, and the offline visual page must all be recomputed and re-rendered; this replacement path is registered in the assumptions.

Overall Design Area: Urban Renewal and Control-Depth Design

Spatial structure: one stroke, one stroke, one origin. The cultural stroke runs south-north along the heritage park as continuous public space; the innovation stroke links the three key areas and two wings; the flagship node "The First Question" platform sits at the Qinghuayuan origin. Land-use partitions fully and seamlessly cover the provisional boundary with four conceptual categories; adjacent partitions share boundary coordinates; areas are recomputed in EPSG:4548 Spatial data Metric.

Renewal follows switchback sequencing. Rather than one-shot demolition, learn from the railway's "extend to climb": first switchback (near term) completes slow-mobility links, the blue-green skeleton, and interim activation — returning publicness to citizens; second switchback (mid term) converts stock space as publicness rises; third switchback (long term) adds new construction only on proven scenario density. Any retain/renovate/demolish conclusion requires property, structural, and heritage data completed by professional teams; this proposal offers only the principles of "retention first, reversible intervention, operate-before-build" Standard.

Honest control-depth boundary. FAR, height, density, setbacks, and redlines remain unknown until official regulatory conditions are published; no conceptual substitutes are offered. The building layer expresses a conceptual service base for pipeline verification only Standard.

AI Ecosystem, Personas, and AI+ Scenarios

Five core personas (for needs synthesis only, never profiling individuals): the neighborhood elder (needs non-registration physical service interfaces) Standard; the student developer (needs low-cost labs and stages); the young family (needs safe parent-child AI education); the international visitor (needs multilingual barrier-free arrival) Standard; the facilities operator (needs maintainable, deactivatable, accountable infrastructure).

Twelve scenario cards (one per station along the interpretive trail):

1. Tactile transfer chain (Qinghuayuan origin): fully tactile navigation from rail to trail. 2. The First Question wall (origin): dual-channel display pairing the century's engineering problems with today's open AI challenges. 3. Algorithm clinic (Origin Community): face-to-face human+AI service desk where residents can question, appeal, and stop any public AI service. 4. Open-source contribution wall (university segment): commit light-strip mirroring global open-source activity; annual stele candidates gallery. 5. Tactile model hall (Origin Community): touchable city and railway models; knowledge without screens. 6. Edge experiment market (Zhongzhiyuan): weekend developer stall-style open experiments the public can watch and try. 7. Red-team court (Zhongzhiyuan): regular AI security exercises with public open days. 8. Open Compute Day (Zhongzhiyuan): monthly public compute-facility hours for small teams. 9. Screen-optional retail street (Dazhongsi): voice-, tactile-, and token-first consumption pilot. 10. The Answer Bell (Dazhongsi): a daily bell whose sound is generated from the day's global open-source activity. 11. Elder slow-mobility loop (Xiaoyuehe wing): speed-limited loop with rest networks optimized for elders. 12. Answer Season main venue (whole belt): a mobile venue mechanism for the annual festival.

Cards 3, 6, and 7 are the three industrial test-and-validation scenarios, testing explainability of public services, public acceptance of edge technology, and openness of safety governance Source. Each card maps to location, audience, operating data, privacy boundary, human review, operator, visual layer, and risks; mappings are kept in compliance_matrix.json under agent.3. All public-data scenarios follow the interim measures for generative AI services on labeling and human review Standard.

Slow-mobility-first cultural stroke. The heritage park spine achieves north-south continuity and east-west stitching: continuous tactile guidance, ramps, rest points, shade, and staffed wayfinding before digital navigation; barrier-free design follows the dual-channel principle Standard.

Rail and access. Beijing North (HSR origin) and Line-13 stations anchor rail access; Dazhongsi station-city integration awaits a transport study. The road layer expresses conceptual service corridors and slow-mobility centrelines only Spatial data.

Municipal and new infrastructure. Blue-Green Space, Public Space, and Urban Character

Blue-green skeleton. The park greenway, Xiaoyuehe blue line, and key-area greens form the belt system; green and public-space ratios recompute from submitted geometry as conceptual allocations Metric Metric.

Three AI pilgrimage landmarks.

1. The First Question (Qinghuayuan origin): a ring-shaped question wall — inner ring carved with the century's engineering problems and solutions (switchback line, Badaling tunnel shaft method, from public records), outer ring a live global open-AI challenge board (screen + print dual channel). During each open-call season, global agent submissions exhibit here. Reversible light-steel ring gallery; protected reuse of the station relic. 2. The Honor Switchback (BUPT–Beihang segment): a linear gallery folding in the human-character shape — one side a century of railway engineering archives, the other the agent-contribution honor wall and open-source gallery (commit light strips, annual stele preview slots). Walking through equals walking through two centuries of technical history; this very open call's stele mechanism gains its physical site here. 3. The Answer Bell (Dazhongsi): an acoustic pavilion whose daily chime is generated from the day's global open-source activity — the more active the contributions, the richer the tone — echoing the ancient bell of Dazhongsi as "the answer of time". Low-intrusion, deactivatable.

All three are conceptual suggestions, not approved construction; signage, portraits, and trademarks require separate clearance. The landmarks close the pilgrimage loop: the First Question poses (for global developers), the Honor Switchback records (for contributors), the Answer Bell returns (for citizens) Source.

Public-space component library. Tactile tracks, dual-channel information columns, commit light strips, removable beacons, token desks, touchable models, staffed service desks, stop switches, paper feedback boxes — all maintainable, recyclable, deactivatable; no spectacles manufactured from mega-screens and continuous broadcast.

Cultural narrative and signage. The twelve-station "Century of Self-Answers" trail uses a question/answer bracket grammar: each station sign is a pair of brackets — the left engraved with that era's question, the right with today's answer; the walker is the stroke between them. Signage separates the belt brand layer from cultural interpretation; historical facts, portraits, and trademarks verified item by item, never replaced by generated imagery Standard.

Renewal Inventory, Policy, and Phasing

Three-switchback phasing. Near term (0–2y) "first switchback": slow-mobility links, landmark pilot segments, scenario-card pilots (algorithm clinic, edge market), interim activation. Mid term (3–5y) "second switchback": stock-space conversion, key-area detailed design, full scenario deployment. Long term (6–10y) "third switchback": new construction on proven density, full stele system Spatial data.

Renewal inventory (conceptual). Cultural-stroke slow-mobility works, the three landmarks, the algorithm-clinic network, tactile model hall, screen-optional retail pilot, elder slow-mobility loop, Answer Season mechanism — each with type, location, dependencies, suggested implementer, and policy needs; all are open co-creation suggestions.

Annual event system and long-term operation. "Answer Season": each August the open call launches, September review and publication, October 2 (Jing-Zhang opening anniversary) the stele ceremony and Answer Bell chime — closing the loop of pose, solve, verify, remember. Developer community: contribution-wall upkeep, monthly Open Compute Day, quarterly red-team open day. International communication: multilingual trail editions, an annual "A Century's Answer" report, case exchanges with global innovation districts. All events, investment, and policy arrangements are conceptual suggestions, not confirmed government commitments Source.
