# 再造京张 / RE:RAIL

一条服务城市延寿的开放再造主线：以构件护照保存记忆，以可逆更新降低试错成本，以材料回流连接产业和社区，以人工可审计的 AI 测试守住公共安全与专业责任。

An open renewal spine for extending urban life: component passports preserve memory, reversible adaptation lowers the cost of learning, material recirculation connects industry and community, and human-audited AI trials protect public safety and professional accountability.
