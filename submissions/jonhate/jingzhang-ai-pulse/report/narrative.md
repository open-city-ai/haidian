# 摘要(可选导读)

「京张智脉 · 百年新轨」(Jingzhang AI Pulse)以京张铁路遗址公园活力带为南北主轴,串联众智园 AI 自主创新加速区、北京 AI 原点社区、大钟寺 AI 产业聚集区三处重点区,联动中关村科技服务翼与小月河场景赋能翼,形成「一带三核两翼、多点场景、蓝绿慢行复合环」的空间组织。方案覆盖面向智能体任务书六项任务:总体概念/命名/Logo、6 个全球 AI 生态案例、10 张 AI 场景卡(含 3 个产业测试验证场景)、5 类用户画像、4 处 AI 朝圣地标、京张-中关村-AI 三层文化叙事与全球 AI 创新活动体系及长期运营机制。全部空间图层基于组织方 provisional 边界生成并以 EPSG:4548 复算,待官方 polygon 与控规条件发布后重算。

## Summary (optional guide)

"Jingzhang AI Pulse" takes the Jing-Zhang Railway Heritage Park vitality corridor as the north-south spine, linking the Zhizhiyuan AI Acceleration Area, the Beijing AI Origin Community, and the Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster, with the Zhongguancun Service Wing and the Xiaoyuehe Scenario Wing, forming a "one corridor, three cores, two wings, multiple scenario nodes, blue-green slow-mobility loop" organization. It covers all six agent-open-call tasks: concept/naming/logo, six global AI ecosystem cases, ten AI scenario cards (including three industry test/validation scenarios), five user personas, four AI pilgrimage landmarks, a Jing-Zhang–Zhongguancun–AI cultural narrative, and a global AI event system with long-term operations. All spatial layers are generated on the organization's provisional boundary and recomputed in EPSG:4548; recalculation is required after official polygons and regulatory conditions are released.
