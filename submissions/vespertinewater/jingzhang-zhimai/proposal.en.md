# 京张·智脉 —— 从自主铁路到自主智能 (Jing-Zhang Intelligence Artery)

## Design Basis and Source List

This proposal takes the Haidian Branch of the Beijing Municipal Commission of Planning and Natural Resources' pre-qualification announcement for the Centennial Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt as its primary controlling basis, and the agent-facing open-call taskbook as the framework for the six required agent tasks [source:OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT] [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK]. All spatial proposals are worded as conceptual suggestions / reference schemes / material for professional teams to deepen, and do not replace statutory planning or constitute government approval. Because only provisional rough polygons derived from the announcement's textual boundaries are available — the official precise redlines are not yet public — this proposal uniformly labels precision boundaries as "pending official data" and commits to recomputing every layer and metric once official polygons are published.

Sources follow the public source registry's tiering [source:SOURCE-REGISTRY]: formal-ready material is used for scope, task, and professional-standard responses; background and provisional material is used only for generation, display, and temporary self-check, and is never promoted to statutory redline or approval basis. Only the most critical citations sit beside judgments here; complete source and standard coverage lives in `sources.json`, `standard_matrix.json`, and `design_depth_matrix.json`.

The core narrative is "from self-built railway to self-reliant intelligence": the Jing-Zhang Railway was China's first trunk railway designed and built without foreign loans or engineers, an origin point of modern Chinese technological autonomy; today this linear heritage corridor runs through Haidian's universities and Zhongguancun, and now carries the new origin point of full-stack AI autonomy, a world-class innovation ecosystem, and a city-intelligence exemplar [depth:existing_conditions_diagnosis]. We therefore reread the century-old "track" as a continuously accelerating "Intelligence Artery" — the spatial skeleton and brand motif of the entire proposal.

## Three-Level Scope Framework

The proposal organizes work into the three announcement-defined levels [standard:PROJECT-OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT]. The coordinated research area (about 43.6 km²) addresses the AI industry ecosystem, innovation chain, and future city form; the overall design area (about 11.4 km²) covers the 1–2 km urban and industrial zone around the heritage park and requires an urban-renewal framework, industry-space layout, transport-municipal support, and urban-character control; the key detailed-design area (about 368.4 ha) focuses on Zhongzhiyuan, the AI Origin Community, and Dazhongsi [depth:three_level_scope_framework]. The three levels are mapped item-by-item in `compliance_matrix.json`, ensuring every mandatory task in announcement 1.3, 1.4, 1.5 and agent.1–agent.6 has section, layer, metric, drawing, and HTML evidence.

The three levels are not separate drawing sets but a progressive chain of "strategy — overall layout — district deepening" [depth:overall_spatial_structure]. Because the submitted boundary is provisional, all spatial structure, scenarios, projects, and metrics are written to be "discussable, recheckable, and recomputable after official boundaries arrive" [data:geometry/site_boundary.geojson#SITE-001] [metric:site_area_sqm].

## Coordinated Research Area: Industry and Future City Research

The coordinated research area's first task is a world-class AI innovation ecosystem. The proposal puts forward a "one axis, three cores, two wings, multiple nodes" structure: the **axis** is the Jing-Zhang Heritage Park as a north–south "Intelligence-Artery spine"; the **three cores** are Zhongzhiyuan (Intelligence Core Reach), the AI Origin Community (Intelligence Origin Reach), and Dazhongsi (Intelligence Conflux Reach); the **two wings** are the Zhongguancun Tech-Service Wing (factors, capital, IP) and the Xiaoyuehe Scenario Wing (scenarios and living-city vitality) [depth:overall_spatial_structure]. The three cores and two wings form a "R&D — ecosystem — industry — service — scenario" loop that directly answers the three positionings and five functions [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK] [standard:PROJECT-AGENT-OPEN-CALL-TASKBOOK].

Naming and visual identity distinguish this from slogan-style naming. The main name "Jing-Zhang Intelligence Artery" (京张·智脉) anchors history and site with "Jing-Zhang" and uses "Artery/脉" to evoke the linear corridor and the flow of intelligence; the district names (Intelligence Core/Origin/Conflux Reach, Tech-Service/Scenario Wing) form an extensible, registrable, transmissible identity system. The logo motif is a railway track that, in perspective, becomes a light/data stream with sleepers turning into neural-network nodes, in a three-color gradient of heritage ochre, innovation blue, and AI light-flow cyan-violet.

Global cases are converted into space and mechanism, not labels. Cambridge Kendall Square's university-industry adjacency becomes the zero-distance ecosystem mixing of the Origin Reach; Paris Station F's reuse of an old station becomes the Conflux Reach's use of railway/industrial heritage for AI-native business; Montreal Mila and Toronto's Vector Institute inform research institutions and global talent channels; Zurich's ETH AI Center informs a trustworthy-AI and governance lab in the Core Reach; Zhongguancun itself serves as local historical depth [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK].

## Overall Design Area: Urban Renewal and Regulatory-Plan-Level Urban Design

The overall design area must reach regulatory-detailed-plan urban-design depth [standard:MOHURD-CONTROL-DETAILED-PLANNING]. Land use uses the territorial land-use classification vocabulary — park green (1401), research (0802), education (0804), commercial (05), residential (0701) — to form a complete, closed, seamless partition [standard:MNR-LAND-USE-CLASSIFICATION-GUIDE] [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-001]. The layout makes the park corridor a continuous green spine, the three key districts functional cores beside it, and residential and supporting uses the east-west wings — exactly isomorphic to "one axis, three cores, two wings."

Building scale and intensity are where this proposal is most careful. No FAR, height, density, green-ratio, or setback controls are public, so all such controls are marked pending confirmation; only geometry-recomputed conceptual massing is given, explicitly not statutory control [depth:development_intensity_controls]. Building footprints are organized as retain / renovate / new-build categories, and everything lacking existing-building, ownership, or engineering conditions is written as pending, never fabricated [depth:retain_renovate_demolish]. Transport forms a "one spine, three cross-links" slow-mobility skeleton — the greenway spine north–south, three east-west links — covering Beiwuhuan crossing, Wudaokou, Tsinghua East Road West, and Dazhongsi Station [depth:traffic_rail_slow_parking] [data:geometry/roads.geojson#ROAD-001].

Municipal and new-infrastructure strategy treats distributed energy, edge compute, and public-service facilities as new-infrastructure prototypes for deepening, not pseudo-precise load or capacity figures [depth:municipal_new_infrastructure].

## Detailed Design of Key Areas

The three key districts are the feasibility-verification layer, each a small scheme of "positioning + spatial structure + building renewal + transport/mobility + public space + AI scenario + implementation risk" [depth:three_key_area_detailed_design].

**Intelligence Core Reach — Zhongzhiyuan AI Acceleration Area** (~192.1 ha): the core of full-stack autonomy (compute, algorithms, chips), carrying the full-stack autonomous innovation system and global AI-governance discourse. Spatial moves emphasize the Qinghe blue-green interface, industry display, low-carbon innovation exchange, and external transport; the "Sovereign-Compute Lighthouse" concept node turns standard-setting, safety evaluation, and model red-teaming into a visitable, reservable, supervised display-and-collaboration node [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-001]. Because this polygon is provisional, scale and height are directional only.

**Intelligence Origin Reach — Beijing AI Origin Community** (~104.3 ha): the source of a world-class AI innovation ecosystem, carrying near-campus innovation, incubation, a talent zone, and open-source systems. Spatial moves stitch campus, park, and district slow-mobility, and add release, talent-service, residential, and open-source collaboration space; the "Intelligence-Origin Zero-Mile" concept landmark echoes the Jing-Zhang Railway's zero-kilometer imagery [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-002] [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK].

**Intelligence Conflux Reach — Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster** (~72.0 ha): the conflux of AI-native new business, carrying leading enterprises, agents, intelligent terminals, content consumption, and data factors. Spatial moves center on Dazhongsi Station integration, four-quadrant pedestrian connection, commercial service, and public-environment renewal; the "Open-Source Gate" concept landmark faces developers and global visitors [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-003] [metric:key_area_count].

## AI Innovation Ecosystem, Personas, and AI+ Scenarios

Personas cover six groups: full-stack R&D engineers (compute, collaboration, open source); university faculty and students (public labs, incubation, cross-disciplinary exchange); AI startup teams (scenario testing, capital, legal, market); residents including the elderly and children (perceivable, reviewable, inclusive AI services); business visitors and global guests (innovation service, exchange space, "pilgrimage" experience); and public governance (explainable, auditable governance demos). Each persona maps to space and self-check boundaries — no resident profiling for commerce, no personal trajectory collection.

At least ten scenario cards are provided, three of which are industry test/validation scenarios, all readable and mapped to location, users, data, privacy, human review, and operator. The three test scenarios are: AI+ traffic adaptive green-wave and driverless shuttles (Testbed ①, along the artery spine); AI+ autonomous last-mile delivery corridor (Testbed ②, park-community interface); AI+ public-space governance for crowd, lighting, and maintenance (Testbed ③, the park corridor). Other cards cover AI+ medical triage, AI+ education open-source workshops, AI+ legal contract pre-review, AI+ life-service multilingual guidance, AI+ new retail, AI+ research collaboration, AI+ culture digital twin, AI+ robot public service, and AI+ governance demo [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK]. Every scenario keeps human-review and opt-out mechanisms; immature technology is never written as fully deployable [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#PUBLIC-001] [data:geometry/green_space.geojson#GREEN-001] [metric:public_space_ratio].

## Land Use, Building Scale, and Retain-Renovate-Demolish Strategy

Land use is expressed as a complete closed partition using the territorial classification vocabulary; industry-function proportions are recomputed from land-use codes — park green about one-fifth, research and education about one-third, commercial and residential the remainder [standard:MNR-LAND-USE-CLASSIFICATION-GUIDE] [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-001]. Building footprints consist of twelve conceptual masses covering R&D, lab, incubator, talent apartment, education, community service, retail, and residential, all inside the land-use partition and recomputable from the layer [data:geometry/buildings.geojson#BLDG-001] [metric:building_footprint_area_sqm].

Because the taskbook forbids writing FAR, height, intensity, or parcel-level demolition as statutory conclusions, this proposal marks all such controls as pending formal conditions, keeping only geometry-recomputed conceptual footprints and density, with the recomputation path recorded in `reason`/`assumptions` [depth:development_intensity_controls]. Retain-renovate-demolish is given as methodology (criteria for each category), not parcel-level demolition conclusions [depth:retain_renovate_demolish].

## Transport, Rail, Municipal Infrastructure, and Public Services

Transport answers station integration, road micro-circulation, slow-mobility gaps, and external links. The artery greenway is the north–south axis stitching the three cores; three east-west links and a slow-mobility loop stitch the two sides of the railway corridor, achieving east-west stitching and north-south continuity [depth:traffic_rail_slow_parking] [data:geometry/roads.geojson#ROAD-001]. Rail connection uses Dazhongsi Station integration as a demonstration; Wudaokou and Tsinghua East Road West anchor near-campus slow-mobility stitching. Because the boundary is provisional, transport conclusions are provisional discussion only.

Municipal and public-service facilities cover AI industry services, innovation platforms, talent life services, new infrastructure, distributed energy, and edge compute, folded into phasing as conceptual strategies [depth:municipal_new_infrastructure]. Anything lacking utility, energy, drainage, or fire data is listed as a formal-deepening precondition.

## Blue-Green Network, Public Space, and Urban Character

The blue-green network uses the heritage-park vitality belt as its skeleton, turning the artery spine into a continuous, walkable, stay-worthy green corridor linking the three districts and the north-south landscape nodes [depth:blue_green_public_space] [data:geometry/green_space.geojson#GREEN-001]. Public space anchors on three concept-landmark plazas linked to the corridor and slow-mobility loop; green and public-space ratios are explained in prose as "supporting everyday talent exchange and innovation serendipity," with full recomputation in `metrics.json` [metric:green_ratio] [metric:public_space_ratio].

Urban character fuses Jing-Zhang railway history, Zhongguancun innovation culture, and new AI culture, layering "history — innovation — intelligence" wayfinding grammars onto one corridor, with a city temperament of "trustworthy, open, youthful, symbiotic." Three AI pilgrimage landmarks (all concept nodes, never presented as approved construction): **Intelligence-Origin Zero-Mile** (Origin Community), **Sovereign-Compute Lighthouse** (Zhongzhiyuan), and **Open-Source Gate** (Dazhongsi), forming an "origin — core — gate" pilgrimage narrative tied to a contribution wall and honor-display system [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK]. All branding, fonts, images, portraits, and enterprise marks must be rights-cleared and must not violate heritage, green, blue-line, or traffic-safety constraints [standard:MOHURD-URBAN-DESIGN-MEASURES].

## Renewal Projects, Implementation Policy, and Phasing

The renewal project list is organized by auditable ID with location, type, dependency, and evidence per item. JZ-01 artery-spine slow-mobility gap stitching, JZ-02 Zhongzhiyuan Qinghe innovation interface, JZ-03 Origin Community near-campus conversion street, JZ-04 Dazhongsi Station four-quadrant pedestrian connection, JZ-05 AI public service and edge-compute node, and JZ-06 Global AI Week public route cover public space, industry, transport, and new infrastructure, each tied to the phasing layer [depth:renewal_project_list] [data:geometry/phasing.geojson#PHASE-001].

Phasing advances "near-term pilot — mid-term renewal — long-term governance": near-term launches the Origin Community with lightweight facilities, operations, and service platforms; mid-term deepens full-stack autonomy and industry acceleration in Zhongzhiyuan; long-term deepens industry clustering and four-quadrant connection in Dazhongsi [depth:phasing_implementation]. The annual event system, developer-community operation, scenario open days, public experience route, and international outreach each state the operator, frequency, responsibility boundary, and conversion path — no slogans, no exaggerated government commitments [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK].

## Metrics, Area Recalculation, and Compliance Matrix

Metrics fall into three classes: geometry-recomputable spatial metrics (boundary area, green/public-space area and ratios, building footprint, park land area, phasing area, key-area count and area); statutory controls requiring official regulatory-plan support (FAR, height, density, setback — currently pending); and performance metrics requiring ongoing operational calibration (AI innovation index, talent density, scenario usage — operational vision, not statutory indicators) [depth:metrics_recalculation]. Every known metric is recomputed from GeoJSON or a credible source; complete values, formulas, and sources live in `metrics.json` [metric:site_area_sqm] [metric:green_ratio].

The compliance matrix is the master file for task responsiveness, covering announcement 1.3, 1.4, 1.5 and agent.1–agent.6 item-by-item; the standard matrix covers all mandatory professional standards; the design-depth matrix covers all fifteen required depth items. Together they guarantee the proposal is both machine-auditable and human-readable.
