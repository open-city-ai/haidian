Three-Level Scope Framework

The concept is **Handover Jing-Zhang**: the railway's operational handover becomes an urban AI protocol. Every AI service must disclose its purpose, identify a responsible human, support review and appeal, provide an equivalent non-AI service, and state its exit condition. The spatial structure is one line, three stations, and two wings: the Jing-Zhang heritage park as the public line; Zhongzhiyuan as the R&D handover station; Beijing AI Origin Community as the co-creation station; Dazhongsi as the adoption station; Zhongguancun as the technology-service wing; and Xiaoyuehe as the everyday-scenario wing. [standard:PROJECT-AGENT-OPEN-CALL-TASKBOOK]

Coordinated Research Area: Industry and Future City Research

The identity system uses a split line: a railway trace joins a data pulse at a square handover node. The visual language has three elements: line, station, and status seal. It uses original geometric marks only, without third-party trademarks, portraits, or unlicensed fonts. Eight reference cases are used as mechanisms rather than claims about Haidian: Toronto Sidewalk Labs, Barcelona Sentilo, Amsterdam Data Commons, Helsinki Kalasatama, Singapore Punggol Digital District, London Queen Elizabeth Olympic Park, Paris Réinventer Paris, and Shenzhen Qianhai smart-city pilots. Transferable lessons are staged testing, public purpose disclosure, reversibility, data authorization, and independent review. [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK]

Overall Design Area: Urban Renewal and Regulatory-Plan-Level Urban Design

The proposal links land use, buildings, roads, green space, public space, constraints, and phasing through machine-readable layers. Missing official controls for FAR, height, density, setbacks, utilities, ownership, heritage, and road redlines remain pending confirmation. The design is therefore a reference scheme for professional deepening, not a statutory plan. [standard:MOHURD-CONTROL-DETAILED-PLANNING] [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-001]

Detailed Design of Key Areas

Zhongzhiyuan is the R&D and safety-testing station; Beijing AI Origin Community is the open-source and talent co-creation station; Dazhongsi is the public adoption and international exchange station. Three proposed AI pilgrimage and honor nodes are the Handover Table, Origin Archive Walk, and Adoption Beacon. These are conceptual public-space nodes, not approved construction projects. [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-001]

AI Innovation Ecosystem, Personas, and AI+ Scenarios

The proposal includes twelve scenario cards: open-source launch hall, safety sandbox, edge-compute kiosk, AI walking navigator, international demo lounge, Qinghe low-carbon innovation edge, near-campus transfer street, data commons lounge, AI everyday-service street, global AI week route, quiet night service, and accessible-route review. The three industry validation scenarios are model safety and standards, open-source artifact handover, and terminal adoption with human fallback. Personas include open-source developers, start-ups, enterprise visitors, residents, and university users. Each scenario follows notice, handover, review, equivalent service, and exit.

Transport, Rail, Municipal Infrastructure, and Public Services

The reference strategy prioritizes rail-station integration, walking and cycling continuity, low-intrusion public-service interfaces, and reversible AI infrastructure. Road redlines, utilities, fire safety, energy capacity, and engineering feasibility remain open professional checks. [data:geometry/roads.geojson#ROAD-001]

Blue-Green Network, Public Space, and Urban Character

The Jing-Zhang heritage park is treated as a public spine connecting cultural memory, daily mobility, and AI demonstrations. Qinghe and Xiaoyuehe are conceptual blue-green interfaces for walking, cycling, ecological learning, and low-carbon testing. The cultural narrative has three handovers: railway engineering cooperation, Zhongguancun open innovation, and accountable AI culture. [data:geometry/green_space.geojson#GREEN-001] [metric:green_ratio]

Renewal Projects, Implementation Policy, and Phasing

Suggested projects include heritage-park walking-gap repair, the Zhongzhiyuan Qinghe interface, an Origin Community transfer street, Dazhongsi station pedestrian continuity, public AI-service nodes, and a global AI week route. A three-stage sequence is proposed: reversible pilots, coordinated renewal, and long-term public auditing. These are reference mechanisms, not confirmed government programs or funding commitments. No official approval, final land ownership, statutory control, guaranteed implementation, or engineering conclusion is claimed. External identities and images are not copied.
