Visual overview / Centennial Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt

AI Pulse Jingzhang: Conceptual Urban Design

This page is the offline visual index of the formal concept package for the Centennial Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt open call. The scheme takes the Jing-Zhang railway heritage park as the cultural spine and proposes a "one pulse, two wings, three cores, multiple nodes" structure: the barrier-free AI Pulse greenway, the Zhongguancun service wing and Xiaoyuehe scenario wing, the Zhongzhiyuan / AI Origin Community / Dazhongsi core areas, and distributed AI scenario stops. Authoritative data remain the GeoJSON layers, metrics.json, matrices, and self-check results.

Three-Level Scope

The announcement defines a coordinated research area, an overall design area, and a key detailed-design area, binding industry strategy, urban renewal, and detailed design to layers and metrics.

planning_limits.json

Coordinated research area

About 43.6 km², bounded by Beiwuhuan Road, Jingzang Expressway, Xizhimenwai Street, and Wanquanhe Road; industry strategy, regional synergy, and future-city research.

  • AI innovation chain and talent chain
  • Global case-transfer mechanisms
  • Brand naming and communication

Overall design area

About 11.4 km² (submitted boundary 11,412,825.386 m²); urban renewal, land use, transport, and the Jing-Zhang heritage park vitality corridor at regulatory-plan depth.

  • Spatial structure, land use, renewal framework
  • Blue-green slow-mobility system
  • Renewal project list

Key detailed-design area

About 368.4 ha with three key areas from north to south: Zhongzhiyuan AI acceleration area, Beijing AI Origin Community, Dazhongsi AI industry cluster.

  • Program and spatial strategy
  • Building renewal and public-space moves
  • Transport interfaces and implementation risk

Key Areas

Each key area states program positioning, spatial strategy, building and public-space actions, transport interfaces, and implementation risk; mapped in key_areas.geojson.

Beijing AI Origin Community

University-adjacent outcomes-conversion district, about 1,043,237 m²; campus-origin innovation, open-source community, talent services, retain/renovate/new, station integration.

Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster

City-scale intelligent-economy district, about 720,454 m²; leading enterprises, agent-native businesses, commercial services, green compound use, four-quadrant connectivity.

Renewal projects

Spine connection (greenway slow mobility, plaza system, heritage narrative); three-core activation (Zhongzhiyuan campus, Origin conversion street, Dazhongsi hub); two-wing stitching (Xiaoyuehe corridor, Zhongguancun services, cross streets).

  • Statutory controls declared unknown

Retain / renovate / new / reserve

Heritage park and heritage clues preserved and activated; existing campuses, parks, and housing upgraded; new R&D, incubation, and talent housing in core and wing potential areas; uncertain areas reserved.
