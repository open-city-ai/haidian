假设与缺口（8条）

A-CONTROLS-001 — Detailed regulatory controls (FAR, building coverage, height), road redlines, ownership, municipal utilities, and engineering constraints are not public and must be confirmed from official attachments before statutory use.影响: Development-intensity metrics stay 'unknown'; intensity and character statements remain conceptual intentions; parcel-level demolish-renovate-retain is not determined.
A-BOUNDARY-001 — All three scope levels and three key-area polygons are provisional rough boundaries inferred from the announcement's textual extents and approximate areas (EPSG:4548-checked). They are not official redlines and must not be used for precise-area claims.影响: When official polygons arrive, recalculate: all land-use unit areas, green/public-space ratios, key-area and phase areas, all five figures, both drawing sets, and the visualization page. The recalculation is mechanical; the design structure (spine, junctions, sections) is expected to survive.
A-EXISTING-001 — Existing-condition features in constraints.geojson (rail corridor, Qinghe and Xiaoyue River, ring-road crossings) are approximations from public common knowledge for design coordination only, with confidence=low.影响: Blue lines, rail protection corridors, and road redlines must be replaced with official data before any statutory or engineering use.
A-STATIONS-001 — The six rail station positions used for station-spine links are approximate public-map locations; interchange schemes are not coordinated with rail operators.影响: Station-connection alignments and the 150 m walking-link claim are conceptual and must be verified against station layouts.
A-PROGRAM-001 — Land-use program shares and the 21 demonstration buildings are conceptual design proposals; buildings.geojson is not an existing-building survey, and retain/renovate/demolish shares (≈70/25/5) are conceptual, not parcel determinations.影响: Program shares guide review of design intent; they are not statutory zoning or approved development scale.
A-CULTURE-001 — Historical narrative of the Jing-Zhang Railway (1909 opening, Zhan Tianyou, Qinglongqiao switchback) follows the cited public authoritative sources; detailed wording and any heritage-site interpretation (e.g., former Qinghuayuan Station) require cultural-heritage professional review.影响: Landmark siting near heritage assets is directional only; heritage protection scopes are not drawn because no official file is available.
A-CASES-001 — The six global ecosystem case summaries are digests of public common knowledge used for mechanism transfer; individual figures were not re-verified for this submission.影响: Cases support mechanism design, not quantitative benchmarking.
A-OPERATION-001 — The Switchback Protocol, event system, Crew community, junction joint ventures, Platform Covenant, and all operating mechanisms are conceptual suggestions with no government or investor commitment.影响: Operational content is evaluated as design thinking; any implementation requires lawful approval, security assessment, and stakeholder agreement.
