Concept: one spine, nine junctions, three sections, two wings

In 1909 the Jing-Zhang Railway crossed the pass by a ren-shaped switchback; here humans and AI couple like paired engines — always keeping the right to reverse.

The Switchback Protocol

Governance

Every public AI deployment: pair a public return car, disclose the model card at the junction, stay reversible within 72 hours, pass the annual overhaul.

Nine switchback junctions

R1–R9

Beijing North, Dazhongsi, Jimen, Zhichun, Origin, Wudaokou, Tsinghua East, Zhongzhiyuan, Qinghe Bay — stitching streets plus reversing platforms.

Four AI pilgrimage landmarks

Conceptual

Ren Origin, Centennial Signal House, Great Bell New Voice, Departure Deck — archive wall for projects, nameplates for people, milestones for versions.

Assumptions and gaps (8)

A-CONTROLS-001 — Detailed regulatory controls (FAR, building coverage, height), road redlines, ownership, municipal utilities, and engineering constraints are not public and must be confirmed from official attachments before statutory use.Impact: Development-intensity metrics stay 'unknown'; intensity and character statements remain conceptual intentions; parcel-level demolish-renovate-retain is not determined.
A-BOUNDARY-001 — All three scope levels and three key-area polygons are provisional rough boundaries inferred from the announcement's textual extents and approximate areas (EPSG:4548-checked). They are not official redlines and must not be used for precise-area claims.Impact: When official polygons arrive, recalculate: all land-use unit areas, green/public-space ratios, key-area and phase areas, all five figures, both drawing sets, and the visualization page. The recalculation is mechanical; the design structure (spine, junctions, sections) is expected to survive.
A-EXISTING-001 — Existing-condition features in constraints.geojson (rail corridor, Qinghe and Xiaoyue River, ring-road crossings) are approximations from public common knowledge for design coordination only, with confidence=low.Impact: Blue lines, rail protection corridors, and road redlines must be replaced with official data before any statutory or engineering use.
A-STATIONS-001 — The six rail station positions used for station-spine links are approximate public-map locations; interchange schemes are not coordinated with rail operators.Impact: Station-connection alignments and the 150 m walking-link claim are conceptual and must be verified against station layouts.
A-PROGRAM-001 — Land-use program shares and the 21 demonstration buildings are conceptual design proposals; buildings.geojson is not an existing-building survey, and retain/renovate/demolish shares (≈70/25/5) are conceptual, not parcel determinations.Impact: Program shares guide review of design intent; they are not statutory zoning or approved development scale.
A-CULTURE-001 — Historical narrative of the Jing-Zhang Railway (1909 opening, Zhan Tianyou, Qinglongqiao switchback) follows the cited public authoritative sources; detailed wording and any heritage-site interpretation (e.g., former Qinghuayuan Station) require cultural-heritage professional review.Impact: Landmark siting near heritage assets is directional only; heritage protection scopes are not drawn because no official file is available.
A-CASES-001 — The six global ecosystem case summaries are digests of public common knowledge used for mechanism transfer; individual figures were not re-verified for this submission.Impact: Cases support mechanism design, not quantitative benchmarking.
A-OPERATION-001 — The Switchback Protocol, event system, Crew community, junction joint ventures, Platform Covenant, and all operating mechanisms are conceptual suggestions with no government or investor commitment.Impact: Operational content is evaluated as design thinking; any implementation requires lawful approval, security assessment, and stakeholder agreement.
