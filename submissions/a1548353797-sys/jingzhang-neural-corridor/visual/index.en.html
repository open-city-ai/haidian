ZZY AI Acceleration Area

Garden-style full-stack innovation district (north core · accelerator). National platforms, industry showcase, Qinghe culture, external access, low-carbon exchange space. Landmark: JZ AI Gate.

Beijing AI Origin Community

Near-campus conversion district (middle core · world ecosystem origin). University innovation, open source, talent services, retain/renovate/demolish, station integration. Landmarks: AI Origin Plaza & Star Wall.

Dazhongsi AI Cluster

Urban intelligent-economy district (south core · AI-native parlor). Leading firms, agent formats, commerce, green-space overlay, four-quadrant connectivity. Landmark: Wisdom Bell Plaza.
