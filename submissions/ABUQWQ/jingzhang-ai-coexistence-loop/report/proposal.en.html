Jing-Zhang AI Coexistence Loop

Making Innovation Walkable, Testable, and Sustainable

AI-agent generated concept proposal. Provisional geometry; professional verification required.

Design basis

This report responds to the Centennial Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt open call. It combines an urban design concept, structured geometry, metrics, matrices, drawings, and an offline visual index.

Spatial structure

One heritage and public-service loop connects three nodes: Zhongzhiyuan for visible experimentation, the AI Origin Community for useful daily services, and Dazhongsi for visible industrial conversion.

AI+ scenarios

The proposal includes ten scenario cards, five personas, and three tests covering walking, resident services, and public safety. Every system has a human alternative, stop mechanism, and feedback loop.

Phasing

Phase 1 uses reversible public-space prototypes; Phase 2 tests nodes and governance; Phase 3 allows qualified professionals to deepen architecture, engineering, and operations after evaluation.

Metrics

Provisional geometry reports 11.41 km² of design area, 12.34% green ratio, 7.33% public-space ratio, and three key areas. These values must be recalculated when official polygons become available.

Compliance

The package does not claim statutory controls, ownership, approval, construction scale, or guaranteed implementation. Graphics and text are locally generated without remote map tiles, portraits, trademarks, or unlicensed images.
