# Jing-Zhang AI Coexistence Loop: Making Innovation Walkable, Testable, and Sustainable

## Design Basis and Source List

This proposal responds to the open call for the Centennial Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt. It is submitted as an urban-design concept, a verifiable data package, and an offline exhibition page. Its basis includes the public brief, agent taskbook, site package, source registry, design-space rules, and standards matrix [source:OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT] [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK] [source:SITE-PACKAGE] [source:SOURCE-REGISTRY].

The current site package contains provisional research boundaries and provisional geometries for three key areas. It does not provide official polygons that can serve as statutory redlines. All areas, positions, and layers in this package are conceptual research inputs and do not represent regulatory plans, ownership, engineering, approval, or government implementation conclusions [source:BOUNDARY-SOURCE] [source:KEY-AREA-SOURCE] [depth:existing_conditions_diagnosis].

The proposition is simple: **AI should not remain inside buildings; it should become a visible, usable, governable, and continuously improved public capability.** The Coexistence Loop uses the Jing-Zhang heritage park as a public spine and links three AI nodes for experimentation, daily life, and industrial conversion.

## Three-Level Scope Framework

- **Coordinated research area**: approximately 43.6 square kilometres for regional innovation networks, talent flows, and Jing-Jin-Ji collaboration. No statutory boundary is modified [data:geometry/site_boundary.geojson#SITE-001].
- **Overall design area**: approximately 11.4 square kilometres around the heritage park, organized as one belt, three nodes, and five connections [metric:site_area_sqm].
- **Key areas**: Zhongzhiyuan AI Acceleration Area, Beijing AI Origin Community, and Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster, treated as three urban prototypes for experiment, living, and conversion [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-001] [metric:key_area_count].

The structure is a research framework for professional deepening, not a new engineering or statutory boundary.

## Coordinated Research Area: Industry and Future City Research

### Overall concept and functional coordination

The brand is **JZ-AI COMMON LOOP / 京张AI共生环**. Its identity combines two railway lines with three open-source nodes. Rust red refers to heritage, smart gold to interaction, night blue to safety and legibility, and park green to ecological infrastructure. Five functional layers structure the belt: AI research and validation; enterprise services and conversion; community life and care; cultural exchange; and public governance [task:agent.1] [standard:PROJECT-OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT].

### Industry and future-city research

The system cycles through compute, data, talent, scenarios, and governance. Zhongzhiyuan hosts early experiments and developer co-creation; the AI Origin Community hosts everyday services; Dazhongsi hosts enterprise showcases and conversion. The three nodes exchange open challenge briefs, testing vouchers, short residencies, and review evidence [task:agent.2].

## Overall Design Area: Urban Renewal and Regulatory-Plan-Level Urban Design

The concept proposes one heritage–walking–public-service loop, three AI nodes, and five connections: cultural, pedestrian, service, data, and talent. Existing buildings and communities are retained first; reversible public-space upgrades come before heavy construction. No parcel demolition/retention plan, FAR, building-height conclusion, road redline, or ownership claim is made [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK] [data:geometry/constraints.geojson#CONSTRAINTS] [depth:urban_renewal_and_regulatory_design].

## Detailed Design of Key Areas

- **Zhongzhiyuan AI Acceleration Area — visible experimentation**: an open test street for robotics, accessible navigation, environmental sensing, and low-speed delivery, with public dashboards and human safety points [task:agent.2] [task:agent.3].
- **Beijing AI Origin Community — useful everyday services**: an Origin Living Room with wayfinding, public-service navigation, health referrals, child learning, elder support, paper alternatives, and a human review desk [task:agent.3] [task:agent.5].
- **Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster — visible conversion**: a public evaluation promenade and application theatre where products disclose methods, limits, accessibility, privacy, and social-impact status [task:agent.4].

## AI Innovation Ecosystem, Personas, and AI+ Scenarios

The proposed precedent library covers open-street laboratories, heritage-park night culture, civic data trusts, accessible navigation, low-speed delivery, developer residencies, community care, and public algorithm audits. These are transferable patterns, not claims about existing local projects [task:agent.2].

Five users are prioritized: developers, residents, older adults and children, enterprises and investors, and city managers. The design provides testing vouchers, public challenge briefs, human alternatives, voice and low-vision access, transparent evaluation, and incident review.

Ten scenario cards are included in the Chinese primary report: heritage guide; accessible walking navigation; community service copilot; elder digital coaching; child AI-safety lab; low-speed delivery trial; thermal-comfort prompts; night-event coordination; enterprise challenge matching; and anonymized safety review [task:agent.3] [task:agent.5] [task:agent.6].

Three validation tests are defined: a multi-ability walking test; a resident service test with free switching between AI and human support; and a safety test with stop, takeover, and post-event review mechanisms.

## Land Use, Building Scale, and Retain-Renovate-Demolish Strategy

The `LAND_USE` and building layers are conceptual design layers, not statutory controls. Mixed blocks combine research, service, community, and open-space functions. The sequence is retain first, reversible renewal second, limited new construction third. Any actual demolition, FAR, height, fire, structural, or ownership conclusion requires official data and qualified professional review [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK] [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-001] [data:geometry/buildings.geojson#BLDG-001].

## Transport, Rail, Municipal Infrastructure, and Public Services

The mobility proposal focuses on experience and management interfaces rather than road or rail engineering. The loop provides shade, lighting, seating, water, toilets, accessible wayfinding, and human assistance. Its 15-minute service chain links the three nodes through public services and walking-scale amenities [data:geometry/roads.geojson#ROAD-001] [depth:mobility_and_municipal_strategy]. Utility capacity, energy load, underground feasibility, and detailed transport engineering remain verification items [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK].

## Blue-Green Network, Public Space, and Urban Character

The heritage park becomes a continuous public spine, supported by shaded pockets, rain gardens, shared lawns, and night plazas. Public environmental prompts disclose heat comfort, noise periods, and rainwater risk without exposing personal data. Current ratios are computed from provisional geometry and must be recalculated after official polygons are available [metric:green_ratio] [metric:public_space_ratio].

## Renewal Projects, Implementation Policy, and Phasing

Five concept projects are proposed: the Coexistence Main Loop; the Co-creation Accelerator; the AI Origin Living Room; the Public Evaluation Promenade; and a three-landmark pilgrimage set. Phase 1 (0–6 months) uses reversible public-space and service prototypes; Phase 2 (6–18 months) tests nodes and governance; Phase 3 deepens architecture, engineering, and operations after evaluation [task:agent.6] [data:geometry/phasing.geojson#PHASE-001].
