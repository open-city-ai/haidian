Overview Map · Overall Concept

Overall design area (provisional): approx. 11.41 km² Coordinated research area: approx. 43.6 km² Key areas: 3 · approx. 368.4 ha

The overall concept "Jing-Zhang AI Neural Corridor" (JZ·NEURAL) translates the century-old Jing-Zhang Railway transport nervous system into an urban AI neural network. The heritage park green spine and slow-mobility spine form the neural spine; Zhongzhiyuan (Training Synapse), the Origin Community (Perception Synapse), and Dazhongsi (Application Synapse) form the three synapse nodes; the Zhongguancun Technology Service Wing and the Xiaoyue River Scenario Empowerment Wing form the two plexus wings; ten scenario cards (SC-01~10) form multi-point AI scenarios. Naming, logo direction, and visual identity are covered under Task Coverage and in proposal.md.

Spatial figures: site-overview.png embedded in proposal.md; provisional boundary for generation, display, and self-check only, not an official redline.

Mobility & Blue-Green Public Space

The neural spine slow axis runs north-south; eight east-west connectors form eight stitch points; Wudaokou, Qinghua East Road West, and Dazhongsi rail stations follow station-city integration. Blue-green system "one spine, two rivers, one network": green spine plus Qinghe and Xiaoyue River corridors; green ratio approx. 23.7%, public-space ratio approx. 23.5%.

Public space nodes: station-front plazas, open-source plaza, contributor honor wall, international roadshow lounge, community parks - together with the green spine forming a walkable, assemblable, and displayable public-space network.
