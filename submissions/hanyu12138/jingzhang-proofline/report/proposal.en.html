2. Renewal method: survey, classify, act

Without rights-cleared existing-building and ownership data, the proposal makes no parcel-specific demolish, retain or build decision. Urban Renewal uses four gates. Gate A checks ownership, use, age, structure and fire safety. Gate B identifies heritage, social, public-service, affordable-space and mature-tree value. Gate C compares life-cycle impacts of retention, repair, adaptive reuse and partial replacement. Gate D reaches a conclusion through public participation and statutory procedures. Only then may a site enter a professional Demolish-Renovate-Retain Strategy. 空间数据 深度

The 24 approximate masses in buildings.geojson are conceptual capacity prototypes, not surveyed buildings. They test coexistence between shared ground floors, research courtyards, talent housing, education collaboration, community services and open space. Conceptual footprints total about 175.4 ha, Building Coverage Ratio about 15.4%, floor-area capacity about 859.4 ha and a capacity ratio about 0.75. All are low confidence; statutory floor_area_ratio is pending official data. 指标 指标
