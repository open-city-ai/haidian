# Pilgrimage Path of Open Source: Centennial Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt Urban Design

## Design Basis and Source List

This proposal is a formal submission for the "Centennial Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt Urban Design Open Call." The design criteria are divided into three layers: the first layer is the official announcement and task book, which includes the 'Centennial Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt Urban Design International Scheme Qualification Pre-Review Announcement' [source:OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT] issued by the Haidian Branch of the Beijing Municipal Commission of Planning and Natural Resources, and the excerpt from the 'Task Book for Conducting the Centennial Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt Urban Design Open Call for Submissions' provided and cleared by the user [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK]. (Centennial Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt Open Call for Urban Design) The second layer is the machine-readable task packages within the warehouse `brief/site-package/`, including `design_brief.json`, `allowed_design_space.json`, `agent_taskbook.json`, `sources.json`, `enums/`, `ranges/planning_limits.json`, `schemas/`, and `standards/standards.json` [source:SITE-PACKAGE]; the third layer is the public data registry `data/source_registry.json` [source:SOURCE-REGISTRY] and the processed reading navigation layer `data/processed/agent_fact_pack.md` [source:PROCESSED-FACT-PACK].

In accordance with the requirements of [standard:PROJECT-OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT], the scheme must achieve the depth of Urban Design in both the Regulatory Detailed Planning and the Integrated Planning Implementation Plan; in accordance with the requirements of [standard:PROJECT-AGENT-OPEN-CALL-TASKBOOK], the scheme must expand upon six intelligent body tasks: naming system, AI ecological case studies, scenario cards, pilgrimage landmarks, cultural narratives, and long-term operations. This plan aligns with [standard:MOHURD-URBAN-DESIGN-MEASURES] (Urban Design should implement planning, shape the character, coordinate Public Space and building control), [standard:MOHURD-CONTROL-DETAILED-PLANNING] (differentiate known control conditions, design recommendations, and pending confirmations), and [standard:MNR-LAND-USE-CLASSIFICATION-GUIDE] (land use classification adopts the logic of the land and sea use classification in the spatial plan), and expresses the results with the depth and organization of drawings as per [standard:MOHURD-ARCH-DESIGN-DEPTH-2016].

Important boundary statement: As of the submission date, the official `SITE_BOUNDARY` and three `KEY_AREA` precise polygons have not been included in the public task package. This plan uses the provisional range marked as `provisional_rough` in `brief/site-package/geometry/provisional_boundaries.geojson` [source:BOUNDARY-SOURCE][source:KEY-AREA-SOURCE]. This Provisional Boundary is only for the purpose of plan generation, self-check, and presentation and does not constitute the Official Planning Boundary, approval basis, or accurate area recalculation basis. Once official data is released, the site boundary, key areas, land use, buildings, roads, green space, public space, phasing, and all area category indicators must be recalculated. The organizers' data gaps do not block the content evaluation of this proposal; related limitations are recorded in `assumptions.json` under A-CONTROLS-001 and in `sources.json`. The depth of the current condition diagnosis for this section is [depth:existing_conditions_diagnosis], and the registration of data gaps is found in [depth:risk_missing_data].

## Three-Level Scope Framework

The work scope is divided into the Coordinated Research Area (approximately 43.6 square kilometers, north to the North Fifth Ring Road, east to the Jingzhang Expressway, south to West Straight Street, west to Wanquanhe Road), the Overall Design Area (approximately 11.4 square kilometers, the urban and industrial areas within 1-2 kilometers around the Jingzhang Site Park), and the Key-Area Detailed Design Area (approximately 368.4 hectares, from north to south: Zhongzhiyuan AI Independent Innovation Acceleration Area, Beijing AI Origin Community, and Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster) [source:OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT][standard:PROJECT-OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT]. The work logic across the three levels is: the Coordinated Research addresses "what the Innovation Belt is in terms of industrial and urban strategy," the Overall Design addresses "how the spatial structure and update framework are to be implemented," and the Key-Area addresses "how the three core areas can achieve the depth required for detailed design." (Jing-Zhang) Each layer is registered as 1.4.1/1.4.2/1.4.3 in `compliance_matrix.json` and is mapped downward to the corresponding entries in `standard_matrix.json` and `design_depth_matrix.json` [depth:three_level_scope_framework].

This scheme's spatial working framework is "One Belt, Three Cores, Two Wings, and Multiple Composite Points": One Belt refers to the Jing-Zhang Heritage Park Vitality Belt (this scheme's "Open Source Pilgrimage Main Axis"), Three Cores refer to the three key areas determined by the announcement, Two Wings refer to the Zhongguancun Technology Services Wing and the Xiaoyue River Scenario Enablement Wing, and Multiple Composite Points refer to the AI scenario nodes and the composite loop of active travel, blue-green spaces, and activities along the main axis. This framework is not an addition of new red lines, but rather translates the announced three-layer scope and the Three Zones and Two Wings into a workable structural framework for drafting, corresponding to [data:geometry/site_boundary.geojson#SITE-001] (Provisional Boundary of the Overall Design Area) and [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-001][data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-002][data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-003] (provisional boundaries of the three key areas), with areas based on [metric:site_area_sqm] and [metric:key_area_count]. Given that the boundaries are provisional, all "structure, proportion, scale" in this section are expressed as design recommendations rather than legal conclusions [depth:overall_spatial_structure].

The three layers of scope are progressively implemented in the work: the industrial judgment at the integrated research level (university origin—open-source collaboration—enterprise transformation—public experience—international dissemination) is incorporated into the spatial structure and update framework at the overall design level, and then into the land use, buildings, transportation, Public Space, and scenario design at the key area level. To support this implementation, the land use zoning ([data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-001]), road framework ([data:geometry/roads.geojson#RD-001]), green network ([data:geometry/green_space.geojson#GS-001]), and public space network ([data:geometry/public_space.geojson#PS-001]) are all derived from a set of Provisional Boundary zones. Adjacent parcels share boundaries, ensuring seamless and gap-free connections, thus guaranteeing that any layer can be recalculated and revert to the element layer.

## Coordinated Research Area: Industry and Future City Research

The core proposition of the Coordinated Research Area is to construct a world-class AI Innovation Ecosystem, and to organize the spatial narrative around three thematic belts: the century-old Jing-Zhang Cultural Belt, the Urban AI Living Experience Belt, and the AI Integration Innovation Belt [source:OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT][standard:PROJECT-AGENT-OPEN-CALL-TASKBOOK]. This plan proposes the **"Open-Source Pilgrimage Trail" (Open-Source Pilgrimage Trail, abbreviated OSPT)** as the overarching concept for one of the belts: connecting the century-old spirit of the Jing-Zhang Railway, which was "self-designed and self-constructed," with the new culture of the open-source community, which is "openly co-built and honors contributors." This will make the innovation belt a digital cultural pilgrimage site for global AI developers and innovators. The naming system includes three levels: the total name of the belt "Open-Source Pilgrimage Trail," the main spatial axis name "Jing-Zhang Open-Source Spine," and the annual public event name "Open-Source Pilgrimage Festival." Logo and visual identity direction adopt the "railway tracks × Fork (open-source branch)" graphic: the parallel lines of the Jing-Zhang Railway sleepers symbolize the steel rails, splitting into the curves of open-source code branches at the endpoints, forming an identifier that resembles both a railway switch and a version branch; the main colors are Jing-Zhang rust red (cultural base color), AI aqua green (innovative base color), and Haidian blue (technological base color), the fonts and layout are based on an engineering blueprint [depth:overall_spatial_structure]. This identifier is a conceptual direction and, without authorization, does not use any real enterprise or institution trademarks or images [standard:PROJECT-AGENT-OPEN-CALL-TASKBOOK].

Benchmarking against the global AI Innovation Ecosystem, the proposal selects 8 publicly verifiable ecosystem cases as a reference library: Silicon Valley,  (Stanford University—venture capital—open-source software companies coupling), Boston Kendall Square,  (research institutions coexisting with biotech/robotics industries), one-north, Singapore (government-led mixed-use park), King's Cross, London, UK (railway industrial heritage updated into a knowledge innovation district), Nanshan District, Shenzhen (hardware innovation and complete industrial chain), Future Science City, Hangzhou (digital economy and cloud ecosystem), High-tech Zone, Hefei (AI and voice recognition cluster), and Adlershof, Berlin, Germany (science city and startup ecosystem). These cases are distilled into five transferable experiences in this proposal: leveraging universities and research institutions as the innovation source, fostering open-source communities as the innovation collaboration layer, showcasing public experience spaces as the ecosystem display layer, converting Testing and Validation Scenarios into industry transformation layers, and attracting talent and brand influence through international events [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK]. Experience transformation into spatial anchoring points is as follows: the strategy source anchor points correspond to the university retention zone ([data:geometry/constraints.geojson#CON-EDU-001]), the collaborative layer corresponds to the "Intelligent Body Co-Research Street" in Zhongzhiyuan, the display layer corresponds to the open-source display corridor on the main axis, the transformation layer corresponds to three Testing and Validation Scenarios, and the gravity layer corresponds to the pilgrimage landmark and annual events.

In terms of the future urban form, the proposal suggests an "AI-Native urban three-layer structure": physical layer (land use, buildings, roads, utilities) + digital layer (edge-side computing power, perception networks, Urban Agents) + operational layer (Scenario Access, test schedules, community activities). This structure does not replace statutory planning but serves as a comprehensive research conceptual framework [standard:MOHURD-URBAN-DESIGN-MEASURES], maintaining strict correspondence with formal-ready sources [source:SOURCE-REGISTRY]. Background and provisional sources are only used for discussion. The conclusions of the comprehensive research layer are ultimately realized through the land use structure at the overall design layer ([data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-001]) and the two wings' spaces ([source:AGENT-TASKBOOK]'s Three Zones and Two Wings), forming an evidence loop at [depth:land_use_layout].

## Overall Design Area: Urban Renewal and Regulatory-Plan-Level Urban Design

The Overall Design Area (approximately 11.4 square kilometers, [metric:site_area_sqm]) requires Urban Design at the depth of control detailed planning [standard:PROJECT-OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT][standard:MOHURD-CONTROL-DETAILED-PLANNING]. The spatial structure of the plan is "one axis, three cores, five belts, two wings, and a circular pedestrian network": one axis is the Jing-Zhang Open Source Axis (the main axis for the pilgrimage of open source, [data:geometry/roads.geojson#RD-001] greenway and pedestrian path combined); three cores are three key areas; five belts are five east-west urban functional belts (industrial belt, educational belt, residential belt, commercial belt, blue-green belt), corresponding to the secondary road framework of [data:geometry/roads.geojson#RD-002] to [data:geometry/roads.geojson#RD-006]; two wings are the Zhongguancun Technology Services Wing and the Xiaoyue River Scenario Enablement Wing. (Regulatory Detailed Planning) Urban Renewal is organized in a framework of "preserving as the base, updating as the path, leaving blank spaces as strategy, and new construction as supplementation": Higher education institutions, cultural heritage sites, and mature residential areas focus on preservation ([data:geometry/constraints.geojson#CON-EDU-001][data:geometry/constraints.geojson#CON-EDU-002]), while outdated industrial buildings and inefficient land use focus on functional updates. Key areas reserve "blank space" for AI autonomous innovation and future industries ([data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-001] class `16`), and new construction focuses on incubators, laboratories, talent apartments, and cultural displays [depth:retain_renovate_demolish][depth:height_massing_character].

The proposal only outlines conceptual control principles: the Building Height along the Jing-Zhang main axis transitions from south to north and from the core area to the periphery, with mid-to-low intensity mixed-use districts as the main feature on both sides of the axis. In key areas and core nodes, high-intensity landmark buildings are allowed but must coordinate with the cultural heritage line-of-sight corridors; the Building Footprint ([data:geometry/buildings.geojson#BLD-001])To express spatial structure rather than engineering scale, the total area is see [metric:building_footprint_area_sqm] All conclusions are marked with "Subject to Master Plan Attachment Confirmation."[depth:development_intensity_controls].

## Detailed Design of Key Areas

The three key areas are organized in detail according to "positioning + spatial structure + building renewal + traffic slow zones + Public Space + AI scenarios + implementation risks," referencing [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-001][data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-002][data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-003] and [depth:three_key_area_detailed_design]. As all three areas are provisional, all conclusions are directionally designed.

**Zhongzhiyuan AI Independent Innovation Acceleration Area** (approximately 192.1 hectares, North Core): positioned as an "AI Full Stack Independent Innovation Accelerator and Co-creation District for Intelligent Agents." The spatial structure is "West Research, East Preservation, Central Pilgrimage": the west side is dedicated to research and development land for AI (as defined by `0802` in [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-001]), the central area features cultural landmarks such as a shrine for the honor wall of intelligent agents and AI milestone nodes, while the east side is left blank for future independent innovation use [depth:land_use_layout]. Building updates focus on upgrading industrial office functions, with transportation and pedestrian access facilitated by secondary roads [data:geometry/roads.geojson#RD-005] connecting to the main axis greenway [data:geometry/roads.geojson#RD-001], and Public Spaces are designated as "Pilgrimage Square" (as defined by [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#PS-001]). The AI scenario is "Collaborative Development of Intelligent Agents + Full Stack Independent Innovation Testing." Implementation Risks: Complex ownership of industrial buildings and uncertain policies regarding vacant plots require a specialized team for further refinement.

**Beijing AI Origin Community** (approximately 104.3 hectares, Zhonghuan): positioned as the "Global AI Talent and Open Source Community Origin." The spatial structure is "west market east research, with a central exhibition zone": the western commercial street (represented by `05` in [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-001]) provides talent living services, while the eastern research and university collaboration area (represented by `0804` in [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-001], adjacent to universities) focuses on research and higher education. The central cultural belt features an "Open Source Results Exhibition Corridor" and an "Open Source Square" (represented by [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#PS-001]). Transportation is based on [data:geometry/roads.geojson#RD-003] and rail connections (indicated by [data:geometry/roads.geojson#RD-008] pointing towards Huairou direction). AI scenario for "AI + Education and Open Source Community Creation + Talent Apartment Life Services" (Public Space type in [data:geometry/buildings.geojson#BLD-001]). Implementation risk: Coordinating with the university boundary and campus renewal is complex, and the public space renovation needs to be phased.

**Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster** (approximately 72.0 hectares, South Core): positioned as an "integrated AI industry and commercial experience cluster with station-city integration." The spatial structure is defined as "west industry, east commerce, and station forecourt": western research and development land (classified as `0802` in [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-001]), eastern commercial land (classified as `05`), and the station forecourt (classified as `1403` in [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-001] and [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#PS-001]) to organize pedestrian flow. Transportation relies on [data:geometry/roads.geojson#RD-002] and the connection to the transit-oriented development (TOD) at the station. The AI scenario includes "AI + commercial experience + robot delivery pilot" (from the [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK] scenario library). Implementation Risks: Integrated development of the site involves the alignment of rail rights-of-way with municipal infrastructure, requiring specialized analysis [depth:traffic_rail_slow_parking].
