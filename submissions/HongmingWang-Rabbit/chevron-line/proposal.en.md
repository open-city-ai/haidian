# The Chevron Line — Centennial Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt. Subtitle: Two Legs, One Climb.

One sentence: Let this corridor, which has been divided by the railway for a hundred years, become a path that people can walk through and are willing to walk.

Contributor: Enter OS · Hongming (GitHub: HongmingWang-Rabbit) · Generated by: Claude Opus 5

Boundary Statement: All spatial recommendations in this document are Conceptual Recommendations and reference solutions, intended for further in-depth study by professional teams; they do not replace statutory planning, nor do they constitute government approval conclusions, approval bases, engineering feasibility judgments, or investment and implementation commitments.

## One. Design Basis and Reference Materials

This scheme is based on the first reference to the announcement issued by the Haidian Branch of the Beijing Municipal Commission of Planning and Natural Resources titled 'Centennial Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt Urban Design International Scheme Qualification Pre-Review Announcement' [source:DATA-SRC-OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT-20260509], and the second reference to the excerpt from the open call task book for global intelligent entities [source:DATA-SRC-AGENT-TASKBOOK-20260518]. The former stipulates the design objectives (1.3), three layers of scope and area (1.4), design content (1.5), and the depth of the results; the latter supplements the three major positions, five major functions, Three Zones and Two Wings, ten co-creation principles, and six mandatory tasks for agents.1 to agent.6, as well as the unified boundary clause. Both are mapped to 23 mandatory tasks in the `compliance_matrix.json`.

The machine-readable basis for the proposal comes from the repository task book data package [source:SITE-PACKAGE], including `design_brief.json`, `allowed_design_space.json`, `enums/`, `ranges/planning_limits.json`, and `schemas/`. Availability of the data is judged from the public data registry [source:SOURCE-REGISTRY]: this proposal uses only the data where `usable_for_formal="yes"`, and strictly limits the use of data marked `provisional_only` to temporary generation, visualization, and self-inspection purposes. The organization of chapters and tasks references the repository processed fact pack [source:PROCESSED-FACT-PACK], but all factual judgments still refer back to the original sources. The basis for professional standards is not external links but local reference snapshots in the repository [source:STANDARDS-LOCAL-REFERENCES], corresponding to [standard:PROJECT-OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT], [standard:PROJECT-AGENT-OPEN-CALL-TASKBOOK], [standard:MOHURD-URBAN-DESIGN-MEASURES], [standard:MOHURD-CONTROL-DETAILED-PLANNING], and [standard:MNR-LAND-USE-CLASSIFICATION-GUIDE] five mandatory standards, as well as the registered [standard:MOHURD-ARCH-DESIGN-DEPTH-2016] for supplementary materials.

![Overall conceptual structure diagram: Main Ridge, Five Gable Arms, Three Key Areas, and Two Wing Interfaces](assets/figures/site-overview.png)

The current diagnosis makes judgments that can be supported by evidence within the existing data gaps [depth:existing_conditions_diagnosis]. The core issues of the corridor are fourfold: it is divided east-west by the railway corridor, which has long split the two sides into non-communicating halves; it is discontinuous north-south, with segments frequently bisected by major roads, making it difficult to walk through in one go; the interface is passive, with the two sides long characterized by backs, walls, and temporary uses; and innovative elements are nearby but unconnected—universities, institutes, enterprises, and communities are spatially close but lack public spaces where daily exchanges can occur. These four judgments can be directly explained by the geometric form and land organization of the corridor, without relying on unobtained current census data. As for building quality, ownership, population, and industrial base data, these are listed as pending data to be collected [depth:risk_missing_data] and are not estimated.

## II. Work Framework for the Second and Third Floors

The three levels provided in the announcement are not three maps, but rather three types of conclusion categories [depth:three_level_scope_framework]. The Coordinated Research Area of 43.6 km² addresses "how the AI Innovation Ecosystem and the future urban form should be organized," and outputs strategies, mechanisms, and relationship judgments; the Overall Design Area of 11.4 km² addresses "where these judgments should be implemented on the ground, on which lines, and on which interfaces," and outputs spatial structure, land use, facilities, and an update framework; the key areas of 368.4 ha address "whether these can actually be implemented," and output district-level spatial actions, project levers, and prerequisite conditions. The relationship between the three levels is one of transmission: the upper level does not leave room for the lower level, so the lower level has no basis; if the lower level cannot be implemented, the strategies of the upper level are just empty talk.

Three key areas extend from north to south as follows: Zhongzhiyuan AI Independent Innovation Acceleration Area (approximately 192.1 hectares, [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-001]), Beijing AI Origin Community (approximately 104.3 hectares, [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-002]), and Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster (approximately 72.0 hectares, [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-003]), totaling [metric:key_area_count] areas. These three areas do not overlap in the submitted geometry and are all located within the design scope, but their boundaries are derived from temporary rough estimates [source:DATA-SRC-PROVISIONAL-BOUNDARIES-20260605]. They are only verified and positioned based on their north-south order and area as announced. Therefore, this plan describes all key areas at the structural relationship and spatial action level, without specifying plot-level boundaries, ownership, or demolition conclusions.

![Land Use Structure and Three-Layer Scope Transmission Diagram: From Comprehensive Research to Master Planning and then to Functional Implementation in Key Areas](assets/figures/land-use-structure.png)

The three-story framework simultaneously determines the expression discipline [standard:PROJECT-OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT] of this scheme: anything that can be recalculated from GeoJSON (area, ratio, length, quantity) should be written as a number and accompanied by a formula; anything that can only be qualitatively judged (ecological mechanisms, cultural narratives, operational pathways) should be described as mechanisms and processes, not as indicators; and anything lacking in data (Floor Area Ratio, height, population, ownership, cultural protection lines) should be left blank and the path to data supplementation should be clearly stated.

## Three. Coordinated Research Area: Industrial and Future City Research

The task of the Coordinated Research Area is to build a world-class AI Innovation Ecosystem. This plan does not repeat descriptions like "comprehensive elements, active innovation," but rather provides a testable criterion: the uniqueness of this strip does not lie in the number of innovation elements it possesses, but in the fact that these elements are separated by only a corridor. Universities, national-level research platforms, leading enterprises, startup teams, and residential communities are in close proximity within a straight-line distance, but are cut off into disconnected segments by railway corridors, major roads, and walls. Therefore, the primary work of the coordinated research area is not to attract new elements, but to reduce the distance between existing elements from "proximity on a map" to "proximity on the ground."

Around this judgment, this proposal reviews seven verifiable international and local practices for mechanism analogy rather than performance indicators. The following information is derived from publicly available urban and planning documents of each project, and does not include unverified data such as investment amounts, revenue figures, or company lists, nor does it constitute endorsement of any party [source:DATA-SRC-AGENT-TASKBOOK-20260518]:

- 1 | Referenced Practice: Paris Station F (Entrepreneurial Campus Adapted from the Halle Freyssinet Railway Freight Station) | Common Features: The railway heritage building is directly repurposed as an innovation carrier | Conversion Mechanism: A single large-span structure accommodates three scales of activities—workstations, events, and exhibitions—forming an open innovation living room | Notable Differences: This corridor is primarily linear, lacking a single large-scale heritage building of equivalent size, necessitating a multi-point connection approach instead of a single-point focus
- 2 | Referenced Practice: London King's Cross (Railway Brownfield Integrated Update) | Commonalities with This District: Railway Brownfield Update + Knowledge Industry Cluster + Continuous Public Space | Transformation Mechanism: Update Sequence Prioritizing Public Space and Phased Release of Sites | Notable Differences: Ownership and Development Subject Structure are Different, This Plan Does Not Propose Arrangements for Land Ownership and Development Subjects
- 3 | Reference Practice: Seoul Seoullo 7017 (Abandoned Railway Transformed into Linear Park) | Commonalities with the Current Site: The linear railway space has been transformed into urban Public Space, stitching together both sides. | Transformative Mechanism: Continuous pedestrian access along the line with frequent small-scale nodes, rather than a few large plazas. | Key Differences to Note: The Seoul case often involved the complete cessation of ground-level rail operations, whereas this site must coordinate with existing operational conditions.
- 4 | Referenced Practice: New York High Line (Abandoned Elevated Railway Turned Park) | Commonalities with the Site: The heritage structure itself becomes a narrative carrier | Conversion Mechanism: Retain structural traces as part of the path and display | Notable Differences: Avoid creating only a tourist-oriented landscape; this plan prioritizes daily use.
- 5 | Reference Practice: 22@Barcelona (Industrial Zone Transformed into Knowledge Industry Zone) | Commonalities with the Current Site: Conversion of Existing Industrial and Warehouse Spaces to Innovative Functions | Transformation Mechanism: Guiding Path for the Update of Mixed-Use Functions and Supporting Infrastructure through Planning Tools | Notable Differences: Involvement of Statutory Planning Tools, This Proposal Only Presents an Approach and Does Not Suggest Specific Zoning Adjustments
- 6 | Reference Practice: Singapore One-North (Research-Industry-Housing Mixed Zone) | Common Features with the Belt: Proximity of R&D, Pilot Testing, Residences, and Public Services | Transformation Mechanism: Incorporating Pilot Testing and Testing Spaces as Planning Elements Rather Than Ancillary Facilities | Notable Differences: Different Conditions for Greenfield Development, with the Belt Focused Primarily on Incremental Updates
- 7 | Referenced Practice: Experience of Zhongguancun in Beijing (From Electronic Street to Innovation District) | Commonalities with the Current Initiative: Verified "University—Enterprise—Community" Coupling within the Local Context | Transformable Mechanism: Service and Exchange Spaces at the Street Scale Are the Real Carriers of Coupling | Notable Differences: New Types of Public Spaces Focused on Open Source and Collaborative Agent Engagement Must Be Added

Thus forming the four strategies for the Coordinated Research Area: Firstly, transform the corridor into a public space, making the relic park a public walkway between elements rather than background greenery; secondly, treat testing as infrastructure, considering pilot testing, validation testing, and Scenario Access sites as infrastructure to be included in spatial supply rather than subject to post facto relocation; thirdly, shift services to the streets, placing enterprise services, talent services, and open-source community services at nodes along the route that are within walking distance, rather than concentrating them in a single large hall; fourthly, use the wings as exchange points, with the Zhongguancun Technology Services Wing and the Xiaoyue River Scenario Enablement Wing primarily located within the coordinated research area but outside the design area. This plan only expresses their interface segments within the design area, as seen in [data:geometry/constraints.geojson#AIZ-004].

Future city forms adapted to the new quality of productivity from AI are concretized in this proposal as three spatial requirements [standard:MOHURD-URBAN-DESIGN-MEASURES]: testable (with continuous spaces that allow for controlled experiments and clear boundaries), observable (with public visual interfaces for invisible infrastructure such as computational power, sensing, and scheduling), and exitable (with spaces and processes for deactivation, degradation, and manual take-over for any AI scenario). These three requirements directly determine the subsequent design of Public Spaces, nodes, and scenarios.

## Four. Overall Design Area Urban Renewal and Control Detailed Urban Design

### 4.1 Overall Concept: Diagonal Line

The proposal is named "The Chevron Line." It refers to two things. The first is a historical engineering fact: the Jing-Zhang Railway, completed in 1909 under the supervision of Zhan Tianyou, used a "chevron" alignment in the Guan Gou section at Qinglong Bridge to solve the slope problem—by making a single reversal, it achieved the height that would have been impossible to climb otherwise. The second is the stance of this proposal towards AI cities: all intelligent entities, algorithms, and scenarios must ultimately return to human daily life and dignity. The "chevron" is not just a shape but also a standard of judgment. It is important to note that the chevron alignment is located in the Guan Gou section, not within the scope of this design. This proposal cites it as a cultural theme, without asserting its spatial location within this band [source:DATA-SRC-OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT-20260509].

The conceptual structure is "one ridge, five person-shaped elements, three cores, and two wings" [depth:overall_spatial_structure]:

- One Spine: Along the Jing-Zhang Heritage Park, the main spine runs north-south, with a continuous length for pedestrian use of [metric:spine_greenway_length_m] (9,749.5 m, approximately 9.75 km), as seen in [data:geometry/roads.geojson#ROAD-001]. It is the only linear feature in the entire corridor that can be walked through in one continuous stretch.
- Five Person Characters: At Dazhongsi, Zaojun Temple, AI Origin, Xiaoyuehe, and Zhongzhiyuan, set "person" shaped east-west stitching arms—two diagonal pedestrian arms intersecting at a vertex on the main spine to form a vertex square. The total length of the stitching paths is [metric:stitch_corridor_length_m] (8,371.3 m, approximately 8.37 km), geometrically defined in [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#PS-CHV-3]. This is the most core spatial action of the entire plan: it transforms "east-west stitching" from a slogan into five measurable, phased, and demonstrable concrete engineering projects.
- Three Cores: Each of the three key areas will undertake a set of functions without overlapping positioning (see Section Five).
- Two Wings: The interface platform between the Zhongguancun Technology Services Wing and the Xiaoyue River Scenario Enablement Wing within the design scope, see [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#PS-WING-W].

Choose the "human" character over the common "horizontal connection," for three spatial reasons: first, the diagonal arms are longer than vertical connections, allowing more street blocks on both sides to be included in a 400-meter walking circle under the same land conditions; second, the apex naturally forms a centripetal Public Space, requiring no additional land for a large plaza; and third, the five apices arranged along the main spine create a recognizable, nameable, and operable rhythm rather than a homogeneous continuous green belt.

### 4.2 Urban Renewal Overall Framework

Update the framework to follow the content requirements of depth Urban Design as per the [standard:MOHURD-CONTROL-DETAILED-PLANNING], organized in four layers: skeleton, facade, nodes, and blank spaces. The skeleton consists of the main spine and five groups of fused arms, which are to be initially connected; the facade is the architectural interface along the corridors, transformed from the rear to the front; the nodes are the stations, apex squares, and scene nodes, which bear daily use; the blank spaces are the temporarily undetermined land uses, reserved for yet-to-mature technologies and business types, comprising 5.6% ([data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-001] and other units on the same level).

The clear boundaries need to be defined: This plan does not propose the statutory conclusions for the Floor Area Ratio, Building Height, Building Coverage Ratio, and the Demolish–Renovate–Retain Strategy. The related control plan conditions in `ranges/planning_limits.json` are all marked as missing [source:SITE-PACKAGE], therefore `metrics.json` keeps `floor_area_ratio` and `building_height_m` unknown and provides the reason [depth:development_intensity_controls]. The style control is expressed through principles of continuous facade, hierarchical massing, and apex visibility, without giving specific numerical values [depth:height_massing_character].

## Five. Detailed Design for Key Areas

The design principles for the three key areas are to avoid redundancy: if all three are labeled as "AI Innovation Hub," the proposal would not address the issue [depth:three_key_area_detailed_design].

![Comparison Diagrams of Detailed Design for Three Key Areas: Positioning Differences, Spatial Anchors, and Data Conditions](assets/figures/key-areas.png)

### 5.1 Zhongzhiyuan AI Independent Innovation Acceleration Area (approximately 192.1 hectares)

Undertake the Full-Stack Independent AI Innovation System and global discourse on AI governance. This is the largest of the three areas in terms of area and has the most available space for reconfiguration. It is also the only area suitable for setting up pilot and testing validation sites. The spatial levers include: Computational Courtyard — make part of the exterior facade and operational status of the computational and data facilities observable as a public interface, see scenario nodes at [data:geometry/constraints.geojson#SN-12]; Pilot and Testing Validation Field — delineate a clear, enclosed, and exitable testing space within the area; North Fifth Ring Gateway Hub — serving as the northern gateway, it will handle external connections and arrival experience. The update method will primarily retain the current industrial office spaces, weaving in pilot and laboratory spaces in the gaps, see related Building Footprints at [data:geometry/buildings.geojson#BLDG-030]. Risks and preconditions: Computational and municipal capacity, safety boundaries for the testing field, and responsibility allocation must be reviewed and confirmed by professional teams and relevant authorities before further development [depth:municipal_new_infrastructure].

### 5.2 Beijing AI Origin Community (approximately 104.3 hectares)

Manage the daily operations of a world-class AI Innovation Ecosystem and developer community. The key word here is "daily": an innovation ecosystem is not about a few annual events but about people working, eating, walking, and meeting their peers every day. There are three spatial levers: Zero-Km Plaza — located at the apex of the pedestrian path, serving as the starting point of the spine and a venue for honoring open-source contributors; Developer Community Living Room — small public spaces along the seam arms, hosting open-source collaboration, demo sessions, and informal exchanges; three seam arms — connecting universities, businesses, and residential communities to the main spine. The update methods focus on incubation acceleration and weaving in talent apartments, as well as enhancing community services. Risks and prerequisites: there is a lack of official data on the proportion and scope of talent housing and public services, `resident_population` remains unknown, and this plan does not make population or scale calculations for the accompanying facilities [depth:risk_missing_data].

### 5.3 Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster (approximately 72.0 hectares)

Integrate intelligent native new business models and urban consumption and business scenarios. This is the southernmost area, most closely connected to the city center, with the highest concentration of existing commercial and business activities, making it suitable for turning AI into city services that ordinary citizens can directly use. The spatial intervention has three key elements: intelligent native consumption streets — a contextual transformation of the interface of existing commercial spaces; a business building AI service hub — a service window for enterprises moved to the street level; and the Echo Bell Corridor — a sound and narrative corridor tracing the cultural heritage of the Dazhongsi bell, serving as a segment of the seam. The update method focuses on the enhancement and transformation of existing commercial and business activities, with cultural functions woven in. Risks and prerequisites: the Dazhongsi area involves cultural heritage protection and urban rail station domain control conditions. This plan has not obtained any legal control line vector data, and `constraints.geojson` does not include cultural heritage lines, blue lines, and green lines. All spatial suggestions must be refined after a special review of cultural heritage and traffic [depth:risk_missing_data].

### 6.4 Scenarios—Spaces—Operational Mapping

The scenario is not a set of icons pasted on a map but a combination of three things: space (where, how much, what the boundaries are), permissions (who approves, where the data ends, how to stop), and operations (who maintains, who pays the cost, how to exit if it fails). Two criteria must be distinguished: the land use criterion for park green spaces accounts for 13.8%, while the green space rate based on the green map layer is 19.0%. The difference arises from the fact that protective green spaces, pocket parks, and expanded green nuclei are located within other land use units. This plan maintains both criteria in the submitted data, and it is clear that the land use proportion is used for land structure assessment, while the green space rate is used for open space supply assessment. These two criteria should not be mixed, and neither equals the statutory green space rate indicator [depth:metrics_recalculation]. Similarly, the green space and Public Space layers have been mutually exclusive—squares, forecourt areas, and connecting arms are considered paved public spaces and have been deducted from the green spaces to avoid double counting.

Urban Character is expressed through three principles, without giving numerical values [standard:MOHURD-URBAN-DESIGN-MEASURES][depth:height_massing_character]: first, facade alignment—buildings along the corridors are oriented with their fronts facing the street, and the ground floor is designed with openings facing the park; second, mass hierarchy—around the apex square, low massing and openness are maintained, while stronger massing is allowed further away, creating a rhythmic progression along the corridor; third, material restraint—iron oxide red, the green of the heritage park, and the bronze-gold triad of the milestone are used as identifying colors, avoiding large-scale high-saturation decorative elements. Specific heights, setbacks, and intensities must be determined after the official control conditions are finalized (A-CONTROLS-003).
