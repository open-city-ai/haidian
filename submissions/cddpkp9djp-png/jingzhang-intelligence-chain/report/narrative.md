# 方案摘要 / Narrative Summary

## 中文

“京张智链”以京张铁路百年工业遗产为文化 spine，串联众智园、AI原点社区、大钟寺三处重点片区，形成“一带三核、双翼六廊”的空间结构。方案回应公告与任务书提出的世界级AI创新生态、适配AI新质生产力的城市形态、全球AI人才向往的高品质城区等目标，提出 13 个 AI 场景节点（含 3 个产业测试验证场景）、5 类用户画像、3 个 AI 朝圣地标，以及近期—中期—远期的分期实施框架。所有空间结论基于仓库提供的临时边界生成，明确标记为 provisional，待官方精确边界与控规条件公布后复算。

## English

"Jing-Zhang Intelligence Chain" takes the century-old Jing-Zhang Railway industrial heritage as a cultural spine, linking the three key areas of Zhongzhiyuan, Beijing AI Origin, and Dazhongsi into a spatial structure of "one chain, three cores, two wings, and six corridors." The proposal responds to the announcement and taskbook goals of a world-class AI innovation ecosystem, urban forms suited to AI new-quality productive forces, and a high-quality district attractive to global AI talent. It proposes 13 AI scenario nodes (including 3 industry test/validation scenarios), 5 user personas, 3 AI pilgrimage landmarks, and a near/mid/long-term phasing framework. All spatial conclusions are generated from the repository's provisional boundary and explicitly tagged as provisional; they must be recalculated when official precise boundaries and regulatory controls become available.
