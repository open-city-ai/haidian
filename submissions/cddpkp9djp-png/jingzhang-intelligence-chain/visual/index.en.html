Core Concept

The Jing-Zhang Railway heritage park forms a north-south "Intelligence Chain spine," linking the Zhongzhiyuan AI Autonomous Acceleration Area in the north, the Beijing AI Origin Community in the centre, and the Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster in the south. The east wing along Zhongguancun provides IP, capital and professional services; the west wing along Xiaoyuehe hosts AI scenario testing and urban-agent sandboxes.
