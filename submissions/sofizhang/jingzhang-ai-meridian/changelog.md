- Two governance additions: a decommissioning registry (each AI facility entering public space registers, at installation, its planned review/retirement date, the party responsible for removal and site restoration, and the restoration standard — same calibre as "exit & spatial reversion"; no registration, no entry) and time-banded service commitments (per-band availability and human-takeover windows — daytime dual channel, night-time degradation to human/self-service with published response times — as the temporal refinement of the non-AI equivalent path). The three-article Covenant structure is unchanged.

- `manifest.json` migrated to the upstream 0.2.x forward contract (schema_version 0.2.0; the eight render entries recast as role=other + role_detail=professional_render per the new rules, all other roles canonical; strict schema validation passing); `design_depth_matrix.json` gains the machine-readable `completeness_limited_by` disclosure on eight constrained items (official boundary polygon unpublished / statutory planning controls unavailable), each mirroring existing narrative declarations, with all status values unchanged.
