## Three-Level Scope Framework

The proposal follows the three scope levels defined by the announcement: the coordinated research area of 43.6 km², which hosts the global AI innovation ecosystem, the three-areas-two-wings layout, and research on the future AI city form; the overall design area of 11.4 km², which hosts urban renewal and urban design at regulatory-planning depth; and the key detailed-design area of 368.4 ha, where the Zhongzhiyuan, AI Origin Community, and Dazhongsi areas receive fine-grained design [source:OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT]. The three levels realize the "First Train" concept at strategy, structure, and node scales: the research level sets the industrial direction, the design level sets the spatial skeleton, and the node level sets the experiential anchor points [depth:three_level_scope_framework].

## Coordinated Research Area: Industry and Future City Research

**Master concept: JZ First Train (京张·首班车).** On 2 October 1909, the Jingzhang Railway, engineered by Zhan Tianyou, opened to traffic; the first train departed near Xizhimen, beginning the era of Chinese-designed trunk railways. More than a century later, this land hosts another "first departure"—the world's first real urban planning exercise in which AI agents participate as formal contributors, handing 43.6 km² of land to agents for co-design and implementation. This proposal takes the "First Train" as the master concept of the belt: the heritage-park vitality belt is a north-south "track"; the three key areas are "stations" on this track; Zhongguancun and the Xiaoyue River form the "signal and dispatch tower" and the "scenic branch line"; and the global AI event system is the "timetable" of this city-train [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK] [depth:overall_spatial_structure].

**Spatial structure: One Line, Three Stations, Two Wings.** The "One Line" is the Jingzhang heritage-park vitality belt (the track), implemented in this overall design as a north-south park-green corridor along the railway heritage, about 2.3 km², the largest single green and public-space carrier [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-021] [metric:green_ratio]. The "Three Stations" are the key areas: Zhongzhiyuan equals the departure station and innovation locomotive depot, hosting R&D, pilot testing, and validation of the full-stack AI autonomous innovation system; the AI Origin Community equals the departure platform and world first-departure station, hosting a world-class AI innovation ecosystem and the commemorative "departure of the first AI-planned train"; Dazhongsi equals the transfer station and smart hub, hosting native-smart new business formats and human-AI transfer experiences [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-002]. The "Two Wings" provide conceptual support: the Zhongguancun technology-service wing (signal and dispatch tower) provides globally configured factors, capital, and IP services; the Xiaoyue River scenario-empowerment wing (scenic branch line) provides scenario enablement, liveability, and blue-green ecology [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK].

**Naming system and logo direction.** The primary name is "京张·首班车" (JZ First Train / FIRST TRAIN). Station-level naming uses railway vocabulary—departure station, departure platform, transfer station, locomotive depot, signal and dispatch tower; node naming uses departure-platform plaza, timetable plaza, signal-tower plaza, open-source results gallery, and agent-contribution honor wall; path naming uses developer honor walk and open-source road. The logo direction overlays a train-head silhouette pulling away from a platform with a circular track, its tail light dissolving into a data stream, echoing the narrative "from iron track to intelligent track"; the palette uses Jingzhang rust red (history), heritage-park green (heritage), and AI streamer purple (intelligence), with a bilingual wordmark extendable to signage, web, and event visuals [depth:overall_spatial_structure] [depth:height_massing_character].

**Five functions as a railway operating system.** The full-stack AI autonomous innovation system maps to the locomotive and marshaling yard; the world-class AI innovation ecosystem to the network and interlining; the AI+ scenario-enablement paradigm to in-carriage service and travel experience; the intelligent and vibrant AI city to stations and corridor towns; and global discourse power in AI governance to signals and dispatch rules. The five functions are no longer parallel slogans but a runnable city-train system [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK].

**Global AI innovation ecosystem cases (5–8) and Haidian translation.** King's Cross in London (railway-heritage renewal into a tech community—the most comparable case: old station, new economy, activated public space); Silicon Valley (the complete research-incubation-capital chain); Shenzhen Nanshan (AI industrialization and scenario opening); Hangzhou Future Sci-Tech City (platform-based innovation ecosystem and talent pull); Singapore Punggol Digital District (AI + urban living scenarios); Hefei (transforming science-education resources into original innovation); Boston Kendall Square (a research-dense district evolving into an AI cluster); and Korea's Daedeok Innopolis (R&D complex and talent concentration). These translate into three mechanisms: spatial (station-city integration and public space for innovation exchange), operational (scenario opening, test validation, developer community), and factor-based (coordination of land, space, industry, capital, talent, compute, data, and scenarios) [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK] [depth:overall_spatial_structure]. All cases draw on public sources as reference illustrations, not definitive descriptions of any institution.

## Overall Design Area: Urban Renewal and Regulatory-Plan-Level Urban Design

Using "One Line, Three Stations, Two Wings" as the spatial skeleton, the overall design area applies three core judgments at regulatory-planning depth: first, a continuous park-green track sutures the east and west urban areas long divided by the railway; second, three station-like key areas concentrate AI innovation functions, forming a vertical linkage of industry, talent, and scenarios; third, a blue-green slow-traffic network and rail connections integrate communities along the line into the innovation belt [depth:land_use_layout] [standard:MOHURD-URBAN-DESIGN-MEASURES].

Land use is organized as a corridor zonation: the central park-green track (1401), with research land at Zhongzhiyuan, the BUAA/BUPT education belt, the AI Origin mixed community, the innovation-talent community, and the Dazhongsi commercial/business district arranged from north to south on both sides [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-001] [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-010]. Research, commercial, and education land form the main body of the innovation belt; residential and community-service land support talent living; green and buffer land protect ecology and recreation [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-020] [standard:MNR-LAND-USE-CLASSIFICATION-GUIDE].

Development intensity follows "high density at stations, medium along the line, open at the track": the three station areas are intensity anchors where cultural, research, and commercial buildings concentrate; residential and education areas stay at low-to-medium intensity; and no buildings are placed within the park track [depth:development_intensity_controls] [depth:height_massing_character]. The estimated floor area ratio of about 1.65 and building density of about 0.15 are design estimates recalculated in `metrics.json` from representative building footprints and assumed storeys, and are conceptual; statutory FAR, height, density, setback, and green ratio await official conditions [metric:floor_area_ratio_estimated] [metric:building_density] [standard:MOHURD-CONTROL-DETAILED-PLANNING].

The overall renewal framework follows "preserve first, upgrade through renovation, limited new construction": railway heritage and heritage buildings along the line are primarily protected and activated; university and research stock is primarily upgraded in function; station surroundings undergo station-city integrated renewal; and conceptual suggestions are clearly distinguished from statutory retain-redevelop-demolish conclusions awaiting confirmation [depth:retain_renovate_demolish].
