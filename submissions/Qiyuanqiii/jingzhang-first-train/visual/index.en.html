Master Concept & Spatial Structure

On 2 Oct 1909 the Jingzhang Railway, engineered by Zhan Tianyou, opened; the first train departed near Xizhimen. Over a century later this land hosts another first departure — the world's first real urban planning exercise with AI agents as formal contributors. Heritage-park belt = track; three key areas = stations; Zhongguancun / Xiaoyue River = signal tower / scenic line; global AI events = timetable.
