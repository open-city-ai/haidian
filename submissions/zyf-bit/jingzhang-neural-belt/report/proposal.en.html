Jingzhang Neural Belt - AI Innovation Belt Urban Design Proposal

Design Basis and Source List

Based on the Centennial Jingzhang AI Innovation Belt Urban Design Open Call brief. The project covers a coordinated research area of 43.6 km², an overall design area of 11.4 km², and three key detailed-design areas totaling 3.684 km².

Three-Level Scope Framework

Research: 43.6 km²Design: 11.4 km²Key: 3.684 km²

The three-level scope integrates coordinated research, overall design, and detailed key-area design into a unified spatial strategy along the Jingzhang railway corridor.

Coordinated Research Area: Industry and Future City Research

The research area covers 43.6 km² spanning from Zhongguancun to Beitaierzhuang, analyzing AI industry distribution, global innovation ecosystem cases, and future city development patterns.

Overall Design Area: Urban Renewal and Regulatory-Plan-Level Urban Design

Neural Belt spatial structure with three-district two-wing framework. The overall design area of 11.4 km² establishes land use layout, development intensity controls, height and massing character, and retain-renovate-demolish strategy.

Detailed Design of Key Areas

Three key areas: Zhongzhiyuan AI R&D District (1.921 km²), Beijing AI Origin Community (1.043 km²), and Dazhongsi Industrial Cluster (0.72 km²). Each area has detailed building scale, public space, and AI scenario design.

AI Innovation Ecosystem, Personas, and AI+ Scenarios

12 AI scenario cards, 6 user personas, 3 AI pilgrimage landmarks including Developer Star Avenue, Open Source Achievement Gallery, and Agent Contribution Honor Wall.

Land Use, Building Scale, and Retain-Renovate-Demolish Strategy

Green: ~17.5%Public: ~13.2%

Land use plan balances AI R&D, industry services, community facilities, and green infrastructure along the railway corridor.

Transport, Rail, Municipal Infrastructure, and Public Services

Three-level network: rail transit, bus system, and slow-mobility corridors. Integration with existing Jingzhang railway and planned municipal infrastructure.

Blue-Green Network, Public Space, and Urban Character

Continuous ecological corridor connecting Jingzhang Heritage Park, Qinghe waterfront, Xiaoyuehe green corridor, and community parks. Public spaces include landmarks, exhibition spaces, and cultural plazas.

Renewal Projects, Implementation Policy, and Phasing

Near-term (2026-2028): Key infrastructure and AI pilot zones. Mid-term (2029-2031): Full key-area development. Long-term (2032-2035): Corridor completion and ecosystem maturation.
