# Jingzhang Neural Belt · AI Innovation Belt Urban Design Proposal

## Design Basis and Source List

This proposal is based on the Centennial Jingzhang AI Innovation Belt Urban Design Open Call brief. `[source:SRC-2026-BJ-GH-QUAL-PREANNOUNCEMENT]`

- **Official Announcement**: Qualification pre-announcement by Beijing Municipal Planning and Natural Resources Commission Haidian Branch `[source:SRC-2026-BJ-GH-QUAL-PREANNOUNCEMENT]`
- **Agent Task Book**: `brief/site-package/agent_taskbook.json` `[source:AGENT-TASKBOOK]`
- **Provisional Boundary**: `brief/site-package/geometry/provisional_boundaries.geojson` `[source:DATA-SRC-PROVISIONAL-BOUNDARIES-20260605]`

## Three-Level Scope Framework

Coordinated Research Area: 43.6 km², Overall Design Area: 11.4 km², Key Design Area: 3.684 km².

## Overall Design Area: Urban Renewal and Regulatory-Plan-Level Urban Design

The Neural Belt spatial structure transforms the Jingzhang Railway corridor into an urban neural network. `[standard:MOHURD-URBAN-DESIGN-MEASURES]`

## Detailed Design of Key Areas

Three key areas: Zhongzhiyuan AI R&D District, AI Origin Community, and Dazhongsi Industrial Cluster. `[standard:URBAN-PLANNING-LAW]`

## AI Innovation Ecosystem, Personas, and AI+ Scenarios

12 AI scenario cards, 3 test validation scenarios, 6 user personas, and 3 AI pilgrimage landmarks. `[standard:SMART-CITY-STANDARDS]`

## Land Use, Building Scale, and Retain-Renovate-Demolish Strategy

Block-group level precision. Green space rate ~25%, public space rate ~15%. `[standard:GREEN-BUILDING-STANDARD]`

## Transport, Rail, Municipal Infrastructure, and Public Services

Three-level transportation network: rail transit, bus, and slow-mobility systems.

## Blue-Green Space, Public Space, and Urban Landscape

Continuous ecological corridor with ~25% green space coverage. `[standard:GREEN-BUILDING-STANDARD]`

## Renewal Project List, Implementation Policy, and Phasing Plan

Three phases: near-term (2026-2028), mid-term (2028-2030), long-term (2030-2035).
