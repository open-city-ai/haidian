Jing-Zhang Signal Corridor · THE SIGNAL CORRIDOR (京张信号带)

Make the AI services roaming public space signal

The Jing-Zhang Railway moved powerful machines safely through the city for a century using signals — green to go, red to stop, every boundary-cross recorded. This proposal translates that railway discipline into a spatial institution for AI governance: a ~9 km signal spine along the Jing-Zhang Heritage Park links three signal stations (Benchmark · Origin · Public) and twelve signal points. Every AI service entering the corridor must "signal" — what it does, what data it uses, who is accountable, when it can be stopped. This is an offline static electronic exhibit; every figure is derived from GeoJSON, metrics.json, matrices and self-check results, with no external resources loaded.
