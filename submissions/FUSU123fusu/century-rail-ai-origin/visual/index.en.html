Land-use structure

Key areas | three cores

1 · Zhongzhiyuan AI Acceleration Area

North innovation pole. R&D pilot bases and HQ clusters at low intensity (concept 3-10 floors); north gateway of the green axis meets the Qinghe country park segment; reserved white land (16) keeps future interfaces open. Announced 192.1 ha | recomputed 192.9 ha. Phasing: long-term (2035-2040).

2 · Beijing AI Origin Community

AI innovation origin and global talent community. Wudaokou TOD with the Origin Living-Room plaza (1403); education (0804) / research (0802) / retail (05) mixed blocks at medium intensity (concept 6-18 floors); first-release ground for AI scenarios. Announced 104.3 ha | recomputed 104.3 ha. Phasing: near-term (2026-2030).

3 · Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster

AI industry cluster with culture-tech fusion. Dazhongsi station plaza as the axis trailhead (1403); ancient-bell culture core (0803) plus retail renewal belt (05), high-intensity mixed development (concept 12-28 floors). Announced 72.0 ha | recomputed 72.0 ha. Phasing: near-term (2026-2030).
