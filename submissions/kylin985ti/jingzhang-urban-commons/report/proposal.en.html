Six-Step Public Participation and Four Equity Floors

Public participation is not an online vote after the plan is complete. It runs through project selection, trial, and exit: first publish the problem, available evidence, and what cannot be promised; then conduct joint walks with residents, disabled people, caregivers, service workers, merchants, and professionals; compare at least the status quo, a light intervention, and a stronger intervention; test a removable pilot before permanent construction; evaluate it with affected people; and publicly explain whether each valid comment was adopted, partly adopted, rejected, or held for evidence, together with the exit arrangement.
