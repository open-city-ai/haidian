## Generated geometry and non-approval boundary

`geometry/site_boundary.geojson` and `geometry/key_areas.geojson` are provisional rough constraints. The conceptual land-use partition, one north-south audit relation, three cross-link audit relations, three reciprocity-platform focus envelopes, one blue-green performance focus envelope and the S1 audit envelope are agent-generated analytical/design records with `approval_status=not_approved`. They are not existing-condition inventories or official boundaries, roads, rail alignments, bridges, tunnels, green-space designations, parcels, buildings, public-space approvals, access rights or engineering designs.

`geometry/buildings.geojson` contains 18 agent-generated `schematic` / `not_approved` test-fit envelopes. They are used only to test adjacency, public interfaces and intensity sensitivity. They are not a verified existing-building inventory, parcel boundary, ownership record, statutory control, demolition/retention decision or approved construction proposal. A trustworthy building inventory remains unavailable and is retained as a disclosed major data gap.
