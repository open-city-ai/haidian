# RECIPROCAL JING-ZHANG: HOW AI GROWTH DELIVERS PUBLIC RETURN TO THE CITY

## Executive Summary

- **This is an urban-system proposal in Haidian, Beijing, organized along the public spaces of the former Jing-Zhang Railway.** The 43.6 km² area coordinates industry, mobility, and governance; the approximately 11.4 km² area addresses urban renewal and spatial capacity; and Zhongzhiyuan, the AI Origin Community, and Dazhongsi test three different industry-city relationships. These areas are announced approximations or recalculations from provisional geometry, not statutory boundaries [source:OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT] [data:geometry/site_boundary.geojson#SITE-001].
- **The central judgment is not to build another “AI green axis,” but to operate the existing public realm as continuing public infrastructure.** Phase II supporting works have been announced complete [source:JZ-PARK-PHASE2-202607], and the Qinghua East Road public-space project has been approved [source:QINGHUA-EAST-PUBLIC-SPACE-202604]. The Line 13 capacity upgrade [source:LINE13-UPGRADE-202602] and Qinghe-Xiaoyue water project [source:QINGHE-XIAOYUE-CSO-202601] remain under implementation. The proposal therefore takes responsibility for access gaps, performance, public return, and long-term operations rather than claiming existing public investment again.
- **The proposal combines one public-realm operating spine, three differentiated “reciprocity platforms,” two service networks, and multiple everyday entrances.** Zhongzhiyuan validates technology, the AI Origin Community translates research outcomes, and Dazhongsi tests real adoption. Whenever industrial growth uses public space, funding, data, or energy, it must deliver a knowledge, economic, spatial, data, climate, or cultural return and remain subject to public evaluation and exit.
- **The decision now is to begin with baselines, joint walks, reversible trials, and operating rules rather than permanent works.** Eight near-term projects are proposed for the first 0-12 months. Surface repairs may be tested after evidence and competent-authority consent; network reorganization is developed only at high-demand, high-detour locations; bridges, tunnels, major utilities, and centralized compute are not conditions for the proposal to work. Every project, budget, target, and gate remains a `not_approved` proposal parameter.

### How to Read Evidence States and Codes

The report separates confirmed facts, information awaiting verification, proposal parameters, and unknown baselines. Public information from competent authorities can establish a task or announced status. `provisional` means temporary geometry used for discussion. `not_approved` means a target, budget, or gate has been defined but not authorized. `unknown/null` means evidence is insufficient and a proposed value cannot stand in for current conditions. Four code families are used only for retrieval: implementation depth (S0-S3), AI scenarios (AI-01-AI-12), near-term projects (P01-P08), and professional gates (G0-G8). Each is introduced in plain language before its code appears.

**This is one complete professional report.** It moves from the project judgment to the three scope levels, industry, and key areas; follows people's complete journeys through mobility, services, blue-green systems, and culture; and ends with projects, budgets, metrics, and stop conditions. Nineteen figures are explained where they become relevant. ## Design Basis and Source List

**Chapter conclusion: Jing-Zhang is no longer a blank site awaiting a plan; the proposal begins with post-completion performance, public return, and clear boundaries of responsibility.**

This proposal starts from a fact that has already changed: the public realm of the Jing-Zhang Railway Heritage Park is not a blank sheet awaiting a plan. In July 2026, the Beijing Municipal Forestry and Parks Bureau announced the completion of Phase II supporting works, and a fishbone-shaped walking and cycling network has already taken shape in the northern section. The public-space project south of Qinghua East Road has been approved, while the combined-sewer overflow pollution-control project for the Qinghe–Xiaoyue River system has entered implementation. Continuing to make a "new nine-kilometre green axis with three paths and one green belt" the central innovation would therefore double-count public investment that has already occurred or is under way [source:JZ-PARK-PHASE2-202607] [source:QINGHUA-EAST-PUBLIC-SPACE-202604] [source:QINGHE-XIAOYUE-CSO-202601].

The new question is not "how to draw another axis," but how AI-industry growth should deliver verifiable returns to the city after consuming space, capital, data, energy, and public attention. Three specialist Agents studied economy and industry, mobility and ecology, and humanities and heritage in parallel; the lead Agent then made unified determinations on evidentiary conflicts, scale and measurement conventions, alternative scenarios, and implementation thresholds. AI is used only for retrieval, structuring, simulation, and communication. Statutory planning, professional engineering, public decisions, and final judgment remain with authorized institutions and people [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK] [standard:PROJECT-AGENT-OPEN-CALL-TASKBOOK].

The repository still lacks official polygons for the three scope levels, precise boundaries for the three key areas, road planning boundaries, existing buildings, parcel ownership, regulatory-plan indicators, heritage-control lines, and municipal capacity. The submission package uses the repository's provisional boundary to generate conceptual geometry and recalculated values solely for intake, discussion, and professional development. ## Three-Level Scope Framework

**Chapter conclusion: the three scales address regional coordination, urban renewal, and key-area trials respectively; they cannot share one level of precision or be replaced by a single master plan.**

The three scope levels address different questions and are not replaced by a single master plan. The 43.6 km² Coordinated Research Area covers industrial chains, talent, cross-district mobility, and governance coordination. The approximately 11.4 km² Overall Design Area addresses connections to the built framework, urban renewal, service networks, and operating organization. The three Key-Area Detailed Design Areas, totaling approximately 368.4 hectares, test three different industry–city reciprocity prototypes. Areas in the announcement are approximate; values recalculated from provisional geometry are not official precise areas [source:OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT] [metric:announced_overall_design_area_sqm] [depth:three_level_scope_framework].

The spatial structure is summarized as "**one public spine, three reciprocity platforms, two exchange networks, and multiple everyday gates**." The public spine is the operating framework of Jing-Zhang public spaces made up of built and yet-to-be-verified sections; it does not claim a newly built continuous corridor. The three reciprocity platforms respectively undertake full-stack validation, origin-stage translation, and market adoption. The Zhongguancun Technology Services Wing supplies IP, legal, financial, standards, talent, and international-market services, while the Xiaoyue River Scenario Enablement Wing supplies real problems, controlled trials, and public evaluation. The "everyday gates" are point-by-point connection tasks at station entrances, community gates, park gates, campus gates, and riverfront entrances; neither their number nor engineering alignments are assumed in advance [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-001] [data:geometry/roads.geojson#MOB-NS-001].

All three levels apply a four-stage evidence gate: fact, assumption, trial, and scale-up. Public information from competent authorities can confirm tasks and project status; provisional layers support only conceptual exploration; site walks and professional models determine small pilots; permanent works are discussed only after official data, property rights, approvals, budgets, and operating responsibilities are all in place. In this way, organizer data gaps do not block creative assessment, but drawings cannot quietly elevate those gaps into approval conclusions.

## Coordinated Research Area: Industry and Future City Research

**Chapter conclusion: the industrial objective is not to accumulate firms and buildings within 43.6 km², but to move research outcomes through independent validation, real scenarios, external first orders, and continuing services.**

### Seven-Layer Industrial Chain and the Three Zones and Two Wings Loop

GDP, enterprise counts, patents, or employment for Haidian District cannot be allocated to the 43.6 km² area in proportion to its size. District-level statistics can establish that the broader regional base is strong, but they cannot establish output inside the research area. Formal evaluation must first create an address-level, de-identified baseline register for enterprises, employment, buildings, and rents [source:HD-STAT-BULLETIN-2025] [metric:economic_baseline_coverage].

The industrial system is organized in seven layers: chips, computing power, and terminals; foundational software and open-source tools; data, models, and AI for science; agents and OPC; AI-enabled industry applications; evaluation, safety, and standards; and IP, capital, talent, and market services. The belt does not seek to build heavy assets for every layer. Instead, it forms a loop: source-stage outcomes from the AI Origin Community → full-stack adaptation and trustworthy validation at Zhongzhiyuan → real-world scenarios along the Xiaoyue River → productization and first orders at Dazhongsi → professional services and international expansion through Zhongguancun → returns of revenue, talent, and problems [source:HD-AI-ORIGIN-202601] [source:BJ-AGENT-MEASURES-202607].

Large-scale training compute, mass manufacturing, and aggregation of sensitive raw data are not assumed to be located in the central city. Within the belt, priority goes to design, low-latency inference, software–hardware adaptation, independent evaluation, first adoption, and professional services. Any centralized computing facility must first demonstrate electricity, cooling, fire safety, flood resilience, energy efficiency, and long-term maintenance; the slogan of an "AI city" does not automatically justify construction [source:BJ-DATACENTER-OPTIMIZATION-202411] [depth:municipal_new_infrastructure].

### How the Three Positionings, Five Functions, and Three Areas–Two Wings Fit Together

The taskbook's three positionings are not parallel slogans but three ways of using the same place. The **Century-old Jing-Zhang Cultural Belt** is carried by the memory compact, segment-by-segment public route, and traceable interpretation. The **Urban AI Life-Experience Belt** is carried by twelve scenarios, complete journeys, essential services, and staffed alternatives. The **AI Integrated-Innovation Belt** is carried by the seven-layer industry stack, three reciprocity platforms, and professional-service wings. ## Overall Design Area: Urban Renewal and Regulatory-Plan-Level Urban Design

**Chapter conclusion: the overall design first connects built spaces, stations, parks, and everyday services; where formal parcels, buildings, and regulatory controls are missing, a concept drawing must not manufacture development precision.**

### From Spatial Supply to "Evidence-Driven Interfaces for Existing Assets"

Overall design does not divide 11.4 km² into four abstract color bands and then declare the Land-Use Plan complete. `land_use.geojson` expresses only the topological relationship among four types of conceptual capacity—research and validation, public access and green space, industrial and business services, and community and everyday services—and cannot replace existing land-use conditions or statutory classifications. Before official parcels, regulatory plans, existing buildings, and ownership data become available, area values are used only to check complete layer coverage, not as construction quotas [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-001] [standard:MNR-LAND-USE-CLASSIFICATION-GUIDE].

For Phase 1, 8,000–12,000 m² of distributed existing space is used as an operating assumption to support a unified outcome portal, shared validation, public-problem trials, and talent and community services. Expansion to a steady-state capacity of approximately 15,800–26,200 m² would be discussed only after six consecutive months of evaluation covering effective utilization, wait-list conversion, rent impacts, and essential services. This range is a light-asset operating estimate, not a commitment on land use, building scale, or investment [metric:operational_space_envelope_sqm].

The building strategy follows `R-A-I`: first Retain assets verified as retainable; then Adapt through reversible reuse; and only then study Infill after regulatory plans, ownership, municipal capacity, fire safety, sunlight, heritage, and public comments have passed their gates. The submitted `buildings.geojson` now contains 18 typological test-fit envelopes generated relative to the provisional key areas. They test functional adjacency, public interfaces, intensity sensitivity, and section relationships only; every record is marked `agent_generated_design`, `existing_condition_claim=false`, and `not_approved`. They are not existing buildings, parcel designs, or retain/adapt/demolish decisions. Actual building stock, height, tenure, and existing FAR remain unknown [data:geometry/buildings.geojson#BLDG-ZHONGZHIYUAN-AI-ACCELERATION-AREA-LAB-A] [metric:schematic_building_envelope_count] [depth:retain_renovate_demolish].

### Zhongzhiyuan: Validation-Return Platform

Zhongzhiyuan is positioned as a platform for full-stack adaptation, independent evaluation, and safety governance across chips, frameworks, models, agents, and terminals. Its first batch is not a new large computing center, but a shared validation catalog, booking system, standardized reports, isolated testing, and equipment circulation. Each enterprise validation must also deliver a publicly shareable compatibility conclusion, risk boundary, or standards recommendation [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-001] [metric:reproducibility_pass_rate].

### AI Origin Community: Translation and Co-Creation Platform

The AI Origin Community is positioned as a unified portal for translating university and research-institute outcomes from zero to one. Each outcome receives an identifier, then passes through a 30-day ownership and demand screen, a 90–180-day proof of concept, and independent validation by non-interested parties. It is then routed toward an open-source public good, technology licensing, joint R&D or collaborative innovation procurement, or a new enterprise. Not every outcome needs to become a startup or raise financing [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-002] [source:MOF-COLLABORATIVE-PROCUREMENT-202404].

Spatial priorities are complete journeys between the two stations, low-cost project rooms, staffed talent services, family friendliness, and public learning. Approximately 1,900 non-motorized-vehicle parking spaces at Qinghua East Road West Exit and Liudaokou are planned supply under an approved project; they are not counted as existing before completion, nor claimed again by this proposal. Campus gates, venues, and intellectual property must be negotiated item by item; "campus–city integration" does not imply access.

The spatial sequence is “knowledge spine–translation court–everyday edge.” Outcome intake, public learning, and technology-transfer services share a legible ground-floor interface; project rooms and controlled proofs of concept surround a translation court; family services, small merchants, quiet rest, and staffed accessibility support occupy the everyday edge. ## Transport, Rail, Municipal Infrastructure, and Public Services

**Chapter conclusion: mobility performance is judged through a person's complete journey from inside the station to the destination and back, not by a station radius, equipment counts, or an in-station facility list.**

### Begin with Three Representative Journey Audits

These are audit scripts to be carried out, not invented claims about current routes, volumes, or travel times. The northern journey follows a service worker through stopping, entrance, workplace, drinking water, rest, toilet, charging, and return. The central journey follows a caregiver and a disabled user through rail, exits, crossings, public space, and staffed help. The southern journey follows an older person or cultural visitor from a community and the area outside Dazhongsi Station to a park or museum branch and back. Each is checked during weekday peaks, weekends, night, extreme heat, and heavy rain, recording lift failure, obstruction, detour, paper wayfinding, and human alternatives. No minutes are assigned until field evidence exists [source:BEIJING-ACCESSIBILITY-REGULATION-2021] [source:BJ-SUBWAY-ACCESSIBILITY-202608].

Mobility shifts from station circles to complete journey chains: carriage and platform → concourse → entrance → crossing → bus, bicycle, or walking connection → entrance to a park, employment campus, university campus, or community. Operator accessibility lists for stations such as Wudaokou and Dazhongsi can prove only registered facilities inside stations, not continuous accessibility within 500 metres outside them [source:BJ-SUBWAY-ACCESSIBILITY-202608] [depth:traffic_rail_slow_parking].

For each station, the proposal records three states: current, during Line 13 construction, and after completion. It verifies entrance opening, bus connections within 50 metres, non-motorized-vehicle parking within 100 metres, taxis and ride-hailing, deliveries, and alternatives during rainstorms or lift failures. New bus routes are not drawn in advance. A feeder service enters study only when actual network walking exceeds 800 metres or a major barrier exists, stable demand persists over multiple consecutive days, existing stop locations or service frequencies cannot address it, and lawful operating conditions are available.

Cross-corridor access uses the detour index `DI = actual lawful network distance / straight-line distance between endpoints`. Only locations with high DI, high demand, clear public benefit, and no conflict with works under construction enter S2. At gated campuses and employment parks, the first studies address shared gate zones, staggered opening, and clearly marked alternatives; a conceptual diagram does not authorize wall removal. Lines in `roads.geojson` represent only audit origins and destinations and conceptual relationships, not engineered roads [data:geometry/roads.geojson#MOB-X-002] [metric:detour_index_reduction].

Public services are calculated through 5-, 10-, and 15-minute baskets based on real entrances and opening hours: toilets, drinking water, shade, first aid, and parking; breakfast, food shops, healthcare and pharmacies, childcare and eldercare, repairs, parcel services, and exercise; and cultural, sports, integrated, and industrial services. Facilities inside universities or enterprises do not count as supply if they are not open to the public, while facilities usable only through an App do not count as complete coverage [source:HAIDIAN-DISTRICT-PLAN-2035] [metric:basic_service_coverage_15m].

Municipal systems use a "minimum facility package": rated and peak electricity, water supply and drainage, communications, fire safety, maintenance access, noise, heat discharge, metering, maintenance, and decommissioning. Safety-related scenarios must be able to degrade safely in 100% of power or network outages, while non-critical AI can shut down. Values without capacity evidence remain unknown; an equipment list cannot stand in for infrastructure planning [data:geometry/constraints.geojson#CONSTRAINTS-UNKNOWN] [metric:safe_degradation_rate].

## Risk, Copyright, and Compliance

**Chapter conclusion: the greatest risk is not a lack of ambition, but allowing provisional geometry, public news, proposed parameters, or Agent reasoning to become statutory boundaries, implementation facts, or government commitments.**

The greatest risk is not that "the drawings lack detail," but that temporary boundaries, event news, project approvals, or Agent inferences are presented as official or implemented facts. The proposal separately registers conflicts between 43.6 and 37 km², Zhongzhiyuan and Xuebeiyuan, Phase II completion and on-site performance, approved and built, and in-station facilities and complete journeys. The formal announcement and later official clarifications take precedence; other definitions serve only as background [source:SOURCE-REGISTRY] [depth:risk_missing_data].

The professional hard gates are: G0 official spatial and ownership data; G1 transport, rail-protection, and construction interfaces; G2 professional accessibility review and user acceptance; G3 water, flood control, and CSO interfaces; G4 trees, habitats, and parks; G5 summer and winter microclimate and occupational health; G6 municipal systems, energy, and computing power; G7 heritage, sound, and vibration; and G8 whole-life operation and public interest. If any gate is not passed, only lower-commitment research or temporary trials are allowed; the proposal may not be elevated to permanent works.

Equity risks are managed through trigger signals: anomalous rents relative to a control, a net decline in essential everyday services, brand events occupying public hours, jobs without contracts or social insurance, algorithms without appeal, vendors unable to export data, failure of historical or copyright review, and any break in the accessible chain. Each triggers a pause, freeze on expansion, redesign, or exit. There is currently no evidence that gentrification has already occurred in the project area, so it is treated only as a risk hypothesis requiring a baseline and control comparison [source:BEIJING-URBAN-RENEWAL-REGULATION-2022].
