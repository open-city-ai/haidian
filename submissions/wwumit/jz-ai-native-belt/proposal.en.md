# JZ AI-Native Belt — A Governance-Driven Three-Level AI Innovation City

## Design Basis and Source List

This formal proposal takes as its primary basis the Pre-Qualification Announcement for the International Open Call for Urban Design Proposals for the Centennial Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt, published by the Haidian Branch of the Beijing Municipal Commission of Planning and Natural Resources, and takes the maintainer-registered provisional rough boundaries, key areas, enumerations, metrics and source lists in `brief/site-package/` as its machine-readable basis. Before generating the proposal, the AI agent must read `design_brief.json`, `allowed_design_space.json`, `sources.json`, `enums/`, `ranges/`, `schemas/`, `data/source_registry.json` and `data/processed/agent_fact_pack.md`, and must use `project_scope_summary.csv`, `agent_task_requirements.csv`, `source_use_matrix.csv` and `missing_data_checklist.csv` to establish the task, scope, source-use and gap checklists. Every design judgment must be decomposed into traceable sources, recalculable metrics, verifiable layers and manually reviewable assumptions. The announcement requires the proposal to reach the urban design depth of Regulatory Detailed Planning and the urban design depth of the Integrated Planning Implementation Plan, so textual narration cannot substitute for the GeoJSON, metric tables, A3 booklet, A0 panels and HTML electronic display deliverables.

The proposal is not an independent vision text, but organizes its deliverables from the announcement, the agent-oriented task book and the site materials; this section only places the most critical basis next to the judgments [source:OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT] [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK] [depth:existing_conditions_diagnosis]. The complete source and standard coverage is stored in `sources.json`, `standard_matrix.json` and `design_depth_matrix.json` respectively, and machine indexes are not repeated in the body text.

The usage boundaries of the source registry are as follows [source:SOURCE-REGISTRY]:

- Current registry summary: 7 formal-usable sources, 1 background source, 1 provisional-only source.
- The agent must not upgrade background_only or provisional_only materials into an official boundary, statutory RDP, formal scoring basis or government implementation commitments.

`data/processed/agent_fact_pack.md` is the reading-navigation layer of this proposal, not a new authoritative source [source:PROCESSED-FACT-PACK]. It helps the agent organize the three-level scope, the three key areas, announcement tasks, agent.1–agent.6, material availability and missing-data items into a readable proposal; factual judgments must still return to the registered original materials [source:OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT] [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK], and the complete source relations are stored by `sources.json`.

## Three-Level Scope Working Framework

The proposal organizes work according to the three levels determined by the announcement: the Coordinated Research Area focuses on the AI industry ecosystem, strategic positioning, innovation chain and future urban form across 43.6 square kilometers; the Overall Design Area focuses on the 1–2 km urban and industrial areas around the 11.4 square kilometers of the Jing-Zhang Railway Heritage Park, and requires an overall urban renewal framework, industrial spatial layout, transport and municipal support, and urban character control; the Key-Area Detailed Design Area focuses on the three detailed-design areas totaling 368.4 hectares, and requires clear functional programs, building scale, demolish–renovate–retain classification, public space connectivity and transport organization. The three-level scope is mapped item by item in `compliance_matrix.json`, ensuring that the mandatory tasks of announcement sections 1.3, 1.4, 1.5 and agent.1–agent.6 all have chapter, layer, metric, drawing and HTML evidence.

The depth items of the three-level working framework are constrained by [depth:three_level_scope_framework] and [depth:overall_spatial_structure]; the spatial evidence is based on [data:geometry/site_boundary.geojson#SITE-001] and [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-001]; the task basis follows [standard:PROJECT-OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT]; and the scope index is navigated through the three-level scope table of `project_scope_summary.csv` in [source:PROCESSED-FACT-PACK].

![三层范围与空间工作框架图](assets/figures/land-use-structure.png)

The three levels of work are not mutually isolated sets of drawings. The coordinated research determines judgments on the industry chain and urban form; the overall design implements those judgments into renewal projects, spatial structure and facility capacity; the key-area detailed design verifies the implementability of specific parcels, buildings, transport, public space and AI application scenarios. When generating the proposal, the agent must first lock in the official or provisional boundary and constraints adopted by the current submission, then generate land use, buildings, roads, green space, public space, phasing and AI service nodes, and finally recalculate metrics from these layers and explain in the body text which conclusions remain constrained by the provisional boundary. Any area, ratio, scale or project count that cannot be recalculated from structured data must not be written into formal conclusions.

The overall concept recommended by this proposal is the "JZ Smart-Vein Symbiosis Belt" (京张智脉共生带): taking the Jing-Zhang Railway Heritage Park as the historical and public-space spine, the three key areas of Zhongzhiyuan, the Beijing AI Origin Community and Dazhongsi as innovation anchors, and universities, enterprises, communities and rail stations as the everyday network, forming a spatial organization of "one belt, three cores, multiple scenario points, and a composite blue-green slow-moving loop". Here the "belt" is not an extra newly drawn red line, but a translation of the announcement's three-level scope into a working method; the "three cores" correspond to the three key areas; the "multiple scenario points" correspond to operable nodes of AI-enabled public services, industrial services and urban life; the "composite loop" corresponds to the linkage of slow-moving routes, green space, public space and activity routes.

## Coordinated Research Area: Industry and Future Urban Form Research

The core task of the Coordinated Research Area is to build a world-class AI innovation ecosystem. The proposal should sort out Haidian's universities and research institutes, leading enterprises, computing-power/algorithm/data elements, incubation platforms, listed companies, unicorns and science-and-technology service resources, and propose a spatial coordination framework for the AI innovation chain, industry chain, talent chain and urban service chain. The naming scheme and logo design should serve the overall recognizability of the "Centennial Jing-Zhang Cultural Belt, Metropolitan AI Life Experience Belt, AI-Integrated Innovation Belt", and, rather than staying at the level of slogans, should explain their relations with the industry ecosystem, public space and cultural resources. The agent-oriented task book also requires responding to the coordination of the "five major functions" and the "Three Zones and Two Wings", forming a naming system, visual identity, overall spatial structure diagram, scenario access and operation mechanism that can be further developed; this section must use [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK] and [standard:PROJECT-AGENT-OPEN-CALL-TASKBOOK] to mark that these requirements come from the agent open-call task book, not from statutory planning control.

The coordinated research does not add pseudo-precise red lines; it connects back to [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-001], [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#PUBLIC-001] and [depth:overall_spatial_structure] through the coordination of urban character, public space and building layout required by [standard:MOHURD-URBAN-DESIGN-MEASURES], explaining that the industry strategy must ultimately land on a visible, reviewable spatial structure.

The future urban form research should answer how artificial intelligence changes work, life, social interaction, learning, transport and public services. The proposal should implement the AI transport system, continuous green space, innovation service facilities and an international living-and-working atmosphere into locatable functional zones, nodes, corridors and scenarios, rather than vaguely describing a technology vision. The agent should write industry strategy metrics, the AI innovation index, talent density, spatial supply types and AI+ vertical-application key areas into the metrics system, and mark which are official, which are design recommendations, and which still await calibration with formal data. If global AI innovation activities, developer communities, open scenarios or pilgrimage routes are proposed, they should be written as "conceptual recommendation / reference proposal / to be deepened by professional teams", and must not be written as confirmed government activities or implementation arrangements.

**Territorial spatial planning innovation approach (agent.1 mandatory item)**: The proposal translates "governance as infrastructure" into three kinds of spatial tools at the territorial spatial planning level — ① map AI governance capabilities (rules → inspection → scoring → reporting) onto spatial control objects: land-use zones into layers [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-001], scenario compliance into metrics ([metric:green_ratio], [metric:public_space_ratio] and the fourth-category governance metrics), and operation mechanisms into phasing [data:geometry/phasing.geojson#PHASE-001]; ② advance territorial spatial planning from a "static blueprint" to a "verifiable, evolvable dynamic governance foundation" where every spatial conclusion can be traced back to layers, metrics and human review ([depth:overall_spatial_structure], [depth:metrics_recalculation]); ③ connect with the GB 45438-2025 public-service AI labelling and [standard:MOHURD-URBAN-DESIGN-MEASURES] urban-form coordination, treating AI governance tools as part of the professional urban-design method rather than an add-on. This approach is at the conceptual-proposal level; formal territorial planning conclusions follow the official regulatory plan.

## Overall Design Area: Urban Renewal and RDP-Depth Urban Design

The Overall Design Area must reach the urban design depth of Regulatory Detailed Planning. The proposal must propose an overall urban renewal spatial structure, identification of inefficient space, a list of renewal projects, implementation policy recommendations, industrial function ratios, spatial organization modes, total building scale and a comprehensive carrying-capacity assessment. `geometry/land_use.geojson` should completely cover the design boundary without overlaps, `geometry/buildings.geojson` should express renewed building footprints or retained building footprints, `geometry/roads.geojson` should express micro-circulation, slow-moving and rail-connection relations, and `metrics.json` should recalculate the core areas, ratios and layer counts.

This section decomposes the RDP-depth content into reviewable objects according to [standard:MOHURD-CONTROL-DETAILED-PLANNING]: [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-001] expresses the land-use structure, [data:geometry/buildings.geojson#BLDG-001] expresses building footprints, [data:geometry/roads.geojson#ROAD-001] expresses transport organization, [metric:building_footprint_area_sqm] is used to review the building footprint area, and [depth:land_use_layout] and [depth:development_intensity_controls] constrain the deliverable depth.

The overall design must also support transport, rail, municipal and supporting facilities. The proposal should propose spatial layouts and implementation paths around transit-station integration, road micro-circulation, non-motorized vehicle parking, parking supply, innovation service platforms, talent life services, new infrastructure, distributed energy and edge computing. Content involving building height, development intensity, road red lines, setbacks and facility standards should, where no official control conditions exist, be written as "subject to confirmation of formal RDP conditions", and agent-inferred values must not be passed off as approved metrics.

This is exactly the global evidence base for this proposal's claim of a "governance-driven AI-native city", and the argument source for the compliance foundation in Section 12.

**Three original claims of this proposal (explicit proposal-level originality)**: ① **Governance as infrastructure** — governance tools (testing, labelling, auditing) are designed as visitable, bookable, governable urban space and public services (scenario card 02 Safe Governance Sandbox, 08 Data Element Lounge); governance is not a constraint on innovation but the ecosystem's attractiveness. ② **Four-capability governance foundation** — the progressive framework of governable (institutions) → controllable (inspection) → trustworthy (disclosure) → sustainable (updates), as the operating mechanism of urban-agent governance (track: civic-agent-governance) (developed in 12.1, entering the fourth-category governance metrics in compliance_matrix.json). ③ **Edge-computing power as a public good** — computing power supplied like water/electricity/gas infrastructure (scenario card 03), edge-first and cloud-on-demand, co-located with public services and low-carbon energy. The three claims echo the six case mechanisms, the ten scenario cards and the fourth-category governance metrics as a "claim—case—scenario—metric" four-layer response, distinguishing this proposal from mere function stacking.

## Risk, Copyright and Compliance Statement

### 12.1 Four-Capability Governance Foundation (Differentiation Core)

This proposal's urban agent governance (track: civic-agent-governance) adopts the **four-capability framework**: Governability (可管) → Controllability (可控) → Trustworthiness (可信) → Sustainability (可持续). The four are not parallel requirements but a progressive logic — governability is the institutional precondition, controllability is the capability condition, trustworthiness is the social foundation, and sustainability is the temporal dimension; if any one of the four is missing for a long time, governance fails.

| Four Capabilities | Urban-Level Landing Point | JZ Belt Mechanism |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Governable** (institutional) | Laws / standards / licenses | AI public service admission rules (PIPL / DSL / CSL), city AI service standards (aligned with the mandatory national standard GB 45438-2025 for content labeling) |
| **Controllable** (inspection) | Testing / auditing / supervision | Scenario Card 02 Safety Governance Sandbox (aligned with Singapore's AI Verify), AI service safety baseline testing (jailbreaks / bias / harmful content / prompt injection) |
| **Trustworthy** (disclosure) | Labeling / transparency / accountability | AI-generated content labeling (GB 45438-2025), public service data disclosure, human review as the safety net |
| **Sustainable** (updating) | Standard evolution / mechanism iteration | Versioning of the governance rule base, standards updated as technology evolves (Book 7.7.3: "safety minimum standards cannot be left to market spontaneity") |

**Governance as infrastructure (claim)**: the six ecosystem cases (6.1) jointly demonstrate that successful AI innovation ecosystems build governance in as infrastructure (Singapore's AI Verify, Hangzhou's City Brain, Shenzhen's planning system, Silicon Valley's market discipline). The JZ Belt's compliance foundation is not "constraining innovation" but "the attractiveness that makes an AI city trustworthy".

**Tool lineage (Book 7.8.1)**: hard regulation (government AI service licensing) → soft governance (disclosure requirements, standard testing, data element compliance front-loading) → self-governance (developer community self-discipline, open-source consensus) — the three-tier lineage lands respectively in Zhongzhiyuan / AI Origin Community / Dazhongsi.
