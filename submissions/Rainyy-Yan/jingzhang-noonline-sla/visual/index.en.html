01 | Overall Urban Structure

A midday public-service line that does not lose its public character when AI is offline

43.6 km² is a coordinated research relationship and c. 11.4 km² is the overall design area. The c. 9 km Jing-Zhang heritage-park corridor, three zones/two wings and focal-area roles are public strategic context. Submitted boundaries, routes, nodes and service bands are conceptual design, not official boundaries or existing GIS.

The noon design judgment

The city first needs a physical route that is readable, walkable, stayable and helpable. Algorithms can improve service, but cannot replace public passage, fixed information, stay reservations and human responsibility.

Evidence language

  • Solid | publicly supported context
  • Coral dash | conceptual intervention
  • Grey dash | provisional relation
  • Gold point | field verification required
  • Purple hatch | professional study required

02 | Three Spatial Prototypes

Three areas hold three public responsibilities. They are not the same node template repeated.

Zhongzhiyuan

A research, prototype and validation interface. Ordinary public, observers, researchers and controlled testing each receive a clear spatial boundary.

  • Public clearway before test activation
  • Observation edge separated from buffer
  • F01 handles permission verification and stop-test handover

AI Origin

An enterable, readable, stayable and askable civic knowledge room. AI is optional, never an entry condition.

  • Physical knowledge line, not phone-only entry
  • N06 is interpretation, not an invented relic
  • F02/F03 reserve human enquiry and handover

Dazhongsi

A conceptual transition between rail arrival and daily use. It does not assume external crossings, entries or accessible routes already exist.

  • Orientation, pause and human help
  • Crossing/access is a verification-dependent decision
  • Uncertainty means reroute, downgrade or suspend

03 | Sections

Space before AI: sections return a route to urban relationships people can use

The four sections express only relative relationships. They do not fabricate dimensions, existing facilities or construction conditions. Shade, seating, entry, crossing, accessibility and human service appear as reservation, verification or professional-study conditions.

07 | Implementation Control

Name who can verify, stop and maintain before a spatial move advances

This is not a responsible-entity or budget commitment. It is a concept delivery control: six work packages, six to-be-appointed role types, three non-financial resource bands and seven stoppable risks limit how a concept drawing may enter pilot, professional deepening or expansion.

Phase 0 | verification and interfaceROLE-02An authorised field-verification lead records publicness, entry, crossing, accessibility and maintenance interface. Without a record, no pilot begins.
Phase 1 | reversible pilotROLE-01The public-space pilot coordinator places removable information, stay space and human fallback only at confirmed interfaces.
Phase 2 | professional deepeningROLE-03The public-realm and accessibility professional deepens transport, heritage, engineering and interface conditions only where confirmed.
