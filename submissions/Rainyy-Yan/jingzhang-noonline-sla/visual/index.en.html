Overview Map / Jing-Zhang Two-Level Spatial Network

V2's auditable service protocol is translated in V3.0 into 11 LineStrings, 12 primary nodes, 8 secondary supports and 5 manual fallback points.

LEVEL 1

The three key areas and direct access. Primary + secondary <=400 m is a concept support-spacing target.

LEVEL 2

ROAD-001 and ROAD-003 connect the three areas, carrying route recognition, the Jing-Zhang sequence and basic AI-OFF wayfinding without claiming continuous SLA coverage.

Three-Level Scope / Key Areas / Land-Use Structure

Public Interest and Digital Inclusion

Noonline SLA puts no-phone users, older adults, accompanying children, accessibility users, park workers, residents and international visitors into one legible, help-seeking public-service logic.

Buildings and Renewal Projects

It proposes interface activation, shared ground floors and reversible service modules; it does not infer ownership, height, FAR or statutory control.

The key areas are not the same node template under different titles.

Slow Mobility / Blue-Green Public Space / AI-OFF

AI OFF

Physical routes, fixed information, S01-S08 and F01-F05 are spatial carriers of basic service. AI only predicts, reminds, dynamically adjusts and supports maintenance.

Still field unknown

Actual shade, water, seating, public-entry opening, crossings, accessible detours and human-service responsibility.
