# V4.1 Narrative Summary

Jing-Zhang Noonline SLA treats noon as an urban stress test. Its core proposition is simple: AI may enhance public service, but a person must still be able to enter, orient, rest, ask and leave when the phone, screen or AI service is unavailable. The package organizes 11 concept LineStrings, 12 primary nodes, 8 secondary supports and 5 manual fallback points into three distinct spatial prototypes: Zhongzhiyuan Research-Test-Validate, AI Origin Understand-Question-Co-create, and Dazhongsi Arrive-Decide-Pause-Enter Daily Life.

The spatial order is physical route, human responsibility, ordinary digital information, then optional AI. In Zhongzhiyuan a controlled test can stop while public walking remains. In AI Origin a physical knowledge line, staying and human help enable full use without AI. In Dazhongsi an unverified external crossing, entry or accessible connection triggers reroute, downgrade or suspension rather than false continuity. Jing-Zhang is translated into public-space rules of physical connection, intelligent assistance and resilient recovery.

V4 uses an explicit Site Evidence Ladder. Publicly supported corridor, task and station/heritage context is distinct from conceptual intervention, field verification and professional study. Existing shade, seating, water, entry, crossing, accessible detour, permit and operating responsibility are not asserted. Target SLA remains distinct from Verified SLA: SLA-A = B, SLA-B = C, SLA-C = C, and AI_OFF_TEST remains PASS_WITH_PROVISIONAL_PHYSICAL_NETWORK.

V4.1 adds an implementation-control register without enlarging the urban system or service claim. The register joins all six work packages to to-be-appointed concept roles, three non-financial resource bands, phase gates, field-ledger tasks and seven stoppable risks. It creates an explicit handoff from a spatial proposition to accountable professional deepening: first confirm who can verify, stop and maintain; then permit a reversible pilot; then deepen only a confirmed interface. Figure 08 now carries the same Phase 0-3, WP, ROLE, RB and RISK controls into both A3 and A0 drawings. It names no delivery entity, budget, land right, procurement, permit or approval.
