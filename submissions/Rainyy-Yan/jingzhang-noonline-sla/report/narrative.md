# V3.0 Narrative Summary

Jing-Zhang Noonline SLA V3.0 spatializes V2's auditable public-service contract. The 11-LineString concept network separates Level 1 SLA service support in the three key areas and direct access from Level 2 inter-zone Jing-Zhang connectors. It locates 12 primary service nodes, 8 secondary physical supports and 5 concept manual-fallback points without presenting any as built or field verified.

AI does not substitute for the city: fixed routes, bilingual information, stay-space reservations and human-help locations are the public-service prerequisite when AI, phones or screens fail. Shade, seating, water, entries, crossings, accessible detours and operating responsibility remain field-verification requirements. Accordingly, Target SLA remains distinct from Verified SLA: SLA-A = B, SLA-B = C, SLA-C = C, and AI_OFF_TEST remains PASS_WITH_PROVISIONAL_PHYSICAL_NETWORK.
