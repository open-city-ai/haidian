# 叙事摘要 / Narrative Summary

京张1909 · AI创新带把 1909 年京张铁路通车的自主创新精神，转译为 AI 时代的城市更新方法：
一轨（遗产轨线慢行文化脊）、两河（清河生态客厅、小月河场景绿廊）、三站（智市/原点/全栈），
以双层更新单元组织 69 个用地单元与 19 个示范更新项目，全部指标可在 EPSG:4548 下复算。

JZ-1909 AI Belt translates the self-reliance spirit of the 1909 Jing-Zhang Railway into an urban
renewal method for the AI era: one heritage rail spine, two riverfront living rooms and three AI
relay stations, organized through two-tier renewal units across 69 land parcels and 19 demonstrative
projects, with every metric recomputable under EPSG:4548.
