# 叙事摘要 / Narrative Summary

京张1909 · AI创新带把 1909 年京张铁路的人字形折返智慧转译为 AI 时代的城市组织方法：
一轨（遗产轨线慢行文化脊）为干、三区两翼为人字两笔、三站（智市/原点/全栈）为接力折返点，
六条东西缝合轴响应街区控规“南北贯通、东西连通”，把遗址公园与城市东西两侧织合；
人字两笔各载一义——一撇 AI 赋能产业、一捺 AI 赋能空间，交点立在以人为本。
以双层更新单元组织 63 个用地单元与 19 个示范更新项目，全部指标可在 EPSG:4548 下复算。
主口号：1909，以人字形破题；2026，以“人”字形立带。

JZ-1909 AI Belt translates the 1909 switchback ingenuity of the Jing-Zhang Railway into an urban
organization method for the AI era: the heritage rail spine as trunk, the Two Wings as the two
strokes of the character 人, three stations (AI Market / AI Origin / Full-Stack) as relay switch
points, and six east-west stitch axes answering the approved regulatory plan's call for north-south
continuity and east-west connection. Each stroke carries a mandate — AI empowering industry on one
side, AI empowering space on the other — meeting at the human: people first.
The package organizes 63 land parcels and 19 demonstrative projects through two-tier renewal units,
with every metric recomputable under EPSG:4548.
Slogan: 1909 — the switchback broke the doubt; 2026 — the human shapes the belt.
