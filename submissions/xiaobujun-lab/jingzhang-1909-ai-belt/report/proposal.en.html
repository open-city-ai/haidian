Coordinated Research Area: Industry and Future City Research

The core judgment at the research level: what makes this corridor unique is not that it hosts AI companies, but that it is the geographic origin of China's engineering self-reliance — the Jing-Zhang Railway, completed in 1909 under Zhan Tianyou, was the first trunk railway surveyed and designed by Chinese engineers, while Wudaokou and Zhongguancun are the cradle of China's internet and AI industries. The proposal therefore names the belt JZ-1909 AI Belt, under the design motif of the New Switchback — a century relay from the "railway that won respect" to the "belt of intelligence"; the naming system, visual identity and Logo direction all unfold from this anchor (see the agent.1 response) 来源.

The New Switchback is a bridge of meaning between two origin moments. The historical significance of 1909: facing the verdict that Chinese engineers could not build a railway, Zhan Tianyou solved the Badaling climb at Qinglongqiao with the switchback — trading length for height through geometry rather than brute locomotive power; the railway thus became China's founding declaration of full modern engineering sovereignty, and the "pride line" was, at heart, the sovereignty of human ingenuity. The future significance of 2026: the AI innovation belt is the origin project of the intelligence era — from railway sovereignty to intelligence sovereignty (full-stack independent innovation), from the movement of goods and people to the movement of data and intelligence; its question is not whether Haidian hosts AI companies, but how an AI-era city should be organized. Just as the 1909 railway defined the industrial-era urban corridor, this belt aims to define the innovative-city paradigm of the next century. Design methodology: the switchback's cleverness-over-force is this proposal's method — against stock ownership constraints, level differences, walking breaks and governance risks, use deft spatial organization instead of bulldozing; and write the character "人" (human) above the AI belt: the stronger the technology, the more present the human must be.

For the industrial ecosystem, the three positionings, five functions and Three Zones and Two Wings are organized into one relay loop: Zhongzhiyuan carries full-stack independent innovation and governance voice, AI Origin Community carries original innovation and technology transfer, Dazhongsi carries the intelligent economy and international exchange, the Zhongguancun Technology Services Wing injects capital and IP services, and the Xiaoyue River Scenario Enablement Wing provides a city-scale testbed 标准. In substance this loop spatializes the innovation chain of basic research, pilot scaling, application and global communication.

Global references (background research, not official data): Pittsburgh's shift from a steel corridor to a robotics corridor shows that university anchors plus legacy industrial stock can work; London's King's Cross knowledge quarter shows that a rail hub can composite universities, corporate headquarters and public space; Stockholm Kista shows that an ICT cluster needs daily-life services to retain talent; Kendall Square in Boston shows that the campus-park interface is the most important innovation interface; Shenzhen Nanshan shows that policy-driven space supply plus open scenarios accelerates agglomeration; Hangzhou's west science corridor shows that corridor layouts need both transit and blue-green skeletons; Munich's Werksviertel shows that cultural regeneration of old industrial districts can create a citywide destination. The shared lesson: an innovation belt is a daily-life belt first and an industry belt second. This proposal translates that into four spatial mechanisms: slow mobility, public space, talent services and open scenarios.

On the future urban form, the proposal holds that AI's first effect on the city is not dehumanization but higher-frequency, finer-grained mixing and verifiability of space use: research, demonstration, social life and housing mix along one corridor at finer grain, and urban services are embedded in public space in explainable, reviewable ways. All spatial propositions are conceptual suggestions for professional teams to deepen, and do not constitute statutory planning conclusions.

Overall Design Area: Urban Renewal and Regulatory-Plan-Level Urban Design

The Overall Design Area organizes the 11.4 sqkm provisional extent into a structure of one rail, two rivers and three stations, delivered to six design layers at RDP urban-design depth 深度. This structure is also the spatial-pattern translation of the New Switchback motif: the rail is the trunk, the two rivers are the branches, and the three stations form a continuous sequence of "switchback nodes" — each station organizes divergence and convergence, separating and then stitching the flows of arrival and departure, display and participation, machine and human service at its forecourt plaza, turning a century-old track geometry into today's way of organizing space.

One rail: the Jing-Zhang heritage rail slow-mobility spine. Along the old railway alignment, a heritage park spine of about 9.8 km length and roughly 116 m width links the three stations and every cultural node, composing slow mobility, cultural display and AI scenarios in one axis 指标. Two rivers: the Qinghe riverside ecological living room at the north end (about 72 hectares of waterfront green interface) and the Xiaoyue River scenario-enablement green corridor on the east (generated about 62 m inside the site's east boundary, answering the taskbook's Xiaoyue River Scenario Enablement Wing). Three stations: Dazhongsi AI Market Station (intelligent economy and international exchange), Wudaokou AI Origin Station (original innovation and technology transfer) and Zhongzhiyuan Full-Stack Station (full-stack independent innovation and governance display), each organized with a forecourt plaza, an interchange hall and a renewal unit 空间数据.

The land-use structure is a complete, gap-free and overlap-free partition using national classification codes: research land (0802) of about 302.8 hectares concentrates in the three stations; commercial services land (05) of about 253.5 hectares anchors Dazhongsi and the Wudaokou gateway; education land (0804) of about 113.4 hectares serves the campus-city transition; residential and community services (0701/0702) of about 198.2 hectares secure the jobs-housing balance; park green, protective green and plazas (1401/1402/1403) total about 273.3 hectares 指标. The normalized industrial mix is research:commercial:education:residential-community at roughly 34:28:13:22, and the green-and-open share is about 24 percent, supporting an integrated work-life-social-learning environment 指标.

The renewal framework uses a two-tier renewal-unit mechanism (conceptual suggestion for professional deepening): the first tier is the three key station renewal units, which bundle underused stock around forecourt plazas and fix functional and building-renewal directions; the second tier is scenario-embedded micro-renewal along the rail spine, mainly pocket parks, interchange nodes and ground-floor use replacement — low-disturbance, phaseable and reversible.
