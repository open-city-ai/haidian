# Jing-Zhang AI Origin Belt — From Railway Origin to AI Origin

> **Concept in one line**: A century ago Zhan Tianyou built China's first fully self-designed railway here — the origin of Chinese "self-reliant engineering." A century later this same corridor becomes the **origin of China's self-reliant AI innovation**. The scheme organizes space as **The Origin Belt** — one axis, three cores, two wings — and upgrades "origin" from historical narrative into an operable public mechanism through a three-layer **Origin Protocol**: contributions are inscribable, scenarios are exitable, conclusions are recomputable.

**v2.0 iteration note.** v1 delivered a complete recomputable package (all four self-checks PASS, repository intake merged). Without changing the spatial skeleton or geometry, v2 deepens three things: (1) the monument-inscription idea becomes a three-layer Origin Protocol running through industry, scenarios and governance; (2) each of the three key areas is expanded to "current issues — spatial moves — action packages — risk conditions" detail; (3) the implementation chapter adds action packages with conceptual owners, prerequisites, stop conditions and service targets, so "implementable" also means "stoppable, exitable, restorable."

## Three-Level Scope Framework

Work is transmitted level by level [depth:three_level_scope_framework]: the 43.6 km² coordinated research area addresses the AI ecosystem and future urban form; the 11.4 km² overall design area delivers regulatory-plan-level urban design; the 368.4 ha key areas deliver detailed design. The overall structure is **one axis, three cores, two wings** [depth:overall_spatial_structure]: the axis is the Jing-Zhang Heritage Park vitality belt ([data:geometry/green_space.geojson#GREEN-001], [metric:green_ratio]); the three cores are Zhongzhiyuan (A1), Beijing AI Origin Community (A2) and Dazhongsi (A3) ([data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-001], [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-002], [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-003]); the two wings extend service and testing westward/along the water. Transmission rule: research-level conclusions never masquerade as design-level premises; each level's outputs are labelled at their own evidence grade. No pseudo-precise redline is added [standard:MOHURD-URBAN-DESIGN-MEASURES].

## Coordinated Research Area: Industry and Future City Research

For a world-class AI ecosystem (1.3.1 / 1.5.1.1, agent.2) the belt builds a **full-stack self-reliant chain** — compute → data → frameworks/algorithms → models → applications/scenarios — anchored by Zhongzhiyuan (full-stack accelerator), the AI Origin Community (open-source and translation hub) and Dazhongsi (smart-economy and international-exchange interface) [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK] [standard:PROJECT-AGENT-OPEN-CALL-TASKBOOK]. Seven global cases inform transferable mechanisms with explicit transfer boundaries (Silicon Valley — near-campus translation only; Kendall Square — walkable coupling, not density; King's Cross — station-front public interface; Station F — single-door services, scale distributed; one-north — graded scenario opening; Shenzhen Nanshan — testing-as-industry-service; Hangzhou Yunqi — open platform catalog, data authorization separate). The future urban form (1.3.2 / 1.5.1.2) lands AI mobility, continuous blue-green space and services as locatable zones, nodes, corridors and scenarios ([data:geometry/roads.geojson#ROAD-001], [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#PUBLIC-001], [metric:public_space_ratio]). Regional synergy links Future Science City and Huairou (research), the city core via Dazhongsi (smart economy), the university cluster via the Xiaoyue wing, and a "R&D in the belt, production in BDA" conceptual division. **Naming/identity (agent.1):** main name *Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt*, concept *The Origin Belt*, logo direction a concentric "origin ⊙" abstracting the railway's herringbone into the AI origin point, extensible as "⊙ + scenario icon" across all wayfinding.

| **L2 Exitable scenarios** | Every AI scenario must carry a six-field entry–run–exit protocol before entering public space; the public holds non-participation, appeal and feedback rights; failing scenarios are downgraded or exited | 12 scenario cards; scenario-operation platform | Residents, visitors, vulnerable groups |

The protocol's operating body is conceptually a tripartite structure (organizer + professional institutions + community representatives) with public rules, review records and exit cases. This is a conceptual mechanism suggestion; legal form and operating boundaries require professional deepening.

## Overall Design Area: Urban Renewal and Regulatory-Plan-Level Urban Design

At regulatory-plan urban-design depth [standard:MOHURD-CONTROL-DETAILED-PLANNING] [depth:land_use_layout], the area is fully partitioned into 12 land-use classes with **complete coverage, no gaps, no overlaps** (gap=0), areas recomputed in EPSG:4548 ([data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-0701], [metric:land_use_polygon_count], [metric:site_area_sqm]). Industry & function (1.5.2.1): research/culture in the north (0802/0803), research-education-housing mix in the middle (0802/0804/0701), commercial in the south (05) ([data:geometry/buildings.geojson#BLDG-001], [metric:building_footprint_area_sqm]). A functional principle: industrial space must not squeeze the living base — any renewal reducing access to community services triggers re-justification (see stop conditions). Urban-renewal framework (1.5.2.2): three renewal targets — inefficient industrial space, broken public interfaces, dormant stock buildings — treated by small-scale incremental block mending, yielding 8 renewal projects [depth:development_intensity_controls]. FAR/density remain to-be-confirmed until official controls exist ([metric:building_density]).

## Detailed Design of Key Areas

All three key areas are mandatory (1.5.3.1–3, agent.4) at comprehensive-implementation depth [depth:three_key_area_detailed_design], inside the site and non-overlapping ([metric:key_area_count]). A shared design grammar — **one core interface + one set of scenario anchors + one mending route** — keeps the three areas coherent.

**A1 Zhongzhiyuan AI Acceleration Area (≈192.1 ha).** Issues (conceptual): park-style layout underuses the Qinghe riverfront; testing functions lack dedicated venues; slow-traffic gaps southward. Positioning: full-stack self-reliant innovation, garden blocks; the industrial side of Protocol L3. Moves: Qinghe low-carbon innovation interface (open testing lawns + industry display loop [data:geometry/green_space.geojson#GREEN-001]); garden-block conversion with sectioned wall opening; a display gateway toward the belt. Anchors: safety-governance sandbox★, embodied-intelligence testbed★, City Agent Pavilion. Risks: blue-line and flood conditions unknown; testing safety radius needs assessment; controls unconfirmed — all in assumptions with stop conditions (JZ-02).

**A2 Beijing AI Origin Community (≈104.3 ha).** Issues (conceptual): campus–park–block management boundaries block walking; translation lacks last-mile venues; talent housing pressure. Positioning: near-campus translation and talent community; the carrier of Protocol L1 — the AI Origin Monument and its ~11,700 m² memorial plaza [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#PUBLIC-004] sit here ([data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-002]). Moves: campus–park–block mending into a 15-minute translation route; open-source release hall and translation street along it; the memorial plaza for inscription ceremonies, open-source events and daily public life. Anchors: release hall, city-model open platform★, translation street, AI Origin Monument. Risks: campus boundaries and tenure to confirm; housing scale depends on controls; plaza permits and inscription rules need clearance — assumptions with stop conditions (JZ-03/JZ-06).

**A3 Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster (≈72.0 ha).** Issues (conceptual): the four station quadrants are poorly connected; stock retail mismatches smart-economy uses; no dedicated international interface. Positioning: urban smart economy and international exchange; the public-experience side of Protocol L2 where scenarios face the strongest public scrutiny. Moves: four-quadrant walk connection around the station [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#PUBLIC-003]; smart-native consumption and data-element interfaces in renovated stock; an international showroom. Anchors: international showroom, data-element salon, AI life-service street. Risks: multi-party station coordination and utility relocation; data-circulation compliance depends on higher policy — assumptions with stop conditions (JZ-04).

## AI Innovation Ecosystem, Personas, and AI+ Scenarios

**Scenario entry–run–exit protocol (Protocol L2, contractualized).** Before entering public space every scenario completes six fields: (1) purpose; (2) boundary — extent, hours, speed/intensity caps; (3) data — what is and is not collected, retention, access; (4) human responsibility — which step a named human finally owns; (5) exit conditions — which metric or event triggers downgrade, pause or removal; (6) correction inlet — how the public reports, appeals and gets responses.

**12 AI scenario cards** (agent.3; 3 industry test/validation scenarios marked ★): open-source release hall; safety-governance sandbox★ (test data never leaves the sandbox); embodied-intelligence testbed★ (bounded venue and hours, on-site human supervision); city-model open platform★ (public data-authorization catalog); autonomous low-speed shuttle (Xiaoyue corridor + belt connector ring, [data:geometry/roads.geojson#ROAD-019], speed caps and human takeover); AI slow-traffic navigation (no individual trajectories); Dazhongsi international showroom; data-element salon; near-campus translation street; AI life-service model street (every service keeps a human counter); building smart-energy; global AI week route ([metric:ai_scenario_card_count]; nodes reconnect to [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#PUBLIC-000], [data:geometry/green_space.geojson#GREEN-002], [metric:green_ratio]).

**6 personas**: returnee founder; big-tech researcher; PhD/developer; senior resident (**all basic services keep non-digital paths**); international visitor; young digital nomad — each with spatial response and a data/privacy boundary.

**Governance boundary and public rights.** On top of data minimization, public sources, explainability and human review, v2 adds three public-rights clauses: (1) **right to non-digital paths** — government, medical and mobility basics always retain human channels, so elderly and digitally vulnerable groups are never excluded by scenario upgrades; (2) **right to appeal and correction** — every scenario's correction inlet is posted on-site and online, with response time in operating KPIs; (3) **right not to be profiled** — public-space scenarios must not output individual profiles; violation triggers the exit clause. City agents assist with gap detection, space heat, maintenance and event safety but never replace approval, output unauthorized profiles, or claim official commitments.

## Land Use, Building Scale, and Retain-Renovate-Demolish Strategy

Land use follows the national classification [standard:MNR-LAND-USE-CLASSIFICATION-GUIDE] as 12 seamless classes ([data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-05], [metric:land_use_polygon_count]). Buildings distinguish retain / renovate / renew / new-build / to-be-confirmed [depth:retain_renovate_demolish]; conceptual principles: retain first (industrial memory and community functions), renovate as default (no large-scale demolition), concentrate new-build at station nodes and service gaps, and default unknown tenure/structure/heritage objects to "to-be-confirmed." Illustrative footprints ([data:geometry/buildings.geojson#BLDG-001], [metric:building_count], [metric:building_footprint_area_sqm], [metric:building_density]) support structure/density study only. Height/massing/character [depth:height_massing_character]: low-mid garden massing near Qinghe and campuses, mid-density talent blocks in the middle, moderate station-integrated intensity near Dazhongsi; heights/setbacks/FAR remain unknown pending official controls ([standard:MOHURD-ARCH-DESIGN-DEPTH-2016]).

## Transport, Rail, Municipal Infrastructure, and Public Services

Mobility [depth:traffic_rail_slow_parking]: a slow-traffic spine + 8 east-west mending links + a perimeter loop + AI connector ring, centerline ≈ [metric:road_centerline_length_m] ([data:geometry/roads.geojson#ROAD-001]); priorities are the North 5th Ring crossing, Dazhongsi station four-quadrant connection, and campus-city links; an AI low-speed connector ring along the vitality belt links the three cores (concept, [data:geometry/roads.geojson#ROAD-019]). **Slow-traffic-first rule:** pedestrians have priority along the full spine; the connector ring is speed-capped and yields; before any motorized AI scenario expands, corridor baselines (pedestrian volume, accessibility needs, noise, safety) must exist — no baseline, no expansion. The Jing-Zhang heritage alignment and Xiaoyue/Qinghe water are shown as indicative lines (not official) ([data:geometry/constraints.geojson#CONSTRAINT-RAIL-1]). Municipal/new infrastructure [depth:municipal_new_infrastructure]: distributed energy and edge-compute prototypes reuse existing buildings and corridors — no standalone land take; edge nodes co-locate with community facilities so new infrastructure serves both industry and neighborhood. Engineering conditions are listed as prerequisites in assumptions.

## Blue-Green Network, Public Space, and Urban Character

Blue-green [depth:blue_green_public_space]: the heritage-park belt as main green axis with pocket parks and buffer greens; green area [metric:green_space_area_sqm], green ratio [metric:green_ratio] ([data:geometry/green_space.geojson#GREEN-001]); public space [metric:public_space_area_sqm], ratio [metric:public_space_ratio] ([data:geometry/public_space.geojson#PUBLIC-001]). **Cultural narrative (agent.5)** fuses three cultures — Jing-Zhang railway heritage, Zhongguancun innovation culture, and AI culture — along a north-south storyline "engineering self-reliance (A1) → intelligent self-reliance (A2) → open confidence (A3)," under a "Jing-Zhang teal + origin red + garden green" palette [standard:MOHURD-URBAN-DESIGN-MEASURES], with an "origin ⊙" signage family. International tagline: "From the railway that proved a nation could build its own, to the AI that proves it can create its own." **AI pilgrimage landmarks (≥3, agent.4)**: AI Origin Monument (inscribing contributors' GitHub names, echoing this call's own mechanism; memorial plaza [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#PUBLIC-004]) — the physical carrier of Protocol L1; Zhan Tianyou–Turing Dialogue Plaza; City Agent Pavilion. The honor system is a component library — digital wall (real-time, searchable, appealable) + annual physical inscription under public rules — replicable at any public node.

## Renewal Projects, Implementation Policy, and Phasing

Eight renewal projects upgraded into **action packages** [depth:renewal_project_list] ([metric:renewal_project_count]), each with conceptual owner, prerequisites and **stop conditions** — "implementable" includes "stoppable": JZ-01 belt gap-mending (stops if construction endangers existing slow traffic); JZ-02 Qinghe interface (reverts to pure green if ecological metrics degrade); JZ-03 translation street (re-justified if community-service access drops); JZ-04 station connection (stays at status-quo optimization if coordination fails); JZ-05 edge-compute nodes (devices go offline if energy/safety exceed limits); JZ-06 memorial plaza (plaza only, no inscription, while rules are disputed [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#PUBLIC-004]); JZ-07 Xiaoyue scenario corridor (sensors removed upon verified privacy complaints); JZ-08 global AI week route (route shrinks if public space overloads) ([data:geometry/phasing.geojson#PHASE-001], [data:geometry/phasing.geojson#PHASE-003], [metric:phasing_area_sqm]). Phasing [depth:phasing_implementation]: near-term (2026–2028) core anchoring + belt mending → mid-term (2029–2032) block renewal + station-city integration → long-term (2033–2035) full-belt connection + global scenario operations — a dependency order, not a rigid timetable. **Conceptual service targets (for operational deepening, not commitments):** zero gaps on the spine; any point in the three cores reaches a scenario anchor and a human service counter within a 15-minute walk; correction response within one operating day; 100% publication of annual inscription rules. **Long-term operations (agent.6):** annual Jing-Zhang Open-Source Festival (Protocol L1 — the inscription ceremony closes it) / City-Agent Challenge (L3 — public evaluation on real urban issues) / AI Pilgrimage Week (L2 — all open scenarios face public testing); a developer-community mechanism seeded by this GitHub open call, whose Issue/PR flow becomes the standing co-creation channel; a scenario-as-a-service platform (apply — protocol review — public run — periodic recheck); a talent-landing conversion pathway. All are conceptual suggestions.
