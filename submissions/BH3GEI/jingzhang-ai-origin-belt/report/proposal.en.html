Jing-Zhang AI Origin Belt — From Railway Origin to AI Origin

> **Concept in one line**: A century ago Zhan Tianyou built China's first fully self-designed railway here — the origin of Chinese "self-reliant engineering." A century later this same corridor becomes the **origin of China's self-reliant AI innovation**. The scheme organizes space as **The Origin Belt**: one axis, three cores, two wings, carrying "self-reliance" from railway spirit into an AI-era urban paradigm.

Three-Level Scope Framework

Work is transmitted level by level [depth:three_level_scope_framework]: the 43.6 km² coordinated research area addresses the AI ecosystem and future urban form; the 11.4 km² overall design area delivers regulatory-plan-level urban design; the 368.4 ha key areas deliver detailed design. The overall structure is **one axis, three cores, two wings** [depth:overall_spatial_structure]: the axis is the Jing-Zhang Heritage Park vitality belt ([data:geometry/green_space.geojson#GREEN-001], [metric:green_ratio]); the three cores are Zhongzhiyuan (A1), Beijing AI Origin Community (A2) and Dazhongsi (A3) ([data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-001]); the two wings extend service and testing westward/along the water. It adds no pseudo-precise redline and reconnects to verifiable land-use and public-space layers [standard:MOHURD-URBAN-DESIGN-MEASURES].

Coordinated Research Area: Industry and Future City Research

For a world-class AI ecosystem (1.3.1 / 1.5.1.1, agent.2) the belt builds a **full-stack self-reliant chain** — compute → data → frameworks/algorithms → models → applications/scenarios — anchored by Zhongzhiyuan (full-stack accelerator), the AI Origin Community (open-source and translation hub) and Dazhongsi (smart-economy and international-exchange interface) [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK] [standard:PROJECT-AGENT-OPEN-CALL-TASKBOOK]. Five to eight global cases inform transferable mechanisms (Silicon Valley; Kendall Square; King's Cross; Station F; Singapore one-north; Shenzhen Nanshan; Hangzhou Yunqi). The future urban form (1.3.2 / 1.5.1.2) lands AI mobility, continuous blue-green space and services as locatable zones, nodes, corridors and scenarios ([data:geometry/roads.geojson#ROAD-001], [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#PUBLIC-001], [metric:public_space_ratio]). **Naming/identity (agent.1):** main name *Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt*, concept *The Origin Belt*, logo direction a concentric "origin ⊙" abstracting the railway's herringbone into the AI origin point.

Detailed Design of Key Areas

The three key areas (1.5.3.required / 1.5.3.1–3, agent.4) reach implementation-plan depth [depth:three_key_area_detailed_design], all within [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-001] and non-overlapping ([metric:key_area_count]). **A1 Zhongzhiyuan (~192.1 ha)** — full-stack self-reliant innovation garden-block: Qinghe low-carbon innovation edge, standards & safety-governance display, embodied-intelligence validation. **A2 Beijing AI Origin Community (~104.3 ha)** — near-campus translation & talent community: campus-park-street slow-traffic mending, open-source release, city-model open platform, the AI Origin Monument. **A3 Dazhongsi (~72.0 ha)** — urban smart economy & international exchange: four-quadrant pedestrian connection at Dazhongsi station, smart-terminal & content consumption, data-element salon.
