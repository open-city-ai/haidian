### The Three-Zones-Two-Wings Coordination Loop: The Spatial Closure of the Industry Chain

**Design judgment.** The three zones are not three isolated parks, but a closed loop of "R&D — translation — market — feedback": Zhongzhiyuan undertakes full-stack independent innovation (computing power, frameworks, models, toolchains) and discourse power in AI governance (permanent venue of the AI Governance Forum, concept); the Origin Community undertakes open-source collaboration, startup incubation, and international talent aggregation, serving as the "translation valve" for full-stack results; Dazhongsi undertakes AI-native new business formats, new AI consumption scenarios, and city-level display, serving as the "amplifier" bringing results to market; real scenario data and market demand generated at Dazhongsi and the Xiaoyue River wing flow back to Zhongzhiyuan, driving iteration of models and toolchains—scenarios feeding back into R&D, closing the loop. The two wings do not stand outside the loop but above it: the Zhongguancun Technology Service Wing (west) provides factor, capital, and IP services to every link, while the Xiaoyue River Scenario Empowerment Wing (east) provides test scenarios and a vibrant waterfront interface to every link. Industry-chain coordination thus turns from an "upstream-downstream roster" into a "spatial queue along the axis."

**Why this judgment.** The single-park model commonly suffers a rupture of "R&D without scenarios" or "scenarios without R&D"; the roughly 10.7 km linear corridor within the site (comprising the southern section of the genuine abandoned Beijing–Baotou alignment plus a designed extension, provisional recalculation) is naturally suited as the physical bus of the industry chain—the R&D end, translation end, and market end are distributed along the axis, scenarios are tested along the axis, and feedback flows back along the axis [metric:heritage_axis_length_m]. This loop simultaneously responds to Announcement 1.5(1)'s dual requirement for a world-class AI innovation ecosystem and industry-chain coordination [source:DATA-SRC-AGENT-TASKBOOK-20260518].

### Future Urban Form: AI Culture, Society, and City

**Design judgment.** AI's transformation of future urban form is essentially a transformation of "how time is used": research and development tend toward 24-hour operation, and the boundaries among work, life, socializing, and learning shift from spatial separation to temporal switching. The formal judgment of this proposal is therefore: from a "zoned-function city" toward a "scenario-mixed city"—on the same axis, daytime is commuting and R&D, evening is sports and socializing, and night is light-and-shadow and consumption. The three layers of issues—culture, society, city—are answered respectively by narrative, inclusion, and services.

- **AI culture**: a three-layer temporal narrative—1909 Jingzhang (industrial self-reliance, Zhan Tianyou's herringbone track) → 1980s Zhongguancun (Electronics Street, reform-and-opening innovation) → 2020s AI New Track (intelligent self-determination, full-stack innovation), with the main storyline "from iron tracks to light tracks"; the international communication tagline is Where Tracks Meet Minds. Culture is not technological decoration; heritage narrative takes precedence and is respected. There are 3+1 AI pilgrimage landmarks (numbered LM-1 to LM-4)—LM-1 "New Track Origin" memorial ground (a 1909 old-style station sign juxtaposed with a parameter-server cabinet, concept proposal, involving no alteration to heritage bodies), LM-2 "Code Platform" (an honor walk for open-source contributors), LM-3 "Zhongzhi Engine" computing-power lighthouse (displaying only aggregated public data), LM-4 "Bell-Signal Moment" (alternative)—together with the New Track Hall of Fame, the open-source project honor track, and the AI-for-good case wall forming an honor display system, all concept proposals [metric:pilgrimage_landmark_count][data:geometry/public_space.geojson#SN-02].
- **AI society**: inclusiveness is the first constraint. Long-term residents demand that "familiar life not be disturbed"; the proposal responds with predominantly retained residential blocks (mainly 0701, about 29.9%, provisional recalculation), AI elder-assistance service stations (S06, voice interaction plus human backstop, mandatory human review), and heritage memory displays; all scenarios follow the overarching principles of minimal collection, edge-first processing, explicit notice, switchability, human-review backstop, and no personal identification.

### Territorial Spatial Planning Innovation: Reserved Land, Mixed Use, and Scenario-as-Infrastructure

**Design judgment.** Facing an AI industry whose iteration speed far exceeds statutory planning cycles, this proposal puts forward three planning-tool innovations, all concept proposals that constitute no regulatory-plan adjustment conclusions:

1. **Reserved land as proactive elasticity.** Reserved land (16) is about 778,000 sqm, 6.8% (provisional recalculation) [metric:land_use_area_by_code_sqm], located mainly in Zhongzhiyuan's strategic reserved blocks. Reservation is not "unplanned"; it is the proactive reservation of temporal elasticity for unforeseeable industrial forms (such as full-stack hardware pilot testing, governance facilities, and future infrastructure); during the reservation period, composite use as green space, scenario testing, and public events is a typology-level concept proposal involving no parcel-specific retain-renovate-demolish conclusions.
2. **Mixed use at the scale of the "block."** The multiple-block system—retained residential blocks (0701, about 29.9%), research translation blocks (0802, about 19.9%), commercial (05, about 18.3%), cultural exhibition blocks (0803), education collaboration blocks (0804), strategic reserved blocks (16)—encourages functional mixing of R&D, residence, commerce, and culture at the block scale, rather than stacking conflicting uses within a single parcel; land use expression follows the land use classification framework for territorial spatial survey, planning, and use control [standard:MNR-LAND-USE-CLASSIFICATION-GUIDE][source:DATA-SRC-MNR-LAND-USE-CLASSIFICATION-202311].
3. **Scenarios as a new type of infrastructure.** The 8 AI scenario nodes [metric:scenario_node_count] are planned, built, maintained, and retired as an infrastructure layer of the same rank as roads and green space: the computing-power sharing post (S05, edge-computing experience and inclusive computing-power reservation, concept), the Xiaoyue River water-quality sensing network (S04, environmental sensing plus predictive early warning, data made public), and municipal new infrastructure such as smart light poles and sensing pipe networks (concept) form a "scenario infrastructure" pedigree; each scenario establishes full-cycle management rules of "siting — capacity — operation — exit," and test scenarios are limited in area and time window and implemented only after approval by the competent authority.

**Why this judgment.** Traditional regulatory-plan tools are denominated in certainty and in parcels, whereas AI-industry demand is denominated in uncertainty and in networks. Reserved land provides temporal elasticity, mixed use provides functional elasticity, and scenario-as-infrastructure turns "experimentation" from a temporary act into a standing urban capability—together the three answer the methodological proposition of the AI age: "how planning can leave room for a rapidly evolving industry."

## Detailed Design of Key Areas

**Design judgment**: The three key areas play three roles on the "One Axis" — Bell-Signal Station at the south end is the "time-announcing station" facing the city and consumption (first-launch and showcase of AI-native new business formats); Origin Station in the middle is the "central hub station" of the innovation ecosystem (the conversion hub for talent, open source, and incubation); and Zhongzhi Station at the north end is the "final assembly yard" of full-stack indigenous innovation (computing power–framework–models–toolchain and governance discourse power). From south to north, scenario density decreases while strategic flexibility increases, forming a gradient of "urban interface — ecosystem hub — strategic reservation." **Basis**: the announcement designates 368.4 ha of key areas (from north to south: Zhongzhiyuan 192.1 ha, AI Origin Community 104.3 ha, Dazhongsi 72.0 ha) and requires them to reach the depth of a comprehensive planning implementation scheme [source:DATA-SRC-OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT-20260509][standard:PROJECT-OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT]; the taskbook's "three areas and two wings" defines the functions of the three areas [source:DATA-SRC-AGENT-TASKBOOK-20260518]. **Layers and metrics**: the three-area boundaries [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#KEY-001][data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#KEY-002][data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#KEY-003] (provisional_constraint, official_boundary=false [source:DATA-SRC-PROVISIONAL-BOUNDARIES-20260605]); recalculated areas: Zhongzhiyuan 1,929,202 sqm (announced 192.1 ha, +0.43%), Origin Community 1,043,237 sqm (announced 104.3 ha, +0.02%), Dazhongsi 720,454 sqm (announced 72.0 ha, +0.06%) [metric:key_area_sqm_by_id][metric:key_area_count]; the detailed design depth is checked against [depth:three_key_area_detailed_design], and the expression of regulatory plan conditions follows [standard:MOHURD-CONTROL-DETAILED-PLANNING]. **Data gaps**: the official precise boundaries, internal ownership, existing buildings, and regulatory plan conditions of the three areas are all missing; all spatial recommendations in this section are concept proposals / reference schemes for professional teams to deepen.

## AI Innovation Ecosystem, Personas, and AI+ Scenarios

### Design Judgment: The Ecosystem Is the Track; the Scenarios Are the Stations

**Design judgment.** This chapter translates the "AI innovation ecosystem" from an abstract industry slogan into a locatable triple structure of space–people–scenarios: the three stations (Bell-Signal Station | Dazhongsi, Origin Station | AI Origin Community, Zhongzhi Station | Zhongzhiyuan) carry three ecosystem roles; six user personas define the served groups; and 12 AI scenario cards (S01–S12) anchor 8 physical scenario nodes and the plaza and green-corridor systems. Among them, S02, S03, and S11 are **industry test-and-validation scenarios** ①②③, satisfying the agent taskbook's hard requirements of "no fewer than 10 scenario cards, no fewer than 3 industry test-and-validation scenarios, and no fewer than 5 user personas" [source:DATA-SRC-AGENT-TASKBOOK-20260518][standard:PROJECT-AGENT-OPEN-CALL-TASKBOOK].

**Why this judgment.** The announcement's "three areas and two wings" framework has already written the ecosystem division of labor: Zhongzhiyuan carries the AI full-stack indigenous innovation system and governance discourse power, the Origin Community carries the world-class AI innovation ecosystem, and Dazhongsi carries AI-native new business formats [source:DATA-SRC-OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT-20260509]. The proposal does not perform a second round of industrial division, but translates that division into spatial products in the vocabulary of "stations": the "final assembly yard" of full-stack innovation in Zhongzhiyuan [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#KEY-001] (recalculated 1,929,202 sqm, provisional [metric:key_area_sqm_by_id]), the "central hub station" of talent and open source in the Origin Community [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#KEY-002] (1,043,237 sqm, provisional), and the "time-announcing station" of new business formats in Dazhongsi [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#KEY-003] (720,454 sqm, provisional). Scenarios are not icons pasted on the ground but real public products running on the approximately 10.7 km New Track Main Axis (provisional recalculation, concept proposal) [metric:heritage_axis_length_m], 13.4% green space [metric:green_ratio] (provisional), and 2.2% public space [metric:public_space_ratio] (provisional), with their spatial skeleton corresponding in depth to the blue-green system [depth:blue_green_public_space][depth:overall_spatial_structure].

**Corresponding layers and metrics.** All 8 AI scenario nodes enter the public-space layer [metric:scenario_node_count], located at [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#SN-01] through [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#SN-08]; the plaza system is PS-01–PS-05, and the linear public spaces are PS-X01–PS-X03; slow-mobility and test corridors rely on the [data:geometry/roads.geojson#RD-07] New Track slow-mobility main axis and the RD-09/10/11 micro-circulation loops (all concept proposals).

**Data gaps.** Official precise boundaries are not public; all areas and lengths use the provisional scope [source:DATA-SRC-PROVISIONAL-BOUNDARIES-20260605]; regulatory plan conditions (floor area ratio, building height, density) are missing, and the scale of carriers on which scenarios depend is uniformly "to be confirmed"; approvals by competent authorities and test permits required for scenario operation have not been obtained, and this chapter is written throughout as "pending approval," constituting no approved commitment [depth:risk_missing_data].

### AI+ Scenario Cards (S01–S12)

The following 12 scenario cards cover the directions of transport, services, consumption, healthcare, education, law, and life services proposed in the announcement [source:DATA-SRC-OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT-20260509], divided into industry-development scenarios and AI-empowered urban-function scenarios; each card specifies the served users, spatial location, operating data, privacy boundary, human review, operating entity, and risks. All spatial locations are concept proposals / reference schemes for professional teams to deepen; all test scenarios operate within restricted areas and restricted time windows and require approval by the competent authorities before implementation (pending approval, not approved). Operating entities uniformly adopt a three-layer operating concept proposal of "government guidance + platform company + community co-governance," with the responsibility emphasis annotated card by card in the table below.

**S01 New Track AR Time-Overlay Touring (public experience scenario)**
- Location and carrier: the AR experience section of the heritage park [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#SN-03], relying on the southern section of the New Track green corridor [data:geometry/green_space.geojson#GS-01] and the heritage corridor control belt [data:geometry/constraints.geojson#CT-HERITAGE-01].
- Served users: P5 visitors/pilgrims, P3 local residents, P4 students.
- Operating data: historical-layer content packages, on-device positioning and orientation data; passenger flow is counted in aggregate only, with no individual trajectories retained.
- Privacy boundary: images and positioning are processed entirely on-device; no identifiable footage is uploaded; no personal identity recognition.
- Human review: historical content is manually proofread by literature-history and heritage consultants before going live; residents' oral content is used with authorization.
- Operating entity: platform company + heritage park management + literature-history consultant team (concept proposal).
- Risks and countermeasures: risk of distorted historical narrative — hedged by historical-source proofreading and explicit "conceptual interpretation" labeling; zero intervention on the heritage body, with the conservation scope to be re-checked after official publication [depth:risk_missing_data].

**S02 Autonomous Shuttle Micro-Circulation (industry test-and-validation scenario ①, pending approval)**
- Location and carrier: the autonomous-shuttle test station [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#SN-04], with operating corridors on the New Track slow-mobility main axis [data:geometry/roads.geojson#RD-07] and the three-station micro-circulation loops [data:geometry/roads.geojson#RD-09][data:geometry/roads.geojson#RD-10][data:geometry/roads.geojson#RD-11] (concept proposal).
- Served users: inter-station commuting for P1 researchers, P2 entrepreneurs, and P4 students; also serves as a restricted-area L4 shuttle testbed.
- Operating data: vehicle operating status, aggregated station passenger-flow counts, safety-officer takeover records; test data is logged per regulatory requirements.
- Privacy boundary: exterior sensing data is desensitized on-device; no passenger identity profiles are built; the test-vehicle identity is explicitly announced.
- Human review: human safety officers throughout; 100% human debriefing of takeovers and abnormal events.
- Operating entity: government guidance (test permits and supervision) + platform company (operation) + resident-enterprise technical teams (by application).
- Risks and countermeasures: traffic-safety and right-of-way risks — restricted areas, restricted time windows, speed-limited operation; the test plan is subject to approval by the competent authorities; no commercialization timeline is committed.

**S03 Urban-Furniture AI Inspection (industry test-and-validation scenario ②, pending approval)**
- Location and carrier: the entire New Track green corridor and the three station plazas; inspection routes run collinear with the slow-mobility main axis [data:geometry/roads.geojson#RD-07] and green-corridor segments (e.g., [data:geometry/green_space.geojson#GS-12]).
- Served users: P6 operators (facility maintenance) and all users (public safety).
- Operating data: facility-condition images (seats, luminaires, paving, signage), defect-classification results, work-order closure records; only imagery of the facilities themselves is retained.
- Privacy boundary: cameras face facilities and paving; pedestrian footage is blurred on-device; no facial recognition or behavior profiling.
- Human review: maintenance work orders are generated only after robot pre-judgment + human review confirmation; human-machine collaboration is mandatory.
- Operating entity: platform company + municipal maintenance unit (concept proposal); during the test period, inclusion in the scenario-open application system.
- Risks and countermeasures: invalid work orders caused by misjudgment — backstopped by human review; equipment investment and maintenance costs are not investment conclusions.

**S04 Xiaoyue River Water-Quality Sensing Network (AI-empowered urban-function scenario)**
- Location and carrier: the Xiaoyue River water-quality sensing and waterfront scenario experiment section [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#SN-05], located in the Xiaoyue River waterfront control belt [data:geometry/constraints.geojson#CT-BLUE-01] and the waterfront greenway [data:geometry/roads.geojson#RD-08].
- Served users: P6 operators (environmental early warning), P3 residents, and the general public (waterfront safety and data awareness).

### General Principles of Privacy and Human Review

**Design judgment.** The 12 scenario cards share a single governance baseline — set the rules first, then discuss the technology: whatever is collected must be minimal; whatever can be on-device must not go to the cloud; whatever involves judgment must involve a human. This is the fundamental reason the proposal defines AI scenarios as "public products" rather than "data entrances" — the credibility of the innovation belt is scarcer than any single-point technology demonstration, and this principle directly responds to the taskbook's requirements on privacy boundaries and human-review mechanisms [source:DATA-SRC-AGENT-TASKBOOK-20260518].

**Six general principles (applicable to all scenario cards):**
1. **Minimal collection**: only the data types and durations necessary for scenario operation are collected; out-of-scope collection is a design defect;
2. **On-device first**: images, voice, posture, and positioning are processed on-device by default; the cloud receives only desensitized aggregated results;
3. **Explicit notice**: device identity, collection scope, and data destination are displayed on site so the public can be informed;
4. **Switch-off capability**: all person-facing interactive functions can be switched off with one click, and switching off does not affect passage or basic services;
5. **Human-review backstop**: for all matters involving safety, health, funds, law, or publication, machine outputs take effect only after human confirmation, and systems issue no final opinions;
6. **No personal identity recognition**: no scenario performs personal identity recognition, builds personal profiles, or is used for commercial recommendation.

**Additional rules for test scenarios.** S02/S03/S11 (and S12 observation activities) all operate within restricted areas and restricted time windows; test protocols are public; implementation requires approval by the competent authorities — this proposal uniformly writes "pending approval," never "approved."

**Corresponding layers and standards.** Governance boundaries and spatial boundaries are managed on the same map: all privacy-sensitive scenario points are located at verifiable positions on the public-space layer [metric:scenario_node_count][depth:blue_green_public_space]; the human-review work-order system connects to the sensing layer of municipal new infrastructure [depth:municipal_new_infrastructure]; the depth of spatial expression follows the Urban Design Management Measures' requirements on public interest and implementability [standard:MOHURD-URBAN-DESIGN-MEASURES][source:DATA-SRC-MOHURD-URBAN-DESIGN-MEASURES].

**Data gaps and risks.** Approval pathways for scenario implementation, data-compliance details, and district-level platform interface conditions are all to be confirmed [depth:risk_missing_data]; AI scenario privacy and ethics risks and activity-operation sustainability risks are registered as a whole in the risk chapter. All scenarios in this chapter are concept proposals / reference schemes for professional teams to deepen; once official boundaries and regulatory plan conditions are obtained, the area and capacity statements of scenario points must be recalculated as a whole with the provisional scope [source:DATA-SRC-PROVISIONAL-BOUNDARIES-20260605][metric:site_area_sqm].

### Supply Logic: Why New Functions Are Located Here

**Design judgment**: New supply follows "three orientations" — research and innovation-source supply is superimposed onto the Xueyuan Road–Shuangqing Road university belt; commercial and new-format supply is clustered toward the three station gateways; green space and public space supply is concentrated along the main axis. Residential use sees no large-scale addition; service gaps are filled against the 15-minute living circle standard.

**Basis and rationale**: First, the spatial overlap of research land (0802, approx. 19.9%) with the university belt corresponds to the transferable mechanism of Silicon Valley's "university–capital–enterprise corridor ecosystem," placing innovation-source functions close to the wellspring of knowledge. Second, commercial land (05, approx. 18.3%) clusters toward Bell-Signal Station and the southern segment of Origin Station, supporting the debut and display functions of "intelligence-native new business formats" and preventing the disorderly infiltration of innovation functions into residential blocks. Third, AI industry formats iterate extremely fast; concentrating approximately 6.8% of strategic reserve land in Zhongzhiyuan institutionalizes "uncertainty" as spatial flexibility — the reserve is not idle land, but a strategic reserve for the "final assembly ground" of full-stack indigenous innovation. All of the above supply placements are concept proposals, available for professional teams to deepen.

**Layer and metric evidence**: The supply structure is mapped onto the land use layer [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-G01] and the key areas layer [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#KEY-001]; the recalculable portion of supply scale is governed by [metric:land_use_area_by_code_sqm], and the recalculation method is described in the metrics chapter [depth:metrics_recalculation].

**Data gaps and items to be confirmed**: The actual use intensity, vacancy rate, and ownership structure of existing land uses are missing; identification of inefficient space can only indicate direction at the category level and cannot be reduced to a parcel-specific list.

## Transport, Rail, Municipal Infrastructure, and Public Services

### Design Judgment: Keep the Arterial Network Untouched; Assign Incremental Flows to Rail and Slow Mobility

**Design judgment**: The site is enclosed by three east–west arterials (existing, RD-01..03) — Xueyuan South Road, Zhichun Road, and Chengfu Road — together with edge arterials such as Xueyuan Road, Heqing Road–Dazhongsi East Road, and Xizhimenwai Street. This proposal does not alter the arterial road network and adds no cross-district connections; incremental traffic organization relies on a three-tier network of "four-station interchange + New Track slow-mobility main axis + three-station microcirculation" — all of which are concept proposals, available for professional teams to deepen.

**Basis and rationale**: All three "innovation stations" are immediately adjacent to existing rail transit stations, and the commuting, interaction, and experiential trips of innovation communities are naturally suited to a "rail + slow mobility" mode share. The heritage green corridor is itself the site's best slow-mobility infrastructure: placing the slow-mobility main axis on the corridor makes commuting routes coincide with cultural experience routes, so that transport facilities double as scenario facilities. This judgment responds to the announcement's requirements for rail-station integration, road microcirculation, slow-mobility breakpoints, and a green transport system [source:DATA-SRC-OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT-20260509], with depth governed by [depth:traffic_rail_slow_parking].

### Four-Station Interchange and the New Track Slow-Mobility Main Axis

**Design judgment**: Anchored on the four existing rail transit stations — Dazhongsi Station (Lines 12/13), Zhichunlu Station (Lines 10/13), Wudaokou Station (Line 13), and Qinghua Donglu Xikou Station (Line 15) — the proposal optimizes station-area integrated interchange (concept proposal). The New Track slow-mobility main axis carries the "last mile" connections between the stations and the innovation districts, with walking, cycling, and autonomous shuttles operating in combination.

**Basis and rationale**: All four stations are built and in operation; the scheme only optimizes interchange relationships and involves no proposals for any rail alignment. The slow-mobility main axis follows the disused alignment — linear, continuous, and low-disturbance — the most direct way to reactivate a stock corridor resource. The autonomous shuttle microcirculation (scenario card S02) is an industrial test-and-validation scenario, restricted to a defined area and defined time windows, conducting L4 shuttle testing with on-board human safety officers, and is subject to implementation only after approval by the competent authorities (pending approval) [source:DATA-SRC-AGENT-TASKBOOK-20260518][standard:PROJECT-AGENT-OPEN-CALL-TASKBOOK].

**Layer and metric evidence**: The New Track slow-mobility main axis [data:geometry/roads.geojson#RD-07] totals approximately 10.7 km within the boundary [metric:heritage_axis_length_m] (provisional recalculation; includes the in-boundary segment of the genuine disused alignment plus a designed extension; concept proposal). The Xiaoyue River waterfront greenway [data:geometry/roads.geojson#RD-08] carries waterfront slow mobility on the east side. Three microcirculation loops (Origin Community, Zhongzhiyuan, Dazhongsi; RD-09/10/11) [data:geometry/roads.geojson#RD-09] stitch internal slow mobility within the station areas. Slow-mobility-priority cross-section retrofits of Chengfu Road and Zhichun Road are concept proposals and involve no road redline conclusions.

No conclusions are made on the engineering feasibility or implementation sequencing of interchange optimization and cross-section retrofits; they are to be verified by professional institutions after transport and municipal base data are completed.

### Microcirculation and Parking Strategy

**Design judgment**: The parking strategy is "peripheral interception + shared parking": motor-vehicle interception is organized at the periphery of the key areas, while arrivals and departures are organized internally via microcirculation loops. Multi-story non-motorized parking nodes are provided (concept proposal), prioritizing the continuity of the slow-mobility main axis and station interchange. This proposal gives no parking-space numerical conclusions.

**Basis and rationale**: All three stations sit within highly built-up urban districts, where the spatial cost of new parking supply is high and runs counter to the green transport orientation; sharing existing spaces plus peripheral interception is the choice consistent with the "rail + slow mobility" mode-share model. The microcirculation loops (RD-09/10/11) serve only internal arrival/departure and emergency access within the key areas, and do not change the external road network pattern.

**Layer and metric evidence**: Recalculated road land is approximately 243,000 sqm, about 2.1% [metric:road_ratio] (includes only the three east–west arterial corridors; local streets are expressed as centerlines; provisional basis). For the microcirculation and slow-mobility network, see [data:geometry/roads.geojson#RD-09].

**Data gaps and items to be confirmed**: The base inventory of existing parking spaces, on-street parking, and building-attached parking provisions is missing; the scale of parking supply will be calculated after transport sector data are completed.

### Municipal New Infrastructure: Turning Infrastructure into an Experiential Innovation Interface

**Design judgment**: The municipal strategy is "traditional municipal works hold the bottom line; new infrastructure forms the interface" — computing-power stations (edge computing), the Xiaoyue River sensing pipe network, 5G/BeiDou augmentation, and smart lamp poles constitute a perceptible new-infrastructure layer; distributed energy and storage are reserved as concepts; the sponge city concept is implemented as a storage-and-regulation concept along the riverside spaces. All of the above are concept proposals, with no capacity conclusions [depth:municipal_new_infrastructure].

**Basis and rationale**: The municipal facilities of an AI innovation belt should not be merely pipelines buried underground, but should become an innovation interface that citizens can experience: shared computing-power stations (scenario card S05, concept) make inclusive computing power bookable and experiential in Zhongzhiyuan; the Xiaoyue River water-quality sensing network (S04) makes environmental monitoring data public, turning the waterfront into an environmental classroom. Smart lamp poles and sensing facilities follow the overarching privacy principles of minimal collection, edge-side priority, and switchability.

**Layer and metric evidence**: New-infrastructure nodes and waterfront sensing segments are mapped onto the public space and green corridor layers (see the Blue-Green Network chapter for scenario nodes); the Xiaoyue River waterfront control belt is a conceptual indication [data:geometry/constraints.geojson#CT-BLUE-01].

**Data gaps and items to be confirmed**: Municipal pipelines, energy, drainage, flood control, and fire-protection engineering data are all missing. Distributed energy capacity, computing-power station consumption, and sponge facility scale are all subject to demonstration after sector data are completed; this section makes no engineering conclusions.

### Public Services: The 15-Minute Living Circle and Embedded AI Services

**Design judgment**: Community service gaps are filled against the 15-minute living circle standard, and AI services enter everyday life in an "embedded-in-community, human-backstopped" manner — AI elderly-assistance service stations (S06, voice interaction + human backstop, mandatory human review), AI government-affairs and legal-advice kiosks (S09, retrieval-augmented + human referral, issuing no final opinions), and health services embedded in retained residential blocks; international education and medical consultation points are located in the Origin Community, serving international talent (concept proposal).

**Basis and rationale**: The public services of the innovation belt must answer the questions of two groups at once: long-term local residents (P3) want "the familiar life undisturbed," while international talent and students (P1/P4) want "accessible international services." Only by insisting on human review as the backstop can AI services avoid turning technological progress into a new threshold for the elderly; this principle is consistent with the taskbook's requirements on AI for good and governance boundaries [source:DATA-SRC-AGENT-TASKBOOK-20260518].

**Layer and metric evidence**: Nodes such as community AI elderly-assistance stations are mapped onto scenario nodes of the public space layer (see the SN series in the Blue-Green Network chapter); the compliance assessment of living-circle service radii will be calculated after public-service base data are completed.

**Data gaps and items to be confirmed**: The base inventory of existing public service facilities and the gap list for education, medical, and elderly-care facilities are missing; living-circle completion projects are directional concept proposals only, with no commitment to facility scale.

## Blue-Green Network, Public Space, and Urban Character

### Design Judgment: Blue-Green Is Not an Accessory, but the Structure

**Design judgment**: The blue-green system of this proposal is "one corridor, one belt, multiple parks, multiple nodes" — the New Track Green Corridor as the one corridor, the Xiaoyue River blue-green scenario belt as the one belt, the pocket park system as the multiple parks, and the squares and AI scenario nodes as the multiple nodes. Blue-green space is not accessory greenery attached to construction land, but the primary infrastructure organizing the entire innovation belt [depth:blue_green_public_space].

**Basis and rationale**: The approximately 110-meter-wide disused railway corridor is the site's largest stock public-space resource, and its linear continuity is a structural value no scattered parcels can replace. The recalculated green ratio under the design-structure basis is 13.4% [metric:green_ratio] and the public space share is 2.2% [metric:public_space_ratio] (provisional basis; the base inventory of existing open space is missing, so no existing-condition conclusions are made), which still shows a structural gap against the positioning requirement of an "urban AI life experience belt"; the structural green corridor and waterfront belt are the principal means of closing this gap. Cases such as Kendall Square demonstrate that high-quality public space is itself a catalyst of innovation intensity. The urban design management measures' requirements for coordinating landscape character and public space are the institutional basis for this judgment [standard:MOHURD-URBAN-DESIGN-MEASURES].

**Data gaps and items to be confirmed**: Statutory indicators such as the controlled green ratio value and park service radii are subject to confirmation by the approved regulatory plan; all ratios in this section are provisional recalculation figures.

### One Corridor, One Belt, Multiple Parks: The Blue-Green Space System

**Design judgment**: The New Track Green Corridor consists of 22 conceptual green-corridor segments (GS-01..22) [data:geometry/green_space.geojson#GS-01], running north–south along the genuine disused Beijing–Baotou railway alignment (the southern in-boundary segment, OSM razed/disused alignment [source:OSM-ODBL]) plus designed extension segments. The Xiaoyue River blue-green scenario belt unfolds along the eastern waterfront, forming with the corridor the blue-green skeleton of the "two belts." Pocket parks (LU-K01..09) are embedded around the three stations and within retained blocks at a "step off the station, step into the park" density.

**Basis and rationale**: The corridor segments echo the urban character of the blocks they pass through — the southern segment near the heritage park emphasizes historic tranquility, the middle segment through Wudaokou emphasizes open interaction, and the northern segment entering Zhongzhiyuan emphasizes a garden-type campus. The Xiaoyue River belt turns "watching the water" into "reading the water": the water-quality sensing network (S04) makes environmental data public content. Pocket parks are the gentlest and most effective spatial compensation for retained residential blocks (0701), letting the benefits of renewal be felt first by local residents.

**Layer and metric evidence**: For corridor segments, see [data:geometry/green_space.geojson#GS-01]; the waterfront control belt is a conceptual indication [data:geometry/constraints.geojson#CT-BLUE-01]; the heritage corridor control belt is a conceptual indication [data:geometry/constraints.geojson#CT-HERITAGE-01]. Recalculated green space is 1,533,340 sqm, a green ratio of 13.4% [metric:green_ratio] (including the New Track Green Corridor, the Xiaoyue River blue-green belt, and pocket parks; provisional basis). The intelligent sports park (S07, posture-recognition fitness guidance, anonymized) and the luminous running track with atmosphere programming (S10, public-participation light programming, non-surveillance) serve as vitality scenarios on the corridor, and both are concept proposals pending approval.

**Data gaps and items to be confirmed**: The blue line of the Xiaoyue River water system, green lines, and heritage protection boundaries are missing. The 110-meter corridor width is a conceptual description; specific cross-sections are to be deepened by professional teams.

### Phasing Plan: South Before North, Scenarios Before Construction

The phasing design judgment is "south before north, scenarios before construction, reserve held back." The reasons: the southern segment (Dazhongsi–Wudaokou) has the best rail-station density and population base, so public space and scenario operations can build public awareness at the lowest cost; the middle segment is the translation zone stitching campus and park, suitable for advancement after scenario validation; the northern Zhongzhiyuan carries full-stack indigenous innovation and strategic reserve functions, and should enter the construction cycle only after both industrial conditions and regulatory plan conditions are clarified. The recalculated areas of the three phases (provisional basis, EPSG:4548) are: PH-1 near term (launch phase), approximately 7,583,000 sqm, covering the Dazhongsi gateway, the Beijing AI Origin Community, and the southern segment of the heritage park [data:geometry/phasing.geojson#PH-1]; PH-2 medium term (formation phase), approximately 1,567,000 sqm, covering the Wudaokou–Shuangqing Road research-translation segment and the waterfront scenario wing [data:geometry/phasing.geojson#PH-2]; PH-3 long term (completion phase), approximately 2,263,000 sqm, covering the Zhongzhiyuan full-stack indigenous innovation area and the Qinghe gateway [data:geometry/phasing.geojson#PH-3]. The difference between the sum and the Overall Design Area stems from the spatial-union basis of the phasing layers; recalculation details are found in [metric:phasing_area_sqm].

It must be stated: phasing is an expression of the advancement logic of urban renewal, not a commitment to engineering scheduling; this proposal provides no investment estimate, no construction-start timetable, and no presumed approval pathway. The preconditions for launching each phase are: obtaining the official precise boundary, confirming regulatory plan conditions, and clarifying ownership and implementing entities — until then, the content of each phase remains a concept proposal [depth:phasing_implementation].

### Implementation Policy Proposals (mechanism level, concept proposals)

### Unknown Metrics: Why They Must Be Left Blank

[metric:total_floor_area_sqm] (total floor area) and [metric:floor_area_ratio] (floor area ratio) are explicitly recorded as **unknown** in this proposal, and building height and regulatory-plan-basis building density are recorded as **to be confirmed**. The reason is rigid: approved regulatory plan conditions (floor area ratio, building height, building density, green ratio, setbacks) are all missing at this stage; total floor area = the sum of building-by-building floor areas, and floor area ratio = total floor area / land area — both formulas depend on inputs that do not exist, so any figure would be fabrication. This treatment corresponds to two standards at once: under [standard:MOHURD-CONTROL-DETAILED-PLANNING], regulatory-plan-depth content must distinguish known control conditions, design proposals, and items to be confirmed, and floor area ratio and building height are typical pending-confirmation control metrics; under [standard:MOHURD-ARCH-DESIGN-DEPTH-2016], the architectural discipline depth provisions are activated upon obtaining official documents, and currently serve only as a missing-data list and a deepening reminder, producing no building-scale conclusions. Leaving blanks is not evasion, but writing "cannot be calculated" into metrics.json as a reviewable metric state (status=unknown, with reason attached), to be recalculated and filled under the same formulas once official regulatory plan conditions are obtained.

### Three-Category Tiered Management of Metrics

The first category, recalculable spatial metrics (the entire table above): recalculated directly from submitted geometry, entered into metrics.json with the provisional statement attached. The second category, pending-confirmation control metrics: floor area ratio, building height, building density, setbacks, road redlines, facility standards — uniformly recorded as unknown/to-be-confirmed, entered into assumptions.json and the missing-data list. The third category, operational performance metrics: scenario usage frequency, event participation, slow-mobility accessibility, talent-service satisfaction, etc. — these are objects of continuous calibration during the operating period; this proposal offers only the metric framework and collection boundaries (aggregated statistics, no personal identification), with no target-value commitments. The purpose of the three-category tiering is to prevent operational visions from being miswritten as approved planning conditions.

### Risk List and Mitigation Mechanisms

- **Risk of scheme adjustment due to data gaps**: after the official boundary and regulatory plan are obtained, areas, ratios, and layout may be adjusted as a whole. Mitigation: all precision-sensitive statements carry the provisional statement, and the recalculation pipeline (same formulas, same projection) can be rerun as a whole.
- **Risk of balancing heritage protection and development**: corridor completion and scenario implantation may touch heritage-sensitive areas. Mitigation: the heritage narrative is prioritized and respected; concepts involve no alteration to heritage fabric; the heritage corridor control belt is managed as a separate layer; heritage protection boundary data must be obtained before formal deepening.
- **Risk to residents' rights in community renewal**: renewal may disturb local life. Mitigation: approximately 70%+ of the area is primarily retained (category-level judgment); AI elderly-assistance and similar services take human backstopping as a mandatory condition; resident profiles are not used for commercial recommendation.
- **Privacy and ethics risks of AI scenarios**: sensing and recognition scenarios carry the possibility of privacy leakage. Mitigation: the six principles (minimal collection, edge-side priority, explicit notification, switchability, human-review backstop, no personal identification); test scenarios are restricted to defined areas / defined time windows and reported to the competent authorities for approval (pending approval).
- **Sustainability risk of event operations**: the events system may be interrupted by the absence of operating entities and funding. Mitigation: the three-tier operating mechanism proposal + application-based scenario opening; no funding or confirmed event arrangements are promised; operational performance metrics are left for calibration during the operating period.
