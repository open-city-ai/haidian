AutoJingZhang: L3-L4 Autonomous Driving Network

This proposal positions the Centennial Jingzhang AI Innovation Belt as the world's first full-domain L3-L4 autonomous driving demonstration area. Core features: 85% of roads transformed into autonomous driving exclusive lanes, private vehicles prohibited in core zone, all travel needs served by autonomous shuttle systems.
