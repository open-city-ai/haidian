假设

  • Detailed regulatory controls, road redlines, ownership, municipal utilities, and engineering constraints must be confirmed from official attachments before statutory use.
  • Site boundary and key-area polygons are the maintainers' provisional rough ranges (provisional_constraint); they are used for AI generation, self-check and display only.
  • Land use, buildings, roads, green space, public space, phasing and AI inn nodes are concept suggestions generated by the AI agent to illustrate spatial structure and scenario organisation.
  • AI scenario cards, test scenarios, annual events and operation mechanisms are concept proposals, not approved operations or confirmed government arrangements.
  • Global AI ecosystem case references are limited to publicly verifiable information registered as background_only in sources.json (CASE-*); no company lists, investment amounts, output values or fiscal commitments are asserted.
  • The Inn-Token authorisation mechanism (one token per section, no token no entry, human handoff, degradable) is an original design concept inspired by the historical Jing-Zhang railway staff-and-token block system; it is not a regulatory or licensing scheme.
  • The pilot section (AI Origin inn + Xiaoyuehe test corridor) and the three independently pausable project packages are concept suggestions for low-cost, reversible implementation.
  • Staffing estimates (one station manager, two operations, three service desk per inn, volunteer rotation) are concept-level calculations for discussion.
  • The emergency-response matrix (fallback per AI scenario, trigger conditions, response times, responsible roles) is a concept plan requiring operator drills and confirmation.
  • Inclusive personas, account-free and smartphone-free fallback paths, and appeal-correction mechanisms follow the Barrier-Free Environment Law and the elderly smart-technology plan (background-level references).
  • All figures, PDFs, HTML and geometry are generated by the declared AI agent with cleared public sources; fonts are Noto Sans CJK (SIL OFL 1.1); the machine-readable rights ledger is in visual/assets/rights-ledger.json.
  • This package is submitted for repository intake and community display (COMMUNITY-DISPLAY-ONLY); gallery publication, award selection and government endorsement are separate decisions by maintainers and organisers.
  • All known metrics are recomputed from submitted geometry in EPSG:4548; metrics with official-data dependencies (FAR, heights, densities) remain unknown with reasons until official regulatory conditions are published.
  • Regional synergy relationships (Beiwei, Future Science City, Huairou, ETDA, Beijing-Tianjin-Hebei) are research hypotheses expressed as interfaces; no formal cooperation is claimed.
  • Historical statements about the Jing-Zhang railway and its staff-and-token block system are based on public historical knowledge (registered as background references); no unauthorised images, portraits or proprietary materials are used.
