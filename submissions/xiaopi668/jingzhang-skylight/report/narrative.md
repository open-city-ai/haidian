# 方案叙述摘要 / Narrative Summary

## 京张天窗带 / JINGZHANG SKYLIGHT LINE

把铁路百年“天窗”养护制度转译为AI创新带的时空治理协议：每项AI服务上线前必须声明运行时段、检修窗口、人工接管方案、数据回滚路径与退役条件，并以绿/黄/红三色窗口状态公开。空间上以“一线（天窗脊）三窗（联试/共修/服务）两翼（中关村科技服务翼、小月河场景赋能翼）”为骨架，覆盖众智园、AI原点社区与大钟寺三处重点区。实施分近期试点、中期贯通、远期机制化三步。

The railway maintenance-window institution becomes a time-space governance protocol for the AI belt: every AI service declares operating hours, maintenance windows, human takeover, data rollback and retirement conditions, with green/yellow/red status public. The skeleton is "one spine, three windows, two wings". Implementation: near-term pilots, mid-term through-connection, long-term institutionalization.
