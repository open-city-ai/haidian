scenarios
01 · 大钟寺智造首发街

面向智能体、智能终端和内容消费企业的首发、首店、首展公共空间；与产业测试验证场景联动。

Dazhongsi Smart-Manufacturing Debut Street — Public space for first-launch / first-store / first-exhibit by smart agents, smart terminals and content-consumption firms; tied to industry test scenarios.

02 · 大钟寺智能终端市集

围绕轨道站点的四象限步行连通，把首发环与日常消费、媒体路演、文化活动连成一片。

Dazhongsi Smart-Terminal Market — Walkable 4-quadrant continuity around the rail station linking the debut loop to daily retail, media launch and cultural events.

03 · AI原点24h公共客厅

24小时开放的AI原住民社区公共客厅；近校办公、开源协作、人才生活、街区展览可共享切换。

AI Origin 24h Public Living Room — 24h public living room for AI-native residents; switchable between near-campus offices, open-source collaboration, talent life and street exhibitions.

04 · AI原点开源发布厅

面向高校和开源社区的成果发布、模型评测、小型路演空间；与北京AI原点社区近校资源绑定。

AI Origin Open-Source Release Hall — Result release, model evaluation and small launch events for universities and open-source communities, anchored to near-campus assets.

05 · 人才生活管家

把医疗、教育、法律、生活缴费等AI+公共服务落到可运营的街区尺度；保留人工办理与无障碍兜底。

Talent Life Concierge — Operational AI+ public services (health, education, legal, living-payment) at block scale; human-service and accessibility fallbacks preserved.

06 · 众智园城市智能体沙盒

在可控公共空间里做交通、运维、服务三类城市智能体测试；数据可审计、过程可中断。

Zhongzhiyuan Urban-Agent Sandbox — Controllable public-space sandbox for traffic, operations and service urban agents; auditable data, interruptible process.

07 · 众智园AI安全治理廊

把标准制定、安全评测、模型红队测试转译为可参观、可预约、可监管的展示与协作节点。

Zhongzhiyuan AI Safety Governance Gallery — Standards, safety evaluation and model red-team testing re-staged as visitable, bookable, supervised showcase and collaboration nodes.

08 · 众智园低碳算力驿站

把分布式能源、端侧算力、低碳展示与公共服务结合；具体容量与并网需正式工程前置条件。

Zhongzhiyuan Low-Carbon Compute Stop — Distributed energy, edge compute, low-carbon display tied to public services; capacity and grid-tie require formal engineering pre-conditions.

09 · 端侧算力走廊（跨三区）

沿南北主轴串联三区端侧算力驿站与公共服务设施；先以轻量、可审计服务启动。

Edge-Compute Corridor (Cross-Trinary) — North-south spine connecting edge-compute stops and public services across the three zones; starts as light, auditable services.

10 · 全球AI活动周路线（跨三区）

从京张文化记忆到开源社区、产业首发到国际路演的可步行、可传播体验路线；具体活动以运营方年度计划为准。

Global AI Activity Week Route (Cross-Trinary) — Walkable, broadcastable experience route from heritage memory to open source, industry launch and international pitch; actual events follow operator's annual plan.
