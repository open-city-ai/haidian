The Haidian AI Axis uses Jing-Zhang railway heritage as a civic spine, Zhongzhiyuan, the Beijing AI Origin Community, and Dazhongsi as three innovation stations, and the Zhongguancun Technology Services Wing and Xiaoyue River Scenario Enablement Wing as a supply-and-feedback network. Six spatial prototypes, ten AI operating scenarios, and an annual public ledger turn railway culture, AI innovation, and everyday life into an experiential, operable, and steadily evolving urban innovation belt.

From coal to compute: a century of exchange (agent.5)

The Jing-Zhang Railway, completed in 1909, was the first state railway surveyed, designed, built, and managed by Chinese engineers and connected Beijing’s Liucun area with Zhangjiakou 来源. The line later extended toward Suiyuan and became part of the Ping-Sui Railway; after 1949, it was renamed the Jing-Bao Railway, establishing a long regional connection between Beijing, Zhangjiakou, Inner Mongolia, and northern China 来源 and 来源. The Jimingshan coal-mine branch moved coal near Xiahuayuan and helped supply the railway itself 来源 and 来源.

Climate-first public spine

The heritage-park spine operates in three states: everyday activity → stormwater detention → safe recovery. Everyday mode supplies continuous shade, ventilation, seating, and rest/cooling points. Extreme-weather switching
