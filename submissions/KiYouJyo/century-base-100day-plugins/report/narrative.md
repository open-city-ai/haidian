## English

At the reviewed baseline, multi-lifespan layering, a century civic base, reversible interfaces, 90/100-day trials, object passports, versioning, rollback, human takeover, physical retirement and site restoration all have close precedents. Bounded Sky adds a budget protocol, versions and recovery actions; Playable Jing-Zhang adds removable components, physical reinstatement and a five-step retirement process; CommonRail adds a four-layer fallback. Spatial counts, landmarks, component counts and field counts therefore carry no originality claim. This proposal limits its contribution to a testable combination: `100D → 10Y → 100Y` is a mandatory directed dependency. A 100D plug-in cannot touch or leave proprietary anchors in 100Y civic invariants; it must bind a named, versioned, vendor-neutral `carrier_interface_id` and pass interface-envelope and base-impact type checks. Uninstall closes only after capped interfaces, asset/data portability, restoration against the pre-release baseline and archive-or-reuse receipts. Six plug-in classes, fifteen components and three landmarks spatialise that rule. This is a narrow independently authored implementation, not a first-of-kind claim.
