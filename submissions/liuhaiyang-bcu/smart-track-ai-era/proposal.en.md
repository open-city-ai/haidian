# Smart Track · AI Era

## Centennial Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt Urban Design Proposal

> **One hundred years ago, Zhan Tianyou carved China's first self-designed railway through this land.**
> **One hundred years later, we will ignite another rail track into the age of intelligence here.**

## 2. Global AI Innovation Ecosystem Case Studies

This proposal studies 7 globally representative cases, extracting mechanisms transferable to Haidian:

### 2.1 Case Matrix

| # | Case | Location | Core Feature | Transferable Mechanism |
|---|------|----------|--------------|------------------------|
| 1 | **Kendall Square / MIT** | Boston | University–industry–capital trinity; world's highest patent density | University boundary dissolution mechanism: channel research output from Tsinghua and Beihang into industrial spaces via an "Open Lab Corridor" [source:SITE-PACKAGE] |
| 2 | **Stanford Research Park** | Silicon Valley | Land trust model; university permanently holds land, enterprises lease long-term | Propose an "Innovation Land Trust": industrial land near universities held by a platform company, with AI startups exchanging achievements for leaseholds |
| 3 | **King's Cross Knowledge Quarter** | London | Railway land regeneration + knowledge economy cluster; Grand Library + creative industries | Jing-Zhang railway heritage can follow suit: retain the industrial heritage skeleton, fill in AI R&D, exhibition, and public functions |
| 4 | **one-north** | Singapore | Integrated industry–academia–research planning; Biopolis + Fusionopolis + LaunchPad | Modular "building-block campus": each building serves three functions — R&D, testing, and exhibition |
| 5 | **Shibuya / Azabudai** | Tokyo | Ultra-tall vertical city + AI living lab; Mori Building's smart city platform | High-density scenario layering: overlay residential, office, and testing scenarios within limited space |
| 6 | **DMC (Digital Media City)** | Seoul | Government-led + market-operated; IT / media / gaming industry cluster | Policy toolkit: scenario opening list + computing subsidy + talent housing package |
| 7 | **Quayside / Waterfront** | Toronto | Open data + AI governance testing ground; citizen-participatory design | Urban digital twin + public participation mechanism: all planning data open to AI agents and citizens |

## 6. Operations and Events Framework

### 6.1 Annual Events Framework

| Time | Event | Scale | Objective |
|------|-------|-------|-----------|
| March | **Jing-Zhang AI Hackathon** | 1,000 participants | 48-hour open-source challenge, producing a batch of deployable prototypes |
| May | **AI Origin Festival** | 5,000 participants | Commemorating the Jing-Zhang Railway; annual open-source achievements release |
| July | **Global AI City Forum** | 500 participants | International exchange on urban AI governance |
| September | **Developer Harvest Season** | 2,000 participants | Open-source project demo day + investor matchmaking |
| November | **AI Art Biennale** | 10,000 visits | AI-generated art and public installation exhibition |
| Year-round | **Weekend AI Market** | 200 / week | Weekly Saturday AI product experience events on Century Smart Way |

### 6.2 Developer Community Operating Mechanism

- **Open-Source Contribution Credits**: Code commits, documentation contributions, and issue responses all accumulate credits
- **Credit Redemption**: Computing hours, shared workstations, priority for talent housing, event VIP access
- **AI Agent Assistant**: Each developer is assigned a dedicated agent to help match resources
- **Quarterly Review**: Community voting selects the best open-source project each quarter, awarding accelerated incubation resources

### 6.3 Scenario Open Operations

- **Scenario Opening List**: A new batch of testable scenarios released each quarter, including datasets, APIs, and evaluation criteria
- **"Test – Validate – Scale" Three-Phase Model**: Scenarios are first tested in the Xiaoyuehe Wing; once mature, they are scaled across the entire belt
- **Human Review Mechanism**: All AI scenarios must incorporate a human review checkpoint, preserving an opt-out channel

  1. North Latitude Community AI R&D node

- **Retain**: Preserve the Jing-Zhang Railway industrial heritage skeleton, university historical building clusters, and the Dazhongsi ancient building height-control zone
- **Adapt**: Transform underperforming industrial parks into AI innovation complexes, retaining industrial memory in facades
- **Add**: Introduce new public spaces, connecting corridors, and landmarks at key nodes to activate spatial continuity

## 8. Transportation and Municipal Infrastructure (Conceptual Proposals)

### 8.1 Transportation System Concept

**"Three-Layer Flow Separation"**
- **Underground Layer**: Rail transit (Line 13, Line 15, Changping Line) + underground logistics corridors
- **Ground Layer**: Pedestrian-priority + autonomous micro-circulation + bus shuttles
- **Aerial Layer**: Drone delivery air routes (conceptual)

**"15-Minute Walk Innovation Circle"**: From any core area, within a 15-minute walk you can reach:
- At least one rail transit station
- At least one AI scenario experience point
- At least one public open space
- At least one daily-life service node

### 8.2 New-Generation Infrastructure (Conceptual Proposals)

- **Edge Computing Nodes**: Deploy one edge computing station in each of the three core areas, providing low-latency computing for AI scenarios
- **Urban Sensing Network**: Deploy environmental, pedestrian-flow, and traffic sensors along Century Smart Way; data feeds into the urban digital twin
- **5G + WiFi 7 Full Coverage**: Seamless coverage across all public areas

## 9. Blue-Green Space and Urban Landscape

### 9.1 Blue-Green Spatial Structure

![Blue-Green Space](../assets/figures/mobility-bluegreen.png)

- **Blue Vein**: Xiaoyuehe–Changhe water system, forming an east–west ecological corridor

**"Three-Section Landscape"**
- **Northern Section (Wisdom Launchpad)**: Tech-forward + academic atmosphere; buildings predominantly in gray-white + metallic tones

- **Southern Section (Dazhongsi)**: Tradition–modernity dialogue; echoing the scale of ancient buildings, controlling the skyline
