# GROUND TRUTH: Jing-Zhang AI Public Interface Belt

**Assertion.** In machine learning, Ground Truth is the model's true reference; in the city, it is the paths underfoot, the doors on the ground floor, the human body, everyday services, and the Public Spaces where one can raise objections face-to-face. A century ago, the Jing-Zhang Railway brought measurement, gradient, and engineering judgment to fruition in a passable track; today, AI innovation should also move from screens and internal company grounds to the ground, subjecting it to re-evaluation of public values. Thus, we propose "one spine, seven transversals, three courtyards, and twelve touchpoints": the Jing-Zhang Heritage Park is the public truth spine, seven transversals re-evaluate the street seams from east to west, three key areas separately undertake technical, social, and market re-evaluations, and twelve scene touchpoints transform AI services into visible, accessible, manageable, and exitable urban interfaces.

This outcome is an Open Co-Creation proposal. All spatial implementation content is either a "Conceptual Recommendation," "Reference Scheme," or "Subject for Further Research by Professional Teams," and does not replace formal planning nor constitute government approval, investment, engineering feasibility, or implementation commitment. ## Overall Design Area: Urban Renewal and Regulatory-Plan-Level Urban Design

The overall space is composed of **one public true spine, seven lateral review streets, five profile strips, and three key courtyards**. The public true spine organizes a continuous walkway, cycling path, shaded walkway, historical interpretation, and public services along the Jing-Zhang Heritage Park. The seven lateral review streets reconnect the adjacent communities, campuses, parks, rail stations, and commercial interfaces back to the main spine. They are conceptual lines in GeoJSON, expressing the need for connections, not road boundaries, bridges, tunnels, or traffic organization conclusions. [data:geometry/roads.geojson#ROAD-GT-00] [metric:concept_mobility_network_length_m] [depth:traffic_rail_slow_parking]

Urban Renewal does not default to large-scale demolition and reconstruction. Each candidate plot is evaluated in four steps: first, confirming historical significance, public services, and buildings that can be continued; second, making light interventions with first-floor access doors, shared lobbies, shaded walkways, and equipment updates; third, discussing local additions; and only after an assessment of ownership, structure, cultural heritage, fire safety, and social impact can a professional team consider demolition. The nine submitted Building Footprints only indicate "possible spatial types for the public first-floor interface," and do not correspond to actual conclusions about building demolition, renovation, and retention. [data:geometry/buildings.geojson#BLDG-GT-11] [depth:retain_renovate_demolish] (Demolish–Renovate–Retain Strategy)

Five parcels of land form a continuous profile from living to research and development, from the green spine to learning and industrial services; the ground floor is prioritized for shared meeting spaces, prototype displays, accessible restrooms, childcare and care services, dining, and nighttime safety services, with upper-level functions and scale to be controlled by the detailed plan and site verification. The statutory Floor Area Ratio `[metric:floor_area_ratio]` and height `[metric:building_height_m]` are both kept at unknown levels; this package only calculates the conceptual Building Footprint, not interpreting it as approved construction quantities. [depth:land_use_layout] [depth:development_intensity_controls] [depth:height_massing_character]

Public Space is defined by the principle of "even without AI, it still works": it should still be passable when the internet is down, still allow for assistance even when identification is disabled, and still enable booking of human services even without a smartphone. Municipal and New Infrastructure adopt maintainable layered interfaces: underground and server room conditions are subject to professional verification; the ground level only sets replaceable equipment positions, maintenance boundaries, and public displays of energy and data status; operational side establishes responsibility assignments, human takeovers, fault notifications, and exit lists. ## Detailed Design of Key Areas

![Three Ground Truth Courts: Technical, Social, and Market Review](assets/figures/key-areas.png)

### Zhongzhiyuan: Technical Truth Court VERIFY YARD

The location aims to push full-stack self-innovations from "model indicators" into a controlled physical environment. The spatial structure consists of a separable test court, several ground-level prototype bays, public observation galleries, and safe exit points; the architectural strategy prioritizes adaptive reuse of verifiable existing spaces, with additional base areas serving only as indicative location types. The Walking and Cycling Network connects employee entrances, observation galleries, and the Jing-Zhang Green Spine into an accessible loop; the physical separation between experimental and public pathways is maintained, with robot, low-speed shuttle, and edge-side energy consumption tests requiring safety officer approval, specific time periods, accident reporting, and manual emergency stops. [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-001] [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#NODE-S01]

Public Space is not a product showcase, but a "method visible" validation court: it announces test objectives, applicable boundaries, failure records, and whether it proceeds to the next phase. Implementation risks include imprecise temporary focus area boundaries, unknown existing buildings, equipment safety, and noise impacts; thus, no specific engineering conclusions can be formed; a professional team needs to supplement structural, fire safety, traffic, and ethical reviews.

### Beijing AI Origin Community: Social True Value Court CIVIC FORUM

Location is intended to allow developers, students, residents, and public service personnel to collectively determine whether AI is worthy of integration into daily life. The spatial structure includes an open-source workshop, a model explanation room, a Human Review station, a community deliberation long table, and a no-AI equivalent service window; the first floor forms an accessible "public model explanation" along the "public truth spine." Slow travel and Public Spaces prioritize short distances, places for lingering, drinking water, and restrooms, without being dominated by large screens and recognition devices. [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-002] [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#NODE-S05]

AI scenarios focus on educational guidance, medical navigation, legal consultation, multilingual signposting, and community care; all recommendations are subject to human confirmation, and sensitive data are not included in public displays. This place also features fully "contributable and memorable" nodes: open-source code, data descriptions, public feedback, and repair records are displayed in a revocable and correctable manner, rather than serving as permanent advertisements for businesses. Risks include privacy concerns, digital exclusion, and community operational burdens, which require mechanisms for appeals, revocation, and independent review.

### Dazhongsi: market true court MARKET STREET

The location is intended to validate whether AI can create payable, explainable, and fair value for small and micro entities within real commercial and service workflows. The spatial structure includes a walkable market street, shared trial counters, compliance and intellectual property service desks, nighttime service nodes, and a public feedback hall; the building's first floor emphasizes small footprints, continuous canopies, and interchangeable displays, without pre-setting specific tenants. The transportation strategy separates discussions on rail connections, walking, cycling, and delivery periods, with specific flow lines to be refined based on official road and pedestrian data. [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-003] [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#NODE-S06]

Scenes include a micro-enterprise compliance assistant, public facility maintenance triage, multilingual signage, and urban open challenges. The pilot cannot be tied to a single supplier; instead, it must publicly disclose service levels, human fallbacks, and data processing post-termination. Risks include commercialization squeezing Public Space, nighttime disturbances, and algorithmic discrimination. Therefore, each counter should have a parallel path that is "AI-free."

## AI Innovation Ecosystem, Personas, and AI+ Scenarios

Ecological mechanisms form a closed loop around eight elements: land provides reversible first-floor interfaces, space offers controlled experimentation and public observation, industry raises verifiable questions, funding only supports pilots that pass phase gates, talent rotates through three courtyards, computing power discloses energy consumption and responsibility, data is collected on a need-to-know basis, and scenarios are capped by Human Review and exit conditions. It grounds the five functional areas in operational spatial responsibilities rather than affixing an AI label to traditional parks. [depth:renewal_project_list]

### Six User Persona Categories

- **Research and Development Engineers and Startup Teams:** Require spaces for compliant pilot testing, shared equipment, method peer review, and failure documentation.
- **Community Residents and Caregivers:** Need daily services that are not mandatory, accessible, allow for human interaction, provide opportunities for rest, and enable the ability to raise objections.
- **Students and Young Developers:** Low-threshold learning, open-source collaboration, mentorship, and pathways for public contributions are needed.
- **Frontline Public Service Personnel:** Need AI to reduce repetitive labor but not undermine professional judgment, and be able to report errors and take over processes.
- **Small Enterprises and Professional Service Providers:** Require affordable trial access, compliance, intellectual property, procurement, and market validation support.
- **Elders, Persons with Disabilities, and International Visitors:** Accessible, multilingual, low-cognitive-load, and smartphone-agnostic services are required.

The number of personas is counted in the text `[metric:persona_count]`, not a statistical representation of actual population sizes; further correction is needed through public engagement and professional social surveys.

### Twelve Scene Cards

**S01 Robot Passage and Accessibility Co-Testing (Industrial Testing).** Zhongzhiyuan controlled testing court; serving research and development teams and users of wheelchairs, canes, and baby strollers; only recording pass rates, near misses, and manual emergency stops, without identity recognition; security personnel allow entry, and any abnormality results in immediate exit. [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#NODE-S01]

**S02 Low-Speed Shuttle Vehicle Takeover Testing (Industrial Test).** Candidate closed loops within the focus area; record the number of takeovers, docking errors, and feedback on barrier-free boarding; driving safety officers and traffic professionals have the final authority; do not enter open roads until road conditions are verified.

**S03 End-Side Computational Energy and Heat Recovery Testing (Industrial Testing).** Zhongzhiyuan data center and equipment interface type; records electricity, water, temperature, and recoverable heat, without collecting personal data; energy and fire personnel conduct Human Review, and testing stops if it does not meet energy consumption and safety thresholds.

**S04 AI Medical Triage Public Counter.** Origin Community; provides information navigation and appointment assistance only; does not diagnose or replace doctors; sensitive inputs are locally processed and can be directed to a human window if desired.

**S05 Open Source Model Learning Workshop.** Origin Community; aimed at students, developers, and residents, it provides open model cards, data boundaries, and failure cases; content is reviewed by teachers and community mentors, and activities for minors require separate authorization.

**S06 Small and Micro Enterprise Compliance Assistant.** Dazhongsi; provides regulatory search, contract issue lists, and intellectual property navigation, which do not constitute legal advice; external use requires confirmation by a professional.

**S07 Multilingual and Accessible Signage.** Seven review streets with track interface; both audio and visual information have physical backup markers, with errors reportable with a single click. The human service desk is responsible for updating the signs.

**S08 Public Space Maintenance Triage.** Full coverage of public spaces; AI provides priority suggestions based on repair texts and equipment status but does not automatically close work orders; municipal maintenance personnel confirm, feedback, and review.

**S09 Microclimate Comfort Negotiation.** Green Spine and Pocket Rain Gardens; Utilize Anonymous Environmental Data and Voluntary Feedback Without Tracking Individuals; Landscape Professionals and the Public Collaborate to Decide Whether to Adjust Shade, Benches, and Mist.

**S10 Jing-Zhang Oral History Co-creation.** Public True Value Spine for the heritage site park; participants provide clear authorization, with facts and personal memories separated, and cultural professionals review. Contributors can correct or withdraw their contributions.

**S11 Community Care Human Relay.** Original point community and livability zone; AI only serves as a resource navigation and reminder. Emergency and complex needs are transferred to human handlers, and predictive results must not be used to determine eligibility for benefits.

**S12 Urban Design Open Challenge.** Feedback Hall at Dazhongsi and Online Public Ledger; the public raises issues, teams submit replicable experiments, and review records are made public; public value is not judged by views.

The twelve nodes and three industry tests are counted by `[metric:scenario_node_count]` and `[metric:industry_test_scenario_count]`, respectively. Scenarios collectively adhere to four gates: legal and necessary, technical and safe, public value, and exit and remediation. If any gate is not passed, the scenario remains in the research or sandbox phase.

## Land Use, Building Scale, and Retain-Renovate-Demolish Strategy

The complete land use zoning consists of five longitudinal conceptual bands sharing boundaries: residential `0701`, research and development `0802`, park and green space `1401`, education `0804`, and commercial service `05`. The areas can be calculated by `[metric:land_use_0701_area_sqm]`, `[metric:land_use_0802_area_sqm]`, `[metric:land_use_1401_area_sqm]`, `[metric:land_use_0804_area_sqm]`, and `[metric:land_use_05_area_sqm]` respectively. They are structured for explanatory purposes at the full boundary level and do not represent actual parcel ownership or approved uses. [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-GT-03]

The architectural strategy employs four categories of actions: "Leave - Modify - Supplement - Retreat." **Leave**: Buildings with historical, social, industrial, or usage value are prioritized for retention after investigation; **Modify**: Through first-floor openness, energy efficiency, accessibility, fire safety, and equipment updates, the lifespan is extended; **Supplement**: Only when there are gaps in public services or continuous street facades is reversible addition discussed; **Retreat**: When experimental facilities expire, have no operational responsibility, or fail to meet safety ethics, they are removed. The absence of a current survey means that this package does not make any judgments about the demolition of any actual buildings.

Conceptual Building Footprint area `[metric:building_footprint_area_sqm]` and ratio `[metric:concept_building_footprint_ratio]` only verify layer consistency and do not represent development scale or Building Coverage Ratio approval values. Actual total gross floor area, Floor Area Ratio, Building Height, density, and Demolish–Renovate–Retain Strategy must be refined by a professional team after official control plans, property rights, structural, fire safety, cultural heritage, and social impact data are in place. Public service facilities are based on human service windows, community spaces, learning workshops, care, and health facilities, with AI serving as an enhancement layer.

## Metrics, Area Recalculation, and Compliance Matrix

![Indicators, spatial objects, and Evidence Chain of review](assets/figures/metrics-evidence.png)

This package contains indicators categorized into three types. The first category consists of official factual statements, such as the total design area of 11.4 square kilometers; the second category includes "scheme internal metrics" recalculated from temporary geometry and design layers, such as green spaces, Public Space, road lengths, land use zones, and node counts; the third category comprises unknown statutory or engineering controls, such as Floor Area Ratio, height limits, and statutory green space ratios. Supplier lock-in is controlled through open interfaces, data portability, and exit clauses.
