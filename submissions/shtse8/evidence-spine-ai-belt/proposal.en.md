# Evidence Backbone: Centennial Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt Urban Design Scheme

> Concept in one sentence: Transform AI innovation into a verifiable urban Evidence Chain — **VERIFY · LIVE · CONVERT · GOVERN**.

## Three-Level Scope Framework

The proposal strictly organizes according to the three layers of the defined scope: the Coordinated Research Area covers approximately 43.6 square kilometers, the Overall Design Area covers approximately 11.4 square kilometers, and the Key-Area Detailed Design Area covers approximately 368.4 hectares [source:OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT] [depth:three_level_scope_framework]. The three levels of division are:

1. **Integrated Layer**: Addressing the world-class AI ecosystem, Three Zones and Two Wings coordination, cultural narrative, international dissemination, and long-term operation.
2. **Overall Layer**: Convert judgments into land use structure, update projects, transportation infrastructure, blue-green Public Spaces, and appearance control [depth:overall_spatial_structure] [depth:land_use_layout].
3. **Key Layer**: At Zhongzhiyuan, AI Origin, and Dazhongsi, validate the block-level scenarios, Public Spaces, and the demolish–renovate–retain strategy [depth:three_key_area_detailed_design]. (Demolish–Renovate–Retain Strategy)

This package submission site area [metric:site_area_sqm] = 11412842.25 sqm; number of key areas [metric:key_area_count] = 3. The compliance_matrix.json maps announcement 1.3/1.4/1.5 to agent.1–agent.6, ensuring that sections, layers, indicators, and drawings are co-evident with the HTML [standard:PROJECT-OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT] [standard:PROJECT-AGENT-OPEN-CALL-TASKBOOK]. The three layers are not three separate and unrelated sets of drawings: the strategic layer determines "why they should form a continuous belt," the overall layer determines "how they should grow into a city," and the key layer determines "how people should validate, live, and transform within the nodes."

### Scene Card Detailed Description (Excerpt for Deepening)

**Open Source Evaluation Day**: Evaluation resources are available for reservation during fixed weekly intervals, with summary results displayed on the wall, and failed cases also visible, forming a "culture of permissible failure in public science." The space is located in the evaluation courtyard and open-source square of Zhongzhiyuan, thereby avoiding the closure of experiments.

**15-Minute Care Ring**: Centered around the community living room, this ring connects walkable health consultation points, temporary childcare drop-off locations, and eldercare appointment points. AI is used for scheduling and reminders, but critical care is still performed by certified personnel to prevent technological substitution of care responsibilities.

**Entrepreneurship Roadshow Week**: The Dazhongsi Roadshow Dome operates on a thematic weekly basis, with all connections and engagements completed in the enterprise service corridor before and after the roadshow, rather than ending immediately after the presentation. The nighttime interface maintains transparency and continuous lighting to reduce the "exhibition island" effect.

**Slow-Travel Co-Governance**: The spine corridor is equipped with dynamic congestion and micro-mobility conflict monitoring, but the publicized information reflects segment status rather than individual trajectories. Community governance meetings can utilize weekly reports to foster spatial co-governance rather than technical control.

All of the above are Conceptual Recommendations and reference solutions, and do not constitute approved policy or investment commitments.

### Preserve–Renovate–Retain (Conceptual Classification) (Demolish–Renovate–Retain Strategy)
- **Leave**: cultural readable interfaces, mature community services, walkable street networks, and valuable existing buildings.
- **Revised**: industrial undersurface interface, Public Space edges, inefficient perimeter walls, and parking encroachments.
- **Remove**: Only in localized discussions regarding safety hazards or severe disruptions to public continuity, and must await ownership and legal conditions.
- **Build**: Evaluation, public living rooms, pitch sessions, and limited incremental additions for supporting services.

All actions are referenced for the proposed scheme, not for site-level statutory demolition and reconstruction [depth:retain_renovate_demolish] [standard:MOHURD-ARCH-DESIGN-DEPTH-2016]. The principle is "public continuity prioritized over incremental performance, and life scale prioritized over formal mass."


## Transport, Rail, Municipal Infrastructure, and Public Services

Traffic Strategy: Slow travel spine, rail transit connectors as wings, and minor streets for auto fringe access. The evidence spine corridor serves as the north-south continuous pedestrian/bicycle path [data:geometry/roads.geojson#RD-SPINE]; the secondary east-west streets handle the dispersal [data:geometry/roads.geojson#RD-EAST] [data:geometry/roads.geojson#RD-WEST]; the alleys serve for access to blocks and parcels; and bicycle lanes and rail transit connector concept corridors are set up [depth:traffic_rail_slow_parking]. No new undesignated rail lines are to be drawn, and no road right-of-way claims are to be made.

Municipal and New Infrastructure recommendations integrate computational power access, sensor ethical boundaries, open data interfaces, emergency communications, and green infrastructure: reserve discussion space for combined utility corridors beneath the spine, synchronize lighting and accessibility, and integrate rainwater and green spaces [depth:municipal_new_infrastructure]. Public services are embedded within a 15-minute catchment area for education, culture, sports, health, and business services, and are densified at the AI Origin and Dazhongsi. All line positions, pipe diameters, red lines, and investments are subject to confirmation in the official attachments [depth:risk_missing_data]. (Conceptual Recommendation)
