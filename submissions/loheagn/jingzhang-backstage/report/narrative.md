# 京张后场 / JINGZHANG BACKSTAGE

让服务被看见，让公园仍是公园。公园公共体验保持连续，送达、交接、维修与回收布置在城市侧边缘的离散节点。三座后场院分别承担受控校验、公共交接和维修回流；四类接口把车辆止于街道侧、机器止于交接线、人工责任带到现场、数据目的公开给公众。十二个场景中三项为封闭验证；所有运营目标都等待基线，所有空间落位都等待官方精确边界、权属和专业核验。

Make service visible. Keep the park a park. Public park experience remains continuous while delivery, handover, repair and return occupy discontinuous city-side interfaces. Three yards prove, relay and return; four interface families stop vehicles and machines at accountable thresholds. Three of twelve scenarios are contained validation tests. Every operational target awaits a baseline and every location awaits exact official geometry, ownership and professional review.
