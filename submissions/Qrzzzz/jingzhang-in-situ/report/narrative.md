# 京张在场 / Jing-Zhang In Situ

“京张在场”是一项面向百年京张 AI 创新带的概念性城市设计建议。它不把 AI 当作附着在传统规划上的标签，而把“人在场、证据在场、责任在场”转译为空间和运营规则：公众能够理解场景状态，专业人员保留最终判断，服务可以切换人工或低技术替代，失败能够被记录并触发退出。

方案以京张铁路遗址公园为南北公共骨架，以十二个待现场核验的东西向生活横断面为评价单元，以众智园 AI 自主创新加速区、北京 AI 原点社区和大钟寺 AI 产业聚集区为三处差异化工作界面，并由中关村科技服务翼和小月河场景赋能翼形成候选支撑回路。外部区域关系仅作为待授权、待核验的协同接口，不代表现有合作。

成果显式回应三大定位、五大功能和 agent.1—agent.6：建立命名与 Logo 方向；比较七项 `background_only` 全球案例机制；组织土地、空间、产业、资金、人才、算力、数据、场景八要素；定义七类用户、十二张完整场景卡和三个产业测试验证场景；提出清河验证庭、原点发布台和大钟寺城市窗口三处公共学习地标；建立京张开放贡献谱与九项公共空间组件；形成“来路—在场—共创”的文化叙事、五级导视、双语国际文案、建议性年度活动和可停止的转化流程。

T06 的 IM01—IM13 将概念项目落实为责任角色、前置数据、专业依赖、建议窗口、定性成本、维护、评价、人工接管与退出动作。所有项目都以正式边界、现场调查、权属、安全、许可和运营条件为 Gate，不构成政府日程、资金安排、招商承诺或工程可行性结论。

当前 SITE 与 KEY_AREA 是临时粗略约束。正式 polygon、控规、逐栋现状、道路、无障碍、市政、文保和真实需求数据仍待补齐；临时面积和比例只能用于方案内复算。官方资料到位后，九类图层、指标、F01—F11、HTML 和 PDF 必须统一更新。

Jing-Zhang In Situ is a conceptual urban-design proposal for the Centennial Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt. It turns “people present, evidence present, responsibility present” into spatial and operating rules: the public can read scenario status, professionals retain final judgment, every service can switch to Human Takeover or a Low-Tech Alternative, and failure can trigger a documented exit.

The Jing-Zhang Railway Heritage Park is the north–south civic backbone. Twelve east–west everyday cross-sections, pending field verification, are evaluation units. Zhongzhiyuan AI Independent Innovation Acceleration Area, Beijing AI Origin Community, and Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster are three differentiated working interfaces, supported conceptually by the Zhongguancun Technology Services Wing and Xiaoyue River Scenario Enablement Wing. External regional links remain candidate interfaces pending evidence and authorization.

The package delivers a naming and Logo direction, seven `background_only` case mechanisms, an eight-element ecosystem, seven personas, twelve complete scenario cards, three industry Testing and Validation Scenarios, three public-learning landmarks, an Open Contribution Ledger, nine public-space components, a cultural and wayfinding system, bilingual international copy, a proposed annual programme, and a reversible conversion pathway. IM01–IM13 assign roles, prerequisites, professional dependencies, qualitative cost, maintenance, evaluation, Human Takeover, and exit actions without claiming government commitment or engineering feasibility.

SITE and KEY_AREA remain provisional rough constraints. Official polygons, regulatory controls, building conditions, transport, accessibility, municipal, heritage, and observed-demand data are still missing. When authoritative data arrives, all spatial layers, metrics, F01–F11, HTML, and PDFs must be recomputed together.
