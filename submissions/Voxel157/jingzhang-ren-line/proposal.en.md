## Coordinated Research Area: Industry and Future City Research

**Overall Concept: Centennial Line · Intelligence Ignited by People.** In 1909, Zhan Tianyou presided over the construction of the Jing-Zhang Railway, overcoming topographical differences with the "person" character-shaped return line at Qinglong Bridge, making it a symbol of China's indigenous engineering spirit. This plan reinterprets the "person" character as the spatial and spiritual prototype of the innovation belt: first, spatially, the north-south Jing-Zhang Heritage Park green axis intersects with the east-west Xiao Yuehe Blue-Green Corridor to form the "person" character framework, with the intersection point featuring the "person" character-shaped square [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#PS-004]; second, spiritually, the "person" character represents "people-oriented" — AI innovation ultimately serves the dignity and public welfare of people, echoing the human-centric governance principles of the co-creation charter [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK]. The main name remains the official project name "Centennial Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt," with the brand concept named **Jing-Zhang Smart Line (JZ AI LINE)**, expressed in English as Centennial Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt. Slogan: "One Line, A Century — Intelligence, Human at Heart." The naming system is **"One Line, Three Stations, Two Wings, and Multiple Points"**: One line is the Jing-Zhang Smart Line; three stations are Zhongzhi Station(Zhongzhiyuan AI Independent Innovation Acceleration Area), Origin Station(Beijing AI Origin Community), and Clock Station(Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster), using the metaphor of railway stations to represent the three stages of innovation — acceleration, origin, and application; two wings are the Zhongguancun Technology Services Wing(factors globalized configuration, capital empowerment)and the Xiaoyue River Scenario Enablement Wing(AI scenario empowerment and vibrant city); multiple points are sacred landmarks, scenario nodes, and developer mileposts [depth:overall_spatial_structure]. The logo direction is a Conceptual Recommendation: two tracks intersect at a node to form a "person" character, where the node is also a neural network node; colors are taken from Jing-Zhang Gray(industrial heritage gray), Zhi Pu Blue(AI), and Blue-Green-Teal(ecology)three colors, with all fonts and graphics being original or open-source licensed, not using any unlicensed trademarks, fonts, or corporate logos [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK].

**Three Key Positions, Five Major Functions, and the Synergistic Loop of the Three Zones and Two Wings**: The century-old Jing-Zhang Cultural Axis along the green axis bears narrative and pilgrimage functions; the Urban AI Life Experience Belt deploys life scenarios based on existing communities and the Little Moon River Wing. The AI Integration Innovation Belt is centered around the R&D land of Zhongzhiyuan and the original point [metric:lu_research_0802_area_sqm]. Five major functions are located as follows: Full-Stack Independent AI Innovation System → Zhongzhi Station, World-Class AI Innovation Ecosystem → Original Point Station, AI-Enabled Scenario Empowerment New Paradigm → Little Moon River Wing, Intelligent AI Vital City → Zhong Station and Life Belt, AI Governance Global Discourse → Zhongzhiyuan AI Governance and Standard Experimental Zone [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-ZZY-04]. In terms of regional synergy, this belt connects to the basic research spillovers of the Future Science City and Huairou Science City to the north, and to the urban core through Beijing North Station and Xizhimen to the south. To the east, it interconnects with the capital and service elements of the Zhongguancun Technology Services Wing, forming a loop of "basic research—technology transfer—scenario validation—capital services." This collaborative relationship serves as a planning research judgment, to be developed further by the professional team [source:OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT].

**Global AI Innovation Ecosystem Cases (Background Reference, 7 Examples)** [source:GLOBAL-AI-ECOSYSTEM-CASES]: 1. Boston Kendall Square——university interface as an innovation interface, high-density mixed-use at the street block scale is the spatial foundation for the "serendipity economy," which can be translated into the mixed-use principle for the university district land use (LU-DX-02/04) [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-DX-02]; 2. Singapore one-north——park, industry, and community boundaries blur, corridor-style innovation belt with green axes connecting clusters, similar to the structure of this plan with a single line and multiple clusters; 4. Paris Saclay——research plateau plus public transport spine, indicating that Zhongzhiyuan needs direct pedestrian and transit connections with the university district [data:geometry/roads.geojson#RD-013]. **Shanghai Zhangjiang Artificial Intelligence Island** —— a model of scene aggregation and display island, which can be transformed into a "visit-test" mechanism for the "Kan Yuehe Scene Experiment Field"; **Shenzhen Bay Super Headquarters Base** —— a model of scene checklist and headquarters economy, which can draw on its "scene release-pledge pilot" operational process; **Zhangjiawan** —— the original point of local innovation culture, whose entrepreneurial history is a continuation of the Jing-Zhang spirit in the contemporary era, forming the second layer of cultural narrative.
