# Centennial Smart Track · Co-Existence Ring —— Jing-Zhang AI Public Spine and Three Zones and Two Wings Synergistic City

**Jing-Zhang AI Commons Loop / JACL**

> Not to encase railway heritage in a technical exhibition case, but to allow history, open-source collaboration, everyday life, and trustworthy AI to continuously occur on the same public spine.

This proposal puts forward the concept of "One Spine, Three Rings, Two Wings, and Twelve Touch Points": A spine connecting north and south via the Jing-Zhang Railway Heritage—AI Public Spine; Zhongzhiyuan, Beijing AI Origin Community, and Dazhongsi form a Safety Governance Ring, an Open Source Transformation Ring, and an Intelligent Economy Ring, respectively; the innovative elements of Zhongguancun and the living scenarios along Xiaoyuehe River constitute the two cooperative wings; and twelve accessible, enterable, and reviewable AI scene touch points translate industrial capabilities into public value. The proposal is not a statutory plan and does not claim approval or government implementation commitment. (Human Review)

## Brand, Identity, and Cultural Narrative

Brand Chinese name: "Jiànbǎi Zhìguǐ·Sēngēng Huan," English name: "Jing-Zhang AI Commons Loop." The logo consists of an open loop with five railway time scales: the open loop symbolizes continuous contribution and public access, the railway time scales represent the century-long depth of the Jing-Zhang railway, the cyan breakpoints indicate that each AI intervention must leave behind an explainable and reversible governance interface. The crimson color represents the memory of railway industry, the deep navy blue represents the public institutional framework, the cyan represents AI collaboration, and the green represents daily shared spaces.

The cultural narrative adopts a three-sentence approach: "heritage as the foundation, contributions forming a loop, and the city bearing witness." Landmarks do not replicate historical structures or anthropomorphize AI as authoritative figures; each landmark simultaneously provides public activities, contribution records, and offline explanations. When involving specific cultural resources such as Tsinghua Garden Railway Station and the North Film Institute, only the suggestion to establish professional investigation and co-creation pathways is made, without delineating conservation control lines [depth:height_massing_character] [depth:risk_missing_data].

## Coordinated Research Area: Industry and Future City Research

The logic of the industry shifts from "park recruitment" to "public validation loops": universities and research institutions output methods and talent; open-source communities form visible contributions; enterprises validate products in governed scenarios; residents, visitors, and urban operators provide feedback on issues; audits and dissemination bring credible results back into the next round of R&D. Spatially, the public spine provides low-threshold meetings and demonstrations, while the three rings carry differentiated R&D and services, and the wings bring industrial capabilities into daily life without collecting irrelevant personal data.

Future cities are not automated neighborhoods filled with sensors, but rather "machine-readable, human-override" public service environments. Each AI touchpoint must disclose its purpose, minimal data set, Human Review, exit mechanism, and failure degradation; high-impact matters are still decided by legally authorized human processes [depth:existing_conditions_diagnosis].

## Overall Design Area: Urban Renewal and Regulatory-Plan-Level Urban Design

Overall structure is "one spine and three rings with two wings": the public spine integrates heritage storytelling, greenways, low-impact displays, and public services; the three rings establish open pedestrian/innovation communities around key areas; the two wings stitch together universities, businesses, rail stations, communities, and waterfront spaces in an east-west direction. Land use adopts a comprehensive zoning framework, with the blue-green public spine as an independent design layer superimposed to avoid misrepresenting conceptual corridors as precise statutory parcels; each layer does not cross the Provisional Boundary, ensuring no overlap or area gaps in land use [depth:land_use_layout].

![Land Use Structure and Complete Coverage Map](assets/figures/land-use-structure.png)

This scheme does not define the Floor Area Ratio, Building Height, or total building scale. The conceptual Building Footprint is only used to verify spatial capacity and Public Space relationships; the final building plan, Demolish–Renovate–Retain Strategy, ownership, and engineering feasibility must be redone after the official controls and measured building data are in place [depth:development_intensity_controls].

## Detailed Design of Key Areas

![Design drawings for three distinct focus areas](assets/figures/key-areas.png)

### Coexistence Loop 01: Safety Governance and Independent Innovation at Zhongzhiyuan

With a focus on "evaluative visibility, risk shareability, and knowledge sharing." The interface along the Lin Qing River will feature a low-carbon operation display, open model testing, standard workshops, and a public explanation hall. The research and development internal area will be layered with authorization from the public exhibition area. The initial phase will use mobile exhibition pavilions and scheduled test fields, avoiding permanent engineering judgments until the conditions of the riverbank, flood protection, and roadways are known.

### Coexistence Loop 02: Beijing AI Origin Open Source Transformation Ring

Positioned as "From Campus Methods to Street Products." The public spine transforms into an open-source release living room, a nearby campus outcome conversion street, a talent living service interface, and an intellectual property/legal service interface; the contribution wall only displays voluntarily disclosed projects and contributions, without individual rankings. Campus—parking lot—community connections prioritize walking, with specific interfaces awaiting ownership and traffic verification.

### Coexistence Loop 03: Dazhongsi Smart Economy and International Exchange Ring

With the positioning of "station arrival directly enters the city innovation lobby." The quadrants of pedestrian stitching, intelligent body roadshow pods, content consumption co-creation fields, and data element audit rooms are arranged around the Public Space; all displays can operate offline, while high-impact data transactions still require compliant authorization and manual audit.

## Land Use, Building Scale, and Retain-Renovate-Demolish Strategy

The land uses adopt categories of research and development, education, commercial services, residential, community services, culture, and park green spaces, fully covering the temporary overall boundary. The number of land use characteristics is verified by [metric:land_use_feature_count]. The conceptual buildings are divided into categories of research and development, education, mixed use, talent apartments, community services, and culture; their area [metric:building_footprint_area_sqm] only illustrates the scheme's graphic representation and does not represent the existing buildings or approved scale.

The Demolish–Renovate–Retain Strategy adopts the approach of "investigate first, grade second, and act last": structures with high heritage value and high structural safety will be retained; those with structurally viable but functionally inappropriate elements can undergo reversible renovations; those lacking in ownership, structural integrity, pollution, or cultural heritage information will be held in abeyance; and only after comprehensive professional arguments and legal procedures can demolition be proposed. All current buildings are conceptual envelopes [depth:retain_renovate_demolish].

## Transport, Rail, Municipal Infrastructure, and Public Services

The transportation system consists of north-south public greenways, three co-generative loop pedestrian connections, two co-operative wing cycling links, and seven east-west micro-circulation routes [metric:road_length_m]. Key nodes should prioritize the verification of continuous accessibility, cycling parking, walkability from rail transit stations to Public Spaces, and nighttime lighting; no changes should be made to expressways or primary roads without redline plans, traffic flow data, and engineering documentation.

![Traffic Slow Zones, Blue-Green Spaces, and Public Space Map](assets/figures/mobility-bluegreen.png)

Municipal and new infrastructure adopt a "small box, replaceable, and degradable" approach: edge computing, energy storage, communication, environmental monitoring, and public service terminals share standardized equipment slots; offline navigation and manual services are retained during a network outage; logs only record operational and maintenance necessary information. Drainage, flood prevention, fire safety, energy supply, and pipeline connections must be confirmed in the professional deepening phase [depth:traffic_rail_slow_parking] [depth:municipal_new_infrastructure].

## Long-term Operation and Governance Loop

It is recommended to establish a six-step mechanism of "public problem pool—scene admission—limited-area trial—third-party evaluation—open debriefing—renewal/exit." Residents, universities, enterprises, and operational units can all propose problems; scene admission must first complete the purpose's legitimacy, minimum data requirements, responsible party, Human Review, and exit plan; each trial sets time and spatial boundaries; evaluation reports publicly disclose non-sensitive results; scenes that do not meet the standards, have excessive intrusiveness, or lack an operational entity are to be exited.

Annual rhythm is suggested as follows: spring public problem collection, summer controlled prototype testing, autumn Jing-Zhang AI Coexistence Week public exhibition, and winter audit and maintenance. This rhythm serves as an operational reference and does not represent official event scheduling. Brand communication focuses on reproducible projects, genuine contributions, and public interest, rather than the number of enterprises or exposure levels at the expense of urban quality.
