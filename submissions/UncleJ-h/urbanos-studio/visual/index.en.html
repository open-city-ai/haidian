The Jingzhang Proving Belt

An innovation belt that turns the city itself into a public proving ground where AI can be applied for, tested, failed at, and withdrawn from — not a park, but a proving right.

Land-use zoning and buildings

Land-use classification follows the MNR land-use classification guide. This proposal gives no statutory planning conclusions — no floor area ratio, no building height, no demolish-renovate-retain classification, no road red line. Existing building footprints come from OSM outlines (1,678 features), used only as existing conditions and never as a building-scale conclusion.

Transport, walkability and the blue-green public realm

Factual starting point: Phase 2 of the Jing-Zhang Railway Heritage Park opened along its full length on 6 August 2026, completing the nine-kilometre greenway. This proposal therefore does not argue about how to connect it, but about how to make an already-built public space start producing evidence.
[to be verified] Phase 2 at approx. 53 ha / 450,000 visits is per public reports and is not entered into metrics.json.

WATER_SYSTEM is a non-editable layer: existing watercourses are referenced from OSM and no new blue line is created. Rail station points are real OSM coordinates. The green ratio 7.1588% and public space ratio 4.3616% are computed against the provisional design area and must be recomputed once official boundaries are released.

Renewal projects and AI scenarios

Thirteen scenario nodes are distributed along the corridor, covering all four proving states — including one retired slot. Every proving project carries a notice board on site stating eight fields: what is being tested, who is testing, what data is used, the privacy boundary, human review points, the start and end dates, the feedback and appeal channel, and the current state.

ApplyingPilotingVerifiedRetired

Retired is the one failure state this scheme is willing to draw. The mechanism requires that a project which fails acceptance at term is retired, with the process archived and published — it is not hidden on the drawing. Every retired slot shown in these figures is a conceptual example and refers to no real project, built or under construction.

Assumptions

This proposal holds under the following assumptions. If official data overturns any of them, the dependent conclusions must be recomputed:

  • The announced textual extents are a reasonable basis for inferring provisional boundaries (GAP-BOUNDARY-001)
  • OSM road network, watercourses, buildings and rail stations are sufficient for existing-conditions diagnosis
  • Where official geometry for the wings is absent, a carrier may be generated from existing roads and watercourses (P2 GAP-2)
  • The proving right, computing vouchers and related arrangements are conceptual recommendations and constitute no confirmed government arrangement
  • The phasing order is a design proposition, not an official implementation sequence

To-be-verified register

  • Haidian AI firm structure (70% / 15% / <5%) — per public reports, not entered into metrics.json
  • Precise bank lines and blue-line extent of the Xiaoyue River (OSM current conditions used instead)
  • Actual composition of the technology-service concentration along the Zhongguancun road network
  • Official polygons for the three key areas (provisional inferred boundaries in use, GAP-BOUNDARY-001)
  • Regulatory conditions: floor area ratio, building height and other statutory metrics (GAP-CONTROL-001)
  • Heritage park Phase 2 at approx. 53 ha / 450,000 visits — per public reports, not entered into metrics.json
  • Actual walkable catchment within 500 m of rail stations (no network accessibility analysis performed)
  • Official definition of the two wings' geometry (P2 GAP-2; currently a self-generated spatial carrier)
