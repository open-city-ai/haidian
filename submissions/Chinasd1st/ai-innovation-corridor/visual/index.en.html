Three-level scope

The proposal proceeds from the research scope, through the overall design scope, to the key-area scope, binding industrial judgements, urban renewal and key-district design to layers and metrics.

project_scope_summary.csv

Research scope

AI industry ecosystem, three-zone two-wing coordination, future urban form and cultural narrative across 43.6 km².

  • AI innovation and talent chains
  • Global case conversion mechanism
  • Naming & communication

Overall design scope

Urban renewal, land-use structure, transport and municipal works, and the Jingzhang Heritage Park active belt across 11.4 km².

  • Regulatory-depth urban design
  • Renewal project list
  • Blue-green slow-traffic system

Key-area scope

Detailed design for the three key areas: program, building renewal, public space and traffic organization.

  • Zhongzhiyuan AI Acceleration Area
  • Beijing AI Origin Community
  • Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster

Key areas

Each key area must state industry positioning, spatial strategy, building and public-space actions, traffic interfaces and implementation risks.

Zhongzhiyuan AI Acceleration Area

Garden-type autonomous innovation block: national platforms, industry display, Qinghe culture, external transport and low-carbon exchange space.

Beijing AI Origin Community

University-proximate outcome-conversion block: university source innovation, open-source community, talent services, retain/renovate/demolish and station integration.

Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster

Urban smart-economy block: leading enterprises, agent economy, commercial services, composite green-space use and four-quadrant connectivity.
