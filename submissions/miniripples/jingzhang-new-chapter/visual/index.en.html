Zhongzhiyuan AI Innovation Accelerator

A garden-style innovation district combining national platforms, industry showcases, Qinghe culture, regional access, and low-carbon social space.

Beijing AI Origin Community

A campus-adjacent technology-transfer district combining university innovation, open-source communities, talent services, retain-renovate-demolish choices, and station integration.

Dazhongsi AI Industry District

An urban intelligent-economy district combining leading firms, agent-based businesses, commercial services, multifunctional green space, and four-quadrant junction connectivity.
