# Jingzhang AI Valley: Switchback Governance Corridor

**English name: Jingzhang AI Valley — Switchback Governance Corridor; motto: make every urban intelligence traceable, stoppable, and reversible along the track.**

## One-Page Executive Summary

**Industry fact anchors (official figures, 2026 Zhongguancun Forum).** Haidian is building the Centennial Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt with the approximately 9 km Jing-Zhang Railway Heritage Park as its "innovation link" and "three areas, two wings" as its spatial layout; Haidian hosts over 2,000 AI enterprises, 26 unicorns, and 130 registered foundation models, with AI core-industry scale above 350 billion yuan, about 30% of the national total [source:HD-AI-CORRIDOR-FACTS-2026].

The 2026 Haidian District Government Work Report states "building an AI innovation district based on the AI Origin Community, the AI Beiwei Community, and the Jing-Zhang Railway Heritage Park innovation belt," and the block-level regulatory plan along the park has passed technical review and is advancing toward approval [source:HD-GOV-WORK-REPORT-2026] [source:HD00-1601-CONTROL-PLAN-NOTICE]. Our "three areas, two wings" naming maps directly onto the official spatial layout; our institutional design adds the piece the official documents leave open: **who decides whether an AI deserves to enter the city**.

**Core judgment.** What Haidian most lacks is not more compute clusters, but a public track where residents, researchers, enterprises, operators and regulators can jointly judge "is this intelligence worth entering the city". Modeled on the Ren-shaped switchback line at Qinglongqiao, this proposal turns the Jingzhang Heritage Park into a walkable **switchback governance corridor**: Zhongzhi Park for full-stack safety evaluation, AI Origin Community for research-to-street conversion, Dazhongsi for public experience and consumer rights; the Zhongguancun service wing and Xiaoyue River scenario wing provide capital, standards, talent and controlled tests [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK] [depth:three_level_scope_framework].

**Institutional loop.** Before entering public space, every urban AI scenario must pass the switchback loop — stop at the node, pass the Teach-Back Gate (report—inquiry—vote), then pass / turn back / enter depot. Not understood means turn back; any party's veto forces a turn-back; no automatic recovery [depth:switchback_governance] [depth:teach_back_gate]. Every turn-back, depot entry and recalculation is recorded in the K-marker chain [depth:kmarker_versioning]. This "Ren-three-meanings — switchback node — teach-back gate — K-marker chain" is the original closed loop: space is not a technology container but an institutional interface where the public can question, reject and re-test urban AI [assumption:A-SWITCHBACK-HISTORY-001].

**First phase only prepares low-regret moves.** First 90 days: official-data replacement list, accessibility audit and issue collection; then three reversible prototypes and a public evaluation protocol; any unresolved major safety/rights/data issue stops progress. Budgets, owners, procurement and construction timings stay unknown where data is missing — no conceptual commitments disguised as implementability [depth:phasing_implementation].

**Four auditable deliverables.** `visual/assets/switchback-gate.json` machine-codes the three switchback states and teach-back gate; `visual/assets/delivery-matrix.json` records action-package dependencies, owners, deliverables, acceptance and hard stops; `geometry/public_space.geojson` provides a 73.37 ha continuous public validation belt; each of the 12 scenario cards binds a grade and a switchback condition [metric:public_space_area_sqm] [metric:scenario_node_count] [metric:machine_readable_contract_count].

**Five fast facts.** ①12 scenario cards, each bound to grade and switchback node; ②73.37 ha continuous public belt (6.43%), 3 belts + 4 plazas + 12 nodes; ③Teach-Back Gate three steps, not understood means turn back; ④K-marker chain — every recalculation/turn-back/depot permanently recorded; ⑤all 56 files declared, zero fabricated values — missing baselines stay pending [metric:public_space_ratio] [metric:sunset_clause_trigger_count].

## Switchback Governance Protocol (Core Institution)
This proposal uses the Ren-shaped switchback line at Qinglongqiao on the Jingzhang Railway as its institutional prototype: a train climbing a steep grade cannot go straight; it must stop at a switchback point, change direction, and continue [source:OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT] [assumption:A-SWITCHBACK-HISTORY-001]. Along the belt, the Qinghuayuan Station site is a municipal-level protected cultural relic with its protection scope and construction-control zone delineated (Jing Zheng Fa [2025] No. 3) [source:HERITAGE-QINGHUAYUAN-STATION-SCOPE]; all heritage-related concepts along the line take special review as a precondition. Innovation works the same way: any urban AI scenario, before reaching its capability limit, should be re-evaluated at a switchback point instead of automatically continuing in its original direction. Four mechanisms:

- **Switchback Nodes (R1–R3)**: every AI scenario sets fixed switchback nodes on its running track [depth:switchback_governance]. R1: at each node the scenario must stop — no automatic continuation [depth:switch_states]. R2: a three-party review — scenario responsible entity (decision), professional review (technical), public representative (rights) — decides "pass / turn back / pull into depot"; any party's veto forces a turn-back [depth:switchback_governance]. R3: a stopped or turned-back scenario must self-check before re-entry; no automatic recovery — returning from depot to mainline requires re-evaluation at a switchback node [depth:switch_states] [depth:kmarker_versioning].
- **Teach-Back Gate**: before the vote, the scenario must pass three steps [depth:teach_back_gate] — **Report**: the responsible entity explains in plain language what the AI does, where it might fail, and how to stop it; **Inquiry**: public representatives ask questions until they confirm understanding; **Vote**: the three parties decide pass / turn back / depot. If the public does not understand, the scenario turns back [metric:public_issue_closure_rate] [assumption:A-TEACHBACK-001].
- **Veto Procedure**: any public representative may file a veto motion (verbally accepted, recorded by the secretariat); within 48 hours the scenario degrades to siding operation (speed/scope/data limited); the responsible entity may request one public rehearing with at least five public representatives present; every veto is recorded in the K-marker chain permanently [depth:switchback_governance] [depth:teach_back_gate].
- **Grade-based Access**: referring to the railway's 33‰ maximum grade, scenarios are graded by climbing difficulty into three levels — gentle (universal-experience, community-level access), medium (industry-validation, requires professional pre-review), steep (research-frontier, requires joint-attack agreement). The higher the grade, the stricter the admission and re-review [depth:grade_based_access].
- **K-marker Versioning**: the proposal uses railway kilometer markers as version anchors; each official data update, major event, or recalculation records a new K-marker, forming a traceable version chain [depth:kmarker_versioning].
- **Switch States**: scenario running states are expressed as "mainline running / siding turn-back / depot maintenance", with no automatic recovery; returning from maintenance to mainline requires re-evaluation at a switchback node [depth:switch_states].

## Three-Level Scope Framework
The switchback governance approach develops a **public-value switchback corridor** along the centennial Jingzhang railway line: problems are raised by residents and enterprises, prototypes are developed in the three areas, factors and scenarios are obtained in the two wings, small-scale validation runs in the public test belt, and public results finally decide pass, turn-back, or pull-into-depot [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#PUB-001] [depth:public_participation].

- **One Belt**: the Jingzhang Heritage Park as the public spine, "walkable, learnable, testable, reviewable".
- **Three Cores**: Zhongzhi Park for full-stack tools and safety evaluation; AI Origin Community for research–startup–community co-creation; Dazhongsi for AI-native consumption, business, and public experience.
- **Two Wings**: the Zhongguancun service wing for capital, IP, talent, and international services; the Xiaoyue River scenario wing for controlled testing of transport, ecology, health, and robotics.
- **Four Segments**: identify problems → sandbox validation → public experience → switchback review, forming a reversible urban renewal mechanism.

## Public Participation Mechanism [depth:public_participation]

A resident's on-the-record criticism of "performance-style" urban construction (issue #1061) [source:PUBLIC-ISSUE-1061] is the real-world starting point for the Teach-Back Gate and the veto procedure: participation is a decision step, not a display step.

- **Representative selection**: 2-4 each from 6 groups (commuters, elderly/disabled, students/researchers, SMEs, tourists, operators); open recruitment + community recommendation
- **Term**: 6 months, renewable once; re-recruited each term
- **Compensation**: 1.5x Beijing minimum wage/hour, transport reimbursement or 50 yuan/meeting, caregiving supplement 50 yuan/hour
- **Non-digital channels**: Paper forms, assisted filling, phone feedback, drop boxes; at least one offline meeting before major decisions, announced 14 days in advance
- **Grouped evaluation**: Participation effectiveness evaluated by group, not aggregated; results published annually with satisfaction, closure rate, response time
- **Veto right**: Public representatives can veto at switchback points for data collection, surveillance, restrictive measures (triggers mandatory switchback) [depth:switchback_governance] [depth:teach_back_gate]
