Switchback Governance Protocol (Core Institution)

This proposal uses the Ren-shaped switchback line at Qinglongqiao on the Jingzhang Railway as its institutional prototype: a train climbing a steep grade cannot go straight; it must stop at a switchback point, change direction, and continue. Innovation works the same way: any urban AI scenario, before reaching its capability limit, should be re-evaluated at a switchback point instead of automatically continuing in its original direction. Four mechanisms:

  • Switchback Nodes: every AI scenario sets fixed switchback nodes on its running track. At each node the train stops and a three-party review — scenario responsible entity (decision), professional review (technical), public representative (rights) — decides "pass / turn back / pull into depot". Any party's veto forces a turn-back; there is no automatic continuation Depth.
  • Grade-based Access: referring to the railway's 33‰ maximum grade, scenarios are graded by climbing difficulty into three levels — gentle (universal-experience, community-level access), medium (industry-validation, requires professional pre-review), steep (research-frontier, requires joint-attack agreement). The higher the grade, the stricter the admission and re-review Depth.
  • K-marker Versioning: the proposal uses railway kilometer markers as version anchors; each official data update, major event, or recalculation records a new K-marker, forming a traceable version chain Depth.
  • Switch States: scenario running states are expressed as "mainline running / siding turn-back / depot maintenance", with no automatic recovery; returning from maintenance to mainline requires re-evaluation at a switchback node Depth.

Switchback Node = Pull-the-Plug Point (AI-Off Equivalence Operational Layer)

A switchback node is not merely a "stop and review" point; it is a pull-the-plug test point: each scenario must declare at its switchback node five machine-checkable fields — ai_off_path (the equivalent path when AI is switched off; must not route to an online path that still depends on the same system), human_handoff (the human takeover role), gate_id (the current gate), operating_mode (mainline / siding / depot), responsible_role (the responsible profession); any point missing one of these fields does not count toward service coverage Spatial data. This proposal declares complete five-field bindings for 8 switchback nodes (covering gentle / medium / steep grades and the three states); both path-completeness and human-handoff-designation rates are 1.0, checkable point by point Metric Metric. Turning the switchback node into a pull-the-plug point moves the "three meanings of the Ren character" from philosophy into data: arrival must be confirmed by a human, and the first piece of evidence for "confirmed by a human" is that the city still works when AI is off. The stop/resume rule set (8 risk dimensions' mitigation and human_review, trigger-then-stop, resume requires evidence not promises) is in risk.json Depth.

Three-Level Scope Framework

The switchback governance approach does not label traditional parks with AI; it develops a public-value switchback corridor along the centennial Jingzhang railway line: problems are raised by residents and enterprises, prototypes are developed in the three areas, factors and scenarios are obtained in the two wings, small-scale validation runs in the public test belt, and public results finally decide pass, turn-back, or pull-into-depot.

  • One Belt: the Jingzhang Heritage Park as the public spine, "walkable, learnable, testable, reviewable".
  • Three Cores: Zhongzhi Park for full-stack tools and safety evaluation; AI Origin Community for research–startup–community co-creation; Dazhongsi for AI-native consumption, business, and public experience.
  • Two Wings: the Zhongguancun service wing for capital, IP, talent, and international services; the Xiaoyue River scenario wing for controlled testing of transport, ecology, health, and robotics.
  • Four Segments: identify problems → sandbox validation → public experience → switchback review, forming a reversible urban renewal mechanism.

Spatial evidence: Spatial data, Spatial data, Spatial data. Overall control prefers activating the ground floors of existing buildings, railway nodes, under-bridge and corner spaces rather than large demolition; any bridge/tunnel, underground-space, heritage-protection, and transport proposal requires special study. OP-01 Desktop Paired Pilot Archive (Methodology Demo)

This section is a physical demonstration of the SWB scoring criteria: a paired test on SC-01 Accessibility Route Assistant (switchback node SWB-01, Wudaokou / AI Origin Community) turns "is the city still usable when AI is off" from a slogan into checkable, case-by-case data. This is a methodology demo using synthetic test cases, not a real-world surveyed baseline — its value is that the method and data are public, so anyone can recompute the equivalence-gap reading and verify the measurement method itself; the real baseline is filled in after the K3–K6 first survey, and is not impersonated before then Depth.

Method: the same 12 accessibility route requests are run under both (A) AI-on and (B) AI-off (ai_off_path = on-site accessibility guide + large-print paper block map + tactile guidance strip) conditions; whether each completes is recorded; withdrawals, technical faults, and non-completion reasons are registered together with completions, with no failure cases dropped. The full 12 cases and per-case results are in visual/assets/pilot-evidence.json, independently replayable.

Recomputable reading (anyone can recalculate): AI-on completion rate 12/12 = 1.0; AI-off completion rate 8/12 = 0.667; equivalence gap 0.333 Metric. The gap concentrates in 4 routes requiring real-time data re-routing (temporary-occupation detour, no-guide night shift, post-rain reroute, large construction detour) — when AI is off, the guide does not hold real-time occupation/closure data. This is not a real baseline, but it turns "which routes remain usable when AI is off, and which do not" into checkable case-by-case data, and checkable case-by-case is exactly what SWB must prove. After real operation, real-time data completion and guide-hour expansion are concrete directions to close the gap. Recalculation script: count(ai_on_completed==true)/12 and count(ai_off_completed==true)/12; the denominator includes all 12 cases (including 4 non-completions), with no failure cases dropped.
