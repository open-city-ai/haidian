# Copyright and generation statement

All diagrams, figures, GeoJSON design layers, HTML, and PDF layouts in this submission were generated for this proposal from repository-cleared sources and participant-authored geometry. No commercial basemap tiles, news images, corporate marks, portraits, or third-party illustration assets are embedded.

Text uses the COMMUNITY-DISPLAY-ONLY proposal license. PDF and raster generation use ReportLab and Pillow; spatial derivation uses Shapely and pyproj. Chinese glyphs are rendered with the locally installed Arial Unicode system font, which is referenced by the build process but not redistributed as a standalone font file. Source facts and case references remain attributed in `sources.json`; case sources are background only.

This submission is an open co-creation concept and does not replace statutory planning, professional engineering, or government approval.
