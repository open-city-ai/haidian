Three-Level Scope Framework

Jing-Zhang Headroom assigns a decision scale to each official scope. The Coordinated Research Area frames industry and regional coordination; the Overall Design Area frames continuous public space and renewal structure; the Key-Area Detailed Design Area tests transferable spatial prototypes. Four kinds of headroom run through all scales: spatial room for changing uses, temporal room to test before fixing, governance room for human takeover and non-digital alternatives, and ecological room for later blue-green and comfort studies. The structure is one Headroom Spine, three Headroom Grounds, four cross seams, and two feedback wings. The spine supports heritage reading, walking, and cycling; the three grounds focus on testing, learning and transfer, and civic adoption; cross seams reconnect daily life east and west; the two wings return professional services and real-use feedback to the innovation cycle.
