03 / STOPPABLE PILOT

No baseline, no promise; no operator, no device

A pilot is an operating contract with resources, a measurement window, success gates, stop gates and a human fallback.

Five-step validation cycle

  1. 01Assign operator, independent reviewer and human emergency stop.
  2. 02Complete a 30-day professional and staffed field baseline.
  3. 03Run for eight weeks in an enclosed or clearly notified setting.
  4. 04Publish the data card, incidents, accessibility findings and unresolved issues.
  5. 05Scale, revise or remove; equipment-free public space remains.

Immediate shutdown

Recoverable speech, unnotified collection, outbound raw data, a collision, failed emergency stop or conflicting emergency channels.

Logs are preserved for independent review; the affected function does not restart automatically.

Static signs, tactile guidance and staffed service continue without AI.

04 / EVIDENCE BOUNDARY

Readable uncertainty is part of the design

Submitted design geometry is reproducible; field conditions, statutory controls and local endorsement are not invented.

Known from this package

12 nodes, 4 restorative points, 3 validation contracts, 4 seasonal operating cycles and a default raw-audio retention target of 0 hours.

Unknown until Phase 0

Current acoustic baseline, users served, official FAR/height/density, road redlines, utilities, ownership, heritage controls and precise station relations.

Privacy default

No voice recording, voiceprint, identity or emotion inference by default. Public data describes conditions and governance, never personal routes or voices.

Geometry warning

Canonical provisional geometry is preserved. PROV-KEY-003 is not anchored to Dazhongsi Station; official data triggers full recalculation.
