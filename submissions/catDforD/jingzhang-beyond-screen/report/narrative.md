# 屏外京张 / Jing-Zhang Beyond the Screen

屏外京张提出一种无屏依赖的 AI 公共基础设施：AI 可以在后台辅助识别、翻译、调度和检验，但公众完成出行、问询、办事、学习、休息与参与，不应被迫拥有智能手机、下载 App 或扫描二维码。方案以一条屏外公共脊、三座界面院、两翼服务回路和十二个场景原型组织概念空间，并把人工兜底、数据最小化与可撤回试点写成基础设施要求。

Jing-Zhang Beyond the Screen proposes screen-optional AI civic infrastructure. AI may assist sensing, translation, coordination, and evaluation in the background, but public access to movement, services, learning, rest, and participation must not depend on a smartphone, app, or QR code. One civic spine, three interface yards, two service loops, and twelve prototypes organize the concept, while human fallback, data minimization, and reversible pilots are treated as infrastructure requirements.
