Design Proposition

AI moves into the background and public life returns to the foreground. Every civic prototype retains a non-smartphone route and human fallback. Digital channels may exist, but they cannot become the only gate to a civic right.

AI Scenarios | Twelve Screen-Optional Prototypes

S01CIVIC PROTOTYPE

Tactile Direction Rail

Continuous texture, shape coding, and high-contrast signs without phone navigation.

Human fallback
On-site steward and printed map
Data boundary
No personal traces; only manual inspection records.
S02CIVIC PROTOTYPE

Audio-Light Crossing Beacon

Audible, tactile, and visible crossing cues prioritizing low-vision and older users.

Human fallback
Human traffic assistance and conventional signals
Data boundary
Device status only; no face or identity records.
S03CIVIC PROTOTYPE

Dual-Channel Civic Service Window

Voice, print, physical buttons, and a staffed window operate in parallel; QR is never mandatory.

Human fallback
In-person service and telephone booking
Data boundary
Sensitive cases stay off public terminals; records are minimized.
S04CIVIC PROTOTYPE

Transit Companion Point

Color, ground texture, and human guidance connect station exits, corners, and public space.

Human fallback
Scheduled volunteers coordinated with station staff
Data boundary
Anonymous issue categories only; no individual travel profiles.
S05CIVIC PROTOTYPE

Civic Repair Bell

Residents report faults through a physical pull, phone, or staffed desk.

Human fallback
Paper work orders and scheduled inspections
Data boundary
Only location, fault type, and resolution status are retained.
S06CIVIC PROTOTYPE

Breathing Thermal-Comfort Canopy

Shade, mist, and ventilation respond to environmental sensing with manual controls retained.

Human fallback
Passive shade and manual maintenance
Data boundary
Environmental data such as temperature and humidity only.
S07INDUSTRY TEST

Shared-Path Robotics Test Yard

Low-speed robots test yielding, docking, and incident handling within tactile edges and human observation.

Human fallback
On-site safety staff can intervene immediately
Data boundary
Test logs are de-identified; purposeless public imagery retention is prohibited.
S08INDUSTRY TEST

Embodied Accessibility Lab

Co-tests handrails, voice, touch, and robotic assistance with disabled participants; screen scores never replace lived experience.

Human fallback
Human assistance and the right to stop testing
Data boundary
Informed consent, tiered authorization, and withdrawal rights.
S09INDUSTRY TEST

Edge Model Inspection Bench

Physical samples and public test cards expose energy use, bias, and failure modes of edge models.

Human fallback
Expert review and printed report
Data boundary
Cleared test sets and auditable records only.
S10CIVIC PROTOTYPE

Open-Source Translation Gallery

Model cards, code contributions, and risk notes become tactile models, printed cards, and live explanation.

Human fallback
Scheduled human tours
Data boundary
Only openly licensed content is shown with attribution.
S11CIVIC PROTOTYPE

Health-Education-Legal Inquiry Table

AI navigates public information only; staff handle interpretation, referral, and high-risk cases.

Human fallback
Professional appointments and printed directories
Data boundary
No medical, education, or legal private records are entered in open space.
S12CIVIC PROTOTYPE

Beyond-the-Screen Urban Week

Physical badges, public tables, walking routes, and live dialogue structure an annual open event.

Human fallback
On-site notice boards and community contacts
Data boundary
Participation is recorded only as aggregate counts.

Buildings, Renewal Projects, and Phasing

Building and Intervention Boundary

Twelve footprints express prototype lab, incubator, community-service, and mixed-use carriers only. No real building receives a retain, renovate, or demolish decision without survey and rights evidence.
