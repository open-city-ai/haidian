CITY OF AGENTS | Centennial Jingzhang Urban Intelligent-Life Corridor

The Jingzhang Railway Heritage Park becomes a continuous memory spine from the North Fifth Ring through Qinghuayuan and Dazhongsi to Beijing North Railway Station. An existing city is upgraded into an accountable Urban Agent System through one spine, three districts, two wings, ten scenario nodes, three test sites and three pilgrimage landmarks.

CITY OF AGENTS | Centennial Jingzhang Urban Intelligent-Life Corridor

> **When everything becomes an Agent, the city wakes up.** In the past we entered the city; in the future the city will come to us.

This proposal does not equate an “AI city” with a collection of robots, blue screens and driverless vehicles. It proposes an accountable **Urban Agent System** in which trees, lights, seats, buildings, vehicles, shops, robots and the memory of the Jingzhang Railway may have identities, abilities and bounded memories. They collaborate through a common protocol, while human safety, privacy, choice and appeal always outrank system efficiency. [standard:PROJECT-AGENT-OPEN-CALL-TASKBOOK] [depth:overall_spatial_structure]

Every spatial statement is a **conceptual recommendation / reference scheme / subject to professional development**. It is not an approval, government commitment or statutory-planning conclusion. Formal figures are generated from the submitted GeoJSON and metrics.json; the seventeen scene images and seven process drafts are participant-cleared AI concept expressions that communicate desired experience or the co-creation process only and are not evidence of existing conditions, boundaries, historical records or metrics. [source:USER-CONCEPT-IMAGES] [source:ASSET-CATALOG]

The visual revision makes the full-corridor base map the primary evidence. Three portrait A0 boards cover the masterplan, system strategies, and scenario/delivery system; five formal panorama figures retain the complete corridor frame from the North Fifth Ring through Qinghuayuan, Wudaokou, the North Fourth Ring, Dazhongsi, the North Third Ring and Beijing North Railway Station. The pale urban texture is context only and is not used for measurement. Formal boundaries, routes, public spaces and nodes remain derived from submitted GeoJSON. AI board drafts and their credibility screening are recorded in report/narrative.md so generated labels and quantities cannot be mistaken for verified conclusions.

Coordinated research: industry and the future city

Name, identity and positioning

**Chinese name:** 百年京张·城市智能生命走廊. **English name:** CITY OF AGENTS. **Tagline:** When everything becomes an Agent, the city wakes up. The logo is an amber vertical line crossing three differently coloured nodes: the railway memory spine, three Agent districts and open collaboration. Amber carries railway memory; blue denotes public service; green denotes ecological life; violet denotes culture and creation. [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK]

Three positionings are proposed:

1. A world-class full-stack AI testing corridor where models, compute, robotics, autonomous mobility and public applications can be tested, exited and audited. 2. An Origin Community for people and urban intelligent life, where AI first addresses ageing, childhood, disability, commuting, thermal comfort and public services. 3. A Time Railway combining Jingzhang history, Zhongguancun innovation and a new AI culture in a distinctly Beijing narrative.

Five functions—R&D validation, talent life, public service, cultural experience and open operation—form three districts: northern innovation/testing, central talent life/scenario incubation, and southern industry/time-railway culture. The east wing connects universities and innovation resources; the west wing connects communities and industry; the central spine makes them one system. Overall urban renewal and control-plan-depth urban design

One spine, three districts, two wings and ten nodes

The railway becomes the **central public path**, not a back edge dividing east and west. ROAD-001 is an approximately 9.54-km conceptual memory/slow-mobility spine. Seven east-west links connect both sides where existing streets, underpasses, intersections and park interfaces permit. No engineering form is predetermined; every crossing requires rail safety, traffic, heritage and ownership confirmation. [data:geometry/roads.geojson#ROAD-001] [metric:memory_spine_length_m] [metric:east_west_stitch_count] [depth:traffic_rail_slow_parking]

P0–P3 renewal procedure

The absent building inventory, ownership, heritage and structural data make parcel-by-parcel demolition conclusions irresponsible. The proposal therefore defines a reusable decision procedure. [depth:retain_renovate_demolish]

P1 RetainOperating roads/rail and sound, useful buildingsRetain function and primary structureGround-floor opening, energy, accessibility, wayfinding and digital-interface upgrades
P2 RenewWeak interfaces, underused ground floors and broken pedestrian linksAdaptive reuse firstLight additions, programme change, shared space and managed night use
P3 AssessAssets with possible safety or public-interest conflictsNo presumed demolitionValue, structure, ownership, carbon, public impact and alternatives must be assessed first

Detailed design for the three key areas

A1 Zhongzhiyuan AI Autonomous-Innovation Accelerator | announced 192.1 ha

The northern gateway combines full-stack R&D, open verification, a compute outpost, developer commons and a controlled Mobility Personality Port. Existing suitable stock is retained and adapted. Walking discontinuities are repaired before new crossings are considered. Autonomous testing begins in an isolatable low-speed sandbox with an on-site safety operator. All locations are directional because the key-area polygon, ownership, operators and traffic conditions require confirmation. [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-001] [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#S02]

A2 Beijing AI Origin Community | announced 104.3 ha

The central community combines talent housing, daily services, learning, health and small innovation spaces around the Tree Council, No-App Crossing, AI City Living Room and Quiet Service Street. Ground-floor accessibility, shade, night safety and non-digital alternatives precede iconic construction. Residents may use speech, light, tactility or human service without installing an app. [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-002] [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#S03] [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#S05]

A3 Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster | announced 72.0 ha

The southern gateway combines Time Railway culture, adaptive industry and night-time creativity. Time Railway 1909, Agent Repair Commons and Beijing Node 01 are three pilgrimage landmarks. Existing stock first receives robotics repair, open hardware, digital culture and innovation-service uses. Night logistics and maintenance avoid residential quiet periods and retain human dispatch. [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-003] [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#S07] [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#S09] [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#S10]

AI ecosystem, personas and AI+ scenarios

Six personas guide the design: developers/start-ups; researchers/students; companies/international visitors; residents/older people; children/families; and disabled or temporarily mobility-impaired users. Appearance must never be used to infer age, disability, occupation, emotion or purchasing power. Preferences are explicit and deletable.

Land use, built scale and retain/renew/remove logic

The four conceptual land-use units completely and non-overlappingly cover the provisional boundary. They express dominant roles, not exclusive use. The concept green network is approximately 12.0% of the temporary area and the public-space network approximately 7.2%; they overlap and cannot be added or treated as statutory ratios. [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-001] [metric:green_ratio] [metric:public_space_ratio]

The P0–P3 procedure applies after an official building inventory becomes available. Retention and adaptive reuse have priority; P3 may only consider removal after heritage, structure, ownership, carbon, public impact and alternatives have been examined. [depth:retain_renovate_demolish]

Mobility, rail, municipal and public-service infrastructure

Priority is walking/accessibility, cycling/micro-mobility, public transport connection, controlled autonomous sandbox, then general motor traffic. Existing operating roads and railway retain their function. OSM is contextual only; every new crossing requires professional engineering. [source:OSM-CONTEXT] [depth:traffic_rail_slow_parking]

Four infrastructure layers combine traditional utilities; minimum-necessary sensing and edge compute; a shared Agent identity/permission/event/log/takeover protocol; and public governance through data catalogues, risk grading, third-party assessment and stop authority. Capacity, investment and carbon savings remain unclaimed pending professional work. [depth:municipal_new_infrastructure]

Blue-green space, public realm and urban character

The memory spine combines heritage interpretation, walking, ecology, stormwater, cooling and public life. Five ecological pockets support Tree Council, biodiversity and thermal comfort. AI interprets professionally verified environmental data but never replaces arboricultural care. [data:geometry/green_space.geojson#GREEN-001]

The public system is one spine, seven stitching links and ten nodes. A **NO SCREEN CITY** does not require scanning, an app or continuous screen attention. Speech, light, sound, tactility and human service coexist, with an equivalent non-AI path always available. [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#PUBLIC-001]

Three pilgrimage landmarks are Agent Repair Commons, Time Railway 1909 and Beijing Node 01. Wayfinding uses an amber spine, three district colours and Agent Passport symbols. Every font, logo, archive, portrait and company mark requires clearance. Text, signs, people and buildings appearing in the concept images cannot be reused as factual or construction material. [metric:pilgrimage_landmark_count] [source:ASSET-CATALOG]

Project list, policy and phasing

Twelve projects cover the spine, seven links and ten nodes. Phase 1 builds reversible interfaces, the central spine and ten scenario pilots; Phase 2 extends testing and public services through the north and centre; Phase 3 strengthens southern cultural operation and full-corridor coordination. Each project depends on relevant rail, road, heritage, ownership, utility, ethics and community approvals. [data:geometry/phasing.geojson#PHASE-001] [data:geometry/phasing.geojson#PHASE-002] [data:geometry/phasing.geojson#PHASE-003]

The annual brand is **CITY OF AGENTS WEEK**, with a spring Tree Council urban-ecology forum, summer Mobility Sandbox safety challenge, autumn Time Railway open-culture festival, winter Agent Night Shift maintenance school, monthly resident co-testing and quarterly safety/public-value reporting. Admission to the district follows problem registration, ethics/safety review, sandbox, resident feedback, accountable sign-off, limited launch and continuous audit. Funding, recruitment and procurement are policy-development suggestions, not existing government arrangements. [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK] [depth:renewal_project_list]

Risk, copyright and compliance

Provisional polygons must be replaced and all dependent metrics rerun. Missing building, ownership, structural, redline, utility, heritage, rail-safety and environmental data prevent demolition, density, height, investment or approval conclusions. AI systems prohibit default facial recognition, hidden emotion inference, cross-context profiling and high-risk decisions without an accountable human. Historical reconstruction discloses provenance, inference and uncertainty. Formal planning figures are original programmatic outputs; OSM is attributed under ODbL; the seventeen scene images and seven process drafts are concept-only assets listed in manifest.json under the shared usage declaration in sources.json.
