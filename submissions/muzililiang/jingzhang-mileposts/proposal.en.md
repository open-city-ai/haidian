# The Jing-Zhang Mileposts: From Mile Zero to a World-Class AI Innovation Belt

The Jing-Zhang Railway once carried real mileposts along every kilometer—measuring not only the track but the first mileage of Chinese independent innovation. A century later, this proposal reinvents "distance" as "milestones": **along the now-complete 9-km Jing-Zhang Railway Heritage Park green corridor, one themed mile station (JZ-K0 to JZ-K8) per kilometer turns innovation achievements into markable, celebratable, traceable public assets** [source:JZ-PARK-PHASE2-2026].

**Proposal at a glance**:

1. **A spine that already exists**—Phase 2 of the Heritage Park opened on August 6, 2026: a 9-km green corridor from Xizhimen to the North Fifth Ring, about 53 ha, serving about 70 communities and 450,000 residents, with 9 urban branch roads opened [source:JZ-PARK-PHASE2-2026]. This proposal does not plan a new axis from scratch; it overlays scenarios, landmarks, and governance onto a main ridge that is already walkable;
2. **A dual-zero narrative**: railway mileage starts at the Xizhimen gateway (K0), innovation mileage starts at the Origin Community (Zero Milepost 0.0 km)—nine mile stations map onto the nine kilometers, each aligned with a completed or publicly notified spatial node;
3. **A three-layer milepost system**: physical mileposts (public-space installation network), digital mileposts (open-source milestone protocol, isomorphic to GitHub Milestones), and cultural mileposts (1909 → Zhongguancun → AI centennial timeline);
4. **A railway-vocabulary state machine**: all 12 AI scenarios are governed by "switch—signal—marshalling yard—turnback—K marker"; red card pauses with human takeover, and retirement is possible; every milestone lighting records a version K marker [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK];
5. **Policy alignment down to document names**: Haidian's Urban Renewal Guidelines (2025) explicitly shape an "innovation exchange belt" along the Heritage Park and promote "four-area" integration in the 53-km² AI innovation district [source:HD-URBAN-RENEWAL-GUIDE-2025]; the district regulatory plan along the corridor has been publicly notified and its acceptance notice issued—this proposal's spatial structure follows the same direction [source:HD00-1601-CONTROL-NOTICE];
6. **An evidence-based industrial base**: by official figures, Haidian hosts over 2,000 AI enterprises, 130 registered foundation models, and an AI core industry scale above 350 billion CNY (30% of the national total); enterprises, revenue, and financing along the belt account for over 70% of the district, and talent over 80% [source:HD-AI-FACTS-2026]—the belt's task is not attracting stock but giving the densest AI industry a public spine on which progress can be measured.

## Coordinated Research Area: Industry and Future City Research

### Overall concept: The Jing-Zhang Mileposts

The Jing-Zhang Railway was the first trunk railway designed and built independently by China; mileposts along every kilometer measured the first mileage of Chinese independent innovation. A century later, the same corridor is reborn as the Jing-Zhang Railway Heritage Park: Phase 2 opened on August 6, 2026, completing the 9-km green corridor from Xizhimen to the North Fifth Ring [source:JZ-PARK-PHASE2-2026]. This proposal reinvents "distance" as "milestones": **nine themed mile stations (JZ-K0 to JZ-K8) along the 9-km corridor**, each aligned with a completed or publicly notified spatial node, forming a three-layer system:

- **Physical mileposts**: K0 Xizhimen gateway (south end of the park · Beijing North Station) → K1 Dazhongsi intelligent-economy station → K2 Sidaokou stitching station (North Third Ring separated interchange) → K3 Zhichun Road scenario station (underpass segment) → K4 Wudaokou · **Zero Milepost** (AI Origin Community) → K5 Qinghua East Road open-source station (Phase 1/2 boundary) → K6 Liudaokou education collaboration station → K7 Xuezhayuan full-stack testing station (Zhongzhiyuan) → K8 North Fifth Ring honor station (Jianqiao · Qinghe green wedge, meeting the "Jing-Zhang Ring" 1909 plaza) [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#PUBLIC-SPINE-0KM];
- **Digital mileposts**: an open-source milestone protocol—when an open-source project, AI model, or scenario test in the belt reaches a defined node, the corresponding mile station "lights up," isomorphic to GitHub Milestones, making contributions versionable;
- **Cultural mileposts**: the 1909 opening, Zhongguancun entrepreneurship, and the new AI culture are organized into a centennial timeline, walkable through the display installations at K0 and K8.

**Dual-zero narrative**: railway mileage starts at the city gateway (K0 Xizhimen), while innovation mileage starts at the Origin Community (Zero Milepost 0.0 km installation)—just as railways also place station-center markers at intermediate stations, the two zeros do not conflict: one is the origin of city and rail, the other is the spiritual origin of the innovation ecosystem. This narrative directly echoes the call's own "agent contribution honor wall / AI milestones" memorial system [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK].

## Overall Design Area: Urban Renewal and Regulatory-Plan-Level Urban Design

### Spatial structure: One Belt · Three Cores · Two Wings · Nine Stations

The overall design area takes the **9-km Heritage Park innovation spine** as its skeleton (One Belt), connects the **three key cores**, links the **Zhongguancun Technology Service Wing and Xiaoyuehe Scenario Empowerment Wing** (Two Wings), and places **nine themed mile stations** along the spine (Nine Stations). The spine is not a new redline; it translates the completed corridor's north-south connection and east-west stitching into a composite system of slow mobility, green space, public space, and AI scenarios: Phase 2 already opened 9 urban branch roads and built the "three trails plus green" gapless slow system (walking/jogging/cycling, linked to the Huilongguan bicycle expressway) [source:JZ-PARK-PHASE2-2026]; this proposal adds station interchange, scenario nodes, and governance installations [standard:MOHURD-CONTROL-DETAILED-PLANNING]. ### Industrial goals, functional layout, and innovation indicator system

### Overall renewal framework and policy alignment

The renewal framework organizes objects into four conceptual categories—retain, renovate, renew, reserve: **retain** the Jing-Zhang heritage, the Qinghuayuan Station site (indicative protection area [data:geometry/constraints.geojson#CONSTR-07]) and existing university research buildings; **renovate** low-efficiency industrial spaces and aging community services; **renew** industrial carriers, slow-mobility gaps, and public-space nodes; **reserve** parcels without sufficient conditions (about 195,000 m², pending ownership and regulatory conditions) [depth:retain_renovate_demolish].

Policy alignment reaches document names (conceptual, not commitments): renewal organization follows the direction of the Haidian Urban Renewal Guidelines (2025)—"four-area" integration, the innovation exchange belt, and an unbounded ground floor [source:HD-URBAN-RENEWAL-GUIDE-2025]; retain-renovate-demolish follows the retention-led principle of the Beijing Urban Renewal Regulation [source:BJ-URBAN-RENEWAL-REGULATION]; the HD00-1601 et al. regulatory plan along the corridor has been publicly notified with its acceptance notice issued and is advancing toward approval [source:HD00-1601-CONTROL-NOTICE], and the Lanjinglijia area has formed the first renewal case implementing the plan's height zoning [source:LANJINGLIJIA-CASE]—**this proposal presets no parcel-level intensity conclusions**; FAR [metric:floor_area_ratio], building height, density, green ratio, and setbacks remain pending the approved regulatory plan, with the recomputation trigger pinned to that document.

### Building scale and retain-renovate-demolish

The conceptual building footprint is about 985,000 m² [metric:building_footprint_area_sqm], organized by building type [data:geometry/buildings.geojson#BLDG-001] [depth:height_massing_character]. Retain-renovate-demolish is organized conceptually in four categories: retain (heritage, protected structures, existing university research buildings), renovate (low-efficiency industrial space, aging community services), renew (industrial carriers, public space), and reserve (parcels pending conditions). Because existing stock, ownership, and regulatory conditions are missing, retain-renovate-demolish expresses method and a pending-calibration list, not parcel-level conclusions [depth:retain_renovate_demolish]; implementation follows the retention-led principle of the Beijing Urban Renewal Regulation [source:BJ-URBAN-RENEWAL-REGULATION].

## Transport, Rail, Municipal Infrastructure, and Public Services

### Transport and slow mobility

The proposal organizes "9-km green-corridor slow spine + rail interchange + branch-road microcirculation" (conceptual): Phase 2 already built the "three trails plus green" gapless slow system and opened 9 urban branch roads [source:JZ-PARK-PHASE2-2026]; this proposal adds mile-station interchange nodes and overpass-crossing research directions: K2 Sidaokou stitching station corresponds to the North Third Ring–Sidaokou separated interchange (confirmed by the regulatory plan acceptance notice [source:HD00-1601-CONTROL-NOTICE]), and K3 Zhichun Road scenario station addresses ground-level pedestrian connectivity over the underpass segment (the notice confirms the segment has no at-grade crossing condition [source:HD00-1601-CONTROL-NOTICE]). The conceptual road network is about 28.7 km [metric:road_network_length_m] [data:geometry/roads.geojson#ROAD-001] [depth:traffic_rail_slow_parking]; bicycle parking and static traffic concentrate at station quadrants and public-space nodes.

### Rail-station integration

Wudaokou, Qinghua East Road West (K4 Origin Community), and Dazhongsi (K1) are organized as mile stations for integrated design; Haidian has designated Wudaokou and Dazhongsi as the two pilot areas of the AI innovation district [source:HD-URBAN-RENEWAL-GUIDE-2025], and the station-city direction follows the guidelines' "transit micro-center" requirement. Stations connect by walking networks to the Zero Milepost Plaza, showcase galleries, and roadshow lounges [source:OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT].

### Municipal infrastructure and new infrastructure

AI industrial-service, innovation-platform, and talent-life facilities are conceptually distributed by service radius; the proposal explores an integrated "distributed energy + edge compute + traditional municipal facilities" model (edge-compute station prototype) [depth:municipal_new_infrastructure]. Utility, energy, drainage, flood-control, and fire-safety data are missing and listed as prerequisites for formal deepening; interchange and road engineering directions from the notice are cited conceptually only, with engineering conclusions awaiting professional review.

## Blue-Green Network, Public Space, and Urban Character

### Blue-green network and the Heritage Park vitality belt

With the 9-km Heritage Park vitality belt as the skeleton, the proposal coordinates the Qinghe and Xiaoyuehe blue-green spaces into a north-south continuous, east-west connected pedestrian, cycle, and green-space system [data:geometry/green_space.geojson#GREEN-001] [depth:blue_green_public_space]. Phase 2 already achieved east-west and north-south connectivity with the Qinghe green corridor [source:JZ-PARK-PHASE2-2026]; the submitted geometry recomputes about 2.679 million m² of green space (23.5% [metric:green_ratio]) and about 1.066 million m² of public space (9.3% [metric:public_space_ratio])—design recomputations supporting the "blue-green foundation" judgment, not approved official ratios.

### AI public space and pilgrimage landmarks (no fewer than three)

Four AI pilgrimage landmarks and honor-display nodes are set along the spine (conceptual) [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK]:

1. **Zero Milepost (0.0 km, K4)**: the Origin Community's zero-point installation—the start of innovation mileage and the spiritual origin of "starting from zero" [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#PUBLIC-SPINE-0KM];
2. **Centennial Mile Wall (K0)**: a Jing-Zhang 1909 → Zhongguancun → AI 2026 timeline installation at the Xizhimen gateway, making a century of independent innovation walkable [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#PUBLIC-SPINE-K2];
3. **Contributor Milepost digital installation (K5/K8)**: linked to the Digital Milepost Protocol—when an open-source project, model, or scenario test reaches a node, one notch lights up and contributor names enter the honor wall, directly echoing the call's "agent contribution honor wall / AI milestones" memorial system [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#PUBLIC-SPINE-K1] [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#PUBLIC-SPINE-K4];
4. **Dazhongsi "intelligent-economy station" urban landmark (K1)**: the International Roadshow Lounge and four-quadrant plaza as a window to the world [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#PUBLIC-001].

The landmark count satisfies the requirement of no fewer than three [metric:ai_landmark_count]. All installations, logos, fonts, images, and personal/business identities require cleared rights; conceptual landmarks are not presented as approved construction.

### One-day route: walking a century along nine kilometers

**Milepost walking line** (conceptual): start at K0 Xizhimen gateway in the morning—read the Jing-Zhang 1909 at the Centennial Mile Wall; walk north to K1 Dazhongsi, watch intelligent-terminal launches at the intelligent-economy station and lunch in the four-quadrant lounge; experience smart crossing (JZ-01 pilot) at K2 Sidaokou stitching station; see the AI life model street at K3 Zhichun Road; reach K4 Wudaokou in the afternoon—scan to light your first contribution at the Zero Milepost, read strangers' PRs in the open-source gallery; turn north through K5 Qinghua East Road and the K6 Liudaokou campus segment; arrive at K7 Xuezhayuan full-stack testing ground in the evening and watch red-team testing run transparently from the outer ring of the platform; finally close at K8 North Fifth Ring honor station—under the canopy of the Jianqiao "Jing-Zhang Ring" 1909 plaza (completed [source:JZ-PARK-PHASE2-2026]) and the Qinghe green wedge, completing the "summit" from city gateway to ecological highland. About 9 km in total; roughly two hours on foot, a full Milepost Festival experience about one day. The corridor's main ridge is largely walkable today; heritage branches, station interchanges, and overpass crossings still require site surveys—this proposal does not claim end-to-end connectivity today.

### Urban character

The urban tone fuses three cultural temperaments (conceptual): the engineering rationality of the Jing-Zhang Railway (rust red, stone, rail symbols), the innovation sharpness of Zhongguancun (glass curtain walls, luminous interfaces, stone grey), and the code order of AI culture (modular, editable, digital display). Heritage resources such as the Qinghuayuan Station site are displayed with low disturbance; Qinghe and Xiaoyuehe waterfronts shape livable interfaces; renewal-capable areas receive guiding directions for height, intensity, character, roof form, massing, and interfaces—specific control values pending approval of the HD00-1601 et al. regulatory plan and heritage conditions [standard:MOHURD-URBAN-DESIGN-MEASURES] [source:HD00-1601-CONTROL-NOTICE].
