Personas

Five core personas: open-source developer, early-stage team, leading enterprise visitor, local resident, faculty and student.

5 core personas

Open-source developer

Need: Publish, collaborate, test, community reputation

Spatial response: Open-source hall, AI Governance Plaza, night co-working

Privacy / review: Aggregate statistics; no personal trajectory

Early-stage team

Need: Low-cost office, compute entry, product test

Spatial response: Zhongzhiyuan shared test ground, edge-compute kiosk

Privacy / review: Compute and data need consent

Leading enterprise visitor

Need: Showcase, business, international reception, recruitment

Spatial response: Dazhongsi international roadshow living room, station

Privacy / review: Enterprise marks need clearance

Local resident

Need: Commute, leisure, community service, low-disturbance renewal

Spatial response: Main spine slow mobility, community service, night grading

Privacy / review: No commercial profiling

Faculty and student

Need: Translation, cross-campus collaboration, daily slow mobility

Spatial response: Campus / park stitch, AI education experience node

Privacy / review: Campus data and academic imagery need clearance
