JING-ZHANG CIVIC FOUNDRY

AN AI CIVIC-PRODUCT CONVERSION LINE CO-AUTHORED BY THE CITY

From railway capacity to civic conversion capacity

This is not another AI park inserted into a park. It turns an existing, roughly nine-kilometre green corridor into a continuous civic-production interface without sacrificing ecological quiet, ordinary life or free public access.

The existing park, railway memory and daily life of approximately 450,000 people are the starting assets—not a blank map awaiting technology.

FIVE DESIGN VETOES

Five canonical books become five design vetoes

Theory is not decoration. It removes construction that does not deserve to exist. An AI mechanism earns space only after all five tests pass.

Jacobs | Street life

No project may displace daily passage, small businesses, neighbourhood contact or active ground-floor guardianship.

Lynch | City image

If ordinary users cannot explain it as One Line, Three Grounds and Nine Rooms, no new hierarchy is added.

McHarg | Suitability

No fixed technology before water, soil, tree, heat, sound and habitat overlays are verified.

Gehl | Human scale

Draw the five-minute walk, pause, shade and staffed help before architectural image.

Alexander | Patterns

Build only reusable, correctable spatial prototypes that can start small and exit.

ONE LINE · THREE GROUNDS · NINE ROOMS

A spatial frame people can understand and walk

Zhongzhiyuan | Quiet–Test–Observe–Exit

Low public exposure, high technical threshold; a complete bypass precedes controlled testing.

AI Origin | Street–Porch–Court–Workshop

Move from everyday street to commission porch and free court, then voluntarily into co-production.

Dazhongsi | Station–Street–Ground–Hall

Connect transit to neighbourhood ground floors and everyday steps before a low-risk premiere hall.

ECOLOGICAL SUITABILITY + PUBLIC LIFE

Not every place needs to be activated

The line follows a quiet–active–quiet rhythm. Sensitive or heavily used places remain quiet; missing everyday infrastructure is repaired first; only hardened, accessible, maintainable interfaces with a passed accountability chain admit reversible tests.
