PUBLIC-INTEREST SAFEGUARDS

01

Free Basic Service

Access, rest, explanation, and help do not require purchase, registration, or data consent.

02

Non-Digital Route

People without a phone, account, or smart device can complete the same public task.

03

Human Takeover

Staff can immediately take over after failure, misclassification, or refusal of automation.

04

Field-Tested Accessibility

Real users test routes, interaction, queues, and information; declarations are insufficient.

05

Quiet / No-Scan Bypass

People can avoid cameras and interaction while retaining continuous access.

06

Complaint-Response-Restoration

Every service records responsible function, response, stop condition, and spatial restoration.
