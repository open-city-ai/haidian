International Design Solicitation · Centennial Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Corridor · Concept Proposal

Jingzhang TrifoldRail · Academy · Intelligence Meridian

Three times in one century, this corridor became the national debut site of a general-purpose-technology infrastructure: the Rail Layer (1909, the first Chinese-built trunk railway, moving people and goods), the Academy Layer (1952, the “Eight Colleges” of Xueyuan Road and Zhongguancun, nurturing people and ideas), and the Intelligence Meridian Layer (today's AI and the next general-purpose technologies, computing data and intelligence). The concept folds the three debuts into one walkable, readable, ever-growing urban innovation belt, structured as One Spine, Two Corridors, Three Harbors and Nine Mileposts.

Concept: The Trifold铁轨 · 学路 · 智脉

For developers this is an innovation corridor; for ordinary people it is the park on the commute, a laboratory for children, a memory hall for parents, and the lamp that stays on late at night. AI stays behind the scenes; people stand in front.

Rail Layer铁轨层 · 1909

The first Chinese-built trunk railway departed from here; Zhan Tianyou's herringbone switchback conquered the Qinglongqiao grade. A logistics base built by railway workers — carried spatially by the spine greenway and the heritage alignment.

Academy Layer学路层 · 1952

The 1952 reorganization raised the “Eight Colleges” along Xueyuan Road and the CAS institutes of Zhongguancun — the knowledge base of a republic. A layer of scholars and readers, carried by the Academy Corridor.

Intelligence Meridian智脉层 · 2020s

Dazhongsi–Zhichunlu–Wudaokou hosts China's densest cluster of AI companies. The layer of dreamers — with spatial mechanisms (reserved land ≈5%) kept open for whatever general-purpose technology comes after AI.

Three-Level Scope三层范围

Coordination study scope · ~43.6 km²

Bounded by North 5th Ring Road, Jingzang Expressway, Xizhimenwai Street and Wanquanhe Road. It answers “why here”: the trifold chain holds at regional scale, making the belt a north–south history–innovation axis. Strategy and research only — no spatial siting conclusions.

Overall design scope · ≈ 11.41 km² (computed)

The urban and industrial areas within 1–2 km of the heritage park, designed to regulatory-detailed-plan urban-design depth; the layer where the spine–corridors–harbors–mileposts structure lands and where all geometry layers apply.

Key-area scope · ~368.4 ha

North to south: Zhongzhiyuan AI Acceleration Area, Beijing AI Origin Community, Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster — the “North, Middle and South Harbors”, each at implementation-plan depth. All three polygons are provisional rough rectangles.

Key Areas: Three Harbors重点区域

SouthDazhongsi AI Cluster · ≈ 72.0 ha

Narrative home of the Rail Layer — the “departure hall”. One plaza, one installation, one market: a city living-room plaza tied to the Dazhongsi metro hub; the “New Bell” light-and-sound installation where the Yongle Bell's voiceprint converses with AI city soundscapes; an open-source market and unmanned-retail test street. Renewal proceeds by negotiated micro-renewal.

MiddleBeijing AI Origin Community · ≈ 104.3 ha

Narrative home of the Academy Layer — the corridor's “kilometre zero”. One origin, one bridge, one trail: the K0 Origin Plaza folds the railway's milepost zero into the AI origin; the Herringbone Bridge stitches the two sides of the spine — “wherever technology switches back, human wisdom is at work”; the Academician Trail tells the stories of the Eight Colleges and Zhongguancun scientists in AR.

NorthZhongzhiyuan Acceleration Area · ≈ 192.9 ha

Narrative home of the Intelligence Meridian — the “acceleration harbor”. One field, one gate, one reserve: a reconfigurable test field hosting robot staging and edge-computing pilots; the K9 Gate of the Future closing the corridor's north end; ~5% reserved land keeping spatial mechanisms open for embodied AI, synthetic biology or quantum computing.

Mobility交通慢行

A slow-mobility-first skeleton

  • The spine greenway runs ~9.6 km north–south, linking all K0–K9 milepost nodes;
  • Twenty-one “stitching streets” cross the corridor east–west at ~500 m spacing, healing a century of railway severance (seven double as secondary vehicular streets; the rest are pedestrian/cycling only);
  • The Xiaoyue River cycleway forms the second north–south slow line. Total road centerline length ≈ 80.31 km (computed on concept alignments).

Rail connections, parking and micro-circulation

  • Each harbor gets one conceptual rail-connection corridor towards Dazhongsi, Wudaokou–Qinghua East Rd West, and Qinghe stations;
  • Vehicle arrival and parking are organized at the harbors' edges; within 200 m of the spine, slow mobility takes priority;
  • Existing local streets are optimized as paired one-way and time-shared schemes — directional suggestions only, no engineering conclusions.

Blue-Green Public Space蓝绿公共空间

Blue-green skeleton and a three-level public space system

The ~120 m-wide spine park and the Xiaoyue River corridor form one longitudinal and one transverse armature, plus ten K-node pocket parks and twenty-one station-park plazas — an “avenue–park–plaza” hierarchy. Standalone green-layer coverage ≈ 20.2% (≈230.92万 m²); 34 standalone plazas and public open spaces total ≈23.89万 m², a public-space ratio of ≈ 2.1% — supporting the concept goal of one stayable public place within every 500 m walk.

Four AI pilgrimage landmarks & honors

  • K0 Origin Plaza (Middle Harbor): paving engraved to the 1435 mm standard gauge;
  • Herringbone Bridge & Qinghuayuan Old Station Memory Hall (near K2): sleepers and spikes re-designed as street furniture;
  • “New Bell” light-and-sound installation (South Harbor): Yongle Bell voiceprint × AI soundscape — the corridor's sonic landmark;
  • K9 Gate of the Future (north end): a nightly light pulse pointing to “the next corridor”.

The “Trifold walk of fame”: railway builders on the Rail Layer, scholars on the Academy Layer, and an annual open-source contributor plaque on the Intelligence Meridian.

Buildings: Retain / Retrofit / New建筑

Concept volumes (design quantities, low confidence)

The building layer places 784 conceptual footprints totalling ≈199.47万 m² — an overall footprint ratio of ≈17.5%. Of these: 121 retained, 104 retrofitted, 416 concept-new (some parcels combined). These are design quantities expressing spatial intent, not statutory building volumes.

Retention logic (directional)

  • Retain: all existing communities, universities, research compounds and heritage points;
  • Retrofit: inefficient factories and campus edges along the corridors — functional swap and interface opening;
  • New: a few landmark public buildings and test facilities inside the three harbors, all within concept parcels;
  • Reserve: the North Harbor interface land for the “fourth wave”, function undefined for now.

Floor area ratio, building height and density controls all remain pending official data until regulatory planning conditions are released; this proposal makes no statutory planning conclusion.
