# Jing-Zhang Evidence Line

> **Public Co-Creation Boundary Statement**: This proposal is a Conceptual Recommendation and reference scheme generated by the AI Agent, intended for further professional development and research. It does not substitute for formal planning, nor does it constitute a government review, approval, investment, or implementation commitment. The overall scope and three key areas use provisional polygons; they are not official planning boundaries and do not support precise area, ownership, or engineering determinations. (Official Planning Boundary)

Co-Verification" is not about placing more sensors in the city, but about ensuring that every AI urban scenario can answer five questions: what the claim is, where it is tested, who will review it, how the evidence will be made public, and how to exit safely in case of failure. The plan is based on the historical spirit of the Jing-Zhang Railway's "engineering is verifiable" as a cultural theme, forming **a co-verification main thread, three verification pods, and two wings of resource networks**. Zhongzhiyuan verifies the model and system, AI Origin community verifies public services and the conversion of results, Dazhongsi verifies intelligent commerce and public experience; the Zhongguancun Technology Services Wing and the Xiaoyue River Scenario Enablement Wing provide professional services and real-world scenarios. Each pilot follows the process of "proposing a claim—sandbox testing—Human Review—public evidence—gradual implementation/safe exit.

## Coordinated Research Area: Industry and Future City Research

The Path of Co-Verification will translate the three positioning statements into three operational capabilities: the Jing-Zhang Cultural Belt provides "engineering evidence and public memory," the Urban AI Living Experience Belt provides "human-machine collaboration and the right to exit," and the AI Fusion Innovation Belt provides "inter-institutional testing and transferable rules." The five functional areas are not fragmented by plot boundaries but are connected by a flow of evidence: full-stack independent innovation generates candidate systems, an innovation ecosystem provides professional services, AI+ scenarios enter sandbox environments, vibrant cities generate public feedback, and governance authority settles into open rules.

The collaborative loop of the Three Zones and Two Wings is as follows: Zhongzhiyuan outputs test protocols and security reports; Origin Community outputs user feedback and public service evaluations; Dazhongsi outputs market availability and public experience; Zhongguancun Technology Services Wing provides intellectual property, compliance, talent, and capital services; Xiaoyue River Scenario Enablement Wing provides low-disturbance real environments. The data fed back is retained only with the minimum necessary and is prioritized for aggregation, with the decision on expanding the pilot determined by a human responsible subject. [data:geometry/constraints.geojson#SCN-01] [depth:overall_spatial_structure]

Logo direction adopts an original geometric symbol combining "crosstie + verification hook + open parenthesis"; Chinese text uses system sans-serif font, with colors deep  gray, signal red, verification cyan, and paper white. The naming hierarchy is "Common Verification Path (overall brand) — Verification Pod (key area) — Evidence Station (scene node) — Common Verification Season (annual event)," avoiding the use of corporate trademarks.

Global case studies only extract public mechanisms, not copying images or brands: Toronto Sidewalk Labs' governance controversy highlights the need for "defining data rights" first; Helsinki AI Register highlights the importance of transparent system purposes; Amsterdam Algorithm Register emphasizes algorithmic transparency; UK Regulatory Sandbox underscores time-limited, geographically restricted, and opt-out conditions; Singapore Open Innovation Platform emphasizes demand matching; Boston New Urban Mechanics advocates for small-scale city experiments; and Barcelona Decidim emphasizes public agenda setting and traceable deliberations. Transformable mechanisms include public registries, pilot contracts, human accountability, independent evaluations, exit conditions, and portable interfaces, rather than replicating institutional models.

## Overall Design Area: Urban Renewal and Regulatory-Plan-Level Urban Design

The spatial structure consists of a north-south "Verification Main Axis," three "Verification Pods," four east-west public interfaces, and wing connections. The site uses six types of verifiable codes to complete seamless zoning within the provisional boundary; the building envelopes are expressed through nine conditional prototypes, without making a judgment on the demolition of existing buildings. [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-001] [data:geometry/buildings.geojson#BLDG-001] [depth:land_use_layout]

Urban Renewal methods are divided into four categories: prioritize retaining applicable spaces after confirming the current conditions; adaptively update through first-floor openness, shared meeting spaces, and barrier-free renovations; embed lightweight evidence stations when ownership and control plan allow; and only discuss reconstruction after completing professional evaluations, public participation, and legal procedures. Due to the lack of current buildings, ownership, and official control plans, this plan does not output specific conclusions on the demolish–renovate–retain strategy, Floor Area Ratio, height, or development scale for individual parcels. [depth:retain_renovate_demolish] [depth:development_intensity_controls] [depth:height_massing_character] (Demolish–Renovate–Retain Strategy)

Urban Design recommendations focus on "readable structure" rather than futuristic forms: railway-scale materials are used for the ground and signage, with verification green indicating auditable services, signal red only marking risks, Human Review points, and exit buttons; new constructions are to remain lightweight, reversible, and non-obstructive to cultural resources. Building massing, roofs, and heights must await formal zoning, cultural preservation, and urban design conditions.

Pursue a garden-type innovation district approach, forming a sequence of "indoor evaluation—semi-open validation—public explanation" with the evaluation hall, standard governance workshop, and open evaluation garden. Educational, health, accessibility, and community issue scenarios are retained for final judgment by teachers, healthcare professionals, users and inspectors, residents' deliberative councils; no service shall be conditional on personal trajectory or non-removable profile. Organize the urban-type portal with track transfers, quadrants of slow mobility connections, smart commercial atriums, and consensus squares. Edge management, business compliance, and transparent recommendations are to be treated as time-limited and location-specific sandboxes: changes are reviewed by traffic dispatchers, compliance suggestions are verified by professionals, users can opt out of recommendations and file appeals. Site and road engineering must await formal redlines, passenger flow data, pipeline information, and traffic organization details. ## Risk, Copyright, and Compliance

Main risks are handled according to the principle of "downgrading first, then supplementing": the Official Planning Boundary and key areas are missing, and all spatial conclusions are kept provisional; control plans, roads, parcels, buildings, cultural relics, municipal, and public service baseline data are missing, and related conclusions are kept as Conceptual Recommendation or unknown. [data:geometry/constraints.geojson#SCN-12] [depth:risk_missing_data]

Scenarios risks are managed through data minimization, purpose limitation, default non-tracking, opt-out options, Human Review, version logging, and independent evaluation. Any personal medical, educational, travel, or community feedback should not be publicly disclosed in the submission package; any automated outputs cannot replace diagnosis, teaching, planning approvals, traffic scheduling, or public decision-making.

The main text, structured data, graphics, offline HTML, and PDF of this package are generated locally by Codex; the graphics are original technical illustrations, the fonts call system fonts, and there are no remote images, commercial map tiles, corporate logos, portraits, or tracking codes.
