## Source and data boundaries

- Organizer-supplied provisional boundaries remain attributed to the repository source package and are marked `official_boundary=false`; they are not redistributed as official redlines.
- OSM and GitHub issues are cited as background or public feedback only. No OSM-derived geometry is published as a replacement boundary.
- Six global institutional websites are used for mechanism background. No local investment, enterprise, workforce, revenue or partnership claim is inferred.

## Generated assets

- Five Chinese and five English PNG figures are generated from the submitted geometry and original text with Pillow.
- The four PDFs are raster presentation outputs assembled locally with ReportLab; they are not editable CAD, statutory drawings or an approval set.
- HTML is offline, uses system font fallbacks, and contains no remote resource or active network call.
- The raster renderer uses local system fonts at generation time; font files are not distributed in this submission.

## Required professional clearance

Before any public construction or service pilot, the authorized organizer and professional team must confirm official geometry, station and road anchors, heritage and accessibility constraints, rights clearance, field evidence, participation consent, privacy retention, insurance, procurement, maintenance and statutory approvals. A merge or gallery state is not that clearance.
