# 摘要 / Narrative Summary

## 中文摘要

「京张智脉」方案以百年京张铁路的自主创新精神为叙事原点，把 11.4 平方公里的总体设计范围组织为"一带一轴、三核两翼、多点网络"的空间结构：智脉绿道贯通南北、学院路—知春路创新策源轴串联东西，众智园（智核·源）、AI原点社区（智核·原点）、大钟寺（智核·汇）三处重点区域承载全栈自主、成果转化与智能经济功能，中关村科技服务翼与小月河场景赋能翼以概念界面延伸协同。方案提出 12 张 AI 场景卡（含 3 个产业测试验证场景）、7 类用户画像、4 个 AI 朝圣地标、"自主创新三部曲"文化叙事与全球 AI 活动体系，全部为概念建议。指标由提交几何在 EPSG:4548 复算；控规、权属、市政与文保条件缺失时统一标记待确认。

## English Summary

The "Jing-Zhang Intelligence Spine" proposal takes the century-old Jing-Zhang railway's spirit of self-reliance as its narrative origin and organizes the 11.4 km² overall design area into a "one belt, one axis, three cores, two wings, multi-node network" structure: the Spine Greenway running north-south, the Xueyuan–Zhichun innovation sourcing axis running east-west, three key areas — Zhongzhiyuan (Core·Source), the AI Origin Community (Core·Origin), and Dazhongsi (Core·Confluence) — carrying full-stack autonomy, conversion, and intelligent-economy functions, with the Zhongguancun service wing and the Xiaoyuehe scenario wing extending as conceptual interfaces. The proposal provides 12 AI scenario cards (including 3 industry testing-validation scenarios), 7 user personas, 4 AI pilgrimage landmarks, a "three chapters of self-reliance" cultural narrative, and a global AI events system — all as conceptual suggestions. Metrics are recomputed from the submitted geometry in EPSG:4548; regulatory, ownership, utility, and heritage conditions are marked to-be-confirmed.
