SAFETY MUST BE ABLE TO STOP

The Safe Charge Line does not draw another set of cabinets. It connects parking, tariffs, status, fault response, local shutdown and battery retirement into an auditable responsibility contract. Individuals use and report; professional operators own product and operating safety; public bodies coordinate space and oversight.

03 / RESPONSIBILITY

FOUR ENTRY GATES

Current-state truth → written responsibility → professional compliance → stoppable operation. AI may flag conflicts, aggregate and assist tickets; local protection and human authority remain supreme.

STOP GATES

  1. Operator or safety lead is missing
  2. No safe local shutdown after network loss
  3. Opaque tariff or misleading on-site status
  4. No isolation and retirement chain for abnormal batteries
04 / 90 DAYS

FALSIFY BEFORE REPLICATION

Days 0–15 audit current conditions and actors. 16–30 add fixed information and synthetic fault rehearsals. 31–60 allow limited validation only after hard gates. 61–90 publish aggregates and retain, revise or exit.
