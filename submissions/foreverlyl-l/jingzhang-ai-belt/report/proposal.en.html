Overall Concept: SIGNAL JINGZHANG

A century ago, the Jing-Zhang Railway guaranteed safe operation of China's first self-built mainline railway through its self-developed signaling, block and dispatching system. Today, the AI Innovation Belt needs an equally rigorous "urban signaling system" to answer "who dispatches AI" — every AI service entering public space should obtain a traceable operating license at a signal box, disclosing its purpose, data, responsible party, human-review point and exit mechanism 来源 深度.

The proposal takes "signaling" as its first-principle concept with three translations:

  • INTERLOCK: binding AI services to human responsibility. No AI service without a human review point may enter public space, echoing the taskbook principles of human-centered governance and final human judgment 来源.
  • BLOCK: dividing the ~9.7 km green spine into signal blocks (block sections), each permitting only AI services matching its function, preventing scenario sprawl and accountability vacuums.
  • DISPATCH: the three signal boxes dispatch at three levels — Zhongzhiyuan dispatches training and evaluation, the Origin Community dispatches human-AI public life, and Dazhongsi dispatches applications and new business forms.
