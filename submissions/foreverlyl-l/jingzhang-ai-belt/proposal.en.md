Official regulatory-control indicators (FAR, building height, density, green ratio, setbacks) are absent from the public package and are treated as data gaps, not pseudo-precise values [depth:existing_conditions_diagnosis].

## Three-Level Scope Framework

The proposal follows the three announced levels: the coordinated research area (43.6 km²) answers the AI industry ecosystem and future urban form; the overall design area (11.4 km²) grounds these judgments into an urban renewal framework, industrial spatial layout, mobility and municipal support, and urban character; the key detailed-design area (368.4 ha) completes detailed design for Zhongzhiyuan, the Beijing AI Origin Community and Dazhongsi [source:OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT] [metric:site_area_sqm] [metric:key_area_count]; the three-level and key-area areas are verifiable from the announcement values and submitted geometry [metric:research_area_sqm] [metric:official_site_area_sqm] [metric:key_detailed_area_sqm].

The three levels are not separate drawings: research determines the industry-chain and urban-form judgment, overall design turns it into renewal projects, spatial structure and facility capacity, and key areas test plot-level, building, mobility, public space and AI scenario feasibility. Any area, ratio, scale or project count that cannot be recomputed from structured data is not written as a formal conclusion; provisional-boundary-affected conclusions are labeled in the narrative and in `assumptions.json` [source:SITE-PACKAGE] [depth:three_level_scope_framework].

## Overall Concept: SIGNAL JINGZHANG

A century ago, the Jing-Zhang Railway guaranteed safe operation of China's first self-built mainline railway through its self-developed **signaling, block and dispatching system**. Today, the AI Innovation Belt needs an equally rigorous "urban signaling system" to answer **"who dispatches AI"** — every AI service entering public space should obtain a traceable operating license at a signal box, disclosing its purpose, data, responsible party, human-review point and exit mechanism [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK] [depth:overall_spatial_structure].

The proposal takes "signaling" as its first-principle concept with three translations:

- **INTERLOCK**: binding AI services to human responsibility. No AI service without a human review point may enter public space, echoing the taskbook principles of human-centered governance and final human judgment [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK].
- **BLOCK**: dividing the ~9.7 km green spine into signal blocks (block sections), each permitting only AI services matching its function, preventing scenario sprawl and accountability vacuums.
- **DISPATCH**: the three signal boxes dispatch at three levels — Zhongzhiyuan dispatches training and evaluation, the Origin Community dispatches human-AI public life, and Dazhongsi dispatches applications and new business forms.

Mapping to the five announced functions [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK]:

| Announced function | Signal-band translation | Spatial anchor |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AI full-stack self-reliant innovation system | Training Signal Box: compute-model-evaluation-standard interlock | Zhongzhiyuan |
| World-class AI innovation ecosystem | Origin Signal Box: open-source collaboration and commercialization | Beijing AI Origin Community |
| New AI+ scenario-empowerment paradigm | Application Signal Box: scenario admission and test validation | Dazhongsi |
| Intelligent vibrant AI city | Signal Green Spine: AI experience and oversight in public space | Jing-Zhang Signal Green Spine |
| Global voice in AI governance | Three-aspect signaling mechanism: visible, askable, stoppable, reversible | Three signal boxes and switch nodes |

**Naming and visual identity (agent.1)**: main name "京张信号带" / "SIGNAL JINGZHANG"; community-level naming as the "signal box" series (Training / Origin / Application), and node naming using railway vocabulary (switch, station yard, block section). The logo direction uses "three-aspect signal light + track switch" as the graphic motif, with signal green/yellow/red and rail gray as palette, sans-serif Chinese plus monospace English as typography, forming a public visual language of "signaling as governance" [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK] [standard:PROJECT-AGENT-OPEN-CALL-TASKBOOK].

## Three Areas, Two Wings and Regional Collaboration

Building on "one line, three signal boxes", the proposal defines the "three areas, two wings" spatial-mechanism loop (agent.1 functional coordination and announcement task 1.5.1) [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK]:

- **West Wing · Zhongguancun Technology-Service Wing**: leveraging technology services, venture capital and professional services along Zhongguancun Avenue, forming a "commercialization-financing-review" loop with the Origin Signal Box: incubated projects enter pilot and financing channels after public review at the signal box.
- **East Wing · Xiaoyuehe Scenario-Empowerment Wing**: leveraging AI+ consumption and AI+ cultural-tourism resources along the Xiaoyue River, forming a "test-operation" loop with the Application Signal Box: smart-terminal and data-element scenarios connect eastward to real consumption and cultural-tourism flows.

**Innovation-chain organization**: a chain framework of "university ideation → open-source collaboration → enterprise commercialization → public experience → international dissemination", coupled with Haidian universities and institutes, leading enterprises, compute/algorithm/data elements, incubators and technology services. Industry strategy indicators, AI innovation index, talent density and spatial supply types are recorded in `metrics.json` with official values distinguished from design suggestions [source:OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT] [metric:ai_ecosystem_case_count].

**Global voice in AI governance**: the signal band treats the "AI service licensing system" as an exportable public good of urban governance, proposing a "signal white paper + interlock protocol + open review" mechanism to support Beijing hosting an international AI-governance agenda in Haidian [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK] [standard:PROJECT-AGENT-OPEN-CALL-TASKBOOK].

## Overall Design Area: Urban Renewal and Regulatory-Plan-Level Urban Design

The overall design area proposes the "**one line, three signal boxes, switches, two wings**" spatial structure [depth:overall_spatial_structure]:

- **One Line (MAINLINE)**: the Jing-Zhang Signal Green Spine, a north-south conceptual public main axis (about 9.7 km, recomputed from green-space geometry in EPSG:4548, see the recalculation log in `metrics.json` [metric:spine_length_m]), with slow-traffic priority, heritage narrative, AI experience and three-aspect signaling oversight facilities, expressed as the green belt in `geometry/green_space.geojson` [data:geometry/green_space.geojson#GRN-001].
- **Three Signal Boxes**: Zhongzhiyuan (Training), Beijing AI Origin Community (Origin), Dazhongsi (Application), carried by the three key areas in `geometry/key_areas.geojson`, with conceptual renewal building footprints in `geometry/buildings.geojson` [data:geometry/buildings.geojson#BLDG-001].
- **Switches**: three east-west stitching lines (south/mid/north) carrying data-flow, scenario-test and ethics-governance interfaces, expressed as conceptual roads in `geometry/roads.geojson` [data:geometry/roads.geojson#ROAD-003].
- **Two Wings**: the west Zhongguancun technology-service wing and the east Xiaoyuehe scenario-empowerment wing (see industry section and `geometry/phasing.geojson`).

**Land-use structure**: research, education, culture-commerce, residential and public-service strips are organized along both sides of the green spine, with training clusters, an open-source innovation quarter and a smart-terminal consumption zone at the three key areas, see `geometry/land_use.geojson` [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-001]. Development intensity and building height are labeled "pending official regulatory-plan conditions" because official controls are missing, and are never presented as approved values [standard:MOHURD-CONTROL-DETAILED-PLANNING].

**Retain/renovate/demolish/new-build**: the principle is "retain railway heritage memory, renovate low-efficiency industrial space, add AI innovation carriers". Retained: the heritage park belt and historic buildings (HERITAGE_PROTECTION layer in `geometry/constraints.geojson` [data:geometry/constraints.geojson#CON-001]); renovated: low-efficiency land in the three key areas (conceptual footprints in `geometry/buildings.geojson`); new: switch stitching nodes, signal-box squares and public space [standard:MOHURD-URBAN-DESIGN-MEASURES].

## Detailed Design of Key Areas

The three key areas are developed at detailed design depth, covering plots, buildings, mobility, public space and AI scenarios [depth:three_key_area_detailed_design].

### Training Signal Box · Zhongzhiyuan AI Self-Reliant Innovation Acceleration Area (192.1 ha)

Around national AI platforms and full-stack self-reliant innovation: compute-training clusters, a model-evaluation center and AI standards & safety laboratories form a "train-evaluate-license" interlock chain; the "Zhongzhi Signal Tower" is proposed as an AI pilgrimage landmark — reflecting the sky by day and publicly displaying training/evaluation status as three-color light beams by night; a low-carbon green innovation milieu is created with Qinghe culture [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-001] [standard:MOHURD-URBAN-DESIGN-MEASURES] [metric:building_count]. External access uses the north boundary connector and integrated rail-station links.

### Origin Signal Box · Beijing AI Origin Community (104.3 ha)

Around near-campus innovation and commercialization: an open-source code square, incubator streets, an outcome showcase hall and talent apartments; the Qinghuayuan Station site is renovated into the "Jing-Zhang Origin Station · Open-Source Monument Square" pilgrimage landmark as the spiritual origin of open-source culture and Chinese self-reliant innovation; campus-park slow links, rail-station integration and youth-friendly daily services are strengthened [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-002] [standard:MOHURD-ARCH-DESIGN-DEPTH-2016]. Building retain/renovate/new follows the order "retain historic station buildings, renovate low-efficiency buildings, add innovation space".

### Application Signal Box · Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster (72.0 ha)

Around leading enterprises, agents and smart-terminal ecosystems: a smart-terminal experience street, content consumption and data-element flow nodes; the "Dazhongsi AI Bell-Tower Square" pilgrimage landmark uses data-flow soundscapes to recreate the bell-drum imagery; green composite use, Dazhongsi station integration and four-quadrant pedestrian connectivity at the intersection bridge commercial services with AI experience interfaces [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-003] [standard:MOHURD-URBAN-DESIGN-MEASURES].

**Building scale**: 14 conceptual building footprints totaling about 406,000 m², concentrated in the three key areas, as "conceptual renewal footprint illustration", not approved construction scale [metric:building_footprint_area_sqm] [metric:building_count] [metric:building_cover_ratio]; FAR, height, density and setback controls are labeled pending official data [standard:MOHURD-CONTROL-DETAILED-PLANNING] [depth:development_intensity_controls] [depth:height_massing_character]. ## Transport, Rail, Municipal Infrastructure, and Public Services

**Mobility and slow traffic**: a slow-traffic main spine along the Signal Green Spine with three switch stitching lines connecting east-west, forming a "one vertical, three horizontal" conceptual network; the east relies on Xueyuan Road/Xitucheng Road and the west on Dazhongsi East Road/Heqing Road as existing roads; all alignments are conceptual and do not represent road redlines [data:geometry/roads.geojson#ROAD-001] [metric:road_centerline_length_m] [standard:MOHURD-URBAN-DESIGN-MEASURES]; slow traffic, rail integration and parking are developed at professional depth [depth:traffic_rail_slow_parking].

**Rail integration**: connecting Dazhongsi station and the Wudaokou area; key areas adopt station-city integrated design, with transfer guidance incorporated into scenario card 9 [source:OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT].

**Municipal and new infrastructure**: the green spine reserves compute-network ducts, edge-compute waste-heat recovery interfaces and data-element flow channels; the Xiaoyue River system serves as a blue-green base for sponge and ecological purification (conceptual; see the water layer in `geometry/constraints.geojson` [data:geometry/constraints.geojson#CON-003]). Public service facilities are configured for the five personas — youth apartments, childcare, medical, sports and community services — with detailed standards pending official facility-support evidence [depth:municipal_new_infrastructure].

## Blue-Green Network, Public Space, and Urban Character

The Jing-Zhang Signal Green Spine is the main blue-green skeleton, linking the three signal-box squares and switch station yards into a "one spine, three squares" public-space system [depth:blue_green_public_space] [metric:green_space_area_sqm] [metric:public_space_area_sqm], with the public-space ratio verifiable by metric recalculation [metric:public_space_ratio]. Signal-box squares use three-aspect signaling installations and public screens to disclose the status of AI services; station yards and pocket parks provide daily encounter and youth-friendly activity space [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#PS-001]. The urban character uses "rail gray + signal three-color" as the tone, controlling street-front interfaces, building height and materials to form a recognizable city identity [standard:MOHURD-URBAN-DESIGN-MEASURES] [depth:height_massing_character].
