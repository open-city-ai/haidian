Overview · 总览地图

Anchored on Zhan Tianyou's 1905 "renzi" railway, this proposal introduces the JING-ZHANG RENZI INNOVATION BELT: stroke 1 is the Centennial Jing-Zhang Cultural Belt, stroke 2 the AI Convergence Innovation Belt, intersecting at the Beijing AI Origin Community. It forms a "one belt, three cores, two wings, multi-scenario, blue-green slow ring" structure. All spatial conclusions are conceptual proposals pending official boundary and regulatory confirmation.
