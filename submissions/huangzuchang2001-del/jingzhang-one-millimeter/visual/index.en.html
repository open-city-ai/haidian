OPEN CALL FOR THE CENTENNIAL JING-ZHANG AI INNOVATION BELT

JING-ZHANG ONE MILLIMETER

A nine-kilometre railway heritage belt that calibrates AI innovation through public evidence

Minimum change × visible evidence × human responsibility = sustainable urban innovation

01 / CONCEPT

Do not place AI in a park; expose technology to urban review

Railway culture supplies common calibration, Zhongguancun supplies reproducible experimentation, and AI governance supplies human accountability. The key parcels contain no existing surface-water system, so the design concentrates on heritage, continuous movement, shade, open ground floors, and reversible testing instead of inventing rivers or landscape pools.

Calibration: one shared language

Project IDs, versions, evidence, and evaluation follow work from laboratory to street. Limits, maintenance cost, and failures travel with the result.

Interface: a public baseline

Accessibility, non-digital service, informed withdrawal, professional authority, and heritage safety precede automation. AI remains switchable.

Cycle: evidence through small steps

Use 30 days for responsibility and data, 90 days for use and maintenance, and 365 days for public review. Each round tests one critical judgment.

Site fact: dry design

Trees, drought-tolerant planting, breathable paving, removable seats, and ordinary drainage construction form the public realm. No water court, wetland, canal, or pond is proposed.

02 / ECOSYSTEM

Move from clustering companies to clustering evidence

The innovation chain is not a recruitment list. Seven gates reduce distinct risks: public problem, basic research, shared technology, scenario validation, first use, capital readiness, and cross-city reuse.

Responsibilities of Three Zones and Two Wings

Zhongzhiyuan validates, AI Origin translates, and Dazhongsi amplifies. The two wings supply professional services and public problems.

Capital follows evidence

Complete 30/90-day testing, first-user acceptance, and maintenance cost before preparing IP, compliance, market, and finance records.

A project may stop

Every gate allows return or exit with a published reason. Failure becomes public knowledge rather than disappearing after a launch.

03 / FUTURE LIFE

Improve space and service before automating

Healthcare, education, and commerce are user journeys rather than technology booths. Each combines a physical setting, human gate, 90-day evidence, and stop condition.

AI + Healthcare: access and referral

AI Origin provides an accessible route, quiet waiting, privacy notice, paper directions, and staffed advice. AI neither reads medical records nor diagnoses.

AI + Education: the district as curriculum

Qinghe material bays, railway construction, shade performance, accessibility, and everyday maintenance become lessons. Teachers and curators approve core content.

AI + Commerce: trust before transaction

Dazhongsi hosts opt-out product trials. Legal, accounting, and IP professionals retain sign-off in the Technology Services Wing.

04 / PUBLIC SPACE

Public space is the first boundary of AI governance

The 38 m section is not a uniform boundary. It checks whether accessibility, cycling, heritage, dry shade, events, and public ground floors can coexist. Cross-links then connect the spine with transit, communities, schools, research institutions, and existing commercial streets.

Priority in narrow areas

Protect accessibility and heritage first, organise cycling, trees, and rest second, and add events or digital equipment last.

Three hubs

Qinghe produces evidence along a 180 m dry test strip, AI Origin translates it along 120 m, and Dazhongsi tests it publicly along 90 m.

Eight reversible components

Every gate, sign, material bay, shade seat, table, frame, review desk, and power cabinet has installation, maintenance, and removal rules.

05 / CULTURE

Write three cultures as six walkable actions

Railway culture supplies measurement, connection, and maintenance; Zhongguancun supplies experimentation, translation, and collaboration; AI culture requires openness, explanation, and human responsibility.

Six-stop route

Calibration, connection, testing, opening, review, and return to life advance through spatial actions rather than a stack of history panels.

The gallery displays responsibility

Every work states its licence, operating conditions, maintainer, known defects, reuse record, and stopped status.

The wall avoids ranking

It recognises problem clarification, merged versions, independent validation, and long maintenance; every entry can be appealed or corrected.

06 / OPERATIONS

An annual project production line, not a festival list

Q1 clarifies problems, Q2 tests betas, Q3 releases components, and Q4 reviews results. Monthly maintenance and quarterly public deliberation keep the place active all year.

Days 0-90

Confirm accountability, missing data, and one non-personal-data scenario, then complete a bounded trial, three user tests, accessibility review, and safety drill.

Months 4-12

Build only one combined hub demonstrator and record 90-day maintenance, complaints, human takeover, and cost. Do not expand if it fails.

Long-term accountability

Steering, professional review, site operations, open-source maintenance, and public panels divide roles. Each project has one final owner and independent stop authority.

Four named seats

A owns the public outcome, D delivers, M maintains, and S holds independent stop authority. If any seat is vacant, the project may not occupy public space.
