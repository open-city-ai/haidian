01 / OVERVIEW

One line. Three yards.
Twelve reversible services.

A 9.50-kilometre conceptual active-mobility line crosses the provisional site. Three yards carry Build→Verify, Verify→Share and Share→Serve. Eight east-west links express connectivity needs only; they are not road redlines or engineering conclusions.

02 / KEY AREAS

Three shifts of duty

Each provisional key area combines programme, spatial action, service protocol, manual takeover and delivery gate.

PROV-KEY-001

Build→Verify Yard

A public validation street, controlled test courts and a green edge host model red teaming, robot sandboxing and edge-compute energy tests.

  • Public and controlled routes separated
  • Physical stop by safety staff
  • Version and risk displayed
PROV-KEY-002

Verify→Share Yard

A public handover table, campus research relay, translation desk and accessible route copilot link open-source work to daily life.

  • Licence and withdrawal visible
  • Human desk retained
  • Resident quiet edge protected
PROV-KEY-003

Share→Serve Yard

A civic handover hall, Century Logbook and oral-history booth connect public service, culture and AI-native commerce.

  • No mandatory facial recognition or app
  • Non-commercial access retained
  • Complaints and exits recorded
03 / CIVIC GROUND

Movement, blue-green ground
and human takeover

Ordinary movement and public service come first; the smart layer is removable. Shade, stormwater, fixed signs, tactile maps, lighting and human help continue when a model or network stops.
