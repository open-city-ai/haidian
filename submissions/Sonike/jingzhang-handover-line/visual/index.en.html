06 / BUILDINGS & DELIVERY

Typology cells,
not a demolition list

The twenty building features are typology test cells, not an existing-stock inventory or a demolition schedule. Demand is validated first through operation and removable elements, then through adaptive repair. No retain-renovate-demolish conclusion is fixed before building-by-building survey, structural, ownership, fire-safety and heritage review.

FOUR-STEP DECISION

R · A · I · D

  • Retain R: keep in use and complete the condition record.
  • Adapt A: fire safety, energy, accessibility and open ground floor first.
  • Insert I: removable elements and short-term use only.
  • Investigate D: awaits survey; it does not mean demolition.

THREE CONDITIONAL PHASES

Evidence, not dates

  • Phase 01 — Evidence baseline: official data, inventory, wayfinding and three low-risk human-led pilots.
  • Phase 02 — Ownership and collaboration: inherits results and opens only after ownership, fire, community, maintenance and recurring-budget review.
  • Phase 03 — Professional and durable operation: only after transport, utilities, heritage, privacy, safety, emergency and exit-asset review.
07 / DUAL-CONTROL SHIFT LEDGER

Dual-Control Shift Ledger:
one hands out, another takes in.

Protocol 0.3 splits one handover into two independent decisions. The outgoing role cannot sign for the incoming role. The receiver must reproduce the minimum evidence and explicitly ACCEPT or REFUSE. Open issues remain open; the smart layer stays off by default.

OUTGOING COPY

  • Declare object, version, scope and checksum
  • List known failures, open items and rollback action
  • Keep performance targets null until owner and baseline exist
  • Material completeness does not prove successful intake

INCOMING COPY

  • A different role independently reproduces the minimum check
  • Every open item receives an explicit response
  • The only decisions are ACCEPT or REFUSE
  • Missing evidence keeps human service active and the smart layer off
