## 00:00 In one sentence

The Jing-Zhang Handover Line translates the railway shift handover — the most ordinary
and most rigorous institution this line ever produced — into an urban protocol for AI.

## 00:12 The test

For any AI service on this belt, the same task must also be completable without AI.
If it cannot, the service does not open.

This is not a supplementary clause. It is the criterion for the whole scheme. It replaces
smart-city metrics that no ordinary person can check — device counts, automation rates —
with a question anyone can answer for themselves: without a smartphone, without reading
Chinese, without sight of a screen, or simply not wanting to use AI, can I still get this done?

## 00:45 The space

One continuous handover line runs north to south along the Jing-Zhang railway heritage
park, linking three handover grounds.

Zhongzhiyuan in the north is the build yard. It answers: can this be trusted? Models,
robots and compute enter testing carrying their version, their risks and their shutdown
procedure.

The AI Origin Community in the middle is the open-source yard. It answers: can this be
explained? Results, licences, failures and limits of applicability are published together.

Dazhongsi in the south is the civic yard. It answers: can this enter daily life? Public
services keep a human counter, a no-AI equivalent, an appeal route and an exit record.

Eight east-west stitch links bring in universities, parks, communities and rail stations.
Two wings support the whole.

## 01:35 The smallest thing

If only one thing could be built first, it would be a public handover table.

One table, and four items that must all be present at once. The release position, holding
four cards: version, scope of applicability, known faults, shutdown procedure. The receiving
position, where the only choices are accept, refuse or defer — and the decision must be
signed. The open-items board, where everything the previous shift failed to close hangs in
public and may not be taken down at handover. And the human counter, depending on no device
at all, which must exist before the smart layer does.

The order of the four cannot be rearranged. If only one segment can be kept, keep the human
counter.

## 02:20 The boundary

Every spatial, brand, event, timing and role arrangement in this proposal is conceptual
advice. It is not statutory planning and not a government commitment. The boundary is
provisional; the geometry recomputes, but it does not carry official precision. The eight
roles have post specifications, not appointments. All twelve scenario shift ledgers stand
at "refused" — because the three acceptance gates are unmet in every one of them.

On paper the protocol correctly refuses all twelve of its own scenarios. That is the
behaviour it should have: prove the refusal path works before discussing release.

## 02:55 Closing

Capability may move. Accountability must stay visible.

Bring a problem you can state in public and a responsibility you are willing to carry, and
complete one handover here that can be withdrawn, reproduced and handed on.
