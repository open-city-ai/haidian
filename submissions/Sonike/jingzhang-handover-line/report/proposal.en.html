JING-ZHANG HANDOVER LINE

Move Good AI into the City Faster, Give Every Handover a Return Path

One line moves AI from the laboratory into daily life. At every stop, someone can receive it, return it and explain it.

Picture an ordinary weekday afternoon. Someone is queuing at a service point near Dazhongsi, and beside the counter hangs a board: who is on duty today, which version of the service is running, and which button returns the matter to a person. They need not know which model sits behind it, and need not install anything — they only need to see that this shift has someone answerable for it. That is what this proposal sets out to build in the city: not a cleverer service, but a signature on every handover.

The Jing-Zhang Railway left more than an alignment. It left a discipline of shift handover: the outgoing shift must state what it is handing over, the incoming shift must decide independently whether to accept it, and unresolved work may not disappear between the two. This proposal translates that discipline into a spatial protocol for civic AI. BUILD hands a capability to VERIFY; VERIFY hands reproducible results to SHARE; SHARE hands a service to the city; complaints, repairs, failures and shutdown reasons then travel back to BUILD. Capability may move faster, while responsibility always retains a signatory and a return ticket.

A task remaining possible without AI is still the public-service floor, but it is no longer the entire story. The positive value of the Handover Line is that useful technology can find a test space, a responsibility interface, a civic carrier and a reusable record more quickly. When conditions change, it can also leave without damaging the basic service. The mechanism therefore advances innovation speed and public trust together Source Standard.

This iteration completes one implementation proof that remains under participant control. Twelve synthetic scenarios were replayed through an offline state machine: smart layer off, injected exception, receiver refusal, human takeover, deletion of temporary synthetic records, and return to basic service. All 12/12 tasks and 48/48 assertions passed. The result proves that the protocol can be executed and audited; it is not presented as field safety, a public trial or launch approval. Coordinated Research Area: Industry and Future City Research

The Handover Line is not another approval wall. It is a shorter and more trustworthy route for good technology to become urban. Conventional innovation chains often break between laboratory output and city use: a product lacks a controlled place to be tested, a service operator cannot identify which version it received, and failed experience does not return to research. The proposal fills this gap with four explicit handovers. BUILD brings version, risk and shutdown procedures to VERIFY. VERIFY passes positive and negative results with limits of applicability to SHARE. SHARE passes licence, maintenance and recall rules to SERVE. SERVE returns complaints, repairs and exit causes to BUILD.

The figures in the previous paragraph only converge the problem, so where is this proposal's own scale? In two layers: demand-side magnitudes can be cited but not allocated; supply-side magnitudes are this proposal's own geometry and can be computed digit by digit.

On the demand side the statistical scope is pinned down first. The planning commentary of the Municipal Commission of Planning and Natural Resources states that "within the 14 km² catchment there are nearly 20 well-known universities and research institutes including Tsinghua, Beihang, Beijing Jiaotong University and the Chinese Academy of Sciences, and the distribution density of technology firms exceeds 800 per km²" Source. How many handovers a year decides whether paper double-signature is physically possible. Seven of the twelve scenarios are permanent (SCN-04 to SCN-10); the other five are by appointment, time-bounded or annual. Reasoning about magnitude from a frequency assumption: the seven permanent scenarios at monthly frequency give 84 per year and at weekly frequency 350 per year; all twelve in operation give 144 and 600 per year respectively.

That magnitude is in the hundreds per year, not the hundred thousands, and that is exactly the condition under which this protocol holds. Dual signature requires the receiving party to reproduce the minimum evidence independently and a second reviewing role to sign again; at 10² that is a few tens of hours a week in total and people can finish it. At 10⁵ people cannot, so it must be automated — and an automatic signature is no signature at all. The condition for this protocol is therefore not "the bigger the better" but that handover is a discrete event whose frequency lands in the hundreds. Once frequency rises above a thousand a year this protocol needs redesigning — that boundary is stated here so it can be refuted, not so it can look safe. Where "faster" actually happens: three acceleration points, one causal chain and two unvalued metrics

The title promises "faster", so it must say which segment gets faster and give a quantity capable of refuting it. One admission first: this proposal is full of gates, and gates slow things down. Acceleration does not happen at the gate; it happens in the rework the gate removes. That causal chain has to be written out, or "faster" is only an adjective.

There is exactly one causal chain, and it is fixed here: trustworthy → less repetition → faster. Not "fewer steps therefore faster" — this proposal plainly has more steps; but "each step's result can be taken on trust by the next, therefore nothing is redone". The chain has an explicit way of breaking: if the receiving side still redoes the validation the handing side already performed, the chain is broken and the claim of "faster" is refuted — handover_rework_rounds exists precisely to observe that.

Who Qualifies as a Receiving Party: Reproduction Comes in Three Tiers, and Not One Tier Gives Up Judgement

This causal chain rests on a premise this proposal had not written down: the receiving party must be able to reproduce. The table above says "the receiving party reproduces and can then rely on it, without retesting", yet the real receiving parties are neighbourhood offices, public service counters, community care providers and facilities maintenance — they have no technical capacity to reproduce an AI validation, and should not be required to have any. Leave that premise unstated and "faster" only holds between technical parties and breaks the moment it reaches the public service side.

The remedy is not to train them first but to divide "reproduction" itself into three tiers; a receiving party picks the tier its actual conditions allow, and all three constitute a completed signature:

Reproduction tierWhat the receiving party actually doesWho it suitsMinimum evidence entering the ledger
R1 Reproduce directlyRuns it once themselves from the minimum evidence suppliedReceiving parties with technical capacity: developers, verifiers, university teamsWhether the reproduction agrees with the handing-over party's statement — both outcomes are recorded
R2 Witness reproductionDoes not run it, but attends while the handing-over party runs it and judges independently whether "what I saw matches what was stated"Receiving parties without technical capacity but able to attend: neighbourhood offices, public service counters, community careAttendance record ＋ consistency judgement ＋ verbatim questions and answers
R3 Delegated reproductionDelegates R1 to the independent reviewing role and judges only "whether this conclusion can be used in my service"Receiving parties with neither capacity nor ready access: cross-district service, night serviceThe delegate's reproduction record ＋ the delegating party's applicability judgement

The one thing no tier gives up is judgement. R2 and R3 give up doing the work; they do not give up saying no. The rejection slot treats all three tiers alike and rejection reasons are still classified R1–R7. Once judgement is given up as well it is no longer tiered reproduction but signature by proxy, and signature by proxy is no signature.

The cost of R3 must be attributed, not counted as saved. Delegating reproduction to the independent reviewing role moves the cost from the receiving party to the reviewer; it does not remove it. That cost lands on the independent reviewing post, one of the three "stop-only" incremental posts, and is an increment this proposal acknowledges.

This also supplies the causal chain with a second refutation condition. The original condition was "if the receiving party still redoes validation the handing-over party has already done, the chain is broken". The second is: if receiving parties on the public service side can in practice only use R3, and R3 stays in the majority over time, then technical capacity on this belt is too concentrated and "faster" is in fact happening only between technical parties — the chain is not broken, but its beneficiaries are narrower than claimed. The share of each reproduction tier should be published with the annual handover ledger; this proposal sets no target for that share, and requires only that it may not go unpublished Metric [assumption:A-BASELINE-001]. Integrated planning, space-industry fusion, and territorial-planning innovation

"Integrated" here does not mean binding thematic chapters together; it means different disciplines working on one recomputable data package. A conventional integrated output is an album binding transport, municipal, green space and industry chapters, reconciled by hand. In this package the geometry, metrics, figures, drawings and three matrices are generated from one source, so a change in any chapter changes the number every other chapter cites, and anything that will not change surfaces immediately as a contradiction. The test of whether an integrated plan is truly integrated is therefore whether its disciplinary conclusions can all be overturned by one recomputation Depth.

Space-industry fusion lands on interfaces, not on lists. This proposal names no companies and promises no output value. The relation between industry and space is expressed through eight shared interfaces — land slots, validation space, open-source results, compute and equipment, data, talent, funding services, and city scenarios — each stating the minimum exchangeable result, the entry condition, and the refusal condition. The reason is falsifiability: a list expires, an interface does not. Five years on, with a different set of companies, the interface specification can still be tested. Industry is not "placed into" a parcel; it connects through a gate that can refuse it and that it can exit. Fusion is measured by how many can actually connect and can actually leave, not by occupancy rate Depth.

Territorial-planning innovation lands on concrete adaptive and evolvable mechanisms, not on adjectives. AI does not add a layer of devices to the city; it changes the scale, the time slot, and the replacement speed of space. The key variable of urban form shifts from "how big do we build" to "how often is it replaced, and does replacement hurt anyone". Three mechanisms answer exactly that: removable components let space evolve on a "removable first, built second" basis; conditional phase gates allow expansion only after a threshold is passed and roll back to the last usable state when it is not; exit records leave a challengeable receipt for every replacement. The credibility of a planning output therefore comes not from the certainty of its conclusions but from who can overturn them, with what, and within how long. All of this is method advice and does not replace statutory preparation or approval Standard.

Dazhongsi was originally Juesheng Temple, built by imperial order in the eleventh year of Yongzheng (1733) and named after the Yongle Bell it houses. That bell carries Buddhist scripture cast over its body in both Chinese and Sanskrit — more than 230,000 characters in all. The temple became the Dazhongsi Ancient Bell Museum in 1985, and Juesheng Temple was listed as a national key cultural relic protection unit in 1996 Source. A bell that carries 230,000 characters cast into its own body is itself a record that cannot be quietly rewritten. The landmark that stands here, the Century Logbook, inherits exactly that: the record can only be appended to and corrected, never erased, and a withdrawn entry keeps its reason for withdrawal rather than vanishing from public memory. The Handover Bell is not here — it stands in the Build Yard to the north, because a bell marks the moment a capability is independently accepted, and that belongs where verification happens; what this Yongle Bell corresponds to was never a moment but a record written full, and impossible to unwrite. Handing over and taking in are two independent judgements; face to face turns them into a conversation, and a conversation tends naturally toward instant merging. Is it hot in summer — the table has a particular answer to that. Dazhongsi's Row of Street Counters: The Waiting Area Cannot Rely on Street Trees for Shade

Dazhongsi's section values lean furthest toward people of the three: A1 waiting depth 3.5 m (the widest on the belt) at 3.5 m frontage, about 12 m² per counter; E planting and rest at 6.0 m (also the widest); C and D both at zero. A 12 m² waiting area holds 8–12 people at 1.0–1.5 m² each, with the same overflow rule as the AI Origin Community: along the frontage, never into A2.

But this stretch has a problem that has to be stated head-on: the waiting area cannot rely on the street trees in band E for shade. One pass of arithmetic makes it clear. At Dazhongsi A ＝ 8.5 m (A1 3.5 ＋ A2 3.0 ＋ A3 1.5 ＋ 0.5 slack), and between the outer edge of A1 and the inner edge of E lie A2 3.0 ＋ A3 1.5 ＋ B 0.6 ＝ 5.1 m (C and D are zero here); a 1.5 m tree pit against E's inner edge puts the trunk about 5.85 m from A1's outer edge and 9.35 m from the building face. At a latitude of about 39.9°N and a summer-solstice declination of 23.44°, noon solar altitude is about 73.5° and a 10 m tree casts a shadow only about 2.96 m long. Noon alone does not settle it, though — noon is the shortest shadow of the year, while the queue is longest in the afternoon. What decides it is that this stretch is an east-west frontage: as the afternoon sun moves west the shadow swings to run along the street, so the component that crosses the frontage gets smaller rather than larger — 1.47 m at 15:00 and 0.18 m at 16:00. Computed minute by minute from 09:00 to 17:00, the across-frontage projection peaks at 3.50 m at 17:00 (altitude 25.9°, shadow 20.56 m); even adding a 2–3 m crown radius the shadow edge reaches only 5.5–6.5 m, just grazing A1's outer edge at 5.85 m, with a further 9.35 m to the building face across the waiting area. Through every hour in which people are queuing, the street trees shade the carriageway and their own feet, not the waiting area.

So the shade over the waiting area has to come from the podium itself. The Dazhongsi street podium takes a 4.5–5.0 m clear ground floor and carries a 2.0–3.5 m deep overhang above A1 — matching the 3.5 m depth of the waiting area exactly; the underside of the overhang stays no lower than 3.0 m so the frontage is not compressed into an arcade. The overhang is a fixed component and falls under "repair" treatment, not on the removable-component list. The street trees in E are still planted, but their job is shading the rest band and passers-by, not shading people in a queue — saying the two separately is what keeps both from failing.

All of the above remains a conceptual proposal; the paragraph above is a minute-by-minute geometric computation, not an insolation simulation. Solar altitude, shadow length and shadow bearing are computed directly from latitude, solstice declination and true solar time, with inputs and formula stated above so anyone can recompute them minute by minute; the figure is the projection of a single point at 10 m above the trunk, with crown spread carried only as a radius. Professional simulation covering crown form, shading from adjacent buildings and the wind environment belongs to the review stage that follows building and tree survey, is carried out by the design institute, and the overhang depth and tree-pit position may change then Depth Standard.

Land Use, Building Scale, and Retain-Renovate-Demolish Strategy

On the same stretch of street, someone runs a test inside the validation workshop during the day while someone from the block above comes down for groceries in the evening — land use has to hold both, not show one of them out. Land use follows the published classification: 0701 residential, 0702 community service, 0802 research, 0803 culture, 0804 education, 09 commercial service and 1401 park green. Within the submitted model they cover the provisional overall area and explain functional relationships. They do not claim surveyed land use, ownership or planning permission Standard Spatial data.

Six of those seven codes are second-level while 09 is first-level. That is not an omission but a stated rule: descend to the second level only where the second-level class is uniquely determined; stay at the first level where a parcel spans two second-level classes or where the only available fit is the residual "other" class. The test can be read straight out of the organizer enumeration: brief/site-package/enums/land_use_codes.json (a project subset of 自然资发〔2023〕234号) tags every code with a level field. 09 商业服务业用地 is level=1, and the four level=2 classes beneath it are 0901 商业用地 (retail), 0902 商务金融用地 (business and finance), 0903 娱乐用地 (entertainment) and 0904 其他商业服务业用地 (other commercial service). 09 and all four are legal values. Transport, Rail, Municipal Infrastructure, and Public Services

Pushing a pram from the north end to the south end, a person meets eight east–west junctions. What she needs is not smarter navigation but that none of those eight requires lifting the wheels. Transport therefore first ensures that a person can complete the route continuously, then adds smart service. The north-south spine retains continuous walking and cycling surfaces while eight east-west links reconnect adjacent districts. The last 500 metres from transit to civic service prioritise orientation, shade, seating, accessibility and human help. When AI navigation fails, fixed wayfinding, a tactile map, an unpowered route and a person remain available Spatial data Depth.

The proposal does not pre-design railway or road engineering. Eight spine-link intersections are registered as potential grade-break review points, with per-point coordinates in geometry/constraints.geojson#data_gap.pending_field_checks.grade_check_points, and anyone can recompute them from geometry/roads.geojson — they are the intersections of the spine ROAD-001 with the eight stitch links ROAD-002 to ROAD-009. The Rail Line Running Alongside: Not Two Parallel Lines but Two Levels of One Corridor

A rail line is already running in this corridor, and it parallels the spine along its entire length. The Beijing Municipal Commission of Planning and Natural Resources states it plainly in its planning commentary: "the operating Rail Line 13 runs parallel to the heritage park along its entire length between Beijing North Station and Qinghe, and the rail line changes its vertical relationship to the city ground several times"; the same commentary also records that "the space under the Line 13 viaduct is brought into the park design, using the vacated space under the bridge north of the North Fourth Ring Road to create a place for cultural exchange" Source. Read together those two sentences are not background; they are a design condition.

First, why this proposal writes no line number and no station name. The existing line is being split and upgraded — "the project splits the existing 'inverted-U' Line 13 for capacity, forming two 'L-shaped' lines between Line 16 and Line 17" — and stations along it are being named in batches Source. Numbers and operating arrangements will change; station names will change. What will not change is that an operating railway runs parallel to the spine along its whole length and repeatedly changes its vertical relationship to the ground. The rail interface in this proposal therefore depends only on that spatial fact, never on a number or a name; once official longitudinal sections arrive, what needs rechecking is where the vertical relationship changes, not which number the line carries.

Bus, Cycles and Parking: All Three Are Interfaces Only, No New Vehicular Capacity

Walking and cycling are covered above; three things still have to be stated:

  • Bus interchange lands on the eight stitch-link crossings, not on the spine. The spine is a continuous walking and cycling surface and carries no bus route; the eight east-west links are where the belt meets the existing bus network. Each crossing therefore carries three requirements: no level change between bus platform and spine (those eight points are exactly the registered grade-check points, so the two matters occur in the same place); the waiting area and the staffed counter at that point share one shelter rather than each raising its own canopy; and the paper route map and tactile map sit on the waiting area, not inside a screen.
  • Cycle parking is fixed to one side of each stitch-link crossing and never occupies the step-free surface. The twenty approach walks total 6,596 m across the belt, and if parking is left unfixed the first thing occupied will be that 2.5 m clear width. It goes to one side of the crossing because the crossing is already where people gather and where the parking demand concentrates.
  • No new car parking spaces and no new vehicular accesses along the spine. This is not thrift; it falls out of the section. Within a one-side width of 15.1–17.1 m the five segments A–E are already full, so a car can only get in by cutting into A2, the step-free surface, or E, the planting and rest band — and those are the two segments this proposal will not give up. Existing vehicular movement and the accesses of adjacent institutions are unaffected; this proposal does not propose changing any existing traffic arrangement.

Within the last five hundred metres, which part this proposal can actually design. "The last five hundred metres from station exit to public service" is a common phrase; on this belt it splits in two. The station-exit-to-spine part depends on station positions, which this proposal does not know and does not design. The spine-to-cell part has already been measured: 245–414 m, mean 330 m, value by value in "Where the Twenty Renewal Cells Sit", where the approach walk's clear width, shade, counter position and vehicle rules are each given a value Source Depth.

Which Signal Governs When the Three Licence Signals Conflict

Three things exist side by side in this package: the machine-readable identifier COMMUNITY-DISPLAY-ONLY in manifest.json, the full grant text given by the rights holders in the previous section, and the third-party exclusion together with the no-government-endorsement statement. The order of precedence in a conflict is fixed and leaves no room for interpretation:

1. The third-party exclusion and the no-endorsement statement override everything. They are prohibitions that no grant can break through: this package does not cover third-party material (it contains zero third-party images, maps, trademarks or protected layouts, and the font rights chain is in the table above), and no use may imply government endorsement, implementation approval, completed construction or completed public participation. 2. Next comes the rights holders' full grant text. It is a grant the rights holders make over rights they themselves hold, enforceable without support from any external text; a reader who complies with CC BY-NC 4.0 is already licensed. 3. The machine-readable identifier ranks last. COMMUNITY-DISPLAY-ONLY is at present only an enumeration value in the organiser's schema, with terms unpublished. If it is later defined more strictly than item 2, the stricter one governs; if it is defined more loosely, this package still grants only within the scope of item 2 — the grant here does not widen automatically because an identifier widens.

The same rule is written into license.precedence_zh/en in manifest.json, so the machine-readable entry point and the text give one and the same decision.
