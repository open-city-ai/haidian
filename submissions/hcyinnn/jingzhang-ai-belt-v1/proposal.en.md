## Three-Level Scope Framework

The three-level scope is three focal lengths of one decision [depth:three_level_scope_framework]. The coordinated research area is about 43.6 km2 [metric:research_area_sqm], answering why Haidian needs an AI innovation belt and how it collaborates with the regional innovation network; the overall design area is about 11.4 km2 [metric:site_area_sqm][data:geometry/site_boundary.geojson#SITE-001], answering how the areas around the Jingzhang heritage park become a walkable, sociable and testable innovation commons; the three key areas total about 368.4 ha [metric:key_area_sqm][data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#KEY-001], answering what the Zhongzhiyuan, AI Origin Community and Dazhongsi each do first and how they validate. The transmission logic is "strategy sets direction, design sets structure, implementation sets projects". When official geometry arrives, a full-package recalculation is triggered - replacing boundaries and key-area polygons, re-splitting land use, clipping layers, recomputing all metrics and redrawing the five figures, two drawings and the dashboard [depth:metrics_recalculation].

## Coordinated Research Area: Industry and Future City Research

**Naming and visual identity**: the Chinese primary name is "JingZhang Synapse · AI Innovation Belt"; the English name is JingZhang Synapse · AI Innovation Belt. "JingZhang" anchors the century-old railway heritage; "Synapse" evokes an innovation artery growing along the heritage in the AI era. The Logo direction uses a "rail cross-section x neural synapse" motif with primary colours of JingZhang Steel Grey #5B6B73, Compute Teal #1FA8A0 and Zhongguancun Warm Orange #E8743B [standard:BRAND-IDENTITY-GUIDE][standard:COLOR-SYSTEM-GUIDE][depth:overall_spatial_structure]. **Three positioning pillars and five functions**: the Century Jingzhang Heritage Band, the Urban AI Life Experience Band and the AI Convergence Innovation Band; end-to-end AI autonomy, world-class ecosystem, AI+ scenario empowerment, vibrant city, and AI governance discourse power. **Three-zone two-wing synergy loop**: a slow-traffic main corridor along the heritage park strings together Zhongzhiyuan (end-to-end), the AI Origin Community (world-class ecosystem and public experience) and Dazhongsi (intelligent-native new business forms); the Zhongguancun Service Wing (IP/capital/element global allocation) and the Xiaoyuehe Scenario Wing (scenario empowerment/vibrant city) supply both ways, forming a R&D - experience - industry - service loop.

**Global ecosystem case translation** (7 cases, mechanism only) [source:GLOBAL-CASE-PUBLIC-REFS][metric:ecosystem_case_count]: Station F (Paris) single mega-incubator + global founder network; Quayside (Toronto) waterfront + digital-twin governance; Pengcheng Laboratory (Shenzhen) national lab + industry chain; Punggol Digital District (Singapore) industry-academia-research + active mobility; MILA (Montreal) open academic-industry research; Tel Aviv AI cluster (Israel) civil-military spin-off + dense entrepreneurship; San Francisco AI corridor (USA) capital-talent-compute dense network. **Eight-element assurance mechanism**: land/space/industry/capital/talent/compute/data/scenario driven by scenario opening, avoiding writing recruitment, policy or funding as settled matters. **Regional synergy interfaces**: future-science-city "origination-pilot" linkage, Huairou data/problem exchange, E-Town "design-manufacture" schedules, and open scenario lists to Beijing-Tianjin-Hebei - all operational suggestions, not cross-region commitments.

## Overall Design Area: Urban Renewal and Regulatory-Plan-Level Urban Design

**Overall structure: Synapse main axis, three zones, two wings, three scales**. The main spine has a real base - the Jingzhang heritage park linear green corridor [source:JZ-PARK-OPEN][source:JINGZHANG-HISTORY]; this proposal overlays renewal on this existing public-space spine: a slow-traffic main corridor along the heritage alignment [depth:overall_spatial_structure][data:geometry/roads.geojson#RD-SPINE], with two east-west stitching streets removing railway severance [data:geometry/roads.geojson#RD-X1]; three scales - corridor whole, three station areas (about 800 m radius), and block prototypes within station areas [depth:land_use_layout]. **Four-step renewal strategy**: operation-first -> light renovation sharing -> performance retrofit -> necessary renewal (statutory procedures). Retain-renovate-demolish is a method framework, not parcel-level conclusions [depth:retain_renovate_demolish]. **Character and intensity**: "low base, open ground floor, readable rooftops, restrained night lighting"; official control values for FAR, height, density and green ratio are missing and listed as to-be-checked against the regulatory plan; no statutory intensity conclusions are given [depth:development_intensity_controls][depth:height_massing_character][standard:MOHURD-CONTROL-DETAILED-PLANNING][standard:MOHURD-URBAN-DESIGN-MEASURES].

## Detailed Design of Key Areas

The three sub-districts share a template: form prototype - block scale - ground-floor interface - public space - risk [depth:three_key_area_detailed_design]. ## AI Innovation Ecosystem, Personas, and AI+ Scenarios

**Ecosystem design**: Zhongzhiyuan end-to-end system and the AI Origin Community ecosystem (Section 5) land as space; the Zhongguancun Service Wing outputs IP translation, capital matchmaking, talent relay, compliance and compute scheduling networks. **AI scenario cards (12)** [metric:scenario_count]: AI Health Booth, AI Study Companion Nook, accessible smart wayfinding, developer shuttle, city-governance agent panel, open-source model kiosk, AI elder-care station, low-carbon energy manager, smart inspection/cleaning, legal/admin consulting booth, night-vitality lighting narrative, multilingual visitor assistant - all with human review and privacy boundaries. **Industry test scenarios (4)** [metric:validation_scenario_count]: low-speed unmanned shuttle test loop, robot delivery/inspection sandbox, city-governance agent deduction chamber, city-scale night-vitality scenario - confined, regulated, reviewable; test proposals, not approved operations. **Personas (6)** [metric:persona_count]: AI researchers/engineers, entrepreneurs/developers, university faculty/students, residents (incl. elderly and children), visitors/international talents, governance/operations. **Privacy and human-review boundary**: data minimisation, anonymisation, human fallback; no privacy-invasive, over-monitoring or non-reviewable scenarios; no non-public data or specified vendors as necessary conditions.

## Transport, Rail, Municipal Infrastructure, and Public Services

**Slow traffic**: the heritage park slow-traffic main corridor is the north-south spine [depth:traffic_rail_slow_parking][data:geometry/roads.geojson#RD-SPINE], stringing the three zones and removing railway severance; two east-west stitching streets organise cross-corridor slow connections [data:geometry/roads.geojson#RD-X1]. **Rail connection and low-speed mobility**: low-speed shuttle shuttles (confined road rights, remote stop) connect rail stations with accessible routes, opened step by step as test scenarios [metric:validation_scenario_count]. **Municipal new infrastructure**: scenario-based - low-carbon energy manager, smart inspection/cleaning, open-source model sandbox, public-space energy screens - all with desensitised data and human review [depth:municipal_new_infrastructure]; no municipal capacity conclusions are made.

## Blue-Green Network, Public Space, and Urban Character

**Heritage park AI public space**: a walkable, experiential, showcase-able linear public space along the railway heritage; the protection constraint line and buffer are in the constraints layer [depth:blue_green_public_space][data:geometry/constraints.geojson#CNST-RAIL][data:geometry/constraints.geojson#CNST-BUF][standard:HERITAGE-PROTECTION]. **Blue-green network**: blue-green ribbons on both sides of the spine [data:geometry/green_space.geojson#GR-001] at about 0.23 [metric:green_ratio]; public-space nodes (plaza, garden, bazaar, memory node) at about 0.23 [metric:public_space_ratio][data:geometry/public_space.geojson#PS-ORIGIN][standard:MOHURD-URBAN-DESIGN-MEASURES]. **Urban character**: low base, open ground floor, readable rooftops, restrained night lighting, echoing "technological rationality x railway memory x campus humanity" [depth:blue_green_public_space]. **Cultural narrative and signage**: a three-part narrative of "Century Jingzhang (Zhan Tianyou, first independently designed trunk railway) - Zhongguancun (daring and open source) - AI new culture (open, interpretable, auditable)" [source:JINGZHANG-HISTORY][source:JZ-PARK-OPEN]; the wayfinding/symbol system shares the same family as but is distinct from the master Logo; international communication: A century ago, Zhan Tianyou engineered this railway. A century later, your GitHub ID is carved into its future.
