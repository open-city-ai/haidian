## 1. 项目目标与原则 / Objective and Principles

方案面向家庭、老人、儿童与照护者、通勤家庭、AI 研发人才、国际访客以及社区服务和一线运营人员，以“可理解、可选择、可验证、可退出”为原则，把风险预防、发现、响应和恢复转化为空间、服务、运营与审计机制。

The proposal serves families, older people, children and caregivers, commuting households, AI researchers, international visitors, and community-service and frontline operations staff. Its principles—understandable, optional, verifiable, and exit-enabled—translate risk prevention, detection, response, and recovery into spatial, service, operating, and audit mechanisms.

## 2. 品牌与核心叙事 / Brand and Core Narrative

- 中文名称：京张可信生活线
- English name: Trusted Living Line
- 中文口号：让城市智能可以被理解、选择、验证和退出
- English slogan: Urban intelligence people can understand, choose, verify, and leave

品牌从百年京张铁路的可信运行历史出发，将信号、调度、检修和责任记录转译为 AI 城市时代的透明提示、人工复核、可回退试验和公共审计。

The brand begins with the Jing-Zhang railway's history of trusted operation and translates signalling, dispatch, maintenance, and responsibility records into transparent prompts, Human Review, reversible trials, and public audit for an AI-enabled city.

## 3. 一线、三核、六类节点 / One Line, Three Cores, Six Node Families

- 一线 / One line：沿京张遗址公园组织可信生活公共主线，串联慢行、避险、健康、无障碍和社区互助服务 / a public Trusted Living Line along the Jing-Zhang Heritage Park linking walking, shelter, health, accessibility, and mutual-aid services.
- 三核 / Three cores：众智园可信 AI 测试核 / Zhongzhiyuan Trusted AI Testing Core；AI 原点跨代生活核 / AI Origin Intergenerational Living Core；大钟寺可信服务核 / Dazhongsi Trusted Service Core.
- 六类节点 / Six node families：健康支持 / health support；气候避险 / climate shelter；无障碍导航 / accessible navigation；儿童友好 / child-friendly service；数字权益 / digital rights；社区互助 / community mutual aid.

三处重点区域分别承担安全标准、算法沙盒、机器人和极端天气测试；跨代生活与照护；以及数字身份、智能终端、公共服务和消费者权益验证。对应公共地标为可信 AI 试验门、跨代生活客厅和算法透明广场。

The three key areas respectively host safety standards, algorithm sandboxes, robotics and extreme-weather testing; intergenerational living and care; and digital-identity, intelligent-terminal, public-service, and consumer-rights verification. Their public landmarks are the Trusted AI Test Gate, Intergenerational Living Room, and Algorithm Transparency Plaza.

## 4. 服务闭环与场景字段 / Service Loop and Scenario Fields

所有场景遵循预防—发现—响应—恢复闭环：风险提示与环境改善；居民主动报告和隐私友好感知；无障碍替代路线、临时避险、人工服务和社区互助；事件复盘、设施修复、算法纠错、版本记录和公开审计。公共空间不默认采用人脸识别。

Every scenario follows a Prevent–Detect–Respond–Recover loop: risk communication and environmental improvement; resident reporting and privacy-friendly sensing; accessible alternatives, temporary shelter, human service, and mutual aid; then retrospective review, repair, algorithm correction, version records, and public audit. Public Space does not use face recognition by default.

每张场景卡必须说明使用者、空间载体、最少必要数据、人工责任人、退出方式、失败回退和可度量评估指标。医疗、教育、法律、安全、规划和工程结论不得由智能体自动作出。

Every scenario card must identify the user, spatial carrier, minimum necessary data, accountable human operator, opt-out, failure fallback, and measurable evaluation metric. Urban Agents must not autonomously make medical, education, legal, safety, planning, or engineering decisions.

## 5. 场景、画像与运营 / Scenarios, Personas, and Operation

正式方案包含十二张场景卡，其中至少三张为产业测试验证场景；六类画像覆盖老人、儿童与照护者、通勤家庭、AI 研发人才、国际访客、社区服务与一线运营人员。场景覆盖气候避险、无障碍通行、儿童安全路径、跌倒与健康支持、社区互助、可信机器人服务、数字身份最小披露、算法投诉与纠错、可信消费终端、夜间安全、多语服务和公共韧性演练。

The formal proposal contains twelve scenario cards, at least three of which are Industry Testing and Validation scenarios, and six persona groups covering older people, children and caregivers, commuting families, AI researchers, international visitors, and community-service and frontline operations staff. Scenarios cover climate shelter, accessible travel, child-safe routes, fall and health support, mutual aid, trusted robotics, minimum-disclosure digital identity, algorithm complaints and correction, trusted consumer terminals, night safety, multilingual service, and public-resilience exercises.

长期运营采用“日常服务—季度测试—年度公开评估—国际可信 AI 周”的节奏，形成开发者参与、居民共评、场景开放、问题修复和国际传播机制。

Long-term operation follows a daily service—quarterly testing—annual public evaluation—International Trusted AI Week cadence, supporting developer participation, resident co-evaluation, scenario access, issue repair, and international exchange.

## 6. 数据、空间与专业边界 / Data, Spatial, and Professional Boundaries

只使用公开、仓库批准或用户明确清权的数据。当前场地和三处重点区来自临时粗略边界，统一标记为 `provisional_constraint`、`official_boundary=false` 和 `boundary_precision=provisional_rough`。在明确披露临时属性、精度和限制的前提下，这些几何可用于概念生成、离线展示、模型自检、设计讨论和内容评分；不得用作官方红线、精确面积或法定控制依据、土地权属、审批或工程结论。GeoJSON 使用 EPSG:4326；面积与线长复算使用 EPSG:4548。

Only public, repository-approved, or explicitly rights-cleared data is used. The current site and three key areas derive from rough provisional boundaries labelled `provisional_constraint`, `official_boundary=false`, and `boundary_precision=provisional_rough`. When their provisional status, precision, and limits are disclosed, they may support concept generation, offline display, model self-check, design discussion, and content scoring. They must not support an official redline, a precise-area or statutory-control basis, ownership, approval, or engineering conclusions. GeoJSON uses EPSG:4326; area and length recalculation uses EPSG:4548.

容积率、建筑高度、法定建筑密度、法定绿地率和退线在缺少官方资料时必须保持未知。正式边界、控规、现状测绘、权属、道路轨道、市政消防、生态防洪、文保和公共服务资料到位后，全部空间、指标、图件和结论必须由相应专业团队整体复核。涉及个人或城市运行数据时，执行最少收集、目的限定、明确告知、可选择退出、人工最终判断、可回退试验和可审计纠错。

Floor Area Ratio, Building Height, statutory Building Coverage Ratio, statutory green ratio, and setbacks remain unknown without official data. When official boundaries, controls, surveys, ownership, road and rail, municipal and fire, ecology and flood, heritage, and public-service materials arrive, the relevant professional teams must review all geometry, metrics, drawings, and conclusions together. Personal and urban-operations data follow minimization, purpose limitation, notice, opt-out, final human judgment, reversible trials, and auditable correction.
