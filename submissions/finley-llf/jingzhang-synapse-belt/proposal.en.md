## 1. Overall Concept & Functional Coordination (agent.1)
- **Chinese name**: 京张智脉 · 百年创新带 (Jing-Zhang Zhi-Mai). "Zhi-Mai" (intelligent vein) dual-means the
  historic railway artery and the AI-era data/compute/talent/scenario pulse.
- **English name**: Jing-Zhang Synapse Belt. *Synapse* = a connection/relay node, mirroring the belt's role as an
  AI-innovation connector; cross-culturally readable as "connect & activate".
- **Naming family** (three areas + two wings, all carry the "脉/vein" root): Origin Node, Commons Spine,
  Zhit Mali Hub, Zhongguancun Service Wing, Yuehe Scenario Wing.
- **Three positions**: Centennial Jing-Zhang Culture Belt · Urban AI Living Experience Belt · AI Fusion Innovation Belt.
- **Five functions**: full-stack autonomous AI system · world-class AI ecosystem · AI+ scenario empowerment ·
  intelligent vibrant city · global AI-governance voice.
- **Synergy loop**: one spine (rail-heritage green corridor) + three nodes (Commons Spine → Origin Node → Zhit Mali
  Hub) + two wings (Zhongguancun service / Yuehe scenario) form a closed innovation loop.

## 2. Full-stack Autonomous AI & World-class Ecosystem (agent.2)
- Seven real, citable global cases (Stanford HAI, MIT Schwarzman, Vector Institute, Montréal/MILA, Alan Turing
  Institute, Station F + Paris AI, AI Singapore) — public-website excerpts only, used for mechanism reference.
- Six-factor ecosystem loop: talent–compute–data–capital–scenario–institution, anchored at Origin Node.
- Commons Spine full-stack autonomy path (concept): chip/framework/foundation-model/toolchain/application.
  No investment/output/fiscal commitments asserted.

## 3. AI+ Scenario Empowerment & Vibrant City (agent.3)
- 12 scenario cards (exceeds the ≥10 requirement), 3 industry test-validation scenarios, 5 user personas.
- Privacy & human-review boundary: data minimization, mandatory human review, opt-out, auditability

  not approved operation".

## 4. AI Public Space, Native New Formats & Landmarks (agent.4)
- AI public space along the rail-heritage park; east-west stitching and north-south connection via the green
  corridor (concept strategy, no bridge/tunnel/underground engineering conclusions).
- Three AI pilgrimage landmarks: the Human-Figure Railway Spirit Tower, the Synapse Data Plaza, the Yuehe Light Gallery.
  None over-entertained or vulgarized.

## 5. Culture Fusion Narrative (agent.5)

## 6. Global AI Activities & Long-term Operation (agent.6)

- Developer-community operation, scenario open-operation (sandbox admission, ethics review, exit), and an
