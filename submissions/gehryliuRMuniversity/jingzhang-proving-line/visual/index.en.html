The Proving Line

Jing-Zhang Proving Line · Centennial Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt

A century ago, every inch of rail on the Jing-Zhang Railway had to pass track-proving acceptance before trains were allowed through; a century later, this 9 km linear green corridor becomes the proving line that global AI capabilities must pass before entering the real city — proved first, then opened, with rollback available and retirement possible. One proving line, three proving districts, five stitching corridors, and one sustainable go-live institution for the city.

Blue-Green & Public Space BLUE-GREEN & PUBLIC SPACE · One spine, one belt, many points

Blue-green structure

Proving-line corridor spine (GS-001) + Fifth Ring protective green belt (GS-002) + two pocket green belts along the Xiaoyue River on the east wing (GS-003/004). Green open space totals about 1.779 million sq m, green space ratio about 15.6% (scheme-recommended value). The Xiaoyue River watercourse is a locked existing element; no in-water engineering is included.

Public space system and pilgrimage landmarks

The proving-line public activity belt + five node plazas (totalling about 628,000 sq m), public space ratio about 5.5% (scheme-recommended value). Four AI pilgrimage landmarks (conceptual recommendations): the Kilometre Zero Marker (in front of Qinghuayuan Station; the double narrative of the historic "Journey to Beijing for the Examination" and today's "AI taking the examination"), the Contributors' Honor Wall (engraving proved schemes and their human and agent contributors), the Go-Live Bell (Dazhongsi; a bell-ringing ceremony for every scenario going live), and the Developers' Platform (Wudaokou; platform-and-herringbone-canopy imagery). A ten-piece public-space component library supports standardized replication.
