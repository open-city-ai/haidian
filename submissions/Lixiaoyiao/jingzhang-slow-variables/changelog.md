- Corrected `PHASE-001..003` into three intentionally overlapping whole-site eligibility envelopes, each equal to provisional `SITE-001`. They show maximum applicability of the evidence gates, not project siting, construction/adaptation footprints or delivery commitments; actual project locations and areas remain unknown.
- Reclassified the twelve `TIME-MARK` polygons as regularised schematic public-space envelopes with lower confidence. Their coordinates and areas are unchanged; they are not component footprints, surveyed public space or service catchments, and actual component siting awaits survey and professional review.
- Preserved the geometry, area and codes of `LU-003` and `LU-004` while narrowing their classification semantics to code 05 commercial/service land and code 0702 urban community-service facilities. AI testing/display/maintenance and lasting-life community services are separate design themes, not industrial-production or complete mixed-use classifications.
- Updated both proposals, metrics, all three matrices, figure/PDF wording and the offline visual. The visual now distinguishes envelopes from centroid symbols, links the two languages, improves text contrast and removes non-functional button behaviour; the English report localises evidence labels and adds accessible names.
- The `PROV-KEY-003` location offset, missing official polygons and three expected `KEY_AREA_PROVISIONAL` minor findings remain disclosed, known and non-blocking. No statutory, engineering, funding, institutional or implementation commitment was added.
