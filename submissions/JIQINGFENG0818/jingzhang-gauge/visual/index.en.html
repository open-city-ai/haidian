00The Concept

What this belt ultimately produces is not buildings but a gauge — a public specification for AI in urban space that anyone can build to, connect to, and audit against. A century ago the Jing-Zhang Railway set its own track gauge; a century later, this place sets its own specification for AI entering public space. Whoever holds the specification gains discourse power over the network without owning a single device.

All spatial content is a conceptual recommendation for further development by qualified professional teams. It does not constitute statutory planning, an approved conclusion, an implementation commitment, or an engineering feasibility conclusion. The boundary used is a provisional substitute boundary and must not be treated as an official red line or a basis for precise area figures.

Datum points: not photo-op sculptures but monuments carrying an actual measurement function

  • D-01 Datum Zero — The physical release point of each specification version; the version number and the principal changes are physically inscribed here at every update.
  • D-02 The Objection Stand — A physical registration point for citizen objections to any standard part; objection numbers and handling status are publicly traceable.
  • D-03 The Decommission Yard — Displays retired standard parts and the reasons for retirement. Showing failure is the source of this system's credibility.
  • D-04 The Contributor Register — Adopted revisions to the specification are physically inscribed by version; a reviewer's standing comes from a traceable record of reproduction, not from title.

Industry testing and validation scenarios

  • T-01 Conformance testing laboratory — Verifies whether a standard part meets the JZ-Gauge specification; certification results are published.
  • T-02 First-installation jury — Acceptability and real utility under actual resident conditions; outputs iteration recommendations or a rollback decision.
  • T-03 Red-team stress testing — Failure behaviour under adversarial input, extreme weather, and network loss; the failure-mode list is published.

09Renewal & Phasing

Phases are divided by proximity to the three stations; they are a conceptual recommendation and do not constitute an implementation plan or an investment commitment.

IIIVolume deploymentScale deployment of certified standard parts, introduction of AI-native business formsD-03 The Decommission Yard opened

Component catalogue spec-card fields

Every standard part must state: function definition / physical specification / data behaviour / human-review triggers / price band / whole life cycle / decommissioning and rollback / version compatibility. The real risk of urban AI installations is not that they cannot be installed — it is that they cannot be removed, that no one is accountable, and that no one knows where the data went; writing down how to take something out demonstrates governance capability more than writing down how to put it in.

13Sources & Assumptions

Sources

  • Area basis: brief/site-package/ranges/planning_limits.json (source SRC-2026-BJ-GH-QUAL-PREANNOUNCEMENT)
  • Provisional boundary and key areas: brief/site-package/geometry/provisional_boundaries.geojson
  • Land-use codes: brief/site-package/enums/land_use_codes.json (project subset; the complete official code table must be imported before formal statistics)
  • Mandatory task basis: brief/site-package/agent_taskbook.json
  • Case references and cultural historical material: public sources are to be completed item by item in the formal version; this version cites no specific case data

Assumptions

  • The design area uses the provisional_constraint provisional boundary, used only for provisional generation, visualisation, and self-check; it must not be used as an official red line, an approval basis, or a precise area conclusion.
  • Right-of-way widths (44 m arterial / 26 m secondary / 16 m local) are conceptual assumed values.
  • Storey counts are assumed values set by land-use class and are used for conceptual massing estimates; they are not a basis for an approved floor area ratio.
  • Component catalogue price bands are conceptual order-of-magnitude ranges; they are not a quotation or a procurement basis.
  • The phase division is a conceptual recommendation and does not constitute an implementation plan, an investment commitment, or a schedule.
  • This proposal provides no bridge, tunnel, underground space, or engineering feasibility conclusion, and makes no judgement on parcel ownership or demolition.
