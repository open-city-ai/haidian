All eight dispatch objects passed schema validation, all eight audit records are complete, and one task exceeded its rehearsal-process energy budget. The failed T1 canary test blocks and seals the candidate output; the failed T3 energy test stops the run and restores the rule baseline. / 提交包记录 T1—T4 各两条、共 8 条离线合成任务：6 条成功、2 条按门控失败；schema 8/8、审计 8/8，且 1 条超过演练进程能耗预算。T1 金丝雀失败会阻断并封存候选输出，T3 能源失败会终止运行并恢复规则基线。

These are fixed-input synthetic rehearsal results only.
