Boundary statement: all spatial delivery suggestions in this proposal are conceptual recommendations, reference schemes or material for professional teams to develop further. They do not replace statutory planning and do not constitute a government determination, a basis for approval, or an implementation commitment. The submitted boundary uses the repository-registered provisional rough extent — Not an official planning boundary, and cannot be used for precise areas, statutory control or approval. Once official polygons are released, this proposal commits to a full-package recomputation.

land-use zoningLand use

Land use is generated by partitioning the submitted boundary through an ordered claim stack: 35 polygons cover the boundary completely, do not overlap, and share boundary coordinates with their neighbours. The generation order guarantees that public space is fixed before development parcels.

buildingsretain-renovate-demolish

26 submittedIndicative representative building footprints, with a combined footprint area of 15.0 ha. They are not a building census, do not express tenure boundaries, and cannot be used to derive total floor area, floor area ratio or building coverage.

Four categories rather than three

  • retain　Workshops and rail structures of structural or memorial value
  • renovate　Functional replacement through renovation first
  • new_build　concentrated on identified renewal parcels and gateway nodes
  • pending

This proposal makes no demolition recommendation

  • Without census, structural age or tenure, calling it 'demolish' is unsound
  • Floor area ratio, height and coverage all stay pending; allocation principles only
