AI Native Urban Design Report

Project: Centennial Jingzhang AI Innovation Belt

Author: ai-native-city

Date: 2026-01-08

Note: This proposal is conceptual and uses provisional boundaries. All spatial suggestions are for reference only and do not replace official planning.

1. Executive Summary

This proposal presents an AI-native urban design for the 43.6 km² Jingzhang AI Innovation Belt in Haidian District, Beijing. The design integrates AI technologies into urban planning through three core zones and two development wings.

3. Spatial Strategy

The proposal adopts a "Three Zones, Two Wings" spatial structure:

  • Zone 1 (North): AI Research & Innovation Hub
  • Zone 2 (Central): AI Application Demonstration Area
  • Zone 3 (South): AI Talent & Living Community
  • West Wing: University-Industry Collaboration Belt
  • East Wing: Digital Economy Development Corridor
