# Jing-Zhang AI Resonance Belt: Tri-Zone Collaborative Urban Design from Full-Stack Innovation to Industrial Deployment

## Design Basis and Source Inventory

This proposal is based on: the Prequalification Announcement for the International Urban Design Competition (May 9, 2026) issued by the Haidian Branch of Beijing Municipal Commission of Planning and Natural Resources; the Agent Open Call Taskbook excerpts (May 18, 2026); the "Three Zones, Two Wings" policy framework (April 3, 2026) from Beijing Municipal Science and Technology Commission; Haidian District's "1+X+1" Modern Industrial System (March 2, 2026); and mandatory professional standards including MOHURD Urban Design Management Measures (2017) [source:SITE-PACKAGE] [standard:MOHURD-URBAN-DESIGN-MEASURES].

**Industrial Base**: Haidian District hosts **2,400+ AI enterprises**, 26 AI unicorns (73% of Beijing's total), **12,300 AI scholars** (>80% of Beijing), and 95,000 AI professionals. Core AI industry output reached approximately 350 billion yuan in 2025. 89 large AI models are registered in the district (nearly one-third of China's total). The Jing-Zhang Railway Heritage Park spans 9 km (fully opened August 6, 2026), serving 70 communities with approximately 450,000 residents [source:SRC-2026-BJ-KW-THREE-AREAS-WINGS].

This proposal uses provisional rough boundaries derived by repository maintainers from official text announcements. All spatial judgments involving precise redlines, regulatory planning indicators, building ownership, and engineering feasibility are labeled "pending official data" [data:geometry/site_boundary.geojson#PROV-SITE-001].

The complete evidence layer — sources, metrics, compliance, standards, and design depth — is recorded in `sources.json`, `metrics.json`, `compliance_matrix.json`, `standard_matrix.json`, and `design_depth_matrix.json` respectively.

## Three-Level Scope Framework

### Coordinated Research Area (43.6 km²)

Bounded by North 5th Ring Road (north), G6 Jingzang Expressway (east), Xizhimenwai Street (south), and Wanquanhe Road (west). This area encompasses Haidian's core AI industry concentration, including Zhongguancun Science City North, the Tsinghua-Peking University Innovation Corridor, Zhongguancun West, and the Xueyuan Road university cluster (37 universities, 646 CAS/CAE academicians).

### Overall Design Area (~11.4 km²)

A 1-2 km depth along both sides of the Jing-Zhang Heritage Park, organized as a "One Belt, Three Zones, Two Wings" spatial structure:
- **One Belt**: Jing-Zhang Heritage Park Innovation Spine (9 km)
- **Three Zones**: Zhongzhiyuan (192 ha) → AI Origin Community (104 ha) → Dazhongsi (72 ha)
- **Two Wings**: Zhongguancun Sci-Tech Service Wing (west), Xiaoyuehe Scenario Enablement Wing (east)

### Key Detailed Design Areas (~368.4 ha)

Three zones from north to south, each designed to comprehensive urban design depth. **Critical note**: All key area polygons are provisional rough. Replace after official polygons become available.

## Core Concept: "Jing-Zhang AI Resonance Belt"

**Narrative**: A century ago, Zhan Tianyou designed China's first independently built railway. A century later, this corridor carries China's full-stack AI innovation — from foundational research to industrial deployment, from chips to applications, from talent cultivation to global governance.

**Name**: Jing-Zhang AI Resonance Belt (京张AI共振带)

"Resonance" carries three meanings:
1. **Industrial Resonance**: Basic research (Origin Community) → Prototype acceleration (Zhongzhiyuan) → Industrial deployment (Dazhongsi) — a closed-loop gear drive, not a linear pipeline
2. **Human-AI Resonance**: Positive feedback between 12,300 scholars, 95,000 professionals, 450,000 residents, and AI-enabled public space
3. **Historical Resonance**: The 1909 railway defined physical connection standards; 2026 AI is defining digital connection standards

## Three Key Areas Detailed Design

### 1. Zhongzhiyuan AI Autonomous Innovation Acceleration Zone (~192 ha)

**Positioning**: "0-to-1" engine for AI full-stack autonomous innovation

**Spatial Structure**: "One Core, One Corridor, Three Clusters"
- **Core**: AI Computing Base Center (national public computing platform)
- **Corridor**: Qinghe Innovation Interface (~2 km waterfront public space + outdoor AI test corridor)
- **Three Clusters**: AI Chip & Hardware Prototype (north) / AI Framework & Base Software (center) / AI Large Model Training (south)

**Anchor Facilities**: National public computing platform (3,500→10,000 PFlops), flexible prototype factories, outdoor AI hardware durability test field

### 2. Beijing AI Origin Community (~104 ha)

**Positioning**: "-1-to-0" engine for AI foundational research and talent cultivation

**Spatial Structure**: "One Loop, Two Axes, Three Centers"
- **Loop**: 15-minute AI innovation living circle
- **Two Axes**: Qinghua East Road Innovation Spillover + Jing-Zhang Park Public Life
- **Three Centers**: Wudaokou AI Public Living Room / Joint AI Research Institute Cluster / Chengfu Road Seed Incubation Street

**Anchor Proposal**: Tsinghua×PKU×CAS Joint AI Research Institute — modeled on the Francis Crick Institute's £650M cross-institutional bet that catalyzed the entire London Knowledge Quarter

**Current Anchors**: 439 AI enterprises already operating (74% of area businesses), 13,000 AI professionals, Chengfu Road "Open Wall to Green" completed (July 2026, 2,000 linear meters)

### 3. Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster (~72 ha)

**Positioning**: "1-to-N" engine for AI industrialization at scale

**Spatial Structure**: "Two Cores, One Belt"
- **Two Cores**: AI International Convention Center + Smart Native Consumption Experience Zone
- **One Belt**: Jing-Zhang Park southern gateway landscape

**Existing Anchor**: ByteDance/Douyin Group has organically formed a cluster — Fangheng Plaza (office) + 1733 commercial complex (60+ brands, opened Dec 2023) + Lanjing Lijia site (RMB 2.8 billion acquisition, 3.95 ha, approved August 2026 as Beijing's first urban renewal project with municipal FAR support). This spontaneous clustering proves market demand for AI industry space far exceeds current supply.

**Core Strategy**: "Big-fish-leads-small-fish" — leverage ByteDance's existing cluster to attract upstream suppliers, downstream partners, and portfolio companies. Modeled on DJI's 500-firm ecosystem cluster in Shenzhen Nanshan.

## AI Pilgrimage Landmarks (3)

1. **"Compute Lighthouse"** (Zhongzhiyuan): Functional building + urban landmark with semi-transparent dynamic media facade displaying real-time computing utilization (de-identified), AI carbon footprint. Ground floor opens to Qinghe waterfront plaza.

2. **"Innovation Kilometre Zero"** (Origin Community, Wudaokou Plaza): Railway-track-inspired ground installation embedded with milestones from China/global AI history, with a new plaque added annually. Central "Zero Kilometre" marker symbolizes the starting point of China's autonomous AI innovation journey.

3. **"The Bell Resonance"** (Dazhongsi Station Plaza): Large-scale sound-and-light AI interactive installation inspired by the ancient Dazhong Temple bell. Visitor speech converted to unique bell tones via local AI (privacy-preserving, no cloud upload), with AI-generated responses projected as light patterns on the bell surface. Metaphor: "Striking ancient bronze to ask AI's questions."
