Mobility 交通慢行交通慢行

  • The ~9.6 km heritage greenway is fully walkable/cyclable, with 12 AI scenario nodes along the spine.
  • TOD: 300–800 m walking sheds of Xizhimen / Dazhongsi / Zhichunlu / Wudaokou cover the three cores.
  • Road hierarchy: arterial – secondary – branch; slow-mobility-first, fine-grain network inside the corridor.

Blue-Green Public Space 蓝绿公共空间蓝绿公共空间网络

  • Water: Xiaoyue River (east) and Qinghe River (north); the Qinghe cultural green waterfront hosts the north-core R&D living room.
  • Green ratio 22.55%, public space ratio 11.31% (recomputed in EPSG:4548).
  • Park green 1401 / protective green 1402 / squares 1403 form a continuous blue-green network.

Buildings 建筑建筑 — 概念基底

  • 91 concept building footprints; 66 existing buildings retained (existing_retained) as the main body.
  • 25 new buildings: 13 AI R&D + 9 mixed-use + 2 residential + 1 education, clustered in cores and TOD circles.
  • Concept building coverage 40.08%; statutory FAR / height controls are missing and pending official data.

Renewal Projects 更新项目更新项目 — 留改拆新建

  • Retain: 66 existing buildings kept, respecting the urban fabric.
  • Renovate: micro-renewal of corridor-facing frontages into innovation services.
  • Demolish: only scattered inefficient structures blocking the spine and blue-green links (concept judgement).
  • New: AI R&D and mixed-use buildings clustered in the three cores.
