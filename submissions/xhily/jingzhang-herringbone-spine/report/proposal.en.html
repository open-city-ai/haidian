Three-Level Scope Framework

The proposal establishes a three-level framework: a coordinated research area (the spillover hinterland of Zhongguancun Science City), an overall design scope (a ~9.7 km belt recomputed at 1141.28 ha / 11.4 km², closely matching the Jing-Zhang Heritage Park), and three key detailed-design areas (Zhongzhiyuan 192.1 ha, AI Origin Community 104.3 ha, Dazhongsi 72.0 ha) Depth Depth. The three levels are unified by the "Herringbone Spine" topology: one trunk (the Heritage Park Vitality Spine) reverses at the origin and bifurcates into an Autonomy Stroke (west) and a Translation Stroke (east), letting R&D, pilot testing, translation, and consumption occur continuously at physical scale.

Coordinated Research Area: Industry and Future City Research

The core value of the Century Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt is not "another science park" but converting a linear heritage into an innovation occurrence chain Depth. Building on Zhongguancun's full-stack autonomy momentum, the corridor targets two industries: the western Autonomy Stroke focuses on compute, models, toolchains, and pilot verification (full-stack autonomy), while the eastern Translation Stroke focuses on native-AI consumption, commercial experience, and scenario testing. The herringbone topology precisely captures the heterogeneous, directional, chained occurrence of an AI innovation chain — R&D on the Autonomy Stroke, translation on the Translation Stroke, meeting at the Origin — unifying "locality" and "innovation."

Overall Design Area: Urban Renewal and Regulatory-Plan-Level Urban Design

At the overall scope, the proposal organizes space at regulatory-plan-level urban-design depth: the Heritage Park Vitality Spine is the ecological base and slow-mobility axis; both sides are partitioned seamlessly and without overlap into research, cultural, commercial, business, residential, green, and plaza uses (see land_use.geojson) Depth Depth. Urban renewal follows "weave-in" rather than "tear-down": new work mainly supplements autonomy and native-AI carriers, while the existing built environment is upgraded through public-space and blue-green connectivity. The railway heritage is the ground, the blue-green network the skeleton, and the contributors' stories the content of a walkable innovation chain.
