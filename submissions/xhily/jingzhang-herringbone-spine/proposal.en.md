## Three-Level Scope Framework

The proposal establishes a three-level framework: a coordinated research area (the spillover hinterland of Zhongguancun Science City), an overall design scope (a ~9.7 km belt recomputed at 1141.28 ha / 11.4 km², closely matching the Jing-Zhang Heritage Park), and three key detailed-design areas (Zhongzhiyuan 192.1 ha, AI Origin Community 104.3 ha, Dazhongsi 72.0 ha) [depth:three_level_scope_framework] [depth:overall_spatial_structure]. The three levels are unified by the "Herringbone Spine" topology: one trunk (the Heritage Park Vitality Spine) reverses at the origin and bifurcates into an Autonomy Stroke (west) and a Translation Stroke (east), letting R&D, pilot testing, translation, and consumption occur continuously at physical scale.

## Coordinated Research Area: Industry and Future City Research

The core value of the Century Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt is not "another science park" but converting a linear heritage into an innovation occurrence chain [depth:three_level_scope_framework]. Building on Zhongguancun's full-stack autonomy momentum, the corridor targets two industries: the western Autonomy Stroke focuses on compute, models, toolchains, and pilot verification (full-stack autonomy), while the eastern Translation Stroke focuses on native-AI consumption, commercial experience, and scenario testing. The herringbone topology precisely captures the heterogeneous, directional, chained occurrence of an AI innovation chain — R&D on the Autonomy Stroke, translation on the Translation Stroke, meeting at the Origin — unifying "locality" and "innovation."

## Overall Design Area: Urban Renewal and Regulatory-Plan-Level Urban Design

At the overall scope, the proposal organizes space at regulatory-plan-level urban-design depth: the Heritage Park Vitality Spine is the ecological base and slow-mobility axis; both sides are partitioned seamlessly and without overlap into research, cultural, commercial, business, residential, green, and plaza uses (see `land_use.geojson`) [depth:overall_spatial_structure] [depth:land_use_layout]. Urban renewal follows "weave-in" rather than "tear-down": new work mainly supplements autonomy and native-AI carriers, while the existing built environment is upgraded through public-space and blue-green connectivity. The railway heritage is the ground, the blue-green network the skeleton, and the contributors' stories the content of a walkable innovation chain.

At regulatory-plan depth, the proposal assigns land use, conceptual FAR, and height zones per block, and enforces public-space and heritage setback controls in key areas; all values are conceptual and subject to official approval. The land partition is recomputed by `spatial_review.py` as seamless and non-overlapping, covering the entire overall scope.

## Detailed Design of Key Areas

The three key detailed-design areas form the two strokes and the intersection of the "人" [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#KEY-001] [depth:three_key_area_detailed_design]:

- **Zhongzhiyuan AI Autonomy Acceleration Area (Autonomy Stroke, west, 192.1 ha):** a compute–model–pilot continuous carrier along the autonomy greenway; gateways at Zhongzhiyuan Gateway Plaza (PS-17) and Autonomy Plaza (PS-02); mid-low-rise high-density R&D blocks, conceptual density 35–45%, height ≤ 60 m (DG-02, `low confidence`).
- **Beijing AI Origin Community (reversal node, 104.3 ha):** the "人" intersection; Origin Plaza (PS-01, conceptual radius ~118 m) is the global AI contributors' honor anchor; cultural + commercial + residential mixed within a 1 km radius enables "R&D–life–display" reversal meeting.
- **Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster (Translation Stroke, east, 72.0 ha):** native-AI consumption and commercial experience along the translation greenway; gateways at Dazhongsi Gateway Plaza (PS-18) and Translation Plaza (PS-03); programs include the native-AI market (PS-16) and seasonal scenario market (PS-23).

## Land Use, Building Scale, and Retain-Renovate-Demolish Strategy

Per `land_use.geojson` (342 seamlessly non-overlapping parcels), conceptual land-use areas are listed in Chapter 4 [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-001]. `buildings.geojson` places 210 conceptual buildings; conceptual building coverage is 23.4% (~267.07 ha footprint), recomputed as [metric:building_footprint_area_sqm] / [metric:site_area_sqm] [depth:development_intensity_controls]. The retain-renovate-demolish strategy is "preserve heritage readability and continuity, weave the existing built environment, build new to supplement innovation carriers": the spine axis and key-area public space are preserved and upgraded, while both sides are woven with mid-low-rise R&D/commercial, avoiding mass demolition [depth:retain_renovate_demolish]. All buildings are conceptual generation (agent_generated_design, confidence: low), not as-built survey, and must not be used as engineering basis (DG-03).

## Transport, Rail, Municipal Infrastructure, and Public Services

Transport centers on slow mobility and rail interchange [data:geometry/roads.geojson#ROAD-001]. The spine-park cycling main corridor (SL-SPINE) and the autonomy/translation greenways form a continuous slow-mobility network; 23 conceptual road centerlines express arterial–secondary–branch hierarchy but are explicitly "conceptual suggested alignments, not road redlines" [depth:traffic_rail_slow_parking]. The rail-interchange plaza (PS-19) strengthens "last-kilometer" continuity; the blue-green network conceptually provides sponge storage (echoing flood and sponge-city requirements) [depth:municipal_new_infrastructure]. Public services are carried by the 24 scenario nodes covering culture, sports, care, and markets, ensuring all-age and accessible use.

## Blue-Green Network, Public Space, and Urban Character

The blue-green network comprises the spine park (~130 m), the autonomy/translation greenways (~25 m each), and east–west green seams (about one per km, ~15–19 m), reaching a 30.2% green ratio (conceptual) [metric:green_ratio] [metric:public_space_ratio]. `public_space.geojson` holds 24 public scenario nodes, one about every 0.8–1.2 km along the spine for walkable access, with gateway plazas at the stroke ends reinforcing the "人" identity [data:geometry/green_space.geojson#GREEN-001] [depth:blue_green_public_space]. Urban character emphasizes "heritage as ground, blue-green as skeleton, innovation as content": buildings set back from the heritage axis and blue-green spine keep public space continuous and the heritage readable, forming a walkable, readable, participatory innovation district.

## Renewal Projects, Implementation Policy, and Phasing

Implementation proceeds in three phases [data:geometry/phasing.geojson#PHASE-001] [depth:phasing_implementation] [depth:renewal_project_list]: Phase 1 (Origin & Spine) establishes the "人" intersection and main-axis publicity via the Origin Community and a spine-park demonstration segment; Phase 2 (Two Strokes) deepens Zhongzhiyuan and Dazhongsi; Phase 3 (Weave & Inclusive) weaves the flanking built environment, completes accessibility and age-friendly provisions, and normalizes scenario markets. Each phase has evaluable intermediate goals (public-space continuity, accessibility coverage, node activation rate), and a renewal-project list spells out carriers, scale, and responsible-party suggestions for professional deepening.
