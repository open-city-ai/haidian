2. Three-Level Scope Framework

The proposal follows the announcement's three nested scopes Standard: the coordinated research area (~43.6 km²) answers AI ecosystem and future-city form; the overall design area (~11. 4 km²) answers urban-renewal and regulatory-plan-depth urban design; the key detailed-design area (~368.4 ha) details the three districts. All are mapped in compliance_matrix.json, ensuring every required task in announcement 1.3, 1.4, 1.5 and agent.1–agent.6 has a chapter, layer, metric, drawing, and HTML evidence.

The overall concept is "Synbiotic Intelligence Belt" (智脉共生带): the Jing-Zhang heritage park is the historical–public-space spine; Zhongzhiyuan, the Beijing AI Origin Community, and Dazhongsi are innovation anchors; universities, enterprises, communities, and rail stations form the daily network — organized as "one belt, three cores, multi-point scenarios, and a blue-green slow-mobility composite ring." "One belt" is a working method translating the three scopes, not a new redline; "three cores" map to the three key areas; "multi-point scenarios" are operable nodes for AI+ public services, industry services, and city life; the "composite ring" links slow mobility, green space, public space, and event routes Spatial data Metric.

11. Metrics, Area Recalculation, and Compliance Matrix

Metrics fall into three classes: ① spatial indicators recomputable from submitted geometry (boundary area, green ratio, public-space ratio, building footprint, phasing area); ② control indicators needing official regulatory plan or brief attachments (FAR, height, density, setback, redline, facility standards); ③ performance indicators needing operational/industry data (AI innovation index, talent density, slow-accessibility, event participation).
