# Centennial Jing-Zhang · Synbiotic Intelligence Belt: An AI-Native Innovation Corridor

## 1. Design Basis and Source List

This formal proposal takes the *Centennial Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt International Urban Design Open Call Pre-qualification Announcement* as its primary basis [source:OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT], and the reviewed public site package in `brief/site-package/` as its machine-readable basis [source:SITE-PACKAGE]. Before generation we read `design_brief.json`, `allowed_design_space.json`, `sources.json`, `enums/`, `ranges/`, `schemas/`, and `data/source_registry.json`, and organized outputs against the task, scope, source-use, and gap inventories in `data/processed/`. Every design judgment is decomposed into traceable sources, recomputable metrics, verifiable layers, and human-reviewable assumptions.

The site package currently provides only **provisional (rough) boundaries** — no official redline, key-area polygons, regulatory plan, road redlines, ownership, municipal, or heritage geometry [source:PROVISIONAL-BOUNDARIES-2026]. Therefore `geometry/site_boundary.geojson` and `geometry/key_areas.geojson` are explicitly marked `official_boundary=false` and `geometry_role=provisional_constraint`, usable only for generation, self-check, visualization, and design discussion — not as official redline, approval basis,  or precise-area basis [data:geometry/site_boundary.geojson#SITE-001]. The organizer's data gap does not block content scoring; once official polygons are released, site boundary, key areas, land use, roads, green space, public space, buildings, phasing, and metrics all require regeneration and recomputation.

Sources are managed by usability tier [source:SOURCE-REGISTRY]: `OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT` and `agent_taskbook.json` are formal-ready; `PROVISIONAL-BOUNDARIES-2026` is for temporary generation and discussion. `data/processed/agent_fact_pack.md` is a reading-navigation layer, not a new authority [source:PROCESSED-FACT-PACK].

## 2. Three-Level Scope Framework

The proposal follows the announcement's three nested scopes [standard:PROJECT-OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT]: the coordinated research area (~43.6 km²) answers AI ecosystem and future-city form; the overall design area (~11. 4 km²) answers urban-renewal and regulatory-plan-depth urban design; the key detailed-design area (~368.4 ha) details the three districts. All are mapped in `compliance_matrix.json`, ensuring every required task in announcement 1.3, 1.4, 1.5 and agent.1–agent.6 has a chapter, layer, metric, drawing, and HTML evidence.

The overall concept is **"Synbiotic Intelligence Belt" (智脉共生带)**: the Jing-Zhang heritage park is the historical–public-space spine; Zhongzhiyuan, the Beijing AI Origin Community, and Dazhongsi are innovation anchors; universities, enterprises, communities, and rail stations form the daily network — organized as "one belt, three cores, multi-point scenarios, and a blue-green slow-mobility composite ring." "One belt" is a working method translating the three scopes, not a new redline; "three cores" map to the three key areas; "multi-point scenarios" are operable nodes for AI+ public services, industry services, and city life; the "composite ring" links slow mobility, green space, public space, and event routes [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-001] [metric:key_area_count].

## 3. Coordinated Research Area: Industry Ecosystem and Future City

The core of the coordinated research area is a world-class AI innovation ecosystem [standard:PROJECT-OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT]. The proposal builds an "origination—conversion—experience—governance" innovation chain and responds to the three positionings (Centennial Jing-Zhang Cultural Belt, Urban AI Living-Experience Belt, AI-Fusion Innovation Belt), five functions (full-stack autonomous innovation, world-class AI ecosystem, AI+ scenario empowerment, intelligent AI-vibrant city, global AI-governance discourse), and the three-areas-two-wings framework [standard:PROJECT-AGENT-OPEN-CALL-TASKBOOK].

**Naming and visual identity.** The primary name "Synbiotic Intelligence Belt" fuses the century-old Jing-Zhang railway cultural vein with the AI intelligence vein. Logo direction: a continuous polyline merging the railway-rail section with a neuron dendrite — a railways→circuit translation symbolizing "from transport artery to intelligence artery," in a three-color system of rail grey-blue, Jing-Zhang red, and ecological green, extensible to signage, event identity, and digital-twin interfaces. All fonts, graphics, and marks are AI-generated conceptual proposals requiring clearance and professional deepening; they are not trademark filings or official-brand conclusions [standard:PROJECT-AGENT-OPEN-CALL-TASKBOOK].

**Global AI innovation ecosystem cases (5–8, background references).** Used to calibrate the "world-class" benchmark; public cases, not commitments:

| Case | City | Lesson | Implication for the Belt [source:AGENT-TASKBOOK] |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Station F | Paris, France | Old railway station reused as largest startup campus | Rail-to-Innovation stock renewal paradigm |
| Sidewalk / Quayside | Toronto, Canada | Sensor city and public data trust | Public-space data governance and privacy boundaries |

**AI innovation ecosystem map.** The "Zhongguancun Technology-Service Wing" (global factor allocation, IP and capital empowerment) and the "Xiaoyuehe Scenario-Empowerment Wing" (AI scenario empowerment, intelligent vibrant city) are two wings linking the three cores — Zhongzhiyuan (full-stack autonomy), Origin Community (conversion), Dazhongsi (smart-native formats) — into an "originate—convert—experience—govern" loop. Regionally, it connects north to Huairou Science City original innovation, east to Future Science City, south to the Economic-Technological Development Area, and west to other Beijing sci-tech districts, fulfilling Beijing-Tianjin-Hebei coordination [depth:three_level_scope_framework].

## 4. Overall Design Area: Urban Renewal and Regulatory-Plan-Depth Urban Design

The overall design area (~11.4 km², provisional) requires regulatory-plan-depth urban design [standard:MOHURD-CONTROL-DETAILED-PLANNING]. The proposal proposes "one axis linking three cores, a blue-green slow-mobility ring, and industry-service clusters": `geometry/land_use.geojson` partitions the submitted boundary seamlessly; `geometry/buildings.geojson` expresses renewal/retained building footprints; `geometry/roads.geojson` expresses micro-circulation and rail connection; `metrics.json` recomputes core areas, ratios, and layer counts [data:geometry/land_use.geojson#LU-001].

Land use follows territorial-spatial classification [standard:MNR-LAND-USE-CLASSIFICATION-GUIDE], including AI R&D innovation (0802 research baseline), blue-green public space (1401 park green), industry-service mix (09 commercial-business), and living support (0702 urban residential). Where official controls for height, FAR, density, green ratio, and setback are missing, they are uniformly marked `unknown` with recalculation paths in `assumptions.json`, never presented as approved values [depth:development_intensity_controls].

For transport and municipal: rail-station integration, road micro-circulation, non-motorized parking, parking supply, innovation-service platforms, talent-life services, distributed energy, and edge computing are prioritized [depth:traffic_rail_slow_parking]. Where road redlines, pipelines, fire, and municipal conditions are missing, they are formal-deepening prerequisites, not approved conclusions [depth:municipal_new_infrastructure].

Blue-green space uses the Jing-Zhang heritage park belt as backbone, integrating Qinghe, Xiaoyuehe, surrounding universities, enterprises, and communities, proposing north–south and east–west connected trails, cycleways, and green systems; identifying broken slow links, ring-road crossings, north/south park nodes; proposing parking, sports, innovation exchange, tech testing, and public-service composite use [depth:blue_green_public_space] [data:geometry/green_space.geojson#GREEN-001] [data:geometry/public_space.geojson#PUBLIC-001].

Urban character fuses Jing-Zhang railway history, Zhongguancun innovation culture, and AI culture, using Qinghua Yuan railway station and Beijing Film Academy resources for tone, building character, roof form, massing, interface, and public-art guidance [standard:MOHURD-URBAN-DESIGN-MEASURES]. Signage, cultural symbols, international narrative, AI pilgrimage landmarks, and honor-display system are proposed; all marks/fonts/images/portraits/enterprise marks require cleared sources.

## 11. Metrics, Area Recalculation, and Compliance Matrix

Indicators include overall-area, key-area area, green/public ratios, building footprint, project count, AI-scenario nodes, and self-check status. All known indicators are recomputable from GeoJSON or trusted sources; unknown indicators state reasons and submission prerequisites [metric:site_area_sqm] [data:geometry/green_space.geojson#GREEN-001].

Metrics fall into three classes: ① spatial indicators recomputable from submitted geometry (boundary area, green ratio, public-space ratio, building footprint, phasing area); ② control indicators needing official regulatory plan or brief attachments (FAR, height, density, setback, redline, facility standards); ③ performance indicators needing operational/industry data (AI innovation index, talent density, slow-accessibility, event participation). They enter `metrics.json`, `assumptions.json`, and `compliance_matrix.json` separately, avoiding conflating vision with approved conditions [depth:metrics_recalculation].

The compliance matrix is the task-response master file: each announcement and agent_taskbook task maps to chapter, layer, metric, drawing, HTML, source, assumption, and self-check. Any proposal omitting announcement  izations 1.3/1.4/1.5 or agent.1–agent.6 is barred from formal professional scoring [depth:risk_missing_data].
