# 展陈叙事 / Exhibition Narrative

“智轨织城”以四条线组织参观顺序：先辨认京张铁路的时间线，再理解开源—评测—试制—采用的创新线，随后观察轨道—步行—蓝绿公共空间如何支撑日常生活，最后以场景公开账本检查责任、人工复核、申诉与退出。三处重点区不是三个相同的 AI 园区，而是把技术从可复现验证送到日常共测，再把采用条件与失败经验送回上游的闭环。

Smart Rail Weaves the City uses four lines to structure the exhibition: rail heritage time; the open-source–evaluation–production–adoption innovation loop; rail–walking–blue-green daily continuity; and a public ledger for ownership, Human Review, grievance, and exit. The three key areas are not repeated AI districts but a feedback loop from reproducible validation to daily co-testing and enterprise adoption.
