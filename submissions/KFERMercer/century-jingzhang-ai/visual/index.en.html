Three-Level Scope

The proposal is organized on the coordinated research area, overall design area and key detailed design area: industrial judgments sit at the coordinated level, renewal and facility capacity at the overall level, and the three-zone detailed design at the key-area level.

compliance_matrix.json

Coordinated Research Area

Builds the AI industry ecology, three-zones/two-wings coordination, future city form and cultural narrative across 43.6 km².

  • AI innovation, talent and urban service chains
  • Global case conversion mechanisms
  • Brand naming, visual identity and communication

Overall Design Area

Organizes urban renewal, land-use structure, transport-municipal works and the Jing-Zhang Heritage Park vitality belt across 11.4 km².

  • Regulatory-plan-depth urban design
  • Renewal project inventory (JZ-01~JZ-06)
  • Blue-green slow-traffic composite system

Key Detailed Design Area

Develops the three key zones to detailed-design depth, with functional programs, building moves, public space and transport organization.

  • Zhongzhiyuan AI Independent Innovation Acceleration Area
  • Beijing AI Origin Community
  • Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster

Key Areas

Each key area states its industrial positioning, spatial strategy, building and public-space moves, transport interfaces and implementation dependencies.

key_areas.geojson

Zhongzhiyuan AI Independent Innovation Acceleration Area

Garden-style independent-innovation district organized around the national platform, industrial display, Qinghe culture, external traffic and low-carbon exchange spaces.

Beijing AI Origin Community

Near-campus achievement-conversion district organized around university source innovation, the open-source community, talent services, retain-renovate-demolish and station integration.

Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster

Urban smart-economy district organized around leading enterprises, agent-era businesses, commercial services, composite green-space use and four-quadrant intersection connectivity.

AI Scenario Cards

Ten scenario cards cover industry services and urban functions; each card specifies its target users, spatial carrier, data boundary, human review point, failure fallback and operating entity (see the scenario card table in the proposal body).

10 AI scenario cards
01 Open-Source Release Hall

Release and model-evaluation node for developers, enterprises and universities, self-governed by the open-source community.

02 Security Governance Sandbox

Test field for agents in controlled public space; evaluation conclusions co-signed by certified experts.

03 Edge Computing Stations

Resource-scheduling node combining distributed energy and edge computing; allocations approved by humans.

04 AI Slow-Traffic Navigation

Breakpoint detection and detour guidance from public maps and voluntary reports; remediation decisions approved by management.

05 Dazhongsi International Roadshow Lounge

Roadshow and matchmaking space for enterprises, investors and media; roadshows and signings hosted by humans.

06 Qinghe Low-Carbon Innovation Corridor

Low-carbon innovation display and energy-saving hints along the Qinghe waterfront; maintenance dispatched by humans.

07 Near-Campus Achievement Street

Street-level interface for university achievement display, matching and conversion; conversion and legal handled by humans.

08 Data Element Reception Hall

Data-element catalog display and compliance hints; transactions and authorizations approved by humans.

09 AI Life Service Model Street

Community-commerce service guidance and queue prediction; complaints handled by humans.

10 Global AI Activity Week Route

Route organization for the developer festival, scenario open day, competitions and city experience.
