About the mark: a railway track growing upward into a layered urban section. The track comes from the century-old Jing-Zhang trunk line; the bars are the four protocol layers; the dots on them are protocol nodes; the amber dot on the top layer stands for the final decision that humans keep.

FRAGO-URBAN-OS · JING-ZHANG AI BELT GOVERNANCE PROTOCOL DESIGNProgrammable City

The Jing-Zhang AI innovation belt is not a city that "uses AI to assist planning". It is a city whose governance is designed as an Agent OS — participation, approval, piloting, evaluation and knowledge capture are all turned into protocols, driven by events and fully auditable. AI and citizens collaborate on one shared protocol, and humans keep the final decision.

Spatially it is one protocol bus (the heritage spine running the full corridor), three protocol loops (closed slow-mobility rings inside the key areas), eight protocol nodes (public interfaces shared by release and review), plus a transverse four-layer protocol section.

Assumptions

Each item below is an explicit treatment forced by a public-data gap or by this proposal's own discipline — not an implicit inference.

AssumptionGapHow this proposal handles it
No official redlineNo official polygon, CAD or GIS redline with a verifiable coordinate system is available publicly.All boundaries use the provisional extents supplied by the repository, flagged in the data as provisional and not an official redline. Once an official redline is released, every layer, metric, drawing and this page must be recomputed as one package.
Road corridor positionsThe announcement gives only road names as written extents, with no alignment data.Road corridors are placed roughly by name as indicative bands, flagged in the data as existing conditions at low precision. They do not represent road redlines, alignments or cross-sections.
Watercourses and heritage extentsNo statutory data is available for blue lines, heritage protection extents or construction control zones.Shown as indicative advisory extents and flagged as low precision; the statutory extents are those published by the water and heritage authorities.
Statutory control indicatorsFloor area ratio, building height, building density, green ratio and setback controls are absent from the public package.The corresponding metrics are left empty with a stated reason rather than filled with guesses, and this proposal also deliberately withholds any development-intensity conclusion.
Existing land-use classificationThere is no authoritative mapped data for existing land-use classification.Existing fabric is classified only conceptually to express structural relationships and is flagged as existing conditions at low precision. It implies no retain/renovate/demolish, tenure or reclassification conclusion.
AI scenario statusNo scenario is approved for operation.All are labelled "pending human review", "conceptual illustration", "not approved for operation", with a human adjudication gate in the mechanism.
