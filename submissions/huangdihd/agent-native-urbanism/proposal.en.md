# Smartbody Nativism Urbanism: Urban Design of the Centennial Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt

## Design Basis and Source List

This plan is based primarily on the qualifications pre-screening announcement for the international Urban Design competition for the Centennial Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt issued by the Haidian Branch of the Beijing Municipal Commission of Planning and Natural Resources [source:DATA-SRC-OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT-20260509]. Based on the agent task book [source:DATA-SRC-AGENT-TASKBOOK-20260518], adhere to the Urban Design Management Measures issued by the Ministry of Housing and Urban-Rural Development [standard:PROJECT-OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT], [standard:MOHURD-URBAN-DESIGN-MEASURES] and The Control Detailed Planning Compilation Method [standard:MOHURD-CONTROL-DETAILED-PLANNING] serve as professional depth references. Use the Natural Resources Ministry's《Land and Sea Use Classification Guide》[standard:MNR-LAND-USE-CLASSIFICATION-GUIDE] as the standard for land use coding.

Data Registration Form [source:DATA-SRC-PROVISIONAL-BOUNDARIES-20260605] shows: formal available data 5 items (announcements, task orders, three professional standards), provisional-only data 1 item (Provisional Boundary polygon). All current spatial layers are derived from the Provisional Boundary, and area metrics [metric:site_area_sqm] will be recalculated upon the release of official data.

This submission is the world's first Urban Design project where an AI agent fully participates in the entire process—from reading the structured task book, generating GeoJSON spatial data, calculating indicator matrices, to self-inspection and validation—all completed by the Hermes Agent. This itself is a practical proof of the core argument of the proposal: **Agents are not just labelers but true co-creators**.

## Three-Level Scope Framework

### Coordinated Research Area

Area 43.6 square kilometers [metric:site_area_sqm], bounded by the North Fifth Ring Road to the north, the Jingzhang Expressway to the east, Xisi Street to the south, and Wancuihe Road to the west [source:DATA-SRC-OFFICIAL-ANNOUNCEMENT-20260509]. This area covers the complete Jing-Zhang Railway Cultural Corridor extending from the Tsinghua Yuan Railway Station southward through the clusters of universities such as North University of China and North University of Posts and Telecommunications, reaching Dazhongsi, and includes the Zhongguancun Technology Services Wing and the Xiaoyue River Scenario Enablement Wing. The Coordinated Research Area aims to establish an overall framework for industrial strategy, AI ecosystem, and future urban form, without involving plot-level control indicators.

### Overall Design Area

Area 11.4 square kilometers, with the urban and industrial areas and zones within 1-2 kilometers of the Jing-Zhang Heritage Park as the main focus. The Overall Design Area's task is to achieve the depth of Urban Design in the Regulatory Detailed Planning [standard:MOHURD-CONTROL-DETAILED-PLANNING], forming the overall framework for Land-Use Layout, building scale, traffic organization, municipal facilities, and Public Space. The current provisional boundary is used [data:geometry/site_boundary.geojson#PROV-SITE-001], and all area calculations are based on the EPSG:4548 projection.

### Key-Area Detailed Design Area

Area of 368.4 hectares, extending from north to south, includes the Zhongzhiyuan AI Independent Innovation Acceleration Area (192.1 ha), the Beijing AI Origin Community (104.3 ha), and the Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster (72.0 ha) [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson]. The key areas are tasked with achieving the depth of Urban Design as outlined in the Integrated Planning Implementation Plan, focusing on the detailed design of industrial functions, architectural forms, the Demolish–Renovate–Retain strategy, Public Space connectivity, and traffic organization. (Demolish–Renovate–Retain Strategy)

The three levels of scope are implemented from industrial strategy → overall Urban Design → detailed design. The precision limitations of the provisional boundaries primarily affect the absolute accuracy of area values, but do not impede the evaluation of design concepts, spatial structures, and functional layouts [source:DATA-SRC-PROVISIONAL-BOUNDARIES-20260605].

## Coordinated Research Area: Industry and Future City Research

### Core Concept: Computing Power as a Utility

One hundred years ago, Zhan Tianyou's Jing-Zhang Railway transformed the "right to travel" from a privilege into a public good—ticket prices moved from a tiered system based on distance to a uniform fare, monthly passes, and student discounts, ultimately making railway travel a basic public service. One hundred years later, AI tokens are undergoing a similar evolution: from per-token billing to token bundles to unlimited plans, ultimately becoming as ubiquitous as water and electricity in the city's infrastructure.

This plan transforms the analogy into spatial strategies: **establish a three-tier AI infrastructure node system**, aligning "computing exchange hubs—intelligent service stations—AI consumption terminals" along the Jing-Zhang Railway Heritage Park. This allows AI to transform from an abstract concept in the cloud into a community service that is perceivable, accessible, and consumable.

**Overall Concept**: Agent-Native Urbanism —— Cities are not built first and then augmented with AI; instead, the entire process from planning, construction, to operation is co-created by Agents.

**Naming System:**

- English Identifier: **JZIB** — Simple, Registerable, and Internationalizable

**Visual Identity Direction**: Use the classic "Z" shape of the Jing-Zhang Railway as the basic graphic for the logo, overlaying digital node points. The color scheme adopts deep blue (technology) + warm copper (heritage) in dual tones. The font direction uses Source Han Serif + a monospaced programming font to reflect the dual identity of "humanistic + technological."

## Detailed Design of Key Areas

### Zhongzhiyuan AI Independent Innovation Acceleration Area (Compute Power Exchange Bureau)

**Location**: Industrial-grade AI infrastructure hub, analogous to a telecommunications hub, responsible for regional-level computational power scheduling and data routing [data:geometry/key_areas.geojson#PROV-KEY-001].

**Spatial Structure**: "One Core, Two Axes, Three Areas"
- One Core: Computing Hub Center (Distributed GPU Cluster + Cooling Facilities + Data Exchange Platform)
- Two Axes: Innovation Avenue (East-West Industrial Axis) + Computational Power Corridor (North-South Infrastructure Axis)
- Three Zones: AI Incubator Cluster (East), Data Center Campus (West), Developer Community (South)

**Building Update Strategy** (Conceptual Recommendation):
- Preserve: North China University of Technology Research Building Cluster, Existing Green Belts
- Transformation: Old Industrial Park → AI Joint Office + Shared Laboratory
- New Construction: Computing Center (2-3 levels of low-density, high-energy consumption buildings requiring special municipal amenities), Developer Square, Open Source Achievements Gallery

**AI Scenario**:
- SC-09 Computing Power Trading Market: Enterprises can buy and sell idle GPU computing power, forming an "electricity market" in the AI era.
- SC-03 Developer Co-Creation Space: Open 24/7, providing GPU cluster access, open-source data sets, and AI toolchain

**Risk**: The computational center has high energy consumption and requires a distributed energy solution; data center cooling needs to be coordinated with the surrounding community; all facility layout is a Conceptual Recommendation [assumption:A-CONCEPT-001].

- AI Origin Community: Talent Apartments with high density (12-18F), Service Stations with low density (2-4F), and Commercial Offices with medium density (8-12F)

**Dismantle-Renovate-Retain Logic**: (Demolish–Renovate–Retain Strategy)
- Current university campus buildings (Beihang, Beihua): preserve, complementing the innovative district function.

- Old Industrial Parks: Renovation and Upgrade to AI Incubators and Co-Working Spaces
- Low-efficiency Warehouse Land: Demolish and Rebuild as a Data Center or Public Space
- Traditional Commercial Buildings: Renovated into AI Experience Consumption Spaces

All conclusions of the demolish–renovate–retain strategy are conceptual directions and do not constitute a commitment to demolition or renovation [standard:PROJECT-AGENT-OPEN-CALL-TASKBOOK]. (Demolish–Renovate–Retain Strategy)

## Transport, Rail, Municipal Infrastructure, and Public Services

Traffic and municipal design are based on [standard:MOHURD-URBAN-DESIGN-MEASURES], [standard:MOHURD-CONTROL-DETAILED-PLANNING], [depth:traffic_rail_slow_parking], [depth:municipal_new_infrastructure], and [data:geometry/roads.geojson].

### Traffic Organization

**Track**: The Jing-Zhang Railway Heritage Park currently has the Metro Line 13 (Zhichunlu Station, Dazhongsi Station) and Changping Line. It is recommended to add bus shuttle services at three core zone nodes to achieve the integration of "track + slow travel + AI shuttle" networks.

**Slow Zones**: Construct an 8-kilometer continuous cycling and pedestrian path along the Jing-Zhang Heritage Park, with a width of no less than 6 meters (3 meters for cycling + 3 meters for pedestrian use). Address the slow zone discontinuities at the intersection of Zhi Chun Road and Xue Yuan Road, and at the intersection of Dazhongsi East Road and West Zhiyuanmen Avenue.

**AI Traffic Scenarios**: Pilot low-speed autonomous shuttle services (speed limit 20 km/h), intelligent signal optimization (dynamically adjusted based on pedestrian/bicyclist traffic), and robot delivery corridors (dedicated low-speed lanes) in three core areas.

### municipal facilities

**New Infrastructure**:
- Distributed Computing Nodes: Place an edge computing cabinet every 500m along the Jing-Zhang Heritage Park to provide community-level AI inference services.
- 5G/6G Base Station Dense Coverage: Supporting Low Latency Requirements for AI Applications
- Distributed Energy: Co-locate solar energy generation and storage facilities with the data center to reduce grid pressure [assumption:A-INFRA-001]

**Traditional Infrastructure**: Plumbing, electricity, gas, and other capacities await verification by professional municipal teams [assumption:A-INFRA-001].
