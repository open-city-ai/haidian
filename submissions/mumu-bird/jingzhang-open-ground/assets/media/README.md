本目录收录封面与场景概念图。所有场景图均服务于方案中的明确空间主张，并在 A3、A0 或离线 HTML 中标注为“概念推演”。其中 `scene-11-inclusive-services.jpg` 用于 A3 第35页，表现人工主导的社区健康、AI教育与无障碍导航；图中人物、设施与运行状态均为设计推演，不代表现状已经建成。

This directory contains the cover and conceptual scene visualisations. Every scene supports a named spatial claim and is labelled as a concept in the A3 booklet, A0 board or offline HTML. `scene-11-inclusive-services.jpg`, used on A3 page 35, visualises human-led community health, AI learning and accessible navigation; people, facilities and operations shown are proposed rather than existing.
