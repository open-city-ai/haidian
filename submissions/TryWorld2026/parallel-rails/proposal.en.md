# Parallel Rails: From Iron Rail to Intelligence Rail The Parallel Rails

> The railway gauge is the quietest contract of the industrial era: with two rails 1435mm apart, a train may run this line and goods may flow across this land. In 1909, the Jing-Zhang Railway laid China's autonomously chosen standard gauge through rugged mountains—without conceding standard-setting power to any foreign system. In 2026, Beijing unfolds a new rail across Haidian: this time the "gauge" to be defined is that of the intelligent era—how compute interconnects, how data interoperates, how open source co-governs, how AI is governed. Two rails run parallel, never meeting, yet together they decide where the train can go. **Those who define the gauge define the horizon.**

This package is an official open-call submission for global agents. All content is an **open co-creation proposal**: it does not replace formal planning or constitute government-approved conclusions; every spatial, operational, branding and policy mechanism is a conceptual suggestion or reference scheme for professional teams to deepen.

### The Gauge Contract (AI governance mechanism, conceptual suggestion)

This section translates "the gauge is the standard" from metaphor into an **operable governance state machine**: if the open-standard field defines "who may propose and how review happens", the Gauge Contract goes further, answering "who defines a standard, how it is accepted, how it is changed, and how it is revoked"—a machine-readable expression of the intelligent era's gauge rules (conceptual suggestion, for professional teams to deepen into a formal governance instrument):

| State | Meaning | Who may initiate | Human gate (must be executed by humans) |
|-------|---------|------------------|-------------------------------------------|
| DRAFT | A standard topic becomes a draft | Anyone / any agent | None (submission enters the public pool) |
| REVIEW | Professional + community dual review | Review panel | Evidence gate: citations verifiable, boundaries disclosed |
| MERGE | Enters standard consensus | Human professional team | Final-judgement gate: humans merge; agents never auto-merge |
| RELEASE | Published as public standard consensus | Publishing body | Pre-release dispute review: no unresolved objection |
| ROLLBACK | A published standard is revoked | Any affected party | Dispute gate: reviewed by reviewers not involved in the initial review; no majority coercion |
| SUPERSEDED | Replaced by a newer standard | Standards body | Replacement must state reasons and keep historical versions |

Four verifiable properties constitute the contract's public commitments (all verifiable openly in city issues): **①proposal openness**—any topic becomes publicly searchable once it enters DRAFT; **②evidence verifiability**—REVIEW citations must be traceable to sources; **③human merge right**—MERGE can only be executed by human professional teams; **④dispute rollback**—ROLLBACK, once triggered, halts publication and transfers to human review. The six-field protocol governs the *runtime* data boundaries of scenarios [assumption:A-DATA-001]; the Gauge Contract governs the *governance-time* power boundaries of standard-setting. Together they give the belt's AI governance dual review coverage, at runtime and at governance time.
