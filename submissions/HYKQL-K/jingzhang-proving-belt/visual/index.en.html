Land-use partition · building scale (用地分区 建筑)

The overall design area is partitioned seamlessly and without overlap into nine longitudinal segments and five transverse bands, giving 45 cells. Land-use codes follow the national land and sea use classification guideline. Building footprints and gross floor area are derived from assumed site coverage and average storeys per cell; they are indicative, not regulatory-plan conclusions. Floor area ratio and height caps await publication of official indicators.

Mobility · blue-green public space (交通慢行 蓝绿公共空间)

The proving-spine greenway along the Jing-Zhang Heritage Park forms the north-south skeleton. Seven east-west pedestrian stitches cross the rail corridor and remove detour barriers; three rail interchange connections carry first-and-last-mile low-speed autonomous validation. Green space runs continuously along the spine; public space comprises four proving plazas and seven corridor forecourts. The greenway carriageway is suggested at no less than 6 m clear width, with robot validation lanes physically separated from footways and human-priority stretches retained.

Assumptions (假设)

  • A-BOUNDARY-001: all areas rest on the organiser-registered provisional rough surrogate extents and must be recomputed in full once the official precise red line is published.
  • A-TOPOLOGY-001: the land-use partition is seamless and non-overlapping, with residual far below the validation tolerance; partition lines are conceptual and express no road red line, parcel tenure or approval boundary.
  • A-COVERAGE-001: building footprint area is generated from an assumed site coverage per land-use cell; it is neither a survey of existing conditions nor a regulatory-plan conclusion.
  • A-FLOORS-001: average storeys are an assumption of this proposal; the gross floor area derived from it is indicative and expresses no floor area ratio control.
  • A-MOBILITY-001: greenway and stitch alignments are conceptual and express no road red line or existing alignment; rail crossings must be developed with the railway and municipal authorities.
  • A-SCENARIO-001: AI scenario points are indicative locations and express no facility siting conclusion; industry validation scenarios require entry conditions agreed jointly by the sector regulator and the participating firms.
  • A-CONTROLS-001: all five official control indicators (floor area ratio, building height, site coverage, green ratio, setback) are currently missing; this proposal neither back-calculates nor assumes them.
