## 一句话方案 / One-sentence scheme

**京张智带 · 城市验证长廊**：把百年京张 AI 创新带设计成一条持续运转、可被公众审计的城市验证长廊，而不是再增加一个 AI 主题园区。

**The Jing-Zhang Intelligence Belt · Urban Proving Corridor**: design the Centennial Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt as a continuously operating, publicly auditable urban proving corridor rather than one more AI-themed park.

## 核心装置：验证单元 / Core device: the validation unit

方案提出可复制的最小单位——**验证单元**，由四件套构成：空间载体、验证协议、公示看板、退出机制。三处重点区共享同一套建造标准：不小于社区级的开放广场或廊段、一块公示看板、一条无障碍连续通行面、一个纯人工服务窗口。

The scheme proposes a replicable minimum unit — the **validation unit** — made of four parts: a spatial carrier, a validation protocol, a disclosure board and an exit mechanism. All three key areas share one construction standard: an open plaza or corridor segment of at least neighbourhood scale, a disclosure board, a continuous barrier-free path of travel, and a staffed non-digital service window.

三条硬约束贯穿全案 / Three hard constraints run through the whole entry:

1. **失败必须公示** — 验证失败、暂停与接管事件与成功案例同等披露，不得选择性公开。
   **Failure must be disclosed** — failures, suspensions and human takeovers are published on the same footing as successes; selective disclosure is not permitted.
2. **人工兜底路径不得更慢更贵** — 每个场景保留不依赖智能终端的人工办理路径，且不得更慢、更贵或需要额外往返。
   **The human fallback may be neither slower nor costlier** — every scenario keeps a non-digital service path that is not slower, not more expensive and requires no extra trip.
3. **默认不参与** — 居民无需任何操作即处于不被采集的状态；本地处理优先，留存期限写入公开协议。
   **Non-participation by default** — residents are outside data capture without taking any action; local processing comes first and retention periods are written into the public protocol.

## 空间骨架 / Spatial skeleton

纵向九段、横向五带。中间的京张遗址公园验证主廊同时是连续公园绿地、南北慢行主线、低速自动驾驶与配送机器人的验证主线，以及公众可直接看见验证过程的展示界面。中关村科技服务翼供给资源，小月河场景赋能翼供给需求，两翼一带构成"资源—验证—需求"的完整回路。

Nine longitudinal segments and five transverse bands. The central Jing-Zhang Heritage Park proving spine is at once a continuous park, the north-south active-travel trunk, the test line for low-speed autonomous vehicles and delivery robots, and the display frontage where the public can watch validation happen. The Zhongguancun technology-service wing supplies resources and the Xiaoyue River scenario-enablement wing supplies demand, so the two wings and the belt close a resource–validation–demand loop.

三处重点区分别承担验证链条的不同环节：众智园 AI 自主创新加速区（约 192.1 ha，北段）承担全栈自主创新与中试；北京 AI 原点社区（约 104.3 ha，中段）承担生活场景与公众参与；大钟寺 AI 产业聚集区（约 72.0 ha，南段）承担智能原生消费与交通接驳。

The three key areas take different links of the validation chain: the Zhongzhi Park AI Autonomous Innovation Acceleration Area (about 192.1 ha, north) carries full-stack autonomous innovation and pilot production; the Beijing AI Origin Community (about 104.3 ha, centre) carries everyday-life scenarios and public participation; the Dazhongsi AI Industry Cluster (about 72.0 ha, south) carries AI-native consumption and transit interchange.

## 使用边界 / Boundaries of use

**本方案没有取得官方精确红线。** 公告给出了三层范围的面积和文字四至，但未附边界图或 GIS 数据；方案使用主办方登记的临时粗略 polygon 作为工作范围，全部要素标注 `official_boundary=false` 与 `geometry_role=provisional_constraint`。同时，容积率、建筑高度、建筑密度、绿地率、退线五项官方控制指标在公开渠道全部缺失，方案将其作为显式指标写入指标体系而非以假定值替代。官方边界与控制条件公布后，须整体复算全部图层、指标、图纸与展示成果，而不是只替换单个文件。

**This entry does not hold the official precise boundary.** The announcement gives areas and written extents for the three scope tiers but attaches no boundary drawing or GIS data; the entry therefore works on the organizer's registered provisional rough polygons, with every feature tagged `official_boundary=false` and `geometry_role=provisional_constraint`. Five official controls — floor area ratio, building height, building density, green ratio and setback — are entirely absent from public channels; rather than substituting assumed values, the entry records the gap as an explicit metric. Once the official boundary and controls are published, all layers, metrics, drawings and display deliverables must be recalculated as a whole, not patched file by file.
