## 1. Executive Summary & Core Positioning

The **Centennial Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt** (百年京张AI创新带) represents Haidian's strategic initiative to bridge historical industrial-railway legacy with cutting-edge artificial intelligence infrastructure. 

This design proposal establishes the **"Compute-Green Dual Corridor" (算绿双轴·智算孪生城)** spatial paradigm:
1. **Underground Axis**: A zero-latency optical compute and liquid-cooled energy infrastructure conduit running beneath the Jing-Zhang linear corridor.
2. **Above-ground Axis**: A linear green ecological park seamlessly integrating autonomous micro-mobility, robotic testbeds, hidden edge-compute mounds, and public developer spaces.

### Three Strategic Positionings
- **百年京张文化带 (Centennial Heritage)**: Honoring Zhan Tianyou's pioneer railway spirit while embedding digital heritage experiences into urban public space.
- **都市AI生活体验带 (Urban AI Lifestyle)**: Human-robot co-living streetscapes, AI accessibility guides, and developer-friendly public amenities.
- **AI融合创新带 (AI Fusion & Innovation)**: Gigawatt-scale green compute hubs, embodied robotics testbeds, and long-term open-source community assets.

## 3. Detailed Urban Design: Zhongzhiyuan Core Hub

As the primary pilot engine, **Zhongzhiyuan (192.1 ha)** is designed with five key infrastructure-spatial innovations:

1. **Underground Infrastructure Conduit (地下算力/能源共同管廊)**
   - Houses high-density optical fiber conduits, liquid-cooling distribution pipes, and high-voltage green microgrid connections, ensuring microsecond-level latency between R&D labs.
2. **Green Thermal Closed-Loop (算力余热-城市生态热泵闭环)**
   - Recovers 100% of liquid-cooled compute waste heat to heat R&D offices, greenhouses, and public amenities during winter, demonstrating zero-carbon urban thermal cycling.
3. **Embedded Edge Compute Mounds (景观隐形算力绿丘)**
   - Integrates small/medium edge computing nodes under artificial green landscape mounds along the railway park, transforming cooling chimneys into artistic light beacons.
4. **Embodied AI & Robotics Testbed (具身智能全场景测试场)**
   - Provides multi-terrain robotic testing tracks, underground automated logistics hubs, and rooftop eVTOL/Drone landing docks.
5. **3D Human-Robot Co-Living Streetscape (三维人机协同街道)**
   - **Sub-level -1**: Autonomous delivery robotics corridor.
   - **Ground Level**: Pedestrian greenways and low-speed autonomous shuttle lanes.
   - **Sky-bridge Level**: Elevated R&D collaboration skywalks.
