项目概述 | Project Overview

中文

"钟鸣道合 分时共生"方案以百年京张铁路为时空纽带，构建AI创新带的全新城市范式。通过分时共享、功能复合、生态融合的设计策略，将43.6平方公里的研究范围打造为集科技创新、文化传承、生态宜居于一体的未来城市示范区。

方案核心理念：以时间维度重构空间使用，实现城市功能的24小时动态平衡，让同一片区在不同时段服务不同人群，最大化空间价值与社会效益。

English

The "Clock Echo · Time-Shared Symbiosis" proposal uses the centennial Jingzhang Railway as a spatiotemporal nexus to create a new urban paradigm for the AI Innovation Belt. Through time-sharing, functional mixing, and ecological integration strategies, the 43.6 km² research scope becomes a future urban demonstration zone integrating technological innovation, cultural heritage, and ecological livability.

Core concept: Reconstruct spatial use through temporal dimensions, achieving 24-hour dynamic balance of urban functions. The same district serves different populations at different times, maximizing spatial value and social benefits.
